# The Reasons for and Importance of
## Carefully Studying and Apprehending
## the Revelation concerning the Triune God
## Message One
## OUTLINE OF
## THE MESSAGES FOR THE FULL-TIME TRAINING
## THE TRIUNE GOD

**Scripture Reading:** Gen. 1:1-3; Exo. 3:6; Eph. 4:4-6; John 17:20-23

## I. The truth concerning the Triune God is the capital truth of the Christian
## faith. To be Christian, in the classic and historical sense of the term, is to
## believe in the Triune God:
### A. To be a Christian is to believe in the person and work of Christ.
### B. Our faith in Christ is one that recognizes that Christ is God just as the Father
### is God. This implies plurality in the Godhead.
### C. Hence, to believe in Christ in this way requires our full belief in and
### appreciation of the Divine Trinity.

## II. To properly understand what God has done and is doing, we must see
## that every operation of His is triune:
### A. This is illustrated in Genesis 1:1-3, where the entire Triune God operates to
### create:
#### 1. "In the beginning God created..." (v. 1), implying the entire Godhead: God
#### (Heb. Elohim) is grammatically plural, while created is grammatically
#### singular.
#### "And the Spirit of God was brooding..." (v. 2), explicitly naming the third of
#### the Divine Trinity.
#### 3. "And God said..." (v. 3), implying the second (the Word) of the Divine
#### Trinity.
### B. This is also illustrated in Ephesians 3:14-19, where the entire Triune God
### operates to constitute the members of the Body of Christ with the divine
### riches for the full expression of God:
#### 1. "I bow my knees unto the Father" (v. 14)—the Father is the source of the
#### constituting.
#### 2. "According to the riches of His glory, to be strengthened with power
#### through His Spirit" (v. 16)—the Spirit is the means by which the riches are
#### constituted into the Body.
#### 3. "That Christ may make His home in your hearts" (v. 17)-Christ is the
#### agent of God's indwelling in the believers to constitute them the Body of
#### Christ.
#### 4. "That you may be filled unto all the fullness of God" (v. 19)—the expression
#### of the riches of God, the fullness of God, is the goal of the constituting of
#### the Body of Christ.

### C. Every single action of God should be understood to be the operation of all
### three of the Divine Trinity.
### D. Hence, to fully understand what God does, we must pay attention to His
### triune existence and operation.

## III. The Scriptures are constructed with the Triune God as their intrinsic
## content:
### A. This is illustrated in Genesis 12—50, where the God of Abraham, the God of
### Isaac, and the God of Jacob (cf. Exo. 3:6) operate:
#### 1. "God's calling to Abraham was the work of God the Father." (LS / Genesis
#### 517)
#### 2. "The experience of Isaac...fits the second of the Triune God, God the Son.
#### The Lord Jesus, as the Son of God, came out of the Father (John 16:28),
#### inherited all that the Father is and has (John 16:15), and accomplished all
#### the Father's will (John 6:38). Isaac's life corresponds to His." (LS / Genesis
#### 518)
#### 3.
#### "In Jacob we see regeneration, discipline, transformation, and the growth
#### and maturity in life. All of this is the work of the Spirit." (LS / Genesis 519)
### B. Other examples can be found in Luke 15, Ephesians 1:3-14, Revelation 21:9
### 22:5.
### C. A great many other portions of the Scriptures manifest the Triune God as
### their intrinsic content.

## IV. As believers we are the members of the Body of Christ, which is the
## organism of the processed and dispensing Triune God and which will
## consummate in the New Jerusalem as the incorporation of the Triune
## God with the tripartite man:
### A. The Body of Christ takes the Father as its source, the Son as its element, and
### the Spirit as its essence-Eph. 4:4-6.
### B. As the organism of the Triune God, the Body of Christ expresses the Son as
### the embodiment of the Triune God.
### C. The Body, as the organism of the Triune God, consummates in the New
### Jerusalem as an incorporation of the Triune God with the redeemed, trans-
### formed, and glorified tripartite man.
### D. As the organism of the Triune God and an incorporation with the Triune God,
### we should express the mode of existence among the three of the Triune God in
### our living in the Body-John 17:20-23.
