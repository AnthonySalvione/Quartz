## I. Deviations being slight shifts from the proper understanding of the
## truth:
### A. As the experience of the Triune God began to wane in the early church, so did
### the proper understanding of the truth concerning the Triune God.
### B. Deviations from the scriptural revelation of the Triune God progressed and
### soon became deviant teachings that were held by segments of the church and
### were used to divide the believers. As such, these teachings became heresies:
#### 1. Deviations are simply shifts from the proper understanding of the scrip-
#### tural revelation. These are less serious.
#### 2. Heresies are deviant teachings that form divisions and "take away" (Gk.
#### hairo) the believers from the Body of Christ. These are more serious.
### C. In reactions to heresies, the ancient church held councils and defined creeds to
### establish the proper understanding of the truth on critical issues and to repel
### dangerous teachings:
#### 1. Councils were invoked by the Roman government less to combat heresy
#### and more to restore peace among the factions in the church.
#### 2. The major councils produced creeds (from the Latin word Credo, I believe')
#### to answer specific heresies that had arisen in the church.
#### 3. The creeds typically relate to the doctrines regarding the person of Christ
#### but always rely on the doctrines of the Trinity. The major creeds are the
#### Apostles (3 century), the Nicean (325), the Nicene-Constantinopolitan
#### (381), and the Chalcedonian (451).
#### 4. The creeds are problematic because they are incomplete:
##### a. No creed can encompass the full perimeter of the truth as the Bible
##### does.
##### b. The creeds are particularly weak in their apprehension of the Spirit,
##### the Third of the Divine Trinity.
#### 5. Brother Lee enumerated at least eighteen items that are missing in the
#### creeds (see The Revelation and Vision of God, pp. 54-62, and The Divine
#### and Mystical Realm, pp. 11-16):
##### a. The last Adam (Christ in the flesh) becoming the life-giving Spirit-the

##### b. The Lord being the Spirit and the Lord Spirit-2 Cor. 3:17-18.
##### c. The Spirit that gives life being not yet before the glorification (resurrec-
##### tion) of Christ-John 7:39.

##### d. The compound Spirit typified by the anointing ointment-Exo. 30:23-
##### 25.

##### f. The seven Spirits of God-Rev. 1:4; 3:1; 4:5; 5:6.
##### g. The Son being called the Eternal Father-Isa. 9:6.
##### h. The coinherence of the Father, the Son, and the Spirit-John 14:10, 20;
##### 10:38; 17:21, 23; 8:29; Luke 4:1.
##### i. The Son being the effulgence of God's (the Father's) glory and the

## II. Deviations that deny the three eternal divine hypostases—modalism:
### A Technically, modalism is one of two varieties of monarchianism, which is the
### teaching that there is no plurality in God. Monarchianism holds that He is one
### (Gk. mono-) existing source of Being (Gk. arche) but that the temporal mani-
### festation of His one Being is manifold.
### B. Modalistic monarchianism, as it is properly called and still popular in the
### modern age, teaches that the one Being of God manifests in time in three
### stages, or modes the Father in the Old Testament, the Son in the Gospels,
### and the Spirit in the church. When each is manifested, the others do not exist.
### C. Dynamic monarchianism, also known as adoptionism and much more popular
### in the early church, teaches that the one God adopted the man Jesus and
### made Him the Son of God by sheer divine power. The Father is viewed as the
### true God and Creator, the Son as a creature (with or without divine essence),
### and the Holy Spirit as some impersonal force.

### D. Modalism is deviant in that it fails to recognize the scriptural revelation that
### the Three of the Godhead exist eternally.

## III. Deviations that deny the one complete God-tritheism:
### A. Tritheism teaches that God exists as three separate persons. God is eternally
### three, but not eternally one.
### B. The notion of three separate persons is deviant according to the teaching of
### the Bible (and of the historic Christian church, for that matter).
### C. A great number of Christians today, if not most of them, are deviant in their
### understanding of the Trinity in that they implicitly hold to a tritheistic view.
### Many understand that when the Son was incarnated, He was separate from
### the Father and the Spirit, that the Father and the Spirit were not there. An
### even greater number hold steadfastly to the mistaken notion that at the cruci-
### fixion of Christ, the Son was alone and that the Father and the Spirit were not
### involved. (Many confuse the ancient heresy called patripassianism with the
### Father's involvement in the death of Christ.)

## IV. Deviations that ignore the revelation of the Trinity of God-indifference:
### A. While many Christians simply do not care for the matter of the Trinity, it is
### the capital truth of the Christian faith. This is a deviation from the scriptural
### revelation concerning the Triune God.
### B. The writers of the New Testament could hardly express their understanding of
### God except in a triune way (cf. Eph. 1:3-14; 3:14-21; 1 Pet. 1:2).
### C. Because our experience and growth in the Christian life, our proper living in
### the church as the Body of Christ, and our ultimate destiny depend on God
### being Triune, we cannot afford to be indifferent to the revelation of the Triune
### God.

## V. Deviations that ignore the revelation of the enjoyment of the Triune
## God-mere theological knowledge:
### A. It is extremely possible to mistake knowledge concerning the Trinity with the
### proper enjoyment of the Triune God. This is also a deviation from the scrip-
### tural revelation concerning the Triune God.
### B. The apostles were fully in the experience of the Triune God (cf. 2 Cor: 13:14),
### not so much in the mode of explaining the mystery of the Trinity
### C. While it has become necessary, because of heresies and deviations, to develop
### and state clearly the truths concerning the Trinity, we should not be satisfied
### with the proper understanding alone. We must arrive at the same experience
### of the apostles, enjoying, living in, working with, and manifesting the Triune God.
