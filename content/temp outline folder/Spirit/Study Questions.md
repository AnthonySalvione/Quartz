# Study Questions
## THE SPIRIT
## TERM 3

## Lesson 1
### 1. Know the Greek and Hebrew equivalents for the words "spirit, breath" and "wind"
### with related verse references.
### 2. Study the three points in the basic revelation concerning God in the Holy Scriptures.
### Be prepared to speak on each point from the standpoint of truth.
### 3. Study the three points in the basic revelation concerning man in the Holy
### Scriptures. Be prepared to speak on each point from the standpoint of truth.

## Lesson 2
### 1. What is the distinction among the Spirit of God, the Spirit of Jehovah, and the Spirit
### of holiness in the Old Testament?
### 2. What is the significance of each of the divine title translations: the Spirit the Holy
### and the Holy Spirit, and connect the titles with the seven Spirits in Revelation?
### 3. What is the difference in essence between the conception of John the Baptist and the
### conception of Jesus Christ?

## Lesson 3
### 1. Using Genesis 1:2 as a starting point, explain how the "intrinsic flow of the Bible is
### the Spirit."
### 2. Study each of the ten aspects of the Spirit in the New Testament (a-j) and be able to
### speak on at least 2 from the standpoint of truth.
### 3. Be prepared to share a personal experience of one of the aspects of the Spirit in the
### New Testament (a-j).

## Lesson 4
### 1. Know the significances and related verse references of each of the five ingredients,
### measures, and numbers of the holy anointing oil. Use Exodus 30:25, note 1.
### 2. Be prepared to share one experience of the anointing oil. Use Exodus 30:26, note 1.
### 3. In what way does the Spirit operate to seal the believers? What is the relationship
### between seal, pledge, and inheritance?

## Lesson 5
### 1. What are the five "missings" in Christianity today?
### 2. Be prepared to speak from the standpoint of truth on at least 2 of the 5 aspects of
### the seven Spirits.
### 3. Using one verse reference for each, explain the distinction between the economical
### and the essential Spirit.

## Lesson 6
### 1. Study and memorize the Scripture references that show that man is a tripartite
### being. Explain the formation of the body, soul and spirit in God's creation.
### 2. Study the five points listed in the chapter related to our spirit being a particular
### organ created by God.
### 3. Know the composition of our spirit including related verse references.

## Lesson 7
### 1.
### "We need to be impressed with the proper knowledge of our human spirit." In
### creation, God "paid the most attention to the creation of our human spirit." Study
### pp. 70-72 (top) and practice speaking this progression of the creation of man's spirit.
### 2. Know the 6 aspects of the importance of our human spirit (I-VI) and be prepared to
### speak on one aspect from the standpoints of both truth and personal experience.

## Lesson 8
### 1. According to 2 Timothy 1:6-7, we need to fan our spirit into flame, particularly when
### we are in difficult or trying situations. Practice fanning your spirit into flame and be
### prepared to share about your experience.
### 2. According to Romans 8:6 we must set our mind on the spirit. After practicing
### fanning your spirit into flame, practice quieting your "talkative" mind and setting it
### on the spirit. Be prepared to share about your experience.

## Lesson 9
### 1. What is the difference between the Spirit's sanctifying in Romans 6:19, 22 and in
### 1 Peter 1:2? What is significant about the preposition, "in," from 1 Peter 1:2?
### 2. How do we see the Spirit's sanctifying work in Luke 15:8-9? What two words are of
### particular note in this portion?
### 3. Know the major points in John 16:8-11 that show the Spirit's sanctifying work.
### 4. Study the two major points that issue from the Spirit's sanctifying work before
### repentance and believing.

## Lesson 10
### 1. What is the purpose of God's choosing and the Spirit's sanctifying (before salvation)?
### 2. Why did God need the human life? Why does man need the divine life?
### 3. Be able to substantiate with verses, why regeneration needs to be through the
### resurrection of Christ?
### 4. What is the meaning of regeneration according to Titus 3:5?

## Lesson 11
### 1. Know the three aspects of sanctification with related verse supports, and be able to
### explain how sanctification is related to God's economy.
### 2. What is the difference between positional and dispositional sanctification?
### 3. Know the five works of the Spirit's dispositional sanctification with related verse
### references.
### 4. Be prepared to share a personal experience of the statement, "the real dispositional
### sanctification is 'correcting plus constituting."

## Lesson 12
### 1. Why is the divine sanctification the "holding line" in the carrying out of the divine
### economy!
### 2. Know the seven steps of the divine sanctification with verse references. Be prepared
### to share on each point.

## Lesson 13
### 1. In the matter of renewing, use Romans 6:19 & 22 to prove that dispositional
### sanctification is ongoing within the believers. Be prepared to share something from
### your personal experience.
### 2. What is the significance of the word "unto" in Ephesians 4:30?
### 3. Using verse supports, be able to explain the fine work carried out by the
### transformation of the sanctifying Spirit in the believers?

## Lesson 14
### 1. What is the relationship between transforming and renewing?
### 2. Describe how the Lord Spirit transforms us.
### 3. The transforming of the sanctifying Spirit issues in at least two aspects. Be prepared
### to speak on each of the two aspects with verse support.
