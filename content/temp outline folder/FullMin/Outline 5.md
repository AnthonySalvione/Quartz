# The Universal Divine-Human Incorporation
## of the Consummated God with the Regenerated Believers
## (4)
## The Hidden Manna, the Best Love,
## and the Shepherding of the Ascended Christ
## Lesson Five
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

## I. We are incorporated into the Triune God to become the New Jerusalem by
## eating the hidden manna
Rev. 2:17:
### A. The tabernacle in the Old Testament is a sign of the universal incorporation.
### B. To eat the hidden manna is to be incorporated into the tabernacle.
### C. The hidden manna corresponds to the four ins in John 14:16-20:
#### 1. The Son is in the Father.
#### 2. The Father is in the Son.
#### 3. The Son is in us.
#### 4. The Spirit of reality is in us.

## II. We are incorporated into the Triune God to become His dwelling place by
## loving the Lord with the best love
Rev. 2:4:
### A. It is the love in God that gives Him the yearning to unite, mingle, and incorporate
### with us, and it is the same love in us (1 John 4:19) that gives us the yearning to
### unite, mingle, and incorporate with Him.
### B. We become God's dwelling place, His sanctuary, the Holy of Holies, by participating
### in the four stages of the divine romance revealed in Song of Songs:
#### 1. The attracting and pursuing stage-1:2-4.
#### 2. The stage of experiencing the cross—2:14.
#### 3. The stage of living in the heavenlies to be God's new creation in resurrection—
#### 4:8.
#### 4. The stage of becoming the sanctuary, the Holy of Holies-6:4:
##### a. "To become the sanctuary of God is to be built up (related to the building of
##### the Body of Christ"-Witness Lee, Crystallization-Study of the Song of Songs
##### (p. 96).
##### b. "This sanctuary is the Holiest of all, which is God Himself" (pp. 98-99).
##### c. "When we enter into the Holiest of all, we enter into God. Then we become the
##### sanctuary. In this sense, we become God" (p. 99).
##### d. "The many lovers of Christ eventually will become duplications of God in life
##### and in nature but not in the Godhead. This is the fulfillment of God becoming
##### a man that man might become God, which is the high peak of the divine
##### revelation" (p. 99).

##### e. "These verses [John 14:23; Eph. 3:17] prove that the very God whom we are
##### pursuing is making us His duplication. To make us His duplication means to
##### make us His dwelling place, His Holy of Holies" (p. 99).
### C. By loving the Lord with the best love and by participating in every aspect of the
### divine romance, we become the New Jerusalem, which is an enlarged Holy of Holies.

## III. In His heavenly ministry, Christ is shepherding us into the enlarged, universal
## divine-human incorporation
Heb. 13:20-21:
### A. In His heavenly ministry the Lord Jesus continues the shepherding which He began
### in His earthly ministry.
### B. The Lord has incorporated the apostolic ministry with His heavenly ministry to take
### care of God's flock, which is the church, issuing in the Body of Christ
John 21:15-17; Acts 20:28; 1 Pet. 5:2.
### C. The ascended Christ is our Shepherd in five stages
Psa. 23:
#### 1. The enjoyment in the green pastures and at the waters of rest—v. 2.
#### 2. Revival and transformation on the paths of righteousness—v. 3.
#### 3. The experience of the presence of the resurrected, pneumatic Christ through the
#### valley of the shadow of death—v. 4.
#### 4. The deeper and higher enjoyment of the resurrected Christ on the battlefield-
#### v. 5.
#### 5. The lifelong enjoyment of the divine goodness and kindness in the house of the
#### Lord—v. 6.
