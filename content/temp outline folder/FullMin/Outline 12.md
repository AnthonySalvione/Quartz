# The Experience of Reigning in Life
## Lesson Twelve
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

## I. To reign in life is to conquer, subdue, and rule over Satan, the world, sin,
## the flesh, ourselves, and all the environmental circumstances
Rom. 8:35,
37.

## II. To reign in life is to subdue all kinds of insubordination
5:17-18, 21; 8:2.

## III. When we reign in life by being under the ruling of the divine life, we are
## delivered from the authority of darkness
Col. 1:13a.

## IV. When we are under the ruling of the divine life, we live in the kingdom of
## the Son of God's love, where we are ruled and restricted in the sweetness of
## love
v. 13b.

## V. To reign in life is to have our hearts directed by the Lord
Prov. 21:1;
2 Thes. 3:5.

## VI. In order to reign in life, we need to receive the abundance of grace
Rom.
5:17, 21.

## VII. Since we reign in life as God does, by reigning in life we become God in life
## and in nature but not in the Godhead
v. 17.

## VIII. We reign in life in living a grafted life
11:17-24; Gal. 2:20a.
