# The Goal of Reigning in Life
## Lesson Thirteen
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

## I. God's will is to have the Body life
Rom. 12:2.

## II. When we are reigning in life, living under the ruling of the divine life, the issue
## is the real and practical Body life.

## III. To see the Body life built up as a practical reality, we must reign in life, that is,
## be under the ruling of the divine life.

## IV. Each item of the living of the Body life in Romans 12—13 requires us to be
## ruled by the divine life:
### A. Being captivated by the compassions of God
v. 1a.
### B. Presenting our bodies a living sacrifice
v. 1b.
### C. Not being fashioned according to this age
v. 2a.
### D. Being transformed by the renewing of the mind
v. 2b.
### E. Not thinking more highly of ourselves than we ought to think
v. 3a.
### F. Thinking so as to be sober-minded, as God has apportioned to each a measure of
### faith
v. 3b.
### G. Considering that we have many members in one body and all the members do not
### have the same function
v. 4.

## V. We can live a life of the highest virtues for the Body life only by reigning in life:
### A. Loving without hypocrisy and loving warmly in brotherly love
vv. 9a, 10a.
### B. Not being slothful in zeal but burning in spirit, serving the Lord
v. 11.
### C. Enduring in tribulation
v. 12b.
### D. Rejoicing with those who rejoice and weeping with those who weep
v. 15.
### E. If possible, as far as it depends on us, living in peace with all men
v. 18.

## VI. The Body life is expressed in the church life:
### A. We must reign in life in order to live the church life:
#### 1. We receive the believers under the reigning in life according to God's receiving
14:1-23.
#### 2. We should receive one another according to the way Christ receives
us
15:1-13.
### B. Only by living under the rule of the divine life, by reigning in life, is it possible for us
### to live the proper church life.
### C. The church is not a police station or a law court but a home, a hospital, and a school.
