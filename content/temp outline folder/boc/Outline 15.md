## I. God cares mainly for four things:
### A. He cares for His divine person, not tolerating anything that is a blasphemy or
### insult to Himself.
### B. He cares for the humanity made by Him for His expression, not allowing anything
### that damages humanity such as fornication, extortion or things like this.
### C. He cares for His Christ, not tolerating anything that insults the Person or
### redemptive work of Christ.
### D. He cares for the Body of Christ, not allowing anything to divide it or damage it.
### E. As long as we care for these four things, not imposing nor opposing minor things,
### we will be persons whom God will use very much.

## II. Life is the primary thing in the practicality of the church life:
### A. Realizing by studying
### the history of Christian practice during the last twenty
### centuries that the best thing, the primary thing, the first thing we must take care
### of is the matter of life.
### B. Having a name that you are living, and you are dead (Rev. 3:1-2) causing the Lord
### to react by raising up the "inner-life line":
#### 1. Our need is to be made alive. When we have life, it takes care of everything.
#### 2. In the Lord's recovery of the church life in a certain place there may be a
#### genuine local church. But without the practicality of life, it will be dead. It may
#### be right in every aspect, but it is dead.
#### 3. We can never be too much for the matter of life. Other things can be over-
#### emphasized, but the matter of life can never be over-emphasized.

#### another; to exercise influence over others is still something of man's doing.

### C. For our practice:
#### 1. If we pay attention to teachings and gifts, either we will be dead or dissenting.

#### D. In Ephesians-4:13, 14-15; 2:21.
### E. In 1 Peter-2:2, 5.
