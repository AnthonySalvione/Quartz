## (1-2)
## Lesson Five
## BODY OF CHRIST-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

## XIV. The recovery of the church:
### A. Typified by the return of the children of Israel from their captivity:
#### 1. From Babylon-the capturing and divisive ground.
#### 2. Back to Jerusalem, the God-ordained unique ground, with all the vessels of the
#### temple of God, which had been captured to Babylon.
#### 3. To rebuild the temple of God, the house of God.
#### 4. To rebuild the city of Jerusalem.
### B. Indicated in the later Epistles of the New Testament:
#### 1. To be separated from the factious—the sects and denominations.
#### 2. To cleanse ourselves from the vessels unto dishonor in the great house the
#### apostate Christendom.
#### 3. Not to contact those who do not abide in the teaching of Christ-the teaching
#### concerning Christ's deity and incarnation by divine conception.
#### 4. To come out of the religious Babylon the Great.
