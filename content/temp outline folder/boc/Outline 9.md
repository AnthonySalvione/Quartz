## X. The status of the church:
### D. The Body of Christ
Eph. 1:22-23; Col. 1:18:
#### 1. Composed of the members
Rom. 12:5:
##### a. As the members.
##### b. For functions with different gifts-v. 6.
#### 2. Baptized in the one Spirit
1 Cor. 12:13:
##### a. Given to drink the one Spirit.
##### b. Having the one Spirit.
#### 3. Constituted with the unsearchable riches of Christ
Eph. 3:8:
##### a. Apprehending the breadth, the length, the height, and the depth of Christ—
##### b. Knowing the knowledge-surpassing love of Christ—v. 19.
##### c. Under the transmission of Christ in His resurrection, ascension, and
##### transcendency to be the Head over all things—1:20-22.
#### 4. With Christ as the Head—5:23; 1:22-23:
##### a. In resurrection-Col. 1:18.
##### b. With preeminence-vv. 18-19.
#### 5. With the individual Christ to be the corporate Christ—1 Cor. 12:12:
##### a. As the new man-Col. 3:10.
##### b. Having Christ as all the members and in all the members—v. 11.
#### 6. To be the fullness of Him who fills all in all
Eph. 1:23:
##### a. Arriving at the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ-4:13.
##### b. To be filled unto all the fullness of God-3:19.
