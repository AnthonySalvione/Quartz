# The Generality of the Church Life
## (1-2)
## Lesson Fourteen
## BODY OF CHRIST-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Rom. 14:1-7, 12-21; 15:3-5; 1 Cor. 8:4-13; 10:25-29; 9:20-22; 7:7-8, 25-
26; 1 Tim. 5:14; Matt. 18:17; Rom. 16:17; 2 Thes. 3:6, 14; 1 Cor. 5:9-13; Titus 3:10;
2 John 9, 10, 11, 7

## I. Practicing the generality of the church life:
### A. Before we can be general, we must be very specific, very particular concerning the
### six items of our Christian faith.
### B. Paul's practice-Acts 21:
#### 1. In the matter of eating things sacrificed to idols-1 Cor. 8:4-13; 10:25-29.
#### 2. In the matter of eating all things or only vegetables-Rom. 14:2-3.
#### 3. In the matter of keeping days—vv. 5-6.
#### 4. In the matter of marriage—1 Cor. 7:7-8, 27; cf. 1 Tim. 5:14.
### C. Toward other local churches.

## II. The better things for building up a local church:
### A. The exercise of the spirit-Isa. 64:7a; 1 Cor. 6:17; 12:13; 1 Tim. 4:7-8; Acts 24:16.
### B. The growth in life—1 Cor. 3:7-9; Eph. 2:21-22; 4:15-16; Col. 2:18-19.
### C. Pray-reading-Jer. 15:16; Psa. 119:103; Eph. 6:17-18; 2 Cor. 3:6.
### D. Feeding on the Lord-John 6:35; 1 Pet. 2:2-3; 1 Tim. 4:6.
### E. Keeping the oneness
Eph. 4:3:
#### 1. If we are special, if we are definite and specific in anything other than our faith,
#### surely the oneness will be damaged.
#### 2. The main cause for the divisions among Christians is the neglect of caring for
#### the generality of the church life—Rom. 14:13-21; 15:3-5; 1 Cor. 8:8-13.

## II. The balance to the generality-Hosea 7:8:
### A. The speciality needs the generality to balance it; the generality also needs
### something to balance it.
### B. There are certain persons with whom we cannot be general, nor with whom we
### should be general:
#### 1. One who neglects to hear the church—Matt. 18:15-17.
#### 2. Those who cause divisions—Rom. 16:17; 2 Thes. 3:6, 14.
#### 3. One who is sinful—1 Cor. 5:1-13.
#### 4. One who is sectarian, factious—Titus 3:10.
#### 5. One who goes beyond the teaching of Christ-2 John 7-11.

## IV. Caring primarily for the proper standing, and not the condition:
### A. In the example of the return of the remnant from captivity, as recorded by
### Nehemiah, Ezra, and Haggai, the condition of the remnant was poor.
### B. If we have seen what the church is and what the recovery of the oneness is, we
### would care mainly for the standing and pay some attention to the condition.
