## XI. The failures in the churches:
### A. Hypocrisy
Acts 5:1-11:
#### 1. Letting Satan fill the heart
v. 3.
#### 2. Lying to and testing the Holy Spirit-God
vv. 3-4.
#### 3. Pretending to be absolute for the Lord
vv. 3b-4a, 8.
### B. Being overcome by racial difference6:1:
#### 1. In the matter of eating
v. 1
#### 2. Causing the practice of having all things common to wane—2:4-45.
### C. Bringing in the legal practices of the Old Testament
15:1-2:
#### 1. Circumcision on the flesh
Phil. 3:2; Gal. 5:2.
#### 2. Annulling the freedom in Christ—2:3-4.
#### 3. Even causing Peter to play hypocrisy, the rest of the Jewish believers who were with
#### him to join him in hypocrisy, and even Barnabas to be carried away by their
#### hypocrisy—w. 12-13.
### D. Compromising with Judaism
Acts 21:20-26:
#### 1. Bringing in the mixture of Judaism with God's pure grace.
#### 2. Spoiling the purity of the church life.
#### 3. Damaging the pure testimony of Jesus Christ as the embodiment of God's pure
#### grace
John 1:17.
#### 4. Resulting in the termination of the church, polluted with the satanic mixture, by the
#### judgment of God through the destruction of Jerusalem by the Roman army under
#### Titus in A.D. 70.
### E. Judging one another in the matters of eating and observing of days
Rom. 14:2-5, 10, 20.
### F. Making divisions and causes of falling contrary to the teaching of the apostles—16:17;
1 Tim. 6:3.
### G. Being puffed up on behalf of one gifted person against another unto strife and
divisions-1 Cor. 1:11-12.
### H. Committing fornication-5:1.
### I. Having lawsuits one against another-6:6.
### J. Questioning the Apostle's apostleship and charging the Apostle with being crafty in
making gain with guile-9:1-3; 2 Cor. 12:16.
### K. Partaking of the table of the Lord and of the table of the demons-1 Cor. 10:21.
### L. Eating the bread and drinking the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner and not
discerning the Body-11:27, 29.
### M. Abusing the gift of tongue-speaking-14:23.
### N. Saying that there is no resurrection of the dead—15:12.
### O. Walking disorderly-2 Thes. 3:11.
