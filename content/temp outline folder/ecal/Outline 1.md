# The Scriptural Meaning of Life
## Lesson One
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
1 John 5:12; Col. 3:4; John 11:25; 14:6; 1:4; 3:16, 36; 10:10

## I. The Scriptures being a book of life:
### A. At the beginning, there is a blueprint with the tree of life as the center.
### B. At the end, there is another picture with the tree of life again as the center.
### C. In God's entire creation, life is the meaning, the center, and the beauty of
### the universe.

## II. The real life being Christ, the Son of God
1 John 5:12:
### A. All the lives in God's creation are not real lives, but figures, types, and
### shadows.
### B. The real thing is Christ Himself.
### C. With life there is beauty, riches, and power.

## III. God being life to us in the Son and through (as) the Spirit:
### A. Second Corinthians 13:14 shows the Triune God as grace, love, and
### fellowship to be transmitted to us.
### B. The Triune God is likened to a fountain, a spring, and a stream to flow the
### living water into us
John 4:14; Rev. 22:1-2.
### C. The Holy Spirit is just Christ as life to us
2 Cor. 3:6; 1 Cor. 15:45b.

## IV. The Father in the Son through (as) the Spirit reaching our human
## spirit
Rom. 8:16; 2 Tim. 4:22.

## V. Hence, we need to discern what life is and what life is not:
### A. Life is not devotion.
### B. Life is not good behavior.
### C. Life is not power.
### D. Life is not gift.
### E. Life is not the increase of knowledge.
### F. Life is not our human life.
### G. Life is God's content and God's flowing out.
### H. Life is Christ.
### I. Life is the Holy Spirit.
