**Scripture Reading:**
Acts 23:1; 24:16; 1 Tim. 1:5, 19; Heb. 9:14; 10:22; 2 Cor. 1:12; Rom. 9:1

## I. The conscience being a part of our human spirit—cf. Rom. 9:1; 8:16.

## I. The operation of the conscience through the intuition of the spirit.

## I. Our conscience and the assurance of faith:
### A. As long as we have an accusation in our conscience, it is impossible for us to
### contact the Lord.
### B. Our assurance of faith has very much to do with a good, pure conscience.

## IV. Three kinds of government:
### A. Before the fall, man was directly under the divine government, the government of
### God.
### B. After the fall, man came under the government of the conscience.
### C. After a further fall, man came under the human government.
### D. The government of the conscience is the stepping stone to be delivered out of the
### human government and translated into the divine government.

## V. Dealing with the conscience being necessary for the growth in life:
### A. Without dealing with the conscience, we can never be persons living in the
### presence of the Lord.
### B. Without dealing with the conscience, we have never grown in life.
