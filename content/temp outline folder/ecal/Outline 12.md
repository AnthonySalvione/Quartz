# Dealing with the Self
## (2)
## Lesson Twelve
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Matt. 16:21-26; Luke 9:25

## I. The self being equivalent to the soul-cf. Matt. 16:26, Luke 9:25:
### A. With the fallen man as the flesh, there are two problems: sin and self, sin being in
### the body and self being in the soul.
### B. Man's soul became the self when it became independent of God.

## II. Opinion being the manifestation and incarnation of the self.

## III. Self being the very essence of Satan in the soul
Matt. 16:21-26:
### A. In the soul is the self, in the self is the mind; and in the mind is the opinion, which
### is one with Satan.
### B. Self is the very essence of Satan in the soul, making the soul independent of God to
### express its self-opinion and self-will.

## IV. The way to deal with the self:
### A. We need to have the revelation that our old man has been crucified on the cross—
Rom. 6:6.
### B. After we have seen that the old man has been crucified on the cross, we need to
### recognize and accept the fact.
### C. We need to apply to ourselves the death of Christ that He accomplished and that
### we have recognized-this is the bearing of the cross:
#### 1. This application of the cross is always in the Spirit and by the Spirit-8:13.
#### 2. The Spirit is the very One who became incarnated and lived on this earth for
#### thirty-three and a half years, who was crucified, resurrected, ascended, and
#### exalted to the heavens.
#### 3. All the elements of incarnation, death, resurrection, ascension, human nature,
#### and divine nature, are all in the Spirit Who is a stream of living water flowing
#### into us.
#### 4. To deal with the self is to live and walk in the Spirit.
### D. Dealing with the self is not a once for all matter; rather, it is day-by-day and
### moment-by-moment carried out by the cross and in the Spirit.
