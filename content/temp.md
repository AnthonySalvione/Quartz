**Ten ways in which we can develop, cultivate, and renew our love for the Lord, so that we may love the Lord with the first and best love:**

1) We need to be constrained by the love of Christ. Not to live to ourselves but the crucified and resurrected Lord. (2 Cor. 5:14-15; and Hymn #101).
2) We must see the Lord's supreme preciousness. One way is by reading the Bible, which testifies concerning the Lord Jesus. (Phil. 3:8; John 5:39).
3) We need to give the Lord the first place in all things. We must be willing to be adjusted, broken, made nothing, so that the Lord could have a way in us. (Col. 1:15, 18).
4) We need to develop a personal, affectionate relationship with the Lord. Song of Songs begins and ends with a kiss, we need to kiss the Lord and He needs to kiss us. (S.S. 1:2)
5) We must remember where we have fallen from and repent. (Rev. 2:5).
6) We need to remain under the genuine New Covenant ministry. Paul, as a New Covenant minister, betrothed the believers as pure virgins to Christ. (2 Cor. 11:2-3; and Hymn #171).
7) We need to receive the dispensing of God as love. The love of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit. (Romans 5:5; Romans 8:35, 39).
8) We need to eat the tree of life to recover our first love. When we love the Lord, He gives us to eat of the Lord as the tree of life. The more we eat, the more we will love the Lord. (Rev. 2:7).
9) In the book titled 'The Experience of Life,' we see that the love between us and the Lord must be renewed. We should pray to the Lord, "Lord, I would like to renew my marriage vow to You."
10) We must be built up with the believers to be the corporate Shulammite, the overcoming bride. We not only need to be mature in life, but also built together as one bride. (S.S. 6:4, 10, 13).
