# Who are we?

- We are local believers in the Lord Jesus Christ who have personally received Him as our Savior. He is the most excellent and enjoyable person.
- We love Him and endeavor to give Him the first place in all things.
- We rejoice to be cleansed by the blood of Jesus, God's Son, born of God the Father to be His children, and filled with the Holy Spirit.
- We warmly receive all believers in Christ and seek to maintain the oneness of the Spirit by keeping our focus on Christ alone.
- We highly treasure the Holy Bible as God's revelation of Himself and of His eternal purpose.
- We hold the common faith revealed in the Bible, which was delivered once for all to the saints.


## The church in Anaheim is not our name; it is our description

- As such, it is an inclusive title, not an exclusive one. We gather together as believers in Anaheim, and we receive all who believe in Jesus Christ as our brothers and sisters.
- We welcome guests and visitors. Our desire is to follow the Lord and to carry out the church life according to the New Testament.

# Receiving by Believing

- [[John-03#v16|John 3:16]] says "For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that everyone who believes into Him would not perish, but would have eternal life." This verse clearly shows us that God is a God of love. Because He loved the world, He gave His only begotten Son to us that we might have eternal life.

- Eternal life is nothing other than God Himself who became a man in Jesus Christ ([[John-01#v1|John 1:1]], [[John-01#v4|4]], [[John-01#v14|14]]). He lived on the earth as a God-man for thirty-three and a half years, and then He was crucified on the cross. In His crucifixion His blood was shed for our sins, and He accomplished a full and complete redemption ([[1 Peter-01#v18|1 Pet. 1:18-19]]; [[Ephesians-01#v7|Eph. 1:7]]). On the third day after His death, He resurrected from the dead ([[1 Corinthians-15#v4|1 Cor. 15:4]]). In and through His resurrection, He was transfigured to become a life-giving Spirit, who is now readily available for us to receive ([[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45]]).

- According to [[John-01#v12|John 1:12]], the way to receive the Lord Jesus is to believe into Him: "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the authority to become children of God, to those who believe into His name." By believing into the Lord Jesus, we receive Him as our eternal life. To receive this life and become a believer, simply come to the Lord with an open and honest heart and say to Him:

> *Lord Jesus, I am a sinner. I need You. Thank You for dying for me. Lord Jesus, forgive me. Cleanse me from all my sins. I believe You rose from the dead. I receive You right now as my Savior and my life. Come into me! Fill me with Your life! Lord Jesus, I give myself to You for Your purpose.*


# Our Purpose

- We just have one goal: to experience Christ and grow in His life for the building up of His Body, the church.
- To reach this goal, we gather frequently to study the Bible, nurture a life in Christ, establish our children and young people in the faith, serve mankind with the gospel, and exercise our God-given functions as members of the Body of Christ.
