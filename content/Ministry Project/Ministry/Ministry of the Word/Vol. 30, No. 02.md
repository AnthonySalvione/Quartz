# Message 47—The Lamb-God Being on the Throne within the City

## Scripture Reading

## The Context to the Book of Revelation in Relation to the Full Ministry of Christ in Three Stages

Before getting into the outline of this message, it is important to realize the context of the book of Revelation in relation to the full ministry of Christ in three stages. The three stages of Christ’s full ministry—incarnation, inclusion, and intensification—are the contents of God’s New Testament economy. Regarding the full ministry of Christ, Brother Lee, in 1997, told the brothers taking care of the full-time training in Anaheim that what the Lord’s recovery will be and how it will be depends upon how we handle this teaching. This crucial teaching is presented in three books published in 1996 : Incarnation, Inclusion, and Intensification; The Vital Groups; and How to Be a Co-worker and an Elder and How to Fulfill Their Obligations. Additionally, in the first chapter of God’s New Testament Economy (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1984,vol. 3, pp. 120-121), and in the Maps and Charts section of the Holy Bible Recovery Version, there is an excellent chart entitled “God’s New Testament Economy,” depicting the three stages of the full ministry of Christ. The terms incarnation, inclusion, and intensification do not appear in this chart; the terms initiation, development, and finalization are used instead. Nevertheless, the contents of the same three stages are laid out in a most helpful way.

In the first stage of incarnation Christ accomplished four major things. First, He brought the infinite God into the finite man. Regarding this truth, the [[Hymn-501#Stanza 1|first stanza of Hymns, #501 says]], “O glorious Christ, Savior mine, / Thou art truly radiance divine; / God infinite, in eternity, /Yet man in time, finite to be.” Second, He united, mingled, and incorporated the Triune God with the tripartite man. Third, Christ expressed in His humanity the bountiful God in His rich attributes through His aromatic virtues, and fourth, He accomplished His all-inclusive judicial redemption.

The second stage of Christ’s ministry is the stage of inclusion. We use the word inclusion because this stage refers to Christ as the all-inclusive life-giving Spirit. In this stage there are three main aspects. First, He was begotten as God’s firstborn Son; second, He became the life-giving Spirit; and third, He regenerated the believers for His Body.

The stage of incarnation is seen in the Gospels, and the stage of inclusion is seen in the Epistles. Then the stage of Christ’s intensification is found in Revelation. In this stage, first, He intensifies His organic salvation; second, He produces the overcomers; and third, He consummates the New Jerusalem.

In chapter 2 of Elders’ Training, Book 5: Fellowship concerning the Lord’s Up-to-date Move the chart “God’s New Testament Economy” appears again (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1985,vol. 3, pp. 344-345) and later in that chapter the third stage of Christ’s full ministry is unveiled in a section entitled “The Intensification of the Triune God to Bring the Degraded Church Back to the Enjoyment of Himself for the Finalization of God’s New Testament Economy” (pp. 349-351). The sevenfold intensified Spirit is the intensification of the Triune God, and He is intensified to bring the degraded church back to the enjoyment of Himself for the finalization of God’s New Testament economy. The intensification of the Triune God is to bring the degraded church back to the enjoyment of Himself specifically as the tree of life, the hidden manna, and the feast for the finalization of God’s New Testament—this is the context of the book of Revelation regarding the full ministry of Christ in its three stages.

### Every Believer Being "A Little New Jerusalem" and Every Local Church Being a Miniature of the New Jerusalem

Every believer should be “a little New Jerusalem,” and every local church should be a miniature of the New Jerusalem. Therefore, when we look at the saints, we should see the New Jerusalem. When we look at them, we should not see Mount Sinai with flames, smoke, and legality but the New Jerusalem as the consummation of the garden of Eden with a flowing river and a tree. When we see the saints, their outstanding features should be a flowing river and a tree. [[Hymn-509#Stanza 1|The first stanza of Hymns,#509 says]], “A flowing river and a tree, / Eden’s outstanding features are, / Man to supply with food and drink, / That he may live fore’er.” Eden’s outstanding features are outstanding features of the New Jerusalem, and they should be our outstanding features as well.

Based on [[Revelation-22#v1|Revelation 22:1-2]], Brother Lee says, “The finalization of God’s economy will be the New Jerusalem with the tree of life in its center” (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1985,vol. 3, “Elders’ Training, Book 5: Fellowship concerning the Lord’s Up-to-date Move,” p. 350), but elsewhere, regarding the New Jerusalem, he warns, “Do not think that the New Jerusalem is merely something objective in the future for a certain group of people. We have to realize that what is recorded in Revelation 21 and 22 should be experienced by us today in a very personal way. Experientially speaking, every proper and normal Christian is ‘a little New Jerusalem’” (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1984,vol. 3, “God’s New Testament Economy,” p. 459).

In God’s New Testament Economy Brother Lee also says, “The church life today as a precursor of the New Jerusalem should be the same. The church life is a miniature of the New Jerusalem, but in its nature and in the intrinsic element of the triune living, the church today should be exactly the same as its full consummation. Individually we are a small New Jerusalem, and corporately as the church life we are also a miniature New Jerusalem” (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1984,vol. 3, p. 499). Every believer should be “a little New Jerusalem,” and every church life, every local church, should be a miniature of the New Jerusalem. All the aspects of the New Jerusalem—all the features and all the characteristics of the New Jerusalem—should be ours as members of the Body of Christ and should be the features and characteristics of every local church on this earth. This is wonderful!

As “a little New Jerusalem” and part of a local church as a miniature of the New Jerusalem, we need to see what we should do in our living and in our service. Our goal is the New Jerusalem, so first in our living we should live out the New Jerusalem; to live out the New Jerusalem is to become the New Jerusalem. Then, second, in our service we should work out the New Jerusalem; to work out the New Jerusalem is to build the New Jerusalem. To live out the New Jerusalem is to become the New Jerusalem, and to work out the New Jerusalem is to build the New Jerusalem. This is because the New Jerusalem is a corporate person.

### The New Jerusalem Being a Great Corporate Person—A Great Corporate God-man

In the book Living in and with the Divine Trinity it says, “The holy city, the New Jerusalem, is not a material city but a person. The holy city is a corporate person, and this corporate person is a couple—the processed Triune God married to the transformed tripartite man. This is the Spirit and the bride becoming one ([[Revelation-22#v17|Rev. 22:17a]]). Divinity and humanity are married together, mingled together, to be one entity. The holy city is a corporate person—a great corporate God-man” (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1988,vol. 1, p. 388). Whereas Revelation 2 and 3 repeatedly say, seven times, “He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches” ([[Revelation-02#v7|2:7]], [[Revelation-02#v11|11]], [[Revelation-02#v17|17]], [[Revelation-02#v29|29]]; [[Revelation-03#v6|3:6]], [[Revelation-03#v13|13]], [[Revelation-03#v22|22]]), [[Revelation-22#v17|Revelation 22:17a]] says, “The Spirit and the bride say.” At the beginning of Revelation, the Spirit speaks to the churches, but at the end of Revelation, the Spirit and the bride, the church, speak as one. In Revelation 22 it is no longer only the Spirit speaking to the churches; it is the Spirit and the bride speaking together, for they are one person—they are a married couple as a corporate person.

In describing God’s New Testament economy, which is His eternal economy, we can say that it is composed of two processes, two consummations, and one marriage. The first process is the process of the Triune God. He came out of eternity into time, bringing divinity into humanity. He passed through incarnation, human living, crucifixion, and resurrection with ascension, and He was consummated to be the Spirit (cf. [[John-07#v39|John 7:39]]). The Spirit is the processed Triune God as our Husband. Now, as the Spirit, He comes into us and dwells in our spirit. Hence, we, the believers, pass through a process. The first process is the process that the Triune God passed through: incarnation, human living, crucifixion, resurrection, with ascension to become the Spirit, the Husband. Now He has come into us, so we also pass through a process. This is the second process, and we are in this process right now. In His being processed He accomplished judicial redemption, but the main process that we pass through is organic salvation. We pass through the processes of regeneration, sanctification, renewing, transformation, conformation, and glorification, and we become the bride. We are consummated to be the bride. The process that we are going through with all the stages of God’s organic salvation is for us to become the bride. There are two processes, two consummations, and ultimately one marriage. The Spirit and the bride are joined in marriage as one corporate person, one great God-man.

Message 64 of the Life-study of Revelation presents this from another angle, saying, “The holy city as the tabernacle of God is for God to dwell in, and God and the Lamb as the temple are for the redeemed saints to dwell in. In the new heaven and new earth, the New Jerusalem will be a mutual dwelling place for God and man for eternity” (pp. 711-712). God is our temple, and we are His tabernacle. We are the tabernacle of God for God to dwell in, and He is the temple of God for us to dwell in. Describing this, [[Hymn-972|Hymns, #972]] says, “God in man and man in God / Mutual dwelling thus possess” ([[Hymn-972#Stanza 9|stanza 9]]). We are God’s home, and God is our home. This is a mutual abode of God and man—God in man and man in God. This mutual abode is a great corporate God-man. In the new heaven and new earth, the New Jerusalem, as the mutual dwelling place of God and man for eternity, will be a great corporate God-man.

The Spirit and the bride are a great corporate God-man as a married couple, and the tabernacle and the temple for God and man to dwell in is also a great corporate God-man as the mutual abode of God and man. God is in man and man is in God forever and ever. This great corporate God-man is the New Jerusalem.
## I. The Lamb-God is on the throne within the city—[[Revelation-04#v2|Rev. 4:2-3]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-5]]; [[Revelation-21#v23|21:23]]

The Lamb-God is on the throne within the city ([[Revelation-04#v2|Rev. 4:2-3]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-5]]; [[Revelation-21#v23|21:23]]). The points on the message outline are very experiential, for this involves our living out the New Jerusalem to become the New Jerusalem and our working out the New Jerusalem to be those who build the New Jerusalem. The Lord said, “I will build My church” ([[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18b]]), and eventually, the built-up church is the New Jerusalem. The New Jerusalem is the consummate stage of the church that the Lord is building today. We must cooperate with Him in this building work to become the New Jerusalem by living out the New Jerusalem and to build the New Jerusalem by working out the New Jerusalem. We are not going to the New Jerusalem; we are becoming the New Jerusalem, for we are becoming the bride, the wife of the Lamb. As such, we need to live out all the features of the New Jerusalem today.

The Lamb-God is on the throne within the city. There are two key portions of Revelation that speak of the throne: [[Revelation-04#v1|4:1-2]] and [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-5]]. [[Revelation-04#v1|Revelation 4:1]] says, “After these things I saw, and behold, a door opened in heaven, and the first voice which I heard was like a trumpet speaking with me, saying, Come up here, and I will show you the things that must take place after these things.” This verse says, “Come up here,” but what is the way to come up? The way is in verse 2 , which says, “Immediately I was in spirit.” When we are in spirit, we come up to the third heaven. Whenever we are in spirit, we come up and experience being in the heavens. 

Verse 2 continues, “Behold, there was a throne set in heaven.” When we are genuinely in our spirit, when we are exercising our spirit, the first thing before us is always the throne. The throne requires that we come under God’s ruling, His reigning, and that we give Him the preeminence, the first place. We should say, “Lord, set up Your throne in my being. I would like to be under Your throne. I would like to be under Your headship. I would like to be under Your lordship.” Verse 2 concludes: “And upon the throne there was One sitting.” When we are in our spirit, we are dethroned. We are dethroned because there is only One sitting on the throne. Our self is not on the throne when we are in our spirit. When we are genuinely in our spirit, we are dethroned, and there is One, Christ Jesus the Lord, sitting upon the throne. 

[[1 John-03#v4|First John 3:4b]] gives us a good definition of sin;it says, “Sin is lawlessness.” Lawlessness is simply not being under the principle of God’s ruling over man. When a man is living in lawlessness, he is not under God’s ruling; this is sin, for sin is lawlessness. Sin is man not being under the principle of God’s ruling over man; it is sin when, instead of God being on the throne in our experience, our self is on the throne. We do not want our self to be on the throne; we want to enthrone Him in our being. We want Him to be enthroned in the center of our being.

[[Revelation-22#v1|Revelation 22:1]] says, “He showed me a river of water of life, bright as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb in the middle of its street.” We must remember that according to [[Revelation-01#v1|Revelation 1:1]] , this is a book of signs. This verse says that God made the revelation of Jesus Christ known by signs to His slave John. Signs are symbols with spiritual significance. The river of water of life and the street of the New Jerusalem are such signs. The river flows in the middle of the street ([[Revelation-22#v1|22:1]]), and this street is pure gold ([[Revelation-21#v21|21:21]]). Gold signifies the divine nature, so the divine life is flowing in the divine nature as our street, our way. When we come to crossroads in our life and need to consider what our way should be and decide what way we will go, we must go by the flow of life within us, the flow of life in the midst of the divine nature. Everything we do has to be according to the divine nature and in the flow of the river of water of life. We believers have the real “GPS,” that is, God’s Presence Satellite; within us, we have the flow of life within the divine nature as the unique street in the New Jerusalem. We can never get lost in the New Jerusalem because there is only one street, and that street is the golden, divine nature of God with the river of water of life flowing in the middle of it. If we do everything according to the divine life flowing within the divine nature, we can never get lost. We can never get lost when we are on the one street of the New Jerusalem. 

[[Revelation-22#v2|Revelation 22:2]] says, “On this side and on that side of the river was the tree of life, producing twelve fruits, yielding its fruit each month; and the leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations.” With the river of water of life, we have the tree of life. The first part of verse 3 continues, “And there will no longer be a curse. And the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it.” The word it in this verse refers to us. While the word it is used to refer to a city, this city is a “city-lady,” a “city-bride.” This city, the New Jerusalem, is the bride, the wife of the Lamb ([[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9-10]]). It is we who are being prepared as the bride to be the wife of the Lamb, so the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in us for eternity. For us to be prepared as the bride, we need to enthrone Him in our being every day of our lives. 

[[Revelation-22#v3|Revelation 22:3]] concludes, “And His slaves will serve Him.” We, His slaves, will serve Him for eternity. The first part of verse 4 then goes on to say, “And they will see His face.” We saw in a previous message how we need to care for His face. Paul’s forgiving the brother in [[2 Corinthians-02#v10|2 Corinthians 2:10]] was “in the person of Christ,” literally meaning that he forgave him in the face of Christ. The Greek word for face,translated “person” here, refers more specifically to the part around the eyes, the look as the index of the inward thoughts and feelings, which shows forth and manifests the whole person. The index of the eyes is the very presence of a person; it gives the person’s feeling and even infuses that person into the one gazing into it. If we have the index of someone’s eyes, we have that person. We should desperately want His eyes, His face, all the time. Then, eventually, we will receive the great reward of seeing His face forever and ever.

The rest of [[Revelation-22#v4|Revelation 22:4]] says, “And His name will be on their foreheads.” This is like having a name badge on our forehead; it designates who we are. Having His name, the name of the Triune God, on our foreheads shows that we belong to the Triune God. Not only does this show that we belong to Him; it also shows that we are identified with Him, that He is in us and we are in Him. We will forever be labeled with the name of the Triune God on our foreheads.

This portion ends with verse 5 , which says, “And night will be no more; and they have no need of the light of a lamp and of the light of the sun, for the Lord God will shine upon them; and they will reign forever and ever.” This becomes our experience by our enthroning the Lord in our being. We should pray, “Lord, set up Your throne in the center of my being. I want You to be my King. I want You to be my Lord. I want You to be my Head. I want You to be my Husband. Set up Your throne in the midst of my being. Lord Jesus, I would like to enthrone You in my being.” When He is enthroned in our being, the river of water of life flows within us in the divine nature ([[Revelation-22#v1|v. 1]]). When He is enthroned in our being, the tree of life grows within us ([[Revelation-22#v2|v. 2]]). When He is enthroned in our being, we will serve Him, and our service will be an enjoyment ([[Revelation-22#v3|v. 3]]). When we enthrone Him, enjoy Him, and serve Him, we will see His face, we will be identified and joined to Him, and we will be one with Him ([[Revelation-22#v4|v. 4]]). When we enthrone, enjoy, and serve Him, we will be shined upon by Him, and we will reign as kings with Him ([[Revelation-22#v5|v. 5]]). All these experiences are ours in the river of water of life, which is flowing out of the throne of God and of the Lamb, and from the tree of life, which will be for our life supply on this side and on that side of the river. How wonderful to have the river flow in us and the tree grow in us, for when the river is flowing in us and the tree is growing in us, we are enjoying the Triune God and serving Him joyfully. When we enjoy Him, we serve Him, we see His face, we are shined upon by Him, and we reign as kings with Him.

### C. This depicts how the Triune God—God, the Lamb, and the Spirit, who is symbolized by the water of life—dispenses Himself into His redeemed under His headship (implied in the authority of the throne) for eternity.

 This depicts how the Triune God—God, the Lamb, and the Spirit, who is symbolized by the water of life—dispenses Himself into His redeemed under His headship (implied in the authority of the throne) for eternity. In [[Revelation-22#v1|Revelation 22:1]] we have the throne of God and of the Lamb, but in 2:13 there is another throne. In this verse the Lord says to the church in Pergamos, “I know where you dwell, where Satan’s throne is.” Satan also has a throne. If we trace this back, we see that there was a most honorable archangel, the most beautiful angel, who had many awesome responsibilities. [[Isaiah-14#v12|Isaiah 14:12-15]] and [[Ezekiel-28#v15|Ezekiel 28:15-18]] tell us why this most honorable archangel became Satan, God’s adversary. In just two verses in Isaiah 14, this most honorable archangel declared, “I will,” five times: “But you, you said in your heart: / I will ascend to heaven; / Above the stars of God / I will exalt my throne. / And I will sit upon the mount of assembly / In the uttermost parts of the north. / I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; / I will make myself like the Most High” ([[Ezekiel-28#v13|vv. 13-14]]). He said, “I will ascend…above the stars of God,” “I will exalt my throne,” “I will sit upon the mount,” “I will ascend above the heights,” and “I will make myself like the Most High.” At that point, for the first time, there was another will besides God’s will in the universe. There was the will of Satan. This is still the situation today, and we human beings are between God’s will and Satan’s will. This is why the first two lines of every stanza of Hymns, #880 are, “To the foe my word is always, ‘No,’ / To the Father it is, ‘Yes.’” We must be those who continually say yes to God’s will and no to Satan’s will.

However, there is something more here in [[Isaiah-14#v13|Isaiah 14:13]], for in speaking concerning Satan’s rebellion, God said to him, “But you, you said in your heart.” Satan said something in his heart. We should pay attention to the words in your heart. Satan said in his heart, “I will exalt my throne.” In his heart he wanted his throne to be above God’s throne, “above the stars of God.” This was Satan’s sin, to say in his heart, “I will exalt my throne”; this is why we say that sin is a dethronement of God.

[[Ezekiel-28#v15|Ezekiel 28:15-18]] describes Satan’s rebellion from another angle, and it uses the words you sinned in verse 16. This was the first instance of these words being uttered in the universe. Satan is the first sinner. Then in verse 17, God again referred to Satan’s heart, saying, “Your heart was lifted up because of your beauty.” Satan looked on his beauty, and his heart was lifted up. It was for that reason that this great, most honorable archangel fell and became Satan. It was because of one thing—pride.

[[Proverbs-16#v18|Proverbs 16:18]] says, “Pride goes before destruction, / And a haughty spirit before a fall.” The word haughty indicates an attitude of superiority, a superiority complex. Satan had a haughty spirit; he was proud. We should always remember this: “Pride goes before destruction.” All of us are proud from our birth; none of us is immune to pride. May the Lord deal with our pride and save us from ever becoming a proud person, for pride goes before destruction. Sadly, we have seen this in our history; some tragically had their whole being brought into a state of destruction—spirit, soul, mind, and body—because of pride.

“Beware of Pride” is a major section in chapter 4 of How to Be a Co-worker and an Elder and How to Fulfill Their Obligations. This chapter unveils sixteen points concerning pride. One point says that “pride makes you a top fool” (The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1994–1997, vol. 5, p. 261). I encourage all of you to write out all these sixteen points concerning pride, even to write it longhand.

There is some benefit to writing longhand; in [[Deuteronomy-17#v14|Deuteronomy 17:14-20]] God set His requirements for a king over His people Israel. Verses 18 through 20 say,

When he sits on the throne of his kingdom, he shall write out for himself a copy of this law in a book, out of that which is before the Levitical priests. And it shall be with him, and he shall read in it all the days of his life, in order that he may learn to fear Jehovah his God by keeping all the words of this law and these statutes and doing them, so that his heart may not be lifted up above his brothers and he may not turn aside from the commandment to the right or to the left; that he and his sons may extend their days over their kingdom in the midst of Israel.

When a king was set up over God’s people, God said, “He shall write out”; that is, he shall write out a copy of the Bible for himself. At that time their Bible was the Pentateuch—Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy. The new king was to write out the whole thing for himself; he was to write out his own personal copy of their Bible, the Pentateuch. Now if we were to write out the first five books of the Bible verse by verse, chapter by chapter, and book by book, we would surely gain something. Time and again we would say, “Wow, look at this. I never saw this before.” Surely, this would have an impact on us.

This is why Brother Lee gave a message to the trainees on taking notes. When Brother Lee shared, we took notes, and many of us still have them. We cannot bring ourselves to throw them away. Of course, we now have all the messages in the books, but there are some things in the notes that are not in the books. We love the books, but we also love our notes.

Regarding pride, it is a particularly dangerous time for us when we are being used by the Lord, for we may look at ourselves and our heart may be lifted up, thinking that we are something or someone. By the Lord’s merciful shining, we must come to realize that we are nothing and no one. [[Galatians-06#v3|Galatians 6:3]] says, “If anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.” We should not deceive ourselves thinking that we are something when actually we are nothing. If we think we are something when we are nothing, we not only deceive ourselves, but Satan is enthroned within us, for our self is enthroned within us.

We saw from [[1 John-03#v4|1 John 3:4]] that sin is lawlessness. To be in lawlessness means that we are not under “the principle of God’s ruling over man” (footnote 2). [[2 Thessalonians-02#v3|Second Thessalonians 2:3]] says that Antichrist is “the man of lawlessness,” and verse 8 calls him “the lawless one.” He is completely outside of the principle of God’s ruling over man and is opposed to God’s ruling over man, so he is the man of lawlessness, the man of sin.

Verse 7 refers to “the mystery of lawlessness that is now operating.” Lawlessness is operating in human society today, and it is a satanic mystery. When someone comes out from under the principle of God’s ruling over man, that one enters into a satanic mystery, the mystery of lawlessness. However, we praise the Lord that there is another mystery in this universe, the mystery of godliness! This is the mystery that we are in; we are in the mystery of godliness. This is a wonderful mystery, the mystery of “God-likeness.” The mystery of godliness is the mystery of God dispensing Himself into our tripartite being to make us the same as He is in life and nature but not in the Godhead until we, in the flesh, express Him in all His glory ([[1 Timothy-03#v16|1 Tim. 3:16]]). 

We can see something related to this from different angles in Revelation 19 and Daniel 2. In Revelation 19 the Lord comes down with the overcomers, with His overcoming bride, to the battle at Armageddon. According to verse 19, when Christ comes down with His overcomers as His army, Antichrist will gather his armies to make war with Him. How utterly foolish and illogical it is for anyone to try to fight against the Triune God directly. At that time there will be the man of lawlessness with the satanic mystery of lawlessness making war with Christ and His overcomers, who come down from the heavens as the mystery of godliness. These two mysteries will collide, and the mystery of lawlessness will be shattered and defeated. [[Daniel-02#v34|Daniel 2:34-35]] describes this defeat, saying, “A stone was cut out without hands, and it struck the image…and it was crushed all at once, and they became like chaff from the summer threshing floors; and the wind carried them away so that no trace of them was found. And the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled the whole earth.” After this, there will be no more lawlessness, not even a trace, but only godliness filling the whole earth, even the entire universe. We should be full of gladness and thanksgiving that we are in the mystery of godliness. We should praise the Lord that we can be under the principle of God’s ruling over man, under God’s throne.

Then through the fire He becomes the glowing electrum to us, and we are filled with the radiant expression of the redeeming God—the glowing electrum. ## VI. Ezekiel 1 says that there is a rainbow as the brightness around the man who is sitting on the throne, signifying the splendor and glory around the Lord on the throne—[[Ezekiel-01#v28|Ezek. 1:28]]; [[Revelation-04#v3|Rev. 4:3]]

### A. At Noah's time the rainbow in the cloud was a sign of God's faithfulness in keeping His covenant with man and every living creature, signifying that He would never again destroy mankind with a flood—[[Genesis-09#v13|Gen. 9:13]].

Ezekiel 1 says that there is a rainbow as the brightness around the man who is sitting on the throne, signifying the splendor and glory around the Lord on the throne ([[Revelation-28#v28|v. 28]]; [[Revelation-04#v3|Rev. 4:3]]). At Noah’s time the rainbow in the cloud was a sign of God’s faithfulness in keeping His covenant with man and every living creature, signifying that He would never again destroy mankind with a flood ([[Genesis-09#v13|Gen. 9:13]]).
### B. A rainbow can be considered as being produced from three basic colors—red, yellow, and blue.

A rainbow can be considered as being produced from three basic colors—red, yellow, and blue: red, the color of fire, refers to God’s holiness; yellow, the color of electrum, signifies God’s glory; and blue, the color of the sapphire throne, signifies God’s righteousness ([[Psalm-89#v14|Psa. 89:14]]). In the rainbow the three basic colors—blue, red, and yellow—signify righteousness, holiness, and glory. Blue signifies righteousness; red refers to holiness; and yellow signifies glory.
#### 1. Red, the color of fire, refers to God's holiness.

#### 2. Yellow, the color of electrum, signifies God's glory.

#### 3. Blue, the color of the sapphire throne, signifies God's righteousness—[[Psalm-89#v14|Psa. 89:14]].

### C. God's righteousness, holiness, and glory are three divine attributes that keep sinners away from God—[[Genesis-03#v24|Gen. 3:24]].

God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory are three divine attributes that keep sinners away from God ([[Genesis-03#v24|Gen. 3:24]]). When man fell, God guarded the way to the tree of life. He did not give man a second chance to eat of the tree of life. [[Genesis-03#v24|Genesis 3:24]] says, “So He drove the man out, and at the east of the garden of Eden He placed the cherubim and a flaming sword which turned in every direction to guard the way to the tree of life.” The sword signifies God’s righteousness, the flame signifies God’s holiness, and the cherubim signify God’s glory. What guarded the way to the tree of life was God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory. His divine attributes prevented man from accessing the tree of life. God did not want man to partake of the tree of life in his fallen condition, because if man had done so, he would have lived forever with his fallen nature. Instead, God guarded the tree of life with His righteousness, holiness, and glory. These are the three divine attributes that keep sinners away from God.
### D. However, Christ came, died on the cross to satisfy the requirements of God's righteousness, holiness, and glory, and was resurrected, and He is now our righteousness, holiness, and glory—[[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]].

However, Christ came, died on the cross to satisfy the requirements of God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory, and was resurrected, and He is now our righteousness, holiness, and glory ([[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]). When Christ came and died on the cross, He fully satisfied the requirements of God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory. Then He was resurrected, and now in His resurrection He is our righteousness, holiness, and glory. It is amazing that the very attributes of righteousness, holiness, and glory that prevented us from eating Him as the tree of life have now been brought into us and assure us access to Christ as the tree of life. We are now experiencing, enjoying, and expressing Him as the tree of life because He has satisfied the demands of God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory on the cross and is now our righteousness, holiness, and glory. This is seen in [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Corinthians 1:30]] , which says, “Of Him you are in Christ Jesus, who became wisdom to us from God: both righteousness and sanctification and redemption.” Of God we are now in Christ Jesus who became wisdom to us from God: both righteousness and sanctification, which is holiness, and redemption, which is glory. 

He as wisdom is being transmitted to us; this is why we corporately as the church are called God’s masterpiece ([[Ephesians-02#v10|Eph. 2:10]]). The word for masterpiece in Greek is poiema. Poiema is a work of art that expresses the maker’s wisdom and design. The church is God’s poiema,which expresses His infinite wisdom and divine design. His infinite wisdom is expressed in the church, in us. We are His masterpiece, expressing His infinite wisdom and divine design. [[Ephesians-03#v10|Ephesians 3:10]] goes further to say, “In order that now to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenlies the multifarious wisdom of God might be made known through the church.” When we are constituted with the riches of Christ, who is wisdom to us from God, we then make known the multifarious wisdom of God to all the angelic rulers and authorities and to the entire universe. The church, God’s masterpiece, is God’s wise exhibition of all that He is. This exhibition is just Christ as wisdom to us from God, having been wrought into us to be our righteousness and sanctification and redemption. Christ as wisdom to us from God is righteousness, holiness, and glory. The very attributes that prevented us from partaking of the tree of life are now being transmitted into us to make us the wise exhibition of all that God is as His poiema, His masterpiece, expressing Him.
### E. Because we, the believers, are now in Christ, in the sight of God we bear the appearance of Christ as righteousness, holiness, and glory; this is the appearance of a rainbow as a testimony of God's faithfulness in sparing us and saving us, the fallen ones, from His judgment on sinners.

Because we, the believers, are now in Christ, in the sight of God we bear the appearance of Christ as righteousness, holiness, and glory; this is the appearance of a rainbow as a testimony of God’s faithfulness in sparing us and saving us, the fallen ones, from His judgment on sinners.
### F. In the New Jerusalem, a city whose foundations have the appearance of a rainbow ([[Revelation-21#v19|Rev. 21:19-20]]), we, the aggregate of the saved ones, will be a rainbow reflecting the brightness of God's righteousness, holiness, and glory and testifying forever that God is righteous and faithful.

In the New Jerusalem, a city whose foundations have the appearance of a rainbow ([[Revelation-21#v19|Rev. 21:19-20]]), we, the aggregate of the saved ones, will be a rainbow reflecting the brightness of God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory and testifying forever that God is righteous and faithful. The foundations of the New Jerusalem have the appearance of a rainbow. We, the aggregate of the saved ones, as constituents of the New Jerusalem, will form a rainbow. Hallelujah! We will be a rainbow reflecting the brightness of God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory, testifying forever that God is righteous, faithful, trustworthy, and reliable.

However, this reality is not limited to the New Jerusalem in the millennial kingdom and eternity; the spiritual reality of this rainbow should also be manifested in the church today. We need to allow God to fill us with His presence of righteousness by giving Him the full opportunity to work in us as the fire of holiness for His splendor of glory through us. From the time of Noah, God gave man the rainbow as a sign of His covenant to never again judge the earth with a flood. We can see from this that the rainbow is a sign of God’s faithfulness in keeping His covenant that there will be no more judgment of death. This was God’s covenant with Noah assuring him and everyone that came after him that there would be no more judgment of death by water.

Today we are people of the new covenant. As such, we must live under the new covenant and not believe in any failures, weaknesses, darkness, or negative things. We are the covenanted people, and we have a verse of promise to meet every situation. [[1 Thessalonians-05#v23|First Thessalonians 5:23]] says, “The God of peace Himself sanctify you wholly, and may your spirit and soul and body be preserved complete, without blame, at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.” Then verse 24 says, “Faithful is He who calls you, who also will do it.” Jude 24 says, “To Him who is able to guard you from stumbling and to set you before His glory without blemish in exultation.” This verse does not say that God will merely pick us up when we stumble; it says that He will guard us from stumbling. The word _exultation_ in this verse means the exuberance of triumphant joy. What good news! These are just a few examples; we have a verse of promise to meet every situation. The Bible is full of all kinds of promises to meet every situation. We should pray-read and muse upon these promises.

As portrayed in Ezekiel 1, the Christian life and the church life will consummate in such a rainbow; at that point God’s eternal plan will have been accomplished. Hallelujah! Praise the Lord! The divine attributes are expressed in our human virtues as righteousness, holiness, and glory. Because He died to satisfy the demands of God’s righteousness, holiness, and glory, He has opened the way to the tree of life, and because we are eating, enjoying, and expressing the tree of life, we express righteousness, holiness, and glory as a rainbow to the whole universe!—E.
