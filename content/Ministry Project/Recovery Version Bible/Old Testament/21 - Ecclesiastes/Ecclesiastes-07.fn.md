### Footnote 6.1 — vanity

**Verse:** [[Ecclesiastes-07#v6|Ecclesiastes 7:6]]

In his searching and testing all things of the human life under the sun, Solomon observed that all things are vanity of vanities, regardless of the kind of persons involved—wise or foolish, diligent or lazy, rich or poor, old or young, high or low, righteous or wicked, good or sinful, clean or unclean—and regardless of how they were born, how they worked, how they died, and what their end was ([[Ecclesiastes-07#v6|7:6]], [[Ecclesiastes-07#v15|15]]; [[Ecclesiastes-08#v10|8:10]], [[Ecclesiastes-08#v14|14]]; [[Ecclesiastes-09#v9|9:9]]; [[Ecclesiastes-11#v8|11:8]], [[Ecclesiastes-11#v10|10]]; [[Ecclesiastes-12#v8|12:8]]).
