# Ecclesiastes-11 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Ecclesiastes-11|Ecclesiastes 11]]

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — young

**Verse:** [[Ecclesiastes-11#v9|Ecclesiastes 11:9]]

In his advice to young men, the writer says that they should endeavor to enjoy the human life in their youth in the light of God’s judgment ([[Ecclesiastes-12#v14|12:14]] and [note](<Ecclesiastes-12.fn#Footnote 14.1 — judgment>)) in order to remove vexation from their heart and put away evil from their flesh ([[Ecclesiastes-11#v9|vv. 9-10]]). He also advises them to remember their Creator while they are young and not hesitate in doing this until old age comes ([[Ecclesiastes-12#v1|12:1]]).
