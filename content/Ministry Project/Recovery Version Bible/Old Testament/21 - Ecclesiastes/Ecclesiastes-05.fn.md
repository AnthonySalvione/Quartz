## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Guard

**Verse:** [[Ecclesiastes-05#v1|Ecclesiastes 5:1]]

Solomon’s word here regarding contacting God is not with the view of encouragement but with the view of caution. This is different from the view of the apostle Paul in his encouraging the believers to approach God to receive mercy and find grace for timely help ([[Hebrews-04#v16|Heb. 4:16]]).
