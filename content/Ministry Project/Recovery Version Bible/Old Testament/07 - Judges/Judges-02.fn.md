# Judges-02 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Judges-02|Judges 2]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Angel

**Verse:** [[Judges-02#v1|Judges 2:1]]

The Angel of Jehovah is God Himself in His Divine Trinity serving His elect as a Servant (cf. [[Hebrews-01#v14|Heb. 1:14]]). The embodiment of the Triune God is Christ, and Christ is the Angel of Jehovah, who took care of Israel as Jehovah in action in the Old Testament (see note 21 in [[Exodus-03|Exo. 3]]). For Christ to be the Angel of Jehovah means that God has appointed and commissioned Himself in His Divine Trinity to act in caring for His people. 

Because Israel did not act as a proper wife, the very Jehovah who was the Husband, the Head, and the King of Israel became a Servant to His wife. He came to her not as a Husband, Head, or King but as the Angel of Jehovah, who was sent by Jehovah ([[Zechariah-02#v9|Zech. 2:9-11]]). Since Israel did not regard Jehovah as the Head, He became a Servant to serve her. His word to Israel in [[Judges-02#v1|vv. 1-3]] was not a rebuke or a command but the admonition of a servant.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — forsook

**Verse:** [[Judges-02#v12|Judges 2:12]]

Man’s forsaking of God began in [[Genesis-03|Gen. 3]], when man, under the seducing of Satan, forsook God as the tree of life and joined himself to Satan as the tree of knowledge ([[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]], [[Genesis-02#v16|16-17]]; [[Genesis-03#v1|3:1-6]]). To take the tree of knowledge is actually to marry Satan and divorce God. Since the beginning of man’s existence man has been forsaking God and joining to Satan, taking many things as replacements of God (idols). This forsaking of God and joining to Satan, portrayed here by Israel’s forsaking Jehovah and joining themselves to idols, is the strongest factor behind the chaos in human society, including that in Israel. See [[Jeremiah-02#v13|Jer. 2:13]] and note.
