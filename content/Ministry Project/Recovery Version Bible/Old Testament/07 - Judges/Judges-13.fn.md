# Judges-13 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Judges-13|Judges 13]]

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — Angel

**Verse:** [[Judges-13#v3|Judges 13:3]]

The Angel of Jehovah in [[Judges-13#v3|vv. 3-21]] is Jehovah, the Triune God ([[Judges-13#v22|vv. 22-23]]). This Angel is Christ as the sent One of God (see [note 2¹](<Exodus-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Angel>) in [[Exodus-03|Exo. 3]]).

### Footnote 3.2 — appeared

**Verse:** [[Judges-13#v3|Judges 13:3]]

Samson’s birth was a miracle initiated by the appearing of the Angel of Jehovah. When Samson was in the womb of his mother, he was sanctified to be a Nazarite. As he grew up, he was clean and pure according to God’s ordination ([[Judges-13#v4|vv. 4-5]], [[Judges-13#v14|14]]), and he was empowered by the Spirit of God as the holy, economical Spirit ([[Judges-13#v25|v. 25]]; [[Judges-14#v5|14:5-6]], [[Judges-14#v19|19]]; [[Judges-15#v14|15:14]]).

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — man

**Verse:** [[Judges-13#v6|Judges 13:6]]

The man of God was Christ as a man. Thus, the Triune God embodied in Christ appeared to Manoah and his wife before Christ’s incarnation. See [note 2¹](<Genesis-18.fn#Footnote 2.1 — men>) in [[Genesis-18|Gen. 18]].

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — wonderful

**Verse:** [[Judges-13#v18|Judges 13:18]]

Every item of what Christ is, is wonderful ([[Isaiah-09#v6|Isa. 9:6]]). He is the wonder in the entire universe; thus, He is wonderful.
