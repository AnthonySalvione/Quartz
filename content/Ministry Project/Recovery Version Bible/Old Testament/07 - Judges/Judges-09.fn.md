# Judges-09 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Judges-09|Judges 9]]

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — slew

**Verse:** [[Judges-09#v5|Judges 9:5]]

The record of this chapter portrays the degradation and corruption of God’s elect Israel in their forsaking Jehovah their God and their worshipping the idols of the Canaanites ([[Judges-08#v33|8:33]]), which issued in their indulgence in fleshly lust by having many wives to produce many sons ([[Judges-08#v30|8:30-31]]; [[Judges-09#v5|9:5]]; [[Judges-10#v4|10:4]]; [[Judges-12#v8|12:8-9]], [[Judges-12#v13|13-14]]; [[Judges-16#v1|16:1-4]]) and in the wantonness of their hatred in slaying one another ([[Judges-12#v1|12:1-6]]; [[Judges-19|Judges 19—20]]), bringing themselves to an end in full destruction.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — olive

**Verse:** [[Judges-09#v8|Judges 9:8]]

The olive tree signifies Christ (cf. [[Romans-11#v17|Rom. 11:17]]; [[Zechariah-04#v11|Zech. 4:11-14]]; [[Revelation-11#v4|Rev. 11:4a]]) as the One who is full of the Holy Spirit and anointed with the Spirit ([[Luke-04#v1|Luke 4:1a]], [[Luke-04#v18|18a]]; [[Hebrews-01#v9|Heb. 1:9]]), signified by the olive oil. The oil of the olive tree was used to honor God and man ([[Judges-09#v9|v. 9]]), signifying that those who walk by the Spirit honor God ([[Galatians-05#v16|Gal. 5:16]], [[Galatians-05#v25|25]]), and those who minister the Spirit honor man ([[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v8|8]]).

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — vine

**Verse:** [[Judges-09#v12|Judges 9:12]]

The vine signifies Christ as the One who sacrificed Himself by being “pressed” on the cross to produce new wine to cheer God and man ([[Matthew-09#v17|Matt. 9:17]]).
