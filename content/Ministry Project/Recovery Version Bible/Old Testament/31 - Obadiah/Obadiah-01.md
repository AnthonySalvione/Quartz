## v1

The vision of Obadiah. Thus says the Lord Jehovah concerning Edom, /  
We have heard a report from Jehovah, /  
And an envoy has been sent among the nations: /  
Arise, and let us rise up against it for battle.

## v2

Indeed I have made you small among the nations; /  
You are very despised.

## v3

The pride of your heart has beguiled you, /  
O you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, /  
Whose habitation is lofty, /  
Who says in his heart, /  
Who will bring me down to the ground?

## v4

Though you set yourself on high like the eagle /  
And though you make your nest among the stars, /  
I will bring you down from there, /  
Declares Jehovah.

## v5

If thieves came to you, /  
If spoilers in the night — /  
How you would be cut off! — /  
Would they not steal only their fill? /  
If grape gatherers came to you, /  
Would they not leave gleanings?

## v6

How thoroughly searched Esau is! /  
How sought out his hidden treasures are!

## v7

All the men of your confederacy /  
Have sent you away to the border; /  
The men who were at peace with you /  
Have deceived you; they have prevailed against you. /  
They make your bread /  
A snare under you. /  
There is no understanding in him.

## v8

Will I not in that day, /  
Declares Jehovah, /  
Destroy the wise men from Edom, /  
And understanding from the mountain of Esau?

## v9

And your mighty men will be dismayed, O Teman, /  
So that each one is cut off from the mountain of Esau by slaughter.

## v10

Because of the violence against your brother Jacob, /  
Shame will cover you, and you will be cut off forever.

## v11

In the day that you stood on the side, /  
In the day that strangers took his substance captive /  
And foreigners entered into his gates /  
And cast lots over Jerusalem, /  
You also were like one of them.

## v12

But do not look to the day of your brother, /  
To the day of his misfortune; /  
Neither rejoice concerning the children of Judah /  
In the day of their destruction, /  
Nor boast /  
In the day of distress.

## v13

Do not enter the gate of My people /  
In the day of their calamity; /  
Do not look, even you, on his affliction /  
In the day of his calamity, /  
Nor send forth your hands on his substance /  
In the day of his calamity.

## v14

And do not stand at the crossroads /  
To cut off those of his who escape, /  
Nor deliver those of his who survive /  
In the day of distress.

## v15

For the day of Jehovah is near /  
Upon all the nations: /  
As you have done, it will be done to you; /  
Your recompense will return upon your head.

## v16

For as you, O people of Judah, have drunk the cup of wrath upon My holy mountain, /  
All the nations will drink the same continually; /  
Indeed they will drink and swallow down, /  
And they will be as though they had not been.

## v17

But in Mount Zion /  
There will be those who have escaped, /  
And it will be holy. /  
And the house of Jacob will possess /  
Their possessions.

## v18

And the house of Jacob will be a fire, /  
And the house of Joseph a flame; /  
And the house of Esau will be as stubble, /  
And they will burn among them and devour them; /  
And there will be no survivor /  
To the house of Esau, /  
For Jehovah has spoken.

## v19

And those of the south will possess the mountain of Esau, /  
And those of the lowlands, the Philistines; /  
Indeed they will possess the field of Ephraim /  
And the field of Samaria, /  
And Benjamin will possess Gilead.

## v20

And the captivity of this host of the children of Israel, /  
Who are among the Canaanites, will possess as far as Zarephath; /  
And the captivity of Jerusalem, who are in Sepharad, /  
Will possess the cities of the south.

## v21

And saviors will come up on Mount Zion /  
To judge the mountain of Esau, /  
And the kingdom will be Jehovah’s.
