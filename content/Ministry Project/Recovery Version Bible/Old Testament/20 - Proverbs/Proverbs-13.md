## v1

A wise son hears his father’s instruction,  
But a scorner does not listen to rebuke.

## v2

A man will eat good from the fruit of his mouth,  
But the desire of the treacherous is violence.

## v3

He who guards his mouth keeps his soul,  
But he who opens wide his lips will have destruction.

## v4

The soul of the sluggard desires and has nothing,  
But the soul of the diligent will be made fat.

## v5

A righteous man hates what is false,  
But a wicked man is odious and is confounded.

## v6

Righteousness guards him who is blameless in the way,  
But wickedness overthrows the sinner.

## v7

There is one who pretends to be rich yet has nothing;  
There is one who pretends to be poor yet has great wealth.

## v8

The ransom of a man’s life is his riches,  
But the poor hears no threatening.

## v9

The light of the righteous rejoices,  
But the lamp of the wicked goes out.

## v10

Through pride comes nothing but strife,  
But wisdom is with those who receive counsel.

## v11

Wealth obtained by vanity will be diminished,  
But he who gathers by labor increases it.

## v12

Hope deferred makes the heart sick,  
But when what is desired comes, it is a tree of life.

## v13

Whoever despises the word brings destruction on himself,  
But he who fears the commandment will be rewarded.

## v14

The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life,  
That one may turn aside from the snares of death.

## v15

Good understanding gives favor,  
But the way of the treacherous is hard.

## v16

Every prudent man works with knowledge,  
But a fool displays his folly.

## v17

A wicked messenger falls into evil,  
But a faithful envoy brings healing.

## v18

Poverty and shame will come to him who refuses correction,  
But he who regards reproof will be honored.

## v19

A desire accomplished is sweet to the soul,  
But it is an abomination to fools to depart from evil.

## v20

He who walks with wise men will be wise,  
But the companion of fools will be troubled.

## v21

Evil pursues sinners,  
But good recompenses the righteous.

## v22

A good man leaves an inheritance to his children’s children,  
But the wealth of the sinner is stored up for the righteous man.

## v23

The fallow ground of the poor yields much food,  
But it is destroyed through injustice.

## v24

He who spares his rod hates his son,  
But he who loves him chastens him early.

## v25

The righteous man eats to the satisfying of his soul,  
But the belly of the wicked will lack.
