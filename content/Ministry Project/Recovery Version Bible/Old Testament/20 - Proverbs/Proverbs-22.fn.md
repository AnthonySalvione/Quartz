## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — fail

**Verse:** [[Proverbs-22#v8|Proverbs 22:8]]

The Septuagint has the additional proverb: “God blesses a cheerful and liberal man, but a man will fully prove the vanity of his works.” The first part of this is the source of the quotation in [[2 Corinthians-09#v7|2 Cor. 9:7]].
