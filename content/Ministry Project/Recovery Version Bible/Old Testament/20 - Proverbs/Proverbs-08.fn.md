# Proverbs-08 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Proverbs-08|Proverbs 8]]

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Proverbs-08#v12|Proverbs 8:12]]

Or, I, wisdom, make prudence my dwelling. In certain portions of this book the wisdom of God is personified ([[Proverbs-01#v20|1:20]]; [[Proverbs-03#v19|3:19]]; [[Proverbs-04#v5|4:5-9]]; [[Proverbs-07#v4|7:4]]; [[Proverbs-08#v1|8:1-36]]; [[Proverbs-09#v1|9:1-11]]). This personification of God’s wisdom is a reference to the second of the Divine Trinity, Christ, the Son of God ([[1 Corinthians-01#v24|1 Cor. 1:24]]; [[Colossians-02#v3|Col. 2:3]]; [[Matthew-11#v19|Matt. 11:19]] and [note 3](<Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 19.3 — works>)), who became wisdom from God to all the New Testament believers ([[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]).
