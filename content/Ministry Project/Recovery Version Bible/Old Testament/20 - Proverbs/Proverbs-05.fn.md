# Proverbs-05 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Proverbs-05|Proverbs 5]]

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — strange

**Verse:** [[Proverbs-05#v3|Proverbs 5:3]]

The charge in [[Proverbs-05#v1|vv. 1-20]] is to hold marriage in honor ([[Hebrews-13#v4|Heb. 13:4]] and [note 1](<Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 4.1 — marriage>)). In holding marriage in honor, the man’s faithfulness is the base, and the woman’s virtues are the building up ([[Proverbs-12#v4|12:4a]]; [[Proverbs-14#v1|14:1a]]; [[Proverbs-31#v10|31:10-31]]). This is one of the principles in Proverbs for man to live a proper human life (see [note 7¹](<Proverbs-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — fear>), par. 1, in [[Proverbs-01|Proverbs 1]]).
