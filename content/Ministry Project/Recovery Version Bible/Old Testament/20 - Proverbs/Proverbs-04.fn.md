### Footnote 18.1 — dawn

**Verse:** [[Proverbs-04#v18|Proverbs 4:18]]

The light of dawn, the sunrise, signifies Christ in His coming ([[Luke-01#v78|Luke 1:78]]; [[2 Peter-01#v19|2 Pet. 1:19]]). This figure may also signify our being revived every morning. The Christian life is like the dawning of the sun ([[Judges-05#v31|Judg. 5:31]]; [[Matthew-13#v43|Matt. 13:43]]). As Christians we should follow the sun to be revived and to have a new beginning every morning. Then we should continue to rise and shine brighter and brighter until the full day.
