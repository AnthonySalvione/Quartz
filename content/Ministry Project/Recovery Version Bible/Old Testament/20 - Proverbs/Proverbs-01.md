## v1

The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, the king of Israel:

## v2

For knowing wisdom and instruction;  
For discerning words of understanding;

## v3

For receiving instruction in wise conduct,  
Righteousness, justice, and equity;

## v4

For giving prudence to the simple,  
Knowledge and discretion to the young man;

## v5

That the wise man may hear and increase in learning,  
And he who has understanding may acquire sound counsel;

## v6

For understanding proverb and figure,  
The words of the wise and their difficult sayings.

## v7

The fear of Jehovah is the beginning of knowledge;  
Fools despise wisdom and instruction.

## v8

Hear, my son, the instruction of your father,  
And do not reject the teaching of your mother;

## v9

For they will be a wreath of grace for your head  
And ornaments for your neck.

## v10

My son, if sinners entice you,  
Do not consent.

## v11

If they say, Come with us; let us lie in wait for blood;  
Let us ambush the innocent without cause;

## v12

Let us swallow them alive like Sheol,  
And whole, like those who go down into the pit.

## v13

We will find all manner of precious goods;  
We will fill our houses with booty;

## v14

Throw in your lot with us;  
We will all have one purse —

## v15

My son, do not walk with them on this way;  
Keep your foot from their path.

## v16

For their feet run toward evil,  
And they hasten to shed blood.

## v17

For in vain is the net spread  
In the eyes of any bird;

## v18

Yet they lie in wait for their own blood;  
They ambush their own lives!

## v19

Such are the ways of everyone greedy for gain;  
It takes away the life of its owners.

## v20

Wisdom cries out in the street;  
She utters her voice in the open squares.

## v21

At the head of the tumultuous street she cries;  
At the entrance of the gates in the city she utters her sayings:

## v22

How long, O simple ones, will you love simpleness,  
And will scoffers delight themselves in scoffing,  
And will fools hate knowledge?

## v23

Turn yourselves at my reproof —  
Immediately I will pour out my spirit on you;  
I will make my words known to you.

## v24

Because I called and you refused;  
I stretched out my hand and no one heeded,

## v25

And you neglected all my counsel  
And would have none of my reproof;

## v26

I also will laugh at your calamity;  
I will mock when what you dread comes,

## v27

When what you dread comes like a storm,  
And your calamity comes like a whirlwind,  
When distress and anguish come upon you.

## v28

Then they will call on me, but I will not answer;  
They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me.

## v29

Because they hated knowledge  
And did not choose the fear of Jehovah,

## v30

And would have none of my counsel  
And despised all my reproof;

## v31

They shall eat of the fruit of their own way  
And be filled with their own devices.

## v32

For the turning away of the simple will kill them,  
And the prosperity of the foolish will cause them to perish;

## v33

But he who listens to me will dwell securely  
And will live at ease, without the dread of evil.
