## v1

My son, if you receive my words  
And treasure up my commandments within you,

## v2

Making your ear attentive to wisdom  
And inclining your heart to understanding;

## v3

Indeed, if you cry out for discernment  
And lift up your voice for understanding;

## v4

If you seek her like silver  
And search for her like hidden treasures,

## v5

Then you will understand the fear of Jehovah  
And find the knowledge of God.

## v6

For Jehovah gives wisdom;  
From His mouth come knowledge and understanding;

## v7

He stores up sound wisdom for the upright;  
He is a shield to those who walk in integrity,

## v8

Guarding the paths of justice  
And keeping the way of His faithful ones.

## v9

Then you will understand righteousness and justice  
And equity, indeed, every good path.

## v10

For wisdom will enter your heart,  
And knowledge will be pleasant to your soul;

## v11

Discretion will watch over you;  
Understanding will keep you:

## v12

To deliver you from the way of evil,  
From the man who speaks perverse things,

## v13

From those who forsake the paths of uprightness  
To walk in the ways of darkness,

## v14

Who rejoice in doing evil  
And delight in the perverseness of evil,

## v15

Whose paths are crooked,  
And who are devious in their ways;

## v16

To deliver you from the strange woman,  
From the adulteress who flatters with her words,

## v17

Who forsakes the companion of her youth  
And forgets the covenant of her God;

## v18

For her house sinks down to death,  
And her paths lead to the dead;

## v19

None who go to her ever return,  
Nor do they attain to the paths of life;

## v20

That you may walk in the way of good men  
And keep the paths of the righteous;

## v21

For the upright will dwell in the land,  
And the perfect will remain in it;

## v22

But the wicked will be cut off from the land,  
And the treacherous will be uprooted from it.
