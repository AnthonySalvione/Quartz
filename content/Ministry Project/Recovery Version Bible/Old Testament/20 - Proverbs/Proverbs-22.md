## v1

A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches,  
And favor is better than silver and gold.

## v2

The rich and the poor have this in common:  
Jehovah is the Maker of them all.

## v3

A prudent man sees evil and hides himself,  
But the simple pass on and suffer punishment for it.

## v4

The reward of humility and the fear of Jehovah  
Are riches and honor and life.

## v5

Thorns and snares are in the way of the perverse;  
He who guards his soul will keep far from them.

## v6

Train up a child according to the way he should go;  
Even when he is old, he will not depart from it.

## v7

The rich man rules over the poor,  
And the borrower is servant to the lender.

## v8

He who sows injustice will reap iniquity,  
And the rod of his wrath will fail.

## v9

He who is generous will be blessed,  
For he gives some of his bread to the poor.

## v10

Cast out the scorner, and contention will go out;  
Indeed, strife and dishonor will cease.

## v11

He who loves pureness of heart  
And whose speech is gracious will have the king as his friend.

## v12

The eyes of Jehovah preserve knowledge,  
But He overthrows the words of the treacherous man.

## v13

The sluggard says, There is a lion outside!  
I will be slain in the streets!

## v14

The mouth of strange women is a deep pit;  
He with whom Jehovah is indignant will fall in there.

## v15

Foolishness is bound up in the heart of a child;  
But the rod of correction will drive it far from him.

## v16

He who oppresses the poor to multiply his gain,  
And he who gives to the rich will only come to want.

## v17

Incline your ear, and hear the words of the wise,  
And apply your heart to my knowledge;

## v18

For it is a pleasant thing if you keep them within you;  
Let them be ready on your lips.

## v19

So that your trust may be in Jehovah,  
I have made them known to you today, even to you.

## v20

Have I not written to you excellent things  
Of counsels and knowledge,

## v21

To make you know the certainty of the words of truth,  
That you may answer back words of truth to those who send you?

## v22

Do not rob the poor, because he is poor;  
Nor oppress the afflicted in the gate;

## v23

For Jehovah will plead their cause,  
And rob the life of those who rob them.

## v24

Make no friendship with a man who is given to anger,  
And with a wrathful man do not go;

## v25

Lest you learn his ways  
And get a snare for your soul.

## v26

Do not be one of those who give their hand as a pledge  
Or one of those who are a surety for debts.

## v27

If you have nothing with which to pay,  
Why should your bed be taken away from under you?

## v28

Do not remove the ancient boundary marker,  
Which your fathers have set up.

## v29

Do you see a man skilled in his work?  
He will stand before kings;  
He will not stand before obscure men.
