## v1

Better is a poor man who walks in his integrity  
Than he who is perverse in his lips and is a fool.

## v2

Also it is not good for a person to be without knowledge,  
And he who makes haste with his feet sins.

## v3

The foolishness of a man subverts his way,  
And his heart is indignant toward Jehovah.

## v4

Wealth adds many friends,  
But the poor man is separated from his friend.

## v5

A false witness will not go unpunished,  
And he who utters lies will not escape.

## v6

Many will entreat the favor of the generous man,  
And every man is a friend to one who gives gifts.

## v7

All the brothers of the poor man hate him;  
How much more do his friends go far from him!  
He pursues them with words, but they are not there.

## v8

He who acquires sense loves his own soul;  
He who keeps understanding will find good.

## v9

A false witness will not go unpunished,  
And he who utters lies will perish.

## v10

Delicate living is not fitting for a fool;  
Much less for a servant to rule over princes.

## v11

The discretion of a man makes him slow to anger,  
And it is his glory to overlook a transgression.

## v12

The king’s wrath is like the roaring of a lion,  
But his favor is like dew upon the grass.

## v13

A foolish son is the ruin of his father,  
And the contentions of a wife are a continual dripping.

## v14

House and wealth are an inheritance from fathers,  
But a prudent wife is from Jehovah.

## v15

Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep,  
And the idle soul will suffer hunger.

## v16

He who keeps the commandment keeps his soul;  
He who is careless of his ways will die.

## v17

He who has pity upon a poor man lends to Jehovah,  
And He will repay him for his good deed.

## v18

Discipline your son, for there is hope;  
But do not set your heart on destroying him.

## v19

A man of great wrath will bear the penalty;  
For if you deliver him, you will only have to do it again.

## v20

Listen to counsel, and receive instruction,  
That you may be wise at the end of your days.

## v21

There are many devices in a man’s heart,  
But it is the counsel of Jehovah that will stand.

## v22

What is desirable in a man is his kindness,  
And a poor man is better than a liar.

## v23

The fear of Jehovah leads to life,  
And he who has it will lodge in contentment;  
He will not be visited with evil.

## v24

The sluggard buries his hand in the dish,  
And will not even bring it back to his mouth.

## v25

Strike a scorner, and the simple will learn prudence;  
And reprove one who has understanding, and he will understand knowledge.

## v26

He who does violence to his father and chases away his mother  
Is a son that causes shame and brings reproach.

## v27

Cease listening, my son, to instruction,  
And you will stray from the words of knowledge.

## v28

A worthless witness mocks justice,  
And the mouth of the wicked swallows up iniquity.

## v29

Judgments are prepared for scorners,  
And blows for the back of fools.
