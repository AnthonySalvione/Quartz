## v1

When you sit to eat with a ruler,  
Consider carefully who is before you;

## v2

And put a knife to your throat  
If you are a man of great appetite.

## v3

Do not desire his delicacies,  
For they are deceitful food.

## v4

Do not weary yourself to become rich;  
Cease from your consideration of it.

## v5

When you set your eyes upon it, it is gone;  
For wealth certainly makes itself wings,  
Like an eagle that flies toward heaven.

## v6

Do not eat the bread of one who is envious,  
And do not desire his delicacies;

## v7

For as he thinks within himself, so he is.  
He says to you, Eat and drink;  
But his heart is not with you.

## v8

You will vomit up the morsel which you have eaten,  
And waste your pleasant words.

## v9

Do not speak in the hearing of a fool,  
For he will despise the wisdom of your words.

## v10

Do not remove the ancient boundary marker,  
Or go into the fields of the fatherless;

## v11

For their Redeemer is strong;  
He will plead their cause against you.

## v12

Apply your heart to instruction,  
And your ears to words of knowledge.

## v13

Do not withhold correction from a child;  
If you beat him with the rod, he will not die.

## v14

If you beat him with the rod,  
You will deliver his soul from Sheol.

## v15

My son, if your heart is wise,  
My own heart will also rejoice;

## v16

My inward parts will also exult,  
When your lips speak right things.

## v17

Do not let your heart envy sinners,  
But live in the fear of Jehovah all day long;

## v18

For surely there is a latter end,  
And your hope will not be cut off.

## v19

Listen, my son, and be wise,  
And direct your heart in the way.

## v20

Do not be among those who get drunk with wine,  
Among gluttonous eaters of flesh;

## v21

For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty,  
And drowsiness will clothe them with rags.

## v22

Listen to your father who begot you,  
And do not despise your mother when she is old.

## v23

Buy truth, and do not sell it;  
Buy wisdom and instruction and understanding.

## v24

The father of the righteous man will greatly exult,  
And he who begets a wise child will rejoice in him.

## v25

Let your father and your mother rejoice,  
And let her who bore you exult.

## v26

My son, give me your heart,  
And let your eyes observe my ways.

## v27

For a harlot is a deep pit,  
And an adulteress is a narrow well.

## v28

Indeed, she lies in wait as a robber,  
And increases the number of the treacherous among men.

## v29

Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions?  
Who has complaining? Who has wounds without cause?  
Who has redness of eyes?

## v30

Those who linger late over wine,  
Those who go to seek out mixed wines.

## v31

Do not look upon the wine when it is red,  
When it sparkles in the cup,  
When it goes down smoothly;

## v32

In the end it bites like a serpent,  
And stings like an adder.

## v33

Your eyes will see strange things,  
And your heart will utter perverse things;

## v34

And you will be like one who lies down while at sea,  
Or like one who lies down at the top of a mast.

## v35

They struck me, you will say, but I was not hurt;  
They beat me, but I did not feel it;  
When will I awake? I will seek another drink.
