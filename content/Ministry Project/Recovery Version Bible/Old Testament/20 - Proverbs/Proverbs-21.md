## v1

The king’s heart is like streams of water in the hand of Jehovah;  
He turns it wherever He wishes.

## v2

Every way of a man is right in his own eyes,  
But Jehovah weighs the hearts.

## v3

To do righteousness and justice  
Is more acceptable to Jehovah than sacrifice.

## v4

A high look and a proud heart —  
The lamp of the wicked — is sin.

## v5

The plans of the diligent lead only to plentifulness,  
But everyone who is hasty comes only to want.

## v6

The getting of treasures by a lying tongue  
Is a fleeting vapor, a snare of death.

## v7

The violence of the wicked will sweep them away  
Because they refuse to do justice.

## v8

The way of a guilty man is crooked;  
But as for the pure, his work is right.

## v9

It is better to dwell in a corner of a housetop  
Than in a house shared with a contentious woman.

## v10

The soul of the wicked man desires evil;  
His neighbor finds no favor in his eyes.

## v11

When the scorner is punished, the simple man becomes wise;  
And when the wise man is instructed, he receives knowledge.

## v12

The righteous One considers the house of the wicked man,  
Overthrowing the wicked to their ruin.

## v13

Whoever closes his ear to the cry of the poor man  
Also will cry and will not be heard.

## v14

A gift in secret pacifies anger;  
And a present in the bosom, strong wrath.

## v15

It is joy to the righteous man to do justice,  
But it is destruction to the workers of iniquity.

## v16

The man who wanders from the way of understanding  
Will rest in the assembly of the dead.

## v17

He who loves pleasure will be a poor man;  
He who loves wine and oil will not be rich.

## v18

The wicked man is a ransom for the righteous man,  
And the treacherous man is in the place of the upright.

## v19

It is better to dwell in a desert land  
Than with a contentious and vexing woman.

## v20

There is precious treasure and oil in the dwelling of the wise man,  
But a foolish man swallows it up.

## v21

He who pursues righteousness and kindness  
Finds life, righteousness, and honor.

## v22

A wise man scales the city of the mighty  
And brings down the strength in which they trust.

## v23

Whoever guards his mouth and his tongue  
Keeps his soul from troubles.

## v24

Proud, Haughty, Scorner are the names  
Of him who works in the arrogance of pride.

## v25

The desire of the sluggard puts him to death,  
For his hands refuse to work.

## v26

All day long one covets greedily,  
But the righteous man gives and does not hold back.

## v27

The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination;  
How much more when he brings it with evil intent!

## v28

A false witness will perish,  
But the man who hears will speak so as to endure.

## v29

A wicked man hardens his face;  
But as for the upright, he establishes his ways.

## v30

There is no wisdom nor understanding  
Nor counsel that can avail against Jehovah.

## v31

The horse is prepared for the day of battle,  
But victory belongs to Jehovah.
