## v1

He who separates himself seeks his own desire  
And rages against all sound wisdom.

## v2

A fool has no delight in understanding,  
But only that his heart may reveal itself.

## v3

When the wicked man comes, contempt also comes;  
And with dishonor comes reproach.

## v4

The words of a man’s mouth are deep waters,  
A gushing stream, a fountain of wisdom.

## v5

To respect the person of the wicked man is not good,  
Nor to turn aside the righteous man in judgment.

## v6

A fool’s lips bring strife,  
And his mouth invites blows.

## v7

A fool’s mouth is his ruin,  
And his lips are the snare of his soul.

## v8

The words of a whisperer are like dainty morsels,  
And they go down into the innermost parts of one’s being.

## v9

He also who is slack in his work  
Is brother to him who is a destroyer.

## v10

The name of Jehovah is a strong tower;  
The righteous man runs into it and is safe.

## v11

The rich man’s substance is his strong city  
And like a high wall in his own imagination.

## v12

Before destruction the heart of man is haughty,  
But humility goes before honor.

## v13

As for him who gives an answer before he hears,  
It is folly and shame.

## v14

The spirit of a man can sustain his sickness,  
But a broken spirit who can bear?

## v15

The heart of one who has understanding acquires knowledge,  
And the ear of the wise seeks knowledge.

## v16

A man’s gift makes room for him  
And brings him before great men.

## v17

The first to plead his case seems just,  
Until his neighbor comes and cross-examines him.

## v18

The lot causes contentions to cease  
And decides between the mighty.

## v19

A brother offended is harder to be gained than a strong city,  
And contentions are like the bars of a castle.

## v20

With the fruit of his mouth a man’s stomach will be satisfied;  
With the produce of his lips he will be satisfied.

## v21

Death and life are in the power of the tongue,  
And those who love it will eat its fruit.

## v22

Whoever finds a wife finds a good thing,  
And obtains favor from Jehovah.

## v23

The poor man utters supplications,  
But the rich man answers roughly.

## v24

A man of many friends comes to destruction,  
But there is a true friend that sticks closer than a brother.
