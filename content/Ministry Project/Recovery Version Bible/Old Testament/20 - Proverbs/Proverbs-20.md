## v1

Wine is a mocker, strong drink a brawler;  
And whoever errs by it does not become wise.

## v2

The terror of a king is like the roaring of a lion;  
He who provokes him to anger sins against his own life.

## v3

It is an honor for a man to keep away from strife,  
But every fool rushes headlong into it.

## v4

The sluggard will not plow because of winter;  
Therefore he will beg in harvest and have nothing.

## v5

Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water,  
But a man of understanding will draw it out.

## v6

Many a man will proclaim his own kindness,  
But who can find a faithful man?

## v7

A righteous man who walks in his integrity,  
Blessed are his children after him.

## v8

A king who sits on the throne of judgment  
Scatters away all evil with his eyes.

## v9

Who can say, I have made my heart clean;  
I am pure from my sin?

## v10

Differing weights and differing measures,  
Both of them are an abomination to Jehovah.

## v11

Even a child makes himself known by his deeds,  
Whether his work is pure and whether it is upright.

## v12

The hearing ear and the seeing eye —  
Jehovah has made both of them.

## v13

Do not love sleep, or else you will come to poverty;  
Open your eyes, and be satisfied with bread.

## v14

It is bad, it is bad, says the buyer;  
But when he has gone away, then he boasts in his purchase.

## v15

There is gold and an abundance of corals,  
But the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel.

## v16

Take his garment when he becomes surety for a stranger,  
And hold the pledge when he becomes surety for foreigners.

## v17

The bread of falsehood is sweet to a man,  
But afterward his mouth will be filled with gravel.

## v18

Every plan is established by counsel,  
And by sound counsel make war.

## v19

He who goes about as a gossip reveals secrets;  
Therefore do not associate with one who opens his lips wide.

## v20

Whoever curses his father or his mother,  
His lamp will go out in deep darkness.

## v21

An inheritance gained hurriedly at the beginning  
Will not be blessed in the end.

## v22

Do not say, I will recompense evil;  
Wait for Jehovah, and He will save you.

## v23

Differing weights are an abomination to Jehovah,  
And false scales are not good.

## v24

A man’s steps are ordered by Jehovah;  
How then can man understand his own way?

## v25

It is a snare to a man to rashly say, It is holy,  
And after making the vows to begin to consider.

## v26

A wise king winnows the wicked  
And drives the threshing wheel over them.

## v27

The spirit of man is the lamp of Jehovah,  
Searching all the innermost parts of the inner being.

## v28

Lovingkindness and truth preserve the king,  
And his throne is upheld by kindness.

## v29

The glory of young men is their strength,  
And the honor of old men is gray hair.

## v30

Stripes that wound purge away evil,  
And strokes reach the innermost parts of the inner being.
