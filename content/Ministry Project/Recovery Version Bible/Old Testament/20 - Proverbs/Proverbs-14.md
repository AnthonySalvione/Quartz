## v1

The wise woman builds her house,  
But the foolish one tears it down with her own hands.

## v2

He who walks in his uprightness fears Jehovah,  
But he who is perverse in his ways despises Him.

## v3

In the mouth of a fool is a rod for his pride,  
But the lips of the wise will preserve them.

## v4

Where no oxen are, the manger is clean,  
But much increase comes by the strength of the ox.

## v5

A faithful witness will not lie,  
But a false witness utters lies.

## v6

A scoffer seeks wisdom and finds none,  
But knowledge comes easily to one who has understanding.

## v7

Go from the presence of a foolish man,  
For you will not perceive in him the lips of knowledge.

## v8

The wisdom of the prudent man is to understand his way,  
But the folly of fools is deceit.

## v9

A trespass offering mocks fools,  
But among the upright there is good will.

## v10

The heart knows its own bitterness,  
And a stranger does not share in its joy.

## v11

The house of the wicked will be destroyed,  
But the tent of the upright will flourish.

## v12

There is a way which seems right to a man,  
But the end of it is the ways of death.

## v13

Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful,  
And the end of joy is grief.

## v14

The backslider in heart will be filled with his own ways,  
And a good man will be satisfied from himself.

## v15

The simple man believes every word,  
But the prudent man considers his steps.

## v16

A wise man fears and departs from evil,  
But the fool is arrogant and is confident.

## v17

He who is quick to anger will deal foolishly,  
And a man of wicked devices is hated.

## v18

The simple inherit folly,  
But the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

## v19

The evil bow down before the good,  
And the wicked, at the gates of the righteous man.

## v20

The poor man is hated even by his own neighbor,  
But the rich man has many friends.

## v21

He who despises his neighbor sins,  
But happy is he who is gracious to the poor.

## v22

Do not they who devise evil go astray?  
But lovingkindness and truth are with those who devise good.

## v23

In all labor there is profit,  
But mere talk leads only to poverty.

## v24

The crown of the wise is their riches,  
But the folly of fools is only folly.

## v25

A truthful witness delivers souls,  
But he who utters lies causes deceit.

## v26

In the fear of Jehovah is strong confidence,  
And his children will have a place of refuge.

## v27

The fear of Jehovah is a fountain of life,  
That one may turn aside from the snares of death.

## v28

In a multitude of people is the king’s glory,  
But in an absence of people is the ruin of the prince.

## v29

He who is slow to anger is of great understanding,  
But he who has a hasty spirit exalts folly.

## v30

A tranquil heart is life to the flesh,  
But envy is rottenness to the bones.

## v31

He who oppresses the poor reproaches his Maker,  
But he who is gracious to the needy honors Him.

## v32

The wicked man is thrust down in his evildoing,  
But the righteous man has a refuge when he dies.

## v33

Wisdom rests quietly in the heart of one who has understanding,  
But in the inward part of fools it makes itself known.

## v34

Righteousness exalts a nation,  
But sin is a reproach to any people.

## v35

The king’s favor is toward a servant who acts prudently,  
But his overflowing wrath will be toward him who causes shame.
