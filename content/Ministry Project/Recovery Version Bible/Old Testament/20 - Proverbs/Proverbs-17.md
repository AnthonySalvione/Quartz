## v1

Better is a dry morsel and quietness with it  
Than a house full of feasting with strife.

## v2

A servant who deals wisely will rule over a son who causes shame,  
And will share in the inheritance among brothers.

## v3

The refining pot is for silver and the furnace for gold,  
But Jehovah tries the hearts.

## v4

An evildoer gives heed to wicked lips,  
And a liar gives ear to a mischievous tongue.

## v5

Whoever mocks the poor reproaches his Maker;  
He who rejoices at calamity will not go unpunished.

## v6

Grandchildren are the crown of old men,  
And the glory of children is their fathers.

## v7

Excellent speech is not fitting for a fool;  
Much less are lying lips for a noble man.

## v8

A bribe is like a precious stone in the eyes of its possessor:  
Wherever he turns, he prospers.

## v9

He who covers a transgression seeks love,  
But he who repeats a matter separates close friends.

## v10

A rebuke strikes deeper into him who has understanding  
Than a hundred stripes into a fool.

## v11

An evil man seeks only rebellion;  
Therefore a cruel messenger will be sent against him.

## v12

Let a bear robbed of her cubs meet a man  
Rather than a fool in his folly.

## v13

Whoever rewards evil for good,  
Evil will not depart from his house.

## v14

The beginning of contention is like letting out water;  
Therefore abandon strife before it breaks out.

## v15

He who justifies the wicked man, and he who condemns the righteous man,  
Both of them are an abomination to Jehovah.

## v16

Why is there a price in the hand of a fool to buy wisdom,  
When he has no sense?

## v17

A friend loves at all times,  
And a brother is born for adversity.

## v18

A man lacking sense gives his hand as a pledge  
And becomes surety in the presence of his neighbor.

## v19

He who loves transgression loves strife;  
He who raises high his entrance seeks destruction.

## v20

He who has a crooked heart finds no good,  
And he who has a perverse tongue falls into evil.

## v21

He who begets a fool does so to his sorrow,  
And the father of a fool has no joy.

## v22

A joyful heart is good medicine,  
But a broken spirit dries up the bones.

## v23

A wicked man receives a bribe from the bosom,  
To pervert the ways of justice.

## v24

Wisdom is before the face of him who has understanding,  
But the eyes of a fool are on the ends of the earth.

## v25

A foolish son is a grief to his father,  
And bitterness to her who bore him.

## v26

To also punish the righteous man is not good,  
Nor to strike nobles for their uprightness.

## v27

He who restrains his words has knowledge,  
And he who is cool in spirit is one who has understanding.

## v28

Even a fool, when he keeps silent, is counted wise;  
When he shuts his lips, he is counted prudent.
