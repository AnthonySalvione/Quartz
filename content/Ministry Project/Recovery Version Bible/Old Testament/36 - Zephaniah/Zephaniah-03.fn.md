## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — pure

**Verse:** [[Zephaniah-03#v9|Zephaniah 3:9]]

The changing of the peoples’ language into a pure language refers to Jehovah’s salvation of the Gentiles in the time of restoration ([[Matthew-19#v28|Matt. 19:28]]; [[Acts-03#v21|Acts 3:21]]). To change a people’s language is to change their culture, for culture is wrapped up with language. The original language of any people is dirty and impure. A people’s language becoming pure indicates that they have been thoroughly saved.
