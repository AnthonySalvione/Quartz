## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — River

**Verse:** [[1 Kings-04#v21|1 Kings 4:21]]

I.e., the Euphrates. So also in [[1 Kings-04#v24|v. 24]]. Solomon’s dominion extended from the great river Euphrates to the land of the Philistines (at the seashore of the Mediterranean) and to the border of Egypt ([[2 Chronicles-09#v26|2 Chron. 9:26]]) as the fulfillment of the promise of God to His elect Israel ([[Genesis-15#v18|Gen. 15:18]]; [[Exodus-23#v31|Exo. 23:31]]; [[Deuteronomy-11#v24|Deut. 11:24]]). The ultimate fulfillment of this promise will be in the millennium, at the restoration of Israel, after the second coming of Christ (see note 181 in [[Genesis-15|Gen. 15]]).

## Footnotes for verse 30

### Footnote 30.1 — wisdom

**Verse:** [[1 Kings-04#v30|1 Kings 4:30]]

Solomon’s wisdom was absolutely in the physical realm, without any spiritual element. His wisdom was altogether different from the wisdom of Paul, which was a spiritual wisdom concerning Christ making His home in our hearts ([[Ephesians-03#v17|Eph. 3:17]]), our walking and having our being according to the spirit ([[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]]), and the two spirits—the divine Spirit and the human spirit ([[Romans-08#v16|Rom. 8:16]]). Solomon’s wisdom was a shadow of the real wisdom that was to come. The real wisdom is God, and God is embodied in Christ ([[Colossians-02#v9|Col. 2:9]]), who has become our wisdom to be in us ([[1 Corinthians-01#v24|1 Cor. 1:24]], [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|30]]), making us one with God and making us the same as God in life and in nature but not in the Godhead. What wisdom this is!

## Footnotes for verse 34

### Footnote 34.1 — came

**Verse:** [[1 Kings-04#v34|1 Kings 4:34]]

Solomon’s splendid kingdom, with his prosperity under the rich blessing of God ([[1 Kings-04#v20|4:20]]—[[1 Kings-05#v18|5:18]]; [[1 Kings-09|1 Kings 9—10]]), is a type of Christ’s kingdom in the millennium. During the millennium all the nations will come to Christ ([[Zechariah-14#v16|Zech. 14:16]]).
