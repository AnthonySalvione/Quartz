# 1 Kings-19 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[1 Kings-19|1 Kings 19]]

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — a

**Verse:** [[1 Kings-19#v12|1 Kings 19:12]]

The fact that God spoke to Elijah in a gentle, quiet voice indicates that God was ushering Elijah into the New Testament age, in which God speaks to His people not by thundering but gently and quietly (cf. [[1 John-02#v27|1 John 2:27]]).

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — seven

**Verse:** [[1 Kings-19#v18|1 Kings 19:18]]

These faithful ones, the overcomers, were still standing with God. Eventually, God punished Israel by sending Israel away from their fathers’ land as captives ([[2 Kings-24|2 Kings 24—25]]). Later, it was the overcomers among the captives who brought Israel back. One of these overcomers, Daniel, prayed with his window open toward Jerusalem ([[Daniel-06#v10|Dan. 6:10]]). Through his gracious prayer God brought Israel back to their fathers’ land.
