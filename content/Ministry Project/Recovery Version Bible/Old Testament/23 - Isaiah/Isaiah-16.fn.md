## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — One

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-16#v5|Isaiah 16:5]]

The ultimate issue of Jehovah’s judgment upon the nations is to provide the all-inclusive Christ as the Savior to meet the need of the beloved Israel and the judged nations ([[Isaiah-16#v5|v. 5]]; [[Isaiah-19#v20|19:20b]]; [[Isaiah-22#v20|22:20-25]]). Christ will reign as a king in the tent of David, i.e., in the kingdom of David, the Messianic kingdom ([[Acts-15#v16|Acts 15:16]]), in the coming age during the restoration of the nation of Israel ([[Amos-09#v11|Amos 9:11]] and [note](<Amos-09.fn#Footnote 11.1 — tabernacle>); [[Luke-01#v32|Luke 1:32-33]] and [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 33.1 — reign|note 33¹]]). Christ’s reigning in the tent of David signifies consolation, encouragement, and restoration. According to [[Isaiah-24#v23|24:23]], the Christ who will reign in the millennium is actually Jehovah of hosts.
