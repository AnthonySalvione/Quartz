## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — glory

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-43#v7|Isaiah 43:7]]

Israel is God’s servant in the sense of fulfilling God’s desire in His economy to have a corporate expression of Himself for His glory ([[Isaiah-41#v8|41:8]]; [[Isaiah-45#v4|45:4]]; [[Isaiah-49#v3|49:3]]). The people of Israel were to be used by God to express Him in a corporate way for His glorification. In this sense Israel was one with Christ as God’s servant ([[Hosea-11#v1|Hosea 11:1]]; cf. [[Matthew-02#v15|Matt. 2:15]]). The servant of Jehovah is corporate, and Israel was part of this corporate servant. See notes [1¹](<Isaiah-41.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Listen>) and [8¹](<Isaiah-41.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Israel>) in [[Isaiah-41|Isaiah 41]]. 

In the present age the church is the testimony of God in the sense of being one with Christ as God’s testimony ([[Revelation-01#v2|Rev. 1:2]] and [note](<Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — testimony>)). In being such a testimony, the church serves God. Thus, all God’s elect can be considered servants of God with Christ for the expression of God’s glory (cf. [[John-17#v22|John 17:22]]; [[Ephesians-03#v21|Eph. 3:21]]). The glorification of God is the purpose of our service. The highest service we can render to God is to express His glory.
