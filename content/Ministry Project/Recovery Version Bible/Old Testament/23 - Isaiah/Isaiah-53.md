## v1

Who has believed our [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 1.1 — report|report]]? /  
And to whom has the arm of Jehovah been [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 1.1 — report|revealed]]?

## v2

[[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 2.1 — For|For]] He grew up like a tender [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 2.2 — plant|plant]] before Him, /  
And like a root out of dry ground. /  
He has no [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 2.3 — attracting|attracting]] form nor majesty that we should look upon Him, /  
Nor beautiful appearance that we should desire Him.

## v3

He was despised and forsaken of men, /  
A man of [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 3.1 — sorrows|sorrows]] and acquainted with grief; /  
And like one from whom men hide their faces, /  
He was despised; and we did not esteem Him.

## v4

Surely He has [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 4.1 — borne|borne]] our [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 4.2 — sicknesses|sicknesses]], /  
And carried our [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 4.2 — sicknesses|sorrows]]; /  
[[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 4.3 — Yet|Yet]] we ourselves esteemed Him stricken, /  
Smitten of God and afflicted.

## v5

[[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 5.1 — But|But]] He was wounded because of our transgressions; /  
He was crushed because of our iniquities; /  
The chastening for our peace was upon Him, /  
And by His stripes we have been [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 5.2 — healed|healed]].

## v6

We all like sheep have gone astray; /  
Each of us has turned to his own way, /  
And Jehovah has caused the iniquity of [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 6.1 — us|us]] all /  
To [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 6.2 — fall|fall]] on Him.

## v7

He was [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 7.1 — oppressed|oppressed]], and it was He who was afflicted, /  
Yet He did not open His mouth; /  
Like a lamb that is led to the slaughter /  
And like a sheep that is dumb before its shearers, /  
So He did not open His mouth.

## v8

[[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 8.1 — By|By]] oppression and by judgment He was taken away; /  
And as for His [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 8.2 — generation|generation]], who among them had the thought /  
That He was cut off out of the land of the living /  
For the transgression of my people to whom the stroke was due?

## v9

And they [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 9.1 — assigned|assigned]] His grave with the wicked, /  
But with a rich man in His [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 9.2 — death|death]], /  
Although He had done [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 9.3 — no|no]] violence, /  
Nor was there any deceit in His mouth.

## v10

But Jehovah was pleased to crush Him, to afflict Him with grief. /  
When He makes Himself an [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 10.1 — offering|offering]] for sin, /  
He will see a [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 10.2 — seed|seed]], He will [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 10.3 — extend|extend]] His days, /  
And the [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 10.4 — pleasure|pleasure]] of Jehovah will prosper in His hand.

## v11

He will see the [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fruit|fruit]] of the travail of His soul, /  
And He will be [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 11.2 — satisfied|satisfied]]; /  
By the [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 11.3 — knowledge|knowledge]] of Him, the righteous One, My Servant, will [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 11.4 — make|make]] the many righteous, /  
And He will bear their iniquities.

## v12

Therefore I will divide to Him a portion with the [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Great|Great]], /  
And He will divide the [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 12.2 — spoil|spoil]] with the [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Great|Strong]]; /  
Because He poured out His [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 12.3 — life|life]] unto death /  
And was [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 12.4 — numbered|numbered]] with the transgressors, /  
Yet He alone bore the sin of many /  
And [[Isaiah-53.fn#Footnote 12.5 — interceded|interceded]] for the transgressors.
