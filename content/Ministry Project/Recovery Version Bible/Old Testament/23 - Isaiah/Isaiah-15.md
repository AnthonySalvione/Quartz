## v1

The burden concerning Moab:  
Indeed in a night it is devastated —  
Ar of Moab is cut off.  
Indeed in a night it is devastated —  
Kir of Moab is cut off.

## v2

They have gone up to their temple and to Dibon,  
That is, to their high places, to weep.  
Over Nebo and over Medeba  
Moab howls;  
Upon all their heads is baldness;  
Every beard is shaved off.

## v3

In their streets they have girded themselves with sackcloth;  
On their roofs  
And in their open squares everyone will howl,  
Melting in tears.

## v4

And Heshbon and Elealeh cry out;  
Their voice is heard as far as Jahaz.  
Because of this the armed men of Moab raise a cry;  
Their soul quivers within them.

## v5

My heart cries out for Moab;  
Its fugitives reach as far as Zoar,  
As far as Eglath-shelishiyah:  
By the ascent of Luhith  
With weeping they will go up;  
On the way that leads to Horonaim  
They will raise up a cry of destruction;

## v6

The waters of Nimrim  
Will be a devastation;  
The foliage will be dried up,  
The grass wasted away;  
There will be nothing green.

## v7

As a result, the abundance they have produced  
And placed in reserve  
Will be carried off  
To the poplar brook.

## v8

For the cry has gone about  
The border of Moab;  
Their howling goes as far as Eglaim,  
Even to Beer-elim their howling reaches.

## v9

For the waters of Dimon are full of blood,  
For I will bring upon Dimon additional things:  
Lions upon those in Moab who escape  
And upon the remnant of the land.
