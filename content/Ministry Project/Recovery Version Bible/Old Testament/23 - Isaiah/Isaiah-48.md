## v1

Hear this, O house of Jacob, /  
Who are called by the name of Israel; /  
And who came out from the waters of Judah, /  
Who swear by the name of Jehovah /  
And make mention of the God of Israel, /  
But not in truth nor in righteousness.

## v2

For they call themselves after the holy city /  
And lean upon the God of Israel; /  
Jehovah of hosts is His name.

## v3

I have declared the former things from the beginning; /  
And they went out from My mouth, and I let them hear them; /  
I did them suddenly, and they came to pass.

## v4

Because I knew that you are obstinate, /  
And your neck is an iron sinew, /  
And your forehead bronze;

## v5

Therefore I declared them to you long ago; /  
Before things came to pass I let you hear; /  
Lest you should say, My idol has done them, /  
And my graven image and my molten image commanded them.

## v6

You have heard; look at all this. And will you not declare it? /  
From this time I will let you hear new things /  
And hidden things which you have not known.

## v7

They have been created now, and not long ago; /  
And before this day you have not even heard them; /  
Lest you should say, Indeed, I knew them.

## v8

You also have not heard and have not known; /  
Your ears were not even open from that time; /  
For I knew that you would deal very treacherously /  
And would be called a transgressor from the womb.

## v9

For My name’s sake I will defer My anger, /  
And for the sake of My praise I will restrain it for you, /  
In order not to cut you off.

## v10

Indeed, I have refined you but not like silver; /  
I have tried you in the furnace of affliction.

## v11

For My own sake, yes, for My own sake, I will do it, /  
For how can My name be profaned? /  
And I will not give My glory to another.

## v12

Listen to Me, O Jacob, /  
And Israel, whom I called; /  
I am He; I am the First, /  
I am also the Last.

## v13

Indeed, My hand laid the foundations of the earth, /  
And My right hand spread out the heavens; /  
When I call to them, /  
They stand together.

## v14

Assemble yourselves, all of you, and hear! /  
Who among them has declared these things? /  
Jehovah loves him; he will do His pleasure /  
On Babylon, and His arm will be upon the Chaldeans.

## v15

I, even I, have spoken; indeed, I have called him; /  
I have brought him, and his way will prosper.

## v16

Draw near to Me and listen to this: /  
From the beginning I have not spoken in secret; /  
From the time it took place, I was there. /  
And now the Lord Jehovah /  
Has sent me and His Spirit.

## v17

Thus says Jehovah, your Redeemer, /  
The Holy One of Israel, /  
I am Jehovah your God, /  
Who teaches you to do that which is profitable, /  
Who leads you in the way that you should go.

## v18

If only you had hearkened to My commandments! /  
Then your peace would have been like a river, /  
And your righteousness like the waves of the sea,

## v19

And your seed would have been like the sand, /  
And the offspring of your bowels like the gravel; /  
His name would not have been cut off /  
Or destroyed before Me.

## v20

Come out from Babylon; /  
Flee from the Chaldeans; /  
With a voice of shouting declare; /  
Let them hear this, /  
Send it out unto the end of the earth; /  
Say, Jehovah has redeemed /  
His servant Jacob.

## v21

And they did not thirst /  
When He led them through the dry places; /  
He caused water to flow /  
From the rock for them; /  
And He split the rock, /  
And the waters gushed out.

## v22

There is no peace, says Jehovah, for the wicked.
