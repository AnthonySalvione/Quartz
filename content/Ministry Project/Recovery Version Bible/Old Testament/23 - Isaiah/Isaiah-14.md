## v1

When Jehovah has compassion on Jacob  
And again chooses Israel  
And settles them in their land,  
The sojourners will join them  
And attach themselves to the house of Jacob.

## v2

And nations will take them and bring them to their place,  
And the house of Israel will take possession of them  
In the land of Jehovah as male and female servants;  
And they will lead captive those who were their captors  
And rule over those who oppressed them.

## v3

In the day when Jehovah gives you rest  
From your pain and turmoil  
And from the hard service  
That was done by you as slaves,

## v4

You will lift up this discourse  
Concerning the king of Babylon  
And you will say:  
How the oppressor has ceased!  
How the raging has ceased!

## v5

Jehovah has broken the staff of the wicked,  
The rod of rulers;

## v6

That struck the peoples in wrath,  
With strikes that would not stop;  
That ruled the nations in anger,  
With pursuit that would not let up.

## v7

All the earth rests and is undisturbed;  
They break forth with a ringing shout.

## v8

Even the cypress trees rejoice over you,  
And the cedars of Lebanon:  
Since you are brought down,  
No tree cutter will come up against us.

## v9

Sheol beneath is excited because of you,  
That it will meet you when you come.  
It rouses the dead because of you,  
All the great ones of the earth.  
It makes all the kings of the nations  
Rise from their thrones.

## v10

All of them will respond  
And say to you,  
Even you have been weakened, just as we have;  
You have become like us.

## v11

Your majesty has been brought down to Sheol,  
Even the sound of your harps.  
Beneath you maggots are spread;  
Worms are your covering.

## v12

How you have fallen from heaven,  
O Daystar, son of the dawn!  
How you have been hewn down to earth,  
You who made nations fall prostrate!

## v13

But you, you said in your heart:  
I will ascend to heaven;  
Above the stars of God  
I will exalt my throne.  
And I will sit upon the mount of assembly  
In the uttermost parts of the north.

## v14

I will ascend above the heights of the clouds;  
I will make myself like the Most High.

## v15

But you will be brought down to Sheol,  
To the uttermost parts of the pit.

## v16

Those who see you will gaze at you;  
They will ponder concerning you, asking,  
Is this the man who made the earth tremble,  
The one who shook kingdoms;

## v17

Who made the world like a wilderness  
And tore down its cities;  
Who did not release his captives to their homes?

## v18

All the kings of the nations,  
All of them, lie in glory,  
Each in his own house.

## v19

But you have been thrown out away from your tomb,  
Like some shoot that is viewed with disgust;  
Or like the garments of the slain,  
Of those pierced with the sword,  
Who go down to the stones of the pit;  
Like a corpse that has been trampled under.

## v20

You shall not be united with them in burial,  
For you have destroyed your land,  
You have slain your people;  
The seed of evildoers  
Will never be renowned.

## v21

Prepare a slaughterhouse for his children  
Because of the iniquity of their fathers,  
So that they do not rise up and possess the land,  
And fill the surface of the world with cities.

## v22

And I will rise up against them,  
Declares Jehovah of hosts.  
And I will cut off from Babylon name and remnant,  
And posterity and progeny,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v23

And I will make it a possession for porcupines  
And muddied pools of water,  
And I will sweep it with the broom of destruction,  
Declares Jehovah of hosts.

## v24

Jehovah of hosts has sworn, saying,  
Surely just as I conceived it, so has it happened;  
And just as I have purposed it, so shall this stand,

## v25

That I will break Assyria in My land,  
And upon My mountains I will trample him.  
Then his yoke will be taken off of them,  
And his burden will be removed from off their shoulders.

## v26

This is the purpose that I have purposed over all the earth,  
And this is the hand that is stretched out over all the nations.

## v27

For Jehovah of hosts has purposed it,  
And who will frustrate it?  
And thus His hand is stretched out,  
And who will turn it back?

## v28

In the year that King Ahaz died  
This burden came:

## v29

Do not rejoice, all of you, Philistia,  
That the rod that strikes you is broken,  
For from the serpent’s root a viper will come forth,  
And his fruit will be a flying fiery serpent.

## v30

And the poorest of the poor will feed,  
And the needy will lie down securely.  
And I will kill your root by famine,  
And he will slay your remnant.

## v31

Howl, O gate!  
Cry out, O city!  
The whole of you, Philistia, is melted away.  
For from the north smoke has come,  
And there are no stragglers in his ranks.

## v32

And what shall one answer this nation’s messengers?  
That Jehovah has founded Zion,  
And in her the poor of His people take refuge.
