# Isaiah-02 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Isaiah-02|Isaiah 2]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — mountain

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-02#v2|Isaiah 2:2]]

The mountain here signifies the kingdom of Israel, whereas the mountains and the hills later in the verse signify the kingdoms of the nations. In the restoration ([[Matthew-19#v28|Matt. 19:28]]; [[Acts-03#v21|Acts 3:21]]) the nation of Israel will be the foremost among the nations.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — in

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-02#v3|Isaiah 2:3]]

Lit., out of; indicating the source of His instruction.

### Footnote 3.2 — instruction

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-02#v3|Isaiah 2:3]]

Heb. torah, frequently translated law. During the millennium, the thousand-year kingdom, the restored nation of Israel will be the priests in the earthly part of the kingdom ([[Isaiah-61#v6|61:6]]; [[Zechariah-08#v20|Zech. 8:20-23]]), the kingdom of the Son of Man ([[Matthew-13#v41|Matt. 13:41]]; [[Revelation-11#v15|Rev. 11:15]]), and the overcoming believers in Christ will be the priests in the heavenly part ([[Revelation-20#v6|Rev. 20:6]]), the kingdom of the Father ([[Matthew-13#v43|Matt. 13:43]]). By this priesthood all the peoples of the nations will be taught and instructed. See [note 20¹](<Zechariah-08.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Peoples>) in [[Zechariah-08|Zech. 8]].

### Footnote 4.1 — judge

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — silver

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-02#v7|Isaiah 2:7]]

The nations were rich not only in money but also in idols ([[Isaiah-02#v8|vv. 8]], [[Isaiah-02#v20|20]]). Idols and money go together. This is the reason that money is called mammon ([[Matthew-06#v24|Matt. 6:24]]; [[Luke-16#v9|Luke 16:9]], [[Luke-16#v11|11]], [[Luke-16#v13|13]]). Because the nations trusted in idols, God judged them in order to humiliate them ([[Isaiah-02#v9|vv. 9-22]]).

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — haughty

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-02#v11|Isaiah 2:11]]

Because the nations were haughty, it was necessary for Jehovah to humiliate them, to bring them down, through judgment. God’s judgment on the haughty nations ushers in the God-man, Christ ([[Isaiah-04#v2|4:2]], [[Isaiah-04#v5|5-6]]), issuing in the restoration of the nation of Israel ([[Isaiah-04#v3|4:3-6]]; [[Isaiah-02#v2|2:2-5]]), which brings in the kingdom and consummates in the new heaven and new earth ([[Isaiah-65#v17|65:17]]).
