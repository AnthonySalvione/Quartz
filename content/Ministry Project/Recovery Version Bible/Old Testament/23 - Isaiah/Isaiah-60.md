## v1

Arise! Shine! For your light has come, /  
And the glory of Jehovah has risen upon you.

## v2

For, behold, the darkness will cover the earth, /  
And deep darkness the peoples; /  
But Jehovah will rise upon you, /  
And His glory will be seen upon you.

## v3

And nations will come to your light, /  
And kings to the brightness of your rising.

## v4

Lift up your eyes around you and see: /  
They all gather together; they come to you; /  
Your sons will come from far away, /  
And your daughters will be carried on the hip.

## v5

Then you will see and you will beam, /  
And your heart will be in awe and will swell with joy, /  
For the abundance of the sea will be turned to you, /  
The wealth of the nations will come to you.

## v6

A multitude of camels will cover you, /  
The young camels of Midian and Ephah; /  
All those from Sheba will come; /  
They will carry gold and frankincense, /  
And they will bear the good news of Jehovah’s praises.

## v7

All the flocks of Kedar will be gathered together to you; /  
The rams of Nebaioth will minister to you; /  
They will go up acceptably upon My altar, /  
And I will beautify the house of My beauty.

## v8

Who are these who fly like a cloud, /  
And like the doves to their dovecote?

## v9

Surely the coastlands will look eagerly for Me; /  
And the ships of Tarshish will come first, /  
To bring your children from afar, /  
Their silver and their gold with them, /  
Unto the name of Jehovah your God /  
And unto the Holy One of Israel; for He has beautified you.

## v10

And the children of foreigners will build up your walls, /  
And their kings will minister to you; /  
For in My wrath I struck you, /  
But in My favor I had compassion on you.

## v11

And your gates will be open continually; /  
They will not be closed day or night, /  
So that they may bring to you the wealth of the nations /  
And their kings being led in procession.

## v12

For the nation and the kingdom that will not serve you will perish, /  
And the nations will be utterly wasted.

## v13

The glory of Lebanon will come to you, /  
The fir tree, the pine tree, and the box tree together, /  
To beautify the place of My sanctuary; /  
And I will make the place for My feet glorious.

## v14

And the sons of those who afflicted you /  
Will come bowing down to you, /  
And all those who despised you will bow down /  
At the soles of your feet; /  
And they will call you the City of Jehovah, /  
The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

## v15

Whereas you have been forsaken and hated, /  
So that no one would pass through you, /  
I will make you an eternal excellency, /  
A joy from generation to generation.

## v16

And you will suck the milk of nations /  
And suck the breast of kings; /  
Thus you will know that I, Jehovah, am your Savior /  
And your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.

## v17

Instead of bronze I will bring gold, /  
And instead of iron I will bring silver; /  
And instead of wood there will be bronze, /  
And instead of stones there will be iron; /  
And I will make peace your officers, /  
And righteousness your rulers.

## v18

Violence will no longer be heard in your land, /  
Nor desolation and destruction within your borders; /  
But you will call your walls Salvation, /  
And your gates Praise.

## v19

You will no longer have the sun for your light by day, /  
Nor for brightness will the moon give you light; /  
But Jehovah will be an eternal light to you, /  
And your God your beauty.

## v20

Your sun will no longer go down, /  
Nor will your moon withdraw; /  
For Jehovah will be an eternal light to you, /  
And the days of your mourning will be ended.

## v21

Then all your people will be righteous; /  
They will possess the land forever, /  
The branch of My planting, /  
The work of My hands, /  
That I may be beautified.

## v22

The least one will become a thousand, /  
And the smallest a strong nation. /  
I, Jehovah, will hasten it in its time.
