## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — covenant

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-54#v10|Isaiah 54:10]]

Christ’s dynamic redemption accomplished through His vicarious death and reproductive resurrection ([[Isaiah-53|Isaiah 53]]) is in relation to His being the covenant for Israel’s security. Christ as the Servant of Jehovah is the reality of this covenant to be a surety to Israel for their security ([[Isaiah-42#v6|42:6]] and [note 1](<Isaiah-42.fn#Footnote 6.1 — covenant>); [[Isaiah-49#v8|49:8]]). The dynamic redemption accomplished by Christ is the security not only to Israel but also to the seed ([[Isaiah-53#v10|53:10]]), which is the church as the corporate Body of the resurrected Christ.
