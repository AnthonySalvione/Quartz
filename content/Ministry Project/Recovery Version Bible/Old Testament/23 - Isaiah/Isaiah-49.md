## v1

Listen to me, O coastlands, /  
And hearken, you peoples from afar. /  
Jehovah has called me from the womb; /  
From the bowels of my mother He has made mention of my name.

## v2

And He has made my mouth like a sharp sword; /  
He has concealed me in the shadow of His hand, /  
And He has made me a polished arrow; /  
He has hidden me in His quiver.

## v3

And He said to me, You are My servant, /  
Israel, in whom I will be glorified.

## v4

But I said, I have labored in vain; /  
I have used up my strength for nothing and vanity; /  
Yet surely the justice due to me is with Jehovah, /  
And my recompense with my God.

## v5

And now says Jehovah, /  
Who formed Me from the womb to be His Servant, /  
To bring Jacob back to Him, /  
So that Israel would be gathered to Him; /  
And I will be glorified in the sight of Jehovah, /  
And My God will be My strength.

## v6

He says, It is too small a thing that You would be My Servant /  
To raise up the tribes of Jacob /  
And bring back the preserved of Israel; /  
I will also set You as a light of the nations /  
That You may be My salvation unto the ends of the earth.

## v7

Thus says Jehovah, /  
The Redeemer of Israel, his Holy One, /  
To the despised One, the One abhorred by the nation, /  
The One subjected to tyrants, /  
Kings will see and arise, /  
And princes will bow down; /  
Because of Jehovah, who is faithful, /  
The Holy One of Israel, who has chosen You.

## v8

Thus says Jehovah, /  
In an acceptable time I have answered You, /  
And in a day of salvation I have helped You; /  
And I will preserve You and give You for a covenant of the people, /  
To restore the land, to apportion the desolate inheritances,

## v9

To say to the prisoners, Come forth, /  
To say to those who are in darkness, Show yourselves. /  
They will feed along the roads, /  
And their pasture will be on all the bare heights;

## v10

They will not hunger or thirst; /  
Neither the scorching heat nor the sun will strike them; /  
For He who has compassion on them will lead them /  
And will guide them beside the springs of water;

## v11

And I will make all My mountains into a road, /  
And My highways will be raised up.

## v12

Indeed, these will come from a distance, /  
And indeed these will come from the north and the west, /  
And these from the land of the Sinim.

## v13

Give a ringing shout, O heavens, and exult, O earth, /  
And break forth into shouting, O mountains; /  
For Jehovah has comforted His people /  
And will have compassion on His afflicted ones.

## v14

But Zion has said, Jehovah has forsaken me, /  
And the Lord has forgotten me.

## v15

Can a woman forget her nursing child, /  
That she would not have compassion on the son of her womb? /  
Even though they may forget, /  
Yet I will not forget you.

## v16

Indeed, I have engraved you upon the palms of My hands; /  
Your walls are continually before Me.

## v17

Your children make haste; /  
Your destroyers and those who lay you waste /  
Will go forth from you.

## v18

Lift up your eyes and look around; /  
All of these gather themselves together and come to you. /  
As I live, declares Jehovah, /  
You will put all of them on like an ornament /  
And gird yourself with them like a bride.

## v19

For your waste and desolate places /  
And your devastated land — /  
For you will be too constricted now because of its inhabitants, /  
And those who swallowed you up will be far away.

## v20

The children of whom you were bereaved /  
Will yet say in your ears, /  
This place is too constricted for me; /  
Make room for me that I may dwell here.

## v21

Then you will say in your heart, /  
Who has borne these for me, /  
Since I have been bereaved of children and am barren, /  
An exile and driven about? /  
And who brought these up? /  
Indeed, I was left alone; /  
From where did these come?

## v22

Thus says the Lord Jehovah, /  
Indeed, I will lift up My hand to the nations, /  
And unto the peoples I will lift up My banner; /  
And they will bring your sons in their arms, /  
And they will carry your daughters on their shoulders.

## v23

And kings will be your foster fathers; /  
And their princesses will be those who nurse you; /  
They will bow down to you with their faces toward the earth /  
And lick the dust of your feet; /  
And you will know that I am Jehovah; /  
Those who wait on Me will not be ashamed.

## v24

Can the booty be taken from the strong man, /  
Or the lawful captives be delivered?

## v25

Surely, thus says Jehovah, /  
Even the captive may be taken from the strong man, /  
And the booty may be released from the tyrant; /  
For I will strive with those who strive with you, /  
And I will save your children;

## v26

And I will feed those who oppress you with their own flesh; /  
And they will be drunk with their own blood as with sweet wine; /  
Then all flesh will know /  
That I am Jehovah your Savior /  
And your Redeemer, the Mighty One of Jacob.
