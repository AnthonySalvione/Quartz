## v1

Then Jehovah said to me,  
Take a large tablet and write on it in plain letters,  
For Maher-shalal-hash-baz;

## v2

And I will take faithful witnesses,  
Urijah the priest and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah.

## v3

And I went to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son.  
And Jehovah said to me,  
Call his name Maher-shalal-hash-baz,

## v4

For before this boy knows how to call, Father, and, Mother,  
They will carry off the wealth of Damascus  
And the spoil of Samaria  
Before the king of Assyria.

## v5

Then Jehovah spoke further to me, saying,

## v6

Because these people have rejected  
The gently flowing waters of Shiloah, and exult  
In Rezin and the son of Remaliah,

## v7

Now therefore the Lord is bringing up upon them  
The mighty and abundant waters of the Euphrates,  
The king of Assyria and all his glory;  
And it will overflow all its channels,  
And go over all its banks.

## v8

It will sweep through Judah; it will overflow and rise  
Until it reaches the neck;  
And the spreading out of its wings  
Will fill the breadth of Your land, O Immanuel.

## v9

Be broken, O peoples, and be shattered;  
Hearken, all you distant places of the earth:  
Gird yourselves, yet be shattered;  
Gird yourselves, yet be shattered.

## v10

Take counsel, yet it will be frustrated;  
Speak the word, yet it will not stand;  
For God is with us.

## v11

For Jehovah spoke to me in this way with great force  
And instructed me not to walk in the way of this people, saying,

## v12

You shall not call it conspiracy  
All that this people calls conspiracy;  
And you shall not fear what they fear nor hold it in awe.

## v13

You shall sanctify Jehovah of hosts;  
He shall be the One to fear  
And He shall be the One to hold in awe.

## v14

Then He will become a sanctuary,  
Yet a stone to strike against  
And a rock of stumbling  
To both houses of Israel,  
A trap and a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

## v15

And many will stumble at these,  
And will fall and be broken to pieces,  
And will be snared and taken captive.

## v16

Bind up the testimony;  
Seal the instruction among my disciples;

## v17

And I will wait on Jehovah,  
Who hides His face from the house of Jacob,  
And I will look eagerly for Him.

## v18

See, I and the children whom Jehovah has given me  
Are for signs and wonders in Israel  
From Jehovah of hosts,  
Who abides on Mount Zion.

## v19

When they say to you,  
Inquire of the necromancers and the familiar spirits,  
Who twitter and mutter;  
Say to them,  
Should not a people inquire of their God?  
Should they go to the dead on behalf of the living —

## v20

To the law and to the testimony!  
If they do not speak according to this word,  
It is because in them there is no dawn.

## v21

And they go through it hard-pressed and hungry;  
And when they are hungry, they rage and curse  
Their king and their God.  
They turn their faces upward,

## v22

And they look to the earth,  
But there is only distress and darkness,  
The gloom of anguish  
And being thrust into darkness.
