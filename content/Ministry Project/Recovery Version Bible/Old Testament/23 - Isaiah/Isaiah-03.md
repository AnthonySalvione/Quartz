## v1

For now the Lord Jehovah of hosts  
Is taking away from Jerusalem and from Judah  
Every kind of support —  
All the support of bread  
And all the support of water;

## v2

The mighty man and the man of war,  
The judge and the prophet,  
And the diviner and the elder;

## v3

The captain of fifty and the highly regarded;  
And the counselor, the wise magician, and the knowledgeable enchanter.

## v4

And I will make youths their rulers,  
And capriciousness will rule over them.

## v5

And the people will be oppressed, each by the other,  
And each by his neighbor.  
The youth will be arrogant to the elder,  
And the contemned one to the one who is honored.

## v6

When a man takes hold of his brother  
In his father’s house and says,  
You have clothing, you be our ruler,  
And these ruins will be under your hand;

## v7

He will solemnly say,  
I will not be the one who binds your wounds,  
For in my house there is no food or clothing;  
Do not appoint me as ruler of the people.

## v8

For Jerusalem stumbles,  
And Judah falls,  
Because their speech and their actions are against Jehovah,  
To rebel against the eyes of His glory.

## v9

The countenance of their faces witnesses against them,  
And they declare their sin like Sodom;  
They do not hide it.  
Woe to their soul!  
For they have brought evil upon themselves.

## v10

Say to the righteous that it will go well with them,  
For they will eat the fruit of their actions.

## v11

Woe to the wicked man! It will go badly with him;  
For the result of his deeds will be done to him.

## v12

My people — their oppressors are children,  
And women rule over them.  
O My people, those who lead you are leading you astray;  
And the path for your ways they have hidden.

## v13

Jehovah stands firm to contend,  
And He stands to judge the people.

## v14

Jehovah will enter into judgment  
With the elders of His people and their rulers.  
It is you who have consumed the vineyard;  
The spoil of the poor is in your houses.

## v15

What do you mean by crushing My people  
And grinding the faces of the poor?  
Declares the Lord Jehovah of hosts.

## v16

Moreover Jehovah said,  
Because the daughters of Zion are haughty,  
And walk around with outstretched necks  
And lusting eyes,  
And trip along with quick, little steps,  
And rattle the anklets on their feet;

## v17

The Lord will strike the scalp of the daughters of Zion with scabs,  
And Jehovah will expose their secret parts.

## v18

In that day the Lord will remove the beauty  
Of their anklets, headbands, and crescents;

## v19

The ear pendants, the bracelets, and the costly veils;

## v20

The headdresses, the ankle chains, the sashes,  
The bottles of aromas, and the amulets;

## v21

The finger rings and the nose rings;

## v22

The formal gowns, the frocks, the wraps, and the purses;

## v23

The mirrors, the fine linen garments, the turbans, and the veils.

## v24

And instead of a sweet smell there will be rottenness;  
And instead of a belt, an encircling rope;  
Instead of well-set hair, baldness;  
And instead of fine garments, the girding of oneself with sackcloth;  
A brand instead of beauty.

## v25

Your men will fall by the sword,  
And your mighty, in battle;

## v26

Then her gates will mourn and lament,  
And she, being desolated, will sit on the ground.
