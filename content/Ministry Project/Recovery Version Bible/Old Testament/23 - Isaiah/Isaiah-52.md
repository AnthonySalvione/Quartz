## v1

Awake! Awake! Put on /  
Your strength, O Zion; /  
Put on your beautiful garments, /  
O Jerusalem, O holy city; /  
For the uncircumcised and the unclean /  
Will no longer come into you.

## v2

Shake yourself from the dust; arise, /  
Sit down, O Jerusalem; /  
Loose yourself from the bonds on your neck, /  
O captive daughter of Zion.

## v3

For thus says Jehovah, /  
You are those who have been sold for nothing, /  
And you will be redeemed without money.

## v4

For thus says the Lord Jehovah, /  
At first My people went down to Egypt to sojourn there; /  
Then the Assyrian oppressed them without cause.

## v5

Now then what do I have here, declares Jehovah, /  
Since My people have been taken away for nothing? /  
Those who rule over them howl, declares Jehovah, /  
And My name is continually blasphemed all day long.

## v6

Therefore My people will know My name; /  
Therefore in that day they will know that I am He who speaks; /  
Here I am.

## v7

How beautiful on the mountains /  
Are the feet of him who brings good news, /  
Of him who announces peace, who brings news of good things, /  
Who announces salvation; /  
Of him who says to Zion, Your God reigns!

## v8

The voice of your watchmen! They lift up their voice; /  
They give a ringing shout together; /  
For they will see with their very own eyes /  
When Jehovah restores Zion.

## v9

Break forth, give a ringing shout together, /  
You wasted places of Jerusalem, /  
For Jehovah has comforted His people, /  
He has redeemed Jerusalem.

## v10

Jehovah has bared His holy arm /  
In the sight of all the nations, /  
And all the ends of the earth will see /  
The salvation of our God.

## v11

Depart! Depart! Go out from there! /  
Do not touch any unclean thing! /  
Go out from the midst of her! Cleanse yourselves, /  
You who bear the vessels of Jehovah!

## v12

For you will not go out in haste, /  
And you will not go in flight; /  
For Jehovah will go before you, /  
And the God of Israel will be your rear guard.

## v13

Indeed, My Servant will act wisely and will prosper; /  
He will be exalted and lifted up and very high.

## v14

Even as many were astonished at Him — /  
His visage was marred more than that of any man, /  
And His form more than that of the sons of men —

## v15

So will He surprise many nations; /  
Kings will shut their mouths because of Him; /  
For what had not been recounted to them they will see, /  
And what they had not heard of they will contemplate.
