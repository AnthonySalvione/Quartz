## v1

And seven women will grasp  
One man in that day,  
Saying, We will eat our own bread  
And wear our own clothes;  
Just let us bear your name;  
Take away our reproach.

## v2

In that day the Shoot of Jehovah will be beauty and glory,  
And the fruit of the earth, excellence and splendor,  
To those of Israel who have escaped.

## v3

And he who is left over in Zion  
And remains in Jerusalem will be called holy,  
Everyone who has been written down in Jerusalem for life;

## v4

When the Lord has washed away  
The filth of the daughters of Zion  
And has cleansed away the bloodstains of Jerusalem from her midst,  
By the judging Spirit and the burning Spirit.

## v5

Jehovah will create over the entire region of Mount Zion  
And over all her convocations  
A cloud of smoke by day,  
And the brightness of a fiery flame by night;  
For the glory will be a canopy over all.

## v6

And there will be a tabernacle  
As a daytime shade from the heat  
And as a refuge and a cover  
From storm and rain.
