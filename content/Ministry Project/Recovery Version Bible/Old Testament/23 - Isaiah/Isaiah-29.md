## v1

Woe to Ariel, to Ariel,  
The city where David camped!  
Add year to year; let the feasts run their course.

## v2

But I will distress Ariel,  
And she will become mourning and lamentation  
And will be like an Ariel to Me.

## v3

And I will encamp in a circle against you,  
And will besiege you with palisades,  
And will raise up siegeworks against you.

## v4

And you will be brought down; from the ground you will speak,  
And your speech will come from low in the dust.  
And your voice will be like that of a departed spirit from the ground,  
And your speech will twitter from the dust.

## v5

But the multitude of your enemies will be like fine dust,  
And the multitude of those who terrorize will be like chaff that passes away;  
And it will happen in an instant, suddenly.

## v6

From Jehovah of hosts you will be visited,  
With thunder and earthquake and great noise,  
With whirlwind and tempest and the flame of consuming fire.

## v7

And the multitude of all the nations that fight against Ariel,  
Even all who fight against her and her stronghold and who distress her,  
Will be like a dream, like a vision in the night.

## v8

And as when a hungry man dreams and finds himself eating,  
But he awakens and what he desires is not there;  
Or as when a thirsty man dreams and finds himself drinking,  
But he awakens and is indeed faint and what he desires is far from him;  
So will the multitude of all the nations be  
That fight against Mount Zion.

## v9

Linger and be astounded,  
Blind yourselves and be blind.  
They are drunk, but not with wine;  
They stagger, but not with liquor.

## v10

For Jehovah has poured out upon you  
A spirit of deep sleep  
And has shut your eyes, the prophets;  
And your heads, the seers, He has covered.

## v11

And all the vision will be to you like the words of a book that has been sealed,  
Which when they give to him who is literate, saying, Please read this,  
He will say, I am not able to, for it is sealed.

## v12

Then the book will be given to him who is illiterate, saying, Please read this,  
And he will say, I am illiterate.

## v13

And the Lord said,  
Because this people draws near with their mouth,  
And with their lips they honor Me,  
Yet they remove their heart far from Me,  
And their fear for Me  
Is a commandment of men merely learned;

## v14

Therefore, indeed, I will once again  
Do something wondrous with this people, something wondrously marvelous;  
And the wisdom of their wise men will perish,  
And the understanding of those who understand will be hidden.

## v15

Woe to those who hide deeply  
Their counsel from Jehovah,  
And whose deeds are in the dark  
And who say, Who sees us? And, Who knows us?

## v16

You turn things upside down!  
Shall the potter be considered to be like the clay,  
That what is made should say of him who made it, He did not make me,  
Or what is formed should say of him who formed it, He has no understanding?

## v17

Is it not yet a little while  
Before Lebanon will be turned into a fruitful field,  
And the fruitful field will be considered to be a forest.

## v18

And in that day the deaf will hear the words of the book,  
And out of gloom and darkness the eyes of the blind will see,

## v19

And the afflicted will increase their joy in Jehovah,  
And the needy of mankind will exult in the Holy One of Israel.

## v20

For he who terrorizes will cease to be,  
And the scorner will be consumed,  
And all who watch for iniquity will be cut off,

## v21

Those who make a man sin by his words  
And ensnare him who judges at the gate  
And turn aside the righteous with an empty argument.

## v22

Therefore thus says Jehovah, who redeemed Abraham, concerning the house of Jacob:  
Jacob will not now be ashamed,  
Nor will his face now turn pale;

## v23

But when he sees his children, the work of My hands, in his midst,  
They will sanctify My name  
And sanctify the Holy One of Jacob,  
And hold the God of Israel in awe.

## v24

And those who have erred in spirit will come to understand,  
And the murmurers will learn teaching.
