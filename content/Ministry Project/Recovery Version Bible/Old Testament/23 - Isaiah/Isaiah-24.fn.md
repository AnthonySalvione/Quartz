## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — host

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-24#v21|Isaiah 24:21]]

Here the host on high refers to Satan and his angels in the air (cf. [[Ephesians-02#v2|Eph. 2:2]]; [[Ephesians-06#v12|6:12]]). Jehovah’s reaction to the nations’ excessive action on Israel deals both with Satan’s army in the air and with the kings on the earth. See [note 13¹](<Isaiah-26.fn#Footnote 13.1 — lords>) in [[Isaiah-26|Isaiah 26]].
