## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — rod

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-10#v5|Isaiah 10:5]]

Because Israel had become corrupt, God used Assyria as a rod and a staff to judge Israel ([[2 Kings-18#v9|2 Kings 18:9-12]]). But Assyria, overstretching the limitation set by God, did not think of the matter in this way, nor did their heart conceive it in this way ([[Isaiah-10#v7|v. 7]]). Thus, Assyria acted excessively, and the king of Assyria became haughty ([[Isaiah-10#v12|vv. 12-15]]). Jehovah, therefore, judged the king of Assyria ([[Isaiah-10#v16|vv. 16-19]]). See [note 13¹](<Isaiah-26.fn#Footnote 13.1 — lords>) in [[Isaiah-26|Isaiah 26]].
