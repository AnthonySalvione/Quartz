## v1

In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah:  
We have a strong city;  
He will set up salvation as walls and a rampart.

## v2

Open the gates  
So that the righteous nation may enter,  
They who maintain faithfulness.

## v3

You will keep the steadfast of mind  
In perfect peace  
Because he trusts in You.

## v4

Trust in Jehovah forever and ever,  
For in Jah Jehovah we have an eternal rock.

## v5

For He has brought down those who dwell on high,  
The lofty city;  
He lays it low, He lays it low to the ground;  
He casts it into the dust.

## v6

The foot will trample it,  
The foot of the poor,  
The steps of the weak.

## v7

The path of the righteous is smoothness;  
O You who are upright,  
You make the path of the righteous level.

## v8

Indeed in the path of Your judgments,  
O Jehovah, we have waited for You.  
Your name, that is, Your memorial,  
Is the desire of our soul.

## v9

My soul desires You in the night;  
Indeed my spirit within me seeks You at the dawn;  
For when Your judgments are in the earth,  
The inhabitants of the world learn righteousness.

## v10

Though the wicked is shown grace,  
He does not learn righteousness;  
In the land of straightforwardness he deals unjustly  
And does not perceive the majesty of Jehovah.

## v11

O Jehovah, Your hand is lifted up,  
But they do not see it.  
They will see Your zeal for the people and will be put to shame;  
Indeed fire will devour Your adversaries.

## v12

O Jehovah, You will ordain peace for us,  
For You have also performed all our works for us.

## v13

O Jehovah our God,  
Other lords besides You have been master to us,  
But through You alone do we mention Your name.

## v14

The dead will not live,  
The deceased will not rise;  
Therefore You have visited and destroyed them,  
And You have caused all memory of them to perish.

## v15

You have increased the nation, O Jehovah;  
You have increased the nation — You are glorified;  
You have far extended all the edges of the land.

## v16

O Jehovah, in distress they sought You;  
They whispered out a prayer,  
For Your chastening was upon them.

## v17

As a pregnant woman draws near to giving birth,  
She writhes and cries out in her pain;  
So we have been before You, O Jehovah.

## v18

We were pregnant; we writhed;  
We gave birth, as it were, to wind.  
We have not accomplished deliverance for the earth,  
Nor have the inhabitants of the world been born.

## v19

Your dead will live;  
My corpses will rise.  
Awake and give a ringing shout, you who dwell in dust,  
For your dew is like the dew of the dawn,  
And the earth will bring forth the deceased.

## v20

Come, my people, enter your rooms,  
And shut your doors behind you;  
Hide for a short moment  
Until the indignation has passed;

## v21

For Jehovah is now coming forth from His place  
To punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity;  
And the earth will reveal her bloodshed  
And will no longer conceal her slain.
