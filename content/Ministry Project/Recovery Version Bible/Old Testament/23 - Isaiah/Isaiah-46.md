## v1

Bel has bowed down; Nebo stoops; /  
Their idols are on beasts and cattle; /  
The things which you carry are a burden, /  
A load for a weary beast.

## v2

They stoop and have bowed down together; /  
They are not able to escape the burden; /  
But they themselves have gone into captivity.

## v3

Listen to Me, O house of Jacob, /  
And all the remnant of the house of Israel, /  
You who have been borne from birth, /  
Who have been carried from the womb,

## v4

Even unto old age, I am He; /  
Even unto your gray-haired years, I will bear you. /  
I have made and I will carry, /  
And I will bear and deliver.

## v5

To whom will you liken Me, /  
And with whom will you make Me equal /  
And compare Me, that we should be considered alike?

## v6

Those who lavish gold from the purse /  
And weigh silver in scales /  
And hire a goldsmith, and he makes a god — /  
They bow down and even worship it;

## v7

They lift it up on their shoulder; they carry it /  
And set it in its place, and it stands. /  
It will not move from its place; /  
Someone even cries out to it, and it does not answer, /  
Nor save him from his distress.

## v8

Remember this and stand fast; /  
Put this in your heart, O transgressors.

## v9

Remember the former things of old, /  
That I am God and there is no one else; /  
I am God and there is no one like Me,

## v10

Who declares the end from the beginning /  
And things which have not been done from ancient times, /  
Saying, My counsel will stand, /  
And all My desire I will accomplish;

## v11

Who calls for a bird of prey from the east, /  
And from a distant land a man to be My counselor. /  
Indeed, I have spoken; surely I will bring it to pass. /  
I have purposed; surely I will do it.

## v12

Listen to Me, O hardhearted ones, /  
Who are far from righteousness.

## v13

I will bring My righteousness near — it is not far off; /  
And My salvation will not delay; /  
And I will give salvation in Zion, /  
And to Israel My glory.
