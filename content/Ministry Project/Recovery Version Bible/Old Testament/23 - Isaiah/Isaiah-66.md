## v1

Thus says Jehovah, /  
Heaven is My throne, /  
And the earth the footstool for My feet. /  
Where then is the house that you will build for Me, /  
And where is the place of My rest?

## v2

For all these things My hand has made, /  
And so all these things have come into being, declares Jehovah. /  
But to this kind of man will I look, /  
To him who is poor and of a contrite spirit, /  
And who trembles at My word.

## v3

He who kills an ox is like him who slays a man; /  
He who sacrifices a lamb, like him who breaks a dog’s neck; /  
He who offers a meal offering is like him who offers the blood of swine; /  
He who burns incense is like him who blesses an idol. /  
As surely as they have chosen their own ways, /  
And their soul delights in their abominations;

## v4

Just as surely I will choose what will treat them ill, /  
And I will bring upon them what they dread; /  
Because I called, but no one answered; /  
I spoke, but they did not listen; /  
But they did evil in My sight, /  
And chose that in which I did not delight.

## v5

Hear the word of Jehovah, /  
You who tremble at His word: /  
Your brothers who hate you, /  
Who cast you out because of My name, said, /  
Let Jehovah be glorified /  
So that we may see your joy. /  
But they will be put to shame.

## v6

A voice of tumult from the city! /  
A voice from the temple! /  
The voice of Jehovah /  
Giving full recompense to His enemies.

## v7

Before she travailed, /  
She brought forth; /  
Before her pain came, /  
She gave birth to a man.

## v8

Who has heard of such a thing? /  
Who has seen such things? /  
Can a land be born in one day? /  
Or can a nation be brought forth all at once? /  
For as soon as Zion travailed, /  
She brought forth her children.

## v9

Shall I bring to the point of birth and not cause to bring forth? /  
Says Jehovah. /  
Or shall I who cause to bring forth shut the womb? /  
Says your God.

## v10

Be joyful with Jerusalem and exult for her, /  
All you who love her; /  
Rejoice with her in joy, /  
All you who mourn over her,

## v11

So that you may nurse and be satisfied /  
With her breasts of consolations, /  
So that you may draw out from her and take excellent delight /  
In the abundance of her glory.

## v12

For thus says Jehovah, /  
I now am extending to her /  
Peace like a river, /  
And the glory of the nations /  
Like an overflowing stream; /  
And you will nurse, /  
You will be carried on the hip, /  
And you will be bounced on the knees.

## v13

As one whom his mother comforts, /  
So will I comfort you; /  
And you will be comforted in Jerusalem.

## v14

And you will see this, and your heart will rejoice, /  
And your bones will flourish like new grass; /  
And the hand of Jehovah will be made known to His servants, /  
And He will be indignant toward His enemies.

## v15

For behold, Jehovah will come with fire, /  
And His chariots will be like a whirlwind, /  
To render His anger with fury /  
And His rebuke with flames of fire.

## v16

For with fire Jehovah will execute judgment, /  
And with His sword, upon all flesh; /  
And those slain by Jehovah will be many.

## v17

Those who sanctify and purify themselves for the gardens, /  
Following after one in the midst, /  
Eating swine’s flesh /  
And what is abominable and even mice, /  
Will come to an end together, declares Jehovah.

## v18

But I — their works and their thoughts I will repay; /  
The time is coming to gather all the nations and tongues; /  
And they will come and see My glory.

## v19

And I will set a sign among them /  
And will send those who have escaped from them unto the nations: /  
To Tarshish, Put, and Lud, to those who draw the bow; /  
To Tubal and Javan, /  
To the distant coastlands that have not heard My fame nor seen My glory; /  
And they will declare My glory among the nations.

## v20

And they will bring all your brothers from all the nations /  
As a meal offering to Jehovah, /  
On horses and in chariots and in wagons and on mules and on camels, /  
To My holy mountain, Jerusalem, says Jehovah, /  
Just as the children of Israel bring their meal offering /  
In a clean vessel to the house of Jehovah.

## v21

And I will also take some of them for priests /  
And for Levites, says Jehovah.

## v22

For as the new heavens /  
And new earth, which I make, /  
Remain before Me, declares Jehovah, /  
So will your seed and your name remain.

## v23

And from new moon to new moon /  
And from Sabbath to Sabbath /  
All flesh will come /  
To bow down before Me, says Jehovah.

## v24

Then they will go forth and look /  
On the carcasses of the men that have transgressed against Me; /  
For their worm will not die, /  
Nor will their fire be quenched; /  
And they will be an abhorrence to all flesh.
