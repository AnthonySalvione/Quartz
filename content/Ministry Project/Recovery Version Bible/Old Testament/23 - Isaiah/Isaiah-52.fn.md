# Isaiah-52 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Isaiah-52|Isaiah 52]]

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — Your

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v7|Isaiah 52:7]]

This is the announcing of the restoration ([[Matthew-19#v28|Matt. 19:28]]). [[Isaiah-40#v9|Isaiah 40:9]] speaks of God coming to accomplish redemption unto salvation for His people, whereas this verse, after redemption has been accomplished, announces that in the restoration, the millennial kingdom, God reigns (cf. [[Revelation-11#v15|Rev. 11:15]]). The reigning God here is the Redeemer, the Savior, mentioned in [[Isaiah-40|Isaiah 40]].

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — Servant

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v13|Isaiah 52:13]]

[[Isaiah-52#v13|Isaiah 52:13]]—[[Isaiah-53#v12|53:12]] reveals Christ as the Servant of Jehovah not in the Old Testament economy but in the New Testament economy, i.e., as God who became a man, who died and resurrected, and who became the life-giving Spirit to enter into His elect and dwell in them as the indwelling Spirit.

### Footnote 13.2 — wisely

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v13|Isaiah 52:13]]

From the day that He came out to minister on this earth, the Lord Jesus acted wisely and prospered in God’s pleasure ([[Isaiah-52#v13|v. 13a]]; [[Isaiah-53#v10|53:10b]]; [[Matthew-11#v19|Matt. 11:19]] and [note 3](<Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 19.3 — works>)). God’s good pleasure was first that the Son would go to the cross and die for God’s chosen people ([[Matthew-26#v39|Matt. 26:39]]; [[Hebrews-10#v5|Heb. 10:5-10]]) and then that He would rise from the dead to regenerate millions of God’s people to be God’s sons ([[1 Peter-01#v3|1 Pet. 1:3]]; [[John-20#v17|John 20:17]]). This is the wisdom by which Christ as the Servant of Jehovah acted wisely. Since His ascension Jesus has been acting prudently and wisely on the earth, and in whatever He has done, He has prospered.

### Footnote 13.3 — exalted

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v13|Isaiah 52:13]]

Christ has been exalted to the third heavens, to the right hand of God ([[Philippians-02#v9|Phil. 2:9]]; [[Hebrews-04#v14|Heb. 4:14]]; [[Hebrews-07#v26|7:26]]; [[Hebrews-08#v1|8:1]]). He has also been lifted up. Before being exalted and lifted up, Christ was humiliated ([[Isaiah-52#v14|v. 14]]; [[Isaiah-53#v1|53:1-10a]]; [[Philippians-02#v8|Phil. 2:8]]).

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — Him

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v14|Isaiah 52:14]]

According to some MSS and ancient versions; other MSS read, you.

### Footnote 14.2 — visage

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v14|Isaiah 52:14]]

Visage denotes the appearance and also refers to the face or facial expression. Christ’s face and His form were marred (disfigured) in order that He might save us. This is astonishing, different from what people expected Christ as a servant of God to be.

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — surprise

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v15|Isaiah 52:15]]

Jesus will surprise many nations, and kings will shut their mouths because of Him, for what He is, is altogether different from what they imagined. People are surprised to learn that a great person such as Christ was actually a small man who lived in the despised region of Galilee, in the despised city of Nazareth, in the poor home of a carpenter, and that He was rejected and put on the cross and crucified ([[Isaiah-53#v2|53:2-10a]]).

### Footnote 15.2 — recounted

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-52#v15|Isaiah 52:15]]

The things to be recounted, to be seen, to be heard of, and to be contemplated are the things reported and revealed in [[Isaiah-53|Isaiah 53]] concerning Christ in His incarnation, crucifixion, resurrection, and ascension according to the New Testament gospel.
