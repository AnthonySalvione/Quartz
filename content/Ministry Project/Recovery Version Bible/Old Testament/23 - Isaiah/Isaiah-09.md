## v1

But gloom does not remain in the place where there was anguish:  
Formerly He treated the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali contemptibly,  
But afterward He treats the way of the [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sea|sea]], across the Jordan,  
With glory, [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Galilee|Galilee]] of the nations.

## v2

The people who walked in the darkness  
Have seen a great [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 2.1 — light|light]];  
Upon those who dwell in the land of the shadow of death  
[[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 2.1 — light|Light]] has shined.

## v3

You have [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 3.1 — multiplied|multiplied]] the nation;  
You have increased their gladness;  
They are glad before You as with the gladness of harvest,  
As men rejoice when they divide the spoil.

## v4

For You break the yoke of their burden  
And the staff on their shoulder,  
The rod of their oppressor,  
As in the day of [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Midian|Midian]].

## v5

For all the boots  
Of those who in boots trample in the battle-quake  
And the garments  
Rolled in blood  
Are for burning;  
They are fuel for fire.

## v6

For a [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.1 — child|child]] is born [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.2 — to|to]] us,  
A [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.1 — child|Son]] is given [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.2 — to|to]] us;  
And the [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.3 — government|government]]  
Is upon His shoulder;  
And His name will be called  
[[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.4 — Wonderful|Wonderful]] Counselor,  
[[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.5 — Mighty|Mighty]] God,  
[[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.5 — Mighty|Eternal]] Father,  
[[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 6.6 — Prince|Prince]] of Peace.

## v7

To the increase of His [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 7.1 — government|government]]  
And to His [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 7.1 — government|peace]] there is no end,  
Upon the [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 7.2 — throne|throne]] of David  
And over His kingdom,  
To establish it  
And to uphold it  
In justice and righteousness  
From now to eternity.  
The zeal of Jehovah of hosts  
Will accomplish this.

## v8

The Lord sends out a word against [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Jacob|Jacob]],  
And it descends upon [[Isaiah-09.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Jacob|Israel]].

## v9

And all the people know it,  
Ephraim and the inhabitants of Samaria,  
Saying in pride and haughtiness of heart,

## v10

The bricks have fallen, but we will build with cut stone;  
The sycamores have been felled, but we will put cedars in their place.

## v11

Then Jehovah exalts the adversaries of Rezin against them  
And goads their enemies on.

## v12

Aram from the east and the Philistines from the west:  
They devour Israel with an open mouth.  
By all this His anger is not turned;  
His hand is still outstretched.

## v13

Yet the people do not turn to Him who strikes them,  
Nor do they seek Jehovah of hosts.

## v14

Then Jehovah cuts off from Israel head and tail,  
Palm branch and marsh reed in one day.

## v15

The elder and the highly regarded — he is the head;  
The prophet and the teacher of falsehood — he is the tail.

## v16

And those who lead this people are those who mislead them;  
And those who are led are those who are confused.

## v17

Because of this the Lord will not rejoice in their young men,  
And He will not have compassion on their orphans and widows;  
For all of them are profane and evildoers,  
And every mouth speaks foolishness.  
By all this His anger is not turned;  
His hand is still outstretched.

## v18

For wickedness burns like a fire;  
It devours thorn and thistle,  
And burns in the forest thickets;  
And they roll up into a column of smoke.

## v19

At the overflowing wrath of Jehovah of hosts a land is burned up,  
And the people are like fuel for fire;  
No one spares even his brother.

## v20

One carves on the right side, but is hungry;  
And another eats on the left side, but is not satisfied.  
Each eats the flesh of his own arm:

## v21

Manasseh, Ephraim, and Ephraim, Manasseh;  
These together against Judah.  
By all this His anger is not turned;  
His hand is still outstretched.
