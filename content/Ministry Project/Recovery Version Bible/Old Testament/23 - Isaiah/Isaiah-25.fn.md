# Isaiah-25 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Isaiah-25|Isaiah 25]]

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — peoples

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-25#v6|Isaiah 25:6]]

Here peoples refers to the nations. In the restoration God will make a satisfying feast for the nations. At that time Jerusalem with Mount Zion will be the most joyful place on earth. It will be a center to which all the peoples of the earth will come to find enjoyment and satisfaction ([[Isaiah-02#v2|2:2-3]]).

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — covering

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-25#v7|Isaiah 25:7]]

Lit., the face of the covering that covers up. Today the people on earth are covered with a veil that keeps them from seeing God’s economy (cf. [[2 Corinthians-03#v15|2 Cor. 3:15]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v4|4:4]]), but in the restoration God will take away this covering. Then all the people will see something concerning God’s eternal economy.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — forever

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-25#v8|Isaiah 25:8]]

Lit., unto perpetuity. The Hebrew phrase, however, also contains the notion of eminence; thus, the Septuagint translated it having prevailed, and Paul freely rendered it unto victory ([[1 Corinthians-15#v54|1 Cor. 15:54]]).
