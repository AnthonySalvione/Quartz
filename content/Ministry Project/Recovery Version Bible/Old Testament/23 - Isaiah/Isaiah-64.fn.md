# Isaiah-64 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Isaiah-64|Isaiah 64]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — come

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-64#v1|Isaiah 64:1]]

[[Isaiah-64#v1|Isaiah 64:1-5a]] and [[Isaiah-66#v15|66:15-16]] reveal the second coming of Christ as the Servant of Jehovah (cf. [[Isaiah-31#v4|31:4-5]]). Christ’s rending the heavens and coming down refers to the consummation of Christ’s coming from heaven to earth, from God to man (see [note 1¹](<Isaiah-64.fn#Footnote 1.1 — come>), pars. 2 and 3, in [[Isaiah-61|Isaiah 61]]).
