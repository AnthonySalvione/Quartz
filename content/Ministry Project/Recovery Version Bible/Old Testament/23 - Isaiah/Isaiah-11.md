## v1

Then a [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sprout|sprout]] will come forth from the stump of Jesse,  
And a [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sprout|branch]] from his roots will bear fruit.

## v2

And the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of Jehovah will rest upon Him,  
The Spirit of wisdom and understanding,  
The Spirit of counsel and might,  
The Spirit of the knowledge and fear of Jehovah.

## v3

He will delight in the fear of Jehovah:  
He will neither judge by what His eyes see,  
Nor decide by what His ears hear.

## v4

But He will judge the poor in righteousness,  
And decide with equity for the afflicted of the land.  
And He will strike the land with the rod of His mouth,  
And with the breath of His lips He will slay the wicked.

## v5

Righteousness will be that which [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 5.1 — girds|girds]] His loins,  
And faithfulness will be that which [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 5.1 — girds|girds]] His hips.

## v6

And the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 6.1 — wolf|wolf]] will dwell with the lamb;  
And the leopard will lie down with the kid,  
And the calf and the young lion and the fatling together;  
And a young boy will lead them about.

## v7

The cow and the bear will graze;  
Their young will lie down together;  
And the lion will eat straw like the ox.

## v8

The nursing child will play by the cobra’s hole,  
And upon the viper’s den  
The weaned child will stretch his hand.

## v9

They will not harm nor destroy  
In all My holy mountain,  
For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of Jehovah,  
As water covers the sea.

## v10

And in that day the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 10.1 — root|root]] of Jesse,  
Who stands as a [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 10.2 — banner|banner]] to the peoples —  
Him will the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 10.3 — nations|nations]] seek,  
And His resting place will be the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 10.4 — glory|glory]].

## v11

And in that day the Lord,  
By His hand for the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 11.1 — second|second]] time again,  
Will [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 11.2 — recover|recover]] the remnant of His people  
Who remain, from Assyria and from Egypt,  
From Pathros, Cush, and Elam,  
From Shinar, Hamath, and the islands of the sea.

## v12

He will lift up a [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — standard|standard]] to the nations,  
And will gather the outcasts of Israel;  
And the dispersed of Judah He will assemble  
From the four corners of the earth.

## v13

And [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Ephraim’s|Ephraim’s]] jealousy will depart,  
And the adversaries in Judah will be cut off;  
Ephraim will not be jealous of Judah,  
Nor will Judah be hostile to Ephraim.

## v14

And they will fly down upon the shoulders of the Philistines on the west;  
Together they will plunder the children of the east.  
Edom and Moab will come into the grasp of their hand,  
And the children of Ammon will submit to them.

## v15

And Jehovah will [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 15.1 — utterly|utterly]] destroy the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 15.2 — tongue|tongue]] of the sea of Egypt;  
And He will wave His hand over the [[Isaiah-11.fn#Footnote 15.2 — tongue|River]] with the scorching of His wind,  
And He will strike it into seven streams,  
And march men through it dryshod.

## v16

And there will be a highway from Assyria  
For the remnant of His people which remain,  
As there was for Israel  
In the day that they came up from the land of Egypt.
