## v1

In the year that King Uzziah died  
I saw the [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Lord|Lord]] sitting on a high and lofty throne,  
And the train of His [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — robe|robe]] filled the temple.

## v2

Seraphim [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — hovered|hovered]] over [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Him|Him]],  
Each having six wings:  
With two he covered his face,  
And with two he covered his feet,  
And with two he flew.

## v3

And one called to the other, saying:  
[[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Holy|Holy]], holy, holy, Jehovah of hosts;  
The whole earth is filled with His glory.

## v4

And the [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — foundations|foundations]] of the threshold [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — shook|shook]]  
At the voice of him who called,  
And the house was filled with [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — shook|smoke]].

## v5

Then I said,  
Woe is me, for I am finished!  
For I am a man of [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — unclean|unclean]] lips,  
And in the midst of a people of [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — unclean|unclean]] lips I dwell;  
Yet my eyes have seen the King, Jehovah of hosts.

## v6

Then one of the seraphim flew to me  
With an [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — ember|ember]] in his hand,  
Which he had taken from the altar  
With a pair of tongs.

## v7

And he touched my mouth with it and said,  
Now that this has touched your lips,  
Your iniquity is taken away,  
And your sin is [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — purged|purged]].

## v8

Then I heard the voice of the Lord, saying,  
Whom shall [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — I|I]] send?  
Who will go for [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — I|Us]]?  
And I said,  
Here am I; send me.

## v9

And He said, [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Go|Go]] and say to this people,  
Hear indeed, but do not perceive;  
And see indeed, but do not understand.

## v10

Make the heart of this people numb;  
Dull their ears,  
And seal their eyes;  
Lest they see with their eyes and hear with their ears,  
And their heart perceive and return,  
And they are [[Isaiah-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — healed|healed]].

## v11

And I said, For how long, Lord?  
And He said,  
Until cities lie devastated,  
Without inhabitants,  
And houses are without people,  
And the land is devastated and a waste;

## v12

And Jehovah has sent men far away from it,  
And desolate places abound in the midst of the land.

## v13

But there will still be a tenth part in it;  
And it in turn is to be burned  
Like a terebinth or an oak,  
Whose stump remains after its felling;  
Its stump will be a holy seed.
