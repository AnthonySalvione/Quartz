# Isaiah-28 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Isaiah-28|Isaiah 28]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Ephraim

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v1|Isaiah 28:1]]

Representing the northern kingdom of Israel.

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — someone

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v2|Isaiah 28:2]]

Probably first the king of Assyria and then the king of Babylon ([[2 Kings-17#v3|2 Kings 17:3-18]]; [[2 Kings-24#v10|24:10-16]]).

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — crown

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v5|Isaiah 28:5]]

Jehovah’s punishing of Israel issues in His restoration. Jehovah of hosts as Christ will be a crown of beauty and a diadem of glory to the remnant of Israel, God’s chosen people, in the age of restoration after all God’s dealing with Israel is completed.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — Rule

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v10|Isaiah 28:10]]

These rules were parts of the law. Ephraim was bothered by the fact that Jehovah’s rules and statutes were scattered repeatedly in pieces.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — foreign

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v11|Isaiah 28:11]]

The tongue of the Gentile invaders, which became a bothering and a shame to Ephraim (cf. [[1 Corinthians-14#v21|1 Cor. 14:21]] and [note 2](<1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 21.2 — By>)).

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — Rule

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v13|Isaiah 28:13]]

See [note 10¹](<Isaiah-28.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Rule>).

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — Jerusalem

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v14|Isaiah 28:14]]

Jehovah destroyed the northern kingdom of Israel by the Assyrian army ([[Isaiah-28#v14|vv. 14-15]], [[Isaiah-28#v17|17-20]], [[Isaiah-28#v22|22]]; [[2 Kings-17#v3|2 Kings 17:3-18]]). This was done as a warning to Jerusalem, which represents the southern kingdom of Judah.

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — stone

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v16|Isaiah 28:16]]

Jehovah’s punishment over Ephraim’s drunkards will also usher in the trustworthy Christ as a foundation stone and a precious cornerstone for God’s building ([[Psalm-118#v22|Psa. 118:22-24]] and notes). The complete fulfillment of Christ as the stone will be in the next age, the age of restoration ([[Psalm-118#v26|Psa. 118:26]] and [note](<Psalm-118.fn#Footnote 26.1 — comes>); cf. [[Matthew-23#v38|Matt. 23:38-39]] and [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 39.1 — He|note 39¹]]). 

In contrast to the priest and the prophet described in [[Isaiah-28#v7|v. 7]], the trustworthy Christ is the faithful High Priest and the trustworthy Prophet to God’s redeemed ([[Hebrews-07#v26|Heb. 7:26]]; [[Acts-03#v22|Acts 3:22-23]]).

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — rise

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v21|Isaiah 28:21]]

This is Jehovah’s reaction of fighting against the Assyrians, who had destroyed Israel excessively (see [note 13¹](<Isaiah-26.fn#Footnote 13.1 — lords>) in [[Isaiah-26|Isaiah 26]]).

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — plowman

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-28#v24|Isaiah 28:24]]

In [[Isaiah-28#v24|vv. 24-29]] Jehovah, as a farmer, deals with people as His crops by different utensils according to His extraordinary counsel and His great wisdom.
