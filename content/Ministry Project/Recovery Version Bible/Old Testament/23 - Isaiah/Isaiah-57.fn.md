## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — refuge

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-57#v13|Isaiah 57:13]]

To take refuge in the Lord is to enter into Him and stay in Him to enjoy Him. When we stay in the Lord, taking Him as our refuge, we enjoy Him, and eventually we will inherit Him as the land and possess Him as our high mountain.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — wicked

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-57#v20|Isaiah 57:20]]

The evil condition of the wicked is that they do not come to the Lord to eat and enjoy the Lord (cf. [[Isaiah-55#v1|55:1-2]]). They do many things, but they do not come to contact the Lord, to take Him, to receive Him, to taste Him, and to enjoy Him. In the sight of God, nothing is more evil than this. Cf. [note 7¹](<Isaiah-55.fn#Footnote 7.1 — evildoer>) in [[Isaiah-55|Isaiah 55]].
