## v1

And you will say in that day,  
I will [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — praise|praise]] You, O Jehovah.  
You were angry with me,  
But Your anger has turned and You have consoled me.

## v2

[[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — God|God]] is now my salvation;  
I will trust and not dread;  
For [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Jah|Jah]] [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — God|Jehovah]] is my [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 2.3 — strength|strength]] and song,  
And He has become my salvation.

## v3

Therefore you will draw [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — water|water]] with rejoicing  
From the [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — springs|springs]] of salvation,

## v4

And you will say in that day,  
Give thanks to Jehovah; [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 4.1 — call|call]] upon His name!  
[[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Make|Make]] His deeds known among the peoples;  
Remind them that His name is exalted.

## v5

Sing psalms to Jehovah,  
For He has done something majestic!  
Let it be made known in all the earth!

## v6

Cry out and give a [[Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 6.1 — ringing|ringing]] shout, O inhabitant of Zion,  
For great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel.
