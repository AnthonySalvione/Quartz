## v1

In the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, the king of Judah,  
Rezin the king of Aram and Pekah the son of Remaliah, the king of Israel,  
Went up to Jerusalem to wage war against it,  
But they were not able to prevail against it.

## v2

And it was reported to the house of David  
That Aram allied with Ephraim,  
And his heart and the heart of his people quivered  
As the trees of the forest quiver in the face of a wind.

## v3

Then Jehovah said to Isaiah,  
Go out to meet Ahaz, you and Shear-jashub your son,  
At the end of the conduit of the upper pool,  
On the road to the Fuller’s Field;

## v4

And say to him,  
Be careful and be quiet; do not fear, and do not be fainthearted  
Because of these two smoking firebrand stubs,  
At the burning anger of Rezin and Aram,  
And the son of Remaliah.

## v5

Because Aram and Ephraim and the son of Remaliah  
Have planned evil against you, saying,

## v6

Let us go up against Judah  
And make it sick with terror and break it open for ourselves,  
And let us set the son of Tabel in its midst as king;

## v7

Thus says the Lord Jehovah,  
It shall not stand, and it shall not happen;

## v8

For the head of Aram is Damascus,  
And the head of Damascus is Rezin;  
And in another sixty-five years  
Ephraim will be shattered as a people.

## v9

And the head of Ephraim is Samaria,  
And the head of Samaria is the son of Remaliah;  
If you do not believe,  
Surely you will not remain standing.

## v10

Then Jehovah spoke further to Ahaz, saying,

## v11

Ask for a sign from Jehovah your God;  
Make it as deep as Sheol,  
Or make it as high as high can go.

## v12

And Ahaz said,  
I will not ask, and I will not try Jehovah.

## v13

And he said,  
Hear now, O house of David,  
Is it too small a thing for you to exhaust the patience of men  
That you will exhaust the patience of my God as well?

## v14

Therefore the Lord Himself will give you a sign:  
Behold, the virgin will conceive and will bear a son,  
And she will call his name Immanuel.

## v15

He will eat curds and honey  
Until he knows how to refuse evil and choose good.

## v16

For before this boy knows how to refuse evil and choose good,  
The land whose two kings you dread will be abandoned.

## v17

Jehovah will bring upon you and your people  
And the house of your father such days as have not been  
Since the days when Ephraim turned away from Judah;  
He will bring upon you the king of Assyria.

## v18

And in that day Jehovah will whistle  
For the flies which are at the ends of the rivers of Egypt  
And for the bees which are in the land of Assyria.

## v19

And they will all come and settle  
In the steep ravines and the clefts of the cliffs  
And on all the thornbushes  
And on all watering places.

## v20

In that day the Lord will shave with the razor  
Hired from beyond the River,  
With the king of Assyria,  
The head and the hair of the legs;  
And it will take away the beard as well.

## v21

And in that day each man will keep alive  
Only a milk cow and two females of the flock.

## v22

And because of the abundance of milk produced,  
He will eat curds;  
For everyone left behind in the midst of the land  
Will eat curds and honey.

## v23

And in that day every place  
Where there could be a thousand vines,  
Worth a thousand shekels of silver,  
Will become thorns and thistles.

## v24

Men will come there with arrows and bow,  
For all the land will be thorns and thistles.

## v25

And to all the hills that once were hoed with the hoe  
You will not go for fear of thorns and thistles;  
But they will become a place  
For cattle to roam in  
And for sheep to trample.
