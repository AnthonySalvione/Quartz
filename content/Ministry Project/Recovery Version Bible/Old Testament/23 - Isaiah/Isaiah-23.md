## v1

The burden concerning Tyre:  
Howl, O ships of Tarshish,  
For it is destroyed — lacking house,  
Lacking entrance.  
From as far away as the land of Kittim  
It is told to them.

## v2

Hush, you inhabitants of the coastland,  
You, whom the merchants of Sidon,  
Who cross the sea, have replenished.

## v3

And by way of many waters  
The grain of Shihor, the harvest of the Nile, was her revenue;  
And she was profit to the nations.

## v4

Be ashamed, O Sidon, for the sea has spoken,  
The stronghold of the sea, saying,  
I have not been in labor nor given birth;  
Neither have I brought up young men,  
Nor have I raised virgins.

## v5

When the report reaches Egypt,  
They will writhe in pain  
At the report concerning Tyre.

## v6

Cross over to Tarshish;  
Howl, you inhabitants of the coastland.

## v7

Is this your jubilant city,  
Whose antiquity is from the days of antiquity,  
Whose feet carried her off afar to sojourn?

## v8

Who has purposed this  
Against Tyre, the giver of crowns,  
Whose merchants were princes,  
Whose traders were the honorable of the earth?

## v9

Jehovah of hosts has purposed it,  
To defile the pride of all beauty,  
To bring into contempt all the honorable of the earth.

## v10

Overflow your land like a river,  
O daughter of Tarshish;  
There is no more restraint.

## v11

He has stretched out His hand over the sea;  
He has shaken kingdoms.  
Jehovah has given commandment concerning Canaan  
To destroy its strongholds.

## v12

And He has said,  
You shall not continue to exult any longer,  
O crushed virgin daughter of Sidon.  
Arise; cross over to Kittim —  
There also you will have no rest.

## v13

See the land of the Chaldeans, this people!  
They were not —  
The Assyrians assigned it to desert animals;  
They raised their siege towers;  
They stripped their citadels;  
They brought it to ruin.

## v14

Howl, O ships of Tarshish,  
For your stronghold is destroyed.

## v15

In that day Tyre will be forgotten for seventy years,  
Like the days of one king.  
At the end of the seventy years  
Tyre will be like her in the harlot’s song:

## v16

Take a harp;  
Compass the city,  
Forgotten harlot.  
Play well;  
Abound in song,  
That you may be remembered.

## v17

And at the end of the seventy years Jehovah will visit Tyre.  
And she will return to the wages of her harlotry  
And will be a harlot to all the kingdoms of the earth  
On land’s surface.

## v18

And her profit and the wages of her harlotry  
Will be dedicated to Jehovah;  
It will not be stored up, nor will it be hoarded,  
For her profit will be for those who dwell before Jehovah,  
As their sufficient food and their choice clothing.
