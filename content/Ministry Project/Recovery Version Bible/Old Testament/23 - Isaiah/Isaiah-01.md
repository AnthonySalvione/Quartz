## v1

The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, the kings of Judah.

## v2

Hear, O heavens, and hearken, O earth,  
For Jehovah has spoken:  
I have brought up children, and I have raised them;  
And yet they have rebelled against Me.

## v3

The ox knows his owner,  
And the donkey, his master’s manger;  
But Israel does not know,  
My people do not much consider.

## v4

Alas, sinful nation,  
A people heavy with iniquity,  
Seed of evildoers,  
Children acting corruptly!  
They have forsaken Jehovah;  
They have despised the Holy One of Israel;  
They have become estranged and have gone backward.

## v5

Where will you be stricken again?  
Will you continue your apostasy?  
The whole head has become sick,  
And the whole heart faint;

## v6

From the sole of the foot even to the head,  
There is no soundness in it,  
Only bruises and blows  
And raw wounds —  
They have not been pressed out nor bound up  
Nor softened with oil.

## v7

Your land is a desolation;  
Your cities are burned with fire;  
Your field — in your sight  
Strangers devour it;  
It is a desolation, like something overthrown by strangers.

## v8

And the daughter of Zion is left  
Like a booth in a vineyard,  
Like a hut in a cucumber field,  
Like a besieged city.

## v9

Unless Jehovah of hosts  
Had left to us a surviving few,  
We would have been like Sodom,  
We would have resembled Gomorrah.

## v10

Hear the word of Jehovah,  
You rulers of Sodom;  
Hearken to the instruction of our God,  
You people of Gomorrah.

## v11

What is the multitude of your sacrifices to Me?  
Says Jehovah.  
I have had My fill of burnt offerings of rams  
And the fat of fed cattle;  
The blood of bulls and lambs  
And goats, I do not delight in.

## v12

When you come to appear before Me,  
Who has required this of your hand,  
To trample My courts?

## v13

Bring no more vain offerings;  
Incense is an abomination to Me.  
New moon and Sabbath, the calling of convocations —  
I cannot bear iniquity and the solemn assembly.

## v14

Your new moons and your appointed feasts  
My soul hates;  
They have become a burden on Me;  
I am weary of bearing them.

## v15

Thus, when you spread forth your hands,  
I will hide My eyes from you;  
Even though you multiply your prayers,  
I will not hear.  
Your hands are full of blood;

## v16

Wash yourselves; cleanse yourselves.  
Turn away the evil of your deeds  
From before My eyes.  
Cease doing what is evil;

## v17

Learn to do good.  
Seek justice;  
Correct the ruthless.  
Defend the orphan;  
Plead for the widow.

## v18

Come now and let us reason together,  
Says Jehovah.  
Though your sins are like scarlet,  
They will be as white as snow;  
Though they are as red as crimson,  
They will be like wool.

## v19

If you are willing and listen,  
You will eat the good of the land;

## v20

But if you refuse and rebel,  
You will be devoured by the sword;  
For the mouth of Jehovah has spoken.

## v21

How the faithful city  
Has become a harlot!  
She who was full of justice,  
She in whom righteousness once lodged,  
But now murderers!

## v22

Your silver has become dross;  
Your wine diluted with water;

## v23

Your rulers are rebellious  
And companions of thieves;  
They all love bribes  
And chase after rewards;  
They do not defend the orphan,  
Nor does the widow’s plea come before them.

## v24

Hence, the Lord Jehovah of hosts,  
The Mighty One of Israel, declares:  
Ah, I will ease Myself of My adversaries,  
And I will avenge Myself of My enemies;

## v25

And I will turn My hand against you.  
I will thoroughly purge away your dross as with lye,  
And I will remove all your alloy.

## v26

And I will restore your judges as at the first  
And your counselors as at the beginning.  
Afterward you will be called the city of righteousness,  
The faithful city.

## v27

Zion will be ransomed with justice,  
And her returning ones with righteousness.

## v28

And there will be the shattering of rebels and sinners together,  
And those who forsake Jehovah will meet their end.

## v29

For you will be ashamed of the terebinths  
That you have taken pleasure in;  
And you will be embarrassed at the gardens  
That you have chosen.

## v30

For you will be like a terebinth  
Whose leaves are falling,  
And like a garden  
In which there is no water;

## v31

And the strong man will become tow,  
And his work a spark;  
And they will both burn together,  
And there will be none to extinguish them.
