## v1

The Spirit of the Lord Jehovah is upon Me, /  
Because Jehovah has anointed Me /  
To bring good news to the afflicted; /  
He has sent Me to bind up the wounds of the brokenhearted, /  
To proclaim liberty to the captives, /  
And the opening of the eyes to those who are bound;

## v2

To proclaim the acceptable year of Jehovah /  
And the day of vengeance of our God; /  
To comfort all who mourn;

## v3

To grant to those who mourn in Zion, /  
To give to them a headdress instead of ashes, /  
The oil of gladness instead of mourning, /  
The mantle of praise instead of a spirit of heaviness; /  
That they may be called the terebinths of righteousness, /  
The planting of Jehovah, that He may be glorified.

## v4

And they will rebuild the ancient ruins; /  
They will raise up the former desolations; /  
And they will repair the cities of waste, /  
The desolations of generation upon generation.

## v5

And strangers will stand and feed your flocks, /  
And the sons of the foreigner will be your plowmen and vinedressers.

## v6

But you will be called the priests of Jehovah; /  
People will speak of you as the ministers of our God. /  
You will eat the wealth of nations, /  
And you will boast in their glory.

## v7

Instead of your shame there will be a double portion, /  
And instead of humiliation they will give a ringing shout for their portion. /  
Therefore in their land they will possess double; /  
Eternal joy will be theirs.

## v8

For I, Jehovah, love justice; /  
I hate robbery with wrong; /  
And I will faithfully give them their recompense, /  
And I will make an eternal covenant with them.

## v9

Then their seed will be known among the nations, /  
And their offspring in the midst of the peoples; /  
All who see them will acknowledge them, /  
That they are the seed whom Jehovah has blessed.

## v10

I will rejoice greatly in Jehovah, /  
My soul will exult in my God; /  
For He has clothed me with the garments of salvation, /  
He has wrapped me with the robe of righteousness; /  
I am like a bridegroom who wears the headdress of the priest, /  
And like a bride who adorns herself with her jewels.

## v11

For as the earth brings forth its sprout, /  
And as the garden causes the things sown in it to spring up, /  
So will the Lord Jehovah cause righteousness and praise /  
To spring up before all the nations.
