## v1

For Zion’s sake I will not keep silent, /  
And for Jerusalem’s sake I will not be quiet, /  
Until her righteousness goes forth like brightness, /  
And her salvation like a burning torch.

## v2

And the nations will see your righteousness, /  
And all the kings, your glory; /  
And you will be called by a new name, /  
Which the mouth of Jehovah will designate.

## v3

You will also be a crown of beauty in the hand of Jehovah, /  
And a royal turban in the palm of your God.

## v4

And it will no longer be said to you, Forsaken! /  
Nor to your land will it be said any longer, Desolate! /  
But you will be called, My delight is in her, /  
And your land, Married; /  
For Jehovah delights in you, /  
And your land will be married.

## v5

For as a young man marries a virgin, /  
Your sons will marry you; /  
And with the joy of the bridegroom over the bride /  
Your God will rejoice over you.

## v6

Upon your walls, O Jerusalem, /  
I have appointed watchmen; /  
All day and all night /  
They will never keep silent. /  
You who remind Jehovah, /  
Do not be dumb;

## v7

And do not give Him quiet /  
Until He establishes /  
And until He makes Jerusalem /  
A praise in the earth.

## v8

Jehovah has sworn by His right hand /  
And by His strong arm: /  
Surely I will never again give your grain /  
As food for your enemies, /  
And surely the children of the foreigner will not drink your new wine /  
For which you have labored;

## v9

But they who have gathered it will eat it, /  
And they will praise Jehovah, /  
And they who have collected it will drink it /  
In the courts of My holiness.

## v10

Go through, go through the gates; /  
Prepare the way for the people; /  
Cast up, cast up the highway; /  
Free it of stones; /  
Raise up a standard for the peoples.

## v11

See, Jehovah has announced /  
To the end of the earth: /  
Say to the daughter of Zion, /  
Your salvation is now coming; /  
Now His reward is with Him /  
And His recompense before Him.

## v12

And they will call them, The holy people, /  
The redeemed of Jehovah; /  
And you will be called, Sought after! /  
A city not forsaken!
