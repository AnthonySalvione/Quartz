# Isaiah-50 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Isaiah-50|Isaiah 50]]

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — me

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-50#v4|Isaiah 50:4]]

Referring to Isaiah, who typifies Christ as the Servant of Jehovah (see [[Isaiah-49#v1|49:1-5]]). [[Isaiah-50#v4|Verses 4-5]] speak of the instruction received by Christ as Jehovah’s Servant.

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-50#v6|Isaiah 50:6]]

[[Isaiah-50#v6|Verses 6-9]] show the life lived by Isaiah as the servant of Jehovah, typifying the life that Christ as the Servant of Jehovah lived on earth ([[Matthew-26#v67|Matt. 26:67]]; [[1 Peter-02#v23|1 Pet. 2:23]]).
