### Footnote 10.1 — God

**Verse:** [[Isaiah-17#v10|Isaiah 17:10]]

As the eternal Rock of our stronghold, Christ is the God of our salvation (see [note 2¹](<Isaiah-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — God>) in [[Isaiah-12|Isaiah 12]]), in whom we should trust. He is also the Rock of Israel for His people to contact Him as their joy ([[Isaiah-30#v29|30:29]]).
