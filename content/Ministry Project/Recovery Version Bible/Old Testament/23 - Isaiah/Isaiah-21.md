## v1

The burden concerning the wilderness of the sea:  
Like storm winds  
In the Negev passing through,  
It comes from the wilderness  
From an awesome land.

## v2

A harsh vision  
Has been announced to me;  
The unfaithful one deals unfaithfully,  
And the destroyer destroys.  
Go up, Elam;  
Lay siege, Media;  
I have put an end  
To all her groaning.

## v3

Therefore my loins  
Are full of anguish;  
Pangs have taken hold of me  
Like the pangs of a woman in labor.  
I am bent over at the hearing of it;  
I am terrified at the seeing of it.

## v4

My heart reels;  
Shuddering overwhelms me.  
The twilight that I love  
Has become a source of trembling to me.

## v5

Setting the table!  
Spreading out the rug!  
Eating! Drinking!  
Rise, princes;  
Oil the shields.

## v6

For thus says  
The Lord to me,  
Go, set a watchman;  
Let him report what he sees.

## v7

And he saw riders,  
A team of horsemen;  
Riders of donkeys,  
Riders of camels;  
And he attends with attention,  
With much attention.

## v8

And he calls out like a lion:  
On the watchtower, Lord,  
I stand continually by day;  
At my guardpost  
I am stationed  
Every night.

## v9

And now there come  
Riding men,  
A team of horsemen.  
And one answers and says,  
Fallen, fallen is Babylon!  
And all the idols of her gods  
Has He shattered to the ground.

## v10

My threshed ones,  
And the son of the threshing floor!  
What I have heard  
From Jehovah of hosts,  
The God of Israel,  
I have declared to you.

## v11

The burden concerning Dumah:  
Someone is calling to me from Seir:  
Guard, what remains of the night?  
Guard, what remains of the night?

## v12

The guard says,  
Morning is coming,  
But so also is night;  
If you will ask, ask;  
Return, come.

## v13

The burden against Arabia:  
In the thicket of Arabia you must spend the night,  
You nomad clans of the Dedanim.

## v14

They bring water  
To meet the thirsty;  
The inhabitants of the land of Tema  
Meet with bread those who flee;

## v15

For they flee from swords —  
From the drawn sword,  
From the bent bow,  
And from the harshness of battle.

## v16

For thus has the Lord said to me,  
Within a year, like a wage worker’s year,  
All the glory of Kedar will end,

## v17

And the remainder of the number of the archers,  
The mighty men of the sons of Kedar,  
Will be few;  
For Jehovah the God of Israel has spoken.
