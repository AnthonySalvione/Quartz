## v1

Draw near, O nations, to hear;  
And listen, O peoples!  
Let the earth and all that fills it hear,  
The world and all that springs forth from it.

## v2

For Jehovah’s indignation is upon all the nations,  
And His wrath is upon all their armies;  
He has utterly destroyed them;  
He has delivered them unto the slaughter.

## v3

Thus their slain are cast away,  
And from their corpses their stench will come up;  
And the mountains will be melted with their blood.

## v4

And all the armies of heaven will be dissolved,  
And the heavens will be rolled up like a scroll,  
And all their host will wither away,  
As the leaf withers from the vine,  
Or like a leaf withering from the fig tree.

## v5

For My sword has drunk its fill in heaven.  
Now it will descend in judgment upon Edom  
And upon the people whom I have devoted to judgment.

## v6

Jehovah’s sword is full of blood;  
It has fattened itself with fat,  
With the blood of lambs and goats,  
With the fat of the kidneys of rams;  
For Jehovah has a sacrifice in Bozrah  
And a great slaughter in the land of Edom.

## v7

Wild oxen will also go down with them,  
The young bulls with the bulls;  
And their land will drink its fill of their blood,  
And their dust will become fat with their fat.

## v8

For Jehovah has a day of vengeance,  
A year of recompense for Zion’s contention.

## v9

And its streams will be turned into pitch,  
And its dust into brimstone;  
And its land will become burning pitch.

## v10

It will not be quenched by night or by day;  
Its smoke will go up forever;  
It will be desolate from generation to generation;  
No one will pass through it forever and ever.

## v11

But the pelican and the porcupine will inherit it,  
And the owl and the raven will dwell in it;  
And He will stretch over it  
The line of nothingness and the plummet weights of emptiness.

## v12

As for its nobles, there is no one there  
Whom they may call to assume the kingdom,  
And all its princes will be nothing.

## v13

And thorns will come up in its citadels,  
Nettles and thistles in its fortified cities;  
And it will be a habitation for jackals,  
And an abode for ostriches.

## v14

Desert animals will meet with hyenas;  
And the demon will call to his kind;  
Indeed there Lilith will settle  
And find a resting place for herself.

## v15

There the owl will make its nest and lay eggs,  
And hatch them and gather them under its protection;  
Indeed there the vultures will be gathered together,  
Each with its kind.

## v16

Seek from the book of Jehovah and read.  
Not one of these will be missing;  
Not one will lack her mate;  
For His mouth has commanded it,  
And it is His Spirit who has gathered them.

## v17

And He has cast the lot for them,  
And His hand has divided it by line unto them;  
They will inherit it forever;  
From generation to generation they will dwell in it.
