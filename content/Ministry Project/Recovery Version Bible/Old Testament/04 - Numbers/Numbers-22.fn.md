# Numbers-22 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Numbers-22|Numbers 22]]

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — Moab

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v4|Numbers 22:4]]

Moab was a son born of Lot and one of his daughters through incest ([[Genesis-19#v30|Gen. 19:30-38]]). Hence, Moab represents the fruit of fleshly lust. Midian was very close to the children of Ishmael, who represents the flesh, in contrast to Isaac, who represents that which is born of the Spirit (cf. [[Galatians-04#v22|Gal. 4:22-23]], [[Galatians-04#v28|28-29]]). Thus, Midian also signifies the flesh.

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — Balaam

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v5|Numbers 22:5]]

Balaam was a Gentile prophet of God (see note 153 in [[2 Peter-02|2 Pet. 2]]). After Israel defeated the three kings on the east of the Jordan ([[Numbers-21#v1|21:1-3]], [[Numbers-21#v21|21-35]]), Satan changed his mode of fighting against Israel and took a religious way.

### Footnote 5.2 — River

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v5|Numbers 22:5]]

I.e., the Euphrates.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — Jehovah

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v8|Numbers 22:8]]

Balaam’s asking God whether he should go with the messengers or not was self-deception. If we seek the Lord’s will but are actually inclined to carry out our own desires, we may deceive ourselves, even as Balaam deceived himself. We should seek the Lord’s will without having any other desire but to know His will and to do it.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — go

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v20|Numbers 22:20]]

Because Balaam wanted to do so, God allowed him to go with the rulers of Moab. It was Balaam’s will to go, not God’s (cf. [[Numbers-22#v22|v. 22]]).

## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — Angel

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v22|Numbers 22:22]]

The Angel of Jehovah here was Jehovah Himself (see note 21 in [[Exodus-03|Exo. 3]]), who came in to deal directly with this greedy prophet ([[2 Peter-02#v15|2 Pet. 2:15]]).

## Footnotes for verse 41

### Footnote 41.1 — Bamoth-baal

**Verse:** [[Numbers-22#v41|Numbers 22:41]]

Or, the high places of Baal. These were the places where people worshipped the idol of Baal.
