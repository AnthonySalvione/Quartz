# Numbers-04 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Numbers-04|Numbers 4]]

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — thirty

**Verse:** [[Numbers-04#v3|Numbers 4:3]]

To serve as a Levite, one had to be between thirty and fifty years old ([[Numbers-04#v3|vv. 3]], [[Numbers-04#v23|23]], [[Numbers-04#v30|30]], [[Numbers-04#v35|35]], [[Numbers-04#v43|43]], [[Numbers-04#v47|47]]). Likewise, the Lord Jesus did not reach the full age for God’s service until He was thirty ([[Luke-03#v23|Luke 3:23]]). To be counted in the service to God, one had to be one month old and above—those at that age needed to grow and mature ([[Numbers-03#v39|3:39]], [[Numbers-03#v43|43]]). To be counted in the warfare for God’s testimony, one had to be twenty years old or above—those at that age were mature and strong, without limit ([[Numbers-01#v3|1:3]]). To be counted in the service that was close to God, one had to be between thirty and fifty years old—those at that age were more mature and were stronger, without deterioration.

### Footnote 3.2 — service

**Verse:** [[Numbers-04#v3|Numbers 4:3]]

See [note 3²](<Numbers-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — for>) in [[Numbers-01|Numbers 1]]. So also in [[Numbers-01#v23|vv. 23]], [[Numbers-01#v30|30]], [[Numbers-01#v35|35]], [[Numbers-01#v39|39]], and 43. The holy service of the priests and the Levites was considered a warfare. Likewise, the service of the New Testament priests of the gospel of God ([[Romans-01#v1|Rom. 1:1]]; [[Romans-15#v16|15:16]]) for the building up of the Body of Christ is a warfare ([[2 Corinthians-10#v3|2 Cor. 10:3-5]]; [[Ephesians-06#v12|Eph. 6:12]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v18|1 Tim. 1:18]]; [[2 TImothy-04#v7|2 Tim. 4:7a]]).

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — sets

**Verse:** [[Numbers-04#v5|Numbers 4:5]]

The holy service of the priests and the Levites was for God’s move. The tabernacle moved on the shoulders of the descendants of the three sons of Levi. This moving was in a very good sequence, with no confusion. In the same manner Christ, the embodiment of God for God’s expansion, moves through those who love Him (cf. [[Acts-01#v8|Acts 1:8]]; [[Acts-13#v1|13:1-4]]).

## Footnotes for verse 19

### Footnote 19.1 — assign

**Verse:** [[Numbers-04#v19|Numbers 4:19]]

In addition to carrying out their own duties in the setting out of the tabernacle ([[Numbers-04#v5|vv. 5-14]]), the priests appointed the Levites to do their service ([[Numbers-04#v19|vv. 19]], [[Numbers-04#v27|27-28]], [[Numbers-04#v33|33]]). In the Old Testament there was a distinction between the priests and the Levites. However, in the New Testament there is only one class, that of the priests ([[Romans-15#v16|Rom. 15:16]]; [[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]], [[1 Peter-02#v9|9]]; [[Revelation-01#v6|Rev. 1:6]]; [[Revelation-05#v10|5:10]]), which includes the Levites. Hence, what the Levites did in the Old Testament in type, the believers as the New Testament priests should also do in reality. The fact that the service of the Levites was under the supervision of the priests indicates that when the New Testament priests do the outward Levitical work, they must do it under the supervision of the inward, spiritual view of the New Testament priesthood. The Levitical service should never be separated from the priestly view. The outward service must become a spiritual activity that ministers life to others.
