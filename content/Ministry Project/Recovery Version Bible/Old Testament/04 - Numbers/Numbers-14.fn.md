## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — How

**Verse:** [[Numbers-14#v11|Numbers 14:11]]

As indicated by the evil report of the ten spies ([[Numbers-13#v31|13:31-33]]) and the murmuring of the people against Moses and Aaron ([[Numbers-14#v1|14:1-4]]), the children of Israel did not care for God but cared only for themselves. In everything and in every way they were for themselves, not for God’s interests. Because of this, they did not believe in God, and they offended God to such an extent that they became abhorrent to Him. Their situation brought in God’s judgment and punishment. God alone is the source of faith. If we would have faith, we must learn to care for God’s interests and not for our benefit.

## Footnotes for verse 13

Moses prayed for the people not according to his concept but according to God’s word ([[Numbers-14#v17|vv. 17-18]]). His prayer bound God according to what He had spoken and what He is.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — pardoned

**Verse:** [[Numbers-14#v20|Numbers 14:20]]

God pardoned the people according to Moses’ prayer; nevertheless, their unbelief had certain negative consequences ([[Numbers-14#v21|vv. 21-38]]). Cf. [[Hebrews-03#v7|Heb. 3:7-19]]. God forgave the sinning Israelites yet still punished them. This shows that God’s forgiveness is of different kinds. One kind is forgiveness through punishment. Cf. notes [32²](<Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 32.2 — age>) in [[Matthew-12|Matt. 12]] and [34¹](<Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 34.1 — delivered>) in [[Matthew-18|Matt. 18]].

## Footnotes for verse 43

### Footnote 43.1 — turned

**Verse:** [[Numbers-14#v43|Numbers 14:43]]

Before this time the children of Israel had followed the Ark, a type of the Triune God in His incarnation embodied in Christ, but from this point they gave God up and would not follow Him any longer.

## Footnotes for verse 44

### Footnote 44.1 — Ark

**Verse:** [[Numbers-14#v44|Numbers 14:44]]

The people were to move only when the Ark moved ([[Numbers-10#v33|10:33]]); but when God asked them to move, they did not. Now they presumed to move by themselves.

## Footnotes for verse 45

### Footnote 45.1 — Canaanites

**Verse:** [[Numbers-14#v45|Numbers 14:45]]

In biblical typology, the Amalekites signify the flesh, the fallen, fleshly people (see [[Exodus-17#v8|Exo. 17:8]] and notes). The Canaanites signify human beings who have joined themselves to the evil spirits, to the satanic power of darkness in the air.
