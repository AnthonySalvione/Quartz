# Numbers-33 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Numbers-33|Numbers 33]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — journeys

**Verse:** [[Numbers-33#v1|Numbers 33:1]]

The forty-two stations in this chapter typify forty-two generations, from Abraham to Christ ([[Matthew-01#v17|Matt. 1:17]] and [note 1](<Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — all>), par. 3). The goal of the forty-two stations was the good land, and the goal of the forty-two generations was Christ. The correspondence here indicates that God’s intention is to bring all His people into the good land, which typifies Christ (see [note 7¹](<Deuteronomy-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — good>) in [[Deuteronomy-08|Deut. 8]]), as the goal. In the Old Testament God’s people journeyed and then arrived at the goal, but in the New Testament we, the believers, having been baptized into Christ ([[Romans-06#v3|Rom. 6:3]]; [[Galatians-03#v27|Gal. 3:27]]), are already in the goal ([[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]; [[Colossians-02#v6|Col. 2:6]] and notes).

## Footnotes for verse 52

### Footnote 52.1 — drive

**Verse:** [[Numbers-33#v52|Numbers 33:52]]

God has ordained Christ to be our portion for our enjoyment ([[Colossians-01#v12|Col. 1:12]]), but we must cooperate with God’s ordination by driving out everything within us that is other than God and Christ. We must destroy all the idols within us and not leave any ground within us for the worship of idols ([[1 John-05#v21|1 John 5:21]] and [note 3](<1 John-05.fn#Footnote 21.3 — idols>), par. 1). Only then can we have the genuine enjoyment of Christ. See notes [23¹](<Exodus-23.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Amorites>) and [31³](<Exodus-23.fn#Footnote 31.3 — you>) in [[Exodus-23|Exo. 23]].
