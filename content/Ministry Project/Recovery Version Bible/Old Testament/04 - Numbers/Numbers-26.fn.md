# Numbers-26 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Numbers-26|Numbers 26]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — sum

**Verse:** [[Numbers-26#v2|Numbers 26:2]]

The renumbering of God’s people in this chapter took place immediately after the purification by the plague on the fornicators and idolaters ([[Numbers-25#v9|25:9]]). The first numbering had taken place in the wilderness of Sinai ([[Numbers-01#v1|1:1-2]]); the renumbering took place in the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho ([[Numbers-26#v3|vv. 3]], [[Numbers-26#v63|63]]), i.e., at the entrance to the good land. The first numbering was for the formation of the army ([[Numbers-01#v3|1:3]]); the renumbering was for the inheriting of the land, signifying the enjoyment of Christ, through fighting ([[Numbers-26#v53|v. 53]]).

## Footnotes for verse 55

### Footnote 55.1 — lot

**Verse:** [[Numbers-26#v55|Numbers 26:55]]

The distributing of the land by lot ([[Numbers-26#v55|vv. 55-56]]) was according to the blessing under God’s sovereignty (see [note 2¹](<Joshua-14.fn#Footnote 2.1 — lot>) in [[Joshua-14|Josh. 14]]), whereas the distributing of the land according to the number of people ([[Numbers-26#v53|vv. 53-54]]) was a matter of human responsibility. Therefore, the distribution of the land depended both on human responsibility and on divine sovereignty.
