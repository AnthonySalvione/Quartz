# Numbers-23 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Numbers-23|Numbers 23]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — offered

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v2|Numbers 23:2]]

It was not clear to whom Balak and Balaam offered—to Jehovah or to Baal ([[Numbers-22#v41|22:41]] and [note](<Numbers-22.fn#Footnote 41.1 — Bamoth-baal>)). Balaam worshipped in God’s way, but he mixed the worship of God with the worship of an idol. This kind of mixture in worship can be found in Roman Catholicism.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — parable

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v7|Numbers 23:7]]

Or, couplet. Balaam’s parable in [[Numbers-23#v7|vv. 7-10]] was actually a God-inspired prophecy.

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — alone

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v9|Numbers 23:9]]

This word indicates that the children of Israel were a holy, sanctified people, a people set apart from the nations. The same is true of the church ([[1 Corinthians-03#v17|1 Cor. 3:17]]; [[Ephesians-01#v4|Eph. 1:4]]; [[Ephesians-05#v27|5:27]]; [[1 Peter-02#v9|1 Pet. 2:9]]).

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — dust

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v10|Numbers 23:10]]

Balaam’s word in [[Numbers-23#v10|v. 10a]] was a blessing of abundant increase, and his word in [[Numbers-23#v10|v. 10b]] was a word of great blessing and appreciation.

### Footnote 10.2 — me

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v10|Numbers 23:10]]

Lit., my soul.

## Footnotes for verse 19

### Footnote 19.1 — lie

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v19|Numbers 23:19]]

Or, fail.

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — iniquity

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v21|Numbers 23:21]]

This word was spoken not according to the human view but according to the divine view. In the sight of God, Israel is without fault. In themselves God’s people have many defects, but in God’s redemption and in Christ they have no defects. When God looks at His people, He does not see them according to what they are in themselves but according to what they are in Christ (cf. [[2 Corinthians-05#v16|2 Cor. 5:16-17]]).

### Footnote 21.2 — king

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v21|Numbers 23:21]]

Ultimately, this king refers to Christ.

## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — He

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v22|Numbers 23:22]]

Referring to Israel. Alternatively, the text can be translated, He is for them like the horns of a wild ox. So also in [[Numbers-24#v8|24:8]].

### Footnote 22.2 — horns

**Verse:** [[Numbers-23#v22|Numbers 23:22]]

The reference to the horns of a wild ox and the reference to the lion and the lioness in [[Numbers-23#v24|v. 24]] indicate that the church is victorious ([[Romans-08#v37|Rom. 8:37]]; [[Romans-16#v20|16:20]]; [[Revelation-12#v11|Rev. 12:11]]).
