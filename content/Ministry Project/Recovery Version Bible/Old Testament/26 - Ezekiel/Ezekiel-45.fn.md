## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — wheat

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-45#v13|Ezekiel 45:13]]

The heave offering typifies the ascended Christ. Ezekiel mentions four items in the heave offering: wheat, barley, oil, and a lamb ([[Ezekiel-45#v13|vv. 13-15]]). Wheat signifies Christ incarnated to die for us ([[John-12#v24|John 12:24]]); barley, the first-ripe grain ([[2 Samuel-21#v9|2 Sam. 21:9]]), signifies Christ as the firstfruits of resurrection ([[1 Corinthians-15#v20|1 Cor. 15:20]]; [[Leviticus-23#v10|Lev. 23:10]]); oil signifies the Holy Spirit ([[Luke-04#v18|Luke 4:18]]); and the lamb signifies the redeeming Christ ([[John-01#v29|John 1:29]]). In our service to God we need to offer such a Christ as a heave offering to God.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — first

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-45#v18|Ezekiel 45:18]]

According to Ezekiel’s record there were the yearly offering ([[Ezekiel-45#v18|vv. 18-20]]), the monthly offering ([[Ezekiel-46#v6|46:6]]), the weekly offering ([[Ezekiel-46#v4|46:4]]), the daily offering ([[Ezekiel-46#v13|46:13]]), and the offerings at the appointed feasts—at the Feast of the Passover, the Feast of Unleavened Bread, and the Feast of Tabernacles ([[Ezekiel-45#v21|vv. 21]], [[Ezekiel-45#v25|25]]). The yearly offering was offered on the first day of the year, signifying that, in principle, every year we should have a new beginning ([[Ezekiel-45#v18|vv. 18-19]]).
