## v1

The word of Jehovah came again to me, saying,

## v2

Now you, son of man, take up a lamentation over Tyre;

## v3

And say to Tyre, O you who dwell at the entry to the sea, merchant of the peoples to many coastlands, Thus says the Lord Jehovah,  
You, O Tyre, have said, /  
I am perfect in beauty.

## v4

Your borders are in the heart of the seas; /  
Your builders have perfected your beauty.

## v5

They have made all your planks of fir trees from Senir; /  
They have taken a cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for you.

## v6

They have made your oars of the oaks of Bashan; /  
They have made your boards of cypresses inlaid with ivory from the coasts of Kittim.

## v7

Your sail was of fine embroidered linen from Egypt, to be your ensign; /  
Your awning was of blue and purple material from the coasts of Elishah.

## v8

The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers; /  
Your skilled men, who were in you, O Tyre, were your helmsmen.

## v9

The elders of Gebal and her skilled men were in you caulking your seams. /  
All the ships of the sea and their sailors were within you to barter for your merchandise.

## v10

The men of Persia and Lydia and Put were in your army as your men of war. /  
They hung shields and helmets on you; they endowed you with your splendor.

## v11

The men of Arvad and Helek were on your walls all around, and valiant men were in your towers; they hung their shields on your walls all around; they perfected your beauty.

## v12

Tarshish conducted business with you because of the abundance of all your wealth. For your wares they gave silver, iron, tin, and lead.

## v13

Javan, Tubal, and Meshech, they were your traders; for your merchandise they gave human beings and vessels of bronze.

## v14

From Beth-togarmah they gave for your wares horses, steeds for riding, and mules.

## v15

The people of Dedan were your traders; many coastlands conducted business on your behalf; they brought back as payment to you ivory tusks and ebony.

## v16

Syria conducted business with you because of the abundance of your products; they gave for your wares carbuncles, purple and embroidered cloth, fine linen, coral, and rubies.

## v17

Judah and the land of Israel were your traders; they gave for your merchandise wheat of Minnith, resin, honey, oil, and balm.

## v18

Damascus conducted business with you for the abundance of your products, because of the abundance of all wealth. They gave the wine of Helbon, and white wool.

## v19

Vedan and Javan traded for your wares from Uzal. Among your merchandise were wrought iron, cassia, and calamus.

## v20

Dedan was your trader in precious clothes for riding.

## v21

Arabia and all the princes of Kedar conducted business on your behalf in lambs, rams, and goats; for these they conducted business with you.

## v22

The traders of Sheba and Raamah were your traders; they gave for your wares the best of all spices, all kinds of precious stones, and gold.

## v23

Haran and Canneh and Eden, the traders of Sheba, Asshur and Chilmad, were your traders.

## v24

These were your traders in the finest fabrics, in rolls of blue and embroidered cloth, and in chests of fine woven articles, tightly bound with cords, among your merchandise.

## v25

The ships of Tarshish transported your merchandise for you;  
And you were replenished and made very glorious /  
In the heart of the seas.

## v26

Those who rowed you have brought you into great waters, /  
But the east wind has shipwrecked you in the heart of the seas.

## v27

Your wealth, your wares, your merchandise, /  
Your sailors, your pilots, /  
Those who caulked your seams, /  
The dealers in your merchandise, /  
And all your men of war, who are in you, /  
With all your company who are in your midst, /  
Will sink into the heart of the seas /  
On the day of your fall.

## v28

At the sound of the cry of your pilots /  
The pasture lands will shake.

## v29

And all who handle the oar /  
Will come down out of their ships; /  
The sailors and all the pilots of the sea /  
Will stand upon the land,

## v30

And they will make their voice heard for you /  
And cry out bitterly, /  
And they will throw dust upon their heads; /  
They will wallow in ashes.

## v31

And they will make themselves bald for you /  
And gird themselves with sackcloth, /  
And they will weep for you in bitterness of soul /  
With bitter mourning.

## v32

And in their wailing they will take up a lamentation for you /  
And lament over you, saying, /  
Who is like Tyre, /  
Like her silenced in the midst of the sea?

## v33

When your wares went out over the seas, /  
You satisfied many peoples. /  
With the abundance of your wealth and your merchandise /  
You enriched the kings of the earth.

## v34

Now you are shipwrecked by the sea /  
In deep water, /  
Your merchandise and all your company /  
Go down in your midst.

## v35

All the inhabitants of the coasts /  
Are astonished at you, /  
And their kings shudder excessively; /  
Their countenance is troubled.

## v36

Those who conducted business with you among the peoples hiss at you; /  
You have become a source of terror, /  
And you will no longer exist.
