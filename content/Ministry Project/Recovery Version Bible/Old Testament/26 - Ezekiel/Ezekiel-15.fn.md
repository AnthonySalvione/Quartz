### Footnote 2.1 — vine

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-15#v2|Ezekiel 15:2]]

Israel was God’s vine for the accomplishing of His economy. In the Scriptures a vine signifies God’s people who are under His care and in union with Him ([[John-15#v1|John 15:1-8]]). This union issues in the flowing forth of the divine life in fruit-bearing, which produces wine to cheer both God and man ([[Judges-09#v13|Judg. 9:13]]). However, in its degradation Israel produced thorns and thistles ([[Ezekiel-02#v6|2:6]]; cf. [[Isaiah-05#v1|Isa. 5:1-7]]).
