# Ezekiel-34 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Ezekiel-34|Ezekiel 34]]

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v11|Ezekiel 34:11]]

In God’s recovery by life, God first sends the watchman to sound the trumpet to His people that they may repent, turn, and live ([[Ezekiel-33|Ezekiel 33]]); then He Himself appears as the Shepherd to search for and seek out His sheep. After John the Baptist sounded the trumpet of repentance ([[Matthew-03#v1|Matt. 3:1-2]]), the Lord Jesus came as the Shepherd ([[Matthew-09#v36|Matt. 9:36]]; [[Luke-15#v1|Luke 15:1-7]]; [[John-10#v11|John 10:11]]).

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — bring

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v13|Ezekiel 34:13]]

As the Shepherd, the Lord will gather His people, His sheep, out of the nations and bring them back to the land of Canaan, which typifies the all-inclusive Christ as the allotted portion of God’s people (see [note 7¹](<Deuteronomy-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — good>) in [[Deuteronomy-08|Deut. 8]]), to dwell on the high mountains ([[Ezekiel-34#v14|v. 14]]), signifying the resurrected and ascended Christ.

### Footnote 13.2 — streams

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v13|Ezekiel 34:13]]

The streams here signify the life-giving Spirit as the living water ([[John-07#v37|John 7:37-39]]), who is given to us by God as our spiritual drink ([[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13]]), and the rich pasture ([[Ezekiel-34#v14|v. 14]]) signifies Christ as the feeding place for God’s people ([[John-10#v9|John 10:9]]). By means of the Spirit as the streams, we enjoy Christ as our rich pasture ([[Psalm-23#v2|Psa. 23:2]]; cf. [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]]).

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — lie

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v14|Ezekiel 34:14]]

In His recovery by life, while the Lord feeds us and gives us something to drink, He also gives us rest and heals us ([[Ezekiel-34#v16|v. 16]]).

## Footnotes for verse 17

### Footnote 17.1 — judge

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v17|Ezekiel 34:17]]

When we experience the Lord’s recovery by life, as portrayed in [[Ezekiel-34#v13|vv. 13-16]], there will be righteous judgments among us, and all the unjust things will be cleared away ([[Ezekiel-34#v17|vv. 17-22]]).

## Footnotes for verse 23

### Footnote 23.1 — David

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v23|Ezekiel 34:23]]

Referring to Christ, who is the real David ([[Matthew-12#v3|Matt. 12:3]]), the real Shepherd of God’s flock ([[Psalm-23|Psa. 23]]; [[John-10#v11|John 10:11]]; [[Hebrews-13#v20|Heb. 13:20]]) and the King ([[Ezekiel-34#v24|v. 24]]) of God’s people ([[Isaiah-09#v7|Isa. 9:7]]; [[Hosea-03#v5|Hosea 3:5]]; [[Micah-05#v2|Micah 5:2]]; [[Luke-01#v32|Luke 1:32-33]]).

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — Prince

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v24|Ezekiel 34:24]]

When the Lord Jesus comes as the Shepherd to care for us, He comes also as the King to govern us. The issue of the Lord’s caring for us as our Shepherd is that we obey Him as our King and come under His kingship and His throne within us.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — covenant

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v25|Ezekiel 34:25]]

In God’s recovery by life, under God’s secure and unchanging covenant of peace, God’s recovered people enjoy peace, freedom from the disturbance of evil beasts (evil persons—[[Acts-20#v29|Acts 20:29]]), full liberty and release from every kind of yoke and slavery ([[Ezekiel-34#v27|v. 27]]), and security from their enemies ([[Ezekiel-34#v28|v. 28]]).

## Footnotes for verse 26

### Footnote 26.1 — blessing

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v26|Ezekiel 34:26]]

In God’s recovery by life, God’s recovered people not only receive His blessing but also become a source of blessing to others that they may be supplied. Under the showers of blessing in season, there will be an abundance of spiritual food not only for us to enjoy but also for us to supply to others ([[Ezekiel-34#v27|vv. 27a]], [[Ezekiel-34#v29|29]]).

## Footnotes for verse 30

### Footnote 30.1 — with

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-34#v30|Ezekiel 34:30]]

God’s recovered people have God’s presence, God is among them, and they are before God ([[Ezekiel-34#v30|vv. 30-31]]). This portrays the perfect fellowship with God, the fellowship in oneness, in the mingling of God and man, in which we are one with God and He is one with us.
