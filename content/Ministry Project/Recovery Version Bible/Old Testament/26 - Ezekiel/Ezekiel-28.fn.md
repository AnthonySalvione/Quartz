## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — king

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v12|Ezekiel 28:12]]

Similar to [[Isaiah-14#v12|Isa. 14:12-15]], which identify Lucifer (who became Satan) with Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, [[Ezekiel-28#v12|vv. 12-19]] of this chapter consider the king of Tyre a figure of Lucifer, giving further details concerning the origin of Satan. See notes in [[Isaiah-14#v12|Isa. 14:12-15]].

### Footnote 12.2 — perfection

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v12|Ezekiel 28:12]]

As God’s creature, Lucifer was full of wisdom and perfect in beauty, perfect in his ways from the day that he was created ([[Ezekiel-28#v15|v. 15a]]).

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — Eden

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v13|Ezekiel 28:13]]

This is not the Eden in [[Genesis-02|Gen. 2]], which was on earth, but the Eden as the garden of God on the holy mountain in the heavenlies ([[Ezekiel-28#v14|v. 14]]; [[Isaiah-14#v13|Isa. 14:13b]]).

### Footnote 13.2 — covering

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v13|Ezekiel 28:13]]

Lucifer was covered with precious stones, indicating his dwelling place (Pember).

### Footnote 13.3 — chrysolite

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v13|Ezekiel 28:13]]

Or, beryl.

### Footnote 13.4 — carbuncle

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v13|Ezekiel 28:13]]

Or, turquoise.

### Footnote 13.5 — tambourines

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v13|Ezekiel 28:13]]

In ancient times musical instruments such as tambourines and pipes were for kings ([[Daniel-03#v5|Dan. 3:5]]; [[Daniel-06#v18|6:18]]). This indicates that before his rebellion Satan was a king, holding the highest position in the preadamic universe (cf. [[Luke-04#v5|Luke 4:5-6]] and [[Luke-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — has|note 6¹]]; [[Jude-01#v9|Jude 9]] and [note 3](<Jude-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — did>)). Satan was the anointed cherub who covered the Ark ([[Ezekiel-28#v14|v. 14]]; [[Exodus-25#v18|Exo. 25:18-21]]), indicating that he was very close to God and bore God’s glory ([[Hebrews-09#v5|Heb. 9:5]]), and might have been the high priest who served God and led the universal worship of God ([[Revelation-04#v6|Rev. 4:6-11]]). Thus, Satan was both a king and a priest (cf. [[Revelation-04#v4|Rev. 4:4]] and [note 2](<Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — elders>)). Because of Satan’s failure, these two positions have been given to the believers in Christ ([[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]], [[1 Peter-02#v9|9]]; [[Revelation-01#v6|Rev. 1:6]]; [[Revelation-05#v10|5:10]]; [[Revelation-02#v26|2:26-27]]; [[Revelation-03#v21|3:21]]; [[Revelation-12#v5|12:5]]; [[Revelation-20#v6|20:6]]; [[Revelation-22#v3|22:3]], [[Revelation-22#v5|5]]).

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — Sidon

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-28#v21|Ezekiel 28:21]]

Sidon was a pricking briar and a painful thorn to the house of Israel ([[Ezekiel-28#v24|v. 24]]). Tyre (see [note 2¹](<Ezekiel-26.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Tyre>) in [[Ezekiel-26|Ezekiel 26]]) and Sidon are considered a pair, indicating that if the believers love the world and care for worldly riches, they will become briars and thorns that damage the church life, frustrating the growth of life in the church as God’s cultivated land ([[Matthew-13#v22|Matt. 13:22]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v9|1 Cor. 3:9]]).
