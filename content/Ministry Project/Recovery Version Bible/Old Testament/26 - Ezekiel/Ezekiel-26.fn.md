# Ezekiel-26 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Ezekiel-26|Ezekiel 26]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — eleventh

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-26#v1|Ezekiel 26:1]]

The vision in [[Ezekiel-26|Ezekiel 26]] was seen in the eleventh year, whereas the vision in [[Ezekiel-29|Ezekiel 29]] was seen in the tenth year. This indicates that Ezekiel’s record is not according to chronology but according to spiritual meaning. See [note 1¹](<Ezekiel-25.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Then>) in [[Ezekiel-25|Ezekiel 25]].

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — Tyre

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-26#v2|Ezekiel 26:2]]

Tyre typifies those who are seeking worldly wealth and do not care for God’s interests. Such persons are a damage to the church life ([[James-02#v6|James 2:6]]). See [note 21¹](<Ezekiel-28.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Sidon>) in [[Ezekiel-28|Ezekiel 28]].

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — Nebuchadrezzar

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-26#v7|Ezekiel 26:7]]

See [note 2¹](<Jeremiah-21.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Nebuchadrezzar>) in [[Jeremiah-21|Jer. 21]]. The Babylonian Empire under Nebuchadnezzar became God’s center for executing His judgment not only on His people Israel but also on the nations.
