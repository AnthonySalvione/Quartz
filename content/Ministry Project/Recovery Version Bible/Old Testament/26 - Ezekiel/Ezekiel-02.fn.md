# Ezekiel-02 — Footnotes

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — rebellious

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-02#v3|Ezekiel 2:3]]

Or, a rebellious nation. The children of Israel were God’s elect, separated from the nations unto God ([[Exodus-19#v6|Exo. 19:6]]), God’s vine for the accomplishing of His economy ([[Ezekiel-15#v1|15:1-8]]; [[Ezekiel-19#v10|19:10]]), God’s bride for His satisfaction ([[Ezekiel-16#v8|16:8]]; [[Ezekiel-23#v4|23:4]]), God’s flock under His care ([[Ezekiel-34|Ezekiel 34]]), and God’s precious and personal treasure ([[Exodus-19#v5|Exo. 19:5]]). However, in contrast with these five statuses, in their degradation they became rebels against God ([[Ezekiel-02#v3|v. 3]]), thistles and thorns ([[Ezekiel-02#v6|v. 6]]), a harlot ([[Ezekiel-06#v9|6:9]]; [[Ezekiel-16#v15|16:15]]; [[Ezekiel-23#v30|23:30]]), scorpions ([[Ezekiel-02#v6|v. 6]]), and dross ([[Ezekiel-22#v18|22:18]]). The causes of Israel’s degradation were (1) their having idols in their hearts ([[Ezekiel-14#v3|14:3-5]]), (2) their being associated with the world, i.e., the heathen nations ([[Ezekiel-25#v8|25:8]]; cf. [[1 Kings-11#v1|1 Kings 11:1-2]]; [[1 Kings-16#v30|16:30-31]]), and (3) their walking according to the manner and customs of the nations ([[Ezekiel-11#v12|11:12]]). These are also the main causes of the degradation of God’s New Testament elect, the church ([[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4]], [[Revelation-02#v12|12-15]]). 

God’s judgment on His people, as seen in [[Ezekiel-02|Ezekiel 2—24]], is based on His righteousness, holiness, and glory. God’s glory is versus idols ([[Ezekiel-08#v2|8:2-4]], [[Ezekiel-08#v10|10]]; [[Ezekiel-09#v3|9:3]]), God’s holiness is versus dross ([[Ezekiel-22#v17|22:17-22]]), and God’s righteousness is versus Israel’s injustice and oppression ([[Ezekiel-22#v29|22:29]]). Anything that does not match God’s glory, holiness, and righteousness will provoke God’s judgment. As seen in this book and in the New Testament, God first judges His people, and then He judges the nations ([[Ezekiel-25|Ezekiel 25—32]]; [[1 Peter-04#v17|1 Pet. 4:17]]; [[Revelation-02|Rev. 2—3]]; [[Revelation-06|6—11]]; [[Revelation-15|15—19]]).
