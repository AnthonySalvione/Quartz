## v1

Now in the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — thirtieth|thirtieth]] year, in the fourth month, on the fifth of the month, while I was among the captives by the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — river|river]] Chebar, the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — heavens|heavens]] were opened and I saw [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — visions|visions]] of God.

## v2

On the fifth of the month (it was the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — fifth|fifth]] year of King Jehoiachin’s captivity)

## v3

The [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — word|word]] of Jehovah came expressly to [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Ezekiel|Ezekiel]] the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — priest|priest]], the son of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Ezekiel|Buzi]], in the land of the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 3.4 — Chaldeans|Chaldeans]] by the river Chebar; and the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 3.5 — hand|hand]] of Jehovah was upon him there.

## v4

And I looked, and there came a storm [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — wind|wind]] from the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — north|north]], a great [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — cloud|cloud]] and a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 4.4 — fire|fire]] flashing incessantly; and there was a brightness around it, and from the midst of it there was something like the sight of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 4.5 — electrum|electrum]], from the midst of the fire.

## v5

And from the midst of it there came the likeness of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — four|four]] living creatures. And this was their appearance: They had the likeness of a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — man|man]].

## v6

And every one had four [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — faces|faces]], and every one of them had four [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — wings|wings]].

## v7

And their feet were straight feet, and the sole of their feet was like the sole of a calf’s [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — foot|foot]]; and they sparkled like the sight of burnished bronze.

## v8

And the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — hands|hands]] of a man were under their wings on their four sides. And the four of them had their faces and their wings thus:

## v9

Their wings were [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — joined|joined]] one to another; they did not turn as they went; each went [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — straight|straight]] forward.

## v10

As for the likeness of their faces, they had the face of a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — man|man]]; and the four of them had the face of a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — man|lion]] on the right side, and the four of them had the face of an [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — man|ox]] on the left side, and the four of them had the face of an [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — man|eagle]].

## v11

[[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — And|And]] thus their faces were. And their wings were spread out upward; two wings of each were [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — joined|joined]] one to another, and two [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — covered|covered]] their bodies.

## v12

And each went [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — straight|straight]] forward; wherever the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] was to go, they went; they did not turn as they went.

## v13

As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — coals|coals]] of fire, like the appearance of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — coals|torches]]; [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — the|the]] fire went to and fro among the living creatures, and the fire was [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — bright|bright]]; and out of the fire went forth [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 13.4 — lightning|lightning]].

## v14

And the living creatures [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — ran|ran]] to and fro like the appearance of a lightning bolt.

## v15

And as I watched the living creatures, I saw a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — wheel|wheel]] upon the earth beside the living creatures, for each of their four [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — faces|faces]].

## v16

The appearance of the wheels and their workmanship were like the sight of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — beryl|beryl]]. And the four of them had [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — one|one]] likeness; that is, their appearance and their workmanship were as it were [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — a|a]] wheel within a wheel.

## v17

Whenever they went, they went in their [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — four|four]] directions; they did not turn as they went.

## v18

As for their rims, they were [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — high|high]] and they were [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — high|awesome]]; and the rims of the four of them were full of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — eyes|eyes]] all around.

## v19

And whenever the living creatures [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — went|went]], the wheels went beside them; and whenever the living creatures were lifted up above the earth, the wheels were lifted up also.

## v20

Wherever the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] was to go, [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — they|they]] went — wherever the Spirit was to go. And the wheels were lifted up alongside [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — they|them]], for the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of the living creature was in the wheels.

## v21

Whenever those went, these went; and whenever those stood still, these stood still; and whenever those were lifted up above the earth, the wheels were lifted up alongside them; for the Spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.

## v22

And over the heads of the living creature there was the likeness of an [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — expanse|expanse]], like the sight of awesome crystal, stretched forth over their heads above.

## v23

And under the expanse one pair of their wings went [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — straight|straight]] out, one toward the other; each had another pair [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 23.2 — covering|covering]] their bodies on this side and on that side.

## v24

And I heard the sound of their wings, like the sound of great waters, like the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — voice|voice]] of the Almighty, as they went; it was the sound of a tumult, like the sound of an army. Whenever they stood still, they dropped their wings.

## v25

And there was a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — voice|voice]] from above the expanse that was over their heads. When they stood still, they dropped their wings.

## v26

And above the expanse that was over their heads was the likeness of a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — throne|throne]], like the appearance of a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 26.2 — sapphire|sapphire]] stone; and upon the likeness of the throne was One in appearance like a [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 26.3 — man|man]], above it.

## v27

Then I saw something like the sight of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — electrum|electrum]], like the appearance of fire encased all around, from the appearance of His loins and upward; and from the appearance of His loins and downward, I saw something like the appearance of [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — electrum|fire]]. And there was brightness all around Him.

## v28

Like the appearance of the [[Ezekiel-01.fn#Footnote 28.1 — rainbow|rainbow]] that is in the cloud on a day of rain, such was the appearance of the brightness all around. This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of Jehovah. And when I saw it, I fell on my face and I heard the voice of someone speaking.
