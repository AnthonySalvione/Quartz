# Ezekiel-38 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Ezekiel-38|Ezekiel 38]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — Gog

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-38#v2|Ezekiel 38:2]]

What is mentioned in [[Ezekiel-38|Ezekiel 38—39]] regarding Gog and Magog probably corresponds to what is written in [[Revelation-14#v19|Rev. 14:19-20]]; [[Revelation-16#v12|16:12-21]]; and 19:11-18 concerning the war at Armageddon. This may indicate that Gog and Magog will take the lead among the nations who seek to destroy Israel at the end of this age. See [note 8¹](<Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Gog>) in [[Revelation-20|Rev. 20]].
