# Ezekiel-21 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Ezekiel-21|Ezekiel 21]]

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — scepter

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v10|Ezekiel 21:10]]

The scepter here refers to the power and authority of Christ, the Son of God ([[Genesis-49#v10|Gen. 49:10]] and [note 1](<Genesis-49.fn#Footnote 10.1 — scepter>)). See [note 17¹](<Ezekiel-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — watchman>) in [[Ezekiel-03|Ezekiel 3]].

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — strike

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v12|Ezekiel 21:12]]

“A sign of alarm and horror ([[Jeremiah-31#v19|Jer. 31:19]])” (Keil and Delitszch).

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — slaughtering

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v15|Ezekiel 21:15]]

Others translate, glittering.

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — teraphim

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v21|Ezekiel 21:21]]

I.e., household idols.

### Footnote 21.2 — observes

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v21|Ezekiel 21:21]]

A Babylonian practice of divination by which guidance was sought by reading the condition of the liver of a sacrificed animal.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — iniquity

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v25|Ezekiel 21:25]]

Or, punishment of the iniquity. So also in [[Ezekiel-21#v29|v. 29]].

## Footnotes for verse 27

### Footnote 27.1 — He

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-21#v27|Ezekiel 21:27]]

Referring to Christ as the One who has the right to inherit the kingdom of Israel ([[Luke-01#v32|Luke 1:32-33]]). At that time God was overturning the kingdom and the nation of Israel, but eventually Christ, the One who has the right to inherit the kingdom of Israel, will come. See [note 17¹](<Ezekiel-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — watchman>) in [[Ezekiel-03|Ezekiel 3]].
