# Ezekiel-14 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Ezekiel-14|Ezekiel 14]]

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — set

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-14#v3|Ezekiel 14:3]]

An idol in our heart is anything within us that we love more than the Lord and that replaces the Lord in our life ([[1 John-05#v21|1 John 5:21]] and [note 3](<1 John-05.fn#Footnote 21.3 — idols>), par. 1). Those who set up idols in their hearts are estranged from the Lord through their idols ([[Ezekiel-14#v5|v. 5]]). All who have idols within them yet seek God in an outward way cannot find Him ([[Ezekiel-14#v3|v. 3]]; cf. [[Jeremiah-29#v13|Jer. 29:13]]).

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — four

**Verse:** [[Ezekiel-14#v21|Ezekiel 14:21]]

The four means, given here and in [[Ezekiel-05#v17|5:17]], by which God exercised His judgment on His Old Testament people typify the means used by God to judge His degraded New Testament elect, the church ([[1 Peter-04#v17|1 Pet. 4:17]]): dissension (sword), a lack of spiritual food (famine), spiritual diseases (pestilence), and evil persons (wild beasts—cf. [[Acts-20#v29|Acts 20:29]]). The results of God’s judgment on Israel, His degraded elect, were (1) the loss of the good land ([[Ezekiel-14#v15|v. 15]]; [[Ezekiel-07#v21|7:21]]; [[2 Chronicles-36#v9|2 Chron. 36:9-10]], [[2 Chronicles-36#v17|17]], [[2 Chronicles-36#v20|20]]; [[Jeremiah-52#v15|Jer. 52:15]]), typifying the loss of the enjoyment of Christ ([[Galatians-05#v2|Gal. 5:2-4]]); (2) the departing of the glory of the Lord ([[Ezekiel-09#v3|9:3]]; [[Ezekiel-11#v22|11:22-23]]), signifying the departing of God’s manifestation from the church ([[Revelation-02#v5|Rev. 2:5]]; cf. [[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15-16]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v25|1 Cor. 14:25]]); and (3) the destruction of the temple and the burning of the city ([[Ezekiel-33#v21|33:21]]; [[2 Chronicles-36#v19|2 Chron. 36:19]]; [[Jeremiah-52#v12|Jer. 52:12-14]]), signifying the loss of God’s dwelling place and God’s kingdom from the church (cf. [[Ephesians-02#v20|Eph. 2:20-22]]; [[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]).
