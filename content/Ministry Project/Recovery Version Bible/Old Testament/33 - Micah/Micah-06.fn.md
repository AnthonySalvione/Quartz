# Micah-06 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Micah-06|Micah 6]]

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — firstborn

**Verse:** [[Micah-06#v7|Micah 6:7]]

In [[Micah-06#v6|vv. 6-8]] Jehovah contended with Israel concerning their idol worship and their offering sacrifices to idols. God would never require anyone to offer his children as a sacrifice, but the people of Israel actually did this in order to fulfill the requirements of their idols.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — But

**Verse:** [[Micah-06#v8|Micah 6:8]]

The prophets are great in their speaking concerning Christ but not in their speaking concerning other things. Micah’s word here concerning what Jehovah requires of His people is not a matter of God’s revelation but a matter of the prophet’s concept (cf. [note 1¹](<Psalm-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Blessed>), par. 2, in [[Psalm-01|Psa. 1]]). The divine concept according to the divine revelation in the New Testament is that Christ has replaced the law ([[Romans-10#v4|Rom. 10:4]]), and God’s people should live Christ rather than keep the law ([[Galatians-02#v19|Gal. 2:19-20]]; [[Philippians-01#v20|Phil. 1:20-21a]]).
