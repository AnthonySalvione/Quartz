## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — Pardoning

**Verse:** [[Micah-07#v18|Micah 7:18]]

God’s pardoning our iniquity and passing over our transgression, treading our iniquities underfoot and casting all our sins into the depths of the sea ([[Micah-07#v18|vv. 18-19]]), reveal how willing God is to forgive our iniquities (cf. [[Psalm-103#v12|Psa. 103:12]]; [[Isaiah-01#v18|Isa. 1:18]]; [[Hebrews-08#v12|Heb. 8:12]]; [[1 John-01#v9|1 John 1:9]]). Micah’s concluding praise, centering not on the virtues of God’s people but on the attributes of God, is a word of comfort.
