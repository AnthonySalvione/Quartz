## v1

Give ear, O heaven, and let me speak;  
And let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

## v2

Let my teaching drop like the rain;  
Let my speech distill like the dew,  
Like raindrops upon tender grass,  
And like abundant showers upon herbage.

## v3

For the name of Jehovah will I declare.  
Ascribe greatness to our God!

## v4

The Rock — perfect is His work,  
For all His ways are justice:  
A God of faithfulness and without injustice;  
Righteous and upright is He.

## v5

Corruptly have they dealt with Him — their blemish is not His sons —  
A twisted and crooked generation are they.

## v6

Do you repay Jehovah with this,  
Foolish and unwise people?  
Is He not your Father who bought you?  
Was it not He who made you and established you?

## v7

Remember the days of long ago;  
Consider the years of generation upon generation;  
Ask your father, and he will inform you;  
Your elders, and they will tell you.

## v8

When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance,  
When He divided the sons of man,  
He set the borders of the peoples  
According to the number of Israel’s children.

## v9

For Jehovah’s portion is His people;  
Jacob is the allotment of His inheritance.

## v10

He found him in a land of wilderness,  
And in a howling desert waste;  
He encircled him, cared for him with all attention;  
He guarded him like the pupil of His eye.

## v11

As the eagle rouses his nest,  
Hovers over his young,  
Spreads his wings, takes them,  
And bears them up upon his pinions;

## v12

So Jehovah alone led him,  
And there was no strange god with Him.

## v13

He made him ride on the high places of the earth,  
And he ate the produce of the field;  
And He made him suck honey out of a crag,  
And oil out of flint rock:

## v14

Curd of cows and milk of sheep,  
With fat of lambs,  
And rams, the offspring of Bashan, and goats,  
With the choicest wheat;  
And the blood of the grape you drank as fermenting wine.

## v15

But Jeshurun grew fat and kicked —  
You have become fat, you have grown thick, you have gorged yourself —  
And forsook God, who made him,  
And treated the Rock of his salvation disdainfully.

## v16

They made Him jealous with strange gods;  
With abominations they provoked Him to anger.

## v17

They sacrificed to demons, to those who were no god,  
To gods they did not know,  
To new ones who had recently come up,  
Before whom your fathers had not shuddered.

## v18

You have neglected the Rock who begot you  
And have forgotten the God who travailed with you.

## v19

And Jehovah saw and held them in contempt,  
Because of anger provoked by His sons and daughters.

## v20

And He said, I will hide My face from them;  
I will see what their latter end will be.  
For they are a generation of perversions,  
Children in whom is no faithfulness.

## v21

They have made Me jealous with a non-God;  
They have provoked Me to anger with their vanities.  
Hence I will make them jealous with a non-people;  
With a foolish nation I will provoke them to anger.

## v22

For a fire is kindled in My anger  
And burns unto nethermost Sheol.  
And it devours the earth and its produce  
And sets ablaze the foundations of the mountains.

## v23

I will heap evils on them;  
I will use up My arrows against them.

## v24

They shall be sucked up by famine,  
And eaten up by fire bolt  
And bitter destruction;  
And I will send the teeth of beasts against them,  
With the venom of those that crawl in the dust.

## v25

The sword shall bereave outside,  
And inside, terror;  
Taking both the young man and the virgin,  
The suckling with the gray-haired man.

## v26

I would have said, I will scatter them,  
I will cause the memory of them to cease from among men;

## v27

Except I dreaded the vexation from the enemy;  
Lest their adversaries misjudge;  
Lest they say, Our hand is exalted,  
And it is not Jehovah who has wrought all this.

## v28

For they are a nation destitute of counsel,  
And there is no understanding in them.

## v29

If they had been wise, they would have comprehended this,  
They would have perceived their latter end.

## v30

How shall one chase a thousand,  
And two put ten thousand to flight,  
Were it not that their Rock sold them,  
And Jehovah delivered them up?

## v31

For their rock is not like our Rock;  
And of this our enemies are judges.

## v32

For their vine is from Sodom’s vine,  
And from Gomorrah’s fields;  
Their grapes are the grapes of poison;  
Clusters of bitterness have they.

## v33

Their wine is the venom of serpents  
And the cruel poison of cobras.

## v34

Is this not laid up in store with Me,  
Sealed up in My treasuries?

## v35

Vengeance is Mine and so is retribution,  
For the time when their foot slips;  
Because the day of their calamity has drawn near,  
And the things destined to happen to them hasten.

## v36

For Jehovah will execute judgment for His people,  
And He will have compassion on His servants,  
When He sees that their support is gone,  
And that neither fettered nor free remain.

## v37

And He will say, Where are their gods,  
The rock they sought refuge in,

## v38

Who ate the fat of their sacrifices,  
And drank the wine of their drink offering?  
Let them rise up and help you;  
Let them be a shelter over you.

## v39

See now that I, I am He,  
And there is no god with Me.  
It is I who kill, and I make alive;  
I wound, and it is I who heal;  
And there is no one who can deliver from My hand.

## v40

For I lift up My hand to heaven  
And say, As I live forever:

## v41

If I sharpen My flashing sword,  
And My hand takes hold of judgment,  
I will repay My adversaries with vengeance,  
And those who hate Me will I recompense.

## v42

I will make My arrows drunk with blood,  
And My sword will devour flesh —  
With the blood of the slain and of the captives,  
The flesh of the long-haired leaders of the enemy.

## v43

Shout joyously, you nations, with His people,  
Because He will avenge the blood of His servants,  
And He will repay His adversaries with vengeance  
And will cover the guilt of His land and of His people.

## v44

And Moses came and spoke all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Hoshea the son of Nun.

## v45

And Moses finished speaking all these words to all Israel.

## v46

And he said to them, Set your heart on all these words which I am testifying to you today. You shall command them to your children that they may be certain to do all the words of this law.

## v47

For it is not a matter too vain for you, because it is your life; and by this matter you will extend your days upon the land into which you are crossing over the Jordan to possess.

## v48

And Jehovah spoke to Moses that same day, saying,

## v49

Go up to this mountain of the Abarim, Mount Nebo, which is in the land of Moab, which faces Jericho, and see the land of Canaan, which I am giving to the children of Israel as a possession;

## v50

And die in the mountain to which you are going up, and be gathered to your people, just as Aaron your brother died in Mount Hor and was gathered to his people;

## v51

Because you were unfaithful to Me among the children of Israel at the waters of Meribah-kadesh, in the wilderness of Zin; because you did not sanctify Me among the children of Israel.

## v52

But from a distance you shall see the land, yet you shall not go there into the land which I am giving to the children of Israel.
