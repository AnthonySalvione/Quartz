# Deuteronomy-03 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Deuteronomy-03|Deuteronomy 3]]

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — took

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-03#v8|Deuteronomy 3:8]]

To defeat King Sihon and King Og was to defeat the two “gate guards” of the land of Canaan (see [note 1²](<Numbers-21.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Arad>) in [[Numbers-21|Num. 21]]). This was the initiation of the taking possession of the good land. The land of those two kings was given to the tribes of Reuben and Gad and the half-tribe of Manasseh ([[Deuteronomy-03#v12|vv. 12-13]]) as the firstfruits of the enjoyment of the God-promised land (see [[Numbers-32|Num. 32]]).

## Footnotes for verse 26

### Footnote 26.1 — Enough

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-03#v26|Deuteronomy 3:26]]

Lit., Enough for you. Moses served God faithfully for forty years, but because his mistake in [[Numbers-20|Num. 20]] (see [[Numbers-20.fn#Footnote 12.1 — sanctify|note 12¹]] there) involved God’s governmental administration, he lost the right to enter into the good land. God’s dealing with Moses helped to perfect the children of Israel, causing them to have more fear of God’s righteous dealing. This is God’s perfecting love for His people (cf. [[Hebrews-12#v5|Heb. 12:5-11]]).
