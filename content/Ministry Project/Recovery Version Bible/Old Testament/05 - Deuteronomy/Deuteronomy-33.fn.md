# Deuteronomy-33 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Deuteronomy-33|Deuteronomy 33]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — blessing

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-33#v1|Deuteronomy 33:1]]

Ultimately, this book shows us that the love of God consummately works for His people that they may enjoy His full blessing according to His will and foreknowledge. In spite of the failure of God’s people in loving God and fearing Him and in spite of their unfaithfulness, God will be faithful to the end ([[2 Timothy-02#v13|2 Tim. 2:13]]), and eventually He will accomplish His intention that His people may enjoy His full blessing. God’s chosen people eventually entered into the Holy Land, possessed it, lived in it, and enjoyed it. This corresponds with the revelation of the entire Bible, which shows that in spite of the unfaithfulness, defeat, and failure of God’s people, God will still enable His chosen people to enter into the rich Christ to possess Him, enjoy Him, experience Him, and even live Him (cf. [[Philippians-03#v7|Phil. 3:7-14]]; [[Philippians-01#v19|1:19-21]]). This is God’s success, and the boast and glory belong to no one other than Him.

Concerning Moses’ blessing of the twelve tribes of Israel in [[Deuteronomy-33#v6|vv. 6-25]], see notes on Jacob’s blessing in [[Genesis-49#v3|Gen. 49:3-28]]. Moses’ blessing here on Reuben was the blessing of increase.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — bring

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-33#v7|Deuteronomy 33:7]]

Since Judah was a tribe accustomed to fighting for the people, this prayer of Moses’ for Judah was a prayer for Judah to be brought back to his people from the battlefield.

### Footnote 7.2 — adversaries

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-33#v7|Deuteronomy 33:7]]

Enemies come from the outside, whereas adversaries arise from within. Cf. note 94 in [[Revelation-12|Rev. 12]].

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — going

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-33#v18|Deuteronomy 33:18]]

This going forth was fulfilled in the Lord’s sending forth the apostles ([[Matthew-28#v16|Matt. 28:16-20]]). Most of the apostles, who were sent forth to disciple the nations, came forth from the land of Zebulun, which was in the region of Galilee ([[Matthew-04#v12|Matt. 4:12-23]]).
