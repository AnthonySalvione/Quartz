## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — It

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-30#v12|Deuteronomy 30:12]]

In [[Romans-10#v6|Rom. 10:6-8]] Paul applies the word spoken by Moses in [[Deuteronomy-30#v11|vv. 11-14]] to Christ, indicating that the commandment, which is the word of God ([[Deuteronomy-30#v11|vv. 11]], [[Deuteronomy-30#v14|14]]), is Christ as the Word ([[John-01#v1|John 1:1]]; [[Revelation-19#v13|Rev. 19:13]]), who, as the breath that proceeds out of God’s mouth (cf. [[Deuteronomy-08#v3|8:3]]; [[2 Timothy-03#v16|2 Tim. 3:16a]]), is in our heart and in our mouth (see [note 8¹](<Romans-10.fn#Footnote 8.1 — The>) in [[Romans-10|Rom. 10]]). As the Word of God, the incarnated, crucified, and resurrected Christ Himself, who has become the life-giving Spirit as the breath breathed out by the speaking God ([[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45]]; [[John-20#v22|John 20:22]]), is the word of the law—including the commandments, the statutes, and the ordinances (judgments)—rehearsed by Moses in this book. Thus, every word in this book is the very Christ, who is now the word of God for us to receive as our life and life supply by calling on Him ([[Romans-10#v12|Rom. 10:12-13]]). See notes [3¹](<Deuteronomy-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — everything>) in [[Deuteronomy-08|Deuteronomy 8]] and [9¹](<Psalm-119.fn#Footnote 9.1 — word>) in [[Psalm-119|Psa. 119]].

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — sea

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-30#v13|Deuteronomy 30:13]]

Whereas this verse speaks of the sea, in [[Romans-10#v7|Rom. 10:7]] Paul speaks of the abyss. The sea is the mouth of the abyss. See notes [7¹](<Romans-10.fn#Footnote 7.1 — abyss>) in [[Romans-10|Rom. 10]] and [1²](<Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 1.2 — sea>) in [[Revelation-13|Rev. 13]].
