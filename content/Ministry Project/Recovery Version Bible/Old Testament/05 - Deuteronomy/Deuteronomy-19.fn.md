## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — boundary

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-19#v14|Deuteronomy 19:14]]

The land was eventually divided by lot ([[Joshua-14#v2|Josh. 14:2]] and [note](<Joshua-14.fn#Footnote 2.1 — lot>)), and boundary markers were set up. To move a boundary marker was to change God’s ordination. This is abominable in the eyes of God. Instead of being greedy and invading another’s portion, we should learn to be contented with the portion of Christ that God has ordained for us. Cf. [[Romans-12#v3|Rom. 12:3]]; [[2 Corinthians-10#v13|2 Cor. 10:13-16]].
