# Deuteronomy-21 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Deuteronomy-21|Deuteronomy 21]]

## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — hang

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-21#v22|Deuteronomy 21:22]]

The one hanged on a tree here is a type of the crucified Christ, who was cursed and hanged on the cross to redeem us out of the curse of the law ([[Galatians-03#v13|Gal. 3:13]]; [[1 Peter-02#v24|1 Pet. 2:24]]) and was buried on the day of His death ([[Deuteronomy-21#v23|v. 23]]; [[John-19#v31|John 19:31]]).
