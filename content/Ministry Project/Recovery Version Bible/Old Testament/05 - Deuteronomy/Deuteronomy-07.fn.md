## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — bless

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-07#v13|Deuteronomy 7:13]]

It is in Christ that we receive and enjoy God’s blessings ([[Ephesians-01#v3|Eph. 1:3]]). The unique commandment God gives to the world is to believe in His Son and receive Him (see note 53 in [[Romans-01|Rom. 1]]). Actually, Christ Himself is the commandment of God. We need to receive Him, keep Him, and be right with Him. If we do this, we will be right with God, and He will love us and bless us ([[John-14#v21|John 14:21]], [[John-14#v23|23]]).
