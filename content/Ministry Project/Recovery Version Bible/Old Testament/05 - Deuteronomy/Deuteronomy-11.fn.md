# Deuteronomy-11 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Deuteronomy-11|Deuteronomy 11]]

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — strong

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-11#v8|Deuteronomy 11:8]]

If we keep Christ and hold fast to Christ as God’s whole commandment (see note 31 in [[Deuteronomy-08|Deuteronomy 8]]), we will be strengthened and encouraged to go on to possess Christ ([[Deuteronomy-11#v8|vv. 8]], [[Deuteronomy-11#v24|24]]), i.e., to gain Christ ([[Philippians-03#v8|Phil. 3:8]]); our days will be extended in Christ ([[Deuteronomy-11#v9|vv. 9]], [[Deuteronomy-11#v21|21]]); God’s eyes will be upon us to care for us and give us the blessing of His presence ([[Deuteronomy-11#v12|v. 12]]); we will enjoy the heavenly rain, the watering of the Spirit, and reap a harvest of Christ ([[Deuteronomy-11#v14|vv. 14-15]]); and we will love God, walk in His ways, and hold fast to Him ([[Deuteronomy-11#v18|vv. 18-22]]).

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — water

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-11#v10|Deuteronomy 11:10]]

I.e., water with treadmills.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — eyes

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-11#v12|Deuteronomy 11:12]]

Because we are practically joined to Christ as the reality of the good land and are enjoying His riches (see note 71 in [[Deuteronomy-08|Deuteronomy 8]]), God’s eyes are upon us continually, causing us to enjoy God’s presence and making us the object of His care.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — lay

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-11#v18|Deuteronomy 11:18]]

Moses’ words in [[Deuteronomy-11#v18|vv. 18-20]] can be applied to Christ and indicate that all the time and everywhere we should be occupied with Christ. We need to love God by laying Christ as the word on our heart and on our soul and by binding this word to everything related to us ([[Colossians-03#v16|Col. 3:16]]).

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — farmost

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-11#v24|Deuteronomy 11:24]]

I.e., the Mediterranean Sea. In this verse the boundaries of the land of Israel are set by God, from the Mediterranean Sea to the Euphrates in today’s Iraq (cf. [[Genesis-15#v18|Gen. 15:18]]; [[Exodus-23#v31|Exo. 23:31]]). This will be fulfilled in the restoration of Israel, after the second coming of Christ ([[Matthew-17#v11|Matt. 17:11]]; [[Matthew-19#v28|19:28]]; [[Acts-01#v6|Acts 1:6]]; [[Acts-03#v21|3:21]]; [[Acts-15#v16|15:16]]).

## Footnotes for verse 26

### Footnote 26.1 — blessing

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-11#v26|Deuteronomy 11:26]]

If we love Christ and hold fast to Him, we will receive the blessing ([[Ephesians-06#v24|Eph. 6:24]]); if we do not, we will receive the curse ([[1 Corinthians-16#v22|1 Cor. 16:22]]).
