## Footnotes for verse 27

### Footnote 27.1 — rebelliousness

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-31#v27|Deuteronomy 31:27]]

What is portrayed in [[Deuteronomy-31#v27|vv. 27-29]] concerning the rebelliousness and stubbornness of the children of Israel should cause us not to have any trust in ourselves, for we are the same in nature as they. Therefore, we surely need the Lord’s mercy and grace. We need to continually come to the Lord and receive Him as the word into our being (see notes 31 in [[Deuteronomy-08|Deuteronomy 8]] and 121 in [[Deuteronomy-30|Deuteronomy 30]]). 

Israel’s stubbornness is in contrast to God’s sovereignty for the showing forth of God’s wisdom and for the accomplishing of God’s economy ([[Romans-09|Rom. 9—11]]).
