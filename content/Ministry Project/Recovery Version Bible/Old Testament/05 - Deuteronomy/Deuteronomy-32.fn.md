## Footnotes for verse 43

### Footnote 43.1 — joyously

**Verse:** [[Deuteronomy-32#v43|Deuteronomy 32:43]]

Many things in the song of Moses are severe, but the ending is very positive. No matter how evil the children of Israel might be and no matter how much God might be provoked in His anger toward them, the result, the issue, will be good. Instead of forsaking His people, God will eventually come in to vindicate them ([[Isaiah-02#v2|Isa. 2:2-3]]; [[Zechariah-08#v20|Zech. 8:20-23]]). This is true concerning Israel and also concerning the believers in Christ.
