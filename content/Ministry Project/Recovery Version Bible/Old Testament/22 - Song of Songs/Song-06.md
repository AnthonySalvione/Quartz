## v1

[[Song-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Where|Where]] has your beloved gone, /  
O you most beautiful among women? /  
Where has your beloved turned, /  
That we may seek him with you?

## v2

My beloved has gone down to his [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — garden|garden]], /  
To the beds of spices, /  
To feed in the gardens /  
And gather lilies.

## v3

I am [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — my|my]] beloved’s, and my beloved is mine; /  
He pastures his flock among the lilies.

## v4

You are as beautiful, my love, as [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Tirzah|Tirzah]], /  
As lovely as [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Tirzah|Jerusalem]], /  
As terrible as an [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — army|army]] with banners.

## v5

Turn your [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — eyes|eyes]] away from me, /  
For they overwhelm me. /  
Your [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — hair|hair]] is like a flock of goats /  
That repose on Gilead.

## v6

Your [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — teeth|teeth]] are like a flock of ewes /  
That have come up from the washing, /  
All of which have borne twins, /  
And none of them is bereaved of her young.

## v7

Your [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — cheeks|cheeks]] are like a piece of pomegranate /  
Behind your veil.

## v8

There are sixty [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — queens|queens]] and eighty [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — queens|concubines]] /  
And [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — queens|virgins]] without number.

## v9

My dove, my perfect one, is but one; /  
She is the only one of her mother; /  
She is the choice one of her who bore her. /  
The daughters saw her, and they called her blessed; /  
The queens and the concubines, /  
They also praised her.

## v10

Who is this woman who looks forth like the dawn, /  
As beautiful as the [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — moon|moon]], /  
As clear as the [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — moon|sun]], /  
As terrible as an [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 10.2 — army|army]] with banners?

## v11

I went down to the [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — orchard|orchard]] of nuts /  
To see the freshness of the valley, /  
To see whether the vine had budded, /  
Whether the pomegranates were in bloom.

## v12

Before I was aware, /  
My soul set me among the [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — chariots|chariots]] of my noble people.

## v13

Return, return, O [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Shulammite|Shulammite]]; /  
Return, return, that we may gaze at you. /  
Why should you gaze at the Shulammite, /  
As upon the dance of [[Song-06.fn#Footnote 13.2 — two|two]] camps?
