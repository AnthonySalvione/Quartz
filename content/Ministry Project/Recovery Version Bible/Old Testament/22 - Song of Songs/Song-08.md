## v1

Oh that you were like a [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — brother|brother]] to me, /  
Who nursed at my mother’s breasts! /  
If I found you [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 1.2 — outside|outside]], I would kiss you, /  
And none would despise me.

## v2

I would lead you and bring you /  
Into my [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — mother’s|mother’s]] house, /  
Who has instructed me; /  
I would make you drink spiced wine /  
From the juice of my pomegranate.

## v3

His left hand would be under my head, /  
And his right hand would embrace me.

## v4

I [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — adjure|adjure]] you, O daughters of Jerusalem, /  
Do not rouse up or awaken my love /  
Until she pleases.

## v5

Who is this who comes up from the wilderness, /  
[[Song-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Leaning|Leaning]] on her beloved? /  
[[Song-08.fn#Footnote 5.2 — I|I]] awakened you under the apple tree: /  
There your mother was in labor with you; /  
There she was in labor and brought you forth.

## v6

Set me as a [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — seal|seal]] on your heart, /  
As a seal on your arm; /  
For love is as strong as death, /  
Jealousy is as cruel as [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Sheol|Sheol]]; /  
Its flashes are the flashes of fire, /  
A flame of [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 6.3 — Jehovah|Jehovah]].

## v7

Many waters cannot quench love, /  
Nor do floods drown it. /  
If a man gave all the substance of his house for love, /  
It would be utterly despised.

## v8

We have a little [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 8.1 — sister|sister]], /  
And she has no breasts: /  
What shall we do for our sister /  
On the day when she is spoken for?

## v9

If she is a [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — wall|wall]], /  
We will build on her a battlement of silver; /  
And if she is a [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — wall|door]], /  
We will enclose her with boards of cedar.

## v10

I am a [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — wall|wall]], and my breasts are like towers; /  
Then I was in his eyes like one who has found peace.

## v11

Solomon had a vineyard at [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Baal-hamon|Baal-hamon]]: /  
He let out the vineyard to keepers; /  
Each was to bring a thousand shekels of silver for its fruit.

## v12

My vineyard, which is mine, is before me. /  
You will have the thousand, O Solomon; /  
And those who keep its fruit, two hundred.

## v13

O you who dwell in the gardens, /  
My companions listen for your [[Song-08.fn#Footnote 13.1 — voice|voice]]; /  
Let me hear it.

## v14

[[Song-08.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Make|Make]] haste, my beloved, /  
And be like a gazelle or a young hart /  
Upon the mountains of spices.
