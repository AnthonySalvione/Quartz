## v1

I am a [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — rose|rose]] of [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Sharon|Sharon]], /  
A lily of the valleys.

## v2

As a [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — lily|lily]] among thorns, /  
So is my love among the daughters.

## v3

As the [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — apple|apple]] tree among the trees of the wood, /  
So is my beloved among the sons:  
In his shade I delighted and sat down, /  
And his fruit was sweet to my taste.

## v4

He brought me into the [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — banqueting|banqueting]] house, /  
And his banner over me was love.

## v5

Sustain me with raisin cakes, /  
Refresh me with apples, /  
For I am sick with love.

## v6

His left hand is under my head, /  
And his right hand embraces me.

## v7

I [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — adjure|adjure]] you, O daughters of Jerusalem, /  
By the gazelles or by the hinds of the fields, /  
Not to rouse up or awaken my love /  
Until she pleases.

## v8

The voice of my beloved! Now he comes, /  
[[Song-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Leaping|Leaping]] upon the mountains, /  
Skipping upon the hills.

## v9

My beloved is like a gazelle or a young hart. /  
Now he stands behind our [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — wall|wall]]; /  
He is looking through the [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — windows|windows]], /  
He is glancing through the [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — windows|lattice]].

## v10

My beloved [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — responds|responds]] and says to me, /  
[[Song-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Rise|Rise]] up, my love, /  
My beauty, and [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Rise|come]] away;

## v11

For now the [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — winter|winter]] is past; /  
The rain is over and gone.

## v12

[[Song-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Flowers|Flowers]] appear on the earth; /  
The time of singing has come, /  
And the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land.

## v13

The fig tree has ripened its figs, /  
And the vines are in blossom — they give forth their fragrance. /  
[[Song-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Rise|Rise]] up, my love, /  
My beauty, and come away.

## v14

[[Song-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — My|My]] dove, in the clefts of the rock, /  
In the covert of the precipice, /  
Let me see your countenance, /  
Let me hear your voice; /  
For your voice is sweet, /  
And your countenance is lovely.

## v15

Catch the [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — foxes|foxes]] for us, /  
The little foxes, /  
That ruin the vineyards /  
While our vineyards are in blossom.

## v16

My beloved is [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — mine|mine]], and I am his; /  
He pastures his flock among the lilies.

## v17

[[Song-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Until|Until]] the day dawns and the shadows flee away, /  
Turn, my beloved, and be like a gazelle or a young hart /  
On the mountains of [[Song-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — Bether|Bether]].
