## v1

The Song of Songs, which is Solomon’s.

## v2

Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth! /  
For your love is better than wine.

## v3

Your anointing oils have a pleasant fragrance; /  
Your name is like ointment poured forth; /  
Therefore the virgins love you.

## v4

Draw me; we will run after you —  
The king has brought me into his chambers — /  
We will be glad and rejoice in you; /  
We will extol your love more than wine. /  
Rightly do they love you.

## v5

I am black but lovely, O daughters of Jerusalem, /  
Like the tents of Kedar, like the curtains of Solomon.

## v6

Do not look at me, because I am black, /  
Because the sun has scorched me. /  
My mother’s sons were angry with me; /  
They made me keeper of the vineyards, /  
But my own vineyard I have not kept.

## v7

Tell me, you whom my soul loves, Where do you pasture your flock? /  
Where do you make it lie down at noon? /  
For why should I be like one who is veiled /  
Beside the flocks of your companions?

## v8

If you yourself do not know, /  
You fairest among women, /  
Go forth on the footsteps of the flock, /  
And pasture your young goats /  
By the shepherds’ tents.

## v9

I compare you, my love, /  
To a mare among Pharaoh’s chariots.

## v10

Your cheeks are lovely with plaits of ornaments, /  
Your neck with strings of jewels.

## v11

We will make you plaits of gold /  
With studs of silver.

## v12

While the king was at his table, /  
My spikenard gave forth its fragrance.

## v13

My beloved is to me a bundle of myrrh /  
That lies at night between my breasts.

## v14

My beloved is to me a cluster of henna flowers /  
In the vineyards of En-gedi.

## v15

Oh, you are beautiful, my love! /  
Oh, you are beautiful! Your eyes are like doves.

## v16

Oh, you are beautiful, my beloved; indeed, pleasant! /  
Indeed, our couch is green.

## v17

The beams of our house are cedars; /  
Our rafters are cypresses.
