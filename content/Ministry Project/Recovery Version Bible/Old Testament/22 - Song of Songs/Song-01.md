## v1

The [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Song|Song]] of Songs, which is Solomon’s.

## v2

Let him kiss me with the [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — kisses|kisses]] of his mouth! /  
For your love is better than wine.

## v3

Your anointing oils have a pleasant fragrance; /  
Your name is like ointment poured forth; /  
Therefore the [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — virgins|virgins]] love you.

## v4

[[Song-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Draw|Draw]] me; we will run after you —  
The king has brought me into his [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — chambers|chambers]] — /  
We will be glad and [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — rejoice|rejoice]] in you; /  
We will [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — rejoice|extol]] your love more than wine. /  
Rightly do they love you.

## v5

I am [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — black|black]] but lovely, O daughters of Jerusalem, /  
Like the tents of Kedar, like the curtains of Solomon.

## v6

Do not look at me, because I am black, /  
Because the sun has [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — scorched|scorched]] me. /  
My mother’s sons were angry with me; /  
They made me keeper of the vineyards, /  
But my own vineyard I have not kept.

## v7

Tell me, you whom my soul loves, [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Where|Where]] do you pasture your flock? /  
Where do you make it lie down at [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — noon|noon]]? /  
For why should I be like one who is veiled /  
Beside the flocks of your companions?

## v8

If you yourself do not know, /  
You fairest among women, /  
[[Song-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Go|Go]] forth on the footsteps of the flock, /  
And pasture your young goats /  
By the shepherds’ tents.

## v9

I compare you, [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — my|my]] love, /  
To a [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — mare|mare]] among Pharaoh’s chariots.

## v10

Your [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — cheeks|cheeks]] are lovely with plaits of ornaments, /  
Your [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — cheeks|neck]] with [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — strings|strings]] of jewels.

## v11

[[Song-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — We|We]] will make you [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — plaits|plaits]] of gold /  
With [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — plaits|studs]] of silver.

## v12

While the king was at his [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — table|table]], /  
My spikenard gave forth its fragrance.

## v13

My beloved is to me a [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — bundle|bundle]] of myrrh /  
That lies at night between my breasts.

## v14

My beloved is to me a cluster of henna flowers /  
In the vineyards of [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — En-gedi|En-gedi]].

## v15

Oh, you are beautiful, my love! /  
Oh, you are beautiful! Your [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — eyes|eyes]] are like doves.

## v16

Oh, you are beautiful, my beloved; indeed, pleasant! /  
Indeed, our [[Song-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — couch|couch]] is green.

## v17

The beams of our house are cedars; /  
Our rafters are cypresses.
