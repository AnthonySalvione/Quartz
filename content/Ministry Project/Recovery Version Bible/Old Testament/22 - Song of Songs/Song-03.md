## v1

On my bed night after night /  
I [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sought|sought]] him whom my soul loves; /  
I sought him, but found him not.

## v2

I will [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — rise|rise]] now and go about in the city; /  
In the streets and in the squares /  
I will seek him whom my soul loves. /  
I sought him, but found him not.

## v3

The [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — watchmen|watchmen]] who go about in the city found me — /  
Have you seen him whom my soul loves?

## v4

Scarcely had I passed them /  
When I found him whom my soul loves; /  
I held him and would not let go /  
Until I had brought him into my [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — mother’s|mother’s]] house /  
And into the [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — mother’s|chamber]] of her who conceived me.

## v5

I [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — adjure|adjure]] you, O daughters of Jerusalem, /  
By the gazelles or by the hinds of the fields, /  
Not to rouse up or awaken my love /  
Until she pleases.

## v6

[[Song-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Who|Who]] is [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 6.2 — she|she]] who comes up from the wilderness /  
Like pillars of smoke, /  
Perfumed with myrrh and frankincense, /  
With all the fragrant powders of the merchant?

## v7

There is Solomon’s [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — bed|bed]]; /  
Sixty mighty men surround it, /  
Of the mighty men of Israel.

## v8

All of them wield the sword and are expert in war; /  
Each man has his sword at his thigh /  
Because of the night alarms.

## v9

King Solomon made himself a [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — palanquin|palanquin]] /  
Of the wood of Lebanon.

## v10

Its posts he made of silver; /  
Its bottom, of gold; /  
Its seat, of purple; /  
Its midst was inlaid with love /  
From the daughters of Jerusalem.

## v11

Go forth, O daughters of Zion, /  
And [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — look|look]] at King Solomon with the [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 11.2 — crown|crown]] /  
With which his mother crowned him /  
On the day of his [[Song-03.fn#Footnote 11.3 — espousals|espousals]], /  
Yes, on the day of the gladness of his heart.
