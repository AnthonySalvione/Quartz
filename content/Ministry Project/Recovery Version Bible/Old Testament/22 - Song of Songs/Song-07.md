## v1

[[Song-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — How|How]] beautiful are your footsteps in [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — sandals|sandals]], /  
O [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 1.3 — prince’s|prince’s]] daughter! /  
Your rounded [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — sandals|thighs]] are like jewels, /  
The work of the hands of a skilled artist.

## v2

Your [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — navel|navel]] is a round goblet /  
That never lacks mixed wine; /  
Your [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — navel|belly]] is a heap of wheat, /  
Fenced in by lilies.

## v3

Your two [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — breasts|breasts]] are like two fawns, /  
Twins of a gazelle.

## v4

Your [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — neck|neck]] is like a tower of ivory; /  
Your [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — neck|eyes]], like the pools in Heshbon /  
By the gate of Bath-rabbim; /  
Your [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — neck|nose]] is like the tower of Lebanon, /  
Which faces Damascus.

## v5

Your [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — head|head]] upon you is like Carmel, /  
And the [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — head|locks]] of your head like purple. /  
The king is fettered by your tresses.

## v6

How [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — beautiful|beautiful]] and how pleasant in delights /  
You are, O love!

## v7

This your stature is like a palm tree, /  
And your breasts are like the clusters.

## v8

I said, I will climb the [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 8.1 — palm|palm]] tree; /  
I will take hold of its branches; /  
And let your breasts be like clusters of the vine, /  
And the fragrance of your nose like apples,

## v9

And the roof of your mouth like the best wine —  
[[Song-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Going|Going]] down smoothly for my beloved, /  
Gliding through the lips of those who sleep.

## v10

I am my beloved’s, /  
And his desire is for me.

## v11

Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fields|fields]]; /  
Let us lodge in the [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fields|villages]].

## v12

Let us rise up early for the [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — vineyards|vineyards]]; /  
Let us see if the vine has budded, /  
If the blossom is open, /  
If the pomegranates are in bloom; /  
There I will give you my love.

## v13

The [[Song-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — mandrakes|mandrakes]] give forth fragrance, /  
And over our doors are all choice fruits, /  
New as well as old. /  
These, my beloved, I have stored up for you.
