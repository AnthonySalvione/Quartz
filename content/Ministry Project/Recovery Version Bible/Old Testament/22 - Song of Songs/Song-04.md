## v1

Oh, you are beautiful, my love! /  
Oh, you are beautiful! Your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — eyes|eyes]] are like doves behind your veil; /  
Your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — eyes|hair]] is like a flock of goats /  
That repose on Mount Gilead.

## v2

Your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — teeth|teeth]] are like a flock of shorn ewes /  
That have come up from the washing, /  
All of which have borne twins, /  
And none of them is bereaved of her young.

## v3

Your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — lips|lips]] are like a scarlet thread, /  
And your mouth is lovely; /  
Your cheeks are like a piece of pomegranate /  
Behind your veil.

## v4

Your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — neck|neck]] is like the tower of David, /  
Built for an armory: /  
A thousand bucklers hang on it, /  
All the shields of the mighty men.

## v5

Your two [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — breasts|breasts]] are like two fawns, /  
Twins of a gazelle, /  
That feed among the lilies.

## v6

Until the day [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — dawns|dawns]] and the shadows flee away, /  
I, for my part, will go to the mountain of myrrh /  
And to the hill of frankincense.

## v7

You are altogether [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — beautiful|beautiful]], my love, /  
And there is no blemish in you.

## v8

Come with me from [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Lebanon|Lebanon]], my bride; /  
With me from [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.2 — Lebanon|Lebanon]] come. /  
Look from the top of [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.3 — Amana|Amana]], /  
From the top of [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.4 — Senir|Senir]] and [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.5 — Hermon|Hermon]], /  
From the [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.6 — lions’|lions’]] dens, /  
From the [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 8.6 — lions’|leopards’]] mountains.

## v9

You have [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — ravished|ravished]] my heart, my [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 9.2 — sister|sister]], my bride; /  
You have [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — ravished|ravished]] my heart with one glance of your eyes, /  
With one strand of your necklace.

## v10

How beautiful is your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — love|love]], my sister, my bride! /  
How much better is your love than wine, /  
And the fragrance of your ointments /  
Than all spices!

## v11

Your [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — lips|lips]] drip fresh honey, my bride; /  
Honey and milk are under your tongue; /  
And the fragrance of your garments /  
Is like the fragrance of Lebanon.

## v12

A [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 12.1 — garden|garden]] enclosed is my [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 12.2 — sister|sister]], my bride, /  
A spring shut up, a fountain sealed.

## v13

Your shoots are an [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — orchard|orchard]] of pomegranates /  
With choicest fruit; /  
Henna with spikenard,

## v14

Spikenard and saffron; /  
Calamus and cinnamon, /  
With all the trees of frankincense; /  
Myrrh and aloes, /  
With all the chief spices.

## v15

A [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — fountain|fountain]] in gardens, /  
A [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — fountain|well]] of living water, /  
And [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — fountain|streams]] from Lebanon.

## v16

Awake, O [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — north|north]] wind; /  
And come, O [[Song-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — north|south]] wind! /  
Blow upon my garden: /  
Let its spices flow forth; /  
Let my beloved come into his garden /  
And eat his choicest fruit.
