## v1

I have come into my garden, my [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sister|sister]], my bride; /  
I have gathered my myrrh with my spice; /  
I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey; /  
I have drunk my wine with my milk. /  
Eat, O [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 1.2 — friends|friends]]; /  
Drink, and drink deeply, O beloved ones!

## v2

I [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — sleep|sleep]], but my heart is awake. /  
A sound! My beloved is knocking. /  
[[Song-05.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Open|Open]] to me, my sister, my love, /  
My dove, my perfect one; /  
For my head is drenched with dew, /  
My locks with the drops of night.

## v3

I have [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — put|put]] off my garment; /  
How can I put it on again? /  
I have [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — put|washed]] my feet; /  
How can I dirty them again?

## v4

My beloved put his [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — hand|hand]] into the opening of the door, /  
And my inner parts yearned for him.

## v5

I rose up to [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — open|open]] to my beloved; /  
And my hands dripped with myrrh, /  
My fingers with liquid myrrh, /  
Upon the handles of the bolt.

## v6

I opened to my beloved, /  
But my beloved had withdrawn; he was gone. /  
My soul failed when he spoke; /  
I sought him, but found him not; /  
I called him — he answered me not.

## v7

The watchmen who go about the city found me. /  
They struck me; they wounded me; /  
The keepers of the walls took my veil from me.

## v8

I adjure you, O [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — daughters|daughters]] of Jerusalem, /  
If you find my beloved, /  
What shall you tell him? /  
That I am sick with love.

## v9

What is your beloved more than some other’s beloved, /  
O you most beautiful among women? /  
What is your beloved more than some other’s beloved, /  
That you adjure us so?

## v10

My beloved is [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — dazzling|dazzling]] white yet ruddy, /  
[[Song-05.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Distinguished|Distinguished]] among ten thousand.

## v11

His [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — head|head]] is the finest gold; /  
His locks are wavy, /  
As black as a raven.

## v12

His [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — eyes|eyes]] are like doves /  
Beside the streams of water, /  
Bathed in milk, /  
Fitly set.

## v13

His [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — cheeks|cheeks]] are like a bed of spices, /  
Mounds of sweetly fragrant herbs; /  
His lips are lilies, /  
Dripping with liquid myrrh.

## v14

His [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — hands|hands]] are tubes of gold, /  
Set with beryl; /  
His belly is an ivory work, /  
Overlaid with sapphires.

## v15

His [[Song-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — legs|legs]] are pillars of white marble, /  
Set upon bases of gold; /  
His appearance is like Lebanon, /  
As excellent as the cedars.

## v16

His mouth is sweetness itself, /  
And he is altogether desirable. /  
This is my beloved, and this is my friend, /  
O daughters of Jerusalem.
