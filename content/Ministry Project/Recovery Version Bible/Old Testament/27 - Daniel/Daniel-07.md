## v1

In the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — first|first]] year of Belshazzar the king of Babylon Daniel saw a dream and [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — visions|visions]] of his head upon his bed. Then he wrote down the dream; he related the sum of the matters.

## v2

Daniel responded and said, I was watching in my vision by night, and there were the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — four|four]] winds of heaven, stirring up the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Great|Great]] Sea.

## v3

And four great [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — beasts|beasts]] came up from the sea, each different from the other.

## v4

The [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — first|first]] was like a lion and had the wings of an eagle. I watched until its wings were plucked and it was lifted up from the earth and made to stand on two feet like a man; and a man’s heart was given to it.

## v5

And there was [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — another|another]] beast, a second one, resembling a bear. And it was raised up on one side, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth; and they said thus to it, Arise, devour much flesh.

## v6

After this I watched and there was [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — another|another]] beast, like a leopard; and it had four wings of a bird on its back; and the beast had four heads, and dominion was given to it.

## v7

After this I watched in the night visions, and there was a [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 7.1 — fourth|fourth]] beast, dreadful and frightful and exceedingly strong; and it had large iron teeth; it devoured and crushed and trampled down the remainder with its feet; and it was different from all the beasts that were before it; and it had ten horns.

## v8

I was considering the horns; then there was another horn, a [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 8.1 — small|small]] one, which came up among them; and three of the first horns were uprooted from before it. And there were [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 8.2 — eyes|eyes]] like the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 8.2 — eyes|mouth]] of a man in this horn and a mouth speaking great things.

## v9

I watched  
Until thrones were set,  
And the Ancient of Days sat down.  
His clothing was like white [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — snow|snow]],  
And the hair of His head was like pure [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — snow|wool]];  
His throne was flames of [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 9.2 — fire|fire]];  
Its wheels, burning fire.

## v10

A stream of fire issued forth  
And came out from before Him.  
Thousands of thousands ministered to Him,  
And ten thousands of ten thousands stood before Him.  
The [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 10.1 — court|court]] of judgment sat,  
And the books were opened.

## v11

I watched then because of the sound of the great things that the horn was speaking; I watched until the beast was [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — slain|slain]] and its body was destroyed and given to the burning fire.

## v12

And as for the rest of the beasts, their dominion was taken away, but an [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — extension|extension]] of life was given to them for a season and a time.

## v13

I watched in the night visions,  
And there with the clouds of heaven  
One like a [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Son|Son]] of Man was coming;  
And He came to the Ancient of Days,  
And they brought Him near before Him.

## v14

And to Him was [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 14.1 — given|given]] dominion, glory, and a kingdom,  
That all the peoples, nations, and languages might serve Him.  
His dominion is an eternal dominion, which will not pass away;  
And His kingdom is one that will not be destroyed.

## v15

As for me, Daniel, my spirit within me was distressed, and the visions of my head alarmed me.

## v16

I came near to one of those standing by and asked of him the exact meaning of all this. So he told me and made the interpretation of the things known to me:

## v17

These great beasts, which are four, are four kings who will arise from the earth.

## v18

But the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 18.1 — saints|saints]] of the Most High will receive the kingdom and possess the kingdom forever, indeed, forever and ever.

## v19

Then I wished to know the exact meaning of the fourth beast, which was different from all the others, exceedingly dreadful, with its teeth of iron and its claws of bronze, which devoured, crushed, and trampled under the remainder with its feet,

## v20

And of the ten horns that were on its head, and the other horn that came up, and before which three horns fell, even that horn that had eyes and a mouth speaking great things and whose appearance was greater than that of its companions.

## v21

I watched and that horn [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 21.1 — waged|waged]] war with the saints and prevailed against them,

## v22

Until the Ancient of Days came; and judgment was given to the saints of the Most High; and the time came when the saints possessed the kingdom.

## v23

Thus he said, The fourth beast will be the fourth kingdom upon the earth, which will be different from all the other kingdoms; and it will devour the whole earth and tread it down and crush it.

## v24

And as for the ten horns, out of this kingdom ten kings will arise, and another will arise after them, and he will be different from the previous ones; and he will subdue three kings.

## v25

And he will speak things against the Most High and wear out the saints of the Most High; and his intention will be to change the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 25.1 — times|times]] and the [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 25.1 — times|law]]; and they will be given into his hand for [[Daniel-07.fn#Footnote 25.2 — a|a]] time and times and half a time.

## v26

But the court of judgment will sit, and they will take away his dominion, so as to annihilate and destroy it unto the end.

## v27

And the kingdom and dominion and the greatness of the kingdoms under the whole heaven will be given to the people of the saints of the Most High; His kingdom is an eternal kingdom, and all the dominions will serve and obey Him.

## v28

Here is the end of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts alarmed me greatly, and my countenance was changed, but I kept the matter in my heart.
