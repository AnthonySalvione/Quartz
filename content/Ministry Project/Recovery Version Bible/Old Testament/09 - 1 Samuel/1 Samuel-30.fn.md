# 1 Samuel-30 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[1 Samuel-30|1 Samuel 30]]

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — inquired

**Verse:** [[1 Samuel-30#v8|1 Samuel 30:8]]

David was a person who trusted in God and walked according to God’s sovereignty in all his trials ([[1 Samuel-17#v36|17:36-37]]; [[1 Samuel-23#v14|23:14-16]]). While he was under trial, he sought God’s leading ([[1 Samuel-30#v6|vv. 6b-10]]). He was one with God and behaved according to God.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — spoil

**Verse:** [[1 Samuel-30#v20|1 Samuel 30:20]]

By fighting the Amalekites David gained many rich spoils. While he was becoming rich, Saul was being defeated. David was flourishing, but Saul was diminishing, even to death ([[1 Samuel-31#v1|31:1-5]]).
