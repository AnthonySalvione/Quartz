# 1 Samuel-10 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[1 Samuel-10|1 Samuel 10]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Jehovah

**Verse:** [[1 Samuel-10#v1|1 Samuel 10:1]]

Lit., Has not Jehovah anointed—? God’s purpose in anointing Saul to be king was to discipline, to train, the children of Israel by means of a negative king, so that they would learn that replacing God with a king was not a matter of blessing ([[1 Samuel-10#v19|v. 19]]; [[1 Samuel-12#v17|12:17]], [[1 Samuel-12#v19|19]]).

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — practice

**Verse:** [[1 Samuel-10#v25|1 Samuel 10:25]]

Moses gave the law to the children of Israel, but before Samuel came they did not have a set of bylaws, a constitution. Samuel taught the people the bylaws, the constitution, the practice, the customs, the manners, the ordinances, and the rules of how to practice the kingdom of God on earth.
