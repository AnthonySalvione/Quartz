### Footnote 6.1 — Jehovah

**Verse:** [[1 Samuel-05#v6|1 Samuel 5:6]]

Jehovah dealt heavily with the Philistines for the protection of His Ark ([[1 Samuel-05#v1|vv. 1-12]]). For God to protect His Ark meant that He was protecting His holiness (cf. [[1 Samuel-06#v19|6:19-20]]).
