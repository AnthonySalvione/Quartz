# Psalm-140 — Footnotes

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Deliver

**Verse:** [[Psalm-140#v1|Psalm 140:1]]

[[Psalm-140|Psalms 140—145]] are David’s final prayers. These psalms do not contain great or important matters, but as David’s prayers (especially in [[Psalm-140#v6|vv. 6-7]]; [[Psalm-141#v3|141:3]], [[Psalm-141#v5|5b]]; [[Psalm-142#v3|142:3a]], [[Psalm-142#v5|5]]; [[Psalm-143#v2|143:2b]], [[Psalm-143#v6|6]], [[Psalm-143#v8|8]], [[Psalm-143#v9|9b-10]]; and [[Psalm-144#v1|144:1-2]], [[Psalm-144#v5|5a]], [[Psalm-144#v12|12-15]]) they are full of the expressions of his intimacy with God. However, as in earlier psalms written by David, four of these six psalms are also full of the principle of good and evil ([[Psalm-140#v9|vv. 9b-13]]; [[Psalm-141#v10|141:10]]; [[Psalm-143#v12|143:12]]; [[Psalm-145#v20|145:20]]). See [note 3¹](<Psalm-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — unrighteousness>) in [[Psalm-07|Psa. 7]].
