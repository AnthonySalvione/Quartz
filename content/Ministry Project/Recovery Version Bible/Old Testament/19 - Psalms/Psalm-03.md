## v1

Jehovah, how many are my adversaries!  
Many are those who rise up against me!

## v2

Many are those who say of my soul,  
There is no salvation for him in God! Selah

## v3

But You, O Jehovah, are a shield around me,  
My glory and the One who lifts up my head.

## v4

With my voice I call out to Jehovah,  
And He answers me from His holy mountain. Selah

## v5

I lay down and slept;  
I awoke, for Jehovah sustains me.

## v6

I will not fear the myriads of the people  
Who have set themselves against me all around.

## v7

Arise, O Jehovah;  
Save me, O my God!  
Oh that You would strike all my enemies on the cheek;  
And break the teeth of the wicked.

## v8

Salvation is of Jehovah:  
Your blessing be upon Your people. Selah
