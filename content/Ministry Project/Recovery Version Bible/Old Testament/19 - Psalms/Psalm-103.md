## v1

Bless Jehovah, O my soul;  
And all that is within me, bless His holy name.

## v2

Bless Jehovah, O my soul;  
And do not forget all His benefits:

## v3

He pardons all your iniquities;  
He heals all your diseases;

## v4

He redeems your life from the pit;  
He crowns you with lovingkindness and compassions;

## v5

He satisfies the prime of your life with good,  
So that your youth is renewed like the eagle.

## v6

Jehovah executes righteous acts,  
And judgments for all who are oppressed.

## v7

He made His ways known to Moses;  
His acts, to the children of Israel.

## v8

Jehovah is compassionate and gracious,  
Long-suffering and abundant in lovingkindness.

## v9

He will not always contend with us,  
Nor will He keep His anger forever.

## v10

He has not dealt with us according to our sins,  
Nor has He recompensed us according to our iniquities.

## v11

For as high as the heavens are above the earth,  
So great is His lovingkindness upon those who fear Him.

## v12

As far as the east is from the west,  
So far has He removed our transgressions from us.

## v13

As compassionate as a father is toward his children,  
So compassionate is Jehovah toward those who fear Him.

## v14

For He knows our frame;  
He remembers that we are dust.

## v15

Man — his days are like grass;  
He flourishes like a field flower:

## v16

For the wind passes over it, and it is no more;  
And its own place knows of it no longer.

## v17

But Jehovah’s lovingkindness is from eternity  
Unto eternity upon those who fear Him,  
And His righteousness is to the children’s children;

## v18

To those who keep His covenant  
And remember His precepts so as to do them.

## v19

Jehovah has established His throne in the heavens,  
And His kingdom rules over all.

## v20

Bless Jehovah, you His angels,  
Who are mighty in strength to perform His word,  
By obeying the voice of His word.

## v21

Bless Jehovah, all you His hosts,  
You His ministers who execute His will.

## v22

Bless Jehovah, all you His works,  
In all places of His dominion.  
Bless Jehovah, O my soul.
