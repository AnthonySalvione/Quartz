## v1

Hear, O God, my cry;  
Give heed to my prayer.

## v2

From the end of the earth  
I will call out unto You  
When my heart faints;  
Lead me to the rock that is higher than I.

## v3

For You are a refuge to me,  
A strong tower before the enemy.

## v4

Let me sojourn in Your tent forever;  
Let me take refuge in the covering of Your wings. Selah

## v5

For You, O God, have heard my vows;  
You have given me the inheritance of those who fear Your name.

## v6

You will add days to the days of the king;  
May his years be like generation after generation.

## v7

May he dwell forever before God;  
Appoint lovingkindness and faithfulness, that they may preserve him.

## v8

So I will sing psalms to Your name forever,  
As I repay my vows day by day.
