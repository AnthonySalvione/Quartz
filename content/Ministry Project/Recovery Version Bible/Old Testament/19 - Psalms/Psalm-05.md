## v1

To my words give ear, O Jehovah;  
Consider my moaning.

## v2

Give heed to the sound of my cry,  
My King and my God;  
For to You do I pray.

## v3

O Jehovah, in the morning  
You will hear my voice;  
In the morning I will set forth my words in order to You,  
And I will watch.

## v4

For You are not a God who delights in wickedness;  
Evil will not sojourn with You.

## v5

Those who boast will not stand  
Before Your eyes;  
You hate all workers of iniquity.

## v6

You will destroy those who speak lies.  
Jehovah abhors a man of bloodshed and deceit.

## v7

But as for me, in the abundance of Your lovingkindness  
I will come into Your house;  
I will bow down toward Your holy temple  
In fear of You.

## v8

O Jehovah, lead me in Your righteousness  
On account of those who lie in wait for me;  
Make Your way straight before me.

## v9

For there is no truth in their mouth;  
Their inward part is corruption itself;  
Their throat is an open grave;  
They flatter with their tongue.

## v10

Hold them guilty, O God;  
Let them fall by their own counsels;  
Because of the multitude of their transgressions thrust them out,  
For they have rebelled against You.

## v11

But let all who take refuge in You rejoice;  
Let them shout for joy forever;  
And may You spread a cover over them;  
And those who love Your name  
Will exult in You.

## v12

For You will bless the righteous man, O Jehovah;  
You will surround him with favor as with a shield.
