### Footnote 13.1 — exalted

**Verse:** [[Psalm-21#v13|Psalm 21:13]]

Here David blessed Jehovah. As the king, he blessed his people ([[Psalm-20#v1|20:1-9]]), and as God’s anointed one, he blessed the anointing God.
