## v1

Deliver me from my enemies, O my God;  
Set me on high, secure from those who rise up against me.

## v2

Deliver me from the workers of iniquity,  
And save me from men of bloodshed.

## v3

For behold, they lie in wait for my soul;  
The strong gather themselves against me.  
Not because of any transgression of mine nor because of any sin of mine, O Jehovah,

## v4

Not for any fault of mine, they run and ready themselves;  
Awake to meet me, and see.

## v5

Indeed You, O Jehovah, God of hosts, God of Israel,  
Arise to punish all the nations;  
Have no mercy upon any who treacherously commit iniquity. Selah

## v6

They return in the evening;  
They growl like a dog  
And go around the city.

## v7

Behold, they belch forth with their mouth;  
Swords are in their lips;  
For, Who, they say, hears?

## v8

But You, O Jehovah, laugh at them;  
You have all the nations in derision.

## v9

O my strength, I will watch for You;  
For God is my high retreat.

## v10

My God in His lovingkindness will meet me;  
God will let me look triumphantly upon those who lie in wait for me.

## v11

Do not slay them, lest my people forget;  
Scatter them by Your power, and bring them down,  
O Lord our shield.

## v12

For the sin of their mouth,  
For the word of their lips,  
Let them even be taken in their arrogance,  
And because of the cursing and the lying that they speak.

## v13

Consume them in wrath; consume them so that they are no more;  
And men will know that God rules in Jacob  
Unto the ends of the earth. Selah

## v14

They return in the evening;  
They growl like a dog  
And go around the city.

## v15

They wander about for food;  
And if they are not filled, they linger.

## v16

But I will sing of Your strength;  
And I will joyfully sing of Your lovingkindness in the morning.  
For You have been my high retreat  
And a refuge in the day of my distress.

## v17

O my strength, unto You I will sing psalms,  
For God is my high retreat,  
My God of lovingkindness.
