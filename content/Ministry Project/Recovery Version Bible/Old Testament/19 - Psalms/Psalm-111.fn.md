# Psalm-111 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-111|Psalm 111]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Hallelujah

**Verse:** [[Psalm-111#v1|Psalm 111:1]]

See [note 35¹](<Psalm-104.fn#Footnote 35.1 — Hallelujah>) in [[Psalm-104|Psa. 104]]. [[Psalm-111|Psalms 111—118]] are a group consisting of the praise of God’s elect, which consummates in the revelation of Christ in His particular position as the cornerstone ([[Psalm-118#v22|118:22]]).

### Footnote 1.2 — I

**Verse:** [[Psalm-111#v1|Psalm 111:1]]

[[Psalm-111|Psalm 111]] is one of the alphabetical, or acrostic, psalms. The first letter of each line, beginning with this line, follows the order of the Hebrew alphabet.

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — works

**Verse:** [[Psalm-111#v2|Psalm 111:2]]

This psalm is the praise of God’s elect for God’s great works according to His covenant that He made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
