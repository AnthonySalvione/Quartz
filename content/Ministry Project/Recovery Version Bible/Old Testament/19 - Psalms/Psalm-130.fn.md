## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — forgiveness

**Verse:** [[Psalm-130#v4|Psalm 130:4]]

[[Psalm-130|Psalm 130]] is the praise of a saint, in his going up to Zion, concerning God’s forgiveness to Israel and His redeeming ([[Psalm-130#v7|v. 7]]) Israel from all their iniquity. We are not better than the persecutors and the haters mentioned in [[Psalm-129|Psa. 129]]. Our imperfections need God’s forgiveness and our filthiness needs His cleansing ([[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]], [[1 John-01#v9|9]]).
