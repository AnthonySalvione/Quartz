> *Of David. A Psalm*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jehovah|Jehovah]] declares to my Lord,  
Sit at My [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 1.2 — right|right]] hand  
Until I make Your enemies  
Your [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 1.3 — footstool|footstool]].

## v2

Jehovah will send forth  
The scepter of Your strength from [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Zion|Zion]]:  
Rule in the midst of Your enemies.

## v3

Your people will [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 3.1 — offer|offer]] themselves willingly  
In the day of Your warfare,  
In the [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 3.2 — splendor|splendor]] of their consecration.  
Your young men will be to You  
Like the [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 3.3 — dew|dew]] from the womb of the dawn.

## v4

Jehovah has sworn,  
And He will not change:  
You are a [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Priest|Priest]] forever  
According to the [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 4.2 — order|order]] of [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 4.3 — Melchizedek|Melchizedek]].

## v5

The [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Lord|Lord]] is at Your right hand;  
He will shatter kings in the day of His anger.

## v6

He will execute judgment among the nations;  
He will fill the place with corpses;  
He will shatter the head  
Over a [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 6.1 — great|great]] land.

## v7

He will drink from the [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 7.1 — brook|brook]] by the way;  
Therefore He will [[Psalm-110.fn#Footnote 7.2 — lift|lift]] up His head.
