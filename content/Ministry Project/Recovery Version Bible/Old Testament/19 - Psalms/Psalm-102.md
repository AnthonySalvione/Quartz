## v1

O Jehovah, hear my prayer;  
And let my cry come unto You.

## v2

Do not hide Your face from me  
On the day of my distress;  
Incline Your ear to me;  
On the day when I call out, answer me quickly.

## v3

For my days are consumed in smoke,  
And my bones burn like a hearth.

## v4

My heart is stricken like grass and dried up;  
Indeed I have even forgotten to eat my bread.

## v5

Because of the voice of my groaning  
My bones cling to my flesh.

## v6

I am like a pelican of the wilderness;  
I have become like an owl of the desolate places.

## v7

I watch, and I am like  
A lone sparrow on a housetop.

## v8

All day long my enemies reproach me;  
Those who deride me use me as a curse.

## v9

For I have eaten ashes like bread,  
And have mixed my drink with weeping,

## v10

Because of Your indignation and Your wrath;  
For You have taken me up and cast me away.

## v11

My days are like a shadow that declines,  
And I am dried up like grass.

## v12

But You, O Jehovah, abide forever;  
And Your memorial is from generation to generation.

## v13

You will arise and have compassion on Zion,  
For it is time to be gracious to her; the appointed time has come.

## v14

For Your servants take pleasure in her stones,  
And show favor to her dust.

## v15

And the nations will fear the name of Jehovah,  
And all the kings of the earth, Your glory;

## v16

For Jehovah has built up Zion;  
He has appeared in His glory.

## v17

He has regarded the prayer of the destitute,  
And has not despised their prayer.

## v18

This will be written for a generation to come,  
And a people to be created will praise Jehovah.

## v19

For He has looked down from the height of His sanctuary;  
From heaven Jehovah beheld the earth,

## v20

To hear the groaning of the prisoner,  
To release those appointed to die;

## v21

That the name of Jehovah may be declared in Zion,  
And His praise, in Jerusalem,

## v22

When the peoples are gathered together,  
And the kingdoms, to serve Jehovah.

## v23

He has weakened my strength in the way;  
He has shortened my days.

## v24

I said, My God,  
Do not take me away in the middle of my days;  
Your years are throughout all generations.

## v25

Of old You laid the foundation of the earth,  
And the heavens are the work of Your hands.

## v26

They will perish, but You endure;  
Indeed all of them will wear out like a garment;  
Like clothing You will change them,  
And they will be changed.

## v27

But You are the same,  
And Your years are without end.

## v28

The children of Your servants will continue,  
And their seed will be established before You.
