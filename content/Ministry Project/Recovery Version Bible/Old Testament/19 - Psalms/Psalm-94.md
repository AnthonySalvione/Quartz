## v1

O God of vengeance, O Jehovah,  
O God of vengeance, shine forth!

## v2

Lift Yourself up, O Judge of the earth;  
Render the reward upon the proud.

## v3

How long, O Jehovah, will the wicked,  
How long will the wicked exult?

## v4

They burst out; they speak arrogantly;  
All the workers of iniquity boast.

## v5

They crush Your people, O Jehovah,  
And afflict Your inheritance.

## v6

They slay the widow and the stranger,  
And they murder orphans.

## v7

And they say, Jehovah does not see,  
And the God of Jacob does not consider.

## v8

Consider, you senseless ones among the people;  
And you foolish ones, when will you become wise?

## v9

He who planted the ear, does He not hear?  
And He who formed the eye, does He not see?

## v10

He who disciplines the nations,  
He who teaches man knowledge, does He not reprove?

## v11

Jehovah knows the thoughts of man,  
That they are vanity.

## v12

Blessed is the man  
Whom You discipline, O Jehovah,  
And teach out of Your law;

## v13

That You may give him rest from the days of evil  
Until the pit is dug for the wicked.

## v14

For Jehovah will not abandon His people,  
Nor will He forsake His inheritance.

## v15

For judgment will return unto righteousness,  
And all the upright in heart will follow it.

## v16

Who will rise up for me against the evildoers?  
Who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

## v17

Unless Jehovah had been my help,  
My soul would have soon dwelt in silence.

## v18

When I said, My foot has slipped,  
Your lovingkindness, O Jehovah, upheld me.

## v19

When my disquieting thoughts within me are many,  
Your comfortings delight my soul.

## v20

Shall the seat of destruction be united with You,  
Which devises trouble through its statutes?

## v21

They band together against the soul of the righteous man  
And condemn innocent blood.

## v22

But Jehovah has been my high tower,  
And my God the rock of my refuge;

## v23

And He will return their iniquity upon them  
And destroy them in their own evil;  
Jehovah our God will destroy them.
