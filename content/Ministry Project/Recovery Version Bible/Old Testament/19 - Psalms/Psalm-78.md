## v1

Give ear, O my people, to my instruction;  
Incline your ears to the words of my mouth.

## v2

I will open my mouth in a parable;  
I will utter riddles from of old,

## v3

Which we have heard and known,  
And our fathers have related to us.

## v4

We will not hide them from their children,  
But will relate to the generation to come  
The praises of Jehovah and His strength  
And His wondrous deeds that He has done.

## v5

Indeed He has established a testimony in Jacob  
And appointed a law in Israel,  
Which He commanded our fathers  
That they should make them known to their children;

## v6

That the generation to come,  
The children to be born, may know them,  
Who should arise and relate them to their children;

## v7

That they might set their hope in God,  
And not forget the deeds of God,  
But observe His commandments;

## v8

That they might not be like their fathers,  
A stubborn and rebellious generation,  
A generation that did not set their heart right,  
And whose spirit was not faithful to God.

## v9

The children of Ephraim, armed archers with bows,  
Turned back in the day of battle.

## v10

They did not keep the covenant of God,  
And they refused to walk in His law.

## v11

And they forgot His doings  
And His wondrous deeds that He had shown them.

## v12

He did something wonderful in the sight of their fathers  
In the land of Egypt, in the field of Zoan.

## v13

He divided the sea and caused them to pass through,  
And He made the water stand as a heap.

## v14

And He led them with the cloud by day  
And all the night with a light of fire.

## v15

He split rocks in the wilderness  
And gave them abundant drink, as from the depths.

## v16

He also brought forth streams from the rock  
And made water run down like rivers.

## v17

Yet they still continued to sin against Him,  
To rebel against the Most High in the desert.

## v18

And they put God to the test in their heart,  
By asking for food according to their desire.

## v19

They also spoke against God;  
They said, Can God prepare a table in the wilderness?

## v20

Behold, He struck the rock,  
And water gushed out,  
And streams overflowed;  
Can He also give us bread,  
Or provide meat for His people?

## v21

Therefore Jehovah heard and was angry.  
And a fire was kindled against Jacob,  
And also anger went up against Israel;

## v22

Because they did not believe in God,  
And they did not trust in His salvation.

## v23

Yet He commanded the skies above  
And opened the doors of heaven;

## v24

And He rained down upon them manna to eat  
And gave them the food of heaven.

## v25

Man ate the bread of the mighty;  
He sent them full provision.

## v26

He made the east wind blow in the heavens  
And guided the south wind by His strength.

## v27

And He rained upon them meat like dust,  
And winged birds like the sand of the seas.

## v28

And He made it fall in the midst of their camp,  
Around their habitations.

## v29

So they ate and were well filled;  
Indeed what they desired He brought to them.

## v30

They were not estranged from what they desired;  
Their food was still in their mouths

## v31

When the anger of God went up against them  
And slew some of their fattest ones  
And struck down the choice men of Israel.

## v32

In spite of all this they still sinned  
And did not believe in His wondrous deeds.

## v33

Therefore He consumed their days with vanity,  
And their years with terror.

## v34

When He slew them, they inquired after Him,  
And they returned and diligently sought God.

## v35

And they remembered that God was their rock,  
And the Most High God, their Redeemer.

## v36

But they flattered Him with their mouth  
And lied to Him with their tongue.

## v37

For their heart was not steadfast toward Him,  
Nor were they faithful to His covenant.

## v38

But He, being merciful, forgave their iniquity  
And did not destroy them;  
Indeed He turned back His anger many times  
And did not stir up all His wrath.

## v39

And He remembered that they were flesh,  
A wind that goes away and does not return.

## v40

How often they rebelled against Him in the wilderness  
And grieved Him in the desert!

## v41

And they repeatedly put God to the test  
And provoked the Holy One of Israel.

## v42

They did not remember His hand,  
The day when He ransomed them from the adversary;

## v43

When He set His signs in Egypt  
And His wonders in the field of Zoan;

## v44

And He turned their rivers into blood,  
As well as their streams, so that they could not drink.

## v45

He sent among them a swarm of flies, which devoured them;  
And frogs, which destroyed them.

## v46

And He gave their increase to the consuming locust,  
And their labor to the swarming locust.

## v47

He killed their vines with hail,  
And their sycamores with ice stones.

## v48

He gave over their cattle also to the hail  
And their herds to the lightning bolts.

## v49

He sent among them the burning fierceness of His anger,  
Overflowing wrath and indignation and trouble,  
A mission of angels of evils.

## v50

He leveled a path for His anger;  
He did not spare their soul from death,  
But gave their life over to the pestilence.

## v51

And He struck all the firstborn in Egypt,  
The firstfruits of their vigor in the tents of Ham.

## v52

But He set forth His people like sheep  
And led them like a flock in the wilderness.

## v53

And He led them to safety, so that they did not fear;  
But the sea covered their enemies.

## v54

And He brought them forth to His holy border,  
The mountain, which His right hand had acquired.

## v55

And He drove out the nations before them  
And allotted them as an inheritance by line,  
And He made the tribes of Israel dwell there in their tents.

## v56

Yet they put Him to the test and rebelled against God the Most High,  
And did not keep His testimonies.

## v57

And they turned back and dealt treacherously, as their fathers had done;  
They turned aside like a deceitful bow.

## v58

Indeed they provoked Him to anger with their high places  
And made Him jealous with their graven images.

## v59

God heard, and He was angered;  
And He greatly abhorred Israel.

## v60

Thus He forsook the tabernacle in Shiloh,  
The tent in which He dwelt among men;

## v61

And He gave over His Ark into captivity,  
And His glory into the hand of the adversary.

## v62

He also delivered His people to the sword  
And was angry with His inheritance.

## v63

Fire devoured their young men,  
And their virgins had no marriage song.

## v64

Their priests fell by the sword,  
And their widows did not lament.

## v65

Then the Lord awoke as if from sleep,  
Like a mighty man who shouts because of wine.

## v66

And He struck His adversaries and sent them back;  
He put an everlasting reproach upon them.

## v67

And He rejected the tent of Joseph,  
And the tribe of Ephraim He did not choose.

## v68

Rather He chose the tribe of Judah,  
Mount Zion, which He loved.

## v69

And He built His sanctuary like the heights,  
Like the earth that He has founded forever.

## v70

Moreover He chose David His servant  
And took him from the sheepfolds;

## v71

From following after the ewes He brought him  
To shepherd Jacob His people  
And Israel His inheritance.

## v72

And he shepherded them according to the integrity of his heart,  
And guided them by the skillfulness of his hands.
