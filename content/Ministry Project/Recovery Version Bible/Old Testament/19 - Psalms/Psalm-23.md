> *A Psalm of David*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jehovah|Jehovah]] is my [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Shepherd|Shepherd]]; I will lack nothing.

## v2

He makes me lie down in [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 2.1 — green|green]] pastures; / He leads me beside [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 2.2 — waters|waters]] of rest.

## v3

He [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 3.1 — restores|restores]] my soul; / He guides me on the [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 3.2 — paths|paths]] of righteousness / For His [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 3.3 — name’s|name’s]] sake.

## v4

Even though I walk / Through the [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 4.1 — valley|valley]] of the shadow of death, / I do not fear evil, / For You are with me; / Your [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 4.2 — rod|rod]] and Your [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 4.2 — rod|staff]], / They comfort me.

## v5

You spread a [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 5.1 — table|table]] before me / In the presence of my adversaries; / You [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 5.2 — anoint|anoint]] my head with oil; / My [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 5.3 — cup|cup]] runs over.

## v6

Surely [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 6.1 — goodness|goodness]] and lovingkindness will follow me / All the days of my life, / And I will dwell in the [[Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 6.1 — goodness|house]] of Jehovah / For the length of my days.
