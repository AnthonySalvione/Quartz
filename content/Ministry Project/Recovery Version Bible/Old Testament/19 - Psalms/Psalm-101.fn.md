# Psalm-101 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-101|Psalm 101]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Psalm-101#v1|Psalm 101:1]]

The I here is David, a type of Christ, indicating that this word is the word spoken by Christ to God. In this psalm David, God’s anointed, typifies Christ as the man anointed by God to reign over the earth for God.

### Footnote 1.2 — lovingkindness

**Verse:** [[Psalm-101#v1|Psalm 101:1]]

Lovingkindness and justice will be the base of the reign and judgment of Christ, typified here by David. Therefore, this psalm unveils how Christ will reign over the earth with lovingkindness and justice and deal with all the wicked ([[Psalm-101#v1|vv. 1]], [[Psalm-101#v6|6]], [[Psalm-101#v8|8]]).
