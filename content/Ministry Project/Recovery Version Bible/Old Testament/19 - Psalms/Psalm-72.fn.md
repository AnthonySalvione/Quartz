# Psalm-72 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-72|Psalm 72]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — king

**Verse:** [[Psalm-72#v1|Psalm 72:1]]

In this psalm the reigning Christ is typified by the reigning Solomon (see superscription), the son of David ([[Matthew-01#v1|Matt. 1:1]]; [[Matthew-22#v42|22:42]]), in his prosperous and flourishing time ([[1 Kings-09|1 Kings 9—10]]). The reign of Christ typified by the reign of Solomon will be in the millennium in the age of restoration ([[Matthew-19#v28|Matt. 19:28]]; [[Revelation-20#v4|Rev. 20:4]], [[Revelation-20#v6|6]]).

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — righteousness

**Verse:** [[Psalm-72#v2|Psalm 72:2]]

The reigning of Christ will be in righteousness and justice (see [note 14¹](<Psalm-89.fn#Footnote 14.1 — foundation>) in [[Psalm-89|Psa. 89]]) and will issue in peace ([[Psalm-72#v3|vv. 3]], [[Psalm-72#v7|7]]; cf. [[Isaiah-32#v17|Isa. 32:17]]; [[Hebrews-12#v11|Heb. 12:11]]).

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — drop

**Verse:** [[Psalm-72#v6|Psalm 72:6]]

In His second coming Christ will gain the earth not mainly by exercising His righteous judgment but by coming like showers to water the people of the earth. He will have mercy on the earth and will come back graciously like showers of rain to satisfy the thirsty ones (cf. [[Revelation-22#v17|Rev. 22:17]]). In that day all the nations will be rained on by Christ and will be happy under His dominion.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — River

**Verse:** [[Psalm-72#v8|Psalm 72:8]]

I.e., the Euphrates.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — coastlands

**Verse:** [[Psalm-72#v10|Psalm 72:10]]

I.e., the islands and shores of the Mediterranean Sea.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — Blessed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-72#v18|Psalm 72:18]]

[[Psalm-41#v13|Psalm 41:13]] concludes Book One of the Psalms, and [[Psalm-72#v18|vv. 18-19]] of this psalm conclude Book Two. The two are similar, but the conclusion in this psalm is improved by the mentioning of God’s glorious name and the filling of the whole earth with His glory because of the gaining of the earth through the enjoyment of God in His house and His city (see [note 29¹](<Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Kings>) in [[Psalm-68|Psa. 68]]).
