# Psalm-69 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-69|Psalm 69]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Save

**Verse:** [[Psalm-69#v1|Psalm 69:1]]

[[Psalm-69|Psalm 69]] is on the suffering Christ, typified by the suffering David, and [[Psalm-72|Psa. 72]] is on the reigning Christ, typified by the reigning Solomon. In this psalm the sufferings of Christ are portrayed in a detailed way: Christ was hated by many without cause ([[Psalm-72#v4|v. 4a]]; [[John-15#v25|John 15:25]]); He was reproached for the sake of God ([[Psalm-72#v7|vv. 7a]], [[Psalm-72#v9|9b]]; [[Romans-15#v3|Rom. 15:3]]); He was devoured by the zeal of God’s house ([[Psalm-72#v9|v. 9a]]; [[John-02#v17|John 2:17]]); He suffered much, and no one took pity on Him ([[Psalm-69#v29|vv. 29a]], [[Psalm-69#v19|19-20]]; [[John-16#v32|John 16:32]]); He wept and entreated God to deliver Him out of death ([[Psalm-72#v10|vv. 10]], [[Psalm-72#v13|13-17]], [[Psalm-72#v1|1-2]]; [[Hebrews-05#v7|Heb. 5:7]]); He was given gall as His food while He was suffering on the cross ([[Psalm-69#v21|v. 21a]]; [[Matthew-27#v34|Matt. 27:34]]); He was given vinegar to drink in His thirst on the cross ([[Psalm-69#v21|v. 21b]]; [[John-19#v28|John 19:28-30]]); He was stricken and wounded by God ([[Psalm-69#v26|v. 26]]; [[Isaiah-53#v10|Isa. 53:10a]]); and He was betrayed by one of His disciples ([[Psalm-69#v25|v. 25]]; [[Acts-01#v16|Acts 1:16-20a]]). After His life of suffering on earth, typified by the sufferings of David, Christ ascended to the heavens, where He is now reigning as the King, typified by Solomon.
