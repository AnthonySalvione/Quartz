> *To the choir director: on the gittith. A Psalm of David*

> [!light] Note on *gittith:*
>  The meaning of the Hebrew is obscure. Perhaps a stringed instrument is meant.


---
## v1

[[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — O|O]] Jehovah our Lord,  
How [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 1.2 — excellent|excellent]] is Your name  
In all the earth,  
You who have set Your glory over the heavens!

## v2

Out of the mouths of [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — babes|babes]] and [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — babes|sucklings]]  
You have [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 2.2 — established|established]] strength  
Because of Your [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 2.3 — adversaries|adversaries]],  
To stop the [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 2.3 — adversaries|enemy]] and the [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 2.3 — adversaries|avenger]].

## v3

When I see Your [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — heavens|heavens]], the works of Your fingers,  
The moon and the stars, which You have ordained,

## v4

What is [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — mortal|mortal]] man, that You [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 4.2 — remember|remember]] him,  
And the son of man, that You [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 4.2 — remember|visit]] him?

## v5

You have made [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Him|Him]] a little lower than [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 5.2 — angels|angels]]  
And have [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 5.3 — crowned|crowned]] Him with glory and honor.

## v6

For You have caused Him to [[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — rule|rule]] over the works of Your hands;  
You have put all things under His feet:

## v7

All sheep and oxen,  
As well as the beasts of the field,

## v8

The birds of heaven and the fish of the sea,  
Whatever passes through the paths of the seas.

## v9

[[Psalm-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — O|O]] Jehovah our Lord,  
How excellent is Your name  
In all the earth!
