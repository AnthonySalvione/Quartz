## v1

In Jehovah have I taken refuge. How can you say to my soul,  
Flee to the mountains like a bird?

## v2

For behold, the wicked bend the bow;  
They ready their arrow on the string  
To shoot in the dark at the upright in heart.

## v3

When the foundations are destroyed,  
What will the righteous man do?

## v4

Jehovah is in His holy temple;  
Jehovah — His throne is in heaven.  
His eyes behold;  
His eyelids try the sons of men.

## v5

Jehovah tries the righteous man;  
But the wicked man and him who loves violence, His soul hates.

## v6

He will rain down snares upon the wicked;  
Fire and brimstone and a scorching wind will be the portion of their cup.

## v7

For Jehovah is righteous; He loves righteous deeds.  
The upright man will behold His face.
