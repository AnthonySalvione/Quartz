## v1

Save me, O God; for the waters  
Threaten my life.

## v2

I sink in deep mire,  
And there is no place to stand;  
I have come to the depths of water,  
And the flood flows over me.

## v3

I am weary of my crying;  
My throat is parched;  
My eyes fail  
While I wait for my God.

## v4

More numerous than the hairs of my head  
Are those who hate me without cause;  
Mighty are those who would destroy me,
Those who are wrongly my enemies.
What I did not steal,
I must then restore.

## v5

O God, You know my foolishness,  
And my sins of guilt are not hidden from You.

## v6

Do not let those who wait on You be put to shame because of me,  
O Lord Jehovah of hosts;  
Do not let those who seek You be humiliated because of me,  
O God of Israel.

## v7

Because for Your sake I have borne reproach;  
Humiliation has covered my face.

## v8

I have become a stranger to my brothers,  
And a foreigner to my mother’s children.

## v9

For the zeal of Your house has devoured me,  
And the reproaches of those who reproach You have fallen on me.

## v10

When I wept, when my soul was in fasting,  
That became a reproach to me.

## v11

When I made sackcloth my clothing,  
I became a proverb to them.

## v12

Those who sit in the gate talk about me,  
And I am the mocking song of those who drink liquor.

## v13

But as for me, my prayer is to You,  
O Jehovah, in an acceptable time;  
O God, in the abundance of Your lovingkindness answer me,  
In the faithfulness of Your salvation.

## v14

Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink;  
May I be delivered from those who hate me,  
And out of the depths of water.

## v15

Do not let the flood of water flow over me,  
Nor let the deep swallow me,  
Nor let the pit close its mouth over me.

## v16

Answer me, O Jehovah; for Your lovingkindness is good;  
According to the abundance of Your compassions turn to me.

## v17

And do not hide Your face from Your servant,  
For I am in distress; hurry and answer me.

## v18

Draw near to my soul; redeem it;  
Ransom me because of my enemies.

## v19

You know my reproach  
And my shame and my humiliation;  
All my adversaries are before You.

## v20

Reproach has broken my heart,  
And I am sick;  
I looked for pity, but there was none;  
And for comforters, but I found none.

## v21

But they gave me gall for my food,  
And for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

## v22

May their table become a snare before them  
And a trap when they are at peace.

## v23

May their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see;  
And make their loins shake continually.

## v24

Pour out Your indignation upon them;  
And may the burning fierceness of Your anger overtake them.

## v25

May their camp be desolate;  
May there be no one to dwell in their tents.

## v26

For they persecute him whom You have stricken,  
And they recount the pain of those whom You have wounded.

## v27

Add iniquity to their iniquity,  
And do not let them come into Your righteousness.

## v28

May they be blotted out of the book of life,  
And may they not be written with the righteous.

## v29

But I am poor and pained;  
May Your salvation, O God, set me on high.

## v30

I will praise the name of God with a song  
And will magnify Him with thanksgiving;

## v31

And it will be better to Jehovah than an ox  
Or a bull with horns and hooves.

## v32

The meek have seen it; they rejoice.  
You who seek God, let your heart revive.

## v33

For Jehovah listens to the needy,  
And He does not despise His prisoners.

## v34

Let the heavens and the earth praise Him,  
The seas and everything that moves in them.

## v35

For God will save Zion  
And build the cities of Judah;  
And they will abide there and possess it.

## v36

And the seed of His servants will inherit it,  
And those who love His name will dwell in it.
