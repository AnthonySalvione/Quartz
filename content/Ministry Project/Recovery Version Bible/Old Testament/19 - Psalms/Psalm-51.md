## v1

Be gracious to me, O God, according to Your lovingkindness;  
According to the greatness of Your compassions blot out my transgressions.

## v2

Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity,  
And from my sin cleanse me.

## v3

For I do know my transgressions,  
And my sin is before me continually.

## v4

Against You and You alone have I sinned,  
And I have done what is evil in Your sight.  
Therefore You are righteous when You speak;  
You are clear when You judge.

## v5

Behold, I was brought forth in iniquity,  
And in sin did my mother conceive me.

## v6

Behold, You delight in truth in the inward parts;  
And in the hidden part You would make known wisdom to me.

## v7

Purge my sin with hyssop, and I will be clean;  
Wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.

## v8

Let me hear gladness and joy,  
That the bones which You have broken may exult.

## v9

Hide Your face from my sins,  
And blot out all my iniquities.

## v10

Create in me a clean heart, O God,  
And renew a steadfast spirit within me.

## v11

Do not cast me from Your presence,  
And do not take the Spirit of Your holiness away from me.

## v12

Restore to me the gladness of Your salvation,  
And sustain me with a willing spirit.

## v13

I will teach transgressors Your ways,  
And sinners will turn back to You.

## v14

Deliver me from the guilt of bloodshed, O God, O God of my salvation;  
And my tongue will ring forth Your righteousness.

## v15

O Lord, open my lips,  
And my mouth will declare Your praise.

## v16

For You do not delight in sacrifice;  
Otherwise I would offer it;  
You take no pleasure in burnt offerings.

## v17

The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit;  
A broken and a contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.

## v18

Do good in Your good pleasure unto Zion;  
Build the walls of Jerusalem.

## v19

Then You will delight in the sacrifices of righteousness,  
In burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings;  
Then they will offer bulls upon Your altar.
