## v1

Be gracious to me, O God, be gracious to me;  
For my soul takes refuge in You;  
Indeed in the shadow of Your wings I will take refuge,  
Until the destruction passes by.

## v2

I call out to God the Most High,  
To the God who accomplishes all things for me.

## v3

He sends forth from heaven and saves me;  
He reproaches him who would trample me; Selah  
God sends forth  
His lovingkindness and His faithfulness.

## v4

My soul is among lions;  
I lie down among those who blaze forth, the sons of men,  
Whose teeth are spears and arrows,  
And whose tongue is a sharp sword.

## v5

Be exalted above the heavens, O God;  
Let Your glory be above all the earth.

## v6

They have prepared a net for my steps;  
My soul is bowed down.  
They have dug a pit before me;  
They have fallen into it themselves. Selah

## v7

My heart is steadfast, O God;  
My heart is steadfast.  
I will sing, indeed I will sing psalms.

## v8

Awake, my glory;  
Awake, O harp and lyre!  
I will awaken the dawn.

## v9

I will give thanks to You among the peoples, O Lord;  
I will sing psalms to You among the nations.

## v10

For Your lovingkindness is as great as the heavens;  
And Your faithfulness reaches unto the skies.

## v11

Be exalted above the heavens, O God;  
Let Your glory be above all the earth.
