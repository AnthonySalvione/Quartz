## v1

Jehovah reigns! Let the earth be glad;  
Let the many islands rejoice.

## v2

Clouds and deep darkness surround Him;  
Righteousness and justice are the foundation of His throne.

## v3

A fire goes before Him  
And burns up His adversaries all around.

## v4

His lightning illumines the world;  
The earth sees and trembles.

## v5

The mountains melt like wax at the presence of Jehovah,  
At the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.

## v6

The heavens declare His righteousness,  
And all the peoples see His glory.

## v7

Let all who serve a graven image be ashamed,  
Who make their boast in idols;  
Worship Him, all you gods.

## v8

Zion hears and rejoices,  
And the daughters of Judah are glad,  
Because of Your judgments, O Jehovah.

## v9

For You, O Jehovah, are most high over all the earth;  
You are exalted far above all gods.

## v10

O you who love Jehovah, hate evil!  
He preserves the souls of His faithful ones;  
He delivers them from the hand of the wicked.

## v11

Light is sown for the righteous man,  
And rejoicing for the upright in heart.

## v12

Rejoice in Jehovah, O righteous ones;  
And praise His holy memorial.
