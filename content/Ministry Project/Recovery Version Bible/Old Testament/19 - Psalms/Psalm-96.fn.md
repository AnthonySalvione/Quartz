# Psalm-96 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-96|Psalm 96]]

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — nations

**Verse:** [[Psalm-96#v3|Psalm 96:3]]

[[Psalm-96#v3|Verses 3-13]] indicate that Jehovah as Christ will come to judge the earth, the world, and the peoples with righteousness and truth, and that He will reign over the nations.

### Footnote 3.2 — peoples

**Verse:** [[Psalm-96#v3|Psalm 96:3]]

The word peoples in [[Psalm-96#v3|vv. 3]], [[Psalm-96#v5|5]], [[Psalm-96#v7|7]], [[Psalm-96#v10|10]], and 13 indicates that people of every race and color will be judged by Christ in His reign over the nations (cf. [[Matthew-25#v31|Matt. 25:31-46]]).
