## v1

O God, You are my God; I seek You diligently.  
My soul thirsts for You,  
My flesh faints in longing for You,  
In a dry and weary land without water.

## v2

So I have looked upon You in the sanctuary,  
Seeing Your power and Your glory.

## v3

Because Your lovingkindness is better than life,  
My lips praise You.

## v4

Thus I bless You while I live;  
I lift up my hands in Your name.

## v5

My soul is satisfied as with the rich fatness,  
And my mouth praises You with lips of joyous shouting,

## v6

When I remember You while on my bed,  
When I consider You in the night watches.

## v7

For You have been my help,  
And in the shadow of Your wings I will shout for joy.

## v8

My soul pursues after You;  
Your right hand upholds me.

## v9

But those who seek my life to destroy it  
Will go into the lower parts of the earth;

## v10

They will be given over to the power of the sword;  
They will be the portion of foxes.

## v11

But the king will rejoice in God;  
Everyone who swears by Him will boast;  
For the mouth of those who speak falsehood will be stopped.
