## v1

Jehovah reigns; let the peoples tremble.  
He is enthroned between the cherubim; let the earth shake.

## v2

Jehovah is great in Zion,  
And He is exalted over all the peoples.

## v3

Let them praise Your great and awesome name —  
It is holy —

## v4

And the strength of the King, who loves justice.  
You have established equity;  
You have executed  
Justice and righteousness in Jacob.

## v5

Exalt Jehovah our God,  
And worship at His footstool;  
Holy is He.

## v6

Moses and Aaron among His priests,  
And Samuel among those who called on His name —  
They called out to Jehovah,  
And He answered them.

## v7

In the pillar of cloud He spoke to them;  
They kept His testimonies and the statute that He gave them.

## v8

O Jehovah our God, You answered them;  
You were a forgiving God to them,  
Yet One avenging their deeds.

## v9

Exalt Jehovah our God,  
And worship on His holy mountain;  
For holy is Jehovah our God.
