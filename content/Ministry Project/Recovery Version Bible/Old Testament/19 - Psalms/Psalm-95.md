## v1

Come, let us sing for joy to Jehovah;  
Let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation.

## v2

Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving;  
Let us make a joyful noise to Him with psalms.

## v3

For Jehovah is a great God  
And a great King above all gods.

## v4

In His hand are the depths of the earth;  
The heights of the mountains are His also.

## v5

The sea is His, and He made it;  
And His hands formed the dry land.

## v6

Come, let us worship and bow down;  
Let us kneel before Jehovah our Maker.

## v7

For He is our God,  
And we are the people of His pasture  
And the flock of His hand.  
Today, if you hear His voice,

## v8

Do not harden your heart as at Meribah,  
As in the day of Massah in the wilderness,

## v9

When your fathers tested Me;  
They tried Me, even though they had seen My work.

## v10

For forty years I loathed that generation,  
And I said, They are a people who go astray in heart;  
And they do not know My ways;

## v11

Therefore I swore in My anger:  
They shall by no means enter into My rest!
