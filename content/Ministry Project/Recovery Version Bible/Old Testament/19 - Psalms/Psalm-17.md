> *A Prayer of David*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-17.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Hear|Hear]], O Jehovah, what is righteous;  
Give heed to my cry;  
Give ear to my prayer,  
Which is not made with lips of deceit.

## v2

May a judgment for me come forth from Your presence;  
May Your eyes regard equity.

## v3

You have examined my heart; You have visited me by night;  
You have tried me: You have found nothing.  
I have resolved that my mouth will not transgress.

## v4

Regarding the works of man, by the word of Your lips,  
I have kept myself from the paths of the violent man.

## v5

My steps have held fast to Your tracks;  
My footsteps have not slipped.

## v6

I call on You, for You will answer me, O God;  
Incline Your ear to me; hear my speaking.

## v7

Wondrously display Your [[Psalm-17.fn#Footnote 7.1 — lovingkindness|lovingkindness]],  
You who save by Your right hand  
Those who take refuge in You from them who rise up against them.

## v8

Guard me like the pupil of Your eye;  
In the shadow of Your wings hide me

## v9

From the presence of the wicked who oppress me,  
From my deadly enemies, who close in upon me.

## v10

They have shut up their fat hearts;  
With their mouths they speak arrogantly.

## v11

Now they have surrounded [[Psalm-17.fn#Footnote 11.1 — us|us]] in our steps;  
They have fixed their eyes on casting us down to the ground.

## v12

He is like a lion that is eager to tear,  
And like a young lion lurking in hidden places.

## v13

Arise, O Jehovah, [[Psalm-17.fn#Footnote 13.1 — confront|confront]] him; bring him down.  
With Your sword rescue my soul from the wicked man,

## v14

From mortal men with Your hand, O Jehovah,  
From mortal men of the world, whose portion is in this life,  
And whose belly You fill with Your treasure;  
They are satisfied with children,  
And they leave their abundance for their little ones.

## v15

As for me, in [[Psalm-17.fn#Footnote 15.1 — righteousness|righteousness]] I will behold Your face;  
When I awake, I will be satisfied with Your [[Psalm-17.fn#Footnote 15.1 — righteousness|likeness]].
