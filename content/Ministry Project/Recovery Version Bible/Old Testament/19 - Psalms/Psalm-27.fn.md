# Psalm-27 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-27|Psalm 27]]

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — house

**Verse:** [[Psalm-27#v4|Psalm 27:4]]

In [[Psalm-27#v4|vv. 4-6]] the psalmist appreciated not the law, as in [[Psalm-01|Psa. 1]], but the enjoyment of God in His house. In God’s house, that is, in Christ ([[John-01#v14|John 1:14]]; [[John-02#v21|2:21]]) and in the church ([[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15]]), we behold God’s beauty and inquire of God ([[Psalm-01#v4|v. 4]]), are concealed from calamities and hidden from our pursuers ([[Psalm-01#v5|v. 5a]]-b), are raised up and have our heads lifted up by God ([[Psalm-01#v5|vv. 5c]], [[Psalm-01#v6|6a]]), and offer up sacrifices of shouts of joy and sing and psalm to God ([[Psalm-01#v6|v. 6b]]-c).

### Footnote 4.2 — beauty

**Verse:** [[Psalm-27#v4|Psalm 27:4]]

Or, loveliness, pleasantness, delightfulness.
