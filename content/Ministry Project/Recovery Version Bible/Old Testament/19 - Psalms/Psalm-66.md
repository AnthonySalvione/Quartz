## v1

Shout joyfully to God, all the earth;

## v2

Sing forth the glory of His name;  
Make glorious His praise.

## v3

Say to God, How awesome are Your deeds!  
Because of the greatness of Your strength, Your enemies come cringing to You.

## v4

All the earth will worship You  
And sing psalms to You;  
They will sing psalms to Your name. Selah

## v5

Come and see the works of God;  
He is awesome in His deeds toward the sons of men.

## v6

He turned the sea into dry land;  
Through the river they crossed on foot.  
There we rejoiced in Him.

## v7

He rules by His might forever;  
His eyes observe the nations:  
Let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah

## v8

Bless our God, O peoples;  
And make the voice of His praise heard,

## v9

Who has kept our soul in life  
And does not allow our feet to slip.

## v10

For You have tried us, O God;  
You have refined us as silver is refined.

## v11

You have brought us into the net;  
You have put a heavy burden on our loins.

## v12

You have made men ride over our heads;  
We have gone through fire and water;  
But You have brought us forth into abundance.

## v13

I will come into Your house with burnt offerings;  
I will pay my vows to You,

## v14

Which my lips uttered  
And my mouth spoke when I was in distress.

## v15

I will offer to You burnt offerings of fatlings  
With the smoke of rams;  
I will offer cattle with goats. Selah

## v16

Come, hear, all you who fear God,  
And I will recount what He has done for my soul.

## v17

To Him I called out with my mouth,  
And He was extolled with my tongue.

## v18

If I regarded iniquity in my heart,  
The Lord did not hear.

## v19

But indeed God has heard;  
He has given heed to the voice of my prayer.

## v20

Blessed be God,  
Who has not turned my prayer away  
Nor His lovingkindness from me.
