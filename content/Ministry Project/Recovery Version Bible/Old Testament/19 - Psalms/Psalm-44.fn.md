# Psalm-44 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-44|Psalm 44]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — O

**Verse:** [[Psalm-44#v1|Psalm 44:1]]

In [[Psalm-44#v1|vv. 1-8]] the psalmists were treasuring their forefathers’ past history, and in [[Psalm-44#v9|vv. 9-26]] they were sighing for their present situation. Both were distractions from their panting and thirsting for God ([[Psalm-42#v1|42:1-2]]), i.e., from their enjoying God. See [note 4¹](<Psalm-42.fn#Footnote 4.1 — remember>) in [[Psalm-42|Psa. 42]].
