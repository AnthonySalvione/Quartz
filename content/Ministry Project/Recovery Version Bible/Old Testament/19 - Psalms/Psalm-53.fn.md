# Psalm-53 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-53|Psalm 53]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — fool

**Verse:** [[Psalm-53#v1|Psalm 53:1]]

This psalm of instruction refers to a time when David was being attacked by the Gentiles. Thus, the persons spoken of in [[Psalm-53#v1|vv. 1-5]] are Gentiles. [[Psalm-53#v1|Verses 1-3]] were quoted by Paul in [[Romans-03#v10|Rom. 3:10-12]], referring to all men.

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — salvation

**Verse:** [[Psalm-53#v6|Psalm 53:6]]

In David’s sentiment this salvation is only for the Jews, not for the Gentiles (cf. [[Ephesians-03#v6|Eph. 3:6]]).
