# Psalm-25 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-25|Psalm 25]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — To

**Verse:** [[Psalm-25#v1|Psalm 25:1]]

This psalm is one of the alphabetical, or acrostic, psalms. See [note 1¹](<Psalm-09.fn#Footnote 1.1 — I>) in [[Psalm-09|Psa. 9]]. [[Psalm-25|Psalms 25—41]] show the mixed expressions of the psalmist’s sentiments in his enjoyment of God in God’s house. The enjoyment of Christ, as portrayed in [[Psalm-02|Psa. 2]], [[Psalm-08|8]], [[Psalm-16|16]], and 22—24, led the psalmist to another state of enjoyment—the enjoyment of God in the house of God. Although God is universal and omnipresent, He has located Himself in His house so that men may contact Him and enjoy Him ([[Psalm-23#v6|23:6]]; [[Psalm-27#v4|27:4]]). In the Old Testament time God’s house, His dwelling place, was both in the heavens ([[1 Kings-08#v30|1 Kings 8:30b]], [[1 Kings-08#v39|39a]], [[1 Kings-08#v43|43a]]) and in the temple on Mount Zion ([[Psalm-76#v2|Psa. 76:2b]]; [[Psalm-135#v21|135:21]]; [[Isaiah-08#v18|Isa. 8:18]]). God’s physical house in the Old Testament is a type of His spiritual house in the New Testament ([[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]]). God’s house in the New Testament is, first, Christ as God’s tabernacle and God’s temple ([[John-01#v14|John 1:14]]; [[John-02#v21|2:21]]; [[Colossians-02#v9|Col. 2:9]]); second, it is the church, the enlargement of Christ, as God’s enlarged temple ([[1 Corinthians-03#v16|1 Cor. 3:16]]; [[Ephesians-02#v21|Eph. 2:21-22]]; [[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15]]; [[Hebrews-03#v6|Heb. 3:6]]). Consummately, God’s house will be the New Jerusalem, a composition of God’s redeemed as the tabernacle ([[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2-3]]) for God to dwell in and enjoy His redeemed, and the redeeming God Himself as the temple ([[Revelation-21#v22|Rev. 21:22]]) for His redeemed to dwell in and enjoy Him—a mutual dwelling of both God and man for their mutual enjoyment. We enjoy God in His house by being in Christ and in the church in this age, and ultimately in the New Jerusalem in the next age and in eternity.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — sins

**Verse:** [[Psalm-25#v7|Psalm 25:7]]

In this verse and in [[Psalm-25#v11|vv. 11]] and 18 David asked God not to remember but to forgive his sins, his transgressions, and his great iniquity. Our confession of sins and God’s forgiveness of our sins are the threshold of the New Testament age ([[Matthew-03#v1|Matt. 3:1-2]]; [[Mark-01#v4|Mark 1:4-5]]; [[Acts-10#v43|Acts 10:43]]; [[Hebrews-08#v12|Heb. 8:12]]; [[1 John-01#v9|1 John 1:9]]).

### Footnote 14.1 — counsel

**Verse:** [[Psalm-25#v14|Psalm 25:14]]
