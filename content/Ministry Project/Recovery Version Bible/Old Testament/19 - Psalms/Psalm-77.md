> *To the choir director, to Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A Psalm*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-77.fn#Footnote 1.1 — My|My]] voice calls out to God, and I will cry out;  
My voice calls out to God, and He will give ear to me.

## v2

In the day of my distress I sought the Lord;  
My hand was stretched out in the night, and it did not drop.  
My soul refused to be comforted.

## v3

I remember God, and I moan;  
I complain, and my spirit faints. Selah

## v4

You hold open my eyelids;  
I am disturbed and cannot speak.

## v5

I consider the days of old,  
The years of ancient times;

## v6

I remember my song in the night;  
I muse with my own heart, and my spirit carefully searches.

## v7

Will the Lord [[Psalm-77.fn#Footnote 7.1 — cast|cast]] us off forever,  
And will He never be favorable to us again?

## v8

Has His lovingkindness ceased forever?  
Has the promise come to an end for all generations?

## v9

Has God forgotten to be gracious?  
Or has He in anger shut up His compassions? Selah

## v10

Then I said, This is my infirmity;  
[[Psalm-77.fn#Footnote 10.1 — But|But]] I recall the years of the right hand of the Most High.

## v11

I will bring to remembrance the deeds of [[Psalm-77.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Jehovah|Jehovah]],  
For I will remember Your wonders from of old.

## v12

And I will meditate on all that You have done  
And muse on Your doings.

## v13

O God, Your way is in the [[Psalm-77.fn#Footnote 13.1 — sanctuary|sanctuary]];  
Who is so great a god as God?

## v14

You are the God who does wonders;  
You have made known Your strength among the peoples.

## v15

You redeemed Your people with Your arm,  
The sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah

## v16

The waters saw You, O God;  
The waters saw You; they were afraid;  
Indeed the depths trembled.

## v17

The clouds poured forth water;  
The skies sent forth a sound;  
Indeed Your arrows went here and there.

## v18

The sound of Your thunder was in the whirlwind;  
Lightning illuminated the world;  
The earth trembled and quaked.

## v19

Your way was in the sea,  
And Your paths in the great waters;  
And Your footsteps were not known.

## v20

You led Your people like a flock  
By the hand of Moses and Aaron.
