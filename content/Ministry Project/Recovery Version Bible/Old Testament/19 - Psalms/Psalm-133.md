> *A Song of Ascents. Of David*

## v1

[[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Behold|Behold]], [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 1.2 — how|how]] good and [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 1.2 — how|how]] pleasant it is / For brothers to dwell in [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 1.3 — unity|unity]]!

## v2

It is like the fine [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 2.1 — oil|oil]] upon the head / That [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 2.2 — ran|ran]] down upon the beard, / Upon Aaron’s beard, / That ran down upon the hem of his garments;

## v3

Like the [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 3.1 — dew|dew]] of Hermon / That came down upon the [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 3.2 — mountains|mountains]] of Zion. / For there Jehovah commanded the blessing: / [[Psalm-133.fn#Footnote 3.3 — Life|Life]] forever.
