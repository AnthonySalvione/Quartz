## v1

[[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Why|Why]] are the [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — nations|nations]] in an uproar,  
And why do the [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — nations|peoples]] contemplate a vain thing?

## v2

The kings of the earth take their stand,  
And the rulers sit in counsel together,  
Against Jehovah and against His [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Anointed|Anointed]]:

## v3

Let us break apart Their bonds  
And cast Their ropes away from us.

## v4

He who sits in the heavens laughs;  
The [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Lord|Lord]] has them in derision.

## v5

Then He will speak to them in His anger,  
And in His burning wrath He will terrify them:

## v6

But I have installed My [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — King|King]]  
Upon [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Zion|Zion]], My holy mountain.

## v7

I will recount the decree of Jehovah;  
He said to Me: [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — You|You]] are My Son;  
Today I have begotten You.

## v8

Ask of Me,  
And I will give the nations as Your inheritance  
And the limits of the earth as Your possession.

## v9

You will break them with an iron rod;  
You will shatter them like a potter’s vessel.

## v10

[[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Now|Now]] therefore, O kings, be prudent;  
Take the admonition, O judges of the earth.

## v11

Serve Jehovah with fear,  
And rejoice with trembling.

## v12

[[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Kiss|Kiss]] the Son  
Lest He be angry and you perish from the way;  
For His anger may suddenly be kindled.  
[[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — Blessed|Blessed]] are all those who [[Psalm-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Kiss|take]] refuge in Him.
