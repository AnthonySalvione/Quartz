## v1

O Jehovah, do not rebuke me in Your anger,  
Nor chasten me in Your burning wrath.

## v2

Be gracious to me, O Jehovah, for I am languishing;  
Heal me, O Jehovah, for my bones are shaken,

## v3

And my soul is greatly shaken.  
But as for You, O Jehovah, how long?

## v4

Return, O Jehovah; deliver my soul;  
Save me for Your lovingkindness’ sake.

## v5

For in death there is no remembrance of You;  
In Sheol who can give thanks to You?

## v6

I am weary with my groaning;  
Every night I cause my bed to swim;  
With my tears I dissolve my couch.

## v7

My eye is wasted because of grief;  
It has aged because of all those who vex me.

## v8

Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity,  
For Jehovah has heard the sound of my weeping.

## v9

Jehovah has heard my supplication;  
Jehovah receives my prayer.

## v10

All my enemies will be ashamed and greatly dismayed;  
They will turn back; they will be put to shame suddenly.
