## v1

O God of my praise, do not be silent;

## v2

For the mouth of the wicked man and the mouth of deceit are opened against me;  
They speak to me with a lying tongue.

## v3

And with words of hatred they have surrounded me  
And have fought against me without cause.

## v4

In return for my love they have become my adversaries,  
But I am all prayer.

## v5

And they have set against me evil in return for good  
And hatred in return for my love.

## v6

Appoint a wicked man over him,  
And let an adversary stand at his right hand.

## v7

When he is judged, let him come forth wicked;  
And let his prayer be counted as sin.

## v8

May his days be few;  
May another take his office.

## v9

May his children be orphans,  
And his wife a widow.

## v10

And may his children wander all about and beg,  
And may they seek food far from their desolated ruins.

## v11

May the creditor seize all that he has,  
And may strangers plunder the fruit of his labor.

## v12

May there be no one to extend him mercy,  
Nor anyone to be kind to his orphans.

## v13

May his posterity be cut off;  
In the generation following may his name be blotted out.

## v14

May his fathers’ iniquity be remembered before Jehovah,  
And may his mother’s sin not be blotted out.

## v15

May they be before Jehovah continually,  
So that He may cut off the memory of them from the earth;

## v16

Because he did not remember to show lovingkindness,  
But persecuted the poor and needy and brokenhearted,  
To put them to death.

## v17

Indeed he loved cursing, so it came on him;  
And he took no delight in blessing, so it was far from him.

## v18

And he clothed himself with cursing like his garment,  
So it came into his inward parts like water  
And into his bones like oil.

## v19

Let it be to him like the cloak that he wraps around himself,  
And as a belt with which he constantly girds himself.

## v20

This is the reward from Jehovah for my adversaries  
And for those who speak evil against my soul.

## v21

But You, O Jehovah Lord, deal with me,  
For Your name’s sake;  
Because Your lovingkindness is good, deliver me.

## v22

For I am poor and needy,  
And my heart is wounded within me.

## v23

I am gone like a shadow that declines;  
I am shaken off like the locust.

## v24

My knees wobble through fasting,  
And my flesh has become lean, without fatness.

## v25

I have also become a reproach to them:  
When they see me, they shake their heads.

## v26

Help me, O Jehovah my God;  
Save me according to Your lovingkindness;

## v27

And they will know that this is Your hand,  
That it is You, O Jehovah, who have done it.

## v28

Let them curse, but You bless;  
When they arise, they will be put to shame,  
And Your servant will rejoice.

## v29

May my adversaries be clothed with humiliation,  
And may they wrap themselves with their own shame as with a mantle.

## v30

I will give much thanks to Jehovah with my mouth,  
And in the midst of the multitude I will praise Him;

## v31

For He stands at the right hand of the needy  
To save him from those who judge his soul.
