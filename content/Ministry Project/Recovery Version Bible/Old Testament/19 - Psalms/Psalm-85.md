## v1

You have been pleased, O Jehovah, with Your land;  
You have turned the captivity of Jacob.

## v2

You have forgiven the iniquity of Your people;  
You have covered all their sin. Selah

## v3

You have taken away all Your wrath;  
You have turned from the fierceness of Your anger.

## v4

Restore us, O God of our salvation,  
And cause Your indignation toward us to cease.

## v5

Will You be angry with us forever?  
Will You extend Your anger from generation to generation?

## v6

Will You not again revive us,  
That Your people may rejoice in You?

## v7

Show us, O Jehovah, Your lovingkindness,  
And grant us Your salvation.

## v8

I will hear what God Jehovah will speak,  
For He will speak peace  
To His people and to His faithful ones;  
But let them not turn again unto folly.

## v9

Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him,  
That glory may dwell in our land.

## v10

Lovingkindness and truth have met together;  
Righteousness and peace have kissed each other.

## v11

Truth has sprung forth from the earth,  
And righteousness has looked down from heaven.

## v12

Indeed Jehovah will give what is good,  
And our land will give its increase.

## v13

Righteousness will go before Him  
And make His footsteps a way to walk in.
