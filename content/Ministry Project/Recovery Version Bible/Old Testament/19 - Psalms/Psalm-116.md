## v1

I love Jehovah because He hears  
My voice, my supplications,

## v2

Because He inclines His ear to me;  
Therefore I will call upon Him all my days.

## v3

The bonds of death encompassed me,  
And the distresses of Sheol fell upon me;  
I fell upon trouble and sorrow.

## v4

But I called upon the name of Jehovah.  
O Jehovah, I pray, deliver my soul.

## v5

Jehovah is gracious and righteous,  
Indeed our God is compassionate.

## v6

Jehovah preserves the simple:  
I was brought low, and He saved me.

## v7

Return to your rest, O my soul;  
For Jehovah has dealt bountifully with you.

## v8

For You have delivered my soul from death,  
My eyes from tears,  
My feet from stumbling.

## v9

I will walk before Jehovah  
In the land of the living.

## v10

I believed, therefore I spoke;  
I was afflicted greatly.

## v11

I said in my haste,  
All men are liars.

## v12

What shall I return to Jehovah  
For all His benefits toward me?

## v13

I will take up the cup of salvation  
And call upon the name of Jehovah.

## v14

I will pay my vows to Jehovah,  
Oh, in the presence of all His people.

## v15

Precious in the sight of Jehovah  
Is the death of His faithful ones.

## v16

O Jehovah!  
Surely I am Your servant;  
I am Your servant, the son of Your female servant;  
You have loosed my bonds.

## v17

To You I will offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving,  
And I will call upon the name of Jehovah.

## v18

I will pay my vows to Jehovah,  
Oh, in the presence of all His people,

## v19

In the courts of the house of Jehovah,  
In the midst of you, O Jerusalem.  
Hallelujah.
