### Footnote 2.1 — gracious

**Verse:** [[Psalm-123#v2|Psalm 123:2]]

[[Psalm-123|Psalm 123]] is the praise of a saint, in his going up to Zion, concerning God’s being gracious to the returned captives. It is not a matter of our loving God’s law but of His being gracious to us.
