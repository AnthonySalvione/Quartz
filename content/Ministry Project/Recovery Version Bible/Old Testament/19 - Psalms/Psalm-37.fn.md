# Psalm-37 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-37|Psalm 37]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Do

**Verse:** [[Psalm-37#v1|Psalm 37:1]]

This psalm is one of the alphabetic, or acrostic, psalms. See [note 1¹](<Psalm-09.fn#Footnote 1.1 — I>) in [[Psalm-09|Psa. 9]]. It reveals David’s sentiment in his logic concerning God’s dealing with the righteous and with the wicked based on the principle of law-keeping, which belongs to the tree of the knowledge of good and evil (see [note 9³](<Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — tree>) in [[Genesis-02|Gen. 2]]).
