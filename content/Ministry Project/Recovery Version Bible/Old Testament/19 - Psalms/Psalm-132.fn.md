# Psalm-132 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-132|Psalm 132]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — David’s

**Verse:** [[Psalm-132#v1|Psalm 132:1]]

[[Psalm-132|Psalm 132]] is the praise of a saint, in his going up to Zion, concerning Jehovah’s habitation and rest in Zion through David (typifying Christ) His anointed.

### Footnote 1.2 — afflictions

**Verse:** [[Psalm-132#v1|Psalm 132:1]]

Typifying all the afflictions of Christ.

As Jehovah’s resting place ([[Psalm-132#v14|v. 14]]), Zion typifies the overcomers in the churches. See [note 2¹](<Psalm-48.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Zion>) in [[Psalm-48|Psa. 48]].

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — This

**Verse:** [[Psalm-132#v14|Psalm 132:14]]

While the psalmist was speaking of God’s choosing and desiring Zion in [[Psalm-132#v13|v. 13]], God suddenly intervened to continue the psalmist’s speaking in [[Psalm-132#v14|vv. 14-18]]. This is the wonderful way in which the Psalms were written.

### Footnote 14.2 — resting

**Verse:** [[Psalm-132#v14|Psalm 132:14]]

[[Psalm-132|Psalm 132]] mentions seven items related to the overcomers: resting with God, dwelling with God ([[Psalm-132#v14|v. 14]]), food for satisfaction ([[Psalm-132#v15|v. 15]]), glorious clothing ([[Psalm-132#v16|v. 16]]), the horn of victory ([[Psalm-132#v17|v. 17]]), the enlightening lamp ([[Psalm-132#v17|v. 17]]), and the shining crown ([[Psalm-132#v18|v. 18]]). These items are at the top of the church life. They portray the situation of the overcomers in Zion, the highest peak of God’s mountain.
