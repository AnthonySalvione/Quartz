## v1

Sing to Jehovah a new song,  
For He has done wonderful things.  
His right hand and His holy arm  
Have accomplished salvation for Him.

## v2

Jehovah has made His salvation known;  
In the sight of the nations  
He has revealed His righteousness.

## v3

He remembered His lovingkindness and His faithfulness  
Toward the house of Israel;  
All the ends of the earth have seen  
The salvation of our God.

## v4

Make a joyful noise to Jehovah, all the earth;  
Break forth, and sing for joy, and sing psalms.

## v5

Sing psalms to Jehovah with the lyre,  
With the lyre and the sound of melody.

## v6

With trumpets and the sound of the horn  
Make a joyful noise before the King, Jehovah.

## v7

Let the sea and its fullness roar,  
The world and those who inhabit it.

## v8

Let the rivers clap their hands;  
Let the mountains sing for joy together.

## v9

Before Jehovah, for He is coming  
To judge the earth.  
He will judge the world with righteousness  
And the peoples with equity.
