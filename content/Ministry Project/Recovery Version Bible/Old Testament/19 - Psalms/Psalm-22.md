> *To the choir director: according to the hind of the dawn. A Psalm of David*

> [!light] Note on *hind:*
>  Perhaps a reference to a melody common at that time. The hind of the dawn here signifies Christ in His resurrection, which took place at early dawn ([[Luke-24#v1|Luke 24:1]]). A hind is a deer known for its leaping and jumping. Christ in resurrection is the leaping One ([[Song-02#v8|S.S. 2:8-9]]).

---
## v1

[[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 1.1 — My|My]] God, my God, why have You [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 1.2 — forsaken|forsaken]] me? / [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Why|Why]] are You so far from saving me, / From the words of my groaning?

## v2

O my God, I call out in the day, but You do not answer; / And in the night, but there is no rest for me.

## v3

But You are holy, You who sit enthroned / Upon the praises of Israel.

## v4

In You our fathers trusted; / They trusted and You rescued them.

## v5

To You they cried out and were delivered; / In You they trusted and were not put to shame.

## v6

[[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 6.1 — But|But]] I am a worm and not a man, / A reproach of men and one despised by the people.

## v7

All who see me deride me; / They sneer at me; they shake their heads, saying,

## v8

[[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 8.1 — He|He]] committed himself to Jehovah; let Him rescue him; / Let Him deliver him since He takes pleasure in him.

## v9

[[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 9.1 — But|But]] You are the One who drew me forth from the womb, / Who made me trust while at my mother’s breasts.

## v10

I was cast upon You from birth; / From my mother’s womb You have been my God.

## v11

Do not be far from me, / For trouble is near, / For there is none to help me.

## v12

[[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Many|Many]] bulls surround me; / The mighty bulls of Bashan encompass me.

## v13

They open their mouth at me, / Like a ravening and roaring lion.

## v14

I am poured out like water, / And all my bones are out of joint. / My heart is like wax; / It is melted within [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 14.1 — me|me]].

## v15

My strength is dried up like a shard, / And my tongue is stuck to my jaws; / [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 15.1 — You|You]] have put me in the dust of death.

## v16

For dogs surround me; / A company of evildoers encloses me; / [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 16.1 — They|They]] pierce my hands and feet.

## v17

I count all my bones; / They look, they stare at me.

## v18

They divide my garments to themselves, / And for my clothing they cast lots.

## v19

[[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 19.1 — But|But]] You, O Jehovah, be not far off; / O my help, hasten to aid me.

## v20

Deliver my soul from the sword, / [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 20.1 — My|My]] precious life from the power of the dog;

## v21

Save me from the mouth of the lion; / Indeed [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 21.1 — while|while]] I am in the horns of wild oxen, answer me.

## v22

I will declare Your name to my [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 22.1 — brothers|brothers]]; / In the midst of the [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 22.2 — assembly|assembly]] I will praise [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 22.3 — You|You]].

## v23

You who fear Jehovah, [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 23.1 — praise|praise]] Him! / All you seed of Jacob, glorify Him! / And stand in awe of Him, all you seed of Israel!

## v24

For He has not despised nor detested / The affliction of him who is afflicted; / And He has not hidden His face from him; / But when he cried out to Him, He heard.

## v25

From You comes my praise in the great assembly; / My vows will I pay before those who fear Him.

## v26

The lowly will eat and be satisfied; / Those who seek after Jehovah will praise Him — / May your hearts live forever!

## v27

All the ends of the earth / Will remember and return to Jehovah, / And all families of the [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 27.1 — nations|nations]] / Will worship before You;

## v28

 For the kingdom is Jehovah’s, / And [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 28.1 — He|He]] rules among the nations.

## v29

All the flourishing of the earth / Will eat and worship. / All who go down into the dust will bow down before Him, / Even he who does not keep his soul alive.

## v30

A [[Psalm-22.fn#Footnote 30.1 — seed|seed]] will serve Him; / That which concerns the Lord will be told to a coming generation.

## v31

They will come and declare His righteousness / To a people yet to be born, that He has done this.
