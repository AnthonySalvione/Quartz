## v1

O God, You have rejected us; You have broken out upon us;  
You have been angry. Restore us.

## v2

You have caused the earth to quake; You have split it open.  
Heal the breaks in it, for it totters.

## v3

You have shown Your people hard things;  
You have made us drink wine to stagger us.

## v4

You have given a banner to those who fear You,  
To be displayed because of the truth. Selah

## v5

That Your beloved ones may be delivered,  
Save with Your right hand, and answer us.

## v6

God has spoken in His holiness: I will exult;  
I will divide Shechem  
And measure out the Valley of Succoth.

## v7

Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine,  
And Ephraim is the helmet for My head;  
Judah is My scepter.

## v8

Moab is My washbowl;  
Upon Edom I will cast My sandal;  
Philistia, shout aloud because of Me.

## v9

Who will bring me into the besieged city?  
Who will lead me to Edom?

## v10

Will not You, O God, who rejected us  
And who did not go forth, O God, with our armies?

## v11

Give us help from the adversary,  
For a salvation by man is vain.

## v12

In God we will do valiantly,  
And it is He who will tread down our adversaries.
