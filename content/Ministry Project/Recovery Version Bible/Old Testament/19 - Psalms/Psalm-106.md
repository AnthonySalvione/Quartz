## v1

Hallelujah.  
Give thanks to Jehovah, for He is good,  
For His lovingkindness is forever.

## v2

Who can utter the mighty acts of Jehovah,  
Or declare all His praise?

## v3

Blessed are those who maintain justice,  
Who execute righteousness at all times.

## v4

Remember me, O Jehovah, with Your favor toward Your people;  
Visit me with Your salvation,

## v5

That I may see the prosperity of Your chosen ones,  
That I may rejoice in the joy of Your nation,  
That I may glory with Your inheritance.

## v6

We have sinned with our fathers;  
We committed iniquity; we have acted wickedly.

## v7

Our fathers in Egypt  
Did not understand Your wonders;  
They did not remember the multitude of Your acts of lovingkindness,  
But rebelled by the sea, at the Red Sea.

## v8

But He saved them for His name’s sake,  
That He might make His power known.

## v9

Thus He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up;  
And He led them through the depths as through the wilderness.

## v10

And He saved them from the hand of him who hated them,  
And redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

## v11

The water then covered their adversaries;  
Not one of them was left.

## v12

Then they believed His words;  
They sang His praise.

## v13

Soon they forgot His works;  
They did not wait for His counsel,

## v14

But lusted exceedingly in the wilderness,  
And tempted God in the desert.

## v15

And He gave them their request,  
But He sent leanness into their soul.

## v16

And they were jealous of Moses in the camp  
And of Aaron, the holy one of Jehovah.

## v17

The earth opened, and it swallowed up Dathan  
And covered the company of Abiram.

## v18

And a fire burned among their company;  
A flame engulfed the wicked.

## v19

They made a calf in Horeb,  
And worshipped a molten image.

## v20

Thus they exchanged their glory  
For the image of an ox that eats grass.

## v21

They forgot God their Savior,  
Who had done great things in Egypt,

## v22

Wondrous deeds in the land of Ham,  
Awesome things by the Red Sea.

## v23

Therefore He said that He would destroy them,  
And would have, had not Moses His chosen one  
Stood in the breach before Him  
To turn away His wrath from destroying them.

## v24

Then they despised the pleasant land;  
They did not believe in His word;

## v25

Rather they murmured in their tents;  
They did not listen to the voice of Jehovah.

## v26

Therefore He lifted up His hand and swore to them  
That He would make them fall in the wilderness,

## v27

And that He would make their seed fall among the nations,  
And that He would disperse them among the lands.

## v28

Then they joined themselves to Baal-peor,  
And ate sacrifices offered to the dead.

## v29

Thus they provoked Him to anger by their actions,  
And the plague broke out among them.

## v30

Then Phinehas stood up and intervened,  
And the plague was stayed;

## v31

And it has been accounted to him as righteousness  
From generation to generation unto eternity.

## v32

Then they angered Him at the waters of Meribah,  
So that it went ill with Moses on account of them;

## v33

For they rebelled against His Spirit,  
And he spoke rashly with his lips.

## v34

They did not destroy the peoples,  
As Jehovah had commanded them;

## v35

But they mingled themselves with the nations  
And learned their practices.

## v36

And they served their idols,  
Which became a snare to them.

## v37

They also sacrificed their sons  
And their daughters to demons,

## v38

And poured out innocent blood,  
The blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan;  
And the land was polluted with bloodshed.

## v39

Thus they were defiled by their practices,  
And went about as harlots by their actions.

## v40

And the anger of Jehovah was kindled against His people,  
And He abhorred His inheritance.

## v41

And He gave them over into the hand of the nations:  
And those who hated them ruled over them,

## v42

And their enemies oppressed them,  
And they were subdued under their hand.

## v43

Many times He delivered them,  
But they were rebellious in their counsel,  
And they were brought low in their iniquity.

## v44

Yet He regarded their distress  
When He heard their cry,

## v45

And for their sake He remembered His covenant,  
And repented according to the multitude of His acts of lovingkindness;

## v46

And He caused them to find compassion  
With all their captors.

## v47

Save us, O Jehovah our God;  
And gather us out of the nations,  
That we may give thanks to Your holy name,  
That we may glory in Your praise.

## v48

Blessed be Jehovah the God of Israel,  
From eternity to eternity.  
And let all the people say, Amen.  
Hallelujah.
