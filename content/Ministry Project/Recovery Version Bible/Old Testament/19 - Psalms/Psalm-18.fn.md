## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — recompensed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-18#v20|Psalm 18:20]]

In [[Psalm-18#v20|vv. 20-28]] David considered his righteousness, perfection, faithfulness, cleanness, and purity as the cause of God’s saving him, and he considered God’s salvation a recompense to him. This is a wrong concept. God saves us not because of our righteousness but because of His mercy and His compassion ([[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22]]; [[Ephesians-02#v1|Eph. 2:1-9]]; [[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5]]) and because of His own purpose ([[2 Timothy-01#v9|2 Tim. 1:9]]).
