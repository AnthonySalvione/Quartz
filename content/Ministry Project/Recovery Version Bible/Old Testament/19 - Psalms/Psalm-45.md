> *To the choir director: according to Shoshannim. Of the sons of Korah. A Maschil; a song of love*

> [!light] Note on Shoshannim:
> Meaning lilies; it may refer to a melody common at that time.

> [!light] Note on Maschil:
> See note on the superscription of [[Psalm-32|Psa. 32]].

> [!light] Note on love:
>  The Hebrew word is feminine, indicating that the love here is that between a male and a female. According to [[Song-01#v14|S.S. 1:14-15]], Christ is our Beloved, and we are His love. The subject of this psalm is love, and the melody is called “lilies.” Here both love and lilies refer to the saints, the lovers of the Lord Jesus. A lily denotes a pure, simple, single life of trusting in God ([[Matthew-06#v28|Matt. 6:28]]). This psalm portrays a life of purity and simplicity with an affectionate love for the Lord.

## v1

My heart overflows with a good matter; / I speak what I have composed concerning the [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 1.1 — King|King]]. / My [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 1.2 — tongue|tongue]] is the pen of a ready writer.

## v2

You are [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 2.1 — fairer|fairer]] than the sons of men; / Grace is poured upon Your lips; / Therefore God has blessed You forever.

## v3

Gird Your sword upon Your thigh, O mighty One, / In Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 3.1 — majesty|majesty]] and Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 3.1 — majesty|splendor]].

## v4

And in Your splendor ride on [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 4.1 — victoriously|victoriously]] / Because of truth and [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 4.2 — meekness|meekness]] and righteousness; / And let Your right hand [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 4.3 — teach|teach]] You [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 4.4 — awesome|awesome]] deeds.

## v5

Your arrows are sharp: / The peoples fall under You; / The arrows are in the heart of the King’s enemies.

## v6

Your throne, O [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 6.1 — God|God]], is forever and ever; / The [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 6.2 — scepter|scepter]] of uprightness is the scepter of Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 6.3 — kingdom|kingdom]].

## v7

You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; / Therefore God, [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Your|Your]] God, has anointed You / With the [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 7.2 — oil|oil]] of gladness above Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 7.2 — oil|companions]].

## v8

All Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 8.1 — garments|garments]] smell of myrrh and aloes, of cassia; / From [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 8.2 — palaces|palaces]] of ivory, harpstrings have made You glad.

## v9

[[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 9.1 — The|The]] [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 9.2 — daughters|daughters]] of kings are among Your most prized; / The [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 9.3 — queen|queen]] stands at Your right hand in the [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 9.4 — gold|gold]] of Ophir.

## v10

Hear, O [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 10.1 — daughter|daughter]], and see; and incline your ear; / And forget your people and your father’s house;

## v11

Thus the King will desire your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 11.1 — beauty|beauty]]. / Because He is your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 11.2 — Lord|Lord]], / Worship Him.

## v12

And the [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 12.1 — daughter|daughter]] of Tyre will come with a gift; / The [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 12.1 — daughter|rich]] among the people will entreat your favor.

## v13

The king’s [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 13.1 — daughter|daughter]] is all glorious within the royal abode; / Her garment is a [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 13.2 — woven|woven]] work inwrought with gold.

## v14

She will be led to the King in [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 14.1 — embroidered|embroidered]] clothing; / The [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 14.2 — virgins|virgins]] behind her, her [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 14.2 — virgins|companions]], / Will be brought to You.

## v15

They will be led with rejoicing and exultation; / They will enter the King’s [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 15.1 — palace|palace]].

## v16

In the place of Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fathers|fathers]] will be Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fathers|sons]]; / You will make them [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fathers|princes]] in all the earth.

## v17

I will cause Your [[Psalm-45.fn#Footnote 17.1 — name|name]] to be remembered in all generations; / Therefore the peoples will praise You forever and ever.
