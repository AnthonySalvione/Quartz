## v1

Why do you boast in evil, O mighty one?  
God’s lovingkindness endures continually.

## v2

Your tongue devises destruction;  
It is like a sharpened razor, O worker of deceit.

## v3

You have loved evil rather than good,  
Falsehood rather than speaking righteousness. Selah

## v4

You have loved all the devouring words,  
O deceitful tongue.

## v5

God will likewise break you down forever;  
He will take you and pluck you up from your tent,  
And uproot you from the land of the living. Selah

## v6

And the righteous will see it and fear,  
And they will laugh at him, saying,

## v7

Behold, this is that mighty man  
Who did not make God his strength,  
But trusted in the multitude of his riches  
And strengthened himself in his desire.

## v8

But I, like a flourishing olive tree  
In the house of God,  
Trust in God’s lovingkindness  
Forever and ever.

## v9

I will give You thanks forever,  
For You have acted;  
And I will hope in Your name in the presence of Your faithful ones,  
For it is good.
