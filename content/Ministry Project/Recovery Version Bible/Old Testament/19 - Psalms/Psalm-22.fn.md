# Psalm-22 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-22|Psalm 22]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — My

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v1|Psalm 22:1]]

[[Psalm-22|Psalms 22—24]] are a group of psalms revealing Christ from His crucifixion to His kingship in the coming age. [[Psalm-22|Psalm 22]] concerns Christ’s death, His resurrection, and His many brothers produced in His resurrection to form His church. [[Psalm-23|Psalm 23]] concerns Christ as the Shepherd in His resurrection. [[Psalm-24|Psalm 24]] concerns Christ as the coming King in God’s kingdom. 

The subject of [[Psalm-22|Psa. 22]] is the Christ who passed through the redeeming death and entered into the church-producing resurrection. [[Psalm-22#v1|Verses 1-21]] give a detailed picture of Christ in His suffering of death (cf. [[Isaiah-53|Isa. 53]]), as typified by David in his suffering, and [[Psalm-22#v22|vv. 22-31]] refer to Christ in His resurrection, as typified by Solomon in his kingly reign.

### Footnote 1.2 — forsaken

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v1|Psalm 22:1]]

The question at the beginning of this psalm was spoken by David in his suffering, but it became a prophecy concerning Christ in His suffering of His redeeming death. While Christ was on the earth, God the Father was with Him all the time ([[John-08#v29|John 8:29]]), but at a certain point in His crucifixion, God left Him ([[Matthew-27#v45|Matt. 27:45-46]]). God’s leaving Him was economical, not essential. God could never leave Christ essentially, but economically God forsook Him for a time. Nevertheless, Christ died as the Son of God, a God-man; hence, in His death there is a divine and eternal element ([[Acts-20#v28|Acts 20:28]]; [[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]]; [[Hebrews-09#v12|Heb. 9:12]]). See notes [22¹](<Luke-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Holy>) in [[Luke-03|Luke 3]], [45¹](<Matthew-27.fn#Footnote 45.1 — sixth>) and [46²](<Matthew-27.fn#Footnote 46.2 — why>) in [[Matthew-27|Matt. 27]], and [7⁴](<1 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.4 — Jesus>), par. 1, in [[1 John-01|1 John 1]].

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — But

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v6|Psalm 22:6]]

[[Psalm-22#v6|Verses 6-8]] display Christ’s suffering unto death through men’s reproach, despising, deriding, sneering, head-shaking, and mocking ([[Hebrews-13#v13|Heb. 13:13b]]; [[Isaiah-53#v3|Isa. 53:3]]; [[Luke-23#v11|Luke 23:11]]; [[Mark-15#v29|Mark 15:29-32]]; [[Matthew-27#v39|Matt. 27:39-44]]). To deride is to make fun of or laugh at in contempt. To sneer is to smile or laugh with facial expressions that show scorn or contempt. When Christ was on the cross, the ridiculers also shook, or wagged, their heads in scorn. To mock is to hold up to scorn or contempt and to imitate or mimic in derision. All these things were suffered by the Lord Jesus Christ while He was nailed on the cross.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — Many

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v12|Psalm 22:12]]

[[Psalm-22#v12|Verses 12-18]] depict in vivid detail how Christ passed through His suffering of crucifixion (cf. [[Mark-15#v16|Mark 15:16-37]]). While Christ was being crucified on the cross, many fierce men, signified by mighty bulls, encompassed Him ([[Psalm-22#v12|v. 12]]). They opened their mouth at Him like a ravening and roaring lion ([[Psalm-22#v13|v. 13]]). Evil men, signified by dogs (cf. [[Philippians-03#v2|Phil. 3:2a]]), surrounded Him, and a congregation of evildoers enclosed Him ([[Psalm-22#v16|v. 16a]]-b). They pierced His hands and feet ([[Psalm-22#v16|v. 16c]]). They divided His garments to themselves, and for His clothing they cast lots ([[Psalm-22#v18|v. 18]]). They also looked, stared at Him with contempt and hatred ([[Psalm-22#v17|v. 17b]]). On the cross He was poured out like water ([[Psalm-22#v14|v. 14a]]; [[Isaiah-53#v12|Isa. 53:12]]). All His bones were out of joint ([[Psalm-22#v14|v. 14b]]) because He could not hold up the weight of His body as it hung on the cross. This caused Him great agony and pain. Also, He counted all His bones ([[Psalm-22#v17|v. 17a]]). His heart was like wax melted within Him ([[Psalm-22#v14|v. 14c]]-d). His strength was dried up like a shard ([[Psalm-22#v15|v. 15a]]), a piece of broken pottery. His tongue was stuck to His jaws ([[Psalm-22#v15|v. 15b]]). God had put Him in the dust of death ([[Psalm-22#v15|v. 15c]]).

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — You

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v15|Psalm 22:15]]

Referring to God. On one hand, man crucified the Lord Jesus; on the other hand, God killed Him. If Jesus had been killed only by man, He would have been merely a martyr and not our Redeemer. But God judged Him and put Him into death for our redemption ([[Isaiah-53#v10|Isa. 53:10a]]). All the sin of the world was laid on Christ as the Lamb of God ([[Isaiah-53#v6|Isa. 53:6b]]; [[1 Peter-02#v24|1 Pet. 2:24a]]; [[John-01#v29|John 1:29]]). On the cross He died a vicarious death for us ([[2 Corinthians-05#v21|2 Cor. 5:21]]; [[1 Peter-03#v18|1 Pet. 3:18]]) to redeem us from our sins, from God’s judgment, and from eternal perdition ([[1 Corinthians-15#v3|1 Cor. 15:3]]; [[John-03#v16|John 3:16-17]]; [[John-05#v24|5:24]]).

## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — brothers

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v22|Psalm 22:22]]

After passing through His redeeming death, Christ entered into the church-producing resurrection. It was in His resurrection that Christ called His disciples His brothers ([[John-20#v17|John 20:17]]; [[Matthew-28#v10|Matt. 28:10]]; [[Hebrews-02#v11|Heb. 2:11]]), for in God’s eternal view His disciples were regenerated and became God’s sons in Christ’s resurrection ([[1 Peter-01#v3|1 Pet. 1:3]]). In His resurrection Christ Himself was begotten to be God’s firstborn Son ([[Psalm-02#v7|2:7]]; [[Acts-13#v33|Acts 13:33]]) and became the life-giving Spirit ([[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]), and all God’s chosen and redeemed people were regenerated to be the many sons of God, the many brothers of Christ ([[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10-12]]; [[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]).

### Footnote 22.2 — assembly

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v22|Psalm 22:22]]

The assembly here signifies the church, indicating that the Lord’s brothers constitute the church ([[Hebrews-02#v11|Heb. 2:11-12]]). Thus, His resurrection is the church-producing resurrection.

### Footnote 22.3 — You

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v22|Psalm 22:22]]

You and Your in this verse refer to the Father. In resurrection Christ declared the Father’s name to His brothers and praised the Father in the church ([[Hebrews-02#v12|Heb. 2:12]] and [note 3](<Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — I>)).

## Footnotes for verse 23

### Footnote 23.1 — praise

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v23|Psalm 22:23]]

Following his praise to God in the assembly, David advised God’s people to praise Jehovah and all the earth to worship Him ([[Psalm-22#v23|vv. 23-26]], [[Psalm-22#v29|29-31]]). Christ took the lead in praising God in the church, and the church follows Him to praise God. Now Israel should follow Christ and the church. Thus far, Israel has not followed, but when Christ comes back, all Israel will repent and be saved ([[Zechariah-12#v10|Zech. 12:10]]; [[Romans-11#v26|Rom. 11:26-27]]). Then they will join the church to praise God.

## Footnotes for verse 27

### Footnote 27.1 — nations

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v27|Psalm 22:27]]

The church ushers in Christ’s kingdom for Christ to rule over the nations ([[Psalm-22#v27|vv. 27-28]]). The church, produced by Christ’s resurrection, is the reality of the kingdom ([[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18-19]]; [[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]) and a precursor to the manifestation of the kingdom in the millennium.

## Footnotes for verse 28

### Footnote 28.1 — He

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v28|Psalm 22:28]]

Jehovah as Christ will rule over the nations in the millennial kingdom ([[Psalm-02#v8|2:8-9]]; [[Revelation-19#v15|Rev. 19:15]]; [[Revelation-20#v4|20:4]], [[Revelation-20#v6|6]]).

## Footnotes for verse 30

### Footnote 30.1 — seed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-22#v30|Psalm 22:30]]

The believers are the seed of Christ, and their declaring the Lord’s righteousness (justification, salvation) to a coming generation ([[Psalm-22#v30|vv. 30-31]]) refers to the preaching of the gospel.
