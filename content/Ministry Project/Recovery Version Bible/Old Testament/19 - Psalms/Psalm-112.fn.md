### Footnote 1.1 — Hallelujah

**Verse:** [[Psalm-112#v1|Psalm 112:1]]

See [note 35¹](<Psalm-104.fn#Footnote 35.1 — Hallelujah>) in [[Psalm-104|Psa. 104]]. [[Psalm-112|Psalm 112]] is the praise of God’s elect for God’s blessing given because of their fearing Him ([[Psalm-112#v1|vv. 1-2]]) and because of their virtues, mainly in their giving to the poor ([[Psalm-112#v4|vv. 4-5]], [[Psalm-112#v9|9]]; cf. [[2 Corinthians-09#v6|2 Cor. 9:6-11]]).

### Footnote 1.2 — Blessed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-112#v1|Psalm 112:1]]

[[Psalm-112|Psalm 112]] is one of the alphabetical, or acrostic, psalms. The first letter of each line, beginning with this line, follows the order of the Hebrew alphabet.
