# Psalm-77 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-77|Psalm 77]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — My

**Verse:** [[Psalm-77#v1|Psalm 77:1]]

[[Psalm-77|Psalms 77—83]] form a cluster of psalms that cover the devastation of three holy things—the holy temple, the holy city, and the holy people. In particular, these psalms reveal the way of restoration—to exalt Christ. These seven psalms were written by Asaph, a Levite who had served in the temple. The fact that these psalms speak of the desolation of the temple, the city of Jerusalem, and the people indicates that they were written after the children of Israel had been taken captive to Babylon.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — cast

**Verse:** [[Psalm-77#v7|Psalm 77:7]]

The psalmist was puzzled and complained to God because God had cast off His people for a while ([[Psalm-77#v1|vv. 1-9]]). God has chosen us, and He will never forsake us ([[Deuteronomy-31#v6|Deut. 31:6]]; [[Joshua-01#v5|Josh. 1:5]]; [[Hebrews-13#v5|Heb. 13:5]]). He may allow us to depart from Him for a time; then He touches us, and we come back to Him (cf. [[Romans-11#v24|Rom. 11:24-25]]).

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — But

**Verse:** [[Psalm-77#v10|Psalm 77:10]]

In [[Psalm-77#v10|vv. 10b-20]] the psalmist recalled the past and mused on God’s wondrous doings for His people. In this way he introduced the subject of restoration. The temple had been destroyed, the city had been devastated, and many of the people had been either killed or taken captive. This does not mean, however, that Israel is finished, for there will be restoration through the exalting of Christ (see [note 17¹](<Psalm-80.fn#Footnote 17.1 — man>) in [[Psalm-80|Psa. 80]]).

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — sanctuary

**Verse:** [[Psalm-77#v13|Psalm 77:13]]

God’s way is hidden in the sea, and His paths in the great waters, with His footsteps, are not known to men ([[Psalm-77#v19|v. 19]]), but His way is revealed in His sanctuary, i.e., in our spirit and in the church ([[Ephesians-02#v22|Eph. 2:22]]; [[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15]]). When we exercise our spirit and live in the church, God’s way becomes clear to us. See [note 17¹](<Psalm-73.fn#Footnote 17.1 — sanctuary>) in [[Psalm-73|Psa. 73]].
