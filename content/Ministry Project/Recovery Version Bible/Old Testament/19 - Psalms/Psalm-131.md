> *A Song of Ascents. Of David*

---
## v1

O Jehovah, my [[Psalm-131.fn#Footnote 1.1 — heart|heart]] is not proud, / Nor are my eyes haughty; / Nor do I go about in things too great / Or too wondrous for me.

## v2

Surely I have calmed / And quieted my soul, / Like a [[Psalm-131.fn#Footnote 2.1 — weaned|weaned]] child with its mother; / Like a weaned child is my soul within me.

## v3

Hope in Jehovah, O Israel, / From now and to eternity.
