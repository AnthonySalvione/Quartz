# Psalm-129 — Footnotes

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — hate

**Verse:** [[Psalm-129#v5|Psalm 129:5]]

[[Psalm-129|Psalm 129]] is the praise of a saint, in his going up to Zion, concerning God’s righteous dealing with the persecutors of Israel and the haters of Zion. Here Israel signifies the New Testament believers ([[Galatians-06#v16|Gal. 6:16]]), and Zion signifies the overcomers in the church (see [note 2¹](<Psalm-48.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Zion>) in [[Psalm-48|Psa. 48]]). The more we love the Lord Jesus, the more we will be hated and persecuted ([[John-15#v18|John 15:18-20a]]).
