# Psalm-128 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-128|Psalm 128]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Blessed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-128#v1|Psalm 128:1]]

The sequence of [[Psalm-128|Psa. 128—134]], the remaining seven Songs of Ascents, is significant. [[Psalm-128|Psalm 128]] speaks of Jehovah’s blessing to Israel from Zion and the prosperity of Jerusalem in Israel’s enjoyment. [[Psalm-129|Psalm 129]] concerns God’s righteous dealing with the persecutors of Israel and the haters of Zion. Because Israel, like their persecutors and haters, has many sins and iniquities, [[Psalm-130|Psa. 130]] is a psalm of forgiveness and redemption. Then [[Psalm-131|Psa. 131]] indicates that after we experience God’s forgiveness, we become humble and quiet before God. In such a situation God can rise up, enter into us, and take us as His resting place. This is Zion becoming God’s habitation, His resting place, as revealed in [[Psalm-132|Psa. 132]]. Next, [[Psalm-133|Psa. 133]] shows that Zion, God’s resting place, is the unique place where the brothers can come together in oneness and receive the commanded blessing of God, even life forever. Finally, in [[Psalm-134|Psa. 134]] the children of Israel first charge the priests to praise God in the night, and then they bless the priests, indicating that the children of Israel, having become Zion, are the highest people, even higher than the priests (cf. [[Hebrews-07#v7|Heb. 7:7]]).

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — bless

**Verse:** [[Psalm-128#v5|Psalm 128:5]]

[[Psalm-128|Psalm 128]] is the praise of a saint, in his going up to Zion, concerning Jehovah’s blessing to Israel from Zion (signifying the overcomers in the churches—see [note 2¹](<Psalm-48.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Zion>) in [[Psalm-48|Psa. 48]]) and the prosperity of Jerusalem (signifying the church as God’s kingdom) in their enjoyment.

### Footnote 5.2 — from

**Verse:** [[Psalm-128#v5|Psalm 128:5]]

God always blesses His people from Zion, that is, from the overcomers (cf. [[Numbers-06#v23|Num. 6:23-27]]; see [note 2¹](<Psalm-48.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Zion>) in [[Psalm-48|Psa. 48]]).
