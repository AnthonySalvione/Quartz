# Psalm-90 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-90|Psalm 90]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — dwelling

**Verse:** [[Psalm-90#v1|Psalm 90:1]]

[[Psalm-90|Psalms 90—92]] concern the saints’ deeper experience of God in their being identified with Christ. [[Psalm-36#v8|Psalm 36:8]] speaks of drinking the river of God’s pleasures and eating the fatness of God’s house, indicating that we can experience the Lord by eating and drinking Him (cf. [[John-06#v48|John 6:48-58]], [[John-06#v63|63]]; [[John-07#v37|7:37]]; [[1 Corinthians-10#v3|1 Cor. 10:3-4]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v13|12:13]]). According to Moses, the giver of the law and the writer of this psalm, we can also dwell in the eternal Triune God as our Lord ([[Psalm-90#v1|v. 1]]; [[Psalm-91#v9|91:9]]; [[Deuteronomy-33#v27|Deut. 33:27]]; cf. [[John-15#v4|John 15:4]]; [[1 John-04#v15|1 John 4:15-16]]; [[Revelation-21#v22|Rev. 21:22]]). To dwell in God is to have our living in God ([[Colossians-02#v6|Col. 2:6]]; [[Colossians-03#v3|3:3]]; [[1 John-04#v16|1 John 4:16]]), taking Him as our everything. This is deeper than eating and drinking Him. To take God as our habitation, our eternal dwelling place, is the highest and fullest experience of God. 

Book Four of the Psalms unveils the saints’ deeper experience of God in the identification with Christ, and God’s recovery of His title and right over the earth. This indicates that our experience of dwelling in God paves the way for Christ to come to possess the earth that God may recover His title (ownership) and right over the earth (see [note 1¹](<Psalm-93.fn#Footnote 1.1 — reigns>) in [[Psalm-93|Psa. 93]]). Without the saints’ deeper experience of God, God has no way to recover this title and right.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — days

**Verse:** [[Psalm-90#v10|Psalm 90:10]]

If we take God as our dwelling place, we will realize that the span of our life on earth is brief and is full of sins and afflictions ([[Psalm-90#v3|vv. 3-11]]). We need to dwell in God, living in Him every minute, for outside of Him there are sins and afflictions ([[Psalm-90#v8|v. 8]]; [[John-16#v33|John 16:33]]).
