## v1

How long, O Jehovah? Will You forget me continually?  
How long will You hide Your face from me?

## v2

How long will I take counsel in my soul  
And sorrow in my heart day after day?  
How long will my enemy be exalted over me?

## v3

Consider and answer me, O Jehovah my God;  
Enlighten my eyes lest I sleep the sleep of death,

## v4

Lest my enemy say, I have prevailed against him,  
And my adversaries exult when I am shaken.

## v5

But I trust in Your lovingkindness;  
My heart will exult in Your salvation;

## v6

I will sing to Jehovah,  
For He has dealt bountifully with me.
