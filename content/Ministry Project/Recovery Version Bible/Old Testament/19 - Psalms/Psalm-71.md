## v1

In You, O Jehovah, do I take refuge;  
Never let me be put to shame.

## v2

In Your righteousness deliver me and rescue me;  
Incline Your ear to me and save me.

## v3

Be to me a rock of habitation for me to come to continually.  
You have given command to save me,  
For You are my rock and my fortress.

## v4

My God, rescue me from the hand of the wicked,  
From the hand of the unjust and cruel;

## v5

For You are my hope, O Lord Jehovah,  
My trust from my youth.

## v6

On You I have been sustained since birth;  
It was You who took me from my mother’s womb;  
My praise is continually of You.

## v7

I am like a wonder to many,  
But You are my strong refuge.

## v8

My mouth is filled with Your praise,  
With Your glory, all day long.

## v9

Do not cast me off in the time of old age;  
When my strength fails, do not forsake me.

## v10

For my enemies speak concerning me,  
And those who watch for my soul take counsel together,

## v11

Saying, God has forsaken him;  
Pursue and lay hold of him, for there is no one to deliver him.

## v12

O God, do not be far away from me;  
My God, hasten to help me.

## v13

May those who are the adversaries to my soul  
Be put to shame and consumed;  
May those who seek my misfortune  
Wrap themselves in reproach and humiliation.

## v14

But I will hope continually  
And will add yet more to all Your praise.

## v15

My mouth will recount Your righteousness,  
Your salvation, all day long;  
For I do not know its full numbers.

## v16

I will come with the proof of the mighty acts of the Lord Jehovah;  
I will remind men of Your righteousness, Yours alone.

## v17

O God, You have taught me from my youth;  
And until now I have declared Your wondrous deeds.

## v18

So even into my old age and grayheadedness,  
O God, do not forsake me,  
Until I have declared Your strength  
Unto the next generation,  
Your might unto everyone who is to come.

## v19

And Your righteousness, O God, is so high,  
You who have done great things.  
O God, who is like You?

## v20

You who have shown me  
Many great troubles  
Will revive me again  
And bring me up again  
From the depths of the earth.

## v21

You will increase my greatness  
And will turn and comfort me.

## v22

I will also praise You with the harp,  
I will praise Your faithfulness, my God;  
I will sing psalms to You with the lyre,  
O Holy One of Israel.

## v23

My lips will give a ringing shout  
When I sing psalms to You,  
And my soul, which You have redeemed.

## v24

My tongue also will declare  
Your righteousness all day long;  
For they are put to shame, for they are confounded,  
They who seek my misfortune.
