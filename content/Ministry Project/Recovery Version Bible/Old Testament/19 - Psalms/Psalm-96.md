## v1

Sing to Jehovah a new song;  
Sing to Jehovah, all the earth.

## v2

Sing to Jehovah, bless His name;  
Proclaim His salvation from day to day.

## v3

Declare His glory among the [[Psalm-96.fn#Footnote 3.1 — nations|nations]],  
His wondrous deeds among all the [[Psalm-96.fn#Footnote 3.2 — peoples|peoples]].

## v4

For Jehovah is great and much to be praised;  
He is to be feared above all gods.

## v5

For all the gods of the peoples are idols,  
But Jehovah made the heavens.

## v6

Majesty and splendor are before Him;  
Strength and beauty are in His sanctuary.

## v7

Ascribe to Jehovah, O families of the peoples,  
Ascribe to Jehovah glory and strength;

## v8

Ascribe to Jehovah the glory of His name;  
Bring an offering, and come into His courts.

## v9

Worship Jehovah in holy [[Psalm-96.fn#Footnote 9.1 — splendor|splendor]];  
Tremble before Him, all the earth.

## v10

Say among the nations, Jehovah reigns;  
Indeed the world has been [[Psalm-96.fn#Footnote 10.1 — established|established]]; it will not be moved;  
He will judge the peoples with equity.

## v11

Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad;  
Let the sea and its fullness roar.

## v12

Let the field and all that is in it exult;  
Then all the trees of the forest will sing for joy

## v13

Before Jehovah, for He is coming;  
For He is coming to judge the earth;  
He will judge the world with righteousness,  
And the peoples with His truth.
