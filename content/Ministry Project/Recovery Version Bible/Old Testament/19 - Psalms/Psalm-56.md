## v1

Be gracious to me, O God; for man tramples me;  
All day long he, fighting, oppresses me.

## v2

Those who lie in wait for me trample me all day long,  
For many are those who arrogantly fight against me.

## v3

When I am afraid,  
I trust in You.

## v4

In God, whose word I praise,  
In God do I trust, and I will not be afraid.  
What can flesh do to me?

## v5

All day long they wrest my words;  
All their thoughts are against me for evil.

## v6

They gather themselves; they lurk;  
They watch my steps,  
As they have waited to take my life.

## v7

Will there be deliverance for them in spite of their iniquity?  
In anger cast down the peoples, O God.

## v8

You have counted my wanderings.  
Put my tears into Your bottle.  
Are they not in Your book?

## v9

Then my enemies will turn back  
When I call;  
This I know,  
That God is for me.

## v10

In God, whose word I praise,  
In Jehovah, whose word I praise,

## v11

In God do I trust; I will not be afraid.  
What can man do to me?

## v12

Your vows, O God, are on me;  
I will render thank offerings to You.

## v13

For You have delivered my soul from death,  
Indeed, my feet from stumbling,  
That I may walk before God  
In the light of the living.
