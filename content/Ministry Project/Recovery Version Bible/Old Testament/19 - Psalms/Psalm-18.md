## Psa. 18:1

I love You, O Jehovah, my strength.

## Psa. 18:2

Jehovah is my crag and my fortress and my Deliverer;  
My God, my rock, in whom I take refuge;  
My shield and the horn of my salvation, my high retreat.

## Psa. 18:3

I called upon Jehovah, who is worthy of praise,  
And from my enemies I was saved.

## Psa. 18:4

The cords of death encompassed me;  
And the torrents of destruction assailed me.

## Psa. 18:5

The cords of Sheol surrounded me;  
The snares of death confronted me.

## Psa. 18:6

In my distress I called upon Jehovah  
And cried out to my God.  
He heard my voice from His temple,  
And my cry before Him came to His ears.

## Psa. 18:7

Then the earth shook and quaked,  
And the foundations of the mountains trembled and shook about,  
For He was furious.

## Psa. 18:8

Smoke went up from His nostrils,  
And fire from His mouth devoured;  
Coals blazed forth from Him.

## Psa. 18:9

He bowed the heavens down and descended,  
And deep darkness was under His feet.

## Psa. 18:10

He rode upon a cherub and did fly;  
He darted upon the wings of the wind.

## Psa. 18:11

He made darkness His hiding place, His pavilion around Him:  
Dark waterclouds, thick clouds of the skies.

## Psa. 18:12

Out of the brightness before Him there broke through His clouds  
Hail and fiery coals.

## Psa. 18:13

Then Jehovah thundered in heaven,  
And the Most High uttered His voice.

## Psa. 18:14

And He sent forth His arrows and scattered my enemies;  
And He shot forth lightning bolts and discomfited them.

## Psa. 18:15

The channels of the sea were seen,  
And the foundations of the habitable land were laid bare  
At Your rebuke, O Jehovah,  
At the blast of the breath of Your nostrils.

## Psa. 18:16

He reached forth from on high; He took me;  
He drew me out of great waters.

## Psa. 18:17

He delivered me from my strong enemy  
And from those who hate me, for they were too mighty for me.

## Psa. 18:18

They confronted me in the day of my calamity,  
But Jehovah became my support.

## Psa. 18:19

He brought me forth to a place broad and free;  
He rescued me, for He took delight in me.

## Psa. 18:20

Jehovah has recompensed me according to my righteousness;  
According to the cleanness of my hands He has repaid me.

## Psa. 18:21

For I have kept the ways of Jehovah  
And have not acted wickedly by turning away from my God.

## Psa. 18:22

For all His judgments were before me,  
And I did not turn His statutes away from me.

## Psa. 18:23

I was perfect toward Him,  
And I kept myself from my iniquity.

## Psa. 18:24

Therefore Jehovah recompensed me according to my righteousness,  
According to the cleanness of my hands in His sight.

## Psa. 18:25

With the faithful You show Yourself faithful,  
With a perfect man You show Yourself perfect,

## Psa. 18:26

With the pure You show Yourself pure,  
And with the perverse You show Yourself contrary.

## Psa. 18:27

For it is You who save the afflicted people,  
But the haughty eyes You bring down;

## Psa. 18:28

For it is You who light my lamp;  
Jehovah my God lights up my darkness;

## Psa. 18:29

For by You I can run up against a troop;  
Indeed by my God I can leap over a wall.

## Psa. 18:30

As for God, His way is perfect;  
The word of Jehovah is tried.  
He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.

## Psa. 18:31

For who is God other than Jehovah,  
And who is a rock but our God?

## Psa. 18:32

The God who girds me with strength  
And makes my way perfect,

## Psa. 18:33

Who makes my feet like hinds’ feet  
And sets me on my high places,

## Psa. 18:34

Who teaches my hands to wage war  
So that my arms may bend a bronze bow.

## Psa. 18:35

And You have given me the shield of Your salvation,  
And Your right hand supports me;  
And Your condescending gentleness has made me great.

## Psa. 18:36

You have broadened the places of my steps under me,  
And my feet have not slipped.

## Psa. 18:37

I pursued my enemies and overtook them,  
And I did not turn back until they were consumed.

## Psa. 18:38

I shattered them, so that they were not able to rise up;  
They fell under my feet.

## Psa. 18:39

And You girded me with strength for war;  
You brought down under me those who rose up against me.

## Psa. 18:40

You also made my enemies turn their back to me,  
And I annihilated those who hated me.

## Psa. 18:41

They cried out, but there was no one to save them;  
They cried out to Jehovah, but He did not answer them.

## Psa. 18:42

Then I beat them like dust before the wind;  
Like the mud of the streets I pounded them.

## Psa. 18:43

You rescued me from the strivings of the people;  
You have made me the head of the nations;  
A people whom I had not known serves me.

## Psa. 18:44

At the mere hearing of a report, they obey me;  
Foreigners come cringing to me;

## Psa. 18:45

Foreigners are discouraged  
And come quaking out of their fortresses.

## Psa. 18:46

Jehovah lives; and blessed be my rock,  
And exalted be the God of my salvation,

## Psa. 18:47

The God who executes vengeance for me  
And subdues peoples under me,

## Psa. 18:48

Who rescues me from my enemies.  
Indeed You exalted me above those who rise up against me;  
From the violent man You delivered me.

## Psa. 18:49

Therefore I give thanks to You among the nations, O Jehovah;  
And I will sing psalms to Your name.

## Psa. 18:50

It is He who magnifies salvation to His king  
And executes lovingkindness to His anointed,  
To David and to his seed forever.
