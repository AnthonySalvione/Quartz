# Psalm-38 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-38|Psalm 38]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — O

**Verse:** [[Psalm-38#v1|Psalm 38:1]]

David’s prayer in [[Psalm-37|Psa. 37]], in which he said that the righteous would inherit the land ([[Psalm-37#v29|37:29]]) and the wicked would be cut off ([[Psalm-37#v34|37:34]]), was according to his natural logic, but his prayer in this psalm, in which he was compelled by God’s chastising ([[Psalm-37#v1|vv. 1]], [[Psalm-37#v3|3]]) to groan before God ([[Psalm-37#v8|vv. 8-9]]), to confess his sin ([[Psalm-37#v3|v. 3]]), and to declare his iniquity ([[Psalm-37#v4|vv. 4]], [[Psalm-37#v18|18]]), was according to God’s revelation. Cf. [note 3¹](<Psalm-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — unrighteousness>) in [[Psalm-07|Psa. 7]].

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — groaning

**Verse:** [[Psalm-38#v8|Psalm 38:8]]

Groaning here indicates that the psalmist had an inward burden to pray, but he did not know what to say. Hence, all he could do was groan. See [note 26²](<Romans-08.fn#Footnote 26.2 — weakness>) in [[Romans-08|Rom. 8]].
