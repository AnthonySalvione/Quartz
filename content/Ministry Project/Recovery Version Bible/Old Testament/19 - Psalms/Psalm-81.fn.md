# Psalm-81 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-81|Psalm 81]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Sing

**Verse:** [[Psalm-81#v1|Psalm 81:1]]

[[Psalm-81|Psalms 81—83]] reveal God’s intention to gain the earth for Christ’s inheritance ([[Psalm-82#v8|82:8]]) through His habitations, the local churches, and Satan’s plot to possess God’s habitation for himself and thus withhold the earth from God ([[Psalm-83#v3|83:3]], [[Psalm-83#v12|12]]).

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — statute

**Verse:** [[Psalm-81#v4|Psalm 81:4]]

According to God’s ordination Israel, God’s elect, should live a joyful life (cf. [[Philippians-04#v4|Phil. 4:4]]). The annual feasts, as well as the sabbatical year and the year of jubilee, are times of rejoicing, singing, and shouting ([[Leviticus-23|Lev. 23]] and [[Leviticus-25|25]], and notes).
