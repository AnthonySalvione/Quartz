# Psalm-104 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-104|Psalm 104]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Bless

**Verse:** [[Psalm-104#v1|Psalm 104:1]]

[[Psalm-104|Psalm 104]] concerns God’s greatness in the creation of the universe and all the things in it. This, too, is a part of God’s history (see [note 1¹](<Psalm-103.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Bless>) in [[Psalm-103|Psa. 103]]).

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — waters

**Verse:** [[Psalm-104#v3|Psalm 104:3]]

I.e., the upper waters above the expanse (cf. [[Genesis-01#v7|Gen. 1:7]]).

## Footnotes for verse 35

### Footnote 35.1 — Hallelujah

**Verse:** [[Psalm-104#v35|Psalm 104:35]]

Meaning praise Jah; Jah being a shortened form of Jehovah. See [note 1¹](<Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Hallelujah>) in [[Revelation-19|Rev. 19]]. The praise of Jehovah in [[Psalm-103|Psa. 103—106]] issues in hallelujahs, beginning in this verse and continuing in [[Psalm-105#v45|105:45]] and [[Psalm-106#v1|106:1]], [[Psalm-106#v48|48]], because the earth has been fully recovered by God and brought under Christ’s dominion (cf. [[Revelation-19#v1|Rev. 19:1-6]]; [[Revelation-11#v15|11:15]]).
