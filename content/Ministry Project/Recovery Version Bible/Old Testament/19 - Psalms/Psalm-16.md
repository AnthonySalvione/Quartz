## v1

Preserve me, O God, for I take refuge in You.

## v2

I say to Jehovah, You are my Lord;  
No good have I beyond You;

## v3

As for the saints who are on the earth, they are the excellent;  
All my delight is in them.

## v4

The sorrows of them who bartered for some other god will be multiplied;  
Their drink offerings of blood I will not offer,  
Nor will I take up their names upon my lips.

## v5

Jehovah is the portion of my inheritance and of my cup;  
You maintain my lot.

## v6

The measuring lines have fallen on pleasant places for me;  
Indeed the inheritance is beautiful to me.

## v7

I will bless Jehovah, who counsels me;  
Indeed in the nights my inward parts instruct me.

## v8

I have set Jehovah before me continually;  
Because He is at my right hand, I shall not be shaken.

## v9

Therefore my heart rejoices and my glory exults;  
Even my flesh dwells securely.

## v10

For You will not abandon my soul to Sheol,  
Nor let Your Holy One see the pit.

## v11

You will make known to me the path of life;  
In Your presence is fullness of joy;  
At Your right hand there are pleasures forever.
