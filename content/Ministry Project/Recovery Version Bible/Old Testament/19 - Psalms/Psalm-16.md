> *A Michtam of David*

> [!light]  Note on *Michtam:* 
> The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain; some understand, a golden jewel (of a poem); others, an epigram.


---
## v1

[[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Preserve|Preserve]] me, O God, for I [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 1.2 — take|take]] refuge in You.

## v2

[[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 2.1 — I|I]] say to Jehovah, [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 2.2 — You|You]] are my Lord;  
No good have I beyond You;

## v3

As for the [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 3.1 — saints|saints]] who are on the earth, they are the excellent;  
All my delight is in them.

## v4

The sorrows of them who bartered for some other [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 4.1 — god|god]] will be multiplied;  
Their drink offerings of blood I will not offer,  
Nor will I take up their names upon my lips.

## v5

Jehovah is the [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 5.1 — portion|portion]] of my inheritance and of my cup;  
You maintain my lot.

## v6

The [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 6.1 — measuring|measuring]] lines have fallen on pleasant places for me;  
Indeed the inheritance is beautiful to me.

## v7

I will bless Jehovah, who [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 7.1 — counsels|counsels]] me;  
Indeed in the nights my inward parts instruct me.

## v8

I have [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 8.1 — set|set]] Jehovah before me continually;  
Because He is at my right hand, I shall not be shaken.

## v9

Therefore my heart rejoices and my [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 9.1 — glory|glory]] exults;  
Even my flesh [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 9.2 — dwells|dwells]] securely.

## v10

For You will not abandon my [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 10.1 — soul|soul]] to Sheol,  
Nor let Your Holy One see the [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 10.2 — pit|pit]].

## v11

You will make known to me the [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 11.1 — path|path]] of life;  
In Your [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 11.2 — presence|presence]] is fullness of joy;  
At Your [[Psalm-16.fn#Footnote 11.2 — presence|right]] hand there are pleasures forever.
