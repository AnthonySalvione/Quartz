## v1

O Shepherd of Israel, give ear,  
You who lead Joseph like a flock;  
You who are enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth.

## v2

Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh  
Stir up Your might,  
And come to save us.

## v3

O God, restore us;  
And cause Your face to shine, and we will be saved.

## v4

O Jehovah God of hosts,  
How long will You fume  
Against the prayer of Your people?

## v5

You have fed them with the bread of tears  
And have made them drink tears in large measure.

## v6

You have made us a cause of strife to our neighbors,  
And our enemies deride us among themselves.

## v7

O God of hosts, restore us;  
And cause Your face to shine, and we will be saved.

## v8

You brought a vine out of Egypt;  
You drove out nations and planted it;

## v9

You cleared the ground before it,  
And it took deep root and filled the land.

## v10

The mountains were covered with its shadow,  
And its boughs were like the cedars of God.

## v11

It put forth its branches unto the sea,  
And its shoots unto the River.

## v12

Why have You broken down its hedges,  
So that all who pass by on the way pluck it?

## v13

The boar from the forest ravages it,  
And that which moves through the field feeds on it.

## v14

O God of hosts, turn, we beseech You;  
Look down from heaven and see,  
And visit this vine,

## v15

Even the stock which Your right hand has planted  
And the son whom You have strengthened for Yourself.

## v16

It is burned with fire; it is cut down;  
They perish at the rebuke of Your countenance.

## v17

Let Your hand be upon the man of Your right hand,  
Upon the son of man whom You have strengthened for Yourself;

## v18

Then we will not turn back from You.  
Revive us, and we will call upon Your name.

## v19

O Jehovah God of hosts, restore us;  
Cause Your face to shine, and we will be saved.
