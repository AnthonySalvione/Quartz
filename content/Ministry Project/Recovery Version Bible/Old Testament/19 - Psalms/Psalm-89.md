## v1

I will sing of the lovingkindness of Jehovah forever;  
From generation to generation I will make known Your faithfulness with my mouth.

## v2

For I have said, Lovingkindness will be built up forever;  
In the heavens themselves You will establish Your faithfulness.

## v3

I have made a covenant with My chosen one;  
I have sworn to David My servant:

## v4

I will establish your seed forever,  
And I will build up your throne from generation to generation. Selah

## v5

And the heavens will praise Your wonders, O Jehovah,  
Indeed Your faithfulness in the congregation of the holy ones.

## v6

For who in the skies can be compared to Jehovah?  
Who among the sons of the mighty is like Jehovah,

## v7

Like God, greatly feared in the council of the holy ones  
And awesome beyond all around Him?

## v8

O Jehovah God of hosts, who is like You, the mighty Jah?  
Your faithfulness also surrounds You.

## v9

You rule the swelling of the sea:  
When its waves rise up, You still them.

## v10

You crushed Rahab like one slain;  
You scattered Your enemies with the arm of Your strength.

## v11

The heavens are Yours; the earth also is Yours;  
The world and its fullness You have founded.

## v12

You created the north and the south;  
Tabor and Hermon shout for joy at Your name.

## v13

You have an arm with might;  
Your hand is strong; Your right hand is high.

## v14

Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne;  
Lovingkindness and truth go before Your face.

## v15

Blessed are the people who know the joyful shout;  
They walk, O Jehovah, in the light of Your countenance.

## v16

In Your name they exult all day long,  
And in Your righteousness they are exalted.

## v17

For You are the glory of their strength,  
And in Your favor our horn is exalted.

## v18

For our shield belongs to Jehovah,  
And our king, to the Holy One of Israel.

## v19

Then You spoke in vision  
Of Your Holy One, and You said,  
I have given help to One who is mighty;  
I have exalted One chosen from My people.

## v20

I have found David My Servant;  
With My holy oil I have anointed Him,

## v21

With whom My hand will be established;  
My arm will also strengthen Him.

## v22

The enemy will not exact anything from Him,  
Nor will the son of wickedness afflict Him.

## v23

And I will crush His adversaries before Him,  
And I will strike those who hate Him.

## v24

And My faithfulness and My lovingkindness will be with Him,  
And in My name His horn will be exalted.

## v25

And I will set His hand on the sea  
And His right hand on the rivers.

## v26

He will call upon Me, saying, You are My Father,  
My God and the rock of My salvation.

## v27

I will also make Him the Firstborn,  
The highest of the kings of the earth.

## v28

I will keep My lovingkindness for Him forever,  
And My covenant will stand firm with Him.

## v29

And I will establish His seed forever,  
And His throne as the days of heaven.

## v30

If His children forsake My law  
And do not walk in My ordinances,

## v31

If they profane My statutes  
And do not keep My commandments,

## v32

I will punish their transgression with a rod  
And their iniquity with stripes.

## v33

But My lovingkindness I will not utterly take away from Him,  
Nor will I be false to My own faithfulness.

## v34

I will not profane My covenant,  
Nor will I change what has gone forth from My lips.

## v35

Once I have sworn by My holiness;  
I will not lie to David.

## v36

His seed shall endure forever,  
And His throne, like the sun before Me;

## v37

It shall be established forever like the moon;  
And the witness in the skies is firm. Selah

## v38

But You have cast off and rejected;  
You have been angry with Your anointed.

## v39

You have abhorred the covenant of Your servant;  
You have profaned his crown by casting it to the ground.

## v40

You have broken down all his walls;  
You have brought his strongholds to ruin.

## v41

All who pass by on the way plunder him;  
He has become a reproach to his neighbors.

## v42

You have exalted the right hand of his adversaries;  
You have caused all his enemies to rejoice.

## v43

Indeed You have turned back the edge of his sword  
And have not made him stand in the battle.

## v44

You have made his brightness cease  
And have cast down his throne to the ground.

## v45

You have shortened the days of his youth;  
You have covered him with shame. Selah

## v46

How long, O Jehovah? Will You hide Yourself forever?  
How long will Your wrath burn like fire?

## v47

Remember how short my time is —  
For what vanity You have created all the sons of men!

## v48

What man will live and not see death?  
Will he deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah

## v49

Where are Your former acts of lovingkindness, O Lord,  
Which You swore to David in Your faithfulness?

## v50

Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Your servants,  
How I bear in my bosom the reproach of all the mighty peoples,

## v51

With which Your enemies have reproached, O Jehovah,  
With which they have reproached the footsteps of Your anointed.

## v52

Blessed be Jehovah forever.  
Amen and Amen.
