## v1

Let God arise; let His enemies be scattered;  
And let those who hate Him flee before Him.

## v2

As smoke is driven away, You will drive them away;  
As wax melts before fire,  
The wicked will perish before God.

## v3

But the righteous will rejoice; they will exult before God  
And be glad with rejoicing.

## v4

Sing to God; sing psalms to His name;  
Triumph in Him who rides through the deserts —  
For Jah is His name — and exult before Him.

## v5

A Father to the orphans and a Judge for the widows  
Is God in His holy habitation.

## v6

God causes the solitary to dwell in a household;  
He brings the prisoners forth into prosperity;  
But the rebellious dwell in a parched land.

## v7

O God, when You went forth before Your people,  
When You marched through the desert, Selah

## v8

The earth trembled, the heavens also dripped,  
At the presence of God;  
Sinai there quaked  
At the presence of God, the God of Israel.

## v9

You shed forth bountiful rain, O God;  
You confirmed Your inheritance when it languished.

## v10

Your living flock dwelt in the land;  
In Your goodness You provided for the poor, O God.

## v11

The Lord gives the command;  
The women who bear the glad tidings are a great host.

## v12

The kings of the armies flee.  
They flee!  
And she who abides at home  
Divides the spoil.

## v13

Though you lie among the sheepfolds,  
There are dove wings covered with silver,  
And its pinions, with greenish-yellow gold.

## v14

When the Almighty scattered  
The kings in the land,  
It snowed on Zalmon.

## v15

O mighty mountain, O mountain of Bashan,  
O many-peaked mountain, O mountain of Bashan:

## v16

Why do you look with envy, O many-peaked mountains,  
At the mountain on which God desires to dwell?  
Indeed Jehovah will dwell there forever.

## v17

The chariots of God are twenty thousand,  
Thousands upon thousands;  
The Lord is among them,  
As at Sinai, in the sanctuary.

## v18

You have ascended on high; You have led captive those taken captive;  
You have received gifts among men,  
Even the rebellious ones also,  
That Jehovah God may dwell among them.

## v19

Blessed be the Lord, who day by day loads us with good;  
God is our salvation. Selah

## v20

God is to us  
A God of deliverance,  
And with Jehovah the Lord  
Are the goings forth even from death.

## v21

Indeed God will smash  
The head of His enemies,  
The hairy crown  
Of him who goes about in his guiltiness.

## v22

The Lord said, I will bring them again from Bashan;  
I will bring them again from the depths of the sea,

## v23

That you may bathe your foot in blood,  
That the tongue of your dogs may have their portion from the enemies.

## v24

They have seen Your goings, O God,  
The goings of my God, my King, in the sanctuary:

## v25

Singers go before; players after;  
In the midst of virgins sounding the tambourines.

## v26

Bless God in the congregations,  
Even Jehovah, O you who are of the fountain of Israel.

## v27

There are little Benjamin, who rules them,  
And the princes of Judah in their company,  
The princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphtali.

## v28

Your God has commanded your strength;  
Strengthen, O God, that which You have done for us.

## v29

Because of Your temple at Jerusalem,  
Kings will bring a gift to You.

## v30

Rebuke the animals of the reeds,  
The herd of the bulls among the calves of the peoples,  
Trampling on those who lust after silver;  
Scatter the peoples who take delight in war.

## v31

Nobles will come out of Egypt;  
Cush will quickly stretch out its hands to God.

## v32

O kingdoms of the earth,  
Sing to God,  
Sing psalms to the Lord, Selah

## v33

To Him who rides upon the ancient heaven of heavens.  
Behold, He utters His voice, a mighty voice.

## v34

Ascribe strength to God;  
His excellence is over Israel,  
And His strength is in the skies.

## v35

You are awesome, O God, from Your sanctuaries.  
The God of Israel, He gives strength and power to the people.  
Blessed be God!
