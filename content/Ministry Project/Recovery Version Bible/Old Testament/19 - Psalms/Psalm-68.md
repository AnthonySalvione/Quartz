> *To the choir director. Of David. A Psalm; a Song*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Let|Let]] God arise; let His [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 1.2 — enemies|enemies]] be scattered;  
And let those who hate Him flee before Him.

## v2

As smoke is driven away, You will drive them away;  
As wax melts before fire,  
The wicked will perish before God.

## v3

But the righteous will rejoice; they will exult before God  
And be glad with rejoicing.

## v4

Sing to God; sing psalms to His name;  
[[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Triumph|Triumph]] in Him who rides through the deserts —  
For [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Jah|Jah]] is His name — and [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Triumph|exult]] before Him.

## v5

A Father to the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 5.1 — orphans|orphans]] and a Judge for the widows  
Is God in His holy habitation.

## v6

God causes the solitary to dwell in a household;  
He brings the prisoners forth into prosperity;  
But the rebellious dwell in a parched land.

## v7

O God, when You went forth before Your people,  
When You marched through the desert, Selah

## v8

The earth trembled, the heavens also dripped,  
At the presence of God;  
Sinai there quaked  
At the presence of God, the God of Israel.

## v9

You shed forth bountiful rain, O God;  
You confirmed Your inheritance when it languished.

## v10

Your living flock dwelt in [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 10.1 — the|the]] land;  
In Your goodness You provided for the poor, O God.

## v11

The Lord gives the command;  
The [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 11.1 — women|women]] who [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 11.2 — bear|bear]] the glad tidings are a great host.

## v12

The [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 12.1 — kings|kings]] of the armies flee.  
They flee!  
And [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 12.2 — she|she]] who [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 12.3 — abides|abides]] at home  
Divides the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 12.4 — spoil|spoil]].

## v13

Though you [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 13.1 — lie|lie]] among the sheepfolds,  
There are [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 13.2 — dove|dove]] wings covered with silver,  
And its pinions, with greenish-yellow gold.

## v14

When the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Almighty|Almighty]] scattered  
The [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 14.2 — kings|kings]] in [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 14.3 — the|the]] land,  
It snowed on Zalmon.

## v15

O [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 15.1 — mighty|mighty]] mountain, O mountain of Bashan,  
O many-peaked mountain, O mountain of Bashan:

## v16

Why do you look with envy, O many-peaked mountains,  
At the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 16.1 — mountain|mountain]] on which God desires to dwell?  
Indeed Jehovah will dwell there forever.

## v17

The chariots of God are twenty thousand,  
Thousands upon thousands;  
The Lord is among them,  
As at Sinai, in the sanctuary.

## v18

You have [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 18.1 — ascended|ascended]] on high; You have led captive [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 18.2 — those|those]] taken captive;  
You have received [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 18.3 — gifts|gifts]] among men,  
Even the rebellious ones also,  
That [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 18.4 — Jehovah|Jehovah]] God may [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 18.5 — dwell|dwell]] among them.

## v19

Blessed be the Lord, who day by day [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 19.1 — loads|loads]] us with [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 19.2 — good|good]];  
God is our salvation. Selah

## v20

God is to us  
A God of deliverance,  
And with Jehovah the Lord  
Are the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 20.1 — goings|goings]] forth even from death.

## v21

Indeed God will [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 21.1 — smash|smash]]  
The head of His enemies,  
The hairy crown  
Of him who goes about in his guiltiness.

## v22

The Lord said, I will bring them again from Bashan;  
I will bring them again from the depths of the sea,

## v23

That you may [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 23.1 — bathe|bathe]] your foot in blood,  
That the tongue of your dogs may have their portion from the enemies.

## v24

[[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 24.1 — They|They]] have seen Your [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 24.1 — They|goings]], O God,  
The goings of my God, my King, in the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 24.1 — They|sanctuary]]:

## v25

Singers go before; players after;  
In the midst of [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 25.1 — virgins|virgins]] sounding the tambourines.

## v26

Bless God in the congregations,  
Even [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Jehovah|Jehovah]], O you who are of the fountain of Israel.

## v27

There are little [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 27.1 — Benjamin|Benjamin]], who rules them,  
And the princes of [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 27.2 — Judah|Judah]] in their company,  
The princes of [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 27.3 — Zebulun|Zebulun]], the princes of [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 27.4 — Naphtali|Naphtali]].

## v28

Your God has commanded your strength;  
[[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Strengthen|Strengthen]], O God, that which You have done for us.

## v29

Because of Your temple at Jerusalem,  
[[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Kings|Kings]] will bring a gift to You.

## v30

Rebuke the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 30.1 — animals|animals]] of the reeds,  
The herd of the bulls among the calves of the peoples,  
Trampling on those who lust after silver;  
Scatter the [[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 30.2 — peoples|peoples]] who take delight in war.

## v31

Nobles will come out of Egypt;  
[[Psalm-68.fn#Footnote 31.1 — Cush|Cush]] will quickly stretch out its hands to God.

## v32

O kingdoms of the earth,  
Sing to God,  
Sing psalms to the Lord, Selah

## v33

To Him who rides upon the ancient heaven of heavens.  
Behold, He utters His voice, a mighty voice.

## v34

Ascribe strength to God;  
His excellence is over Israel,  
And His strength is in the skies.

## v35

You are awesome, O God, from Your sanctuaries.  
The God of Israel, He gives strength and power to the people.  
Blessed be God!
