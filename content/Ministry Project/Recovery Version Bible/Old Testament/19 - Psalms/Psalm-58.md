## v1

[[Psalm-58.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Do|Do]] you indeed, O [[Psalm-58.fn#Footnote 1.2 — judges|judges]], speak righteousness?  
Do you judge in uprightness, O sons of men?

## v2

No, in heart you perform injustice;  
You weigh out the violence of your hands on earth.

## v3

The wicked are estranged from the womb;  
They err from their birth, speaking lies.

## v4

Their venom is like the venom of a serpent;  
They are like the deaf cobra that has shut its ear,

## v5

Which does not hear the sound of the charmers  
Or the wise spellbinder.

## v6

O God, break their teeth in their mouth;  
Break off the fangs of the young lions, O Jehovah.

## v7

Let them melt like water that runs off.  
When he shoots his arrows,  
Let them be as though they were cut off.

## v8

Let them be like a snail that melts and goes away,  
Like a woman’s miscarriage, which by no means sees the sun.

## v9

Before your pots can feel the fire of the thorns,  
Whether green or burning, He will sweep them away.

## v10

The righteous man will rejoice when he sees vengeance;  
He will wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.

## v11

And men will say, Indeed there is fruit for the righteous man;  
Indeed there is a God who judges on earth.
