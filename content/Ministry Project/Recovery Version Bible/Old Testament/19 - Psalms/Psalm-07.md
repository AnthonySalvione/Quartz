## v1

O Jehovah my God, in You do I take refuge.  
Save me from all who pursue me, and deliver me;

## v2

Lest he tear my soul like a lion,  
Tearing it apart without anyone to deliver it.

## v3

O Jehovah my God, if I have done this,  
If there is unrighteousness in my hands,

## v4

If I have recompensed with evil him who was at peace with me  
(Rather, I have delivered him who vexed me without cause);

## v5

Let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake me,  
And let him trample my life to the ground  
And lay my glory down in the dust. Selah

## v6

Arise, O Jehovah, in Your anger;  
Lift Yourself up against the overflowing wrath of those who vex me,  
And awake for me: You have commanded judgment.

## v7

And let the assembly of the peoples surround You,  
And over it return on high.

## v8

Jehovah will execute judgment on the peoples;  
Judge me, O Jehovah, according to my righteousness  
And according to my integrity that is with me.

## v9

Oh, let the evil of the wicked come to an end,  
But establish the righteous man.  
For the righteous God  
Tries the hearts and the inward parts.

## v10

My shield is with God,  
Who saves the upright in heart.

## v11

God is a righteous Judge  
And a God who has indignation every day.

## v12

If one does not turn, He will whet His sword.  
He bends His bow and makes it ready.

## v13

And He prepares instruments of death against him;  
He has made His arrows fiery shafts.

## v14

Behold, the evil man travails with iniquity;  
He conceives mischief and brings forth falsehood.

## v15

He digs a pit and hollows it out,  
Then he falls into the hole that he has made.

## v16

His mischief returns upon his own head,  
And upon the crown of his own head his violence comes down.

## v17

I will give thanks to Jehovah according to His righteousness,  
And I will sing psalms to the name of Jehovah the Most High.
