## v1

Clap your hands, all you peoples;  
Shout to God with the voice of a triumphant sound.

## v2

For Jehovah Most High is awesome:  
A great King over all the earth.

## v3

He subdues peoples under us,  
And nations under our feet.

## v4

He chose our inheritance for us,  
The excellency of Jacob, whom He loved. Selah

## v5

God ascends amidst shouting;  
Jehovah, at the sound of a trumpet.

## v6

Sing psalms to God, sing psalms;  
Sing psalms to our King, sing psalms.

## v7

For God is the King of all the earth;  
Sing psalms with understanding.

## v8

God reigns over the nations;  
God sits upon His holy throne.

## v9

The princes of the peoples have been gathered  
As the people of the God of Abraham;  
For the shields of the earth belong to God;  
He is greatly exalted.
