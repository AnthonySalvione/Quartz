# Psalm-137 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-137|Psalm 137]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — remembered

**Verse:** [[Psalm-137#v1|Psalm 137:1]]

[[Psalm-137|Psalm 137]] speaks of Israel’s remembering Zion and not forgetting Jerusalem ([[Psalm-137#v5|v. 5]]) in their captivity. After returning from their captivity, the captives had a sweet remembrance of the sorrowful experience of captivity. In spiritual experience, when we fall and are brought back by the Lord, in our reminiscence we taste the sweetness of the Lord’s mercy in restoring us.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — Against

**Verse:** [[Psalm-137#v7|Psalm 137:7]]

The psalmist’s prayer here against Edom and his cursing of Babylon in [[Psalm-137#v8|vv. 8-9]] are according to the principle of good and evil (see [note 7¹](<Psalm-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — strike>) in [[Psalm-03|Psa. 3]]). According to the teaching of the New Testament, we should bless and not curse those who oppose us ([[Romans-12#v14|Rom. 12:14]]), and we should pray for those who persecute us ([[Matthew-05#v44|Matt. 5:44]]).
