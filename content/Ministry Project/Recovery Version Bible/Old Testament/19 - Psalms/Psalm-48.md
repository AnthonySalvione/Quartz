## v1

Great is Jehovah,  
And much to be praised  
In the city of our God,  
In His holy mountain.

## v2

Beautiful in elevation,  
The joy of the whole earth,  
Is Mount Zion, the sides of the north,  
The city of the great King.

## v3

God has made Himself known  
In her palaces as a high retreat.

## v4

For, behold, the kings have assembled themselves;  
They passed by together.

## v5

They saw; therefore they were astounded.  
They were dismayed; they hurried off in alarm.

## v6

Trembling seized them there;  
Writhing, like that of a woman in labor;

## v7

With an east wind You shattered  
The ships of Tarshish.

## v8

As we have heard,  
So we have seen  
In the city of Jehovah of hosts,  
In the city of our God.  
God will establish her forever. Selah

## v9

We consider, O God, Your lovingkindness  
In the midst of Your temple.

## v10

As Your name is, O God,  
So is Your praise  
Unto the ends of the earth;  
Your right hand is full of righteousness.

## v11

Let Mount Zion rejoice;  
Let the daughters of Judah exult  
Because of Your judgments.

## v12

Walk about Zion, and go around her;  
Count her towers.

## v13

Consider carefully her bulwarks;  
Pass between her palaces;  
That you may recount it to the generation following.

## v14

For this God is our God forever and ever.  
He will guide us even unto death.
