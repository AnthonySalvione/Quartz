# Psalm-17 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-17|Psalm 17]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Hear

**Verse:** [[Psalm-17#v1|Psalm 17:1]]

[[Psalm-17|Psalms 17—21]] reveal David’s concept concerning a man before God and concerning David’s kingship before God. His concept was based on the keeping of the law and the principle of good and evil. In God’s New Testament economy we should not keep the law by ourselves but should live Christ by His living in us ([[Galatians-02#v19|Gal. 2:19-20]]; [[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21a]]).

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — lovingkindness

**Verse:** [[Psalm-17#v7|Psalm 17:7]]

[[Psalm-17#v7|Verses 7-9]] indicate an improvement in David’s concept from his own righteousness in [[Psalm-17#v1|vv. 1-6]] to God’s lovingkindness and God’s hiding him in the shadow of His wings.

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — righteousness

**Verse:** [[Psalm-17#v15|Psalm 17:15]]

The end of this psalm shows David’s improvement in his being satisfied with God’s likeness (i.e., presence), yet David still remained in his righteousness before God. David boasted of his righteousness in spite of his great sin of taking Bathsheba and murdering her husband, Uriah ([[2 Samuel-11|2 Sam. 11]]).
