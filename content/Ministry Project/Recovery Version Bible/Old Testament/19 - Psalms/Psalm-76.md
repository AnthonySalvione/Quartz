> *To the choir director: on the stringed instruments. A Psalm of Asaph; a Song*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-76.fn#Footnote 1.1 — God|God]] is known in Judah;  
His name is great in Israel.

## v2

And in [[Psalm-76.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Salem|Salem]] is His tabernacle,  
And His dwelling place, in [[Psalm-76.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Zion|Zion]].

## v3

[[Psalm-76.fn#Footnote 3.1 — There|There]] He broke the flashing arrows of the bow,  
The shield and the sword and the battle. Selah

## v4

More glorious are You,  
More excellent than the mountains of prey.

## v5

The stouthearted are despoiled;  
They slept their sleep,  
And none of the men of strength  
Have found their hands.

## v6

At Your rebuke, O God of Jacob,  
Both chariot and horse fall into deep sleep.

## v7

You, indeed You, are to be feared;  
And who may stand before You once You are angry?

## v8

You caused judgment to be heard from the heavens;  
The earth feared and was still,

## v9

When God rose for judgment,  
To save all the meek of the earth. Selah

## v10

For the wrath of man will praise You;  
You will gird Yourself with the remainder of wrath.

## v11

Make vows and pay them to Jehovah your God;  
Let all who are around Him bring gifts to Him who is to be feared.

## v12

He will cut off the spirit of the princes;  
He is terrible to the kings of the earth.
