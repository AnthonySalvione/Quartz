## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — thanksgiving

**Verse:** [[Psalm-50#v14|Psalm 50:14]]

This psalm tells us that some of the saints of God were offering burnt offerings and other kinds of offerings ([[Psalm-50#v8|vv. 8-13]]), but because they were lacking in affection, they would not offer the sacrifice of thanksgiving to God. Thus, the psalmist, speaking for God, indicated that what God wants is not the burnt offering but the thanksgiving offering. We can offer certain offerings without being touched in our hearts, but when we are thankful to God and offer to Him a prayer of thanksgiving, our hearts are deeply touched and we are filled with affection and tender feeling toward God. This is the kind of offering God desires.

Now we need to call on the Lord according to this covenant that we may enjoy Him as our portion ([[Psalm-116#v13|116:13]] and [note](<Psalm-116.fn#Footnote 13.1 — cup>)).
