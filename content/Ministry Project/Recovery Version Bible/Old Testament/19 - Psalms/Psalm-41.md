> *To the choir director. A Psalm of David*

---
## v1

Blessed is he who considers the poor; / In the day of evil Jehovah will deliver him.

## v2

May Jehovah keep him and preserve him alive; / May he be [[Psalm-41.fn#Footnote 2.1 — blessed|blessed]] in the land; / And do not give him over to the will of his enemies.

## v3

Jehovah will sustain him on his sickbed; / You make all his bed in his illness.

## v4

I said, O Jehovah, be gracious to me; / Heal my soul, for I have sinned against You.

## v5

My enemies speak evil against me: / When will he die and his name perish?

## v6

And if he comes to see me, he speaks falsehood. / His heart gathers iniquity to itself; / When he goes out, he speaks it.

## v7

All who hate me whisper together against me; / Against me they devise my misfortune.

## v8

[[Psalm-41.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Some|Some]] evil illness, they say, has been poured out on him; / And when he lies down, he will not rise again.

## v9

[[Psalm-41.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Even|Even]] my familiar friend, in whom I trusted, / Who ate my bread, has lifted his heel against me.

## v10

But You, O Jehovah, be gracious to me and raise me up / That I may [[Psalm-41.fn#Footnote 10.1 — recompense|recompense]] them.

## v11

By this I know that You delight in me, / That my enemy has not shouted in triumph over me.

## v12

And as for me, You uphold me in my [[Psalm-41.fn#Footnote 12.1 — integrity|integrity]], / And You set me in Your presence forever.

## v13

Blessed be Jehovah the God of Israel, / From eternity to eternity. / Amen and Amen.
