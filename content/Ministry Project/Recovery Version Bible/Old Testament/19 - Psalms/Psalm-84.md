> *To the choir director: on the gittith. Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm*

> [!light] Note on gittith:
> See note on the superscription of [[Psalm-08|Psa. 8]].
---
## v1

How [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 1.1 — lovely|lovely]] are Your [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 1.2 — tabernacles|tabernacles]],  
O Jehovah of hosts!

## v2

My soul [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 2.1 — longs|longs]], indeed even [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 2.1 — longs|faints]],  
For the courts of Jehovah;  
My heart and my flesh cry out  
To the living God.

## v3

At Your [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 3.1 — two|two]] altars even the [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 3.2 — sparrow|sparrow]] has found a [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 3.3 — home|home]];  
And the [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 3.2 — sparrow|swallow]], a [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 3.3 — home|nest]] for herself,  
Where she may lay her young,  
O Jehovah of hosts, my King and my God.

## v4

Blessed are those who dwell in Your [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 4.1 — house|house]];  
They will yet be praising You. Selah

## v5

Blessed is the man whose strength is in You,  
In whose heart are the [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 5.1 — highways|highways]] to Zion.

## v6

Passing through the valley of [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Baca|Baca]],  
They make it a [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 6.2 — spring|spring]];  
Indeed the [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 6.3 — early|early]] rain covers it with blessings.

## v7

They go from strength to strength;  
Each appears before God in [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Zion|Zion]].

## v8

O Jehovah God of hosts, hear my prayer;  
Give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah

## v9

Behold our [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 9.1 — shield|shield]], O God;  
And look upon the face of Your [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 9.1 — shield|anointed]].

## v10

For a day in Your courts is better than a thousand;  
I would rather stand at the threshold of the house of my God  
Than dwell in the tents of the wicked.

## v11

For Jehovah God is a [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 11.1 — sun|sun]] and a shield;  
Jehovah gives grace and glory;  
He does not withhold anything good  
From [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 11.2 — those|those]] who walk uprightly.

## v12

O Jehovah of hosts, blessed is the [[Psalm-84.fn#Footnote 12.1 — man|man]]  
Who trusts in You.
