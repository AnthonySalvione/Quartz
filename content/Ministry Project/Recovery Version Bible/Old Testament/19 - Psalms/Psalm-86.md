## v1

Incline Your ear, O Jehovah; answer me;  
For I am poor and needy.

## v2

Keep my soul; for I am faithful;  
Save Your servant who trusts in You, O You who are my God.

## v3

Be gracious to me, O Lord;  
For to You do I call out all day long.

## v4

Cause the soul of Your servant to rejoice,  
For unto You, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.

## v5

For You, O Lord, are good and ready to forgive  
And abundant in lovingkindness to all who call upon You.

## v6

Give ear, O Jehovah, to my prayer,  
And give heed to the voice of my supplications.

## v7

In the day of my distress I call upon You,  
For You answer me.

## v8

There is none like You among the gods, O Lord;  
Nor are there any works like Yours.

## v9

All the nations which You have made will come  
And worship before You, O Lord;  
And they will glorify Your name.

## v10

For You are great, and You do wondrous deeds;  
You alone are God.

## v11

Teach me, O Jehovah, Your way;  
I will walk in Your truth.  
Make my heart single in fearing Your name.

## v12

I will praise You, O Lord my God, with all my heart;  
And I will glorify Your name forever.

## v13

For Your lovingkindness is great toward me,  
And You have delivered my soul from the lowest Sheol.

## v14

O God, the proud have risen up against me,  
And a company of violent men seek my life;  
And they do not set You before them.

## v15

But You, O Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God,  
Long-suffering and abundant in lovingkindness and truth.

## v16

Turn to me and be gracious to me;  
Give Your strength to Your servant,  
And save the son of Your female servant.

## v17

Show me a token of goodness,  
That those who hate me may see it and be ashamed;  
For You, O Jehovah, have helped me and comforted me.
