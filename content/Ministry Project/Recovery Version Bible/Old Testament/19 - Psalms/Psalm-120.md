> *A Song of Ascents*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-120.fn#Footnote 1.1 — In|In]] my [[Psalm-120.fn#Footnote 1.2 — distress|distress]] I called out to Jehovah, / And He answered me.

## v2

O Jehovah, deliver my soul from lying lips, / From a tongue of deceit.

## v3

What should be given to you, / And what further shall be done to you, / O tongue of deceit?

## v4

The sharp arrows of a mighty man, / With the burning coals of the broom tree!

## v5

Woe is me, for I sojourn in [[Psalm-120.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Meshech|Meshech]], / I dwell among the tents of [[Psalm-120.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Meshech|Kedar]].

## v6

Long has my soul had its dwelling / With him who hates peace.

## v7

I am for peace; but when I speak, / They are for war.
