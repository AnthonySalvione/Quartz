# Psalm-131 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-131|Psalm 131]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — heart

**Verse:** [[Psalm-131#v1|Psalm 131:1]]

[[Psalm-131|Psalm 131]] is the praise of a saint, in his going up to Zion, concerning his humbled heart and quieted soul ([[Psalm-131#v2|v. 2]]) before Jehovah. This was a result of God’s forgiveness and redemption mentioned in [[Psalm-130|Psa. 130]].

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — weaned

**Verse:** [[Psalm-131#v2|Psalm 131:2]]

The psalmist had been weaned, or stripped, of everything except the Lord (cf. [[Psalm-73|Psa. 73]]). When we have become like the psalmist, humble, calm, quiet, and weaned, we can advise others to hope in God ([[Psalm-131#v3|v. 3]]).
