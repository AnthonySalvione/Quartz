## v1

Give thanks to Jehovah; call upon His name;  
Make known His deeds among the peoples.

## v2

Sing to Him; sing psalms to Him;  
Muse upon all His wondrous deeds.

## v3

Glory in His holy name;  
Let the heart of those who seek Jehovah rejoice.

## v4

Seek Jehovah and His strength;  
Seek His face continually.

## v5

Remember His wondrous deeds, which He has done,  
His signs and the judgments of His mouth,

## v6

O seed of Abraham His servant,  
O children of Jacob, His chosen ones.

## v7

He is Jehovah our God;  
His judgments are in all the earth.

## v8

He has remembered His covenant forever,  
The word that He commanded to a thousand generations,

## v9

The covenant that He made with Abraham,  
And His oath unto Isaac.

## v10

And He confirmed it unto Jacob as a statute,  
Unto Israel as an eternal covenant,

## v11

Saying, To you I will give the land of Canaan,  
The portion of your inheritance;

## v12

When they were men few in number,  
Indeed very few and strangers in the land.

## v13

And they went about from nation to nation,  
From one kingdom to another people.

## v14

He allowed no man to oppress them;  
Indeed He rebuked kings for their sake.

## v15

Saying, Do not touch My anointed ones,  
And do My prophets no harm.

## v16

And He called a famine upon the land;  
He broke the whole staff of bread.

## v17

He sent a man before them;  
Joseph was sold as a servant.

## v18

They afflicted his feet with fetters;  
He himself was placed in irons.

## v19

Until the time when his word came to pass,  
The word of Jehovah tried him.

## v20

The king sent men and freed him,  
Even the ruler of the peoples, and let him go.

## v21

He made him lord of his house  
And ruler over all his possessions,

## v22

To imprison his princes at will,  
And to teach his elders wisdom.

## v23

Then Israel came into Egypt,  
And Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

## v24

And He made His people very fruitful,  
And caused them to be mightier than their adversaries.

## v25

He turned their heart to hate His people,  
To deal craftily with His servants.

## v26

He sent Moses His servant,  
And Aaron, whom He had chosen.

## v27

They set His signs among them,  
And wonders in the land of Ham.

## v28

He sent darkness and made it dark;  
And they did not rebel against His words.

## v29

He turned their waters into blood,  
And killed their fish.

## v30

Their land teemed with frogs,  
Even in the chambers of their kings.

## v31

He spoke, and a swarm of flies came;  
There were lice within all their borders.

## v32

He gave them hail for rain,  
And flaming fire in their land.

## v33

He struck their vines and their fig trees,  
And He broke the trees of their borders.

## v34

He spoke, and the swarming locusts came,  
And the licking locusts, even without number.

## v35

And they ate every herb in their land,  
And they ate the fruit of their ground.

## v36

He struck all the firstborn in their land,  
The firstfruits of all their vigor.

## v37

And He brought them out with silver and gold,  
And there was none among His tribes who stumbled.

## v38

Egypt rejoiced at their going forth,  
For a dread of them had fallen upon them.

## v39

He spread a cloud for a covering,  
And a fire to give light by night.

## v40

They asked, and He brought quails;  
And He satisfied them with the bread of heaven.

## v41

He opened the rock, and water gushed forth;  
It ran in the dry places like a river.

## v42

For He remembered His holy word  
And Abraham His servant.

## v43

And He brought forth His people with gladness,  
His chosen ones with a ringing shout.

## v44

And He gave them the lands of the nations,  
And they took possession of the labor of the peoples,

## v45

So that they might keep His statutes  
And observe His laws.  
Hallelujah.
