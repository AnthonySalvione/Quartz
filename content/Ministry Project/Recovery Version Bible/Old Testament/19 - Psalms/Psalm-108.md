## v1

My heart is fixed, O God;  
I will sing, indeed I will sing psalms,  
Even with my glory.

## v2

Awake, O harp and lyre!  
I will awaken the dawn.

## v3

I will give thanks to You among the peoples, O Jehovah;  
And I will sing psalms to You among the nations.

## v4

For Your lovingkindness is higher than the heavens,  
And Your faithfulness reaches to the sky.

## v5

Be exalted above the heavens, O God;  
And let Your glory be above all the earth.

## v6

That Your beloved ones may be delivered,  
Save with Your right hand, and answer us.

## v7

God has spoken in His holiness: I will exult;  
I will divide Shechem  
And measure out the Valley of Succoth.

## v8

Gilead is Mine; Manasseh is Mine;  
And Ephraim is the helmet for My head;  
Judah is My scepter.

## v9

Moab is My washbowl;  
Upon Edom I will cast My sandal;  
Over Philistia I will shout.

## v10

Who will bring me into the fortified city?  
Who will lead me to Edom?

## v11

Will not You, O God, who rejected us  
And who did not go forth, O God, with our armies?

## v12

Give us help from the adversary,  
For a salvation by man is vain.

## v13

In God we will do valiantly,  
And it is He who will tread down our adversaries.
