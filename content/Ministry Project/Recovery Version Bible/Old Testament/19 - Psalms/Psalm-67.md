## v1

God be gracious to us and bless us;  
May He make His face shine upon us; Selah

## v2

That Your way may be known on earth,  
And Your salvation among all the nations.

## v3

Let the peoples praise You, O God;  
Let the peoples praise You, all of them.

## v4

Let the nations rejoice and shout for joy,  
For You will judge the peoples equitably  
And will guide the nations on the earth. Selah

## v5

Let the peoples praise You, O God;  
Let the peoples praise You, all of them.

## v6

The earth has yielded its increase;  
God, our God, blesses us.

## v7

God blesses us;  
And all the ends of the earth will fear Him.
