# Psalm-127 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-127|Psalm 127]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Unless

**Verse:** [[Psalm-127#v1|Psalm 127:1]]

[[Psalm-127|Psalm 127]] is the praise of the saints, in their going up to Zion, concerning Jehovah’s care for and blessing to His people.

### Footnote 1.2 — house

**Verse:** [[Psalm-127#v1|Psalm 127:1]]

Unless the Lord builds the church as God’s house ([[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]), those who build it ([[1 Corinthians-03#v10|1 Cor. 3:10-12]]; [[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-12]], [[Ephesians-04#v16|16]]) labor in vain. Unless the Lord keeps the city, i.e., keeps the church as the kingdom ([[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18-19]]; [[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]), those who guard the church ([[Acts-20#v28|Acts 20:28-31]]) watch in vain.

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — vain

**Verse:** [[Psalm-127#v2|Psalm 127:2]]

If the Lord does not do anything for us, whatever we do will be in vain. We need to labor in the Lord and by His grace ([[1 Corinthians-15#v10|1 Cor. 15:10]], [[1 Corinthians-15#v58|58]]; [[Colossians-01#v28|Col. 1:28-29]]), but what the result will be depends not on our labor but on God’s care and blessing. Instead of toiling and striving in ourselves, we need to trust in Him and rest in Him, for He gives to His beloved even while they sleep.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — children

**Verse:** [[Psalm-127#v3|Psalm 127:3]]

Instead of striving without trusting in the Lord, we should simply be a good father producing children. We cannot gain much by our toil, but we can produce spiritual children (cf. [[1 Corinthians-04#v15|1 Cor. 4:15]]). In addition to His caring for us, God blesses us with increase, with children. Increase does not depend on what we do; it depends on how much God gives. Both the care and the blessing come from Him.
