## v1

Bless Jehovah, O my soul.  
O Jehovah my God, You are very great.  
You are clothed with majesty and splendor,

## v2

Wrapped with light as with a garment;  
You stretch out the heavens like a tent curtain.

## v3

He lays the beams of His upper chambers in the waters;  
He makes the clouds His chariot;  
He walks upon the wings of the wind.

## v4

He makes winds His messengers,  
Flames of fire His ministers.

## v5

He established the earth upon its foundations,  
So that it cannot be moved forever and ever.

## v6

You covered it with the deep as with a garment;  
The waters stood above the mountains.

## v7

At Your rebuke they fled;  
At the voice of Your thunder they rushed away —

## v8

The mountains rose, the valleys sank —  
To the place that You established for them.

## v9

You set a border that they may not pass over,  
That they may not turn back to cover the earth.

## v10

He makes the springs gush forth in the valleys;  
They run among the mountains;

## v11

They give drink to every beast of the field;  
The wild asses quench their thirst.

## v12

The birds of heaven dwell alongside them;  
They utter their voice among the branches.

## v13

He waters the mountains from His upper chambers.  
The earth is satisfied with the fruit of Your works.

## v14

He causes the grass to grow for the cattle,  
And herbage for man’s use,  
That he may bring forth food from the earth:

## v15

Even wine that cheers man’s heart,  
Oil that makes his face shine,  
And bread that sustains man’s heart.

## v16

The trees of Jehovah are full of sap,  
The cedars of Lebanon, which He planted,

## v17

Where the birds build their nests;  
As for the stork, the cypresses are her house.

## v18

The high mountains are for the wild goats;  
The cliffs are a refuge to the rock badgers.

## v19

He made the moon to mark seasons;  
The sun knows the time of its going down.

## v20

You make darkness, and it is night,  
In which all the beasts of the forest creep about.

## v21

The young lions roar after their prey,  
Even to seek their food from God.

## v22

The sun rises: they retreat  
And lie down in their dens.

## v23

Man goes forth to his work  
And to his labor until evening.

## v24

How many are Your works, O Jehovah!  
In wisdom You have made all of them;  
The earth is full of Your creatures.

## v25

Yonder is the sea, great and extensively wide;  
There the creeping things are, even without number,  
The small animals with the great.

## v26

There the ships go;  
Leviathan is there, whom You have formed  
To play in it.

## v27

They all wait for You,  
That You may give them food at the proper time.

## v28

You give to them; they gather.  
You open Your hand; they are satisfied with good.

## v29

You hide Your face; they are troubled.  
You take away their breath; they perish,  
And return to their dust.

## v30

You send forth Your Spirit; they are created,  
And You renew the surface of the ground.

## v31

May the glory of Jehovah endure forever!  
May Jehovah rejoice in His works,

## v32

He who looks on the earth, and it trembles;  
He who touches the mountains, and they smoke!

## v33

I will sing to Jehovah while I live;  
I will sing psalms to my God while I yet have being.

## v34

May my musing be sweet to Him;  
I will rejoice in Jehovah.

## v35

May the sinners be consumed from off the earth,  
And may the wicked be no more.  
Bless Jehovah, O my soul.  
Hallelujah.
