# Psalm-20 — Footnotes

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — May

**Verse:** [[Psalm-20#v1|Psalm 20:1]]

[[Psalm-20|Psalms 20]] and 21 reveal David’s concept concerning his kingship before God. In this psalm David blessed his people with what God is and has. According to the principle of the Bible, the greater blesses the lesser ([[Hebrews-07#v7|Heb. 7:7]]). That David could bless his people indicates that he had attained to a higher stature in life (cf. [[Genesis-47#v7|Gen. 47:7]] and [note](<Genesis-47.fn#Footnote 7.1 — blessed>)).
