## v1

When I call out, answer me,  
O God of my righteousness;  
Make room for me when I am in straits;  
Be gracious to me and hear my prayer.

## v2

O sons of men, how long will my glory be made a reproach?  
How long will you love vanity, will you seek a lie? Selah

## v3

But know that Jehovah sets apart the faithful man for Himself.  
Jehovah hears when I call out to Him.

## v4

Be angry, yet do not sin;  
Consider in your heart upon your bed,  
And be silent. Selah

## v5

Offer sacrifices of righteousness,  
And trust in Jehovah.

## v6

Many are those who say, Who will show us any good?  
Lift up upon us the light of Your countenance, O Jehovah.

## v7

You have put joy in my heart,  
More than when the grain and new wine of others abound.

## v8

In peace I will both lie down and sleep,  
For You alone, O Jehovah, cause me to dwell in safety.
