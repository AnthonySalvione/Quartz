## v1

Surely God is good to Israel,  
To those who are pure in heart.

## v2

But as for me, my feet were nearly turned aside;  
My steps had almost slipped.

## v3

For I was envious of the arrogant,  
When I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

## v4

For they have no pangs in their death,  
And their body is well nourished.

## v5

They do not find themselves in the hardship of men,  
Nor are they plagued like other men.

## v6

Therefore pride is a necklace for them;  
Violence covers them like a garment.

## v7

Their eyes bulge out from fatness;  
The imaginations of their heart overflow.

## v8

They mock and wickedly speak of oppression;  
They speak loftily.

## v9

They set their mouth against the heavens,  
And their tongue walks about on the earth.

## v10

Therefore His people return here,  
And waters of abundance are drained by them;

## v11

And they say, How does God know?  
And is there knowledge with the Most High?

## v12

Behold, these are the wicked;  
And always at ease, they heap up riches.

## v13

Surely I have purified my heart in vain,  
And I have washed my hands in innocence.

## v14

For I have been plagued all day long  
And chastened every morning.

## v15

If I had said, I will speak thus;  
Behold, I would have betrayed the generation of Your children.

## v16

When I considered this in order to understand it,  
It was a troublesome task in my sight,

## v17

Until I went into the sanctuary of God;  
Then I perceived their end.

## v18

Surely You set them in slippery places;  
You cast them down into ruins.

## v19

How they are made desolate in a moment!  
They are utterly consumed by terrors.

## v20

Like a dream, when one awakes, You, O Lord,  
Upon arising, will despise their image.

## v21

When my heart was embittered,  
And inwardly I was pricked;

## v22

I was brutish and knew nothing;  
I was like a beast before You.

## v23

Nevertheless I am continually with You;  
You have taken hold of my right hand.

## v24

You will guide me with Your counsel,  
And afterward You will receive me in glory.

## v25

Whom do I have in heaven but You?  
And besides You there is nothing I desire on earth.

## v26

My flesh and my heart fail,  
But God is the rock of my heart and my portion forever.

## v27

For behold, those who are far from You will perish;  
You destroy all who go about as harlots away from You.

## v28

But as for me, drawing near to God is good for me;  
I have made the Lord Jehovah my refuge,  
That I may declare all Your works.
