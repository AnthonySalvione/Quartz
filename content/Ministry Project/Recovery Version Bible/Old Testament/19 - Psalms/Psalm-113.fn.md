# Psalm-113 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-113|Psalm 113]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Hallelujah

**Verse:** [[Psalm-113#v1|Psalm 113:1]]

See [note 35¹](<Psalm-104.fn#Footnote 35.1 — Hallelujah>) in [[Psalm-104|Psa. 104]]. This psalm is the praise of God’s elect for God’s exaltation ([[Psalm-104#v4|vv. 4-5]]) and His humility ([[Psalm-104#v6|v. 6]]) in taking care of the lowly and needy ones ([[Psalm-104#v7|vv. 7-9]]). God’s humility was manifested in His incarnation. Christ humbled Himself and, because of His humility, was exalted ([[Philippians-02#v5|Phil. 2:5-11]]).
