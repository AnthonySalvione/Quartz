## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — righteousness

**Verse:** [[Psalm-04#v1|Psalm 4:1]]

In [[Psalm-04#v1|vv. 1-4]] David asked God to vindicate him of his righteousness, proclaimed that he was a godly man whom God set apart for Himself and who was heard by God when he called out to Him, and admonished others not to sin. In light of his great failure in murdering Uriah and robbing him of his wife ([[2 Samuel-11|2 Sam. 11]]), he should have asked God to enlighten him so that he could see how evil and sinful he was.
