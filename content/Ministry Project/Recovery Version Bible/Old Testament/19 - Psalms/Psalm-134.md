> *A Song of Ascents*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-134.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Bless|Bless]] Jehovah now, / All you servants of Jehovah / Who stand by night in the house of Jehovah.

## v2

Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, / And bless Jehovah.

## v3

May Jehovah, who made heaven and earth, / Bless you [[Psalm-134.fn#Footnote 3.1 — from|from]] Zion.
