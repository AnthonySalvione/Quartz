> *A Psalm of David*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 1.1 — The|The]] earth is Jehovah’s, and its fullness, / The habitable land and those who dwell in it.

## v2

For it is He who founded it upon the seas / And established it upon the streams.

## v3

[[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Who|Who]] may ascend the [[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 3.2 — mountain|mountain]] of Jehovah, / And who may stand in His holy place?

## v4

He who has clean hands and a pure heart, / Who has not lifted up his soul to falsehood / Or sworn deceitfully.

## v5

He will receive blessing from Jehovah, / And righteousness from the God of his salvation.

## v6

This is the generation of those who seek Him, / Those who seek Your face, even Jacob. Selah

## v7

[[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Lift|Lift]] up your heads, O [[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 7.2 — gates|gates]]; / And be lifted up, O [[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 7.3 — long|long]] enduring [[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 7.2 — gates|doors]]; / And the [[Psalm-24.fn#Footnote 7.4 — King|King]] of glory will come in.

## v8

Who is the King of glory? / Jehovah strong and mighty! / Jehovah mighty in battle!

## v9

Lift up your heads, O gates; / And lift up, O long enduring doors; / And the King of glory will come in.

## v10

Who is this King of glory? / Jehovah of hosts — / He is the King of glory! Selah
