## v1

Give ear, O God, to my prayer;  
And do not hide Yourself from my supplication.

## v2

Give heed to me, and answer me.  
I grow restless in my complaint and moan,

## v3

Because of the voice of the enemy,  
Because of the oppression of the wicked man;  
For they drop iniquity upon me,  
And in anger they make me their enemy.

## v4

My heart writhes within me,  
And the terrors of death have fallen upon me.

## v5

Fear and trembling have come upon me,  
And shuddering overwhelms me.

## v6

And I say, Oh that I had wings like a dove!  
Then I would fly away and be at rest.

## v7

Indeed I would wander far away;  
I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah

## v8

I would hasten my escape  
From the storm wind and the tempest.

## v9

Confuse, O Lord, divide their tongue;  
For I have seen violence and contention in the city.

## v10

Day and night they go around it,  
Upon its walls;  
And iniquity and trouble are in the midst of it.

## v11

Utter ruin is in the midst of it,  
And oppression and deceit  
Do not depart from its streets.

## v12

For it is not an enemy who reproaches me;  
Otherwise I could bear it.  
It is not he who hates me who magnifies himself against me;  
Otherwise I could hide myself from him.

## v13

But it is you, my equal,  
My close friend and my acquaintance.

## v14

We were in sweet counsel together;  
We walked with the throng in the house of God.

## v15

Let death surprise them;  
May they go down into Sheol alive.  
For evil is in their dwelling, in the midst of them.

## v16

As for me, I call out to God,  
And Jehovah will save me.

## v17

Evening and morning and at noontime  
I complain and moan,  
And He hears my voice.

## v18

He has redeemed my soul in peace  
From the battle against me,  
For many are they who strive with me.

## v19

God hears and answers them —  
Even He who sits enthroned from of old — Selah  
Because with them there is no change,  
And they do not fear God.

## v20

He put forth his hands against those who were at peace with him;  
He profaned his covenant.

## v21

Smooth were the buttery words of his mouth,  
But his heart was war;  
More soothing than oil were his words,  
But they were in fact drawn swords.

## v22

Cast your burden upon Jehovah,  
And He will sustain you;  
He will never allow the righteous to be moved.

## v23

But You, O God, will bring them down  
Into the pit of decay.  
Men of bloodshed and deceit  
Will not live out half their days;  
But I will trust in You.
