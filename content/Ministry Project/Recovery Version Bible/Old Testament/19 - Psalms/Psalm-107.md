## v1

Give thanks to Jehovah, for He is good,  
For His lovingkindness is forever.

## v2

Let the redeemed of Jehovah say so,  
Whom He redeemed from the hand of the adversary,

## v3

And gathered out of the lands,  
From the east and from the west,  
From the north and from the south.

## v4

They wandered in the wilderness, on the desert way;  
They found no city of habitation.

## v5

Hungry and thirsty,  
Their soul fainted within them.

## v6

Then they cried out to Jehovah in their trouble,  
And He delivered them from their distresses.

## v7

And He led them on a straight way  
That they might come to a city of habitation.

## v8

Let them give thanks to Jehovah for His lovingkindness  
And for His wondrous deeds to the children of men.

## v9

For He satisfies the thirsty soul,  
And the famished soul He fills with good.

## v10

Some inhabited darkness and the shadow of death,  
And were bound in affliction and irons,

## v11

Because they rebelled against the words of God  
And spurned the counsel of the Most High;

## v12

Therefore He brought down their heart with labor;  
They stumbled, and there was no one to help them.

## v13

Then they cried out to Jehovah in their trouble,  
And He saved them from their distresses.

## v14

He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death,  
And He broke apart their bonds.

## v15

Let them give thanks to Jehovah for His lovingkindness  
And for His wondrous deeds to the children of men.

## v16

For He breaks down the doors of bronze  
And cuts down the bars of iron.

## v17

Some were fools, because of their way of transgression;  
And because of their iniquities, they were afflicted.

## v18

Their soul loathed all food,  
And they drew near to the gates of death.

## v19

Then they cried out to Jehovah in their trouble,  
And He saved them out of their distresses.

## v20

He sent forth His word and healed them,  
And He delivered them from the pits of destruction.

## v21

Let them give thanks to Jehovah for His lovingkindness  
And for His wondrous deeds to the children of men.

## v22

And let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving  
And declare His doings in joyful song.

## v23

Those who go down to the sea in ships,  
Conducting business on the mighty waters,

## v24

These see Jehovah’s doings  
And His wondrous deeds in the deep.

## v25

For He commanded and raised up a storm wind,  
And it lifted up the waves of the sea.

## v26

They mounted up to the heavens; they went down to the depths;  
Their soul melted in the calamity.

## v27

They reeled and staggered like a drunken man,  
And all their skill was swallowed up.

## v28

Then they cried out to Jehovah in their trouble,  
And He brought them out of their distresses.

## v29

He made the storm be still,  
And the waves of the sea were calm;

## v30

And they rejoiced that they were quiet,  
And He brought them to the haven they desired.

## v31

Let them give thanks to Jehovah for His lovingkindness  
And for His wondrous deeds to the children of men.

## v32

And let them exalt Him in the congregation of the people  
And praise Him in the assembly of the elders.

## v33

He turns rivers into a wilderness,  
And springs of water into thirsty ground;

## v34

Fruitful land into a salt flat,  
Because of the wickedness of those who dwell in it.

## v35

He turns a wilderness into a pool of water,  
And a dry land into springs of water.

## v36

And He makes the hungry dwell there,  
That they may prepare a city of habitation

## v37

And sow fields and plant vineyards  
And get a fruitful yield.

## v38

He also blesses them, and they are multiplied greatly;  
And He does not let their cattle decrease.

## v39

When they are diminished and bowed down  
Through oppression, trouble, and sorrow,

## v40

He pours contempt on princes,  
And makes them wander in a pathless waste.

## v41

But He raises the needy man high above affliction  
And makes his families like flocks.

## v42

The upright see it and rejoice;  
And all iniquity stops its mouth.

## v43

Who is wise? Then let him observe these things  
And consider the lovingkindness of Jehovah.
