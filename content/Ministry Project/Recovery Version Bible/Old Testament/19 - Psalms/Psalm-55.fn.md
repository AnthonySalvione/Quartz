## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Give

**Verse:** [[Psalm-55#v1|Psalm 55:1]]

In [[Psalm-55#v1|vv. 1-2]], [[Psalm-55#v4|4-8]], [[Psalm-55#v16|16-18a]], [[Psalm-55#v22|22]], and [[Psalm-55#v23|23b]] David sought to experience God’s salvation. At the same time, while under the oppression of his enemy, he asked God to deal with his enemy ([[Psalm-55#v3|vv. 3]], [[Psalm-55#v9|9-15]], [[Psalm-55#v18|18b-21]], [[Psalm-55#v23|23a]]). His request was based not on the principle of mercy and grace but on the principle of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. See [note 7¹](<Psalm-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — strike>) in [[Psalm-03|Psa. 3]].

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — my

**Verse:** [[Psalm-55#v13|Psalm 55:13]]

Lit., a man according to my own estimate. In these verses David prophesied concerning Judas’ betraying of Christ ([[Psalm-41#v9|41:9]]; [[John-13#v18|John 13:18]]). David was a type of the suffering Christ (see [note 1¹](<1 Kings-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — David’s>) in [[1 Kings-02|1 Kings 2]]). In his pious expression of his complex sentiment concerning his suffering of his enemies’ oppression, he uttered this prophecy, indicating that Judas’ betrayal of Christ was a part of the sufferings of Christ.
