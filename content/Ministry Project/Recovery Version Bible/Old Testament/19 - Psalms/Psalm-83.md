## v1

O God, do not keep silent;  
Do not be quiet, and do not be still, O God.

## v2

For, behold, Your enemies are in tumult,  
And those who hate You lift up their heads.

## v3

They devise crafty counsel against Your people,  
And conspire against Your hidden ones.

## v4

They have said, Come and let us destroy them from being a nation,  
That the name of Israel may no longer be remembered.

## v5

For they have conspired together with one heart;  
It is against You that they have made an alliance:

## v6

The tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites,  
Moab and the Hagarites,

## v7

Gebal and Ammon and Amalek,  
Philistia along with the inhabitants of Tyre;

## v8

Assyria is also joined with them;  
They have been a help to the children of Lot. Selah

## v9

Do with them as with Midian,  
As with Sisera and Jabin, at the brook Kishon:

## v10

They were destroyed at Endor;  
They became dung for the earth.

## v11

Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb,  
And all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna,

## v12

Who said, Let us possess for ourselves  
The habitations of God.

## v13

O my God, make them like a tumbleweed,  
Like stubble before the wind.

## v14

Like fire that burns a forest  
And like a flame that sets mountains on fire,

## v15

So pursue them with Your tempest,  
And terrify them with Your storm.

## v16

Fill their faces with shame,  
That they may seek Your name, O Jehovah.

## v17

May they be ashamed and dismayed forever,  
And may they be confounded and perish;

## v18

That they may know that You alone, whose name is Jehovah,  
Are the Most High over all the earth.
