## v1

The Mighty One, God Jehovah,  
Speaks and summons the earth  
From the rising of the sun to its setting.

## v2

Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty,  
God shines forth.

## v3

Our God has come and will not be silent;  
Fire devours before Him,  
And around Him it is exceedingly tempestuous.

## v4

He calls to the heavens above,  
And to the earth that He may judge His people:

## v5

Gather My saints to Me,  
Those who have made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.

## v6

Then the heavens declare His righteousness,  
For God Himself is Judge. Selah

## v7

Hear, O My people, and I will speak;  
O Israel, and I will testify against you:  
I am God, your God.

## v8

I will not reprove you because of your sacrifices,  
And your burnt offerings are before Me continually.

## v9

I will not take a bull from your house  
Nor goats from your folds,

## v10

For every animal of the forest is Mine,  
The cattle upon a thousand hills.

## v11

I know every bird of the mountains,  
And I am aware of everything that moves in the field.

## v12

If I were hungry, I would not tell you;  
For the world is Mine and the fullness thereof.

## v13

Shall I eat the flesh of bulls  
Or drink the blood of goats?

## v14

Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving,  
And repay your vows to the Most High;

## v15

And call upon Me in the day of trouble:  
I will deliver you, and you will glorify Me.

## v16

But to the wicked God says,  
What right do you have to declare My statutes  
And take up My covenant in your mouth,

## v17

Seeing that you hate correction  
And cast My words behind you?

## v18

When you see a thief, you take pleasure in him;  
And your portion is with adulterers.

## v19

You let your mouth loose for evil,  
And your tongue frames deceit.

## v20

You sit and speak against your brother;  
You defame the son of your mother.

## v21

These things you do, and I keep silent;  
You think that I am altogether like you.  
I will rebuke you and set forth the matter in order before your eyes.

## v22

Consider this then, you who forget God,  
Lest I tear you apart and there be none to deliver you.

## v23

Whoever offers a sacrifice of thanksgiving glorifies Me;  
And to him who sets his way right I will show the salvation of God.
