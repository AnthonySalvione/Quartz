# Psalm-76 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-76|Psalm 76]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — God

**Verse:** [[Psalm-76#v1|Psalm 76:1]]

This psalm concerns the victory of God in His dwelling place. [[Psalm-76#v1|Verses 1-5]] are a declaration concerning the victory of God, as the glorious and excellent One, in His tabernacle. [[Psalm-76#v6|Verses 6-12]] are the praise of the psalmist concerning the wrath and fearfulness of God.

### Footnote 3.1 — There

**Verse:** [[Psalm-76#v3|Psalm 76:3]]
