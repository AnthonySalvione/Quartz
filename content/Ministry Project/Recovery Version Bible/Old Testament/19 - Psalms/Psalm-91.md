## v1

He who dwells in the secret place of the Most High  
Will abide in the shadow of the Almighty.

## v2

I say of Jehovah,  
My refuge and My fortress,  
My God in whom I trust!

## v3

For He will deliver You  
From the snare of the fowler,  
From the deadly pestilence.

## v4

With His pinions He will cover You,  
And under His wings You will take refuge;  
His truth is a shield and a buckler.

## v5

You will not be afraid of the terror by night,  
Or of the arrow that flies by day,

## v6

Or of the pestilence that walks in darkness,  
Or of the destruction that lays waste at noon.

## v7

A thousand will fall at Your side,  
And ten thousand at Your right hand;  
But it will not come near to You.

## v8

You will only look on with Your eyes  
And see the recompense of the wicked.

## v9

For You have made Jehovah, who is my refuge,  
Even the Most High, Your habitation;

## v10

No evil will befall You,  
Nor will any plague come near Your tent.

## v11

For He will give His angels charge concerning You  
To keep You in all Your ways.

## v12

They will bear You up in their hands,  
Lest You dash Your foot against a stone.

## v13

You will tread upon the lion and the cobra;  
You will trample the young lion and the serpent.

## v14

Because He has set His love upon Me, I will rescue Him;  
I will set Him on high, because He has known My name.

## v15

He will call upon Me, and I will answer Him.  
I will be with Him in distress;  
I will deliver Him and honor Him.

## v16

With an extension of His days I will satisfy Him,  
And I will show Him My salvation.
