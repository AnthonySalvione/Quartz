## v1

O God, give Your judgments to the king,  
And Your righteousness to the son of the king.

## v2

He will judge Your people in righteousness,  
And Your poor with justice.

## v3

The mountains will bear peace to the people,  
And the hills, in righteousness.

## v4

He will judge the poor of the people;  
He will save the children of the needy  
And crush the oppressor.

## v5

They will fear You as long as the sun endures  
And as long as the moon endures  
Throughout the generations.

## v6

He will drop like rain upon mown grass,  
Like abundant showers dripping on the earth.

## v7

In His days the righteous will flourish,  
And there will be an abundance of peace  
Until the moon is no more.

## v8

And He will have dominion from sea to sea  
And from the River unto the ends of the earth.

## v9

The desert dwellers will bow down before Him,  
And His enemies will lick the dust.

## v10

The kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands  
Will pay tribute;  
The kings of Sheba and Seba  
Will present gifts.

## v11

And all the kings will bow down before Him;  
All the nations will serve Him.

## v12

For He will deliver the needy man when he cries,  
And the poor man who has no one to help him.

## v13

He will have pity on the weak and needy,  
And the souls of the needy He will save.

## v14

From oppression and violence  
He will redeem their soul,  
And their blood will be precious in His sight.

## v15

So He shall live and to Him shall be given  
The gold of Sheba;  
And prayer shall be made continually for Him;  
He shall be blessed all the day long.

## v16

There will be an abundance of grain on the earth,  
Even at the tops of the mountains.  
Its fruit will wave like the forests of Lebanon,  
And those of the city will blossom like the foliage of the earth.

## v17

His name will be forever;  
As long as the sun endures, His name will spread;  
And men will be blessed in Him;  
All the nations will call Him blessed.

## v18

Blessed be Jehovah God, the God of Israel,  
Who alone does wondrous deeds;

## v19

And blessed be His glorious name forever;  
And may His glory fill the whole earth.  
Amen and Amen.

## v20

The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.
