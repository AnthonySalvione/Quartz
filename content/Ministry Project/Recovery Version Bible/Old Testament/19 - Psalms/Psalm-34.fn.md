# Psalm-34 — Footnotes

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Psalm-34#v1|Psalm 34:1]]

### Footnote 1.2 — bless

**Verse:** [[Psalm-34#v1|Psalm 34:1]]

To bless God is to speak well of Him, praising Him with adoration. See [note 3¹](<Ephesians-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Blessed>) in [[Ephesians-01|Eph. 1]].

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — saved

**Verse:** [[Psalm-34#v6|Psalm 34:6]]

Here David gave God the credit for delivering him from Abimelech (Achish the king of Gath—[[1 Samuel-21#v10|1 Sam. 21:10]]). Actually, David delivered himself in a dishonorable way by disguising himself as being insane ([[1 Samuel-21#v10|1 Sam. 21:10]]—[[1 Samuel-22#v1|22:1a]]).

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — Taste

**Verse:** [[Psalm-34#v8|Psalm 34:8]]

God with His goodness can be tasted by us ([[1 Peter-02#v3|1 Pet. 2:3]] and [note 1](<1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — tasted>)). We taste and see that God is good in His house, that is, in Christ, in the church, and eventually in the New Jerusalem. See [note 1¹](<Psalm-25.fn#Footnote 1.1 — To>) in [[Psalm-25|Psa. 25]].

### Footnote 8.2 — Blessed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-34#v8|Psalm 34:8]]

Or, Happy.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — Not

**Verse:** [[Psalm-34#v20|Psalm 34:20]]

Here, in describing his own suffering, David typified the suffering Christ ([[John-19#v33|John 19:33]], [[John-19#v36|36]]; cf. [[Exodus-12#v46|Exo. 12:46]]). See [note 36²](<John-19.fn#Footnote 36.2 — bone>) in [[John-19|John 19]].
