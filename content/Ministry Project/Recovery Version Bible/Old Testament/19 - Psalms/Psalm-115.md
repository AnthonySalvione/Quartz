## v1

Not to us, O Jehovah, not to us,  
But to Your name give glory,  
For the sake of Your lovingkindness and Your truth.

## v2

Why should the nations say,  
Where then is their God?

## v3

But our God is in the heavens;  
He does whatever He pleases.

## v4

Their idols are mere silver and gold,  
The work of human hands.

## v5

They have mouths, but they do not speak;  
They have eyes, but they do not see.

## v6

They have ears, but they do not hear;  
They have noses, but they do not smell.

## v7

They have hands, but they do not feel;  
They have feet, but they do not walk;  
They make no sound in their throat.

## v8

Those who make them,  
All who trust in them,  
Are like them.

## v9

O Israel, trust in Jehovah.  
He is their help and their shield.

## v10

O house of Aaron, trust in Jehovah.  
He is their help and their shield.

## v11

O you who fear Jehovah, trust in Jehovah.  
He is their help and their shield.

## v12

Jehovah has been mindful of us; He will bless us;  
He will bless the house of Israel;  
He will bless the house of Aaron;

## v13

He will bless those who fear Jehovah,  
Both small and great.

## v14

May Jehovah give you increase,  
Both you and your children.

## v15

May you be blessed of Jehovah,  
Maker of heaven and earth.

## v16

The heavens are the heavens of Jehovah,  
But He has given the earth to the children of men.

## v17

The dead do not praise Jehovah,  
Nor do any that go down into silence.

## v18

But we will bless Jehovah  
From now and to eternity.  
Hallelujah.
