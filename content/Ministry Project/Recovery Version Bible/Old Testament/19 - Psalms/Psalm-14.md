## v1

The fool has said in his heart,  
There is no God.  
They are corrupt; they commit abominable deeds;  
There is none who does good.

## v2

Jehovah looked down from heaven  
Upon the sons of men  
To see if there was anyone who had insight,  
Who seeks after God.

## v3

They have all turned aside;  
They are together perverse.  
There is none who does good;  
There is not even one.

## v4

Have they no knowledge, all the workers of iniquity,  
Who eat up my people as they would eat up bread  
And do not call upon Jehovah?

## v5

There they were in great fear,  
For God is among the generation of the righteous.

## v6

You put the counsel of the afflicted to shame,  
But Jehovah is their refuge.

## v7

Oh that the salvation of Israel might come forth from Zion!  
When Jehovah turns the captivity of His people,  
Jacob will exult, Israel will rejoice.
