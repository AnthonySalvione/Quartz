# Psalm-139 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-139|Psalm 139]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — searched

**Verse:** [[Psalm-139#v1|Psalm 139:1]]

The subject of [[Psalm-139|Psa. 139]] is the psalmist’s asking Jehovah for His searching and trying. [[Psalm-139#v1|Verses 1-6]] speak of God’s omniscience, and [[Psalm-139#v7|vv. 7-12]], of God’s omnipresence.

## Footnotes for verse 19

### Footnote 19.1 — slay

**Verse:** [[Psalm-139#v19|Psalm 139:19]]

[[Psalm-139#v19|Verses 19-22]] describe the psalmist’s hating and loathing the wickedness of the wicked according to the principle of good and evil. See [note 3¹](<Psalm-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — unrighteousness>) in [[Psalm-07|Psa. 7]].
