# Psalm-103 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-103|Psalm 103]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Bless

**Verse:** [[Psalm-103#v1|Psalm 103:1]]

[[Psalm-103|Psalms 103—106]] are the praising of Jehovah, who has recovered the title and the right over the whole earth through the reign of Christ. In these four psalms there is a narration of God’s history. This psalm speaks of God’s history in His lovingkindness and compassions in His forgiving of sins, healing, redeeming, and caring for His people. This is the first part of God’s history.

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — prime

**Verse:** [[Psalm-103#v5|Psalm 103:5]]

The meaning of the Hebrew is uncertain; some translate, your years; others, your desires.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — ways

**Verse:** [[Psalm-103#v7|Psalm 103:7]]

See [note 10²](<Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — ways>) in [[Hebrews-03|Heb. 3]]. To the experienced ones, such as Moses, God makes His ways known, and to the younger ones, to the children, He makes His acts known. Both God’s ways and God’s acts are benefits to us.
