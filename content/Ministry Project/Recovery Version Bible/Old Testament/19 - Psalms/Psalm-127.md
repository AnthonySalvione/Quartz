> *A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon*

---
## v1

[[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Unless|Unless]] Jehovah builds the [[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 1.2 — house|house]], / Those who build it labor in vain. / Unless Jehovah keeps the [[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 1.2 — house|city]], / The guard watches in vain.

## v2

It is [[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 2.1 — vain|vain]] for you to rise up early, / To stay up late, / To eat the bread of toil; / All the same, He gives to His beloved while they sleep.

## v3

Behold, [[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 3.1 — children|children]] are the heritage of Jehovah, / The fruit of the womb a reward.

## v4

Like arrows in the hand of a mighty man, / So are the children of one’s youth.

## v5

Blessed is the man / Who fills his quiver with them. / [[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 5.1 — He|He]] will not be put to shame / When [[Psalm-127.fn#Footnote 5.1 — He|he]] speaks with enemies in the gate.
