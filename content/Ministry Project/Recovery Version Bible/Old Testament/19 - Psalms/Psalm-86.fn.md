## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Incline

**Verse:** [[Psalm-86#v1|Psalm 86:1]]

This psalm concerns David’s seeking for his personal salvation. Salvation is not for ourselves. God saves people for His economy, for His Christ, for Zion, and for His house and His city in order that one day He might gain the entire earth through Christ with His overcomers.
