> *A Song; a Psalm of the sons of Korah. To the choir director: according to mahalath leannoth. A Maschil of Heman the Ezrahite*

> [!light] Note on mahalath:
> The meaning of the Hebrew is obscure. It may refer to the singing of a sad melody with a subdued voice.

> [!light] Note on Maschil:
> See note on the superscription of [[Psalm-32|Psa. 32]].
---
## v1

O Jehovah, the God of my [[Psalm-88.fn#Footnote 1.1 — salvation|salvation]],  
I have cried out by day and in the night before You.

## v2

May my prayer come before You;  
Incline Your ear to my cry.

## v3

For my soul is full of troubles,  
And my life draws near to Sheol.

## v4

I am accounted among those who go down into the pit;  
I am like a man without any [[Psalm-88.fn#Footnote 4.1 — help|help]],

## v5

Abandoned among the dead,  
Like the slain that lie in the grave,  
Whom You remember no more,  
And they are cut off from Your hand.

## v6

You have put me in the lowest pit,  
In the dark places, in the depths.

## v7

Your wrath lies heavily upon me,  
And You have afflicted me with all Your waves. Selah

## v8

You have put my acquaintances far from me;  
You have made me an abomination to them.  
I am shut in and cannot go out.

## v9

My eye wastes away at the affliction;  
I have called out to You, O Jehovah, every day;  
I have spread out my hands to You.

## v10

Will You perform wonders for the dead?  
Will the deceased rise up and praise You? Selah

## v11

Will Your lovingkindness be declared in the grave,  
Or Your faithfulness in [[Psalm-88.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Abaddon|Abaddon]]?

## v12

Will Your wonders be made known in the darkness,  
Or Your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

## v13

But I, O Jehovah, have cried out to You;  
And in the morning my prayer comes before You.

## v14

Why, O Jehovah, have You rejected my soul?  
Why do You hide Your face from me?

## v15

I have been afflicted and about to die since my youth;  
I bear Your terrors; I am [[Psalm-88.fn#Footnote 15.1 — overwhelmed|overwhelmed]].

## v16

Your fierce wrath has gone over me;  
Your terrors have cut me off.

## v17

They surrounded me like water all day long;  
They altogether encompassed me.

## v18

You have put far from me  
Lover and friend;  
My acquaintances are in darkness.
