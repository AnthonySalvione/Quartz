# Psalm-78 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-78|Psalm 78]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — instruction

**Verse:** [[Psalm-78#v1|Psalm 78:1]]

The subject of this psalm is man’s failure and God’s restoration. [[Psalm-78#v1|Verses 1-58]] are the psalmist’s recalling of Israel’s failure in the past; [[Psalm-78#v59|vv. 59-64]] describe the issue of Israel’s failure; and [[Psalm-78#v65|vv. 65-72]] is a word concerning God’s restoration of Israel.

## Footnotes for verse 61

### Footnote 61.1 — Ark

**Verse:** [[Psalm-78#v61|Psalm 78:61]]

Lit., strength. This refers to the capture of the Ark by the Philistines before the temple was built ([[1 Samuel-04|1 Sam. 4]]).

### Footnote 61.2 — glory

**Verse:** [[Psalm-78#v61|Psalm 78:61]]

Or, beauty.

## Footnotes for verse 71

### Footnote 71.1 — shepherd

**Verse:** [[Psalm-78#v71|Psalm 78:71]]

Or, pasture, feed. So also in the next verse. Here David ([[Psalm-78#v70|v. 70]]) is a type of Christ as the Shepherd of God’s people ([[Matthew-02#v6|Matt. 2:6]]; [[John-10#v11|John 10:11]]; [[Hebrews-13#v20|Heb. 13:20]]; [[1 Peter-02#v25|1 Pet. 2:25]]; [[1 Peter-05#v4|5:4]]). See [note 1²](<Psalm-23.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Shepherd>) in [[Psalm-23|Psa. 23]]. In the restoration of God’s people there is always the shepherding, the feeding, of Christ.
