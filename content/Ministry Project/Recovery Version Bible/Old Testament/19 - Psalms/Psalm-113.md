## v1

Hallelujah!  
Praise, you servants of Jehovah,  
Praise the name of Jehovah.

## v2

Let the name of Jehovah be blessed,  
From now and to eternity.

## v3

From the rising of the sun to its setting  
The name of Jehovah is to be praised.

## v4

Jehovah is high above all the nations,  
And His glory is above the heavens.

## v5

Who is like Jehovah our God,  
Who has set His throne on high,

## v6

Who humbles Himself to behold  
The heavens and the earth?

## v7

He raises the poor from the dust;  
From the ash heap He lifts the needy;

## v8

That He may seat them with princes,  
With the princes of His people.

## v9

He causes the barren woman to keep house  
As a joyful mother of children.  
Hallelujah.
