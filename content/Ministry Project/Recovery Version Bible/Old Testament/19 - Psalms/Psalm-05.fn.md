## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — For

**Verse:** [[Psalm-05#v4|Psalm 5:4]]

David highly uplifted and exalted the law and knew that God does not delight in wickedness but hates iniquity. But he conspired against Uriah, murdered him, and stole his wife ([[2 Samuel-11|2 Sam. 11]]), thus breaking all the last five commandments in the law. This shows that the law and the knowledge of it avail nothing.
