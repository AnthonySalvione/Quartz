# Psalm-107 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Psalm-107|Psalm 107]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — redeemed

**Verse:** [[Psalm-107#v2|Psalm 107:2]]

[[Psalm-107|Psalm 107]] concerns God’s care for and deliverance of His redeemed in Christ. The word redeemed here implies Christ, for He is our Redeemer ([[Galatians-03#v13|Gal. 3:13]]; [[1 Peter-01#v18|1 Pet. 1:18-19]]) and even our redemption ([[Luke-02#v38|Luke 2:38]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]). In Christ we enjoy God’s care and deliverance, which imply His rescue and saving every day.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — gathered

**Verse:** [[Psalm-107#v3|Psalm 107:3]]

This gathering corresponds to [[Revelation-05#v9|Rev. 5:9]], which speaks of those purchased for God by Christ’s blood “out of every tribe and tongue and people and nation.”

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — wandered

**Verse:** [[Psalm-107#v4|Psalm 107:4]]

God’s care and deliverance of the famished wanderers in [[Psalm-107#v4|vv. 4-9]], of those imprisoned in darkness and the shadow of death in [[Psalm-107#v10|vv. 10-16]] (cf. [[Acts-26#v18|Acts 26:18]]), of the ones who are sick because of their way of transgression in [[Psalm-107#v17|vv. 17-22]] (cf. [[Matthew-09#v2|Matt. 9:2]]), of the seafarers in [[Psalm-107#v23|vv. 23-32]], and of the needy in [[Psalm-107#v34|vv. 34-43]], typify His care for and deliverance of His believers in the church life. According to the New Testament revelation, God’s care for the believers and His deliverance of the believers require that Christ be sitting in His ascension at the right hand of God, as revealed in [[Psalm-110|Psa. 110]]. Because Christ is our High Priest in the heavens interceding for us ([[Romans-08#v34|Rom. 8:34]]; [[Hebrews-07#v25|Heb. 7:25]]), God’s care is Christ’s care for us and God’s deliverance is Christ’s deliverance of us.
