## v1

It is good to give thanks to Jehovah  
And to sing psalms to Your name, O Most High;

## v2

To declare Your lovingkindness in the morning  
And Your faithfulness in the nights,

## v3

Upon an instrument of ten strings and upon a harp,  
Upon the higgaion of the lyre.

## v4

For You have made me rejoice, O Jehovah, because of what You have done;  
Because of the works of Your hands I will shout for joy.

## v5

How great are Your works, O Jehovah!  
So very deep are Your thoughts!

## v6

A senseless man does not know them,  
Nor does a fool understand them.

## v7

When the wicked sprout like grass,  
And all the workers of iniquity blossom,  
It is so that they may be destroyed forever.

## v8

But You are on high forever, O Jehovah.

## v9

For behold, Your enemies, O Jehovah;  
For behold, Your enemies will perish;  
All the workers of iniquity will be scattered.

## v10

But You have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox;  
I am anointed with fresh oil.

## v11

And my eye has seen its desire for my enemies;  
My ears have heard their desire for the evildoers who rise up against me.

## v12

The righteous man will flourish like the palm tree;  
He will grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

## v13

Planted in the house of Jehovah,  
They will flourish in the courts of our God.

## v14

They will still bring forth fruit in old age;  
They will be full of sap and green;

## v15

To declare that Jehovah is upright;  
He is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.
