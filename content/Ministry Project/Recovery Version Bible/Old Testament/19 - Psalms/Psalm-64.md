## v1

Hear, O God, my voice in my complaint;  
Preserve my life from the enemy’s dread.

## v2

Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers,  
From the throng of the workers of iniquity,

## v3

Who have sharpened their tongue like a sword,  
Who have aimed their arrow, their bitter word,

## v4

To shoot in secret places at the perfect man.  
They shoot suddenly at him and do not fear.

## v5

They firm up for themselves an evil matter;  
They talk of laying snares secretly;  
They say, Who will see them?

## v6

They seek out injustices, saying:  
We have completed a carefully sought-out plan.  
Indeed each man’s inward thought  
And heart are deep.

## v7

But God will shoot an arrow at them;  
Suddenly they are wounded.

## v8

They are made to stumble over each other by their own tongue;  
All who see them shake their heads.

## v9

And all men will fear  
And declare what has been done by God,  
And they will ponder His work.

## v10

The righteous man will rejoice in Jehovah and take refuge in Him,  
And all the upright in heart will boast.
