## v1

Save, O Jehovah; for the faithful man is no more,  
For the trustworthy have vanished from among the sons of men.

## v2

Each man speaks falsehood with his neighbor;  
With flattering lips and a double heart they speak.

## v3

May Jehovah cut off all the flattering lips,  
The tongue speaking great things,

## v4

Those who said, With our tongue we will prevail;  
Our lips are our own: Who is our lord?

## v5

Because of the devastation of the poor, because of the sighing of the needy,  
I will now arise, says Jehovah;  
I will set him in the safety that he longs for.

## v6

The words of Jehovah are pure words,  
Silver refined in a furnace on the earth,  
Purified seven times.

## v7

You, O Jehovah, will keep them;  
You will guard them from this generation forever.

## v8

The wicked go about all around,  
While vileness is exalted among the sons of men.
