## v1

O God, hasten to deliver me;  
O Jehovah, hasten to help me.

## v2

May those who seek my life  
Be put to shame and confounded;  
May those who take delight in my misfortune  
Be turned back and humiliated;

## v3

May those who say, Aha! Aha!  
Turn away because of their shame;

## v4

May all those who seek You  
Be glad and rejoice in You;  
And may those who love Your salvation  
Say continually,  
May God be magnified!

## v5

But I am poor and needy;  
O God, hasten to me.  
You are my help and my Deliverer;  
O Jehovah, do not delay.
