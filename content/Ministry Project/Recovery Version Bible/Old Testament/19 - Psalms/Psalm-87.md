## v1

His foundation is in the holy mountains.

## v2

Jehovah loves the gates of Zion  
More than all the dwellings of Jacob.

## v3

Glorious things are spoken of you,  
O city of God. Selah

## v4

I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon as those who know Me;  
Behold, Philistia and Tyre along with Cush:  
This one was born there, they say.

## v5

But of Zion it will be said,  
This one and that one were born in her,  
And the Most High Himself will establish her.

## v6

Jehovah will count  
When He records the peoples:  
This One was born there. Selah

## v7

Then singing as well as dancing, they will say,  
All my springs are in you.
