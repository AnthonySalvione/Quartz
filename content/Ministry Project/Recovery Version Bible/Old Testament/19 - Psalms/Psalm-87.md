> *Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm; a Song*

---
## v1

His [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 1.1 — foundation|foundation]] is in the holy mountains.

## v2

Jehovah loves the [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 2.1 — gates|gates]] of [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Zion|Zion]]  
More than all the dwellings of Jacob.

## v3

Glorious things are spoken of you,  
O city of God. Selah

## v4

I will make mention of [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Rahab|Rahab]] and Babylon as those who know Me;  
Behold, Philistia and Tyre along with [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Cush|Cush]]:  
[[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 4.3 — This|This]] one was born there, they say.

## v5

But of Zion it will be said,  
[[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 5.1 — This|This]] one and that one were born in her,  
And the Most High Himself will establish her.

## v6

Jehovah will count  
When He records the peoples:  
This [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 6.1 — One|One]] was born there. Selah

## v7

Then singing as well as dancing, they will say,  
All my springs are in [[Psalm-87.fn#Footnote 7.1 — you|you]].
