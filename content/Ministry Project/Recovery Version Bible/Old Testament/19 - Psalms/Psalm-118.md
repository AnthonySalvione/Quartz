## v1

Give thanks to Jehovah, for He is good,  
For His lovingkindness is forever.

## v2

Let Israel say,  
His lovingkindness is forever.

## v3

Let the house of Aaron say,  
His lovingkindness is forever.

## v4

Let those who fear Jehovah say,  
His lovingkindness is forever.

## v5

Out of my distress I called upon Jehovah;  
Jehovah answered me and set me in a place broad and free.

## v6

Jehovah is for me; I will not be afraid.  
What can man do to me?

## v7

Jehovah is for me as my Helper,  
And I will see what should happen to those who hate me.

## v8

It is better to take refuge in Jehovah  
Than to trust in man.

## v9

It is better to take refuge in Jehovah  
Than to trust in princes.

## v10

All nations surrounded me;  
In the name of Jehovah I indeed cut them down!

## v11

They surrounded me; yes, they surrounded me;  
In the name of Jehovah I indeed cut them down!

## v12

They surrounded me like bees;  
They were extinguished like a fire of thorns;  
In the name of Jehovah I indeed cut them down!

## v13

I was pushed hard that I might fall;  
But Jehovah helped me.

## v14

Jehovah is my strength and my song,  
And He has become my salvation.

## v15

The voice of rejoicing and salvation  
Is in the tents of the righteous.  
The right hand of Jehovah does valiantly;

## v16

The right hand of Jehovah is exalted;  
The right hand of Jehovah does valiantly.

## v17

I will not die, but live,  
And declare the works of Jehovah.

## v18

Jehovah has severely chastened me,  
But He has not given me over to death.

## v19

Open to me the gates of righteousness:  
I will enter in through them; I will give thanks to Jehovah.

## v20

This is the gate of Jehovah;  
The righteous shall enter in through it.

## v21

I will give thanks to You, for You have answered me  
And have become my salvation.

## v22

The stone which the builders rejected  
Has become the head of the corner.

## v23

This is from Jehovah;  
It is wonderful in our sight.

## v24

This is the day that Jehovah has made;  
Let us exult and rejoice in it.

## v25

O Jehovah, do save, we pray!  
O Jehovah, do send prosperity, we pray!

## v26

Blessed is He who comes in the name of Jehovah;  
We bless you from the house of Jehovah.

## v27

Jehovah is God,  
And He has given us light;  
Bind the sacrifice of the feast with cords  
To the horns of the altar.

## v28

You are my God, and I give thanks to You.  
You are my God; I will exalt You.

## v29

Give thanks to Jehovah, for He is good,  
For His lovingkindness is forever.
