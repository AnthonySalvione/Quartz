## v1

Hear this, all you peoples;  
Give ear, all you inhabitants of the world,

## v2

Both low and high,  
Rich and poor together;

## v3

My mouth will speak wisdom,  
And the meditation of my heart will be that of understanding.

## v4

I will incline my ear to a parable;  
I will disclose my riddle upon the harp.

## v5

Why should I fear in the days of evil,  
When the iniquity of those who supplant me surrounds me,

## v6

Those who trust in their wealth  
And boast in the multitude of their riches?

## v7

None can by any means redeem his brother  
Or give to God a ransom for him

## v8

(For the redemption of their soul is costly  
And must be given up forever),

## v9

That he would yet live always  
And not see corruption.

## v10

For he will see that wise men die;  
The foolish one and the senseless one perish alike  
And leave their wealth to others.

## v11

Their inward thought is that their houses will remain forever,  
That their habitations will remain from generation to generation;  
They call lands after their own names.

## v12

But man in honor does not remain;  
He is like the beasts that perish.

## v13

Such is the way of those who are self-confident,  
And of those after them who approve of their sayings. Selah

## v14

Like a flock, they are appointed for Sheol;  
Death will shepherd them.  
And straight to the grave they will descend,  
And their form will be for Sheol to consume  
That there be no abode for them.

## v15

But God will redeem my soul  
From the power of Sheol,  
For He will receive me. Selah

## v16

Do not fear when a man becomes rich,  
When the glory of his house increases.

## v17

For when he dies, he will take nothing;  
His glory will not descend after him.

## v18

Though he blessed his soul while he lived —  
And men praise you when you do good for yourself —

## v19

It will go to the generation of his fathers;  
They will never see light.

## v20

A man in honor but without understanding  
Is like the beasts that perish.
