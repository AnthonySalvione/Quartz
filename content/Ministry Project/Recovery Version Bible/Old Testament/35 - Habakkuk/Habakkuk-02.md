## v1

I will stand at my watch  
And station myself on the fortress,  
And I will watch to see what He will speak to me  
And what I will reply concerning my complaint.

## v2

Then Jehovah answered me and said,  
Write the vision, and render it plainly upon tablets,  
That even he who runs by may read it.

## v3

For the vision is yet for the appointed time,  
And it hastens toward the end and will not lie.  
Though it tarries, wait for it;  
For it will surely come; it will not delay.

## v4

See, he who is puffed up,  
His soul is not upright within him,  
But the righteous one will live by his faith.

## v5

Furthermore his wine is treacherous:  
A boasting man, he does not abide at home,  
He who enlarges his appetite like Sheol,  
And is like death and cannot be satisfied,  
But gathers to himself all the nations  
And collects for himself all the peoples.

## v6

Will not all of these take up a parable against him,  
Indeed a mocking song and a riddle against him, and say,  
Woe to him who increases what is not his (For how long?)  
And who loads himself with the burden of pledges?

## v7

Will not those who bite you rise up suddenly,  
And those who shake you awake,  
And you will become booty to them?

## v8

Because you have plundered many nations;  
All the remnant of the peoples will plunder you,  
Because of the bloodshed of men and the violence done to the land,  
To the city, and to all the inhabitants in it.

## v9

Woe to him who by violence takes evil gain for his house,  
So as to place his nest on high  
That he may be delivered from the hand of evil!

## v10

You have devised shame against your own house  
By cutting off many peoples,  
And have sinned against your own soul.

## v11

For the stone will cry out from the wall,  
And the rafter will answer it from the timber.

## v12

Woe to him who builds a city by bloodshed  
And establishes a town by iniquity!

## v13

Indeed is it not of Jehovah of hosts  
That the peoples toil for the fire  
And the nations weary themselves for vanity?

## v14

For the earth will be filled  
With the knowledge of the glory of Jehovah  
As water covers the sea.

## v15

Woe to him who makes his neighbor drink,  
Adding in his poison,  
And also making him drunk  
In order to look at their nakedness!

## v16

You are sated with shame, not glory.  
You also, drink and be shown to be uncircumcised!  
The cup of Jehovah’s right hand will come around to you,  
And disgrace will be upon your glory.

## v17

For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you,  
As well as the destruction of the beasts which frightened them,  
Because of the bloodshed of men and the violence done to the land,  
To the city, and to all the inhabitants in it.

## v18

What profit is the graven idol that its maker should engrave it,  
Or the molten idol, even the teacher of lies,  
That he who forms its form depends on it  
To make dumb idols?

## v19

Woe to him who says to wood, Awake!  
And, Arise! to silent stone.  
Will it teach him?  
Behold, it is set in gold and silver,  
And there is no breath at all within it.

## v20

But Jehovah is in His holy temple:  
Be silent before Him, all the earth!
