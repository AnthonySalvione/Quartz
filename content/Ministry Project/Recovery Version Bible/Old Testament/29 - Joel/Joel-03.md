## v1

For indeed, in those days and at that time  
When I turn the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem,

## v2

I will gather all the nations  
And will bring them down to the valley of Jehoshaphat;  
And I will enter into judgment with them there  
Because of My people and My inheritance Israel,  
Whom they have scattered among the nations.  
And they have divided My land

## v3

And have cast lots for My people;  
And they have given a boy for a prostitute  
And sold a girl for wine to drink.

## v4

Indeed then, what are you to Me, Tyre and Sidon  
And all the regions of Philistia?  
Will you repay Me with a recompense?  
But if you recompense Me,  
Swiftly, speedily,  
I will return your recompense upon your own head.

## v5

In that you have taken My silver and My gold,  
And have brought My fine precious things into your temples,

## v6

And have sold to the children of the Javanim  
The children of Judah and the children of Jerusalem,  
In order to remove them  
Far from their borders;

## v7

I will rouse them out of the place  
To which you have sold them,  
And will return your recompense upon your own head.

## v8

And I will sell your sons and your daughters  
Into the hand of the children of Judah,  
And they will sell them to the Sabeans,  
To a nation that is far off,  
For Jehovah has spoken.

## v9

Proclaim this among the nations:  
Prepare the battle;  
Rouse the mighty men;  
Let all the men of battle  
Draw near; let them go up.

## v10

Beat your plowshares into swords,  
And your pruning knives into spears;  
Let the weak say,  
I am mighty.

## v11

Hurry and come,  
All you surrounding nations,  
And be gathered.  
There cause Your mighty ones to descend, O Jehovah!

## v12

Let the nations rouse themselves and come up  
To the valley of Jehoshaphat.  
For there I will sit to judge  
All the surrounding nations.

## v13

Send forth the sickle,  
For the harvest is ripe;  
Come, tread,  
For the winepress is full;  
The wine vats overflow,  
For their evil is great.

## v14

Multitudes, multitudes,  
In the valley of decision;  
For the day of Jehovah has drawn near  
In the valley of decision.

## v15

The sun and the moon grow dark,  
And the stars withdraw their shining.

## v16

And Jehovah will roar from Zion,  
And from Jerusalem He will utter His voice,  
And the heavens and the earth will shake.  
But Jehovah will be a shelter to His people  
And a stronghold to the children of Israel.

## v17

Thus you will know that I am Jehovah your God,  
Dwelling in Zion, My holy mountain.  
And Jerusalem will be holy,  
And strangers will pass through her no longer.

## v18

And in that day  
The mountains will drop down fresh wine,  
And the hills will flow with milk;  
And all the waterways of Judah  
Will flow with water,  
And a fountain will go forth from the house of Jehovah,  
And it will water the valley of Shittim.

## v19

Egypt will be a desolation,  
And Edom will be a desolate wilderness,  
Because of the violence against the children of Judah,  
In that they have shed innocent blood in their land.

## v20

But Judah will abide forever,  
And Jerusalem, from generation to generation;

## v21

And I will acquit them of their bloodshed that I had not acquitted them of,  
For Jehovah dwells in Zion.
