# 1 Chronicles-27 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[1 Chronicles-27|1 Chronicles 27]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — served

**Verse:** [[1 Chronicles-27#v1|1 Chronicles 27:1]]

The officers who served the king were of twelve divisions, twenty-four thousand in each division, taking care of the king’s affairs month by month throughout all the months of the year ([[1 Chronicles-27#v1|vv. 1-15]]). David’s standard of living was excessive and too luxurious, and it became a hidden factor in the rebellion that occurred after the time of Solomon. See [note 4¹](<1 Kings-12.fn#Footnote 4.1 — yoke>) in [[1 Kings-12|1 Kings 12]].

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — over

**Verse:** [[1 Chronicles-27#v16|1 Chronicles 27:16]]

The governmental order in David’s administration typifies the submission to the authority and headship of Christ in the church in the New Testament ([[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-05#v24|5:24]]; [[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18a]]).
