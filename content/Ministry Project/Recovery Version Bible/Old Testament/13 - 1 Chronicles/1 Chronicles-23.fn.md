## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — priests

**Verse:** [[1 Chronicles-23#v2|1 Chronicles 23:2]]

David’s arranging in order Israel’s services to God related to the temple of God ([[1 Chronicles-06#v31|6:31-48]]; [[1 Chronicles-23|1 Chronicles 23—26]]) typifies the Spirit’s arranging in order the church services in the New Testament ([[1 Corinthians-12#v4|1 Cor. 12:4-11]]). It typifies further that as the Head of the Body, Christ has set up an order in His Body for all the members to keep ([[1 Corinthians-14#v40|1 Cor. 14:40]]).
