## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — man

**Verse:** [[Genesis-32#v24|Genesis 32:24]]

According to [[Hosea-12#v4|Hosea 12:4]], this man was the Angel of Jehovah, Christ (see [note 2¹](<Exodus-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Angel>) in [[Exodus-03|Exo. 3]]). The Lord as a man wrestled with Jacob so that He might touch Jacob’s natural strength, signified by the socket of Jacob’s hip at the thigh muscle ([[Genesis-32#v25|vv. 25]], [[Genesis-32#v32|32]]). The Lord’s wrestling with Jacob lasted a considerable time, fully exposing how natural Jacob was.

### Footnote 25.2 — touched

**Verse:** [[Genesis-32#v25|Genesis 32:25]]

The touching of the socket of Jacob’s hip at the thigh muscle ([[Genesis-32#v32|v. 32]]), the strongest muscle in the body, signifies the touching of Jacob’s natural life, his natural strength. This was the beginning of Jacob’s transformation. As [[Genesis-33|Genesis 33]] shows (see notes [[Genesis-33.fn#Footnote 4.1 — kissed|4¹]] and [[Genesis-33.fn#Footnote 17.1 — built|17¹]] there), after this experience Jacob was still natural. Nevertheless, although there was no change in Jacob’s outward living, his natural life, his inward natural strength, had been dealt with by the Lord. This is signified by the fact that Jacob walked with a limp ([[Genesis-32#v31|v. 31]]). The way of religion is to change man’s outward behavior; the way of God in His economy is to touch man’s inward life in order to change his inward being.

## Footnotes for verse 29

### Footnote 29.1 — there

**Verse:** [[Genesis-32#v29|Genesis 32:29]]

It does not say that the man came to Jacob before wrestling with him ([[Genesis-32#v24|v. 24]]), nor does it say that the man left Jacob after wrestling with him. This means that the Lord who wrestled with Jacob was with him all the time and never left him. Cf. [[John-20#v26|John 20:26]] and [note 3](<John-20.fn#Footnote 26.3 — came>).
