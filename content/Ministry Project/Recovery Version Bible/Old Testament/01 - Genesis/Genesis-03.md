## v1

Now the [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — serpent|serpent]] was more crafty than any other animal of the field that Jehovah God had made. And he said to the [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — woman|woman]], [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Did|Did]] God really say, You shall not eat of any tree of the garden?

## v2

And the woman [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — said|said]] to the serpent, Of the fruit of the trees of the garden we may eat;

## v3

But of the fruit of the tree which is in the middle of the garden, God has said, You shall not eat of it, nor shall you touch it, lest you die.

## v4

And the serpent said to the woman, You shall not surely die!

## v5

[[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — For|For]] God knows that in the day you eat of it your eyes will be opened, and you will become like [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — God|God]], knowing good and evil.

## v6

And when the woman saw that the tree was good for food and that it was a delight to the eyes and that the tree was to be desired to make oneself wise, she [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — took|took]] of its fruit and ate; and she also gave some to her husband with her, and he ate.

## v7

[[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — And|And]] the eyes of both of them were opened, and they [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — knew|knew]] that they were naked; and they sewed [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 7.3 — fig|fig]] leaves together and made loincloths for themselves.

## v8

And they heard the [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — sound|sound]] of Jehovah God walking about in the garden in the [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — cool|cool]] of the day, and the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of Jehovah God among the trees of the garden.

## v9

And [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Jehovah|Jehovah]] God called to the man and said to him, [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Where|Where]] are you?

## v10

And he said, I heard the sound of You in the garden, and I was afraid because I am naked; so I hid myself.

## v11

And He said, [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Who|Who]] told you that you are naked? Have you eaten of the tree of which I commanded you not to eat?

## v12

And the man said, The woman whom You gave to be with me, she gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate.

## v13

And Jehovah God said to the woman, What is this that you have done? And the woman said, The serpent deceived me, and I ate.

## v14

And Jehovah God said to the serpent,  
Because you have done this, /  
You are [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — cursed|cursed]] more than all the cattle /  
And more than all the animals of the field: /  
Upon your [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — stomach|stomach]] you will go, /  
And dust you will eat /  
All the days of your life.

## v15

And I will put enmity /  
Between you and the [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — woman|woman]] /  
And between your [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — seed|seed]] and her [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 15.3 — seed|seed]]; /  
He will [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 15.4 — bruise|bruise]] you on the head, /  
But you will [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 15.4 — bruise|bruise]] him on the heel.

## v16

To the woman He said,  
I will greatly multiply /  
Your pain in your [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — childbearing|childbearing]]; /  
In pain you will bring forth children. /  
And your desire will be to your husband, /  
And he will [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — childbearing|rule]] over you.

## v17

And to Adam He said, Because you listened to the voice of your wife and have eaten of the tree concerning which I commanded you, saying, You shall not eat of it;  
Cursed is the ground because of you; /  
In [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — toil|toil]] will you eat of it /  
All the days of your life.

## v18

And thorns and thistles will it bring forth for you, /  
And you will eat the herb of the field;

## v19

By the sweat of your face /  
You will eat bread /  
Until you return to the ground, /  
Because from it you were taken; /  
For dust you are, /  
And to dust you shall return.

## v20

And the man called his wife’s name [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Eve|Eve]], because she was the mother of all living.

## v21

And Jehovah God made coats of [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — skin|skin]] for Adam and for his wife and [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 21.2 — clothed|clothed]] them.

## v22

And Jehovah God said, Behold, the man has become like one of Us, knowing good and evil; and now, [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — lest|lest]] he put forth his hand and take also from the tree of life and eat and live forever —

## v23

Then Jehovah God sent him forth from the garden of Eden, to work the ground from which he was taken.

## v24

So He drove the man out, and at the east of the garden of Eden He placed the [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 24.1 — cherubim|cherubim]] and a [[Genesis-03.fn#Footnote 24.1 — cherubim|flaming]] sword which turned in every direction to guard the way to the tree of life.
