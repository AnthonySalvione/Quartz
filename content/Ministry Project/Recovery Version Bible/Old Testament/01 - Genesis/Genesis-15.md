## v1

[[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 1.1 — After|After]] these things the word of Jehovah came to Abram in a vision, saying, Do not be afraid, Abram; I am your [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 1.2 — shield|shield]] and your exceedingly great [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 1.2 — shield|reward]].

## v2

And Abram said, O Lord Jehovah, what will You give me, for I go childless, and the heir of my house is Eliezer of Damascus?

## v3

And Abram said, Since You have given me no [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 3.1 — seed|seed]], therefore, a [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 3.2 — servant|servant]] in my house is my heir.

## v4

But then the word of Jehovah came to him, saying, This man shall not be your heir, but he who will come out from your own [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 4.1 — body|body]] shall be your heir.

## v5

And He brought him outside and said, Look now toward the heavens, and count the [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 5.1 — stars|stars]], if you are able to count them. And He said to him, So shall your seed be.

## v6

And he [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 6.1 — believed|believed]] Jehovah, and He accounted it to him as righteousness.

## v7

Then He said to him, I am Jehovah who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldeans to give you this land to inherit it.

## v8

And he said, O Lord Jehovah, [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 8.1 — how|how]] will I know that I will inherit it?

## v9

And He said to him, Bring Me a three-year-old [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 9.1 — heifer|heifer]] and a three-year-old female goat and a three-year-old ram and a turtledove and a young pigeon.

## v10

And he [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 10.1 — brought|brought]] Him all these and cut them in two in the middle and laid the half of each opposite the other, but the birds he did not cut in two.

## v11

And the [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 11.1 — birds|birds]] of prey came down upon the carcasses, and Abram drove them away.

## v12

And when the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, behold, a terror and great darkness fell upon him.

## v13

And He said to Abram, Know assuredly that your seed will be sojourners in a land that is not theirs, and they will serve them; and they will [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 13.1 — afflict|afflict]] them [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 13.2 — four|four]] hundred years.

## v14

But I will also judge that nation, whom they will serve, and afterward they will come out with great possessions.

## v15

But as for you, you will go to your fathers in peace; you will be buried in a good old age.

## v16

And in the fourth generation they will come here again, for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet complete.

## v17

And when the sun went down and it was dark, there came a smoking [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 17.1 — furnace|furnace]] and a [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 17.1 — furnace|torch]] of fire which passed between these pieces.

## v18

On that day Jehovah made a covenant with Abram, saying,  
To your seed do I give this land, /  
From the [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 18.1 — river|river]] of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates:

## v19

The land of the Kenites and the Kenizzites and the Kadmonites

## v20

And the Hittites and the Perizzites and the [[Genesis-15.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Rephaim|Rephaim]]

## v21

And the Amorites and the Canaanites and the Girgashites and the Jebusites.
