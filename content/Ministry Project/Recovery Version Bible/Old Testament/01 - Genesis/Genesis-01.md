
## v1

[[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — In|In]] the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — beginning|beginning]] [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — God|God]] [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — created|created]] the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 1.5 — heavens|heavens]] and the earth.

## v2

[[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — But|But]] the earth became [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — waste|waste]] and emptiness, and [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — waste|darkness]] was on the surface of the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — deep|deep]], [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — and|and]] the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 2.5 — Spirit|Spirit]] of God was brooding upon the surface of the waters. 

## v3

And God [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — said|said]], Let there be [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — said|light]]; and there was light.

## v4

And God saw that the light was good, and God [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — separated|separated]] the light from the darkness.

## v5

And God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night. And there was evening and there was morning, one day.

## v6

And God said, Let there be an [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — expanse|expanse]] in the midst of the waters, and let it [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — separate|separate]] the waters from the waters.

## v7

And God made the expanse and separated the waters which were under the expanse from the waters which were above the expanse, and it was so.

## v8

And God called the expanse Heaven. And there was evening and there was morning, a second [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — day|day]].

## v9

And God said, Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — dry|dry]] land appear; and it was so.

## v10

And God called the dry land [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Earth|Earth]], and the gathering together of the waters He called [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Earth|Seas]]; and God saw that it was good.

## v11

And God said, Let the earth sprout [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — grass|grass]], herbs yielding seed, and fruit trees bearing fruit according to their kind with their seed in them upon the earth; and it was so.

## v12

And the earth brought forth grass, herbs yielding seed according to their kind, and trees bearing fruit with their seed in them according to their kind; and God saw that it was good.

## v13

And there was evening and there was morning, a third day.

## v14

And God said, Let there be [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — light-bearers|light-bearers]] in the expanse of heaven to separate the day from the night, and let them be for [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — signs|signs]] and for seasons and for days and years;

## v15

And let them be light-bearers in the expanse of heaven to give light on the earth; and it was so.

## v16

And God [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — made|made]] the two great light-bearers, the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — greater|greater]] light-bearer to rule the day and the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — lesser|lesser]] light-bearer to rule the night, and the [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 16.4 — stars|stars]].

## v17

And God set them in the expanse of heaven to give light on the earth.

## v18

And to [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — rule|rule]] over the day and over the night and to [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — rule|separate]] the light from the darkness, and God saw that it was good.

## v19

And there was evening and there was morning, a fourth day.

## v20

And God said, Let the waters swarm with swarms of [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — living|living]] animals, and let [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — birds|birds]] fly above the earth in the open expanse of heaven.

## v21

And God created the great sea creatures and every living animal that moves, with which the waters swarmed, according to their kind, and every winged bird according to its kind; and God saw that it was good.

## v22

And God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds multiply on the earth.

## v23

And there was evening and there was morning, a fifth day.

## v24

And God said, Let the earth bring forth living animals according to their kind, [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — cattle|cattle]] and [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 24.2 — creeping|creeping]] things and [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — cattle|animals]] of the earth according to their kind; and it was so.

## v25

And God made the animals of the earth according to their kind and the cattle according to their kind and everything that creeps on the ground according to its kind, and God saw that it was good.

## v26

And God said, [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Let|Let]] Us make [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.2 — man|man]] in Our [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.3 — image|image]], according to Our [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.3 — image|likeness]]; and let [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.4 — them|them]] have [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.5 — dominion|dominion]] over the fish of the sea and over the birds of heaven and over the cattle and over all the earth and over every [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 26.6 — creeping|creeping]] thing that creeps upon the earth.

## v27

And God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.

## v28

And God blessed them; and God said to them, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 28.1 — subdue|subdue]] it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of heaven and over every living thing that moves upon the earth.

## v29

And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb that produces seed that is on the surface of all the earth and every tree [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 29.1 — which|which]] has fruit that produces seed; they shall be for you as [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 29.2 — food|food]].

## v30

And to every animal of the earth and to every bird of heaven and to everything that creeps upon the earth, in which is a living soul, I have given every green herb for food; and it was so.

## v31

And God saw everything that He had made, and indeed, it was [[Genesis-01.fn#Footnote 31.1 — very|very]] good. And there was evening and there was morning, the sixth day.
