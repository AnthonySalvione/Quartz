## v1

Thus the heavens and the earth and all their host were finished.

## v2

And on the seventh day God finished His work which He had done, and He [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — rested|rested]] on the seventh day from all His work which He had done.

## v3

And God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it because in it He rested from all His work which God had [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — created|created]] and made.

## v4

[[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — These|These]] are the generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — created|created]]. [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 4.3 — When|When]] [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 4.4 — Jehovah|Jehovah]] God [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — created|made]] earth and heaven,

## v5

And no plant of the field was yet in the earth, and no herb of the field had yet sprung up — for Jehovah God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was no man to [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — work|work]] the ground,

## v6

But a mist went up from the earth and watered the whole surface of the ground —

## v7

Jehovah God [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — formed|formed]] [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — man|man]] from the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 7.3 — dust|dust]] of the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 7.4 — ground|ground]] and breathed into his nostrils the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 7.5 — breath|breath]] of life, and man became a living [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 7.6 — soul|soul]].

## v8

And Jehovah God planted a garden in [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Eden|Eden]], in the east; and there He put the man whom He had formed.

## v9

And out of the ground Jehovah God caused to grow every tree that is [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — pleasant|pleasant]] to the sight and good for food, as well as the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — tree|tree]] of life in the middle of the garden and the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — tree|tree]] of the knowledge of good and evil.

## v10

And a [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — river|river]] went [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — forth|forth]] from Eden to water the garden, and from there it divided and became [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 10.3 — four|four]] [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 10.4 — branches|branches]].

## v11

The name of the first is Pishon; it is the one that goes around the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold.

## v12

And the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — gold|gold]] of that land is good; [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — gold|bdellium]] and [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — gold|onyx]] stone are there.

## v13

And the name of the second river is Gihon; it is the one that goes around the whole land of Cush.

## v14

And the name of the third river is Hiddekel; it is the one that goes east of Asshur. And the fourth river is the Euphrates.

## v15

And Jehovah God took the man and [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — put|put]] him in the garden of Eden to [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — work|work]] it and to [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 15.3 — keep|keep]] it.

## v16

And Jehovah God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden you may eat freely,

## v17

But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, of it you shall [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — not|not]] eat; for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — die|die]].

## v18

And Jehovah God said, It is not good for the [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — man|man]] to be alone; I will make him a helper as his [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 18.2 — counterpart|counterpart]].

## v19

Now Jehovah God had formed from the ground every animal of the field and every bird of heaven. And He brought them to the man to see what he would call them; and whatever the man called any living animal, that was its name.

## v20

And the man gave names to all cattle and to the birds of heaven and to every animal of the field, but for Adam there was [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — not|not]] found a helper as his counterpart.

## v21

And Jehovah God caused a [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — deep|deep]] sleep to fall upon the man, and he slept; and He took one of his [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 21.2 — ribs|ribs]] and closed up the flesh in its place.

## v22

And Jehovah God [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — built|built]] the rib, which He had taken from the man, into a [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 22.2 — woman|woman]] and [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 22.3 — brought|brought]] her to the man.

## v23

And the man said,  
This time this is bone of my bones /  
And flesh of my flesh; /  
This one shall be called [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Woman|Woman]] /  
Because out of [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — Man|Man]] this one was taken.

## v24

Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and shall cleave to his wife, and they shall become one [[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 24.1 — flesh|flesh]].

## v25

[[Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 25.1 — And|And]] both the man and his wife were naked and were not ashamed before each other.
