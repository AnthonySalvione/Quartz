## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — God

**Verse:** [[Genesis-31#v24|Genesis 31:24]]

The crucial person in this chapter is neither Jacob nor Laban, but the invisible, transforming God, who was sovereignly preparing the environment for Jacob’s transformation (cf. [[Romans-08#v28|Rom. 8:28-29]]). Jacob was under the secret care of Christ as the Angel of God ([[Genesis-31#v11|v. 11]]) because Christ would eventually be born of Jacob’s descendant Mary ([[Matthew-01#v2|Matt. 1:2]], [[Matthew-01#v16|16]]).

## Footnotes for verse 52

### Footnote 52.1 — witness

**Verse:** [[Genesis-31#v52|Genesis 31:52]]

The pillar set up here by Jacob was a witness, a testimony. The thought of a pillar as a testimony is seen also in the two pillars (two is the number for a witness, a testimony—[[Matthew-18#v16|Matt. 18:16]]; [[Luke-10#v1|Luke 10:1]]) set up by Solomon in front of the temple ([[1 Kings-07#v21|1 Kings 7:21]]), and in the church, the house of God, as the pillar that upholds the truth by testifying, manifesting, the truth ([[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15-16]]). The three pillars set up by Jacob in [[Genesis-31|Genesis 31]] and 35 were three landmarks of Jacob’s life. (The pillar set up in [[Genesis-28#v18|28:18]] was in response to a dream and was not part of Jacob’s spiritual experience; hence, it is not considered a landmark in his life. See [note 14¹](<Genesis-35.fn#Footnote 14.1 — pillar>) in [[Genesis-35|Genesis 35]].) The first pillar, set up at Galeed ([[Genesis-31#v45|vv. 45]], [[Genesis-31#v47|47]]), was a testimony of God’s sovereign care for him in the first stage of his experience. The second pillar, set up at Bethel ([[Genesis-35#v14|35:14]]), was a testimony of God’s building, God’s house, in the second stage. The third pillar, set up on Rachel’s grave on the way to Bethlehem ([[Genesis-35#v20|35:20]]), was a testimony of the death of Jacob’s natural choice for the bringing forth of Christ, typified by Benjamin, in the third stage of his experience (see notes [18¹](<Genesis-35.fn#Footnote 18.1 — died>) and [18³](<Genesis-35.fn#Footnote 18.3 — Benjamin>) in [[Genesis-35|Genesis 35]]).
