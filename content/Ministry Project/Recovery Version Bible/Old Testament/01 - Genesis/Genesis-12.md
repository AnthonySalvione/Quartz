## v1

[[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Now|Now]] Jehovah [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 1.2 — said|said]] to Abram,  
Go from your land /  
And from your relatives /  
And from your [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 1.3 — father’s|father’s]] house /  
To the land that I will show you;

## v2

[[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — And|And]] I will make of you a [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 2.2 — great|great]] nation, / 
And I will bless you /  
And make your name great; /  
And you shall be a blessing.

## v3

And I will bless those who bless you, /  
And him who [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — curses|curses]] you I will curse; /  
And in you [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — all|all]] the families of the earth will be [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 3.3 — blessed|blessed]].

## v4

So Abram went as Jehovah had spoken to him, and Lot went with him. Now Abram was seventy-five years old when he went out of Haran.

## v5

And Abram took Sarai his wife and Lot his brother’s son and all their possessions that they had gathered and the souls that they had acquired in Haran, and they [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 5.1 — went|went]] out to go to the land of Canaan. And they [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 5.2 — came|came]] to the land of [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 5.3 — Canaan|Canaan]].

## v6

And Abram passed through the land to the place of Shechem, to the oak of Moreh. And at that time the Canaanites were in the land.

## v7

And Jehovah [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 7.1 — appeared|appeared]] to Abram and said, To your [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 7.2 — seed|seed]] I will give this [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 7.2 — seed|land]]. And there he built an [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 7.3 — altar|altar]] to Jehovah who had appeared to him.

## v8

And he proceeded from there to the mountain on the east of [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Bethel|Bethel]] and pitched his [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 8.2 — tent|tent]], with Bethel on the west and [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Bethel|Ai]] on the east; and there he built an altar to Jehovah and [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 8.3 — called|called]] upon the name of Jehovah.

## v9

And Abram journeyed onward, journeying toward the [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Negev|Negev]].

## v10

And there was a [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 10.1 — famine|famine]] in the land; and Abram went down to Egypt to sojourn there, for the famine was severe in the land.

## v11

And when he was about to enter into Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, I know indeed that you are a beautiful woman to look at;

## v12

And when the Egyptians see you, they will say, This is his wife, and they will kill me; but they will keep you alive.

## v13

Now say you are my sister, that it may be well with me on account of you and [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 13.1 — I|I]] may live because of you.

## v14

And it came about that when Abram came to Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful.

## v15

And Pharaoh’s officials saw her and praised her before Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh’s house.

## v16

And he treated Abram well on account of her, and there were given to him sheep and oxen and donkeys and male servants and female servants and female donkeys and camels.

## v17

And Jehovah struck Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai, Abram’s wife.

## v18

And Pharaoh called Abram and said, What is this you have done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife?

## v19

Why did you say, She is my sister, so that I took her to be my wife? Now then here is your wife; take her and go.

## v20

And Pharaoh commanded his men concerning him, and they sent him away [[Genesis-12.fn#Footnote 20.1 — with|with]] his wife and all that he had.
