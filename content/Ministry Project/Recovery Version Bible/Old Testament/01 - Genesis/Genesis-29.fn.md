## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — Leah

**Verse:** [[Genesis-29#v25|Genesis 29:25]]

Jacob was a crafty supplanter, but Laban was more subtle than Jacob. This was sovereignly arranged by God. Everything Laban did to cheat and to “squeeze” Jacob ([[Genesis-31#v7|31:7]], [[Genesis-31#v40|40-42]]), plus the competition, envy, and wrestling between Jacob’s wives in their bearing children ([[Genesis-29#v31|29:31]]—[[Genesis-30#v24|30:24]]), were sovereignly used by God to deal with Jacob’s natural disposition so that God could transform him. Jacob’s history shows that God sovereignly arranges each aspect of the environment of His chosen ones so that He may carry out His work of transformation in them ([[Romans-08#v28|Rom. 8:28-30]]).
