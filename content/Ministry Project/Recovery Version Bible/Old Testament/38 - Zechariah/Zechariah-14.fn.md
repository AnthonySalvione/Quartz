# Zechariah-14 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Zechariah-14|Zechariah 14]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — half

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v2|Zechariah 14:2]]

In the great tribulation Antichrist and his armies will slaughter two-thirds of the Jews ([[Zechariah-13#v8|13:8]] and [note](<Zechariah-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Two>)). Of the one-third remaining in the land, most will probably be in the area of Jerusalem. Out of this one-third, half the city will be captured by Antichrist. In his mercy God will preserve the remaining half. Antichrist will attempt to destroy them also; however, as revealed in [[Zechariah-14#v3|vv. 3-7]], Jehovah as Christ will come with His saints ([[Zechariah-14#v3|vv. 3]], [[Zechariah-14#v5|5]]) and go forth to fight against and defeat Antichrist and his followers, the nations ([[Joel-03#v11|Joel 3:11]]; [[Revelation-17#v14|Rev. 17:14]]; [[Revelation-19#v11|19:11-21]]). This will result in Israel’s household salvation ([[Romans-11#v26|Rom. 11:26-27]]). See [[Zechariah-12#v8|12:8-10]] and notes.

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — the

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v6|Zechariah 14:6]]

The shining ones here are the sun, the moon, and the stars. The things that take place in [[Zechariah-14#v6|vv. 6-7]] indicate that God will change a number of things in the universe in order to judge the evil human beings and to save His elect.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — living

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v8|Zechariah 14:8]]

In the millennium there will be no thirst, for living waters will go forth from Jerusalem. This is similar to the record in [[Ezekiel-47#v1|Ezek. 47:1-2]].

### Footnote 8.2 — eastern

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v8|Zechariah 14:8]]

I.e., the Dead Sea.

### Footnote 8.3 — western

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v8|Zechariah 14:8]]

I.e., the Mediterranean Sea.

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — King

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v9|Zechariah 14:9]]

In the millennium Jehovah as Christ will be King over all the earth ([[Zechariah-09#v10|9:10b]]; [[Psalm-72#v8|Psa. 72:8]]), and He will be the one God and His name the one name.

## Footnotes for verse 17

### Footnote 17.1 — no

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v17|Zechariah 14:17]]

Because the present age is the age of grace, God sends rain on the just and the unjust alike ([[Matthew-05#v45|Matt. 5:45]]). However, the coming age will be the age of righteousness. Those who do not go up to Jerusalem to worship the King and to keep the Feast of Tabernacles will not receive rain.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — pots

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-14#v20|Zechariah 14:20]]

Pots are small, whereas the basins are large; but in the millennium the pots in the house of God will be as large as the basins before the altar.
