# Zechariah-03 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Zechariah-03|Zechariah 3]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Joshua

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v1|Zechariah 3:1]]

Joshua the high priest typifies Christ as the High Priest sent by God to His people ([[Hebrews-03#v1|Heb. 3:1]]; [[Hebrews-04#v14|4:14-15]]; [[Hebrews-07#v26|7:26]]). Joshua also represents and signifies Israel as a nation of priests ([[Exodus-19#v6|Exo. 19:6]]; [[Zechariah-08#v20|Zech. 8:20-23]]; [[Isaiah-02#v2|Isa. 2:2-4a]]). 

Joshua was standing before the Angel of Jehovah to be perfected, established, and strengthened in the priesthood ([[Zechariah-03#v1|vv. 1-10]]). The Angel of Jehovah would do this by measuring Joshua. Christ’s care for Joshua in this chapter is a continuation of His measuring in [[Zechariah-02#v1|2:1-2]].

### Footnote 1.2 — Satan

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v1|Zechariah 3:1]]

Or, the accuser, the adversary. See notes [10¹](<Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Satan>) in [[Matthew-04|Matt. 4]] and [10²](<Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 10.2 — accuser>) in [[Revelation-12|Rev. 12]]. As the adversary, Satan’s intention was to belittle Joshua in front of his fellows ([[Zechariah-03#v8|v. 8]]) and to frustrate the rebuilding of God’s temple. 

In [[Zechariah-03#v1|vv. 1-2]] there are three parties: Joshua, the Angel of Jehovah, and Satan. This is a repetition of the scene in the garden of Eden, where God put the man He had created in front of the tree of life, denoting God, and the tree of knowledge, denoting Satan ([[Genesis-02#v8|Gen. 2:8-9]]).

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — filthy

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v3|Zechariah 3:3]]

In type, garments signify one’s conduct as one’s expression ([[Isaiah-64#v6|Isa. 64:6]]; [[Revelation-19#v8|Rev. 19:8]]). That Joshua the high priest was clothed with filthy garments indicates that our conduct may still be unclean because we still live in the flesh, which is altogether filthy ([[Romans-07#v18|Rom. 7:18]]; [[2 Corinthians-07#v1|2 Cor. 7:1]]). Joshua’s filthy garments were the basis of Satan’s accusation.

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — Remove

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v4|Zechariah 3:4]]

The perfection of Christ as the Angel of Jehovah was extended to Joshua by the removing of the filthy garments from him, thus making his iniquity pass from him. Joshua was also clothed with stately robes, with garments befitting his office and status as high priest. These garments signify the expression of Christ in His divine glory and His human beauty ([[Exodus-28#v2|Exo. 28:2]] and notes).

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — He

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v5|Zechariah 3:5]]

Following some ancient versions; the Hebrew text reads, I.

### Footnote 5.2 — clean

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v5|Zechariah 3:5]]

The clean turban signifies that Joshua had been fully cleansed and was now clean in the presence of Christ as the Angel of Jehovah.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — servant

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v8|Zechariah 3:8]]

This refers to Zerubbabel, who is a type of Christ as the Servant of Jehovah, the Shoot of David ([[Jeremiah-23#v5|Jer. 23:5]]), in His humanity and royal faithfulness ([[Zechariah-06#v12|6:12]]). Although he was not a king but was a governor in the position of a king ([[Haggai-01#v1|Hag. 1:1]]), Zerubbabel was nevertheless a descendant, a shoot, of the royal family of David. As such, he typifies Christ.

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — stone

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v9|Zechariah 3:9]]

This stone (Zerubbabel) set before Joshua also typifies Christ as the stone for God’s building ([[Psalm-118#v22|Psa. 118:22]]; [[Isaiah-28#v16|Isa. 28:16]]; [[Matthew-21#v42|Matt. 21:42]]; [[1 Peter-02#v4|1 Pet. 2:4]]). Zerubbabel was a stone set before Joshua to carry out God’s economy.

### Footnote 9.2 — seven

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v9|Zechariah 3:9]]

The seven eyes of the stone (Christ) are the seven eyes of Jehovah and the seven eyes of the Lamb, Christ, which are the seven Spirits of God, the sevenfold intensified Spirit ([[Zechariah-04#v10|4:10]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|Rev. 5:6]]). See [note 10¹](<Zechariah-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — seven>) in [[Zechariah-04|Zechariah 4]].

### Footnote 9.3 — engrave

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v9|Zechariah 3:9]]

Jehovah’s engraving of the stone indicates that God will work on Christ as the stone for the accomplishing of God’s redemption, salvation, and building. To engrave is to cut. When Christ was dying on the cross, He was engraved, cut, by God.

### Footnote 9.4 — remove

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v9|Zechariah 3:9]]

This indicates that the Christ on whom God has worked will remove the sin of the land of Israel in one day, the day of His crucifixion ([[1 Peter-02#v24|1 Pet. 2:24]]). Through His death on the cross, Christ, the Lamb of God, took away the sin of the world ([[John-01#v29|John 1:29]]).

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — vine

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-03#v10|Zechariah 3:10]]

After our sin has been taken away ([[Zechariah-03#v9|v. 9]]) and our situation with God has been appeased, there is peace between us and God, and we can come together to enjoy Christ as the vine ([[John-15#v1|John 15:1]], [[John-15#v5|5]]), the tree of life (see [note 9²](<Genesis-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — tree>) in [[Genesis-02|Gen. 2]] and notes [2¹](<Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.1 — tree>) and [2²](<Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.2 — fruits>) in [[Revelation-22|Rev. 22]]), and as the fig tree, full of the fruit of life ([[Judges-09#v10|Judg. 9:10-11]]). Christ came to accomplish redemption, bearing the Spirit and being cut by God on the cross (see notes [9²](<Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — seven>) and [9³](<Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — engrave>)). This issued in our enjoyment of Him as the vine and as the fig tree. The measuring carried out by Christ as the Angel of Jehovah results in such a wonderful situation.
