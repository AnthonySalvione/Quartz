## v1

[[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Then|Then]] I lifted up my eyes again and I saw, and there was a [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 1.2 — flying|flying]] scroll.

## v2

And he said to me, What do you see? And I said, I see a flying scroll, its [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — length|length]] twenty cubits and its breadth ten cubits.

## v3

Then he said to me, This is the [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — curse|curse]] that goes forth over the face of all the land; for everyone who [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 3.2 — steals|steals]] will be purged according to what is on one side, and everyone who [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 3.2 — steals|swears]] will be purged according to what is on the other side.

## v4

I will cause [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — it|it]] to go forth, declares Jehovah of hosts; and it will enter the house of him who steals and the house of him who swears falsely by My name; and it will lodge overnight within his house and consume it, both with its timbers and its stones.

## v5

Then the angel who spoke with me went forth and said to me, Lift up now your eyes and see what this is that goes forth.

## v6

And I said, What is it? And he said, This is [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — the|the]] ephah vessel that goes forth; and he said, This is their [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 6.2 — appearance|appearance]] in all the land;

## v7

(Then a lead cover was lifted up) and this is a [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — woman|woman]] sitting within the ephah vessel.

## v8

Then he said, This is Wickedness. And he threw her down into the midst of the ephah vessel and threw the [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — lead|lead]] weight over its opening.

## v9

Then I lifted up my eyes and I looked, and there were [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — two|two]] women going forth, and the wind was in their wings; and they had wings like the wings of a stork. And they lifted up the ephah vessel between the earth and the heavens.

## v10

And I said to the angel who spoke with me, Where are they taking the ephah vessel?

## v11

And he said to me, [[Zechariah-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — To|To]] build a house for her in the land of Shinar; and when it is prepared, she will be set there in her own place.
