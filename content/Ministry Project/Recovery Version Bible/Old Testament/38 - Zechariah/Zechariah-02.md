## v1

Then I lifted up my eyes and I looked, and there was a [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — man|man]], and in His hand was a [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — measuring|measuring]] line.

## v2

And I said, Where are You going? And He said to me, To measure [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Jerusalem|Jerusalem]]; to see how great its breadth is and how great its length is.

## v3

Then at that point the angel who spoke with me went forth and another angel came forth to meet him.

## v4

And he said to him, Run, speak to this young man, saying, Jerusalem will lie as an open region due to the multitude of men and cattle within it,

## v5

For I will be her [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — wall|wall]] of fire round about, declares Jehovah, and I will be the [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — wall|glory]] within her.

## v6

Ho! Ho! Flee from the land of the north, declares Jehovah, for I have [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — spread|spread]] you out like the four winds of the heavens, declares Jehovah.

## v7

Ho, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon.

## v8

For thus says Jehovah of hosts, [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — After|After]] the glory [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — He|He]] has sent [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — He|Me]] against the nations who plunder you; for he who touches you touches the [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — pupil|pupil]] of His eye.

## v9

For I am now waving My hand over them, and they will be plunder for those who served them; and you will know that Jehovah of hosts has sent Me.

## v10

Give a ringing shout and rejoice, O daughter of Zion, for now [[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — I|I]] am coming, and I will dwell in your midst, declares Jehovah.

## v11

And many nations will join themselves to Jehovah in that day and will become My people; and I will dwell in your midst, and you will know that Jehovah of hosts has sent Me to you.

## v12

And Jehovah will inherit Judah as His portion upon the holy land, and He will again choose Jerusalem.

## v13

[[Zechariah-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Hush|Hush]], all flesh, before Jehovah! For He is roused up from His holy habitation.
