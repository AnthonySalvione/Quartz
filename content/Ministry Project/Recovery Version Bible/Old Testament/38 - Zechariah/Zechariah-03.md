## v1

Then He showed me [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Joshua|Joshua]] the high priest standing before the Angel of Jehovah and [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Satan|Satan]] standing at his right hand to be his adversary.

## v2

And Jehovah said to Satan, Jehovah rebuke you, Satan! Indeed, Jehovah, who has chosen Jerusalem, rebuke you! Is this not a brand plucked from the fire?

## v3

Now Joshua was clothed with [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — filthy|filthy]] garments and was standing before the Angel.

## v4

And He answered and spoke to those standing before Him, saying, [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Remove|Remove]] the filthy garments from him. Then He said to him, See, I now make your iniquity pass from you and clothe you with stately robes.

## v5

And [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — He|He]] said, Let them put a [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — clean|clean]] turban upon his head. And they put the clean turban upon his head and clothed him with garments while the Angel of Jehovah stood by.

## v6

And the Angel of Jehovah testified to Joshua, saying,

## v7

Thus says Jehovah of hosts, If you will walk in My ways and if you will keep My charge, then you will both judge My house and keep My courts, and I will grant you to walk among these standing here.

## v8

Hear then, Joshua the high priest, you and your fellows who sit before you — for they are men of symbol — for I am about to bring forth My [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — servant|servant]], the shoot.

## v9

For here is the [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — stone|stone]] that I have set before Joshua — upon one stone are [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — seven|seven]] eyes. I will [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — engrave|engrave]] its engraving, declares Jehovah of hosts, and I will [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 9.4 — remove|remove]] the iniquity of that land in one day.

## v10

In that day, declares Jehovah of hosts, each of you will invite his neighbor to come under the [[Zechariah-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — vine|vine]] and under the fig tree.
