# Zechariah-07 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Zechariah-07|Zechariah 7]]

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — weep

**Verse:** [[Zechariah-07#v3|Zechariah 7:3]]

According to [[Zechariah-07#v3|vv. 3-6]] and [[Zechariah-08#v19|8:19a]], in their ritualistic religion the people of Israel wept, fasted, and separated themselves in the tenth month to express their grieving for the attacking and besieging of Jerusalem by the Babylonians ([[Jeremiah-52#v4|Jer. 52:4]]), in the fourth month to express their grieving for the breaking down of the city of Jerusalem ([[2 Kings-25#v3|2 Kings 25:3-4]]), in the fifth month to express their grieving for the burning of the temple of God and the city of Jerusalem ([[Jeremiah-52#v12|Jer. 52:12-13]]), and in the seventh month to express their grieving for the murder of Gedaliah ([[2 Kings-25#v22|2 Kings 25:22-26]]). In [[Zechariah-07#v7|vv. 7-14]] and in [[Zechariah-08#v16|8:16-17]], [[Zechariah-08#v19|19b]], Jehovah advised Israel to turn from the vanity of their ritualistic religion to Jehovah and to the reality of a godly life, a life full of righteousness, lovingkindness, compassion, truth, and peace. This would bring in the times of restoration mentioned in [[Zechariah-08#v2|8:2-23]].
