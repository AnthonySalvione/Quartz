# 2 Samuel-08 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[2 Samuel-08|2 Samuel 8]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — control

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v1|2 Samuel 8:1]]

Lit., the bridle of the mother (city). The meaning of the phrase is disputed. [[1 Chronicles-18#v1|First Chronicles 18:1]] reads, Gath and her villages.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — River

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v3|2 Samuel 8:3]]

I.e., the Euphrates; many MSS insert, Euphrates.

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — Syrians

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v5|2 Samuel 8:5]]

Heb. Aram; i.e., the Arameans. So throughout the book.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — Joram’s

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v10|2 Samuel 8:10]]

Lit., his.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — Syrians

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v12|2 Samuel 8:12]]

Several MSS and some versions read, Edom. So also in the next verse.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — was

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v18|2 Samuel 8:18]]

Following some ancient versions and [[2 Samuel-20#v23|20:23]] and [[1 Chronicles-18#v17|1 Chron. 18:17]]; the Hebrew text reads, and.

### Footnote 18.2 — Cherethites

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v18|2 Samuel 8:18]]

I.e., the bodyguard and the runners.

### Footnote 18.3 — chief

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-08#v18|2 Samuel 8:18]]

Lit., priests; but not used in the technical sense of the word. So also in [[2 Samuel-20#v26|20:26]].
