### Footnote 18.1 — threshing

**Verse:** [[2 Samuel-24#v18|2 Samuel 24:18]]

The threshing floor of Araunah (who is called Ornan in [[1 Chronicles-21#v15|1 Chron. 21:15-28]]) became the site where the temple of God was built ([[2 Chronicles-03#v1|2 Chron. 3:1]]).
