# Jeremiah-14 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Jeremiah-14|Jeremiah 14]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — drought

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-14#v1|Jeremiah 14:1]]

Jehovah’s punishing Israel with drought ([[Jeremiah-03#v3|3:3a]]) was a curse for Israel’s breaking of Jehovah’s covenant ([[Deuteronomy-11#v17|Deut. 11:17]]; [[Deuteronomy-28#v23|28:23]]). It was the ceasing of God’s heavenly supply to the earth. Such a thing took place during the time of Elijah ([[1 Kings-17#v1|1 Kings 17:1]]), and it will happen again in the future ([[Revelation-11#v6|Rev. 11:6a]]; [[Zechariah-14#v17|Zech. 14:17-18]]).
