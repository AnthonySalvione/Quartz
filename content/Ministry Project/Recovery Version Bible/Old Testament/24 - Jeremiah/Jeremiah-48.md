## v1

Concerning Moab: / Thus says Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel: /  
Woe to Nebo, for it is destroyed; /  
Kiriathaim is put to shame; it is captured; /  
The lofty retreat is put to shame and dismayed.

## v2

The praise of Moab is no more; /  
In Heshbon they have devised evil against her, saying: /  
Come and let us cut her off from being a nation. /  
You also, O Madmen, will be brought to silence; /  
A sword will go after you.

## v3

The sound of a cry from Horonaim, /  
Desolation and great destruction!

## v4

Moab is destroyed; /  
Her young have caused a cry to be heard.

## v5

For by the ascent of Luhith /  
Continual weeping will go up; /  
For by the descent of Horonaim /  
They have heard the distress of the cry of destruction.

## v6

Flee, save your life, /  
And be like a shrub in the wilderness.

## v7

For because of your trust in your works and in your treasures, /  
You also will be captured; /  
And Chemosh will go forth into exile; /  
His priests and his princes together.

## v8

And a destroyer will come to every city, /  
And no city will escape; /  
The valley will also perish, /  
And the plateau will be destroyed, /  
As Jehovah has said.

## v9

Give wings to Moab, /  
That she may fly and go away; /  
And her cities will become a waste, /  
Without inhabitant in them.

## v10

Cursed is he who does the work of Jehovah negligently, /  
And cursed is he who keeps his sword from blood.

## v11

Moab has been at ease from his youth; /  
And he is settled on his lees /  
And has not been emptied from vessel to vessel; /  
Nor has he gone into exile. /  
Therefore his taste remains in him, /  
And his scent is not changed.

## v12

Therefore indeed, days are coming, declares Jehovah, /  
When I will send pourers to him, /  
And they will pour him out /  
And empty his vessels /  
And shatter his jars.

## v13

And Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, /  
As the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel, their confidence.

## v14

How can you say, /  
We are mighty men /  
And valiant men for war?

## v15

Moab has been destroyed, /  
And its cities have gone up in smoke, /  
And its choice young men have gone down to slaughter, /  
Declares the King, whose name is Jehovah of hosts.

## v16

The calamity of Moab is near to coming, /  
And his affliction hastens quickly.

## v17

Lament for him, all who are around him /  
And all who know his name; /  
Say, How the strong rod is broken, /  
The glorious staff.

## v18

Come down from glory and sit in thirst, /  
O daughter who dwells in Dibon, /  
For the destroyer of Moab has come up against you; /  
He has destroyed your strongholds.

## v19

Stand by the way and watch, /  
O inhabitant of Aroer; /  
Ask him who flees and her who escapes, /  
And say, What has happened?

## v20

Moab is put to shame, for it is shattered. /  
Howl and cry out; /  
Declare by the Arnon /  
That Moab is devastated.

## v21

And judgment has come upon the land of the Plateau, /  
On Holon and on Jahzah and on Mephaath,

## v22

And on Dibon and on Nebo and on Beth-diblathaim,

## v23

And on Kiriathaim and on Beth-gamul and on Beth-meon,

## v24

And on Kerioth and on Bozrah and on all the cities of the land of Moab, /  
Far and near.

## v25

The horn of Moab is cut off, /  
And his arm is broken, declares Jehovah.

## v26

Make him drunk, because he magnified himself against Jehovah; /  
So Moab will wallow in his vomit /  
And will also be in derision.

## v27

For was not Israel a derision to you? /  
Or was he found among thieves? /  
For as often as you speak of him, /  
You shake your head.

## v28

Leave the cities and dwell in the rock, /  
O inhabitants of Moab, /  
And be like a dove who nests /  
On the sides of the mouth of the pit.

## v29

We have heard of the pride of Moab — /  
He is extremely proud — /  
Of his loftiness and his pride and his arrogance /  
And of the haughtiness of his heart.

## v30

I know of his insolence, declares Jehovah; /  
His boastings are all untrue; /  
They have accomplished nothing.

## v31

Therefore I will wail for Moab; /  
I will even cry out for all Moab; /  
There will be mourning for the men of Kir-heres.

## v32

With more than the weeping of Jazer /  
I will weep for you, O vine of Sibmah. /  
Your shoots have crossed over to the sea; /  
They have reached as far as the sea, to Jazer; /  
On your summer fruit and on your vintage /  
The destroyer has fallen.

## v33

Rejoicing and exultation are taken away /  
From the fruited field and from the land of Moab. /  
And I have caused the wine from the wine presses to cease; /  
No one will tread them with the harvest shout; /  
The harvest shout will be no harvest shout.

## v34

From the cry of Heshbon as far as Elealeh, /  
Even as far as Jahaz, they have uttered their voice, /  
From Zoar as far as Horonaim and to Eglath-shelishiyah; /  
For even the waters of Nimrim will become a devastation.

## v35

And I will bring to an end in Moab /  
The one who offers up in high places /  
And burns incense to his gods, declares Jehovah.

## v36

Therefore My heart will moan for Moab like flutes; /  
For the men of Kir-heres /  
My heart will also moan like flutes. /  
As a result, the abundance he has produced has perished.

## v37

For every head is bald, /  
And every beard is shaved off; /  
There are cuts on all the hands, /  
And sackcloth on the loins.

## v38

On all the roofs of Moab and in her open squares /  
There is wailing everywhere, /  
For I have broken Moab like a vessel which no one desires, /  
Declares Jehovah.

## v39

How it is shattered! they howl. /  
How Moab has turned his back in shame! /  
So Moab has become a derision /  
And a terror to all who are around him.

## v40

For thus says Jehovah, /  
One will swoop down like an eagle /  
And spread its wings against Moab.

## v41

Kerioth is captured, /  
And the strongholds are seized, /  
And the heart of the mighty men of Moab in that day /  
Will be like the heart of a woman in labor.

## v42

And Moab will be destroyed from being a people, /  
Because he magnified himself against Jehovah.

## v43

Dread, pit, and snare /  
Are upon you, O inhabitant of Moab, /  
Declares Jehovah.

## v44

He who flees from dread /  
Will fall into the pit, /  
And he who comes up out of the pit /  
Will be taken in the snare; /  
For I will bring upon her, upon Moab, /  
The year of their punishment, declares Jehovah.

## v45

In the shadow of Heshbon /  
Those who flee stand without strength; /  
For fire has gone forth from Heshbon /  
And a flame from the midst of Sihon; /  
And it has consumed the corner of Moab /  
And the top of the head of the sons of tumult.

## v46

Woe to you, O Moab! /  
The people of Chemosh have perished, /  
For your sons have been taken captive, /  
And your daughters have gone into captivity.

## v47

But I will turn again the captivity of Moab /  
In the last days, declares Jehovah. /  
Thus far is the judgment of Moab.
