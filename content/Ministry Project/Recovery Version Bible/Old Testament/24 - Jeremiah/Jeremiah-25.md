## v1

The word that came to Jeremiah  
Concerning all the people of Judah  
In the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah,  
The king of Judah  
(That is, the first year of Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon),

## v2

Which Jeremiah the prophet spoke  
To all the people of Judah  
And to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying,

## v3

From the thirteenth year of Josiah the son of Amon,  
The king of Judah,  
Even unto this day, these twenty-three years,  
The word of Jehovah came to me,  
And I spoke to you, rising up early and speaking,  
But you have not listened.

## v4

And Jehovah sent to you all His servants the prophets,  
Rising up early and sending them,  
But you have not listened  
Or inclined your ear to hear,

## v5

Saying,  
Turn now every one from his evil way  
And from the evil of your deeds,  
And dwell upon the land  
Which Jehovah has given you and your fathers  
From eternity to eternity;

## v6

And do not go after other gods  
To serve them and worship them;  
And do not provoke Me to anger  
With the work of your hands,  
And I will do you no harm.

## v7

Yet you have not listened to Me, declares Jehovah,  
That you might provoke Me to anger  
With the work of your hands  
To your own harm.

## v8

Therefore thus says Jehovah of hosts,  
Because you have not listened to My words,

## v9

I will send for and take all the families of the north,  
Declares Jehovah,  
That is, for Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon,  
My servant,  
And I will bring them against this land  
And against its inhabitants  
And against all these surrounding nations  
And will utterly destroy them  
And make them an astonishment  
And a hissing  
And an eternal reproach.

## v10

And I will cause the voice of gladness  
And the voice of joy to perish from them,  
The voice of the bridegroom  
And the voice of the bride,  
The sound of the millstones  
And the light of the lamp.

## v11

And this whole land will become  
A desolation and a waste,  
And these nations will serve  
The king of Babylon  
Seventy years.

## v12

Then after the seventy years have been fulfilled,  
I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation  
For their iniquity, declares Jehovah,  
As well as the land of the Chaldeans,  
And I will make it an eternal desolation.

## v13

And I will bring upon that land  
All My words which I have spoken against it,  
Everything written in this book,  
Which Jeremiah prophesied  
Against all the nations.

## v14

For many nations and great kings  
Will make them serve them, even them,  
And I will recompense them  
According to their doing  
And according to the work of their hands.

## v15

For thus said Jehovah the God of Israel to me,  
Take this cup of the wine of wrath from My hand  
And make all the nations  
To whom I am sending you  
Drink it.

## v16

And they will drink and stagger  
And become mad  
Because of the sword  
Which I am sending among them.

## v17

And I took the cup from Jehovah’s hand  
And made all the nations  
To whom Jehovah sent me  
Drink it:

## v18

Jerusalem and the cities of Judah  
And her kings, her princes,  
To make them a desolation, a waste,  
A hissing and a curse,  
As it is this day;

## v19

Pharaoh, the king of Egypt,  
And his servants and his princes  
And all his people;

## v20

And all the mingled people,  
And all the kings of the land of Uz,  
And all the kings of the land of the Philistines  
(Even Ashkelon and Gaza and Ekron  
And the remnant of Ashdod);

## v21

Edom and Moab  
And the children of Ammon;

## v22

And all the kings of Tyre  
And all the kings of Sidon  
And the kings of the islands across the sea;

## v23

And Dedan and Tema and Buz  
And all who cut the corners of their hair;

## v24

And all the kings of Arabia  
And all the kings of the mingled people  
Who dwell in the wilderness;

## v25

And all the kings of Zimri  
And all the kings of Elam  
And all the kings of Media;

## v26

And all the kings of the north, near and far,  
One with another,  
And all the kingdoms of the earth  
Which are upon the surface of the ground;  
And the king of Sheshach  
Will drink it after them.

## v27

And you shall say to them,  
Thus says Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel,  
Drink and be drunk, and vomit and fall,  
And rise no longer,  
Because of the sword  
Which I am sending among you.

## v28

And if they refuse to take the cup  
From your hand to drink,  
Then you shall say to them,  
Thus says Jehovah of hosts,  
You shall surely drink it.

## v29

For I am now beginning to bring evil  
On the city which is called by My name,  
And will you indeed go unpunished?  
You will not go unpunished,  
For I am calling for a sword  
Against all the inhabitants of the earth,  
Declares Jehovah of hosts.

## v30

Therefore you shall prophesy against them  
All these words and say to them:  
Jehovah will roar from on high,  
And He will utter His voice  
From His holy habitation;  
He will roar mightily against His habitation;  
He will respond with a shout  
Like those who tread grapes,  
Against all the inhabitants of the earth.

## v31

The tumult comes to the ends of the earth,  
For Jehovah has a dispute with the nations.  
He is entering into judgment with all flesh  
And will give up the wicked to the sword,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v32

Thus says Jehovah of hosts,  
Evil is now going forth  
From nation to nation,  
And a great storm wind is being stirred up  
From the uttermost parts of the earth.

## v33

And those slain by Jehovah on that day  
Will be from one end of the earth  
Unto the other end of the earth;  
They will not be mourned  
Or gathered or buried;  
They will be as dung  
On the surface of the ground.

## v34

Howl, O shepherds, and cry out,  
And roll in ashes, O majestic ones of the flock;  
For your days of slaughter have been fulfilled,  
And I will disperse you,  
So that you fall like a precious vessel.

## v35

And refuge will perish from the shepherds,  
And an escape from the majestic ones of the flock.

## v36

The sound of the cry of the shepherds!  
And the howl of the majestic ones of the flock!  
For Jehovah is destroying their pasture;

## v37

And the habitations of peace are destroyed  
Because of the burning anger of Jehovah.

## v38

Like a young lion He has left His lair,  
For their land has become a waste,  
Because of the oppressing sword  
And because of His burning anger.
