# Jeremiah-25 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Jeremiah-25|Jeremiah 25]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Nebuchadrezzar

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-25#v1|Jeremiah 25:1]]

See [note 2¹](<Jeremiah-21.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Nebuchadrezzar>) in [[Jeremiah-21|Jeremiah 21]]. Shortly after Nebuchadnezzar came into power as king of Babylon, he attacked Judah, carried away some of the vessels in the temple, and captured a number of the people ([[2 Chronicles-36#v6|2 Chron. 36:6-7]]; [[Daniel-01#v1|Dan. 1:1-6]]). In his prophecy, Jeremiah told the people of the evils, the calamities, that were coming upon them, and he advised them to repent and return to God.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — Seventy

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-25#v11|Jeremiah 25:11]]

This word and that in [[Jeremiah-29#v10|29:10-14]] were a comfort to Jeremiah, assuring him that the captivity of Israel would last only seventy years. Some of the captured ones, such as Daniel, would still be alive at the expiration of the seventy years ([[Daniel-01#v21|Dan. 1:21]]; [[Daniel-06#v28|6:28]]). Just as God gave the people up to captivity, He would bring them back, not as captives but as triumphant warriors.
