### Footnote 1.1 — Philistines

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-47#v1|Jeremiah 47:1]]

The Philistines were located alongside the nation of Israel ([[Exodus-15#v14|Exo. 15:14]]). They typify the world that is close to the interest of God on earth and attacks and damages the testimony of God on earth to hinder God’s economy ([[Judges-13#v1|Judg. 13:1]]; [[Judges-14#v4|14:4]]; [[1 Samuel-04#v3|1 Sam. 4:3-11]]; [[1 Samuel-17#v1|17:1]], [[1 Samuel-17#v4|4]], [[1 Samuel-17#v45|45-49]]).
