## v1

Hear the word which Jehovah speaks to you,  
O house of Israel.

## v2

Thus says Jehovah,  
Do not learn the way of the nations,  
And do not be terrified by the signs of the heavens,  
Although the nations are terrified by them.

## v3

For the customs of the peoples are vanity,  
Because their god is a tree cut from the forest,  
The work of the hands of a craftsman with an axe.

## v4

He adorns it with silver and with gold;  
They fasten it with nails and hammers  
So that it cannot totter.

## v5

They are like a post in a cucumber field,  
And they cannot speak;  
They must be carried  
Because they cannot walk.  
Do not be afraid of them,  
For they cannot do evil,  
And neither is it in them to do good.

## v6

There is none like You, O Jehovah;  
You are great,  
And Your name is great in might.

## v7

Who would not fear You,  
O King of the nations?  
For it befits You,  
For among all the wise of the nations  
And in all their kingdoms  
There is none like You.

## v8

But they are altogether stupid and foolish;  
The instruction of their idols is mere wood.

## v9

Beaten silver  
Is brought from Tarshish,  
And gold from Uphaz,  
The work of a craftsman  
And of the hands of a goldsmith.  
Their clothing is blue and purple;  
All of them are the work of skillful men.

## v10

But Jehovah is the true God;  
He is the living God and the eternal King.  
At His wrath the earth quakes,  
And the nations cannot endure His indignation.

## v11

Thus you shall say to them,  
The gods that did not make the heavens and the earth,  
These will perish from the earth  
And from under the heavens.

## v12

It is He who made the earth by His power,  
Who established the world by His wisdom,  
And by His understanding  
He stretched out the heavens.

## v13

When He utters His voice,  
There is a tumult of waters in the heavens,  
And He causes the vapors to rise  
From the ends of the earth;  
He makes lightning for the rain  
And brings forth wind from His treasuries.

## v14

Every man is stupid, without knowledge;  
Every goldsmith is put to shame by his idol;  
For his molten image is falsehood,  
And there is no breath in them.

## v15

They are vanity, the work of delusion;  
At the time of their punishment  
They will perish.

## v16

The Portion of Jacob is not like these;  
For He is the One who formed everything,  
And Israel is the tribe of His inheritance;  
Jehovah of hosts is His name.

## v17

Gather up your bundle from the ground,  
O inhabitant under siege.

## v18

For thus says Jehovah,  
I will sling out  
The inhabitants of the land  
At this time,  
And I will bring distress on them,  
That they may find it.

## v19

Woe is me because of my hurt!  
My wound is grievous.  
But I said,  
Truly this is my affliction,  
And I must bear it.

## v20

My tent is destroyed,  
And all my cords are broken;  
My children have gone from me and they are not;  
There is no one who will spread out my tent again  
And set up my curtains.

## v21

For the shepherds are stupid  
And have not sought Jehovah;  
Therefore they have not prospered,  
And all their flock is scattered.

## v22

The sound of a report! Here it comes,  
Even a great commotion from the land of the north,  
To make the cities of Judah  
A desolation, a habitation of jackals.

## v23

I know, O Jehovah,  
That a man’s way is not in himself;  
It is not in the man who walks  
To direct his step.

## v24

Correct me, O Jehovah, but in measure,  
Not in Your anger  
Lest You bring me to nothing.

## v25

Pour out Your wrath on the nations  
Who do not know You  
And upon the families  
Who do not call upon Your name;  
For they have devoured Jacob, yes, devoured him  
And brought him to an end,  
And they have desolated his habitation.
