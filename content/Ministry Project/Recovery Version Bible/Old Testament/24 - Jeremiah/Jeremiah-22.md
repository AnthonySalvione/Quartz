## v1

Thus says Jehovah,  
Go down to the house of the king of Judah,  
And speak this word there,

## v2

And say,  
Hear the word of Jehovah,  
O king of Judah, who sits on the throne of David,  
You and your servants and your people,  
Who enter through these gates:

## v3

Thus says Jehovah,  
Execute judgment and righteousness,  
And deliver him who has been robbed  
From the hand of the oppressor,  
And do no wrong, do no violence,  
To the sojourner, the orphan, or the widow;  
Nor shed innocent blood in this place.

## v4

For if you indeed do this thing,  
Then kings sitting in David’s place on his throne,  
Riding in chariots and on horses —  
The king and his servants and his people —  
Will come through the gates of this house;

## v5

But if you do not listen to these things,  
I swear by Myself, declares Jehovah,  
That this house will become a ruin.

## v6

For thus says Jehovah  
Concerning the house of the king of Judah:  
You are Gilead to Me,  
The summit of Lebanon;  
Yet I will surely make you a wilderness,  
Cities that are not inhabited.

## v7

And I will set apart destroyers against you,  
Each with his weapons;  
And they will cut down your choice cedars  
And cast them into the fire.

## v8

And many nations will pass by this city,  
And they will say each one to his neighbor,  
Why has Jehovah done this to this great city?

## v9

And they will say,  
It is because they forsook the covenant  
Of Jehovah their God  
And bowed down to other gods  
And served them.

## v10

Do not weep for the one who died,  
Nor lament for him;  
But weep bitterly for the one who goes away,  
For he will no longer return and see  
The land of his birth.

## v11

For thus says Jehovah  
Concerning Shallum the son of Josiah,  
The king of Judah,  
Who became king in the place of Josiah his father,  
Who went away from this place:  
He will not return there anymore;

## v12

But in the place where they have exiled him  
He will die  
And never see this land again.

## v13

Woe to him who builds his house by unrighteousness  
And his upper rooms by injustice,  
Who uses his neighbor’s service without wages  
And does not give him recompense for his work,

## v14

Who says,  
I will build myself a large house  
With spacious upper chambers,  
And cuts out for it windows;  
And it is paneled with cedar  
And painted with vermilion.

## v15

Are you a king because you  
Excel in cedar?  
Did not your father eat and drink,  
And execute judgment and righteousness?  
Thus it was well with him.

## v16

He judged the cause of the poor and needy;  
Thus it was well.  
Is not this to know Me?  
Declares Jehovah.

## v17

But you have eyes and a heart  
Only for your unjust gain  
And for shedding innocent blood  
And for oppression and doing violence.

## v18

Therefore thus says Jehovah  
Concerning Jehoiakim the son of Josiah,  
The king of Judah:  
They will not wail for him, saying,  
Alas, my brother! or, Alas, sister!  
They will not wail for him, saying,  
Alas, lord! or, Alas, his majesty!

## v19

He will be buried with the burial of a donkey —  
Dragged and cast out  
Beyond the gates of Jerusalem.

## v20

Go up to Lebanon and cry out;  
And lift up your voice in Bashan,  
And cry out from Abarim,  
For all your lovers have been shattered.

## v21

I spoke to you in your prosperity;  
But you said, I will not listen.  
This has been your way from your youth,  
That you have not listened to My voice.

## v22

The wind will shepherd all your shepherds,  
And your lovers will go into captivity;  
Then you will be ashamed and confounded  
Because of all your wickedness.

## v23

O inhabitant of Lebanon,  
Nested among the cedars,  
How you will be pitied when pains come to you,  
Anguish like that of a woman giving birth.

## v24

As I live, declares Jehovah,  
Even though Coniah the son of Jehoiakim,  
The king of Judah,  
Were a signet ring on My right hand,  
Yet I would tear you off,

## v25

And I would give you into the hand  
Of those who seek your life  
And into the hand of those whom you fear,  
Even into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon,  
And into the hand of the Chaldeans.

## v26

I will cast you and your mother who bore you  
Far away into another land  
Where you were not born,  
And there you will die.

## v27

But to the land to which they desire to return,  
They will not return.

## v28

Is this man Coniah a despised, shattered container?  
Or is he a vessel no one delights in?  
Why are he and his seed thrown away  
And cast into a land which they do not know?

## v29

O land, land, land,  
Hear the word of Jehovah:

## v30

Thus says Jehovah,  
Write down this man as childless,  
A man who will not prosper in his days;  
For none of his seed will prosper  
By sitting on the throne of David  
Or by ruling again in Judah.
