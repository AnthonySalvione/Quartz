# Jeremiah-24 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Jeremiah-24|Jeremiah 24]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — figs

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-24#v1|Jeremiah 24:1]]

The fig tree is a symbol of the nation of Israel ([[Joel-01#v7|Joel 1:7]]). See [[Matthew-21#v18|Matt. 21:18-22]] and notes.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — those

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-24#v8|Jeremiah 24:8]]

Those dwelling in Egypt were those who had escaped from the Holy Land to Egypt for their security.

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — proverb

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-24#v9|Jeremiah 24:9]]

Here proverb signifies a formal expression of derision, and byword indicates mocking.
