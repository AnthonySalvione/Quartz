## v1

Woe to the shepherds  
Who destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v2

Therefore thus says Jehovah the God of Israel  
Concerning the shepherds who shepherd My people,  
You have scattered My flock and driven them away  
And have not visited them;  
I will visit upon you  
The evil of your deeds,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v3

Then I will gather the remnant of My flock  
Out of all the lands where I have driven them,  
And I will bring them back to their pasture;  
And they will be fruitful and multiply.

## v4

And I will raise up shepherds over them  
Who will shepherd them,  
And they will no longer fear or be dismayed,  
Nor will any be missing,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v5

Indeed, days are coming,  
Declares Jehovah,  
When I will raise up to David a righteous Shoot;  
And He will reign as King and act prudently  
And will execute justice and righteousness in the land.

## v6

In His days Judah will be saved,  
And Israel will dwell securely;  
And this is His name by which He will be called:  
Jehovah our righteousness.

## v7

Therefore indeed, days are coming, declares Jehovah,  
When they will no longer say,  
As Jehovah lives,  
Who brought up the children of Israel  
Out of the land of Egypt;

## v8

But, As Jehovah lives,  
Who brought up and led back  
The seed of the house of Israel  
Out of the land of the north  
And out of all the lands where I had driven them,  
That they might dwell in their own land.

## v9

Concerning the prophets:  
My heart is broken within me;  
All my bones fail.  
I have become like a man who is drunk,  
Even like a mighty man who is overcome by wine,  
Because of Jehovah  
And because of the words of His holiness.

## v10

For the land is full of adulterers;  
For the land mourns because of the curse.  
The pastures of the wilderness are dried up,  
And the course they run is evil,  
And their might is not right.

## v11

For both the prophet and the priest are profane;  
Even in My house I have found their wickedness,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v12

Therefore their way will become like slippery paths for them;  
In the darkness they will be driven and will fall in it;  
For I will bring evil on them  
In the year of their punishment,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v13

And in the prophets of Samaria  
I have seen an offensive thing:  
They prophesied by Baal  
And led My people Israel astray.

## v14

And yet in the prophets of Jerusalem  
I have seen a horrible thing:  
The committing of adultery and walking in falsehood;  
And they strengthen the hands of evildoers,  
So that none turn  
From their wickedness.  
All of them have become to Me like Sodom  
And her inhabitants like Gomorrah.

## v15

Therefore thus says Jehovah of hosts concerning the prophets:  
I will feed them with wormwood  
And give them poisonous water to drink,  
For profaneness has gone forth  
From the prophets of Jerusalem into all the land.

## v16

Thus says Jehovah of hosts,  
Do not listen to the words of the prophets  
Who are prophesying to you;  
They are leading you to vanity;  
They speak a vision of their own heart,  
Not out of the mouth of Jehovah.

## v17

They say continually to those who despise Me,  
Jehovah has spoken, You will have peace;  
And to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his own heart,  
They say,  
Evil will not come upon you.

## v18

For who has stood in the council of Jehovah,  
That he should perceive and hear His word?  
Who has given heed to His word and listened?

## v19

Look, the storm wind of Jehovah!  
Wrath has gone forth,  
Even a whirling tempest;  
It will whirl down on the head of the wicked.

## v20

The anger of Jehovah will not turn back  
Until He has executed  
And until He has accomplished  
The purposes of His heart;  
In the last days  
You will understand it clearly.

## v21

I did not send the prophets,  
But they ran forth;  
I did not speak to them,  
Yet they prophesied.

## v22

But if they had stood in My council  
And had caused My people to hear My words,  
They would have turned them from their evil way  
And from the evil of their deeds.

## v23

Am I a God who is near, declares Jehovah,  
And not a God who is far off?

## v24

Can a man hide himself in secret places,  
So that I will not see him? declares Jehovah.  
Do I not fill the heavens and the earth?  
Declares Jehovah.

## v25

I have heard what the prophets say,  
Who prophesy falsehood in My name, saying,  
I have dreamed; I have dreamed.

## v26

How long will this be  
In the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehood  
And who are prophets of the deceit of their own heart,

## v27

Who think to cause My people  
To forget My name with their dreams which they tell,  
Each one to his neighbor,  
Even as their fathers forgot My name because of Baal?

## v28

The prophet who has a dream,  
Let him tell the dream;  
And he with whom is My word,  
Let him speak My word faithfully.  
What is the straw to the grain?  
Declares Jehovah.

## v29

Is not My word thus — like fire, declares Jehovah,  
And like a hammer that breaks the rock in pieces?

## v30

Therefore indeed, I am against the prophets,  
Declares Jehovah,  
Who steal My words,  
Each one from his neighbor.

## v31

Indeed, I am against the prophets,  
Declares Jehovah,  
Who use their tongues and declare,  
He has declared.

## v32

Indeed, I am against those who prophesy false dreams,  
Declares Jehovah,  
And who tell them and lead My people astray  
By their lies and by their recklessness,  
Although I did not send them or command them;  
Neither do they profit this people at all,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v33

And when this people or the prophet or priest asks you, saying,  
What is the burden of Jehovah?  
Then you shall say to them,  
What burden?  
I will even cast you off,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v34

And as for the prophet and the priest and the people  
Who say, The burden of Jehovah,  
I will punish that man and his household.

## v35

Thus you shall say every one to his neighbor  
And every one to his brother,  
What has Jehovah answered?  
Or, What has Jehovah spoken?

## v36

And you shall mention no more the burden of Jehovah,  
For every man’s word will be his own burden;  
For you have perverted the words of the living God,  
Jehovah of hosts, our God.

## v37

Thus you shall say to the prophet,  
What has Jehovah answered you?  
Or, What has Jehovah spoken?

## v38

But if you say, The burden of Jehovah,  
Therefore thus says Jehovah,  
Because you have said this word,  
The burden of Jehovah,  
And I have sent to you, saying,  
You shall not say, The burden of Jehovah,

## v39

Therefore, I will utterly forget you  
And cast away from My presence  
You and the city  
Which I gave to you and to your fathers.

## v40

And I will bring upon you  
Eternal reproach and eternal humiliation,  
Which will not be forgotten.
