## Footnotes for verse 23

### Footnote 23.1 — Cushite

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-13#v23|Jeremiah 13:23]]

I.e., Ethiopian. Having forsaken God as the source, the fountain of living waters ([[Jeremiah-02#v13|2:13]]), Israel became evil, having a heart that was deceitful above all things and incurable ([[Jeremiah-17#v9|17:9]]) and having an unchangeable sinful nature, like the Cushite’s skin and the leopard’s spots, which cannot be changed. This exposes the true condition of fallen man.
