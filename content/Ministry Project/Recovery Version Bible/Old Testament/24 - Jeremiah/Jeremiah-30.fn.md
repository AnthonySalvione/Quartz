### Footnote 9.1 — David

**Verse:** [[Jeremiah-30#v9|Jeremiah 30:9]]

Referring to Christ, who is the real David (see [note 16¹](<2 Samuel-07.fn#Footnote 16.1 — house>) in [[2 Samuel-07|2 Sam. 7]]) and who will be the King in the restoration, i.e., the millennium ([[Isaiah-32#v1|Isa. 32:1]]; [[Revelation-20#v4|Rev. 20:4]], [[Revelation-20#v6|6]]). David was a type of Christ as the King.
