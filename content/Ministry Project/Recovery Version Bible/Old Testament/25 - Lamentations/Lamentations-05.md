## v1

Remember, O Jehovah, what has come upon us; /  
Look and see our reproach.

## v2

Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, /  
Our houses to foreigners.

## v3

We are orphans without father; /  
Our mothers are like widows.

## v4

We have drunk our water for money; /  
Our wood comes to us at a price.

## v5

On our necks are our pursuers; /  
We are weary; we have no rest.

## v6

We have given our hand to Egypt /  
And to Assyria, so as to be satisfied with bread.

## v7

Our fathers sinned and they are not, /  
And we ourselves bear their iniquities.

## v8

Slaves rule over us; /  
There is no one to deliver us from their hand.

## v9

We get our bread at the risk of our lives /  
Because of the sword from the wilderness.

## v10

Our skin is black like an oven /  
Because of the burning heat of famine.

## v11

They have ravished the women in Zion, /  
The virgins in the cities of Judah.

## v12

Princes were hanged by them. /  
The faces of the elders were not honored.

## v13

The young men bore the mill, /  
And the young boys stumbled under the wood.

## v14

The old men have ceased from the gate, /  
The young men from their music.

## v15

The joy of our heart has ceased; /  
Our dance has been turned into mourning.

## v16

The crown has fallen from our head. /  
Woe to us! For we have sinned.

## v17

Because of this our heart is faint; /  
Because of these things our eyes are dim;

## v18

Because of Mount Zion, which is desolate — /  
Foxes rove in it.

## v19

You, O Jehovah, abide forever; /  
Your throne is from generation to generation.

## v20

Why do You forget us forever /  
And forsake us for so long a time?

## v21

Turn us back to You, O Jehovah, and we will be turned; /  
Renew our days as before.

## v22

Or have You utterly rejected us? /  
Are You exceedingly angry with us?
