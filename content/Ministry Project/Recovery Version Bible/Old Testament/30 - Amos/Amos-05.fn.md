## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — feasts

**Verse:** [[Amos-05#v21|Amos 5:21]]

On the one hand, the people of Israel were evil; on the other hand, they still worshipped God with feasts, solemn assemblies, offerings, and singing ([[Amos-05#v21|vv. 21-23]]).

### Footnote 21.2 — delight

**Verse:** [[Amos-05#v21|Amos 5:21]]

Lit., smell (sweetness).

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — justice

**Verse:** [[Amos-05#v24|Amos 5:24]]

Instead of Israel’s hypocritical worship, God wanted Israel to exercise justice and practice righteousness.

## Footnotes for verse 26

### Footnote 26.1 — Sikkuth

**Verse:** [[Amos-05#v26|Amos 5:26]]

Understood by the Septuagint translators to read, tabernacle of Moloch and the star of your god Raiphan (i.e., Rompha), their images (cf. [[Acts-07#v43|Acts 7:43]]).
