## v1

Hear this word that I take up as a lamentation over you, O house of Israel.

## v2

Fallen and no more to rise /  
Is the virgin of Israel; /  
She has been cast down upon her land; /  
There is none to raise her up.

## v3

For thus says the Lord Jehovah,  
The city that sent forth a thousand /  
Will have a hundred left, /  
And that which sent forth a hundred /  
Will have ten left, /  
To the house of Israel.

## v4

For thus says Jehovah to the house of Israel,  
Seek Me, and live;

## v5

And do not seek Bethel, /  
Nor go to Gilgal, /  
Nor pass on to Beer-sheba; /  
For Gilgal will surely go into captivity, /  
And Bethel will become vanity.

## v6

Seek Jehovah, and live; /  
Lest He rush upon the house of Joseph like fire, /  
And it devour it, and there be none to extinguish the fire in Bethel.

## v7

They turn justice to wormwood /  
And thrust righteousness down to the ground.

## v8

He who makes the Pleiades and Orion /  
And turns the shadow of death into the morning /  
And darkens the day with the night; /  
He who calls for the water of the sea /  
And pours it upon the surface of the earth, /  
Jehovah is His name,

## v9

Who causes destruction to flash forth upon the strong, /  
So that destruction comes upon the fortress.

## v10

They hate him who reproves in the gate, /  
And him who speaks with integrity they abhor.

## v11

Therefore because you trample upon the poor /  
And take a forced gift of wheat from them; /  
You have built houses of hewn stone, /  
But you will not dwell in them; /  
You have planted desirable vineyards, /  
But you will not drink their wine.

## v12

For I know that your transgressions are many /  
And that your sins are mighty, /  
You who vex the righteous and take ransom payment /  
And turn aside justice for the needy in the gate.

## v13

Therefore he who has insight will keep silent at this time, for it is an evil time.

## v14

Seek good and not evil, /  
In order that you may live; /  
And so Jehovah the God of hosts will be with you, /  
As you say.

## v15

Hate evil and love good, /  
And set up justice in the gate. /  
Perhaps Jehovah the God of hosts will be gracious /  
To the remnant of Joseph.

## v16

Therefore thus says Jehovah, /  
The God of hosts, the Lord: /  
In all the open plazas there will be wailing, /  
And in all the streets they will say, Alas! Alas! /  
And they will call the farmer to mourning, /  
And for wailing to those skilled in lamentation.

## v17

In all the vineyards there will be wailing, /  
For I will pass through your midst, says Jehovah.

## v18

Woe to you who desire /  
The day of Jehovah! /  
What good is the day of Jehovah to you? /  
It will be darkness and not light,

## v19

As when a man flees from before a lion /  
And encounters a bear, /  
Or goes into the house and leans his hand against the wall, /  
And a serpent bites him.

## v20

Will not the day of Jehovah be darkness and not light, /  
Even deep darkness and no brightness to it?

## v21

I hate, I despise, your feasts, /  
And I will not delight in your solemn assemblies.

## v22

For if you offer up to Me burnt offerings /  
And your meal offerings, I will not accept them; /  
Nor will I regard the peace offerings of your fatted animals.

## v23

Take the noise of your songs away from Me, /  
For I will not hear the melody of your harps.

## v24

But let justice roll down like water, /  
And righteousness, like an ever-flowing river.

## v25

Did you bring Me sacrifices and meal offerings /  
In the wilderness for forty years, O house of Israel?

## v26

Indeed, you took up Sikkuth your king /  
And Kiyyun your images, the star of your gods that you made for yourselves.

## v27

Thus I will bring you into captivity /  
Beyond Damascus, says Jehovah; /  
The God of hosts is His name.
