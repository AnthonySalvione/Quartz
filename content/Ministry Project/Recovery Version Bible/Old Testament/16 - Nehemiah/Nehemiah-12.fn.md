# Nehemiah-12 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Nehemiah-12|Nehemiah 12]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — priests

**Verse:** [[Nehemiah-12#v1|Nehemiah 12:1]]

The main serving ones in the worship of God, which is the main thing in God’s kingdom, were not the kings but the priests with the Levites, who were the servants of the priests ([[Numbers-18#v2|Num. 18:2]], [[Numbers-18#v6|6]]).

## Footnotes for verse 44

### Footnote 44.1 — portions

**Verse:** [[Nehemiah-12#v44|Nehemiah 12:44]]

Nehemiah not only brought the serving Levites and priests into function; he also supplied their daily necessities ([[Nehemiah-12#v44|vv. 44-47]]). Before that time, no one had taken care of this matter properly. See notes on [[Numbers-18#v8|Num. 18:8-32]].
