# Joshua-09 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Joshua-09|Joshua 9]]

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — but

**Verse:** [[Joshua-09#v14|Joshua 9:14]]

The children of Israel were deceived by the Gibeonites because they were like a wife who forgot her husband. What they did here was exactly the same as what Eve did in [[Genesis-03|Gen. 3]] (see note 21 there). As God’s wife ([[Isaiah-54#v5|Isa. 54:5]]; [[Isaiah-62#v5|62:5]]; [[John-03#v29|John 3:29]]; [[2 Corinthians-11#v2|2 Cor. 11:2]]; [[Ephesians-05#v23|Eph. 5:23-32]]; [[Revelation-21#v9|Rev. 21:9-10]]), God’s people should co-live with Him, always relying on Him and being one with Him. This is the intrinsic significance of this chapter. See notes 31 and 41 in [[Joshua-07|Joshua 7]].
