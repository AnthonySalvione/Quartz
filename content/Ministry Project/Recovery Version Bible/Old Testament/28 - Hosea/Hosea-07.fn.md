# Hosea-07 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Hosea-07|Hosea 7]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — iniquity

**Verse:** [[Hosea-07#v1|Hosea 7:1]]

The sins of Israel were in two categories: sins in their social life among the people and the rulers ([[Hosea-07#v1|vv. 1-7]]) and evils in their relationship with Jehovah ([[Hosea-07#v8|vv. 8-16]]). Israel was wrong with man and also with God.

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — the

**Verse:** [[Hosea-07#v6|Hosea 7:6]]

Lit., it.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — judges

**Verse:** [[Hosea-07#v7|Hosea 7:7]]

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — Not

**Verse:** [[Hosea-07#v8|Hosea 7:8]]

This symbolizes that Israel was stubborn, remaining in a “charcoaled” condition and being unwilling to turn to Jehovah.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — understanding

**Verse:** [[Hosea-07#v11|Hosea 7:11]]

Israel’s being likened to a silly dove symbolizes that they were lacking the proper knowledge of God in His economy.

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — deceitful

**Verse:** [[Hosea-07#v16|Hosea 7:16]]

Israel’s being likened to a deceitful bow symbolizes that they were rebellious toward God and were not faithful to fight the battle for God’s kingdom.
