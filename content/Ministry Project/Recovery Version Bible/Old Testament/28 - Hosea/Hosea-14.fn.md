## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Hosea-14#v4|Hosea 14:4]]

[[Hosea-14#v4|Verses 4-8]] portray Israel in the restoration ([[Matthew-19#v28|Matt. 19:28]]), as revealed in [[Hosea-02#v15|2:15-23]]; [[Hosea-03#v5|3:5]]; [[Hosea-06#v1|6:1-3]]; [[Hosea-10#v12|10:12]]. Israel’s transformation as described in [[Hosea-14#v4|vv. 4-7]] is based on the factor of love in life (see [note 1¹](<Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — loved>) in [[Hosea-11|Hosea 11]]). Love in affection does not transform, but love in life transforms people through the growth in life. Our relationship with God is of the divine, eternal life of God ([[1 John-05#v11|1 John 5:11]]). This life enlivens us, regenerates us, sanctifies us positionally and dispositionally, renews us, transforms us, conforms us, matures us, and glorifies us, making us the same as God in life, nature, appearance, and glory. At the beginning of this book Israel was a harlot, but at the end of this book, Israel has become a son ([[Hosea-11#v1|11:1]]). Thus, the outcome of this book is transformation in life by God’s love (cf. [[Romans-08#v28|Rom. 8:28-39]]; [[Hebrews-12#v5|Heb. 12:5-10]]).

### Footnote 8.2 — From

**Verse:** [[Hosea-14#v8|Hosea 14:8]]

Ephraim’s bearing fruit from Jehovah indicates Israel’s oneness with Jehovah. This is more than an organic union; it is the oneness of two in one life, in one nature, and in one living.
