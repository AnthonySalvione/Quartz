## v1

A trumpet to your lips!  
Like an eagle he comes against the house of Jehovah,  
Because they have trespassed My covenant  
And have transgressed against My law.

## v2

They will cry unto Me,  
My God, we — Israel — know You.

## v3

Israel has cast off what is good;  
The enemy will pursue him.

## v4

They have set up kings, but not by Me;  
They have set up princes, but I did not acknowledge it.  
With their silver and their gold they made  
Idols for themselves,  
That they might be cut off.

## v5

Your calf has cast you off, O Samaria;  
My anger burns against them.  
How long will they be incapable of innocence?

## v6

For from Israel is this!  
A workman made it;  
Thus it is no God.  
The calf of Samaria  
Will be shattered to pieces.

## v7

For they have sown wind,  
And they will reap a whirlwind.  
It has no stalk; should there be a sprout,  
It will not produce flour;  
If it should produce,  
Strangers will swallow it up.

## v8

Israel has been swallowed up;  
Now they have become among the nations  
Like a vessel in which no one takes pleasure.

## v9

For they have gone up to Assyria  
Like a wild ass gone alone by itself;  
Ephraim has hired lovers.

## v10

Indeed though they have hired such among the nations,  
I will now gather them;  
And they will begin to be diminished because of the burden  
Of the king of the princes.

## v11

Because Ephraim has multiplied altars for sin,  
Altars will be to him for sin.

## v12

I wrote for him the ten thousand things of My law,  
But they are considered as a strange thing.

## v13

As for the sacrifices of My offerings,  
They sacrifice flesh and eat it,  
But Jehovah has no delight in them.  
Now He will remember their iniquity,  
And He will punish their sins;  
They will return to Egypt.

## v14

For Israel has forgotten his Maker  
And has built palaces,  
And Judah has multiplied fortified cities;  
But I will send fire into his cities,  
And it will devour its citadels.
