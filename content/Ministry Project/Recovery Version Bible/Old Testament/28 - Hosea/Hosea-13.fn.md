### Footnote 11.1 — king

**Verse:** [[Hosea-13#v11|Hosea 13:11]]

The king given by Jehovah in His anger might have been Saul, who was eventually taken away by Jehovah in His overflowing wrath ([[1 Samuel-08#v4|1 Sam. 8:4-7]]; [[1 Samuel-09#v17|9:17]]; [[1 Samuel-10#v1|10:1]]; [[1 Samuel-31#v1|31:1-13]]).

### Footnote 14.2 — Repentance

**Verse:** [[Hosea-13#v14|Hosea 13:14]]

God’s love for Israel is everlasting (see [note 1¹](<Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — loved>) in [[Hosea-11|Hosea 11]]; [[Jeremiah-31#v3|Jer. 31:3]]), and He will never repent of this love (cf. [[Romans-11#v28|Rom. 11:28-29]]).
