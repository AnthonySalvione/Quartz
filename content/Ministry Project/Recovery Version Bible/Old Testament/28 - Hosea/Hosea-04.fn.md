## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — faithfulness

**Verse:** [[Hosea-04#v1|Hosea 4:1]]

Or, truth. As the unchaste wife of Jehovah, Israel became evil. Once a wife becomes unchaste, all kinds of evils follow. Once we forsake God, we too can do any kind of evil.

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — Gilgal

**Verse:** [[Hosea-04#v15|Hosea 4:15]]

Gilgal was the center of idolatry ([[Hosea-09#v15|9:15]]; [[Hosea-12#v11|12:11]]; [[Amos-04#v4|Amos 4:4]]; [[Amos-05#v5|5:5]]).

### Footnote 15.2 — Beth-aven

**Verse:** [[Hosea-04#v15|Hosea 4:15]]

Meaning house of nothingness, or house of idolatry. It was probably applied contemptuously to Bethel, which had become a seat of idolatry ([[1 Kings-12#v26|1 Kings 12:26-33]]; cf. [[Hosea-05#v8|Hosea 5:8]]; [[Hosea-10#v5|10:5]]).
