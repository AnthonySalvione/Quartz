# Hosea-09 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Hosea-09|Hosea 9]]

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — Moph

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v6|Hosea 9:6]]

Probably Memphis, an Egyptian city near the Nile; elsewhere called Noph.

### Footnote 6.2 — things

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v6|Hosea 9:6]]

Idols made of silver.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — inspired

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v7|Hosea 9:7]]

Lit., man of the spirit. Israel’s considering a prophet as a fool and considering a man of the spirit, an inspired one, as mad indicates that they did not care for God’s interests. On the contrary, they thought that caring for God’s interests was foolishness.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — watchman

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v8|Hosea 9:8]]

This indicates that in the past the northern kingdom of Israel was quite good.

### Footnote 8.2 — prophet

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v8|Hosea 9:8]]

This may mean that the prophets had become so evil that wherever they went they were a trap to snare the people. In the temple the prophets created enmity; they did not have love or sympathy.

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — Gibeah

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v9|Hosea 9:9]]

The corruption of Israel was so great that it matched the corruption in the days of Gibeah as described in [[Judges-19#v15|Judges 19:15-30]].

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — found

**Verse:** [[Hosea-09#v10|Hosea 9:10]]

This refers to the time of the exodus from Egypt, when God considered Israel a young wife ([[Jeremiah-02#v2|Jer. 2:2]]). However, when this wife grew up, she left God for Baal and went to Baal-peor (a city named after the idol Baal of Peor—[[Numbers-25#v3|Num. 25:3]]; [[Psalm-106#v28|Psa. 106:28]]) and sanctified herself unto the shameful thing (idol) and became an abomination, like the thing (idol) that she loved.
