## v1

When Israel was a child, I [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — loved|loved]] him,  
And out of Egypt I called My [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 1.2 — son|son]].

## v2

As [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 2.1 — they|they]] called them,  
So they went from them;  
To the Baals they sacrificed,  
And to the idols they burned incense.

## v3

And it was I who taught Ephraim to walk  
(He took them in His arms),  
But they did not know that I healed them.

## v4

I drew them [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — with|with]] cords of a man,  
With bands of love;  
And I was to them like those  
Who lift off the [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 4.2 — yoke|yoke]] on their jaws;  
And I gently caused them to [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 4.2 — yoke|eat]].

## v5

They will not return to the land of Egypt,  
But the Assyrian will be their king,  
For they refused to return to Me.

## v6

And the sword will whirl about in their cities  
And will consume their bars,  
And it will devour them because of their own counsels.

## v7

Indeed My people are bent upon turning away from Me;  
Though they call them to Him who is on high,  
None at all exalts Him.

## v8

How shall I give you up, O Ephraim?  
How shall I deliver you up, O Israel?  
How can I make you like [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Admah|Admah]]?  
How can I treat you like [[Hosea-11.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Admah|Zeboim]]?  
My heart is turned within Me;  
All My compassions have warmed.

## v9

I will not execute the fierceness of My anger;  
I will not return to destroy Ephraim;  
For I am God and not man,  
The Holy One in the midst of you,  
And I will not come in wrath.

## v10

They will walk after Jehovah;  
He will roar like a lion.  
For He will roar,  
And the children will come trembling from the west.

## v11

They will come trembling like a bird from Egypt  
And like a dove from the land of Assyria.  
And I will cause them to dwell in their houses,  
Declares Jehovah.

## v12

Ephraim encompasses Me with lies,  
And the house of Israel, with deceit;  
And Judah is yet unsteadfast with God  
And with the Holy One, the Faithful One.
