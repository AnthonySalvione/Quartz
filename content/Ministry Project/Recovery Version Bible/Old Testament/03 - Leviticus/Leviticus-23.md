## v1

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v2

Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, As to the appointed [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 2.1 — feasts|feasts]] of Jehovah, which you shall proclaim as holy [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 2.2 — convocations|convocations]], these are My appointed feasts.

## v3

Six days work shall be done, but on the seventh day there is a [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Sabbath|Sabbath]] of complete rest, a holy convocation. You shall do no work; it is a Sabbath to Jehovah in all your dwelling places.

## v4

These are the appointed [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 4.1 — feasts|feasts]] of Jehovah, even the holy convocations, which you shall proclaim at their appointed time:

## v5

In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 5.1 — at|at]] twilight, is Jehovah’s [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Passover|Passover]],

## v6

And on the fifteenth day of this month is the Feast of Unleavened Bread to Jehovah; seven days you shall eat unleavened bread.

## v7

On the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 7.1 — first|first]] day you shall have a holy convocation; you shall do no work of labor.

## v8

But you shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 8.1 — seven|seven]] days. On the seventh day there shall be a holy convocation; you shall do no work of labor.

## v9

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v10

Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them, When you come into the land which I am giving you, and reap its harvest, then you shall bring the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 10.1 — sheaf|sheaf]] of the firstfruits of your harvest to the priest;

## v11

And he shall wave the sheaf before Jehovah for your [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 11.1 — acceptance|acceptance]]; on the day after the Sabbath the priest shall wave it.

## v12

And on the day when you wave the sheaf, you shall offer a male lamb a year old without blemish as a [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 12.1 — burnt|burnt]] offering to Jehovah.

## v13

And its [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 13.1 — meal|meal]] offering shall be two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mingled with oil, an offering by fire to Jehovah for a satisfying fragrance. And its [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 13.2 — drink|drink]] offering shall be of wine, a fourth of a hin.

## v14

And you shall eat [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 14.1 — no|no]] bread or parched grain or fresh ears until that same day, until you have brought the offering of your God. It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.

## v15

And you shall count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath; from the day that you brought the sheaf of the wave offering there shall be seven complete [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Sabbaths|Sabbaths]].

## v16

You shall count [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fifty|fifty]] days until the day after the seventh Sabbath; then you shall present a new meal offering to Jehovah.

## v17

You shall bring out of your dwelling places [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 17.1 — two|two]] loaves as a wave offering; they shall be of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour, baked with leaven, as firstfruits to Jehovah.

## v18

And you shall present with the bread seven lambs, a year old without blemish, and one bull of the herd and two rams; they shall be a [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 18.1 — burnt|burnt]] offering to Jehovah with their [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 18.1 — burnt|meal]] offering and their [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 18.1 — burnt|drink]] offerings, an offering by fire for a satisfying fragrance to Jehovah.

## v19

And you shall offer one male goat for a [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 19.1 — sin|sin]] offering and two male lambs, a year old, for a sacrifice of [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 19.1 — sin|peace]] offerings.

## v20

And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits as a wave offering before Jehovah with the two lambs; they shall be holy to Jehovah for the priest.

## v21

And you shall make a proclamation on that same day; you shall have a holy convocation; you shall do [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 21.1 — no|no]] work of labor. It shall be a perpetual statute in all your dwelling places throughout your generations.

## v22

And when you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not completely reap the corners of your field, nor shall you gather the gleanings of your harvest; you shall leave them for the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 22.1 — poor|poor]] and for the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 22.1 — poor|sojourner]]; I am Jehovah your God.

## v23

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v24

Speak to the children of Israel, saying, In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a complete rest, a memorial commemorated by the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 24.1 — blowing|blowing]] of trumpets, a holy convocation.

## v25

You shall do no work of labor, but you shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah.

## v26

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v27

And on the tenth day of this seventh month is the Day of [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 27.1 — Expiation|Expiation]]; you shall have a holy convocation, and you shall [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 27.2 — afflict|afflict]] your souls and present an offering by fire to Jehovah.

## v28

And you shall do [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 28.1 — no|no]] work on that same day, because it is a Day of Expiation, to make expiation on your behalf before Jehovah your God.

## v29

If there is any person who does not afflict himself on that same day, he shall be cut off from his people.

## v30

And if any person does any work on that same day, I will destroy that person from among his people.

## v31

You shall do no work; it shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations in all your dwelling places.

## v32

It shall be to you a Sabbath of complete rest, and you shall afflict your souls; on the ninth day of the month in the evening, from evening to evening, you shall keep your Sabbath.

## v33

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v34

Speak to the children of Israel, saying, On the fifteenth day of this seventh month is the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 34.1 — Feast|Feast]] of [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 34.2 — Tabernacles|Tabernacles]] for [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 34.3 — seven|seven]] days to Jehovah.

## v35

On the first day shall be a holy convocation; you shall do no work of labor.

## v36

[[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 36.1 — Seven|Seven]] days you shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah. On the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 36.2 — eighth|eighth]] day you shall have a holy convocation, and you shall present an offering by fire to Jehovah. It is a solemn assembly; you shall do no work of labor.

## v37

These are the appointed feasts of Jehovah, which you shall proclaim as holy convocations, to present an offering by fire to Jehovah, burnt offerings and meal offerings, sacrifices and drink offerings, each on its own day,

## v38

Besides the Sabbaths of Jehovah and besides your gifts and besides all your vows and besides all your freewill offerings which you give to Jehovah.

## v39

Then on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, when you have [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 39.1 — gathered|gathered]] in the produce of the land, you shall keep the feast of Jehovah seven days; on the first day shall be a complete rest, and on the eighth day shall be a complete rest.

## v40

And on the first day you shall take for yourselves the [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 40.1 — product|product]] of stately trees, branches of [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 40.2 — palm|palm]] trees and boughs of leafy trees and willows of the brook; and you shall rejoice before Jehovah your God for seven days.

## v41

And you shall keep it as a feast to Jehovah seven days in the year. It shall be a perpetual statute throughout your generations; you shall keep it in the seventh month.

## v42

You shall dwell in [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 42.1 — booths|booths]] seven days — all who are native in Israel shall dwell in booths —

## v43

So that your [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 43.1 — descendants|descendants]] may know that I made the children of Israel to dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt; I am Jehovah your God.

## v44

And Moses declared to the children of Israel the appointed [[Leviticus-23.fn#Footnote 44.1 — feasts|feasts]] of Jehovah.
