# Leviticus-22 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Leviticus-22|Leviticus 22]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — be

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v2|Leviticus 22:2]]

Lit., separate themselves from; Heb. nazar, the root from which the word Nazarite comes.

### Footnote 2.2 — holy

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v2|Leviticus 22:2]]

The holy things, the things offered to God by His people, signify Christ, whom God gives to His serving ones for their enjoyment.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — uncleanness

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v3|Leviticus 22:3]]

The regulations in [[Leviticus-22#v3|vv. 3-9]] concerning the priests’ not eating the holy things in their uncleanness, unless they bathe their flesh in water, that they might not sin and die, signify that we should not enjoy Christ while we are defiled with unclean things until we deal thoroughly with the defilement, mainly by washing ourselves in the Holy Spirit ([[1 Corinthians-06#v11|1 Cor. 6:11]]), that we may not be condemned and suffer spiritual death.

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — body

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v6|Leviticus 22:6]]

Lit., flesh.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — stranger

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v10|Leviticus 22:10]]

A stranger, a sojourner, or a hired servant being forbidden to eat any holy thing signifies that an unsaved person or one who has no share in God’s service may not enjoy Christ. A purchased slave or one born in a priest’s house being permitted to eat ([[Leviticus-22#v11|v. 11]]) signifies that the ones purchased by Christ with His precious blood and born of God in His house may enjoy Christ.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — daughter

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v12|Leviticus 22:12]]

The word in [[Leviticus-22#v12|vv. 12-13]] signifies that a believer (a priest’s daughter) who is attracted away by an outsider may not enjoy the ascended Christ (the heave offering). But if he cuts off his relationship with the outsider without leaving any connection and returns to the church, he may recover his enjoyment of Christ.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — vows

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v18|Leviticus 22:18]]

A vow is an oath made with God that cannot be retracted, whereas a freewill offering is a consecration that is not stable or sure. Hence, the offerings for a vow are stronger than those for a freewill offering (cf. [[Leviticus-22#v23|v. 23]]).

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — make

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v21|Leviticus 22:21]]

Lit., be wonderful to make a vow. So also in [[Leviticus-27#v2|27:2]].

## Footnotes for verse 23

### Footnote 23.1 — superfluous

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v23|Leviticus 22:23]]

Superfluous means exceeding the limit, and lacking means being under the proper measure. The word in this verse signifies that we may, in a general way (as a freewill offering), offer to God as food the Christ whom we experience too much or too little, but if we offer in a stronger way (as a vow), our offering will not be accepted.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — foreigner

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v25|Leviticus 22:25]]

The offering from the hand of a foreigner not being accepted by God signifies that the offering of Christ to God in a way that is according to the unbelievers will be rejected by God.

## Footnotes for verse 27

### Footnote 27.1 — eighth

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v27|Leviticus 22:27]]

This signifies that our experience of Christ must grow to the level of resurrection (the eighth day, the beginning of a new week—[[John-20#v1|John 20:1]]) before it will be accepted as an offering to God for His food. This requires us to learn how to reject our natural life and live by the divine life in resurrection ([[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]).

## Footnotes for verse 30

### Footnote 30.1 — same

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-22#v30|Leviticus 22:30]]

Eating the sacrifice of thanksgiving ([[Leviticus-22#v29|v. 29]]) on the same day and leaving none of it until the morning signify that our offering of Christ as a thanksgiving to God must be fresh and new, with nothing left to become stale ([[Hebrews-13#v15|Heb. 13:15]]).
