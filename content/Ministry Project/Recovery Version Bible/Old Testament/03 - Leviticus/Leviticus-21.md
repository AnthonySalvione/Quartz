## v1

And Jehovah said to Moses, Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them, No one shall [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 1.1 — defile|defile]] himself for a dead person among his people,

## v2

Except for his relative who is near to him, for his mother and for his father and for his son and for his daughter and for his brother

## v3

And for his virgin sister, who is near to him, who has had no husband; for her he may defile himself.

## v4

He shall not defile himself, as a [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 4.1 — leader|leader]] among his people, so as to profane himself.

## v5

They shall not make any [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 5.1 — baldness|baldness]] on their head or [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 5.1 — baldness|shave]] off the edges of their beard or make any [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 5.1 — baldness|cuttings]] in their flesh.

## v6

They shall be [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 6.1 — holy|holy]] to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God, for they present Jehovah’s offerings by fire, the food of their God; therefore they shall be holy.

## v7

They shall not take a [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 7.1 — woman|woman]] who is a harlot or profaned, nor shall they take a woman put away from her husband; for the [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 7.2 — priest|priest]] is holy to his God.

## v8

You shall sanctify him therefore, for he presents the food of your God. He shall be holy to you, for I Jehovah, who sanctifies you, am holy.

## v9

And the daughter of any priest, if she profanes herself through prostitution, she profanes her father; she shall be burned with fire.

## v10

And he who is the high priest among his brothers, on whose head the [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 10.1 — anointing|anointing]] oil has been poured and who has been consecrated to [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 10.1 — anointing|put]] on the garments, shall not let the hair of his head go [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 10.1 — anointing|loose]] nor [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 10.1 — anointing|tear]] his clothes.

## v11

Neither shall he approach any dead person nor [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 11.1 — defile|defile]] himself even for his father or for his mother.

## v12

Neither shall he go out of the sanctuary nor profane the sanctuary of his God, for the [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 12.1 — consecration|consecration]] of the anointing oil of his God is upon him; I am Jehovah.

## v13

And he shall take a wife in her virginity.

## v14

A widow or a divorced woman or a profaned woman, a harlot, these he shall not take; but rather a virgin of his own people he shall take as wife.

## v15

And he shall not profane his seed among his people, for I am Jehovah who sanctifies him.

## v16

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v17

Speak to Aaron, saying, No one of your seed throughout their generations who has a [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 17.1 — defect|defect]] shall approach to present the food of his God.

## v18

For no one who has a [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 18.1 — defect|defect]] shall approach: one who is blind or lame or with a disfigured nose or with an extended limb,

## v19

Or one who has a broken foot or a broken hand,

## v20

Or a hunchback or a dwarf, or one who has a defect in his eye or eczema or scale or crushed testicles.

## v21

No one of the seed of Aaron the priest who has a defect shall come near to present Jehovah’s offerings by fire. He has a defect; he shall not come near to present the food of his God.

## v22

He may [[Leviticus-21.fn#Footnote 22.1 — eat|eat]] the food of his God, both of the most holy and of the holy,

## v23

But he shall not go in unto the veil or come near to the altar, because he has a defect, so that he does not profane My holy places; for I am Jehovah who sanctifies them.

## v24

So Moses spoke to Aaron and to his sons and to all the children of Israel.
