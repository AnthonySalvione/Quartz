## v1

And if anyone [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sins|sins]] when he hears the voice of adjuration and he is a witness, either seeing or knowing about a matter, if he [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 1.2 — does|does]] not speak up, then he shall bear his iniquity.

## v2

Or if anyone touches any unclean thing, whether the [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — carcass|carcass]] of an unclean animal or the carcass of unclean cattle or the carcass of unclean [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 2.2 — swarming|swarming]] things, and it escapes his notice, and he is unclean, then he will be guilty.

## v3

Or if he touches the uncleanness of [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — man|man]], whatever his uncleanness is with which he becomes unclean, and it escapes his notice, when he comes to know it, then he will be guilty.

## v4

Or if anyone swears [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — rashly|rashly]] with his lips to do evil or to do good, whatever it is that a man utters rashly with an oath, and it escapes his notice, when he comes to know it, then he shall be guilty in one of these things.

## v5

And when he is guilty in one of these things, he shall confess that in which he has sinned;

## v6

And he shall bring his [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — trespass|trespass]] offering to Jehovah for his sin which he has committed, a [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 6.2 — female|female]] from the flock, a sheep or a goat, as a [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 6.3 — sin|sin]] offering; and the priest shall make expiation for him on account of his sin.

## v7

And if he cannot afford a lamb, then he shall bring as his trespass offering to Jehovah for that in which he has sinned two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — sin|sin]] offering and the other for a [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — sin|burnt]] offering.

## v8

And he shall bring them to the priest, who shall present first that which is for the sin offering; and the [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — priest|priest]] shall wring off its head at the front of its neck but shall not sever it;

## v9

And he shall sprinkle some of the [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — blood|blood]] of the sin offering upon the side of the altar, and the rest of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar; it is a sin offering.

## v10

And he shall offer the second as a burnt offering according to the ordinance. And the priest shall make expiation for him on account of his sin which he has committed, and he will be forgiven.

## v11

But if he cannot afford two turtledoves or two young pigeons, then he shall bring as his offering for that which he has sinned, the tenth of an ephah of [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fine|fine]] flour for a sin offering; he shall put no [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 11.2 — oil|oil]] upon it nor put any [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 11.2 — oil|frankincense]] upon it, for it is a sin offering.

## v12

And he shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — a|a]] handful of it as its memorial portion and [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 12.2 — burn|burn]] it on the altar, upon Jehovah’s offerings by fire; it is a sin offering.

## v13

And the priest shall make expiation for him concerning his sin that he has committed in any one of these things, and he will be forgiven. And the [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — rest|rest]] shall be the priest’s, like the meal offering.

## v14

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v15

If anyone acts unfaithfully and sins without intent in the holy things of Jehovah, then he shall bring his trespass offering to Jehovah, a [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — ram|ram]] without blemish out of the flock according to your valuation in silver by shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass offering.

## v16

And he shall make [[Leviticus-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — restitution|restitution]] for the holy thing in which he has sinned, and he shall add to it a fifth part of it and give it to the priest. And the priest shall make expiation for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and he will be forgiven.

## v17

And if anyone sins and does any one of the things which Jehovah has commanded not to be done, though he did not know, he is still guilty and shall bear his iniquity.

## v18

And he shall bring to the priest a ram without blemish out of the flock according to your valuation for a trespass offering. And the priest shall make expiation for him concerning the matter in which he erred without intent and did not know, and he will be forgiven.

## v19

It is a trespass offering; he is certainly guilty before Jehovah.
