## v1

[[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Then|Then]] Jehovah called to Moses and [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — spoke|spoke]] to him out of the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Tent|Tent]] of Meeting, saying,

## v2

Speak to the children of Israel and say to them, When any one of you presents an [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — offering|offering]] to Jehovah, you shall present your offering from the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — cattle|cattle]], of the herd or of the flock.

## v3

If his offering is a [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — burnt|burnt]] offering from the herd, he shall present it, a [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — male|male]] without [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — male|blemish]]; he shall present it at the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — entrance|entrance]] of the Tent of Meeting, that he may be accepted before Jehovah.

## v4

And he shall lay his [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — hand|hand]] on the head of the burnt offering, and it shall be accepted for him, to make [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — expiation|expiation]] for him.

## v5

And he shall [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — slaughter|slaughter]] the young bull before Jehovah; and Aaron’s sons the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — priests|priests]] shall present the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — blood|blood]] and sprinkle the blood on and around the altar that is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

## v6

And he shall [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — skin|skin]] the burnt offering and [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — cut|cut]] it into its pieces.

## v7

And the sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire on the altar and arrange the wood in order on the fire.

## v8

Then Aaron’s sons the priests shall arrange the pieces, the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — head|head]], and the fat in order on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar;

## v9

But its inward parts and its legs he shall [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — wash|wash]] with water. Then the priest shall [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — burn|burn]] the whole on the altar, as a burnt offering, an offering by [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — fire|fire]], a satisfying [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 9.4 — fragrance|fragrance]] to Jehovah.

## v10

And if his offering is from the flock, of the sheep or of the goats, for a burnt offering, he shall present it, a male without blemish.

## v11

And he shall slaughter it on the side of the altar northward before Jehovah, and Aaron’s sons the priests shall sprinkle its blood on and around the altar.

## v12

And he shall [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — cut|cut]] it into its pieces with its head and its fat, and the priest shall arrange them in order on the wood that is on the fire which is on the altar;

## v13

But the inward parts and the legs he shall wash with water. Then the priest shall present the whole and burn it on the altar; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a satisfying fragrance to Jehovah.

## v14

And if his offering to Jehovah is a burnt offering of [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — birds|birds]], then he shall present his offering of turtledoves or of young pigeons.

## v15

And the priest shall present it at the altar and wring off its head and burn it on the altar; and its blood shall be drained out on the side of the altar.

## v16

And he shall take away its crop with its feathers and cast it beside the altar toward the east, in the place of the [[Leviticus-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — ashes|ashes]].

## v17

Then he shall tear it open by its wings without dividing it, and the priest shall burn it on the altar on the wood that is on the fire; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a satisfying fragrance to Jehovah.
