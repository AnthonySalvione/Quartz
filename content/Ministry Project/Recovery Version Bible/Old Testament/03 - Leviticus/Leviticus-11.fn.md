# Leviticus-11 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Leviticus-11|Leviticus 11]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Then

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v1|Leviticus 11:1]]

The section of this book from [[Leviticus-11|Leviticus 11—27]] concerns the living of God’s people, a living that is holy, clean, and full of rejoicing. See note 11 in [[Leviticus-01|Leviticus 1]].

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — eat

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v2|Leviticus 11:2]]

To eat is to contact things outside of us and to receive them into us, with the result that they eventually become our inner constitution. In this chapter all the animals signify different kinds of people, and eating signifies our contacting of people (cf. [[Acts-10#v9|Acts 10:9b-14]], [[Acts-10#v27|27-29]]). For God’s people to live a holy life as required by the holy God, they must be careful about the kind of people they contact (cf. [[Leviticus-11#v46|vv. 46-47]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v33|1 Cor. 15:33]]; [[2 Corinthians-06#v14|2 Cor. 6:14-18]]).

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — has

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v3|Leviticus 11:3]]

Lit., divides the hoof, that is, splits the split of its hoofs (cf. [[Leviticus-11#v7|vv. 7]], [[Leviticus-11#v26|26]]). Animals that divide the hoof and chew the cud ([[Leviticus-11#v3|v. 3]]; cf. [[Leviticus-11#v4|vv. 4-8]], [[Leviticus-11#v26|26-28]]) signify persons who have discernment in their activities ([[Philippians-01#v9|Phil. 1:9-10]]) and who receive the word of God with much reconsideration (see note 151 in [[Psalm-119|Psa. 119]]).

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — fins

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v9|Leviticus 11:9]]

In the Bible the sea signifies the fallen, corrupted world ([[Daniel-07#v3|Dan. 7:3]], [[Daniel-07#v17|17]]; [[Revelation-17#v15|Rev. 17:15]]). Fins help fish to move, to act, in water according to their wishes, and scales protect the fish and keep those fish that live in the sea from being salted. Hence, aquatic animals that have fins and scales ([[Leviticus-11#v9|vv. 9-12]]) signify persons who can move and act freely in the world and at the same time resist its influence.

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — birds

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v13|Leviticus 11:13]]

Birds that have wings for flying and that eat seeds of life as their food supply signify persons who can live and move in a life that is away from and above the world and who take things of life as their life supply. The unclean birds mentioned in [[Leviticus-11#v13|vv. 13-19]], which do not feed on seeds but feed on carcasses, signify persons who feed on the things of death. See note 71 in [[Genesis-08|Gen. 8]].

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — flying

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v20|Leviticus 11:20]]

Lit., winged swarming things. So also in [[Leviticus-11#v21|vv. 21]] and [[Leviticus-11#v23|23]].

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — legs

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v21|Leviticus 11:21]]

Insects that have wings and have legs above their feet for leaping on the ground ([[Leviticus-11#v21|vv. 21-22]], cf. [[Leviticus-11#v23|vv. 23-25]]) signify persons who can live and move in a life that is above the world and who can keep themselves from the world.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — wash

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v25|Leviticus 11:25]]

A person defiled by the carcass of an animal had to wash his clothes ([[Leviticus-11#v25|vv. 25]], [[Leviticus-11#v28|28a]], [[Leviticus-11#v40|40]]), signifying that our conduct in our daily life (signified by clothing—[[Isaiah-64#v6|Isa. 64:6a]]) should be washed from the defilement of death.

## Footnotes for verse 29

### Footnote 29.1 — swarming

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v29|Leviticus 11:29]]

Or, crawling things that crawl. So also in [[Leviticus-11#v41|vv. 41-43]] (cf. [[Leviticus-11#v31|vv. 31]], [[Leviticus-11#v44|44]], [[Leviticus-11#v46|46]]). All the creatures that crawl on the earth were unclean ([[Leviticus-11#v41|vv. 41-44]]). Creatures that go on their stomach or on all fours or have many feet ([[Leviticus-11#v42|v. 42]]) signify Satan with all the evil spirits and demons, persons who are filled with Satan, who contact evil spirits and demons, and persons who live in the world and cleave to the world, being unable to sever themselves from the world (cf. [[Genesis-03#v14|Gen. 3:14]]; [[Revelation-12#v9|Rev. 12:9]]; [[Luke-10#v19|Luke 10:19]] and note 2).

## Footnotes for verse 31

### Footnote 31.1 — dead

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v31|Leviticus 11:31]]

In this chapter the word carcass (or carcasses) is used thirteen times, and dead (or dies) is used three times. All dead things are unclean ([[Leviticus-11#v8|vv. 8]], [[Leviticus-11#v11|11]], [[Leviticus-11#v24|24]], [[Leviticus-11#v27|27]], [[Leviticus-11#v31|31-40]]). Uncleanness, therefore, is synonymous with death. The mentioning of death in relation to diet indicates that our diet, our eating, is a matter of life and death. Life is of the tree of life, and death is of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil ([[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]], 17 and notes). Death is abominable to God. Therefore, in order to live a holy life, we need to abstain from death, i.e., spiritual death.

### Footnote 31.2 — until

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v31|Leviticus 11:31]]

According to the Bible, death is more defiling and abominable than sin. Through the trespass offering any sin could be forgiven immediately ([[Leviticus-05|Leviticus 5]]), but a person who touched the carcass of any animal was unclean until the evening ([[Leviticus-11#v24|vv. 24-25]], [[Leviticus-11#v27|27b-28a]], [[Leviticus-11#v31|31b-32]], [[Leviticus-11#v39|39-40]]). Our sins are forgiven by the Lord immediately after we confess them to Him ([[1 John-01#v9|1 John 1:9]]), but a certain period of time must pass before we can be cleansed from the defilement of spiritual death. A person who touched the carcass of a man was unclean for seven days ([[Numbers-19#v11|Num. 19:11]], [[Numbers-19#v13|13]]), indicating that in the eyes of God, fallen human beings are the most defiling element (cf. [[Matthew-15#v17|Matt. 15:17-20]]).

## Footnotes for verse 32

### Footnote 32.1 — water

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v32|Leviticus 11:32]]

Anything upon which the carcass of any animal fell was unclean and had to be put into water, signifying that the defilement of death should be washed away by the cleansing of the Spirit of life (signified here by water—[[John-07#v38|John 7:38-39]]).

## Footnotes for verse 33

### Footnote 33.1 — vessel

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v33|Leviticus 11:33]]

Lit., it. An earthen vessel signifies the natural, created man ([[Romans-09#v21|Rom. 9:21]], [[Romans-09#v23|23]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v7|2 Cor. 4:7]]). The carcass of any animal falling into an earthen vessel or upon an oven or stove made it unclean, and that vessel or that oven or stove had to be broken ([[Leviticus-11#v33|vv. 33]], [[Leviticus-11#v35|35]]). This signifies that our natural man, our being, our self, that has been defiled in our daily life needs to be broken by the cross of Christ (cf. [[Luke-09#v23|Luke 9:23]]).

## Footnotes for verse 34

### Footnote 34.1 — food

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v34|Leviticus 11:34]]

Any food upon which water came from a vessel that had been defiled by a carcass, and any drink in such a vessel, were unclean, signifying that a person (signified by the food or the drink) who is touched by the earthly (worldly) flow or mixed with it in his daily life is easily affected by the defilement of death.

## Footnotes for verse 36

### Footnote 36.1 — spring

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v36|Leviticus 11:36]]

A spring or a cistern collecting water remained clean, signifying that anything that has the flow of living water, which washes away the defilement of death at any time, remains clean. The spring of living water signifies the Spirit ([[John-04#v14|John 4:14]]; [[John-07#v38|7:38-39]]), and the cistern of living water signifies Christ, who holds the living water ([[John-04#v10|John 4:10]]; [[John-07#v37|7:37]]). As long as we have the spring and the cistern, the Spirit and Christ, in our daily life, we shall remain clean.

## Footnotes for verse 37

### Footnote 37.1 — seed

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v37|Leviticus 11:37]]

The seed for sowing remained clean, signifying that anything that is living and possesses life that has the strength to resist defilement remains clean. The seed here typifies Christ as the seed of life (see note 31 in [[Mark-04|Mark 4]]).

## Footnotes for verse 47

### Footnote 47.1 — clean

**Verse:** [[Leviticus-11#v47|Leviticus 11:47]]

The matter of eating involves cleanness, the reality of which is Christ, who is our content, life supply, and spiritual food ([[John-06#v35|John 6:35]], [[John-06#v57|57]]). All the clean living creatures are types of Christ. Only Christ is able to keep us holy, as God is holy ([[Leviticus-11#v45|v. 45b]]). Only Christ and that which is out of Christ are clean for our food supply. This only is what we should contact, eat, and receive.
