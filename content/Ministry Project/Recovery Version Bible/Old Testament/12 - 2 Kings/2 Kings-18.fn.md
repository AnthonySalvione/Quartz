## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — Nehushtan

**Verse:** [[2 Kings-18#v4|2 Kings 18:4]]

Meaning a piece of bronze. Moses, according to God’s instructions, had made that bronze serpent in order to rescue the people from death ([[Numbers-21#v8|Num. 21:8-9]]). However, in their superstition the people eventually regarded the bronze serpent as an idol, and for this reason Hezekiah broke it in pieces.
