## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — leper

**Verse:** [[2 Kings-15#v5|2 Kings 15:5]]

This was because of Azariah’s overstepping in not keeping God’s ordinance, which said that only the priests ordained by God could participate in the priestly service ([[2 Chronicles-26#v16|2 Chron. 26:16-21]]).
