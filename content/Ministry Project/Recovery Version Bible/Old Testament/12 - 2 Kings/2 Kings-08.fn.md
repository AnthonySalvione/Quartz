## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — Go

## Footnotes for verse 19

### Footnote 19.1 — David

**Verse:** [[2 Kings-08#v19|2 Kings 8:19]]

I.e., because of Christ, who was to be incarnated as one of David’s descendants ([[Matthew-01#v1|Matt. 1:1]]; [[Romans-01#v3|Rom. 1:3]]). See note 71 in [[Matthew-01|Matt. 1]].

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — Joram

**Verse:** [[2 Kings-08#v21|2 Kings 8:21]]

A spelling variant of Jehoram in [[2 Kings-08#v16|v. 16]] (cf. [[2 Chronicles-21#v9|2 Chron. 21:9]]). He is the king of Judah.
