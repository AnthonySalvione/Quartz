### Footnote 6.1 — float

**Verse:** [[2 Kings-06#v6|2 Kings 6:6]]

Elisha’s causing an ax head that had fallen into the water to float by means of a wooden stick signifies Christ’s recovering through His cross in resurrection the lost power of sinners that had fallen into the death water ([[Ephesians-02#v1|Eph. 2:1-6]]).
