## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — Feast

**Verse:** [[Ezra-03#v4|Ezra 3:4]]

The keeping of the Feast of Tabernacles and the offering of the daily burnt offerings and the offerings of the new moons and of all the appointed feasts of Jehovah ([[Ezra-03#v4|vv. 4-6a]]) indicate that Israel recovered the worship of God, which had been lost for at least seventy years.
