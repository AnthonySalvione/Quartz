## v1

Then Elihu continued and said,

## v2

Do you consider this to be just?  
Do you say, My righteousness is more than God’s,

## v3

So that you say, What advantage is there to me,  
What do I profit, more than if I had sinned?

## v4

I will respond to you with words,  
And to your companions with you.

## v5

Look unto heaven and see;  
And behold the skies: They are higher than you.

## v6

If you sin, what do you accomplish against Him?  
And if your transgressions are multiplied, what do you do to Him?

## v7

If you are righteous, what can you give to Him,  
Or what does He receive from your hand?

## v8

Your wickedness affects a man like you,  
And your righteousness, a son of man.

## v9

Because of the multitude of oppressions they cry out;  
They cry for help because of the arm of the mighty.

## v10

But no one says, Where is God my Maker,  
Who gives songs in the night,

## v11

Who teaches us more than the beasts of the earth  
And makes us wiser than the birds of heaven?

## v12

There they cry, but He does not answer,  
Because of the pride of evil men.

## v13

Surely God does not hear an empty cry,  
And the Almighty does not regard it.

## v14

How much less when you say that you do not behold Him,  
That the cause is before Him and you are waiting on Him!

## v15

But now, because He has not visited in His anger  
Nor regarded such great arrogance,

## v16

Job opens his mouth in vanity;  
He multiplies words without knowledge.
