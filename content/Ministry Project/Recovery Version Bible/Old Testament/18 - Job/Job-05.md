## v1

Call now! Is there anyone who will answer you?  
And to which of the holy ones will you turn?

## v2

Surely vexation slays the fool,  
And jealousy kills the simpleton.

## v3

I have seen the fool taking root,  
But suddenly I cursed his habitation.

## v4

His children are far from safety,  
And they are crushed in the gate; and there is no one to deliver them.

## v5

The hungry eat his harvest  
And even take it out from the thorns,  
And the thirsty pant after his wealth.

## v6

For evil does not come forth from the dust,  
Nor does trouble spring forth from the ground.

## v7

But man is born unto trouble,  
Just as sparks fly upward.

## v8

But as for me, I would seek after God,  
And I would commit my cause to God,

## v9

Who does great things that cannot be searched,  
Wonderful deeds that cannot be numbered;

## v10

Who gives rain upon the surface of the earth  
And sends water upon the surface of the fields;

## v11

Who sets on high those who are lowly  
And puts those who mourn safely above all.

## v12

He frustrates the devices of the crafty,  
So that their hands achieve no enterprise.

## v13

He seizes the wise in their own craftiness,  
And the counsel of the wily is brought to a sudden end.

## v14

By day they encounter darkness,  
And at noon they grope about as in the night.

## v15

But He saves the needy from the sword of their mouth,  
From the hand of the strong.

## v16

Thus the poor have hope,  
And Injustice stops her mouth.

## v17

Indeed, blessed is the man whom God corrects;  
Therefore do not reject the chastening of the Almighty.

## v18

For He wounds, but He binds up;  
He strikes, but His hands heal.

## v19

In six troubles He will deliver you;  
Indeed in seven no evil will touch you.

## v20

In famine He will redeem you from death,  
And in battle, from the power of the sword.

## v21

From the scourge of the tongue you will be hidden,  
And you will not be afraid of destruction when it comes.

## v22

At destruction and at famine you will laugh,  
And you will not be afraid of the animals of the earth.

## v23

For you will be in an alliance with the stones of the field,  
And the animals of the field will be at peace with you.

## v24

And you will know that your tent is at peace,  
And you will inspect your fold and miss nothing.

## v25

And you will know that your seed will be great,  
And your offspring like the grass of the earth.

## v26

You will come to the grave at a ripe old age,  
Like a shock of grain coming up in its season.

## v27

This is it. We have searched it out; it is so.  
Hear then and know it for yourself.
