## v1

Then Job answered and said,

## v2

I have heard many such things;  
Troubling comforters are you all.

## v3

Do words of wind have an end?  
Or what has provoked you that you so respond?

## v4

I also could speak like you,  
If your soul were in my soul’s stead;  
I could join my words together against you  
And shake my head at you.

## v5

I could strengthen you with my mouth,  
And the movement of my lips would mitigate your pain.

## v6

If I speak, my pain is not mitigated;  
And if I forbear, how much departs from me?

## v7

But now He has worn me out;  
You have desolated all my company.

## v8

And You have seized me; it is a testimony against me;  
And my leanness rises up against me; it testifies to my face.

## v9

In His wrath He has torn me to pieces and been adverse toward me;  
He has gnashed His teeth at me.  
My Adversary sharpens His eyes at me.

## v10

With their mouth they gape at me;  
They strike my cheek reproachfully;  
They mass themselves together against me.

## v11

God has delivered me over to the unjust  
And cast me down into the hands of the wicked.

## v12

I was at ease, and He broke me apart;  
Indeed He took me by my neck and dashed me to pieces.  
And He set me up as His target;

## v13

His archers surrounded me;  
He split open my kidneys and did not desist;  
He poured out my gall upon the earth.

## v14

He broke me open with breach upon breach;  
He ran at me like a mighty man of war.

## v15

I have sewn sackcloth upon my skin  
And have cast my horn into the dust.

## v16

My face is reddened with weeping,  
And on my eyelids is the shadow of death;

## v17

Though there is no violence in my hands,  
And my prayer is pure.

## v18

O earth, do not cover my blood;  
And let there be no resting place for my crying out.

## v19

Even now, there in heaven is my Witness,  
And He who vouches for me is in the heights.

## v20

My friends deride me;  
My eye pours out tears to God,

## v21

That He would plead for a man in his case with God  
And for a son of man in his case with his Neighbor.

## v22

For when years few in number have come,  
I will go the way from which I cannot return.
