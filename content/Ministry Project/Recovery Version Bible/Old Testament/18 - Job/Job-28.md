## v1

There is certainly a mine for silver,  
And a place for gold to be refined.

## v2

Iron is taken out of the earth,  
And copper is smelted from rock.

## v3

The miner sets an end to the darkness,  
And to the very end he searches out  
The stones of deep darkness and of gloom.

## v4

He breaks open a shaft apart from where men dwell;  
They are forgotten by the foot;  
They are suspended apart from men, they sway about.

## v5

The earth — from it comes forth food,  
Yet underneath it, it is turned up as it were by fire.

## v6

Its stones are the place of sapphires,  
And its dust has gold.

## v7

It is a path known by no bird of prey,  
And the falcon’s eye has not seen it.

## v8

The proud beasts have not trodden it;  
The fierce lion does not pass along it.

## v9

The miner sends forth his hand into the flinty rock;  
He overturns the mountains by their roots.

## v10

He cuts out channels in the rocks,  
And his eye sees every precious thing.

## v11

He dams up the streams so that they do not trickle,  
And that which is hidden he brings out to the light.

## v12

But where shall wisdom be found?  
And where is the place of understanding?

## v13

Man does not know its price,  
Nor is it found in the land of the living.

## v14

The deep says, It is not in me;  
And the sea says, It is not with me.

## v15

Rare gold cannot be given for it,  
Nor can silver be weighed out for its price.

## v16

It cannot be balanced with the gold of Ophir,  
With precious onyx or with sapphire.

## v17

Gold and glass cannot compare with it,  
Nor can vessels of fine gold be exchanged for it.

## v18

Coral and crystal cannot be mentioned with it;  
Indeed the acquisition of wisdom is above that of pearls.

## v19

The topaz of Cush cannot compare with it,  
Nor can it be balanced with pure gold.

## v20

From where then does wisdom come?  
And where is the place of understanding?

## v21

For it is hidden from the eyes of all living  
And concealed from the birds of heaven.

## v22

Abaddon and Death say,  
We have heard a report of it with our ears.

## v23

God understands the way to it;  
He knows its place.

## v24

For He sees to the ends of the earth;  
He beholds all that is under heaven.

## v25

When He gave weight to the wind  
And apportioned the water by measure,

## v26

When He made a decree for the rain  
And a way for the thunderbolt;

## v27

Then He saw it and declared it;  
He established it and even searched it out.

## v28

And He said to man,  
Indeed, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom;  
And to depart from evil is understanding.
