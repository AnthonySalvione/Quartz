## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — Judge

**Verse:** [[Job-09#v15|Job 9:15]]

Job thought that he could not win his case, even though he was righteous and perfect ([[Job-09#v15|vv. 15]], [[Job-09#v20|20-21]]), because God is mighty and does not turn back His anger in His judgment ([[Job-09#v13|vv. 13-24]]). Here Job had a wrong concept, thinking that God was dealing with him in anger. However, God’s dealing with Job was not God’s anger but God’s good pleasure ([[Ephesians-01#v5|Eph. 1:5]], [[Ephesians-01#v9|9]]). It was not God’s judging but God’s stripping, consuming, and tearing down that He might rebuild Job with Himself.
