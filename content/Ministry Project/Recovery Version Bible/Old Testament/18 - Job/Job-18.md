## v1

Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said,

## v2

How long will you hunt for words?  
Consider, and afterward we will speak.

## v3

Why are we considered to be like beasts  
And have become unclean in your eyes?

## v4

You who tear yourself in your anger,  
Will the earth be forsaken for your sake,  
Or will the rock be removed from its place?

## v5

Indeed the light of the wicked goes out,  
And the flame of his fire does not shine.

## v6

The light is darkness in his tent,  
And his lamp above him goes out.

## v7

The steps of his strength are confined,  
And his counsel has cast him down.

## v8

For he has been cast into a net by his own feet,  
And he walks about on the webbing of a pitfall.

## v9

A snare grabs him by the heel;  
A trap lays hold on him.

## v10

A rope is hidden for him on the ground,  
And a trap for him, on the path.

## v11

Terrors frighten him all around  
And chase him at his heels.

## v12

His strength is famished,  
And calamity is prepared at his side.

## v13

It devours the members of his body;  
The firstborn of death devours his members.

## v14

He is rooted out of his tent, in which he trusts;  
And he is made to march to the king of terrors.

## v15

That which is not his dwells in his tent;  
Brimstone is scattered upon his habitation.

## v16

Beneath, his roots are dried up;  
And above, his branch is withered.

## v17

The memory of him perishes from the earth,  
And he has no name on the open plain.

## v18

He is thrust out from the light to the darkness  
And driven from the world.

## v19

He has no posterity and no progeny among his people,  
And there are none remaining where he sojourned.

## v20

Those who come after will be astonished at his day,  
As those who went before were horrified.

## v21

Surely these are the dwellings of the unjust,  
And this is the place of him who does not know God.
