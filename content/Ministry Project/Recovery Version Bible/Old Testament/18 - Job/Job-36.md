## v1

And Elihu added this and said,

## v2

Bear with me a little, and I will show you;  
For there is more to say for God.

## v3

I will bring my knowledge from afar  
And will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.

## v4

For truly my words are not false;  
One perfect in knowledge is with you.

## v5

Indeed, God is mighty and does not despise;  
He is mighty in strength of understanding.

## v6

He does not preserve the wicked man alive,  
But gives justice to the afflicted.

## v7

He does not withdraw His eyes from the righteous;  
And with kings on the throne,  
He sets them forever, and they are exalted.

## v8

And if they are bound in fetters,  
If they are caught in the cords of affliction,

## v9

He shows them their work  
And their transgressions, that they have acted arrogantly.

## v10

He also opens their ear to instruction  
And commands that they return from iniquity.

## v11

If they hear and serve Him,  
They will spend their days in prosperity  
And their years in pleasantness.

## v12

But if they do not hear, they will pass away by the sword  
And die without knowledge.

## v13

But those who are profane in heart lay up anger;  
They do not cry for help when He binds them.

## v14

They die in youth,  
And their life ends among the most defiled.

## v15

He rescues the afflicted by their affliction  
And opens their ear through oppression.

## v16

Indeed He allures you from the jaws of distress  
Into a spacious place, where there is no constraint;  
And what is upon your table is full of fatness.

## v17

But you are filled with the judgment of the wicked;  
Judgment and litigation have taken hold of you.

## v18

Beware lest wrath allures you into scoffing,  
And do not let the greatness of the ransom turn you aside.

## v19

Will your cry keep you from being in distress,  
Or all the forces of your strength?

## v20

Do not long for the night,  
When people are taken from their place.

## v21

Take heed; do not turn toward iniquity;  
For you have chosen this rather than affliction.

## v22

Indeed, God is exalted in His power:  
Who is a teacher like Him?

## v23

Who has appointed His way for Him?  
And who says, You have done wrong?

## v24

Remember to magnify His work,  
Of which men sing.

## v25

All mankind has gazed on it;  
Man beholds it from afar.

## v26

Indeed, God is great, and we do not know Him;  
The number of His years we also cannot search out.

## v27

For He draws up the drops of water  
Which distill into rain from His mist,

## v28

Which the skies pour down  
And drop upon man abundantly.

## v29

Can they indeed understand the spreading of the clouds,  
The thunderings of His pavilion?

## v30

Indeed, He scatters His lightning around Him  
And covers the roots of the sea.

## v31

For by these He judges the peoples;  
He gives food in abundance.

## v32

He fills His hands with lightning  
And commands it to strike the mark.

## v33

Its noise tells of Him;  
The cattle as well tell concerning Him who is coming.
