## v1

Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said,

## v2

Because of this, my disquieting thoughts answer me;  
And hence my haste is in me.

## v3

I hear the reproof that humiliates me,  
And the spirit of my understanding answers me.

## v4

Do you not know this from of old,  
Since man was set upon the earth,

## v5

That the joyous shouting of the wicked is short,  
And the rejoicing of the profane is but for a moment?

## v6

Though his arrogance goes up to heaven,  
And his head touches the clouds;

## v7

Like his own dung he perishes forever;  
Those who have seen him say, Where is he?

## v8

Like a dream he flies away and is not found;  
Indeed he is chased away like a vision of the night.

## v9

The eye looks on him then sees him no more,  
Nor does his place observe him anymore.

## v10

His children seek the favor of the poor,  
And his hands return his wealth.

## v11

His bones are full of youthful vigor,  
Yet it lies down with him in the dust.

## v12

Though wickedness is sweet in his mouth,  
Though he hides it under his tongue,

## v13

Though he favors it and will not forsake it,  
But holds it in his mouth;

## v14

His food in his bowels is changed;  
It is the venom of asps within him.

## v15

He swallows down riches and vomits them up;  
God casts them forth from his stomach.

## v16

He sucks the poison of asps;  
The tongue of the viper slays him.

## v17

He will not look on the rivers,  
The streams flowing with honey and butter.

## v18

He will return what he toiled for and will not swallow it down;  
And he will not rejoice according to the wealth of his trading.

## v19

For he has oppressed and abandoned the poor;  
He has violently seized a house that he did not build.

## v20

Because he knew no respite in his craving,  
Of that which he desired he will save nothing.

## v21

There will be nothing left of what he has devoured;  
Thus his prosperity will not endure.

## v22

In the fullness of his sufficiency he will be distressed;  
The hand of everyone in trouble will come against him.

## v23

In order to fill his belly,  
God will send the burning fierceness of His wrath on him  
And will rain it upon him as his food.

## v24

He will flee from the iron weapon,  
But the bronze bow will strike him through.

## v25

He draws the arrow out, and it comes out of his body;  
Indeed the glittering point goes forth from his gall.  
Terrors come upon him.

## v26

Total darkness is laid up as his treasures;  
A fire not fanned by man will devour him;  
It will feed on what is left in his tent.

## v27

The heavens will reveal his iniquity,  
And the earth will rise up against him.

## v28

The increase of his house will depart  
As things swept away in the day of His wrath.

## v29

This is the wicked man’s portion from God  
And the inheritance decreed to him by God.
