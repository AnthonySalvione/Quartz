# Job-10 — Footnotes

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — hidden

**Verse:** [[Job-10#v13|Job 10:13]]

This indicates that Job could not find the reason for God’s treatment of him, but he believed that there had to be some reason hidden in God’s heart. Job was right; something was hidden in God’s heart—the mystery of the ages ([[Ephesians-03#v9|Eph. 3:9]]). After creating man in His image and according to His likeness ([[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]), God kept His intention hidden throughout the ages. Before the New Testament time He did not unveil to anyone what His purpose was ([[Ephesians-03#v4|Eph. 3:4-5]]). 

The mystery hidden in God’s heart is God’s eternal economy ([[Ephesians-01#v10|Eph. 1:10]]; [[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v4|1 Tim. 1:4]]), which is God’s eternal intention with His heart’s desire to dispense Himself in His Divine Trinity as the Father in the Son by the Spirit into His chosen people to be their life and nature that they may be the same as He is as His duplication ([[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[1 John-03#v2|1 John 3:2]]), to become an organism, the Body of Christ as the new man ([[Ephesians-02#v15|Eph. 2:15-16]]), for God’s fullness, God’s expression ([[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-03#v19|3:19]]), which will consummate in the New Jerusalem ([[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]]—[[Revelation-22#v5|22:5]]). Not knowing God’s intention, Job misunderstood God and thought that God was angry with him and was judging him and punishing him. God’s intention was not to judge Job or to punish him but to tear him down and then rebuild him with Himself, to make Job a new man in God’s new creation ([[2 Corinthians-05#v17|2 Cor. 5:17]]; [[Galatians-06#v15|Gal. 6:15]]). This is the answer to Job, to the book of Job, and to Job’s vindication.
