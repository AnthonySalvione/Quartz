## v1

Then Job answered and said,

## v2

How you have helped him who is without power!  
How you have saved the arm of him who is without strength!

## v3

How you have counseled him who is without wisdom  
And have plentifully declared sound knowledge!

## v4

To whom have you uttered words?  
And whose [[Job-26.fn#Footnote 4.1 — spirit|spirit]] has come forth from you?

## v5

The deceased are made to tremble  
Beneath the waters, and those who inhabit them.

## v6

[[Job-26.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Sheol|Sheol]] is naked before Him,  
And [[Job-26.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Abaddon|Abaddon]] has no covering.

## v7

He stretches out the north over the void;  
He hangs the earth upon [[Job-26.fn#Footnote 7.1 — nothing|nothing]].

## v8

He binds up the waters in His thick clouds,  
And the cloud is not rent under them.

## v9

He covers the face of His throne;  
He spreads His cloud over it.

## v10

He drew a circle on the surface of the waters  
As a boundary of the light and the darkness.

## v11

The pillars of heaven shook  
And were astounded at His rebuke.

## v12

By His power He stilled the sea,  
And by His understanding He struck down [[Job-26.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Rahab|Rahab]].

## v13

By His [[Job-26.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] the heavens became beauty;  
His hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

## v14

Indeed, these are but the fringes of His ways;  
And how small a whisper do we hear of Him!  
But as for the thundering of His mightiness, who can comprehend it?
