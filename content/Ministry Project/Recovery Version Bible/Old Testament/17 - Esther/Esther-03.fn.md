# Esther-03 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Esther-03|Esther 3]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Agagite

**Verse:** [[Esther-03#v1|Esther 3:1]]

The Agagites were enemies of God. God had charged Saul to slay all the Agagites, but he failed to do this and thereby offended God ([[1 Samuel-15|1 Sam. 15]]).

### Footnote 1.2 — advanced

**Verse:** [[Esther-03#v1|Esther 3:1]]

Haman’s being advanced by the king was no doubt at the instigation of Satan, the adversary of God.
