# Malachi-02 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Malachi-02|Malachi 2]]

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — wife

**Verse:** [[Malachi-02#v14|Malachi 2:14]]

[[Malachi-02#v13|Verses 13-14]] indicate that the treachery of a priest toward his wife damages his priesthood. If a servant of the Lord cannot live properly with his wife, his service will be annulled.

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — seed

**Verse:** [[Malachi-02#v15|Malachi 2:15]]

In marriage God has made the husband and wife one in order to produce “the seed of God,” i.e., godly children.

### Footnote 15.2 — spirit

**Verse:** [[Malachi-02#v15|Malachi 2:15]]

Our spirit is the source of our conduct. God cares for our spirit ([[Zechariah-12#v1|Zech. 12:1]]), and we must take heed to it (cf. [[Acts-24#v16|Acts 24:16]]; [[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4-6]]; [[2 Corinthians-02#v13|2 Cor. 2:13]]).

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — behaves

**Verse:** [[Malachi-02#v16|Malachi 2:16]]

In the sight of God, divorce is a violent act.
