## v1

And bring near to yourself [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Aaron|Aaron]] your brother and his sons with him, from among the children of Israel, that he may serve Me as a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 1.2 — priest|priest]] — Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron’s sons.

## v2

And you shall make holy [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 2.1 — garments|garments]] for Aaron your brother, [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 2.2 — for|for]] glory and for beauty.

## v3

And you shall speak to all who are wise in heart, whom I have filled with the spirit of wisdom, that they make Aaron’s garments to sanctify him, that he may serve Me as a priest.

## v4

And these are the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 4.1 — garments|garments]] which they shall make: a breastplate and an [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 4.2 — ephod|ephod]] and a robe and a tunic of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 4.3 — checkered|checkered]] work, a turban and a girding sash. So they shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother and for his sons that he may serve Me as a priest.

## v5

They therefore shall take the gold and the blue and the purple and the scarlet strands and the fine linen,

## v6

And they shall make the ephod of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 6.1 — gold|gold]] thread, of blue and purple and scarlet strands, and fine twined linen, the work of a skillful workman.

## v7

It shall have two [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 7.1 — shoulder|shoulder]] pieces joined to its two edges, so that it may be joined together.

## v8

And the skillfully woven band, which is on it [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 8.1 — for|for]] its fastening, shall be like it in workmanship, of one piece with it; of gold, of blue and purple and scarlet strands and fine twined linen.

## v9

And you shall take two [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 9.1 — onyx|onyx]] stones and engrave on them the names of the sons of Israel,

## v10

Six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the remaining six on the other stone, according to their birth.

## v11

With the work of an engraver in stone, like the engravings of a signet, you shall engrave the two stones, according to the names of the sons of Israel; you shall make them enclosed in [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 11.1 — settings|settings]] of gold.

## v12

And you shall put the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 12.1 — remembrance|remembrance]] for the sons of Israel. And Aaron shall bear their names before Jehovah on his two shoulders for a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 12.1 — remembrance|memorial]].

## v13

And you shall make settings of gold,

## v14

And two [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 14.1 — chains|chains]] of pure gold; you shall make them twisted, a corded work, and you shall put the chains of cords on the settings.

## v15

And you shall make a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 15.1 — breastplate|breastplate]] of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 15.2 — judgment|judgment]], the work of a skillful workman; [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 15.3 — like|like]] the work of the ephod you shall make it; of gold, of blue and purple and scarlet strands, and of fine twined linen you shall make it.

## v16

It shall be [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 16.1 — square|square]] and [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 16.1 — square|doubled]]; a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 16.2 — span|span]] its length and a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 16.2 — span|span]] its width.

## v17

And you shall enclose in it enclosures of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 17.1 — stones|stones]], [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 17.2 — four|four]] rows of stones: the first row shall be a row of a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 17.3 — sardius|sardius]], a topaz, and an emerald;

## v18

And the second row, a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 18.1 — carbuncle|carbuncle]], a sapphire, and a diamond;

## v19

And the third row, a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;

## v20

And the fourth row, a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 20.1 — chrysolite|chrysolite]], and an onyx, and a jasper; they shall be set in gold in their enclosures.

## v21

And the stones shall be according to the names of the sons of Israel, twelve, according to their names; they shall be like the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 21.1 — engravings|engravings]] of a signet, each according to its name, for the twelve tribes.

## v22

And you shall make on the breastplate twisted [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 22.1 — chains|chains]], a corded work of pure gold.

## v23

And you shall make on the breastplate two [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 23.1 — rings|rings]] of gold and shall put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.

## v24

And you shall put the two cords of gold in the two rings at the ends of the breastplate.

## v25

And you shall put the two other ends of the two cords on the two settings, and put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front of it.

## v26

And you shall make two rings of gold, and you shall put them upon the two ends of the breastplate upon its edge, which is toward the ephod on the inside.

## v27

And you shall make two rings of gold and shall put them on the bottom of the two shoulder pieces of the ephod on the front of it, close to the place where it is joined, above the skillfully woven band of the ephod.

## v28

And they shall bind the breastplate by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 28.1 — cord|cord]] of blue strands, that it may be upon the skillfully woven band of the ephod and that the breastplate may not come loose from the ephod.

## v29

So Aaron shall bear the names of the sons of Israel in the breastplate of judgment on his heart when he goes into the sanctuary, for a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 29.1 — memorial|memorial]] before Jehovah continually.

## v30

And you shall put in the breastplate of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 30.1 — judgment|judgment]] the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 30.2 — Urim|Urim]] and the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 30.3 — Thummim|Thummim]]; and they shall be on Aaron’s heart when he goes in before Jehovah, and Aaron shall bear the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 30.1 — judgment|judgment]] of the children of Israel on his heart before Jehovah continually.

## v31

And you shall make the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 31.1 — robe|robe]] of the ephod all of [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 31.2 — blue|blue]] strands.

## v32

And there shall be an opening for the head in its center; around its opening there shall be a binding edge of woven work, like the opening of a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 32.1 — coat|coat]] of mail, so that it will not be torn.

## v33

And you shall make on its hem [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 33.1 — pomegranates|pomegranates]] of blue and purple and scarlet strands, all around its hem, and [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 33.1 — pomegranates|bells]] of gold between them all around:

## v34

A golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate, on the hem of the robe all around.

## v35

And it shall be upon Aaron when he ministers, and its sound shall be heard when he goes into the sanctuary before Jehovah and when he comes out, so that he may not die.

## v36

And you shall make a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 36.1 — plate|plate]] of pure gold and engrave upon it, like the engravings of a signet: [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 36.2 — HOLINESS|HOLINESS]] TO JEHOVAH.

## v37

And you shall put it on a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 37.1 — cord|cord]] of blue strands, and it shall be on the turban; it shall be at the front of the turban.

## v38

And it shall be upon Aaron’s forehead, and Aaron shall bear the iniquity of the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 38.1 — holy|holy]] things, which the children of Israel sanctify for all their [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 38.1 — holy|holy]] gifts; and it shall always be on his forehead, that they may be accepted before Jehovah.

## v39

And you shall weave the [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 39.1 — tunic|tunic]] of fine linen, and you shall make a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 39.1 — tunic|turban]] of fine linen, and you shall make a [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 39.1 — tunic|girding]] sash, the work of an embroiderer.

## v40

And for Aaron’s sons you shall make tunics, and you shall make for them girding sashes, and you shall make for them high hats, for glory and for beauty.

## v41

And you shall put them upon Aaron your brother and upon his sons with him, and you shall anoint them and [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 41.1 — consecrate|consecrate]] them and sanctify them, that they may serve Me as priests.

## v42

And you shall make them linen trousers to cover their [[Exodus-28.fn#Footnote 42.1 — naked|naked]] flesh; they shall reach from the loins even to the thighs;

## v43

And they shall be upon Aaron and upon his sons when they go into the Tent of Meeting or when they come near to the altar to minister in the sanctuary, that they may not bear iniquity and die. It shall be a perpetual statute for him and for his seed after him.
