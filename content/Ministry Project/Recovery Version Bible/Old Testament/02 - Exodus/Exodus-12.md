## v1

Then Jehovah spoke to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,

## v2

This month will be the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — beginning|beginning]] of months for you; it shall be the first of the months of the year to you.

## v3

Speak to all the assembly of Israel, saying, On the tenth of this month each man shall take a [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — lamb|lamb]] according to his fathers’ house, a lamb for a [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — household|household]].

## v4

And if the household is too small for a lamb, then he and his [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 4.1 — neighbor|neighbor]] next to his house shall take one according to the number of the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 4.2 — persons|persons]] in the houses; according to each man’s eating you shall make your count for the lamb.

## v5

Your lamb shall be without blemish, a [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 5.1 — year-old|year-old]] male; you may take it from the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 5.2 — sheep|sheep]] or from the goats.

## v6

And you shall keep it until the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 6.1 — fourteenth|fourteenth]] day of this month; then the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 6.2 — whole|whole]] congregation of the assembly of Israel shall kill it [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 6.3 — at|at]] twilight.

## v7

And they shall take some of the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 7.1 — blood|blood]] and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel of the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 7.2 — houses|houses]] in which they eat it.

## v8

And they shall eat the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 8.1 — flesh|flesh]] in that night, roasted with fire, and they shall eat it with [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 8.2 — unleavened|unleavened]] bread with bitter herbs.

## v9

Do not eat any of it [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — raw|raw]] or [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — raw|boiled]] at all with water, but [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — raw|roasted]] with fire — its [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 9.2 — head|head]] with its [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 9.2 — head|legs]] and with its [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 9.2 — head|inward]] parts.

## v10

And you shall not let any of it [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 10.1 — remain|remain]] until the morning, but any of it that remains until the morning you shall burn with fire.

## v11

And this is how you shall eat it: [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 11.1 — with|with]] your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand; and you shall eat it in haste. It is Jehovah’s [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 11.2 — passover|passover]].

## v12

For I will pass through the land of Egypt on that night and will strike all the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 12.1 — firstborn|firstborn]] in the land of Egypt, both man and beast. Also against all the [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 12.2 — gods|gods]] of Egypt I will execute judgments. I am Jehovah.

## v13

And the blood shall be a sign for you upon the houses where you are; and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and there will be no plague upon you to destroy you when I strike the land of Egypt.

## v14

And this day will be a [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 14.1 — memorial|memorial]] to you, and you shall keep it as a [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 14.2 — feast|feast]] to Jehovah; throughout your generations as a perpetual statute you shall keep it as a feast.

## v15

[[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Seven|Seven]] days you shall eat [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 15.2 — unleavened|unleavened]] bread; on the first day you shall remove leaven out of your houses, for whoever eats anything leavened from the first day until the seventh day, that [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 15.3 — person|person]] shall be cut off from Israel.

## v16

And on the first day you shall have a holy convocation, and on the seventh day a holy convocation. [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 16.1 — No|No]] work at all shall be done on them, except to prepare what every person will eat; that alone may be done by you.

## v17

And you shall keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread, for on this very day I have brought your armies out of the land of Egypt; therefore you shall keep this day throughout your generations as a perpetual statute.

## v18

In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month in the evening, you shall eat unleavened bread, until the twenty-first day of the month in the evening.

## v19

For seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses. For whoever eats what is leavened, that person shall be cut off from the assembly of Israel, whether a sojourner or a native of the land.

## v20

You shall eat nothing leavened; in all your dwelling places you shall eat unleavened bread.

## v21

Then Moses called for all the elders of Israel and said to them, Draw out and take lambs according to your families, and slaughter the passover.

## v22

And you shall take a bunch of [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 22.1 — hyssop|hyssop]] and dip it in the blood that is in the basin, and you shall apply some of the blood that is in the basin to the lintel and to the two doorposts; and none of you shall go [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 22.2 — outside|outside]] the entrance of his house until the morning.

## v23

For Jehovah will pass through to smite the Egyptians; and when He sees the blood upon the lintel and on the two doorposts, Jehovah will pass over the entrance and will not allow the destroyer to come into your houses to smite you.

## v24

And you shall observe this matter as a statute for you and for your sons in perpetuity.

## v25

And when you come to the land which Jehovah will give you, as He has promised, you shall observe this service.

## v26

And when your children say to you, What do you mean by this service?

## v27

You shall say, It is the passover sacrifice to Jehovah, who passed over the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt when He smote the Egyptians and delivered our houses. And the people bowed and worshipped.

## v28

And the children of Israel went and did so; just as Jehovah had commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.

## v29

And at midnight Jehovah [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 29.1 — struck|struck]] all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh who sat on his throne to the firstborn of the captive who was in the dungeon, and all the firstborn of cattle.

## v30

And Pharaoh arose in the night, he and all his servants and all the Egyptians; and there was a great cry in Egypt, for there was no house where there was not someone dead.

## v31

And he called for Moses and Aaron in the night and said, Rise up, go out from the midst of my people, both you and the children of Israel; and go, serve Jehovah, as you have said.

## v32

Take both your flocks and your herds, as you have said; and go and bless me also.

## v33

And the Egyptians [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 33.1 — urged|urged]] the people on, so as to hasten their departure out of the land, for they said, All of us will be dead.

## v34

And the people took their dough before it was [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 34.1 — leavened|leavened]], their kneading bowls being bound up in their clothes upon their shoulders.

## v35

And the children of Israel did according to the word of Moses, and they asked the Egyptians for articles of silver and gold and for clothing.

## v36

And Jehovah gave the people favor in the sight of the Egyptians, so that they let them have what they asked. Thus they [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 36.1 — plundered|plundered]] the Egyptians.

## v37

And the children of Israel journeyed from Rameses to Succoth, about six hundred thousand men on foot, besides children.

## v38

And a [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 38.1 — mixed|mixed]] multitude went up also with them, as well as flocks and herds, a very large number of livestock.

## v39

And they baked the dough which they brought out of Egypt into unleavened cakes, for it had not been leavened because they were driven out of Egypt and could not delay; nor had they prepared for themselves any provisions.

## v40

Now the time of the sojourning of the children of Israel, who dwelt in [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 40.1 — Egypt|Egypt]], was [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 40.2 — four|four]] hundred thirty years.

## v41

And at the end of four hundred thirty years, on that very day, all the armies of Jehovah went out from the land of Egypt.

## v42

It was for Jehovah a night of [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 42.1 — watching|watching]], to bring them out from the land of Egypt. That same night is a night of [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 42.1 — watching|watching]] to Jehovah for all the children of Israel throughout their generations.

## v43

And Jehovah said to Moses and Aaron, This is the statute of the passover: No [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 43.1 — foreigner|foreigner]] shall eat of it.

## v44

However, any man’s servant who is bought with money, when you have circumcised him, then he shall eat of it.

## v45

A sojourner and a hired servant shall not eat of it.

## v46

It shall be eaten in one house. You shall not take any of the flesh outside the house, nor shall you break any of its [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 46.1 — bones|bones]].

## v47

All the assembly of Israel shall keep it.

## v48

But if a stranger sojourns with you and will observe the Passover to Jehovah, let all his males be circumcised, and then let him come near to observe it; and he will be like one who is a native of the land. But no uncircumcised person shall eat of it.

## v49

One law shall be for the native and for the stranger who sojourns among you.

## v50

And all the children of Israel did so; just as Jehovah commanded Moses and Aaron, so they did.

## v51

And on that very day Jehovah brought the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt by their [[Exodus-12.fn#Footnote 51.1 — armies|armies]].
