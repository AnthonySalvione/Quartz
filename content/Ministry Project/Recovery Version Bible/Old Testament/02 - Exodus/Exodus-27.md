## v1

And you shall make the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 1.1 — altar|altar]] of [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 1.2 — acacia|acacia]] wood, a length of [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 1.3 — five|five]] cubits and a width of [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 1.3 — five|five]] cubits; the altar shall be [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 1.4 — square|square]]; and [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 1.5 — three|three]] cubits, its height.

## v2

And you shall make its [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 2.1 — horns|horns]] upon its four corners; its horns shall be of one piece with it, and you shall overlay it with [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 2.2 — bronze|bronze]].

## v3

And you shall make its pots to remove its ashes, and its shovels and its basins and its forks and its firepans; all its [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 3.1 — utensils|utensils]] you shall make of bronze.

## v4

And you shall make a [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 4.1 — grating|grating]], a network of bronze for it; and on the net you shall make four bronze [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 4.2 — rings|rings]] at its four ends.

## v5

And you shall put it below, under the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 5.1 — ledge|ledge]] of the altar so that the net may reach [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 5.2 — halfway|halfway]] up the altar.

## v6

And you shall make [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 6.1 — poles|poles]] for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with bronze.

## v7

And its poles shall be put into the rings, and the poles shall be on the two sides of the altar when it is carried.

## v8

You shall make it hollow with boards; as it was shown to you in the mountain, so shall they make it.

## v9

And you shall make the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 9.1 — court|court]] of the tabernacle: On the south side southward there shall be [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 9.2 — hangings|hangings]] for the court of fine twined [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 9.3 — linen|linen]], a length of one hundred cubits for one side;

## v10

And its twenty [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 10.1 — pillars|pillars]] and their twenty sockets shall be of bronze; the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 10.2 — hooks|hooks]] of the pillars and their [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 10.2 — hooks|connecting]] rods shall be of silver.

## v11

And likewise on the north side, in length there shall be hangings a length of one hundred cubits, and its twenty pillars and their twenty sockets of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their connecting rods shall be of silver.

## v12

And for the width of the court on the west side there shall be hangings of fifty cubits, with their ten pillars and their ten sockets.

## v13

And the width of the court on the east side eastward shall be fifty cubits.

## v14

And there shall be fifteen cubits of hangings for the one side of the gate, with their three pillars and their three sockets.

## v15

And there shall be fifteen cubits of hangings for the other side, with their three pillars and their three sockets.

## v16

And for the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 16.1 — gate|gate]] of the court there shall be a screen of twenty cubits, of blue and purple and scarlet strands and fine twined linen, the work of an [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 16.2 — embroiderer|embroiderer]]; with their four pillars and their four sockets.

## v17

All the pillars around the court shall be connected with silver, with their hooks of silver and their sockets of bronze.

## v18

The length of the court shall be one hundred cubits; and the width, [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 18.1 — fifty|fifty]] at each end; and the height, [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 18.2 — five|five]] cubits; of fine twined linen, and their sockets of bronze.

## v19

All the utensils of the tabernacle used in all its service and all its [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 19.1 — pegs|pegs]] and all the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 19.1 — pegs|pegs]] of the court shall be of [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 19.2 — bronze|bronze]].

## v20

And you shall command the children of Israel to bring to you pure [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 20.1 — oil|oil]] of beaten olives for the light, to [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 20.2 — make|make]] the lamps burn continually.

## v21

In the [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Tent|Tent]] of Meeting, outside the veil which is [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 21.2 — before|before]] the Testimony, [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 21.3 — Aaron|Aaron]] and his sons shall maintain it in order from [[Exodus-27.fn#Footnote 21.4 — evening|evening]] to morning before Jehovah; it shall be a perpetual statute to be observed throughout their generations by the children of Israel.
