## v1

And you shall make an [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 1.1 — altar|altar]] on which to burn incense; you shall make it of [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 1.2 — acacia|acacia]] wood.

## v2

Its [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 2.1 — length|length]] shall be a cubit, and its width, a cubit; it shall be square, and two cubits its height; its [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 2.2 — horns|horns]] shall be of one piece with it.

## v3

And you shall overlay it with pure [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 3.1 — gold|gold]], its top and its sides around it and its horns; and you shall make a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 3.2 — rim|rim]] of gold around it.

## v4

And you shall make for it two [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 4.1 — rings|rings]] of gold under its rim; on its two sides, on its opposite sides, you shall make them; and they shall be as holders for [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 4.1 — rings|poles]] with which to carry it.

## v5

And you shall make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

## v6

And you shall put it [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 6.1 — before|before]] the veil that is over the Ark of the Testimony, before the expiation cover that is over the Testimony, where I will meet with you.

## v7

And Aaron shall burn on it fragrant [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 7.1 — incense|incense]]; every morning when he [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 7.2 — dresses|dresses]] the lamps he shall burn it.

## v8

And when Aaron sets up the lamps at twilight, he shall burn it, a perpetual incense before Jehovah throughout your generations.

## v9

You shall not offer any [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 9.1 — strange|strange]] incense on it, or a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 9.2 — burnt|burnt]] offering or a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 9.2 — burnt|meal]] offering; and you shall not pour a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 9.2 — burnt|drink]] offering on it.

## v10

And Aaron shall make [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 10.1 — expiation|expiation]] on its horns once a year; with the [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 10.2 — blood|blood]] of the sin offering of expiation once a year he shall make expiation for it throughout your generations. It is most holy to Jehovah.

## v11

And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v12

When you take the [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 12.1 — sum|sum]] of the children of Israel, according to their numbering, every man shall give a ransom for [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 12.2 — himself|himself]] to Jehovah when you number them, that there may be no plague among them when you number them.

## v13

This they shall give, each one who was enrolled among their numbering, half a shekel according to the shekel of the sanctuary (the shekel is twenty gerahs), half a shekel as a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 13.1 — heave|heave]] offering to Jehovah.

## v14

Each one who was enrolled among their numbering, from [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 14.1 — twenty|twenty]] years old and over, shall give the heave offering of Jehovah.

## v15

The rich shall not give more and the poor shall not give less than the half shekel when you give the heave offering of Jehovah to make expiation for your souls.

## v16

And you shall take the expiation silver from the children of Israel and shall give it for the [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 16.1 — service|service]] of the Tent of Meeting, that it may be a memorial for the children of Israel before Jehovah to make expiation for your souls.

## v17

And Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v18

You shall also make a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 18.1 — laver|laver]] of [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 18.2 — bronze|bronze]], with its base of bronze, for washing. And you shall put it [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 18.3 — between|between]] the Tent of Meeting and the altar, and you shall put [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 18.4 — water|water]] in it.

## v19

And Aaron and his sons shall [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 19.1 — wash|wash]] their hands and their feet with water from it;

## v20

When they go into the Tent of Meeting, they shall wash with water, that they may not [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 20.1 — die|die]]; or when they come near to the altar to minister, to burn an offering by fire to Jehovah,

## v21

They shall wash their hands and their feet, that they may not die. And it shall be a perpetual statute to them, for him and for his seed throughout their generations.

## v22

Moreover Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v23

You also take the finest spices: of flowing myrrh five hundred shekels, and of fragrant cinnamon half as much, two hundred fifty shekels, and of fragrant calamus two hundred fifty shekels,

## v24

And of cassia five hundred shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary, and a hin of olive oil.

## v25

And you shall make it a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 25.1 — holy|holy]] anointing oil, a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 25.2 — fragrant|fragrant]] ointment compounded according to the work of a compounder; it shall be a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 25.1 — holy|holy]] anointing oil.

## v26

And with it you shall [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 26.1 — anoint|anoint]] the Tent of Meeting and the Ark of the Testimony,

## v27

And the table and all its utensils, and the lampstand and its utensils, and the altar of incense,

## v28

And the altar of burnt offering with all its utensils, and the laver and its base.

## v29

Thus you shall sanctify them that they may be most holy; whatever touches them shall be holy.

## v30

And you shall anoint [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 30.1 — Aaron|Aaron]] and his sons and sanctify them that they may serve Me as priests.

## v31

And you shall speak to the children of Israel, saying, This shall be a holy anointing oil to Me throughout your generations.

## v32

Upon the [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 32.1 — flesh|flesh]] of man it shall not be poured, nor shall you make any [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 32.2 — like|like]] it, according to its composition; it is holy, and it shall be holy to you.

## v33

Whoever compounds any like it or whoever puts any of it upon a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 33.1 — stranger|stranger]], he shall be cut off from his people.

## v34

And Jehovah said to Moses, Take fragrant [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 34.1 — spices|spices]] — stacte and onycha and galbanum — fragrant spices with pure [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 34.1 — spices|frankincense]]; there shall be an [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 34.2 — equal|equal]] part of each;

## v35

And you shall make of it [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 35.1 — incense|incense]], a [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 35.2 — fragrant|fragrant]] compound according to the work of a compounder, seasoned with [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 35.3 — salt|salt]], pure and holy.

## v36

And you shall [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 36.1 — beat|beat]] some of it very fine, and put some of it [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 36.2 — before|before]] the Testimony in the Tent of Meeting, where I will meet with you; it shall be to you most holy.

## v37

And the incense which you shall make, you shall not make for [[Exodus-30.fn#Footnote 37.1 — yourselves|yourselves]] according to its composition; it shall be holy to you for Jehovah.

## v38

Whoever shall make any like it, to smell it, shall be cut off from his people.
