## v1

[[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Then|Then]] Jehovah spoke to Moses, saying,

## v2

Tell the children of Israel to take for Me a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 2.1 — heave|heave]] offering. From every man whose heart makes him willing you shall take My heave offering.

## v3

And this is the heave offering which you shall take from them: [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 3.1 — gold|gold]] and silver and bronze,

## v4

And [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 4.1 — blue|blue]] and purple and scarlet strands, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 4.2 — fine|fine]] linen, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 4.3 — goats’|goats’]] hair,

## v5

And [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 5.1 — rams’|rams’]] skins dyed red, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 5.2 — porpoise|porpoise]] skins, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 5.3 — acacia|acacia]] wood,

## v6

[[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Oil|Oil]] for the light, [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 6.2 — spices|spices]] for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense,

## v7

Onyx stones and stones to be set for the ephod and for the breastplate.

## v8

And let them make a sanctuary for Me that I may dwell in their midst;

## v9

According to all that I show you, the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 9.1 — pattern|pattern]] of the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 9.2 — tabernacle|tabernacle]] and the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 9.1 — pattern|pattern]] of all its furnishings, even so shall you make it.

## v10

And they shall make an [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 10.1 — ark|ark]] of [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 10.2 — acacia|acacia]] wood: [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 10.3 — two|two]] and a half cubits shall be its length; and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 10.3 — two|one]] and a half cubits, its width; and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 10.3 — two|one]] and a half cubits, its height.

## v11

And you shall overlay it with pure [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 11.1 — gold|gold]]; inside and outside you shall overlay it; and you shall make a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 11.2 — rim|rim]] of gold upon it all around.

## v12

And you shall cast four [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 12.1 — rings|rings]] of gold for it, and put them on its four feet; and two [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 12.2 — rings|rings]] shall be on one side of it, and two rings on the other side of it.

## v13

And you shall make [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 13.1 — poles|poles]] of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

## v14

And you shall [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 14.1 — put|put]] the poles into the rings on the sides of the Ark to carry the Ark with them.

## v15

The poles shall be in the rings of the Ark; they shall not be taken from it.

## v16

And you shall [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 16.1 — put|put]] into the Ark the Testimony which I will give you.

## v17

And you shall make an [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 17.1 — expiation|expiation]] cover of pure gold: [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 17.2 — two|two]] and a half cubits shall be its length, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 17.2 — two|one]] and a half cubits, its width.

## v18

And you shall make two [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 18.1 — cherubim|cherubim]] of gold; of beaten work you shall make them, at the two ends of the expiation cover.

## v19

And make one cherub at one end and one cherub at the other end; of [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 19.1 — one|one]] piece with the expiation cover you shall make the cherubim on its two ends.

## v20

And the cherubim shall spread out their [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 20.1 — wings|wings]] above the cover, covering the expiation cover with their wings, with their [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 20.1 — wings|faces]] toward one another; the faces of the cherubim shall be toward the expiation cover.

## v21

And you shall [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 21.1 — put|put]] the expiation cover upon the Ark above it, and into the Ark you shall [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 21.2 — put|put]] the Testimony that I will give you.

## v22

And there I will [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 22.1 — meet|meet]] with you, and I will speak with you from above the expiation cover, from between the two cherubim which are upon the Ark of the Testimony, of everything which I will give you in commandment to the children of Israel.

## v23

And you shall make a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 23.1 — table|table]] of [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 23.2 — acacia|acacia]] wood: [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 23.3 — two|two]] cubits shall be its length, and a cubit its width, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 23.4 — one|one]] and a half cubits its height.

## v24

And you shall overlay it with pure gold and make a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 24.1 — rim|rim]] of gold around it.

## v25

And you shall make a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 25.1 — frame|frame]] of a handbreadth around it; and you shall make a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 25.1 — frame|rim]] of gold for its frame around it.

## v26

And you shall make for it four [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 26.1 — rings|rings]] of gold and put the rings on the four corners that are on its four feet.

## v27

The rings shall be close to the frame as holders for the poles to carry the table.

## v28

And you shall make the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 28.1 — poles|poles]] of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, and the table shall be carried with them.

## v29

And you shall make its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 29.1 — plates|plates]] and its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 29.1 — plates|cups]] and its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 29.1 — plates|pitchers]] and its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 29.1 — plates|bowls]] with which to pour out drink offerings; of pure gold you shall make them.

## v30

And you shall set the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 30.1 — bread|bread]] of the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 30.2 — Presence|Presence]] upon the table before Me always.

## v31

And you shall make a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.1 — lampstand|lampstand]] of [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.2 — pure|pure]] gold. The lampstand with its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.3 — base|base]] and its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.3 — base|shaft]] shall be made of [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.4 — beaten|beaten]] work; its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.5 — cups|cups]], its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.5 — cups|calyxes]], and its [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 31.5 — cups|blossom]] buds shall be of one piece with it.

## v32

And there shall be [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 32.1 — six|six]] branches going out of its sides; [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 32.1 — six|three]] branches of the lampstand out of one of its sides, and [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 32.1 — six|three]] branches of the lampstand out of its other side;

## v33

[[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 33.1 — Three|Three]] cups made like almond blossoms in one branch, a calyx and a blossom bud; and three cups made like almond blossoms in the other branch, a calyx and a blossom bud — so for the six branches going out of the lampstand.

## v34

And there shall be on the lampstand [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 34.1 — four|four]] cups made like almond blossoms, its calyxes and its blossom buds;

## v35

And a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 35.1 — calyx|calyx]] under two branches of one piece with it, and a calyx under two branches of one piece with it, and a calyx under two branches of one piece with it, for the [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 35.2 — six|six]] branches going out of the lampstand.

## v36

Their calyxes and their branches shall be of one piece with it; all of it one beaten work of pure gold.

## v37

And you shall make its lamps, [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 37.1 — seven|seven]]; and set up its lamps to give [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 37.2 — light|light]] to the area in front of it.

## v38

And its tongs and its firepans shall be of pure gold.

## v39

It shall be made of a [[Exodus-25.fn#Footnote 39.1 — talent|talent]] of pure gold, with all these utensils.

## v40

And see that you make them according to their pattern, which was shown to you in the mountain.
