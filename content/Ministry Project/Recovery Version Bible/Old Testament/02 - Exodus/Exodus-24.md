## v1

Then He said to Moses, [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Come|Come]] up to Jehovah, you and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel; and worship at a distance.

## v2

And Moses alone shall come near to Jehovah, but they shall not come near, nor shall the people come up with him.

## v3

And Moses came and told the people all the words of Jehovah and all the ordinances; and all the people answered with one voice and said, [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 3.1 — All|All]] the words which Jehovah has spoken we will do.

## v4

And Moses wrote down all the words of Jehovah. And he rose up early in the morning and built an [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 4.1 — altar|altar]] at the base of the mountain and twelve [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 4.2 — pillars|pillars]] for the twelve tribes of Israel.

## v5

And he sent young men of the children of Israel, who offered [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 5.1 — burnt|burnt]] offerings and sacrificed [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 5.1 — burnt|peace]] offerings of bulls to Jehovah.

## v6

And Moses took half of the blood and put it in basins, and half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.

## v7

And he took the book of the covenant and read it in the hearing of the people; and they said, [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 7.1 — All|All]] that Jehovah has spoken we will do, and we will be obedient.

## v8

So Moses took the [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 8.2 — blood|blood]] and sprinkled it on the [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 8.1 — people|people]] and said, Here is the blood of the covenant, which Jehovah has made with you in accordance with all these words.

## v9

Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up.

## v10

And they [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 10.1 — saw|saw]] the God of Israel, and under His feet there was something like a [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 10.2 — paved|paved]] work of sapphire, even like heaven itself for clearness.

## v11

And He did not stretch out His hand upon the nobles of the children of Israel. And they [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 11.1 — beheld|beheld]] God and ate and drank.

## v12

And Jehovah said to Moses, Come up to Me at the top of the mountain, and be there; and I will give you the tablets of stone with the law and the commandment, which I have inscribed for their instruction.

## v13

And [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Moses|Moses]] rose up with Joshua his attendant, and Moses went up to the top of the mountain of God.

## v14

And he had said to the elders, Wait here for us until we return to you. Both Aaron and Hur are here with you; whoever has a cause, let him come near to them.

## v15

And Moses went up to the top of the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

## v16

And the glory of Jehovah settled on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days; and on the seventh day He called to Moses out of the midst of the cloud.

## v17

And the appearance of the glory of Jehovah was like consuming fire on the top of the mountain to the eyes of the children of Israel.

## v18

And Moses entered into the midst of the cloud and went up on the mountain; and Moses was on the mountain [[Exodus-24.fn#Footnote 18.1 — forty|forty]] days and forty nights.
