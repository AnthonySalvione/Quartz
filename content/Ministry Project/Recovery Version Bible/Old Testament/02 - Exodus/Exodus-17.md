## v1

And all the assembly of the children of Israel journeyed from the wilderness of Sin by their [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 1.1 — stages|stages]] according to the command of Jehovah and encamped in Rephidim, and there was [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 1.2 — no|no]] water for the people to drink.

## v2

Therefore the people [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 2.1 — contended|contended]] with Moses and said, Give us water that we may drink. And Moses said to them, Why are you contending with me? Why do you test Jehovah?

## v3

So the people thirsted there for water, and the people murmured against Moses and said, For what reason did you bring us up out of Egypt; to kill us and our children and our livestock with thirst?

## v4

So Moses cried out to Jehovah, saying, What shall I do with this people? [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 4.1 — A|A]] little more, and they will stone me.

## v5

And Jehovah said to Moses, Pass on before the people, and take with you some of the elders of Israel; and take in your hand your staff with which you struck the River, and go.

## v6

I will be standing before you there upon the [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 6.1 — rock|rock]] in Horeb; and you shall [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 6.2 — strike|strike]] the rock, and [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 6.3 — water|water]] will come out of it so that the people may drink. And Moses did so in the sight of the elders of Israel.

## v7

And he called the name of the place [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Massah|Massah]] and [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Meribah|Meribah]], because of the contention of the children of Israel and because they tested Jehovah, saying, Is Jehovah among us or not?

## v8

Then [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Amalek|Amalek]] came and [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 8.2 — fought|fought]] with Israel in Rephidim.

## v9

And Moses said to [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Joshua|Joshua]], Choose men for us, and go out; fight with Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill with the staff of God in my hand.

## v10

So Joshua did as Moses had said to him and fought with Amalek; and Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill.

## v11

And when Moses [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 11.1 — lifted|lifted]] his hand up, Israel prevailed; and when he let his hand down, Amalek prevailed.

## v12

But Moses’ hands were [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 12.1 — heavy|heavy]], so they took a stone and put it under him, and he sat on it; and Aaron and Hur supported his hands, one on one side and one on the other side. So his hands were steady until the going down of the sun.

## v13

And Joshua defeated Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.

## v14

And Jehovah said to Moses, Write this as a memorial in a book and [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 14.1 — recite|recite]] it to Joshua, that I will utterly [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 14.2 — blot|blot]] out the memory of Amalek from under heaven.

## v15

And Moses built an altar and called the name of it [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Jehovah-nissi|Jehovah-nissi]];

## v16

For he said, For there is a [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 16.1 — hand|hand]] against the throne of [[Exodus-17.fn#Footnote 16.2 — Jah|Jah]]! Jehovah will have war with Amalek from generation to generation.
