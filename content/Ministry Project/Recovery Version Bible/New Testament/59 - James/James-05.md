---
## v1

Come now, you [[James-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — rich|rich]], weep, howling over your miseries, which are coming upon you!

## v2

Your riches have rotted and your garments have become moth-eaten;

## v3

Your gold and your silver have rusted, and their rust will be a testimony against you and will eat your flesh like fire. You have stored up treasure in the last days.

## v4

Behold, the wages of the workmen who mowed your fields, which have been withheld by you, cry out; and the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — cries|cries]] of those who reaped have entered into the ears of the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Lord|Lord]] of hosts.

## v5

You have lived luxuriously on the earth and have given yourselves to pleasure; you have [[James-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — nourished|nourished]] your hearts in a day of slaughter.

## v6

You condemned, you murdered the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — righteous|righteous]]; he does not resist you.

## v7

Therefore be [[James-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — long-suffering|long-suffering]], brothers, until the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 7.2 — coming|coming]] of the Lord. Behold, the farmer eagerly awaits the precious fruit of the earth, exercising long-suffering over it until it receives the early and late rain.

## v8

You also be [[James-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — long-suffering|long-suffering]]; establish your hearts because the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 8.2 — coming|coming]] of the Lord has drawn near.

## v9

Do not [[James-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — complain|complain]], brothers, against one another lest you be judged. Behold, the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Judge|Judge]] stands before the doors.

## v10

As an example, brothers, of [[James-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — suffering|suffering]] evil and of [[James-05.fn#Footnote 10.2 — long-suffering|long-suffering]], take the prophets, who spoke [[James-05.fn#Footnote 10.3 — in|in]] the name of the Lord.

## v11

Behold, we call those who [[James-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — endured|endured]] blessed. You have heard of the endurance of Job, and you have seen his end from the Lord, that the Lord is very tenderhearted and compassionate.

## v12

But [[James-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — above|above]] all things, my brothers, do [[James-05.fn#Footnote 12.2 — not|not]] swear, neither by heaven nor by earth nor with any other oath; but let your [[James-05.fn#Footnote 12.3 — yes|yes]] be yes, and your no, no, [[James-05.fn#Footnote 12.4 — lest|lest]] you fall under judgment.

## v13

Does anyone among you suffer evil? Let him [[James-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — pray|pray]]. Is anyone cheerful? Let him [[James-05.fn#Footnote 13.2 — sing|sing]] praise.

## v14

Is anyone among you [[James-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — ill|ill]]? Let him [[James-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — call|call]] for the elders of the church, and let them pray over him, [[James-05.fn#Footnote 14.3 — anointing|anointing]] him with oil [[James-05.fn#Footnote 14.4 — in|in]] the name of the Lord.

## v15

And the [[James-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — prayer|prayer]] of faith will save the one who is [[James-05.fn#Footnote 15.2 — sick|sick]], and the Lord will raise him up; and if he has [[James-05.fn#Footnote 15.3 — committed|committed]] sins, it will be forgiven him.

## v16

Therefore confess your sins to one another and pray for one another that you may be healed. The petition of a righteous man [[James-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — avails|avails]] much in its working.

## v17

Elijah was a man of like feeling with us, and he [[James-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — earnestly|earnestly]] prayed that it would not rain; and it did not rain on the earth for three years and six months.

## v18

And he prayed again, and heaven gave rain, and the earth sprouted forth with its fruit.

## v19

My brothers, if any one among you is [[James-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — led|led]] astray from the truth and someone turns him back,

## v20

Let him know that he who turns a [[James-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — sinner|sinner]] back from the error of his way will [[James-05.fn#Footnote 20.2 — save|save]] that one’s soul from [[James-05.fn#Footnote 20.3 — death|death]] and will [[James-05.fn#Footnote 20.4 — cover|cover]] a multitude of [[James-05.fn#Footnote 20.5 — sins|sins]].
