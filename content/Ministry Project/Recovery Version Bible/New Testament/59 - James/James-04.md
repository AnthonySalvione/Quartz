---
Author: James
---
## v1

Where do wars and fightings among you come from? Are they not from this, from your [[James-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — pleasures|pleasures]] that war in your members?

## v2

You [[James-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — lust|lust]] and do not have; you murder and are jealous and are not able to obtain; you fight and make war. You do not have because you do not ask;

## v3

You ask and do not receive because you ask evilly that you may spend it on your pleasures.

## v4

[[James-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Adulteresses|Adulteresses]], do you not know that the [[James-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — friendship|friendship]] of the [[James-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — world|world]] is enmity with God? Therefore whoever determines to be a friend of the world is constituted an [[James-04.fn#Footnote 4.4 — enemy|enemy]] of God.

## v5

Or do you think that the Scripture says in vain: The [[James-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Spirit|Spirit]], whom He has caused to [[James-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — dwell|dwell]] in us, longs unto envy?

## v6

But He gives greater grace; therefore [[James-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — it|it]] says, “[[James-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — God|God]] resists the [[James-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — proud|proud]] but gives grace to the humble.”

## v7

[[James-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Be|Be]] subject therefore to God; but [[James-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — withstand|withstand]] the [[James-04.fn#Footnote 7.3 — devil|devil]], and he will flee from you.

## v8

Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, sinners, and purify your hearts, you [[James-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — double-souled|double-souled]]!

## v9

[[James-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Endure|Endure]] misery and mourn and weep. Let your laughter be turned into mourning, and your joy into dejection.

## v10

[[James-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Be|Be]] humbled before the Lord, and He will exalt you.

## v11

Do not speak against one another, brothers. He who speaks against a brother or judges his brother speaks against the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a [[James-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — doer|doer]] of the law but a judge.

## v12

One is Lawgiver and Judge, who is able to save and destroy. But who are you who judge your neighbor?

## v13

Come now, you who say, Today or tomorrow we [[James-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — will|will]] go into this or that city and spend a year there and do business and make a profit;

## v14

Whereas you do not know the matter of tomorrow, what your life will be; for you are a [[James-04.fn#Footnote 14.1 — vapor|vapor]], which appears for a little while and then disappears.

## v15

Instead you ought to say, If the Lord wills, we will both live and do this or that.

## v16

But now you boast in your [[James-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — arrogance|arrogance]]; all such boasting is evil.

## v17

[[James-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] to him who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.
