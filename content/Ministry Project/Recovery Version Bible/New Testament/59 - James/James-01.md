---
## v1

[[James-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — James|James]], a slave of God and of the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Lord|Lord]] Jesus Christ, to the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — twelve|twelve]] tribes in the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — dispersion|dispersion]]: [[James-01.fn#Footnote 1.5 — Rejoice|Rejoice]]!

## v2

Count it all joy, my brothers, whenever you fall into various [[James-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — trials|trials]],

## v3

Knowing that the proving of your [[James-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — faith|faith]] works out endurance.

## v4

And let endurance have its perfect work that you may be [[James-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — perfect|perfect]] and entire, lacking in nothing.

## v5

But if any one of you lacks [[James-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — wisdom|wisdom]], let him [[James-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — ask|ask]] of God, who gives to all [[James-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — liberally|liberally]] and does [[James-01.fn#Footnote 5.4 — not|not]] reproach, and it will be given to him.

## v6

But let him ask in faith, [[James-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — doubting|doubting]] nothing, for he who [[James-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — doubting|doubts]] is like the surge of the sea, driven by the wind and tossed about.

## v7

For that man must not suppose that he will receive anything from the Lord;

## v8

He is a [[James-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — double-souled|double-souled]] man, unstable in all his ways.

## v9

And let the lowly brother [[James-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — boast|boast]] in his [[James-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — exaltation|exaltation]],

## v10

And the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — rich|rich]] in his being brought low, because like a flower of the grass he will pass away.

## v11

For the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — sun|sun]] rises with its [[James-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — scorching|scorching]] heat and withers the grass, and its flower falls off, and the beauty of its appearance is destroyed; so also the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — rich|rich]] man will fade away in his pursuits.

## v12

Blessed is the man who endures [[James-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — trial|trial]], because when he has become [[James-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — approved|approved]] by testing, he will receive the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — crown|crown]] of [[James-01.fn#Footnote 12.4 — life|life]], which He promised to those who [[James-01.fn#Footnote 12.5 — love|love]] Him.

## v13

Let no one say when he is [[James-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — tempted|tempted]], I am tempted [[James-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — of|of]] God; for God [[James-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — cannot|cannot]] be tempted of evil, and He Himself [[James-01.fn#Footnote 13.4 — tempts|tempts]] no one.

## v14

But each one is tempted when he is drawn away and enticed by his own lusts;

## v15

Then the lust, having conceived, [[James-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — gives|gives]] birth to sin; and the sin, when it is fully grown, brings forth death.

## v16

Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers.

## v17

All good [[James-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — giving|giving]] and every perfect [[James-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — giving|gift]] is from above, coming down from the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — Father|Father]] of lights, with whom is no [[James-01.fn#Footnote 17.3 — variation|variation]] or shadow cast by turning.

## v18

He [[James-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — brought|brought]] us forth by the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — word|word]] of truth, [[James-01.fn#Footnote 18.3 — purposing|purposing]] that we might be a kind of [[James-01.fn#Footnote 18.4 — firstfruits|firstfruits]] of His creatures.

## v19

[[James-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — You|You]] know this, my beloved brothers; but let every man be quick to hear, [[James-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — slow|slow]] to speak, slow to wrath;

## v20

For the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — wrath|wrath]] of man does not accomplish the righteousness of God.

## v21

Therefore putting away all filthiness and the abundance of malice, receive in meekness the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — implanted|implanted]] word, which is able to [[James-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — save|save]] your souls.

## v22

And become doers of the word and not hearers only, who delude themselves.

## v23

Because if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, this one is like a man considering in a mirror the face he was born with;

## v24

For he considers himself and goes away, and immediately forgets what kind of person he is.

## v25

But he who [[James-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — looks|looks]] into the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 25.2 — perfect|perfect]] law, the law of freedom, and continues in it, becoming not a forgetful hearer but a doer of the work, this one will be blessed in his doing.

## v26

If anyone thinks himself to be [[James-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — religious|religious]] and yet does [[James-01.fn#Footnote 26.2 — not|not]] bridle his tongue but deceives his heart, this one’s [[James-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — religious|religion]] is vain.

## v27

This is pure and undefiled religion before our God and Father: to visit [[James-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — orphans|orphans]] and widows in their affliction and to keep oneself [[James-01.fn#Footnote 27.2 — unspotted|unspotted]] from the [[James-01.fn#Footnote 27.3 — world|world]].
