---
Author: John
---
## v1

These things Jesus spoke, and lifting up His eyes to heaven, He said, Father, the hour has come; [[John-17.fn#Footnote 1.1 — glorify|glorify]] Your Son that the Son may glorify You;

## v2

Even as [[John-17.fn#Footnote 2.1 — You|You]] have given Him authority over all flesh to give [[John-17.fn#Footnote 2.2 — eternal|eternal]] life to all whom You have given Him.

## v3

And this is [[John-17.fn#Footnote 3.1 — eternal|eternal]] life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Him whom You have [[John-17.fn#Footnote 3.2 — sent|sent]], Jesus Christ.

## v4

I have [[John-17.fn#Footnote 4.1 — glorified|glorified]] You on earth, finishing the work which You have given Me to do.

## v5

And now, [[John-17.fn#Footnote 5.1 — glorify|glorify]] Me along with Yourself, Father, with the glory which I had with You before the world was.

## v6

I have manifested [[John-17.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Your|Your]] name to the men whom You gave Me out of the world. They were Yours, and You gave them to Me, and they have kept Your [[John-17.fn#Footnote 6.2 — word|word]].

## v7

Now they have come to know that all that You have given Me is from You,

## v8

For the words which You gave Me I have given to them, and they received them and knew truly that I came forth [[John-17.fn#Footnote 8.1 — from|from]] You, and they have believed that You sent Me.

## v9

I ask concerning them; I do not ask concerning the world, but concerning those whom You have given Me, for they are Yours;

## v10

And all that is Mine is Yours, and Yours Mine; and I have been [[John-17.fn#Footnote 10.1 — glorified|glorified]] [[John-17.fn#Footnote 10.2 — in|in]] them.

## v11

And I am no longer in the world; yet they are in the world, and I am coming to You. [[John-17.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Holy|Holy]] Father, keep them [[John-17.fn#Footnote 11.2 — in|in]] Your name, which You have given to Me, that they may be one even as We are.

## v12

When I was with them, I kept them in Your name, which You have given to Me, and I guarded them; and not one of them [[John-17.fn#Footnote 12.1 — perished|perished]], except the son of perdition, that the Scripture might be fulfilled.

## v13

But now I am coming to You, and these things I speak in the world that they may have My joy made full in themselves.

## v14

I have given them Your [[John-17.fn#Footnote 14.1 — word|word]], and the [[John-17.fn#Footnote 14.2 — world|world]] has hated them, because they are not [[John-17.fn#Footnote 14.3 — of|of]] the [[John-17.fn#Footnote 14.2 — world|world]] even as I am not [[John-17.fn#Footnote 14.3 — of|of]] the [[John-17.fn#Footnote 14.2 — world|world]].

## v15

I do not ask that You would take them out of the world, but that You would keep them [[John-17.fn#Footnote 15.1 — out|out]] of the hands of the [[John-17.fn#Footnote 15.2 — evil|evil]] one.

## v16

They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.

## v17

[[John-17.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Sanctify|Sanctify]] them [[John-17.fn#Footnote 17.2 — in|in]] the truth; Your [[John-17.fn#Footnote 17.3 — word|word]] is truth.

## v18

As You have [[John-17.fn#Footnote 18.1 — sent|sent]] Me into the world, I also have [[John-17.fn#Footnote 18.1 — sent|sent]] them into the world.

## v19

And for their sake I [[John-17.fn#Footnote 19.1 — sanctify|sanctify]] Myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth.

## v20

And I do not ask concerning these only, but concerning those also who believe into Me through their word,

## v21

That they all may be [[John-17.fn#Footnote 21.1 — one|one]]; even as You, Father, are in Me and I in You, that they also may be in Us; that the world may believe that You have sent Me.

## v22

And the [[John-17.fn#Footnote 22.1 — glory|glory]] which You have given Me I have given to them, that they may be [[John-17.fn#Footnote 22.2 — one|one]], even as We are one;

## v23

I in them, and You in Me, that they may be perfected into one, that the world may know that You have sent Me and have [[John-17.fn#Footnote 23.1 — loved|loved]] them even as You have loved Me.

## v24

Father, concerning that which You have given Me, I desire that they also may [[John-17.fn#Footnote 24.1 — be|be]] with Me where I am, that they may behold My glory, which You have given Me, for You loved Me before the foundation of the world.

## v25

[[John-17.fn#Footnote 25.1 — Righteous|Righteous]] Father, though the world has not known You, yet I have known You, and these have known that You have sent Me.

## v26

And I have made Your name known to them and will yet make it known, that the [[John-17.fn#Footnote 26.1 — love|love]] with which You have loved Me may be in them, and I in them.
