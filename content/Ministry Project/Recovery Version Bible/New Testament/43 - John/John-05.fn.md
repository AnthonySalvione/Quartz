# John-05 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[John-05|John 5]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — Sheep

**Verse:** [[John-05#v2|John 5:2]]

The Sheep Gate signifies the door to the sheepfold of the religion of law keeping ([[John-10#v1|10:1]]).

### Footnote 2.2 — Bethesda

**Verse:** [[John-05#v2|John 5:2]]

Bethesda means house of mercy, signifying that the people who practiced law keeping needed the mercy of God because they were impotent, weak, and wretched, as portrayed in [[Romans-07#v7|Rom. 7:7-24]].

### Footnote 2.3 — five

**Verse:** [[John-05#v2|John 5:2]]

Porticoes signify the shelter of the religion of law keeping, a shelter like that provided by a sheepfold. The number five signifies responsibility.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — In

**Verse:** [[John-05#v3|John 5:3]]

This signifies that under the shelter of law keeping, in the sheepfold of religion, there are many who are blind, unable to see; many who are lame, unable to walk; and many who are withered, lacking the life supply.

### Footnote 3.2 — waiting

**Verse:** [[John-05#v3|John 5:3]]

Some MSS omit this last part of [[John-05#v3|v. 3]] and all of [[John-05#v4|v. 4]].

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — angel

**Verse:** [[John-05#v4|John 5:4]]

The angel here signifies the agent through which the law, which could not give life, was given ([[Galatians-03#v19|Gal. 3:19]], [[Galatians-03#v21|21]]).

### Footnote 4.2 — stirring

**Verse:** [[John-05#v4|John 5:4]]

The stirring up of the water to make people well signifies the attempt to make people perfect by the practice of law keeping.

### Footnote 4.3 — well

**Verse:** [[John-05#v4|John 5:4]]

Or, whole, sound. So in [[John-05#v6|v. 6]].

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — man

**Verse:** [[John-05#v5|John 5:5]]

With this sick and impotent man there was no happiness, even on the joyful day of a feast ([[John-05#v1|v. 1]]), and there was no rest, even on the Sabbath day ([[John-05#v10|v. 10]]).

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — He

**Verse:** [[John-05#v6|John 5:6]]

This sign signifies that when the practice of law keeping in the Jewish religion became an impossibility because of the impotence of man ([[Romans-08#v3|Rom. 8:3]]), the Son of God came to enliven the dead ([[John-05#v25|v. 25]]). The law could not give life ([[Galatians-03#v21|Gal. 3:21]]), but the Son of God gives life to the dead ([[John-05#v21|v. 21]]). “While we were yet weak” ([[Romans-05#v6|Rom. 5:6]]), He came to enliven us.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — Sir

**Verse:** [[John-05#v7|John 5:7]]

Or, Lord.

### Footnote 7.2 — I

**Verse:** [[John-05#v7|John 5:7]]

There was a means for healing in the religion of law keeping, but it did not profit the impotent man, because he had no strength to fulfill the law’s requirements. The law keeping in religion depends on man’s effort, man’s doing, man’s self-cultivation. Since man is impotent, the law keeping in religion becomes ineffective. The holy city, the holy temple, the feast, the Sabbath, the angels, Moses, and the Scriptures are all good things of this religion, but they could do nothing for this impotent man. In the eyes of the Lord he was a dead person ([[John-05#v25|v. 25]]), in need not only of healing but also of enlivening. With the Lord’s enlivening there is no requirement. The impotent man heard His voice and was enlivened ([[John-05#v25|v. 25]]).

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — mat

**Verse:** [[John-05#v8|John 5:8]]

A small mattress or pad; so in [[John-05#v9|vv. 9]], [[John-05#v10|10]], [[John-05#v11|11]], and 12.

## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — became

**Verse:** [[John-05#v9|John 5:9]]

According to [[John-05#v24|vv. 24-25]], this is to pass out of death into life and to live. In the principle set forth in [[John-02|John 2]], this is the changing of death into life.

### Footnote 9.2 — took

**Verse:** [[John-05#v9|John 5:9]]

Formerly, the mat carried the impotent man, but now the enlivened man carried the mat.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — it

**Verse:** [[John-05#v10|John 5:10]]

Life’s enlivening broke religion’s ritual. Religion was offended by life and began its opposition to life from this point ([[John-05#v16|vv. 16]], [[John-05#v18|18]]). 

The Sabbath is for man ([[Mark-02#v27|Mark 2:27]]) and should be a rest to man. Religion’s law keeping did not bring rest to this man who had been sick for thirty-eight years, but life’s enlivening did. Yet the religious cared only for their ritual of Sabbath keeping; they had no concern for the sick man’s rest.

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — sin

**Verse:** [[John-05#v14|John 5:14]]

This indicates that the man’s former sickness was due to his sin.

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — and

**Verse:** [[John-05#v16|John 5:16]]

Some MSS omit, and sought to kill Him.

## Footnotes for verse 17

### Footnote 17.1 — working

**Verse:** [[John-05#v17|John 5:17]]

God’s work in creation was finished ([[Genesis-02#v1|Gen. 2:1-3]]), but the Father and the Son were still working for redemption and building.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — sought

**Verse:** [[John-05#v18|John 5:18]]

On one hand, the religious people kept the Sabbath, but on the other hand, they sought to kill Jesus. How could they have had rest? Because of their religious concept, they thought that to kill those who would not keep their religious ritual was to offer service to God ([[John-16#v2|16:2]]). This is Satan’s poisoning of people with religion, causing them to murder, just as he poisons people with sin.

### Footnote 18.2 — equal

**Verse:** [[John-05#v18|John 5:18]]

Actually, the Son and the Father are one ([[John-10#v30|10:30]]).

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — eternal

**Verse:** [[John-05#v24|John 5:24]]

See [note 15¹](<John-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — eternal>) in [[John-03|John 3]].

### Footnote 24.2 — judgment

**Verse:** [[John-05#v24|John 5:24]]

Or, condemnation.

### Footnote 24.3 — death

**Verse:** [[John-05#v24|John 5:24]]

The source of death is the tree of knowledge, and the source of life is the tree of life (cf. [[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]], [[Genesis-02#v17|17]]). Hence, to pass out of death into life is to change the source of one’s living.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — dead

**Verse:** [[John-05#v25|John 5:25]]

Not those who are dead physically but those who are dead in spirit, in accordance with [[Ephesians-02#v1|Eph 2:1]], 5 and [[Colossians-02#v13|Col 2:13]]. Hence, in this verse to live means to be alive in spirit. It does not signify resurrection of the physical body, which is mentioned in [[John-05#v28|vv. 28-29]].

## Footnotes for verse 27

### Footnote 27.1 — Son

**Verse:** [[John-05#v27|John 5:27]]

The Lord is the Son of God ([[John-05#v25|v. 25]]); hence, He can give life ([[John-05#v21|v. 21]]). He is also the Son of Man; hence, He can execute judgment.

## Footnotes for verse 28

### Footnote 28.1 — all

**Verse:** [[John-05#v28|John 5:28]]

Referring to those who are dead physically and are buried in a tomb. Hence, their coming forth from the tomb in [[John-05#v29|v. 29]] is the resurrection of the physical body.

## Footnotes for verse 29

### Footnote 29.1 — resurrection

**Verse:** [[John-05#v29|John 5:29]]

This is the resurrection of the saved believers, which will take place before the millennium ([[Revelation-20#v4|Rev. 20:4]], [[Revelation-20#v6|6]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v23|1 Cor. 15:23]], [[1 Corinthians-15#v52|52]]; [[1 Thessalonians-04#v16|1 Thes. 4:16]]). At the Lord Jesus’ coming back, the dead believers will be resurrected to enjoy eternal life. Hence, this resurrection is called the resurrection of life.

### Footnote 29.2 — practiced

**Verse:** [[John-05#v29|John 5:29]]

See [note 20¹](<John-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — practices>) in [[John-03|John 3]].

### Footnote 29.3 — resurrection

**Verse:** [[John-05#v29|John 5:29]]

This is the resurrection of the unbelievers who have perished; it will take place after the millennium ([[Revelation-20#v5|Rev. 20:5]], [[Revelation-20#v12|12]]). All the dead unbelievers will be resurrected after the thousand years to be judged at the great white throne ([[Revelation-20#v11|Rev. 20:11-15]]). Hence, this resurrection is called the resurrection of judgment. 

In this chapter [[John-05#v24|vv. 24-26]] speak concerning the enlivening of the spirit; [[John-05#v28|vv. 28-29]], concerning the resurrection of the whole being, including the body.

### Footnote 29.4 — judgment

**Verse:** [[John-05#v29|John 5:29]]

Or, condemnation.

## Footnotes for verse 33

### Footnote 33.1 — truth

**Verse:** [[John-05#v33|John 5:33]]

The same Greek word as for reality in [[John-01#v14|1:14]], [[John-01#v17|17]]. The preceding verse says that John testified concerning Christ; this verse says that John testified to the truth. This proves that truth here is Christ ([[John-14#v6|14:6]]). According to the entire revelation of this Gospel, truth is the divine reality embodied, revealed, and expressed in Christ, the Son of God. See [note 6⁶](<1 John-01.fn#Footnote 6.6 — truth>) in [[1 John-01|1 John 1]].

## Footnotes for verse 36

### Footnote 36.1 — sent

**Verse:** [[John-05#v36|John 5:36]]

See [note 6¹](<John-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — sent>) in [[John-01|John 1]]; so in [[John-01#v38|v. 38]].

## Footnotes for verse 37

### Footnote 37.1 — testified

**Verse:** [[John-05#v37|John 5:37]]

This refers to the Father’s testimony concerning the Lord, given at the time He was baptized ([[Matthew-03#v17|Matt. 3:17]]). At that time the Jews heard the Father’s voice and saw the form of the Holy Spirit ([[Luke-03#v22|Luke 3:22]]), although prior to that time they had neither heard His voice at any time nor seen His form.

## Footnotes for verse 39

### Footnote 39.1 — search

**Verse:** [[John-05#v39|John 5:39]]

To “search the Scriptures” may be separated from “come to Me” ([[John-05#v40|v. 40]]). The Jewish religionists searched the Scriptures but were not willing to come to the Lord. These two should go together; because the Scriptures testify concerning the Lord, they should not be separated from the Lord. We may contact the Scriptures, yet not contact the Lord. Only the Lord can give life.

## Footnotes for verse 40

### Footnote 40.1 — come

**Verse:** [[John-05#v40|John 5:40]]

See [note 39¹](<John-05.fn#Footnote 39.1 — search>).

## Footnotes for verse 43

### Footnote 43.1 — in

**Verse:** [[John-05#v43|John 5:43]]

The Son being in the name of the Father is equivalent to the Son being the Father; hence, He is called the Father ([[Isaiah-09#v6|Isa. 9:6]]). This proves that the Son and the Father are one ([[John-10#v30|10:30]]). See [note 26²](<John-14.fn#Footnote 26.2 — Father>) in [[John-14|John 14]].
