## v1

And the [[John-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — third|third]] day a [[John-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — wedding|wedding]] took place in [[John-02.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Cana|Cana]] of [[John-02.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Galilee|Galilee]], and the mother of Jesus was there.

## v2

And Jesus also was invited, as well as His disciples, to the wedding.

## v3

And when the [[John-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — wine|wine]] [[John-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — ran|ran]] out, the [[John-02.fn#Footnote 3.3 — mother|mother]] of Jesus said to Him, They have no wine.

## v4

And Jesus said to her, [[John-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Woman|Woman]], [[John-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — what|what]] do I have in this that concerns you? My hour has not yet come.

## v5

His mother said to the servants, Whatever He says to you, do.

## v6

Now there were [[John-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — six|six]] stone waterpots lying there, according to the [[John-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Jews’|Jews']] rite of purification, holding [[John-02.fn#Footnote 6.3 — two|two]] or three measures each.

## v7

Jesus said to them, Fill the waterpots with [[John-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — water|water]]. And they filled them up to the brim.

## v8

And He said to them, Draw some out now and take it to the master of the feast. And they took it to him.

## v9

And when the master of the feast tasted the [[John-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — water|water]] which had become wine and did not know where it came from, though the servants who had drawn the water knew, the master of the feast called the bridegroom

## v10

And said to him, Every man sets out the good wine first, and when they have drunk freely, then that which is worse; you have kept the good wine until now.

## v11

This [[John-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — beginning|beginning]] of [[John-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — signs|signs]] Jesus performed in Cana of Galilee and [[John-02.fn#Footnote 11.3 — manifested|manifested]] His glory, and His disciples believed into Him.

## v12

After this He went down to Capernaum, He and His mother and His brothers and His disciples; and they remained there not many days.

## v13

And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

## v14

And He found in the [[John-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — temple|temple]] those selling oxen and sheep and doves, and the moneychangers sitting there.

## v15

And [[John-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — having|having]] made a whip out of [[John-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — cords|cords]], He drove them all out of the temple, as well as the sheep and the oxen, and He poured out the money of the moneychangers and overturned their tables.

## v16

And to those who were selling the doves He said, Take these things away from here; do not make My Father’s house a house of merchandise.

## v17

His disciples remembered that it was written, “The zeal of Your house shall devour Me.”

## v18

The Jews then answered and said to Him, What sign do You show us, seeing that You do these things?

## v19

Jesus answered and said to them, Destroy this [[John-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — temple|temple]], and in [[John-02.fn#Footnote 19.2 — three|three]] days I will raise it up.

## v20

Then the Jews said, This temple was built in forty-six years, and You will raise it up in three days?

## v21

But He spoke of the temple of His body.

## v22

When therefore He was raised from the dead, His disciples remembered that He had said this, and they believed the Scripture and the word which Jesus had spoken.

## v23

Now when He was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed into His name when they saw the signs which He did.

## v24

But Jesus Himself did not entrust Himself to them, for He knew all men,

## v25

And because He did not need anyone to testify concerning man, for He Himself knew what was in man.
