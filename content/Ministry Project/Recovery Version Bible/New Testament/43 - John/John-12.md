---
Author: John
---
## v1

Then Jesus, six days before the Passover, came to [[John-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Bethany|Bethany]], where Lazarus was, whom Jesus had raised from the dead.

## v2

Therefore they made Him a supper there; and Martha served, but Lazarus was one of the ones reclining at table with Him.

## v3

Then Mary took a [[John-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — pound|pound]] of ointment, of very valuable pure nard, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped His feet with her hair; and the [[John-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — house|house]] was filled with the fragrance of the ointment.

## v4

But Judas Iscariot, one of His disciples, who was about to betray Him, said,

## v5

Why was this ointment not sold for three hundred [[John-12.fn#Footnote 5.1 — denarii|denarii]] and given to the poor?

## v6

But he said this not because it mattered to him concerning the poor, but because he was a thief, and holding the purse, carried off what was put into it.

## v7

Then [[John-12.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Jesus|Jesus]] said, Leave her alone; she has reserved it for the day of My burial.

## v8

For the poor you always have with you, but you do not always have Me.

## v9

Then a great crowd of the Jews found out that He was there, and they came, not because of Jesus only, but that they might also see Lazarus, whom He had raised from the dead.

## v10

And the chief priests took counsel to kill Lazarus also,

## v11

Because on account of him many of the Jews went away and believed into Jesus.

## v12

On the next day, the great crowd who had come to the feast, when they heard that Jesus was coming into Jerusalem,

## v13

Took the branches of the palm trees and went out to meet Him, and cried out, [[John-12.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Hosanna|Hosanna]]! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord, even the King of Israel!

## v14

And Jesus, having found a young donkey, sat on it, as it is written,

## v15

“Fear not, daughter of Zion; behold, your King comes, sitting on a donkey’s colt.”

## v16

These things His disciples did not understand at first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written of Him and that they had done these things to Him.

## v17

The crowd therefore that was with Him when He called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead testified.

## v18

For this reason the crowd also went and met Him, because they heard that He had done this sign.

## v19

The Pharisees then said to one another, You see that you are not doing anything worthwhile; behold, the world has gone after Him.

## v20

And there were some Greeks among those who went up to worship at the feast.

## v21

These then came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida of Galilee, and asked him, saying, Sir, we wish to see Jesus.

## v22

Philip came and told Andrew; Andrew came, and Philip too, and they told Jesus.

## v23

And Jesus answered them, saying, The hour has come for the Son of Man to be [[John-12.fn#Footnote 23.1 — glorified|glorified]].

## v24

Truly, truly, I say to you, Unless the grain of wheat [[John-12.fn#Footnote 24.1 — falls|falls]] into the ground and dies, it abides alone; but if it dies, it bears [[John-12.fn#Footnote 24.2 — much|much]] fruit.

## v25

He who loves his [[John-12.fn#Footnote 25.2 — soul-life|soul-life]] loses it; and he who hates his [[John-12.fn#Footnote 25.2 — soul-life|soul-life]] in this world shall keep it unto [[John-12.fn#Footnote 25.1 — eternal|eternal]] life.

## v26

If anyone serves Me, let him follow Me; and where I am, there also My servant will be. If anyone serves Me, the Father will honor him.

## v27

Now is My [[John-12.fn#Footnote 27.1 — soul|soul]] troubled; and what shall I say? Father, save Me out of this hour. But for [[John-12.fn#Footnote 27.2 — this|this]] reason I have come to this hour.

## v28

Father, [[John-12.fn#Footnote 28.1 — glorify|glorify]] Your name. Then a voice came out of heaven: I have both [[John-12.fn#Footnote 28.1 — glorify|glorified]] it and will [[John-12.fn#Footnote 28.1 — glorify|glorify]] it again.

## v29

The crowd therefore which stood by and heard it said that there had been thunder; others said, An angel has spoken to Him.

## v30

Jesus answered and said, This voice has not [[John-12.fn#Footnote 30.1 — come|come]] for My sake, but for your sake.

## v31

Now is the [[John-12.fn#Footnote 31.1 — judgment|judgment]] of this [[John-12.fn#Footnote 31.2 — world|world]]; now shall the ruler of this world be cast out.

## v32

And I, if I be [[John-12.fn#Footnote 32.1 — lifted|lifted]] up from the earth, will draw all men to Myself.

## v33

But He said this signifying by what kind of death He was about to die.

## v34

The crowd therefore answered Him, We have heard out of the law that the Christ abides forever; and how is it that You say, The Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?

## v35

Jesus then said to them, The light is still among you a little while. Walk while you have the light so that darkness may not overcome you; and he who walks in the darkness does not know where he is going.

## v36

While you have the light, believe into the light, so that you may become sons of light. Jesus said these things, and He went away and was hidden from them.

## v37

But though He had done so many signs before them, they did not believe into Him,

## v38

That the word of the prophet Isaiah which he said might be fulfilled, “Lord, who has believed our report? And to whom has the [[John-12.fn#Footnote 38.1 — arm|arm]] of the Lord been revealed?”

## v39

For this reason they could not believe, because again Isaiah said,

## v40

“He has [[John-12.fn#Footnote 40.1 — blinded|blinded]] their eyes and He hardened their heart, that they might not see with their eyes and understand with their heart and turn, and I will heal them.”

## v41

These things said Isaiah because he saw [[John-12.fn#Footnote 41.1 — His|His]] glory and spoke concerning Him.

## v42

Nevertheless even many of the rulers believed into Him, but because of the Pharisees they did not confess Him, so that they would not be put out of the synagogue;

## v43

For they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God.

## v44

But Jesus [[John-12.fn#Footnote 44.1 — cried|cried]] out and said, He who believes into Me does not believe into Me, but into Him who sent Me;

## v45

And he who beholds Me beholds Him who sent Me.

## v46

I have come as a light into the world, that everyone who believes into Me would not remain in darkness.

## v47

And if anyone hears My [[John-12.fn#Footnote 47.1 — words|words]] and does not keep them, I do not judge him; for I have not come to judge the world, but to save the world.

## v48

He who rejects Me and does not receive My words has one who judges him; the word which I have spoken, that will judge him in the last day.

## v49

For I have not spoken from Myself; but the Father who sent Me, He Himself has given Me commandment, what to say and what to speak.

## v50

And I know that His commandment is [[John-12.fn#Footnote 50.1 — eternal|eternal]] life. The things therefore that I speak, even as the Father has said to Me, so I speak.
