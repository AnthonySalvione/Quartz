---
Author: John
---
## v1

I am the [[John-15.fn#Footnote 1.1 — true|true]] vine, and My Father is the [[John-15.fn#Footnote 1.2 — husbandman|husbandman]].

## v2

Every branch in Me that does not bear fruit, He takes it away; and every branch that bears fruit, He [[John-15.fn#Footnote 2.1 — prunes|prunes]] it that it may bear more fruit.

## v3

You are already clean because of the word which I have spoken to you.

## v4

Abide in Me and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit [[John-15.fn#Footnote 4.1 — of|of]] itself unless it abides in the vine, so neither can you unless you abide in Me.

## v5

I am the vine; you are the branches. He who abides in Me and I in him, he bears much fruit; for apart from Me you can do nothing.

## v6

If one does not abide in Me, he is [[John-15.fn#Footnote 6.1 — cast|cast]] out as a branch and is dried up; and they gather them and cast them into the fire, and they are burned.

## v7

If you abide in Me and My [[John-15.fn#Footnote 7.1 — words|words]] abide in you, [[John-15.fn#Footnote 7.2 — ask|ask]] whatever you will, and it shall be done for you.

## v8

In this is My Father [[John-15.fn#Footnote 8.1 — glorified|glorified]], that you bear much fruit and so you will become My disciples.

## v9

As the Father has loved Me, I also have loved you; abide in My love.

## v10

If you [[John-15.fn#Footnote 10.1 — keep|keep]] My commandments, you will abide in My love; even as I have kept My Father’s commandments and abide in His love.

## v11

These things I have spoken to you that My [[John-15.fn#Footnote 11.1 — joy|joy]] may be in you and that your joy may be made full.

## v12

This is My commandment, that you love one another even as I have loved you.

## v13

No one has greater love than this, that one lay down his [[John-15.fn#Footnote 13.1 — life|life]] for his friends.

## v14

You are My friends if you do what I command you.

## v15

No longer do I call you slaves, for the slave does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, for all the things which I have heard from My Father I have made known to you.

## v16

You did not choose Me, but I chose you, and I [[John-15.fn#Footnote 16.1 — set|set]] you that you should [[John-15.fn#Footnote 16.2 — go|go]] forth and bear fruit and [[John-15.fn#Footnote 16.3 — that|that]] your fruit should [[John-15.fn#Footnote 16.4 — remain|remain]], that whatever you [[John-15.fn#Footnote 16.5 — ask|ask]] the Father in My name, He may give you.

## v17

These things I command you that you may [[John-15.fn#Footnote 17.1 — love|love]] one another.

## v18

If the world hates you, know that it has hated Me before you.

## v19

If you were [[John-15.fn#Footnote 19.1 — of|of]] the world, the world would love its own; but because you are not [[John-15.fn#Footnote 19.1 — of|of]] the world, but I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.

## v20

Remember the word which I said to you, A slave is not greater than his master. If they have persecuted Me, they will persecute you also; if they have kept My word, they will keep yours also.

## v21

But all these things they will do to you because of My name, because they do not know Him who sent Me.

## v22

If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin.

## v23

He who hates Me hates My Father also.

## v24

If I did not do among them the works which no one else has done, they would not have sin; but now they have both seen and hated both Me and My Father.

## v25

But it is so that the word written in their law may be fulfilled, “They hated Me without cause.”

## v26

But when the Comforter comes, whom I will send to you [[John-15.fn#Footnote 26.1 — from|from]] the Father, the Spirit of reality, who proceeds [[John-15.fn#Footnote 26.1 — from|from]] the Father, He will testify concerning Me;

## v27

And you testify also, because from the beginning you have been with Me.
