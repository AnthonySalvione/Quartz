---
## v1

[[John-21.fn#Footnote 1.1 — After|After]] these things Jesus [[John-21.fn#Footnote 1.2 — manifested|manifested]] Himself again to the disciples at the Sea of Tiberias. And He manifested Himself in this way:

## v2

Simon Peter and Thomas, called [[John-21.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Didymus|Didymus]], and Nathanael from Cana of Galilee and the sons of Zebedee and two others of His disciples were there together.

## v3

Simon Peter said to them, I am [[John-21.fn#Footnote 3.1 — going|going]] fishing. They said to him, We also are coming with you. They went forth and got into the boat, and that night they caught [[John-21.fn#Footnote 3.2 — nothing|nothing]].

## v4

Now as soon as the morning broke, Jesus stood on the shore; however the disciples did not know that it was Jesus.

## v5

Then Jesus said to them, Little children, you do not have any [[John-21.fn#Footnote 5.1 — fish|fish]] to eat, do you? They answered Him, [[John-21.fn#Footnote 5.2 — No|No]].

## v6

And He said to them, Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some. They cast therefore, and they were no longer able to haul it in because of the [[John-21.fn#Footnote 6.1 — abundance|abundance]] of fish.

## v7

Then that disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, It is the Lord! Therefore when Simon Peter heard that it [[John-21.fn#Footnote 7.1 — was|was]] the Lord, he put his outer garment around himself, for he was naked; and he threw himself into the sea.

## v8

But the other disciples came in the little boat, for they were not far from the land, but about [[John-21.fn#Footnote 8.1 — two|two]] hundred cubits away, dragging the net of fish.

## v9

Then when they got out onto the land, they saw a fire of coals laid there, and [[John-21.fn#Footnote 9.1 — fish|fish]] lying on it and bread.

## v10

Jesus said to them, Bring some of the fish that you have just now caught.

## v11

Simon Peter therefore went up and hauled the net to the land full of large fish, a hundred and fifty-three; and though there were so many, the net was not torn.

## v12

Jesus said to them, [[John-21.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Come|Come]] and have breakfast. But none of the disciples dared to inquire of Him, Who are you? knowing that it was the Lord.

## v13

Jesus came and took the [[John-21.fn#Footnote 13.1 — bread|bread]] and gave it to them, and the fish likewise.

## v14

This was now the third time that Jesus was manifested to the disciples after He had been raised from the dead.

## v15

Then when they had eaten breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter, [[John-21.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Simon|Simon]], son of John, do you [[John-21.fn#Footnote 15.2 — love|love]] Me more than these? He said to Him, Yes, Lord, You know that I [[John-21.fn#Footnote 15.3 — love|love]] You. He said to him, [[John-21.fn#Footnote 15.4 — Feed|Feed]] My [[John-21.fn#Footnote 15.5 — lambs|lambs]].

## v16

He said to him again a second time, Simon, son of John, do you love Me? He said to Him, Yes, Lord, You know that I love You. He said to him, [[John-21.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Shepherd|Shepherd]] My [[John-21.fn#Footnote 16.2 — sheep|sheep]].

## v17

He said to him the [[John-21.fn#Footnote 17.1 — third|third]] time, Simon, son of John, do you love Me? Peter was grieved that He said to him the third time, Do you love Me? And he said to Him, Lord, You [[John-21.fn#Footnote 17.2 — know|know]] all things; You [[John-21.fn#Footnote 17.2 — know|know]] that I love You. Jesus said to him, Feed My sheep.

## v18

Truly, truly, I say to you, When you were younger, you girded yourself and [[John-21.fn#Footnote 18.1 — walked|walked]] where you wished; but when you grow old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will gird you and carry you where you do not wish to go.

## v19

Now this He said, signifying by what [[John-21.fn#Footnote 19.1 — kind|kind]] of death he would glorify God. And when He had said this, He said to him, Follow Me.

## v20

Peter, turning around, saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following, who also reclined on His breast at the supper and said, Lord, who is the one betraying You?

## v21

Peter therefore, seeing him, said to Jesus, Lord, and what about this man?

## v22

Jesus said to him, If I want him to remain until I [[John-21.fn#Footnote 22.1 — come|come]], what is that to you? You [[John-21.fn#Footnote 22.1 — come|follow]] Me.

## v23

This word therefore went out among the brothers, that that disciple would not die, yet Jesus did not say to him that he would not die, but, If I want him to remain until I [[John-21.fn#Footnote 23.1 — come|come]], what is that to you?

## v24

This is the disciple who testifies concerning these things, and the one who has written these things; and we know that his testimony is true.

## v25

And there are also [[John-21.fn#Footnote 25.1 — many|many]] other things which Jesus did, which, if they were written one by one, I suppose that not even the world itself could contain the books written.
