---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

And He entered and was passing through [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jericho|Jericho]].

## v2

And behold, there was a man whose name was called Zaccheus; and he was a chief [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 2.1 — tax|tax]] collector, and he was rich.

## v3

And he was seeking to see Jesus, who He was, and could not because of the crowd, for he was small in stature.

## v4

And he ran on ahead and climbed up in a [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 4.1 — sycamore|sycamore]] tree in order to see Him, for He was about to pass through that way.

## v5

And as He came to the place, Jesus looked up and said to him, Zaccheus, hurry and come down, for today I must stay in your house.

## v6

And he hurried and came down, and received Him, rejoicing.

## v7

And when they saw it, they all murmured, saying, He has gone in to lodge with a sinful man.

## v8

And Zaccheus stood and said to the Lord, Behold, the [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 8.1 — half|half]] of my possessions, Lord, I give to the poor, and if I have [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 8.2 — taken|taken]] anything from anyone by false accusation, I restore [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 8.3 — four|four]] times as much.

## v9

And Jesus said to him, Today salvation has come to this house, because he [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 9.1 — also|also]] is a son of Abraham.

## v10

For the Son of Man has come to [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 10.1 — seek|seek]] and to save that which is lost.

## v11

And as they were listening to these things, He spoke [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 11.1 — further|further]] a parable, because He was near Jerusalem and they supposed that the kingdom of God was to [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 11.2 — appear|appear]] immediately.

## v12

He said therefore, A [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 12.1 — certain|certain]] man of noble birth [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 12.2 — went|went]] to a distant country to receive for himself a kingdom and to [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 12.3 — return|return]].

## v13

And he called [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 13.1 — ten|ten]] of his own slaves and gave them ten [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 13.2 — minas|minas]], and he said to them, Do business [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 13.3 — until|until]] I come.

## v14

But his [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 14.1 — citizens|citizens]] hated him and sent an envoy after him, saying, [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 14.2 — We|We]] do not want this man to reign over us.

## v15

And when he came back, having received the kingdom, he commanded that those slaves to whom he had given the money should be called to him so that he might know what they had gained by doing business.

## v16

And the first appeared, saying, Master, your mina has gained ten minas.

## v17

And he said to him, Well done, good slave. Because you have become faithful in the least, [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 17.1 — have|have]] authority over ten cities.

## v18

And the second came, saying, Your mina, master, has made five minas.

## v19

And he said to this one as well, And you, be over [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 19.1 — five|five]] cities.

## v20

[[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 20.1 — And|And]] the other came, saying, Master, behold your mina, which I [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 20.2 — kept|kept]] laid away in a handkerchief;

## v21

For I was afraid of you, because you are a [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 21.1 — harsh|harsh]] man: you withdraw what you did not deposit, and you reap what you did not sow.

## v22

He said to him, Out of your mouth I will [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 22.1 — judge|judge]] you, wicked slave. You knew that I am a harsh man, withdrawing what I did not deposit and reaping what I did not sow.

## v23

Why then did you not put my money [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 23.1 — in|in]] the bank, and when I came, I would have collected it with interest?

## v24

And to those standing by he said, Take the mina away from him, and give it to the one who has the ten minas.

## v25

And they said to him, Master, he has ten minas.

## v26

I tell you that to everyone who has, more shall be given, but from him who does not have, even that which he has shall be taken away.

## v27

However, these enemies of mine, who did not want me to reign over them, bring them here and [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 27.1 — slay|slay]] them before me.

## v28

And having said these things, He went on before them, [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 28.1 — going|going]] up to Jerusalem.

## v29

[[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 29.1 — And|And]] as He drew near to Bethphage and Bethany, near the mount which is called Olivet, He sent two of the disciples,

## v30

Saying, Go into the village opposite you, in which, as you are entering, you will find a colt tied, on which no man has ever yet sat; untie it and lead it here.

## v31

And if anyone asks you, Why are you untying it? thus shall you say, Because the Lord has need of it.

## v32

And those who were sent went away and found it even as He had told them.

## v33

And as they were untying the colt, its masters said to them, Why are you untying the colt?

## v34

And they said, Because the Lord has need of it.

## v35

And they brought it to Jesus. And when they had thrown their garments on the colt, they put Jesus on it.

## v36

And as He went, they [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 36.1 — spread|spread]] their own garments in the road.

## v37

And as He was already drawing near to the descent of the Mount of Olives, all the multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the works of power which they had seen,

## v38

Saying, Blessed is the King who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!

## v39

And some of the Pharisees from the crowd said to Him, Teacher, rebuke Your disciples.

## v40

And He answered and said, I tell you, If these shall be silent, the stones will cry out.

## v41

And as He drew near, He saw the city and wept over it,

## v42

Saying, If you knew in this day, even you, the things that are for your [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 42.1 — peace|peace]]! But now they have been hidden from your eyes.

## v43

For the days will come upon you when your enemies will throw up a rampart before you, and will encircle you, and will press you in on every side,

## v44

And they will [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 44.1 — level|level]] you to the ground and your children within you, and they will not leave a stone upon a stone in you, because you did not know the time of your [[Luke-19.fn#Footnote 44.2 — visitation|visitation]].

## v45

And He entered into the temple and began to cast out those who were selling,

## v46

Saying to them, It is written, “And My house shall be a house of prayer,” but you have made it a den of robbers.

## v47

And He taught daily in the temple. But the chief priests and the scribes and the leaders of the people sought to destroy Him;

## v48

Yet they could not find anything that they might do, for all the people were hanging upon Him, listening.
