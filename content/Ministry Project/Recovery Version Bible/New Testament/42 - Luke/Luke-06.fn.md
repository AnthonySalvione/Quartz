## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — Blessed

**Verse:** [[Luke-06#v20|Luke 6:20]]

Whatever was decreed in [[Matthew-05|Matt. 5—7]], as the constitution of the kingdom of the heavens, constitutes the reality of the kingdom of the heavens. Whatever is cited here in [[Luke-06#v20|vv. 20-49]], as principles of the character of God’s children, governs and measures the behavior of the believers, who have been born of God and possess His life and nature. At the time it was spoken, this entire portion (except [[Luke-06#v24|vv. 24-26]], [[Luke-06#v39|39]]) applied to the believing remnant of the Jews. In terms of the standard of character, the principles contained in this portion far surpassed the law that the Jews received from Moses.
