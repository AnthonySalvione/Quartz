## v1

Now all the tax collectors and sinners were drawing near to Him to hear Him.

## v2

And both the Pharisees and the scribes murmured among themselves, saying, This man welcomes sinners and eats with them.

## v3

And He told them this [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 3.1 — parable|parable]], saying,

## v4

Which man of you, who has a hundred sheep and has lost one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 4.1 — wilderness|wilderness]] and go after the one which is lost until he finds it?

## v5

And when he finds it, he [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 5.1 — lays|lays]] it on his shoulders, rejoicing.

## v6

And when he comes into his house, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep that was lost.

## v7

I tell you that in the same way there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner repenting than over ninety-nine righteous persons who have no need of repentance.

## v8

Or what woman having ten [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 8.1 — silver|silver]] coins, if she loses one silver coin, does not light a [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 8.2 — lamp|lamp]] and [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 8.3 — sweep|sweep]] the house and [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 8.4 — seek|seek]] carefully until she finds it?

## v9

And when she finds it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin which I lost.

## v10

In the same way, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner repenting.

## v11

And He said, A certain man had two sons.

## v12

And the younger of them said to the father, Father, give me the [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 12.1 — share|share]] of the estate that falls to me. And he distributed to them his [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 12.2 — living|living]].

## v13

And not many days after, the younger son, having gathered everything together, went abroad to a [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 13.1 — distant|distant]] country and there squandered his estate by living [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 13.2 — dissolutely|dissolutely]].

## v14

And when he had spent all, a severe famine occurred throughout that country, and he began to be in want.

## v15

And he went and joined himself to one of the citizens of that country; and he sent him into his fields to [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 15.1 — feed|feed]] hogs.

## v16

And he longed to be satisfied with the [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 16.1 — carob|carob]] pods which the hogs were eating, and no one gave him anything.

## v17

But when he came to himself, he said, [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 17.1 — How|How]] many of my father’s hired servants abound in bread, but I am perishing here in famine!

## v18

I will [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 18.1 — rise|rise]] up and go to my father, and I will say to him, Father, [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 18.2 — I|I]] have sinned against heaven and before you.

## v19

I am [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 19.1 — no|no]] longer worthy to be called your son; make me like one of your [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 19.2 — hired|hired]] servants.

## v20

And he rose up and came to his own father. But while he was still a long way off, his father [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 20.1 — saw|saw]] him and was moved with compassion, and he [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 20.2 — ran|ran]] and [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 20.3 — fell|fell]] on his neck and kissed him affectionately.

## v21

And the son said to him, Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; I am no longer worthy to be called your son.

## v22

[[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.1 — But|But]] the father said to his slaves, Bring out [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.2 — quickly|quickly]] [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.3 — the|the]] [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.4 — best|best]] [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.5 — robe|robe]] and put it on him, and put a [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.6 — ring|ring]] on his hand and [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 22.7 — sandals|sandals]] on his feet.

## v23

And bring the [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 23.1 — fattened|fattened]] calf; [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 23.2 — slaughter|slaughter]] it, and let us eat and be merry,

## v24

Because this son of mine was [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 24.1 — dead|dead]] and lives again; he was [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 24.1 — dead|lost]] and has been found. And they began to be merry.

## v25

Now his [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 25.1 — older|older]] son was in the field. And as he came and drew near to the house, he heard music and dancing,

## v26

And calling to him one of the servants, he inquired what these things might be.

## v27

And he said to him, Your brother has come, and your father has slaughtered the fattened calf because he has received him back healthy.

## v28

But he became angry and was not willing to enter. And his father came out and entreated him.

## v29

But he answered and said to his father, Behold, so many years I have been [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 29.1 — slaving|slaving]] for you and have never [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 29.2 — neglected|neglected]] a command of yours, and you have never given me a goat that I might be merry with my friends;

## v30

But when this son of yours came, who devoured your [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 30.1 — living|living]] with harlots, you slaughtered for him the fattened calf.

## v31

And he said to him, Child, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours.

## v32

But we had to be merry and rejoice, because this brother of yours was [[Luke-15.fn#Footnote 32.1 — dead|dead]] and has come to life, and he was lost and has been found.
