## Footnotes for verse 36

### Footnote 36.1 — prevail

**Verse:** [[Luke-21#v36|Luke 21:36]]

Have strength and ability. The strength and ability to escape the great tribulation come from watchfulness and beseeching.

### Footnote 36.2 — escape

**Verse:** [[Luke-21#v36|Luke 21:36]]

To be taken, raptured, before the great tribulation ([[Matthew-24#v21|Matt. 24:21]]; [[Luke-17#v34|Luke 17:34-36]] and [[Luke-17.fn#Footnote 34.2 — taken|note 34²]]), which will be a severe trial upon the whole inhabited earth ([[Revelation-03#v10|Rev. 3:10]]). To be thus raptured is to be kept “out of the hour of trial, which is about to come on the whole inhabited earth, to try them who dwell on the earth” ([[Revelation-03#v10|Rev. 3:10]]).

### Footnote 36.3 — all

**Verse:** [[Luke-21#v36|Luke 21:36]]

All the things of the great tribulation.

### Footnote 36.4 — stand

**Verse:** [[Luke-21#v36|Luke 21:36]]

Corresponding with standing in [[Revelation-14#v1|Rev. 14:1]], indicating that the raptured overcomers will stand before the Savior on Mount Zion in the heavens before the great tribulation (cf. [[Revelation-12#v5|Rev. 12:5-6]], [[Revelation-12#v14|14]]).
