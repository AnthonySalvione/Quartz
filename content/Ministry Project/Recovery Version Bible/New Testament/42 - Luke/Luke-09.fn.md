## Footnotes for verse 57

### Footnote 57.1 — someone

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v57|Luke 9:57]]

He was one of the scribes ([[Matthew-08#v19|Matt. 8:19]]), who usually lived comfortably. He saw crowds being attracted to the Savior ([[Matthew-08#v18|Matt. 8:18]]) and wanted to follow Him out of curiosity, without counting the cost (see [note 19¹](<Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 19.1 — follow>) in [[Matthew-08|Matt. 8]]). Hence, the Savior cautioned him by pointing out that although crowds were attracted to Him, He had nowhere to rest, indicating to the scribe that to follow Him would cost him considerable suffering.

## Footnotes for verse 58

### Footnote 58.1 — nowhere

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v58|Luke 9:58]]

The Savior’s human life was one of suffering. In the inn, at His birth, there was no room in which He could lie ([[Luke-02#v7|2:7]]); and in His marvelous ministry there was no place for Him to rest. Suffering was a sign of His human life ([[Luke-02#v12|2:12]]).

## Footnotes for verse 59

### Footnote 59.1 — another

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v59|Luke 9:59]]

This one was called by the Savior to follow Him. But he considered his duty to his dead father and would not follow Him immediately. Hence, the Savior encouraged him to pay the price (see [note 21¹](<Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 21.1 — go>) in [[Matthew-08|Matt. 8]]) that he might become a follower in His great commission to announce the kingdom of God.

## Footnotes for verse 60

### Footnote 60.1 — dead

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v60|Luke 9:60]]

The burying one was spiritually dead ([[John-05#v25|John 5:25]]; [[Ephesians-02#v1|Eph. 2:1]]), and the one to be buried was physically dead. To engage in such a burial was to do a dead work.

### Footnote 60.2 — announce

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v60|Luke 9:60]]

To announce the kingdom of God is a living deed, a deed that makes the dead alive so that they can enter into the kingdom of God.

## Footnotes for verse 61

### Footnote 61.1 — another

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v61|Luke 9:61]]

This third one volunteered to follow the Savior, but would not do so before bidding farewell to his family. Hence, the Savior warned him not to let anything hold him back from the kingdom of God.

## Footnotes for verse 62

### Footnote 62.1 — plow

**Verse:** [[Luke-09#v62|Luke 9:62]]

To plow, one must concentrate all his attention on the line being plowed. Being distracted just a little, to say nothing of looking back, may lead the plower off the straight line. To follow the Savior, we must forget everything else and press straight ahead for the kingdom of God.
