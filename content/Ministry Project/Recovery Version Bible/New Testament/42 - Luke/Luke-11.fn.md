### Footnote 13.2 — ask

**Verse:** [[Luke-11#v13|Luke 11:13]]

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — finger

**Verse:** [[Luke-11#v20|Luke 11:20]]

A Hebraic expression. The finger ([[Luke-11#v46|v. 46]]) is smaller than the hand and the arm. The casting out of demons does not require the hand of God ([[John-10#v28|John 10:28-29]]) or the arm of God ([[Isaiah-53#v1|Isa. 53:1]]); the finger of God is powerful enough to do it. Still, it is done by the Spirit of God ([[Matthew-12#v28|Matt. 12:28]]).

## Footnotes for verse 41

### Footnote 41.1 — within

**Verse:** [[Luke-11#v41|Luke 11:41]]

Within refers to the contents of the cup and to the items on the platter ([[Luke-11#v39|v. 39]]); these signify what was in the heart of the Pharisees. They had covetousness within their heart; hence, they were full of extortion and wickedness ([[Luke-11#v39|v. 39]]). Therefore, the Lord commanded them to give as alms what they were coveting in their heart, that all things might be clean to them.

## Footnotes for verse 49

### Footnote 49.1 — wisdom

**Verse:** [[Luke-11#v49|Luke 11:49]]

Referring to Christ as the wisdom of God in God’s New Testament economy ([[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]); He is the “I” in [[Matthew-23#v34|Matt. 23:34]]. The Lord’s word in [[Matthew-23#v34|Matt. 23:34-36]] and [[Luke-11#v49|Luke 11:49-51]] was based on the record in [[2 Chronicles-24#v20|2 Chron. 24:20-22]] concerning the slaying of Zechariah, and it expanded the word in [[2 Chronicles-24#v19|2 Chron. 24:19]], applying it to those who were sent by God in both the Old Testament and the New Testament.
