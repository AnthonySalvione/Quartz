---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

Now after these things, the Lord appointed [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 1.1 — seventy|seventy]] others and sent them [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 1.2 — two|two]] by two before His face into every city and place where He Himself was about to come.

## v2

And He said to them, The harvest is great, but the workers few; therefore, [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 2.1 — beseech|beseech]] the Lord of the harvest that He would thrust out workers [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 2.2 — into|into]] His harvest.

## v3

Go; behold, I send you as lambs in the midst of wolves.

## v4

Do [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 4.1 — not|not]] carry a purse, nor a bag, nor sandals; and greet no one on the way.

## v5

And into whatever house you enter, first say, Peace to this house.

## v6

And if a [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 6.1 — son|son]] of peace is there, your peace shall rest upon [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 6.2 — it|it]]; but if not, it shall return upon you.

## v7

And in that house remain, eating and drinking the things from them, for the worker is worthy of his wages. Do not move from house to house.

## v8

And into whatever city you enter and they receive you, eat what is set before you;

## v9

And heal the sick who are in it and say to them, The [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 9.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God has drawn near to you.

## v10

But into whatever city you enter and they do not receive you, go out into its streets and say,

## v11

Even the dust from your city which clings to our feet, we wipe off against you; yet know this, that the kingdom of God has drawn near.

## v12

I say to you that it will be [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 12.1 — more|more]] tolerable for Sodom in that day than for that city.

## v13

Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the works of power which took place in you had taken place in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.

## v14

Yet it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the judgment than for you.

## v15

And you, Capernaum, who have been exalted to heaven, to [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Hades|Hades]] you will be brought down.

## v16

He who hears you hears Me, and he who rejects you rejects Me, and he who rejects Me rejects Him who sent Me.

## v17

And the seventy returned with joy, saying, Lord, even the demons are subject to us in Your name.

## v18

And He said to them, I was watching Satan [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 18.1 — fall|fall]] like lightning out of heaven.

## v19

Behold, I have given you the [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 19.1 — authority|authority]] to tread upon [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 19.2 — serpents|serpents]] and [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 19.2 — serpents|scorpions]] and over all the [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 19.1 — authority|power]] of the enemy, and nothing shall by any means hurt you.

## v20

However do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rejoice that your names are recorded in the heavens.

## v21

[[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 21.1 — In|In]] that hour He exulted in the Holy Spirit and said, I extol You, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because You have hidden these things from the wise and intelligent and have revealed them to babes. Yes, Father, for thus it has been well pleasing in Your sight.

## v22

All things have been delivered to Me by My Father; and no one knows who the Son is except the Father, and who the Father is except the Son and him to whom the Son wills to reveal Him.

## v23

And turning to the disciples, He said privately, Blessed are the eyes which see the things that you see.

## v24

For I tell you that many prophets and kings have wanted to perceive the things that you see, and have not perceived them, and to hear the things that you hear, and have not heard them.

## v25

And behold, a certain lawyer stood up and put Him to the [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 25.1 — test|test]], saying, Teacher, [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 25.2 — what|what]] should I do to [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 25.3 — inherit|inherit]] eternal life?

## v26

And He said to him, What is written in the law? How do you read it?

## v27

And he answered and said, “You shall love the Lord your God from your whole heart and [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 27.1 — with|with]] your whole soul and [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 27.1 — with|with]] your whole strength and [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 27.1 — with|with]] your whole mind, and your neighbor as yourself.”

## v28

And He said to him, You have answered correctly; do this, and you shall have life.

## v29

But he, wanting to [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 29.1 — justify|justify]] himself, said to Jesus, And who is my neighbor?

## v30

Jesus, taking up the question, said, A [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.1 — certain|certain]] man was going [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.2 — down|down]] from [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.3 — Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] to [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.3 — Jerusalem|Jericho]], and he fell among [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.4 — robbers|robbers]], who having both [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.5 — stripped|stripped]] him and [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.6 — beaten|beaten]] him, went away, [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 30.7 — leaving|leaving]] him half dead.

## v31

And by coincidence a certain [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 31.1 — priest|priest]] was going down on that road; and when he saw him, he passed by on the opposite side.

## v32

And likewise also a [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 32.1 — Levite|Levite]], when he came to the place and saw him, passed by on the opposite side.

## v33

But a certain [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 33.1 — Samaritan|Samaritan]], who was journeying, came upon him; and when he saw him, he was moved with compassion;

## v34

And he came to him and [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 34.1 — bound|bound]] up his wounds and poured oil and wine on them. And placing him on his own beast, he brought him to an inn and took care of him.

## v35

And on the next day he took out two [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 35.1 — denarii|denarii]] and gave them to the innkeeper and said, Take care of him; and whatever you spend in addition to this, when I return, I will repay you.

## v36

Which of these three, does it seem to you, has [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 36.1 — become|become]] a neighbor to him who fell into the hands of the robbers?

## v37

And he said, [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 37.1 — The|The]] one who showed mercy to him. And Jesus said to him, Go, and you do likewise.

## v38

Now as they went, He entered into a certain [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 38.1 — village|village]], and a certain woman named [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 38.2 — Martha|Martha]] received Him into her home.

## v39

And she had a sister called [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 39.1 — Mary|Mary]], who also sat at the Lord’s feet and was listening to His word.

## v40

But Martha was [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 40.1 — being|being]] drawn about with much serving, and she came up to Him and said, Lord, does it not matter to You that my sister has left me to serve alone? Tell her then to do her part with me.

## v41

But the Lord answered and said to her, Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things;

## v42

But there is need of one thing, for Mary has chosen the [[Luke-10.fn#Footnote 42.1 — good|good]] part, which shall not be taken away from her.
