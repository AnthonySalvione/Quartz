---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

After He completed all His words in the [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — hearing|hearing]] of the people, He [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — entered|entered]] into Capernaum.

## v2

And a certain centurion’s slave, who was highly valued by him, was ill and about to die.

## v3

And when he heard about Jesus, he sent to Him some elders of the Jews, requesting that He would come and [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — completely|completely]] heal his slave.

## v4

And when they came to Jesus, they entreated Him earnestly, saying, He is worthy of Your granting this to him,

## v5

For he loves our nation, and he himself built the synagogue for us.

## v6

And Jesus went with them. But already, when He was not far from the house, the centurion sent friends, saying to Him, Lord, do not trouble Yourself, for I am not fit for You to enter under my roof;

## v7

Therefore, I did not count even myself worthy to come to You. But speak a word, and let my servant be healed.

## v8

For I also am a man set under authority, having soldiers under me. And I say to this one, Go, and he goes; and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my slave, Do this, and he does it.

## v9

And when Jesus heard these things, He marveled at him, and turning to the crowd following Him, He said, I say to you, Not even in Israel have I found such great faith.

## v10

And when those who had been sent returned to the house, they found the slave in good health.

## v11

And [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — soon|soon]] afterward He went into a city called Nain, and His disciples went with Him, as well as a large crowd.

## v12

And as He came near the gate of the city, behold, one who had died was being carried out, an only son of his mother, and she was a widow; and a considerable crowd from the city was with her.

## v13

And when the Lord saw her, He was [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — moved|moved]] with compassion for her and said to her, Do not weep.

## v14

And He came near and touched the bier, and those carrying it stood still. And He said, Young man, to you I say, Arise.

## v15

And the dead man sat up and began to speak. And He gave him to his mother.

## v16

And fear took hold of all, and they glorified God, saying, A great prophet has been raised up among us, and, God has visited His people!

## v17

And this report concerning Him went out in the whole of [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Judea|Judea]] and in all the surrounding region.

## v18

And [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 18.1 — John’s|John’s]] disciples reported to him concerning all these things.

## v19

And John called a certain two of his disciples to him and sent them to [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 19.1 — the|the]] Lord, saying, Are You the Coming One, or should we expect another?

## v20

And the men came to Him and said, John the Baptist sent us to You, saying, Are You the Coming One, or should we expect another?

## v21

In that hour He healed many from diseases and afflictions and evil spirits, and to many who were blind He graciously gave sight.

## v22

And He answered and said to them, Go and report to John what you have seen and heard: the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed and the deaf hear, the dead are raised, the poor have the gospel [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — announced|announced]] to them.

## v23

And [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 23.1 — blessed|blessed]] is he who is not stumbled because of Me.

## v24

And when the messengers of John went away, He began to say to the crowds concerning John, What did you go out into the wilderness to look at? A reed shaken by the wind?

## v25

But what did you go out to see? A man arrayed in soft garments? Behold, those who live in splendid apparel and luxury are in royal palaces.

## v26

But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and much more than a prophet.

## v27

This is he concerning whom it is written, “Behold, I send My messenger before Your face, who will prepare Your way before You.”

## v28

I say to you, Among those born of women there is no one greater than John, yet he who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.

## v29

And when all the people and the tax collectors heard this, they acknowledged that God was righteous, since they had been baptized with the baptism of John.

## v30

But the Pharisees and the [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 30.1 — lawyers|lawyers]] [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 30.2 — rejected|rejected]] the counsel of God for themselves since they had not been baptized by him.

## v31

And Jesus said, To what then shall I liken the men of this generation, and what are they like?

## v32

They are like little children sitting in the marketplace and calling to one another, who say, We have played the flute to you, and you did not dance; we have sung a dirge, and you did not weep.

## v33

For John the Baptist has come neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and you say, He has a demon.

## v34

The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, Behold, a gluttonous man and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners.

## v35

Yet wisdom is justified by all her children.

## v36

Now a certain one of the Pharisees asked Him to eat with him. And He entered into the Pharisee’s house and reclined at table.

## v37

And behold, there was a woman in the city who was a sinner, and when she learned that He was reclining at table in the Pharisee’s house, she [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 37.1 — brought|brought]] an alabaster flask of ointment.

## v38

And she stood behind Him at His feet weeping and began to wet His feet with her tears and wiped them with the [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 38.1 — hair|hair]] of her head, and she kissed His feet [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 38.2 — affectionately|affectionately]] and anointed them with the [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 38.3 — ointment|ointment]].

## v39

But when the Pharisee who had invited Him saw this, he said within himself, This man, [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 39.1 — if|if]] He were a prophet, would know who and what sort of woman this is who is touching Him, because she is a sinner.

## v40

And Jesus [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 40.1 — answered|answered]] and said to him, Simon, I have something to say to you. And he said, Teacher, say it.

## v41

A certain moneylender had [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 41.1 — two|two]] debtors: one owed five hundred [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 41.2 — denarii|denarii]] and the other fifty.

## v42

But since they had [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 42.1 — nothing|nothing]] with which to repay, he graciously [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 42.2 — forgave|forgave]] them both. Which of them [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 42.3 — therefore|therefore]] will love him more?

## v43

Simon answered and said, I suppose the one whom he graciously forgave the more. And He said to him, You have judged correctly.

## v44

And turning to the woman, He said to Simon, Do you see this woman? I entered into your house; you did not [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 44.1 — give|give]] Me water for My feet, but she, with her tears, has wet My feet and with her hair has wiped them.

## v45

You did not give Me a kiss, but she, from the time I entered, has not stopped kissing My feet affectionately.

## v46

You did not anoint My head with oil, but she has anointed My feet with ointment.

## v47

For this reason I say to you, Her sins which are many are forgiven, because she [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 47.1 — loved|loved]] much; but to whom little is forgiven, he loves little.

## v48

And He said to her, [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 48.1 — Your|Your]] sins are forgiven.

## v49

And those who were reclining at table with Him began to say within themselves, [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 49.1 — Who|Who]] is this who even forgives sins?

## v50

But He said to the woman, Your [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 50.1 — faith|faith]] has saved you. Go [[Luke-07.fn#Footnote 50.2 — in|in]] peace.
