## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — has

**Verse:** [[Luke-04#v6|Luke 4:6]]

This must have occurred in the preadamic age. The devil’s word here indicates that when God anointed the archangel to be the head of the preadamic age ([[Ezekiel-28#v13|Ezek. 28:13-14]]), the authority and glory of the kingdom of the earth must have been given to him. The Lord’s word in [[John-12#v31|John 12:31]] confirms this. After he rebelled against God and became God’s enemy, Satan, he was judged by God ([[Isaiah-14#v12|Isa. 14:12-15]]), but the full execution of God’s judgment upon him will not be completed until the end of the millennium ([[Revelation-20#v7|Rev. 20:7-10]]). Hence, until that time he has authority over the kingdoms of the earth. He tempted the Lord Jesus by offering this authority and its glory to Him. His evil offer was rejected by God’s Christ, but it will be accepted by the Antichrist, the man of lawlessness ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v3|2 Thes. 2:3-4]]), at the end of this age ([[Revelation-13#v4|Rev. 13:4]]) for the executing of Satan’s evil strategy against God. Only by Christ, who lives in us, can we reject the evil tempter.

**Verse:** [[Luke-04#v26|Luke 4:26]]

This was a case of feeding, signifying the Lord’s feeding of the hungry ([[John-06#v33|John 6:33]], [[John-06#v35|35]]). The Savior’s mentioning of these two cases implied that His gospel would turn to the Gentiles ([[Acts-13#v45|Acts 13:45-48]]; see notes [21¹](<Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Tyre>) and [22¹](<Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Canaanite>) in [[Matthew-15|Matt. 15]])—not because His standard of morality was unable to embrace the Jews but because of the Jews’ hardhearted rejection of Him.

## Footnotes for verse 43

### Footnote 43.1 — announce

**Verse:** [[Luke-04#v43|Luke 4:43]]

The Greek word means to announce good news, to declare (bring) glad tidings, to preach (the gospel). Hence, to announce the gospel of the kingdom of God is to preach the kingdom of God as the gospel, the good news.

### Footnote 43.2 — kingdom

**Verse:** [[Luke-04#v43|Luke 4:43]]

The kingdom of God is the Savior (see [note 21¹](<Luke-17.fn#Footnote 21.1 — kingdom>) in [[Luke-17|Luke 17]]) as the seed of life sown into His believers, God’s chosen people ([[Mark-04#v3|Mark 4:3]], [[Mark-04#v26|26]]), and developing into a realm over which God can rule as His kingdom in His divine life. The entrance into the kingdom is regeneration ([[John-03#v5|John 3:5]]), and the development of the kingdom is the believers’ growth in the divine life ([[2 Peter-01#v3|2 Pet. 1:3-11]]). The kingdom is the church life today, in which the faithful believers live ([[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]), and it will develop into the coming kingdom as a reward to be inherited ([[Galatians-05#v21|Gal. 5:21]]; [[Ephesians-05#v5|Eph. 5:5]]) by the overcoming saints in the millennium ([[Revelation-20#v4|Rev. 20:4]], [[Revelation-20#v6|6]]). Eventually, it will consummate in the New Jerusalem as the eternal kingdom of God, an eternal realm of the eternal blessing of God’s eternal life, which all God’s redeemed will enjoy in the new heaven and new earth for eternity—[[Revelation-21#v1|Rev. 21:1-4]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-5]], [[Revelation-22#v14|14]] (see notes [15¹](<Mark-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — kingdom>) in [[Mark-01|Mark 1]], and [3¹](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — sower>), [3²](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — sow>), [26¹](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 26.1 — kingdom>), [26³](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 26.3 — seed>) in [[Mark-04|Mark 4]]). Such a kingdom, the kingdom of God, is what the Savior announced here as the gospel, the good news, to those who were alienated from the life of God ([[Ephesians-04#v18|Eph. 4:18]]).
