---
## v1

[[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Inasmuch|Inasmuch]] as [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — many|many]] have undertaken to [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — draw|draw]] up a narrative concerning the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — matters|matters]] which have been fully accomplished among us,

## v2

Even as [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — those|those]] who from the beginning became eyewitnesses and [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — ministers|ministers]] of the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — word|word]] have [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — delivered|delivered]] them to us,

## v3

It seemed good to [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — me|me]] also, having carefully investigated all things from the first, to write them out for you in an orderly fashion, most excellent [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Theophilus|Theophilus]],

## v4

So that you may fully know the certainty of the things concerning which you were instructed.

## v5

In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there was a certain priest named Zachariah, of the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — course|course]] of Abijah; and his wife was of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth.

## v6

And [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — they|they]] were both [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — righteous|righteous]] before God, [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 6.3 — walking|walking]] in all the commandments and [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 6.4 — ordinances|ordinances]] of the Lord, blameless.

## v7

And they had [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — no|no]] child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they were both advanced in their days.

## v8

And while he served as priest in the order of his course before God,

## v9

According to the custom of the priestly service, the lot fell to him to enter into the temple of the Lord and [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — burn|burn]] incense.

## v10

And all the multitude of the people were [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — praying|praying]] outside at the hour of the burning of incense.

## v11

And an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing at the right side of the altar of incense.

## v12

And Zachariah was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him.

## v13

But the angel said to him, Do not be afraid, Zachariah, because your petition has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — bear|bear]] you a son, and you shall call his name [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — John|John]].

## v14

And you will have joy and exultation, and many will rejoice at his birth.

## v15

For he will be great in the sight of the Lord, and he shall by [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — no|no]] means drink wine and liquor. And he will be filled with the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit, even from his mother’s womb,

## v16

And many of the sons of Israel he will turn to the Lord their God.

## v17

And it is he who will go before Him in the spirit and power of [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Elijah|Elijah]] to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the disobedient [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — to|to]] the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 17.3 — prudence|prudence]] of the righteous, to prepare for the Lord a people made ready.

## v18

And Zachariah said to the angel, By what shall I know this? For I am an old man and my wife is advanced in [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — years|years]].

## v19

And the angel answered and said to him, I am Gabriel, who stands before God, and I was sent to speak to you and to announce the good news of these things to you.

## v20

And behold, you shall be silent and not able to speak until the day that these things happen, because you did [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — not|not]] believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their time.

## v21

And the people were waiting for Zachariah, and they marveled at his delaying in the temple.

## v22

And when he came out, he was not able to speak to them, and they realized that he had seen a vision in the temple. And he made signs to them and remained dumb.

## v23

And when the days of his service were fulfilled, he went to his house.

## v24

And after these days Elizabeth his wife conceived and stayed in seclusion for five months, saying,

## v25

In this way has the Lord dealt with me in the days in which He looked upon me to take away my reproach among men.

## v26

And in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Galilee|Galilee]] named Nazareth,

## v27

To a virgin engaged to a man named Joseph, of the house of [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — David|David]]; and the virgin’s name was Mary.

## v28

And he came to her and said, Rejoice, you who have been graced! The Lord is with [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 28.1 — you|you]].

## v29

And she was greatly troubled at this saying and began reasoning what kind of greeting this might be.

## v30

And the angel said to her, Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found grace with God.

## v31

And behold, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you shall call His name [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 31.1 — Jesus|Jesus]].

## v32

He will be great and will be called Son of the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 32.1 — Most|Most]] High; and the Lord God will give to Him the throne of [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 32.2 — David|David]] His father,

## v33

And He will [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 33.1 — reign|reign]] over the house of Jacob forever, and of His [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 33.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] there will be no end.

## v34

But Mary said to the angel, How will this be, since I have not known a man?

## v35

And the angel answered and said to her, The [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 35.1 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 35.2 — overshadow|overshadow]] you; therefore also the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 35.3 — holy|holy]] thing which is born will be called the Son of God.

## v36

And behold, Elizabeth your relative, she also has conceived a son in her old age, and this is the sixth month for her who is called barren,

## v37

Because no word will be [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 37.1 — impossible|impossible]] [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 37.2 — with|with]] God.

## v38

And Mary said, Behold, the slave of the Lord. May it happen to me according to your word. And the angel departed from her.

## v39

And Mary rose up in those days and went to the hill country with haste, to a city of Judah,

## v40

And entered into the house of Zachariah and greeted Elizabeth.

## v41

And when Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 41.1 — baby|baby]] leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.

## v42

And she lifted up her voice with a loud cry and said, [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 42.1 — Blessed|Blessed]] are you among women, and blessed is the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 42.2 — fruit|fruit]] of your womb!

## v43

And how has this happened to me, that the mother of my [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 43.1 — Lord|Lord]] should come to me?

## v44

For behold, when the sound of your greeting came to my ears, the baby leaped with exultation in my womb.

## v45

[[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 45.1 — And|And]] blessed is she who has [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 45.2 — believed|believed]], because there will be a [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 45.3 — completion|completion]] of the things spoken to her from the Lord.

## v46

And Mary said, [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 46.1 — My|My]] soul [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 46.2 — magnifies|magnifies]] the Lord,

## v47

And my spirit [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 47.1 — has|has]] exulted in God my Savior;

## v48

Because He has looked upon the low estate of His slave. For behold, from now on all generations will count me blessed,

## v49

Because the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 49.1 — Mighty|Mighty]] One has done great things for me, and holy is His name.

## v50

And His [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 50.1 — mercy|mercy]] is unto generations and generations, unto those who fear Him.

## v51

He has exercised might with His arm; He has scattered those who are proud in the understanding of their heart.

## v52

He has brought down potentates from thrones and has exalted the humble;

## v53

The hungry He has filled with good things, and the rich He has sent away empty.

## v54

He has upheld Israel His servant to remember mercy,

## v55

Even [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 55.1 — as|as]] He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed forever.

## v56

And Mary remained with her about three months, and returned to her home.

## v57

Now the time was fulfilled for Elizabeth to give birth, and she bore a son.

## v58

And her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy with her, and they rejoiced with her.

## v59

And on the eighth day they came to circumcise the little child, and they tried to call him after the name of his father, Zachariah.

## v60

Yet his mother answered and said, No, but he shall be called John.

## v61

And they said to her, There is no one among your relatives who is called by this name.

## v62

And they gestured to his father as to what he would want him to be called.

## v63

And asking for a tablet, he wrote, saying, John is his name. And they all marveled.

## v64

And his [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 64.1 — mouth|mouth]] was opened instantly, and his tongue loosed, and he began to speak, blessing God.

## v65

And fear came on all those dwelling around them, and in all the hill country of Judea all these [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 65.1 — things|things]] were spread about.

## v66

And all who heard these things put them in their heart, saying, What then will this little child be? For indeed the hand of the Lord was with him.

## v67

And Zachariah his father was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied, saying,

## v68

Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, because He has visited and accomplished [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 68.1 — redemption|redemption]] for His people,

## v69

And raised a [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 69.1 — horn|horn]] of salvation for us in the house of David His servant,

## v70

Even [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 70.1 — as|as]] He spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 70.2 — from|from]] of old —

## v71

Salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us,

## v72

To accomplish [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 72.1 — mercy|mercy]] with our fathers and to [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 72.2 — remember|remember]] His holy covenant,

## v73

The [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 73.1 — oath|oath]] which He swore to Abraham our father,

## v74

To grant us that we, having been delivered out of the hand of our enemies, might [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 74.1 — serve|serve]] Him without fear,

## v75

In [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 75.1 — holiness|holiness]] and righteousness before Him all our days.

## v76

And you also, little child, shall be called a prophet of the Most High, for you will go before the [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 76.1 — Lord|Lord]] to [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 76.2 — prepare|prepare]] His ways,

## v77

To give knowledge of salvation to His people by the forgiveness of their sins,

## v78

Because of the merciful compassions of our God, in which the rising [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 78.1 — sun|sun]] will visit us from on high,

## v79

To shine upon those sitting in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.

## v80

And the little child grew and became strong in [[Luke-01.fn#Footnote 80.1 — spirit|spirit]], and he was in the wilderness until the day of his presentation to Israel.
