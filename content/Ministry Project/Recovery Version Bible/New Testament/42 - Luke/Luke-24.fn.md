## Footnotes for verse 47

### Footnote 47.1 — forgiveness

**Verse:** [[Luke-24#v47|Luke 24:47]]

Forgiveness of sins could be proclaimed only after the Man-Savior’s vicarious death for the sinners’ sins had been accomplished and had been verified by His resurrection ([[Luke-24#v46|v. 46]]; cf. [[Romans-04#v25|Rom. 4:25]]). The Gospel of John, as the Gospel of the God-Savior, emphasizes life for fruit-bearing ([[John-15#v5|John 15:5]]). The Gospel of Luke, as the Gospel of the Man-Savior, stresses forgiveness of sins for proclaiming. To bear fruit in life requires the Spirit of life essentially, received through the breathing of the Spirit ([[John-20#v22|John 20:22]]). To proclaim forgiveness of sins requires the Spirit of power economically, received through the baptism in the Spirit ([[Acts-01#v5|Acts 1:5]], [[Acts-01#v8|8]]). To bear fruit is a matter of life; to proclaim forgiveness of sins is a matter of power.

## Footnotes for verse 49

### Footnote 49.1 — promise

**Verse:** [[Luke-24#v49|Luke 24:49]]

The promise given in [[Joel-02#v28|Joel 2:28-29]], fulfilled on the day of Pentecost ([[Acts-01#v4|Acts 1:4-5]], [[Acts-01#v8|8]]; [[Acts-02#v1|2:1-4]], [[Acts-02#v16|16-18]]). It concerned the outpouring of the Spirit as the power from on high for the believers’ ministry economically. This is different from the Spirit of life being breathed into the disciples ([[John-20#v22|John 20:22]] and [note 1](<John-20.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Spirit>), par. 1) by the resurrected Savior on the day of His resurrection for His indwelling that He might be life to them essentially.

### Footnote 49.2 — stay

**Verse:** [[Luke-24#v49|Luke 24:49]]

Lit., sit.

### Footnote 49.3 — put

**Verse:** [[Luke-24#v49|Luke 24:49]]

Regarding the Spirit of life, we need to breathe Him in as the breath ([[John-20#v22|John 20:22]]); regarding the Spirit of power, we need to put Him on as the uniform, typified by the mantle of Elijah ([[2 Kings-02#v9|2 Kings 2:9]], [[2 Kings-02#v13|13-15]]). The former, like the water of life, requires our drinking ([[John-07#v37|John 7:37-39]]); the latter, like the water of baptism, requires our being immersed ([[Acts-01#v5|Acts 1:5]]). These are the two aspects of the one Spirit for our experience ([[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13]]). The indwelling of the Spirit of life is essential and is for our life and living; the outpouring of the Spirit of power is economical and is for our ministry and work.

## Footnotes for verse 51

### Footnote 51.1 — carried

**Verse:** [[Luke-24#v51|Luke 24:51]]

In this book Luke displays and presents mainly five crucial and excellent aspects concerning the Man-Savior: His birth, ministry, death, resurrection, and ascension. 

(1) His birth was of and with the Spirit of God essentially, making Him a God-man so that He could be the Man-Savior ([[Luke-01#v35|1:35]]). 

(2) His ministry was by and through the Spirit of God economically for the carrying out of God’s economy in His jubilee ([[Luke-04#v18|4:18-19]]). 

(3) His death was by His being the God-man to accomplish God’s redemption for man ([[Luke-23#v42|23:42-43]]) and to release Himself into man as the fire of life to burn on the earth ([[Luke-12#v49|12:49-50]]). 

(4) His resurrection was (a) God’s vindication of Him and of His work; (b) His success in all His achievements; and (c) His victory over the universal enemy of God. 

(5) His ascension was God’s exaltation of Him, which made Him the Christ of God and the Lord of all ([[Acts-02#v36|Acts 2:36]]) that He might carry out His heavenly ministry on the earth as the all-inclusive Spirit poured out from the heavens upon His Body, which is constituted of His believers ([[Acts-02#v4|Acts 2:4]], [[Acts-02#v17|17-18]]), as recorded by Luke in his further writing, the Acts.
