## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — eat

**Verse:** [[Luke-22#v15|Luke 22:15]]

The eating and drinking in [[Luke-22#v15|vv. 15-18]] were the keeping of the last Feast of the Passover before the instituting of the Lord’s supper in [[Luke-22#v19|vv. 19-20]].

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — it

**Verse:** [[Luke-22#v16|Luke 22:16]]

It, and what is mentioned in [[Luke-22#v18|v. 18]], refer to the last passover in [[Luke-22#v15|v. 15]], which will be fully fulfilled in the coming kingdom of God, when the Savior will feast with the overcoming saints ([[Luke-22#v30|v. 30]]; [[Luke-13#v28|13:28-29]]).

## Footnotes for verse 36

### Footnote 36.1 — buy

**Verse:** [[Luke-22#v36|Luke 22:36]]

At that time people carried a sword while traveling, just as they carried a purse and a bag. The Savior’s word did not mean that He wanted the disciples to arm themselves to resist the coming arrest (cf. [[Luke-22#v49|vv. 49-51]]; [[Matthew-26#v51|Matt. 26:51-54]]); rather, it indicated that the people’s attitude toward Him had changed.
