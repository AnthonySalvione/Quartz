### Footnote 10.2 — catching

**Verse:** [[Luke-05#v10|Luke 5:10]]

This was the Lord’s calling of Peter by means of a miracle in fishing. (See [note 6¹](<John-21.fn#Footnote 6.1 — abundance>), par. 2, in [[John-21|John 21]].) The Greek word for catch alive is a compound of living and to catch; hence, to catch alive, to take captive alive (in war), instead of killing. The common fishers catch fish unto death. But Peter was called by the Lord to be a fisher of men ([[Matthew-04#v19|Matt. 4:19]]) that he might catch men unto life ([[Acts-02#v38|Acts 2:38]]; [[Acts-11#v18|11:18]]).
