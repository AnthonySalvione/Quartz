---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

I [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 1.1 — commend|commend]] to you Phoebe our sister, who is a deaconess of the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 1.2 — church|church]] which is in Cenchrea,

## v2

That you receive her in the Lord in a manner [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 2.1 — worthy|worthy]] of the saints and assist her in whatever matter she may have need of you; for she herself has also been the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 2.2 — patroness|patroness]] of many, of myself as well.

## v3

Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,

## v4

Who [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 4.1 — risked|risked]] their own necks for my [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 4.2 — life|life]], to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 4.3 — churches|churches]] of the Gentiles;

## v5

And greet the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 5.1 — church|church]], which is in their [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 5.2 — house|house]]. Greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Asia unto Christ.

## v6

Greet Mary, one who has labored much for you.

## v7

Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners, who are of note among the apostles, who also were in Christ before me.

## v8

Greet Ampliatus, my beloved in the Lord.

## v9

Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved.

## v10

Greet Apelles, [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 10.1 — approved|approved]] in Christ. Greet [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 10.2 — those|those]] of the household of Aristobulus.

## v11

Greet Herodion, my kinsman. Greet [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 11.1 — those|those]] of the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.

## v12

Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Greet Persis, the beloved sister, one who has labored much in the Lord.

## v13

Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother as well as [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 13.1 — mine|mine]].

## v14

Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them.

## v15

Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them.

## v16

Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 16.1 — churches|churches]] of Christ greet you.

## v17

Now I exhort you, brothers, to mark those who make divisions and causes of [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 17.1 — stumbling|stumbling]] contrary to the teaching which you have learned, and [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 17.2 — turn|turn]] away from them.

## v18

For such men do not [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 18.1 — serve|serve]] our Lord Christ, but their own [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 18.2 — stomach|stomach]], and through smooth words and flattering speech deceive the hearts of the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 18.3 — simple|simple]].

## v19

For the report of your obedience has reached to all; therefore I rejoice over you, but I want you to be wise as to what is good and guileless as to what is evil.

## v20

Now the God of peace will [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 20.1 — crush|crush]] Satan under your feet shortly. The [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 20.2 — grace|grace]] of our Lord Jesus be with you.

## v21

Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you, as well as Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen.

## v22

I, Tertius, who write this epistle, greet you in the Lord.

## v23

Gaius, my host and host of the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, greets you, and Quartus the brother.

## v24

[[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 24.1 — The|The]] grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.

## v25

Now to Him who is able to [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 25.1 — establish|establish]] you according to [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 25.2 — my|my]] gospel, that is, the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 25.3 — mystery|mystery]], which has been kept in silence in the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 25.4 — times|times]] of the ages

## v26

But has now been manifested, and through the prophetic writings, according to the command of the [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 26.1 — eternal|eternal]] God, has been made known to all the Gentiles for the obedience of faith;

## v27

To the only [[Romans-16.fn#Footnote 27.1 — wise|wise]] God through Jesus Christ, to Him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.
