---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

There is [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — now|now]] then no [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 1.2 — condemnation|condemnation]] to those who are [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 1.3 — in|in]] Christ Jesus.

## v2

For the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — law|law]] of the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] of [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 2.3 — life|life]] has [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 2.4 — freed|freed]] [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 2.5 — me|me]] in Christ Jesus from the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 2.6 — law|law]] of sin and of death.

## v3

For that which the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — law|law]] could [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 3.2 — not|not]] do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God, sending His own Son in the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 3.3 — likeness|likeness]] of the flesh of sin and concerning sin, condemned sin in the flesh,

## v4

That the righteous requirement of the law might be [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — fulfilled|fulfilled]] in us, who do not [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 4.2 — walk|walk]] according to the flesh but according to the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 4.3 — spirit|spirit]].

## v5

For those who are according to the flesh mind the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — things|things]] of the flesh; but those who are [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 5.2 — according|according]] to the spirit, the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 5.3 — things|things]] of the Spirit.

## v6

For [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — the|the]] mind set on the flesh is [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 6.2 — death|death]], but [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 6.3 — the|the]] mind set on the spirit is [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 6.2 — death|life]] and peace.

## v7

Because the mind set on the flesh is enmity against God; for it is not subject to the law of God, for neither can it be.

## v8

And [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 8.1 — those|those]] who are [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 8.2 — in|in]] the flesh cannot please God.

## v9

But you are not in the flesh, but in the spirit, if indeed the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of God [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 9.2 — dwells|dwells]] in you. Yet [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 9.3 — if|if]] anyone does not have the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 9.4 — Spirit|Spirit]] of Christ, he is not [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 9.5 — of|of]] Him.

## v10

But if [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Christ|Christ]] is [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.2 — in|in]] you, though the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.3 — body|body]] is [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.4 — dead|dead]] because of sin, the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.5 — spirit|spirit]] is [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.6 — life|life]] because of [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 10.7 — righteousness|righteousness]].

## v11

And if the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 11.2 — the|the]] One who raised Jesus from the dead [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 11.3 — dwells|dwells]] in you, He who raised Christ from the dead will also [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 11.4 — give|give]] life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit who indwells you.

## v12

So then, brothers, we are debtors not to the flesh to [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 12.1 — live|live]] according to the flesh;

## v13

For if you live according to the flesh, you must [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 13.1 — die|die]], but if by the Spirit you [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 13.2 — put|put]] to death the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 13.3 — practices|practices]] of the body, you will live.

## v14

For as many as are [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 14.1 — led|led]] by the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] of God, these are [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 14.3 — sons|sons]] of God.

## v15

For you have not received a spirit of slavery bringing you into fear again, but you have received a [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 15.1 — spirit|spirit]] of sonship in which we cry, [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Abba|Abba]], Father!

## v16

The Spirit Himself witnesses [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 16.1 — with|with]] [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 16.2 — our|our]] spirit that [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 16.3 — we|we]] are children of God.

## v17

And if children, heirs also; on the one hand, heirs of God; on the other, joint heirs with Christ, [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 17.1 — if|if]] indeed we suffer with Him that we may also be glorified with Him.

## v18

For I [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 18.1 — consider|consider]] that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the coming glory to be revealed [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 18.2 — upon|upon]] us.

## v19

For the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 19.1 — anxious|anxious]] watching of the creation eagerly awaits the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 19.2 — revelation|revelation]] of the sons of God.

## v20

For the creation was made subject to vanity, not of its own will, but because of Him who subjected it,

## v21

In hope that the creation itself will also be [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 21.1 — freed|freed]] from the slavery of corruption into the freedom of the glory of the children of God.

## v22

For we know that the whole creation groans together and travails in pain together until now.

## v23

And not only so, but we ourselves also, who have [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 23.1 — the|the]] firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 23.2 — groan|groan]] in ourselves, eagerly awaiting [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 23.3 — sonship|sonship]], the redemption of our body.

## v24

For we were saved in hope. But a hope that is seen is not hope, for who hopes for what he sees?

## v25

But if we hope for what we do not see, we eagerly await it through endurance.

## v26

Moreover, [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 26.1 — in|in]] like manner the Spirit also joins in to help us in our [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 26.2 — weakness|weakness]], for we do not know for what we should pray as is fitting, but the Spirit Himself intercedes for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.

## v27

But He who searches the hearts knows what the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 27.1 — mind|mind]] of the Spirit is, because He intercedes for the saints [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 27.2 — according|according]] to God.

## v28

And we know that [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 28.1 — all|all]] things [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 28.2 — work|work]] together for [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 28.3 — good|good]] to those who [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 28.4 — love|love]] God, to those who are called according to His [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 28.5 — purpose|purpose]].

## v29

Because those whom He [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 29.1 — foreknew|foreknew]], He also [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 29.2 — predestinated|predestinated]] to be [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 29.3 — conformed|conformed]] to the image of His Son, that He might be the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 29.4 — Firstborn|Firstborn]] among [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 29.5 — many|many]] brothers;

## v30

And those whom He predestinated, these He also called; and those whom He called, these He also justified; and those whom He [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 30.1 — justified|justified]], these He also [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 30.2 — glorified|glorified]].

## v31

What then shall we say to these things? If God is for us, who can be against us?

## v32

Indeed, He who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, how shall He not also with Him freely give us all things?

## v33

Who shall bring a charge against God’s chosen ones? [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 33.1 — It|It]] is God who justifies.

## v34

Who is he who condemns? [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 34.1 — It|It]] is Christ Jesus who died and, rather, who was raised, who is also [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 34.2 — at|at]] the right hand of God, who also [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 34.3 — intercedes|intercedes]] for us.

## v35

Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation or anguish or persecution or famine or nakedness or peril or sword?

## v36

As it is written, “For Your sake we are being put to death all day long; we have been accounted as sheep for slaughter.”

## v37

But in all these things we [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 37.1 — more|more]] than conquer through Him who loved us.

## v38

For I am persuaded that neither death nor life nor angels nor principalities nor things present nor things to come nor powers

## v39

Nor height nor depth nor any other creature will be able to separate us from the [[Romans-08.fn#Footnote 39.1 — love|love]] of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
