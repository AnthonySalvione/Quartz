---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

What then is the advantage of the Jew? Or what is the profit of the circumcision?

## v2

Much in every way. First, that they were entrusted with the oracles of God.

## v3

For what if some disbelieved? Shall their unbelief annul the faithfulness of God?

## v4

[[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Absolutely|Absolutely]] not! But let God be true and every man a liar, as it is written, “That You may be [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 4.2 — declared|declared]] righteous in Your words and may overcome when You are judged.”

## v5

But if our unrighteousness commends the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is the God who inflicts wrath unrighteous? I speak according to man.

## v6

Absolutely not! Otherwise how shall God judge the world?

## v7

But if the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — truthfulness|truthfulness]] of God has abounded in my lie unto His glory, why still am I also being judged as a sinner?

## v8

And why not say (as we are slanderously charged and as some affirm that we say), Let us do evil that good may come? whose [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — judgment|judgment]] is just.

## v9

What then? Are we better? Not at all! For we have previously charged both Jews and Greeks that they are all under sin,

## v10

Even as it is written, “There is none righteous, not even one;

## v11

There is none who understands, there is none who seeks out God.

## v12

All have turned aside; together they have become useless; there is none who does [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — good|good]]; there is not so much as one.

## v13

Their throat is an opened grave; with their tongues they practiced deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips;

## v14

Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.

## v15

Swift are their feet to shed blood,

## v16

Destruction and misery are in their ways,

## v17

And the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — way|way]] of peace they have not known.

## v18

There is no fear of God before their eyes.”

## v19

Now we know that whatever things the law says, it speaks to those who are under the law, [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 19.1 — that|that]] every mouth may be stopped and all the world may fall under the judgment of God;

## v20

Because out of the works of the law no flesh shall be justified before Him; for through the law is the clear knowledge of sin.

## v21

But now, [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — apart|apart]] from the law, the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 21.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] of God has been [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 21.3 — manifested|manifested]], witness being borne to it by the Law and the Prophets;

## v22

Even the righteousness of God through the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — faith|faith]] of Jesus Christ to all those who believe, for there is no distinction;

## v23

For all have sinned and fall short of the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 23.1 — glory|glory]] of God,

## v24

Being [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 24.1 — justified|justified]] [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 24.2 — freely|freely]] by His grace through the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 24.3 — redemption|redemption]] which is in Christ Jesus;

## v25

Whom God [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 25.1 — set|set]] forth as a [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 25.2 — propitiation|propitiation]] place through faith in His blood, for the demonstrating of His righteousness, in that in His forbearance God [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 25.3 — passed|passed]] over the sins that had previously occurred,

## v26

With a view to the demonstrating of His righteousness in the present time, so that He might be righteous and the One who [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 26.1 — justifies|justifies]] him who is of the [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 26.2 — faith|faith]] of Jesus.

## v27

Where then is boasting? [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 27.1 — It|It]] is excluded. Through what kind of law? That of works? No, but through the law of faith.

## v28

For we account that a man is justified by faith apart from the works of the law.

## v29

Or is He the God of the Jews only? Is He not of the Gentiles also? Yes, of the Gentiles also,

## v30

If indeed God is one, who will justify the circumcision [[Romans-03.fn#Footnote 30.1 — out|out]] of faith and the uncircumcision through faith.

## v31

Do we then make the law of no effect through faith? Absolutely not! Rather, we establish the law.
