---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Or are you ignorant, brothers (for I speak to those who know the law), that the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — law|law]] lords it over the man as long as he lives?

## v2

For the married woman is bound by the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — law|law]] to her [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 2.2 — husband|husband]] while he is living; but if the husband dies, she is discharged from the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — law|law]] regarding the husband.

## v3

So then if, while the husband is living, she is joined to [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — another|another]] man, she will be called an adulteress; but if the husband dies, she is free from the law, so that she is not an adulteress, though she is joined to [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — another|another]] man.

## v4

So then, my brothers, [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — you|you]] also have been made dead to the law through the body of Christ so that [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — you|you]] might be [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 4.2 — joined|joined]] to another, to Him who has been raised from the dead, that we might [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 4.3 — bear|bear]] fruit to God.

## v5

For when we were in the flesh, the passions for sins, which acted through the law, operated in our members to bear fruit to death.

## v6

But now we have been [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — discharged|discharged]] from the law, having died to that in which we were held, so that we [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 6.2 — serve|serve]] in [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 6.3 — newness|newness]] of [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 6.4 — spirit|spirit]] and not in oldness of letter.

## v7

What then shall we say? Is the law sin? Absolutely not! But [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 7.1 — I|I]] did not know sin except through the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 7.2 — law|law]]; for neither did I know coveting, except the law had said, “[[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 7.3 — You|You]] shall not covet.”

## v8

But [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 8.1 — sin|sin]], seizing the opportunity through the commandment, worked out in me coveting of every kind; for without the law sin is dead.

## v9

And I was alive without the law once; but when the commandment came, sin revived and I died.

## v10

And the commandment, which was unto life, this very commandment was found to me to be unto [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 10.1 — death|death]].

## v11

For sin, seizing the opportunity [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — through|through]] the commandment, deceived me and through it killed me.

## v12

So then the law is holy, and the commandment holy and righteous and good.

## v13

Did then that which is good become death to me? Absolutely not! But sin did, that it might [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — be|be]] shown to be sin by working out death in me through that which is good, that sin through the commandment might become exceedingly sinful.

## v14

For we [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 14.1 — know|know]] that the law is [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 14.2 — spiritual|spiritual]]; but I am [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 14.3 — fleshy|fleshy]], sold under sin.

## v15

For what I work out, I [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 15.1 — do|do]] not acknowledge; for what I will, this I do not practice; but what I hate, this I do.

## v16

But if what I do not will, this I do, I agree with the law that it is good.

## v17

Now then it is no longer I that work it out but sin that [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 17.1 — dwells|dwells]] in me.

## v18

For I [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 18.1 — know|know]] that in me, that is, in my [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 18.2 — flesh|flesh]], [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 18.3 — nothing|nothing]] good dwells; for [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 18.4 — to|to]] will is present with me, but to work out the good is not.

## v19

For I do not do the good which I will; but the evil which I do not will, this I practice.

## v20

But if what I do not will, this I do, it is no longer I that work it out but sin that dwells in me.

## v21

I find then [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 21.1 — the|the]] law, with me who wills to do the good, because the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 21.2 — evil|evil]] is present with me.

## v22

For I delight in the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — law|law]] of God according to the inner man,

## v23

But I see a different [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 23.1 — law|law]] in my members, [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 23.2 — warring|warring]] against the law of my mind and making me a [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 23.3 — captive|captive]] to the law of sin which is in my members.

## v24

[[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 24.1 — Wretched|Wretched]] man that I am! Who will deliver me from [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 24.2 — the|the]] body of [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 24.3 — this|this]] death?

## v25

Thanks be to God, through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 25.1 — with|with]] the mind I myself [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 25.2 — serve|serve]] the law of God, but with the flesh, the [[Romans-07.fn#Footnote 25.3 — law|law]] of sin.
