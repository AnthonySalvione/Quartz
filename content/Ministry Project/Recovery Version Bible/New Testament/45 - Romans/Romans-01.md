---
## v1

[[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Paul|Paul]], a [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — slave|slave]] of [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Christ|Christ]] [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Jesus|Jesus]], a [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.5 — called|called]] [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.6 — apostle|apostle]], [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.7 — separated|separated]] [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.8 — unto|unto]] the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 1.9 — gospel|gospel]] of God,

## v2

[[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Which|Which]] He promised [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — beforehand|beforehand]] through His prophets in the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — holy|holy]] Scriptures,

## v3

Concerning [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — His|His]] Son, who [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — came|came]] [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — out|out]] of the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 3.4 — seed|seed]] of David according to the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 3.5 — flesh|flesh]],

## v4

Who was [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — designated|designated]] the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Son|Son]] of God in [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — power|power]] according to the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 4.4 — Spirit|Spirit]] of [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 4.5 — holiness|holiness]] [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 4.6 — out|out]] of the resurrection of the dead, Jesus Christ our Lord;

## v5

Through whom we have received [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — grace|grace]] and [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — apostleship|apostleship]] unto the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — obedience|obedience]] of [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 5.4 — faith|faith]] among all the Gentiles [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 5.5 — on|on]] behalf of His name,

## v6

Among whom you also are the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — called|called]] ones of Jesus Christ;

## v7

To all who are in Rome, beloved of God, the called [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — saints|saints]]: [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Grace|Grace]] to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v8

First, I thank my God through Jesus Christ for you all because your faith is proclaimed throughout the whole world.

## v9

For God is my witness, whom I [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — serve|serve]] in my [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — spirit|spirit]] in the gospel of His Son, how unceasingly I make mention of you always in my prayers,

## v10

Beseeching if by any means now at last I may be prospered in the will of God to come to you.

## v11

For I long to see you that I may impart to you some spiritual gift so that you may be established;

## v12

That is, that I with you may be [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — encouraged|encouraged]] among you through the faith which is in one another, both yours and mine.

## v13

And I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that often I purposed to come to you (yet have been hindered until the present), that I might have some fruit among you also, even as among the rest of the Gentiles.

## v14

I am debtor both to Greeks and to [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — barbarians|barbarians]], both to wise and to foolish;

## v15

So, for my part, I am ready to announce the gospel to you also who are in Rome.

## v16

For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — power|power]] of God unto [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — salvation|salvation]] to everyone who believes, both to Jew first and to Greek.

## v17

For the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — righteousness|righteousness]] of God is revealed in it [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — out|out]] of faith to faith, as it is written, “But the righteous one shall [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 17.3 — have|have]] life and live by [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 17.4 — faith|faith]].”

## v18

[[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — For|For]] the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — wrath|wrath]] of God is revealed from heaven [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 18.3 — upon|upon]] all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men who [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 18.4 — hold|hold]] down [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 18.5 — the|the]] truth in unrighteousness,

## v19

Because that which is known of God is manifest [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — within|within]] them, for God manifested it to them.

## v20

For the invisible things of Him, both His eternal power and [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — divine|divine]] characteristics, have been clearly seen since the creation of the world, [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — being|being]] perceived by the things made, so that they would be without excuse;

## v21

Because though [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — they|they]] knew God, they did not glorify Him as God or thank Him, but rather became [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — vain|vain]] in their reasonings, and their heart, lacking understanding, was darkened.

## v22

Professing to be wise, they became fools

## v23

And [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — changed|changed]] the glory of the incorruptible God into the likeness of an image of corruptible man and of birds and four-footed animals and reptiles.

## v24

Therefore God [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — gave|gave]] them up in the lusts of their hearts to uncleanness, so that they dishonor their bodies among themselves,

## v25

Who exchanged the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — truth|truth]] of God for the lie, and worshipped and served the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen.

## v26

Therefore God gave them up to passions of dishonor; for their females exchanged the natural use for that which is contrary to nature;

## v27

And likewise also the males, leaving the natural use of the female, burned in their craving toward one another, males with males committing unseemliness and fully receiving in themselves the retribution of their error which was due.

## v28

And even as they did not approve of holding God in their full knowledge, God gave them up to a disapproved mind, to do the things which are not fitting,

## v29

Being filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, covetousness, malice; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, malignity;

## v30

Whisperers, slanderers, hateful to God, insolent, arrogant, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

## v31

Senseless, faithless, affectionless, merciless;

## v32

Who, though fully knowing the [[Romans-01.fn#Footnote 32.1 — righteous|righteous]] judgment of God, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, not only do them, but also have fellow delight in those who practice them.
