---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Brothers, the good pleasure of my heart and my petition to God for them is for their salvation.

## v2

For I bear them witness that they have a zeal for God, but not according to full knowledge;

## v3

For because they were ignorant of God’s righteousness and sought to [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 3.1 — establish|establish]] their own righteousness, they were not subject to the righteousness of God.

## v4

For Christ is the [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 4.1 — end|end]] of the law unto righteousness to everyone who believes.

## v5

For Moses writes concerning the righteousness which is out of the law: “The man who does them shall live by them.”

## v6

But the righteousness which is out of faith speaks in this way, Do not say in your heart, “Who will ascend into heaven?” that is, to [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 6.1 — bring|bring]] Christ down;

## v7

Or, Who will descend into the [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 7.1 — abyss|abyss]]? that is, to [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 7.2 — bring|bring]] Christ up from the dead.

## v8

But what does it say? “[[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 8.1 — The|The]] word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart,” that is, the word of the faith which we proclaim,

## v9

That if you confess with your [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 9.1 — mouth|mouth]] Jesus as Lord and believe in your heart that God has [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 9.2 — raised|raised]] Him from the dead, you will be saved;

## v10

For [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 10.1 — with|with]] the heart there is believing [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 10.2 — unto|unto]] righteousness, and [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 10.1 — with|with]] the mouth there is confession [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 10.2 — unto|unto]] salvation.

## v11

For the Scripture says, “Everyone who believes on Him shall not be put to shame.”

## v12

For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, for the same Lord is Lord of all and [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 12.1 — rich|rich]] to all who call upon Him;

## v13

For “whoever [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 13.1 — calls|calls]] upon the name of the Lord shall be [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 13.2 — saved|saved]].”

## v14

How then shall they call upon Him into whom they have not [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 14.1 — believed|believed]]? And how shall they believe into Him of whom they have not [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 14.2 — heard|heard]]? And how shall they hear without one who proclaims Him?

## v15

And how shall they proclaim Him unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who announce the news of good things!”

## v16

But not all have [[Romans-10.fn#Footnote 16.1 — obeyed|obeyed]] the gospel, for Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed what he has heard from us?”

## v17

So faith comes out of hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.

## v18

But I say, Have they not heard? Yes surely: “Their voice has gone out into all the earth and their words to the ends of the inhabited earth.”

## v19

But I say, Did Israel not know this? First, Moses says, “I will provoke you to jealousy by them who are not a nation; by a nation without understanding I will anger you.”

## v20

And Isaiah is very bold and says, “I was found by those who did not seek Me; I became manifest to those who did not ask for Me.”

## v21

But with regard to Israel he says, “All the day long I have stretched out My hands to a people who disobey and contradict.”
