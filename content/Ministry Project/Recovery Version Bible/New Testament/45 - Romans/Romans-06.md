---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

What then shall we say? Should we continue in sin that grace may abound?

## v2

Absolutely not! We who have died to sin, how shall we still live in it?

## v3

Or are you ignorant that all of us who have been [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — baptized|baptized]] into [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Christ|Christ]] Jesus have been baptized into [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 3.3 — His|His]] death?

## v4

We have been [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — buried|buried]] therefore with Him through [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — baptism|baptism]] into His death, in order that just as Christ was raised from the dead through the [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 4.3 — glory|glory]] of the Father, so also we might [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 4.4 — walk|walk]] in newness of life.

## v5

For if we have [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — grown|grown]] together with Him in the [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — likeness|likeness]] of His death, indeed we will also be [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 5.3 — in|in]] the [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — likeness|likeness]] of His resurrection,

## v6

[[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Knowing|Knowing]] this, that our [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — old|old]] man has been [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 6.3 — crucified|crucified]] with Him in order that the [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 6.4 — body|body]] of sin might be [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 6.5 — annulled|annulled]], that we should no longer serve sin as slaves;

## v7

For he who has died is justified from [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — sin|sin]].

## v8

Now if we have died [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — with|with]] Christ, we believe that we will also live [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — with|with]] Him,

## v9

Knowing that Christ, having been raised from the dead, dies [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — no|no]] more; death lords it over Him no more.

## v10

For the death which He died, He died to sin once for all; but the life which He lives, He lives to God.

## v11

So also you, [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — reckon|reckon]] yourselves to be dead to sin, but living to God in Christ Jesus.

## v12

[[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Do|Do]] not let sin therefore reign in your mortal body so that you obey [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — the|the]] body’s lusts;

## v13

Neither present your members as weapons of unrighteousness to sin, but [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — present|present]] yourselves to God as alive from the dead, and your members as [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 13.2 — weapons|weapons]] of righteousness to God.

## v14

For sin will not [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — lord|lord]] it over you, [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 14.2 — for|for]] you are [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 14.3 — not|not]] under the law but under grace.

## v15

What then? Should we sin, because we are not under the law but under grace? Absolutely not!

## v16

Do you not know that to whom you present yourselves as slaves for obedience, his slaves you are whom you obey, whether of sin [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unto|unto]] death or of obedience [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unto|unto]] righteousness?

## v17

But thanks be to God that though you were slaves of sin, you have obeyed from the heart the form of teaching into which you were delivered.

## v18

And having been [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — freed|freed]] from sin, you were enslaved to righteousness.

## v19

I speak in human terms because of the weakness of your flesh. For just as you presented your members as slaves to uncleanness and lawlessness unto lawlessness, so now present your members as slaves to righteousness [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 19.1 — unto|unto]] [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 19.2 — sanctification|sanctification]].

## v20

For when you were slaves of sin, you were free with regard to righteousness.

## v21

What fruit then did you have at that time? Things of which you are now ashamed, for the end of those things is death.

## v22

But now, having been freed from sin and enslaved to God, you have your fruit unto [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 22.1 — sanctification|sanctification]], and the end, eternal [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 22.2 — life|life]].

## v23

For the [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 23.1 — wages|wages]] of sin is [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 23.2 — death|death]], but the gift of God is [[Romans-06.fn#Footnote 23.3 — eternal|eternal]] life in Christ Jesus our Lord.
