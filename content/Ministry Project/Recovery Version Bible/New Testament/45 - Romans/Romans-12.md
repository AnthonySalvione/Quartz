---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — I|I]] [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.2 — exhort|exhort]] you therefore, brothers, through the [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.3 — compassions|compassions]] of God to [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.4 — present|present]] your bodies a [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.5 — living|living]] sacrifice, holy, well pleasing to God, which is your [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.6 — reasonable|reasonable]] [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 1.7 — service|service]].

## v2

And do [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — not|not]] be fashioned according to [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 2.2 — this|this]] age, but be [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 2.3 — transformed|transformed]] by the [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 2.4 — renewing|renewing]] of the mind that you may [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 2.5 — prove|prove]] what the [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 2.6 — will|will]] of God is, that which is good and well pleasing and perfect.

## v3

For I say, through the grace given to me, to everyone who is among you, not to [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — think|think]] more highly of himself than he ought to think, but to think so as to be sober-minded, as God has apportioned to each a measure of faith.

## v4

For just as in one body we have many members, and all the members do not have the same [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 4.1 — function|function]],

## v5

So we who are many are one Body [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 5.1 — in|in]] Christ, and individually [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 5.2 — members|members]] one of another.

## v6

And having [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 6.1 — gifts|gifts]] that differ according to the grace given to us, whether [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 6.2 — prophecy|prophecy]], let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith;

## v7

Or [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 7.1 — service|service]], let us be faithful in that service; or he who teaches, in that teaching;

## v8

Or he who exhorts, in that exhortation; he who [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 8.1 — gives|gives]], in simplicity; he who [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 8.2 — leads|leads]], in diligence; he who [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 8.3 — shows|shows]] mercy, in cheerfulness.

## v9

[[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Let|Let]] love be without hypocrisy. Abhor what is evil; cling to what is good.

## v10

Love one another warmly in brotherly love; take the lead in showing honor one to another.

## v11

Do not be slothful in zeal, but be [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 11.1 — burning|burning]] in spirit, [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 11.2 — serving|serving]] the Lord.

## v12

Rejoice in hope; endure in tribulation; persevere in prayer.

## v13

[[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Contribute|Contribute]] to the needs of the saints; pursue hospitality.

## v14

[[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Bless|Bless]] those who persecute you; bless and do not curse.

## v15

[[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Rejoice|Rejoice]] with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.

## v16

Be of the same mind toward one another, not setting your mind on the high things but going along with the [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 16.1 — lowly|lowly]]; do not be wise in yourselves.

## v17

Repay no one evil for evil; [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 17.1 — take|take]] forethought for things honorable in the sight of all men.

## v18

If possible, as far as it depends on you, live in peace with all men.

## v19

Do not [[Romans-12.fn#Footnote 19.1 — avenge|avenge]] yourselves, beloved, but give place to the wrath of God, for it is written, “Vengeance is Mine; I will repay, says the Lord.”

## v20

But “if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him a drink; for in doing this you will heap coals of fire upon his head.”

## v21

Do not be conquered by evil, but conquer evil with good.
