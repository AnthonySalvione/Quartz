## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — judgment

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v2|Romans 2:2]]

Referring to God's eternal judgment, which will be carried out mainly at the great white throne, as revealed in [[Revelation-20#v11|Rev. 20:11-15]]. See [note 10¹](<Romans-14.fn#Footnote 10.1 — judgment>) in [[Romans-14|Romans 14]].

### Footnote 2.2 — truth

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v2|Romans 2:2]]

Truth here and in [[Romans-02#v8|vv. 8]], 20 denotes the reality revealed through the creation and through the Bible concerning God, the universe, man, the relationship between man and God, the relationships among men, and man’s obligation to God. It also refers to the actual situation and condition of man, and it may be the terms of God’s judgment upon man, as indicated in [[Romans-02#v6|vv. 6-15]]. According to these terms God will execute His judgment righteously.

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — by

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v14|Romans 2:14]]

The nature of man, as created by God, was originally good and corresponded with God and with His law. Although it was poisoned by the fall, this good nature remains within man. Hence, if anyone lives according to his nature and does by his nature the things of the law, the evil in him will be restricted.

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — conscience

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v15|Romans 2:15]]

Man’s conscience corresponds with the God-created human nature and enables man to know what God justifies and what He condemns.

## Footnotes for verse 17

### Footnote 17.1 — But

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v17|Romans 2:17]]

In this section, which specifically deals with God’s condemnation on the religious, Paul first points out the pitiful condition of the Jews, who represent the religious people. They have only the vanity of their religious rituals, letters, and knowledge; they do not have God as their reality.

### Footnote 17.2 — boast

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v17|Romans 2:17]]

In the Jewish religion God is known only outwardly and objectively, not inwardly and subjectively. This shows the vanity of human religion.

### Footnote 28.1 — For

### Footnote 28.2 — outwardly

## Footnotes for verse 29

### Footnote 29.1 — But

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v29|Romans 2:29]]

Lit., But the Jew in what is hidden is a Jew.

### Footnote 29.2 — in

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v29|Romans 2:29]]

I.e., based on. Whatever we are, whatever we do, and whatever we have must be in spirit. This will keep us from the vanity of religion. See [note 9²](<Romans-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — spirit>) in [[Romans-01|Romans 1]]. The reality of all spiritual things depends on the Spirit of God, and the Spirit of God is in our spirit. Hence, the reality of all spiritual matters depends on our spirit, not on anything apart from our spirit. Whatever is in us is vanity unless it is in our spirit. Everything that God is to us is in our spirit.

### Footnote 29.3 — from

**Verse:** [[Romans-02#v29|Romans 2:29]]

Lit., out of.
