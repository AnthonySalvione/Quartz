---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Therefore having been justified out of faith, we have peace [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — toward|toward]] God through our Lord Jesus Christ,

## v2

Through whom also we have obtained access by [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — faith|faith]] into this [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 2.2 — grace|grace]] in which we [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 2.3 — stand|stand]] and [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 2.4 — boast|boast]] because of the [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 2.5 — hope|hope]] of the glory of God.

## v3

And not only so, but we also boast in our [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — tribulations|tribulations]], knowing that tribulation produces [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 3.2 — endurance|endurance]];

## v4

And endurance, [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — approvedness|approvedness]]; and approvedness, hope;

## v5

And hope does not put us to shame, because the [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — love|love]] of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us.

## v6

For while we were yet [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — weak|weak]], in due time Christ died for the ungodly.

## v7

For scarcely for a righteous man will anyone die, though perhaps for the good man someone would even dare to die.

## v8

But God commends His own love to us in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.

## v9

Much more then, having now been justified in His blood, we will be saved through Him from the wrath.

## v10

For if we, being enemies, were [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — reconciled|reconciled]] to God through the death of His Son, [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 10.2 — much|much]] more we will be [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 10.3 — saved|saved]] [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 10.4 — in|in]] His [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 10.5 — life|life]], having been reconciled,

## v11

And not only so, but also [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — boasting|boasting]] in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the reconciliation.

## v12

Therefore just as through [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — one|one]] man [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 12.2 — sin|sin]] entered into the [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 12.3 — world|world]], and through sin, [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 12.4 — death|death]]; and thus [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 12.4 — death|death]] passed on to all men because all have sinned —

## v13

For until the law sin was in the world, but sin is not [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — charged|charged]] to one’s account when there is no law.

## v14

But [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — death|death]] reigned from [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Adam|Adam]] until Moses, even over those who had not sinned after the likeness of Adam’s [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 14.3 — transgression|transgression]], who is a [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 14.4 — type|type]] of Him who was to come.

## v15

But it is not that as the offense was, so also the gracious gift is; for if by the offense of the one the many died, much more the grace of God and the free gift in grace of the one [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — man|man]] Jesus Christ have [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 15.2 — abounded|abounded]] to the many.

## v16

And it is not that as through one who sinned, so also the free [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — gift|gift]] is; for the judgment was out of one offense unto condemnation, but the gracious gift is out of many offenses unto justification.

## v17

For if, by the offense of the one, death reigned through the one, much more those who receive the [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — abundance|abundance]] of grace and of the gift of [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 17.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] will [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 17.3 — reign|reign]] in [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 17.4 — life|life]] through the One, Jesus Christ.

## v18

So then as it was through one offense unto condemnation to all men, so also it was through one [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 18.1 — righteous|righteous]] act unto justification of [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 18.2 — life|life]] to all men.

## v19

For just as through the disobedience of one man the many were [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — constituted|constituted]] sinners, so also through the [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 19.2 — obedience|obedience]] of the One the many will be [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — constituted|constituted]] righteous.

## v20

And the law entered in alongside that the offense might [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — abound|abound]]; but where sin [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — abound|abounded]], grace has superabounded,

## v21

In order that just as [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 21.1 — sin|sin]] reigned in death, so also grace might reign through [[Romans-05.fn#Footnote 21.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] unto eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
