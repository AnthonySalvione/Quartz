---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is to [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — judge|judge]] the living and the dead, and by [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — His|His]] appearing and His kingdom:

## v2

Proclaim the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — word|word]]; [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.2 — be|be]] ready [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.3 — in|in]] season and out of season; convict, [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.4 — rebuke|rebuke]], exhort with [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.5 — all|all]] long-suffering and teaching.

## v3

For the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — time|time]] will come when they will not tolerate the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — healthy|healthy]] teaching; but according to their own lusts they will heap up to themselves teachers, having [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.3 — itching|itching]] ears,

## v4

And they will [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — turn|turn]] away their ear from the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — truth|truth]] and will be turned aside to [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — myths|myths]].

## v5

But you, be sober in all things, suffer evil, do the work of an evangelist, [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — fully|fully]] accomplish your [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — ministry|ministry]].

## v6

For I am already being [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — poured|poured]] out, and the time of my [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — departure|departure]] is at hand.

## v7

I have [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — fought|fought]] the good fight; I have [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — finished|finished]] the course; I have kept the faith.

## v8

Henceforth there is [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — laid|laid]] up for me the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.2 — crown|crown]] of righteousness, with which the Lord, the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.3 — righteous|righteous]] Judge, will recompense me in [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.4 — that|that]] day, and not only me but also all those who have loved His [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.5 — appearing|appearing]].

## v9

Be diligent to come to me quickly;

## v10

For Demas has abandoned me, having [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — loved|loved]] the present age, and has gone to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia; Titus to [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Dalmatia|Dalmatia]].

## v11

Luke alone is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for the ministry.

## v12

But Tychicus I have sent to Ephesus.

## v13

The [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — cloak|cloak]] which I left in [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Troas|Troas]] with Carpus, bring when you come, and the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 13.3 — scrolls|scrolls]], especially the parchments.

## v14

Alexander the coppersmith did many evil things to me; the Lord will recompense him according to his works.

## v15

You also guard against him, for he greatly opposed our words.

## v16

At my first defense no one was with me to support me, but all abandoned me. May it not be counted against them.

## v17

But the Lord stood with me and empowered me that through me the proclamation of the gospel might be fully accomplished and all the Gentiles might hear; and I was delivered out of the [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — lion’s|lion’s]] mouth.

## v18

The Lord will deliver me from every evil work and will save me into His [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 18.1 — heavenly|heavenly]] kingdom, to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

## v19

Greet Prisca and Aquila and the house of Onesiphorus.

## v20

Erastus remained in Corinth, and Trophimus I left at [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Miletus|Miletus]] [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 20.2 — sick|sick]].

## v21

Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as well as Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers.

## v22

The Lord be with your [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 22.1 — spirit|spirit]]. [[2 TImothy-04.fn#Footnote 22.2 — Grace|Grace]] be with you.
