---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God according to the [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — promise|promise]] of life, which is in Christ Jesus,

## v2

To Timothy, beloved child: Grace, [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — mercy|mercy]], peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

## v3

I thank God, whom I [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — serve|serve]] [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — from|from]] my forefathers in a [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — pure|pure]] conscience, while unceasingly I have remembrance concerning you in my petitions night and day,

## v4

Longing to see you, remembering your tears, that I may be filled with joy;

## v5

Having been reminded of the unfeigned faith in you, which [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — dwelt|dwelt]] first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am persuaded dwells also in you.

## v6

For which cause I remind you to [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — fan|fan]] into flame the gift of God, which is in you through the [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — laying|laying]] on of my hands.

## v7

For God has not given us a [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — spirit|spirit]] of cowardice, but [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — of|of]] power and of love and of sobermindedness.

## v8

[[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] do [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — not|not]] be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord nor of me His prisoner; but suffer evil with the [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — gospel|gospel]] [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 8.4 — according|according]] to the power of God;

## v9

Who has saved us and [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — called|called]] us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to His own [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — purpose|purpose]] and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — before|before]] the times of the ages

## v10

But now has been [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — manifested|manifested]] through the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — nullified|nullified]] death and brought [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 10.3 — life|life]] and [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 10.4 — incorruption|incorruption]] to light through the gospel,

## v11

For [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — which|which]] I was appointed a [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — herald|herald]] and an apostle and a teacher.

## v12

For which [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — cause|cause]] also I suffer these things; but I am [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — not|not]] ashamed, for I know [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — whom|whom]] I have believed, and I am persuaded that He is able to guard [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 12.4 — my|my]] deposit unto that [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 12.5 — day|day]].

## v13

Hold a [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — pattern|pattern]] of the [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — healthy|healthy]] words that you have heard from me, in the [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — faith|faith]] and love which are in Christ Jesus.

## v14

Guard the good [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — deposit|deposit]] through the Holy Spirit who [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — dwells|dwells]] in us.

## v15

This you know, that all who are in [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Asia|Asia]] [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — turned|turned]] away from me, of whom are [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — Phygelus|Phygelus]] and Hermogenes.

## v16

May the Lord grant mercy to the house of [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Onesiphorus|Onesiphorus]], for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain;

## v17

But being in Rome, he sought me out diligently and found me.

## v18

May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord in [[2 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — that|that]] day. And in how many things he served me in Ephesus, you know best.
