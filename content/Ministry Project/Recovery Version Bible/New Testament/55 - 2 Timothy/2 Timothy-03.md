---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — But|But]] know this, that in the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — last|last]] days [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 1.3 — difficult|difficult]] times will come.

## v2

For [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — men|men]] will be [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — lovers|lovers]] of self, lovers of money, boasters, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — arrogant|arrogant]], [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.4 — revilers|revilers]], disobedient to parents, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.5 — unthankful|unthankful]], unholy,

## v3

Without natural affection, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — implacable|implacable]], slanderers, without self-control, savage, not lovers of good,

## v4

[[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Traitors|Traitors]], [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 4.2 — reckless|reckless]], [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 4.3 — blinded|blinded]] with pride, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 4.4 — lovers|lovers]] of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

## v5

Having an [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — outward|outward]] form of godliness, though denying its [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — power|power]]; from these also turn away.

## v6

For of these are those who creep into houses and take captive silly women heaped with sins, who are led by various desires

## v7

And are always learning yet never able to come to the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — full|full]] knowledge of the truth.

## v8

And in the manner that [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Jannes|Jannes]] and Jambres opposed Moses, so these also oppose the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — truth|truth]]; men corrupted in mind, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.3 — disapproved|disapproved]] concerning the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.4 — faith|faith]].

## v9

But they will not advance farther, for their [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — folly|folly]] will be completely manifest to all, as also the folly of [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — those|those]] became.

## v10

But you have closely followed my teaching, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — conduct|conduct]], [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — purpose|purpose]], faith, long-suffering, love, endurance,

## v11

Persecutions, sufferings, such as befell me in Antioch, in Iconium, in [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Lystra|Lystra]]. Such persecutions I bore, and out of them all the Lord delivered me.

## v12

And indeed all who [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — desire|desire]] to [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 12.2 — live|live]] godly in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.

## v13

But evil men and [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — impostors|impostors]] will grow worse and worse, deceiving and being deceived.

## v14

But you, [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — continue|continue]] in the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — things|things]] which you have learned and have been assured of, knowing from which ones you have learned them

## v15

[[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — And|And]] that from a babe you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise unto salvation through the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — faith|faith]] which is in Christ Jesus.

## v16

[[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — All|All]] Scripture is [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — God-breathed|God-breathed]] and profitable for teaching, for [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.3 — conviction|conviction]], for [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.4 — correction|correction]], for [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.5 — instruction|instruction]] in righteousness,

## v17

That the [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — man|man]] of God may be [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 17.2 — complete|complete]], fully [[2 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 17.3 — equipped|equipped]] for every good work.
