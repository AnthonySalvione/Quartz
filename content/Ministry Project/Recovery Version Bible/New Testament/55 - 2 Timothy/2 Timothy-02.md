---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

You therefore, my child, be [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — empowered|empowered]] in the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — grace|grace]] which is in Christ Jesus;

## v2

And the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — things|things]] which you have heard from me through many witnesses, these [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — commit|commit]] to [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 2.3 — faithful|faithful]] men, who will be competent to teach others also.

## v3

Suffer evil with me as a good [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — soldier|soldier]] of Christ Jesus.

## v4

No one serving as a soldier [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — entangles|entangles]] himself with the affairs of this [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — life|life]], that he may please the one who enlisted him.

## v5

And also if anyone [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — contends|contends]] in the games, he is not [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 5.2 — crowned|crowned]] unless he contends lawfully.

## v6

The laboring [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — farmer|farmer]] must be the first to partake of the fruit.

## v7

Consider what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in all things.

## v8

Remember Jesus Christ, [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — raised|raised]] from the dead, of the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — seed|seed]] of David, according to [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — my|my]] gospel,

## v9

In which I suffer evil unto bonds as a criminal; but the word of God is [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — not|not]] bound.

## v10

Therefore I endure all things for the sake of the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — chosen|chosen]] ones, that they themselves also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — with|with]] eternal glory.

## v11

Faithful is the word: [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — For|For]] if we [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — died|died]] with Him, we will also [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 11.3 — live|live]] with Him;

## v12

If we [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — endure|endure]], we will also [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — reign|reign]] with Him; if we [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — deny|deny]] Him, He also will deny us;

## v13

If we are faithless, He remains [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — faithful|faithful]], for He [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — cannot|cannot]] deny Himself.

## v14

Remind them of these things, solemnly charging them before God not to have contentions of words, which is useful for nothing, to the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — ruin|ruin]] of those who hear.

## v15

Be diligent to present yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman, [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — cutting|cutting]] straight the word of the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — truth|truth]].

## v16

But avoid [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — profane|profane]], vain babblings, for they will advance to more [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 16.2 — ungodliness|ungodliness]],

## v17

And their word will [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — spread|spread]] like [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — gangrene|gangrene]], of whom are Hymenaeus and Philetus,

## v18

Who concerning the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — truth|truth]] have [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 18.2 — misaimed|misaimed]], saying that the resurrection has [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 18.3 — already|already]] taken place, and overthrow the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 18.4 — faith|faith]] of some.

## v19

However the firm [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — foundation|foundation]] of God stands, having this [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 19.2 — seal|seal]], The Lord knows those who are His, and, Let everyone who names the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.

## v20

[[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — But|But]] in a [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 20.2 — great|great]] house there are not only [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 20.3 — gold|gold]] and silver vessels but also [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 20.4 — wooden|wooden]] and earthen; and some are unto honor, and some unto dishonor.

## v21

If therefore anyone [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — cleanses|cleanses]] himself from [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 21.2 — these|these]], he will be a vessel [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 21.3 — unto|unto]] honor, sanctified, useful to the master, prepared unto every good work.

## v22

But flee youthful [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — lusts|lusts]], and pursue [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 22.2 — righteousness|righteousness]], faith, love, peace with those who [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 22.3 — call|call]] on the Lord out of a [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 22.4 — pure|pure]] heart.

## v23

But [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — foolish|foolish]] questionings and those arising from an [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — untrained|untrained]] mind refuse, knowing that they beget contentions.

## v24

But a slave of the Lord ought not to contend but be gentle toward all, apt to teach, bearing with wrong;

## v25

In meekness correcting those who oppose, if perhaps God may give them [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 25.1 — repentance|repentance]] unto the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 25.2 — full|full]] knowledge of the truth,

## v26

And they may [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 26.1 — return|return]] to soberness out of the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 26.2 — snare|snare]] of the [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 26.3 — devil|devil]], having been caught alive by him, unto [[2 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 26.4 — His|His]] will.
