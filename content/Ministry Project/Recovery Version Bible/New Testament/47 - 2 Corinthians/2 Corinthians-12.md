---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

To boast is [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — necessary|necessary]], though indeed not expedient; yet I will [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 1.2 — come|come]] to [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 1.3 — visions|visions]] and revelations of the Lord.

## v2

I know a [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — man|man]] in Christ, fourteen years ago (whether in the body I do not know, or outside the body I do not know; God knows) such a one was [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 2.2 — caught|caught]] away [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 2.3 — to|to]] the [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 2.4 — third|third]] heaven.

## v3

[[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — And|And]] I know such a man (whether in the body or [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — outside|outside]] the body, I do not know; God knows),

## v4

That he was caught away into [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Paradise|Paradise]] and heard unspeakable words, which it is not allowed for a man to speak.

## v5

On behalf of such a one I will boast, but on behalf of myself I will not boast, except in my weaknesses.

## v6

For if I desire to boast, I will not be foolish, for I will speak the truth; but I refrain lest anyone account of me something above what he sees me to be or hears from me.

## v7

And because of the [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 7.1 — transcendence|transcendence]] of the revelations, in order that I might not be exceedingly lifted up, there was given to me a [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 7.2 — thorn|thorn]] in the flesh, a messenger of Satan, that he might [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 7.3 — buffet|buffet]] me, in order that I might not be exceedingly lifted up.

## v8

Concerning this I entreated the Lord three times that it might depart from me.

## v9

And He has said to me, My [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — grace|grace]] is sufficient for you, for My [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 9.2 — power|power]] is perfected in weakness. Most gladly therefore I will rather boast in my weaknesses that the power of Christ might [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 9.3 — tabernacle|tabernacle]] over me.

## v10

Therefore I am [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 10.1 — well|well]] pleased in weaknesses, in [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 10.2 — insults|insults]], in [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 10.3 — necessities|necessities]], in persecutions and [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 10.4 — distresses|distresses]], on behalf of Christ; for when I am [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 10.5 — weak|weak]], then I am [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 10.5 — weak|powerful]].

## v11

I have become foolish; you yourselves have compelled me. For it is I who should have been commended by you; for in nothing am I inferior to the [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 11.1 — super-apostles|super-apostles]], even though I am nothing.

## v12

Indeed the [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 12.1 — signs|signs]] of an apostle were wrought among you in all endurance by [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 12.2 — signs|signs]] and [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 12.3 — wonders|wonders]] and [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 12.4 — works|works]] of power.

## v13

For what is there in which you were [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 13.1 — treated|treated]] in a manner inferior to the rest of the churches, except that I myself was not a burden to you? [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Graciously|Graciously]] forgive me this [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Graciously|injustice]]!

## v14

Behold, this third time I am ready to come to you, and I will not be a burden; for I do not seek what is yours but you. For the children ought not to store up for the parents, but the parents for the children.

## v15

But I, I will most gladly [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 15.1 — spend|spend]] and [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 15.2 — be|be]] utterly spent on behalf of your souls. If I love you more abundantly, am I loved less?

## v16

But [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 16.1 — let|let]] it be so! I did not burden you; but, as some of you say, being [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 16.2 — crafty|crafty]], I took you by guile.

## v17

Did I take advantage of you through anyone whom I have sent to you?

## v18

I entreated Titus and sent with him the brother. Titus did not take advantage of you, did he? Did we not walk in the same [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 18.1 — spirit|spirit]]? In the same steps?

## v19

All this time you have been thinking that we are defending ourselves to you. [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Before|Before]] God in Christ we speak; but all things, beloved, are for your building up.

## v20

For I fear that perhaps when I come, I may find you not such as I wish, and I may be found by you not such as you wish; that perhaps there may be [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 20.1 — strife|strife]], jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambitions, [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 20.2 — slanders|slanders]], [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 20.3 — whisperings|whisperings]], [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 20.4 — demonstrations|demonstrations]] of being puffed up, [[2 Corinthians-12.fn#Footnote 20.5 — tumults|tumults]];

## v21

That when I come again my God may humble me before you and I may mourn over many who have sinned before and who have not repented of the uncleanness and fornication and licentiousness which they have practiced.
