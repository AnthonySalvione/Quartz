---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — will|will]] of God, and Timothy the brother, to the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — church|church]] of God which is [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — in|in]] Corinth, [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — with|with]] all the saints who are in the whole of [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.5 — Achaia|Achaia]]:

## v2

Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v3

Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — compassions|compassions]] and God of all [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — comfort|comfort]],

## v4

Who comforts us in all our affliction that we may be [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — able|able]] to comfort those who are in every affliction through the comforting with which we ourselves are comforted by God.

## v5

For even as the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — sufferings|sufferings]] of [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — the|the]] Christ abound unto us, so through [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — the|the]] Christ our comfort also abounds.

## v6

But whether we are afflicted, it is for your comforting and salvation; or whether we are comforted, it is for your comforting, which operates in the endurance of the same sufferings which we also suffer.

## v7

And our hope for you is firm, knowing that as you are partakers of the sufferings, so also you are of the comfort.

## v8

For we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, of our affliction which befell us in Asia, that we were excessively [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — burdened|burdened]], beyond our power, so that we [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — despaired|despaired]] even of [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — living|living]].

## v9

[[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Indeed|Indeed]] we ourselves had the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — response|response]] of death in ourselves, that we should not base our confidence on ourselves but on [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — God|God]], who raises the dead;

## v10

Who has delivered us out of so great a death, and [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — will|will]] deliver us; in whom we have hoped that He will also yet deliver us,

## v11

If you also [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — help|help]] in this by petition on our behalf, that for the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — gift|gift]] to us through many, thanks may be given by many [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — persons|persons]] on our behalf.

## v12

For our boasting is this, the testimony of our [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — conscience|conscience]], that in [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — singleness|singleness]] and [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — sincerity|sincerity]] of God, not in [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.4 — fleshly|fleshly]] wisdom but in the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.5 — grace|grace]] of God, we have conducted ourselves in the world, and more abundantly toward you.

## v13

For no other things do we write to you than what you read or even know; and I hope that you will know unto the end,

## v14

Even as also you know us in part, that we are your boast, just as you also are ours in the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — day|day]] of our Lord Jesus.

## v15

And in this confidence I intended to come to you previously that you might have [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — double|double]] grace,

## v16

And through you to pass through into [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Macedonia|Macedonia]], and again from Macedonia to come to you and be sent forward by you into Judea.

## v17

This therefore intending, did I then use fickleness? Or the things which I purpose, do I purpose according to the flesh, so that with me there should be Yes, yes and No, no?

## v18

[[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — But|But]] as God is faithful, our [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — word|word]] toward you is not yes and no.

## v19

[[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — For|For]] the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you through us, through me and [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — Silvanus|Silvanus]] and Timothy, did not become yes and no, but our word has become yes in Him.

## v20

[[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — For|For]] as many promises of God as there are, in [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — Him|Him]] is the [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.3 — Yes|Yes]]; therefore also through Him is [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.4 — the|the]] Amen to God, [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.5 — for|for]] glory through [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.6 — us|us]] to God.

## v21

[[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — But|But]] the One who [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — firmly|firmly]] attaches [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.3 — us|us]] [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.4 — with|with]] you unto [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.5 — Christ|Christ]] and has [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.6 — anointed|anointed]] us is God,

## v22

He who has also [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — sealed|sealed]] us and given the Spirit in our [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 22.2 — hearts|hearts]] as a [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 22.3 — pledge|pledge]].

## v23

But I call on God as a witness [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — against|against]] my soul that to [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 23.2 — spare|spare]] you I have not yet come to Corinth.

## v24

Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers with you for your joy; for by [[2 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — faith|faith]] you stand.
