---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

This third time I am coming to you; at the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established.

## v2

I have previously said and I say beforehand, when I was present the second time and being absent now, to those who have sinned before and to all the rest, that if I come again, I will not spare,

## v3

Since you seek a proof of the Christ who is speaking in me, who is not weak unto you but is [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 3.1 — powerful|powerful]] in you.

## v4

For indeed He was crucified out of [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 4.1 — weakness|weakness]], but He lives [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 4.2 — by|by]] the power of God. For indeed [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 4.3 — we|we]] are weak in Him, but we will live together with Him [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 4.2 — by|by]] the power of God directed toward you.

## v5

Test yourselves whether you are in the [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 5.1 — faith|faith]]; [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 5.2 — prove|prove]] yourselves. Or do you not realize about yourselves that [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 5.3 — Jesus|Jesus]] Christ is in you, unless you are [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 5.4 — disapproved|disapproved]]?

## v6

But I hope that you will know that [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 6.1 — we|we]] are not disapproved.

## v7

Now we pray to God that you do nothing wrong, [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 7.1 — not|not]] that we may appear approved, but that you yourselves may do what is good and we may be as if disapproved.

## v8

[[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — For|For]] we are not able to do anything against the [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 8.2 — truth|truth]] but rather for the truth.

## v9

For we rejoice whenever we are [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 9.1 — weak|weak]] and you are powerful; this also we pray for, your [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 9.2 — perfecting|perfecting]].

## v10

Therefore I write these things while being absent in order that when present I would not have to use severity, according to the authority which the Lord has given me for building up and not for overthrowing.

## v11

Finally, brothers, [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.1 — rejoice|rejoice]], be [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.2 — perfected|perfected]], be [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.3 — comforted|comforted]], [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.4 — think|think]] the same thing, be [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.5 — at|at]] peace; and the God of [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.6 — love|love]] and peace will be with you.

## v12

Greet one another with a [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 12.1 — holy|holy]] kiss.

## v13

All the saints greet you.

## v14

The [[2 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 14.1 — grace|grace]] of the Lord Jesus Christ and the love of God and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.
