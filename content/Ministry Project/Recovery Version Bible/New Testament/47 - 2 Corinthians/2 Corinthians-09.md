---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

For concerning the ministry to the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you;

## v2

For I know your eagerness, of which I boast on your behalf to the Macedonians, that [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Achaia|Achaia]] has been prepared since last year; and your zeal has stirred up the greater number of them.

## v3

But I sent the brothers that our boasting on your behalf might not be made void in this respect, that, as I said, you might be prepared;

## v4

Lest, if the Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we, not to mention you, be put to shame in this [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 4.1 — confidence|confidence]].

## v5

Therefore I thought it necessary to entreat the brothers to go before to you and make ready beforehand your previously promised [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 5.1 — blessing|blessing]], that thus this might be ready [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 5.2 — as|as]] a blessing and not [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 5.2 — as|as]] a matter of covetousness.

## v6

But take note of this: He who sows sparingly shall also sparingly reap; and he who sows with [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 6.1 — blessings|blessings]] shall also with [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 6.2 — blessings|blessings]] reap;

## v7

Each one as he has purposed in his heart, not out of sorrow or out of [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 7.1 — necessity|necessity]], for [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 7.2 — God|God]] loves a [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 7.3 — cheerful|cheerful]] giver.

## v8

And God is able to make [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 8.1 — all|all]] grace abound unto you, that, in everything always having all sufficiency, you may abound unto every good work;

## v9

Even as it is written, “He has [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 9.1 — scattered|scattered]] abroad; he has given to the [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 9.2 — poor|poor]]; his [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 9.3 — righteousness|righteousness]] abides forever.”

## v10

Now [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 10.1 — He|He]] who bountifully supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed and cause the fruits of your righteousness to increase.

## v11

You in everything are being enriched unto all [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 11.1 — liberality|liberality]], which works out through us thanksgiving to God,

## v12

Because the ministry of this [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 12.1 — service|service]] is not only filling up the lack of the saints, but is also abounding through many thanksgivings to God.

## v13

They, through the [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 13.1 — approving|approving]] of this ministry, are glorifying God based upon the subjection unto the gospel of Christ that issues from your confession, and upon the liberality of the [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 13.2 — fellowship|fellowship]] to them and to all;

## v14

While in petition on your behalf they also long after you on account of the [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 14.1 — surpassing|surpassing]] [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 14.2 — grace|grace]] of God upon you.

## v15

Thanks be to God for His [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 15.1 — indescribable|indescribable]] [[2 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 15.2 — gift|gift]]!
