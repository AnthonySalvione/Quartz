---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.1 — But|But]] I myself, Paul, entreat you through the [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.2 — meekness|meekness]] and [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.3 — gentleness|gentleness]] of Christ, who (as you say) in person am [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.4 — base|base]] among you, but while absent am [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.5 — bold|bold]] toward you,

## v2

I beseech you that when present I would not have to be bold in the confidence with which I count myself, to dare do to some who count us as ones who are walking according to flesh.

## v3

For though we walk [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 3.1 — in|in]] flesh, we do not war according to flesh;

## v4

For the weapons of our warfare are [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 4.1 — not|not]] fleshly but [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 4.2 — powerful|powerful]] before God for the overthrowing of strongholds,

## v5

As we overthrow [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 5.1 — reasonings|reasonings]] and every [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 5.2 — high|high]] thing rising up against the knowledge of God, and take captive every [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 5.1 — reasonings|thought]] unto the obedience of Christ.

## v6

And we are ready to [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 6.1 — punish|punish]] all disobedience when your [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 6.2 — obedience|obedience]] is fulfilled.

## v7

You look at things according to their appearance. If someone [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 7.1 — has|has]] confidence in himself to be of Christ, let him take account of this again concerning himself, that even as he is of Christ, so also are we.

## v8

For even if I should boast somewhat more abundantly concerning our authority, which the Lord has given for [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 8.1 — building|building]] you up and not for overthrowing you, I will not be put to shame.

## v9

I say this so as not to seem as if I am terrifying you through my letters,

## v10

Because while his letters, someone says, are weighty and strong, his bodily presence is [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 10.1 — weak|weak]] and his speech [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 10.2 — contemptible|contemptible]].

## v11

Let such a one take account of this, that such as we are in word by letters when absent, such also we are in deed when present.

## v12

For we do not dare to class or compare ourselves with any of those who commend themselves; but they, measuring themselves by themselves and comparing themselves with themselves, are without understanding.

## v13

But we will [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.1 — not|not]] boast beyond our measure but [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.2 — according|according]] to the measure of the [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.3 — rule|rule]] which the God of measure has apportioned to us, to reach even as far as you.

## v14

For we are not [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 14.1 — extending|extending]] ourselves beyond our bounds, as if we did not reach you, for we were the first to come even as far as unto you in the gospel of Christ.

## v15

We are not boasting beyond our measure in others’ labors, but have the hope, as your faith is increasing, to be [[2 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 15.1 — magnified|magnified]] in you according to our rule unto abundance,

## v16

So that we may announce the gospel unto those parts beyond you, not so that we may boast in another man’s rule in things already prepared.

## v17

But he who boasts, let him boast in the Lord;

## v18

For it is not the one who commends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.
