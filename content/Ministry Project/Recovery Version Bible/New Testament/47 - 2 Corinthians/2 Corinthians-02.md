---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

But I [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — determined|determined]] this for myself, that I would not come again to you in sorrow.

## v2

For if I cause you sorrow, who then is the one who makes me glad, except the one who is made sorrowful by me?

## v3

And I [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — wrote|wrote]] this very thing to you that when I come I would not have sorrow from those who ought to make me rejoice, having confidence in you all that my joy is the joy of you all.

## v4

For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not that you would be made sorrowful but that you would know the love which I have more abundantly toward you.

## v5

But if anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused me to sorrow, but in part (lest I [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — lay|lay]] too heavy a burden) all of you.

## v6

Sufficient for such a one is this punishment by the majority,

## v7

So that on the contrary you should rather [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — forgive|forgive]] and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one be swallowed up with excessive sorrow.

## v8

Therefore I exhort you to [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — confirm|confirm]] your love toward him.

## v9

For to this end also I wrote, that I might [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — know|know]] your [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — approvedness|approvedness]], whether you are obedient in all things.

## v10

But whom you [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — forgive|forgive]] anything, I also forgive; for also what I have [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — forgiven|forgiven]], if I have forgiven anything, it is for your sake in the [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 10.3 — person|person]] of Christ;

## v11

That we may not be taken advantage of by [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Satan|Satan]], for we are not ignorant of his [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — schemes|schemes]].

## v12

[[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Furthermore|Furthermore]], when I came to Troas for the gospel of Christ and a door was open to me [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — in|in]] the Lord,

## v13

I had no rest in my [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — spirit|spirit]], for I did not find Titus my brother; but taking leave of them, I went forth into [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Macedonia|Macedonia]].

## v14

But thanks be to God, who always [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — leads|leads]] [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — us|us]] in triumph in [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.3 — the|the]] Christ and manifests the [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.4 — savor|savor]] of the knowledge of Him through [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.5 — us|us]] in every place.

## v15

For we [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — are|are]] a fragrance of Christ to God in those who are being saved and in those who are perishing:

## v16

To some a savor out of death [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unto|unto]] death, and to the others a savor out of life [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unto|unto]] life. And who is [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 16.2 — sufficient|sufficient]] for these things?

## v17

For we are not like the many, [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — adulterating|adulterating]] the word of God for profit; but as out of sincerity, but as out of God, before God we [[2 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — speak|speak]] in Christ.
