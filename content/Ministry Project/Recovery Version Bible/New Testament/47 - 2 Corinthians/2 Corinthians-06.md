## v1

[[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — And|And]] working [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — together|together]] with Him, we also [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 1.3 — entreat|entreat]] you not to receive the [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 1.4 — grace|grace]] of God in vain;

## v2

For He says, “In an acceptable time I listened to you, and in the day of [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — salvation|salvation]] I helped you.” Behold, now is the [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — well-acceptable|well-acceptable]] time; behold, now is the day of salvation.

## v3

[[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — We|We]] give no occasion of stumbling in anything that the [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 3.2 — ministry|ministry]] may not be faulted,

## v4

But in everything we commend ourselves as ministers of God, in much [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — endurance|endurance]], in afflictions, in [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — necessities|necessities]], in distresses,

## v5

In stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labors, in [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — watchings|watchings]], in [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — fastings|fastings]];

## v6

In pureness, in [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — knowledge|knowledge]], in long-suffering, in kindness, in a holy [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — spirit|spirit]], in unfeigned love,

## v7

In the word of truth, in the power of God; through the [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — weapons|weapons]] of righteousness [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 7.2 — on|on]] the right and on the left,

## v8

Through [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — glory|glory]] and dishonor, through [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 8.2 — evil|evil]] report and good report; as [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 8.3 — deceivers|deceivers]] and yet true;

## v9

As [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — unknown|unknown]] and yet well known; as [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 9.2 — dying|dying]] and yet behold we live; as [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 9.3 — being|being]] disciplined and yet not being put to death;

## v10

As [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — made|made]] sorrowful yet always rejoicing; as [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 10.2 — poor|poor]] yet enriching many; as [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 10.3 — having|having]] nothing and yet possessing all things.

## v11

Our mouth is [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — opened|opened]] to you, Corinthians; our heart is [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — opened|enlarged]].

## v12

You are not [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — constricted|constricted]] in us, but you are constricted in your [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — inward|inward]] parts.

## v13

But for a [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — recompense|recompense]] in kind, I speak as to [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 13.2 — children|children]], you also [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 13.3 — be|be]] enlarged.

## v14

[[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Do|Do]] not become [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 14.2 — dissimilarly|dissimilarly]] yoked [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 14.4 — with|with]] unbelievers. For what [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 14.3 — partnership|partnership]] do righteousness and lawlessness have? Or what fellowship does light have with darkness?

## v15

And what concord does Christ have [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — with|with]] Belial? Or what part does a believer have with an unbeliever?

## v16

And what agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For we are the temple of the [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — living|living]] God, even as God said, “I will dwell among them and walk among them; and I will be their God, and they will be My people.”

## v17

Therefore “come out from their midst and be [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 17.1 — separated|separated]], says the Lord, and do not touch [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 17.2 — what|what]] is unclean; and I will [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 17.3 — welcome|welcome]] you”;

## v18

“And I will be [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — a|a]] Father to you, and you will be [[2 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 18.2 — sons|sons]] and daughters to Me, says the Lord Almighty.”
