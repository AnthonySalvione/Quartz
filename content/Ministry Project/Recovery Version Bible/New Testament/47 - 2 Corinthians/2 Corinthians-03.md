## v1

Are we beginning again to commend ourselves? Or do we need, as some do, letters of commendation to you or from you?

## v2

You are [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — our|our]] letter, inscribed in [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — our|our]] hearts, known and read by all men,

## v3

Since you are being manifested that you are a [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — letter|letter]] of Christ [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — ministered|ministered]] by us, inscribed not [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 3.3 — with|with]] ink but with the Spirit of the living God; not in tablets of stone but in tablets of [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 3.4 — hearts|hearts]] of flesh.

## v4

And such confidence we have through Christ toward God,

## v5

Not that we are [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — sufficient|sufficient]] of ourselves to [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — account|account]] anything as from ourselves; but our [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 5.3 — sufficiency|sufficiency]] is from God,

## v6

Who has also made us sufficient as ministers of a new covenant, ministers not of the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — letter|letter]] but of the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Spirit|Spirit]]; for the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 6.3 — letter|letter]] kills, but the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 6.4 — Spirit|Spirit]] gives life.

## v7

Moreover if the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — ministry|ministry]] of death, engraved in stone in letters, came about in [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — glory|glory]], so that the sons of Israel were not able to gaze at the face of Moses because of the glory of his face, a glory which was being done away with,

## v8

How shall the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — ministry|ministry]] of the Spirit not be more in [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — glory|glory]]?

## v9

For if there is glory with the ministry of [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — condemnation|condemnation]], much more the ministry of [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — abounds|abounds]] with glory.

## v10

For also that which has been [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — glorified|glorified]] [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — in|in]] this respect [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 10.3 — has|has]] not been glorified on account of the surpassing glory.

## v11

For if that which was [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — being|being]] done away with was [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 11.2 — through|through]] glory, much more that which remains is in glory.

## v12

Therefore since we have such [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — hope|hope]], we use much [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 12.2 — boldness|boldness]],

## v13

And are not like Moses, who put a veil on his face so that the sons of Israel would not gaze [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — at|at]] the end of that which was being done away with.

## v14

But their [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — thoughts|thoughts]] were hardened; for until the present day the same veil remains at the reading of the old covenant, [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — it|it]] not being unveiled to them that the veil is being done away with in Christ.

## v15

Indeed unto this day, whenever [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Moses|Moses]] is read, a veil lies on their heart;

## v16

But whenever [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — their|their]] heart turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away.

## v17

[[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — And|And]] the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.2 — Lord|Lord]] is the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.3 — Spirit|Spirit]]; and where the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.4 — Spirit|Spirit]] of the Lord is, there is [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.5 — freedom|freedom]].

## v18

[[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — But|But]] [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.2 — we|we]] all with [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.3 — unveiled|unveiled]] face, [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.4 — beholding|beholding]] and reflecting like a [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.5 — mirror|mirror]] the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.6 — glory|glory]] of the Lord, are [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.7 — being|being]] transformed into the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.8 — same|same]] image [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.9 — from|from]] glory to glory, even as [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.10 — from|from]] the [[2 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.11 — Lord|Lord]] Spirit.
