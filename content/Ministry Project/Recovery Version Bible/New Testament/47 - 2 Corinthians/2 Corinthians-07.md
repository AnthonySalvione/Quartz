---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] since we have [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — these|these]] promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 1.3 — defilement|defilement]] of flesh and of spirit, [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 1.4 — perfecting|perfecting]] holiness in the [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 1.5 — fear|fear]] of God.

## v2

[[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Make|Make]] room for us; we have [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 2.2 — wronged|wronged]] no one, we have corrupted no one, we have taken advantage of no one.

## v3

I do not say this to condemn you, for I have said before that you are [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — in|in]] our hearts for our dying together and our living together.

## v4

Great is my boldness toward you, great is my boasting on your behalf; I am filled with [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — comfort|comfort]], I overflow with [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — comfort|joy]] in all our affliction.

## v5

For even when we came into Macedonia, our [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — flesh|flesh]] had no rest, but we were afflicted in everything; without were fightings, within were fears.

## v6

But He who comforts those who are [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — downcast|downcast]], that is, God, [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 6.2 — comforted|comforted]] us by the [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 6.3 — coming|coming]] of Titus;

## v7

And not only by his coming, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted because of you, declaring to us your longing, your lamentation, your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced the more.

## v8

For though I caused you sorrow by the [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 8.1 — letter|letter]], I do not regret it. Even if I [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 8.2 — did|did]] regret it (for I see that that letter, even if for an hour, [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 8.3 — caused|caused]] you sorrow),

## v9

Now I rejoice, not that you were made sorrowful, but that you were made sorrowful to [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — repentance|repentance]]; for you were made [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 9.2 — sorrowful|sorrowful]] according to God, that in nothing you might suffer loss from us.

## v10

For sorrow according to God works repentance unto [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 10.1 — salvation|salvation]], which is without regret; but the sorrow of the world works out death.

## v11

For behold this very thing, your being made sorrowful according to God, what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — earnestness|earnestness]] it has [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.2 — worked|worked]] out in you, [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.3 — indeed|indeed]] what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.4 — defense|defense]], indeed what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.5 — indignation|indignation]], indeed what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.6 — fear|fear]], indeed what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.7 — longing|longing]], indeed what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.8 — zeal|zeal]], indeed what [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.9 — punishment|punishment]]! In everything you have commended yourselves to be pure in [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.10 — the|the]] matter.

## v12

So then, though I wrote to you, it was not for the sake of [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — him|him]] who did the wrong, nor for the sake of [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 12.2 — him|him]] who was wronged, but for the sake of [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 12.3 — manifesting|manifesting]] to you before God your earnestness for us.

## v13

Because of this we have been comforted. And in addition to our comfort, we rejoiced [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — more|more]] abundantly over the joy of [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Titus|Titus]], because his [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 13.3 — spirit|spirit]] has been refreshed by all of you.

## v14

For if in anything I boasted to him on your behalf, I have not been put to shame; on the contrary, as we spoke all things in truth to you, so also our boasting before Titus turned out to be the truth.

## v15

And his [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 15.1 — affections|affections]] are abundantly toward you, he remembering the obedience of you all, how you received him with fear and trembling.

## v16

I rejoice that in everything I [[2 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 16.1 — have|have]] boldness because of you.
