---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] having [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — this|this]] ministry as we have been shown [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.3 — mercy|mercy]], we do not lose heart;

## v2

But we have renounced the hidden things of shame, not walking in craftiness nor [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — adulterating|adulterating]] the word of God, but by the manifestation of the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 2.2 — truth|truth]] commending ourselves to every conscience of men before God.

## v3

And even if our gospel is [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — veiled|veiled]], it is veiled in those who are perishing,

## v4

In whom the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — god|god]] of this age has [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — blinded|blinded]] the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — thoughts|thoughts]] of the unbelievers that the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 4.4 — illumination|illumination]] of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God, might not [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 4.5 — shine|shine]] on them.

## v5

[[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — For|For]] we do not preach ourselves but Christ Jesus as [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Lord|Lord]], and ourselves as your [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 5.3 — slaves|slaves]] for Jesus’ sake.

## v6

[[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Because|Because]] the God who said, Out of darkness light shall shine, is the One who shined [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — in|in]] [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — our|our]] hearts to [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.4 — illuminate|illuminate]] the knowledge of the glory of God in the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.5 — face|face]] of Jesus Christ.

## v7

[[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — But|But]] we have this [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — treasure|treasure]] in earthen vessels that the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 7.3 — excellency|excellency]] of the power may be of God and not out of us.

## v8

We are [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — pressed|pressed]] on every side but not [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 8.2 — constricted|constricted]]; unable to find a way out but not utterly without a way out;

## v9

[[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Persecuted|Persecuted]] but not [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.2 — abandoned|abandoned]]; [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.3 — cast|cast]] down but not [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.4 — destroyed|destroyed]];

## v10

Always bearing about in the body the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — putting|putting]] to death of Jesus [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 10.2 — that|that]] the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 10.3 — life|life]] of Jesus also may be manifested in our body.

## v11

For we who are alive are always being delivered unto death for Jesus’ sake that the life of [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Jesus|Jesus]] also may be manifested in our mortal [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 11.2 — flesh|flesh]].

## v12

So then death operates in us, but [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 12.1 — life|life]] in you.

## v13

And having the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — same|same]] [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 13.2 — spirit|spirit]] of faith according to that which is written, “I believed, therefore I spoke,” we also believe, therefore we also speak,

## v14

Knowing that He who raised the Lord Jesus will [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 14.1 — raise|raise]] us also with Jesus and will make us stand before Him with you.

## v15

For all things are for your sakes that the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — grace|grace]] which has abounded through the greater number may cause the thanksgiving to abound to the glory of God.

## v16

Therefore we do not lose heart; but though our [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — outer|outer]] man is [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 16.2 — decaying|decaying]], yet our [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — outer|inner]] man is being [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 16.3 — renewed|renewed]] day by day.

## v17

For our momentary lightness of [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — affliction|affliction]] works out for us, [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 17.2 — more|more]] and more surpassingly, an [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 17.3 — eternal|eternal]] weight of [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 17.4 — glory|glory]],

## v18

Because we do not regard the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 18.1 — things|things]] which are seen but the [[2 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 18.2 — things|things]] which are not seen; for the things which are seen are temporary, but the things which are not seen are eternal.
