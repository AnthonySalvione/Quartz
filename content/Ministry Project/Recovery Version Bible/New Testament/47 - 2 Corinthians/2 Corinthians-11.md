---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

I wish that you would bear with me in a little [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — foolishness|foolishness]]; but indeed you do bear with me.

## v2

For I am jealous over you with a [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 2.1 — jealousy|jealousy]] of God; for I betrothed you to one husband to present you as a pure [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 2.2 — virgin|virgin]] to Christ.

## v3

But I fear lest somehow, as the serpent deceived Eve by his craftiness, your [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 3.1 — thoughts|thoughts]] would be corrupted from the [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 3.2 — simplicity|simplicity]] and the purity toward Christ.

## v4

For if indeed he who comes preaches [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — another|another]] Jesus, whom we have not preached, or you receive a [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — another|different]] spirit, which you have not received, or a [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — another|different]] gospel, which you have not accepted, you bear [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 4.2 — well|well]] with him.

## v5

But I count myself to be inferior to the [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 5.1 — super-apostles|super-apostles]] in nothing.

## v6

But even if I am a [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 6.1 — layman|layman]] in speech, yet I am not in knowledge; indeed in every way we have made this manifest in all things to you.

## v7

Or did I commit a sin, abasing myself that you might be exalted, because I announced the gospel of God to you free of charge?

## v8

I robbed other churches, taking wages for the ministry to you.

## v9

And when I was present with you and lacked, I was not a burden to anyone; for the brothers who came from Macedonia filled up my lack, and in everything I kept myself from being burdensome to you, and will keep myself.

## v10

The [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 10.1 — truthfulness|truthfulness]] of Christ is in me, that this boasting shall not be stopped as it regards me in the regions of Achaia.

## v11

Why? Because I do not love you? God knows.

## v12

But what I do, I also will do, that I may cut off the opportunity of those desiring an opportunity, that in the thing in which they boast, they may be found even as we.

## v13

For such ones are [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 13.1 — false|false]] apostles, deceitful workers, [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 13.2 — transfiguring|transfiguring]] themselves into apostles of Christ.

## v14

And no wonder, for [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Satan|Satan]] himself transfigures himself into an [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 14.2 — angel|angel]] of light.

## v15

Therefore it is no great thing if also his [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 15.1 — ministers|ministers]] transfigure themselves into ministers of righteousness, whose end will be according to their works.

## v16

Again I say, Let no one think me to be foolish; but if otherwise, accept me even as if I were foolish, that I also may boast a little.

## v17

What I speak, I speak not according to the Lord but as if in foolishness, in this [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 17.1 — confidence|confidence]] of boasting.

## v18

Since many boast according to the flesh, I also will boast.

## v19

For you bear gladly with fools because you yourselves are [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 19.1 — wise|wise]].

## v20

For you bear it if anyone enslaves you, if anyone devours you, if anyone [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 20.1 — takes|takes]] you, if anyone lifts himself up, if anyone beats you in the face.

## v21

[[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 21.1 — By|By]] way of self-disparagement I say this, [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 21.2 — Supposedly|Supposedly]] we ourselves were weak. But in whatever anyone else is daring, I speak in foolishness, I also am daring.

## v22

[[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Hebrews|Hebrews]] are they? I also. [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Hebrews|Israelites]] are they? I also. The [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Hebrews|seed]] of Abraham are they? I also.

## v23

Ministers of Christ are they? I speak as being [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 23.1 — beside|beside]] myself, I more so! In labors more abundantly, in imprisonments more abundantly, in stripes [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 23.2 — excessively|excessively]], in deaths often.

## v24

Under the hands of the Jews five times I received forty stripes less one;

## v25

Three times I was beaten with [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 25.1 — rods|rods]], once I was stoned, [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 25.2 — three|three]] times I was shipwrecked, a night and a day I have spent in the deep;

## v26

In journeys often, in dangers of [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 26.1 — rivers|rivers]], in dangers of [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 26.2 — robbers|robbers]], in dangers from my race, in dangers from the Gentiles, in dangers in the city, in dangers in the wilderness, in dangers in the sea, in dangers among [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 26.3 — false|false]] brothers;

## v27

In labor and hardship; in [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.1 — watchings|watchings]] often; in [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.2 — hunger|hunger]] and thirst; in [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.2 — hunger|fastings]] often; in [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.3 — cold|cold]] and [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.4 — nakedness|nakedness]] —

## v28

Apart from the things which have [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 28.1 — not|not]] been mentioned, there is this: the crowd of cares pressing upon me daily, the [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 28.2 — anxious|anxious]] concern for all the churches.

## v29

Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is stumbled, and [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 29.1 — I|I]] myself do not [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 29.2 — burn|burn]]?

## v30

If I must boast, I will boast of the [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 30.1 — things|things]] of my weakness.

## v31

The God and Father of the Lord Jesus, He who is blessed forever, knows that I am not lying.

## v32

In Damascus, the [[2 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 32.1 — governor|governor]] under Aretas the king guarded the city of the Damascenes in order to seize me;

## v33

And in a basket I was lowered through a window, through the wall, and escaped his hands.
