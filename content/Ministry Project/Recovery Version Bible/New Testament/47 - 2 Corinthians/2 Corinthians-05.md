## v1

[[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — For|For]] we know that if our [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.2 — earthly|earthly]] [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.3 — tabernacle|tabernacle]] dwelling is taken down, we have a [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.4 — building|building]] [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.5 — from|from]] God, a [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.6 — dwelling|dwelling]] not made with hands, eternal, [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.7 — in|in]] the heavens.

## v2

For also in [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — this|this]] we groan, longing to be [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 2.2 — clothed|clothed]] upon with our [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 2.3 — dwelling|dwelling]] place [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 2.4 — from|from]] heaven,

## v3

If indeed, being clothed, we will not be found [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — naked|naked]].

## v4

For also, we who are in this tabernacle groan, being [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — burdened|burdened]], [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.2 — in|in]] that we do not desire to be unclothed, but clothed upon, that [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.3 — what|what]] is mortal may be [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.4 — swallowed|swallowed]] up by life.

## v5

Now He who has [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — wrought|wrought]] us for this very thing is God, who has given to us the Spirit as a [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.2 — pledge|pledge]].

## v6

Therefore being always of good courage and knowing that while we are [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — at|at]] home in the body, we are abroad from the Lord

## v7

(For we [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — walk|walk]] by faith, not by [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 7.2 — appearance|appearance]]) —

## v8

We are of good courage then and are well pleased rather [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — to|to]] be abroad from the body and at home with the Lord.

## v9

Therefore also we are [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — determined|determined]], whether [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 9.2 — at|at]] home or abroad, to gain the honor of being well pleasing to Him.

## v10

[[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — For|For]] we must all be manifested before the [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 10.2 — judgment|judgment]] seat of Christ, that each one may [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 10.3 — receive|receive]] the things done [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 10.4 — through|through]] the body according to what he has practiced, whether good or [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 10.5 — bad|bad]].

## v11

[[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Knowing|Knowing]] therefore the [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 11.2 — fear|fear]] of the Lord, we [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 11.3 — persuade|persuade]] men, but we are made manifest to God; yet I hope that we are made manifest also in your consciences.

## v12

We are not again commending ourselves to you, but so speak in order to give you an opportunity to boast on our behalf, that you may [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — have|have]] an answer for those who are boasting in outward [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 12.2 — appearance|appearance]] and not in [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 12.3 — heart|heart]].

## v13

For whether we were [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — beside|beside]] ourselves, it was to God; or whether we are [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 13.2 — sober-minded|sober-minded]], it is for you.

## v14

For the [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — love|love]] of Christ [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — constrains|constrains]] us because we [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 14.3 — have|have]] judged this, that [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 14.4 — One|One]] died for all, [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 14.5 — therefore|therefore]] all died;

## v15

And He died for all that those who live may [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — no|no]] longer live [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 15.2 — to|to]] themselves but to Him who died for them and has been raised.

## v16

[[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — So|So]] then we, from now on, [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 16.2 — know|know]] no one according to the flesh; even though we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we know Him so no longer.

## v17

[[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — So|So]] then if anyone is [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 17.2 — in|in]] Christ, he is a [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 17.3 — new|new]] creation. The [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 17.4 — old|old]] things have passed away; [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 17.5 — behold|behold]], [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 17.6 — they|they]] have become new.

## v18

But [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 18.1 — all|all]] things are out from God, who has reconciled us to Himself through Christ and has given to us the ministry of reconciliation;

## v19

Namely, that God in Christ was reconciling the world to Himself, not accounting their offenses to them, and has put in us the [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — word|word]] of reconciliation.

## v20

On behalf of Christ then we are [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — ambassadors|ambassadors]], as God entreats you through us; we beseech you on behalf of Christ, Be [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 20.2 — reconciled|reconciled]] to God.

## v21

Him who did [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 21.1 — not|not]] know [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 21.2 — sin|sin]] He made sin on our behalf that we might become the [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 21.3 — righteousness|righteousness]] of God [[2 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 21.4 — in|in]] Him.
