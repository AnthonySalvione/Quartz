---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Furthermore we make known to you, brothers, the [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — grace|grace]] of God which has been given in the churches of Macedonia,

## v2

That in much [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — proving|proving]] of affliction the abundance of their joy and the depth of their poverty abounded unto the riches of their [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 2.2 — liberality|liberality]];

## v3

That according to their power, I testify, and beyond their power, of their own accord,

## v4

With much entreaty they besought of us the [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — grace|grace]] and the fellowship of the ministry to the saints;

## v5

And this, not as we had hoped, but they gave themselves [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — first|first]] to the Lord, [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 5.2 — and|and]] to us [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 5.3 — through|through]] the will of God.

## v6

So we entreated Titus that, even as he began before, so also he would complete for you this [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — grace|grace]] [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.2 — as|as]] well.

## v7

But just as you abound in everything, in faith and in [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — word|word]] and in knowledge and in all [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.2 — earnestness|earnestness]] and in [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.3 — the|the]] love in you [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.4 — from|from]] us, abound in this [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.5 — grace|grace]] also.

## v8

I am not speaking by way of command, but through the earnestness of others, proving also the genuineness of your love.

## v9

For you know the [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — grace|grace]] of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, for your sakes He became poor in order that you, because of His poverty, might become rich.

## v10

And I give my [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — opinion|opinion]] in this matter, for this is profitable for you who were the first to begin a year ago not only to do this but even to intend to do this.

## v11

But now complete also the doing, so that as there was the eagerness in intending, so also there would be the completing out of what you have.

## v12

For if the eagerness is there, it is acceptable according to whatever one has, not according to what he does not have.

## v13

For it is not that to others there would be relief, yet to you affliction,

## v14

But it is out of equality; at the present time your abundance for their lack that their abundance also may be for your lack, so that there may be equality;

## v15

As it is written, “[[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 15.1 — He|He]] who gathered much had no excess, and he who gathered little had no lack.”

## v16

But thanks be to God who puts the same [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 16.1 — earnestness|earnestness]] on your behalf in the heart of Titus,

## v17

Because he not only received the [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 17.1 — entreaty|entreaty]], but being more earnest, he also went forth of his own accord to you.

## v18

And we sent together with him the brother whose praise in the gospel is throughout all the churches,

## v19

And not only this, but who has also been selected by the churches as our fellow traveler in this [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 19.1 — grace|grace]] which is being ministered by us, to the glory of the Lord Himself and to demonstrate our eagerness;

## v20

[[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Avoiding|Avoiding]] this, that anyone should find fault with us in this [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 20.2 — abundance|abundance]] which is being ministered by us.

## v21

For we [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 21.1 — exercise|exercise]] foresight for what is honorable not only in the sight of the Lord but also in the sight of men.

## v22

And we sent with them [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 22.1 — our|our]] brother, whom we have proved to be [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 22.2 — diligent|diligent]] in many things many times, yet now much more diligent because of the great confidence he has toward you.

## v23

Whether concerning Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker for you; or our brothers, they are [[2 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 23.1 — apostles|apostles]] of the churches, they are Christ’s glory.

## v24

Show therefore to them, in the face of the churches, the proof of your love and of our boasting on your behalf.
