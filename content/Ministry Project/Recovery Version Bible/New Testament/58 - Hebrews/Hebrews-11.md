---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Now [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — faith|faith]] is the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 1.2 — substantiation|substantiation]] of things [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 1.3 — hoped|hoped]] for, the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 1.4 — conviction|conviction]] of things [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 1.5 — not|not]] seen.

## v2

For in this the elders have obtained a good testimony.

## v3

[[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 3.1 — By|By]] faith we understand that the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 3.2 — universe|universe]] has been framed by the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 3.3 — word|word]] of God, so that what is seen has not come into being out of things which appear.

## v4

By faith Abel offered to God a [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — more|more]] excellent sacrifice than Cain, through which he obtained the testimony that he was righteous, God testifying to his gifts; and through [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 4.2 — faith|faith]], though he has died, he still speaks.

## v5

By faith Enoch was translated so that he should not see death; and he was not found, because God had translated him. For before his translation he obtained the testimony that he had been well pleasing to God.

## v6

But without faith it is impossible to [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 6.1 — be|be]] well pleasing to Him, for he who comes forward to God must believe that He is and that He is a rewarder of those who [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 6.2 — diligently|diligently]] seek Him.

## v7

By faith Noah, having been divinely instructed concerning things not yet seen and being moved by pious fear, prepared an ark for the salvation of his house, through which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is according to faith.

## v8

By faith Abraham, being called, obeyed to go out unto a place which he was to receive as an inheritance; and he went out, [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 8.1 — not|not]] knowing where he was going.

## v9

By faith he dwelt as a foreigner in the land of promise as in a foreign land, making his home in tents with Isaac and Jacob, the fellow heirs of the same promise;

## v10

For he eagerly waited for the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 10.1 — city|city]] which has the foundations, whose Architect and Builder is God.

## v11

By faith also Sarah herself received power to conceive seed, even beyond the season of age, since she considered Him faithful who promised.

## v12

Therefore also there were born of one, and that of him who had become dead, as many as even the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — stars|stars]] of heaven in multitude and as the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — stars|sand]] by the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 12.2 — seashore|seashore]] innumerable.

## v13

All these died [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 13.1 — in|in]] faith, not receiving the promises but seeing them from afar and joyfully greeting them and confessing that they were strangers and [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 13.2 — sojourners|sojourners]] on the earth.

## v14

For those who say such things make it manifest that they seek after a country of their own.

## v15

And if indeed they continued to remember that country from which they went out, they would have had opportunity to return;

## v16

But as it is, they long after a better country, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed of them, to be called their God, for He has prepared a city for them.

## v17

By faith Abraham, being tested, offered up Isaac; indeed he who gladly received the promises was offering up his only begotten,

## v18

Of whom it was said, “In Isaac shall your seed be called”;

## v19

Counting that God was able to raise men even from the dead, from which he also received him back in [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 19.1 — figure|figure]].

## v20

By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau, even concerning things to come.

## v21

By faith Jacob, while he was dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph and worshipped God, while [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 21.1 — leaning|leaning]] on the top of his staff.

## v22

By faith Joseph, nearing his end, made mention of the exodus of the sons of Israel and gave orders concerning his bones.

## v23

By faith Moses, when he was born, was hidden for three months by his parents because they saw that the child was lovely, and they did not fear the king’s decree.

## v24

By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter,

## v25

Choosing rather to be ill-treated with the people of God than to have the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 25.1 — temporary|temporary]] [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 25.2 — enjoyment|enjoyment]] of sin,

## v26

[[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Considering|Considering]] the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 26.2 — reproach|reproach]] of the Christ greater riches than the treasures of Egypt; for he looked away to the [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 26.3 — reward|reward]].

## v27

By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king, for he [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 27.1 — persevered|persevered]] as one seeing the unseen One.

## v28

By faith he instituted the Passover and the pouring out of the blood so that the one destroying the firstborn would not touch them.

## v29

By faith they passed through the Red Sea as through dry land, in which the Egyptians, while making the attempt, were swallowed up.

## v30

[[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 30.1 — By|By]] faith the walls of Jericho fell, having been encircled for a period of seven days.

## v31

By faith Rahab the harlot did not perish with those who were disobedient, since she had received the spies with peace.

## v32

And what more shall I say? For time will fail me if I tell of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, of David and Samuel and the prophets,

## v33

Who through faith overcame kingdoms, worked righteousness, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions,

## v34

Quenched the power of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, were made strong in weakness, became mighty in war, routed the armies of foreigners.

## v35

Women received their dead by resurrection; and others were tortured to death, not accepting [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 35.1 — deliverance|deliverance]], in order that they might obtain a [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 35.2 — better|better]] resurrection.

## v36

And still others underwent the trial of mockings and scourgings, even, moreover, of bonds and imprisonment.

## v37

They were stoned, they were sawn in two, they were tempted, they died by the murder of the sword, they wandered about in sheepskins, in goatskins, destitute, afflicted, ill-treated

## v38

(Of [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 38.1 — whom|whom]] the world was not worthy), wandering over deserts and mountains and in caves and holes of the earth.

## v39

And these all, having obtained a good testimony through their faith, did not obtain the promise,

## v40

Because God has [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 40.1 — provided|provided]] something [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 40.2 — better|better]] for us, so that [[Hebrews-11.fn#Footnote 40.3 — apart|apart]] from us they would not be made perfect.
