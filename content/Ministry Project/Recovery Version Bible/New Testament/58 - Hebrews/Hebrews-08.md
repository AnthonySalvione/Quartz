---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Now in the things which are being said the chief point is this: We have such a High Priest, who sat down on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — heavens|heavens]],

## v2

A [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Minister|Minister]] of the [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 2.2 — holy|holy]] places, even of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, not man.

## v3

For every high priest is established for the offering of both [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — gifts|gifts]] and sacrifices; hence it is necessary that this One also have something which He may offer.

## v4

Now then if He were on earth, He would not be a Priest at all, there being those who offer the gifts according to the law,

## v5

Who serve the [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — example|example]] and shadow of the heavenly things, even as Moses was divinely instructed when he was about to complete the tabernacle; for, “See,” He said, “that you make all things according to the pattern that was shown to you in the mountain.”

## v6

But now He has obtained a more excellent [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — ministry|ministry]] inasmuch as He is also the [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Mediator|Mediator]] of a [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 6.3 — better|better]] covenant, which has been [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 6.4 — enacted|enacted]] upon [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 6.5 — better|better]] promises.

## v7

For if that [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — first|first]] covenant had been faultless, no [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 7.2 — place|place]] would have been sought for a [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — first|second]].

## v8

For finding fault with them He says, “Behold, the days are coming, says the Lord, and I will consummate a new covenant upon the house of Israel and upon the house of Judah,

## v9

Not according to the covenant which I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by their hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt, because they did not continue in My covenant, and I disregarded them, says the Lord.

## v10

For this is the covenant which I will covenant with the house of Israel after those days, says the Lord: I will impart My [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — laws|laws]] into their [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 10.2 — mind|mind]], and on their hearts I will [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 10.3 — inscribe|inscribe]] them; and [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 10.4 — I|I]] will be God to them, and [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 10.5 — they|they]] will be a people to Me.

## v11

And they shall by no means each teach his fellow citizen and each his brother, saying, [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Know|Know]] the Lord; for all will [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Know|know]] Me from the little one to the great one among them.

## v12

For I will be [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 12.1 — propitious|propitious]] to their unrighteousnesses, and their sins I shall by no means remember anymore.”

## v13

In saying, A new covenant, He has made the [[Hebrews-08.fn#Footnote 13.1 — first|first]] old. But that which is becoming old and growing decrepit is near to disappearing.
