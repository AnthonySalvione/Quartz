---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Therefore, [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — holy|holy]] brothers, [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — partakers|partakers]] of a [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 1.3 — heavenly|heavenly]] calling, consider the [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Apostle|Apostle]] and High Priest of our confession, Jesus,

## v2

Who was faithful to Him who constituted Him, as also Moses was in all His house.

## v3

For He has been counted worthy of more [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — glory|glory]] than Moses, by as much as He who [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — built|built]] the house has more [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — glory|honor]] than [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 3.3 — the|the]] house.

## v4

For every house is built by someone, but He who built all things is God.

## v5

And Moses was faithful in all His house as a [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — servant|servant]], for a [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — testimony|testimony]] of the things to be spoken later;

## v6

But Christ was faithful as a Son over His house, whose [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — house|house]] we are if indeed we hold fast the boldness and the boast of hope firm to the end.

## v7

[[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Therefore|Therefore]], even as the Holy Spirit says, “Today if you hear His voice,

## v8

Do not harden your hearts as in the provocation, in the day of trial in the wilderness,

## v9

Where your fathers tried Me by testing Me and saw My works for forty years.

## v10

Therefore I was displeased with this generation, and I said, They always [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — go|go]] astray in their heart, and they have not known My [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — ways|ways]];

## v11

As I swore in My wrath, [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — They|They]] shall not enter into My rest!”

## v12

Beware, brothers, lest perhaps there be in any one of you an evil heart of unbelief in [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — falling|falling]] away from the living God.

## v13

But exhort one another each day, as long as it is called “today,” lest any one of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin —

## v14

For we have become [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — partners|partners]] of Christ, if indeed we hold fast the beginning of the assurance firm to the end —

## v15

While it is said, “Today if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as in the provocation.”

## v16

For who provoked Him when they heard? Indeed was it not all who came out of Egypt by [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Moses|Moses]]?

## v17

And with whom was He displeased for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose carcasses fell in the wilderness?

## v18

And to whom did He swear that they should not enter into His rest, except to the [[Hebrews-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — disobedient|disobedient]]?

## v19

And we see that they were not able to enter in because of unbelief.
