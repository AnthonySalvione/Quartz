---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Therefore leaving the [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — word|word]] of the [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — beginning|beginning]] of Christ, let us [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 1.3 — be|be]] brought on to [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 1.4 — maturity|maturity]], not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith in God,

## v2

Of the teaching of [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — baptisms|baptisms]] and of the laying on of hands, of the resurrection of the dead and of eternal judgment.

## v3

And [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — this|this]] we will do if God permits.

## v4

For it is [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — impossible|impossible]] for those who have once been enlightened and have tasted of the [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — heavenly|heavenly]] gift and have become [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 4.3 — partakers|partakers]] of the Holy Spirit

## v5

And have tasted the good [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — word|word]] of God and the [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — powers|powers]] of the age to come,

## v6

And yet have [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — fallen|fallen]] away, to renew themselves again unto repentance, [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — crucifying|crucifying]] again for themselves the Son of God and [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — crucifying|putting]] Him to open shame.

## v7

For the earth, which drinks the [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — rain|rain]] which often comes upon it and [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 7.2 — produces|produces]] vegetation suitable to those for whose sake also it is cultivated, partakes of blessing from God.

## v8

But if it brings forth [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — thorns|thorns]] and thistles, it is [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 8.2 — disapproved|disapproved]] and [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 8.3 — near|near]] a curse, whose end is to be [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 8.4 — burned|burned]].

## v9

But we are persuaded of better [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — things|things]] concerning you, beloved, and things which belong to [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 9.2 — salvation|salvation]], though we speak thus.

## v10

For God is not unrighteous, so as to forget your work and the love which you have shown toward His name in having [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — ministered|ministered]] to the saints and in ministering still.

## v11

But we desire earnestly that each of you show the same diligence unto the full assurance of your hope until the end,

## v12

That you may not be [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — sluggish|sluggish]], but imitators of those who through faith and long-suffering are inheriting the promises.

## v13

For God, when He made the promise to Abraham, since He could swear by no one greater, swore by Himself,

## v14

Saying, “Surely blessing, I will bless you; and multiplying, I will multiply you.”

## v15

And thus when Abraham had patiently endured, he obtained the promise.

## v16

For men swear by the greater, and for all disputes among them an oath is final for confirmation.

## v17

Therefore God, intending to show more abundantly to the heirs of the promise the unchangeableness of His counsel, interposed with an oath,

## v18

In order that by [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — two|two]] unchangeable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we may have strong encouragement, we who have [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 18.2 — fled|fled]] for [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 18.3 — refuge|refuge]] to lay hold of the hope set before us,

## v19

Which we have as an [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 19.1 — anchor|anchor]] of the soul, both secure and firm and which [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 19.2 — enters|enters]] within the veil,

## v20

Where the [[Hebrews-06.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Forerunner|Forerunner]], Jesus, has entered for us, having become forever a High Priest according to the order of Melchizedek.
