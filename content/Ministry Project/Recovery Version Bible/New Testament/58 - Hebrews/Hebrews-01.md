---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — God|God]], having spoken of old in [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — many|many]] portions and in many ways to the fathers in the prophets,

## v2

Has at the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — last|last]] of these days spoken to us in the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Son|Son]], [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — whom|whom]] He appointed [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — Heir|Heir]] of all things, through whom also He made the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 2.5 — universe|universe]];

## v3

Who, being the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — effulgence|effulgence]] of His glory and the impress of His substance and upholding and bearing all things by the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — word|word]] of His power, having made [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — purification|purification]] of sins, [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 3.4 — sat|sat]] down on the right hand of the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 3.5 — Majesty|Majesty]] on [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 3.6 — high|high]];

## v4

Having become as much better than the angels as to have inherited a [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — more|more]] distinguished name than they.

## v5

For to which of the angels has He ever said, “You are My Son; [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — this|this]] day have I begotten You”? And again, “I will be a Father to Him, and He will be a Son to Me”?

## v6

And when He brings again the [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Firstborn|Firstborn]] into the inhabited earth, He says, “And let all the angels of God worship Him.”

## v7

And of the angels He says, “Who makes His angels [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — winds|winds]] and His ministers a flame of fire”;

## v8

But of the Son, “Your throne, [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — O|O]] God, is forever and ever, and the scepter of uprightness is the scepter of Your kingdom.

## v9

You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; therefore God, [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Your|Your]] God, has anointed You with the oil of exultant joy above Your [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — partners|partners]]”;

## v10

And, “You in the beginning, [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Lord|Lord]], laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the works of Your hands;

## v11

They will perish, but You remain perpetually; and they all will become old like a garment,

## v12

And like a mantle You will roll them up; like a garment they will also be changed; but You are the same, and Your years will not fail.”

## v13

But to which of the angels has He ever said, “Sit at My right hand [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — until|until]] I set Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet”?

## v14

Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth for service for the sake of [[Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — those|those]] who are to inherit salvation?
