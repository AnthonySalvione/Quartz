---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — For|For]] this [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Melchizedek|Melchizedek]], [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Melchizedek|king]] of Salem, priest of the Most High God, who met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him,

## v2

To whom also Abraham apportioned a tenth part of all; first being interpreted [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — king|king]] of righteousness, and then also king of Salem, which is [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — king|king]] of peace;

## v3

Being [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — without|without]] father, without mother, without genealogy; having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but likened to the Son of God, abides a priest perpetually.

## v4

And consider how [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — great|great]] this one was, to whom the patriarch Abraham gave a tenth of the choice spoils.

## v5

And they of the sons of Levi, who received the priestly service, have a commandment to take tithes from the people according to the law, that is, from their brothers, though they have come forth from the loins of Abraham.

## v6

But he whose genealogy is not counted from them has taken tithes from Abraham and has [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — blessed|blessed]] him who has the promises.

## v7

But without any dispute the [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 7.1 — lesser|lesser]] is blessed by the [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 7.2 — greater|greater]].

## v8

And here men, who die, receive tithes; but there, one of whom it is testified that he lives.

## v9

And, so to speak, through Abraham, [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Levi|Levi]] also, he who receives tithes, has been made to pay tithes,

## v10

For he was still in the loins of his father when Melchizedek met him.

## v11

If indeed then perfection were through the Levitical priesthood (for under it the people have received the law), what need was there still that a different [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Priest|Priest]] should arise according to the order of Melchizedek and that He should not be said to be according to the order of Aaron?

## v12

For since the priesthood is [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — transferred|transferred]], of necessity there comes into being a [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 12.2 — transfer|transfer]] of law also.

## v13

For He of whom these things are said [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — belongs|belongs]] to a different tribe, from which no one has attended to the altar.

## v14

For it is evident that our Lord has [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 14.1 — risen|risen]] out of [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Judah|Judah]], concerning which tribe Moses spoke nothing about priests.

## v15

And it is still more abundantly evident since it is according to the likeness of Melchizedek that a different Priest arises,

## v16

Who has been appointed not according to the law of a fleshy commandment but according to the power of an [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 16.1 — indestructible|indestructible]] life.

## v17

For it is testified, “You are a Priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.”

## v18

For there is, on the one hand, the [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 18.1 — setting|setting]] aside of the preceding commandment because of its weakness and unprofitableness

## v19

(For the law [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 19.1 — perfected|perfected]] nothing), and, on the other hand, the bringing in [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 19.2 — thereupon|thereupon]] of a [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 19.3 — better|better]] hope, through which we draw near to God.

## v20

And inasmuch as He was not made a Priest without the taking of an oath

## v21

(For they are appointed priests without the taking of an oath, but He, with the taking of an oath by Him who said to Him, “The Lord has sworn and will not regret it, You are a Priest forever”),

## v22

By so much Jesus has also become the [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — surety|surety]] of a better covenant.

## v23

And they are appointed priests many in number because death prevents them from continuing;

## v24

But He, because He abides forever, has His priesthood unalterable.

## v25

Hence also He is able to save [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 25.1 — to|to]] the uttermost those who come forward to God through Him, since He lives always to [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 25.2 — intercede|intercede]] for them.

## v26

For such a High Priest was also fitting to us, holy, guileless, undefiled, separated from sinners and having become [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 26.1 — higher|higher]] than the heavens,

## v27

Who does not have daily need, as the high priests do, to offer up sacrifices first for his own sins and then for those of the people; for this He did once for all when He offered up Himself.

## v28

For the law establishes men as high priests who have weakness, but the word of the oath, which was after the law, establishes the Son, [[Hebrews-07.fn#Footnote 28.1 — perfected|perfected]] forever.
