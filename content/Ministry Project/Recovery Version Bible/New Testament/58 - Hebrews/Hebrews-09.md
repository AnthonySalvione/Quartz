---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Now then the first covenant also had ordinances of service, and its [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sanctuary|sanctuary]] was of this world.

## v2

For a tabernacle was prepared, the first, in which were the lampstand and the table and the loaves that were set forth, which is called the Holy Place;

## v3

And after the second veil, a tabernacle, which is called the Holy of Holies,

## v4

[[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Having|Having]] a golden [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 4.2 — altar|altar]] and the Ark of the Covenant covered about everywhere with gold, in which were the golden [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 4.3 — pot|pot]] that had the manna and Aaron’s rod that budded and the tablets of the covenant,

## v5

And above it cherubim of glory overshadowing the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 5.1 — expiation|expiation]] cover, concerning which it is not now the time to speak in detail.

## v6

Now these things having been thus prepared, into the first tabernacle the priests enter continually, accomplishing their worship;

## v7

But into the second, only the high priest enters, once a year and not without blood, which he offers for himself and for the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 7.1 — sins|sins]] of ignorance of the people,

## v8

The Holy Spirit thus making this clear, that the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 8.1 — way|way]] of the Holy of Holies has not yet been manifested while the first tabernacle still has its standing,

## v9

Which is a [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 9.1 — figure|figure]] for the present time. According to this tabernacle both [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 9.2 — gifts|gifts]] and sacrifices are offered, which are unable to perfect, according to conscience, him who worships,

## v10

Consisting only of foods and drinks and various [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 10.1 — washings|washings]], being ordinances of the flesh, imposed until the time of [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 10.2 — setting|setting]] things right.

## v11

But Christ, having come as a High Priest of [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 11.1 — the|the]] good things that have come into being, through the greater and more perfect tabernacle not made by hands, that is, not of this creation,

## v12

And not through the blood of goats and calves but through His own blood, entered once for all into the Holy of Holies, [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 12.1 — obtaining|obtaining]] an eternal [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 12.2 — redemption|redemption]].

## v13

For if the blood of goats and bulls and the ashes of a heifer sprinkling those who are [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 13.1 — defiled|defiled]] sanctify to the purity of the flesh,

## v14

How much more will the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 14.1 — blood|blood]] of Christ, who through the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 14.2 — eternal|eternal]] Spirit offered Himself without blemish to God, purify our [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 14.3 — conscience|conscience]] from [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 14.4 — dead|dead]] works to serve the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 14.5 — living|living]] God?

## v15

And because of this He is the Mediator of a new covenant, so that, death having taken place for redemption of the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 15.1 — transgressions|transgressions]] under the first covenant, those who have been called might receive the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 15.2 — promise|promise]] of the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 15.3 — eternal|eternal]] inheritance.

## v16

For where there is a [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 16.1 — testament|testament]], the death of him who made the testament must of necessity be established.

## v17

For a [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 17.1 — testament|testament]] is confirmed [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 17.2 — in|in]] the case of the dead, since it never has force when he who made the testament is living.

## v18

Hence neither was the first covenant [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 18.1 — initiated|initiated]] without blood;

## v19

For when every commandment according to the law had been spoken by Moses to all the people, he took the blood of the calves and the goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, and sprinkled both the scroll of the covenant itself and all the people,

## v20

Saying, “This is the blood of the covenant which God has commanded to you.”

## v21

And both the tabernacle and all the vessels of the service he sprinkled in like manner with the blood.

## v22

And almost all things are purified by blood according to the law, and [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 22.1 — without|without]] shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.

## v23

It was necessary therefore for the examples of the things in the heavens to be purified by these, but the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 23.1 — heavenly|heavenly]] things themselves, by [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 23.2 — better|better]] sacrifices than these.

## v24

For Christ did not enter into a [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 24.1 — holy|holy]] place made by hands, a [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 24.2 — figure|figure]] of the true, but into heaven itself, to appear now before the face of God for us;

## v25

Nor in order that He might offer Himself often, just as the high priest enters into the Holy of Holies year by year by the blood of other creatures;

## v26

Since then He would have had to suffer often since the foundation of the world. But now once at the [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 26.1 — consummation|consummation]] of the ages He has been manifested for the putting away of sin through the sacrifice of Himself.

## v27

And inasmuch as it is [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 27.1 — reserved|reserved]] for men to die [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 27.2 — once|once]], and after this comes judgment,

## v28

So Christ also, having been offered once to bear the sins of many, will appear a second time to those who eagerly await Him, [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 28.1 — apart|apart]] from sin, unto [[Hebrews-09.fn#Footnote 28.2 — salvation|salvation]].
