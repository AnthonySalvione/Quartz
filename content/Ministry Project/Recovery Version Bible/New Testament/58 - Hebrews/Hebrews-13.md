---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Let [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 1.1 — brotherly|brotherly]] love [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 1.2 — continue|continue]].

## v2

Do not forget hospitality, for through this some, without knowing it, have entertained angels.

## v3

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Remember|Remember]] those who are imprisoned as bound with them, those who are ill-treated as being yourselves also likewise treated in body.

## v4

Let [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 4.1 — marriage|marriage]] be held in honor [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 4.2 — among|among]] all, and the bed undefiled; for fornicators and adulterers God will [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 4.3 — judge|judge]].

## v5

Let your way of life be without the [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 5.1 — love|love]] of money, being satisfied with the things which are at hand; for He Himself has said, “I shall by no means give you up, neither by any means shall I abandon you”;

## v6

So that being of good courage, we say, “The Lord is my Helper, and I will not fear. What shall man do to me?”

## v7

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Remember|Remember]] the ones leading you, who have spoken to you the word of God; and considering the issue of their manner of life, imitate their faith.

## v8

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Jesus|Jesus]] Christ is the same yesterday and today, yes, even forever.

## v9

Do not be carried away by [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 9.1 — various|various]] strange teachings, for it is good for the heart to be [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 9.2 — confirmed|confirmed]] by grace, not by the [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 9.3 — food|food]] of sacrifices, by which those who walk were not profited.

## v10

We have an [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 10.1 — altar|altar]] from which they who are serving the tabernacle have no right to eat.

## v11

For the [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 11.1 — bodies|bodies]] of those animals whose blood is brought into the Holy of Holies for sin by the high priest are burned up outside the camp.

## v12

Therefore also Jesus, that He might [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 12.1 — sanctify|sanctify]] the people through His own [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 12.2 — blood|blood]], suffered outside the [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 12.3 — gate|gate]].

## v13

Let us therefore go forth unto Him [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 13.1 — outside|outside]] the [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 13.2 — camp|camp]], bearing His reproach.

## v14

For [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 14.2 — we|we]] do not have here a [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 14.1 — remaining|remaining]] city, but we seek after the one to come.

## v15

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Through|Through]] Him then let us offer up a sacrifice of praise continually to God, that is, the fruit of lips confessing His name.

## v16

But do not forget doing good and [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 16.1 — sharing|sharing]] with others, for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.

## v17

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Obey|Obey]] the ones leading you and submit to them, for they watch over your souls as those who will render an account, that they may do this with joy and not groaning; for this would be unprofitable to you.

## v18

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Pray|Pray]] for us, for we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, desiring in all things to conduct ourselves honorably.

## v19

And I exhort you much more to do this that I may be restored to you more quickly.

## v20

Now the God of peace, He who brought up from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great Shepherd of the [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 20.1 — sheep|sheep]], in the blood of an [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 20.2 — eternal|eternal]] covenant,

## v21

Perfect you in every good work for the doing of His will, [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 21.1 — doing|doing]] in us that which is well pleasing in His sight through Jesus Christ; to Him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

## v22

Now I beseech you, brothers, [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 22.1 — bear|bear]] with the word of this exhortation, for indeed I have written to you in few words.

## v23

Know that our brother [[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Timothy|Timothy]] has been released, with whom, if he comes soon, I will see you.

## v24

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 24.1 — Greet|Greet]] all the ones leading you and all the saints. Those from Italy greet you.

## v25

[[Hebrews-13.fn#Footnote 25.1 — Grace|Grace]] be with you all. Amen.
