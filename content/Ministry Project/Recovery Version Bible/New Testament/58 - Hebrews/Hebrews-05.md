---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

For every high priest, being taken from among men, is established on behalf of men in the things pertaining to God that he may offer both [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — gifts|gifts]] and [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — gifts|sacrifices]] for sins.

## v2

He is able to exercise [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — compassion|compassion]] toward the ignorant and erring since he also is encompassed with weakness;

## v3

And because of it, he ought, even as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins.

## v4

And no one takes the honor upon himself, but only as he is called by God, just as Aaron also was.

## v5

So also Christ did not [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — glorify|glorify]] Himself in becoming a High Priest, but it was He who said to Him, “You are My Son; this day have I [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 5.2 — begotten|begotten]] You”;

## v6

Even as also in another place He says, “[[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — You|You]] are a Priest forever according to the [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 6.2 — order|order]] of Melchizedek.”

## v7

This One, in the days of His flesh, having offered up both petitions and supplications with strong crying and tears to Him who was able to save Him [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — out|out]] of death and having been heard because of His piety,

## v8

Even though He was a Son, learned [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — obedience|obedience]] from the things which He suffered.

## v9

And having been [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — perfected|perfected]], He became to all those who obey Him the [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 9.2 — source|source]] of [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 9.3 — eternal|eternal]] salvation,

## v10

Being [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — addressed|addressed]] by God as a High Priest according to the order of Melchizedek;

## v11

Concerning whom we have much to say that is also difficult to interpret since you have become dull of hearing.

## v12

For when because of the time you ought to be teachers, you have need again for someone to teach you what the [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — rudiments|rudiments]] of the beginning of the [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 12.2 — oracles|oracles]] of God are and have become those who have need of [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 12.3 — milk|milk]] and not of solid food.

## v13

For everyone who partakes of [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — milk|milk]] is inexperienced in the [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — milk|word]] of righteousness, for he is an infant;

## v14

But solid food is for the full-grown, who because of practice have their [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — faculties|faculties]] exercised for discriminating between both [[Hebrews-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — good|good]] and evil.
