---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] we ought to give heed more abundantly to the things which have been heard lest perhaps we [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — drift|drift]] away.

## v2

For if the word spoken through angels proved firm and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompense,

## v3

How shall we [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — escape|escape]] if we have neglected so great a [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — salvation|salvation]], which, having had its beginning in being spoken by the Lord, has been confirmed to us by those who heard,

## v4

God bearing witness with them both by signs and wonders and by various works of power and by [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — distributions|distributions]] of the Holy Spirit according to His will?

## v5

For it was not to angels that He subjected the coming [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — inhabited|inhabited]] earth, concerning which we speak.

## v6

But one has solemnly testified somewhere, saying, “[[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — What|What]] is man, that You bring him to mind? Or the son of man, that You care for him?

## v7

You have made Him [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — a|a]] little inferior to the angels; You have crowned Him with glory and honor and have set Him over the works of Your hands;

## v8

You have subjected all things under His feet.” For in subjecting all things to Him, He left nothing unsubject to Him. But now we do not yet see all things subjected to Him,

## v9

But we see Jesus, who was made a little [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — inferior|inferior]] to the angels because of the suffering of death, crowned with [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — glory|glory]] and honor, so that by the grace of God He might taste death on behalf of [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — everything|everything]].

## v10

For it was fitting for [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Him|Him]], for whom are all things and through whom are all things, in leading [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — many|many]] sons into [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 10.3 — glory|glory]], to make the [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 10.4 — Author|Author]] of their salvation [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 10.5 — perfect|perfect]] through sufferings.

## v11

For both [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — He|He]] who sanctifies and those who are being sanctified are all [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — of|of]] One, for which cause He is not ashamed to call them brothers,

## v12

Saying, “I will [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — declare|declare]] Your name to My brothers; in the midst of the [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — church|church]] [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — I|I]] will sing hymns of praise to You.”

## v13

And again, “I will trust in Him.” And again, “Behold, I and the children whom God has given to Me.”

## v14

Since therefore the children have shared in blood and flesh, He also Himself in like manner partook of the same, that through death He might [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — destroy|destroy]] him who has the might of death, that is, the devil,

## v15

And might [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — release|release]] those who because of the fear of death through all their life were held in slavery.

## v16

For assuredly it is not to angels that He [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — gives|gives]] help, but He [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — gives|gives]] help to the seed of Abraham.

## v17

Hence He should have been [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — made|made]] like His brothers in all things that He might become a merciful and faithful [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — High|High]] Priest in the [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 17.3 — things|things]] pertaining to God, to make [[Hebrews-02.fn#Footnote 17.4 — propitiation|propitiation]] for the sins of the people.

## v18

For being tempted in that which He Himself has suffered, He is able to help those who are being tempted.
