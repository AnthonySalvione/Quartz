---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Be therefore [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — imitators|imitators]] of God, as beloved children;

## v2

And walk in [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — love|love]], even as [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Christ|Christ]] also loved [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 2.3 — us|us]] and gave Himself up for us, an [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 2.4 — offering|offering]] and a [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 2.4 — offering|sacrifice]] to God for a [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 2.5 — sweet-smelling|sweet-smelling]] savor.

## v3

But [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — fornication|fornication]] and all uncleanness or greediness, let it not even be named among you, as is fitting for [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 3.2 — saints|saints]],

## v4

And obscenity and foolish talking or sly, filthy jesting, which are not becoming, but rather the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — giving|giving]] of thanks.

## v5

For this you [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — realize|realize]], [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 5.2 — knowing|knowing]] that every fornicator or unclean person or greedy person (who is an idolater) has no inheritance in the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 5.3 — kingdom|kingdom]] of Christ and of God.

## v6

Let no one deceive you with vain words, for because of these things the wrath of God is coming upon the sons of disobedience.

## v7

Therefore do not be partakers with them;

## v8

For you were once [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — darkness|darkness]] but are now [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — darkness|light]] in the Lord; [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 8.2 — walk|walk]] as [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 8.3 — children|children]] of light

## v9

(For the fruit of the light consists in all [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — goodness|goodness]] and righteousness and truth),

## v10

Proving what is well pleasing to the Lord.

## v11

And do not participate in the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — unfruitful|unfruitful]] works of darkness, but rather even [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 11.2 — reprove|reprove]] them.

## v12

For the things which are done by them in secret it is shameful even to speak of.

## v13

But all things which are [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — reproved|reproved]] are made manifest by the light; for everything that makes manifest is light.

## v14

Therefore He says, Awake, [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — sleeper|sleeper]], and arise from the dead, and [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Christ|Christ]] will shine on you.

## v15

[[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Look|Look]] therefore carefully how you walk, not as unwise, but as wise,

## v16

[[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Redeeming|Redeeming]] the time, because the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 16.2 — days|days]] are evil.

## v17

Therefore do not be foolish, but [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — understand|understand]] what the will of the Lord is.

## v18

And do not be [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 18.1 — drunk|drunk]] with wine, in which is dissoluteness, but be [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 18.1 — drunk|filled]] in spirit,

## v19

[[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Speaking|Speaking]] to one another in [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 19.2 — psalms|psalms]] and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and psalming with your heart to the Lord,

## v20

Giving thanks [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — at|at]] all times for all things [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 20.2 — in|in]] the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to our God and Father,

## v21

Being [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 21.1 — subject|subject]] to [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 21.2 — one|one]] another in the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 21.3 — fear|fear]] of Christ:

## v22

[[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Wives|Wives]], be subject [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 22.2 — to|to]] your [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 22.3 — own|own]] husbands as to the Lord;

## v23

For a husband is [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 23.1 — head|head]] of the wife as also Christ is Head of the church, He Himself being the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 23.2 — Savior|Savior]] of the Body.

## v24

[[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 24.1 — But|But]] as the church is subject to Christ, so also let the wives be subject to their husbands in [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 24.2 — everything|everything]].

## v25

Husbands, [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 25.1 — love|love]] your wives even [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 25.2 — as|as]] Christ also loved the church and gave Himself up for her

## v26

That He might [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 26.1 — sanctify|sanctify]] her, cleansing her by the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 26.2 — washing|washing]] of the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 26.3 — water|water]] in the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 26.4 — word|word]],

## v27

That He might [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 27.1 — present|present]] the [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 27.2 — church|church]] to Himself [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 27.3 — glorious|glorious]], not having [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 27.4 — spot|spot]] or wrinkle or any such things, but that she would be [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 27.5 — holy|holy]] and without blemish.

## v28

In the same way the husbands also ought to love their own wives as their own bodies; he who loves his own wife loves himself.

## v29

For no one ever hated his own flesh, but [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 29.1 — nourishes|nourishes]] and cherishes it, even as Christ also the church,

## v30

Because we are members of His Body.

## v31

For this cause a man shall [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 31.1 — leave|leave]] his father and mother and shall be joined to his wife, and the two shall be [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 31.2 — one|one]] flesh.

## v32

This mystery is great, but I speak with regard to [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 32.1 — Christ|Christ]] and the church.

## v33

Nevertheless you also, each one of you, in the same way love [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 33.1 — your|your]] own wife as yourself; and the wife should [[Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 33.2 — fear|fear]] her husband.
