## v1

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Children|Children]], obey your parents [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — in|in]] the Lord, for this is [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 1.3 — right|right]].

## v2

"[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Honor|Honor]] your father and mother," which is the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — first|first]] commandment with a promise,

## v3

"That it may be [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — well|well]] with you and that you may [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — well|live]] long on the earth."

## v4

And fathers, do not [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — provoke|provoke]] your children to anger, but nurture them in the discipline and [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — admonition|admonition]] of the Lord.

## v5

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Slaves|Slaves]], [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — be|be]] obedient to those who are your masters according to the flesh with [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 5.3 — fear|fear]] and trembling, in [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 5.4 — singleness|singleness]] of your heart, [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 5.5 — as|as]] to Christ;

## v6

Not with eye-service as men-pleasers but [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — as|as]] slaves of Christ, doing the will of God [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — from|from]] the soul;

## v7

With good will serving as slaves, [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — as|as]] serving the Lord and not men;

## v8

Knowing that whatever good thing each one does, [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — this|this]] he will receive back from the Lord, whether he is a slave or a free man.

## v9

And masters, do the same things toward them, [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — giving|giving]] up your threatening, knowing that both their Master and yours is in the heavens, and there is no respect of persons with Him.

## v10

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Finally|Finally]], [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 10.2 — be|be]] [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 10.3 — empowered|empowered]] [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 10.4 — in|in]] the Lord and in the might of His strength.

## v11

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Put|Put]] on the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 11.2 — whole|whole]] armor of God that you may be able to [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 11.3 — stand|stand]] against the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 11.4 — stratagems|stratagems]] of the devil,

## v12

For our wrestling is not against [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — blood|blood]] and flesh but against [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — the|the]] rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 12.3 — this|this]] [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 12.4 — darkness|darkness]], against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenlies.

## v13

Therefore [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — take|take]] up the whole armor of God that you may be able to [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 13.2 — withstand|withstand]] in the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 13.3 — evil|evil]] day, and having done all, to [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 13.4 — stand|stand]].

## v14

Stand therefore, [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — having|having]] [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 14.2 — girded|girded]] your loins with [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 14.3 — truth|truth]] and having [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 14.4 — put|put]] on the breastplate of righteousness.

## v15

And having [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — shod|shod]] your feet with the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 15.2 — firm|firm]] foundation of the gospel of peace;

## v16

Besides all these, having [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — taken|taken]] up the shield of [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 16.2 — faith|faith]], with which you will be able to quench all the flaming [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 16.3 — darts|darts]] of the evil one.

## v17

And receive the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 17.1 — helmet|helmet]] of salvation and the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 17.2 — sword|sword]] of the Spirit, [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 17.3 — which|which]] Spirit is the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 17.4 — word|word]] of God,

## v18

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — By|By]] means of all [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 18.2 — prayer|prayer]] and petition, praying at every time in [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 18.3 — spirit|spirit]] and [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 18.4 — watching|watching]] unto this in [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 18.5 — all|all]] perseverance and [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 18.6 — petition|petition]] concerning all the saints,

## v19

And for me, that [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 19.1 — utterance|utterance]] may be given to me in the opening of my mouth, to make known in boldness the [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 19.2 — mystery|mystery]] of the gospel,

## v20

For which I am an [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 20.1 — ambassador|ambassador]] in a [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 20.2 — chain|chain]], that in it I would speak boldly, as I ought to speak.

## v21

But that you also may know the things concerning me, how I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 21.1 — minister|minister]] in the Lord, will [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 21.2 — make|make]] all things known to you.

## v22

Him I have sent to you for this very thing, that you may know the things concerning us and that he may comfort your hearts.

## v23

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Peace|Peace]] to the brothers and [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 23.2 — love|love]] with faith [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 23.3 — from|from]] God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v24

[[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 24.1 — Grace|Grace]] be with all those who [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 24.2 — love|love]] our Lord Jesus Christ in [[Ephesians-06.fn#Footnote 24.3 — incorruptibility|incorruptibility]].
