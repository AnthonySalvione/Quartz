## v1

The [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — elder|elder]] to [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Gaius|Gaius]] the beloved, whom I love in [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — truthfulness|truthfulness]].

## v2

Beloved, concerning [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — all|all]] things I [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — wish|wish]] that you may [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — prosper|prosper]] and be in [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — health|health]], even as your [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.5 — soul|soul]] [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — prosper|prospers]].

## v3

For I [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — rejoiced|rejoiced]] greatly at the brothers’ coming and testifying to [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — your|your]] steadfastness in the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 3.4 — truth|truth]], even as you [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — walk|walk]] in truth.

## v4

I have no greater joy than these things, that I hear that my children are walking in the truth.

## v5

Beloved, you do faithfully in [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — whatever|whatever]] you have wrought for the brothers, and this for [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — strangers|strangers]],

## v6

Who testified to your [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — love|love]] before the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — church|church]]; whom you [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 6.3 — will|will]] do well to send forward in a manner [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 6.4 — worthy|worthy]] of God;

## v7

For on behalf of the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Name|Name]] they went out, taking nothing from the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Gentiles|Gentiles]].

## v8

We therefore ought to [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — support|support]] such ones that we may become fellow workers in the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — truth|truth]].

## v9

I wrote something to the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — church|church]]; but [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Diotrephes|Diotrephes]], who loves [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — to|to]] be first among them, does not [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.4 — receive|receive]] us.

## v10

For this reason, if I come, I will bring to remembrance his works which he does, [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — babbling|babbling]] against us with [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — evil|evil]] words; and not being satisfied with these, neither does he himself receive the brothers, and those intending to do so he forbids and casts out of the church.

## v11

Beloved, do not imitate the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — evil|evil]], but the good. He who [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — does|does]] good is [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — of|of]] God; he who [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 11.4 — does|does]] evil has [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 11.5 — not|not]] seen God.

## v12

To [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Demetrius|Demetrius]] testimony has been borne by [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — all|all]] and by the [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — truth|truth]] itself; and [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.4 — we|we]] also testify, and you know that our testimony is [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.5 — true|true]].

## v13

I had many things to write to you, but I do not want to write to you with ink and pen;

## v14

But I [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — hope|hope]] to see you shortly, and we will speak [[3 John-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — face|face]] to face. Peace to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
