## v1

Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy the brother, to Philemon our beloved and fellow worker

## v2

And to [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Apphia|Apphia]] the sister and to [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Apphia|Archippus]] our fellow soldier and to the [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — church|church]], which is in your house:

## v3

Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v4

I thank my God always, making mention of you in my prayers,

## v5

Hearing of your [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — love|love]] and the faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and to all the saints,

## v6

That the fellowship of your faith may become effective in the [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — full|full]] knowledge of [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — every|every]] good thing which is in [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 6.3 — you|you]] [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 6.4 — for|for]] Christ.

## v7

[[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — For|For]] I had much joy and encouragement over your love, because the [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — inward|inward]] parts of the saints have been [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 7.3 — refreshed|refreshed]] through you, brother.

## v8

Therefore though I have much boldness in Christ to charge you what is fitting,

## v9

Because of love I rather entreat, being such a one as Paul [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — the|the]] aged and now also a [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — prisoner|prisoner]] of Christ Jesus.

## v10

I entreat you concerning my child, whom I have [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — begotten|begotten]] in my bonds, [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Onesimus|Onesimus]],

## v11

Who formerly was [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — useless|useless]] to you but now is [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — useful|useful]] both to you and to me.

## v12

Him I have sent back to you — him, that is, my [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — very|very]] heart —

## v13

Whom I intended to keep with myself that on your behalf he might minister to me in the bonds of the gospel.

## v14

But [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — without|without]] your mind I did not want to do anything, that your goodness would not be as of necessity, but voluntary.

## v15

[[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — For|For]] [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — perhaps|perhaps]] for this reason he was separated from you for but an hour, that you might [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — fully|fully]] have him forever,

## v16

[[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — No|No]] longer as a slave, but [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — above|above]] a slave, a [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — beloved|beloved]] brother, especially to me, but how much more to you, [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 16.4 — both|both]] in the flesh and in the Lord.

## v17

If then [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — you|you]] hold me as a [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — partner|partner]], receive him as myself.

## v18

And if he has [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — wronged|wronged]] you in anything or owes anything, charge that to [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — my|my]] account.

## v19

I, Paul, have written with my own hand: I will [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — repay|repay]]; not to say to you that you owe me even [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — your|your]] own self besides.

## v20

Yes, brother, may I have [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — profit|profit]] from you in the Lord; [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — refresh|refresh]] my [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 20.3 — inward|inward]] parts in Christ.

## v21

Having confidence in your obedience, I have written to you, knowing that you will do even beyond the things that I say.

## v22

And at the same time also prepare me a lodging, for I [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — hope|hope]] that through your prayers I will be [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 22.2 — graciously|graciously]] given to you.

## v23

[[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Epaphras|Epaphras]], my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you;

## v24

As do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow workers.

## v25

The [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — grace|grace]] of the Lord Jesus Christ be with [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 25.2 — your|your]] [[Philemon-01.fn#Footnote 25.3 — spirit|spirit]].
