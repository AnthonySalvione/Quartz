## v1

Everyone who [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — believes|believes]] that Jesus is the Christ has been begotten of God, and everyone who loves Him who has begotten loves him also who has been begotten of Him.

## v2

In this we know that we love the children of God, when we [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — love|love]] God and [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 2.2 — do|do]] His commandments.

## v3

For this is the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — love|love]] of God, that we [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 3.2 — keep|keep]] His commandments; and His commandments are not [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 3.3 — burdensome|burdensome]].

## v4

For [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — everything|everything]] that has been [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 4.2 — begotten|begotten]] of God [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 4.3 — overcomes|overcomes]] the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 4.4 — world|world]]; and this is the victory which has overcome the world — our [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 4.5 — faith|faith]].

## v5

And who is he who overcomes the world except him who [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — believes|believes]] that Jesus is the Son of God?

## v6

This is [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — He|He]] who came through water and [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.2 — blood|blood]], Jesus Christ; not [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.3 — in|in]] the water only, but [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.3 — in|in]] the water and [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.3 — in|in]] the blood; and the Spirit is He who [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.4 — testifies|testifies]], because the Spirit is the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 6.5 — reality|reality]].

## v7

For there are three who testify,

## v8

The Spirit and the water and the blood, and the three are [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — unto|unto]] the one thing.

## v9

If we receive the testimony of men, the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — testimony|testimony]] of God is greater, because this is the testimony of God that He has testified concerning His Son.

## v10

He who [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — believes|believes]] into the Son of God has the testimony in himself; he who does not believe God has made Him a liar because he has not believed in the testimony which God has testified concerning His Son.

## v11

And this is the testimony, that God [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — gave|gave]] to us eternal life and this life is in His Son.

## v12

He who [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — has|has]] the Son has the life; he who does not have the Son of God does not have the life.

## v13

I have written [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — these|these]] things to you that you may know that you have eternal life, to you who believe into the name of the Son of God.

## v14

And [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — this|this]] is the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — boldness|boldness]] which we have toward Him, that if we [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 14.3 — ask|ask]] anything according to His will, He hears us.

## v15

And if we [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — know|know]] that He hears us in whatever we [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 15.2 — ask|ask]], we know that we have the requests which we have asked from Him.

## v16

If anyone sees his brother sinning a sin not [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unto|unto]] death, he shall [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 16.2 — ask|ask]] and he [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 16.3 — will|will]] give [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 16.4 — life|life]] to him, to those sinning not unto death. There is a [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 16.5 — sin|sin]] unto death; I do not say that he should make request concerning that.

## v17

All [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — unrighteousness|unrighteousness]] is sin, and there is sin not unto death.

## v18

We know that everyone who is [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 18.1 — begotten|begotten]] of God does not sin, but [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 18.2 — he|he]] who has been begotten of God [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 18.3 — keeps|keeps]] himself, and the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 18.4 — evil|evil]] one does not [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 18.5 — touch|touch]] him.

## v19

We know that we are [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — of|of]] God, and the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 19.2 — whole|whole]] world [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 19.3 — lies|lies]] in the [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 19.4 — evil|evil]] one.

## v20

And we [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.3 — know|know]] that the Son of God has [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — come|come]] and has given us an [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.2 — understanding|understanding]] that we might know [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.4 — Him|Him]] who is [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.5 — true|true]]; and we are [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.6 — in|in]] Him who is true, [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.7 — in|in]] His Son Jesus Christ. [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 20.8 — This|This]] is the true God and eternal life.

## v21

[[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Little|Little]] children, [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 21.2 — guard|guard]] yourselves from [[1 John-05.fn#Footnote 21.3 — idols|idols]].
