# Jude 1 — Outline

```rcv-outline
- I. Introduction—to those called, beloved, and kept — 1:1–2 ^rcvo-i
- II. Contending for the faith — 1:3 ^rcvo-ii
- III. Heresies of the apostates — 1:4 ^rcvo-iii
- IV. Historical examples of the Lord’s judgment upon apostasy — 1:5–7 ^rcvo-iv
- V. The evils of the apostates and their punishment under the Lord’s judgment — 1:8–19 ^rcvo-v
- VI. Charges to the believers — 1:20–23 ^rcvo-vi
    - A. To build up themselves upon the most holy faith and to live in the Triune God — 1:20–21 ^rcvo-vi-a
    - B. To care for others with mercy in fear — 1:22–23 ^rcvo-vi-b
- VII. Conclusion—praise to Him who is able to guard and set the believers before His glory — 1:24–25 ^rcvo-vii
```
