## v1

[[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jude|Jude]], a slave of Jesus Christ and a brother of [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jude|James]], to those who are called, beloved in God the Father, and kept [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — by|by]] Jesus Christ:

## v2

[[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Mercy|Mercy]] to you and peace and love be multiplied.

## v3

Beloved, while using all diligence to write to you concerning our [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — common|common]] salvation, I found it necessary to write to you and exhort you to earnestly [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — contend|contend]] for the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — faith|faith]] once for all delivered to the saints.

## v4

[[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — For|For]] certain men have [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — crept|crept]] in unnoticed, who of old have been [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — written|written]] of beforehand for [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.4 — this|this]] judgment, ungodly men, [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.5 — perverting|perverting]] the grace of our God into [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.6 — licentiousness|licentiousness]] and [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.5 — perverting|denying]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 4.7 — our|our]] only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

## v5

But I intend to remind you, though you know all things once for all, that [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — the|the]] Lord, having saved a people out of the land of Egypt, [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — afterward|afterward]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — destroyed|destroyed]] those who did not believe;

## v6

And angels who did not keep their own [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — principality|principality]] but abandoned their [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — own|own]] dwelling place, He has kept in eternal bonds under [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 6.3 — gloom|gloom]] for the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 6.4 — judgment|judgment]] of the great day;

## v7

How Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, who [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — in|in]] like manner with these gave themselves over to fornication and went after different flesh, are set forth as an example, undergoing the penalty of eternal fire.

## v8

Nevertheless in like manner [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — these|these]] dreamers also defile the flesh and despise [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — lordship|lordship]] and revile [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — dignities|dignities]].

## v9

But [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Michael|Michael]] the archangel, when he contended with the devil and disputed concerning the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — body|body]] of Moses, [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — did|did]] not dare to bring a reviling judgment against him but said, The Lord rebuke you.

## v10

But [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — these|these]] revile the things that they do not [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — know|know]]; and the things that they [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — know|understand]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.3 — naturally|naturally]], like [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.4 — animals|animals]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.5 — without|without]] reason, [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.6 — in|in]] these they are being [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 10.7 — corrupted|corrupted]].

## v11

Woe to them! For they have gone in the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — way|way]] of Cain and have [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — rushed|rushed]] out in the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — error|error]] of Balaam for reward and have [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 11.4 — perished|perished]] in the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 11.5 — rebellion|rebellion]] of Korah.

## v12

These are the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — hidden|hidden]] reefs in your [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — love|love]] feasts, feasting together with you without fear, [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — shepherding|shepherding]] themselves; [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 12.4 — waterless|waterless]] clouds being carried off by winds; [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 12.5 — autumn|autumn]] trees without fruit, having died twice, rooted up;

## v13

[[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Wild|Wild]] waves of the sea, foaming out their own shames; [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — wandering|wandering]] stars, for whom the gloom of darkness is kept for eternity.

## v14

And Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied also of these, saying, Behold, the Lord [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — came|came]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — with|with]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 14.3 — myriads|myriads]] of His saints

## v15

To execute [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — judgment|judgment]] against all and to convict all the ungodly concerning all their ungodly works which they have done in ungodliness, and concerning all the hard things which ungodly sinners have spoken against Him.

## v16

These are murmurers, complainers, going on according to their own lusts; and their mouth speaks great swelling things, flattering [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — persons|persons]] for the sake of advantage.

## v17

But you, beloved, remember the words spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ,

## v18

That they said to you, In the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — last|last]] time there will be mockers, going on according to their own lusts for ungodliness.

## v19

These are those who make divisions, [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — soulish|soulish]], having no [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — spirit|spirit]].

## v20

But you, beloved, building up yourselves [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — upon|upon]] your most holy [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — faith|faith]], praying in the Holy Spirit,

## v21

[[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Keep|Keep]] yourselves in the love of God, awaiting the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — mercy|mercy]] of our [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 21.3 — Lord|Lord]] Jesus Christ [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 21.4 — unto|unto]] eternal life.

## v22

And [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — on|on]] some have mercy, on those who are [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 22.2 — wavering|wavering]];

## v23

Save them by [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — snatching|snatching]] them out of the [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 23.2 — fire|fire]]. On others have mercy [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 23.3 — in|in]] fear, hating even the inner garment spotted from the flesh.

## v24

[[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — But|But]] to Him who is able to guard you from stumbling and to set you before His [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 24.2 — glory|glory]] without blemish [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 24.3 — in|in]] [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 24.4 — exultation|exultation]],

## v25

To the only [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — God|God]] our Savior through [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — God|Jesus]] Christ our Lord be [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 25.2 — glory|glory]], majesty, might, and authority [[Jude-01.fn#Footnote 25.3 — before|before]] all time and now and unto all eternity. Amen.
