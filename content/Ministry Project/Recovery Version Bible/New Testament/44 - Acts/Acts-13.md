---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

Now there were in Antioch, in the local [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.1 — church|church]], [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.2 — prophets|prophets]] and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.3 — teachers|teachers]]: [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Barnabas|Barnabas]] and Simeon, who was called [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.5 — Niger|Niger]], and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.6 — Lucius|Lucius]] the Cyrenian, and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.7 — Manaen|Manaen]], the foster brother of [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.8 — Herod|Herod]] the tetrarch, and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 1.9 — Saul|Saul]].

## v2

And as they were [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 2.1 — ministering|ministering]] to the Lord and fasting, the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit said, [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 2.3 — Set|Set]] apart for Me now Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.

## v3

Then, when they had [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 3.1 — fasted|fasted]] and prayed and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 3.2 — laid|laid]] their hands on them, they sent them away.

## v4

They then, having been sent out by the Holy [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Spirit|Spirit]], [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 4.2 — went|went]] down to Seleucia; and from there they sailed away to Cyprus.

## v5

And when they were in Salamis, they announced the word of God [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 5.1 — in|in]] the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 5.2 — synagogues|synagogues]] of the Jews. And they also had John as their attendant.

## v6

And when they had passed through the whole island as far as Paphos, they found a certain man, a magician and Jewish false prophet, whose name was Bar-jesus,

## v7

Who was with the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 7.1 — proconsul|proconsul]], Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man; the latter called Barnabas and Saul to him and sought to hear the word of God.

## v8

But Elymas the magician (for so his name is translated) opposed them, seeking to turn the proconsul away from the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — faith|faith]].

## v9

But Saul, who is also [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Paul|Paul]], [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 9.2 — filled|filled]] with the Holy Spirit, looked intently at him

## v10

And said, O you who are full of all deceit and all unscrupulousness, son of the devil, enemy of all righteousness, will you not cease perverting the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 10.1 — straight|straight]] ways of the Lord?

## v11

And now, behold, the hand of the Lord is upon you, and you will be blind, not seeing the sun for a time. And instantly a mist and a darkness fell upon him; and he went about and sought some to lead him by the hand.

## v12

Then the proconsul, seeing what had happened, believed, being astonished at the teaching of the Lord.

## v13

And putting out to sea from Paphos, Paul and his companions came to Perga of Pamphylia; and John [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 13.1 — departed|departed]] from them and returned to Jerusalem.

## v14

And they passed through from Perga and arrived at Pisidian Antioch. And they went into the synagogue [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 14.1 — on|on]] the Sabbath day and sat down.

## v15

And after the reading of the Law and the Prophets, the synagogue rulers sent word to them, saying, [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Men|Men]], brothers, if you have any word of exhortation for the people, say it.

## v16

And Paul, rising up and motioning with his hand, said, [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Men|Men]] of Israel and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 16.2 — those|those]] who fear God, listen.

## v17

The God of this people Israel chose our fathers and exalted the people during their sojourn in the land of Egypt, and with a [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 17.1 — high|high]] arm He led them out of it.

## v18

And for a time of about forty years [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 18.1 — He|He]] carried them as a nurse in the wilderness.

## v19

And when He had overthrown seven nations in the land of Canaan, He distributed their land as an inheritance.

## v20

And after these things, for about [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 20.1 — four|four]] hundred and fifty years, He gave them judges until Samuel the prophet.

## v21

And afterward they asked for a king; and God gave them Saul, the son of Kish, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.

## v22

And when He had deposed him, He raised up David for them as king, to whom also He testified and said, I have found David, the son of Jesse, a man according to My [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 22.1 — heart|heart]], who will do all My will.

## v23

From this man’s seed, God, according to promise, brought to Israel a Savior, Jesus,

## v24

After John had proclaimed, [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 24.1 — prior|prior]] to His public entrance, a [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 24.2 — baptism|baptism]] of repentance to all the people of Israel.

## v25

Now as John was completing his course, he said, What do you [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 25.1 — suppose|suppose]] that I am? I am not the Christ. But behold, One is coming after me, the sandals of whose feet I am not worthy to untie.

## v26

[[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Men|Men]], brothers, sons of the race of Abraham, and [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 26.2 — those|those]] among you who fear God, to us the word of this salvation has been sent forth.

## v27

For those dwelling in Jerusalem and their rulers, being ignorant of this One and of the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 27.1 — words|words]] of the prophets which are read every Sabbath, fulfilled them by [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 27.2 — judging|judging]] Him.

## v28

And though they did not find one cause of death in Him, they asked of Pilate that He be done away with.

## v29

And when they had accomplished all that was written concerning Him, they took Him down from the tree and laid Him in a tomb.

## v30

But [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 30.1 — God|God]] raised Him from the dead.

## v31

And for many days He appeared to those who had come up with Him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are now His witnesses to the people.

## v32

And we announce to you the gospel of the promise made to the fathers,

## v33

That God has fully fulfilled this promise to us their children in raising up Jesus, as it is also written in the second Psalm, “You are My Son; this day have I [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 33.1 — begotten|begotten]] You.”

## v34

And as to His having raised Him up from the dead, no longer to return to corruption, He spoke in this way, “I will give you [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 34.1 — the|the]] [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 34.2 — holy|holy]] things of David, the faithful things.”

## v35

Therefore He also says in another place, “You will not [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 35.1 — permit|permit]] [allow] Your Holy One to see corruption.”

## v36

Now David, having [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 36.1 — served|served]] his own generation by the counsel of God, did indeed fall asleep and was [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 36.2 — buried|buried]] with his fathers and saw corruption;

## v37

But He whom [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 37.1 — God|God]] has raised did not see corruption.

## v38

Therefore let it be known to you, [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 38.1 — men|men]], brothers, that through this One forgiveness of sins is announced to you;

## v39

And from all the things from which you were not able to be justified [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 39.1 — by|by]] the law of Moses, in this One everyone who believes is [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 39.2 — justified|justified]] .

## v40

Therefore beware that what is spoken in the prophets does not come upon you:

## v41

“Look, you despisers, and marvel, and vanish away; for I am doing a work in your days, a work which you will by no means believe, though someone tells it to you in detail.”

## v42

And as Paul and Barnabas were going out, the people begged that these words would be spoken to them on the next Sabbath.

## v43

And when the synagogue gathering had been dismissed, many of the Jews and the devout [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 43.1 — proselytes|proselytes]] followed Paul and Barnabas, who spoke to them and urged them to continue in the [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 43.2 — grace|grace]] of God.

## v44

And on the following Sabbath almost all the city gathered together to hear the word of God.

## v45

But when the Jews saw the crowds, they were filled with jealousy and contradicted the things being spoken by Paul and blasphemed.

## v46

And Paul and Barnabas spoke boldly and said, It was necessary for the word of God to be spoken to you first. Since you thrust it away and do [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 46.1 — not|not]] judge yourselves worthy of eternal life, behold, we turn to the Gentiles.

## v47

For so the Lord has commanded us, “I have set [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 47.1 — you|you]] as a light of the Gentiles, that you would be for salvation unto the uttermost part of the earth.”

## v48

And the Gentiles, hearing this, rejoiced and glorified the word of the Lord; and as many as were [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 48.1 — appointed|appointed]] to eternal life believed.

## v49

And the word of the Lord was carried through the whole region.

## v50

But the Jews incited the women of high standing who worshipped God, and the chief men of the city, and raised up a persecution against Paul and Barnabas and cast them out from their borders.

## v51

But they shook off the dust of their feet against them and came to Iconium.

## v52

And the disciples were [[Acts-13.fn#Footnote 52.1 — filled|filled]] with joy and with the Holy Spirit.
