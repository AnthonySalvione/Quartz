---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

Now there was in Caesarea a certain man named Cornelius, a centurion of what was called the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Italian|Italian]] [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 1.2 — cohort|cohort]],

## v2

[[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Devout|Devout]] and one who feared God with all his household, giving many alms to the people and beseeching God continually.

## v3

He saw clearly in a [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 3.1 — vision|vision]] around the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 3.2 — ninth|ninth]] hour of the day an angel of God coming in to him and saying to him, Cornelius.

## v4

And he looked intently at him and became terrified and said, What is it, Lord? And he said to him, Your prayers and your alms have ascended as a [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 4.1 — memorial|memorial]] before God.

## v5

And now send men to Joppa and send for a certain Simon, who is surnamed Peter;

## v6

This man is lodging with a certain Simon, a tanner, whose house is by the sea.

## v7

And when the angel who was speaking to him went away, he called two of his household servants and a devout soldier of those who waited on him;

## v8

And relating all things to them, he sent them to Joppa.

## v9

And on the next day as they were journeying and drawing near to the city, Peter went up on the housetop to [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 9.1 — pray|pray]] around the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 9.2 — sixth|sixth]] hour.

## v10

And he became very [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 10.1 — hungry|hungry]] and wanted to [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 10.2 — eat|eat]]. And while they were preparing, a [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 10.3 — trance|trance]] came upon him;

## v11

And he beheld [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 11.1 — heaven|heaven]] opened, and a certain [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 11.2 — vessel|vessel]] like a great sheet descending, being let down by four corners onto the earth,

## v12

In which were all the four-footed [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 12.1 — animals|animals]] and reptiles of the earth and birds of heaven.

## v13

And a voice came to him: Rise up, Peter; slay and [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 13.1 — eat|eat]]!

## v14

But Peter said, By no means, Lord, for I have [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 14.1 — never|never]] eaten anything common and unclean.

## v15

And a voice came to him again a second time: The [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 15.1 — things|things]] that God has cleansed, do not make common.

## v16

And this occurred three times; and immediately the vessel was taken up into heaven.

## v17

Now as Peter was utterly perplexed in himself as to what the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 17.1 — vision|vision]] which he had seen might be, behold, the men who were sent by Cornelius, having made diligent inquiry for Simon’s house, stood at the gate.

## v18

And they called out and inquired whether Simon, who is surnamed Peter, was lodging there.

## v19

And while Peter was pondering over the vision, the Spirit said to him, Behold, there are three men seeking you.

## v20

But rise up, go down and go with them, doubting nothing, because [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 20.1 — I|I]] have sent them.

## v21

And Peter went down to the men and said, Behold, I am he whom you seek; what is the cause for which you have come?

## v22

And they said, Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous man and one who fears God and is well attested to by the whole nation of the Jews, was divinely instructed by a holy angel to send for you to come to his house and hear words from you.

## v23

So he called them in and lodged them. And on the next day he rose up and went away with them; and [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 23.1 — some|some]] of the brothers from Joppa accompanied him.

## v24

And on the next day they entered into Caesarea. And Cornelius was awaiting them, having called together his relatives and intimate friends.

## v25

And when Peter entered, Cornelius met him, and falling at his feet, worshipped him.

## v26

But Peter raised him up, saying, Rise up; I myself am also a man.

## v27

And as he talked with him, he entered and found many who had come together.

## v28

And he said to them, You understand that it is unlawful for a [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 28.1 — man|man]] who is a Jew to join himself to or come near one of another race; yet God has shown me that I should not call any man common or unclean.

## v29

Hence, even without objection I came when I had been sent for. I ask therefore, For what reason have you sent for me?

## v30

And Cornelius said, Four days ago to this hour I was praying at the ninth hour in my house, and behold, a man stood before me in bright clothing,

## v31

And he said, Cornelius, your prayer has been heard and your alms have been remembered before God.

## v32

Send therefore to Joppa and call for Simon, who is surnamed Peter; this man is lodging in the house of Simon, a tanner, by the sea.

## v33

Therefore I sent men at once to you, and you have done nobly in coming. Now therefore we all are present before God to hear all the things that have been commanded to you by the Lord.

## v34

And opening his mouth, Peter said, In truth I perceive that God is not a respecter of persons,

## v35

But in every nation he who fears Him and works righteousness is [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 35.1 — acceptable|acceptable]] to Him.

## v36

The word which He sent to the sons of Israel in announcing the gospel of peace through Jesus Christ (this One is Lord of [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 36.1 — all|all]])

## v37

You yourselves know, [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 37.1 — the|the]] word which has been spoken throughout the whole of Judea, beginning from Galilee after the baptism which John proclaimed:

## v38

Jesus, the One from Nazareth, how God anointed Him with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all those who were being oppressed by the devil, for God was with Him.

## v39

And we are witnesses of all the things which He did, both in the land of the Jews and in Jerusalem; whom also they did away with by hanging Him on a tree.

## v40

This One, [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 40.1 — God|God]] raised on the third day; and He has made Him [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 40.2 — manifest|manifest]],

## v41

Not to all the people, but to witnesses appointed beforehand by God, to us, ones who ate and drank with Him after [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 41.1 — He|He]] rose from the dead.

## v42

And He has charged us to proclaim to the people and solemnly testify that this is the One who was designated by God to be the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 42.1 — Judge|Judge]] of the living and the dead.

## v43

To this One all the prophets testify that through His name everyone who believes into Him will receive [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 43.1 — forgiveness|forgiveness]] of sins.

## v44

While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 44.1 — fell|fell]] upon all those [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 44.2 — hearing|hearing]] the word.

## v45

And the believers who were of the circumcision, as many as had accompanied Peter, were amazed, because on the Gentiles also the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 45.1 — gift|gift]] of the Holy Spirit had been [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 45.2 — poured|poured]] out;

## v46

For they heard them [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 46.1 — speaking|speaking]] in tongues and [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 46.2 — magnifying|magnifying]] God. Then Peter answered,

## v47

Can anyone forbid the water so that these would not be baptized, who have received the Holy Spirit even [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 47.1 — as|as]] we?

## v48

And he charged them to be [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 48.1 — baptized|baptized]] [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 48.2 — in|in]] the [[Acts-10.fn#Footnote 48.3 — name|name]] of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to remain for some days.
