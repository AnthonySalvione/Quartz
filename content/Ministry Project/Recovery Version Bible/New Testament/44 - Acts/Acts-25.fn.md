## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — appeal

**Verse:** [[Acts-25#v11|Acts 25:11]]

For his defense Paul wanted to appeal to Caesar (see notes [1²](<Acts-22.fn#Footnote 1.2 — defense>) in [[Acts-22|Acts 22]] and [32²](<Acts-26.fn#Footnote 32.2 — not>) in [[Acts-26|Acts 26]]). This would allow him to fulfill his desire to see Rome for the furtherance of the Lord’s testimony ([[Acts-19#v21|19:21]]) and was according to the Lord’s indication to him ([[Acts-23#v11|23:11]]). Without this appeal he would have been killed by the Jews who plotted against him (cf. [[Acts-23#v12|23:12-15]]; [[Acts-25#v1|25:1-3]], [[Acts-25#v9|9]]) and would not have been able to write his last eight Epistles. Before his appeal to Rome, he had written only six Epistles: 1 and 2 Thessalonians, Galatians, Romans, and 1 and 2 Corinthians. During his first imprisonment in Rome he wrote Colossians, Ephesians, Philippians, and Philemon. After that imprisonment he wrote 1 Timothy, Titus, and Hebrews. Then, during his second imprisonment he wrote 2 Timothy. Without these last eight Epistles, what a lack the divine revelation would have and what a loss the church would have suffered! His appeal did render great profit and benefit to the Lord’s interest.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — council

**Verse:** [[Acts-25#v12|Acts 25:12]]

The council of a Roman province, composed of the councilors or assessors chosen by the governor of the province, with whom the governor usually consulted concerning an appeal like Paul’s.

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — Agrippa

**Verse:** [[Acts-25#v13|Acts 25:13]]

Herod Agrippa II, who reigned over the region north and east of Galilee, a Jew by religion, the son of the Herod in [[Acts-12|Acts 12]].

### Footnote 13.2 — Bernice

**Verse:** [[Acts-25#v13|Acts 25:13]]

The sister of Drusilla, Felix’s wife ([[Acts-24#v24|24:24]]). She was also a sister of Agrippa, with whom she lived incestuously. This again showed the corruption of the politicians in the circle of Roman politics. See [note 1³](<Acts-23.fn#Footnote 1.3 — conscience>) in [[Acts-23|Acts 23]].
