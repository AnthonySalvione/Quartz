# Acts-11 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Acts-11|Acts 11]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — circumcision

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v2|Acts 11:2]]

Circumcision was an outward ordinance inherited by the Jews from their forefathers, beginning from Abraham ([[Genesis-17#v9|Gen. 17:9-14]]). It made them distinct and separate from the Gentiles. It became a dead, traditional formality, a mere mark on the flesh without any spiritual significance, and became a great obstacle to the spread of God’s gospel according to His New Testament economy ([[Acts-15#v1|15:1]]; [[Galatians-02#v3|Gal. 2:3-4]]; [[Galatians-06#v12|6:12-13]]; [[Philippians-03#v2|Phil. 3:2]]; see [note 14¹](<Acts-10.fn#Footnote 14.1 — never>) in ch. 10).

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — life

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v18|Acts 11:18]]

Gk. zoe, referring to the life of God ([[Ephesians-04#v18|Eph. 4:18]]), the eternal life ([[1 John-01#v2|1 John 1:2]]), the uncreated, indestructible life ([[Hebrews-07#v16|Heb. 7:16]]), which is Christ Himself ([[John-14#v6|John 14:6]]; [[John-11#v25|11:25]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]]) as the life-giving Spirit ([[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45]]), of which life the Spirit is ([[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]]). This life is received by the believers through their believing into Christ ([[John-03#v15|John 3:15-16]]) after repentance, and issues in their full salvation ([[Romans-05#v10|Rom. 5:10]]). The gospel preached by Peter comprises the divine blessings not only of forgiveness ([[Acts-05#v31|5:31]]; [[Acts-10#v43|10:43]]) and salvation ([[Acts-02#v21|2:21]]; [[Acts-04#v12|4:12]]) but also of the Spirit ([[Acts-02#v38|2:38]]) and life. Forgiveness deals with people’s sins, and life, with people’s death ([[John-05#v24|John 5:24]]; [[1 John-03#v14|1 John 3:14]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v4|2 Cor. 5:4]]).

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v19|Acts 11:19]]

## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — sent

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v22|Acts 11:22]]

The Greek word denotes being sent out (on a mission) as an authoritative representative. Barnabas was sent out from Jerusalem to visit the believers in other places; he was sent with authority from the apostles, not from the church, because the apostles were there in Jerusalem.

### Footnote 22.2 — Barnabas

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v22|Acts 11:22]]

Saul was saved by the Lord directly without any preaching channel ([[Acts-09#v3|9:3-6]]) and was brought into identification with the Body of Christ through Ananias, a member of the Body of Christ ([[Acts-09#v10|9:10-19]]). However, he was introduced to practical fellowship with the disciples in Jerusalem through Barnabas ([[Acts-09#v26|9:26-28]]). Now Barnabas was sent from Jerusalem to go as far as Antioch to encourage the believers, and he went to Tarsus to bring Saul to Antioch ([[Acts-11#v25|vv. 25-26]]). This was a great step. It initiated Saul into the Lord’s move in spreading the gospel of His kingdom to the Gentile world ([[Acts-13#v1|13:1-3]]; see [note 25¹](<Acts-12.fn#Footnote 25.1 — Barnabas>) in [[Acts-12|Acts 12]]).

## Footnotes for verse 29

### Footnote 29.1 — disciples

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v29|Acts 11:29]]

This verse shows that the believers’ having all things in common ([[Acts-02#v44|2:44-45]]; [[Acts-04#v32|4:32]]), which was practiced at the time of Pentecost, had become a thing of the past at the time the matter here was mentioned, no more than ten years after Pentecost, long before Paul began his ministry to the Gentiles ([[Acts-13#v2|13:2-4]]). This word indicates further that there was no uniformly prescribed way in the early days of the church concerning the believers’ giving of material things; rather, it was left to each believer to give out of his own will and his own initiation.

### Footnote 29.2 — prospered

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v29|Acts 11:29]]

The Greek word here means to progress favorably, or to go on smoothly; hence, it denotes to thrive smoothly, to prosper, in an enterprise, an occupation, or other activities. Prosperity is given by God, and it issues in surplus and abundance.

### Footnote 29.3 — for

**Verse:** [[Acts-11#v29|Acts 11:29]]

Lit., for a service. Derived from the same Greek word as the word for ministered in [[Matthew-04#v11|Matt. 4:11]].
