---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

Now Peter and John were going up into the [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — temple|temple]] at the [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — ninth-hour|ninth-hour]] prayer;

## v2

And a certain man who had been lame from his mother’s womb was being carried there, whom they had laid day by day at the door of the temple which is called Beautiful to ask alms from those entering into the temple.

## v3

And he, seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple, began to ask to receive alms.

## v4

And Peter, gazing at him with John, said, Look at us!

## v5

And he turned his attention to them, expecting to receive something from them.

## v6

But Peter said, [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Silver|Silver]] and gold I do not possess, but what I have, this I give to you: In the name of Jesus Christ the [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Nazarene|Nazarene]] [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 6.3 — rise|rise]] up and walk.

## v7

And he seized him by the right hand and raised him up; and instantly his feet and ankles were made strong.

## v8

And leaping up and about, he stood and began to walk, and he entered with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God.

## v9

And all the people saw him walking and praising God,

## v10

And they recognized him, that he was the one who had been sitting for alms at the temple’s Beautiful Gate; and they were filled with amazement and astonishment at what had happened to him.

## v11

And while he was holding on to Peter and John, all the people ran together toward them at the portico called Solomon’s, greatly amazed.

## v12

And when Peter saw this, he replied to the people, [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Men|Men]] of Israel, why are you marveling at this? Or why are you gazing at us, as though by our own power or godliness we have made him walk?

## v13

The [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — God|God]] of Abraham and [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Isaac|Isaac]] and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 13.3 — glorified|glorified]] His Servant Jesus, whom you delivered up and denied in the presence of Pilate, when he had decided to release Him.

## v14

But you denied the holy and righteous One and asked that a man who was a murderer be granted to you;

## v15

And the [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Author|Author]] of life you killed, whom [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — God|God]] has raised from the dead, of [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 15.3 — which|which]] we are witnesses.

## v16

And [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — upon|upon]] faith in His name, His [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — name|name]] has made this man strong, whom you behold and know; and the faith which is through Him has given him this wholeness of health before you all.

## v17

And now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, as also your rulers did;

## v18

But the things which God [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — announced|announced]] beforehand through the mouth of all the prophets, that His Christ would suffer, He has thus fulfilled.

## v19

Repent therefore and turn, that your sins may be wiped away,

## v20

So that seasons of [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — refreshing|refreshing]] may come from the presence of the [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 20.2 — Lord|Lord]] and that He may send the Christ, who has been previously appointed for you, Jesus,

## v21

Whom heaven must indeed receive until the [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — times|times]] of the restoration of all things, of which God spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 21.2 — from|from]] of old.

## v22

Moses said, “A [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Prophet|Prophet]] will the Lord your God raise up unto you from your brothers, like me; Him shall you hear in whatever things He speaks to you.

## v23

And it shall be that every soul who does not hear that Prophet shall be utterly destroyed from among the people.”

## v24

And also all the prophets, from Samuel and those in succession after him, as many as spoke, also announced these days.

## v25

You are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant which God covenanted with your fathers, saying to Abraham, “And in your [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 25.1 — seed|seed]] shall all the families of the earth be blessed.”

## v26

To you first, God, having raised up His Servant, has sent [[Acts-03.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Him|Him]] to bless you in turning each of you away from your wicked deeds.
