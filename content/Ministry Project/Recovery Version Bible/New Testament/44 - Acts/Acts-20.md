---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

And after the uproar had ceased, Paul, having sent for the disciples and having exhorted them, took leave of them and went off to go into [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Macedonia|Macedonia]].

## v2

And when he had passed through those parts and had exhorted them with many words, he came into Greece,

## v3

And spent [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 3.1 — three|three]] months there. And when a plot was made against him by the Jews as he was about to set sail for Syria, he [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 3.2 — resolved|resolved]] to return through Macedonia.

## v4

And Sopater of Berea, the son of Pyrrhus, accompanied him, as well as Aristarchus and Secundus of the Thessalonians and [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Gaius|Gaius]] of Derbe and Timothy and the Asians, Tychicus and Trophimus.

## v5

These had gone on ahead and were waiting for us at Troas.

## v6

And we sailed away from Philippi after the days of Unleavened Bread and in five days came to them in Troas, where we spent seven days.

## v7

And on the [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 7.1 — first|first]] day of the week, when [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 7.2 — we|we]] gathered together to break bread, Paul conversed with them since he was to go forth on the next day; and he extended his message until midnight.

## v8

And there were a considerable number of lamps in the upper room where we were gathered together.

## v9

And a certain young man named Eutychus was sitting in the window and began to sink into deep sleep while Paul conversed longer; and when he had been overcome by sleep, he fell down from the third story and was taken up dead.

## v10

But Paul went down and fell upon him, and embracing him, he said, Do not make a commotion, for his soul is in him.

## v11

And when he had gone up and broken the bread and [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 11.1 — eaten|eaten]] and had spoken for a considerable time, until daybreak, he therefore went forth.

## v12

And they brought the boy alive and were comforted, and that not moderately.

## v13

But we, going ahead onto the ship, set sail for Assos, from there intending to pick up Paul, for so he had arranged it, himself intending to go by foot.

## v14

And when he met us in Assos, we picked him up and came to Mitylene.

## v15

And sailing away from there, on the following day we arrived opposite Chios; and on the next day we put in at Samos. And on the day following we came to Miletus,

## v16

For Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus so that he might not have to spend time in Asia; for he was hurrying so that if possible he might be in Jerusalem on the [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 16.1 — day|day]] of Pentecost.

## v17

And from Miletus he sent word to Ephesus and called for the elders of the church.

## v18

And when they came to him, he said to them, You yourselves know, from the first day that I set foot in Asia, how I was with you all the time,

## v19

Serving the Lord as a slave with all humility and tears and trials which came upon me by the plots of the Jews;

## v20

How I did not withhold any of those things that are profitable by not declaring them to you and by not teaching you publicly and from house to house,

## v21

Solemnly testifying both to Jews and to Greeks repentance unto God and faith [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 21.1 — in|in]] our Lord [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 21.2 — Jesus|Jesus]].

## v22

And now, behold, I am going [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 22.1 — bound|bound]] in the [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 22.2 — spirit|spirit]] to Jerusalem, not knowing what will meet me [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 22.3 — there|there]],

## v23

[[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Except|Except]] that the Holy Spirit solemnly testifies to me in city after city, saying that bonds and afflictions await me.

## v24

But I [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 24.1 — consider|consider]] my [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 24.2 — life|life]] of no account as if precious to myself, in order that I may finish my course and the ministry which I have received from the Lord Jesus to solemnly testify of the gospel of the grace of God.

## v25

And now, behold, I know that you all, among whom I have gone about proclaiming the [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 25.1 — kingdom|kingdom]], will see my face [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 25.2 — no|no]] more.

## v26

Therefore I testify to you [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 26.1 — on|on]] this day that [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 26.2 — I|I]] am clean from the blood of all men,

## v27

For I did not shrink from declaring to you all the counsel of God.

## v28

Take heed to yourselves and to all the [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 28.1 — flock|flock]], among whom the Holy [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 28.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] has placed you as [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 28.3 — overseers|overseers]] to [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 28.4 — shepherd|shepherd]] the church of God, which He obtained through [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 28.5 — His|His]] own blood.

## v29

I know that after my departure fierce wolves will come in among you, not [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 29.1 — sparing|sparing]] the flock.

## v30

And from among you yourselves men will rise up, speaking perverted things to [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 30.1 — draw|draw]] away the disciples after them.

## v31

Therefore watch, remembering that for three years, night and day, I did not cease admonishing each one with tears.

## v32

And now I commit you to [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 32.1 — God|God]] and to the word of His [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 32.2 — grace|grace]], which is able to build you up and to give you the [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 32.3 — inheritance|inheritance]] among all those who have been [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 32.4 — sanctified|sanctified]].

## v33

I have coveted no one’s silver or gold or clothing.

## v34

You yourselves know that these [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 34.1 — hands|hands]] have ministered to my needs and to those who are with me.

## v35

In all things I have shown you by example that toiling in this way we ought to support [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 35.1 — the|the]] weak and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, that He Himself said, [[Acts-20.fn#Footnote 35.2 — It|It]] is more blessed to give than to receive.

## v36

And when he had said these things, he knelt down with them all and prayed.

## v37

And there was considerable weeping by all; and falling on Paul’s neck, they kissed him affectionately,

## v38

Especially pained over the word which he had spoken, that they would no longer behold his face. And they escorted him to the ship.
