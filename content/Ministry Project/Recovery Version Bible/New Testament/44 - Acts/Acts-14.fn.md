## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — souls

**Verse:** [[Acts-14#v22|Acts 14:22]]

Man’s soul is composed of his mind, emotion, and will. To establish the souls of the disciples is to establish them (1) in their mind, that they may know and understand the Lord and the things concerning Him ([[1 Corinthians-02#v16|1 Cor. 2:16]]; [[Philippians-03#v10|Phil. 3:10]]); (2) in their emotion, that they may love the Lord and have a heart for the Lord’s interest ([[Mark-12#v30|Mark 12:30]]; [[Romans-16#v4|Rom. 16:4]]); and (3) in their will, that they may be strong to remain with the Lord and do the things that please the Lord ([[Acts-11#v23|11:23]]; [[Colossians-01#v10|Col. 1:10]]; [[1 Thessalonians-04#v1|1 Thes. 4:1]]).

### Footnote 22.2 — continue

**Verse:** [[Acts-14#v22|Acts 14:22]]

## Footnotes for verse 23

### Footnote 23.1 — elders

**Verse:** [[Acts-14#v23|Acts 14:23]]

All the churches here in which elders were appointed by the apostles had been established within less than one year. Hence, the elders appointed in these churches could not have been fully mature. They must have been considered elders because they were comparatively the most mature among the believers. They were not voted in by their congregations but were appointed by the apostles according to their maturity of life in Christ. They were charged by the apostles to care for the leadership and shepherding in their churches (see notes [2¹](<1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — overseer>) in [[1 Timothy-03|1 Tim. 3]] and [3³](<1 Peter-05.fn#Footnote 3.3 — becoming>) in [[1 Peter-05|1 Pet. 5]]).

### Footnote 23.2 — in

**Verse:** [[Acts-14#v23|Acts 14:23]]

In every church equals in every city in [[Titus-01#v5|Titus 1:5]] (see [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — every|note 5¹]] there).
