---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

The [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — former|former]] account I have made, O [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Theophilus|Theophilus]], concerning all the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — things|things]] that Jesus began both to do and to teach,

## v2

Until the day on which He was taken up, after He had given commandment through the Holy [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] to the apostles whom He chose;

## v3

To whom also He [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — presented|presented]] Himself alive after His suffering by many irrefutable proofs, [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — appearing|appearing]] to them through a period of [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — forty|forty]] days and speaking the things concerning the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 3.4 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God.

## v4

And as He [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — met|met]] together with them, He charged them not to depart from Jerusalem, but to wait for the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — promise|promise]] of the Father, which, He said, You heard from Me;

## v5

For John [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — baptized|baptized]] with [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — water|water]], but you shall be baptized in the Holy Spirit not many days from now.

## v6

So the ones who came together asked Him, saying, Lord, are You at this time restoring the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] to Israel?

## v7

But He said to them, It is not for you to know times or seasons which the Father has set by His own authority.

## v8

But you shall [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — receive|receive]] power when the Holy Spirit comes [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — upon|upon]] you, and you shall be My [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — witnesses|witnesses]] both in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria and unto the uttermost part of the earth.

## v9

And when He had said these things, while they were looking on, He was lifted up, and a cloud took Him away from their sight.

## v10

And while they were looking intently into heaven as He went, behold, two men in white clothing stood beside them,

## v11

Who also said, [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Men|Men]] of Galilee, why do you stand looking into [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — heaven|heaven]]? This Jesus, who has been taken up from you into heaven, will [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — come|come]] in the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 11.4 — same|same]] way as you [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 11.5 — beheld|beheld]] Him going into heaven.

## v12

Then they returned to [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] from the mount called Olivet, which is near Jerusalem, a [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — Sabbath|Sabbath]] day’s journey away.

## v13

And when they entered, they went up to the upper room where they were residing, Peter and John and James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James the son of Alphaeus and Simon the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Zealot|Zealot]] and Judas the [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — brother|brother]] of James.

## v14

These all [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — continued|continued]] steadfastly [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — with|with]] one accord in [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 14.3 — prayer|prayer]], together with the women and [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 14.4 — Mary|Mary]] the mother of Jesus, and with His brothers.

## v15

And in those days Peter stood up in the midst of the brothers and [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — said|said]] (there was a group of persons gathered together, about a hundred and twenty),

## v16

[[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Men|Men]], brothers, the Scripture had to be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit spoke before through the mouth of David concerning Judas, who became a guide to those who seized Jesus;

## v17

For he was numbered among us and was allotted his portion of [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — this|this]] ministry.

## v18

(Now this man acquired a piece of land with the wages of unrighteousness; and falling headlong, he burst in the middle, and all his inward parts gushed out.

## v19

And it became known to all those dwelling in Jerusalem, so that that piece of land was called in their own dialect, [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Akeldamach|Akeldamach]], that is, Field of [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — Blood|Blood]].)

## v20

For it is written in the book of Psalms, “Let his habitation become desolate, and let no one dwell in it”; and, “His overseership let another take.”

## v21

It is necessary therefore that of the men who accompanied us all the time in which the Lord Jesus went in and went out [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — among|among]] us,

## v22

Beginning from the baptism of John until the day on which He was taken up from us, one of these should become a witness of His [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — resurrection|resurrection]] with us.

## v23

And they proposed two, Joseph, called Barsabbas, who was surnamed Justus, and Matthias.

## v24

And they prayed and said, You Lord, Knower of the hearts of all, show us clearly the one whom You have chosen of these two

## v25

To take the place of this ministry and apostleship, from which Judas turned aside to go to his own place.

## v26

And they cast [[Acts-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — lots|lots]] for them, and the lot fell on Matthias; and he was counted with the eleven apostles.
