# Acts-12 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Acts-12|Acts 12]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — Herod

**Verse:** [[Acts-12#v1|Acts 12:1]]

Herod, Agrippa I, whose successor was King Agrippa (II) in [[Acts-25#v13|25:13]]. Both were different from Herod the tetrarch in [[Acts-13#v1|13:1]]. Thus far the church had suffered persecution only from the Jewish religion. Now Gentile politics began to participate in the persecution in cooperation with the Jewish religion ([[Acts-12#v11|v. 11]]).

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — quaternions

**Verse:** [[Acts-12#v4|Acts 12:4]]

A quaternion was a group of four soldiers.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — came

**Verse:** [[Acts-12#v11|Acts 12:11]]

Perhaps Peter felt that he had gone out of himself as in a trance; now he came to himself, that is, became sober. Cf. [note 10³](<Acts-10.fn#Footnote 10.3 — trance>) in [[Acts-10|Acts 10]].

## Footnotes for verse 15

### Footnote 15.1 — his

**Verse:** [[Acts-12#v15|Acts 12:15]]

Not only the apostle Peter had an angel; even the little ones among the believers have their angels ([[Matthew-18#v10|Matt. 18:10]]). Angels are servants to the believers who inherit God’s salvation (see [note 14¹](<Hebrews-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — those>) in [[Hebrews-01|Heb. 1]]). This is an arrangement in God’s eternal economy.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — Barnabas

**Verse:** [[Acts-12#v25|Acts 12:25]]

[[Acts-12#v1|Verses 1-24]] are a parenthetical section, a record of Peter’s persecution. This verse is a continuation of [[Acts-11#v30|11:30]], which, with the preceding verses from [[Acts-11#v22|11:22]], is a narration of Paul’s initiation into his apostolic ministry (see [note 22²](<Acts-11.fn#Footnote 22.2 — Barnabas>) in [[Acts-11|Acts 11]]). The passage from [[Acts-11#v19|11:19]]—[[Acts-12#v25|12:25]] is a transitional record between Peter’s apostolic ministry to the Jews in [[Acts-02|Acts 2—11]] and Paul’s apostolic ministry to the Gentiles in [[Acts-13|Acts 13—28]] (cf. [[Galatians-02#v7|Gal. 2:7-8]]).
