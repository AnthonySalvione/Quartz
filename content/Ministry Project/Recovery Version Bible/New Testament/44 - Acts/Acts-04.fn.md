## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — gathered

**Verse:** [[Acts-04#v5|Acts 4:5]]

This was a gathering of the Jewish Sanhedrin ([[Acts-04#v15|v. 15]]). In the four Gospels this Sanhedrin, composed of the Jewish leaders, became the strongest opponent of the Lord Jesus and His ministry and condemned Him to death ([[Matthew-26#v59|Matt. 26:59]]). Now, in this book, the same Sanhedrin, with the same constituents, began the persecution of the apostles and their ministry ([[Acts-05#v21|5:21]]; [[Acts-06#v12|6:12]]; [[Acts-22#v30|22:30]]). This indicates that Judaism had fallen into the hand of God’s enemy, Satan the devil, and was being used by him in his attempt to frustrate and even destroy God’s move in His New Testament economy for the carrying out of His eternal purpose, that is, to bring His kingdom to the earth by establishing and building up the churches through the preaching of the gospel of Christ.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — stone

**Verse:** [[Acts-04#v11|Acts 4:11]]

This word was spoken by the Lord in [[Matthew-21#v42|Matt. 21:42]] (see [note 11¹](<Acts-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — stone>) there), quoted from [[Psalm-118#v22|Psa. 118:22]]. It unveiled the Jewish leaders’ rejection of Him and God’s honoring of Him for the building of His habitation among His people on the earth. By this word Peter learned to know the Lord as the precious stone held in honor by God, as he expounded concerning Him in his first Epistle ([[1 Peter-02#v4|1 Pet. 2:4-7]]). Peter’s quoting of this word indicates that he preached Christ not only as the Savior for the sinners’ salvation but also as the stone for God’s building. It is such a Christ who is the unique salvation to sinners, and it is in His unique name under heaven, a name despised and rejected by the Jewish leaders but honored and exalted by God ([[Philippians-02#v9|Phil. 2:9-10]]), that sinners must be saved ([[Acts-04#v12|v. 12]]) not only from sin ([[Matthew-01#v21|Matt. 1:21]]) but also to participate in God’s building ([[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]]).
