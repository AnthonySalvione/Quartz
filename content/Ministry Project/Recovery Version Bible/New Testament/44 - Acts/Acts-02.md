---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

And as the day of [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Pentecost|Pentecost]] was being fulfilled, they were all together in the same place.

## v2

And suddenly there was a sound out of heaven, as of a rushing violent [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — wind|wind]], and it [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — filled|filled]] the whole house where they were sitting.

## v3

And there appeared to them [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — tongues|tongues]] as of [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — fire|fire]], which were distributed; and it [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 3.3 — sat|sat]] on each one of them;

## v4

And they were [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — all|all]] [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — filled|filled]] with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in different [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 4.3 — tongues|tongues]], even as the Spirit gave to them to [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 4.4 — speak|speak]] forth.

## v5

Now there were dwelling in Jerusalem [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Jews|Jews]], devout men, from every nation under heaven.

## v6

And when this sound occurred, the multitude came together and was confounded because each one heard them speaking in his own dialect.

## v7

And they were amazed and marveled, saying, Behold, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?

## v8

And how is it that we each hear them in our own dialect in which we were born?

## v9

Parthians and Medes and Elamites and those dwelling in Mesopotamia, both in Judea and Cappadocia, in Pontus and Asia,

## v10

Both in Phrygia and Pamphylia, in Egypt and the parts of Libya around Cyrene, and the sojourning of Rome, both Jews and [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — proselytes|proselytes]],

## v11

Cretans and Arabians, we hear them speaking in our [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — tongues|tongues]] the magnificent works of God?

## v12

And they were all amazed and perplexed, saying to one another, What does this mean?

## v13

But others jeered and said, They are full of [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — new|new]] wine!

## v14

But Peter, standing with the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — eleven|eleven]], lifted up his voice and spoke forth to them: [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Men|Men]] of Judea, and all who are dwelling in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and give ear to my words.

## v15

For these men are not drunk, as you suppose, for it is the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — third|third]] hour of the day;

## v16

But this is what is spoken through the prophet Joel:

## v17

“And it shall be in the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — last|last]] days, says God, that I will [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — pour|pour]] out [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 17.3 — of|of]] My Spirit [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 17.4 — upon|upon]] [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 17.5 — all|all]] flesh, and your sons and your daughters shall [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 17.6 — prophesy|prophesy]], and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream things in dreams;

## v18

And indeed upon My slaves, both men and women, I will pour out of My Spirit in those days, and they shall prophesy.

## v19

And I will [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — show|show]] wonders in heaven above and signs on the earth below, blood and fire and vapor of smoke.

## v20

The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and notable [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — day|day]] of the Lord comes.

## v21

And it shall be that everyone who [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — calls|calls]] on the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 21.2 — name|name]] of the Lord shall be [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 21.3 — saved|saved]].”

## v22

[[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Men|Men]] of Israel, hear these words: Jesus the Nazarene, a [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 22.2 — man|man]] [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 22.3 — shown|shown]] by God to you to be approved by works of power and wonders and signs, which God did through Him in your midst, even as you yourselves know —

## v23

This man, delivered up by the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — determined|determined]] counsel and [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — foreknowledge|foreknowledge]] of God, you, through the hand of [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 23.3 — lawless|lawless]] men, nailed to a [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 23.4 — cross|cross]] and killed;

## v24

Whom [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 24.1 — God|God]] has raised up, having loosed the pangs of death, since it was [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 24.2 — not|not]] possible for Him to be held by it.

## v25

For David says regarding Him, “[[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 25.1 — I|I]] saw the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 25.2 — Lord|Lord]] continually before me, because [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 25.3 — He|He]] is on my right hand, that I may not be shaken.

## v26

Therefore my heart was made glad and my [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 26.1 — tongue|tongue]] exulted; moreover, also my flesh will [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 26.2 — rest|rest]] in hope,

## v27

Because You will not abandon my soul to [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 27.1 — Hades|Hades]], nor will You permit Your [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 27.2 — Holy|Holy]] One to see [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 27.3 — corruption|corruption]].

## v28

You have made known to me the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 28.1 — ways|ways]] of life; You will make me full of gladness with Your [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 28.2 — presence|presence]].”

## v29

[[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Men|Men]], brothers, I can say to you plainly concerning the patriarch David that he both deceased and was buried, and his tomb is among us until this day.

## v30

Therefore, being a prophet and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him to seat One from the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 30.1 — fruit|fruit]] of his loins upon his [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 30.2 — throne|throne]],

## v31

He, seeing this beforehand, spoke concerning the resurrection of the Christ, that neither was He abandoned to Hades, nor did His flesh see corruption.

## v32

This Jesus [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 32.1 — God|God]] has raised up, of [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 32.2 — which|which]] we all are witnesses.

## v33

Therefore having been exalted to the right hand of God and having received the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 33.1 — promise|promise]] of the Holy Spirit from the Father, He has poured out this which you both see and hear.

## v34

For David did [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 34.1 — not|not]] ascend into the heavens, but he himself says, “The [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 34.2 — Lord|Lord]] said to my Lord, Sit at My [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 34.3 — right|right]] hand

## v35

Until I [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 35.1 — set|set]] Your enemies as a footstool for Your feet.”

## v36

Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly that God has made Him both [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 36.1 — Lord|Lord]] and Christ, this Jesus whom [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 36.2 — you|you]] have crucified.

## v37

And when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and they said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, What should we do, [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 37.1 — brothers|brothers]]?

## v38

And Peter said to them, [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.1 — Repent|Repent]] and each one of you be [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.2 — baptized|baptized]] [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.3 — upon|upon]] the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.4 — name|name]] of Jesus Christ for the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.5 — forgiveness|forgiveness]] of your sins, and you will receive the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.6 — gift|gift]] of the Holy [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 38.7 — Spirit|Spirit]].

## v39

For to [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 39.1 — you|you]] is the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 39.2 — promise|promise]] and to your children, and to all [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 39.3 — who|who]] are far off, [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 39.4 — as|as]] many as the Lord our God calls to Himself.

## v40

And with many other words he solemnly [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 40.1 — testified|testified]] and exhorted them, saying, [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 40.2 — Be|Be]] saved from this crooked [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 40.3 — generation|generation]].

## v41

Those then who received his word were [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 41.1 — baptized|baptized]], and there were added on that day about three thousand souls.

## v42

And they continued steadfastly in the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 42.1 — teaching|teaching]] and the fellowship of the apostles, in the breaking of bread and the prayers.

## v43

And fear was upon every soul; and many [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 43.1 — wonders|wonders]] and signs took place through the apostles.

## v44

And all those who believed were together and had all things [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 44.1 — common|common]];

## v45

And they [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 45.1 — sold|sold]] their properties and possessions and divided them to all, as anyone had need.

## v46

And day by day, continuing steadfastly with one accord in the [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 46.1 — temple|temple]] and [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 46.2 — breaking|breaking]] bread from [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 46.3 — house|house]] to house, they partook of their food with exultation and [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 46.4 — simplicity|simplicity]] of heart,

## v47

Praising God and [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 47.1 — having|having]] grace with all the people. And the Lord added [[Acts-02.fn#Footnote 47.2 — together|together]] day by day those who were being saved.
