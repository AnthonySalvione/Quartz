---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

And in these days, as the disciples were multiplying in number, a murmuring of the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Hellenists|Hellenists]] against the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Hebrews|Hebrews]] occurred, because their widows were being overlooked in the daily dispensing.

## v2

And the twelve called the multitude of the disciples to them and said, It is not [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — fitting|fitting]] for us to forsake the word of God and serve tables.

## v3

But brothers, look for seven well-attested men from among you, [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — full|full]] of the Spirit and of [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 3.2 — wisdom|wisdom]], whom we will appoint over this need.

## v4

But we will continue steadfastly in [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — prayer|prayer]] and in the ministry of the word.

## v5

And the word pleased all the multitude; and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit, and Philip and Prochorus and Nicanor and Timon and Parmenas and Nicolas, a [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — proselyte|proselyte]] of Antioch,

## v6

Whom they set before the apostles; and when they had prayed, they [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — laid|laid]] their hands on [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — them|them]].

## v7

And the word of God [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — grew|grew]], and the number of the disciples in Jerusalem multiplied greatly; and a large number of the priests obeyed the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 7.2 — faith|faith]].

## v8

And Stephen, full of grace and power, did great [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — wonders|wonders]] and signs among the people.

## v9

But some of those of the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — synagogue|synagogue]] which is called the synagogue of the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Libertines|Libertines]] and of the Cyrenians and of the Alexandrians and of those from Cilicia and Asia rose up and disputed with Stephen;

## v10

Yet they were not able to withstand the wisdom and the Spirit with which he spoke.

## v11

Then they instigated some men to say, We have heard him speaking blasphemous words against Moses and God.

## v12

And they stirred up the people and the elders and the scribes, and they came upon him and seized him and led him to the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Sanhedrin|Sanhedrin]].

## v13

And they set up false witnesses, saying, This man does not cease speaking words against this holy [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — place|place]] and the law,

## v14

For we have heard him saying that this Jesus the Nazarene will [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — destroy|destroy]] this place and will change the customs which Moses delivered to us.

## v15

And all those sitting in the Sanhedrin looked intently at him and saw his face as though it were the [[Acts-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — face|face]] of an angel.
