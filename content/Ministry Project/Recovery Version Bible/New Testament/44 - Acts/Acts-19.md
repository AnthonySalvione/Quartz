---
Author: Luke
---
## v1

Now while Apollos was in Corinth, Paul, having passed through the upper districts, came down to Ephesus and found some disciples,

## v2

And he said to them, Did you [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 2.1 — receive|receive]] the Holy Spirit when you believed? And they said to him, On the contrary, we did [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 2.2 — not|not]] even hear whether there is a Holy Spirit.

## v3

And he said, Into what then were you baptized? And they said, Into [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 3.1 — John’s|John’s]] baptism.

## v4

And Paul said, John baptized with a baptism of repentance, telling the people that they should believe into the One coming after him, that is, into Jesus.

## v5

And when they heard this, they were baptized [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 5.1 — into|into]] the name of the Lord Jesus.

## v6

And when Paul [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 6.1 — laid|laid]] his hands on them, the Holy Spirit came [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 6.2 — upon|upon]] them, and they [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 6.3 — spoke|spoke]] in tongues and prophesied.

## v7

And there were in all about twelve men.

## v8

And he [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 8.1 — entered|entered]] into the [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 8.2 — synagogue|synagogue]] and spoke boldly for three months, reasoning and persuading them in the things concerning the [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 8.3 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God.

## v9

But when some were hardened and would not be persuaded, speaking evil of the [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Way|Way]] before the multitude, he withdrew from them and separated the disciples, reasoning daily in the school of [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Tyrannus|Tyrannus]].

## v10

And this took place for two years, so that all those dwelling in Asia heard the word of the Lord, both Jews and Greeks.

## v11

And God did [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 11.1 — works|works]] of power of no ordinary kind through the hands of Paul,

## v12

So that even handkerchiefs or aprons were carried away from his [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 12.1 — body|body]] to the sick, and their diseases left them, and the evil [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 12.2 — spirits|spirits]] went out.

## v13

But some of the Jewish exorcists who also went about took it upon themselves to name over those who had evil spirits the name of the Lord Jesus, saying, I adjure you by the Jesus whom Paul preaches.

## v14

And there were seven sons of a certain Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, doing this.

## v15

And the evil spirit answered and said to them, Jesus I know, and with Paul I am acquainted; but who are you?

## v16

And the man in whom the evil spirit was leaped upon them, subdued both of them, and overpowered them, so that they escaped out of that house naked and wounded.

## v17

And this became known to all, both Jews and Greeks, dwelling in Ephesus; and fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was magnified.

## v18

And many of those who had believed came, [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 18.1 — confessing|confessing]] and making known their [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 18.2 — practices|practices]].

## v19

And a considerable number of those who practiced magic brought their books together and [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 19.1 — burned|burned]] them before all; and they counted up the price of them and found it to be fifty thousand [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 19.2 — pieces|pieces]] of silver.

## v20

[[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Thus|Thus]], the word of the Lord grew mightily and prevailed.

## v21

And when these things were fulfilled, Paul [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 21.1 — purposed|purposed]] in his [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 21.2 — spirit|spirit]] to pass through Macedonia and Achaia and [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 21.3 — go|go]] to Jerusalem, saying, After I have been there, I must also [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 21.4 — see|see]] Rome.

## v22

And having sent into Macedonia two of those who served him, Timothy and [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Erastus|Erastus]], he himself [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 22.2 — stayed|stayed]] in Asia for a time.

## v23

And about that time no small disturbance took place concerning the [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Way|Way]].

## v24

For a certain man named [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 24.1 — Demetrius|Demetrius]], a silversmith who [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 24.2 — made|made]] silver shrines of [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 24.3 — Artemis|Artemis]], afforded no little business to the craftsmen.

## v25

These he [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 25.1 — assembled|assembled]] together with the workmen of similar trades as well and said, [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 25.2 — Men|Men]], you know that from this business we have our prosperity.

## v26

And you observe and hear that not only at Ephesus but in almost all of Asia this Paul has persuaded and perverted a considerable number, saying that the gods being made by hands are no gods at all.

## v27

And not only is there danger that our trade will come into disrepute, but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis will be counted as nothing and that the magnificence of her whom the whole of Asia and the inhabited earth worship will also be overthrown.

## v28

And when they heard this, they became full of rage and began to cry out, saying, Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!

## v29

And the city was filled with confusion; and they rushed with one accord into the theater, seizing [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Gaius|Gaius]] and Aristarchus of Macedonia, Paul’s traveling companions.

## v30

And when Paul intended to enter into the populace, the disciples would not allow him.

## v31

And some of the [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 31.1 — Asiarchs|Asiarchs]] also, who were his friends, sent to him and entreated him not to venture into the theater.

## v32

So then some were crying out one thing, and some another; for the assembly was in confusion, and the majority did not know for what cause they had come together.

## v33

And they drew [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 33.1 — Alexander|Alexander]] out of the crowd, the Jews having pushed him forward; and Alexander, motioning with his hand, wanted to make a defense to the populace.

## v34

But when they realized that he was a Jew, one voice arose from all for about two hours, crying out, Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!

## v35

And the town clerk, having quieted the crowd, said, [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 35.1 — Men|Men]] of Ephesus, which of you men is there then who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is the temple keeper of the great Artemis and of the image [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 35.2 — fallen|fallen]] from Zeus?

## v36

Since these things therefore cannot be disputed, you ought to be quiet and do nothing rash;

## v37

For you have brought forth these men who are neither temple robbers nor blasphemers of our goddess.

## v38

If then Demetrius and the craftsmen with him have a matter against anyone, courts are being conducted and there are proconsuls; let them bring charges against one another.

## v39

But if you seek after anything further, it shall be settled in the [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 39.1 — legal|legal]] assembly.

## v40

For indeed we are in danger of being charged with insurrection for today’s affair, since there is no reason for it; and with reference to it we will not be able to give an account concerning this disorderly gathering.

## v41

And when he had said these things, he [[Acts-19.fn#Footnote 41.1 — dismissed|dismissed]] the assembly.
