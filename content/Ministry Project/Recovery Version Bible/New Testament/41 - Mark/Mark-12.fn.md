## Footnotes for verse 9

### Footnote 9.1 — others

**Verse:** [[Mark-12#v9|Mark 12:9]]

The Jewish leaders, as the builders of God’s building, rejected the Slave-Savior, who was the stone that became the head of the corner of the building of God ([[Mark-12#v10|v. 10]]). Because of this, God rejected them and gave His building work to others—the church. In this Gospel this is the only direct indication regarding the church.

## Footnotes for verse 37

### Footnote 37.1 — son

**Verse:** [[Mark-12#v37|Mark 12:37]]

In the type, the passover lamb was examined for four days before it was killed ([[Exodus-12#v3|Exo. 12:3-6]]). The Slave-Savior also, as the real Passover lamb ([[1 Corinthians-05#v7|1 Cor. 5:7]]), was examined for four days before He was killed. He came to Bethany six days before the Passover ([[John-12#v1|John 12:1]]; [[Mark-11#v1|Mark 11:1]]). On the next day He came into Jerusalem and went back to Bethany ([[John-12#v12|John 12:12]]; [[Mark-11#v11|Mark 11:11]]). On the third day He came to Jerusalem again ([[Mark-11#v12|11:12-15]]) and began to be examined according to the Jewish law by the leaders of the Jews ([[Mark-11#v27|11:27]]—[[Mark-12#v37|12:37]]; [[Mark-14#v53|14:53-65]]; [[John-18#v13|John 18:13]], [[John-18#v19|19-24]]) and according to the Roman law by Pilate, the Roman governor ([[John-18#v28|John 18:28]]—[[John-19#v6|19:6]]; see [note 1²](<Mark-15.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Pilate>) in [[Mark-15|Mark 15]]). He was examined until the day of the Passover, when He was crucified ([[Mark-14#v12|14:12]]; [[John-18#v28|John 18:28]]; see [note 12²](<Mark-14.fn#Footnote 12.2 — sacrificed>) in [[Mark-14|Mark 14]]). This insidious and ensnaring examination, coming from many angles, took exactly four days, and He passed, proving that He was fully qualified to be the Lamb required by God for the accomplishing of His redemption, that He may pass over the sinners, both Jews and Gentiles. See notes [1²](<Mark-15.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Pilate>) in [[Mark-15|Mark 15]], [2¹](<Matthew-26.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Passover>) in [[Matthew-26|Matt. 26]], and [13¹](<John-18.fn#Footnote 13.1 — led>) in [[John-18|John 18]].

## Footnotes for verse 41

### Footnote 41.1 — watched

**Verse:** [[Mark-12#v41|Mark 12:41]]

The Slave-Savior was God living in His humanity. As such, He was concerned to observe how God’s people expressed their loyalty in their offering to Him. By this kind of observation He praised the widow’s loyalty to God. The Slave-Savior’s observation is more penetrating than man’s.

### Footnote 41.2 — money

**Verse:** [[Mark-12#v41|Mark 12:41]]

Lit., copper; referring to copper money.
