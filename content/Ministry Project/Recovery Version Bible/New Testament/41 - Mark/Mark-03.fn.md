## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — anger

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v5|Mark 3:5]]

Toward the opposers the Savior’s anger was exercised, and He was greatly grieved at their hardness; but toward the sick His compassion was exercised, and He restored the withered member. His anger and grief may be considered the expression of the genuineness of His humanity, whereas His compassion and healing were a merging of His human virtue with His divine power, which were manifested simultaneously to men. Thus, His deity in His humanity was again expressed before men. See [note 14¹](<Mark-10.fn#Footnote 14.1 — indignant>) in [[Mark-10|Mark 10]].

### Footnote 5.2 — restored

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v5|Mark 3:5]]

See [note 31¹](<Mark-01.fn#Footnote 31.1 — left>) in [[Mark-01|Mark 1]]. The restoration of the withered hand displayed the power of the Slave-Savior’s deity.

### Footnote 22.4 — ruler

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v22|Mark 3:22]]

The Slave-Savior cast out demons, the evil workers for the dark kingdom of Satan, yet the opposers said that He did it by the ruler of the demons. What a subtlety of the evil one, who motivated these evil opposers to say this! They were the co-workers of the evil one and even were one with him.

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — kingdom

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v24|Mark 3:24]]

This indicates that Satan has not only a house but also a kingdom. His house is a house of sin ([[1 John-03#v8|1 John 3:8]], [[1 John-03#v10|10]]), and his kingdom is a kingdom of darkness ([[Colossians-01#v13|Col. 1:13]]). The sinners belong to both his house and his kingdom. The demons belong to his kingdom and possess people for his kingdom.

## Footnotes for verse 27

### Footnote 27.1 — goods

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v27|Mark 3:27]]

Sinners kept in Satan’s house for his kingdom. The Slave-Savior bound Satan, the strong man, and entered into his house to plunder the sinners so that through regeneration they could be brought into the house of God ([[Ephesians-02#v19|Eph. 2:19]]) for the kingdom of God ([[John-03#v5|John 3:5]]).

### Footnote 27.2 — binds

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v27|Mark 3:27]]

This indicates that while the Slave-Savior was doing the gospel service, He was binding the strong man, Satan. The gospel service is a warfare to destroy Satan and his kingdom of darkness.

## Footnotes for verse 29

### Footnote 29.1 — Holy

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v29|Mark 3:29]]

This shows that the Slave-Savior’s gospel service, especially in casting out the demons to destroy the dark kingdom of Satan, was by the Holy Spirit, with whom He was anointed and by whom He was being led continuously in His move ([[Luke-04#v18|Luke 4:18]], [[Luke-04#v1|1]]; [[Mark-01#v12|Mark 1:12]]).

### Footnote 29.2 — everlasting

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v29|Mark 3:29]]

A sin that will not be forgiven forever.

## Footnotes for verse 31

### Footnote 31.1 — mother

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v31|Mark 3:31]]

After the opposers’ blasphemy, which was instigated by Satan, the relatives of the Slave-Savior came again to trouble Him with their natural concern for Him. Undoubtedly, this too was motivated by the evil one.

## Footnotes for verse 33

### Footnote 33.1 — Who

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v33|Mark 3:33]]

See [note 48¹](<Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 48.1 — Who>) in [[Matthew-12|Matt. 12]]. This indicated the Slave-Savior’s rejection of His relatives’ natural concern. To defeat the evil one’s schemes and to fulfill His gospel service, He refused to remain in any relationship in the natural life. This displayed His absoluteness for God in His humanity.

## Footnotes for verse 35

### Footnote 35.1 — does

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v35|Mark 3:35]]

See [note 50¹](<Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 50.1 — does>) in [[Matthew-12|Matt. 12]].

### Footnote 35.2 — My

**Verse:** [[Mark-03#v35|Mark 3:35]]

Through His gospel service the Slave-Savior made the believing sinners His spiritual relatives, who became His many brothers ([[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[Hebrews-02#v11|Heb. 2:11]]) in the house of God ([[Hebrews-03#v5|Heb. 3:5]]) and His many members for the building up of His mystical Body ([[Ephesians-05#v30|Eph. 5:30]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v12|1 Cor. 12:12]]) to do the will of God.
