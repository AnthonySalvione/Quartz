## v1

[[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — And|And]] the Pharisees and some of the scribes gathered together to Him when they had come from Jerusalem

## v2

And [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — had|had]] seen that some of His disciples ate bread with common hands, that is, unwashed.

## v3

(For the Pharisees and all the Jews, unless they [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — carefully|carefully]] wash their hands, do not eat, holding the tradition of the [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 3.2 — elders|elders]];

## v4

And when they come from the marketplace, unless they [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — wash|wash]] themselves, they do not eat. And there are many other things which they have received to hold, the dipping of cups and pitchers and copper [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 4.2 — vessels|vessels]].)

## v5

And the [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] and the scribes questioned Him, Why do Your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat bread with common hands?

## v6

And He said to them, Well has Isaiah prophesied concerning you [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — hypocrites|hypocrites]], as it is written, “This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart stays far away from Me;

## v7

But in vain do they [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 7.1 — worship|worship]] Me, teaching as teachings the commandments of [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 7.2 — men|men]].”

## v8

While leaving the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men.

## v9

And He said to them, You nicely [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — set|set]] aside the commandment of God that you may keep your tradition.

## v10

For Moses said, “Honor your father and your mother,” and, “He who speaks evil of father or mother, [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 10.1 — let|let]] him be put to death.”

## v11

But you say: If a man says to his father or his mother, Whatever you would have been profited by me is now [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — corban|corban]] (that is, a gift to God),

## v12

Allow him to no longer do anything for his father or his mother.

## v13

Thus you deprive the word of God of its authority by your tradition which you have handed down. And many things similar to these you do.

## v14

And He called the crowd to Him again and said to them, Hear Me, all of you, and understand.

## v15

There is nothing outside of a man which entering into him can [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 15.1 — defile|defile]] him, but the things coming out of a man are the things which defile a man.

## v16

[[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 16.1 — If|If]] anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.

## v17

And when He entered into a house from the crowd, His disciples questioned Him concerning the parable.

## v18

And He said to them, Are you also in the same manner without understanding? Do you not understand that everything that enters from outside into a man is not able to defile him,

## v19

Because it does not enter into his heart, but into the stomach, and goes out into the drain? (In saying this, He made all foods clean.)

## v20

And He said, That which goes out of the man, that defiles the man.

## v21

For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil reasonings, fornications, thefts, murders,

## v22

Adulteries, covetousness, wickedness, [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — deceit|deceit]], licentiousness, [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 22.2 — envy|envy]], blasphemy, arrogance, foolishness.

## v23

All these [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 23.1 — wicked|wicked]] things proceed from within and defile the man.

## v24

[[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 24.1 — And|And]] He rose up from there and went away into the borders of Tyre [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 24.2 — and|and]] Sidon. And entering into a house, He wanted [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 24.3 — no|no]] one to know about it; yet He could not escape notice.

## v25

But a woman whose little daughter had an unclean spirit heard about Him, and immediately she came and fell at His feet.

## v26

Now the woman was a Greek, [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Syrophoenician|Syrophoenician]] by race. And she asked Him to cast the demon out of her daughter.

## v27

And He said to her, First let the children be satisfied, for it is not good to take the children’s bread and throw it to the [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 27.1 — little|little]] dogs.

## v28

And she answered and said to Him, Yes, Lord, but even the little dogs under the table eat of the little children’s crumbs.

## v29

And He said to her, Because of this word, go. The [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 29.1 — demon|demon]] has gone out of your daughter.

## v30

And she went away to her house and found the little child lying on the couch and the demon gone out.

## v31

And again He came out of the borders of Tyre and came through Sidon to the Sea of [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 31.1 — Galilee|Galilee]] in the midst of the borders of [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 31.2 — Decapolis|Decapolis]].

## v32

And they brought to Him [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 32.1 — one|one]] who was deaf and who spoke with difficulty, and they entreated Him to lay His hand on him.

## v33

And He took him aside from the crowd privately and [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 33.1 — put|put]] His fingers into his ears, and He spat and touched his tongue.

## v34

And looking up to heaven, He groaned and said to him, [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 34.1 — Ephphatha|Ephphatha]]! that is, Be opened!

## v35

And immediately his ears were opened, and the bond on his tongue was released and he spoke clearly.

## v36

And He charged them [[Mark-07.fn#Footnote 36.1 — not|not]] to tell anyone. But the more He charged them, so much the more they proclaimed it.

## v37

And they were utterly astounded, saying, He has done all things well; He makes both the deaf hear and the dumb speak.
