---
Author: Mark
---
## v1

[[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — In|In]] those days, since there was again a great crowd and they did not have anything to eat, He called the disciples to Him and said to them,

## v2

I am [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — moved|moved]] with compassion for the crowd, because for three days now they have [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — moved|remained]] with Me and they do [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — moved|not]] have anything to eat.

## v3

And if I send them away to their home hungry, they will [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — faint|faint]] on the way; and some of them have [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — faint|come]] from a distance.

## v4

And His disciples answered Him, Where will anyone be able to get enough to satisfy these people with bread here in this desolate place?

## v5

And He asked them, How many loaves do you have? And they said, Seven.

## v6

And He charged the crowd to recline on the ground. And taking the seven loaves, He gave thanks and broke them, and gave them to His disciples to set before them, and they set them before the crowd.

## v7

And they had a few small fish, and having blessed them, He said that these also should be set before them.

## v8

And they ate and were satisfied. And they took up the surplus of broken pieces, [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 8.1 — seven|seven]] baskets.

## v9

And [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — there|there]] were about four thousand people. And He sent them away.

## v10

And immediately He got into the boat with His disciples and came into the parts of [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Dalmanutha|Dalmanutha]].

## v11

[[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — And|And]] the [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 11.2 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] came out and began to dispute with Him, seeking from Him a [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 11.3 — sign|sign]] from heaven and tempting Him.

## v12

And He [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 12.1 — groaned|groaned]] deeply in His spirit and said, Why does this generation seek a sign? Truly I tell you, [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 12.2 — No|No]] sign shall be given to this generation.

## v13

And leaving them, He again got into a boat and went away to the other side.

## v14

And they forgot to take bread, and except for one loaf, they did not have any with them in the boat.

## v15

And He charged them, saying, Watch! Beware of the [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 15.1 — leaven|leaven]] of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod.

## v16

And they reasoned with one another, [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 16.1 — saying|saying]], It is because we do not have bread.

## v17

And Jesus, knowing it, said to them, Why are you reasoning because you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive nor understand? Do you have your heart hardened?

## v18

Having eyes, do you not see? And having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember?

## v19

When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many handbaskets full of broken pieces did you take up? They said to Him, Twelve.

## v20

And when I broke the seven for the four thousand, how many baskets filled with broken pieces did you take up? And they said, Seven.

## v21

And He said to them, Do you not yet understand?

## v22

And they came to Bethsaida. And they brought to Him a [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 22.1 — blind|blind]] man and entreated Him to touch him.

## v23

And He [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 23.1 — took|took]] hold of the hand of the blind man and [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 23.2 — led|led]] him forth outside the village. And He [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 23.3 — spat|spat]] on his eyes and [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 23.4 — laid|laid]] His hands on him and asked him, Do you see anything?

## v24

And he looked up and said, I [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 24.1 — see|see]] men, for I see them as trees, walking.

## v25

Then again He laid His hands upon his eyes, and the man looked intently and was [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 25.1 — restored|restored]], and he began to see all things clearly.

## v26

And He sent him to his house, saying, Do [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 26.1 — not|not]] even enter into the [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 26.2 — village|village]].

## v27

[[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 27.1 — And|And]] Jesus and His disciples went out into the villages of Caesarea Philippi. And on the way He questioned His disciples, saying to them, Who do men say that I am?

## v28

And they told Him, saying, John the Baptist; and others, Elijah; but others, One of the prophets.

## v29

And He questioned them, But you, who do you say that I am? Peter answered and said to Him, You are the Christ!

## v30

And He charged them [[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 30.1 — not|not]] to tell anyone concerning Him.

## v31

[[Mark-08.fn#Footnote 31.1 — And|And]] He began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and the chief priests and the scribes and be killed and after three days rise.

## v32

And He spoke this word openly. And Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him.

## v33

But turning around and looking at His disciples, He rebuked Peter and said, Get behind Me, Satan! For you are not setting your mind on the things of God, but on the things of men.

## v34

And He called the crowd to Him with His disciples and said to them, If anyone wants to follow after Me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me.

## v35

For whoever wants to save his soul-life shall lose it; but whoever will lose his soul-life for My sake and the gospel’s shall save it.

## v36

For what does it profit a man to gain the whole world and forfeit his soul-life?

## v37

For what could a man give in exchange for his soul-life?

## v38

For whoever is ashamed of Me and of My words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man also will be ashamed of him when He comes in the glory of His Father with the holy angels.
