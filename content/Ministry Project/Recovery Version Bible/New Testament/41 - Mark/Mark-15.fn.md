### Footnote 1.2 — Pilate

**Verse:** [[Mark-15#v1|Mark 15:1]]

See [note 2¹](<Matthew-27.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Pilate>) in [[Matthew-27|Matt. 27]]. Under God’s sovereignty the Slave-Savior was judged not only by the Jewish leaders, in [[Mark-14#v53|14:53-65]], like a sheep before the shearers ([[Isaiah-53#v7|Isa. 53:7]]), but also by the Roman governor, in [[Mark-15#v1|vv. 1-15]], as a criminal before the accusers ([[Mark-14#v64|14:64]]), that He might die to serve sinners with His life as a ransom ([[Mark-10#v45|10:45]]) not only for the Jews, represented by the Jewish leaders, but also for the Gentiles, represented by the Roman governor. See [note 37¹](<Mark-12.fn#Footnote 37.1 — son>) in [[Mark-12|Mark 12]].
