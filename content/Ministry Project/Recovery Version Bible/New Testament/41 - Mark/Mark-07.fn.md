## Footnotes for verse 26

### Footnote 26.1 — Syrophoenician

**Verse:** [[Mark-07#v26|Mark 7:26]]

Syrian by tongue, Phoenician by race (cf. [[Acts-21#v2|Acts 21:2-3]]). Since the Phoenicians were descendants of the Canaanites, she was a Canaanite woman ([[Matthew-15#v22|Matt. 15:22]]). What made her a Greek—religion, marriage, or some other factor—is difficult to determine.

## Footnotes for verse 32

### Footnote 32.1 — one

**Verse:** [[Mark-07#v32|Mark 7:32]]

Signifying one who is spiritually deaf and dumb, unable to hear the voice of God and to praise Him ([[Isaiah-35#v6|Isa. 35:6]]) and speak for Him ([[Isaiah-56#v10|Isa. 56:10]]). The dumbness of such a person is due to his deafness. The Slave-Savior’s healing salvation is well able to heal the deafness and dumbness of such a one, first by dealing with his ears and then by touching his tongue.

## Footnotes for verse 33

### Footnote 33.1 — put

**Verse:** [[Mark-07#v33|Mark 7:33]]

Or, thrust. The Slave-Savior’s thrusting of His fingers into the deaf one’s ears signifies His dealing with his hearing organ (cf. [[Isaiah-50#v4|Isa. 50:4-5]]; [[Job-33#v14|Job 33:14-16]]), and His touching of his tongue with His spittle signifies His anointing of the dumb one’s speaking organ with the word that proceeds out of His mouth. This was a healing (see [note 31¹](<Mark-01.fn#Footnote 31.1 — left>) in [[Mark-01|Mark 1]]).
