## v1

The [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — beginning|beginning]] of the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — gospel|gospel]] of [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Jesus|Jesus]] Christ, [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — the|the]] Son of God,

## v2

[[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Even|Even]] as it is written in Isaiah the prophet: “Behold, [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — I|I]] send My [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — messenger|messenger]] before [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — Your|Your]] face, who will [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 2.5 — prepare|prepare]] Your way,

## v3

A [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — voice|voice]] of one crying in the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — wilderness|wilderness]]: [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — Prepare|Prepare]] the way of the Lord; make straight His paths.”

## v4

John [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — came|came]] baptizing in the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — wilderness|wilderness]] and preaching a [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — baptism|baptism]] of repentance [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 4.4 — for|for]] forgiveness of sins.

## v5

And all the region of [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Judea|Judea]] went out to him, and all the people of Jerusalem; and they were [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — baptized|baptized]] by him in the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — Jordan|Jordan]] River, as they confessed their sins.

## v6

And John was [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — clothed|clothed]] in camel’s hair and had a leather girdle around his loins, and he ate locusts and wild honey.

## v7

And he preached, saying, [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — He|He]] who is stronger than I comes after me, the thong of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and untie.

## v8

I have baptized you in [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — water|water]], but He Himself will baptize you in the Holy [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — water|Spirit]].

## v9

And in those days Jesus came from [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Nazareth|Nazareth]] of Galilee and was [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — baptized|baptized]] [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — in|in]] the Jordan by John.

## v10

And [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — immediately|immediately]], coming up out of the water, He saw the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — heavens|heavens]] being parted and the Spirit as a [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 10.3 — dove|dove]] descending upon [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 10.4 — Him|Him]].

## v11

And a voice came out of the heavens: [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — You|You]] are My Son, the Beloved; in You I have found My delight.

## v12

And immediately the Spirit [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — thrust|thrust]] Him out into the wilderness.

## v13

And He was in the wilderness [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — forty|forty]] days, being tempted by [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Satan|Satan]]; and He was with the wild animals, and the angels ministered to Him.

## v14

And after John was delivered up, Jesus came into [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Galilee|Galilee]], [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — proclaiming|proclaiming]] the gospel [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 14.3 — of|of]] God,

## v15

And saying, The time is fulfilled and the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God has drawn near. [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Repent|Repent]] and [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — believe|believe]] in the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 15.4 — gospel|gospel]].

## v16

And passing beside the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Sea|Sea]] of Galilee, He saw Simon and Andrew, Simon’s brother, [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — casting|casting]] a [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — net|net]] in the sea, for they were fishermen.

## v17

And Jesus said to them, Come after Me, and I will cause you to become fishers of men.

## v18

And immediately leaving the nets, they [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — followed|followed]] Him.

## v19

And going on a little, He saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, who also were in their boat, [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — mending|mending]] the nets.

## v20

And immediately He called them, and leaving their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, they [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — went|went]] after Him.

## v21

And they went into Capernaum, and immediately, on the Sabbath, He entered into the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — synagogue|synagogue]] and [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — taught|taught]].

## v22

And they were astounded at His teaching, for He taught them as One having [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — authority|authority]] and not like the scribes.

## v23

And immediately, there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — spirit|spirit]], and he cried out, saying,

## v24

[[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — What|What]] have we to do with You, Jesus, Nazarene? Have You come to destroy us? I know who You are — the Holy One of God.

## v25

And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Be quiet and come out of him!

## v26

And the unclean spirit, convulsing him and shouting with a loud voice, came out of him.

## v27

And they were all amazed, so that they discussed among themselves, saying, What is this? A new teaching! With [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — authority|authority]] He orders even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him.

## v28

And immediately the report concerning Him went out everywhere into all the surrounding region of Galilee.

## v29

And immediately, going out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew with James and John.

## v30

And Simon’s mother-in-law was lying down with a [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 30.1 — fever|fever]], and immediately they told Him about her.

## v31

And He came to her and raised her up, holding her hand, and the fever [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 31.1 — left|left]] her, and she [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 31.2 — served|served]] them.

## v32

And when evening fell and the sun had set, they brought to Him all those who were ill and those who were demon-possessed.

## v33

And the whole city was gathered together at the door.

## v34

And He [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 34.1 — healed|healed]] many who were ill with various diseases, and He [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 34.2 — cast|cast]] out many demons and did not allow the demons to speak, because [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 34.3 — they|they]] knew Him.

## v35

And rising very early in the morning, while it was still night, He went out and went away to a deserted place, and there He [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 35.1 — prayed|prayed]].

## v36

And Simon and those with him hunted for Him,

## v37

And they found Him and said to Him, All are seeking You.

## v38

And He said to them, Let us go elsewhere into the nearby towns that I may [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 38.1 — preach|preach]] there also, because for [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 38.2 — this|this]] purpose I came out.

## v39

And He went preaching in their synagogues in the whole of Galilee and casting out the demons.

## v40

And a [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 40.1 — leper|leper]] came to Him, entreating Him and falling on his knees and saying to Him, If You are willing, You can cleanse me.

## v41

And He was moved with [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 41.1 — compassion|compassion]], and [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 41.2 — stretching|stretching]] out His hand, He touched him and said to him, I am [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 41.1 — compassion|willing]]; be [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 41.3 — cleansed|cleansed]]!

## v42

And immediately the leprosy left him, and he was cleansed.

## v43

And sternly charging him, immediately He thrust him out,

## v44

And said to him, See that you [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 44.1 — say|say]] nothing to anyone; but go, show yourself to the priest and offer for your cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.

## v45

But he went out and began to proclaim it much and to spread the [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 45.1 — matter|matter]] abroad, so that He could [[Mark-01.fn#Footnote 45.2 — no|no]] longer enter openly into a city, but He stayed outside in deserted places. And they came to Him from everywhere.
