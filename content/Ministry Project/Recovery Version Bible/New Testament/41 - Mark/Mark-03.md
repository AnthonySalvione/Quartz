---
Author: Mark
---
## v1

[[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — And|And]] He entered again into the synagogue; and there was a man there who had a withered hand.

## v2

And they were watching Him closely to see if He would heal him on the Sabbath, so that they might accuse Him.

## v3

And He said to the man who had the withered hand, Rise and stand in the midst.

## v4

And He said to them, Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — save|save]] [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 4.2 — life|life]] or to kill? But they remained silent.

## v5

And looking around at them with [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — anger|anger]] and being greatly grieved with the hardness of their heart, He said to the man, Stretch out your hand. And he stretched it out, and his hand was [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — restored|restored]].

## v6

And the [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] went out and immediately took counsel with the Herodians against Him as to how they might destroy Him.

## v7

And Jesus withdrew with His disciples to the sea, and a great multitude from Galilee followed; and from Judea

## v8

And from Jerusalem and from Idumea and beyond the Jordan and around Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, hearing what things He did, came to Him.

## v9

And He told His disciples to have a little [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — boat|boat]] ready near Him because of the crowd, so that they would not throng Him;

## v10

For He [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — healed|healed]] many, so that as many as had [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — afflictions|afflictions]] [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 10.3 — pressed|pressed]] toward Him to touch Him.

## v11

And the unclean spirits, when they beheld Him, fell before Him and cried out, saying, You are the Son of God!

## v12

And He [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — charged|charged]] them many times not to make Him known.

## v13

And He went up to the mountain and called to Him those whom He Himself wanted, and they went to Him.

## v14

And He [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — appointed|appointed]] [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — twelve|twelve]], [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 14.3 — whom|whom]] He also named apostles, so that they might be with Him and that He might send them to [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 14.4 — preach|preach]]

## v15

And to have the authority to [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — cast|cast]] out the demons.

## v16

[[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — And|And]] He appointed the twelve: and to Simon He [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — gave|gave]] the name Peter;

## v17

And James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James, and He gave them the name, [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Boanerges|Boanerges]], that is, Sons of Thunder;

## v18

And Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Cananaean,

## v19

And Judas Iscariot, who also [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 19.1 — betrayed|betrayed]] Him.

## v20

And [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — He|He]] came into a house, and again a crowd came together, so that they could [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 20.2 — not|not]] even eat bread.

## v21

And when His relatives heard about it, they went out to lay hold of Him, for they said, [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — He|He]] is beside Himself.

## v22

[[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — And|And]] the [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 22.2 — scribes|scribes]] who came down from Jerusalem said, He has [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 22.3 — Beelzebul|Beelzebul]], and by the [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 22.4 — ruler|ruler]] of the demons He casts out the demons.

## v23

And He called them to Himself and said to them in parables, How can Satan cast out Satan?

## v24

And if a [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 24.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] is divided against itself, that kingdom is not able to stand;

## v25

And if a house is divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand;

## v26

And if Satan has risen up against himself and is divided, he is not able to stand but has come to an end.

## v27

But no one can enter into the house of the strong man and thoroughly plunder his [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 27.1 — goods|goods]] unless he first [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 27.2 — binds|binds]] the strong man, and then he will thoroughly plunder his house.

## v28

Truly I say to you that all sins will be forgiven the sons of men, and whatever blasphemies they blaspheme,

## v29

But whoever blasphemes against the [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit has no forgiveness forever, but is guilty of an [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 29.2 — everlasting|everlasting]] sin.

## v30

He said this because they said, He has an unclean spirit.

## v31

And His [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 31.1 — mother|mother]] and His brothers came, and standing outside, they sent word to Him and called Him.

## v32

And a crowd sat around Him, and they said to Him, Behold, Your mother and Your brothers and Your sisters are outside seeking You.

## v33

And He answered them and said, [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 33.1 — Who|Who]] is My mother and My brothers?

## v34

And looking around at those sitting in a circle around Him, He said, Behold, My mother and My brothers!

## v35

For whoever [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 35.1 — does|does]] the will of God, this one is [[Mark-03.fn#Footnote 35.2 — My|My]] brother and sister and mother.
