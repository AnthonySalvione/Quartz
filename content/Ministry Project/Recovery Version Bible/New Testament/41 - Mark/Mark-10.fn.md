# Mark-10 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Mark-10|Mark 10]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — borders

**Verse:** [[Mark-10#v1|Mark 10:1]]

The Slave-Savior ministered in His gospel service for over three years in the despised region of Galilee (see [note 14¹](<Mark-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Galilee>), par. 2, in [[Mark-01|Mark 1]]), far from the holy temple and the holy city, the place where He had to die for the accomplishing of God’s eternal plan. As the Lamb of God ([[John-01#v29|John 1:29]]), He had to be offered to God at Mount Moriah, where Abraham offered Isaac and enjoyed God’s provision of a ram as a substitute for his son ([[Genesis-22#v2|Gen. 22:2]], [[Genesis-22#v9|9-14]]) and where the temple was built in Jerusalem ([[2 Chronicles-03#v1|2 Chron. 3:1]]). It had to be there that He would be delivered, according to the counsel determined by the Trinity of the Godhead ([[Acts-02#v23|Acts 2:23]]), to the Jewish leaders ([[Mark-09#v31|9:31]]; [[Mark-10#v33|10:33]]) and be rejected by them as the builders of God’s building ([[Mark-08#v31|8:31]]; [[Acts-04#v11|Acts 4:11]]). It had to be there that He would be crucified according to the Roman form of capital punishment ([[John-18#v31|John 18:31-32]] and notes; [[John-19#v6|19:6]], [[John-19#v14|14-15]]) to fulfill the type concerning the kind of death He would die ([[Numbers-21#v8|Num. 21:8-9]]; [[John-03#v14|John 3:14]]). Moreover, according to Daniel’s prophecy ([[Daniel-09#v24|Dan. 9:24-26]]), that very year was the year that Messiah (Christ) was to be cut off (killed). Furthermore, as the Passover lamb ([[1 Corinthians-05#v7|1 Cor. 5:7]]), He had to be killed in the month of the Passover ([[Exodus-12#v1|Exo. 12:1-11]]). Hence, He had to go to Jerusalem ([[Mark-10#v33|v. 33]]; [[Mark-11#v1|11:1]], [[Mark-11#v11|11]], [[Mark-11#v15|15]], [[Mark-11#v27|27]]; [[John-12#v12|John 12:12]]) before the Passover ([[John-12#v1|John 12:1]]; [[Mark-14#v1|Mark 14:1]]) that He might die there on the day of the Passover ([[Mark-14#v12|14:12-17]]; [[John-18#v28|John 18:28]]) at the place and the time foreordained by God.

## Footnotes for verse 33

### Footnote 33.1 — delivered

**Verse:** [[Mark-10#v33|Mark 10:33]]

The Slave-Savior had predicted His death and resurrection twice already ([[Mark-08#v31|8:31]]; [[Mark-09#v31|9:31]]). Since the time for His death was at hand (see [note 1¹](<Mark-10.fn#Footnote 1.1 — borders>)), He went to Jerusalem willingly, even going before His followers with a speed and boldness that amazed them ([[Mark-10#v32|v. 32]]). This was His obedience to God unto death ([[Philippians-02#v8|Phil. 2:8]]), according to the counsel of God ([[Acts-02#v23|Acts 2:23]]), for the fulfillment of God’s redemptive plan ([[Isaiah-53#v10|Isa. 53:10]]). The Slave-Savior knew that through His death He would be glorified in resurrection ([[Luke-24#v25|Luke 24:25-26]]) and that His divine life would be released to produce many brothers for His expression ([[John-12#v23|John 12:23-24]]; [[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]). For the joy set before Him, He despised the shame ([[Hebrews-12#v2|Heb. 12:2]]) and volunteered to be delivered to the Satan-usurped leaders of the Jews and to be condemned by them to death. For this, God exalted Him to the heavens, seated Him at His right hand ([[Mark-16#v19|16:19]]; [[Acts-02#v33|Acts 2:33-35]]), bestowed on Him the name which is above every name ([[Philippians-02#v9|Phil. 2:9-10]]), made Him both Lord and Christ ([[Acts-02#v36|Acts 2:36]]), and crowned Him with glory and honor ([[Hebrews-02#v9|Heb. 2:9]]).

## Footnotes for verse 38

### Footnote 38.1 — cup

**Verse:** [[Mark-10#v38|Mark 10:38]]

Both the cup and the baptism refer to the Slave-Savior’s death ([[John-18#v11|John 18:11]]; [[Luke-12#v50|Luke 12:50]]). The cup signifies that His death was the portion given to Him by God, the portion He took to redeem sinners for God. Baptism denotes that His death was ordained by God as the way for Him to pass through for the accomplishing of God’s redemption for sinners. For us He willingly drank this cup and was baptized with this baptism.

**Verse:** [[Mark-10#v49|Mark 10:49]]
