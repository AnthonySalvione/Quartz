## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — sower

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v3|Mark 4:3]]

Signifying the Slave-Savior ([[Matthew-13#v37|Matt. 13:37]]), who was the Son of God coming to sow Himself as the seed of life (see [note 26³](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 26.3 — seed>)) in His word ([[Mark-04#v14|v. 14]]) into men’s hearts that He might grow and live in them and be expressed from within them.

### Footnote 3.2 — sow

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v3|Mark 4:3]]

This sowing was the Slave-Savior’s proclaiming of the gospel of God, which brought in the kingdom of God ([[Mark-01#v14|1:14-15]]). It was, as in [[Mark-04#v26|v. 26]], the sowing of the seed of life in the word ([[Mark-04#v14|v. 14]]) spoken by the Slave-Savior, indicating that His gospel service was to sow the divine life into the people whom He served. The growth of this life depends on the condition of the ones served, and its issue differs according to their various conditions, as portrayed in this parable.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — mystery

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v11|Mark 4:11]]

God’s economy concerning His kingdom was a hidden mystery, which has been unveiled to the Slave-Savior’s disciples. Yet since the nature and character of the kingdom of God are wholly divine, and the elements through which it is brought forth are the divine life and the divine light (see notes [3¹](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — sower>), [26¹](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 26.1 — kingdom>), and [21¹](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 21.1 — lamp>)), the kingdom of God, especially in its reality as the genuine church in this age ([[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]), is still entirely a mystery to the natural man. Divine revelation is required to understand it.

### Footnote 11.2 — are

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v11|Mark 4:11]]

Or, are done, become.

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — lamp

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v21|Mark 4:21]]

The lamp here, which shines light, indicates that the Slave-Savior’s gospel service not only sows life into the people whom He serves but also brings light to them. Hence, such a divine service issues in the believers as luminaries ([[Philippians-02#v15|Phil. 2:15]]) and the churches as lampstands ([[Revelation-01#v20|Rev. 1:20]]) that shine as His testimony in this dark age and consummate in the New Jerusalem, which has the outstanding characteristics of life and light ([[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]]; [[Revelation-21#v11|21:11]], [[Revelation-21#v23|23-24]]).

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — With

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v24|Mark 4:24]]

In [[Matthew-07#v2|Matt. 7:2]] and [[Luke-06#v38|Luke 6:38]] this word is applied to the way we treat others. Here it is applied to the way we hear the word of the Lord. The measure that can be given to us by the Lord depends on the measure of our hearing. As much as we can hear, that much He will give.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — has

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v25|Mark 4:25]]

The word here, as in [[Matthew-13#v10|Matt. 13:10-13]] and [[Luke-08#v18|Luke 8:18]], is concerned with how we hear the word of the Lord.

## Footnotes for verse 26

### Footnote 26.1 — kingdom

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v26|Mark 4:26]]

The kingdom of God is the reality of the church, which is brought forth by the resurrection life of Christ through the gospel ([[1 Corinthians-04#v15|1 Cor. 4:15]]). Regeneration is the entrance to the kingdom ([[John-03#v5|John 3:5]]), and the growth of the divine life within the believers is the kingdom’s development ([[2 Peter-01#v3|2 Pet. 1:3-11]]). See [note 15¹](<Mark-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — kingdom>) in [[Mark-01|Mark 1]].

### Footnote 26.2 — man

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v26|Mark 4:26]]

The Slave-Savior as the sower. See [note 3¹](<Mark-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — sower>).

### Footnote 26.3 — seed

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v26|Mark 4:26]]

The seed of the divine life ([[1 John-03#v9|1 John 3:9]]; [[1 Peter-01#v23|1 Pet. 1:23]]) sown into the Slave-Savior’s believers, indicating that the kingdom of God, which is the issue and goal of the Slave-Savior’s gospel, and the church in this age ([[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]) are a matter of life, the life of God, which sprouts, grows, bears fruit, matures, and produces a harvest; they are not a matter of lifeless organization produced through man’s wisdom and ability. The apostles’ words in [[1 Corinthians-03#v6|1 Cor. 3:6-9]] and [[Revelation-14#v4|Rev 14:4]], [[Revelation-14#v15|15-1]]6 confirm this.

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v27|Mark 4:27]]

The words sleeps and rises night and day and how, he does not know should not be applied to the Slave-Savior. This verse illustrates the spontaneity of the growth of the seed (cf. [[Mark-04#v28|v. 28]]).

### Footnote 27.2 — lengthens

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v27|Mark 4:27]]

## Footnotes for verse 28

### Footnote 28.1 — earth

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v28|Mark 4:28]]

I.e., the good earth ([[Mark-04#v8|v. 8]]), signifying the good heart that was created by God ([[Genesis-01#v31|Gen. 1:31]]) so that His divine life can grow in man. Such a good heart works together with the seed of the divine life sown into it, allowing the seed to grow and bear fruit spontaneously for the expression of God. The word here enables us to have faith in this spontaneity. Thus, here, in contrast to [[Matthew-13#v24|Matt 13:24-30]], tares are not mentioned, on the negative side.

### Footnote 28.2 — by

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v28|Mark 4:28]]

Or, spontaneously.

## Footnotes for verse 38

### Footnote 38.1 — sleeping

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v38|Mark 4:38]]

The Slave-Savior was sleeping and resting in the boat, which was being beaten by the windstorm, while the disciples were threatened by the storm. This indicates that He was above the threatening windstorm and was not bothered by it. As long as the disciples had Him with them in their boat, they should, by faith in Him ([[Mark-04#v40|v. 40]]), have shared His rest and enjoyed His peace.

## Footnotes for verse 39

### Footnote 39.1 — rebuked

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v39|Mark 4:39]]

While the disciples were following Him, the Slave-Savior, as a man with divine authority, controlled the storm that threatened them.

## Footnotes for verse 41

### Footnote 41.1 — obey

**Verse:** [[Mark-04#v41|Mark 4:41]]

This not only displayed the Slave-Savior’s divine authority but also testified that He was the very Creator of the universe ([[Genesis-01#v9|Gen. 1:9]]; [[Job-38#v8|Job 38:8-11]]). He is the Creator, the One who has the authority!
