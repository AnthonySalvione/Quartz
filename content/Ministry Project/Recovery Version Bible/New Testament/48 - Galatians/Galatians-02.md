---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Then [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — after|after]] a period of fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus with me also.

## v2

And I went up according to [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — revelation|revelation]], and I laid before them the gospel which I proclaim among the Gentiles, but privately to those who were of repute, lest perhaps I should be running, or had run, in vain.

## v3

But [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — not|not]] even Titus, who was with me, though he is a Greek, was compelled to be [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — circumcised|circumcised]];

## v4

And this, because of the [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — false|false]] brothers, brought in secretly, who stole in to spy out our [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — freedom|freedom]] which we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 4.3 — slavery|slavery]].

## v5

To them we yielded with the subjection demanded not even for an hour, that the [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — truth|truth]] of the gospel might remain with you.

## v6

But from those who were reputed to be something — whatever they were makes no difference to me; God does not accept man’s person; for to me those who were of repute added nothing.

## v7

But on the contrary, seeing that I had been entrusted with the gospel to the uncircumcision, even as Peter with the gospel to the circumcision

## v8

(For He who operated in Peter for the apostleship to the circumcision operated also in me for the Gentiles),

## v9

And perceiving the grace given to me, [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — James|James]] and Cephas and John, who were reputed to be pillars, gave to me and to Barnabas the right hand of fellowship that we should go to the Gentiles, and they, to the circumcision.

## v10

Only they requested that we remember the poor, which very thing I was also eager to do.

## v11

But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face because he stood condemned.

## v12

For before some came [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — from|from]] James, he [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — continually|continually]] ate with the Gentiles; but when they came, he began to shrink back and separate himself, [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — fearing|fearing]] those of the circumcision.

## v13

And the [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — rest|rest]] of the Jews also joined him in this [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — hypocrisy|hypocrisy]], so that even [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 13.3 — Barnabas|Barnabas]] was carried away in their hypocrisy.

## v14

But when I saw that they were not [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — walking|walking]] in a straightforward way in relation to the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas before them all, If you, being a Jew, [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — live|live]] like the Gentiles and not like the Jews, how is it that you compel the Gentiles to [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 14.3 — live|live]] like the Jews?

## v15

We are Jews by nature and not sinners from among the Gentiles;

## v16

And knowing that a man is not justified out of works of law, but through [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — faith|faith]] in Jesus Christ, we also have believed into Christ Jesus that we might be justified out of faith in Christ and not out of the works of law, because out of the works of law no [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 16.2 — flesh|flesh]] will be justified.

## v17

But if, while seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves also have been found sinners, is then Christ a [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — minister|minister]] of sin? [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — Absolutely|Absolutely]] not!

## v18

For if I [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — build|build]] again the things which I have [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 18.2 — destroyed|destroyed]], I prove myself to be a transgressor.

## v19

For I [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — through|through]] law have [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 19.2 — died|died]] to law that I might [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 19.3 — live|live]] to God.

## v20

I am [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — crucified|crucified]] with Christ; and it is [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.2 — no|no]] longer I who live, but it is [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.3 — Christ|Christ]] who lives in me; and the [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.4 — life|life]] which I now live in the flesh I live in [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.5 — faith|faith]], the faith [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.6 — of|of]] the [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.7 — Son|Son]] of God, who [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 20.8 — loved|loved]] me and gave Himself up for me.

## v21

I do not nullify the [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — grace|grace]] of God; [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 21.3 — for|for]] if [[Galatians-02.fn#Footnote 21.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] is through law, then Christ has died for nothing.
