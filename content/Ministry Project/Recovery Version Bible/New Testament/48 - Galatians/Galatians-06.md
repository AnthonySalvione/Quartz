---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Brothers, even if a man is overtaken in some offense, you who are [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — spiritual|spiritual]] restore such a one in a [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — spirit|spirit]] of meekness, looking to yourself lest you also be tempted.

## v2

Bear one another’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — law|law]] of Christ completely.

## v3

For if anyone [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — thinks|thinks]] he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.

## v4

But let each man prove his own work, and then he will have his boast in regard to himself alone and not in regard to another.

## v5

For each one shall bear his own load.

## v6

But let him who is instructed in the word share in all [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — good|good]] things with him who instructs.

## v7

Do not be [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — deceived|deceived]]: God is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, this he will also reap.

## v8

For he who sows [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — unto|unto]] his own [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 8.2 — flesh|flesh]] will reap [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 8.3 — corruption|corruption]] of the flesh, but he who sows [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — unto|unto]] the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 8.2 — flesh|Spirit]] will of the Spirit reap [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 8.3 — corruption|eternal]] life.

## v9

And let us not lose heart in doing what is good, for in the proper season we will reap if we [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — do|do]] not faint.

## v10

So then, as we have the opportunity, let us do what is [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — good|good]] toward all, but especially toward those of the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 10.2 — household|household]] of the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 10.3 — faith|faith]].

## v11

See with what [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — large|large]] letters I have written to you with my own hand.

## v12

As many as desire to make a [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — good|good]] show [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — in|in]] the flesh, these compel you to be circumcised, but only that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.

## v13

For neither do they that become circumcised keep the law themselves, but they desire you to be circumcised that they may boast in your flesh.

## v14

But far be it from me to boast except in the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — cross|cross]] of our Lord Jesus Christ, through [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 14.2 — whom|whom]] the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 14.3 — world|world]] has been crucified to me and I to the world.

## v15

For neither is [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — circumcision|circumcision]] anything nor uncircumcision, but a [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — circumcision|new]] creation is what matters.

## v16

And as many as [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — walk|walk]] by this [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 16.2 — rule|rule]], [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 16.3 — peace|peace]] be upon them and mercy, even upon the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 16.4 — Israel|Israel]] of God.

## v17

Henceforth let no one trouble me, for I bear in my body the [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 17.1 — brands|brands]] of Jesus.

## v18

The [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — grace|grace]] of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 18.2 — spirit|spirit]], [[Galatians-06.fn#Footnote 18.3 — brothers|brothers]]. Amen.
