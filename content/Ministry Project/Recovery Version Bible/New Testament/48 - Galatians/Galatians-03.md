---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

O foolish Galatians, who has bewitched you, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was openly portrayed [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — crucified|crucified]]?

## v2

This only I wish to learn from you, Did you [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — receive|receive]] the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] out of the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — works|works]] of law or out of the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — works|hearing]] of faith?

## v3

Are you so foolish? Having [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — begun|begun]] by the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Spirit|Spirit]], are you now being [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — begun|perfected]] by the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Spirit|flesh]]?

## v4

Have you [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — suffered|suffered]] so many things in vain, if indeed it was in vain?

## v5

He therefore who [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — bountifully|bountifully]] supplies to you the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] and [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 5.3 — does|does]] [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 5.4 — works|works]] of power among you, does He do it out of the works of law or out of the hearing of faith?

## v6

Even as “[[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Abraham|Abraham]] believed God, and it was accounted to him as righteousness.”

## v7

Know then that they who are of [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — faith|faith]], these are [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — sons|sons]] of Abraham.

## v8

And the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles out of faith, announced the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — gospel|gospel]] beforehand to Abraham: “In you shall all the nations be blessed.”

## v9

So then they who are of [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — faith|faith]] are [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — blessed|blessed]] with [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — believing|believing]] Abraham.

## v10

For as many as are of the works of law are under a [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — curse|curse]]; for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who does not continue in all the things written in the book of the law to do them.”

## v11

And that [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — by|by]] law no one is justified before God is evident because, “The righteous one shall have life and live [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 11.2 — by|by]] faith”;

## v12

But the law is not of faith, yet, “He who does them shall live [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — because|because]] of them.”

## v13

Christ has redeemed us out of the curse of the law, having [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — become|become]] a curse on our behalf; because it is written, “Cursed is everyone hanging on a [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — tree|tree]]”;

## v14

In order that the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — blessing|blessing]] of Abraham might come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, that we might [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — receive|receive]] the promise of the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 14.3 — Spirit|Spirit]] through faith.

## v15

Brothers, I speak according to man, though it is a man’s covenant, yet when it has been ratified, no one nullifies it or makes additions to it.

## v16

But to Abraham were the promises spoken and to his seed. He does not say, And to the seeds, as concerning many, but as concerning one: “And to your seed,” who is [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Christ|Christ]].

## v17

And I say this: A [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — covenant|covenant]] previously ratified by God, the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 17.2 — law|law]], having come [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 17.3 — four|four]] hundred and thirty years after, does not annul so as to make the promise of none effect.

## v18

For if the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — inheritance|inheritance]] is of law, it is no longer of promise; but to Abraham God has graciously given it through promise.

## v19

Why then the law? It was [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 19.1 — added|added]] because of the transgressions until the seed should come to whom the promise was made, it being [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 19.2 — ordained|ordained]] through angels in the hand of a [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 19.3 — mediator|mediator]].

## v20

But a [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — mediator|mediator]] is not a mediator for one, but God is one.

## v21

Is then the law against the promises of God? Absolutely not! For if a law had been given which was able to [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — give|give]] life, [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 21.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] would have indeed been of law.

## v22

But the Scripture has [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — shut|shut]] up [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 22.2 — all|all]] under sin in order that the promise out of [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 22.3 — faith|faith]] in Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.

## v23

But before [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 23.1 — faith|faith]] came we were [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 23.2 — guarded|guarded]] under law, being [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 23.3 — shut|shut]] up [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 23.4 — unto|unto]] the [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 23.1 — faith|faith]] which was to be revealed.

## v24

So then the law has become our [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 24.1 — child-conductor|child-conductor]] unto Christ that we might be justified out of faith.

## v25

But since faith has come, we are [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 25.1 — no|no]] longer under a child-conductor.

## v26

For you are all [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 26.1 — sons|sons]] of God through [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 26.2 — faith|faith]] in Christ Jesus.

## v27

For as many of you as were baptized [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 27.1 — into|into]] Christ have put on Christ.

## v28

There [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 28.1 — cannot|cannot]] be [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 28.2 — Jew|Jew]] nor Greek, there cannot be [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 28.3 — slave|slave]] nor free man, there cannot be [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 28.4 — male|male]] and female; for you are all [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 28.5 — one|one]] in Christ Jesus.

## v29

And if you are of Christ, then you are [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Abraham’s|Abraham’s]] seed, heirs according to [[Galatians-03.fn#Footnote 29.2 — promise|promise]].
