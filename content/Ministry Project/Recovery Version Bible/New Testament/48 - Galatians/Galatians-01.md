---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Paul|Paul]], an apostle ([[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — not|not]] from men nor through man but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — raised|raised]] Him from the dead),

## v2

And all the [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — brothers|brothers]] who are with me, to the [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — churches|churches]] of Galatia:

## v3

[[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Grace|Grace]] to you and [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — peace|peace]] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ,

## v4

Who gave Himself for our sins that He might [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — rescue|rescue]] us out of the present evil [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — age|age]] according to the will of our God and Father,

## v5

To whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

## v6

I marvel that you are so quickly [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — removing|removing]] from Him who has called you [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — in|in]] the [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 6.3 — grace|grace]] of Christ to a [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 6.4 — different|different]] gospel,

## v7

Which is [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — not|not]] another gospel, only there are [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — some|some]] who trouble you and desire to [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 7.3 — pervert|pervert]] the gospel of Christ.

## v8

But if even we or an angel out of heaven should announce to you a gospel beyond that which we have announced to you, let him be accursed.

## v9

As we have said before, now also I say again, If anyone announces to you a gospel beyond that which you have received, let him be accursed.

## v10

For am I now [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — trying|trying]] to win the assent of men or of God? Or am I seeking to please men? If I were still trying to please men, I would not be a slave of Christ.

## v11

For I make known to you, brothers, concerning the gospel announced by me, that it is not according to man.

## v12

For [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — neither|neither]] did I receive it from man, nor was I taught it, but I received it through a [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — revelation|revelation]] by Jesus Christ.

## v13

For you have heard of my manner of life formerly in [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Judaism|Judaism]], that I persecuted the church of God excessively and ravaged it.

## v14

And I advanced in Judaism beyond many contemporaries in my race, being more abundantly a zealot for the [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — traditions|traditions]] of my fathers.

## v15

But when it pleased God, who [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — set|set]] me apart [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — from|from]] my mother’s womb and [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — called|called]] me through His [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 15.4 — grace|grace]],

## v16

To [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — reveal|reveal]] His [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — Son|Son]] [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — in|in]] me that I might announce [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.4 — Him|Him]] as the gospel among the [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.5 — Gentiles|Gentiles]], immediately I did [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.6 — not|not]] confer with [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 16.7 — flesh|flesh]] and blood,

## v17

Neither did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away to [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Arabia|Arabia]] and again returned to Damascus.

## v18

Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — visit|visit]] Cephas, and I remained with him fifteen days.

## v19

But I saw none of the other apostles [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — except|except]] James, the brother of the Lord.

## v20

Now concerning the things which I am writing to you, behold, before God, I am not lying.

## v21

Then I went into the regions of [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Syria|Syria]] and Cilicia.

## v22

Yet I was still [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — unknown|unknown]] by face to the churches of Judea, which are in Christ.

## v23

But they [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — only|only]] heard this: He who was formerly persecuting us is now announcing as the gospel the [[Galatians-01.fn#Footnote 23.2 — faith|faith]] which formerly he ravaged.

## v24

And they glorified God because of me.
