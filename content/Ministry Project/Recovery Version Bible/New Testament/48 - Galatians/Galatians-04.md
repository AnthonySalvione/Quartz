---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

But I say, As long as the heir is a [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — child|child]], he does not differ at all from a slave, though he is lord of all;

## v2

But he is under [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — guardians|guardians]] and stewards until the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 2.2 — time|time]] appointed by the father.

## v3

So also we, when we were children, were kept in slavery under the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — elements|elements]] of the world;

## v4

But when the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — fullness|fullness]] of the time came, God sent forth His Son, born of a [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — woman|woman]], born under [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — law|law]],

## v5

That He might [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — redeem|redeem]] those under law that we might receive the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — sonship|sonship]].

## v6

And because you are sons, God has sent forth the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of His Son into our [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — hearts|hearts]], [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — crying|crying]], [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 6.4 — Abba|Abba]], Father!

## v7

So then you are [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — no|no]] longer a slave but a son; and if a [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — son|son]], an [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 7.3 — heir|heir]] also [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 7.4 — through|through]] God.

## v8

However at that time, not knowing God, you were slaves to the gods which by [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — nature|nature]] are not gods;

## v9

But now, having come to know God, or rather having been known by God, how is it that you turn again to the weak and poor elements, to which you desire to [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — be|be]] enslaved yet again?

## v10

You observe [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — days|days]] and [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 10.2 — months|months]] and [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 10.3 — seasons|seasons]] and [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 10.4 — years|years]];

## v11

I fear for you, lest I have labored upon you in [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — vain|vain]].

## v12

Become [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 12.1 — as|as]] I am, because I also am [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 12.2 — as|as]] you are, brothers, I beseech you; you have [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 12.3 — not|not]] wronged me at all.

## v13

And you know that [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — because|because]] of weakness of my flesh I announced the gospel to you the first time.

## v14

And that which was a trial to you in my flesh you did not despise nor [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 14.1 — loathe|loathe]], but you received me as an angel of God, indeed as Christ Jesus.

## v15

Where then is [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — your|your]] felicitation? For I testify concerning you that if possible you would have plucked out your [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 15.2 — eyes|eyes]] and given them to me.

## v16

So then have I become your enemy by presenting the truth to you?

## v17

They are [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — zealous|zealous]] of you, but [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 17.2 — not|not]] rightly; but they desire to [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 17.3 — shut|shut]] you out that you may be [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 17.4 — zealous|zealous]] of them.

## v18

But it is always good to be zealous in a good thing, and [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 18.1 — not|not]] only when I am present with you.

## v19

My [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 19.1 — children|children]], with whom I [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 19.2 — travail|travail]] again in birth until [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 19.3 — Christ|Christ]] is [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 19.4 — formed|formed]] in you,

## v20

Indeed, I wish I could be present with you now and [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 20.1 — change|change]] my [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 20.2 — tone|tone]] because I am [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 20.3 — perplexed|perplexed]] about you.

## v21

[[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Tell|Tell]] me, you who desire to be [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 21.2 — under|under]] the law, do you not hear the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 21.3 — law|law]]?

## v22

For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one of the maidservant and one of the free woman.

## v23

However the one of the maidservant was born [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 23.1 — according|according]] to the flesh, but the one of the free woman was born [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 23.1 — according|through]] promise.

## v24

These things are spoken allegorically, for [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 24.1 — these|these]] women are [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 24.2 — two|two]] covenants, one from [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 24.3 — Mount|Mount]] Sinai, bringing forth children unto [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 24.4 — slavery|slavery]], which is [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 24.5 — Hagar|Hagar]].

## v25

Now this Hagar is Sinai the mountain in Arabia and corresponds to the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 25.1 — Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] which now [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 25.2 — is|is]], for she is in [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 25.3 — slavery|slavery]] with her children.

## v26

But the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 26.1 — Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] above is free, which is our mother;

## v27

For it is written, “Rejoice, barren one who does not bear; break forth and shout, you who are not travailing, because [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 27.1 — many|many]] are the children of her who is [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 27.2 — desolate|desolate]] rather than of her who has her husband.”

## v28

But you, brothers, in the way Isaac was, are [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 28.1 — children|children]] of promise.

## v29

But just as at that time he who was born [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 29.1 — according|according]] to the flesh [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 29.2 — persecuted|persecuted]] him who was born [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 29.1 — according|according]] to the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 29.3 — Spirit|Spirit]], so [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 29.4 — also|also]] it is now.

## v30

But what does the Scripture say? “Cast out the maidservant and her [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 30.1 — son|son]], for the son of the maidservant shall by no means inherit with the [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 30.2 — son|son]] of the free woman.”

## v31

So then, brothers, we are [[Galatians-04.fn#Footnote 31.1 — not|not]] children of the maidservant but of the free woman.
