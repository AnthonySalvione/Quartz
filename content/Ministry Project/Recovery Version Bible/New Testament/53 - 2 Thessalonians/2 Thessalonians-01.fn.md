Here, in this Epistle, faith and love are growing and increasing in the Thessalonians’ Christian life.

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — endurance

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-01#v4|2 Thessalonians 1:4]]

The endurance issued from and was supported by the hope of the Lord’s coming back ([[1 Thessalonians-01#v3|1 Thes. 1:3]]). Such endurance resulting from hope is always accompanied by faith. Hence, the phrase endurance and faith is used here. Both endurance and faith are needed in persecutions and afflictions.

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — righteous

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-01#v5|2 Thessalonians 1:5]]

God’s judgment is righteous and just upon all men. It will be finalized in the future ([[Romans-02#v5|Rom. 2:5-9]]; [[Revelation-20#v11|Rev. 20:11-15]]). The way God deals with different people in this age is an indication, a token, a proof, of the future execution of His righteous judgment.

### Footnote 5.2 — kingdom

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-01#v5|2 Thessalonians 1:5]]

The believers have been called into God’s kingdom and glory ([[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thes. 2:12]]). To enter into this kingdom, we need to pass through afflictions ([[Acts-14#v22|Acts 14:22]]). Hence, the persecutions and afflictions are a plain indication of God’s righteous judgment, showing that we may be accounted worthy of the kingdom of God.

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — rest

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-01#v7|2 Thessalonians 1:7]]

Or, relief, ease, repose, liberty. In this age the believers suffer persecutions and troubles for the Lord. At the Lord’s coming back they will be relieved of their sufferings and will enter into the Lord’s rest and enjoy its liberty.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — glorified

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-01#v10|2 Thessalonians 1:10]]

The Lord is the Lord of glory ([[1 Corinthians-02#v8|1 Cor. 2:8]]); He was glorified in His resurrection and ascension ([[John-17#v1|John 17:1]]; [[Luke-24#v26|Luke 24:26]]; [[Hebrews-02#v9|Heb. 2:9]]). Now He is in us as the hope of glory ([[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27]]) to bring us into glory ([[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]). At His coming back, on the one hand He will come from the heavens with glory ([[Revelation-10#v1|Rev. 10:1]]; [[Matthew-25#v31|Matt. 25:31]]), and on the other hand He will be glorified in His saints; that is, His glory will be manifested from within His members, causing their body of humiliation to be transfigured into His glory, conforming it to the body of His glory ([[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]). Thus, the unbelievers will marvel at Him, admire Him, wonder at Him, in us, His believers.
