---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Paul and Silvanus and Timothy to the [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — church|church]] of the [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Thessalonians|Thessalonians]] in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:

## v2

Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v3

We ought to thank God always concerning you, brothers, even as it is fitting, because your [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — faith|faith]] grows exceedingly and the love of each one of you all to one another is increasing,

## v4

So that we ourselves boast in you among the churches of God concerning your [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — endurance|endurance]] and faith in all your persecutions and the afflictions which you bear,

## v5

A plain indication of the [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — righteous|righteous]] judgment of God, that you may be accounted worthy of the [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God, for which also you suffer;

## v6

Since it is just with God to repay with affliction those afflicting you,

## v7

And to you who are being afflicted, [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — rest|rest]] with us at the revelation of the Lord Jesus from heaven with the angels of His power, in flaming fire,

## v8

Rendering vengeance to those who do not know God and to those who do not obey the gospel of our Lord Jesus [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Christ|Christ]].

## v9

They will pay the penalty of eternal destruction from the [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — presence|presence]] of the Lord and from the glory of His strength

## v10

When He comes to be [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — glorified|glorified]] in His saints and to be marveled at in all those who have believed (because our testimony to you was believed) in that day.

## v11

For which also we pray always concerning you, that our God may count you worthy of your calling and may fulfill in power your every [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — good|good]] intention for goodness and your work of faith,

## v12

So that the name of our Lord Jesus may be glorified in you, and you in Him, according to the [[2 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — grace|grace]] of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
