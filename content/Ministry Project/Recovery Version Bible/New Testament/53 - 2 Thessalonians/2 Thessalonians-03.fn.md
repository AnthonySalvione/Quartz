## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — direct

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|2 Thessalonians 3:5]]

By the leading of the Spirit, through whom the love of God has been poured out into our hearts ([[Romans-08#v14|Rom. 8:14]]; [[Romans-05#v5|5:5]]).

### Footnote 5.2 — into

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|2 Thessalonians 3:5]]

I.e., to love God with the love of God that we have enjoyed and experienced.

### Footnote 5.3 — love

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|2 Thessalonians 3:5]]

Our love toward God, issuing from the love of God ([[1 John-04#v19|1 John 4:19]]) that has been poured out into our hearts.

### Footnote 5.4 — into

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|2 Thessalonians 3:5]]

I.e., to endure with the endurance of Christ that we have enjoyed and experienced.

### Footnote 5.5 — endurance

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|2 Thessalonians 3:5]]

On the positive side, we need to enjoy the love of God that we may love Him in order to live for Him, and on the negative side, we need to participate in the endurance of Christ that we may endure sufferings, as He did, to stand against Satan, the enemy of God. Loving God and enduring sufferings are excellent characteristics of a Christian living.

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — grace

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-03#v18|2 Thessalonians 3:18]]

It is only when the Lord is enjoyed by us as grace that we can guard the church life from any kind of misleading and disorderliness. To live a proper church life and to keep the church life in order, we need to enjoy the Lord as the supplying grace. See [note 28¹](<1 Thessalonians-05.fn#Footnote 28.1 — grace>) in [[1 Thessalonians-05|1 Thes. 5]].
