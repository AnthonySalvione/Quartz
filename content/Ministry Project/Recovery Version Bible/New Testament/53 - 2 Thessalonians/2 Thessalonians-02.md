## v1

Now we ask you, brothers, [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — with|with]] regard to the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — coming|coming]] of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 1.3 — to|to]] Him,

## v2

That you be not quickly shaken [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — in|in]] mind nor alarmed, neither by a [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — spirit|spirit]] nor by word nor by a letter as if by us, to the effect that the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 2.3 — day|day]] of the Lord has come.

## v3

Let no one [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — deceive|deceive]] you in any way, because it will not come unless the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — apostasy|apostasy]] comes first and the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 3.3 — man|man]] of lawlessness is revealed, the son of perdition,

## v4

Who opposes and [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — exalts|exalts]] himself above all that is called God or an object of worship, so that he sits in the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — temple|temple]] of God, setting himself forth, saying that he is God.

## v5

Do you not remember that while I was still with you, I said these things to you?

## v6

And now you know that which [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — restrains|restrains]], so that he might be revealed in his own [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — time|time]].

## v7

For it is the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — mystery|mystery]] of lawlessness that is now operating, but only until the one now restraining [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — goes|goes]] out of the way.

## v8

And then the lawless one will be revealed (whom the Lord Jesus will [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — slay|slay]] by the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — breath|breath]] of His mouth and bring to nothing by the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — manifestation|manifestation]] of His [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 8.4 — coming|coming]]),

## v9

The coming of whom is [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — according|according]] to Satan’s operation in all power and signs and wonders of a [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — lie|lie]]

## v10

And in all deceit of unrighteousness among those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth that they might be saved.

## v11

And [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — because|because]] of this God sends to them an operation of error that they might believe the lie,

## v12

So that all who have not believed the truth but have taken [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — pleasure|pleasure]] in unrighteousness might be judged.

## v13

But we ought to thank God always concerning you, brothers beloved of the Lord, because God [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — chose|chose]] you [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — from|from]] the beginning unto salvation in [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.3 — sanctification|sanctification]] of the Spirit and [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.4 — belief|belief]] of the truth,

## v14

To [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — which|which]] also He called you through our gospel unto the obtaining of the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — glory|glory]] of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## v15

So then, brothers, stand firm and hold to the [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — things|things]] which were handed down to you and which you were taught, whether by word or by our letter.

## v16

Now our [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Lord|Lord]] Jesus Christ Himself and God our Father, who has loved us and given us [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 16.2 — eternal|eternal]] comfort and [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 16.3 — good|good]] hope in [[2 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 16.4 — grace|grace]],

## v17

Comfort your hearts and establish you in every good work and word.
