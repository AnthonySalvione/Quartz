# 2 Thessalonians-02 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[2 Thessalonians-02|2 Thessalonians 2]]

### Footnote 1.2 — coming

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v1|2 Thessalonians 2:1]]

Gk. parousia, meaning presence. See notes [19¹](<1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — coming>) in [[1 Thessalonians-02|1 Thes. 2]] and [3³](<Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.3 — coming>) in [[Matthew-24|Matt. 24]]. Two matters are covered here: the Lord’s parousia (presence) and our gathering together (rapture) to Him. The Lord’s parousia will begin with the rapture of the overcomers to the throne of God in heaven ([[Revelation-12#v5|Rev. 12:5-6]]); at the end of the great tribulation, which will occur in the last three and a half years of this age, the second half of the last week in [[Daniel-09#v27|Dan. 9:27]] ([[Matthew-24#v21|Matt. 24:21]] and [note](<Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 21.1 — great>); [[Revelation-11#v2|Rev. 11:2]] and [note 4](<Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 2.4 — forty-two>)), it will come to the air ([[Revelation-10#v1|Rev. 10:1]] and [note 1²](<Revelation-10.fn#Footnote 1.2 — another>)) and last for a time; and it will end with its manifestation, “the manifestation of His parousia” ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v8|v. 8]]; [[Matthew-24#v30|Matt. 24:30]] and [note 4](<Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.4 — on>)). During the time in which the Lord’s parousia remains in the air, the majority of the believers will be raptured to meet the Lord there ([[1 Thessalonians-04#v17|1 Thes. 4:17]]). According to the context, the day of the Lord in [[2 Thessalonians-02#v2|v. 2]] refers to the day of the Lord’s parousia (coming), in which the rapture of the majority of the believers will occur. [[2 Thessalonians-02#v3|Verse 3]] tells us affirmatively that before that day Antichrist will be revealed to play the major role in the great tribulation ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v4|v. 4]]; [[Revelation-13#v1|Rev. 13:1-8]], [[Revelation-13#v12|12-15]]). This reveals clearly and definitely that the Lord’s coming (parousia) to the air and the rapture of the majority of the believers cannot take place before the great tribulation.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — deceive

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v3|2 Thessalonians 2:3]]

Or, beguile; “not only making a false impression, but actually leading astray” (Vincent).

### Footnote 3.2 — apostasy

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v3|2 Thessalonians 2:3]]

I.e., falling away (from the straight way of God’s economy as revealed in the Scriptures).

### Footnote 3.3 — man

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v3|2 Thessalonians 2:3]]

Antichrist, as prophesied in [[Daniel-07#v20|Dan. 7:20-21]], [[Daniel-07#v24|24-26]]; [[Daniel-09#v27|9:27]]; [[Daniel-11#v36|11:36-37]]; [[Revelation-13#v1|Rev. 13:1-8]], [[Revelation-13#v12|12-18]]; [[Revelation-19#v19|19:19-20]]. He will be the man of lawlessness and will change laws, destroy and corrupt many to an extraordinary degree, blaspheme God, and deceive men. Hence, the Lord will utterly destroy him, and he will become the son of perdition.

### Footnote 4.2 — temple

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v4|2 Thessalonians 2:4]]

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — chose

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v13|2 Thessalonians 2:13]]

God loved us ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v16|v. 16]]), chose us from the beginning, and called us through the gospel ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v14|v. 14]]). He chose us unto His salvation, which is by sanctification of the Spirit, and He called us to the obtaining of the Lord’s glory. Now He is taking us on with eternal comfort and good hope in grace.

### Footnote 13.2 — from

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v13|2 Thessalonians 2:13]]

I.e., from eternity past (cf. [[Ephesians-01#v4|Eph. 1:4]]). Some ancient MSS read, as firstfruits.

### Footnote 13.3 — sanctification

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v13|2 Thessalonians 2:13]]

Sanctification of the Spirit consists of three steps: (1) the Spirit’s seeking us and convicting us at the time that He caused us to repent and believe ([[1 Peter-01#v2|1 Pet. 1:2]]; [[John-16#v8|John 16:8]]); (2) His sanctifying us both positionally and dispositionally ([[Hebrews-13#v12|Heb. 13:12]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v11|1 Cor. 6:11]]) at the time we were saved; and (3) His sanctifying us dispositionally as we pursue the growth in life ([[Romans-06#v19|Rom. 6:19]], [[Romans-06#v22|22]]). By these three steps of the Spirit’s sanctification, God’s salvation is applied to us that we may obtain and enjoy it fully. The three steps of the Spirit’s sanctification not only separate us from all old and negative things, making us holy unto God, but also sanctify us, making us a new creation full of the Lord’s essence and element, that we may obtain the Lord’s glory.

### Footnote 13.4 — belief

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v13|2 Thessalonians 2:13]]

Or, faith. By the Spirit’s sanctification God’s salvation is revealed and brought to us; by our believing we receive all that is revealed and brought to us by the Spirit, so that we obtain God’s salvation.

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — which

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v14|2 Thessalonians 2:14]]

Referring to salvation in sanctification— and belief— in the preceding verse. God chose us unto salvation in eternity, and then in time He called us unto the obtaining of the glory of our Lord. Salvation in sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth is the procedure; the obtaining of the glory of our Lord is the goal.

### Footnote 14.2 — glory

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v14|2 Thessalonians 2:14]]

The glory of the Lord is that He, as the Son of God the Father, possesses the Father’s life and nature that He may express the Father. To obtain the Lord’s glory is to be in the same position as the Son of God that we may express the Father ([[John-17#v22|John 17:22]] and [note 14¹](<John-17.fn#Footnote 14.1 — word>)).

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — Lord

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v16|2 Thessalonians 2:16]]

Our Lord Jesus Christ and God the Father work together to comfort and establish the believers ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v17|v. 17]]).

### Footnote 16.2 — eternal

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v16|2 Thessalonians 2:16]]

Not temporary and transitory comfort and strengthening but eternal comfort by the divine life. This comfort is sufficient for any kind of environment and situation; thus, it is with good hope.

### Footnote 16.3 — good

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v16|2 Thessalonians 2:16]]

The hope of glory ([[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27]]), which is the hope of the Lord’s coming ([[1 Thessalonians-01#v3|1 Thes. 1:3]]), when we will be either resurrected or transfigured into glory ([[1 Thessalonians-04#v13|1 Thes. 4:13-14]]; [[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]; [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]).

### Footnote 16.4 — grace

**Verse:** [[2 Thessalonians-02#v16|2 Thessalonians 2:16]]

God Himself in Christ for us to enjoy (see [note 17¹](<John-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — law>) in [[John-01|John 1]]) that we may be sanctified by His Spirit ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v13|v. 13]]) and comforted and established ([[2 Thessalonians-02#v17|v. 17]]) with eternal comfort and good hope.
