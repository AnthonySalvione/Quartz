## v1

The [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — elder|elder]] to the chosen [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — lady|lady]] and to her children, whom I love in [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — truthfulness|truthfulness]], and not only I but also all [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — those|those]] who know the [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 1.5 — truth|truth]],

## v2

[[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — For|For]] the sake of the [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — truth|truth]] which abides in us and will be with us forever:

## v3

Grace, mercy, peace will be with [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — us|us]] from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — truth|truth]] and love.

## v4

I [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — rejoiced|rejoiced]] greatly that I have found some of your children walking in [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — truth|truth]], even as we received [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — commandment|commandment]] from the Father.

## v5

And now I ask you, lady, not as writing a new commandment to you but [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — that|that]] which we have had [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — from|from]] the beginning, that we love one another.

## v6

And this is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the commandment, even as you heard from the beginning, that you walk in [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — love|love]].

## v7

For many [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — deceivers|deceivers]] went out into the world, those who [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — do|do]] not confess Jesus Christ coming [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.3 — in|in]] the flesh. This is the deceiver and the [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 7.4 — antichrist|antichrist]].

## v8

[[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Look|Look]] to yourselves that you do not [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — lose|lose]] the [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — things|things]] which we wrought, but that you may receive a full [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 8.4 — reward|reward]].

## v9

Everyone who [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — goes|goes]] beyond and does not abide in the [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — teaching|teaching]] of Christ does not have God; he who abides in the teaching, he [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — has|has]] both the Father and the Son.

## v10

If anyone comes to you and does not [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — bring|bring]] this teaching, do not receive [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — him|him]] into your house, and do not say to him, [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 10.3 — Rejoice|Rejoice]]!

## v11

For he who says to him, Rejoice, shares in his [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — evil|evil]] works.

## v12

Though I have many things to write to you, I do not want to do so with paper and ink, but I am [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — hoping|hoping]] to be with you and to speak [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — face|face]] to face that [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — our|our]] joy may be made full.

## v13

The [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — children|children]] of your chosen [[2 John-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — sister|sister]] greet you.
