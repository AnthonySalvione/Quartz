## v1

[[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] putting away all [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — malice|malice]] and all guile and hypocrisies and envyings and all evil speakings,

## v2

As [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — newborn|newborn]] babes, long for the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — guileless|guileless]] milk [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.3 — of|of]] the word in order that by it you may [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.4 — grow|grow]] [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.5 — unto|unto]] salvation,

## v3

If you have [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — tasted|tasted]] that the Lord is [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — good|good]].

## v4

Coming to Him, a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — living|living]] stone, rejected by men but with God chosen and [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — precious|precious]],

## v5

You yourselves also, as [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — living|living]] stones, are being [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.2 — built|built]] up as a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.3 — spiritual|spiritual]] [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.4 — house|house]] [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.5 — into|into]] a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.6 — holy|holy]] [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.7 — priesthood|priesthood]] to offer up [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.8 — spiritual|spiritual]] sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

## v6

For it is contained in Scripture: “Behold, I lay in Zion a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — cornerstone|cornerstone]], [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — chosen|chosen]] and [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 6.3 — precious|precious]]; and he who believes on Him shall by no means be put to shame.”

## v7

To you therefore who believe is the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — preciousness|preciousness]]; but to the unbelieving, “The stone which the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — builders|builders]] rejected, this has [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 7.3 — become|become]] the head of the corner,”

## v8

And, “A stone of [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — stumbling|stumbling]] and a rock of offense”; who stumble at [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — the|the]] word, being disobedient, to [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — which|which]] also they were appointed.

## v9

But you are a chosen [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — race|race]], a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — royal|royal]] priesthood, a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — holy|holy]] nation, a people acquired for a possession, so that you may [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.4 — tell|tell]] out the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.5 — virtues|virtues]] of Him who has called you out of [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.6 — darkness|darkness]] into His marvelous [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.6 — darkness|light]];

## v10

[[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Who|Who]] once were not a people but now are the people of God; who had not previously been shown mercy but have now been shown mercy.

## v11

Beloved, I entreat you as strangers and sojourners to abstain from [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fleshly|fleshly]] lusts, which war against the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fleshly|soul]],

## v12

Having your [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — manner|manner]] of life [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — excellent|excellent]] among the Gentiles, so that in the matter concerning which they speak against you as evildoers they may, by your good works, as they see them with their own eyes, glorify God in the day of His [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — visitation|visitation]].

## v13

[[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Be|Be]] subject to every human [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — institution|institution]] [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 13.3 — for|for]] the Lord’s sake, whether to a king as being supreme,

## v14

Or to governors as being sent by him for vengeance on evildoers and praise of those who do good.

## v15

For so is the will of God, that by doing good you would muzzle the ignorance of [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — foolish|foolish]] men;

## v16

As free, and yet not having freedom as a covering for evil, but as slaves of God.

## v17

Honor all men. Love the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — brotherhood|brotherhood]]. Fear God. Honor the king.

## v18

Household servants, be subject in all [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — fear|fear]] to your masters, not only to the good and forbearing but also to the crooked.

## v19

For this is [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — grace|grace]], if anyone, [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 19.2 — because|because]] of a consciousness of God, bears sorrows by suffering [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 19.3 — unjustly|unjustly]].

## v20

For what [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — glory|glory]] is it if, while sinning and being buffeted, you endure? But if, while doing good and suffering, you endure, this is grace with God.

## v21

For to [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — this|this]] you were called, because Christ also suffered on your behalf, leaving you a [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 21.2 — model|model]] so that you may follow in His steps;

## v22

Who committed no sin, nor was guile found in His mouth;

## v23

Who being reviled did not revile in return; suffering, He did not threaten but kept committing [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — all|all]] to Him who [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — judges|judges]] righteously;

## v24

Who Himself [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.1 — bore|bore]] up our sins in His body on the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.2 — tree|tree]], in order that we, [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.3 — having|having]] died to sins, might [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.4 — live|live]] [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.5 — to|to]] righteousness; by whose [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.6 — bruise|bruise]] you were [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 24.7 — healed|healed]].

## v25

For you were like sheep being led astray, but you have now [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 25.1 — returned|returned]] to the [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 25.2 — Shepherd|Shepherd]] and Overseer of your [[1 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 25.3 — souls|souls]].
