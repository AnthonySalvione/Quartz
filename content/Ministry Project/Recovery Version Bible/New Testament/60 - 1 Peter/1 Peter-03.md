## v1

In [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — like|like]] manner, wives, be subject to your own husbands, that even if any disobey the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — word|word]], they will be gained without the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — word|word]] through the manner of life of their wives,

## v2

Seeing with their own eyes your [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — pure|pure]] manner of life [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — in|in]] [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — fear|fear]].

## v3

Let your adorning not be the outward [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — plaiting|plaiting]] of hair and putting on of gold or clothing with garments,

## v4

But the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — hidden|hidden]] man of the heart in the incorruptible adornment of a meek and quiet spirit, which is very costly in the sight of God.

## v5

For in this manner formerly the holy women also, who hoped in God, adorned themselves, being subject to their own husbands,

## v6

As Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord; whose children you have become, if you do good and do not fear any [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — terror|terror]].

## v7

Husbands, in like manner dwell together with them [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — according|according]] to knowledge, as with the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — weaker|weaker]], female [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.3 — vessel|vessel]], [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.4 — assigning|assigning]] honor to them as also to fellow heirs of the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.5 — grace|grace]] of life, that your prayers may not be hindered.

## v8

And finally be all of the same mind, sympathetic, loving the brothers, tenderhearted, humble-minded;

## v9

Not rendering evil for evil or reviling for reviling, but on the contrary [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — blessing|blessing]] one another, because you were called [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — to|to]] this, that you might inherit blessing.

## v10

For “he who desires to love life and see [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — good|good]] days, let him cause his tongue to cease from evil and his lips to speak no guile.

## v11

And let him turn away from evil and do good; let him seek peace and pursue it;

## v12

For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous, and His ears incline to their petition; but the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.”

## v13

And who will harm you if you become zealous for what is good?

## v14

But even if you suffer because of righteousness, you are blessed. And do not be afraid with fear from them, nor be troubled,

## v15

But [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — sanctify|sanctify]] Christ as Lord in your hearts, being always ready for a defense to everyone who asks of you an account concerning the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — hope|hope]] which is in you,

## v16

Yet with meekness and [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fear|fear]], having a good [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — conscience|conscience]], so that in the matter in which you are spoken against, those who revile your good manner of life [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.3 — in|in]] Christ may be put to shame.

## v17

For it is better, if the will of God should will it, to suffer for doing good than for doing evil.

## v18

For Christ also has suffered once for [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — sins|sins]], the Righteous [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.2 — on|on]] behalf of the unrighteous, that He might bring you to God, on the one hand being put to death in the flesh, but on the other, made alive in the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.3 — Spirit|Spirit]];

## v19

In [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 19.1 — which|which]] also He went and [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 19.2 — proclaimed|proclaimed]] to the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 19.3 — spirits|spirits]] in [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 19.4 — prison|prison]],

## v20

[[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Who|Who]] had formerly disobeyed when the long-suffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was being prepared; entering into which, a few, that is, eight souls, were [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 20.2 — brought|brought]] safely through [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 20.3 — by|by]] water.

## v21

[[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Which|Which]] water, as the antitype, also now saves you, that is, [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 21.2 — baptism|baptism]], [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 21.3 — not|not]] a putting away of the filth of the flesh but the [[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 21.4 — appeal|appeal]] of a good conscience unto God, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ,

## v22

[[1 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Who|Who]] is at the right hand of God, having gone into heaven, angels and authorities and powers being subjected to Him.
