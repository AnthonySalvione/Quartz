# 1 Peter 1 — Outline

```rcv-outline
- I. Introduction—to the sojourning believers under the operation of the Triune God — 1:1–2 ^rcvo-i
- II. The full salvation of the Triune God and its issues — 1:3–25 ^rcvo-ii

    - B. The Spirit’s application—through the prophets’ prophesying and the apostles’ preaching — 1:10–12 ^rcvo-ii-b
    - C. Christ’s redemption—unto a holy life by the holy nature and unto brotherly love through purification by the sanctifying truth, based on regeneration by the incorruptible seed through the living word of God — 1:13–25 ^rcvo-ii-c
```
