## v1

Since Christ therefore has [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — suffered|suffered]] in the flesh, you also [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — arm|arm]] yourselves with the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 1.3 — same|same]] mind (because he who has [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 1.4 — suffered|suffered]] in the flesh has ceased from sin),

## v2

[[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — No|No]] longer to live the rest of the time in the flesh in the lusts of men, but in the will of God.

## v3

For the time which has passed is sufficient for you to have carried out the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — desire|desire]] of the Gentiles, having gone on [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — in|in]] licentiousness, lusts, debaucheries, carousings, drinking bouts, and lawless idolatries.

## v4

In this they think it [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — strange|strange]] that you are not [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — running|running]] together with them into the same [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — flood|flood]] of dissoluteness, [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 4.4 — slandering|slandering]] you;

## v5

[[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Who|Who]] will [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — render|render]] an account to Him who is ready to [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 5.3 — judge|judge]] the living and the dead.

## v6

For unto this end the gospel was announced also to [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — those|those]] who are now dead, that they might be [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — judged|judged]] in the flesh according to men but live in the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — spirit|spirit]] according to God.

## v7

But the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — end|end]] of all things has drawn near. Therefore [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — be|be]] sober-minded and [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 7.3 — be|be]] sober unto prayers.

## v8

Above all, have fervent love among yourselves, because love covers a multitude of sins.

## v9

Be hospitable to one another without murmuring,

## v10

Each one, as he has received a gift, [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — ministering|ministering]] it among yourselves as good stewards of the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 10.2 — varied|varied]] grace of God.

## v11

If anyone speaks, as speaking [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — oracles|oracles]] of God; if anyone ministers, as ministering out of the strength which God [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 11.2 — supplies|supplies]]; that in all things God may be glorified [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 11.3 — through|through]] Jesus Christ, to whom is the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 11.4 — glory|glory]] and the might forever and ever. Amen.

## v12

Beloved, do not think that the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 12.1 — fiery|fiery]] ordeal among you, coming to you for a [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 12.2 — trial|trial]], is [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 12.3 — strange|strange]], as if it were a [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 12.3 — strange|strange]] thing happening to you;

## v13

But inasmuch as you share in the sufferings of Christ, rejoice, so that also at the revelation of His glory you may rejoice exultingly.

## v14

If you are reproached in the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 14.1 — name|name]] of Christ, you are blessed, because [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 14.2 — the|the]] Spirit of glory and of God [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 14.3 — rests|rests]] upon you.

## v15

For let none of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or an evildoer or as a [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — meddler|meddler]] into others’ affairs;

## v16

But if as a [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Christian|Christian]], let him not be ashamed, but let him [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 16.2 — glorify|glorify]] God in this name.

## v17

For it is time for the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — judgment|judgment]] to begin from the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 17.2 — house|house]] of God; and if first from us, [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 17.3 — what|what]] will be the end of those who [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 17.4 — disobey|disobey]] the gospel of God?

## v18

And if the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 18.1 — righteous|righteous]] man is [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 18.2 — saved|saved]] only with difficulty, where will the ungodly and the sinner [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 18.3 — appear|appear]]?

## v19

So then let those [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 19.1 — also|also]] who suffer according to the [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 19.2 — will|will]] of God [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 19.3 — commit|commit]] their [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 19.4 — souls|souls]] in [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 19.5 — well-doing|well-doing]] to a faithful [[1 Peter-04.fn#Footnote 19.6 — Creator|Creator]].
