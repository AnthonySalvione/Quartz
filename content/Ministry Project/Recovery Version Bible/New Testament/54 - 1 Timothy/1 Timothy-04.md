## v1

[[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — But|But]] the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] says expressly that in [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.3 — later|later]] times some will depart from the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.4 — faith|faith]], giving heed to [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.5 — deceiving|deceiving]] spirits and teachings of [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 1.6 — demons|demons]].

## v2

[[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — By|By]] means of the hypocrisy of men who speak lies, of men who are [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.2 — branded|branded]] in their own conscience as with a [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 2.3 — hot|hot]] iron,

## v3

Who forbid [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — marriage|marriage]] and command abstaining from [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — marriage|foods]], which [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — God|God]] has created to be partaken of with thanksgiving by those who [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.3 — believe|believe]] and have [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 3.4 — full|full]] knowledge of the truth.

## v4

For [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — every|every]] creature of God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if received with thanksgiving;

## v5

For it is [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — sanctified|sanctified]] through the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — word|word]] of God and [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 5.3 — intercession|intercession]].

## v6

If you lay these things before the brothers, you will be a good [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — minister|minister]] of Christ Jesus, [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — being|being]] nourished with the words of the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — faith|faith]] and of the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.4 — good|good]] teaching which you [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 6.5 — have|have]] closely followed.

## v7

But the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — profane|profane]] and old-womanish [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — myths|myths]] [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 7.3 — refuse|refuse]], and [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 7.4 — exercise|exercise]] yourself unto godliness.

## v8

For bodily exercise is profitable for [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — a|a]] little, but godliness is profitable for [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.2 — all|all]] things, having [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 8.3 — promise|promise]] of the present life and of that which is to come.

## v9

Faithful is the word and worthy of all acceptance.

## v10

For to this end we labor and strive, because we have set our hope on the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — living|living]] God, who is the Savior of all men, especially of those who believe.

## v11

Charge and teach these things.

## v12

Let no one despise your [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 12.1 — youth|youth]], but be a pattern to the believers in [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 12.2 — word|word]], in conduct, in love, in faith, in [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 12.3 — purity|purity]].

## v13

Until I come, attend to the public [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — reading|reading]], to the exhortation, to the teaching.

## v14

Do not neglect the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 14.1 — gift|gift]] which is [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 14.2 — in|in]] you, which was given to you by means of [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 14.3 — prophecy|prophecy]] with the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 14.4 — laying|laying]] on of the hands of the [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 14.5 — presbytery|presbytery]].

## v15

Practice these things; [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — be|be]] in them, that your [[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 15.2 — progress|progress]] may be manifest to all.

## v16

[[1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Take|Take]] heed to yourself and to your teaching; continue in these things; for in doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.
