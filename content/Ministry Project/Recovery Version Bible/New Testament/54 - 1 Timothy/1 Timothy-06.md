## v1

As many as are slaves under the yoke should regard their own masters as worthy of all honor, lest the name of God and [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — our|our]] teaching be [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — blasphemed|blasphemed]].

## v2

And those who have believing masters should not despise them, because they are brothers; but rather they should [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — serve|serve]] them, because those who recompense them for the kindly service received are believers and beloved. These things teach and exhort.

## v3

If anyone teaches [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — different|different]] things and does not consent to [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 3.2 — healthy|healthy]] words, those of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the teaching which is according to [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 3.3 — godliness|godliness]],

## v4

He is [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — blinded|blinded]] with pride, understanding nothing, but is [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — diseased|diseased]] with questionings and contentions of words, out of which come envy, strife, [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 4.3 — slanders|slanders]], evil suspicions,

## v5

[[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Perpetual|Perpetual]] wranglings of men corrupted in mind and [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — deprived|deprived]] of the [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 5.3 — truth|truth]], [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 5.4 — supposing|supposing]] godliness to be a means of gain.

## v6

But godliness with [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — contentment|contentment]] is [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — great|great]] gain;

## v7

For [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — we|we]] have brought nothing into the world, because neither can we carry anything out.

## v8

But having food and [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — covering|covering]], with these we will be [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 8.2 — content|content]].

## v9

But those who [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — intend|intend]] to be rich fall into temptation and a snare and many foolish and harmful [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 9.2 — desires|desires]], which [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 9.3 — plunge|plunge]] men into [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 9.4 — destruction|destruction]] and ruin.

## v10

For the love of money is [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — a|a]] root of all evils, because of which some, [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 10.2 — aspiring|aspiring]] after money, have [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 10.3 — been|been]] led away from the [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 10.4 — faith|faith]] and pierced themselves through with many pains.

## v11

But you, O [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — man|man]] of God, flee these things, and pursue [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.2 — righteousness|righteousness]], [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.3 — godliness|godliness]], [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.4 — faith|faith]], [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.5 — love|love]], [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.6 — endurance|endurance]], [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 11.7 — meekness|meekness]].

## v12

[[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Fight|Fight]] the good fight of the faith; lay hold on the [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — eternal|eternal]] life, [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 12.3 — to|to]] which you were called and have [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 12.4 — confessed|confessed]] the good confession before many witnesses.

## v13

I charge you before God, who preserves all things in life, and Christ Jesus, who testified the good confession before Pontius Pilate,

## v14

To keep [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — the|the]] commandment spotless, without reproach, until the [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 14.2 — appearing|appearing]] of our Lord Jesus Christ,

## v15

[[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Which|Which]] in its own times [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 15.2 — the|the]] blessed and only Sovereign will show, the King of those who reign as kings and Lord of those who rule as lords,

## v16

Who alone has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light, whom no man has seen nor can see, to whom be honor and eternal might. Amen.

## v17

Charge those who are rich in the present age not to be high-minded, nor to set their hope on the uncertainty of riches but on God, who affords us all things richly for our enjoyment;

## v18

To do [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — good|good]], to be [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 18.2 — rich|rich]] in good works, to be ready to distribute, to be ones willing to share;

## v19

Laying away for themselves a good foundation as a treasure for the [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 19.1 — future|future]], that they may lay hold on [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 19.2 — that|that]] which is really life.

## v20

O Timothy, [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 20.1 — guard|guard]] the deposit, turning away from profane, vain babblings and oppositions from [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 20.2 — what|what]] is falsely called knowledge,

## v21

Because of which some, professing this, have [[1 Timothy-06.fn#Footnote 21.1 — misaimed|misaimed]] regarding the faith. Grace be with you.
