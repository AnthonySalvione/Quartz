---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Do not [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — upbraid|upbraid]] an elderly man, but exhort him as a father, younger men as brothers,

## v2

Elderly women as mothers, younger women as sisters, in all purity.

## v3

[[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Honor|Honor]] widows who are really widows.

## v4

But if any widow has children or grandchildren, let them first learn to [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — show|show]] respect toward their own household and to render a [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 4.2 — return|return]] to their parents; for this is acceptable in the sight of God.

## v5

Now she who is really a widow and is left alone has set her hope on God and continues in petitions and prayers night and day.

## v6

But she who gives herself to pleasure is dead while living.

## v7

These things also charge, that they may be without reproach.

## v8

But if anyone does not provide for [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — his|his]] own, and especially for those of his household, he has denied the [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 8.2 — faith|faith]] and is worse than an unbeliever.

## v9

Let a widow be enrolled, not under sixty years old, having been the wife of one man,

## v10

[[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Well|Well]] attested for good works, if she has brought up children, if she has shown hospitality to strangers, if she has washed the saints’ feet, if she has assisted the afflicted, if she has diligently followed every good work.

## v11

But younger widows refuse; for when they follow their passions in disregard of Christ, they desire to marry,

## v12

And thus have condemnation because they have set aside their first [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — pledge|pledge]].

## v13

And at the same time they also learn to be idle, going around from house to house; and they are not only idle but also gossips and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not.

## v14

I will therefore that [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — younger|younger]] widows marry, [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — bear|bear]] children, keep house, give no opportunity to the opposer for reproach;

## v15

For already some have turned aside after Satan.

## v16

If any believing woman has widows in her family, let her assist them, and let not the church be burdened, that it may assist those who are really widows.

## v17

Let the elders who take the lead well be counted worthy of double [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — honor|honor]], [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 17.2 — especially|especially]] those who labor in [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 17.3 — word|word]] and teaching.

## v18

For the Scripture says, “You shall not muzzle an ox that is treading out the grain,” and, “The workman is worthy of his pay.”

## v19

Against an elder do not [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — receive|receive]] an accusation, except based upon two or three witnesses.

## v20

The ones who sin [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — reprove|reprove]] before [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 20.2 — all|all]] that the rest also may have fear.

## v21

I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus and the chosen [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 21.1 — angels|angels]] that you keep these things without [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 21.2 — prejudice|prejudice]], doing nothing by way of [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 21.3 — partiality|partiality]].

## v22

[[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Lay|Lay]] hands [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 22.2 — quickly|quickly]] on no man, nor participate in others’ sins; keep yourself pure.

## v23

[[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 23.1 — No|No]] longer drink water only, but use a little wine for the sake of your stomach and your frequent [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 23.2 — illnesses|illnesses]].

## v24

The [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 24.1 — sins|sins]] of some men are openly [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 24.2 — manifest|manifest]], going before to judgment; and for others, [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 24.3 — they|they]] [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 24.4 — also|also]] follow after.

## v25

Likewise also the [[1 Timothy-05.fn#Footnote 25.1 — good|good]] works are openly manifest, and those that are otherwise cannot be hidden.
