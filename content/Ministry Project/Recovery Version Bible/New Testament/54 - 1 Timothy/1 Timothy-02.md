---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

I exhort therefore, first of all, that [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — petitions|petitions]], prayers, [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — intercessions|intercessions]], thanksgivings be made on behalf of all men;

## v2

On behalf of kings and all who are in high position, that we may lead a [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — quiet|quiet]] and tranquil life in all [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — godliness|godliness]] and [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 2.3 — gravity|gravity]].

## v3

This is good and acceptable in the sight of our Savior God,

## v4

Who desires [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — all|all]] men to be saved and to come to the [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — full|full]] knowledge of the truth.

## v5

For there is [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — one|one]] God and one [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Mediator|Mediator]] of God and men, the [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 5.3 — man|man]] Christ Jesus,

## v6

Who [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — gave|gave]] Himself as a [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — ransom|ransom]] for all, the [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 6.3 — testimony|testimony]] to be borne in its own times.

## v7

For which I was appointed a [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — herald|herald]] and an apostle (I speak the truth; I do not lie), a [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — teacher|teacher]] of the Gentiles in [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 7.3 — faith|faith]] and truth.

## v8

I desire therefore that men [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — pray|pray]] in every place, lifting up [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — holy|holy]] [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — hands|hands]], without [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.4 — wrath|wrath]] and [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 8.5 — reasoning|reasoning]];

## v9

Similarly, that women adorn themselves in [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — proper|proper]] clothing with [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — modesty|modesty]] and [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — sobriety|sobriety]], not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly clothing,

## v10

But, what befits women professing [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — godly|godly]] reverence, by good works.

## v11

Let a woman learn in [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — quietness|quietness]] in all [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — subjection|subjection]];

## v12

But I do not permit a woman to [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — teach|teach]] or to assert authority over a man, but to be in [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — quietness|quietness]].

## v13

For [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Adam|Adam]] was [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — formed|formed]] first, then Eve;

## v14

And [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Adam|Adam]] was not deceived; but the [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — woman|woman]], having been quite deceived, has fallen into transgression.

## v15

But she will be saved through her [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — childbearing|childbearing]], if they remain in [[1 Timothy-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — faith|faith]] and love and holiness with sobriety.
