## v1

Faithful is the word: If anyone [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — aspires|aspires]] to the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — overseership|overseership]], he desires a good work.

## v2

The [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — overseer|overseer]] then must be [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — without|without]] reproach, the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — husband|husband]] of one wife, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.4 — temperate|temperate]], [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.5 — of|of]] a sober mind, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.6 — orderly|orderly]], [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.7 — hospitable|hospitable]], [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 2.8 — apt|apt]] to teach;

## v3

[[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Not|Not]] an excessive drinker; [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — not|not]] a striker, but [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 3.3 — forbearing|forbearing]]; [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 3.4 — not|not]] contentious;  [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 3.5 — not|not]] fond of money;

## v4

One who [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — manages|manages]] well his own house, having his children in subjection with all [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 4.2 — gravity|gravity]]

## v5

(But if one does not know how to manage his own house, how will he care for the church of God?);

## v6

Not a [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — new|new]] convert, lest being [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 6.2 — blinded|blinded]] with pride he fall into the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 6.3 — judgment|judgment]] suffered by the devil.

## v7

And he also must have a [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — good|good]] testimony from [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — those|those]] outside, that he may not fall into reproach and the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 7.3 — snare|snare]] of the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 7.4 — devil|devil]].

## v8

[[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Deacons|Deacons]] must similarly be grave, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — not|not]] double-tongued, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.3 — not|not]] addicted to much wine, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 8.4 — not|not]] greedy for base gain;

## v9

Holding the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — mystery|mystery]] of the faith in a [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — pure|pure]] conscience.

## v10

And these also must first [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — be|be]] proved; then let them [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — minister|minister]] if they are [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 10.3 — unreprovable|unreprovable]].

## v11

[[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Women|Women]] similarly must be grave, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 11.2 — not|not]] slanderers, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 11.3 — temperate|temperate]], [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 11.4 — faithful|faithful]] in all things.

## v12

Deacons must be [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — husbands|husbands]] of one wife, [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 12.2 — managing|managing]] their children and their own houses well.

## v13

For those who have [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — ministered|ministered]] well obtain for themselves a [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — good|good]] standing and [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 13.3 — much|much]] boldness in faith, which is in Christ Jesus.

## v14

These things I write to you, hoping to come to you shortly.

## v15

But if I delay, I write that you may know [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — how|how]] one ought to conduct himself in the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — house|house]] of God, which is the church of the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.3 — living|living]] God, the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.4 — pillar|pillar]] and base of the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 15.5 — truth|truth]].

## v16

And [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — confessedly|confessedly]], great is the [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — mystery|mystery]] of godliness:  
[[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.3 — He|He]] who was [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.4 — manifested|manifested]] in the flesh, / [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.5 — Justified|Justified]] in the Spirit, / [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.6 — Seen|Seen]] by angels, / [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.7 — Preached|Preached]] among the nations, / [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.8 — Believed|Believed]] on in the world, / [[1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.9 — Taken|Taken]] up in glory.
