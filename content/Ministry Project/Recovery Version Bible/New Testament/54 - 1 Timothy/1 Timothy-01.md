## v1

[[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Paul|Paul]], an apostle of Christ Jesus according to the [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — command|command]] of [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — God|God]] our Savior and of Christ Jesus our [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — hope|hope]],

## v2

To [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Timothy|Timothy]], genuine child in [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — faith|faith]]: Grace, mercy, peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

## v3

Even as I exhorted you, when I was [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — going|going]] into Macedonia, to remain in Ephesus in order that you might charge [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — certain|certain]] ones not to teach [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — different|different]] things

## v4

Nor to give heed to [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — myths|myths]] and unending [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — genealogies|genealogies]], which produce questionings rather than God’s [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — economy|economy]], which is in [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 4.4 — faith|faith]].

## v5

But the end of the [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — charge|charge]] is [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — love|love]] out of a [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — pure|pure]] heart and out of a good conscience and out of unfeigned faith;

## v6

From which things some, having [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — misaimed|misaimed]], have turned aside to [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — vain|vain]] talking,

## v7

Desiring to be [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — teachers|teachers]] of the law, though they understand neither the things that they say, nor concerning what they [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — confidently|confidently]] affirm.

## v8

But we know that the law is good, if one uses it lawfully

## v9

And knows this, that the law is not enacted for a righteous man but for the lawless and unruly, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and profane, for those who [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — strike|strike]] their fathers and those who [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — strike|strike]] their mothers, for murderers,

## v10

For fornicators, homosexuals, kidnappers, liars, perjurers, and whatever other thing that is opposed to the [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — healthy|healthy]] teaching,

## v11

According to the [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — gospel|gospel]] of the glory of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.

## v12

I give thanks to Him who [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — empowers|empowers]] me, Christ Jesus our Lord, that He has counted me faithful, appointing me to the ministry,

## v13

Who formerly was a [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — blasphemer|blasphemer]] and a persecutor and an [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — insulting|insulting]] person; but I was shown [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — mercy|mercy]] because, being [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.4 — ignorant|ignorant]], I acted in [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 13.4 — ignorant|unbelief]].

## v14

And the [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — grace|grace]] of our Lord superabounded with [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — faith|faith]] and love in Christ Jesus.

## v15

Faithful is the word and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — came|came]] into the world to save sinners, of whom I am foremost.

## v16

But because of this I was shown mercy, that in me, the foremost, Jesus Christ might display all His long-suffering for a [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — pattern|pattern]] to those who are to believe on Him unto [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — eternal|eternal]] life.

## v17

[[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Now|Now]] to the King of the ages, incorruptible, invisible, the only God, be [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — honor|honor]] and glory forever and ever. Amen.

## v18

This [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — charge|charge]] I commit to you, my child Timothy, according to the [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — prophecies|prophecies]] previously made concerning you, that by them you might [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 18.3 — war|war]] the good warfare,

## v19

Holding [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 19.3 — faith|faith]]|faith]] and a good conscience, concerning which some, thrusting these away, have become [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — shipwrecked|shipwrecked]] regarding the faith;

## v20

Of whom are [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Hymenaeus|Hymenaeus]] and [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — Alexander|Alexander]], whom I have delivered to Satan that they may be [[1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 20.3 — disciplined|disciplined]] not to blaspheme.
