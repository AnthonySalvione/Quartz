# 1 Timothy-05 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[1 Timothy-05|1 Timothy 5]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — upbraid

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v1|1 Timothy 5:1]]

Rebuke sharply.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — Honor

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v3|1 Timothy 5:3]]

The stress is on the supplying of material things.

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — show

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v4|1 Timothy 5:4]]

The verb form of the Greek word for godliness. See [note 16²](<1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — mystery>) in [[1 Timothy-03|1 Timothy 3]].

### Footnote 4.2 — return

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v4|1 Timothy 5:4]]

Requital, recompense, offered to show gratitude to one’s parents.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — his

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v8|1 Timothy 5:8]]

I.e., his relatives. All the instructions in this chapter are very human, normal, and ordinary; nothing is special, miraculous, or supernatural, including even the matter of healing. The whole book is written in the same principle. This is necessary for the church life.

### Footnote 8.2 — faith

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v8|1 Timothy 5:8]]

See notes [19³](<1 Timothy-01.fn#Footnote 19.3 — faith>) in [[1 Timothy-01|1 Timothy 1]] and [9¹](<1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — mystery>) in [[1 Timothy-03|1 Timothy 3]].

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — Well

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v10|1 Timothy 5:10]]

See [note 7¹](<1 Timothy-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — good>) in [[1 Timothy-03|1 Timothy 3]].

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — pledge

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v12|1 Timothy 5:12]]

Lit., faith; denoting a pledge, a promise. This indicates that some promised, pledged, to devote themselves in their widowhood to some service of the church.

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — younger

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v14|1 Timothy 5:14]]

The word in [[1 Corinthians-07#v8|1 Cor. 7:8]] was the apostle’s wish in his earlier ministry. In this verse is the apostle’s advice in his later ministry, given according to his experiences concerning young widows.

### Footnote 14.2 — bear

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v14|1 Timothy 5:14]]

Childbearing and housekeeping are a rescue and safeguard to the idle busybodies ([[1 Timothy-05#v13|v. 13]]). This is God’s ordination in order to restrict and protect fallen women ([[Genesis-03#v16|Gen. 3:16]]).

## Footnotes for verse 17

### Footnote 17.1 — honor

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v17|1 Timothy 5:17]]

The noun form of honor in [[1 Timothy-05#v3|v. 3]]. According to [[1 Timothy-05#v18|v. 18]], the emphasis here is on material supply.

### Footnote 17.2 — especially

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v17|1 Timothy 5:17]]

All elders should be able to take the lead in a local church, but some, not all, have a special capacity for teaching.

### Footnote 17.3 — word

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v17|1 Timothy 5:17]]

Word here denotes the general speaking of doctrines; teaching denotes special instruction concerning particular things.

## Footnotes for verse 19

### Footnote 19.1 — receive

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v19|1 Timothy 5:19]]

Timothy was charged by the apostle Paul to receive an accusation against an elder. This indicates that after they have appointed men as elders, the apostles still have the authority to deal with the elders.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — reprove

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v20|1 Timothy 5:20]]

This too indicates the apostles’ authority over the elders.

### Footnote 20.2 — all

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v20|1 Timothy 5:20]]

Referring to the whole church. A sinning elder should receive public reproof because of his public position.

## Footnotes for verse 21

### Footnote 21.1 — angels

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v21|1 Timothy 5:21]]

The elders in a local church are God’s deputy authority. Dealing with the elders is a solemn thing before God. Hence, the apostle solemnly charged Timothy before God, Christ, and the angels to carry out such a dealing, that the chosen angels, the good angels with God’s authority, might see that His authority was established and was being maintained among His redeemed people on earth.

### Footnote 21.2 — prejudice

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v21|1 Timothy 5:21]]

Prejudgment, condemnation, formed or applied before hearing the case.

### Footnote 21.3 — partiality

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v21|1 Timothy 5:21]]

Inclination, favor, bias. Without prejudice implies that no prejudgment should be made to the credit of the accuser; nothing by way of partiality implies that no perverted favor should be shown to the accused (elder).

## Footnotes for verse 22

### Footnote 22.1 — Lay

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v22|1 Timothy 5:22]]

See [note 14⁴](<1 Timothy-04.fn#Footnote 14.4 — laying>) in [[1 Timothy-04|1 Timothy 4]]. According to the context of the previous verses, the laying on of hands here refers primarily to the laying of hands on the elders.

### Footnote 22.2 — quickly

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v22|1 Timothy 5:22]]

Or, hastily.

## Footnotes for verse 23

### Footnote 23.1 — No

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v23|1 Timothy 5:23]]

According to the context, this verse implies that the state of one’s physical health may affect his dealing with others spiritually.

### Footnote 23.2 — illnesses

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v23|1 Timothy 5:23]]

Or, weaknesses.

## Footnotes for verse 24

### Footnote 24.1 — sins

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v24|1 Timothy 5:24]]

This word indicates that [[1 Timothy-05#v24|v. 24]] is a continuation of [[1 Timothy-05#v22|v. 22]]. It explains that the sins of some people are openly manifest earlier, and the sins of others, later. Hence, one should not lay hands on people in haste.

### Footnote 24.2 — manifest

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v24|1 Timothy 5:24]]

What is mentioned here concerning the judging of sins is a principle that is applicable to both man’s judgment and God’s.

### Footnote 24.3 — they

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v24|1 Timothy 5:24]]

Referring to sins.

### Footnote 24.4 — also

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v24|1 Timothy 5:24]]

The sins of others also will be manifest, going to judgment later.

## Footnotes for verse 25

### Footnote 25.1 — good

**Verse:** [[1 Timothy-05#v25|1 Timothy 5:25]]

The implication of the apostle’s charge to Timothy in these two verses is that one should not approve a person hastily because his sins are not openly manifest, nor condemn a person in haste because his good works are not manifest.
