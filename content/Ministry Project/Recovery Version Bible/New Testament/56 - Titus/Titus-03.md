Author: Paul
---
## v1

Remind them to be [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — subject|subject]] to rulers, to authorities, to be obedient, to be ready unto every good work,

## v2

To slander no one, to be [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — uncontentious|uncontentious]], [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — forbearing|forbearing]], showing all meekness toward all men.

## v3

For we also [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — were|were]] once foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving as slaves various [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — lusts|lusts]] and pleasures, spending our lives in malice and envy, hateful, hating one another.

## v4

But when the [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — kindness|kindness]] and the love to man of our Savior God appeared,

## v5

Not out of [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — works|works]] in righteousness which we did but according to His [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — mercy|mercy]] He saved us, through the [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 5.3 — washing|washing]] of [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 5.4 — regeneration|regeneration]] and the renewing of the [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 5.5 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit,

## v6

Whom He [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — poured|poured]] out upon us richly through Jesus Christ our Savior,

## v7

[[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — In|In]] order that having been justified by [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — His|His]] grace, we might become [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 7.3 — heirs|heirs]] according to the hope of eternal life.

## v8

Faithful is [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — the|the]] word, and concerning [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — these|these]] things I desire you to [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 8.3 — confidently|confidently]] affirm, so that those who have believed in God may be careful to maintain respectable works. These things are good and profitable to men.

## v9

But [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — avoid|avoid]] foolish [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — questionings|questionings]] and [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — genealogies|genealogies]] and [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.4 — strifes|strifes]] and [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.5 — contentions|contentions]] about the [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.6 — law|law]], for they are unprofitable and [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 9.7 — vain|vain]].

## v10

A [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — factious|factious]] man, after a first and second admonition, [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — refuse|refuse]],

## v11

Knowing that such a one is [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — perverted|perverted]] and is sinning, though he is condemned by his own self.

## v12

When I send Artemas to you or Tychicus, be diligent to come to me at [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Nicopolis|Nicopolis]], for I have decided to spend the winter there.

## v13

[[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Zenas|Zenas]] the [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — lawyer|lawyer]] and Apollos send forward diligently that nothing may be lacking to them.

## v14

And let those also who are ours learn to maintain respectable works for their necessary needs, that they may not be unfruitful.

## v15

All who are with me greet you. Greet those who love us in [[Titus-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — faith|faith]]. Grace be with you all.
