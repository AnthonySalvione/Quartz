## v1

Paul, a slave of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ according to the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — faith|faith]] of God’s [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — chosen|chosen]] ones and the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — full|full]] knowledge of the truth, which is [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — according|according]] to godliness,

## v2

In the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — hope|hope]] of [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — eternal|eternal]] life, which God, who cannot lie, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — promised|promised]] [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — before|before]] the times of the ages,

## v3

But in [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — its|its]] own times He manifested it as [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — His|His]] word in the proclamation with which I was entrusted according to the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — command|command]] of our Savior God;

## v4

To Titus, genuine child according to the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — common|common]] faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.

## v5

For this cause I left you in Crete, that you might set in order the things which I have begun that remain and appoint elders in [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — every|every]] city, as I directed you:

## v6

If anyone is unreprovable, the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — husband|husband]] of one wife, having believing children not accused of dissoluteness or unruly.

## v7

For the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — overseer|overseer]] must be unreprovable as a steward of God, not self-willed, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — not|not]] quick tempered, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 7.3 — not|not]] an excessive drinker, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 7.4 — not|not]] a striker, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 7.5 — not|not]] greedy for base gain;

## v8

But [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — hospitable|hospitable]], a lover of good, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — of|of]] a sober mind, righteous, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — holy|holy]], self-controlled;

## v9

[[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Holding|Holding]] to the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — faithful|faithful]] word, which is according to the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — teaching|teaching]] of the apostles, that he may be able both to exhort by the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 9.4 — healthy|healthy]] teaching and to [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 9.5 — convict|convict]] [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 9.6 — those|those]] who oppose.

## v10

For there are many unruly men, vain talkers and deceivers, especially [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — those|those]] of the circumcision,

## v11

Whose mouths must be [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — stopped|stopped]], who overthrow whole households, teaching things [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — for|for]] the sake of base gain, which they ought not to do.

## v12

One from among [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — themselves|themselves]], a [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — prophet|prophet]] of their own, said, Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, idle [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — gluttons|gluttons]].

## v13

This testimony is true; for which cause [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — reprove|reprove]] them [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — severely|severely]] that they may be [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — healthy|healthy]] in the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 13.4 — faith|faith]],

## v14

Not paying attention to Jewish [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — myths|myths]] and the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — commandments|commandments]] of men who [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 14.3 — turn|turn]] away from the [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 14.4 — truth|truth]].

## v15

[[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — All|All]] things are pure to the pure; yet to those who are [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — defiled|defiled]] and unbelieving nothing is pure, but both their [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — mind|mind]] and their conscience are [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — defiled|defiled]].

## v16

They [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — profess|profess]] to know God, but by their works they deny Him, being abominable and disobedient and, as to every good work, [[Titus-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — disapproved|disapproved]].
