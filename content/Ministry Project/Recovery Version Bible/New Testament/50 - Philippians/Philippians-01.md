---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Paul and Timothy, slaves of Christ Jesus, to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are in [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Philippi|Philippi]], [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — with|with]] the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — overseers|overseers]] and [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — deacons|deacons]]:

## v2

[[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Grace|Grace]] to you and [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — peace|peace]] from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v3

I thank my God upon all my remembrance of you,

## v4

Always in my every petition on behalf of you all, making my petition with joy,

## v5

For your [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — fellowship|fellowship]] unto the furtherance of the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — gospel|gospel]] from the first day until now,

## v6

Being confident of this very thing, that He who has begun in you a good work will complete it until the day of Christ Jesus;

## v7

Even as it is right for me to think this concerning you all because you have me in your heart, since both in my bonds and in the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — defense|defense]] and confirmation of the gospel you are all [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — fellow|fellow]] partakers [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 7.3 — with|with]] me of grace.

## v8

For God is my witness how I long after you all in the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — inward|inward]] parts of Christ Jesus.

## v9

And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — full|full]] knowledge and all [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — discernment|discernment]],

## v10

So that you may approve by testing the things which differ and are more excellent, that you may be [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — pure|pure]] and [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — without|without]] offense unto the day of Christ,

## v11

Being filled with the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fruit|fruit]] of righteousness, which is through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.

## v12

Now I want you to know, brothers, that the things concerning me have turned out rather to the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — advancement|advancement]] of the gospel,

## v13

So that my bonds have become manifest as being [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — in|in]] Christ among the whole [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Praetorian|Praetorian]] guard and to all the rest.

## v14

And most of the brothers, being confident in the Lord because of my bonds, are more exceedingly bold to speak the word of God without fear.

## v15

[[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Some|Some]] preach Christ even because of envy and [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — strife|strife]], and [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — some|some]] also because of good will,

## v16

These out of love, knowing that I am set for the defense of the gospel.

## v17

But the others announce Christ out of [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — selfish|selfish]] ambition, not purely, thinking to raise up [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — affliction|affliction]] in my bonds.

## v18

What then? Only that in every way, whether in pretense or in truthfulness, Christ is announced; and in this I [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — rejoice|rejoice]]; yes, and I will rejoice;

## v19

For I know that for me this will turn out to [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — salvation|salvation]] through [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — your|your]] petition and the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 19.3 — bountiful|bountiful]] supply of the [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 19.4 — Spirit|Spirit]] of Jesus Christ,

## v20

According to my earnest expectation and hope that in nothing I will be put to shame, but with all boldness, as always, even now Christ will be [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — magnified|magnified]] in my body, whether through life or through death.

## v21

For to me, to [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — live|live]] is Christ and to die is [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — gain|gain]].

## v22

But if I am to live in the flesh, if this to me is fruit for my work, then I do not know what I will choose.

## v23

But I am constrained between the two, having the desire to depart and be [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — with|with]] Christ, for this is far better;

## v24

But to remain in the flesh is more necessary for [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — your|your]] sake.

## v25

And being confident of this, I know that I will remain and continue with you all for your [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — progress|progress]] and joy of [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 25.2 — the|the]] faith,

## v26

That your [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — boast|boast]] may abound in Christ Jesus in me through my coming again to you.

## v27

Only, conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, that whether coming and seeing you or being absent, I may hear of the things concerning you, that you stand firm in one spirit, with [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — one|one]] soul [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 27.2 — striving|striving]] together along with [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 27.3 — the|the]] faith of the gospel,

## v28

And in nothing being frightened by the opposers, which is to them a proof of their [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 28.1 — destruction|destruction]], but of your [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 28.2 — salvation|salvation]], and that from God;

## v29

Because to you it has been graciously granted on behalf of Christ not only to believe [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 29.1 — into|into]] Him but also to [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 29.2 — suffer|suffer]] on His behalf,

## v30

Having the same struggle which you saw in [[Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 30.1 — me|me]] and now hear to be in me.
