---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Now I make known to you, brothers, the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 1.1 — gospel|gospel]] which I announced to you, which also you received, in which also you [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 1.2 — stand|stand]],

## v2

Through which also you are [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 2.1 — being|being]] saved, if you hold fast the word which I announced to you as the gospel, unless you have believed in vain.

## v3

For I delivered to you, first of all, that which also I received, that [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Christ|Christ]] died for our sins according to the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Scriptures|Scriptures]];

## v4

And that He was buried, and that He has been raised on the third day according to the Scriptures;

## v5

And that He [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 5.1 — appeared|appeared]] to Cephas, then to the twelve;

## v6

Then He appeared to over five hundred brothers at one time, of whom the majority remain until now, but some have fallen asleep;

## v7

Then He appeared to James, then to all the apostles;

## v8

And last of all He appeared to me also, as it were to one born prematurely.

## v9

For I am the least of the apostles, who am not fit to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.

## v10

But by the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 10.1 — grace|grace]] of God I am what I am; and His grace unto me did not turn out to be in vain, but, on the contrary, I labored more abundantly than all of them, yet [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 10.2 — not|not]] I but the grace of God which is with me.

## v11

Therefore whether it is I or they, so we preach and so you have believed.

## v12

But if Christ is proclaimed that He has been raised from the dead, how is it that some among you say that there is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 12.1 — no|no]] resurrection of the dead?

## v13

But if there is no resurrection of the dead, neither has Christ been raised.

## v14

And if Christ has not been raised, then our proclamation is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 14.1 — vain|vain]]; your faith is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 14.1 — vain|vain]] also.

## v15

And also we are found to be false witnesses of God because we have testified concerning God that He raised Christ, whom He did not raise, if indeed the dead are not raised.

## v16

For if the dead are not raised, neither has Christ been raised.

## v17

And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 17.1 — futile|futile]]; you are [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 17.2 — still|still]] in your sins.

## v18

Then also those who have [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 18.1 — fallen|fallen]] asleep in Christ have [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 18.2 — perished|perished]].

## v19

If it is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 19.1 — only|only]] that we have hoped in Christ in this life, we are of all men most miserable.

## v20

[[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 20.1 — But|But]] now Christ has been raised from the dead, the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 20.2 — firstfruits|firstfruits]] of those who have fallen asleep.

## v21

For since through [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 21.1 — man|man]] came death, through [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 21.2 — man|man]] also came the resurrection of the dead.

## v22

For just as in Adam all [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 22.1 — die|die]], so also in Christ all will be [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 22.2 — made|made]] alive.

## v23

But each one in his own order: the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 23.1 — firstfruits|firstfruits]], Christ; then [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 23.2 — those|those]] who are Christ’s at His coming;

## v24

Then the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 24.1 — end|end]], [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 24.2 — when|when]] He [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 24.3 — delivers|delivers]] up the kingdom to His God and Father, [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 24.2 — when|once]] He has abolished all rule and all authority and power.

## v25

For He must [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 25.1 — reign|reign]] until God puts all His enemies under His feet.

## v26

Death, the last enemy, is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 26.1 — being|being]] abolished.

## v27

For [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 27.1 — He|He]] has subjected all things under [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 27.2 — His|His]] feet. But when [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 27.1 — He|He]] says that all things are subjected, it is evident that all things are except [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 27.1 — He|Him]] who has subjected all things to [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 27.2 — His|Him]].

## v28

And when all things have been subjected to [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Him|Him]], then the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 28.2 — Son|Son]] Himself also will be subjected to [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 28.3 — Him|Him]] who has subjected all things to [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Him|Him]], that God may be [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 28.4 — all|all]] in all.

## v29

[[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Otherwise|Otherwise]] what shall they who are [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 29.2 — baptized|baptized]] for the dead do? If the dead are actually not raised, why then are they baptized for them?

## v30

Why also do we brave danger every hour?

## v31

I protest by the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 31.1 — boasting|boasting]] in you, brothers, which I have [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 31.2 — in|in]] Christ Jesus our Lord, I [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 31.1 — boasting|die]] daily.

## v32

If [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 32.1 — after|after]] the manner of men I fought with [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 32.2 — wild|wild]] beasts at Ephesus, what does it profit me? If the dead are not raised, [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 32.3 — let|let]] us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.

## v33

Do not be deceived: [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 33.1 — Evil|Evil]] [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 33.2 — companionships|companionships]] corrupt good morals.

## v34

[[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 34.1 — Awake|Awake]] from the drunken stupor righteously and do not sin, for some of you [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 34.2 — are|are]] ignorant of God. I speak to your shame.

## v35

But someone will say, How are the dead raised? And with what kind of body do they come?

## v36

Foolish man, [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 36.1 — what|what]] you sow is not made alive unless it dies;

## v37

And what you sow, you do not sow the body that will be, but a bare grain, perhaps of wheat or of some other of the rest.

## v38

But God gives it a [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 38.1 — body|body]] even as He willed, and to each of the seeds its own [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 38.1 — body|body]].

## v39

[[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 39.1 — All|All]] flesh is not the same flesh, but one is of men, and another flesh is of cattle, and another is of birds, and another is of fish.

## v40

There are also heavenly bodies and earthly bodies; but the glory of the heavenly is one thing, and that of the earthly is another.

## v41

There is another glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for star differs from star in glory.

## v42

So also is the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption;

## v43

It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory; it is sown in weakness, it is raised in power;

## v44

It is sown a [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 44.1 — soulish|soulish]] body, it is raised a [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 44.1 — soulish|spiritual]] body. If there is a soulish body, there is also a spiritual one.

## v45

So also it is written, “The first man, Adam, became a living [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 45.1 — soul|soul]]”; the last Adam became a life-giving [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 45.1 — soul|Spirit]].

## v46

But [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 46.1 — the|the]] spiritual is not first but the soulish, then [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 46.2 — the|the]] spiritual:

## v47

The first man is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 47.1 — out|out]] of the earth, earthy; the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 47.2 — second|second]] man is [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 47.3 — out|out]] of heaven.

## v48

As [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 48.1 — the|the]] earthy is, such are [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 48.2 — they|they]] also that are earthy; and as [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 48.3 — the|the]] heavenly is, such are [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 48.4 — they|they]] also that are heavenly.

## v49

And even as we have borne the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 49.1 — image|image]] of the earthy, we will also bear the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 49.1 — image|image]] of the heavenly.

## v50

Now this I say, brothers, that [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 50.1 — flesh|flesh]] and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God, neither does corruption inherit incorruption.

## v51

Behold, I tell you a [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 51.1 — mystery|mystery]]: We will not all [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 51.2 — sleep|sleep]], but we will all be [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 51.3 — changed|changed]],

## v52

In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 52.1 — last|last]] trumpet; for the trumpet will sound, and the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 52.2 — dead|dead]] will be raised incorruptible, and [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 52.3 — we|we]] will be changed.

## v53

For [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 53.1 — this|this]] corruptible must put on incorruption, and [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 53.1 — this|this]] mortal must put on immortality.

## v54

And [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 54.1 — when|when]] this corruptible will put on incorruption and this mortal will put on immortality, then the word which is written will come to pass, “Death has been swallowed up [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 54.2 — unto|unto]] [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 54.3 — victory|victory]].”

## v55

[[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 55.1 — Where|Where]], O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?

## v56

The [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 56.1 — sting|sting]] of death is sin, and the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 56.2 — power|power]] of sin is the law.

## v57

But thanks be to God who gives us the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 57.1 — victory|victory]] through our Lord Jesus Christ.

## v58

Therefore, my beloved brothers, be [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 58.1 — steadfast|steadfast]], immovable, always abounding in the [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 58.2 — work|work]] of the Lord, knowing that your [[1 Corinthians-15.fn#Footnote 58.3 — labor|labor]] is not in vain in the Lord.
