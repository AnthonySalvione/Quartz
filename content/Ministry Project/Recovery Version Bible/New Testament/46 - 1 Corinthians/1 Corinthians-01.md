---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Paul, a [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — called|called]] apostle of Christ Jesus through the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — will|will]] of God, and [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — Sosthenes|Sosthenes]] the brother,

## v2

To the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — church|church]] of God which is [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — in|in]] Corinth, [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — to|to]] those who have been [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — sanctified|sanctified]] [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.5 — in|in]] Christ Jesus, the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.6 — called|called]] saints, [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.7 — with|with]] all those who [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.8 — call|call]] upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, who is [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 2.9 — theirs|theirs]] and ours:

## v3

Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

## v4

I thank my God always concerning you [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — based|based]] upon the grace of God which was given to you in Christ Jesus,

## v5

That in everything you were enriched in Him, in all [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — utterance|utterance]] and all knowledge,

## v6

Even as the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — testimony|testimony]] of Christ was confirmed [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — in|in]] you,

## v7

So that you do not lack in any [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — gift|gift]], eagerly awaiting the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — revelation|revelation]] of our Lord Jesus Christ,

## v8

[[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Who|Who]] will also [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 8.2 — confirm|confirm]] you until the end unreprovable in the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 8.3 — day|day]] of our Lord Jesus Christ.

## v9

[[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — God|God]] is faithful, through whom you were called [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — into|into]] the fellowship of His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

## v10

Now I beseech you, brothers, through the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — name|name]] of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — speak|speak]] the same thing and that there be no [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 10.3 — divisions|divisions]] among you, but that you be [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 10.4 — attuned|attuned]] in the same mind and in the same [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 10.5 — opinion|opinion]].

## v11

For it has been made clear to me concerning you, my brothers, by those of the household of [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Chloe|Chloe]], that there are strifes among you.

## v12

Now I mean this, that each of you says, [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — I|I]] am of Paul, and I of Apollos, and I of Cephas, and I of [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — Christ|Christ]].

## v13

Is [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Christ|Christ]] divided? Was Paul [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — crucified|crucified]] for you? Or were you baptized into the name of Paul?

## v14

I thank God that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,

## v15

That no one may say that you were baptized into my name.

## v16

And I did baptize the household of Stephanas also; beyond that I do not know if I baptized any other.

## v17

For Christ did not [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — send|send]] me to baptize but to announce the gospel, not in [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — wisdom|wisdom]] of speech that the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 17.3 — cross|cross]] of Christ may not be made void.

## v18

For the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — word|word]] of the cross is to those who are perishing foolishness, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.

## v19

For it is written, “I will destroy the wisdom of the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — wise|wise]], and the understanding of those who understand I will set aside.”

## v20

Where is the wise? Where is the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — scribe|scribe]]? Where is the disputer of this age? Has not God made the wisdom of the world foolish?

## v21

For since in the wisdom of God the world through its wisdom did not know God, God [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — was|was]] well pleased through the foolishness of the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — preaching|preaching]] to save those who believe.

## v22

For indeed Jews require [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — signs|signs]] and Greeks seek wisdom,

## v23

But we preach [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Christ|Christ]] crucified, to Jews a stumbling block, and to Gentiles foolishness,

## v24

But to [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — those|those]] who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 24.2 — power|power]] of God and the wisdom of God.

## v25

Because the foolishness of God is wiser than men, and the weakness of God is stronger than men.

## v26

For consider your calling, brothers, that there are not many wise according to flesh, not many powerful, not many [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — wellborn|wellborn]].

## v27

But God has [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — chosen|chosen]] the foolish things of the world that He might shame those who are wise, and God has chosen the weak things of the world that He might shame the things that are strong,

## v28

And the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 28.1 — lowborn|lowborn]] things of the world and the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 28.2 — despised|despised]] things God has chosen, things which [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 28.3 — are|are]] not, that He might bring to nought the [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 28.4 — things|things]] which are,

## v29

[[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 29.1 — So|So]] that no flesh may boast before God.

## v30

But [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 30.1 — of|of]] Him you are in Christ Jesus, who became wisdom to us from God: both [[1 Corinthians-01.fn#Footnote 30.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] and sanctification and redemption,

## v31

That as it is written, “He who boasts, let him boast in the Lord.”
