---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

And I, brothers, was not able to speak to you as to [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — spiritual|spiritual]] men, but as to [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — fleshy|fleshy]], as to [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 1.3 — infants|infants]] in Christ.

## v2

I [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — gave|gave]] you [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — milk|milk]] to drink, not [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — milk|solid]] food, for you were not yet able to receive it. But [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — neither|neither]] yet now are you able,

## v3

For you are still fleshly. For if there is [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — jealousy|jealousy]] and strife among you, are you not fleshly and do you not walk [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — according|according]] to the manner of man?

## v4

For when someone says, I am of Paul, and another, I of Apollos, are you not men of flesh?

## v5

What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Ministers through whom you believed, even as the Lord gave to each one of them.

## v6

I [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — planted|planted]], Apollos watered, but God caused the growth.

## v7

So then [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — neither|neither]] is he who plants anything nor he who waters, but God who causes the growth.

## v8

Now he who plants and he who waters are one, but each will receive his own [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — reward|reward]] according to his own labor.

## v9

For we are [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — God’s|God’s]] fellow workers; you are God’s [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — cultivated|cultivated]] land, God’s building.

## v10

According to the grace of God given to me, as a wise [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — master|master]] builder I have laid a foundation, and another builds upon it. But let each man [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — take|take]] heed how he builds upon it.

## v11

For [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — another|another]] foundation no one is able to lay besides that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

## v12

[[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — But|But]] if anyone builds upon the foundation [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 12.2 — gold|gold]], silver, precious stones, [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 12.3 — wood|wood]], grass, stubble,

## v13

The work of each will become manifest; for [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — the|the]] day will declare it, because it is revealed by [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — fire|fire]], and the fire itself will prove each one’s work, of what sort it is.

## v14

If anyone’s [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — work|work]] which he has built upon the foundation remains, he will receive a [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — reward|reward]];

## v15

If anyone’s [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — work|work]] is consumed, he will suffer [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — loss|loss]], but he himself will be [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 15.3 — saved|saved]], yet so as [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 15.4 — through|through]] fire.

## v16

Do you not know that you are the [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — temple|temple]] of God, and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?

## v17

If anyone [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — destroys|destroys]] the temple of God, God will [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.2 — destroy|destroy]] him; for the temple of God is [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.3 — holy|holy]], and [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 17.4 — such|such]] are you.

## v18

Let no one [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — deceive|deceive]] himself; if anyone [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.2 — thinks|thinks]] that he is wise among you in this age, let him [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.3 — become|become]] foolish that he may [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 18.4 — become|become]] wise.

## v19

For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God; for it is written, “He grasps the wise in their craftiness”;

## v20

And again, “The Lord knows the reasonings of the wise, that they are [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — vain|vain]].”

## v21

So then let no one boast in men, for all things are yours,

## v22

Whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or things present or things to come; [[1 Corinthians-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — all|all]] are yours,

## v23

But you are Christ’s, and Christ is God’s.
