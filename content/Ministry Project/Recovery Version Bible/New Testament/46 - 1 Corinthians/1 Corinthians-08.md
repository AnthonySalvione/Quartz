## v1

Now concerning [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — things|things]] sacrificed to idols, we [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.2 — know|know]] that we all have [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.3 — knowledge|knowledge]]. [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Knowledge|Knowledge]] puffs up, but [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Knowledge|love]] [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 1.5 — builds|builds]] up.

## v2

If anyone thinks that he [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — knows|knows]] anything, he has not yet come to [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — knows|know]] as he ought to [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — knows|know]];

## v3

But if anyone [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — loves|loves]] God, this one is known by Him.

## v4

Therefore concerning the eating of things sacrificed to idols, we [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — know|know]] that an idol is nothing in the world and that there is no God but one.

## v5

For even if there are so-called gods, either in heaven or on earth, even as there are many gods and many lords,

## v6

Yet to [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — us|us]] there is [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.2 — one|one]] God, the [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.3 — Father|Father]], [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.4 — out|out]] from whom are all things, and we are [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.4 — out|unto]] Him; and [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.5 — one|one]] Lord, [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.6 — Jesus|Jesus]] Christ, [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.4 — out|through]] whom are all things, and we are [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 6.4 — out|through]] Him.

## v7

But this knowledge is not in all men; but some, being accustomed to the idol until now, eat the food as an idol sacrifice, and their [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — conscience|conscience]], being weak, is [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 7.2 — defiled|defiled]].

## v8

But food will not commend us to God; neither if we do not eat are we lacking, nor if we eat do we [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 8.1 — abound|abound]].

## v9

But beware lest somehow this [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — right|right]] of yours become a stumbling block to the [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 9.2 — weak|weak]] ones.

## v10

For if anyone sees you who have knowledge reclining at table in an idol temple, will not his conscience, if he is weak, be [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — emboldened|emboldened]] to eat the things sacrificed to the idols?

## v11

For the one who is weak is being [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — destroyed|destroyed]] by your knowledge, the brother because of whom Christ died.

## v12

And sinning in this way against the brothers and [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 12.1 — wounding|wounding]] their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.

## v13

Therefore if food [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 13.1 — stumbles|stumbles]] my brother, I shall by no means eat [[1 Corinthians-08.fn#Footnote 13.2 — meat|meat]] forever, that I may not stumble my brother.
