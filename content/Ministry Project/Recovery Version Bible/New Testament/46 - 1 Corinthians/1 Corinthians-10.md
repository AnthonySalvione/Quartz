---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.1 — For|For]] I do not want you to be [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.2 — ignorant|ignorant]], brothers, that [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.3 — all|all]] our fathers were under the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 1.4 — cloud|cloud]], and all passed through the sea;

## v2

And all were [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 2.1 — baptized|baptized]] [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 2.2 — unto|unto]] Moses [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 2.3 — in|in]] the cloud and in the sea;

## v3

And all ate the same [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 3.1 — spiritual|spiritual]] food,

## v4

And all drank the same [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 4.1 — spiritual|spiritual]] drink; for they drank of a [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 4.2 — spiritual|spiritual]] rock which followed them, and the rock was Christ.

## v5

But with most of them God was not well pleased, for they were [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 5.1 — strewn|strewn]] along in the wilderness.

## v6

Now these things occurred as [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 6.1 — examples|examples]] to [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 6.2 — us|us]], that we should not be ones who lust after evil things, even as they also lusted.

## v7

Neither become [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 7.1 — idolaters|idolaters]], as some of them did; as it is written, “The people sat down to eat and drink, and stood up to play.”

## v8

Neither let us commit [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 8.1 — fornication|fornication]], as some of them committed fornication, and there [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 8.2 — fell|fell]] in one day twenty-three thousand.

## v9

Neither let us [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 9.1 — test|test]] Christ, as some of them tested Him and were destroyed by the serpents.

## v10

Neither [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 10.1 — murmur|murmur]], just as some of them murmured and perished by the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 10.2 — destroyer|destroyer]].

## v11

Now these things [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 11.1 — happened|happened]] to them as an example, and they were written for our admonition, unto whom the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 11.2 — ends|ends]] of the ages [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 11.3 — have|have]] come.

## v12

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 12.1 — So|So]] then let him who thinks he stands take heed lest he [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 12.2 — fall|fall]].

## v13

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.1 — No|No]] [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.2 — temptation|temptation]] has taken you except [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.3 — that|that]] which is common to man; and God is faithful, who will not allow that you be [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.4 — tempted|tempted]] beyond what you are able, but will, with the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 13.2 — temptation|temptation]], also make the way out, that you may be able to endure it.

## v14

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Therefore|Therefore]], my beloved, flee from [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 14.2 — idolatry|idolatry]].

## v15

I speak as to prudent men; you judge what I say.

## v16

The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fellowship|fellowship]] of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fellowship|fellowship]] of the body of Christ?

## v17

Seeing that there is [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 17.1 — one|one]] bread, we who are many are one Body; for we all [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 17.2 — partake|partake]] of the one bread.

## v18

Look at Israel according to the flesh. Are not those who eat the sacrifices those who [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 18.1 — have|have]] fellowship with the [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 18.2 — altar|altar]]?

## v19

What then am I saying? That what is sacrificed to an idol is anything? Or that an idol is anything?

## v20

No, but that the things which the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 20.1 — demons|demons]] and not to God; and I do not want you to become [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 20.2 — those|those]] who have fellowship with demons.

## v21

You cannot [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 21.1 — drink|drink]] the Lord’s cup and the demons’ cup; you cannot partake of the Lord’s table and of the demons’ table.

## v22

Or do we [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 22.1 — provoke|provoke]] the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?

## v23

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 23.1 — All|All]] things are [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 23.2 — lawful|lawful]], but not all things are [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 23.3 — profitable|profitable]]; all things are [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 23.2 — lawful|lawful]], but not all things [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 23.4 — build|build]] up.

## v24

Let no one seek his own profit, but that of the other.

## v25

Everything sold in a [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 25.1 — meat|meat]] market eat, asking no question for conscience’ sake;

## v26

For the earth is the Lord’s and the fullness thereof.

## v27

If any of the unbelievers invites you and you wish to go, eat everything that is set before you, asking no question for conscience’ sake.

## v28

But if anyone says to you, This has been offered in sacrifice, do not eat, for his sake who pointed it out and for conscience’ sake.

## v29

Conscience, I say, not your own, but the other’s. For why is my freedom judged by some other conscience?

## v30

If I [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 30.1 — partake|partake]] with thankfulness, why am I spoken evil of concerning that for which I give thanks?

## v31

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 31.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 31.2 — whether|whether]] you eat or drink, or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God.

## v32

[[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 32.1 — Do|Do]] not become a stumbling block, both to [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 32.2 — Jews|Jews]] and to Greeks and to the church of God;

## v33

Even as [[1 Corinthians-10.fn#Footnote 33.1 — I|I]] also please all men in all things, not seeking my own advantage but that of the many, that they may be saved.
