---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

If I speak in the tongues of men and of angels but do not have love, I have become [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sounding|sounding]] brass or a [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 1.1 — sounding|clanging]] cymbal.

## v2

And if I have the gift of prophecy and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so as to remove mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.

## v3

And if I dole out all my possessions to feed others, and if I [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 3.1 — deliver|deliver]] up my body [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 3.2 — that|that]] I may boast, but do not have love, I profit nothing.

## v4

[[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Love|Love]] suffers long. Love is kind; it is not jealous. Love does not brag and is not puffed up;

## v5

It does not behave unbecomingly and does not seek its own things; it is not provoked and does not take account of evil;

## v6

It does not rejoice because of [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 6.1 — unrighteousness|unrighteousness]], but rejoices with the [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 6.1 — unrighteousness|truth]];

## v7

It [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 7.1 — covers|covers]] all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.

## v8

Love [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — never|never]] falls away. But whether prophecies, they will be rendered useless; or tongues, they will cease; or knowledge, it will be rendered useless.

## v9

For we know in part, and we prophesy in part;

## v10

But [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 10.1 — when|when]] that which is [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 10.2 — complete|complete]] comes, [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 10.3 — that|that]] which is in part will be rendered useless.

## v11

[[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.1 — When|When]] I was a [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.2 — child|child]], I spoke as a child, I thought as a child, I [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.3 — reasoned|reasoned]] as a child; [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 11.4 — since|since]] I have become a man, I have done away with childish things.

## v12

For [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 12.1 — now|now]] we see [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 12.2 — in|in]] a mirror [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 12.3 — obscurely|obscurely]], but at [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 12.4 — that|that]] time face to face; now I know in part, but at [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 12.4 — that|that]] time I will fully know even as also I was fully known.

## v13

Now there abide [[1 Corinthians-13.fn#Footnote 13.1 — faith|faith]], hope, love, these three; and the greatest of these is love.
