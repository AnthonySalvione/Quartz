---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Does any one of you who has a [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — case|case]] against another dare to be judged before the [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — unrighteous|unrighteous]] and not before the saints?

## v2

Or do you not know that the saints will [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — judge|judge]] the world? And if the world is judged by you, are you unworthy of the [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — smallest|smallest]] [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 2.3 — judgments|judgments]]?

## v3

Do you not know that we will [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — judge|judge]] angels, not to mention things of this life?

## v4

If then you hold judgments over things of this life, do you seat as judges [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — those|those]] who are of no account in the church?

## v5

I say this to your shame. So there is no one wise among you, who will be able to discern between his brothers?

## v6

But brother [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — goes|goes]] to court with brother, and this before unbelievers.

## v7

Already then it is altogether a [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — defeat|defeat]] to you that you have [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 7.2 — lawsuits|lawsuits]] with one another. Why not [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 7.3 — rather|rather]] be wronged? Why not rather be defrauded?

## v8

But you wrong and defraud, and this your brothers.

## v9

Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — inherit|inherit]] the kingdom of God? Do not be led astray; neither fornicators nor idolaters nor adulterers nor effeminate nor homosexuals

## v10

Nor thieves nor the covetous, not drunkards, not revilers, not the rapacious will inherit the kingdom of God.

## v11

And these things were some of you; but you were [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — washed|washed]], but you were [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — washed|sanctified]], but you were [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — washed|justified]] in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ and in the Spirit of our God.

## v12

[[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — All|All]] things are [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — lawful|lawful]] to me, but not all things are [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.3 — profitable|profitable]]; all things are [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — lawful|lawful]] to me, but I will not be [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 12.4 — brought|brought]] under the power of anything.

## v13

[[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Foods|Foods]] are for the stomach, and the stomach for foods; but God will bring to nought both it and them. But the [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 13.2 — body|body]] is not for fornication but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.

## v14

And God has both raised up the Lord and will [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — raise|raise]] us up through His power.

## v15

Do you not know that your bodies are [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — members|members]] of Christ? Shall I then take the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Absolutely|Absolutely]] not!

## v16

Or do you not know that he who is joined to a prostitute is one body? For He says, “The two shall be one flesh.”

## v17

But he who is [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 17.1 — joined|joined]] to the Lord is [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 17.2 — one|one]] spirit.

## v18

Flee fornication. Every [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 18.1 — sin|sin]] which a man may do is outside the body, but he who commits fornication sins against his own body.

## v19

Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit within you, whom you have from God, and you are not your own?

## v20

For you have been bought with a [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 20.1 — price|price]]. So then [[1 Corinthians-06.fn#Footnote 20.2 — glorify|glorify]] God in your body.
