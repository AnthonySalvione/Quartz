---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

And I, when I came to you, brothers, came not according to [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — excellence|excellence]] of speech or of wisdom, announcing to you the [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — mystery|mystery]] of God.

## v2

For I did [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — not|not]] determine to know anything among you except [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Jesus|Jesus]] Christ, and this One crucified.

## v3

And I was with you in [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — weakness|weakness]] and in [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — fear|fear]] and in much trembling;

## v4

And my speech and my proclamation were not in [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — persuasive|persuasive]] words of wisdom but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power,

## v5

In order that your faith would not [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — stand|stand]] in the [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 5.2 — wisdom|wisdom]] of men but in the power of God.

## v6

But we do speak wisdom among those who are full-grown, yet a wisdom not of this age nor of the rulers of this age, who are being [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — brought|brought]] to nought;

## v7

But we speak [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — God’s|God’s]] wisdom in a mystery, the wisdom which has been hidden, which God predestined [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — before|before]] the ages for our [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 7.3 — glory|glory]],

## v8

Which none of the rulers of this age have known; for if they had known, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory;

## v9

But as it is written, “Things which [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — eye|eye]] has not seen and ear has not heard and which have not come up [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — in|in]] man’s heart; things which God has prepared for those who [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — love|love]] Him.”

## v10

But to us God has [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — revealed|revealed]] them through the Spirit, for the Spirit [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — searches|searches]] all things, even the [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 10.3 — depths|depths]] of God.

## v11

For who among men knows the things of man, except the [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — spirit|spirit]] of man which is in him? In the same way, the things of God also no one has known except the Spirit of God.

## v12

But we have [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — received|received]] not the spirit of the world but the Spirit which is from God, that we may know the things which have been graciously given to us by God;

## v13

Which things also we speak, not in words taught by human wisdom but in words taught by the Spirit, [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — interpreting|interpreting]] spiritual things [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — with|with]] spiritual words.

## v14

[[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — But|But]] a [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — soulish|soulish]] man does not receive the [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.3 — things|things]] of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him and he is [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.4 — not|not]] able to know them because they are [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.5 — discerned|discerned]] [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 14.6 — spiritually|spiritually]].

## v15

But the [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — spiritual|spiritual]] man discerns all things, but he himself is discerned by no one.

## v16

For who has known the mind of the Lord and will instruct Him? But we [[1 Corinthians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — have|have]] the mind of Christ.
