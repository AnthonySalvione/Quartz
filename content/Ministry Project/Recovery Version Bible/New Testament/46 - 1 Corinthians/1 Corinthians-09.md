---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Am|Am]] I not [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 1.2 — free|free]]? Am I not an [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 1.3 — apostle|apostle]]? Have I not [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 1.4 — seen|seen]] Jesus our Lord? Are you not my [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 1.5 — work|work]] in the Lord?

## v2

If to others I am not an apostle, yet surely I am to [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 2.1 — you|you]]; for you in the Lord are the [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 2.2 — seal|seal]] of my apostleship.

## v3

My defense to those who examine me is [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 3.1 — this|this]].

## v4

[[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Do|Do]] we not have a [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 4.2 — right|right]] to [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 4.3 — eat|eat]] and to drink?

## v5

Do we not have a right to take along a sister as a wife, even as the rest of the apostles and the brothers of the Lord and Cephas?

## v6

Or do only I and Barnabas not have the right not to work?

## v7

What soldier ever serves by his own wages? Who plants a vineyard and does not partake of its fruit? Or who shepherds a flock and does not partake of the milk of the flock?

## v8

Am I speaking these things according to man? Or does the law not also say these things?

## v9

For in the law of Moses it is written: “You shall not muzzle the ox [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 9.1 — while|while]] it is treading out the grain.” Is it for oxen that God cares?

## v10

Or does He say it altogether for our sake? Yes, for our sake it was written because the plowman should plow in hope, and he who threshes, in hope of partaking.

## v11

If we have sown to you the spiritual things, is it a great thing if we shall reap from you the fleshly things?

## v12

If others partake of this right over you, should not rather we? Yet we did not use this right, but we [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 12.1 — bear|bear]] all things that we may not cause any hindrance to the gospel of Christ.

## v13

Do you not know that those who labor on the [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 13.1 — sacred|sacred]] things eat the things of the sacred temple, that those who attend to the altar have their portion with the altar?

## v14

So also the Lord directed those who announce the gospel to live from the gospel.

## v15

But I myself have not used any of these things; and I have not written these things that it may be so with me; for it is good for me [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 15.1 — rather|rather]] to die [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 15.2 — than|than]] — No one shall make my boast void.

## v16

For if I preach the gospel, I have no boast, for necessity is laid upon me; for woe to me if I do not preach the gospel.

## v17

If I do this of my own will, I have a [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 17.1 — reward|reward]]; but if not of my own will, I am entrusted with a [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 17.2 — stewardship|stewardship]].

## v18

What then is my reward? That in preaching the gospel I may present the gospel without charge, so as not to use to the full my right in the gospel.

## v19

For though I am free from [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 19.1 — all|all]], I have enslaved myself to [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 19.1 — all|all]] that I might gain the more.

## v20

And to the Jews I became as a Jew in order that I might gain Jews; to those under law, as under law (though I myself am not under law), that I might gain those under law.

## v21

To those without law, as without law (though I am not [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 21.1 — without|without]] law to God but [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 21.2 — within|within]] law to Christ), that I might gain those without law.

## v22

To the weak I became weak that I might gain the weak. To all men I have [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 22.1 — become|become]] all things that I might by all means save some.

## v23

And I do all things for the sake of the gospel that I may become a [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 23.1 — fellow|fellow]] partaker of it.

## v24

Do you not know that those who [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 24.1 — run|run]] on a racecourse all run, but one receives the [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 24.2 — prize|prize]]? Run in this way, that you may [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 24.3 — lay|lay]] hold.

## v25

And everyone who contends exercises self-control in all things; they then, that they may receive a corruptible [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 25.1 — crown|crown]], but we, an incorruptible.

## v26

I therefore run in this way, not as though without a clear aim; I box in this way, not as though beating the air;

## v27

But I [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 27.1 — buffet|buffet]] my body and [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 27.2 — make|make]] it my slave, [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 27.3 — lest|lest]] perhaps having [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 27.4 — preached|preached]] to others, I myself may become [[1 Corinthians-09.fn#Footnote 27.5 — disapproved|disapproved]].
