---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

A man should [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — account|account]] us [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — in|in]] this way, as [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.3 — servants|servants]] of Christ and [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 1.4 — stewards|stewards]] of the mysteries of God.

## v2

[[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Here|Here]], furthermore, it is sought in stewards that one be found faithful.

## v3

But to me it is a very small thing that I should be [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — examined|examined]] by you or by [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — man’s|man’s]] day; rather I do not even examine myself.

## v4

For I am conscious of nothing against myself; but I am not justified in this, but He who examines me is the Lord.

## v5

So then do not [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — judge|judge]] anything [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — before|before]] the time, until the Lord comes, who will both bring to light the hidden things of darkness and make manifest the counsels of the hearts, and then there will be praise to each from God.

## v6

Now [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — these|these]] things, brothers, I have [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — transferred|transferred]] in figure to myself and Apollos for your sakes, that you may learn in us the matter of not going beyond [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — what|what]] has been written, that none of you may be puffed up on behalf of one, against the other.

## v7

For [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — who|who]] distinguishes you? And what do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as though not having received it?

## v8

[[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Already|Already]] you are filled; already you have become rich; you have reigned without us. And I would have it indeed that you did reign, that we also might reign with you.

## v9

For, I think, God has set forth us the apostles [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — last|last]] of all as [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.2 — doomed|doomed]] to death, because we have become a [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 9.3 — spectacle|spectacle]] to the world, both to angels and to men.

## v10

We are [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — fools|fools]] because of Christ, but you are prudent in Christ; we are [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 10.2 — weak|weak]], but you are strong; you are [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 10.3 — glorious|glorious]], but we are dishonored.

## v11

Until the present hour we both hunger and thirst, and are naked and buffeted and wander without a home;

## v12

And we labor, working with our own hands. Reviled we bless; persecuted we endure;

## v13

[[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Defamed|Defamed]] we [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 13.2 — exhort|exhort]]. We have become as the [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 13.3 — offscouring|offscouring]] of the world, the scum of all things, until now.

## v14

It is not to shame you that I write these things but to admonish you as my beloved children.

## v15

For though you have ten thousand [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — guides|guides]] in Christ, yet you do not have many [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 15.2 — fathers|fathers]]; for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the gospel.

## v16

I [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — exhort|exhort]] you therefore, Become imitators of me.

## v17

Because of this I have sent Timothy to you, who is my beloved and faithful child in the Lord, who will remind you of my [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — ways|ways]] which are in Christ, even as I teach [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 17.2 — everywhere|everywhere]] in every church.

## v18

Now some have become puffed up as though I were not coming to you.

## v19

But I will come to you shortly, if the Lord wills, and I will ascertain not the speech of those who are puffed up but the power.

## v20

For the [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 20.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God is not in speech but in power.

## v21

What do you want? Should I come to you with a [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 21.1 — rod|rod]] or in love and a [[1 Corinthians-04.fn#Footnote 21.2 — spirit|spirit]] of meekness?
