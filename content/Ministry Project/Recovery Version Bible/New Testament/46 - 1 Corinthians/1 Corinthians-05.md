---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

It is actually reported that there is [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — fornication|fornication]] among you, and such fornication that does not even occur among the Gentiles, that someone has his [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 1.2 — stepmother|stepmother]].

## v2

And you are puffed up? And have you not rather mourned, that the one who has done this deed might be [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 2.1 — removed|removed]] from your midst?

## v3

For I, on my part, though being absent in the body but present [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — in|in]] the spirit, have already judged, as if being present, him who has thus done this,

## v4

[[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — In|In]] the name of our Lord Jesus, when you and [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.2 — my|my]] spirit have been assembled, [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — In|with]] the power of our Lord Jesus,

## v5

[[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — To|To]] deliver such a one to Satan for the [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.2 — destruction|destruction]] of his [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.3 — flesh|flesh]], [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.4 — that|that]] his spirit may be saved in the [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 5.5 — day|day]] of the Lord.

## v6

Your [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — boasting|boasting]] is not good. Do you not know that a [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 6.2 — little|little]] [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 6.3 — leaven|leaven]] leavens the whole lump?

## v7

Purge out the old leaven that you may be a [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — new|new]] lump, even as you are unleavened; for our [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Passover|Passover]], Christ, also has been sacrificed.

## v8

So then let us keep the [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — feast|feast]], not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

## v9

I wrote to you in my letter not to [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — mingle|mingle]] with fornicators,

## v10

But not altogether with the fornicators of this world, or with the covetous and rapacious, or idolaters, since then you would have to go out of the world.

## v11

But now I have written to you not to mingle with anyone who is called a brother, if he is a [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fornicator|fornicator]] or a covetous man or an idolater or a reviler or a drunkard or a rapacious man, with such a one not even to eat.

## v12

For what have I to do with judging those who are outside the church? Do you not judge those who are within the church?

## v13

But those who are outside, God will judge. [[1 Corinthians-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Remove|Remove]] the evil man from among yourselves.
