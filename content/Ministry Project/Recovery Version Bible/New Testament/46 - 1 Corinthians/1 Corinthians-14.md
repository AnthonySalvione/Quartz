---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Pursue|Pursue]] love, and [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 1.2 — desire|desire]] earnestly spiritual gifts, but especially that you may [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 1.3 — prophesy|prophesy]].

## v2

[[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 2.1 — For|For]] he who speaks in a tongue does not speak to men but to God; for no one hears, but in his spirit he speaks mysteries.

## v3

But he who prophesies speaks [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 3.1 — building|building]] up and encouragement and consolation to men.

## v4

He who speaks in a tongue [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 4.1 — builds|builds]] up himself, but he who prophesies builds up the church.

## v5

I desire that you all speak in tongues, but especially that you would prophesy; and greater is he who prophesies than he who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets, that the church may receive building up.

## v6

But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, what will I profit you, unless I speak to you either in revelation or in knowledge or in prophecy or in teaching?

## v7

Yet even [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 7.1 — lifeless|lifeless]] things, whether flute or harp, in giving a sound, if they give [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 7.2 — no|no]] distinction in the tones, how will what is played on the flute or on the harp be known?

## v8

For also if the trumpet gives an uncertain sound, who will prepare himself for battle?

## v9

So also you, unless you give with the tongue a word easy to understand, how will what is being said be known? For you will be speaking into the air.

## v10

There are perhaps many kinds of [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 10.1 — voices|voices]] in the world, and not one is [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 10.2 — without|without]] significance.

## v11

If then I do not know the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 11.1 — meaning|meaning]] of the voice, I will be to him who is speaking a [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 11.2 — barbarian|barbarian]], and he who is speaking a [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 11.2 — barbarian|barbarian]] to me.

## v12

So also you, since you are zealous of [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 12.1 — spirits|spirits]], seek that you may [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 12.2 — excel|excel]] for the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 12.3 — building|building]] up of the church.

## v13

Therefore let him who speaks in a tongue pray that he may interpret.

## v14

For if I pray in a tongue, my [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 14.1 — spirit|spirit]] prays, but my [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 14.1 — spirit|mind]] is unfruitful.

## v15

What then? I will pray with the spirit, and I will pray [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 15.1 — also|also]] [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 15.2 — with|with]] the mind; I will sing with the spirit, and I will sing [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 15.1 — also|also]] [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 15.2 — with|with]] the mind.

## v16

Otherwise if you bless with the spirit, how shall he who fills the place of the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unlearned|unlearned]] in tongues say the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 16.2 — Amen|Amen]] at your giving of thanks, since he does not know what you are saying?

## v17

For you give thanks well, but the other is not [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 17.1 — built|built]] up.

## v18

I thank God, I speak in tongues more than all of you,

## v19

But in the church I would rather speak [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 19.1 — five|five]] words [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 19.2 — with|with]] my mind, that I might instruct others also, than ten thousand words in a tongue.

## v20

Brothers, do not be [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 20.1 — children|children]] in your [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 20.2 — understanding|understanding]], but in malice be babes and in your [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 20.2 — understanding|understanding]] be [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 20.3 — full-grown|full-grown]].

## v21

In the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 21.1 — law|law]] it is written, “[[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 21.2 — By|By]] those of other tongues and with the lips of others I will speak to this people; even so they will not listen to Me, says the Lord.”

## v22

So then tongues are for a [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 22.1 — sign|sign]], not to those who believe but to the unbelievers; but prophecy is for a sign not to the unbelievers but to those who believe.

## v23

If therefore the whole church comes together in one place, and [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 23.1 — all|all]] speak in tongues, and some unlearned in tongues or unbelievers enter, will they not say that you are [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 23.2 — insane|insane]]?

## v24

But if [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 24.1 — all|all]] prophesy and some unbeliever or unlearned person enters, he is [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 24.2 — convicted|convicted]] by all, he is examined by all;

## v25

The secrets of his heart become manifest; and so falling on his face, he will worship [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 25.1 — God|God]], declaring that indeed [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 25.1 — God|God]] is among you.

## v26

What then, brothers? Whenever you come together, each one [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 26.1 — has|has]] a psalm, has a teaching, has a revelation, has a tongue, has an interpretation. Let all things be done for [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 26.2 — building|building]] up.

## v27

If anyone speaks in a tongue, it should be by two, or at the most three, and in turn, and one should interpret;

## v28

But if there is no interpreter, he should be silent in the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 28.1 — church|church]], and speak to himself and to God.

## v29

And as to prophets, two or three should speak, and the others [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 29.1 — discern|discern]].

## v30

But if something is revealed to another sitting by, the first should be silent.

## v31

For [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 31.1 — you|you]] can all prophesy one by one that all may learn and all may be [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 31.2 — encouraged|encouraged]].

## v32

And the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 32.1 — spirits|spirits]] of prophets are subject to prophets;

## v33

For God is not a God of confusion but of [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 33.1 — peace|peace]]. [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 33.2 — As|As]] in all the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 33.3 — churches|churches]] of the saints,

## v34

The women should be silent [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 34.1 — in|in]] the churches, for they are not permitted to [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 34.2 — speak|speak]]; but they should be subject, even as the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 34.3 — law|law]] also says.

## v35

But if they desire to learn anything, they should ask their own husbands at home; for it is a shame for a woman to speak in the church.

## v36

Or was it from you that the word of God went forth? Or [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 36.1 — did|did]] it reach only to you?

## v37

If anyone thinks he is a [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 37.1 — prophet|prophet]] or spiritual, let him fully know the things which I write to you, that they are the [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 37.2 — commandment|commandment]] of the Lord.

## v38

But if anyone ignores this, let him ignore it.

## v39

So then, my brothers, [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 39.1 — desire|desire]] earnestly the prophesying, and [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 39.1 — desire|do]] not forbid the speaking in tongues.

## v40

But all things must be done [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 40.1 — becomingly|becomingly]] and in [[1 Corinthians-14.fn#Footnote 40.1 — becomingly|order]].
