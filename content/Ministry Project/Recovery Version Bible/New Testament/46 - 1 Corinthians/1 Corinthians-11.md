---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Be|Be]] imitators of [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 1.2 — me|me]], as I also am of Christ.

## v2

But I praise you because in all things you have remembered me and hold fast the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 2.1 — things|things]] that I have handed down even as I handed them down to you.

## v3

But I want you to know that [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Christ|Christ]] is the head of every [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 3.2 — man|man]], and the man is the head of the woman, and [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 3.3 — God|God]] is the head of Christ.

## v4

Every man praying or [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — prophesying|prophesying]] with his head covered [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 4.2 — disgraces|disgraces]] his head.

## v5

But every woman praying or prophesying with her head [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 5.1 — uncovered|uncovered]] disgraces her head; for it is one and the same as she who is [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 5.2 — shaved|shaved]].

## v6

For if a woman is not covered, let her hair also be cut off; but if it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut off or to be shaved, [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 6.1 — let|let]] her be covered.

## v7

For a man ought not to have his head covered, since he is [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 7.1 — God’s|God’s]] image and glory; but the woman is the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 7.2 — glory|glory]] of the man.

## v8

For man is not out of woman, but [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 8.1 — woman|woman]] out of man;

## v9

For also man was not [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 9.1 — created|created]] for the sake of the woman, but [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 9.2 — woman|woman]] for the sake of the man.

## v10

Therefore the woman ought to have a [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 10.1 — sign|sign]] of submission to authority on her head for the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 10.2 — sake|sake]] of the angels.

## v11

However neither is woman without man, nor man without woman, [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 11.1 — in|in]] the Lord.

## v12

For just as the woman is [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — out|out]] from the man, so also is the man [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — out|through]] the woman; but all things are out from God.

## v13

Judge this in your own selves: Is it fitting for a woman to pray uncovered to God?

## v14

Does not even [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 14.1 — nature|nature]] itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a dishonor to him,

## v15

But if a woman has long hair, it is a glory to her? For her long hair has been given to her for a covering.

## v16

But if anyone seems to be contentious, we do not have [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 16.1 — such|such]] a custom of being so, neither the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 16.2 — churches|churches]] of God.

## v17

[[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 17.1 — But|But]] I give you [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 17.2 — this|this]] charge and do not praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.

## v18

For first of all, when you come together [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 18.1 — in|in]] the church, I hear that divisions exist among you; and some part of it I believe.

## v19

For there must even be [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 19.1 — parties|parties]] among you, that those who are [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 19.2 — approved|approved]] may become [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 19.3 — manifest|manifest]] among you.

## v20

When therefore you come together in the same place, it is not to eat the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Lord’s|Lord’s]] supper;

## v21

For in your eating, each one takes his own [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 21.1 — supper|supper]] first, and one is hungry and the other is drunk.

## v22

Do you not have houses to eat and drink in? Or do you despise the church of God and put those to shame who have not? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you? In this I do not praise you.

## v23

For I received from the Lord that which also I delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus in the night in which He was [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 23.1 — betrayed|betrayed]] took bread,

## v24

And having given thanks, He [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 24.1 — broke|broke]] it and said, This is My body, which is given for you; this do [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 24.2 — unto|unto]] the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 24.3 — remembrance|remembrance]] of Me.

## v25

Similarly also the cup after they had dined, saying, This [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 25.1 — cup|cup]] is the new covenant established in My blood; this do, as often as you [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 25.2 — drink|drink]] it, unto the remembrance of Me.

## v26

For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 26.1 — declare|declare]] the Lord’s death [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 26.2 — until|until]] He comes.

## v27

So then whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.1 — unworthy|unworthy]] manner will [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 27.2 — be|be]] guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord.

## v28

But let a man [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 28.1 — prove|prove]] himself, and in this way let him eat of the bread and drink of the cup.

## v29

For he who eats and drinks, eats and drinks [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 29.1 — judgment|judgment]] to himself if he does not [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 29.2 — discern|discern]] [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 29.3 — the|the]] body.

## v30

Because of [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 30.1 — this|this]] many among you are [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 30.2 — weak|weak]] and sick, and a number [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 30.3 — sleep|sleep]].

## v31

But if we [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 31.1 — discerned|discerned]] ourselves, we would not be judged.

## v32

But being [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 32.1 — judged|judged]] by the Lord, we are disciplined that we may not be [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 32.2 — condemned|condemned]] with the world.

## v33

So then, my brothers, when you come together to eat, [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 33.1 — wait|wait]] for one another.

## v34

If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, that you may not come together for judgment. And the [[1 Corinthians-11.fn#Footnote 34.1 — rest|rest]] I will set in order when I come.
