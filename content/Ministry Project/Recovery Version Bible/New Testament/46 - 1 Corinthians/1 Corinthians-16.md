---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Now concerning the [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 1.1 — collection|collection]] for the saints, [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 1.2 — just|just]] as I directed the churches of Galatia, so you also do.

## v2

On the [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 2.1 — first|first]] day of the week each one of you should lay aside in store to himself whatever he may have been prospered, that no collections be made when I come.

## v3

And when I arrive, whomever you approve, I will send them with letters to carry your [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 3.1 — gift|gift]] to Jerusalem;

## v4

And if it is fitting for me also to go, they will go with me.

## v5

Now I will come to you when I pass through Macedonia, for I will pass through Macedonia.

## v6

And perhaps I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, that you may send me forward wherever I may go.

## v7

For I do not wish to see you now just in passing, for I am hoping to remain with you for some time, if the Lord permits.

## v8

But I will remain in [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Ephesus|Ephesus]] until Pentecost,

## v9

For a door is opened to me, great and efficacious, and there are many opposers.

## v10

Now if Timothy comes, see that he is with you without fear; for he is working the work of the Lord, even as I am.

## v11

Let no one therefore despise him, but send him forward in peace that he may come to me; for I am awaiting him with the brothers.

## v12

And [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 12.1 — concerning|concerning]] our brother Apollos, [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 12.2 — I|I]] urged him many times to come to you with the brothers; yet it was [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 12.2 — I|not]] at all his desire to come now, but he will come when he has opportunity.

## v13

Watch, [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 13.1 — stand|stand]] firm in the [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 13.2 — faith|faith]], be [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 13.3 — full-grown|full-grown]] men, be strong.

## v14

Let all your matters be done in [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 14.1 — love|love]].

## v15

Now I exhort you, brothers: You know the household of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and they have set themselves to minister to the saints;

## v16

You also be subject to such ones, and to everyone co-working and laboring.

## v17

Now I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because these have filled up the lack caused by your absence;

## v18

For they [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 18.1 — refreshed|refreshed]] my [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 18.2 — spirit|spirit]] and yours. Acknowledge therefore such ones.

## v19

The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you much in the Lord, with the [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 19.1 — church|church]], which is in their house.

## v20

All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

## v21

[[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 21.1 — The|The]] greeting in my own hand — Paul.

## v22

If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 22.1 — accursed|accursed]]! [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 22.2 — The|The]] Lord comes!

## v23

The [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 23.1 — grace|grace]] of the Lord Jesus be with you.

## v24

My [[1 Corinthians-16.fn#Footnote 24.1 — love|love]] in Christ Jesus be with you all.
