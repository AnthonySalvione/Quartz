---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Now concerning the [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — things|things]] of which you wrote: It is good for a man not to touch a woman.

## v2

But because of [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — fornication|fornication]], let each man have his own wife, and let each woman have her own husband.

## v3

Let the husband render to his wife that which is due, and likewise also the wife to the husband.

## v4

The wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband does; and likewise the husband also does not have authority over his own body, but the wife does.

## v5

Do not deprive each other, except by agreement for a time that you may [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — devote|devote]] yourselves to prayer, and then be together again, that [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Satan|Satan]] may not tempt you because of your [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 5.3 — lack|lack]] of self-control.

## v6

But this I say by way of [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — concession|concession]], not by way of [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — concession|command]].

## v7

Yet I [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 7.1 — wish|wish]] all men to be even as I am myself; but each has his own [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 7.2 — gift|gift]] from God, one in this way, the other in that.

## v8

But I say to the unmarried and to the widows, It is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 8.1 — good|good]] for them if they remain even as I am.

## v9

But if they do not have [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — selfcontrol|self-control]], let them marry; for it is better to marry than to burn with desire.

## v10

But to the married [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 10.1 — I|I]] charge, [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 10.2 — not|not]] I but the Lord, A wife must not be separated from her husband

## v11

(But if indeed she is separated, let her [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — remain|remain]] unmarried or be reconciled to her husband), and a husband must not leave his wife.

## v12

But to the rest [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — I|I]] say, I, not the Lord, If any brother has an unbelieving wife and she consents to dwell with him, he must not leave her;

## v13

And a wife who has an unbelieving husband, and if he consents to dwell with her, must not leave her husband.

## v14

For the unbelieving husband is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 14.1 — sanctified|sanctified]] by the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the brother; otherwise your children are unclean, but now they are holy.

## v15

But if the unbelieving one separates, let him separate; the brother or the sister is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 15.1 — not|not]] enslaved in such cases, [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 15.2 — but|but]] God has called us in peace.

## v16

[[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 16.1 — For|For]] [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 16.2 — how|how]] do you know, O wife, whether you will save your husband? Or how do you know, O husband, whether you will save your wife?

## v17

[[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 17.1 — However|However]] as the Lord has apportioned to each one, as God has called each one, so let him walk. And so I direct in all the churches.

## v18

Was anyone called having been circumcised? He need not [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 18.1 — efface|efface]] it. Has anyone been called in uncircumcision? He need not be circumcised.

## v19

[[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Circumcision|Circumcision]] is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of God’s commandments is what counts.

## v20

Each one, in the calling in which he was called, in this let him remain.

## v21

Were you called as a slave? [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Let|Let]] it not concern you; [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 21.2 — but|but]] even if you are able to become free, use your status as a slave rather.

## v22

For the slave who has been called in the Lord is the [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Lord’s|Lord’s]] freedman; likewise the free man who has been called is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Lord’s|Christ’s]] slave.

## v23

You were bought with a [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 23.1 — price|price]]; do not be [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 23.2 — slaves|slaves]] of men.

## v24

Each one, brothers, in what status he was called, in this let him remain [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 24.1 — with|with]] God.

## v25

Now concerning virgins I have no [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 25.1 — commandment|commandment]] of the Lord, but I give my opinion as one who has been shown mercy by the Lord to be faithful.

## v26

I consider then that this is good because of the [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 26.1 — present|present]] [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 26.2 — necessity|necessity]], that it is good for a man to be as he is.

## v27

Have you been bound to a wife? Do not seek a release. Have you been released from a wife? Do not seek a wife.

## v28

But even if you do marry, you have not sinned; and if the virgin marries, she has not sinned; but such ones will have affliction in the flesh, and I am trying to spare you.

## v29

But this I say, brothers, the time is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 29.1 — shortened|shortened]]. Henceforth both those who have wives should be as though they had none,

## v30

And those who weep as though they did not weep, and those who rejoice as though they did not rejoice, and those who buy as though they did not possess,

## v31

And those who use the world as though they did not abuse it; for the fashion of this world is passing away.

## v32

But I desire you to be without care. The unmarried cares for the things of the Lord, how he may please the Lord;

## v33

But he who has married cares for the things of the world, how he may please his wife,

## v34

And is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 34.1 — distracted|distracted]]. Both the unmarried woman and the virgin care for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and in spirit; but she who has married cares for the things of the world, how she may please her husband.

## v35

But this I say for your own profit, not that I may [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 35.1 — put|put]] a noose upon you but that you may be comely and may wait on the Lord without distraction.

## v36

But if anyone thinks that he is behaving unbecomingly to his virgin daughter, if she is past the bloom of youth and thus it must be so, let him do what he wishes, he does not sin; let them marry.

## v37

But he who stands firm in his heart, [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 37.1 — being|being]] under no constraint, and has authority with respect to his own will and has [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 37.2 — decided|decided]] this in his own heart, to keep his own virgin daughter, he will do well.

## v38

So then both he who gives his own virgin daughter in marriage does well, and he who does not give her in marriage will do better.

## v39

A wife is bound for so long a time as her husband lives; but should the husband fall asleep, she is free to be married to whom she wishes, only to one in the Lord.

## v40

But she is [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 40.1 — more|more]] blessed if she so remains, according to my opinion; but I think that I [[1 Corinthians-07.fn#Footnote 40.2 — also|also]] have the Spirit of God.
