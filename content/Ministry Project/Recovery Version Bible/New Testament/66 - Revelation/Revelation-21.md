---
Author: John
---
## v1

And I saw a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 1.1 — new|new]] heaven and a new earth; for the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 1.2 — first|first]] heaven and the first earth passed away, and the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 1.3 — sea|sea]] is no more.

## v2

And I saw the holy city, [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 2.1 — New|New]] Jerusalem, [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 2.2 — coming|coming]] down out of heaven from God, prepared as a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 2.3 — bride|bride]] adorned for her husband.

## v3

And I heard a loud voice out of the throne, saying, Behold, the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 3.1 — tabernacle|tabernacle]] of God is with men, and He will tabernacle with them, and they will be His peoples, and God Himself will be with them and be their God.

## v4

And He will [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 4.1 — wipe|wipe]] away every tear from their eyes; and [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 4.2 — death|death]] will be no more, nor will there be sorrow or crying or pain anymore; for the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 4.3 — former|former]] things have passed away.

## v5

And He who sits on the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And He said, Write, for these words are faithful and true.

## v6

And He said to me, [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 6.1 — They|They]] have come to pass. I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. I will give to him who thirsts from the spring of the water of life freely.

## v7

He who [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 7.1 — overcomes|overcomes]] will inherit these things, and I will be God to him, and he will be a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 7.2 — son|son]] to Me.

## v8

But the cowardly and unbelieving and abominable and murderers and fornicators and sorcerers and idolaters and all the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 8.1 — false|false]], their part will be in the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 8.2 — lake|lake]] which burns with fire and brimstone, which is the second death.

## v9

And [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 9.1 — one|one]] of the seven angels who had the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues came and spoke with me, saying, Come here; I will show you the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 9.2 — bride|bride]], the wife of the Lamb.

## v10

And he carried me away in spirit onto a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 10.1 — great|great]] and high mountain and showed me the holy city, Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God,

## v11

Having the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 11.1 — glory|glory]] of God. Her [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 11.2 — light|light]] was like a most [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 11.3 — precious|precious]] stone, like a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 11.4 — jasper|jasper]] stone, as clear as crystal.

## v12

It had a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 12.1 — great|great]] and high wall and had [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 12.2 — twelve|twelve]] gates, and at the gates twelve [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 12.3 — angels|angels]], and names inscribed, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the sons of [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 12.4 — Israel|Israel]]:

## v13

On the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 13.1 — east|east]] three gates, and on the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 13.1 — east|north]] three gates, and on the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 13.1 — east|south]] three gates, and on the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 13.1 — east|west]] three gates.

## v14

And the wall of the city had twelve [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 14.1 — foundations|foundations]], and on them the twelve names of the twelve [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 14.2 — apostles|apostles]] of the Lamb.

## v15

And he who spoke with me had a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 15.1 — golden|golden]] reed as a measure that he might measure the city and its gates and its wall.

## v16

And the city lies [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 16.1 — square|square]], and its length is as great as the breadth. And he measured the city with the reed to a length of [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 16.2 — twelve|twelve]] thousand [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 16.3 — stadia|stadia]]; the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 16.4 — length|length]] and the breadth and the height of it are equal.

## v17

And he measured its wall, a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 17.1 — hundred|hundred]] and forty-four cubits, according to the measure of a [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 17.2 — man|man]], that is, of an [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 17.2 — man|angel]].

## v18

And the building work of its wall was [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 18.1 — jasper|jasper]]; and the city was [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 18.2 — pure|pure]] [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 18.3 — gold|gold]], like [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 18.4 — clear|clear]] glass.

## v19

The [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 19.1 — foundations|foundations]] of the wall of the city were adorned with every precious stone: the first foundation was [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 19.2 — jasper|jasper]]; the second, sapphire; the third, chalcedony; the fourth, emerald;

## v20

The fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolite; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, topaz; the tenth, chrysoprase; the eleventh, jacinth; the twelfth, amethyst.

## v21

And the twelve gates were twelve [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 21.1 — pearls|pearls]]; each one of the gates was, respectively, of [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 21.2 — one|one]] pearl. And the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 21.3 — street|street]] of the city was pure gold, like [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 21.4 — transparent|transparent]] glass.

## v22

And I saw [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 22.1 — no|no]] [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 22.2 — temple|temple]] in it, for the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb are its [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 22.2 — temple|temple]].

## v23

And the city has [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 23.1 — no|no]] need of the sun or of the moon that they should shine in it, for the glory of God illumined it, and its [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 23.2 — lamp|lamp]] is the Lamb.

## v24

And the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 24.1 — nations|nations]] will walk by its [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 24.2 — light|light]]; and the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 24.3 — kings|kings]] of the earth bring their [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 24.4 — glory|glory]] into it.

## v25

And its gates shall by [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 25.1 — no|no]] means be shut by day, for there will be no [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 25.2 — night|night]] there.

## v26

And they will bring the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 26.1 — glory|glory]] and the [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 26.2 — honor|honor]] of the nations into it.

## v27

And anything common and he who makes an abomination and a lie shall by no means enter into it, but only [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 27.1 — those|those]] who are written in the Lamb’s [[Revelation-21.fn#Footnote 27.2 — book|book]] of life.
