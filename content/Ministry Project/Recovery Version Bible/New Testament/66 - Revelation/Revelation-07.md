---
Author: John
---
## v1

[[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — After|After]] this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth, holding the four [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — winds|winds]] of the earth, so that [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 1.3 — no|no]] wind would blow on the earth nor on the sea nor on any tree.

## v2

And I saw [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — another|another]] Angel ascending from the rising of the sun, having the seal of the living God; and He cried with a loud voice to the four angels to whom authority was given to harm the earth and the sea,

## v3

Saying, Do not harm the earth or the sea or the trees until we seal the slaves of our God upon their foreheads.

## v4

And I heard the number of [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 4.1 — those|those]] who were sealed, a hundred and forty-four thousand, sealed out of every tribe of the sons of Israel:

## v5

Out of the tribe of [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Judah|Judah]], twelve thousand sealed; out of the tribe of Reuben, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of Gad, twelve thousand;

## v6

Out of the tribe of Asher, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of Naphtali, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Manasseh|Manasseh]], twelve thousand;

## v7

Out of the tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand;

## v8

Out of the tribe of Zebulun, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand; out of the tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand sealed.

## v9

After these things I saw, and behold, there was a [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 9.1 — great|great]] multitude which no one could number, out of every nation and all tribes and peoples and tongues, [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 9.2 — standing|standing]] before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed in [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 9.3 — white|white]] robes and [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 9.4 — palm|palm]] branches in their hands.

## v10

And they cry with a loud voice, saying, [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Salvation|Salvation]] to our God who sits upon the throne and to the Lamb.

## v11

And all the angels stood around the throne and the elders and the four living creatures; and they fell on their faces before the throne and worshipped God,

## v12

Saying, [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Amen|Amen]]. The blessing and the glory and the wisdom and the thanks and the honor and the power and the strength be to our God forever and ever. Amen.

## v13

And one of the elders answered, saying to me, These who are clothed in the white robes, who are they, and where did they come from?

## v14

And I said to him, My lord, you know. And he said to me, These are those who come out of the [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 14.1 — great|great]] tribulation, and they have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.

## v15

Because of this [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 15.1 — they|they]] are before the throne of God and [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 15.2 — serve|serve]] Him day and night in His temple; and He who sits upon the throne [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 15.3 — will|will]] tabernacle over them.

## v16

They will not hunger anymore, neither will they thirst anymore, neither will the sun [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 16.1 — beat|beat]] upon them, nor any heat;

## v17

For the Lamb who is in the midst of the throne will [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 17.1 — shepherd|shepherd]] them and guide them to springs of [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 17.2 — waters|waters]] of life; and God will wipe away every [[Revelation-07.fn#Footnote 17.2 — waters|tear]] from their eyes.
