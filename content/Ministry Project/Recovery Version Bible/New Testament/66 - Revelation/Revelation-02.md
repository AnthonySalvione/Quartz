---
Author: John
---
## v1

[[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — To|To]] the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Ephesus|Ephesus]] write: These things says [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 1.3 — He|He]] who [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 1.4 — holds|holds]] the seven stars in His right hand, He who [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 1.4 — holds|walks]] in the midst of the seven golden lampstands:

## v2

I know your works and your labor and your endurance and that you cannot bear evil men; and you have tried those who call themselves apostles and are not, and have found them to be false;

## v3

And you have endurance and have borne all things because of My name and have not grown weary.

## v4

But I have one thing against you, that you have left your [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — first|first]] [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — love|love]].

## v5

Remember therefore where you have fallen from and repent and do the first works; but if not, I am coming to you and will [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — remove|remove]] your lampstand out of its place, unless you repent.

## v6

But this you have, that you hate the works of the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Nicolaitans|Nicolaitans]], which I also hate.

## v7

He who has an [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — ear|ear]], let him hear what the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Spirit|Spirit]] says to the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.3 — churches|churches]]. To him who [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.4 — overcomes|overcomes]], to him I will give to [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.5 — eat|eat]] of the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.6 — tree|tree]] of life, which is in the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 7.7 — Paradise|Paradise]] of God.

## v8

And to the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Smyrna|Smyrna]] write: These things says the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — First|First]] and the Last, who became dead and [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — lived|lived]] again:

## v9

I know your [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — tribulation|tribulation]] and [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — poverty|poverty]] (but you are [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — poverty|rich]]) and the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — slander|slander]] from those who call themselves Jews and are [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.4 — not|not]], but are a [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.5 — synagogue|synagogue]] of [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 9.6 — Satan|Satan]].

## v10

Do not fear the things that you are about to suffer. Behold, the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — devil|devil]] is about to cast some of you into prison that you may be tried, and you will have tribulation for [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — ten|ten]] days. Be faithful unto death, and I will give you the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 10.3 — crown|crown]] of life.

## v11

He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. He who [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — overcomes|overcomes]] shall by no means be hurt of the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — second|second]] death.

## v12

And to the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Pergamos|Pergamos]] write: These things says He who has the sharp two-edged sword:

## v13

I know where you dwell, where [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Satan’s|Satan’s]] throne is; and you hold fast My [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — name|name]] and have not denied My [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — name|faith]], even in the days of [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 13.3 — Antipas|Antipas]], My witness, My faithful one, who was killed among you, where Satan dwells.

## v14

But I have a few things against you, that you have some there who hold the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — teaching|teaching]] of [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Balaam|Balaam]], who taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the sons of Israel, to eat [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 14.3 — idol|idol]] sacrifices and to commit [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 14.3 — idol|fornication]].

## v15

In the same way you also have some who hold in like manner the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — teaching|teaching]] of the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Nicolaitans|Nicolaitans]].

## v16

Repent therefore; but if not, I am [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — coming|coming]] to you quickly, and I will make war with them with the sword of My mouth.

## v17

He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To him who [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — overcomes|overcomes]], to him I will give of the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — hidden|hidden]] manna, and to him I will give a white [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 17.3 — stone|stone]], and upon the stone a [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 17.4 — new|new]] name written, which no one knows except him who receives it.

## v18

And to the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Thyatira|Thyatira]] write: These things says the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 18.2 — Son|Son]] of God, He who has [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 18.3 — eyes|eyes]] like a flame of fire, and His feet are like shining bronze:

## v19

I know your [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — works|works]] and love and faith and service and your endurance and that your last works are more than the first.

## v20

But I have something against you, that you tolerate the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — woman|woman]] Jezebel, she who calls herself a [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 20.2 — prophetess|prophetess]] and teaches and [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 20.3 — leads|leads]] My slaves astray to commit [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 20.4 — fornication|fornication]] and to eat [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 20.4 — fornication|idol]] sacrifices.

## v21

And I gave her time that she might repent, and she is not willing to repent of her fornication.

## v22

Behold, I cast her into a [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — bed|bed]], and those who commit adultery with her, into [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 22.2 — great|great]] tribulation, unless they repent of her works;

## v23

And her children I will [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — kill|kill]] with death; and all the churches will know that I am He who searches the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — inward|inward]] parts and the hearts; and I will give to each one of you according to your works.

## v24

But I say to you — the rest in Thyatira, as many as do not have this teaching, who have not known the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 24.1 — deep|deep]] things of Satan, as they say — I put no other burden upon you;

## v25

Nevertheless what you have hold fast [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 25.1 — until|until]] I come.

## v26

And he who [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 26.1 — overcomes|overcomes]] and he who keeps [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 26.2 — My|My]] works until the end, to him I will give [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 26.3 — authority|authority]] over the nations;

## v27

And he will [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 27.1 — shepherd|shepherd]] them with an iron rod, as vessels of pottery are broken in pieces, as [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 27.2 — I|I]] also have received from My Father;

## v28

And to him I will give the [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 28.1 — morning|morning]] star.

## v29

He who has an [[Revelation-02.fn#Footnote 29.1 — ear|ear]], let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.
