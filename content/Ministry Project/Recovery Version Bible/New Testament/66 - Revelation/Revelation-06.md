---
Author: John
---
## v1

And I saw when the Lamb [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — opened|opened]] one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — living|living]] creatures saying like the sound of thunder, Come.

## v2

And I saw, and behold, a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — white|white]] horse, and [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — he|he]] who sits on it had a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 2.3 — bow|bow]]; and a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 2.4 — crown|crown]] was given to him, and he went forth [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 2.5 — conquering|conquering]] and to conquer.

## v3

And when He opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, Come.

## v4

And [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — I|I]] saw, and behold, another horse, a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — red|red]] one, went forth; and to him who sits on it, to him authority was given to [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 4.3 — take|take]] peace from the earth, and that men should slay one another; and to him a great sword was given.

## v5

And when He opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, Come. And I saw, and behold, a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — black|black]] horse; and he who sits on it had a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 5.2 — balance|balance]] in his hand.

## v6

And I heard as it were a voice in the midst of the four living creatures, saying, A [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — choenix|choenix]] of wheat for a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — denarius|denarius]] and three choenixes of barley for a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 6.2 — denarius|denarius]]; and do not harm the [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 6.3 — oil|oil]] and the wine.

## v7

And when He opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature, saying, Come.

## v8

And I saw, and behold, a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — pale|pale]] horse, and he who sits upon it, his name is Death; and [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 8.2 — Hades|Hades]] followed with him. And authority was given to them over the fourth part of the earth to kill with the sword and with famine and with [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 8.3 — death|death]] and by the [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 8.4 — beasts|beasts]] of the earth.

## v9

And when He opened the [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — fifth|fifth]] seal, I saw [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 9.2 — underneath|underneath]] the altar the souls of those who had been slain because of the word of God and because of the testimony which they had.

## v10

And they [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — cried|cried]] with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Master, holy and true, will You not judge and avenge our blood on those who dwell on the earth?

## v11

And to each of them was given a [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — white|white]] robe; and it was said to them that they should rest yet a little while, until also the number of their fellow slaves and their brothers who were about to be [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 11.2 — killed|killed]], even as they were, is completed.

## v12

And I saw when He opened the [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — sixth|sixth]] seal, and there was a great [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 12.2 — earthquake|earthquake]], and the sun became black like sackcloth made of hair, and the whole moon became like blood;

## v13

And the stars of heaven fell to the earth as a fig tree casts its unripe figs when shaken by a great wind.

## v14

And heaven was removed like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.

## v15

And the kings of the earth and the great men and the [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 15.1 — generals|generals]] and the rich and the strong and every slave and free man hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains;

## v16

And they [[Revelation-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — say|say]] to the mountains and to the rocks, Fall on us and hide us from the face of Him who sits upon the throne and from the wrath of the Lamb;

## v17

For the great day of Their wrath has come, and who is able to stand?
