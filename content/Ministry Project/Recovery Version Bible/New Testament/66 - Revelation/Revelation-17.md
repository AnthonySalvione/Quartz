---
Author: John
---
## v1

And one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, Come here; I will show you the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 1.1 — judgment|judgment]] of the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 1.2 — great|great]] [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 1.3 — harlot|harlot]] who sits upon the many waters,

## v2

With whom the kings of the earth have committed [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 2.1 — fornication|fornication]], and those who dwell on the earth have been made drunk with the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 2.2 — wine|wine]] of her fornication.

## v3

And he carried me away in [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 3.1 — spirit|spirit]] into a [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 3.2 — wilderness|wilderness]]; and I saw a woman sitting upon a [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 3.3 — scarlet|scarlet]] [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 3.4 — beast|beast]], full of names of [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 3.5 — blasphemy|blasphemy]], having [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 3.6 — seven|seven]] heads and ten horns.

## v4

And the woman was clothed in [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 4.1 — purple|purple]] and [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 4.2 — scarlet|scarlet]], and gilded with [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 4.3 — gold|gold]] and precious stone and pearls, having in her hand a [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 4.4 — golden|golden]] cup full of abominations [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 4.5 — and|and]] the unclean things of her fornication.

## v5

And on her forehead there was a name written, [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 5.1 — MYSTERY|MYSTERY]], BABYLON THE GREAT, THE [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 5.2 — MOTHER|MOTHER]] OF THE HARLOTS AND THE [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 5.3 — ABOMINATIONS|ABOMINATIONS]] OF THE EARTH.

## v6

And I saw the woman [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 6.1 — drunk|drunk]] with the blood of the saints and with the blood of the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 6.2 — witnesses|witnesses]] of Jesus. And I marveled with great marvel when I saw her.

## v7

And the angel said to me, Why did you marvel? I will tell you the mystery of the woman and of the beast who bears her, who has the seven heads and the ten horns.

## v8

The [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 8.1 — beast|beast]] that you saw was and is not and is about to come up out of the abyss and [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 8.2 — go|go]] into perdition. And those who dwell on the earth, those whose names are not written from the foundation of the world in the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 8.3 — book|book]] of life, will marvel when they see the beast, that he was and is not and will be present.

## v9

Here is the mind which has wisdom. The seven heads are the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 9.1 — seven|seven]] mountains where the woman sits

## v10

And are [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 10.1 — seven|seven]] kings: five have fallen, one is, the other has not yet come; and when he comes, he must remain only a short time.

## v11

And the beast who was and is not, he himself is [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 11.1 — also|also]] the eighth and is out of the seven and goes into perdition.

## v12

And the ten horns which you saw are [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 12.1 — ten|ten]] kings, who have not yet received a kingdom but receive authority as kings for one hour with the beast.

## v13

These have one [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 13.1 — mind|mind]], and they give their power and authority to the beast.

## v14

These will make [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 14.1 — war|war]] with the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, for He is Lord of lords and King of kings; and they who are with Him, the called and [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 14.2 — chosen|chosen]] and faithful, will also overcome them.

## v15

And he said to me, The [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 15.1 — waters|waters]] which you saw, where the harlot sits, are peoples and multitudes and nations and tongues.

## v16

And the ten horns which you saw and the beast, these will [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 16.1 — hate|hate]] the harlot and will make her desolate and naked and will eat her flesh and burn her utterly with fire.

## v17

For [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 17.1 — God|God]] has put it into their hearts to perform His [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 17.2 — mind|mind]] and to perform one mind and to give their kingdom to the beast until the words of God are accomplished.

## v18

And the [[Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 18.1 — woman|woman]] whom you saw is the great city, which has a kingdom over the kings of the earth.
