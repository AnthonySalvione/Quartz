## v1

And [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 1.1 — he|he]] showed me a [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 1.2 — river|river]] of [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 1.3 — water|water]] of life, bright as [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 1.4 — crystal|crystal]], proceeding out of the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 1.5 — throne|throne]] of God and of the Lamb in the middle of its [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 1.6 — street|street]].

## v2

And on this side and on that side of the river was the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.1 — tree|tree]] of life, producing twelve [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.2 — fruits|fruits]], yielding its fruit [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.3 — each|each]] [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.4 — month|month]]; and the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 2.5 — leaves|leaves]] of the tree are for the healing of the nations.

## v3

And there will no longer be [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 3.1 — a|a]] [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 3.2 — curse|curse]]. And the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 3.3 — throne|throne]] of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and His slaves will [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 3.4 — serve|serve]] [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 3.5 — Him|Him]];

## v4

And they will [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 4.1 — see|see]] His face, and His name will be on their foreheads.

## v5

And [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 5.1 — night|night]] will be no more; and they have no need of the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 5.2 — light|light]] of a lamp and of the light of the sun, for the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 5.3 — Lord|Lord]] God will shine upon them; and they will [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 5.4 — reign|reign]] forever and ever.

## v6

And [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 6.1 — he|he]] said to me, These words are faithful and true; and the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Lord|Lord]], the God of the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 6.3 — spirits|spirits]] of the prophets, has sent His angel to show to His slaves the things which must quickly take place.

## v7

And behold, [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 7.1 — I|I]] come quickly. Blessed is he who keeps the words of the prophecy of this scroll.

## v8

And I John am he who hears and sees these things. And when I heard and saw, I fell to worship before the feet of the angel who showed me these things.

## v9

And he said to me, Do not do that! I am your fellow slave and a fellow slave of your brothers the prophets and of those who keep the words of this scroll. Worship God.

## v10

And he said to me, Do [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 10.1 — not|not]] seal the words of the prophecy of this scroll, for the time is near.

## v11

[[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Let|Let]] him who does unrighteousness do unrighteousness still; and let him who is filthy be filthy still; and let him who is righteous do righteousness still; and let him who is holy be holy still.

## v12

Behold, [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 12.1 — I|I]] come quickly, and My [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 12.2 — reward|reward]] is with Me to render to each one as his work is.

## v13

[[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 13.1 — I|I]] am the Alpha and the Omega, the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 13.2 — First|First]] and the Last, the Beginning and the End.

## v14

[[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Blessed|Blessed]] are those who [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 14.2 — wash|wash]] their robes that [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 14.3 — they|they]] may have [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 14.4 — right|right]] to the tree of life and may enter [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 14.5 — by|by]] the gates into the city.

## v15

[[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Outside|Outside]] are the dogs and the sorcerers and the fornicators and the murderers and the idolaters and everyone who loves and makes a lie.

## v16

I Jesus have sent My angel to testify to you these things [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 16.1 — for|for]] the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 16.2 — churches|churches]]. I am the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 16.3 — Root|Root]] and the Offspring of David, the bright [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 16.4 — morning|morning]] star.

## v17

And the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] and the bride say, [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 17.2 — Come|Come]]! And let him who hears say, Come! And let him who is thirsty [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 17.3 — come|come]]; let [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 17.4 — him|him]] who wills take the water of life freely.

## v18

[[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 18.1 — I|I]] testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this scroll: If anyone adds to them, God will add to him the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 18.2 — plagues|plagues]] which are written in this scroll;

## v19

And if anyone takes away from the words of the scroll of this prophecy, God will take away his part from the [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 19.1 — tree|tree]] of life and out of the holy city, which are written in this scroll.

## v20

He who testifies these things says, Yes, [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 20.1 — I|I]] come quickly. Amen. [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 20.2 — Come|Come]], Lord Jesus!

## v21

The [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 21.1 — grace|grace]] of the Lord [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 21.2 — Jesus|Jesus]] be with all [[Revelation-22.fn#Footnote 21.3 — the|the]] saints. Amen.
