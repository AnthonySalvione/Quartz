# Revelation-18 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Revelation-18|Revelation 18]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — another

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v1|Revelation 18:1]]

This Angel with great authority is Christ. As He comes down out of heaven, the earth is illumined with His glory. In [[Revelation-10#v1|10:1]] Christ is still clothed with a cloud, and in [[Revelation-14#v14|14:14]] He is sitting on the cloud; whereas here His glory shines over the earth, indicating that His coming back to earth is nearer at hand than the coming mentioned in [[Revelation-10#v1|10:1]] and [[Revelation-14#v14|14:14]]. He will come out of heaven, first in the cloud secretly, then on the cloud openly; eventually, He will shine over the earth to destroy Babylon the Great, the city of Rome, with His great authority.

### Footnote 1.2 — coming

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v1|Revelation 18:1]]

This is Christ’s coming (parousia) reaching the earth at the end of the great tribulation for Him to take full possession of the earth as His kingdom. At this time the material Babylon the Great will suffer judgment and fall.

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — Fallen

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v2|Revelation 18:2]]

This is the fall of the material Babylon, the city of Rome, at the end of the great tribulation (see notes [8¹](<Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Babylon>) in [[Revelation-14|Revelation 14]], [19²](<Revelation-16.fn#Footnote 19.2 — Babylon>) in [[Revelation-16|Revelation 16]], and [5¹](<Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 5.1 — MYSTERY>) and [18¹](<Revelation-17.fn#Footnote 18.1 — woman>) in [[Revelation-17|Revelation 17]]). The Bible reveals three aspects of Babylon: (1) the literal aspect, referring to ancient Babel or Babylon ([[Genesis-11#v9|Gen. 11:9]]), today’s Iraq; (2) the religious aspect, referring to the Roman Catholic Church, spoken of in [[Revelation-17|Revelation 17]]; and (3) the material aspect, referring to the city of Rome, mentioned in this chapter.

### Footnote 2.2 — hold

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v2|Revelation 18:2]]

Or, prison.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — have

### Footnote 3.2 — drunk

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v3|Revelation 18:3]]

Rome, signified by Babylon the Great in a twofold way, has both religious and material aspects. The nations have drunk of the wine—and the kings of the earth have committed— refers to the religious aspect ([[Revelation-14#v8|14:8]]; [[Revelation-17#v2|17:2]]), while the merchants of the earth have become— refers to the material aspect.

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — Come

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v4|Revelation 18:4]]

Since Babylon the Great has two aspects, to come out of her is to come out of both the religious Babylon and the material Babylon. God’s people should come out of these two aspects of Babylon.

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — burned

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v8|Revelation 18:8]]

In [[Revelation-17#v16|17:16]] the religious Babylon is utterly burned. Here, the material Babylon is utterly burned. [[Revelation-17|Revelation 17]] records the destruction of the religious Babylon; [[Revelation-18|Revelation 18]], the destruction of the material Babylon.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — Cargo

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v12|Revelation 18:12]]

That the cargo listed in [[Revelation-18#v12|vv. 12-13]] consists of various material things proves that Babylon the Great in this chapter is the material Babylon. The cargo is of seven categories: (1) ornaments, from gold to pearls; (2) clothing, from fine linen to scarlet; (3) furniture and decorations, from thyine wood to marble; (4) spices, from cinnamon to frankincense; (5) food, from wine to sheep; (6) transportation, horses and chariots; and (7) employment, slaves and souls of men.

### Footnote 12.2 — thyine

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v12|Revelation 18:12]]

An aromatic and coniferous tree.

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — amomum

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v13|Revelation 18:13]]

A spice plant.

### Footnote 13.2 — slaves

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v13|Revelation 18:13]]

Lit., bodies; referring to the bodies of those who are in slavery.

### Footnote 13.3 — souls

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v13|Revelation 18:13]]

Of the cargo sold by Babylon, the first item is gold and the last is the souls of men. Souls of men refers to men who sell themselves for employment. This depicts not only the coming Babylon but also today’s world. People sell their soul, their life, i.e., themselves, to their occupation, neglecting God and their eternal destiny.

### Footnote 19.2 — one

**Verse:** [[Revelation-18#v19|Revelation 18:19]]

Antichrist and his armies will be destroyed by Christ in one hour ([[Revelation-17#v12|17:12-14]]; [[Revelation-19#v19|19:19-21]]), and the material Babylon the Great also will be destroyed by the Lord in one hour. Perhaps both of them will be destroyed by the Lord in the same hour of His coming to the earth in His shining glory ([[Revelation-18#v1|18:1]]; [[2 Thessalonians-02#v8|2 Thes. 2:8]]).
