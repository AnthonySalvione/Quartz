Author: John
---
## v1

And I saw another sign in heaven, great and wonderful, [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 1.1 — seven|seven]] angels having seven plagues, the last plagues; for in them the fury of God was finished.

## v2

And I saw as it were a [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 2.1 — glassy|glassy]] sea mingled with fire and [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 2.2 — those|those]] who come away victorious from the beast and from his image and from the number of his name [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 2.3 — standing|standing]] on the glassy sea, having harps of God.

## v3

And they sing the [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 3.1 — song|song]] of Moses, the slave of God, and the [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 3.2 — song|song]] of the Lamb, saying, Great and wonderful are Your [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 3.3 — works|works]], Lord God the Almighty! Righteous and true are Your [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 3.3 — works|ways]], O King of the [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 3.4 — nations|nations]]!

## v4

Who will not fear, O Lord, and glorify Your name? For You alone are [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 4.1 — holy|holy]]; for all the nations will come and worship before You, for Your [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 4.2 — righteous|righteous]] judgments have been manifested.

## v5

And after these things I saw, and the [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 5.1 — temple|temple]] of the Tabernacle of the [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Testimony|Testimony]] in heaven was opened,

## v6

And the seven angels who had the seven plagues came out of the temple, [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 6.1 — clothed|clothed]] in clean bright linen and girded around the breasts with golden girdles.

## v7

And one of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the fury of God, who lives forever and ever.

## v8

And the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from His power, and [[Revelation-15.fn#Footnote 8.1 — no|no]] one could enter into the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were finished.
