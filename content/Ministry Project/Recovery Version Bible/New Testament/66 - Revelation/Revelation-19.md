---
Author: John
---
## v1

After these things I heard as it were a loud voice of a great multitude in heaven, saying, [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Hallelujah|Hallelujah]]! The salvation and the glory and the power are of our God.

## v2

For true and righteous are His judgments; for He has judged the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 2.1 — great|great]] harlot who corrupted the earth with her fornication, and He avenged the blood of His slaves [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 2.2 — at|at]] her hand.

## v3

And a second time they said, Hallelujah! And her smoke goes up forever and ever.

## v4

And the twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and worshipped God, who sits upon the throne, saying, [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Amen|Amen]], Hallelujah!

## v5

And a [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 5.1 — voice|voice]] came out from the throne, saying, Praise our God, all His slaves and those who fear Him, the small and the great.

## v6

And I heard as it were the voice of a great multitude and like the sound of [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 6.1 — many|many]] waters and like the sound of [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 6.1 — many|mighty]] thunders, saying, Hallelujah! For the Lord our God the Almighty reigns.

## v7

Let us rejoice and exult, and let us give the glory to Him, for the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 7.1 — marriage|marriage]] of the Lamb has come, and His [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 7.2 — wife|wife]] has made herself ready.

## v8

And it was given to her that she should be clothed in fine linen, bright and [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 8.1 — clean|clean]]; for the fine linen is the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 8.2 — righteousnesses|righteousnesses]] of the saints.

## v9

And he said to me, Write, Blessed are they who are called to the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 9.1 — marriage|marriage]] [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 9.2 — dinner|dinner]] of the Lamb. And he said to me, These are the true words of God.

## v10

And I fell before his feet to worship him. And he said to me, Do not do this. I am your fellow slave and a fellow slave of your brothers who have the testimony of Jesus. Worship God. For the testimony of Jesus is the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 10.1 — spirit|spirit]] of the prophecy.

## v11

And I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 11.1 — He|He]] who sits on it called [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 11.2 — Faithful|Faithful]] and True, and in [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 11.3 — righteousness|righteousness]] He judges and makes war.

## v12

And His [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 12.1 — eyes|eyes]] are a flame of fire, and on His head are [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 12.2 — many|many]] diadems, and He has a name written [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 12.3 — which|which]] no one knows but Himself.

## v13

And He is clothed with a garment [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 13.1 — dipped|dipped]] in blood; and His name is called the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Word|Word]] of God.

## v14

And the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 14.1 — armies|armies]] which are in heaven followed Him on white horses, [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 14.2 — dressed|dressed]] in fine linen, white and clean.

## v15

And out of His mouth proceeds a sharp [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 15.1 — sword|sword]], that with it He might smite the nations; and He will [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 15.2 — shepherd|shepherd]] them with an iron rod; and He treads the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 15.3 — winepress|winepress]] of the fury of the wrath of God the Almighty.

## v16

And He has on His [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 16.1 — garment|garment]] and on His thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS.

## v17

And I saw an angel standing in the sun, and he cried out with a loud voice, saying to all the birds that fly in mid-heaven, Come here; gather yourselves to the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 17.1 — great|great]] dinner of God,

## v18

That you may eat the flesh of kings and the flesh of [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 18.1 — generals|generals]] and the flesh of mighty men and the flesh of horses and of those who sit on them and the flesh of all men, both free and slave and small and great.

## v19

And I saw the beast and the [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 19.1 — kings|kings]] of the earth and their [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 19.2 — armies|armies]] gathered together to make [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 19.3 — war|war]] with Him who sits on the horse and with His army.

## v20

And the beast was seized, and with him the false prophet, who in his presence had done the signs by which he deceived those who received the mark of the beast and those who worshipped his image. These two were [[Revelation-19.fn#Footnote 20.1 — cast|cast]] alive into the lake of fire, which burns with brimstone.

## v21

And the rest were killed with the sword which proceeds out of the mouth of Him who sits on the horse, and all the birds were filled with their flesh.
