---
## v1

And a great sign was seen in heaven: a [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — woman|woman]] clothed with the sun, and the moon underneath her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars;

## v2

And she was with [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — child|child]], and she [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 2.2 — cried|cried]] out, [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 2.3 — travailing|travailing]] in birth and being in pain to bring forth.

## v3

And another sign was seen in heaven; and behold, there was a great red [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — dragon|dragon]], having [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — seven|seven]] heads and ten horns, and on his heads [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 3.3 — seven|seven]] diadems.

## v4

And his tail drags away the third part of the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 4.1 — stars|stars]] of heaven, and he cast them to the earth. And the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 4.2 — dragon|dragon]] stood before the woman who was about to bring forth, so that when she brings forth he might devour her child.

## v5

And she [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 5.1 — brought|brought]] forth a son, a [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 5.2 — man-child|man-child]], who is to shepherd all the nations with an iron rod; and her child was [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 5.3 — caught|caught]] up to God and to His throne.

## v6

And the woman fled into the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 6.1 — wilderness|wilderness]], where she has a place there prepared by God so that they might nourish her there a thousand two hundred and sixty days.

## v7

And there was [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 7.1 — war|war]] in heaven: [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Michael|Michael]] and his angels went to war with the dragon. And the dragon warred and [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 7.3 — his|his]] angels.

## v8

And they did not prevail, neither was their place found any longer in heaven.

## v9

And the great dragon was [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 9.1 — cast|cast]] down, the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 9.2 — ancient|ancient]] serpent, he who is called the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 9.3 — Devil|Devil]] and [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 9.4 — Satan|Satan]], he who deceives the whole inhabited earth; he was cast to the earth, and his angels were cast down with him.

## v10

And I heard a loud voice in heaven, saying, Now has come the salvation and the power and the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 10.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of our God and the authority of His Christ, for the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 10.2 — accuser|accuser]] of our brothers has been cast down, who accuses them before our God day and night.

## v11

And [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 11.1 — they|they]] overcame him because of the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 11.2 — blood|blood]] of the Lamb and because of the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 11.3 — word|word]] of their testimony, and they [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 11.4 — loved|loved]] not their soul-life even [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 11.5 — unto|unto]] death.

## v12

Therefore be glad, O heavens and those who [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 12.1 — dwell|dwell]] in them. Woe to the earth and the sea because the devil has come down to you and has [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 12.2 — great|great]] rage, knowing that he has only a [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 12.3 — short|short]] time.

## v13

And when the dragon saw that he was cast to the earth, he [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 13.1 — persecuted|persecuted]] the woman who brought forth the man-child.

## v14

And to the woman there were given the two wings of the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 14.1 — great|great]] eagle that she might fly into the wilderness into her place, where she is nourished for [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 14.2 — a|a]] time and times and half a time from the face of the serpent.

## v15

And the serpent cast [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 15.1 — water|water]] as a river out of his mouth after the woman that he might cause her to be carried away by its current.

## v16

And the earth helped the woman, and the earth [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 16.1 — opened|opened]] its mouth and swallowed the river which the dragon cast out of his mouth.

## v17

And the dragon became angry with the woman and went away to make war with the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 17.1 — rest|rest]] of her seed, who keep the commandments of God and have the testimony of Jesus.

## v18

And [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 18.1 — he|he]] stood on the sand of the [[Revelation-12.fn#Footnote 18.2 — sea|sea]].
