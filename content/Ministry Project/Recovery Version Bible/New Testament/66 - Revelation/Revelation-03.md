---
Author: John
---
## v1

And to the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Sardis|Sardis]] write: These things says He who has the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — seven|seven]] Spirits of God and the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — seven|seven]] stars: I know your works, that you have a [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 1.3 — name|name]] that you are living, and yet you are dead.

## v2

Become watchful and establish the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — things|things]] which remain, which were about to die; for I have found none of your works [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — completed|completed]] before My God.

## v3

Remember therefore how you have received and heard, and keep it and repent. If therefore you will not watch, I will come as a [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — thief|thief]], and you shall by no means know at what hour I will come upon you.

## v4

But you have a few names in Sardis who have not [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — defiled|defiled]] their garments, and they will walk with Me in [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 4.2 — white|white]] because they are worthy.

## v5

He who [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — overcomes|overcomes]] will be [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — clothed|clothed]] thus, in white garments, and I shall by no means [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 5.3 — erase|erase]] his name out of the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 5.4 — book|book]] of life, and I will [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 5.5 — confess|confess]] his name before My Father and before His angels.

## v6

He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

## v7

And to the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Philadelphia|Philadelphia]] write: These things says the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Holy|Holy]] One, the true One, the One who has the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 7.3 — key|key]] of David, the One who opens and no one will shut, and shuts and no one opens:

## v8

I know your works; behold, I have put before you an [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — opened|opened]] door which no one can shut, because you have a [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — little|little]] power and have kept My [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 8.3 — word|word]] and have not denied My [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 8.3 — word|name]].
## v9

Behold, I will make those of the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — synagogue|synagogue]] of Satan, those who call themselves Jews and are [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — not|not]], but lie — behold, I will cause them to come and fall [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — prostrate|prostrate]] before your feet and to know that I have loved you.

## v10

Because you have kept the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — word|word]] of My endurance, I also will [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — keep|keep]] you out of the hour of trial, which is about to come on the whole inhabited earth, to try them who dwell on the earth.

## v11

I [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — come|come]] quickly; hold fast what you have that no one take your [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 11.2 — crown|crown]].

## v12

He who [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — overcomes|overcomes]], him I will make a [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 12.2 — pillar|pillar]] in the temple of My God, and he shall by no means go out anymore, and I will write upon him the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 12.3 — name|name]] of My God and the name of the city of My God, the New Jerusalem, which descends out of heaven from My God, and My new name.

## v13

He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

## v14

And to the messenger of the church in [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Laodicea|Laodicea]] write: These things says the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Amen|Amen]], the faithful and true Witness, the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 14.3 — beginning|beginning]] of the creation of God:

## v15

I know your works, that you are neither cold nor [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — hot|hot]]; I wish that you were cold or [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — hot|hot]].

## v16

So, because you are lukewarm and neither hot nor cold, I am about to [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — spew|spew]] you out of My mouth.

## v17

Because you [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — say|say]], I am wealthy and have become rich and have need of nothing, and do not know that you are [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 17.2 — wretched|wretched]] and miserable and poor and blind and naked,

## v18

I counsel you to [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — buy|buy]] from Me [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 18.2 — gold|gold]] refined by fire that you may be rich, and [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 18.3 — white|white]] garments that you may be clothed and that the shame of your nakedness may not be manifested, and [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 18.4 — eyesalve|eyesalve]] to anoint your eyes that you may see.

## v19

As many as I love I reprove and discipline; be [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 19.1 — zealous|zealous]] therefore and repent.

## v20

Behold, I stand at the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 20.1 — door|door]] and knock; if anyone hears My voice and [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 20.2 — opens|opens]] the door, then I will come in to him and [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 20.3 — dine|dine]] with him and he with Me.

## v21

He who [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 21.1 — overcomes|overcomes]], to him I will give to [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 21.2 — sit|sit]] with Me on My throne, as I also overcame and sat with My Father on His throne.

## v22

He who has an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the [[Revelation-03.fn#Footnote 22.1 — churches|churches]].
