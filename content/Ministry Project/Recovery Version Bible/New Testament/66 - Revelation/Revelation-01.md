---
## v1

The [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — revelation|revelation]] of Jesus Christ which God gave to Him to show to His slaves the things that must quickly take place; and He made it known by [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — signs|signs]], sending it by His angel to His slave John,

## v2

Who testified the word of God and the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — testimony|testimony]] of Jesus Christ, even all that he saw.

## v3

Blessed is he who reads and those who hear the words of the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — prophecy|prophecy]] and keep the things written in it, for the time is near.

## v4

John to the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — seven|seven]] churches which are in [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Asia|Asia]]: Grace to you and peace from [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 4.3 — Him|Him]] who is and who was and [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 4.4 — who|who]] is coming, and from the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 4.5 — seven|seven]] Spirits who are before His throne,

## v5

And from Jesus Christ, the faithful Witness, the Firstborn of the dead, and the Ruler of the kings of the earth. To Him who loves us and has [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — released|released]] us from our sins [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — by|by]] His blood

## v6

And made us a [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — kingdom|kingdom]], [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — priests|priests]] to His God and Father, to Him be the glory and the might forever and ever. Amen.

## v7

Behold, He [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — comes|comes]] with the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 7.2 — clouds|clouds]], and every eye will see Him, even those who pierced Him; and all the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 7.3 — tribes|tribes]] of the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 7.4 — land|land]] will [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 7.5 — mourn|mourn]] over Him. Yes. Amen.

## v8

I am the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Alpha|Alpha]] and the Omega, says the Lord God, He who is and who was and who is coming, the Almighty.

## v9

I John, your brother and fellow partaker in the tribulation and kingdom and [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — endurance|endurance]] in [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Jesus|Jesus]], was on the island called [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — Patmos|Patmos]] because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus.

## v10

I was in [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — spirit|spirit]] on the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Lord’s|Lord’s]] Day and heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet,

## v11

Saying, What you see write in a scroll and [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — send|send]] it to the seven churches: to Ephesus and to Smyrna and to Pergamos and to Thyatira and to Sardis and to Philadelphia and to Laodicea.

## v12

And I [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — turned|turned]] to see the voice that spoke with me; and when I [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — turned|turned]], I saw seven [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — golden|golden]] [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — lampstands|lampstands]],

## v13

And [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — in|in]] the midst of the lampstands One like the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Son|Son]] of Man, clothed with a [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — garment|garment]] reaching to the feet, and [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 13.4 — girded|girded]] about at the breasts with a golden girdle.

## v14

And His head and [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — hair|hair]] were as white as [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — white|white]] wool, as [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — white|snow]]; and His [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 14.3 — eyes|eyes]] were like a flame of [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 14.4 — fire|fire]];

## v15

And His [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — feet|feet]] were like shining [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — feet|bronze]], as having been [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — fired|fired]] in a furnace; and His voice was like the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 15.3 — sound|sound]] of many waters.

## v16

And He had in His right hand seven [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — stars|stars]]; and out of His [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — mouth|mouth]] proceeded a [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — mouth|sharp]] two-edged sword; and His [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — face|face]] shone as the sun shines in its power.

## v17

And when I saw Him, I fell at His feet as dead; and He placed His right hand on me, saying, Do not fear; I am the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — First|First]] and the Last

## v18

And the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — living|living]] One; and I became dead, and behold, I am living forever and ever; and I have the keys of [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 18.2 — death|death]] and of Hades.

## v19

Write therefore the things which you have seen and the things which are and the things which are about to take place after these things.

## v20

The [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — mystery|mystery]] of the seven stars which you saw upon My right hand and the seven golden lampstands: The seven stars are the [[Revelation-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — messengers|messengers]] of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.
