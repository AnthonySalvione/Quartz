Author: John
---
## v1

And when He opened the seventh seal, there was silence in [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — heaven|heaven]] for about half an hour.

## v2

And I saw the seven angels who stand before God, and [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — seven|seven]] trumpets were given to them.

## v3

And [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — another|another]] Angel came and stood at the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.2 — altar|altar]], having a [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.3 — golden|golden]] censer, and much [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.4 — incense|incense]] was given to Him to [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.5 — offer|offer]] with the prayers of all the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.6 — saints|saints]] upon the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 3.7 — golden|golden]] altar which was before the throne.

## v4

And the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — smoke|smoke]] of the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 4.2 — incense|incense]] went up with the prayers of the saints out of the hand of the Angel before God.

## v5

And the Angel took the censer and [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — filled|filled]] it with the fire of the altar and [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 5.2 — cast|cast]] it to the earth; and there were thunders and voices and lightnings and an earthquake.

## v6

And the seven angels who had the seven trumpets prepared themselves [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 6.1 — to|to]] trumpet.

## v7

And the first trumpeted, and there was hail and fire mingled with blood, and it was cast to the earth; and the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 7.1 — third|third]] part of the earth was burned up, and the third part of the trees were burned up, and all green grass was burned up.

## v8

And the second angel trumpeted, and as it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea; and the third part of the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 8.1 — sea|sea]] became blood,

## v9

And the third part of the creatures which were in the sea and had [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — life|life]] died, and the third part of the ships were destroyed.

## v10

And the third angel trumpeted, and a great star fell out of heaven, burning like a torch, and it fell upon the third part of the rivers and upon the springs of waters.

## v11

And the name of the star is called [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Wormwood|Wormwood]]; and the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 11.2 — third|third]] part of the waters became wormwood, and many of the men died from the waters because they were made bitter.

## v12

And the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 12.1 — fourth|fourth]] angel trumpeted, and the [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 12.2 — third|third]] part of the sun and the third part of the moon and the third part of the stars were smitten, so that the third part of them would be darkened and the day would not appear for the third part of it, and the night likewise.

## v13

And I saw, and I heard an eagle flying in mid-heaven, saying with a loud voice, [[Revelation-08.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Woe|Woe]], woe, woe, to those who dwell on the earth because of the remaining trumpet sounds of the three angels who are about to trumpet!
