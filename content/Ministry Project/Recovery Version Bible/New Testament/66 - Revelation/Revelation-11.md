## v1

And there was given to me a [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — reed|reed]] like a [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 1.1 — reed|rod]], and someone said, Rise and [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 1.2 — measure|measure]] the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 1.3 — temple|temple]] of God and the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 1.4 — altar|altar]] and those who worship in it.

## v2

And the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 2.1 — court|court]] which is outside the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 2.2 — temple|temple]] cast out and do not measure it, because it has been given to the Gentiles, and they will trample the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 2.3 — holy|holy]] city for [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 2.4 — forty-two|forty-two]] months.

## v3

And I will [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 3.1 — cause|cause]] My [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 3.2 — two|two]] witnesses to prophesy a [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 3.3 — thousand|thousand]] two hundred and sixty days, clothed in [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 3.4 — sackcloth|sackcloth]].

## v4

These are the two [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 4.1 — olive|olive]] trees and the two [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 4.2 — lampstands|lampstands]] which stand before the Lord of the earth.

## v5

And if anyone desires to harm them, fire proceeds out of their mouth and devours their enemies. And if anyone desires to harm them, thus must he be killed.

## v6

These have the authority to shut heaven that no rain may fall during the days of their prophecy; and they have authority over the waters to turn them into blood and to smite the earth with every plague as often as they desire.

## v7

And when they have completed their [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 7.1 — testimony|testimony]], the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 7.2 — beast|beast]] who comes up out of the abyss will make war with them and will overcome them and kill them.

## v8

And their corpses will be on the street of the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 8.1 — great|great]] city, which spiritually is called [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 8.2 — Sodom|Sodom]] and Egypt, where also their Lord was crucified.

## v9

And those of the peoples and tribes and tongues and nations see their corpses for three and a half days, and they do not allow their corpses to be placed in a tomb.

## v10

And those who dwell on the earth rejoice over them and make merry; and they will send gifts to one another because these two prophets tormented those who dwell on the earth.

## v11

And after the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 11.1 — three|three]] and a half days, the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 11.2 — breath|breath]] of life out of God entered into them, and they [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 11.3 — stood|stood]] on their feet; and great fear fell upon those beholding them.

## v12

And they heard a loud voice out of heaven saying to them, Come up here. And they [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — went|went]] up into heaven in the cloud, and their enemies beheld them.

## v13

And in that hour there was a great earthquake, and the tenth part of the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 13.1 — city|city]] fell; and seven thousand [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 13.2 — men|men]] were killed in the earthquake, and the rest became terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.

## v14

The second woe has passed; behold, the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 14.1 — third|third]] woe is coming quickly.

## v15

And the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 15.1 — seventh|seventh]] angel [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 15.2 — trumpeted|trumpeted]]; and there were loud voices in heaven, saying, The [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 15.3 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of His Christ, and He will [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 15.4 — reign|reign]] forever and ever.

## v16

And the twenty-four elders who sit before God on their thrones fell on their faces and worshipped God,

## v17

Saying, We thank You, Lord God the Almighty, He who is and who was, because You have taken Your great power and have reigned.

## v18

And the nations became angry, and Your [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 18.1 — wrath|wrath]] came, and the time came for the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 18.2 — dead|dead]] to be judged, and the time to give the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 18.3 — reward|reward]] to Your slaves the prophets and to the saints and to those who [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 18.4 — fear|fear]] Your name, to the small and to the great, and to destroy [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 18.5 — those|those]] who destroy the earth.

## v19

And the [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 19.1 — temple|temple]] of God which is in heaven was opened, and the Ark of His Covenant was seen in His [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 19.1 — temple|temple]]; and there were lightnings and voices and thunders and an [[Revelation-11.fn#Footnote 19.2 — earthquake|earthquake]] and great hail.
