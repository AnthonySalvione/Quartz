---
Author: John
---
## v1

And I saw an angel coming down out of heaven, having the key of the abyss and a great chain in his hand.

## v2

And he laid hold of the dragon, the ancient serpent, who is the Devil and Satan, and [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 2.1 — bound|bound]] him for a thousand years

## v3

And cast him into the abyss and shut it and sealed it over him, that he might not deceive the nations any longer until the thousand years were completed; after these things he must be loosed for a little while.

## v4

And I saw thrones, and [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 4.1 — they|they]] sat upon them, and judgment was given to them. And I saw the souls of [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 4.2 — those|those]] who had been beheaded because of the testimony of Jesus and because of the word of God, and of [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 4.3 — those|those]] who had not worshipped the beast nor his image, and had not received the mark on their forehead and on their hand; and they [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 4.4 — lived|lived]] and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.

## v5

The [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 5.1 — rest|rest]] of the dead did not live again until the thousand years were completed. This is the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 5.2 — first|first]] resurrection.

## v6

[[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 6.1 — Blessed|Blessed]] and holy is he who has part in the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 6.2 — first|first]] resurrection; over these the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 6.3 — second|second]] death has no authority, but they will be [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 6.4 — priests|priests]] of God and of Christ and will reign with Him for [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 6.5 — a|a]] thousand years.

## v7

And when the thousand years are completed, Satan will be released from his [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 7.1 — prison|prison]]

## v8

And will go out to deceive the nations which are in the four corners of the earth, [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Gog|Gog]] and Magog, to gather them together for the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 8.2 — war|war]]. Their number is like that of the sand of the sea.

## v9

And they went up upon the breadth of the earth and surrounded the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 9.1 — camp|camp]] of the saints and the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 9.1 — camp|beloved]] city; and fire came down [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 9.2 — out|out]] of heaven and devoured them.

## v10

And the devil, who deceived them, was cast into the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 10.1 — lake|lake]] of fire and brimstone, where also the beast and the false prophet were; and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.

## v11

And I saw a great [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 11.1 — white|white]] throne and [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 11.2 — Him|Him]] who sat upon it, from whose face [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 11.3 — earth|earth]] and heaven fled away, and no place was found for them.

## v12

And I saw the dead, the great and the small, [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 12.1 — standing|standing]] before the throne, and [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 12.2 — scrolls|scrolls]] were opened; and another [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 12.3 — scroll|scroll]] was opened, which is the book of life. And the dead were judged by the things which were written in the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 12.2 — scrolls|scrolls]], according to their works.

## v13

And the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 13.1 — sea|sea]] gave up the dead which were in it, and death and Hades gave up the dead which were in them; and they were judged, each of them, according to their works.

## v14

And [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 14.1 — death|death]] and Hades were cast into the lake of fire. This is the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 14.2 — second|second]] death, the lake of fire.

## v15

And if [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 15.1 — anyone|anyone]] was not found written in the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 15.2 — book|book]] of life, he was cast into the [[Revelation-20.fn#Footnote 15.3 — lake|lake]] of fire.
