---
Author: John
---
## v1

And I saw a [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 1.1 — beast|beast]] coming up out of the [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 1.2 — sea|sea]], having [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 1.3 — ten|ten]] horns and seven heads, and on his horns ten diadems, and on his heads names of [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 1.4 — blasphemy|blasphemy]].

## v2

And the beast which I saw was [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 2.1 — like|like]] a leopard, and his feet like those of a bear, and his mouth like the mouth of a lion. And the dragon gave him his [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 2.2 — power|power]] and his throne and great authority.

## v3

And [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 3.1 — one|one]] of his heads was as it were slain to death, and his death stroke was healed. And the whole earth marveled after the beast.

## v4

And they worshipped the dragon because he gave his authority to the beast; and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like the beast? And who can make war with him?

## v5

And there was given to him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies, and authority was given to him to act for [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 5.1 — forty-two|forty-two]] months.

## v6

And he opened his mouth for blasphemies against God, to blaspheme His name and His tabernacle, which tabernacle in heaven.

## v7

And permission was given to him to make war with the [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 7.1 — saints|saints]] and to overcome them; and authority was given to him over every tribe and people and tongue and nation.

## v8

And all those dwelling on the earth will worship him, everyone whose name is not [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — written|written]] in the [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 8.2 — book|book]] of life of the Lamb who was slain from the foundation of the world.

## v9

If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

## v10

If anyone is for captivity, into captivity he goes; if anyone kills with the sword, with the sword he is to be killed. Here is the endurance and the faith of the [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 10.1 — saints|saints]].

## v11

And I saw [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 11.1 — another|another]] beast coming up out of the [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 11.2 — earth|earth]], and he had two horns [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 11.3 — like|like]] a lamb, and he spoke like a dragon.

## v12

And he exercises all the authority of the first beast in his sight, and he causes the earth and those who dwell in it to worship the first beast, whose death stroke was healed.

## v13

And he does great [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 13.1 — signs|signs]], so that he even makes fire come down out of heaven to the earth before men.

## v14

And he deceives those who dwell on the earth on account of the signs which he was given power to do before the beast, [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 14.1 — commanding|commanding]] those who dwell on the earth to make an image to the beast, who had the stroke of the sword and revived.

## v15

And power was given to him to give [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 15.1 — breath|breath]] to the image of the beast that the image of the beast might even [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 15.2 — speak|speak]] and cause whoever would not worship the image of the beast to be killed.

## v16

And he causes all, the small and the great, and the rich and the poor, and the freemen and the slaves, to be given a mark on their right hand or on their forehead,

## v17

And that no one may be able to buy or sell except him who has the mark, that is, the name of the beast or the number of his name.

## v18

Here is wisdom. Let him who has [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 18.1 — understanding|understanding]] calculate the [[Revelation-13.fn#Footnote 18.2 — number|number]] of the beast, for it is the number of a man; and his number is six hundred sixty-six.
