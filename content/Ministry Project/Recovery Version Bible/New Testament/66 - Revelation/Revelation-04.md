## v1

After these things I saw, and behold, a door [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — opened|opened]] in heaven, and the first [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — voice|voice]] which I heard was like a trumpet speaking with me, saying, Come up here, and I will show you the things that must take place after these things.

## v2

Immediately I was in [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — spirit|spirit]]; and behold, there was a [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 2.2 — throne|throne]] set in heaven, and upon the throne there was One sitting;

## v3

And He who was sitting was like a [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — jasper|jasper]] stone and a [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — sardius|sardius]] in appearance, and there was a [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 3.3 — rainbow|rainbow]] around the throne like an [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 3.4 — emerald|emerald]] in appearance.

## v4

And around the throne there were [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — twenty-four|twenty-four]] thrones, and upon the thrones [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — twenty-four|twenty-four]] [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — elders|elders]] sitting, clothed in [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — white|white]] garments, and upon their heads golden crowns.

## v5

And out of the throne come forth lightnings and voices and thunders. And there were [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — seven|seven]] lamps of fire burning before the throne, which are the  [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — seven|seven]] Spirits of God;

## v6

And before the throne there was as it were a [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — glassy|glassy]] sea like crystal; and in the midst of the throne and around the throne, there were [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — four|four]] living creatures [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — full|full]] of eyes in front and behind.

## v7

And the first living creature was [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — like|like]] a lion, and the second living creature like a calf, and the third living creature had a face like that of a man, and the fourth living creature was like a flying eagle.

## v8

And the four living creatures, each one of them having six wings apiece, are full of eyes around and within; and they have no rest day and night, saying, [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Holy|Holy]], holy, holy, Lord God the Almighty, [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 8.2 — who|who]] was and who is and who is coming.

## v9

And when the four living creatures give [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — glory|glory]] and honor and thanks to Him who sits upon the throne, to Him who lives forever and ever,

## v10

The twenty-four elders will fall before Him who sits upon the throne and worship Him who lives forever and ever; and they will [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — cast|cast]] their crowns before the throne, saying,

## v11

You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive the [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — glory|glory]] and the honor and the power, for You have created all things, and because of Your [[Revelation-04.fn#Footnote 11.2 — will|will]] they were, and were created.
