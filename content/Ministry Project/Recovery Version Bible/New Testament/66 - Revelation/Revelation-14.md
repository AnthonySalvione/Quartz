---
Author: John
---
## v1

And I saw, and behold, the Lamb standing on Mount [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Zion|Zion]], and with Him a [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 1.2 — hundred|hundred]] and forty-four thousand, having [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 1.3 — His|His]] name and the name of His Father written on their foreheads.

## v2

And I heard a voice out of heaven like the sound of [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 2.1 — many|many]] waters and like the sound of [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 2.2 — loud|loud]] thunder; and the voice which I heard was like the sound of [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 2.3 — harp-singers|harp-singers]] playing on their harps.

## v3

And they sing a new song before the throne and before the four living creatures and the elders; and [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 3.1 — no|no]] one could learn the song except the hundred and forty-four thousand, who have been purchased [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 3.2 — from|from]] the earth.

## v4

These are they who have not been defiled with women, for they are [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 4.1 — virgins|virgins]]. These are they who follow the Lamb wherever He may go. These were purchased from among men as [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 4.2 — firstfruits|firstfruits]] to God and to the Lamb.

## v5

And in their mouth [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 5.1 — no|no]] lie was found; they are [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 5.2 — without|without]] blemish.

## v6

And I saw another angel flying in mid-heaven, having an [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 6.1 — eternal|eternal]] gospel to announce to those [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 6.2 — dwelling|dwelling]] on the earth, even to every nation and tribe and tongue and people,

## v7

Saying with a loud voice, [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Fear|Fear]] God and give Him glory because the hour of His [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 7.2 — judgment|judgment]] has come; and [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 7.3 — worship|worship]] Him who has made heaven and earth and the sea and the springs of waters.

## v8

And another angel, a second one, followed, saying, Fallen, fallen is [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Babylon|Babylon]] the Great, who has made all the nations drink of the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 8.2 — wine|wine]] of the fury of her fornication!

## v9

And another angel, a third one, followed them, saying with a loud voice, If anyone worships the beast and his image and receives a mark on his forehead or on his hand,

## v10

He also shall drink of the wine of the fury of God, which is mixed undiluted in the cup of His wrath; and he shall be tormented in [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 10.1 — fire|fire]] and brimstone before the holy angels and before the Lamb.

## v11

And the smoke of their tormenting goes up forever and ever; and they have no rest day and night, those who worship the beast and his image, and whoever receives the mark of his name.

## v12

Here is the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 12.1 — endurance|endurance]] of the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 12.2 — saints|saints]], those who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus.

## v13

And I heard a voice out of heaven, saying, Write, Blessed are the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 13.1 — dead|dead]] who die in the Lord from now on. Yes, says [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 13.2 — the|the]] Spirit, that they may rest from their labors; for their works follow with them.

## v14

And I saw, and behold, there was a white cloud, and [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 14.1 — on|on]] the cloud One like the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Son|Son]] of Man sitting, having a [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 14.3 — golden|golden]] crown on His head and a [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 14.4 — sharp|sharp]] sickle in His hand.

## v15

And another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to Him who sat on the cloud, Send forth Your sickle and reap, for the hour to reap has come because the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 15.1 — harvest|harvest]] of the earth is [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 15.2 — ripe|ripe]].

## v16

And He who sat on the cloud thrust His [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 16.1 — sickle|sickle]] upon the earth, and the earth was [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 16.2 — reaped|reaped]].

## v17

And another angel came out of the temple which is in heaven, he also having a sharp sickle.

## v18

And another angel came out of the altar, he who has authority over fire, and he cried with a loud voice to [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 18.1 — him|him]] who had the sharp sickle, saying, Send forth your sharp sickle and gather the clusters of the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 18.2 — vine|vine]] of the earth, for her grapes are fully ripened.

## v19

And the angel thrust his sickle into the earth and gathered the vine of the earth and cast it into the great [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 19.1 — winepress|winepress]] of the fury of God.

## v20

And the winepress was trodden outside the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 20.1 — city|city]], and blood came out of the winepress up to the bridles of the [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 20.2 — horses|horses]] for a [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 20.3 — thousand|thousand]] six hundred [[Revelation-14.fn#Footnote 20.4 — stadia|stadia]].
