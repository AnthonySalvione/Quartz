---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And He called His [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 1.1 — twelve|twelve]] disciples to Him and gave them [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 1.2 — authority|authority]] over unclean spirits, so that they would cast them out and heal every disease and every sickness.

## v2

And the names of the twelve [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 2.1 — apostles|apostles]] are these: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; and James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother;

## v3

Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Matthew|Matthew]] the [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 3.2 — tax|tax]] collector; James the son of Alphaeus, and [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 3.3 — Thaddaeus|Thaddaeus]];

## v4

Simon the [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Cananaean|Cananaean]], and Judas [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Iscariot|Iscariot]], who also [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 4.3 — betrayed|betrayed]] Him.

## v5

These twelve Jesus sent forth, charging them, saying, Do not go into the way of the [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Gentiles|Gentiles]], and do not enter into any city of the [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Gentiles|Samaritans]].

## v6

But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

## v7

And as you go, proclaim, saying, The kingdom of the heavens has [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 7.1 — drawn|drawn]] near.

## v8

Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers, cast out demons. Freely you have received; freely give.

## v9

Do [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 9.1 — not|not]] acquire for yourselves gold nor silver nor copper for your belts;

## v10

No bag for the journey nor two tunics nor sandals nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food.

## v11

And into whatever city or village you enter, find out who in it is worthy; and there remain until you depart.

## v12

And as you enter into the house, greet it.

## v13

And if the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.

## v14

And whoever does not receive you nor hear your words, as you go out of that house or city, shake off the dust from your feet.

## v15

Truly I say to you, It will be [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 15.1 — more|more]] tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah in the day of judgment than for that city.

## v16

Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves. Be therefore [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 16.1 — prudent|prudent]] as serpents and guileless as doves.

## v17

And beware of men, for they will [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 17.1 — deliver|deliver]] you up to [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 17.2 — sanhedrins|sanhedrins]], and in their synagogues they will scourge you.

## v18

And you will also be brought before [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 18.1 — governors|governors]] and kings for My sake, for a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.

## v19

But when they deliver you up, do not be anxious about how or what you should speak, for it will be given to you in that hour what you should speak;

## v20

For you are not the ones speaking, but the [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of your Father is the One speaking in you.

## v21

And [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 21.1 — brother|brother]] will deliver up brother to death, and father his child; and children will rise up against their parents and put them to death.

## v22

And you will be hated by all because of My name. But he who has endured to the end, this one shall be [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 22.1 — saved|saved]].

## v23

And when they persecute you in this city, flee into another. For truly I say to you, You shall by no means [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 23.1 — finish|finish]] the cities of Israel until the Son of Man comes.

## v24

A disciple is not [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 24.1 — above|above]] the teacher, nor a slave above his master.

## v25

It is sufficient for the disciple that he become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they have called the Master of the house [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 25.1 — Beelzebul|Beelzebul]], how much more those of His household!

## v26

Therefore do not fear them; for there is nothing covered which will not be revealed, and hidden which will not be made known.

## v27

What I say to you in the darkness, speak in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim on the housetops.

## v28

And do not fear those who kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul; but rather fear [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Him|Him]] who is able to destroy both soul and body in [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 28.2 — Gehenna|Gehenna]].

## v29

Are not two sparrows sold for an [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 29.1 — assarion|assarion]]? And not one of them will fall to the earth apart from your Father.

## v30

But even the hairs of your head are all numbered.

## v31

Therefore do not fear; you are of more value than many sparrows.

## v32

Everyone therefore who will confess [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 32.1 — in|in]] Me before men, I also will confess [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 32.2 — in|in]] him before My Father who is in the heavens;

## v33

But whoever will [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 33.1 — deny|deny]] Me before men, I also will [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 33.1 — deny|deny]] him before My Father who is in the heavens.

## v34

Do not think that I have [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 34.1 — come|come]] to bring peace on the earth; I have not come to bring peace, but a sword.

## v35

For I have come to set men at variance: a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

## v36

And a man’s enemies will be those of his [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 36.1 — household|household]].

## v37

He who loves father or mother [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 37.1 — above|above]] Me is not worthy of Me; and he who loves son or daughter above Me is not worthy of Me;

## v38

And he who does not [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 38.1 — take|take]] his cross and follow after Me is not worthy of Me.

## v39

He who [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 39.1 — finds|finds]] his soul-life shall [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 39.1 — finds|lose]] it, and he who loses his soul-life for My sake shall find it.

## v40

He who [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 40.1 — receives|receives]] you receives Me, and he who receives Me receives Him who has sent Me.

## v41

He who [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 41.1 — receives|receives]] a [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 41.2 — prophet|prophet]] in the name of a prophet shall receive a prophet’s reward, and he who [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 41.1 — receives|receives]] a [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 41.2 — prophet|righteous]] man in the name of a righteous man shall receive a righteous man’s reward.

## v42

And whoever gives to one of these little ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, truly I say to you, he shall by no means lose his [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 42.1 — reward|reward]].
