---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And when Jesus finished these words, He departed from Galilee and came into the borders of Judea beyond the Jordan.

## v2

And great crowds followed Him, and He healed them there.

## v3

And some Pharisees came to Him, [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 3.1 — testing|testing]] Him and saying, Is it lawful for a man to [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 3.2 — divorce|divorce]] his wife for any cause?

## v4

And He answered and said, Have you not read that [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 4.1 — He|He]] who created them from the beginning made them male and female,

## v5

And said, “For this cause shall a man leave his father and his mother and shall be joined to his wife; and the two shall be one flesh”?

## v6

So then they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has yoked together, let man not separate.

## v7

They said to Him, Why then did Moses [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 7.1 — command|command]] us to give her a certificate of divorce and divorce her?

## v8

He said to them, Moses, because of your hardness of heart, allowed you to divorce your wives, but from the [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 8.1 — beginning|beginning]] it has not been so.

## v9

But I say to you that whoever divorces his wife, except for [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 9.1 — fornication|fornication]], and marries another, commits adultery; [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 9.2 — and|and]] he who marries her who has been divorced commits adultery.

## v10

His disciples said to Him, If the case of the man with his wife is [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 10.1 — like|like]] this, it is not profitable to marry.

## v11

And He said to them, [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Not|Not]] all men can [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 11.2 — accept|accept]] this word, but only those to whom it has been given.

## v12

For there are eunuchs who were born so from their mother’s womb, and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by men, and there are eunuchs [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 12.1 — who|who]] made themselves eunuchs because of the [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 12.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens. He who can accept it, let him accept it.

## v13

Then little children were brought to Him that He might lay His hands on them and pray, but the disciples rebuked them.

## v14

But Jesus said, Allow the little children and do not prevent them from coming to Me, for of [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 14.1 — such|such]] is the kingdom of the heavens.

## v15

And after He laid His hands on them, He went on from there.

## v16

And behold, someone came to Him and said, Teacher, what good thing shall I do that I may [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 16.1 — have|have]] eternal life?

## v17

And He said to him, Why do you ask Me concerning what is good? There is only [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 17.1 — One|One]] who is [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 17.2 — good|good]]. But if you want to [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 17.3 — enter|enter]] into life, [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 17.4 — keep|keep]] the commandments.

## v18

He said to Him, Which ones? And Jesus said, “You shall not murder; you shall not commit adultery; you shall not steal; you shall not bear false witness;

## v19

Honor your father and mother; and you shall love your neighbor as yourself.”

## v20

The young man said to Him, All these things I have kept. What do I still lack?

## v21

Jesus said to him, If you want to be [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 21.1 — perfect|perfect]], go, sell your possessions and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in the heavens; and come, [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 21.2 — follow|follow]] Me.

## v22

But the young man, hearing this word, went away [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 22.1 — sorrowing|sorrowing]], for he had many possessions.

## v23

And Jesus said to His disciples, Truly I say to you, Only with difficulty will a rich man enter into the kingdom of the heavens.

## v24

And again I say to you, It is [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 24.1 — easier|easier]] for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 24.2 — God|God]].

## v25

And when the disciples heard this, they were greatly astonished and said, Who then can be [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 25.1 — saved|saved]]?

## v26

And looking upon them, Jesus said to them, With men this is [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 26.1 — impossible|impossible]], but with God all things are [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 26.1 — impossible|possible]].

## v27

Then Peter answered and said to Him, Behold, we have left all and followed You. What then will there be for us?

## v28

And Jesus said to them, Truly I say to you that you who have followed Me, in the [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 28.1 — restoration|restoration]], when the Son of Man sits on the throne of His glory, you also shall sit on twelve thrones, [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 28.2 — judging|judging]] the twelve tribes of Israel.

## v29

And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or children or fields for My name’s sake shall [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 29.1 — receive|receive]] [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 29.2 — a|a]] hundred times as much and shall [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 29.3 — inherit|inherit]] eternal life.

## v30

But many [[Matthew-19.fn#Footnote 30.1 — first|first]] will be last, and many last first.
