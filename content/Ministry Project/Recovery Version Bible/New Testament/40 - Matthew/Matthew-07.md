---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

[[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Do|Do]] [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 1.2 — not|not]] judge, that you be not judged.

## v2

For [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 2.1 — with|with]] what judgment you judge, you shall be judged; and with what measure you measure, it shall be measured to you.

## v3

And why do you look at the splinter which is in your brother’s eye, but the beam in your eye you do not [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 3.1 — consider|consider]]?

## v4

Or how can you say to your brother, Let me remove the splinter from your eye, and behold, the beam is in your eye?

## v5

[[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Hypocrite|Hypocrite]], first remove the beam from your eye, and then you will [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 5.2 — see|see]] clearly to remove the splinter from your brother’s eye.

## v6

Do not give [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — that|that]] which is holy to the [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 6.2 — dogs|dogs]], neither cast your [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 6.1 — that|pearls]] before the [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 6.2 — dogs|hogs]], lest they trample them with their feet, and turn and tear you.

## v7

[[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Ask|Ask]] and it shall be given to you; seek and you shall find; knock and it shall be opened to you.

## v8

For everyone who [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 8.1 — asks|asks]] receives, and he who [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 8.2 — seeks|seeks]] finds, and to him who [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 8.3 — knocks|knocks]] it shall be opened.

## v9

Or what man is there among you who, when his son asks him for a loaf, will give him a stone?

## v10

Or also when he asks for a fish, will give him a serpent?

## v11

If you then being evil know how to give good gifts to your children, [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 11.1 — how|how]] much more will your Father who is in the heavens give [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 11.2 — good|good]] things to those who ask Him!

## v12

Therefore all that you wish men would do to you, so also you do to them; for this is the [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 12.1 — law|law]] and the prophets.

## v13

[[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Enter|Enter]] in through the narrow gate, for wide is the gate and broad is the way that [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 13.2 — leads|leads]] to [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 13.3 — destruction|destruction]], and many are those who enter through it.

## v14

Because [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 14.1 — narrow|narrow]] is the gate and constricted is the way that leads to [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 14.2 — life|life]], and few are those who find it.

## v15

Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 15.1 — ravenous|ravenous]] wolves.

## v16

By their fruits you will recognize them. Do men gather grapes from thorns, or figs from thistles?

## v17

Even so every good tree produces good fruit, but the corrupt tree produces bad fruit.

## v18

A good tree cannot produce bad fruit, neither can a corrupt tree produce good fruit.

## v19

Every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and cast into the fire.

## v20

So then, by their fruits you will recognize them.

## v21

Not everyone who says to Me, Lord, Lord, will [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 21.1 — enter|enter]] into the kingdom of the heavens, but he who does the will of My Father who is in the heavens.

## v22

Many will say to Me in [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 22.1 — that|that]] day, Lord, Lord, was it not in Your name that we prophesied, and in Your name cast out demons, and in Your name did many works of power?

## v23

And then I will declare to them: I never [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 23.1 — knew|knew]] you. Depart from Me, you workers of lawlessness.

## v24

Everyone therefore who hears these words of Mine and does them shall be likened to a prudent man who built his house upon the [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 24.1 — rock|rock]].

## v25

And the [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 25.1 — rain|rain]] descended, and the rivers came, and the winds blew, and they beat against that house; and it did [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 25.2 — not|not]] fall, for it was founded on the rock.

## v26

And everyone who hears these words of Mine and does not do them shall be likened to a foolish man who built his house upon the [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 26.1 — sand|sand]].

## v27

And the rain descended, and the rivers came, and the winds blew, and they dashed against that house; and it [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 27.1 — fell|fell]], and its fall was great.

## v28

And when Jesus finished these words, the crowds were astounded at His teaching,

## v29

For He taught them as One having [[Matthew-07.fn#Footnote 29.1 — authority|authority]] and not like their scribes.
