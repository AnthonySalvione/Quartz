---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

[[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 1.1 — On|On]] that day Jesus went out of the house and sat beside the sea.

## v2

And great crowds were gathered to Him, so that He stepped into a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 2.1 — boat|boat]] and sat, and all the crowd stood on the shore.

## v3

And He spoke many things to them in parables, saying, [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Behold|Behold]], the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 3.2 — sower|sower]] went out to sow.

## v4

And as he sowed, some [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 4.1 — seeds|seeds]] fell [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 4.2 — beside|beside]] the way, and the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 4.3 — birds|birds]] came and devoured them.

## v5

And others fell on the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 5.1 — rocky|rocky]] places, where they did not have much earth, and immediately they sprang up because they had no depth of earth.

## v6

But when the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 6.1 — sun|sun]] rose, they were scorched; and because they had no root, they withered.

## v7

And others fell on the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 7.1 — thorns|thorns]], and the thorns came up and choked them.

## v8

But others fell on the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 8.1 — good|good]] earth and yielded fruit, one a hundredfold, and one sixtyfold, and one thirtyfold.

## v9

He who has [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 9.1 — ears|ears]] to hear, let him hear.

## v10

And the disciples came and said to Him, Why do You speak in parables to them?

## v11

And He answered and said to them, Because to you it has been given to know the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 11.1 — mysteries|mysteries]] of the kingdom of the heavens, but to them it has not been given.

## v12

For [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 12.1 — whoever|whoever]] has, it shall be given to him, and he will abound; but [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 12.2 — whoever|whoever]] does not have, even that which he has shall be taken away from him.

## v13

For this reason I speak to them in parables, because seeing they do not see, and hearing they do not hear, nor do they understand.

## v14

And in them the prophecy of Isaiah is being fulfilled, which says, “In hearing you shall hear and by no means understand, and seeing you shall see and by no means perceive.

## v15

For the heart of this people has become fat, and with their ears they have heard heavily, and their eyes they have closed, lest they perceive with their eyes and hear with their ears and understand with their heart, and they turn around, and I will heal them.”

## v16

But blessed are your eyes because they see, and your ears because they hear.

## v17

For truly I say to you that many prophets and righteous men have desired to perceive the things that you [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 17.1 — see|see]], and have not perceived them, and to hear the things that you [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 17.1 — see|hear]], and have not heard them.

## v18

You therefore hear the parable of the one who sowed.

## v19

When anyone hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand, the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 19.1 — evil|evil]] one comes and snatches away that which has been sown in his heart. This is the one sown beside the way.

## v20

And the one sown on the rocky places, this is he who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy;

## v21

Yet he does not have root in himself but lasts only for a time, and when affliction or persecution occurs because of the word, immediately he is stumbled.

## v22

And the one sown in the thorns, this is he who hears the word, and the anxiety of the age and the deceitfulness of riches utterly choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.

## v23

But the one sown on the good earth, this is he who hears the word and understands, who by all means bears fruit and produces, one a hundredfold, and one sixtyfold, and one thirtyfold.

## v24

Another parable He set before them, saying, The [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 24.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens has become like a man sowing good seed in his field.

## v25

But while the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 25.1 — men|men]] slept, his enemy came and sowed [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 25.2 — tares|tares]] in the midst of the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 25.3 — wheat|wheat]] and went away.

## v26

And when the blade sprouted and produced fruit, then the tares appeared also.

## v27

And the slaves of the master of the house came and said to him, Sir, did you not sow good seed in your field? Where then did the tares come from?

## v28

And he said to them, An enemy has done this. And the slaves said to him, Do you want us then to go and [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 28.1 — collect|collect]] them?

## v29

But he said, No, lest while [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 29.1 — collecting|collecting]] the tares, you uproot the wheat along with them.

## v30

Let both [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 30.1 — grow|grow]] together until the harvest, and at the time of the harvest I will say to the reapers, Collect first the tares and bind them into bundles to burn them up, but the wheat gather into my barn.

## v31

Another parable He set before them, saying, The kingdom of the heavens is like a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 31.1 — mustard|mustard]] seed, which a man took and sowed in his field,

## v32

And which is smaller than all the seeds; but when it has grown, it is greater than the herbs and becomes a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 32.1 — tree|tree]], so that the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 32.2 — birds|birds]] of heaven come and roost in its branches.

## v33

Another parable He spoke to them: The kingdom of the heavens is like [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 33.1 — leaven|leaven]], which a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 33.2 — woman|woman]] took and hid in three measures of [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 33.3 — meal|meal]] until the whole was leavened.

## v34

All these things Jesus spoke in parables to the crowds, and apart from a parable He spoke nothing to them,

## v35

So that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, “I will open My mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 35.1 — from|from]] the foundation of the world.”

## v36

Then, leaving the crowds, He went into the house. And His disciples came to Him, saying, Make the parable of the tares of the field clear to us.

## v37

And He answered and said, He who sows the good seed is the Son of Man;

## v38

And the field is the world; and the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 38.1 — good|good]] seed, these are the sons of the kingdom; and the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 38.1 — good|tares]] are the sons of the evil one;

## v39

And the enemy who sowed them is the devil; and the harvest is the consummation of the age; and the reapers are angels.

## v40

Therefore just as the tares are collected and burned up with fire, so will it be at the consummation of the age.

## v41

The Son of Man will send His angels, and they will collect out of [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 41.1 — His|His]] kingdom all the stumbling blocks and those who practice lawlessness,

## v42

And will cast them into the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 42.1 — furnace|furnace]] of fire. In that place there will be the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.3 — weeping|weeping]] and the gnashing of teeth.

## v43

Then the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 43.1 — righteous|righteous]] will shine forth like the sun in the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 43.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of their Father. He who has ears to hear, let him hear.

## v44

The kingdom of the heavens is like a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 44.1 — treasure|treasure]] hidden in the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 44.2 — field|field]], which a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 44.3 — man|man]] found and hid, and in his joy goes and sells all that he has, and buys that field.

## v45

Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 45.1 — merchant|merchant]] seeking fine pearls;

## v46

And finding one [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 46.1 — pearl|pearl]] of great value, he went and sold all that he had and bought it.

## v47

Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 47.1 — net|net]] cast into the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 47.2 — sea|sea]] and gathering from [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 47.3 — every|every]] species,

## v48

Which, when it was filled, they brought onto the shore, and sat down and collected the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 48.1 — good|good]] into vessels; but the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 48.1 — good|foul]] they cast out.

## v49

So it will be at the consummation of the age: the angels will go forth and separate the evil from the midst of the righteous

## v50

And will cast them into the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 50.1 — furnace|furnace]] of fire. In that place there will be the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.3 — weeping|weeping]] and the gnashing of teeth.

## v51

Have you understood all these things? They said to Him, Yes.

## v52

And He said to them, For this reason every scribe discipled to the kingdom of the heavens is like a householder who brings forth out of his [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 52.1 — treasure|treasure]] things new and old.

## v53

And when Jesus had finished these parables, He departed from there.

## v54

And He came into His own country and taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astounded and said, Where did this man get this wisdom and these works of power?

## v55

Is not this the [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 55.1 — carpenter’s|carpenter’s]] son? Is not His mother called Mary, and His brothers James and Joseph and Simon and Judas?

## v56

And His sisters, are they not all with us? Where then did this man get all these things?

## v57

And they were stumbled because of Him. But Jesus said to them, A prophet is not without honor except in his own country and in his own house.

## v58

And He did [[Matthew-13.fn#Footnote 58.1 — not|not]] do many works of power there because of their unbelief.
