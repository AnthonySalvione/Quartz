---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And stepping into a boat, He crossed over and came to His [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 1.1 — own|own]] city.

## v2

And behold, they brought to Him a paralytic, lying on a bed. And Jesus, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 2.1 — seeing|seeing]] their faith, said to the paralytic, Take courage, child; your [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 2.2 — sins|sins]] are forgiven.

## v3

And behold, some of the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 3.1 — scribes|scribes]] said within themselves, This man [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 3.2 — blasphemes|blasphemes]].

## v4

And Jesus, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 4.1 — knowing|knowing]] their [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 4.2 — thoughts|thoughts]], said, Why are you thinking evil things in your hearts?

## v5

For which is [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 5.1 — easier|easier]], to say, Your sins are forgiven, or to say, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Rise|Rise]] and walk?

## v6

But that you may know that the Son of Man has [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 6.1 — authority|authority]] on earth to forgive sins — then He said to the paralytic, Rise, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 6.2 — take|take]] up your bed and [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 6.3 — go|go]] to your house.

## v7

And he [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 7.1 — rose|rose]] and went away to his house.

## v8

And when the crowds saw this, they feared and glorified God, who had given such authority to men.

## v9

And as Jesus passed on from there, He saw a man sitting at the tax office, called [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Matthew|Matthew]], and He said to him, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Follow|Follow]] Me. And he rose and [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 9.3 — followed|followed]] Him.

## v10

And as He was reclining at table in the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 10.1 — house|house]], behold, many [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 10.2 — tax|tax]] collectors and sinners came and reclined together with Jesus and His disciples.

## v11

And when the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] saw it, they said to His disciples, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 11.2 — Why|Why]] does your Teacher eat with the tax collectors and sinners?

## v12

Now when He heard this, He said, Those who are strong have no need of a [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 12.1 — physician|physician]], but those who are [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 12.2 — ill|ill]].

## v13

But go and [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 13.1 — learn|learn]] what this means, “I desire [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 13.2 — mercy|mercy]] and not sacrifice,” for I did not come to call the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 13.3 — righteous|righteous]], but sinners.

## v14

Then the disciples of John came to Him, saying, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Why|Why]] do we and the Pharisees [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 14.2 — fast|fast]] [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 14.3 — much|much]], but Your disciples do [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 14.4 — not|not]] fast?

## v15

And Jesus said to them, The [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 15.1 — sons|sons]] of the bridechamber cannot mourn as long as the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 15.2 — bridegroom|bridegroom]] is with them, can they? But days will come when the bridegroom will be [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 15.3 — taken|taken]] away from them, and then they will fast.

## v16

No one puts a patch of [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 16.1 — unfulled|unfulled]] cloth on an [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 16.2 — old|old]] garment, for that which fills it up pulls away from the garment, and a worse tear is made.

## v17

Neither do they put [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 17.1 — new|new]] wine into [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 17.2 — old|old]] wineskins; otherwise, the wineskins burst, and the wine pours out, and the wineskins are ruined; but they put new wine into [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 17.3 — fresh|fresh]] wineskins, and both are preserved.

## v18

[[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 18.1 — As|As]] He was speaking these things to them, behold, a [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 18.2 — ruler|ruler]] came and worshipped Him, saying, My daughter has just died, but come and lay Your hand on her, and she will live.

## v19

And Jesus rose and followed him, and so did His disciples.

## v20

And behold, a woman who had been suffering from a [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 20.1 — hemorrhage|hemorrhage]] for [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 20.2 — twelve|twelve]] years [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 20.3 — approached|approached]] from behind and touched the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 20.4 — fringe|fringe]] of His garment,

## v21

For she said within herself, If only [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 21.1 — I|I]] touch His garment, I will be [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 21.2 — healed|healed]].

## v22

And Jesus, turning and seeing her, said, Take courage, daughter; your faith has healed you. And the woman was healed from that hour.

## v23

And when Jesus came to the ruler’s [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 23.1 — house|house]] and saw the flute players and the crowd making a commotion,

## v24

He said, Depart, for the girl has not died but is sleeping. And they laughed scornfully at Him.

## v25

But when the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 25.1 — crowd|crowd]] had been put out, He entered and took hold of her hand, and the girl [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 25.2 — rose|rose]] up.

## v26

And this report went out into all that land.

## v27

And as Jesus passed on from there, two [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 27.1 — blind|blind]] men followed Him, crying out and saying, Have mercy on us, [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 27.2 — Son|Son]] of David!

## v28

And when He came into the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 28.1 — house|house]], the blind men came to Him; and Jesus said to them, Do you believe that I am able to do this? They said to Him, Yes, Lord.

## v29

Then He touched their eyes, saying, According to your faith, let it be done to you.

## v30

And their [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 30.1 — eyes|eyes]] were opened. And Jesus sternly charged them saying, See that no one knows!

## v31

But they went out and spread reports about Him in all that land.

## v32

And as they were going out, behold, they brought to Him a [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 32.1 — dumb|dumb]] man possessed with a demon.

## v33

And when the demon was cast out, the dumb man [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 33.1 — spoke|spoke]]. And the crowds marveled, saying, Never has anything like this been seen in Israel.

## v34

But the Pharisees said, By the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 34.1 — ruler|ruler]] of the demons He casts out the demons.

## v35

And Jesus went about all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues and preaching the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 35.1 — gospel|gospel]] of the kingdom and healing [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 35.2 — every|every]] disease and every sickness.

## v36

And seeing the crowds, He was moved with compassion for them, because they were [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 36.1 — harassed|harassed]] and cast away like [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 36.2 — sheep|sheep]] not having a [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 36.2 — sheep|shepherd]].

## v37

Then He said to His disciples, The [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 37.1 — harvest|harvest]] is great, but the workers few;

## v38

Therefore [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 38.1 — beseech|beseech]] the [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 38.2 — Lord|Lord]] of the harvest that He would thrust out workers [[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 38.3 — into|into]] His harvest.
