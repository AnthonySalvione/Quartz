---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

[[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 1.1 — For|For]] the kingdom of the heavens is like a [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 1.2 — man|man]], who is a householder, who went out early in the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 1.3 — morning|morning]] to hire [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 1.4 — workmen|workmen]] for his [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 1.5 — vineyard|vineyard]].

## v2

And having [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 2.1 — agreed|agreed]] with the workmen for a [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 2.2 — denarius|denarius]] a day, he sent them into his vineyard.

## v3

And he went out about the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 3.1 — third|third]] hour and saw others standing [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 3.2 — idle|idle]] in the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 3.3 — marketplace|marketplace]],

## v4

And to those he said, You also go into the vineyard, and whatever is right I will give you. And they went.

## v5

And again he went out about the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 5.1 — sixth|sixth]] and the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 5.2 — ninth|ninth]] hour and did likewise.

## v6

And about the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 6.1 — eleventh|eleventh]] hour he went out and found others standing, and said to them, Why have you been standing here all the day idle?

## v7

They said to him, Because [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 7.1 — no|no]] one has hired us. He said to them, You [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 7.2 — also|also]] go into the vineyard.

## v8

And when [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 8.1 — evening|evening]] fell, the master of the vineyard said to his steward, Call the workmen and pay them their wages, beginning from the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 8.2 — last|last]] ones to the first.

## v9

And when those hired about the eleventh hour came, they received each a denarius.

## v10

And when the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 10.1 — first|first]] ones came, they supposed that they would receive more. And they themselves also received each a denarius.

## v11

And when they received it, they murmured against the householder,

## v12

[[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Saying|Saying]], These last ones worked one hour, and you have made them [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 12.2 — equal|equal]] to us who bore the burden of the day and the scorching heat.

## v13

But he answered [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 13.1 — one|one]] of them and said, [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Friend|Friend]], I am doing nothing unrighteous to you. Did you not [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 13.3 — agree|agree]] with me for a denarius?

## v14

[[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Take|Take]] what is yours and go. I [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Take|want]] to give to this last one even as I have given to you.

## v15

Is it not lawful for me to do what I [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 15.1 — wish|wish]] with what is mine? Or is your eye [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 15.2 — evil|evil]] because I am good?

## v16

Thus shall the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 16.1 — last|last]] be first and the first last.

## v17

And as Jesus was going up to Jerusalem, He took the twelve disciples aside privately, and on the way He said to them,

## v18

Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be delivered to the chief priests and scribes. And they will condemn Him to [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 18.1 — death|death]],

## v19

And deliver Him to the Gentiles to mock and to scourge and to crucify Him, and on the third day He will be raised.

## v20

Then the mother of the [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 20.1 — sons|sons]] of Zebedee came to Him with her sons, worshipping and asking something of Him.

## v21

And He said to her, What do you want? She said to Him, Say that these two sons of mine will sit, one on Your right and one on Your left, in Your kingdom.

## v22

But Jesus answered and said to them, You do not know what you are asking. Are you able to [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 22.1 — drink|drink]] the cup which I am about to drink? They said to Him, We are able.

## v23

He said to them, My cup you shall indeed drink, but to sit on My right and on My left, this is [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 23.1 — not|not]] Mine to give, but it is for those for whom it has been prepared by My Father.

## v24

And when the ten heard this, they were indignant concerning the two brothers.

## v25

But Jesus called them to Him and said, You know that the rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and the great exercise authority over them.

## v26

It shall not be so among you; but whoever wants to become great among you shall be your [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 26.1 — servant|servant]],

## v27

And whoever wants to be first among you shall be your [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 26.1 — servant|slave]];

## v28

Just as the Son of [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Man|Man]] did not come to be served, but to serve and to give His [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 28.2 — life|life]] as a ransom for many.

## v29

And as they were going out from Jericho, a great crowd followed Him.

## v30

And behold, two blind men sitting by the road, hearing that Jesus was passing by, cried out, saying, Have mercy on us, Lord, [[Matthew-20.fn#Footnote 30.1 — Son|Son]] of David!

## v31

And the crowd rebuked them so that they would be silent, but they cried out the more, saying, Have mercy on us, Lord, Son of David!

## v32

And Jesus, standing still, called them and said, What do you want Me to do for you?

## v33

They said to Him, Lord, that our eyes may be opened.

## v34

And Jesus, moved with compassion, touched their eyes, and immediately they received their sight and followed Him.
