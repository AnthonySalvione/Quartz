---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

[[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 1.1 — But|But]] take care not to do your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 1.2 — righteousness|righteousness]] [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 1.3 — before|before]] men in order to be gazed at by them; otherwise, you have no reward with your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Father|Father]] who is in the heavens.

## v2

Therefore when you give alms, do not [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.1 — sound|sound]] a trumpet before you as the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — hypocrites|hypocrites]] do in the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.3 — synagogues|synagogues]] and in the streets, so that they may be glorified by men. Truly I say to you, They have their reward in full.

## v3

But you, when you give alms, do not let your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 3.1 — left|left]] hand know what your right hand is doing,

## v4

So that your alms may be in [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 4.1 — secret|secret]]; and your Father who [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 4.2 — sees|sees]] in secret will [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 4.3 — repay|repay]] you.

## v5

And when you pray, you shall not be like the hypocrites, because they love to pray standing in the synagogues and on the street corners, so that they may be seen by men. Truly I say to you, They have [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 5.1 — their|their]] reward in full.

## v6

But you, when you pray, enter into your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 6.1 — private|private]] room, and shut your door and pray to your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.

## v7

And in praying do [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 7.1 — not|not]] babble empty words as the Gentiles do; for they suppose that in their multiplicity of words they will be heard.

## v8

Therefore do not be like them, for your Father knows the things that you have need of before you [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 8.1 — ask|ask]] Him.

## v9

You then pray [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 9.1 — in|in]] this way: Our Father who is in the heavens, [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Your|Your]] name be sanctified;

## v10

Your kingdom come; Your will be done, as in heaven, so also on [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 10.1 — earth|earth]].

## v11

[[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Give|Give]] us [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 11.2 — today|today]] our [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 11.3 — daily|daily]] bread.

## v12

And [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — forgive|forgive]] us our debts, as we also have [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 12.1 — forgive|forgiven]] our debtors.

## v13

And do [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 13.1 — not|not]] bring us into temptation, but deliver us from [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 13.2 — the|the]] evil one. [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 13.3 — For|For]] Yours is the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 13.4 — kingdom|kingdom]] and the power and the glory forever. [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 13.5 — Amen|Amen]].

## v14

[[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 14.1 — For|For]] if you forgive men their offenses, your heavenly Father will forgive you also;

## v15

But if you do not forgive men their offenses, neither will your Father forgive your offenses.

## v16

And when you [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 16.1 — fast|fast]], do not be like the sullen-faced hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces so that they may appear to men to be fasting. Truly I say to you, They have their reward in full.

## v17

But you, when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face,

## v18

So that you may not appear to men to be fasting, but to your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.

## v19

[[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Do|Do]] not store up for yourselves treasures on the earth, where moth and rust consume and where thieves dig through and steal.

## v20

But [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 20.1 — store|store]] up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust consumes and where thieves do not dig through nor steal.

## v21

For where your treasure is, there will your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 21.1 — heart|heart]] be also.

## v22

The lamp of the body is the eye. If therefore your eye is [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 22.1 — single|single]], your whole body will be full of light;

## v23

But if your eye is [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 23.1 — evil|evil]], your whole body will be dark. If then the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 23.2 — light|light]] that is in you is darkness, how great is the darkness!

## v24

No one can [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 24.1 — serve|serve]] two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 24.2 — hold|hold]] to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 24.3 — mammon|mammon]].

## v25

Because of this, I say to you, Do not be anxious for your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 25.1 — life|life]], what you should eat or what you should drink; nor for your body, what you should put on. Is not the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 25.1 — life|life]] [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 25.2 — more|more]] than food, and the body than clothing?

## v26

Look at the birds of heaven. They do not sow nor reap nor gather into barns, yet your heavenly Father nourishes them. Are you not of more value than they?

## v27

Who among you by being anxious can add one cubit to [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 27.1 — his|his]] stature?

## v28

And why are you anxious concerning clothing? Consider well the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 28.1 — lilies|lilies]] of the field, how they grow. They do not toil, neither do they spin thread.

## v29

But I tell you that not even Solomon in all his glory was clothed like one of these.

## v30

And if God so arrays the grass of the field, which is here today and tomorrow is cast into the furnace, will He not much more clothe you, you of little faith?

## v31

Therefore do not be anxious, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, With what shall we be clothed?

## v32

For all these things the Gentiles are anxiously seeking. For your [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 32.1 — heavenly|heavenly]] Father knows that you need all these things.

## v33

But seek first His [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 33.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] and His [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 33.2 — righteousness|righteousness]], and all these things will be added to you.

## v34

Therefore do not be anxious for [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 34.1 — tomorrow|tomorrow]], for tomorrow will be anxious for itself; sufficient for the day is its own [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 34.2 — evil|evil]].
