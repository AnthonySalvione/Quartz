## v1

Then some Pharisees and scribes [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 1.1 — came|came]] to Jesus from Jerusalem, saying,

## v2

Why do Your disciples [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 2.1 — transgress|transgress]] the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread.

## v3

And He answered and said to them, Why do you also [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 3.1 — transgress|transgress]] the commandment of God because of your tradition?

## v4

For God said, “[[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 4.1 — Honor|Honor]] your father and your mother,” and, “He who [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 4.2 — speaks|speaks]] evil of father or mother, let him [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 4.3 — be|be]] put to death.”

## v5

But you say, Whoever says to his father or his mother, Whatever you would have been profited by me is now a [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 5.1 — gift|gift]] to God,

## v6

Shall by no means honor his father or his mother; and you have deprived the word of God of its authority because of your tradition.

## v7

[[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — hypocrites|Hypocrites]]! Well has Isaiah prophesied concerning you, saying,

## v8

“This people honors Me with their lips, but their [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 8.1 — heart|heart]] stays far away from Me;

## v9

But in [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 9.1 — vain|vain]] do they worship Me, teaching as teachings the commandments of men.”

## v10

And He called the crowd to Him and said to them, Hear and understand:

## v11

It is not that which enters into the mouth that defiles the man; but that which proceeds out of the mouth, this [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 11.1 — defiles|defiles]] the man.

## v12

Then the disciples came and said to Him, Do You know that the Pharisees were stumbled when they heard this word?

## v13

And He answered and said, Every plant which My heavenly Father has [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 13.1 — not|not]] planted shall be rooted up.

## v14

Let them be. They are [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 14.1 — blind|blind]] guides of the blind; and if a blind man guides a blind man, both will fall into a pit.

## v15

And Peter answered and said to Him, Explain this parable to us.

## v16

And He said, Are you also still without understanding?

## v17

Do you not understand that everything that goes into the mouth passes into the stomach and is expelled into the drain?

## v18

But the things which proceed out of the mouth come out of the heart, and those defile the man.

## v19

For out of the heart come [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 19.1 — evil|evil]] reasonings, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witnessings, blasphemies.

## v20

These are the things which defile the man, but eating with unwashed hands does not defile the man.

## v21

And Jesus went from there and withdrew into the parts of [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Tyre|Tyre]] and Sidon.

## v22

And behold, a [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Canaanite|Canaanite]] woman came out from those borders and cried out, saying, Have mercy on me, [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 22.2 — Lord|Lord]], Son of David! My daughter suffers terribly from demon possession.

## v23

But He did not answer her a word. And His disciples came and implored Him, saying, Send her away, for she is crying out after us.

## v24

But He answered and said, I was not [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 24.1 — sent|sent]] except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

## v25

But she came and [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 25.1 — worshipped|worshipped]] Him, saying, [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 25.2 — Lord|Lord]], help me!

## v26

But He answered and said, It is not good to take the children’s [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 26.1 — bread|bread]] and throw it to the [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 26.2 — little|little]] dogs.

## v27

And she said, Yes, Lord, for even the little dogs eat of the [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 27.1 — crumbs|crumbs]] which fall from their masters’ table.

## v28

Then Jesus answered and said to her, O woman, great is your faith! Be it done to you as you wish. And her daughter was healed from that hour.

## v29

And Jesus departed from there and came beside the Sea of [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Galilee|Galilee]]; and He went up to the mountain and sat there.

## v30

And great crowds came to Him, having with them the lame, the blind, the crippled, the dumb, and many other sick ones; and they [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 30.1 — laid|laid]] them at His feet, and He healed them,

## v31

So that the crowd marveled, because they saw the dumb speaking, the crippled healthy and the lame walking, and the blind seeing. And they glorified the God of Israel.

## v32

And Jesus called His disciples to Him and said, I am moved with compassion for the crowd, because for three days now they have remained with Me and they do not have anything to eat. And I am [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 32.1 — not|not]] willing to send them away hungry, lest they faint on the way.

## v33

And the disciples said to Him, Where can we get so many loaves in this [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 33.1 — desolate|desolate]] place to satisfy so great a crowd?

## v34

And Jesus said to them, [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 34.1 — How|How]] many loaves do you have? And they said, Seven, and a few small fish.

## v35

And after He commanded the crowd to recline on the ground,

## v36

He [[Matthew-15.fn#Footnote 36.1 — took|took]] the seven loaves and the fish, and giving thanks, He broke them and gave them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.

## v37

And they all ate and were satisfied. And they took up what was left over of the broken pieces, seven baskets full.

## v38

And those who ate were four thousand men, apart from women and children.

## v39

And after He sent the crowds away, He stepped into the boat and came to the borders of Magadan.
