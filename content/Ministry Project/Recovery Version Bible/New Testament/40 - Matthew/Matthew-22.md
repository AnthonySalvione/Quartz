---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And Jesus answered and spoke again in parables to them, saying,

## v2

The [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 2.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens has become like a [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 2.2 — king|king]] who prepared a [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 2.3 — wedding|wedding]] feast for his son.

## v3

And he sent his [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 3.1 — slaves|slaves]] to call those who had been called to the wedding feast, yet they would not come.

## v4

Again, he sent [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 4.1 — other|other]] slaves, saying, Tell those who have been called, Behold, I have prepared my [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 4.2 — dinner|dinner]]: my [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 4.3 — oxen|oxen]] and my [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 4.3 — oxen|fatted]] cattle have been slain, and all things are ready. Come to the wedding feast.

## v5

But they disregarded it and went off, one to his own field and another to his business,

## v6

And the rest took hold of his slaves, treated them shamefully, and killed them.

## v7

And the king became angry, and he sent his [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 7.1 — troops|troops]] and destroyed those murderers and burned their city.

## v8

Then he said to his slaves, The wedding feast is ready, but those who have been called were [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 8.1 — not|not]] worthy.

## v9

Go therefore to the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 9.1 — crossroads|crossroads]], and as many as you find, call to the wedding feast.

## v10

And those slaves went out into the streets and gathered all whom they found, both evil and good, and the wedding feast was filled with those reclining at table.

## v11

But when the king came in to look at those reclining at table, he saw there a man who was not clothed with a [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 11.1 — wedding|wedding]] garment,

## v12

And he said to him, Friend, how did you come in here without a wedding garment? And he was speechless.

## v13

Then the king said to the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 13.1 — servants|servants]], Bind his feet and hands, and [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 13.2 — cast|cast]] him out into the outer darkness. In that place there will be the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.3 — weeping|weeping]] and the gnashing of teeth.

## v14

For many are [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 14.1 — called|called]] but few are [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 14.1 — called|chosen]].

## v15

Then the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] went and took counsel how they might ensnare Him in His words.

## v16

And they sent to Him their disciples with the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Herodians|Herodians]], saying, Teacher, we know that You are true and that You teach the way of God in truth and [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 16.2 — do|do]] not fear anyone, for You do not [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 16.3 — regard|regard]] the person of men.

## v17

Tell us therefore, What do You think? [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Is|Is]] it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not?

## v18

But Jesus, knowing their evil intent, said, Why do you tempt Me, [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — hypocrites|hypocrites]]?

## v19

[[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 19.1 — Show|Show]] Me the coin for the tribute. And they brought to Him a [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 19.2 — denarius|denarius]].

## v20

And He said to them, Whose is this image and inscription?

## v21

They said, Caesar’s. Then He said to them, Render then the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 21.1 — things|things]] that are Caesar’s to Caesar and the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 21.2 — things|things]] that are God’s to God.

## v22

And when they heard this, they marveled. And they left Him and went away.

## v23

On that day some [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Sadducees|Sadducees]], who say that there is no resurrection, came to Him and questioned Him,

## v24

Saying, Teacher, Moses said, If anyone dies and does not have children, his brother as next of kin shall marry his wife and raise up seed to his brother.

## v25

Now there were seven brothers with us. And the first married and died, and having no seed, he left his wife to his brother;

## v26

Likewise also the second and the third until the seventh.

## v27

And last of all the woman died.

## v28

In the resurrection, then, whose wife will she be of the seven? For they all had her.

## v29

And Jesus answered and said to them, You err, not [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 29.1 — knowing|knowing]] the Scriptures nor the power of God.

## v30

For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.

## v31

But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read that which was spoken to you by God, saying,

## v32

“I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob”? He is not the God of the dead, but of the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 32.1 — living|living]].

## v33

And when the crowds heard this, they were astounded at His teaching.

## v34

But the Pharisees, when they heard that He had muzzled the Sadducees, gathered themselves together.

## v35

And one of them, a [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 35.1 — lawyer|lawyer]], asked Him a question, testing Him,

## v36

Teacher, which is the great commandment in the law?

## v37

And He said to him, “You shall love the Lord your God [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 37.1 — with|with]] all your heart and [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 37.1 — with|with]] all your soul and [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 37.1 — with|with]] all your mind.”

## v38

This is the great and first commandment.

## v39

And the second is like it: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”

## v40

On these [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 40.1 — two|two]] commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.

## v41

Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus questioned them,

## v42

Saying, What do you think concerning the [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 42.1 — Christ|Christ]]? Whose son is He? They said to Him, David’s.

## v43

He said to them, How then does David in [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 43.1 — spirit|spirit]] call Him Lord, saying,

## v44

“The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at My right hand until I put Your enemies underneath Your feet”?

## v45

If then David calls Him [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 45.1 — Lord|Lord]], how is He his [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 45.1 — Lord|son]]?

## v46

And [[Matthew-22.fn#Footnote 46.1 — no|no]] one was able to answer Him a word, nor did anyone from that day dare to question Him anymore.
