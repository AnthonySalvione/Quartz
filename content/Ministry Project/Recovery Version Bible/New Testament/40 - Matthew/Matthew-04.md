---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

Then [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jesus|Jesus]] was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit to be [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 1.2 — tempted|tempted]] by the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 1.3 — devil|devil]].

## v2

And when He had fasted [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 2.1 — forty|forty]] days and forty nights, afterward He became hungry.

## v3

And the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — tempter|tempter]] came and said to Him, If You are the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Son|Son]] of God, speak that these [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 3.3 — stones|stones]] may become loaves of bread.

## v4

But He answered and said, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — It|It]] is written, “[[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Man|Man]] shall not live on [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — bread|bread]] alone, but on every [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 4.4 — word|word]] that proceeds out through the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 4.5 — mouth|mouth]] of God.”

## v5

Then the devil took Him into the holy city and set Him on the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — wing|wing]] of the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 5.2 — temple|temple]],

## v6

And said to Him, If You are the Son of God, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — cast|cast]] Yourself down; for [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — it|it]] is written, “To His angels He shall give charge concerning You, and on their hands they shall bear You up, lest You strike Your foot against a stone.”

## v7

Jesus said to him, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Again|Again]], it is written, “You shall not [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — test|test]] the Lord your God.”

## v8

Again, the devil took Him to a very high mountain and showed Him all the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — kingdoms|kingdoms]] of the world and their glory.

## v9

And he said to Him, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — All|All]] these will I give You if You will fall down and worship me.

## v10

Then Jesus said to him, Go away, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 10.1 — Satan|Satan]]! For it is written, “You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 10.2 — only|only]] shall you serve.”

## v11

Then the devil [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 11.1 — left|left]] Him, and behold, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 11.2 — angels|angels]] came and ministered to Him.

## v12

Now when He heard that John was delivered up, He [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 12.1 — withdrew|withdrew]] into Galilee.

## v13

And leaving Nazareth, He came and dwelt in Capernaum, which is beside the sea in the borders of Zebulun and Naphtali,

## v14

In order that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

## v15

“Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the way to the sea, beyond the Jordan, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Galilee|Galilee]] of the Gentiles:

## v16

The people sitting in darkness have seen a [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — great|great]] light; and to those sitting in the region and shadow of death, to them light has risen.”

## v17

From that time Jesus began to proclaim and to say, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Repent|Repent]], for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.

## v18

And while He was walking beside the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Sea|Sea]] of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 18.2 — casting|casting]] a [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 18.3 — net|net]] into the sea, for they were fishermen.

## v19

And He said to them, Come after Me, and I will make you fishers of men.

## v20

And immediately leaving the nets, they [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 20.1 — followed|followed]] Him.

## v21

And going on from there, He saw another two brothers, James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 21.1 — mending|mending]] their nets; and He called them.

## v22

And immediately leaving the boat and their father, they [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 22.1 — followed|followed]] Him.

## v23

And Jesus went about in all of Galilee, teaching in their [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 23.1 — synagogues|synagogues]] and proclaiming the [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 23.2 — gospel|gospel]] of the kingdom and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.

## v24

And the report concerning Him went out into all of Syria; and they brought to Him all who were ill, those afflicted with various diseases and torments and those possessed by demons and epileptics and paralytics; and He healed them.

## v25

And great [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 25.1 — crowds|crowds]] followed Him from Galilee and [[Matthew-04.fn#Footnote 25.2 — Decapolis|Decapolis]] and Jerusalem and Judea and from beyond the Jordan.
