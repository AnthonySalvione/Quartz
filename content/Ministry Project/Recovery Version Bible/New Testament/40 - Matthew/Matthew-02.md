---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

Now after Jesus was born in [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Bethlehem|Bethlehem]] of Judea in the days of Herod the king, behold, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem,

## v2

Saying, Where is He who has been born [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — King|King]] of the Jews? For we saw His [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — star|star]] at its rising and have come to worship Him.

## v3

And when Herod the king heard this, he was troubled and all Jerusalem with him.

## v4

And gathering together all the chief [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — priests|priests]] and scribes of the people, he inquired of them where the Christ was to be born.

## v5

And they said to him, In Bethlehem of Judea, for so it is written through the prophet:

## v6

“And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, by no means are you the least among the [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — princes|princes]] of Judah; for out of you shall come forth a Ruler, One who will shepherd My people Israel.”

## v7

Then Herod, secretly calling the magi, determined accurately from them the time that the star appeared.

## v8

And sending them to Bethlehem, he said, Go and find out accurately about the child. And when you find Him, report to me, so that I also may come and worship Him.

## v9

And after they heard the king, they went their way, and behold, the [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — star|star]] which they saw at its rising led them until it came and stood over the place where the child was.

## v10

And when they saw the star, they rejoiced exceedingly with great joy.

## v11

And they came into the [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — house|house]] and saw the child with Mary His mother. And falling down, they [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — worshipped|worshipped]] Him; and opening their treasures, they offered [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 11.3 — gifts|gifts]] to Him, gold and frankincense and myrrh.

## v12

And because they had been divinely instructed in a dream not to [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — return|return]] to Herod, they departed by [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — another|another]] way to their country.

## v13

Now when they had departed, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph, saying, Arise, take the child and His mother and flee into Egypt, and stay there until I tell you; for Herod is going to seek the child to destroy Him.

## v14

And he arose and took the child and His mother by night and departed into Egypt,

## v15

And was there until the death of Herod, in order that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — fulfilled|fulfilled]], saying, “Out of Egypt I called My [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Son|Son]].”

## v16

Then Herod, seeing that he had been mocked by the magi, became greatly enraged, and sending out men, he [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — did|did]] away with all the boys who were in Bethlehem and in all its borders from two years old and under, according to the time that he had accurately determined from the magi.

## v17

At that time what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying,

## v18

“A voice in Ramah was heard, weeping and great lamentation: [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Rachel|Rachel]] weeping for her children, and she would not be comforted, because they are no more.”

## v19

And when Herod had died, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,

## v20

Saying, Arise, take the child and His mother and go into the land of Israel, for those who seek the [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — life|life]] of the child have died.

## v21

And he arose and took the child and His mother and entered into the land of Israel.

## v22

But because he heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there; and having been divinely instructed in a dream, he departed into the regions of [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — Galilee|Galilee]].

## v23

And he came and settled in a city called [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Nazareth|Nazareth]], so that what was spoken through the [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — prophets|prophets]] might be fulfilled, He shall be called a [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 23.3 — Nazarene|Nazarene]].
