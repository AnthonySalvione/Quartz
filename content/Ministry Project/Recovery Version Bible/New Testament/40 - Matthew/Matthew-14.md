---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

At that time Herod the tetrarch heard the report concerning Jesus,

## v2

And he said to his servants, This is John the Baptist; he has been raised from the dead, and because of this these works of power [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 2.1 — are|are]] operating through him.

## v3

For Herod had seized John and bound him and put him in prison on account of Herodias, the wife of Philip his brother.

## v4

For John had been saying to him, It is not lawful for you to have her.

## v5

And though he desired to kill him, he feared the crowd because they held him as a prophet.

## v6

But when Herod’s birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced in their midst and pleased Herod;

## v7

Hence, he promised with an oath to give her whatever she asked.

## v8

And she, being instigated by her mother, said, Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptist.

## v9

And the king was grieved, yet because of the oaths and those reclining at table with him, he commanded it to be given.

## v10

And he sent men and [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 10.1 — beheaded|beheaded]] John in the prison.

## v11

And his head was brought on a platter and given to the girl, and she brought it to her mother.

## v12

And his disciples came and took the corpse and buried it; and they came and reported the matter to Jesus.

## v13

And when Jesus heard this, He [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 13.1 — withdrew|withdrew]] from there in a boat to a deserted place privately. And when the crowds heard of it, they [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 13.2 — followed|followed]] Him on foot from the cities.

## v14

And going forth, He saw a great crowd, and He was moved with compassion for them and healed their sick.

## v15

Now when evening fell, the disciples came to Him, saying, This place is deserted and the hour is already [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 15.1 — late|late]]. Send the crowds away that they may go into the villages and buy food for themselves.

## v16

But Jesus said to them, They do not need to go away. You [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 16.1 — give|give]] them something to [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 16.2 — eat|eat]].

## v17

And they said to Him, We do not have anything here except five [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 17.1 — loaves|loaves]] and two [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 17.1 — loaves|fish]].

## v18

And He said, [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Bring|Bring]] them here to Me.

## v19

And after commanding the crowds to [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 19.1 — recline|recline]] on the grass, He took the five loaves and the two fish, and [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 19.2 — looking|looking]] up to heaven, He blessed and [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 19.3 — broke|broke]] the loaves and [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 19.4 — gave|gave]] them to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.

## v20

And they all ate and were satisfied. And they took up what was left over of the broken pieces, [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 20.1 — twelve|twelve]] handbaskets full.

## v21

And those who ate were about five thousand men, apart from women and children.

## v22

And immediately He [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 22.1 — compelled|compelled]] the disciples to step into the boat and to go before Him to the other side, while He sent the crowds away.

## v23

And after He sent the crowds away, He went up to the mountain privately to [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 23.1 — pray|pray]]. And when [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 23.2 — night|night]] fell, He was there alone.

## v24

Now the boat was already [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 24.1 — in|in]] the midst of the sea, being battered by the waves, for the wind was contrary.

## v25

And in the [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 25.1 — fourth|fourth]] watch of the night He came to them, [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 25.2 — walking|walking]] on the sea.

## v26

And the disciples, seeing Him walking on the sea, were startled, saying, It is a ghost! And they cried out for fear.

## v27

But immediately Jesus talked to them, saying, Take courage. [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 27.1 — It|It]] is I. Do not be afraid.

## v28

And Peter answered Him and said, Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water.

## v29

And He said, Come. And Peter, coming down from the boat, walked on the water and came toward Jesus.

## v30

But [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 30.1 — seeing|seeing]] the strong wind, he became frightened; and as he began to sink, he cried out, saying, Lord, save me!

## v31

And immediately Jesus stretched out His hand and took hold of him and said to him, You of little faith, why did you [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 31.1 — doubt|doubt]]?

## v32

And when they got up into the boat, the [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 32.1 — wind|wind]] ceased.

## v33

And those who were in the boat worshipped Him, saying, Truly You are the [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 33.1 — Son|Son]] of God.

## v34

And they crossed over and came to land at Gennesaret.

## v35

And when the men of that place recognized Him, they sent word into all that surrounding region and brought to Him all who were ill.

## v36

And they begged Him that they might only touch the fringe[[Matthew-09.fn#Footnote 20.4 — fringe|fringe]] of His garment. And as many as touched were completely [[Matthew-14.fn#Footnote 36.2 — healed|healed]].
