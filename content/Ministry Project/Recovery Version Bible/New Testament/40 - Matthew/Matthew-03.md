Author: Matthew
---
## v1

Now in those days [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — John|John]] the Baptist appeared, preaching in the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 1.2 — wilderness|wilderness]] of Judea

## v2

And saying, [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Repent|Repent]], for the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens has [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 2.3 — drawn|drawn]] near.

## v3

For this is he who was spoken of through Isaiah the prophet, saying, “A voice of one crying in the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — wilderness|wilderness]], [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Prepare|Prepare]] the way of the Lord; make straight His paths.”

## v4

Now this John had his garment of [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — camel’s|camel’s]] hair and a leather girdle around his loins, and his food was [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — camel’s|locusts]] and wild honey.

## v5

At that time Jerusalem and all Judea and all the surrounding region of the Jordan went out to him,

## v6

And they were [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — baptized|baptized]] by him in the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 6.2 — Jordan|Jordan]] River as they confessed their sins.

## v7

But when he saw many of the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] and [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — Sadducees|Sadducees]] coming to his baptism, he said to them, Offspring of vipers, who prompted you to flee from the coming wrath?

## v8

Produce then fruit worthy of your repentance.

## v9

And do not presume to say within yourselves, We have Abraham as our father; for I tell you that God is able, out of these stones, to [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — raise|raise]] up children to Abraham.

## v10

And already the axe is laid at the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that does not produce good fruit is cut down and cast into the fire.

## v11

I baptize you in water unto repentance, but He who is coming after me is stronger than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He Himself will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — fire|fire]],

## v12

Whose winnowing fan is in His hand. And He will thoroughly cleanse His threshing floor and will gather His [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — wheat|wheat]] into the barn, but the chaff He will burn up with unquenchable fire.

## v13

Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to John to be [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — baptized|baptized]] by him.

## v14

But John tried to prevent Him, saying, It is I who have need of being baptized by You, and You come to me?

## v15

But Jesus answered and said to him, Permit it for now, for it is fitting for us in this way to fulfill all [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — righteousness|righteousness]]. Then he permitted Him.

## v16

And [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — having|having]] been baptized, Jesus went up immediately from the water, and behold, the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — heavens|heavens]] were opened to Him, and He saw the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 16.3 — Spirit|Spirit]] of God descending like a [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 16.4 — dove|dove]] and coming upon Him.

## v17

And behold, a voice out of the heavens, saying, [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — This|This]] is My Son, the Beloved, in whom I have found My delight.
