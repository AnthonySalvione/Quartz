# Matthew-23 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Matthew-23|Matthew 23]]

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — phylacteries

**Verse:** [[Matthew-23#v5|Matthew 23:5]]

Phylacteries are small leather boxes containing parts of the law written on parchment. According to [[Deuteronomy-06#v8|Deut 6:8]] and [[Deuteronomy-11#v18|11:18]], they were worn on the forehead as a frontlet and on the left arm. The scribes and the Pharisees broadened them and considered them a charm.

### Footnote 5.2 — fringes

**Verse:** [[Matthew-23#v5|Matthew 23:5]]

The law required the Israelites to make fringes on the borders of their garments with a band of blue. The fringes signified that their conduct (typified by the garment) was regulated by the heavenly rule (indicated by the band of blue), and they were to be a reminder to them to keep God’s commandments ([[Numbers-15#v38|Num. 15:38-39]]). The scribes and Pharisees enlarged the fringes, pretending that they were keeping God’s commandments and were being regulated by them to a surpassing degree.

## Footnotes for verse 37

### Footnote 37.1 — I

**Verse:** [[Matthew-23#v37|Matthew 23:37]]

It was always God Himself who cared for Jerusalem, as a bird flutters over her young ([[Isaiah-31#v5|Isa. 31:5]]; [[Deuteronomy-32#v11|Deut. 32:11-12]]). Hence, when the Lord Jesus said, “I desired to gather your children together, the way a hen gathers her brood under her wings,” He indicated that He was God Himself.

## Footnotes for verse 38

### Footnote 38.1 — house

**Verse:** [[Matthew-23#v38|Matthew 23:38]]

Since house here is singular, it must denote the house of God, which was the temple ([[Matthew-21#v12|21:12-13]]). It had been the house of God, but now it was called “your house” because the Jews had made it a den of robbers ([[Matthew-21#v13|21:13]]).

### Footnote 38.2 — desolate

**Verse:** [[Matthew-23#v38|Matthew 23:38]]

This prophecy corresponds with that in [[Matthew-24#v2|24:2]], which was fulfilled when Titus and the Roman army destroyed Jerusalem in A.D. 70.
