---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

In that hour the disciples came to Jesus, saying, Who then is greatest [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 1.1 — in|in]] the kingdom of the heavens?

## v2

And He called a little child to Him and stood him in their midst

## v3

And said, Truly I say to you, Unless you turn and become like little children, you shall by no means enter into the kingdom of the heavens.

## v4

He therefore who will humble himself like this little child, he is the greatest in the kingdom of the heavens.

## v5

And whoever receives one such little child [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 5.1 — because|because]] of My name, receives Me;

## v6

And whoever stumbles one of these little ones who believe into Me, it is [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 6.1 — more|more]] profitable for him that a [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 6.2 — great|great]] millstone be hung around his neck and he be drowned in the open sea.

## v7

Woe to the world because of stumbling blocks! For it is necessary for stumbling blocks to come, but woe to that man through whom the stumbling block comes.

## v8

If your hand or your foot stumbles you, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 29.1 — pluck|cut]] it off and cast it from you; it is better for you to enter into life maimed or lame than to have two hands or two feet and be cast into the eternal fire.

## v9

And if your eye stumbles you, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 29.1 — pluck|pluck]] it out and cast it from you; it is better for you to enter into life with one eye than to have two eyes and be cast into the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.8 — Gehenna|Gehenna]] of fire.

## v10

See that you do not despise one of these little ones, for I say to you that their angels in the heavens continually behold the face of My Father who is in the heavens.

## v11

[[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 11.1 — For|For]] the Son of Man has come to save that which is lost.

## v12

What do you think? If any man has a hundred sheep and one of them goes astray, will he not leave the ninety-nine on the mountains and go and seek the one that has gone astray?

## v13

And if it so happens that he finds it, truly I say to you that he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine which have not gone astray.

## v14

In the same way, it is not the will of [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 14.1 — your|your]] Father who is in the heavens that one of these little ones perish.

## v15

Moreover if your brother sins against you, go, [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 15.1 — reprove|reprove]] him [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 15.2 — between|between]] you and him alone. If he hears you, you have gained your brother.

## v16

But if he does not hear you, take with you one or two more, that by the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

## v17

And if he refuses to hear them, [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 17.1 — tell|tell]] it to the [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 17.2 — church|church]]; and if he refuses to hear the church also, let him be to you [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 17.3 — just|just]] like the Gentile and the tax collector.

## v18

Truly I say to you, Whatever you [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 18.1 — bind|bind]] on the earth shall [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.4 — have|have]] been bound in heaven, and whatever you [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 18.1 — bind|loose]] on the earth shall [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.4 — have|have]] been loosed in heaven.

## v19

Again, truly I say to you that if two of you are [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 19.1 — in|in]] harmony on earth concerning any matter for which they [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 19.2 — ask|ask]], [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 19.3 — it|it]] will be done for them from My Father who is in the heavens.

## v20

For where there are [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 20.1 — two|two]] or three [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 20.2 — gathered|gathered]] into My name, there am I in their midst.

## v21

Then Peter came and said to Him, Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me and I forgive him? Up to seven times?

## v22

Jesus said to him, I do not say to you, Up to seven times, but, Up to seventy times seven.

## v23

For this reason the kingdom of the heavens has become like a [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 23.1 — king|king]] who desired to [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 23.2 — settle|settle]] accounts with his slaves.

## v24

And when he began to settle them, one who owed him [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 24.1 — ten|ten]] thousand talents was brought to him.

## v25

But since he did not have the means to repay, the master commanded him to be sold, as well as his wife and children and all that he had, and repayment to be made.

## v26

Then the slave fell down and worshipped him, saying, Be patient with me and I will repay you all.

## v27

And the master of that slave was moved with compassion and released him and [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 27.1 — forgave|forgave]] him the loan.

## v28

But that slave went out and found one of his fellow slaves who owed him a [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 28.1 — hundred|hundred]] denarii, and he took hold of him and began to choke him, saying, Repay me what you owe.

## v29

Then his fellow slave fell down and begged him, saying, Be patient with me and I will repay you.

## v30

But he would not; instead, he went away and threw him into prison until he would repay what was owed.

## v31

Then his fellow slaves, seeing what had taken place, were greatly [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 31.1 — grieved|grieved]] and came and explained fully to their master all that had taken place.

## v32

Then his master called him to him and said to him, Evil slave, all that debt I forgave you, because you begged me.

## v33

Should you not also have had mercy on your fellow slave even as I had mercy on you?

## v34

And his master became angry and [[Matthew-18.fn#Footnote 34.1 — delivered|delivered]] him to the torturers until he would repay all that was owed.

## v35

So also will My heavenly Father do to you if each of you does not forgive his brother from your hearts.
