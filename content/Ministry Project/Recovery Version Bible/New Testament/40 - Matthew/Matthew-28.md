Author: Matthew
---
## v1

Now late on the Sabbath, as it began to [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 1.1 — dawn|dawn]] toward the [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 1.2 — first|first]] day of the week, Mary the Magdalene and the other Mary came to look at the grave.

## v2

And behold, there was a great [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 2.1 — earthquake|earthquake]], for an angel of the Lord descended out of heaven and came and rolled away the stone and sat upon it.

## v3

And his appearance was like lightning, and his garment as white as snow.

## v4

And for fear of him the guards shook and became like dead men.

## v5

And the angel answered and said to the women, Do not be afraid, for I know that you are seeking Jesus, the crucified.

## v6

[[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 6.1 — He|He]] is not here, for He has been raised, even as He said. Come, see the place where He was lying.

## v7

And go quickly and tell His disciples that He has been raised from the dead, and behold, He goes before you into [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Galilee|Galilee]]. There you will see Him. Behold, I have told you.

## v8

And they went away quickly from the tomb with [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 8.1 — fear|fear]] and great joy and ran to report this to His disciples.

## v9

And behold, Jesus [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 9.1 — met|met]] them, saying, [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Rejoice|Rejoice]]! And they came to Him and took hold of His feet and worshipped Him.

## v10

Then Jesus said to them, Do not be afraid. Go and report to My [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 10.1 — brothers|brothers]] that they should go into [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 10.2 — Galilee|Galilee]], and there they will see Me.

## v11

And as they were going, behold, some from the guard came into the city and reported to the chief priests all that had happened.

## v12

And after the chief priests had gathered with the elders and had taken counsel, they gave a large sum of money to the soldiers,

## v13

Saying, Say this, [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 13.1 — His|His]] disciples came by night and stole Him away while we slept.

## v14

And if this comes to the governor’s ears, we will [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 14.1 — persuade|persuade]] him and make sure that you have nothing to worry about.

## v15

And they took the money and did as they were instructed. And this account has been widely [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 15.1 — spread|spread]] among the Jews until this very day.

## v16

And the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 16.1 — mountain|mountain]] where Jesus directed them.

## v17

And when they saw Him, they worshipped Him, though some [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 17.1 — doubted|doubted]].

## v18

And Jesus came and spoke to them, saying, All [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 18.1 — authority|authority]] has been given to Me in heaven and on earth.

## v19

Go [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 19.1 — therefore|therefore]] and [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 19.2 — disciple|disciple]] all the nations, [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 19.3 — baptizing|baptizing]] them [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 19.4 — into|into]] the [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 19.5 — name|name]] of the [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 19.6 — Father|Father]] and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,

## v20

[[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Teaching|Teaching]] them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 20.2 — with|with]] you all the days until the consummation of the [[Matthew-28.fn#Footnote 20.3 — age|age]].
