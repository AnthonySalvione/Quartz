---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to His disciples,

## v2

Saying, The [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — priests|scribes]] and the [[Matthew-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Pharisees|Pharisees]] have seated themselves in Moses’ seat;

## v3

Therefore all that they tell you, do and keep; but do not do according to their works, for they say things and do not do them.

## v4

And they bind burdens, heavy and hard to bear, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with their finger.

## v5

And all their works they do to be seen by men, for they broaden their [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 5.1 — phylacteries|phylacteries]] and enlarge the [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 5.2 — fringes|fringes]] of their garments,

## v6

And they love the place of honor at the dinners and the chief seats in the synagogues

## v7

And the greetings in the marketplaces and to be called by men, [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Rabbi|Rabbi]].

## v8

But you, do not be called Rabbi, for [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 8.1 — One|One]] is your Teacher, and you are all brothers.

## v9

And do not call anyone on earth your father, for [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 9.1 — One|One]] is your Father, [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 9.2 — He|He]] who is in the heavens.

## v10

Neither be called [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 10.1 — instructors|instructors]], because [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 10.2 — One|One]] is your Instructor, the Christ.

## v11

And the greatest among you shall be your servant.

## v12

And he who will exalt himself shall be humbled, and he who will humble himself shall be exalted.

## v13

But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — hypocrites|hypocrites]]! For you close off the kingdom of the heavens in the face of men; for you do [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 13.2 — not|not]] enter in yourselves, nor do you allow those who are entering to enter.

## v14

[[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 14.1 — But|But]] woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour the widows’ houses, even while for a pretense you make long prayers. For this reason you shall receive greater [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 14.2 — judgment|judgment]].

## v15

Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you go about the sea and the dry land to make one proselyte; and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.8 — Gehenna|Gehenna]] as yourselves.

## v16

Woe to you, blind guides, who say, Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the temple, [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 16.1 — he|he]] is bound.

## v17

Fools and blind men, which is greater, the gold or the temple which [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 17.1 — sanctifies|sanctifies]] the gold?

## v18

And, Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift which is upon it, he is bound.

## v19

Blind men, which is greater, the gift or the altar which [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 19.1 — sanctifies|sanctifies]] the gift?

## v20

Therefore he who swears by the altar swears by it and by all the things upon it;

## v21

And he who swears by the temple swears by it and by Him who dwells in it;

## v22

And he who swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by Him who sits upon it.

## v23

Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you give a tenth of the mint and the anise and the cummin, and have neglected the weightier matters of the law — [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 23.1 — justice|justice]] and mercy and faithfulness. But these you should have done and [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 23.2 — not|not]] neglected the others.

## v24

Blind guides, who strain out the gnat but swallow the camel!

## v25

Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you cleanse the outside of the cup and the dish, but inwardly they are full of extortion and self-indulgence.

## v26

Blind Pharisee, cleanse first the inside of the cup and the dish so that their outside also may become clean.

## v27

Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you resemble whitewashed graves, which outwardly appear beautiful but inwardly are full of dead men’s bones and all uncleanness.

## v28

So you also outwardly appear righteous to men, but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.

## v29

Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you build up the graves of the prophets and adorn the tombs of the righteous,

## v30

And say, If we had been there in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.

## v31

So then you testify against yourselves that you are the sons of those who murdered the prophets.

## v32

And you, fill up the measure of your fathers!

## v33

Serpents! Brood of vipers! How shall you escape the judgment of Gehenna?

## v34

Therefore, behold, I send to you [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 34.1 — prophets|prophets]] and wise men and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify, and some of them you will scourge in your synagogues and persecute from city to city,

## v35

So that upon you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zachariah, son of Barachiah, whom you murdered between the temple and the altar.

## v36

Truly I say to you, [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 36.1 — All|All]] these things shall come upon this generation.

## v37

Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those who are sent to her! How often [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 37.1 — I|I]] desired to gather your children together, the way a hen gathers her brood under her wings, and you would not!

## v38

Behold, your [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 38.1 — house|house]] is left to you [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 38.2 — desolate|desolate]].

## v39

For I say to you, You shall by no means see Me from now on until you say, Blessed is [[Matthew-23.fn#Footnote 39.1 — He|He]] who comes in the name of the Lord.
