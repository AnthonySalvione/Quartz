---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

[[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 1.1 — At|At]] that time Jesus went on the Sabbath through the grainfields. And His disciples became hungry and began to pick ears of grain and eat.

## v2

But the Pharisees, seeing this, said to Him, Behold, Your disciples are doing what is [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 2.1 — not|not]] lawful to do on the Sabbath.

## v3

But He said to them, Have you not [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 3.1 — read|read]] what [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 3.2 — David|David]] did when he became hungry, and those who were with him;

## v4

How he entered into the house of God, and they ate the bread of the Presence, which was not lawful for him to eat, nor for those who were with him, except for the priests only?

## v5

Or have you not [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 5.1 — read|read]] in the law that on the Sabbath the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath and are guiltless?

## v6

But I say to you that something [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 6.1 — greater|greater]] than the temple is here.

## v7

But if you knew what this means, “I desire mercy and not sacrifice,” you would not have condemned the guiltless.

## v8

For the Son of Man is [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Lord|Lord]] of the Sabbath.

## v9

And departing from there, He came into their synagogue.

## v10

And behold, a man who had a withered [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 10.1 — hand|hand]] was there. And they asked Him, saying, Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath? so that they might accuse Him.

## v11

And He said to them, What man will there be among you who will have one sheep, and if it falls into a pit on the Sabbath, will not take hold of it and lift it out?

## v12

Of how much more value then is a man than a sheep! So then it is lawful to do well on the Sabbath.

## v13

Then He said to the man, Stretch out your hand. And he stretched it out and it was [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 13.1 — restored|restored]], as sound as the other.

## v14

But the Pharisees, going out, took counsel against Him as to how they might [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 14.1 — destroy|destroy]] Him.

## v15

But Jesus, knowing it, withdrew from there. And many followed Him, and He healed them all;

## v16

And He charged them not to make Him known,

## v17

In order that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

## v18

“Behold, My [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Servant|Servant]] whom I have chosen, My Beloved in whom My soul has found delight. I will put My Spirit upon Him, and He will announce [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 18.2 — justice|justice]] to the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 18.3 — Gentiles|Gentiles]].

## v19

He will not strive nor cry out, nor will anyone hear His voice in the streets.

## v20

A [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 20.1 — bruised|bruised]] reed He will not break, and smoking [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 20.2 — flax|flax]] He will not quench until He brings forth justice unto victory.

## v21

And in His name will the Gentiles hope.”

## v22

Then there was brought to Him one possessed by a demon, [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 22.1 — blind|blind]] and dumb, and He healed him, so that the dumb man spoke and saw.

## v23

And all the crowds were amazed and said, Is this not the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Son|Son]] of David?

## v24

But the Pharisees, hearing this, said, This man does not cast out the demons except by [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 24.1 — Beelzebul|Beelzebul]], ruler of the demons.

## v25

But knowing their thoughts, He said to them, Every kingdom divided against itself becomes desolate, and every city or house divided against itself will not stand.

## v26

And if Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then will his [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 26.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] stand?

## v27

And if I, by Beelzebul, cast out the demons, by whom do your sons cast them out? Therefore they shall be your judges.

## v28

But if I, by the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Spirit|Spirit]] of God, cast out the demons, then the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 28.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God has come upon you.

## v29

Or how can anyone enter into the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 29.1 — house|house]] of the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 29.2 — strong|strong]] man and plunder his [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 29.3 — goods|goods]] unless he first [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 29.4 — binds|binds]] the strong man? And then he will thoroughly plunder his house.

## v30

[[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 30.1 — He|He]] who is not with Me is against Me, and he who does not gather with Me scatters.

## v31

Therefore I say to you, Every sin and [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 31.1 — blasphemy|blasphemy]] will be forgiven men, but blasphemy against the Spirit will not be forgiven.

## v32

And whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but whoever [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 32.1 — speaks|speaks]] against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, neither in this [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 32.2 — age|age]] nor in the one to come.

## v33

Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree corrupt and its fruit corrupt; for [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 33.1 — by|by]] the fruit the tree is known.

## v34

Offspring of vipers, how can you, being evil, speak good things? For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks.

## v35

The good man, out of his good treasure, brings forth good things, and the evil man, out of his evil treasure, brings forth evil things.

## v36

And I say to you that every [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 36.1 — idle|idle]] word which men shall speak, they will render an account concerning it in the day of judgment.

## v37

For by your [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 37.1 — words|words]] you shall be justified, and by your words you shall be condemned.

## v38

Then some of the scribes and Pharisees answered Him, saying, Teacher, we want to see a [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 38.1 — sign|sign]] from You.

## v39

But He answered and said to them, An evil and adulterous [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 39.1 — generation|generation]] seeks after a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.

## v40

For just as Jonah was in the belly of the great fish three days and three nights, so will the Son of Man be in the [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 40.1 — heart|heart]] of the earth three days and three nights.

## v41

Ninevite men will stand up in the judgment with this generation and will condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah, and behold, something [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 41.1 — more|more]] than Jonah is here.

## v42

The queen of the south will rise up in the judgment with this generation and will condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and behold, something more than Solomon is here.

## v43

When the unclean spirit goes out from the man, it roams through [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 43.1 — waterless|waterless]] places, seeking rest, and does not find it.

## v44

Then it says, I will return to my house from which I came out. And it comes and finds it unoccupied, swept, and decorated.

## v45

Then it goes and takes along with itself seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they enter in and settle down there. And the last state of that man becomes worse than the first. Thus shall it be also with [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 45.1 — this|this]] evil generation.

## v46

While He was still speaking to the crowds, behold, His mother and His brothers stood outside seeking to speak to Him.

## v47

And someone said to Him, Behold, Your mother and Your brothers are standing outside seeking to speak to You.

## v48

But He answered and said to him who spoke to Him, [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 48.1 — Who|Who]] is My mother, and who are My brothers?

## v49

And stretching out His hand toward His disciples, He said, Behold, My mother and My brothers!

## v50

For whoever [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 50.1 — does|does]] the will of My Father who is in the heavens, he is My brother and sister and mother.
