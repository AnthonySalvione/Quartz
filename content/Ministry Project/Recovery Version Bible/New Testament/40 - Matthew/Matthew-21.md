---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And when they [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 1.1 — drew|drew]] near to Jerusalem and came to Bethphage at the Mount of Olives, then Jesus sent two disciples,

## v2

Saying to them, Go into the village opposite you, and immediately you will [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 2.1 — find|find]] a donkey tied and a colt with her; untie them and lead them to Me.

## v3

And if anyone says anything to you, you shall say, The [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Lord|Lord]] has need of them; [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 3.2 — and|and]] immediately he will send them.

## v4

Now this took place in order that what was spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,

## v5

“Say to the [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 5.1 — daughter|daughter]] of Zion, Behold, your King is coming to you, meek and mounted on a donkey, and on a colt, a [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 5.2 — foal|foal]] of a beast of burden.”

## v6

And the disciples went and did as Jesus directed them,

## v7

And led the donkey and the colt. And they put their [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 7.1 — garments|garments]] on them, and He sat upon them.

## v8

And most of the crowd spread their own garments in the road, and others cut [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 8.1 — branches|branches]] from the trees and spread them in the road.

## v9

And the crowds who went before Him and those who followed cried out, saying, [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 9.1 — Hosanna|Hosanna]] to the [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Son|Son]] of David! Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!

## v10

And when He entered into Jerusalem, all the city was stirred, saying, Who is this?

## v11

And the crowds said, This is the prophet Jesus from Nazareth of Galilee.

## v12

And Jesus entered into the temple and cast out all those who were selling and buying in the temple. And He overturned the tables of the money changers and the seats of those who were selling the doves,

## v13

And He said to them, It is written, “My house shall be called a house of prayer,” but you are making it a den of robbers.

## v14

And the blind and the lame came to Him in the temple, and He healed them.

## v15

But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the wonders that He did and the children who were crying out in the temple and saying, Hosanna to the Son of David, they were [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 15.1 — indignant|indignant]].

## v16

And they said to Him, Do You hear what these are saying? And Jesus said to them, Yes. Have you never read, “Out of the mouth of infants and sucklings You have perfected praise”?

## v17

And leaving them, He went out of the city to [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Bethany|Bethany]] and lodged there.

## v18

Now in the morning as He returned to the city, He became [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 18.1 — hungry|hungry]].

## v19

And seeing a [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 19.1 — fig|fig]] tree on the way, He came to it and found nothing on it except leaves only. And He said to it, May there [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 19.2 — no|no]] longer be fruit from you forever! And the fig tree was instantly [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 19.3 — dried|dried]] up.

## v20

And when the disciples saw this, they marveled, saying, How did the fig tree instantly dry up?

## v21

And Jesus answered and said to them, Truly I say to you, If you have faith and do not doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, Be taken up and cast into the sea, it will happen.

## v22

And all that you ask in prayer, if you believe, you will receive.

## v23

And when He came into the temple, the chief priests and elders of the people came to Him as He was teaching and said, By what authority do You do these things? And who gave You this authority?

## v24

And Jesus answered and said to them, I also will ask you one [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 24.1 — thing|thing]], which if you tell Me, I also will tell you by what authority I do these things.

## v25

The baptism of John, from where did it come, from heaven or from men? And they reasoned among themselves, saying, If we say, From heaven, He will say to us, Why then did you not believe him?

## v26

But if we say, From men, we fear the crowd, for all hold John as a prophet.

## v27

And they answered Jesus and said, We do [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 27.1 — not|not]] know. He too said to them, [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 27.2 — Neither|Neither]] do I tell you by what authority I do these things.

## v28

But what do you think? A man had two children. And he came to his first son and said, Child, go today and work in the vineyard.

## v29

And he answered and said, I do not want to; yet later he regretted and went.

## v30

And he came to the other and said likewise. And he answered and said, I will, sir; yet he did not go.

## v31

Which of the two did the will of the father? They said, The [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 31.1 — first|first]]. Jesus said to them, Truly I say to you that the tax collectors and the harlots are going into the kingdom of God before you.

## v32

For John came to you in the way of [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 32.1 — righteousness|righteousness]], and you did not believe him, but the tax collectors and the harlots believed him. And you, when you saw it, did not later regret it to believe him.

## v33

Hear another parable. There was a man, a [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 33.1 — householder|householder]], who planted a [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 33.1 — householder|vineyard]] and put a hedge around it, and dug a winepress in it, and built a tower; and he leased it out to [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 33.1 — householder|vinedressers]] and went abroad.

## v34

And when the time to harvest the fruit drew near, he sent his [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 34.1 — slaves|slaves]] to the vinedressers to get his fruit.

## v35

And the vinedressers took his slaves and [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 35.1 — beat|beat]] one, and killed another, and stoned another.

## v36

Again, he sent some other slaves, more than the first, and they did likewise to them.

## v37

Then later he sent to them his [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 37.1 — son|son]], saying, They will respect my son.

## v38

But the vinedressers, when they saw the son, said among themselves, [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 38.1 — This|This]] is the heir. Come, let us kill him and take possession of his inheritance.

## v39

And they took him and cast him [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 39.1 — out|out]] of the vineyard and killed him.

## v40

Therefore when the master of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those vinedressers?

## v41

They said to Him, He will miserably [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 41.1 — destroy|destroy]] those evil men and will lease the vineyard to [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 41.2 — other|other]] vinedressers, who will give the fruit to him in their seasons.

## v42

Jesus said to them, Have you never read in the Scriptures, “The [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 42.1 — stone|stone]] which the [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 42.1 — stone|builders]] rejected, this has become the [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 42.2 — head|head]] of the corner. This was from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes”?

## v43

Therefore I say to you that the [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 43.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of God shall be taken from you and shall be given to a [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 43.2 — nation|nation]] producing its fruit.

## v44

And [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 44.1 — he|he]] who falls on this stone shall be broken to pieces; but on [[Matthew-21.fn#Footnote 44.2 — whomever|whomever]] it falls, it shall crush him to powder and scatter him like chaff.

## v45

And when the chief priests and the Pharisees heard His parables, they perceived that He was speaking concerning them.

## v46

And though they sought to seize Him, they feared the crowds because they held Him as a prophet.
