---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And when Jesus had finished giving instructions to His twelve disciples, He departed from there to teach and to preach in their cities.

## v2

Now when John heard in the prison of the works of the Christ, he sent word through his disciples

## v3

And said to Him, [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Are|Are]] You the Coming One, or should we expect [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 3.2 — another|another]]?

## v4

And Jesus answered and said to them, Go, report to John the things that you hear and see:

## v5

The [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 5.1 — blind|blind]] receive their sight and the [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 5.2 — lame|lame]] walk; the [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 5.3 — lepers|lepers]] are cleansed and the [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 5.4 — deaf|deaf]] hear; and the [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 5.5 — dead|dead]] are raised and the [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 5.6 — poor|poor]] have the gospel announced to them;

## v6

And blessed is he who is not [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 6.1 — stumbled|stumbled]] because of Me.

## v7

And as these went away, Jesus began to [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 7.1 — say|say]] to the crowds concerning John, What did you go out into the wilderness to [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 7.2 — look|look]] at? A [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 7.3 — reed|reed]] shaken by the wind?

## v8

But what did you go out to [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 8.1 — see|see]]? A [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 8.2 — man|man]] arrayed in soft clothing? Behold, those who wear soft clothing are in the houses of kings.

## v9

But why did you go out? To see a prophet? Yes, I say to you, and [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 9.1 — much|much]] more than a prophet.

## v10

This is he concerning whom it is written, “Behold, I send My messenger before Your face, who will prepare Your way before You.”

## v11

Truly I say to you, Among those born of women there has not arisen one greater than John the Baptist, yet he who is [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 11.1 — least|least]] [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 11.2 — in|in]] the kingdom of the heavens is greater than he.

## v12

But from the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of the heavens is taken by [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 12.1 — violence|violence]], and violent men seize it.

## v13

For all the prophets and the law prophesied [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 13.1 — until|until]] John;

## v14

And if you are willing to receive [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 14.1 — it|it]], he is [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 14.2 — Elijah|Elijah]], who is to come.

## v15

He who has ears to hear, let him hear.

## v16

But to what shall I liken this generation? It is like little children sitting in the marketplaces, who call to the others

## v17

And say, We have [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 17.1 — played|played]] the flute to you, and you did not dance; we have sung a dirge, and you did not mourn.

## v18

For John came [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 18.1 — neither|neither]] eating nor drinking; and they say, He has a [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 18.2 — demon|demon]].

## v19

The Son of Man came [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 18.1 — neither|eating]] and drinking; and they say, Behold, a gluttonous man and a drunkard, a [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 19.1 — friend|friend]] of tax collectors and sinners. Yet [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 19.2 — wisdom|wisdom]] is justified by her [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 19.3 — works|works]].

## v20

Then He began to reproach the cities in which most of His works of power took place, because they did not repent:

## v21

Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the works of power which took place in you had taken place in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.

## v22

But I say to you, It will be [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 22.1 — more|more]] tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the day of judgment than for you.

## v23

And you, Capernaum, who have been exalted to heaven, to [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Hades|Hades]] you will be brought down. For if the works of power which took place in you had taken place in Sodom, it would have remained until today.

## v24

But I say to you that it will be more tolerable for the land of Sodom in the day of judgment than for you.

## v25

At that time Jesus [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 25.1 — answered|answered]] and said, I [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 25.2 — extol|extol]] You, Father, [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 25.3 — Lord|Lord]] of heaven and of earth, because You have hidden [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 25.4 — these|these]] things from the [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 25.5 — wise|wise]] and intelligent and have revealed them to [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 25.6 — infants|infants]].

## v26

Yes, Father, for thus it has been well pleasing [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 26.1 — in|in]] Your sight.

## v27

[[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 27.1 — All|All]] things have been delivered to Me by My Father, and no one fully [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 27.2 — knows|knows]] the Son except the Father; neither does anyone fully [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 27.2 — knows|know]] the Father except the Son and him to whom the Son wills to reveal Him.

## v28

Come to Me all who [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 28.1 — toil|toil]] and are burdened, and I will give you [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 28.2 — rest|rest]].

## v29

[[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 29.1 — Take|Take]] My [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 29.2 — yoke|yoke]] upon you and learn from Me, for I am [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 29.3 — meek|meek]] and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 29.4 — souls|souls]].

## v30

For My [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 30.1 — yoke|yoke]] is [[Matthew-11.fn#Footnote 30.2 — easy|easy]] and My burden is light.
