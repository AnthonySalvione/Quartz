---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And when He came [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 1.1 — down|down]] from the mountain, great crowds followed Him.

## v2

And behold, a [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 2.1 — leper|leper]], coming near, [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 2.2 — worshipped|worshipped]] Him, saying, Lord, if You are willing, You can cleanse me.

## v3

And stretching out His hand, He [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 3.1 — touched|touched]] him, saying, I am willing; be cleansed! And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.

## v4

And Jesus said to him, See that you tell no one; but go, [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 4.1 — show|show]] yourself to the priest, and offer the gift which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.

## v5

And when He entered into Capernaum, a [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 5.1 — centurion|centurion]] came to Him, beseeching Him

## v6

And saying, Lord, my servant is lying in the house paralyzed, terribly tormented.

## v7

And He said to him, I will come and heal him.

## v8

But the centurion answered and said, Lord, I am not fit for You to enter under my roof; but only speak a word, and my servant will be healed.

## v9

For I also am a man under [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 9.1 — authority|authority]], having soldiers under me. And I say to this one, Go, and he goes; and to another, Come, and he comes; and to my slave, Do this, and he does it.

## v10

Now when Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those who followed, Truly I say to you, [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 10.1 — With|With]] no one in Israel have I found such great faith.

## v11

But I say to you that many will come [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 11.1 — from|from]] the east and the west and will recline at table with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 11.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens,

## v12

But the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.1 — sons|sons]] of the kingdom will be cast out into the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.2 — outer|outer]] darkness. In that place there will be the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.3 — weeping|weeping]] and the gnashing of teeth.

## v13

And Jesus said to the centurion, Go; as you have believed, so be it done to you. And his servant was healed in that hour.

## v14

And Jesus, coming into Peter’s house, saw [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 14.1 — his|his]] mother-in-law lying down and in a fever.

## v15

And He touched her hand, and the fever left her. And she arose and served Him.

## v16

And when evening fell, they brought to Him [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 16.1 — many|many]] who were demon-possessed, and He cast out the spirits with a word, and [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 16.1 — many|all]] those who were ill He healed,

## v17

So that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, “[[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 17.1 — He|He]] Himself took away our infirmities and bore our diseases.”

## v18

Now when Jesus saw a crowd around Him, He gave orders to [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 18.1 — depart|depart]] to the other side.

## v19

And a scribe came near and said to Him, Teacher, I will [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 19.1 — follow|follow]] You wherever You [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 19.2 — go|go]].

## v20

And Jesus said to him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of heaven have roosts, but the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 20.1 — Son|Son]] of Man has [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 20.2 — nowhere|nowhere]] to lay His head.

## v21

And another of the disciples said to Him, Lord, permit me first to [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 21.1 — go|go]] and bury my father.

## v22

But Jesus said to him, Follow Me, and let the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 22.1 — dead|dead]] bury their own [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 22.1 — dead|dead]].

## v23

And when He stepped into the boat, His disciples followed Him.

## v24

And behold, there arose a great [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 24.1 — tempest|tempest]] in the sea, so that the boat was covered by the waves; but He was sleeping.

## v25

And they came near and roused Him, saying, Lord, save us; we are perishing!

## v26

And He said to them, Why are you cowardly, you of little [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 26.1 — faith|faith]]? Then He arose and [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 26.2 — rebuked|rebuked]] the winds and the sea, and there was a [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 26.3 — great|great]] calm.

## v27

And the men marveled, saying, What kind of man is this that even the winds and the sea [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 27.1 — obey|obey]] Him?

## v28

And when He came to the other side, into the region of the Gadarenes, two possessed by demons met Him, as they were coming out of the tombs, so exceedingly fierce that no one was able to pass by on that road.

## v29

And behold, they cried out, saying, [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 29.1 — What|What]] do we have to do with You, [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 29.2 — Son|Son]] of God? Have You come here [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 29.3 — before|before]] the time to torment us?

## v30

Now there was, a good way off from them, a herd of many hogs feeding.

## v31

And the demons [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 31.1 — entreated|entreated]] Him, saying, If You cast us out, send us into the herd of hogs.

## v32

And He said to them, [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 32.1 — Go|Go]]! And they came out and went into the hogs. And behold, the whole herd [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 32.2 — rushed|rushed]] down the steep slope into the sea, and they died in the waters.

## v33

And those who were feeding them fled and went away into the city and reported everything, including what had happened to those possessed by demons.

## v34

And behold, the whole city came out to meet Jesus; and when they saw Him, they entreated Him to [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 34.1 — depart|depart]] from their borders.
