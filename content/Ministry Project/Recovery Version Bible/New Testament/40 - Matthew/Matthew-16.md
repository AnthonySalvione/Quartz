---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And the Pharisees and Sadducees came and, testing Him, asked Him to show them a [[Matthew-12.fn#Footnote 38.1 — sign|sign]] out of heaven.

## v2

But He answered and said to them, [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 2.1 — When|When]] evening falls, you say, There will be fair weather, for the sky is red;

## v3

And in the morning, It will be stormy today, for the sky is red and gloomy. The [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 3.1 — face|face]] of the sky you know how to discern, but you cannot [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 3.2 — discern|discern]] the signs of the times.

## v4

An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it, except the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 4.1 — sign|sign]] of Jonah. And He left them and went away.

## v5

And when the disciples came to the other side, they had forgotten to take [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 5.1 — bread|bread]].

## v6

And Jesus said to them, Watch and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

## v7

But they reasoned among themselves, saying, It is because we did not take bread.

## v8

And Jesus, knowing it, said, Why are you reasoning among yourselves, you of little faith, because you have no bread?

## v9

Do you not yet understand nor remember the five loaves of the five thousand and how many [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 9.1 — handbaskets|handbaskets]] you took up?

## v10

Nor the seven loaves of the four thousand and how many [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 10.1 — baskets|baskets]] you took up?

## v11

How is it that you do not understand that it was not about bread that I spoke to you? But beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

## v12

Then they understood that He did not say to beware of the leaven of bread, but of the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 12.1 — teaching|teaching]] of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

## v13

Now when Jesus came into the parts of [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Caesarea|Caesarea]] Philippi, He asked His disciples, saying, Who do men say that the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 13.2 — Son|Son]] of Man is?

## v14

And they said, [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Some|Some]], John the Baptist; and others, Elijah; and still others, Jeremiah or one of the prophets.

## v15

He said to them, But you, who do you say that I am?

## v16

And Simon Peter answered and said, You are the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Christ|Christ]], the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Christ|Son]] of the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 16.2 — living|living]] God.

## v17

And Jesus answered and said to him, Blessed are you, Simon [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Barjona|Barjona]], because [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 17.2 — flesh|flesh]] and blood has not revealed this to you, but My [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 17.3 — Father|Father]] who is in the heavens.

## v18

And I [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 18.1 — also|also]] say to you that you are [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 18.2 — Peter|Peter]], and upon [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 18.3 — this|this]] rock I will [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 18.4 — build|build]] My [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 18.5 — church|church]], and the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 18.6 — gates|gates]] of Hades shall not prevail against it.

## v19

I will give to you the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.1 — keys|keys]] of the [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens, and whatever you [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.3 — bind|bind]] on the earth shall [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.4 — have|have]] been bound in the heavens, and whatever you [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.3 — bind|loose]] on the earth shall [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 19.4 — have|have]] been loosed in the heavens.

## v20

Then He charged the disciples [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 20.1 — not|not]] to tell anyone that He was the Christ.

## v21

From that time Jesus began to show to His disciples that He [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 21.1 — must|must]] go to Jerusalem and suffer many things from the elders and chief priests and scribes and be killed and on the third day be raised.

## v22

And Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him, saying, God be merciful to You, Lord! This shall by [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 22.1 — no|no]] means happen to You!

## v23

But He turned and said to Peter, Get behind Me, [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Satan|Satan]]! [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 23.2 — You|You]] are a stumbling block to Me, for you are [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 23.3 — not|not]] setting your mind on the things of God, but on the things of men.

## v24

Then Jesus said to His disciples, If anyone wants to come after Me, let him [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 24.1 — deny|deny]] [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 24.2 — himself|himself]] and take up his [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 24.3 — cross|cross]] and [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 24.4 — follow|follow]] Me.

## v25

For whoever wants to save his soul-life shall [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 25.1 — lose|lose]] it; but whoever [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 39.1 — finds|loses]] his soul-life for My sake shall find it.

## v26

For what shall a man be profited if he gains the whole world, but forfeits his [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 26.1 — life|soul-life]]? Or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul-life?

## v27

[[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 27.1 — For|For]] the Son of Man is to come in the glory of His Father with His angels, and then He will [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 27.2 — repay|repay]] each man according to his doings.

## v28

Truly I say to you, There are some of those standing here who shall by no means taste death until they see the Son of Man [[Matthew-16.fn#Footnote 28.1 — coming|coming]] in His kingdom.
