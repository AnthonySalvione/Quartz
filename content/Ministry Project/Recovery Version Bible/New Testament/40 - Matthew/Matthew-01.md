## v1

The book of the generation of [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Jesus|Jesus]] [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Christ|Christ]], the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — son|son]] of David, the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 1.4 — son|son]] of Abraham:

## v2

Abraham begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Isaac|Isaac]], and Isaac begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 2.2 — Jacob|Jacob]], and Jacob begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 2.3 — Judah|Judah]] and his [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 2.4 — brothers|brothers]],

## v3

And Judah begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Pharez|Pharez]] and Zarah of [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — Tamar|Tamar]], and Pharez begot Hezron, and Hezron begot Aram,

## v4

And Aram begot Aminadab, and Aminadab begot Nahshon, and Nahshon begot Salmon,

## v5

And Salmon begot Boaz of [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — Rahab|Rahab]], and [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — Boaz|Boaz]] begot Obed of [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — Ruth|Ruth]], and Obed begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 5.4 — Jesse|Jesse]],

## v6

And Jesse begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — David|David]] the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — king|king]]. And David begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 6.3 — Solomon|Solomon]] of her who had been the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 6.4 — wife|wife]] of Uriah,

## v7

And Solomon begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — Rehoboam|Rehoboam]], and Rehoboam begot Abijah, and Abijah begot Asa,

## v8

And Asa begot Jehoshaphat, and Jehoshaphat begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Joram|Joram]], and Joram begot Uzziah,

## v9

And Uzziah begot Jotham, and Jotham begot Ahaz, and Ahaz begot Hezekiah,

## v10

And Hezekiah begot Manasseh, and Manasseh begot Amon, and Amon begot Josiah.

## v11

And [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — Josiah|Josiah]] begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — Jeconiah|Jeconiah]] and his [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 11.3 — brothers|brothers]] at the time of the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 11.4 — deportation|deportation]] to Babylon.

## v12

And after the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — deportation|deportation]] to Babylon, Jeconiah begot Salathiel, and Salathiel begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — Zerubbabel|Zerubbabel]],

## v13

And Zerubbabel begot Abiud, and Abiud begot Eliakim, and Eliakim begot Azor,

## v14

And Azor begot Zadok, and Zadok begot Achim, and Achim begot Eliud,

## v15

And Eliud begot Eleazar, and Eleazar begot Matthan, and Matthan begot Jacob,

## v16

And [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — Jacob|Jacob]] begot [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — Joseph|Joseph]] the husband of [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — Mary|Mary]], of whom was born Jesus, who is called [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 16.4 — Christ|Christ]].

## v17

Thus [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — all|all]] the generations from Abraham [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — until|until]] David are fourteen generations, and from David [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 17.3 — until|until]] the deportation to Babylon, fourteen generations, and from the deportation to Babylon [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 17.4 — until|until]] the Christ, fourteen generations.

## v18

Now the origin [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — of|of]] Jesus Christ was in this way: His mother, Mary, after she had been engaged to Joseph, before they came together, was found to be with child of the Holy Spirit.

## v19

And Joseph her husband, being [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — righteous|righteous]] and not willing to disgrace her openly, intended to [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — send|send]] her away secretly.

## v20

But while he pondered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary your wife, for that which has been [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — begotten|begotten]] in her is [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — of|of]] the Holy Spirit.

## v21

And she will bear a son, and you shall call His name [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Jesus|Jesus]], for it is He who will save His people from their sins.

## v22

Now all this has happened so that what was spoken by the Lord through the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — prophet|prophet]] might be fulfilled, saying,

## v23

“Behold, the virgin shall be with child and shall bear a [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — son|son]], and they shall call His name [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 23.2 — Emmanuel|Emmanuel]]” (which is translated, God [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 23.3 — with|with]] us).

## v24

And when Joseph awoke from his sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him and took to himself his wife.

## v25

And he did not know her until she [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — bore|bore]] a son. And he called His name Jesus.
