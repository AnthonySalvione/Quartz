## v1

And Jesus came out [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 1.1 — from|from]] the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 1.2 — temple|temple]] and was going away, and His disciples came to Him to show Him the buildings of the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 1.2 — temple|temple]].

## v2

But He answered and said to them, Do you not see all these things? Truly I say to you, There shall by no means be left here a stone upon a stone, which shall not be [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 2.1 — thrown|thrown]] down.

## v3

And as He sat on the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Mount|Mount]] of Olives, the disciples came to Him privately, saying, Tell us, [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.2 — When|When]] will these things be? And what will be the sign of Your [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.3 — coming|coming]] and of the consummation of the age?

## v4

And Jesus [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 4.1 — answered|answered]] and said to them, [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 4.2 — See|See]] that no one leads you astray.

## v5

For many will come in My name, saying, I am the Christ, and they will lead many astray.

## v6

And you will be hearing of [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 6.1 — wars|wars]] and rumors of wars. See that you are not alarmed, for it must happen; but the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 6.2 — end|end]] is not yet.

## v7

For [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 7.1 — nation|nation]] will rise up against nation, and [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 7.1 — nation|kingdom]] against kingdom; and there will be [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 7.2 — famines|famines]] and [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 7.3 — earthquakes|earthquakes]] in various places.

## v8

All these things are the beginning of [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 8.1 — birth|birth]] pangs.

## v9

Then they will deliver [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 9.1 — you|you]] up to tribulation and will kill you, and you will be hated by all the nations because of My name.

## v10

And at that time many will be stumbled and will deliver up one another and will hate one another.

## v11

And many false prophets will arise and will lead many astray.

## v12

And because lawlessness will be multiplied, the love of the many will grow cold.

## v13

But he who has endured to the end, this one shall be [[Matthew-10.fn#Footnote 22.1 — saved|saved]].

## v14

And this [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 14.1 — gospel|gospel]] of the kingdom will be preached in the whole inhabited earth for a [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 14.2 — testimony|testimony]] to all the nations, and then the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 6.2 — end|end]] will come.

## v15

[[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Therefore|Therefore]] when you see the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 15.2 — abomination|abomination]] of [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 15.3 — desolation|desolation]], which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 15.4 — holy|holy]] place (let him who reads understand),

## v16

Then let those in Judea flee to the mountains;

## v17

Let him who is on the housetop not come down to take the things out of his house;

## v18

And let him who is in the field not turn back to take his garment.

## v19

But woe to those who are [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 19.1 — pregnant|pregnant]] and to those who are nursing babies in those days.

## v20

And pray that your flight may not be in [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 20.1 — winter|winter]], nor on a [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 20.2 — Sabbath|Sabbath]],

## v21

For at that time there will be [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 21.1 — great|great]] tribulation, such as has not occurred from the beginning of the world until now, nor shall by any means ever occur.

## v22

And unless those days had been cut [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 22.1 — short|short]], no flesh would be saved; but on account of the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 22.2 — chosen|chosen]], those days will be cut short.

## v23

At that time if anyone says to you, Behold, here is the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Christ|Christ]]! or, Here! do not believe it.

## v24

For [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 24.1 — false|false]] Christs and [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 24.2 — falsed|false]] prophets will arise and will [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 24.3 — show|show]] great signs and wonders so as to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen.

## v25

Behold, I have told you beforehand.

## v26

Therefore if they say to you, Behold, He is in the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 26.1 — wilderness|wilderness]], do not go forth; Behold, He is in the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 26.2 — inner|inner]] rooms, do not believe it.

## v27

For just as the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 27.1 — lightning|lightning]] comes forth from the east and shines to the west, so will the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.3 — coming|coming]] of the Son of Man be.

## v28

Wherever the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 28.1 — corpse|corpse]] is, there will the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 28.1 — corpse|vultures]] be gathered together.

## v29

And immediately [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 29.1 — after|after]] the tribulation of those days, the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 29.2 — sun|sun]] will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken.

## v30

And at that time the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.1 — sign|sign]] of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and then all the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.2 — tribes|tribes]] of the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.2 — tribes|land]] will [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.3 — mourn|mourn]], and they will see the Son of Man coming [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.4 — on|on]] the clouds of heaven with [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 30.5 — power|power]] and great glory.

## v31

And He will send His angels with a loud trumpet call, and they will [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 31.1 — gather|gather]] His chosen together from the four winds, from one end of the heavens to the other end.

## v32

[[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 32.1 — But|But]] learn the parable from the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 32.2 — fig|fig]] tree: As soon as its branch has [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 32.3 — become|become]] tender and [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 32.4 — puts|puts]] forth its leaves, you know that the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 32.5 — summer|summer]] is near.

## v33

So also you, when you see [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 33.1 — all|all]] these things, know that [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 33.2 — it|it]] is near, at the doors.

## v34

Truly I say to you that this [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 34.1 — generation|generation]] shall by no means pass away until all these things happen.

## v35

Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words shall by no means pass away.

## v36

But concerning that day and hour, no one knows, not even the angels of the heavens nor the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 36.1 — Son|Son]], but the Father only.

## v37

For just as the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 37.1 — days|days]] of Noah were, so will the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.3 — coming|coming]] of the Son of Man be.

## v38

[[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 38.1 — For|For]] as they were in those days before the flood, [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 38.2 — eating|eating]] and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day in which Noah entered into the ark,

## v39

And they did not know that judgment was [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 3.3 — coming|coming]] until the flood came and took all away, so also will the coming of the Son of Man be.

## v40

[[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 40.1 — At|At]] that time two [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 40.2 — men|men]] will be [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 40.3 — in|in]] the field; one is [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 40.4 — taken|taken]] and one is left.

## v41

Two [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 41.1 — women|women]] will be [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 40.3 — in|grinding]] at the mill; one is [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 40.4 — taken|taken]] and one is left.

## v42

[[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 42.1 — Watch|Watch]] therefore, for you do not know on what day [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 42.1 — Watch|your]] Lord comes.

## v43

But know this, that if the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 43.1 — householder|householder]] had known in which watch the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 43.2 — thief|thief]] was coming, he would have watched and would not have allowed his house to be broken into.

## v44

For this reason you also be ready, because at an hour when you do not expect it, the Son of Man is [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 44.1 — coming|coming]].

## v45

Who then is the [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 45.1 — faithful|faithful]] and [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 45.1 — faithful|prudent]] slave, whom the master has set over his [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 45.2 — household|household]] to [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 45.3 — give|give]] them food at the proper time?

## v46

[[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 46.1 — Blessed|Blessed]] is that slave whom his master, when he comes, will find so doing.

## v47

Truly I say to you that he will set him [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 47.1 — over|over]] all his possessions.

## v48

But if that [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 48.1 — evil|evil]] slave says in his heart, My master delays,

## v49

And begins to [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 49.1 — beat|beat]] his fellow slaves and [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 49.2 — eats|eats]] and drinks with the drunken,

## v50

The master of that slave will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour which he does not know,

## v51

And will [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 51.1 — cut|cut]] him asunder and appoint his [[Matthew-24.fn#Footnote 51.2 — portion|portion]] with the [[Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 2.2 — hypocrites|hypocrites]]. In that place there will be the [[Matthew-08.fn#Footnote 12.3 — weeping|weeping]] and the gnashing of teeth.
