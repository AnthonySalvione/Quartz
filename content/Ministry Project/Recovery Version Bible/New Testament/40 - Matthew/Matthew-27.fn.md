# Matthew-27 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[Matthew-27|Matthew 27]]

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — Pilate

**Verse:** [[Matthew-27#v2|Matthew 27:2]]

Pilate, an agent of Caesar Tiberius, was a Roman procurator in Judea (Palestine) from A.D. 26-35. Not long after he unjustly delivered up the Lord Jesus to be crucified, his rule ended abruptly. He was banished and committed suicide. 

In the evil conspiracy the Jewish religionists convinced the heathen politician to collaborate with them to kill the Lord Jesus.

### Footnote 26.1 — scourged

### Footnote 26.2 — crucified

**Verse:** [[Matthew-27#v26|Matthew 27:26]]

The Jewish death penalty was carried out by stoning ([[Leviticus-20#v2|Lev. 20:2]], [[Leviticus-20#v27|27]]; [[Leviticus-24#v14|24:14]]; [[Deuteronomy-13#v10|Deut. 13:10]]; [[Deuteronomy-17#v5|17:5]]). Crucifixion was a heathen practice ([[Ezra-06#v11|Ezra 6:11]]) adopted by the Romans for the execution of slaves and heinous criminals only. The Lord Jesus’ being crucified was a fulfillment not only of the Old Testament prophecies ([[Deuteronomy-21#v23|Deut. 21:23]]; [[Galatians-03#v13|Gal. 3:13]]; [[Numbers-21#v8|Num. 21:8-9]]) but also of the Lord’s word concerning how He would die ([[John-03#v14|John 3:14]]; [[John-08#v28|8:28]]; [[John-12#v32|12:32]]). These could not have been fulfilled by stoning. See [note 32¹](<John-18.fn#Footnote 32.1 — kind>) in [[John-18|John 18]].

## Footnotes for verse 45

### Footnote 45.1 — sixth

**Verse:** [[Matthew-27#v45|Matthew 27:45]]

The sixth hour was 12:00 noon, and the ninth was 3:00 p.m. The Lord was crucified from the third hour, 9:00 a.m. ([[Mark-15#v25|Mark 15:25]]), until the ninth hour, 3:00 p.m. He suffered on the cross for six hours. In the first three hours He was persecuted by men for doing God’s will; in the last three hours He was judged by God to accomplish our redemption. It was during this time that God counted Him as our Substitute who suffered for our sin ([[Isaiah-53#v10|Isa. 53:10]]). Darkness fell over all the land ([[Matthew-27#v45|v. 45]]) because our sin and sins and all negative things were being dealt with there; and because of our sin God forsook Him ([[Matthew-27#v46|v. 46]]).
