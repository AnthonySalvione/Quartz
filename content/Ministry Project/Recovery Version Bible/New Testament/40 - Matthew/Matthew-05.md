---
Author: Matthew
---
## v1

And when He saw the crowds, He [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 1.1 — went|went]] up to the mountain. And after He sat down, His [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 1.2 — disciples|disciples]] came to Him.

## v2

And opening His mouth, He taught them, saying,

## v3

[[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Blessed|Blessed]] are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 3.2 — poor|poor]] in [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 3.3 — spirit|spirit]], for theirs is the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 3.4 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens.

## v4

Blessed are those who [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 4.1 — mourn|mourn]], for they shall be [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 4.2 — comforted|comforted]].

## v5

Blessed are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 5.1 — meek|meek]], for they shall [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 5.2 — inherit|inherit]] the earth.

## v6

Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 6.1 — righteousness|righteousness]], for they shall be [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 6.2 — satisfied|satisfied]].

## v7

Blessed are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 7.1 — merciful|merciful]], for they shall be [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 7.2 — shown|shown]] mercy.

## v8

Blessed are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 8.1 — pure|pure]] in heart, for they shall [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 8.2 — see|see]] God.

## v9

Blessed are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 9.1 — peacemakers|peacemakers]], for they shall be called the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 9.2 — sons|sons]] of God.

## v10

Blessed are those who are [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 10.1 — persecuted|persecuted]] for the sake of righteousness, for [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 10.2 — theirs|theirs]] is the kingdom of the heavens.

## v11

Blessed are you when they [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 11.1 — reproach|reproach]] and persecute you and, while speaking lies, say every evil thing against you [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 11.2 — because|because]] of Me.

## v12

Rejoice and exult, for your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 12.1 — reward|reward]] is great in the heavens; for so they persecuted the [[Matthew-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — prophet|prophets]] who were before you.

## v13

[[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 13.1 — You|You]] are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 13.2 — salt|salt]] of the earth. But if the salt has become [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 13.3 — tasteless|tasteless]], with what shall it be salted? It is no longer good for anything except to be [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 13.4 — cast|cast]] out and [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 13.5 — trampled|trampled]] underfoot by men.

## v14

You are the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 14.1 — light|light]] of the world. It is impossible for a [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 14.2 — city|city]] situated upon a mountain to be hidden.

## v15

Nor do men light a lamp and place it under the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 15.1 — bushel|bushel]], but on the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 15.2 — lampstand|lampstand]]; and it shines to all who are in the house.

## v16

In the same way, let your light shine before men, so that they may see your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 16.1 — good|good]] works and [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 16.2 — glorify|glorify]] your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 16.3 — Father|Father]] who is in the heavens.

## v17

[[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 17.1 — Do|Do]] not think that I have come to abolish the law or the prophets; I have not come to abolish, but to [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 17.2 — fulfill|fulfill]].

## v18

For truly I say to you, Until [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 18.1 — heaven|heaven]] and earth pass away, one [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 18.2 — iota|iota]] or one [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 18.3 — serif|serif]] shall by no means pass away from the law until all come to pass.

## v19

Therefore whoever annuls one of the least of these [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 19.1 — commandments|commandments]], and teaches men so, shall be called the least in the kingdom of the heavens; but whoever practices and teaches them, he shall be called great in the kingdom of the heavens.

## v20

For I say to you that unless your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 20.1 — righteousness|righteousness]] surpasses that of the [[Matthew-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — priests|scribes]] and Pharisees, you shall by no means [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 20.3 — enter|enter]] into the kingdom of the heavens.

## v21

You have heard that it was said to the ancients, “You shall not murder, and whoever murders shall be liable to the judgment.”

## v22

But [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.1 — I|I]] say to you that everyone who is [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.2 — angry|angry]] with his [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.3 — brother|brother]] shall be liable to the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.4 — judgment|judgment]]. And whoever says to his brother, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.5 — Raca|Raca]], shall be liable to the judgment of the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.6 — Sanhedrin|Sanhedrin]]; and whoever says, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.7 — Moreh|Moreh]], shall be liable to the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.8 — Gehenna|Gehenna]] of fire.

## v23

Therefore if you are offering your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 23.1 — gift|gift]] at the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 23.2 — altar|altar]] and there you remember that your brother has [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 23.3 — something|something]] against you,

## v24

Leave your gift there before the altar, and [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 24.1 — first|first]] go and [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 24.2 — be|be]] reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift.

## v25

Be well disposed [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 25.1 — quickly|quickly]] toward your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 25.2 — opponent|opponent]] at law, while you are with him [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 25.3 — on|on]] the way, lest the opponent [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 25.4 — deliver|deliver]] you to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.

## v26

Truly I say to you, You shall by no means [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 26.1 — come|come]] out from there until you pay the last [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 26.2 — quadrans|quadrans]].

## v27

You have heard that it was said, “You shall not commit adultery.”

## v28

But I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman in order to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 28.1 — heart|heart]].

## v29

So if your right eye stumbles you, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 29.1 — pluck|pluck]] it out and cast it from you; for it is more profitable for you that one of your members perish than for your whole body to be cast into [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.8 — Gehenna|Gehenna]].

## v30

And if your right hand stumbles you, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 29.1 — pluck|cut]] it off and cast it from you, for it is more profitable for you that one of your members perish than for your whole body to pass away into [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 22.8 — Gehenna|Gehenna]].

## v31

And it was said, Whoever [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 31.1 — divorce|divorce]] his wife, let him give her a certificate of divorce.

## v32

But I say to you that [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 32.1 — everyone|everyone]] who divorces his wife, except for the cause of [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 32.2 — fornication|fornication]], causes her to commit adultery, and whoever marries her who has been divorced commits adultery.

## v33

Again, you have heard that it was said to the ancients, “[[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 33.1 — You|You]] shall not break an oath, but you shall [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 33.2 — render|render]] to the Lord your oaths.”

## v34

But I tell you not to swear at all; neither by [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 34.1 — heaven|heaven]], because it is the throne of God;

## v35

Nor by the earth, because it is the footstool of His feet; nor unto Jerusalem, because it is the city of the great King;

## v36

Neither shall you swear by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black.

## v37

But let your word be, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 37.1 — Yes|Yes]], yes; No, no; for anything more than these is [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 37.2 — of|of]] [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 37.3 — the|the]] evil one.

## v38

You have heard that it was said, “An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.”

## v39

But I tell you not to resist him who is evil; rather whoever slaps you on your right cheek, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 39.1 — turn|turn]] to him the other also.

## v40

And to him who wishes to sue you and take your [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 40.1 — tunic|tunic]], yield to him your cloak also;

## v41

And whoever compels you to go one [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 41.1 — mile|mile]], go with him two.

## v42

To him who asks of you, [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 42.1 — give|give]]; and from him who wants to borrow from you, do not turn away.

## v43

You have heard that it was said, “You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.”

## v44

But I say to you, Love your enemies, and pray for those who persecute you,

## v45

So that you may become [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 45.1 — sons|sons]] of your Father who is in the heavens, because He causes His sun to rise on the evil and the good and [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 45.2 — sends|sends]] rain on the just and the unjust.

## v46

For if you love those who love you, what [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 46.1 — reward|reward]] do you have? Do not even the [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 46.2 — tax|tax]] collectors do the same?

## v47

And if you greet only your brothers, what better thing are you doing? Do not even the Gentiles do the same?

## v48

You therefore shall be [[Matthew-05.fn#Footnote 48.1 — perfect|perfect]] as your heavenly Father is perfect.
