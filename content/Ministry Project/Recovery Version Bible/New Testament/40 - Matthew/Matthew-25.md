## v1

At that time the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 1.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens will be likened to [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 1.2 — ten|ten]] [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 1.3 — virgins|virgins]], who took their [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 1.4 — lamps|lamps]] and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 1.5 — went|went]] forth to meet the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 1.6 — bridegroom|bridegroom]].

## v2

And [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 2.1 — five|five]] of them were [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 2.2 — foolish|foolish]] and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 2.1 — five|five]] were prudent.

## v3

For the foolish, when they took their lamps, did not take [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 3.1 — oil|oil]] with them;

## v4

But the prudent took oil in their [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 4.1 — vessels|vessels]] with their lamps.

## v5

And while the bridegroom delayed, they all became [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 5.1 — drowsy|drowsy]] and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 5.2 — slept|slept]].

## v6

But at [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 6.1 — midnight|midnight]] there was a [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 6.2 — cry|cry]], Behold, the bridegroom! Go forth to [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 6.3 — meet|meet]] him!

## v7

Then all those virgins [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 7.1 — arose|arose]] and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 7.2 — trimmed|trimmed]] their own lamps.

## v8

And the foolish said to the prudent, [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Give|Give]] us some of your oil, for our lamps are [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 8.2 — going|going]] out.

## v9

But the prudent answered, saying, Perhaps there will [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 9.1 — not|not]] be enough for us and for you; go rather to [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 9.2 — those|those]] who sell, and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 9.3 — buy|buy]] for yourselves.

## v10

And as they were going away to buy, the bridegroom [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 10.1 — came|came]]; and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 10.2 — those|those]] who were [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 10.3 — ready|ready]] [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 10.4 — went|went]] in with him to the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 10.5 — wedding|wedding]] feast. And the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 10.6 — door|door]] was shut.

## v11

And later the rest of the virgins [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 11.1 — came|came]] also, saying, Lord, lord, open to us!

## v12

But he answered and said, Truly I say to you, I do [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 12.1 — not|not]] know you.

## v13

[[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Watch|Watch]] therefore, for you do not know the day nor the hour.

## v14

For the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 14.1 — kingdom|kingdom]] of the heavens is just like a [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 14.2 — man|man]] about to go [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 14.2 — man|abroad]], who called his own [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 14.3 — slaves|slaves]] and delivered to them [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 14.4 — his|his]] possessions.

## v15

To one he gave five [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 15.1 — talents|talents]], and to another two, and to another one, to each according to his [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 15.2 — own|own]] ability. And he went abroad.

## v16

Immediately he who had [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 16.1 — received|received]] the five talents went and traded with them and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 16.2 — gained|gained]] another five.

## v17

Similarly, he who had received the two gained another two.

## v18

But he who had received the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 18.1 — one|one]] went off and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 18.2 — dug|dug]] in the earth and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 18.3 — hid|hid]] his master’s money.

## v19

Now after a [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 19.1 — long|long]] time the master of those slaves [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 19.2 — came|came]] and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 19.3 — settled|settled]] accounts with them.

## v20

And he who had received the five talents [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 20.1 — came|came]] and brought another five talents, saying, Master, you delivered to me five talents; behold, I have gained [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 20.2 — another|another]] five talents.

## v21

His master said to him, Well done, good and faithful slave. You were faithful over a [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 21.1 — few|few]] things; I will set you [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 21.2 — over|over]] [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 21.3 — many|many]] things. Enter into the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 21.4 — joy|joy]] of your master.

## v22

And he who had received the two talents also came and said, Master, you delivered to me two talents; behold, I have gained another two talents.

## v23

His master said to him, [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Well|Well]] done, good and faithful slave. You were faithful over a few things; I will [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 23.2 — set|set]] you over many things. Enter into the joy of your master.

## v24

Then he who had received the one talent also [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 24.1 — came|came]] and said, Master, I [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 24.2 — knew|knew]] about you, that you are a [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 24.3 — hard|hard]] man, reaping where you did [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 24.4 — not|not]] sow, and gathering where you did [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 24.4 — not|not]] [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 24.5 — winnow|winnow]].

## v25

And I was [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 25.1 — afraid|afraid]] and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 25.2 — went|went]] off and hid your talent in the earth; behold, [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 25.3 — you|you]] have what is yours.

## v26

And his master answered and said to him, Evil and slothful slave, you knew that [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 26.1 — I|I]] reap where I did not sow and gather where I did not winnow.

## v27

Therefore you should have [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 27.1 — deposited|deposited]] my money with the money changers; and when I came, I would have recovered what is mine with [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 27.2 — interest|interest]].

## v28

[[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 28.1 — Take|Take]] away therefore the talent from him and [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 28.2 — give|give]] it to him who has the ten talents.

## v29

For [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 29.1 — to|to]] everyone who has, more shall be given, and he shall abound; but from him who does not have, even that which he has shall be taken away from him.

## v30

And [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 30.1 — cast|cast]] out the useless slave into the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 30.2 — outer|outer]] darkness. In that place [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 30.3 — there|there]] will be the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 30.4 — weeping|weeping]] and the gnashing of teeth.

## v31

[[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 31.1 — But|But]] when the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 31.2 — Son|Son]] of Man [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 31.3 — comes|comes]] in His [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 31.4 — glory|glory]] and all the angels with Him, at that time He will sit on the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 31.5 — throne|throne]] of His glory.

## v32

And all the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 32.1 — nations|nations]] will be gathered before Him, and He will separate them from one another, just as the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 32.2 — shepherd|shepherd]] separates the sheep from the goats.

## v33

And He will set the sheep on His [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 33.1 — right|right]] hand and the goats on the left.

## v34

Then the King will say to those on His right hand, Come, you who are blessed of My Father, [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 34.1 — inherit|inherit]] the kingdom prepared for you [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 34.2 — from|from]] the foundation of the world.

## v35

For I was [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 35.1 — hungry|hungry]] and you gave Me something to eat; I was thirsty and you gave Me a drink; I was a stranger and you took Me in;

## v36

Naked and you clothed Me; I was sick and you visited Me; I was in prison and you came to Me.

## v37

Then the righteous will answer Him, saying, Lord, when have we seen You hungry and have fed You, or thirsty and have given You a drink?

## v38

And when have we seen You a stranger and have taken You in, or naked and have clothed You?

## v39

And when have we seen You sick or in prison and have come to You?

## v40

And the King will answer and say to them, Truly I say to you, Inasmuch as you have [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 40.1 — done|done]] it to one of [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 40.2 — these|these]], the least of My [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 40.3 — brothers|brothers]], you have done it to [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 40.4 — Me|Me]].

## v41

Then He will say also to those on the left, Go away from Me, you who are cursed, into the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 41.1 — eternal|eternal]] fire [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 41.2 — prepared|prepared]] for the devil and his angels.

## v42

For I was hungry and you did not give Me anything to eat; I was thirsty and you did not give Me a drink;

## v43

I was a stranger and you did not take Me in; naked and you did not clothe Me; sick and in prison, and you did not visit Me.

## v44

Then they also will answer, saying, Lord, when have we seen You hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison and did not minister to You?

## v45

Then He will answer them, saying, Truly I say to you, Inasmuch as you [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 45.1 — did|did]] not do it to one of the least of these, neither have you done it to Me.

## v46

And these shall go away [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 46.3 — into|into]] eternal [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 46.1 — punishment|punishment]], but the [[Matthew-25.fn#Footnote 46.2 — righteous|righteous]] into eternal life.
