# 1 Thessalonians-02 — Footnotes

**Chapter:** [[1 Thessalonians-02|1 Thessalonians 2]]

## Footnotes for verse 1

### Footnote 1.1 — entrance

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v1|1 Thessalonians 2:1]]

The apostle stressed repeatedly their entrance toward the believers ([[1 Thessalonians-01#v5|1:5]], [[1 Thessalonians-01#v9|9]]). This shows that their manner of life played a great role in infusing the gospel into the new converts. It was not only what they said but what they were.

## Footnotes for verse 2

### Footnote 2.1 — Philippi

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v2|1 Thessalonians 2:2]]

See [note 1¹](<Philippians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Philippi>) in [[Philippians-01|Phil. 1]].

### Footnote 2.2 — in

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v2|1 Thessalonians 2:2]]

This was the apostles’ experience of God in enjoying Him as their boldness in the struggle for the gospel.

## Footnotes for verse 3

### Footnote 3.1 — deception

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v3|1 Thessalonians 2:3]]

Deception refers to the goal, uncleanness to the motive, and guile to the means. All three are of and by the subtle and deceiving devil.

## Footnotes for verse 4

### Footnote 4.1 — approved

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v4|1 Thessalonians 2:4]]

God’s entrusting depends on His approval, which comes out of His testing. The apostles were first tested and approved by God and then were entrusted by Him with the gospel. Hence, their speaking, the preaching of the gospel, was not of themselves to please men but of God to please Him. He proved, examined, and tested their hearts continually ([[Psalm-26#v2|Psa. 26:2]]; [[Psalm-139#v23|139:23-24]]).

## Footnotes for verse 5

### Footnote 5.1 — pretext

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v5|1 Thessalonians 2:5]]

Or, pretense, cloak. To have any pretext for covetousness is to peddle or adulterate the word of God ([[2 Corinthians-02#v17|2 Cor. 2:17]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v2|4:2]]). It is also to pretend to be godly for the sake of gain ([[1 Timothy-06#v5|1 Tim. 6:5]]; [[Titus-01#v11|Titus 1:11]]; [[2 Peter-02#v3|2 Pet. 2:3]]).

## Footnotes for verse 6

### Footnote 6.1 — seek

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v6|1 Thessalonians 2:6]]

To seek glory from men is a real temptation to every Christian worker. Many have been devoured and spoiled by this matter.

### Footnote 6.2 — stood

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v6|1 Thessalonians 2:6]]

Or, asserted authority. Lit., been able to be in weight; i.e., been burdensome (cf. [[1 Thessalonians-02#v9|v. 9]]; [[1 Corinthians-09#v4|1 Cor. 9:4-12]]). To assert authority, dignity, or right in Christian work damages that work. The Lord Jesus, while on earth, gave up His dignity ([[John-13#v4|John 13:4-5]]), and the apostle preferred not to use his right ([[1 Corinthians-09#v12|1 Cor. 9:12]]).

## Footnotes for verse 7

### Footnote 7.1 — nursing

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|1 Thessalonians 2:7]]

In some instances the Greek word means a mother; hence, a nursing mother (cf. [[Galatians-04#v19|Gal. 4:19]]).

### Footnote 7.2 — cherish

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|1 Thessalonians 2:7]]

Cherishing, which includes nourishing, indicates care that is more tender than mere nourishing. See [note 29¹](<Ephesians-05.fn#Footnote 29.1 — nourishes>) in [[Ephesians-05|Eph. 5]].

## Footnotes for verse 8

### Footnote 8.1 — Yearning

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v8|1 Thessalonians 2:8]]

Being affectionately fond of, affectionately desirous of, like a nursing mother affectionately interested in her child, whom she nourishes and cherishes. This was what the apostles did with new believers. See [note 17³](<1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 17.3 — eager>).

### Footnote 8.2 — our

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v8|1 Thessalonians 2:8]]

To live a clean and upright life, as portrayed in [[1 Thessalonians-02#v3|vv. 3-6]] and [[1 Thessalonians-02#v10|10]], and to love the new converts, even by giving our own souls to them, as described in [[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|vv. 7-9]] and [[1 Thessalonians-02#v11|11]], are the prerequisites for infusing them with the salvation conveyed in the gospel that we preach.

## Footnotes for verse 10

### Footnote 10.1 — holy

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v10|1 Thessalonians 2:10]]

See [note 75¹](<Luke-01.fn#Footnote 75.1 — holiness>) in [[Luke-01|Luke 1]]. Holy refers to conduct toward God, righteous to conduct toward men, and blameless to conduct toward all—God, men, and Satan.

## Footnotes for verse 11

### Footnote 11.1 — how

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v11|1 Thessalonians 2:11]]

The apostle was strong in stressing what or how they were ([[1 Thessalonians-01#v5|1:5]]), because what they were opened the way to bring their new converts into God’s full salvation.

### Footnote 11.2 — father

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v11|1 Thessalonians 2:11]]

In cherishing the believers as their own children, the apostles considered themselves a nursing mother; in exhorting the believers, a father.

### Footnote 11.3 — exhorting

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v11|1 Thessalonians 2:11]]

Or, entreating.

## Footnotes for verse 12

### Footnote 12.1 — calls

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thessalonians 2:12]]

God’s calling is according to His selection and follows His selection ([[1 Thessalonians-01#v4|1:4]]).

### Footnote 12.2 — kingdom

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thessalonians 2:12]]

As worshippers of idols ([[1 Thessalonians-01#v9|1:9]]), the believers were in the kingdom of Satan ([[Matthew-12#v26|Matt. 12:26]]). Now, through the salvation in Christ they were called, and they have believed into the kingdom of God, which is the sphere in which they can worship and enjoy God under the divine ruling with the view of entering into God’s glory.

### Footnote 12.3 — glory

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thessalonians 2:12]]

God’s glory goes with His kingdom. See [note 13⁴](<Matthew-06.fn#Footnote 13.4 — kingdom>) in [[Matthew-06|Matt. 6]].

## Footnotes for verse 13

### Footnote 13.1 — the

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v13|1 Thessalonians 2:13]]

Lit., the word of hearing of God from us.

### Footnote 13.2 — from

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v13|1 Thessalonians 2:13]]

The word received by the believers was from the apostles, but it was of God. Its source, its origin, was God and not the apostles.

### Footnote 13.3 — operates

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v13|1 Thessalonians 2:13]]

Since the word of God is living and operative ([[Hebrews-04#v12|Heb. 4:12]]), it operates in the believing ones.

## Footnotes for verse 14

### Footnote 14.1 — imitators

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v14|1 Thessalonians 2:14]]

The apostle taught the same thing in all the churches ([[1 Corinthians-04#v17|1 Cor. 4:17]]; [[1 Corinthians-07#v17|7:17]]; [[1 Corinthians-11#v16|11:16]]), indicating that all the churches should bear the same testimony of Jesus. Hence, the churches are all lampstands of the same kind ([[Revelation-01#v9|Rev. 1:9]], [[Revelation-01#v20|20]]).

## Footnotes for verse 16

### Footnote 16.1 — uttermost

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v16|1 Thessalonians 2:16]]

Lit., to the end; hence, to the uttermost.

## Footnotes for verse 17

### Footnote 17.1 — bereaved

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v17|1 Thessalonians 2:17]]

This word implies that the apostles considered the new converts precious and dear to them. Paul likened their departure from them to a bereavement, a loss that they suffered through missing them.

### Footnote 17.2 — a

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v17|1 Thessalonians 2:17]]

Lit., the season of an hour.

### Footnote 17.3 — eager

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v17|1 Thessalonians 2:17]]

This is the apostles’ yearning over the new converts ([[1 Thessalonians-02#v8|v. 8]]).

## Footnotes for verse 18

### Footnote 18.1 — Satan

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v18|1 Thessalonians 2:18]]

Because the apostles were carrying out the will of God, Satan frustrated them.

## Footnotes for verse 19

### Footnote 19.1 — coming

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v19|1 Thessalonians 2:19]]

Gk. parousia, meaning presence, as in [[1 Thessalonians-03#v13|3:13]] and [[1 Thessalonians-04#v15|4:15]]. The Lord’s coming is His presence. In this light these two early Epistles were written. Every chapter of the first Epistle ends with the Lord’s coming back.

## Footnotes for verse 20

### Footnote 20.1 — glory

**Verse:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v20|1 Thessalonians 2:20]]

Since the apostles were the believers’ nursing mother and exhorting father ([[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|vv. 7]], [[1 Thessalonians-02#v11|11]]), the believers, as the apostles’ children, were their glory and joy.
