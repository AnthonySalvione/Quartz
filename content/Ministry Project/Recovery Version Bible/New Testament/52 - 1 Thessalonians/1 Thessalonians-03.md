## v1

Therefore when we could bear it no longer, we thought it good to be left in [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Athens|Athens]] alone,

## v2

And we sent Timothy, our brother and [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — God’s|God’s]] fellow worker in the gospel of Christ, to establish and [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 2.2 — encourage|encourage]] you for the sake of your faith,

## v3

That no one would be [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — shaken|shaken]] by these afflictions; for you yourselves know that we are [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — appointed|appointed]] for this.

## v4

For even when we were with you, we [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — told|told]] you beforehand that we are to be afflicted, even as it also came to pass and you know.

## v5

Because of this, when I also could bear it no longer, I sent to find out concerning your faith, lest perhaps the [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — tempter|tempter]] had tempted you and our labor would be [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — in|in]] vain.

## v6

But Timothy, having just come [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — to|to]] us from you and announcing the good news to us of your faith and love and that you have kind remembrance of us always and long to see us, just as we also you,

## v7

Because of this we were [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — comforted|comforted]] because of you, brothers, in all our [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — necessity|necessity]] and affliction, through your faith;

## v8

Because now we [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — live|live]] if you [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — stand|stand]] firm in the Lord.

## v9

For what thanks can we return to God concerning you for all the joy with which we rejoice because of you before our God,

## v10

Night and day petitioning exceedingly so that we may see your face and [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — complete|complete]] the things that are lacking in your faith?

## v11

Now may our God and Father Himself and our Lord Jesus [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — make|make]] straight our path to you;

## v12

And the Lord cause you to increase and abound in [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — love|love]] to one another and to all, just as we also to you,

## v13

[[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — So|So]] that He may establish your [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — hearts|hearts]] blameless in [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 13.3 — holiness|holiness]] before our God and Father at the [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 13.4 — coming|coming]] of our Lord Jesus with [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 13.5 — all|all]] His [[1 Thessalonians-03.fn#Footnote 13.6 — saints|saints]].
