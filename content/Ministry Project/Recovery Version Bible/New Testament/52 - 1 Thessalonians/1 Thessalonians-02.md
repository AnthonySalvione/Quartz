---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

For you yourselves know, brothers, our [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — entrance|entrance]] toward you, that it has not been in vain.

## v2

But having suffered previously and having been outrageously treated, even as you know, in [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — Philippi|Philippi]], we were bold [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — in|in]] our God to speak to you the gospel of God in much struggle.

## v3

For our exhortation is not out of [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — deception|deception]] nor out of uncleanness nor in guile;

## v4

But even as we have been [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — approved|approved]] by God to be entrusted with the gospel, so we speak, not as pleasing men but God, who proves our hearts.

## v5

For neither were we found at any time with flattering speech, even as you know, nor with a [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — pretext|pretext]] for covetousness; God is witness.

## v6

Nor did we [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — seek|seek]] glory from men, neither from you nor from others, though we could have [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — stood|stood]] on our authority as apostles of Christ.

## v7

But we were gentle in your midst, as a [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — nursing|nursing]] mother would [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — cherish|cherish]] her own children.

## v8

[[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Yearning|Yearning]] in this way over you, we were well pleased to impart to you not only the gospel of God but also [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — our|our]] own souls, because you became beloved to us.

## v9

For you remember, brothers, our labor and hardship: While working night and day so as not to be burdensome to any of you, we proclaimed to you the gospel of God.

## v10

You are witnesses, as well as God, how in a [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — holy|holy]] and righteous and blameless manner we conducted ourselves toward you who believe,

## v11

Just as you know [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — how|how]] we were to each one of you, as a [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — father|father]] to his own children, [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 11.3 — exhorting|exhorting]] you and consoling you and testifying,

## v12

So that you might walk in a manner worthy of God, who [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — calls|calls]] you into His own [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — kingdom|kingdom]] and [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — glory|glory]].

## v13

And because of this we also thank God unceasingly that when you received [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — the|the]] word of God, which you heard [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — from|from]] us, you accepted it not as the word of men but even as it truly is, the word of God, which also [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 13.3 — operates|operates]] in you who believe.

## v14

For you, brothers, became [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — imitators|imitators]] of the churches of God which are in Judea in Christ Jesus, for you also suffered the same things from your own countrymen, even as they also from the Jews,

## v15

Who both killed the Lord Jesus and the prophets and drove us out; and they are not pleasing to God and are contrary to all men,

## v16

In that they forbid us to speak to the Gentiles that they may be saved, so that they fill up their sins always. But wrath has come upon them to the [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — uttermost|uttermost]].

## v17

But we, brothers, having been [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — bereaved|bereaved]] of you for [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — a|a]] little while, in presence, not in heart, were more abundantly [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 17.3 — eager|eager]] with great desire to see your face.

## v18

Therefore we wanted to come to you, indeed I Paul, both once and again, yet [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Satan|Satan]] hindered us.

## v19

For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting before our Lord Jesus at His [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — coming|coming]]? Are not even you?

## v20

For you are our [[1 Thessalonians-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — glory|glory]] and joy.
