## v1

Paul and Silvanus and Timothy to the [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — church|church]] of the [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — Thessalonians|Thessalonians]] in [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 1.3 — God|God]] the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace to you and peace.

## v2

We thank God always concerning all of you, making mention of you in our prayers,

## v3

Remembering unceasingly your [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — work|work]] of [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 3.2 — faith|faith]] and [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 3.3 — labor|labor]] of love and [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 3.4 — endurance|endurance]] of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ, before our God and Father;

## v4

Knowing, brothers, beloved of God, your [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — selection|selection]];

## v5

For our gospel did not come to you in word only, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit and in much assurance, even as you know [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — what|what]] kind of men we were among you for your sake.

## v6

And you became [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — imitators|imitators]] of us and of the Lord, having received the [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — word|word]] in much affliction with joy of the Holy Spirit,

## v7

So that you became a [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — pattern|pattern]] to all those who believe in Macedonia and in Achaia.

## v8

For from you the word of the Lord has sounded out; not only in Macedonia and in Achaia, but in every place, your [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 8.1 — faith|faith]] toward God has gone out, so that we have no need of saying anything.

## v9

For they themselves report concerning us [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — what|what]] kind of entrance we had toward you and how you [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — turned|turned]] to God from the idols to [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 9.3 — serve|serve]] a [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 9.4 — living|living]] and true God

## v10

And [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — await|await]] His Son from the heavens, whom He raised from the dead, Jesus, who delivers us from the [[1 Thessalonians-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — wrath|wrath]] which is coming.
