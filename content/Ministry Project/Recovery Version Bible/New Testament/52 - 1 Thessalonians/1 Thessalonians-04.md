---
Author: Paul
---
## v1

Furthermore, brothers, we ask and exhort you in the Lord Jesus that even as you received from us how you ought to walk and please God, even as indeed you do walk, that you abound still more.

## v2

For you know what charges we gave you through the Lord Jesus.

## v3

For this is the will of God, your [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 3.1 — sanctification|sanctification]]: that you abstain from [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 3.2 — fornication|fornication]];

## v4

That each one of you know how to [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 4.1 — possess|possess]] his own [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 4.2 — vessel|vessel]] in [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 4.3 — sanctification|sanctification]] and honor,

## v5

Not in the passion of lust, like the Gentiles who do [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 5.1 — not|not]] know God;

## v6

That no one [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 6.1 — overstep|overstep]] and [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 6.2 — take|take]] advantage of his brother in [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 6.3 — the|the]] matter, because the Lord is the [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 6.4 — avenger|avenger]] concerning all [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 6.5 — these|these]] things, even as we also said before to you and solemnly charged.

## v7

For God has not called us [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 7.1 — for|for]] [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 7.2 — uncleanness|uncleanness]] but [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 7.3 — in|in]] [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 7.4 — sanctification|sanctification]].

## v8

[[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 8.1 — Consequently|Consequently]], he who [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 8.2 — rejects|rejects]], rejects not man but God, who also gives His [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 8.3 — Holy|Holy]] Spirit to you.

## v9

Now concerning [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 9.1 — brotherly|brotherly]] love, you have no need for me to write to you, for you yourselves are taught of God to [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 9.2 — love|love]] one another;

## v10

For you also do this unto all the brothers in the whole of Macedonia. But we exhort you, brothers, to abound still more

## v11

And to aspire to be quiet and attend to your own affairs and work with your hands, even as we charged you,

## v12

That you may walk becomingly toward those outside and may have need of nothing.

## v13

But we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning those who are [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 13.1 — sleeping|sleeping]], that you would not grieve even as also the rest who have no hope.

## v14

For if we believe that Jesus died and rose, so also those who have fallen asleep through Jesus, God will bring with Him.

## v15

For this we say to you by the word of the Lord, that we who are living, who are left remaining unto the [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 15.1 — coming|coming]] of the Lord, shall by no means precede those who have fallen asleep;

## v16

Because the Lord Himself, with a [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 16.1 — shout|shout]] of command, with the voice of the archangel and with the [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 16.2 — trumpet|trumpet]] of God, will descend from heaven, and the dead in Christ will rise first.

## v17

Then we who are living, who are left remaining, will be [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 17.1 — caught|caught]] up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord [[1 Thessalonians-04.fn#Footnote 17.2 — in|in]] the air; and thus we will be always with the Lord.

## v18

Therefore comfort one another with these words.
