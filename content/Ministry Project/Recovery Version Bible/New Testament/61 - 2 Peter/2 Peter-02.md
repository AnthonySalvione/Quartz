## v1

[[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.1 — But|But]] there arose also false prophets among the people, as also among you there will be false teachers, who will [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.2 — secretly|secretly]] bring in destructive [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.3 — heresies|heresies]], even denying the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.4 — Master|Master]] who bought them, bringing upon themselves swift [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 1.5 — destruction|destruction]].

## v2

And many will [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — follow|follow]] their licentiousness, because of whom the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — way|way]] of the truth will be reviled.

## v3

And in covetousness, with fabricated words, they will make merchandise of you, for whom the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — judgment|judgment]] [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 3.2 — of|of]] old is not idle, and their [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 3.3 — destruction|destruction]] does not slumber.

## v4

For if God did not spare the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — angels|angels]] who sinned but delivered them to gloomy pits, having cast them down to [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — Tartarus|Tartarus]], they being kept [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 4.3 — for|for]] [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 4.4 — judgment|judgment]];

## v5

And did not spare the ancient world but guarded Noah, a herald of [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — righteousness|righteousness]], with seven others, when He brought a flood upon the world of the ungodly;

## v6

And having reduced to ashes the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah, condemned them to [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — ruin|ruin]], having set them as an example to those who intend to [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — live|live]] an ungodly life,

## v7

And rescued righteous Lot, who had been oppressed by the licentious [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — manner|manner]] of life of the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — lawless|lawless]]

## v8

(For that righteous man, who settled down among them, in seeing and hearing tormented his righteous soul day after day with their lawless works);

## v9

The Lord knows how to deliver the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — godly|godly]] out of trial and how to keep the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — unrighteous|unrighteous]] under punishment [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — for|for]] the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.4 — day|day]] of [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 9.5 — judgment|judgment]],

## v10

And especially [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — those|those]] who go after the flesh in the lust for defilement and despise [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — lordship|lordship]]. Daring, [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 10.3 — self-willed|self-willed]], they do not tremble while reviling [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 10.4 — dignities|dignities]];

## v11

Whereas [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — angels|angels]], though they are greater in strength and power, [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — do|do]] not bring a reviling judgment against [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — angels|them]] before the Lord.

## v12

But these, like [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — animals|animals]] [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — without|without]] reason, born natural [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — for|for]] capture and [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.4 — destruction|destruction]], reviling in things of which they are ignorant, will also in their corrupting of others be [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 12.5 — destroyed|destroyed]];

## v13

[[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — Suffering|Suffering]] unrighteousness as the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — wages|wages]] of unrighteousness; considering luxury in the day to be pleasure; [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 13.3 — spots|spots]] and blemishes, reveling in their deceits while feasting together with you;

## v14

Having eyes full of [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — adultery|adultery]] and not ceasing from sin; enticing unstable souls, having a heart exercised for covetousness, children of curse.

## v15

Forsaking the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — straight|straight]] way, they have gone astray, [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — following|following]] the way of [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 15.3 — Balaam|Balaam]], the son of Beor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness

## v16

But had reproof for his own transgression: a dumb [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — beast|beast]] of burden, uttering with a man’s voice, restrained the madness of the prophet.

## v17

These are [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — springs|springs]] without water and mists driven by a storm, for whom the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — gloom|gloom]] of darkness is kept.

## v18

For uttering great swelling words of vanity, they entice by lusts of the flesh, with licentiousness, those who are barely escaping from them who [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — live|live]] in error;

## v19

Promising freedom to them, while they themselves are slaves of [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — corruption|corruption]]; for by whom anyone has been defeated, [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 19.2 — by|by]] this one he has been enslaved.

## v20

For if, having escaped the defilements of the world by the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ but having again been entangled in these, they are defeated, the last state has become worse for them than the first.

## v21

For it would be better for them not to have known the [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — way|way]] of righteousness than, knowing it, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them.

## v22

It has happened to them according to the true proverb: The [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — dog|dog]] has turned [[2 Peter-02.fn#Footnote 22.2 — to|to]] its own vomit, and the washed sow to wallowing in the mud.
