## v1

This second letter, beloved, I now write to you; in them both I stir up your sincere mind with a reminder,

## v2

So that you would remember the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 2.1 — words|words]] spoken before by the holy prophets and the commandment of the Lord and Savior preached by your apostles;

## v3

Knowing this first, that in the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 3.1 — last|last]] of days [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 3.2 — mockers|mockers]] will come with mocking, going on according to their own lusts

## v4

And saying, Where is the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 4.1 — promise|promise]] of His [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 4.2 — coming|coming]]? For since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue in this way from the beginning of creation.

## v5

For [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 5.1 — this|this]] escapes them by their own choosing, that by the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 5.2 — word|word]] of God the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 5.3 — heavens|heavens]] were of old and the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 5.3 — heavens|earth]] was [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 5.4 — compacted|compacted]] out of water and through water,

## v6

Through [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 6.1 — which|which]] the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 6.2 — world|world]] [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 6.3 — then|then]], being [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 6.4 — flooded|flooded]] with water, [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 6.5 — perished|perished]].

## v7

But the heavens and the earth [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.1 — now|now]], by the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.2 — same|same]] word, have been stored up for [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.3 — fire|fire]], being kept unto the day of [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.4 — judgment|judgment]] and [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.5 — destruction|destruction]] of [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 7.6 — ungodly|ungodly]] men.

## v8

But do not let this one thing [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 8.1 — escape|escape]] you, beloved, that with the Lord [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 8.2 — one|one]] day is like a thousand years and a thousand years like one day.

## v9

The Lord [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.1 — does|does]] not [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.3 — delay|delay]] regarding the promise, as [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.2 — some|some]] count delay, but is [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.4 — long-suffering|long-suffering]] toward you, not intending that any [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.5 — perish|perish]] but that [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.6 — all|all]] advance to [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 9.7 — repentance|repentance]].

## v10

But the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.1 — day|day]] of the Lord will [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.2 — come|come]] as a thief, in [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.3 — which|which]] the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.4 — heavens|heavens]] will pass away with a [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.5 — roar|roar]], and the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.6 — elements|elements]], burning with intense heat, will be [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.7 — dissolved|dissolved]], and the earth and the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.8 — works|works]] in it will be [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 10.7 — dissolved|burned]] up.

## v11

Since [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 11.1 — all|all]] these things [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 11.2 — are|are]] to be thus dissolved, what kind of persons ought you to be [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 11.3 — in|in]] holy manner of life and godliness,

## v12

[[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Expecting|Expecting]] and hastening the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 12.2 — coming|coming]] of the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 12.3 — day|day]] of God, on account of [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 12.4 — which|which]] the heavens, being on fire, will be dissolved, and the elements, burning with intense heat, are to be melted away?

## v13

But according to His [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 13.1 — promise|promise]] we are expecting [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 13.2 — new|new]] heavens and a new earth, in which [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 13.3 — righteousness|righteousness]] [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 13.4 — dwells|dwells]].

## v14

Therefore, beloved, since you expect these things, be diligent to be [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 14.1 — found|found]] by Him in peace [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 14.2 — without|without]] spot and without blemish;

## v15

And [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 15.1 — count|count]] the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 15.2 — long-suffering|long-suffering]] of our Lord to be [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 15.3 — salvation|salvation]], even as [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 15.4 — also|also]] our beloved brother Paul, according to the wisdom given to him, wrote to you,

## v16

As also in [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.1 — all|all]] his letters, speaking in them concerning [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — these|these]] things, in which some things are hard to understand, which the unlearned and unstable [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.3 — twist|twist]], as also the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.2 — these|rest]] of the Scriptures, to their own [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 16.4 — destruction|destruction]].

## v17

You therefore, beloved, since you know these things beforehand, [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 17.1 — be|be]] on your guard lest being [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 17.2 — carried|carried]] away by the error of the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 17.3 — lawless|lawless]], you [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 17.4 — fall|fall]] from your own steadfastness.

## v18

But [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.1 — grow|grow]] in the [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.2 — grace|grace]] and [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.3 — knowledge|knowledge]] of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. [[2 Peter-03.fn#Footnote 18.4 — To|To]] Him be the glory both now and unto the day of eternity. Amen.
