Author: Paul
---
## v1

[[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 1.1 — Paul|Paul]], an apostle of Christ Jesus through the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 1.2 — will|will]] of God, and Timothy the brother,

## v2

To the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 2.1 — saints|saints]] in Colossae and faithful brothers in Christ: Grace to you and peace from God our Father.

## v3

We give thanks to God, the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 3.1 — Father|Father]] of our Lord Jesus Christ, praying always concerning you,

## v4

Because we have heard of your [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — faith|faith]] in Christ Jesus and the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 4.1 — faith|love]] which you have unto all the saints

## v5

Because of the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 5.1 — hope|hope]] [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 5.2 — laid|laid]] up for you in the heavens, of which you heard before in the word of the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 5.3 — truth|truth]] of the gospel,

## v6

Which has come to you, even as it is also in all the world, bearing fruit and growing, as also in you, since the day you heard and knew the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 6.1 — grace|grace]] of God in [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 6.2 — truth|truth]];

## v7

Even as you learned from Epaphras, our beloved fellow slave, who is a faithful [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 7.1 — minister|minister]] of Christ on your behalf,

## v8

Who also has made known to us your love in the Spirit.

## v9

Therefore we also, since the day we heard of it, do not cease praying and asking on your behalf that you may be filled with the full knowledge of [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 9.1 — His|His]] will in all [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 9.2 — spiritual|spiritual]] wisdom and understanding,

## v10

To [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 10.1 — walk|walk]] worthily of the Lord to please Him [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 10.2 — in|in]] all things, [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 10.3 — bearing|bearing]] fruit in every good work and growing by the full [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 10.4 — knowledge|knowledge]] of God,

## v11

Being empowered with all [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 11.1 — power|power]], according to the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 11.2 — might|might]] of His glory, unto all endurance and long-suffering with joy,

## v12

Giving thanks to the Father, who has [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 12.1 — qualified|qualified]] you for a share of the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 12.2 — allotted|allotted]] portion of the saints in the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 12.3 — light|light]];

## v13

Who [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 13.1 — delivered|delivered]] us out of the authority of [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 13.2 — darkness|darkness]] and transferred us into the kingdom of the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 13.3 — Son|Son]] of His love,

## v14

In whom we have [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 14.1 — redemption|redemption]], the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 14.2 — forgiveness|forgiveness]] of sins;

## v15

Who is the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 15.1 — image|image]] of the invisible God, the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 15.2 — Firstborn|Firstborn]] of all creation,

## v16

Because [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 16.1 — in|in]] Him all things were created, in the heavens and on the earth, the visible and the invisible, whether [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 16.2 — thrones|thrones]] or [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 16.3 — lordships|lordships]] or rulers or authorities; all things have been created [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 16.4 — through|through]] Him and [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 16.5 — unto|unto]] Him.

## v17

And He is [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 17.1 — before|before]] all things, and all things [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 17.2 — cohere|cohere]] in Him;

## v18

And He is the Head of the Body, the church; He is the beginning, the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 18.1 — Firstborn|Firstborn]] from the dead, that He Himself might have the first place in all things;

## v19

For in Him [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 19.1 — all|all]] the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 19.2 — fullness|fullness]] was pleased to dwell

## v20

And [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — through|through]] Him to reconcile [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 20.2 — all|all]] things to [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 20.3 — Himself|Himself]], [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 20.4 — having|having]] made peace [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 20.1 — through|through]] the blood of His cross — through Him, whether the things on the earth or the things in the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 20.5 — heavens|heavens]].

## v21

And you, though once alienated and [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 21.1 — enemies|enemies]] in your [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 21.2 — mind|mind]] because of your evil works,

## v22

[[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — He|He]] now has reconciled in the body of His flesh through death, to present you holy and without blemish and without reproach before [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 22.1 — He|Him]];

## v23

If indeed you continue in the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 23.1 — faith|faith]], grounded and steadfast and not being moved away from the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 23.2 — hope|hope]] of the gospel, which you heard, which was proclaimed in all creation under heaven, of which I Paul became a minister.

## v24

I now rejoice in my sufferings on your behalf and [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 24.1 — fill|fill]] up on my part that which is lacking of the afflictions of Christ in my flesh for His Body, which is the church;

## v25

Of [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 25.1 — which|which]] I became a [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 25.2 — minister|minister]] according to the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 25.3 — stewardship|stewardship]] of God, which was given to me for you, to [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 25.4 — complete|complete]] the word of God,

## v26

The [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 26.1 — mystery|mystery]] which has been hidden [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 26.2 — from|from]] the ages and from the generations but now has been manifested to His saints;

## v27

To whom God willed to make known what are the [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 27.1 — riches|riches]] of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles, [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 27.2 — which|which]] is [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 27.3 — Christ|Christ]] [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 27.4 — in|in]] you, the hope of [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 27.5 — glory|glory]],

## v28

Whom we announce, admonishing every man and teaching every man in all wisdom that we may present every man [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 28.1 — full-grown|full-grown]] [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 28.2 — in|in]] Christ;

## v29

For which also I labor, [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 29.1 — struggling|struggling]] according to His operation which operates in me in [[Colossians-01.fn#Footnote 29.2 — power|power]].
