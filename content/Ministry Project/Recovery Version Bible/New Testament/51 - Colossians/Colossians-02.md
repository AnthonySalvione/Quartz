## v1

For I want you to know how great a struggle I have for you and for those in Laodicea, even all who have not seen my face in the flesh,

## v2

That their hearts may be [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 2.1 — comforted|comforted]], they being knit together in [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 2.2 — love|love]] and unto all the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 2.3 — riches|riches]] of the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 2.4 — full|full]] assurance of understanding, unto the full knowledge of the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 2.5 — mystery|mystery]] of God, Christ,

## v3

In whom all the treasures of [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 3.1 — wisdom|wisdom]] and knowledge are hidden.

## v4

This I say that no one may [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 4.1 — delude|delude]] you with [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 4.2 — persuasive|persuasive]] speech.

## v5

For even though I am absent in the flesh, yet in the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 5.1 — spirit|spirit]] I am with you, rejoicing and seeing your order and the solid basis of your faith [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 5.2 — in|in]] Christ.

## v6

As therefore you have [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 6.1 — received|received]] the Christ, Jesus the Lord, [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 6.2 — walk|walk]] in Him,

## v7

Having been [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 7.1 — rooted|rooted]] and being built up in Him, and being established [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 7.2 — in|in]] the faith even as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving.

## v8

Beware that no one carries you off as spoil through his [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 8.1 — philosophy|philosophy]] and empty deceit, according to the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 8.2 — tradition|tradition]] of men, according to the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 8.3 — elements|elements]] of the world, and not according to [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 8.4 — Christ|Christ]];

## v9

For in Him dwells all the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 9.1 — fullness|fullness]] of the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 9.2 — Godhead|Godhead]] [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 9.3 — bodily|bodily]],

## v10

And you have been made [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 10.1 — full|full]] in Him, who is the Head of all [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 10.2 — rule|rule]] and authority.

## v11

In Him also you were circumcised with a [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 11.1 — circumcision|circumcision]] [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 11.2 — not|not]] made with hands, in the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 11.3 — putting|putting]] off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ,

## v12

[[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 12.1 — Buried|Buried]] together with Him in baptism, in which also you were [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 12.2 — raised|raised]] together with Him through the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 12.3 — faith|faith]] of the operation of God, who raised Him from the dead.

## v13

And you, though [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 13.1 — dead|dead]] in your offenses and in the uncircumcision of your flesh, He [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 13.2 — made|made]] alive together with Him, having forgiven us all our offenses;

## v14

[[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 14.1 — Wiping|Wiping]] out the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 14.2 — handwriting|handwriting]] in [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 14.3 — ordinances|ordinances]], which was against us, which was contrary to us; and He has taken it out of the way, [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 14.4 — nailing|nailing]] it to the cross.

## v15

[[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 15.1 — Stripping|Stripping]] off the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 15.2 — rulers|rulers]] and the authorities, [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 15.3 — He|He]] made a [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 15.4 — display|display]] of them openly, triumphing over them in [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 15.5 — it|it]].

## v16

Let no one therefore judge you [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 16.1 — in|in]] eating and in drinking or in respect of a [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 16.2 — feast|feast]] or of a [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 16.3 — new|new]] moon or of the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 16.4 — Sabbath|Sabbath]],

## v17

Which are a [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 17.1 — shadow|shadow]] of the things to come, but the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 17.2 — body|body]] is of [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 17.3 — Christ|Christ]].

## v18

Let no one [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 18.1 — defraud|defraud]] you by judging you unworthy of your prize, in self-chosen [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 18.2 — lowliness|lowliness]] and the worship of the angels, [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 18.3 — dwelling|dwelling]] on the things which he has [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 18.4 — seen|seen]], vainly puffed up by [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 18.5 — his|his]] mind set on the flesh,

## v19

And [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.1 — not|not]] holding the Head, [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.2 — out|out]] from whom all the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.3 — Body|Body]], being richly supplied and knit together by means of the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.4 — joints|joints]] and [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.5 — sinews|sinews]], [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.6 — grows|grows]] with the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 19.7 — growth|growth]] of God.

## v20

[[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 20.1 — If|If]] you [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 20.2 — died|died]] with Christ from the [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 20.3 — elements|elements]] of the world, why, as living in the world, do you subject yourselves to ordinances:

## v21

[[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 21.1 — Do|Do]] not handle, nor taste, nor touch,

## v22

(Regarding things which are all to [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 22.1 — perish|perish]] when [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 22.2 — used|used]]) according to the commandments and teachings of men?

## v23

[[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 23.1 — Such|Such]] things indeed have a [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 23.2 — reputation|reputation]] of wisdom in self-imposed worship and lowliness and [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 23.3 — severe|severe]] treatment of the body, but are [[Colossians-02.fn#Footnote 23.4 — not|not]] of any value against the indulgence of the flesh.
