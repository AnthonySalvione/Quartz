# June 2026 Semiannual Training Study Guidelines

## 1—Mandatory study time

There is a mandatory study time for *all* full-time trainees from 9:00AM to 12:00PM every day from Tuesday through Saturday. Your attendance in the mornings is *not* optional.

## 2—Study groups

Trainees are required to come together to study in groups of 8-10, preferably in the meeting halls of nearby churches, or in homes if the halls are not available. You should stay with the same study group for all five days. As much as possible, bled with saints from other localities.

## 3—Suggested time breakdown

Prayer and singing — 9:00AM-9:15AM
Group study (first message) — 9:15AM-10:15AM
Break — 10:15AM-10:30AM
Group study (second message) — 10:30AM-11:30AM
Prophesying — 11:30AM-12:00AM

## 4—Structure study sections

Structure your study sessions around a message outline. Go through an entire message portion by portion (e.g., a Roman numeral or a sub-point under it) with the help of your notes and the *Recovery Version* of the Bible with its footnotes and cross-references.

## 5—Apportion your time

Apportion your time. Do not linger on the lesser points. Avoid tangential and otherwise unprofitable discussions. Study with an exercised spirit and an attentive mind. Different group members (especially the experiences and mature ones) may take turns to moderate the different study questions. Support one another and do not criticize.

## 6—Prophesying time

For the prophesying times at the end of the study sessions, several study groups should join together, when possible, for a time of review and mutual speaking of the points studied in the morning.

## 7—Friendly reminder

The best study groups and the best study time are those in which *everyone* participates.

## 8—Additional note

It is expected that the co-workers and leading brothers (both local and out-of-town) in the different areas will help the saints in grouping, time keeping, and with any other problems which may arise.

## 9—Enjoy the Lord

Enjoy the Lord in His word and blend with each other in these times!
