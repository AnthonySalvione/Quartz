# Training Regulations

## I. General Regulations

### A. This is a training, 24 hours a day for 6 days.

### B. Meeting schedule
1. There will be two meetings daily at **4:00 PM** and **7:30 PM**.
2. Meetings begin **Monday, June 29, 2026**, and conclude after the evening meeting on **Saturday, July 4, 2026**.

### C. Arriving at the LSM Campus
1. Come early, singing, praising, praying, and rejoicing.
2. Be in your seat **5 minutes before** each meeting (3:55 PM and 7:25 PM).
3. Do all greeting, visiting, and fellowshipping outside the building.
4. Once inside the meeting hall, all talking should cease; there should only be praying, praising, and singing.

### D. Health consciousness
1. Avoid shaking hands to prevent the spread of germs.
2. Wash or sanitize your hands frequently.

### E. Assigned seating
1. Every attendee has an assigned seat.
2. Do not offer your seat or name badge to anyone.
3. Do not sit in another seat without permission from LSM Registration.
4. An empty assigned seat will be marked as an absence.
5. During open meetings, remain in your assigned seat.

## II. During the Meetings

### A. During the message
1. Exercise to receive the word.
2. No talking during the message or testimonies, especially in the back rows.
3. No eating or drinking is permitted.
4. Only sealed containers of water may be brought into the meeting hall.

### B. Leaving your seat
1. Do not leave your seat during the meeting.
2. If you leave before dismissal, including for the restroom, your seat number will be recorded.
3. Those with urgent restroom needs may request reassignment through LSM Registration.

### C. After the message
1. Exercise to practice prophesying.
2. Please be brief.

### D. Leaving the meeting area
1. Exit promptly after dismissal.
2. Do not talk or stand in the aisles, near the doors, or outside the exits.

### E. Absences
1. Unexcused absences are not permitted.
2. Two excused absences are grounds for dismissal from the training.

## III. Administrative Matters

### A. Badge holders
1. All badge holders belong to Living Stream Ministry.
2. Return them after the final meeting when collected by the ushers.

### B. Hospitality
1. Guests assigned to LSM Full-time Training hospitality who have not received housing assignments should report to the Hospitality Desk in the northwest corner of the room.

### C. Testing
1. Prepare using your message notes, outlines, and Scripture verses.

## IV. Study

### A. Study sessions for trainees
1. Study three hours every morning.
2. Groups of **8–10** are preferred.
3. Consult the Training Study Guidelines for details.
4. Rest time is **1:30–2:30 PM**.
5. No phone calls during rest time.

### B. Young people's study time
1. Study sessions are mandatory for all English-speaking college-age young people.
2. Meetings are held daily from **9:00 AM–11:45 AM** at the Ministry Conference Center.
3. Responsible ones should ensure young people return to hospitality from **12:00–3:00 PM** each day.
4. Do not leave young people at the LSM campus during this time.

## V. Facilities and Services

### A. Living Stream Ministry Bookstore
1. Located in the northeast corner of the meeting room and in the North Lobby.
2. Open **45 minutes before and after each meeting** and during the break.
3. No food or drink is permitted inside.
4. The bookstore in Building 4 is open **Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–3:00 PM**.

### B. Concessions
1. Limited concessions are provided by Acaciawood during the dinner break.
2. Located on the east side of Building 7.
3. Follow posted directional signs.

### C. Lost and Found
1. Located at the Information Window in the southeast corner of the room.

## VI. Safety and Conduct

### A. Security
1. Especially for the sisters, do not walk alone after dark.
2. Walk in groups of two or more whenever possible.

### B. Pictures
1. Do not take pictures during any training meeting.

### C. Recording
1. Audio and video recording of messages is prohibited.
2. Live streaming the messages is prohibited.
3. Violations are grounds for dismissal from the training.

### D. Note-taking
1. Handwritten and electronic notes are for personal study only.
2. Notes may not be circulated.
3. No electrical outlets are provided for laptops or other devices.
4. Do not use campus electrical outlets.

### E. Dress code
1. Let the peace of Christ arbitrate in your hearts.
2. Consider one another in brotherly love.

### F. Parking
1. Follow the parking diagram in your training packet.
2. Follow the directions of the police and parking brothers.
3. Vehicles without an LSM Event Parking permit in designated stalls are subject to towing at the owner's expense.
4. This includes vehicles parked in the bus parking area.

### G. Cell phones
1. Turn off all cell phones during the training meetings.

### H. Special meetings
1. For meetings in the northwest rooms, use the northwest exterior doors.
2. Refer to the map in your registration packet.
