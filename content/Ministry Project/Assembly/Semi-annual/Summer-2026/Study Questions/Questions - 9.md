# Message Nine—Study Questions

1. What does it mean to overcome in the epistle to Philadelphia in [[Revelation-03#v7|Revelation 3:7-13]]?



2. Have some fellowship concerning the three aspects of “this ministry” ([[2 Corinthians-04#v1|2 Cor. 4:1]]), the unique New Testament ministry, the ministry of the new covenant as revealed in the book of 2 Corinthians.



3. Have some fellowship concerning the three characteristics of the overcomers in Philadelphia. In your fellowship, include some testimonies concerning your experience of either having a little power, keeping the word, or not denying the Lord's name.



4. What is the supply for us to “shine as luminaries in the world” ([[Philippians-02#v15|Phil. 2:15]])?



5. What are murmurings and reasonings? What is the significance of murmurings and reasonings being mentioned in [[Philippians-02#v12|Philippians 2:12-16]]? What is the way to be saved from murmurings and reasonings?
