# Message Three—Study Questions

1. The four obstacles that restrict God’s life in man and the unique solution to these impediments.



2. The concept of “martyrdom” applies to the sowing of the word of the kingdom into us, which through growth and development makes us sons of the kingdom, the good seed, to produce the realm of the kingdom through fruitfulness and multiplication.



3. Our genuine service to the Lord must originate from God and issue from God’s work in and for us.



4. Salvation from obsession and self-deception is to live under the shining of the divine light in God’s word and the sense of life.

1. The four obstacles that restrict God’s life in man and the unique solution

The believers as the wheat have the divine life within them, but this life needs room to grow, spread, and fill our whole being. The four obstacles that restrict God’s life in us are: first, not knowing the pathway of life and not taking Christ as our life; second, hypocrisy, or outward spirituality without taking care of Christ; third, rebellion, which is ignoring, rejecting, or disobeying the living Christ within us; and fourth, our natural capabilities, such as our natural wisdom, strength, power, and gift. The unique solution is that we must experience the breaking of the cross, the killing death of Christ in the all-inclusive Spirit. If these obstacles are dealt with and removed, Christ as life has the way to grow, spread, and be expressed through us. Lord, save us from being a hindrance to Your life. Make us the real wheat of life.

2. Martyrdom and the sowing of the word of the kingdom into us

The believers are the good seed, the sons of the kingdom, because Christ as the unique good seed has been sown into us through the word of God. According to Matthew 13:38, the good seed are the sons of the kingdom. The word of God is sown into us, grows in us, and multiplies to make us the good seed. But this growth requires death. First Corinthians 15:36 says that what we sow is not made alive unless it dies. This is why the sowing of the good seed is martyrdom. We may not all be physical martyrs, but we can experience psychological and spiritual martyrdom as our soul-life and natural life are killed. Through this death, the divine life is released, grows, and bears fruit in resurrection. This fruitfulness produces the realm of the kingdom, where God can rule and have His authority.

3. Genuine service must originate from God and issue from God’s work in and for us

All our service to the Lord must originate from God, not from ourselves. Even if we love God and have the ability to do something for Him, we still need to stop when God’s word comes. David is the pattern. He loved God and desired to build the temple, but when God said no, David stopped. His stopping testified that all work in the universe should come from God, not from man, and that what matters is not mainly what man does for God, but what God does for man. Even the builder of the temple and the site of the temple came out of David’s being forgiven, out of what God did for David. This shows that genuine service issues from God’s work in us and for us. As M. E. Barber said, “Whoever cannot stop working for the sake of God cannot work for the sake of God.”

4. Salvation from obsession and self-deception by divine light

The believers are the light of the world, but to be saved from obsession and self-deception, we must live in the light and be open to God’s shining. Obsession means that what a person thinks and does may be totally wrong, yet he believes he is totally right. This is worse than merely sinning, because to sin and then justify it is a matter of darkness. The reasons for this self-deception include loving darkness rather than light, pride, not receiving the love of the truth, and not seeking the glory that is from the only God. The way to be saved is not to generate our own light, but to come under God’s light. Life comes from the shining of light, light is in the word of God, and light is also the inner sense of life. So we need the living Word and the inner sense in our spirit to shine on us, expose us, and bring us into life.
