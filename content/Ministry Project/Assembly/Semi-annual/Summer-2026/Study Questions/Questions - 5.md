
# Message Five—Study Questions

1. Present the line of development concerning the revelation of “stone” in the Bible from the Old and the New Testaments.



2. What is the intrinsic significance of stone in the Bible? Show the secret of experiencing the Stone-Christ through the seven eyes upon the stone (Zech. 3:9) and the seven lamps of fire before God’s throne (Rev. 4:5).



3. How do we practically experience Christ as the corporate smiting stone to crush the aggregate of human government to bring in God’s kingdom?



4. What is the significance of the Shepherd-Christ exercising righteous judgment between the sheep?


5. What is the significance of God’s people being shepherded under His Servant David and the significance of the covenant of peace that God made with His people?
