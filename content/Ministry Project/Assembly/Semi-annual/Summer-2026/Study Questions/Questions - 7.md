
# Message Seven—Study Questions

1. What is it to be an “open vessel”? How does this relate to your experience of being a vessel of mercy unto honor and glory?



2. How can you as a vessel of honor inoculate others against the decline of the church?



3. How can your appreciation of Christ as your indwelling treasure constitute you a vessel of glory for His expression?



4. Relate your experience of being a grain of wheat to that of your being an “open vessel.”



5. Relate your experience of abiding in Him as a branch of the vine to your being an “open vessel” of mercy unto honor and glory and to your being a grain of wheat. Describe experiential keys that are able to strengthen your abiding in Him.
