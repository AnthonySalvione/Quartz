
# Message Eight—Study Questions
1. Explain the significance of Christ being the Son of Man as revealed in Matthew and how this revelation can be applied to your experience.



2. Explain the significance of how Christ began His ministry for the kingdom of the heavens with the heavenly light. What does this mean in your experience?



3. Explain the significance of Christ being the Physician and the Bridegroom, and share how these two aspects of the revelation of Christ help your enjoyment of Christ.



4. Share your experience of Christ being your Shepherd and the Lord of the harvest.
