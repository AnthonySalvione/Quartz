# Overflow

As the believers, we have a wonderful status: we are the brothers of Christ as the Firstborn Son of God. However, in order for us to achieve such a status, we cannot work it out by ourselves. There is the need of the process of designation, both for Christ and for us. Praise the Lord, He was the only begotten Son of God from eternity past. However, through His incarnation, Christ put on His humanity. Such a humanity is part of the old creation. This is why He needed to go through the process of death and resurrection. In His death, His humanity was crucified on the cross, and in resurrection, especially the Spirit of holiness rose up in Him and operated in Him, and put His divinity into His humanity so that He could uplift His humanity into divinity.

So, in resurrection, Christ was designated as the firstborn Son of God. Today, Christ has gone through these processes. But how about us? We also need to pass through the same process He went through. Especially, I was very touched that just like a seed is put in the earth, we also must go through death and resurrection. As we go through this process, our outer man is consumed for the inner life to grow and to develop, ultimately to blossom in resurrection. Also, the process of our designation is the process of resurrection with four main aspects: sanctification, transformation, conformation, and glorification. What is the key to experience this? That is the Spirit of Christ, the rising-up Spirit in our spirit, the designating Spirit, the power of life in our spirit. Our need today is to walk according to the spirit. The more we touch this Spirit, the more we are sanctified, transformed, and glorified to become God in life and in nature. Then, the more we are conformed to the image of His Son, that He may be the Firstborn among many brothers.
