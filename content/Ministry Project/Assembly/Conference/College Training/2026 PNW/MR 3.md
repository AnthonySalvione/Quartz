Brothers and sisters, where is our inner chamber? It's our spirit. 

If we want to spend time with the Lord, contact Him, see HIm and be infused with Him... we have to know where He is. HE IS IN OUR SPIRIT!! So, we must become learners of how to get to our spirit. One of the hinderences of this is our mind. Have you ever woken up - "oh, what is the schedle today?!? What time should I leave by?!? etc..." Then, your time is gone. Or a sudden worry comes up, or you suddenly remember you left something... you don't remember after your morning time, or yesterday night, but RIGHT when you come to the Lord to spend time with Him. We need to learn to CALM and QUIET our mind... even to tell our mind to be quiet, "not now. Right now is my time with the Lord" So, that is on the negative side

On the positive side - what is the exercise of our spirit? It's to exercise the FUNCTIONS of our spirit. The primary, leading function of our spirit is our conscience.
