# Message Six — Branches in the Vine

## Scripture Reading

John 15:1-11

## I. The believers in Christ are fruit-bearing branches abiding in the vine.

### A. The vine (Christ) with its branches (the believers in Christ) is the organism of the Triune God in God's economy to grow with His riches and to express His divine life—John 15:1-5.

### B. We can do nothing apart from Christ as the vine; thus, we need to abide in Him, to remain, stay, and dwell in Him, living by Christ Himself—vv. 4-5.

### C. To abide in Christ is to abide in His love so that we may love Him and love one another to express the divine life in fruit-bearing—John 15:9-10, 16-17.

### D. To abide in Christ is to keep ourselves in the fellowship with Him daily and hourly, not allowing anything to come between us and Him; the entire church life depends on the divine fellowship, which is the circulating Triune God flowing and dispensing all that He is into us for our enjoyment—1 John 1:3; 2 Cor. 13:14.

### E. In order for the Lord to abide in us, we must let His words abide in us; if we abide in His written Word by coming to Him for life, His instant words will abide in us as spirit and life—John 15:7; 5:39-40; 8:31; 6:63.

## II. Fruit-bearing is the normal living of the branches in the vine—John 15:4-5.

### A. The vine is very good for propagating life and for multiplying life; the vine tree is good only for bearing fruit.

### B. The Lord is not life for people to appreciate as blossoms; the Lord is life to bring forth life and to produce life—John 12:24.

### C. Fruit-bearing is the overflow of the riches of the inner life; out of the abundance of the inner life there will be a flow that will reach others, penetrating into their lives, so that they may become remaining fruit for the Father's glorification—John 15:16.

#### 1. Fruit in John 15 refers to new believers, new converts; this is proven by verse 16 which says that we should "go forth" and bear fruit.

#### 2. This is different from the fruit of the Spirit in Galatians 5:22, which is the expression of the life of Christ within us; to bear the fruit of the Spirit, there is no need to "go forth."

### D. All the fruit-bearing branches need to be pruned, which is to cut off anything old so that the branch can remain new, fresh, and tender—v. 2; cf. 1 John 1:5-7.

### E. Not bearing fruit will result in losing the enjoyment of the riches of Christ, missing the rich fellowship of the other branches, being separated from the expression of God, and being cut off from the purpose of God—v. 6.

## III. All the branches that bear fruit are related to one another; our fruit-bearing is in one Body and with one life.

## Focus

As branches abiding in Christ as the vine, we are enjoying the divine fellowship and remaining new, fresh, and tender in order to bear remaining fruit for His glorification.
