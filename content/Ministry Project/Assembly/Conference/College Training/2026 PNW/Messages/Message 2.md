# Message Two — Evangelists

## Scripture Reading

Acts 21:8; Eph. 4:11-12; 2 Tim. 4:5; Acts 5:42; Acts 2:46-47

## I. Our Savior God's desire is for all men to be saved—1 Tim. 2:4; John 3:16-17.

## II. The work of an evangelist is to gain people for the building up of the Body of Christ:

### A. For the building up of the Body of Christ, we need material; in order to gather material for the building up of the Body, we need to go to sinners and help them to believe into the Lord, thus making them members of the Body—Hag. 1:8; Eph. 4:12.

### B. God has established a category of people in the church called evangelists; but to those who are not evangelists, the command of the apostle is that they should do the work of an evangelist—Eph. 4:11; 2 Tim. 4:5.

#### 1. Through his ministry and preaching of the gospel as recorded in Acts 8, Philip was manifested to be an evangelist; because he had an outstanding gift of gospel preaching, he was eventually called "Philip the evangelist"—Acts 21:8.

#### 2. The young apostle Timothy was not in himself an evangelist, but Paul asked him to do the work of an evangelist—2 Tim. 4:5.

## III. "Do the work of an evangelist"—2 Tim. 4:5.

### A. We all need to spend time practicing to do the work of preaching the gospel—Acts 8:5, 25, 35.

### B. In our daily life the Lord may send us to our neighbors, relatives, classmates, or friends for us to speak to them—2 Tim. 4:2.

### C. Sometimes the opportunity is ripe, so you need to encourage them to believe; at other times the opportunity is not ripe, but you still need to encourage them.

### D. In order to preach the gospel in a prevailing way for the spreading of Jesus, we need prayer, the Spirit, the Word, and the homes—Acts 1:14; 2:4, 42, 46-47; 6:4; 13:1-5; 16:25, 31-34.

## IV. The best place to preach the gospel is in the homes—Acts 5:42; 2:46-47.

### A. We need to use our mouth to speak the gospel, but we also need our daily living to confirm our preaching—Phil. 1:27; 1 Thes. 1:5.

### B. The New Testament shows that the gospel was preached in the homes of sinners and in the homes of God-fearing people—Acts 5:42; 10:22; 16:32; 20:20.

### C. Watchman Nee notes that the homes of believers have played a crucial role in the history of the church from the earliest of days:

> "In the homes there is a delightfully unofficial air about the gatherings."

### D. When we preach the gospel in our homes, we can do many things that cannot be done in big meetings; we can freely give our testimonies, and our gospel friends can freely ask questions.

#### 1. Many who might not be willing to go to a church may be happy to come to a private home.

#### 2. George Müller's first home meeting as an unbeliever was filled with hymn singing, prayer, reading the Bible, and fellowship over the Word.

### E. If we take care of keeping our home open and preserving the warm atmosphere in our home, we will be able to bring people to the Lord frequently.

## Focus

Every believer can do the work of an evangelist and gain people for the building of the Body; to do the work of an evangelist we can practice to pray, be filled with the Spirit, pour out the Word, and open our homes to bring some sinners to the Lord every year.
