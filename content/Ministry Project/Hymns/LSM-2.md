# The Triune God, a mystery
## Stanza 1
The Triune God, a mystery:
God is one and He’s three!
O Triune God, You’re wonderful!
You’re wonder—wonderful!
The Triune God a mystery:
God is one and He’s three!
O Triune God, You’re marvelous!
You’re marvel—marvelous!

## Chorus 1
	B: A Child is born to us;
	S: The mighty God is He.
	B: A Son is given to us;
	S: Eternal Father He.
	B: The Child, the mighty God;
	S: His name is Wonderful!
	B: A Son and Father one;
	T: A wonderful Person! 

## Stanza 2
The Lord is now the Spirit;
A wondrous mystery.
Christ, the life-giving Spirit
Has entered into me!
The Lord is now the Spirit.
Oh, this is glorious!
Christ, the life-giving Spirit,
Has entered into us.

## Chorus 2
	B: The last Adam became
	S: The life-giving Spirit.
	B: The Lord is the Spirit
	S: The all-inclusive Christ.
	B: The last Adam became
	S: The life-giving Spirit.
	B: Christ is the Lord Spirit;
	T: He’s now indwelling us.

## Stanza 3
Our Father God, the source;
God the Son has reached me.
God as the Spirit's flowing free;
He flowed God into me!
Our Father God, the source;
God the Son is the course.
God as the Spirit entered me:
'Tis the greatest mystery!

## Chorus 3
	B: The Triune God in me;
	S: The greatest mystery!
	B: Oh, this is glorious;
	S: The Triune God in us!
	B: The Triune God in me;
	S: The greatest mystery!
	B: Oh, this is marvelous;
	T: The Triune God in us!
