# Wash me, Lord, wash me how
## Stanza 1
Wash me, Lord, wash me now!
I've been contaminated somehow
In my walk on this earth
I've been covered in dirt
But I trust now in Your cleansing pow'r.

## Stanza 2
Wash me from more than sin
From the dryness and deadness within
In Your life, in Your Word,
In Your Spirit, wash me, Lord,
And restore my love for You again.
