# A master of dreams
## Stanza 1
A standing sheaf,
A shining star.
My dream reveals
Who God's people are.
We're full of light,
And life divine,
We live in Christ,
In us He shines.

## Chorus 1
	A "master of dreams,"
	I choose to live in what I've seen.
	For Christ in me beams,
	A shining star, a living sheaf.

## Stanza 2
Though many fail,
And fall to sin.
It doesn't change—
God’s chosen them.
We’re not a judge,
But shepherds true.
We overcome
So they can too.
> *(Sing Chorus 1)

## Stanza 3
The world is but
A glance away.
It speaks to me
To sin each day.
But I will flee,
With others too.
We reign as kings,
The lust subdue.

## Chorus 2
	Yes, I’ve seen the dream!
	I live in Christ and He in me!
	His word I believe!
	The vision’s real and reigns in me.

We won't be swayed
To love the world.
Though ev'n betrayed,
We'll gain the Lord.
Restricted now,
In grace He'll train,
The coming age
With Him to reign.
> *(Sing Chorus 2)
