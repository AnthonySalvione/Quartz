# I'm in Christ, now rejoicing
## Stanza 1
I'm in Christ, now rejoicing—
From old Adam I am free!
All old things are becoming
Both new and heavenly!
Praise God! I'm tasting and enjoying
Life and peace and liberty!
Praise God! I'm in Christ eternally!
