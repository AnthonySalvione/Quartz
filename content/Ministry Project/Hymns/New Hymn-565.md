# Melt in His Presence

*A short song based on an Augustinian quote*

## Stanza 1
Melt in His presence, 
Joy before His face;
Rest in His nearness,
Lost in His embrace.
Gaze at His beauty,
Faint upon His sight;
Bathe in His glory,
Bide in His delight.

## Stanza 2
Melt in His presence,
Sit still at His feet; 
Hide in His bosom,
Fix on His heartbeat;
Yearn for His whisper,
Long after His voice;
Care for His senses,
Live unto His choice.

## Stanza 3
Melt in His presence,
Sink into His Word;
Breathe in His each breath,
Feast on all His words.
Must on His speaking,
Read into His thought;
Join to His being,
Share in all His lot.

## Stanza 4
Melt in His presence,
See into His heart;
Take on His burden,
Mind His secret charge.
Pray in His Spirit,
Rhyme with His desire;
Ask with His boldness,
Glow with His own fire.

## Stanza 5
Melt in His presence,
Joy beneath His gaze;
Praise for His wonder,
Sing of His amaze;
Bless for His sweetness,
Shout to His true grace;
Wait for His coming,
Watch for His dear trace!
