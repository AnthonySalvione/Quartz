# I belong to Jesus
## Stanza 1
I belong to Jesus;
I am not my own;
All I have and all I am
Shall be His alone.

## Stanza 2
I belong to Jesus;
He is Lord and King,
Reigning in my inmost heart
Over everything.

## Stanza 3
I belong to Jesus;
What can hurt or harm,
When He folds around my soul
His almighty Arm?

## Stanza 4
I belong to Jesus;
Blessed, blessed thought!
With His own most precious blood
Has my soul been bought.

## Stanza 5
I belong to Jesus;
He has died for me;
I am His and He is mine
Through eternity.

## Stanza 6
I belong to Jesus;
He will keep my soul,
If the deathly waters dark
Round about me roll.

## Stanza 7
I belong to Jesus;
And ere long I’ll be
With my precious Savior there
In His royalty.
