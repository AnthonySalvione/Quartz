# Ephesians 2 Memorization

#flashcards/Ephesians/Chapter-2

## How to Use This Note

1. Review only a few new cards each day. Three or four new verse cards is a good starting pace.
2. Say the entire answer aloud before revealing it.
3. Grade yourself strictly:
   - **Again** — any omitted, added, substituted, or reordered word.
   - **Hard** — the wording was exact, but you needed a long pause or nearly missed something.
   - **Good** — word-perfect after normal effort.
   - **Easy** — word-perfect and immediate.
4. Learn the individual verses first. The transition cards then teach you how the verses connect, and the passage cards build continuous recitation.
5. Punctuation can guide your phrasing, but grade primarily for exact wording and word order.
