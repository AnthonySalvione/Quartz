Ephesians 3:1 ➤ For this cause I Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus on behalf of you, the Gentiles—
Ephesians 3:2 ➤ If indeed you have heard of the stewardship of the grace of God which was given to me for you,
Ephesians 3:3 ➤ That by revelation the mystery was made known to me, as I have written previously in brief,
Ephesians 3:4 ➤ By which, in reading it, you can perceive my understanding in the mystery of Christ,
Ephesians 3:5 ➤ Which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men, as it has now been revealed to His holy apostles and prophets in spirit,
Ephesians 3:6 ➤ That in Christ Jesus the Gentiles are fellow heirs and fellow members of the Body and fellow partakers of the promise through the gospel,
Ephesians 3:7 ➤ Of which I became a minister according to the gift of the grace of God which was given to me according to the operation of His power.
Ephesians 3:8 ➤ To me, less than the least of all saints, was this grace given to announce to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ as the gospel
Ephesians 3:9 ➤ And to enlighten all that they may see what the economy of the mystery is, which throughout the ages has been hidden in God, who created all things,
Ephesians 3:10 ➤ In order that now to the rulers and authorities in the heavenlies the multifarious wisdom of God might be made known through the church,
Ephesians 3:11 ➤ According to the eternal purpose which He made in Christ Jesus our Lord,
Ephesians 3:12 ➤ In whom we have boldness and access in confidence through faith in Him.
Ephesians 3:13 ➤ Therefore I ask you not to faint at my afflictions for your sake, since they are your glory.
Ephesians 3:14 ➤ For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father,
Ephesians 3:15 ➤ Of whom every family in the heavens and on earth is named,
Ephesians 3:16 ➤ That He would grant you, according to the riches of His glory, to be strengthened with power through His Spirit into the inner man,
Ephesians 3:17 ➤ that you, being rooted and grounded in love,
Ephesians 3:18 ➤ May be full of strength to apprehend with all the saints what the breadth and length and height and depth are
Ephesians 3:19 ➤ And to know the knowledge-surpassing love of Christ,
Ephesians 3:20 ➤ But to Him who is able to do superabundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power which operates in us,
Ephesians 3:21 ➤ To Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus unto all the generations forever and ever. Amen.
