
# Question concerning gospel contacts 
I was telling Mike tonight about our conversation and about Anthony serving at Fullerton. We have a few questions. Maybe he could read on a piece of paper some of his experiences and you could just take a picture of it and send it back. 

First is getting a solid follow-up. What have you learned? People write their name down on the Bible table list or when you're out in the Square, and then they don't respond back. 

Do they try to make contact with the students that have prayed or seem real positive within a day or two? 

And then I would say just anything that he feels has been helpful to him in being on campus that I can share with the other people that serve with the BFA table. 
It was so wonderful seeing both of you today!

# My response
Thanks for reaching out and for the thoughtful questions about gospel contacts!! I'm happy to respond!

I ran into this situation a lot regarding follow-up responses. One thing that really helped me was defining clearly what the goal of reaching out is. It may differ a little depending on where you are and what the campus work is like. 

In Fullerton, our main goal was to gain a general understanding of who the contact is, and then after that, to inform them of any activities related to our Christian club events or college meetings in the homes.

The main reason for getting an understanding of the contact first before inviting them to something, is to discern where they are at in their faith. Through conversation, we've found that some of our contacts were currently meeting, or have met in the past with groups that are negative towards the Lord's Recovery (this is just one example). The concern was that they may take whatever we say or read in a meeting, and bring it to their pastor or somebody like that who may interpret it the wrong way. In these cases, we liked to meet with them individually to gain more of a connection and 'inoculate them' to the ministry. 

Another big reason why we converse with them, is so that we have more ways to pray for them and their specific needs. We would always bring certain matters to our team group, so we could corporately pray for these individuals. I found that this impacted our success immensely, and gave me more of a burden for them.

I think that contacting ones who approached the table should be done quickly. The goal here is just to get any kind of response back from them, so we try to include a question in all of our texts. We try to text them after the time classes would get out, so that they would be more likely to be free to respond, rather than seeing the message and forgetting to respond later.

In general, the template that we liked to use in Fullerton was something like this:

1st text: "Hey👋 is this (so and so)?"

2nd text: "This is Anthony with the Fullerton Christian Students, we met you at our club table on campus!" (reminding them of who we are in case they're wondering)

Then if they respond, try to make some conversation. Some examples would be "How have classes been going for you so far?" (If it's the beginning of the quarter), "How do you like the school/campus here?", "Are you living in the dorms here on campus?" Or maybe, "Are you a Christian? Do you believe in Jesus?" 

Then after this, we would invite them to our bible study on campus. We might also invite them to the college meetings at the meeting hall, or even to the Lord's table (depending on our inward sense/feeling). 

This text is something like "We'll be having a large group Bible study tomorrow at lunch time. Would you be free to come and check it out?"
