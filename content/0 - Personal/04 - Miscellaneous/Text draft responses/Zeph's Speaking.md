## Full-min class—Message One

Context: Ron Kangas asked some of the fourth-term brothers to explain what is God's economy?

In the outline, we see in Acts 13:33—“This day I have begotten You.” This refers to Christ’s being born again as the firstborn Son of God for the reproduction and duplication of Himself. So, God’s economy is something that originates from His good pleasure and purpose—that is, to have His corporate expression. First, it was Jesus as the only begotten, and was the Father’s delight. But it was the Father’s delight that He would be multiplied in us. Jesus is the life-giving Spirit now. In the stage of inclusion, He is including us in what He is. This will meet God’s heart’s desire.

## Full-min class—Message Six Overflow

In His heavenly ministry, Christ is shepherding us into this divine-universal incorporation. Hebrews 13:21 shows us that the God of peace *is* the great Shepherd of the sheep who perfects us in every good work. The work that Christ is doing now is shepherding. He is perfecting us by shepherding. In this age of inclusion, He is carrying out the same work of shepherding—shepherding the flock of God. In John chapter 21, we see Him say to Peter, "If you love Me, shepherd My sheep". So, *we also* are charged to shepherd the flock of God. This is what God is doing to incorporate Himself with us to become one with us. We need to carry out a shepherding work to bring others into this divine-human incorporation. Christ has much more shepherding work to do.

## God's Economy class—Message Six Overflow

In Galatians 5:16 and 22-23, we see the matter of walking by the Spirit and the fruit of the Spirit fulfills God's economy. In the reading there was the example of working out self-control in ourselves. This results in a gnashing of teeth. This is because when we came into the church life, we brought in our natural attributes. These things may seem profitable, but they do not accomplish God's Economy. The essence of our living should be the consummated Triune God. When we walk by the Spirit by contacting our mingled Spirit, there is a dispensing occurring within us—this dispensing of the Spirit is how Christ is expressed out of us as the fruit of the Spirit. As a result, there is a church life. This is how God's purpose and intention is fulfilled. *For this*, in our walking, we must do two things to remain in our mingled spirit: Watch and pray, watch lest we leave our spirit, and pray to remain and enter back into our spirit. These are the key indicators of when we have left the spirit: murmuring, dissatisfaction, not in oneness, and when we lack love to others. This is an indicator that we have left our spirit!! We need to turn to our spirit by praying! More dispensing goes on when this happens!

## Saturday Video class—Message Seven Overflow

Redemption in Ephesians 1:7 is a matter of life and grace. For example, when we eat the food in the cafeteria, we often do not think about the process behind it: the groceries being grown, gathered, cooked, and prepared so that the food can be delivered to us.
