## Positive Idols

- Image of man or woman (Num. 4:16).
- Image of any animal on the earth, and any winged bird which flies in the sky (Num. 4:17).
- Image of anything that crawls on the ground, and any fish that is in the water (Num. 4:18).
- The sky, sun, moon and stars, all the host of heaven can draw us away to worship them (Num. 4:19). Mentioned again in (Deut. 17:3).
- Catch all, the form of *anything at all*, can be an idol. Implying idols can be anything other than God. (Num. 4:23)

## Negative Idols?

- 1 Samuel 6:17 ➤ The Philistines made golden images of certain curses that had been placed on them, which I find interesting. In this case, they made golden models of rats or mice... which had been infesting their land.
- 1 Samuel 6:18 ➤ Along with that, they made golden models of tumors, representing the swollen lumps or afflicted body parts from their curse.
