# Good — טוֹב (_tov_)

> [!summary] Basic Meaning  
> Describes what is positively evaluated as pleasant, beneficial, suitable, excellent, valuable, kind, or morally right. Its precise sense depends on what is being evaluated, who evaluates it, and according to what standard.

## Light

God should be the unique Evaluator, as the unique standard for what is good. Hence, only God should have knowledge of good and evil. When man partook of the tree of good and evil, he received knowledge according to what is good and what is evil, which automatically causes man to produce an evaluation of his own. Satan operates by speaking lies to us, with the intent to alter our sight and take in different evaluations other than God Himself. What is truly good and what is truly evil is only determined by God, it is not something objective like we often think.

Mentioned this study to my house, and Kam reminded me of [[Isaiah-07#v15|Isaiah 7:15-16]]. There is clearly a relationship between choosing good and eating Christ. It is a positive cycle. 

## Grammatical Uses

BDB treats טוֹב as both an **adjective** and a **noun**. These are overlapping uses of the same word, not different kinds of goodness.

| Form             | Function                   | Typical Sense                                    | Example                        |
| ---------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| טוֹב (_tov_)     | Adjective                  | Something or someone is good                     | [[Genesis-01#v4\|Gen. 1:4]]    |
| טוֹב (_tov_)     | Masculine substantive/noun | Good, the good, welfare, benefit, or good things | [[Genesis-02#v17\|Gen. 2:17]]  |
| טוֹבָה (_tovah_) | Feminine adjective         | Agrees with a feminine noun                      | “A good land”                  |
| טוֹבָה (_tovah_) | Feminine noun              | Welfare, bounty, benefit, or a good outcome      | [[Genesis-50#v20\|Gen. 50:20]] |

> [!note] Grammatical Gender  
> The masculine and feminine forms do not indicate masculine and feminine goodness. Context determines whether the emphasis is a good quality, “the good,” a benefit, or a favorable outcome.

## The Governing Issue: Who Determines What Is Good?

==Because _tov_ is an evaluative word, the decisive question is not merely **“What is good?”** but **“Who sees and determines that it is good, and according to what standard?”**==

|   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|
|Passage|Evaluator|What Is Evaluated as Good?|Significance|
|[[Genesis-01#v4\|Gen. 1:4]]|God|The light|God first establishes what is good by His own seeing.|
|[[Genesis-01#v31\|Gen. 1:31]]|God|His completed creation with man|What fulfills and is suitable for God’s purpose is very good in His sight.|
|[[Genesis-02#v9\|Gen. 2:9]]|God’s provision/narrative evaluation|The trees as food|God provides what is good, beneficial, and nourishing for man.|
|[[Genesis-02#v17\|Gen. 2:17]]|Good and evil as objects of knowledge|“The good” and “the evil”|Man is warned not to take the position of possessing this knowledge independently of God as life.|
|[[Genesis-03#v6\|Gen. 3:6]]|The woman|The forbidden tree|Human sight and desire now evaluate as good what God’s word had forbidden.|
|[[Genesis-06#v2\|Gen. 6:2]]|The sons of God|The daughters of men|The pattern continues: man sees, evaluates, chooses, and takes according to desire.|
|[[Genesis-50#v20\|Gen. 50:20]]|God’s intention contrasted with man’s|The outcome of Joseph’s suffering|Man intended evil, but God intended the same circumstances for good and the preservation of life.|

> [!important] Central Contrast  
> **Genesis moves from God seeing and declaring what is good to man seeing and choosing what appears good independently of God. It concludes by showing that God’s intention—not man’s evil intention—determines the ultimate issue for good.**

## Development of the Biblical Thought

1. **God is the true Evaluator of good.** He sees and declares His creation good according to its suitability for His economy—[[Genesis-01#v4|Gen. 1:4]], [[Genesis-01#v31|31]].

2. **Initially man was placed under God's speaking concerning what is good for food.** God's speaking produces a seeing governed by God and His purpose—[[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]], [[Genesis-02#v17|17]].

3. **Satan introduces a different kind of speaking, thereby altering man's seeing and valuation**—[[Genesis-03#v5|Gen. 3:5-6]].

4. **Man now sees, evaluates, chooses, and takes in independently from God**—[[Isaiah-05#v21|Isa. 5:21]].

## God as Good

The revelation progresses from the things God calls and gives as good to **God Himself as good**:

- **Jehovah is good and kind**—[[Psalm-34#v8|Psa. 34:8]]; [[Psalm-100#v5|100:5]]; [[Psalm-145#v9|145:9]]; [[Nahum-01#v7|Nah. 1:7]].
    
- **Jehovah is good and upright**—[[Psalm-25#v8|Psa. 25:8]].
    
- **God is good and does good**—[[Psalm-119#v68|Psa. 119:68]].
    
- His **hand**, **Spirit**, **name**, **lovingkindness**, **judgments**, **commandments**, and **promises** are called good—[[Ezra-07#v9|Ezra 7:9]]; [[Nehemiah-09#v20|Neh. 9:20]]; [[Psalm-52#v9|Psa. 52:9]]; [[Psalm-69#v16|69:16]]; [[Psalm-119#v39|119:39]]; [[Nehemiah-09#v13|Neh. 9:13]]; [[Joshua-21#v45|Josh. 21:45]].
    

> [!light] Intrinsic Significance  
> **Good is not ultimately an independent standard that man possesses and applies by himself. Genuine good has its source, standard, and determination in God Himself. Man’s fall involved accepting another source and evaluating what was good according to his own sight and desire. God’s intention is that man would depend on Him, receive Him as life, and allow what is good to proceed from God’s own nature, evaluation, and purpose.**
