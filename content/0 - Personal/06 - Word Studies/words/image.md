# Image — צֶ֫לֶם (_tselem)

> [!summary] Basic Meaning
> This word is from an unused root word meaning "to shade;" or a phantom. There are two contexts in which this word appears: (1) the image bestowed by God, and (2) the images fabricated by man. It is concerning the outward appearance, whereas "likeness" concerns the inward substance.
