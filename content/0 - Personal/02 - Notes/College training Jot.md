
# Burden 1 -

John 21 - fishing. I want to develop this point. When the fishers went fishing again after Christ died... and they were doing it EXACTLY THE SAME WAY as when the Lord found them at the beginning of his ministry... and it wasn't working. Then - the contrast.... the Lord fills their net, to the point where it was about to burst. The principle I saw was - WE ARE RELIGIOUS, but then when we have a lot of increase and the Lord blesses our gospel preaching, we have no choice but to break our old religion, our old ways of doing things, our own concept - and JUST NEED TO ENTER INTO THE LABOR!!! New ones can strengthen the relatinoship we have with our other laboring priests, bringing us together to care for them. Clearly one thing I see is that - we should focus on the increase, and catching and saving... and the WORK which we used to focus on, just TAKES CARE OF ITSELF AND FULLFILLS GODS PURPOSE.

Questions I have - what is the significance of the Lrod calling these fishers who fished in their natural profession?
