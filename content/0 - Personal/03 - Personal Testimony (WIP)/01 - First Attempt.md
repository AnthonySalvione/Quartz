## My grandparents

I believe that my family history is an indispensable part of my own testimony, probably the most impactful matter. I say this, because prayer can be the only way I am where I am today. Somebody must have been praying for me, and I know that my grandparents have so much love for me and my family, and have prayed innumerable amount of prayers for me. So, all that the Lord has done for my family in the past is crucial for me to be where I am today. I just have to praise the Lord for this privilege of being born in this family and receiving all this prayer.

My mom's parents (named John and Sue) have been dear pursuers of the Lord for the majority of their life. They both had Christians households that gave them a love for the Lord that they kept for their whole lives. 

I had the privilege of getting to know Sue's mom, my great-grandma while growing up. She was a missionary, however I didn't realize until later how big of a price she had paid to serve the Lord Jesus, because she just seemed so normal to me. She decided to leave everything she had behind in the United States, and moved to Bolivia in South America to spread the gospel and bring salvation to the poor regions in that area. Throughout this time, they were in an Orthodox Church, meeting regularly there. ## Context leading up to gardening

The biggest part of my life, and what consumed the most of my headspace was track & field. I always thought running was enjoyable, and I even had been doing a running club as early as elementary school. One thing I found pretty early on was that my feet were extremely pronated (flat feet), and my legs and back would tighten up and hurt so much when I ran longer distances. (I think part of this is due to how sensitive I am to pain). So I didn't have that much hope that I would ever be that great at the sport... but then I distinctly remember this one time at the end of 6th grade where they got all the boys together at the elementary school to have a race across the field as one of the many activities on the last day of school. I was really excited and nervous because I knew that I was pretty good, and had a shot at winning. When I ended up winning, that was the first time I had tasted what it was like to be glorified in something... and I think that planted a seed that lasted with me for quite some time. Clearly it was a pivotal moment for me because I clearly remember that day and how I felt afterwards, and there aren't many moments at that age that lasted in my memory.
