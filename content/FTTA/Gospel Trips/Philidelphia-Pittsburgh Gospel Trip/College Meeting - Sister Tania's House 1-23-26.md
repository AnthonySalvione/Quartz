# The Revelation of Jesus Christ and Loving Him with Our First Love

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-01#v1|Revelation 1:1]]; [[Revelation-01#v3|1:3]]; [[Revelation-02#v4|2:4]]; [[Revelation-03#v8|3:8]]; [[1 Corinthians-02#v9|1 Corinthians 2:9–10]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v5|1 Timothy 1:5]]

But you, who do you say that I am? We see in Matthew 16:17, Peter said "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God". Flesh and blood has not revealed this to you but the father who is in heavens. We see that the Lord did not just bring Peter into a Revelation, but He brought him into a VISION in Matthew 17. 

A vision governs everything that we do. It changes how we handle our schooling, our family, and everything else that we do. I hope that from this reading, your life would not be the same.

When Paul wrote to the Thessaloneans, he was writing to brand new believers. They were very young in the Lord. Paul would stay there maybe 3-4 weeks. Every chapter, Paul reveals something very profound. In every chapter you will find Paul saying that the Lord is coming back. In chapter 5, He opens a deeper matter. 

This is what we will be entering into this semester. Mainly the book of Revelation. This book may seem like a lot of different things, like frogs... but we must look at the first verse in this book. "The revelation of Jesus Christ." We must receive a revelation, but go even further to ask the Lord for vision. Something that will govern our entire lives. We must ask the question "Lord, who are you?"

Where is our love? In Revelation 2 and 3, there are a picture of seven churches. These are actually all local churches established in Asia Minor. The first church that is mentioned is Ephasus. You may know this church from an earlier section in the Bible. The name Ephasus means 'desireable'. Everything was going okay in this church. Nothing was a problem, except for one thing. That one thing was that they lost the first love. This was the beginning of the degradation of the church.

We need to ask two questions... "Lord, who are you?", and "Lord, where is Your love?". We need to come before the Lord with a sincere heart and open up to Him. The question should be to us, "where is our love?". When the Lord comes back, "where is our love?".
## I. The whole Bible reveals Christ; the book of Revelation especially, as the conclusion, completion, and consummation of the whole Bible, is “the revelation of Jesus Christ.”
*(Rev. 1:1; 1 Cor. 2:9–10)*

We need to be clear that the subject matter of this entire book is the revelation of Jesus Christ. God desires that we would love Christ in a particular way, so that He can reveal to us things that eye cannot hear and ears have not heard.

Can we all say this verse, "Lord, speak, I am listening.".

The Lord reveals Himself to those who love Him. The Spirit searches all things, even the depth of God... Can you imagine having God's depths reveal to you?
### A.
John first heard the voice (Rev. 1:10) and then saw the vision (Rev. 1:12). In spiritual matters, seeing depends on hearing (Rev. 2:7; cf. Rom. 10:17).

I really want to share this verse with you - Romans 10:17. I want to ask the question to you "how is your faith?". "How is your faith?". It seems like a rollercoaster, right? Sometimes it seems like it's almost impossible to open the Bible. I have something to tell you that will strengthen your faith. "The Author and Perfector of our Faith is the AUTHOR of the Bible". During this semester, if your faith is going up then down then up then down, just come here and receive the hearing of faith. You will realize that what you need is to be infused with faith. Don't try to run this race by yourself. "Nathan's a runner, right?". We can't run this race all on our own. You love all these different things... like sports and videogames - "I'm talking to myself".

You need to realize that the Lord can do this in us. All we need to do is open to Him. He will do it in us. We need to ask the Lord to not hold back. We have a picture with Mary, who poured out everything that she had on the Lord.
### B.
If our ears are dull and cannot hear, we cannot see ([[Isaiah-06#v9|Isa. 6:9–10]]).

This is a spiritual principle. We see this mainly in the book of Proverbs, also in Revelation. John first heard the voice of the Lord, then turned in order to see the vision.

In 1 Thessaloneans 5:23 Paul opens up a very important matter. He addresses our whole three-partite being. The utterance is very clear in this verse. "May your SPIRIT, your SOUL, and SPIRIT be preserved complete without blame." For the longest time in my Christian life, I thought that my spirit and my soul were the exact same thing... who else has thought that?
### C.
John received visions in his spirit:  
“I was in spirit on the Lord’s Day” (Rev. 1:10);  
“Immediately I was in spirit” (Rev. 4:2);  
“And he carried me away in spirit” (Rev. 17:3; Rev. 21:10).

There was four times that John mentioned being in spirit while writing the book of revelation. We see in these cases that the way to receive the Spirit is to use our own spirit.

We need to know our spirit. Have you ever gone to the gym before? You might be going to the gym with some friends all excited, but then the next day you feel a muscle that is so sore and you didn't even know that it was there. This is like our spirit!!! Sometimes we don't even know that it's there! The way to be in Spirit is to call upon the name of the Lord Jesus. Let's call on the name of the Lord together. Sometimes it's impossible to trust in what we feel, the only way to know for sure that we are in Spirit is to call upon the name of the Lord.
### D.
The blessing in this book is related to reading, hearing, and keeping the things written (Rev. 1:3).

We need to realize that this is a progression. We first read, then hear, and then keep the things that were written. What we don't realize is that we can also hear ourselves. If we go into our bedroom alone and pray-read these verses... we can both read, speak and hear these words. This is something that can greatly help us.

## II. To the messenger of the church in Ephesus write:
*(Rev. 2:1)*  
“But I have one thing against you, that you have left your first love” (Rev. 2:4).

### A.
The church in Ephesus was a desirable church, yet it had left its first love. The Lord’s rebuke was not concerning works, but concerning love toward Himself.

Again, "where is our love?". If you get into the details of these seven churches... yes, they were there in a literal sense... but also those seven churches were for telling a prophecy of the condition of the churches today, and also the condition of our heart today. We need to realize that the condition of our heart today as well as the condition of the church begins with our love. "Where is our love?".
### B.
The Greek word for *first* is the same as translated *best* in Luke 15:22. Our first love toward the Lord must be the best love for Him.

Can someone read this verse? The Father said to His slaves to bring our quickly the best robe. Christ is not only the first... but He is also the best. Therefore, we must love the Lord with our first and our best love. Our top love, first love and best love, must be reserved for Christ.

Our Christian life is not just an individual matter. Look at all of us today, we're all sitting together. Obviously the church is not just an individual matter.
### C.
The Body of Christ—the church—as the bride of Christ is a matter of love.

What if my wife said, "I love you, but just your head... not the rest of your body". This may sound funny, but this is what happens in the church today. The body of Christ, the church, is the Lord Himself. If we love Christ the head, we will love His Body. We must love one another. We must love the brothers and sister. Once we receive this vision, we will say wow. We will love one another. We will have a tremendous desire to save those who are on our heart.

To love the Lord is to love His body. Don't you desire those who you are close to to be saved?
### D.
Nothing but love can keep us in a proper relationship with the Lord.

When we get to the church in Philadelphia, the Lord recovers the genuine love, the first love of Christ in the church.

Nothing but love can keep us. You may thing, "Wow, I have so many things between me and the Lord... so many things that I do and say". There is a solution though. The solution is the blood of Christ. NOTHING but the blood can cleanse us from every sin, every problem. Whenever we take the blood, our relationship with the Lord is recovered.

The Lord's enemy, Satan, is very smart... He has so many strategies prepared for us to keep us from loving the Lord. One of his tactics is to prevent us from taking the blood of Christ.

Think about when Adam had eaten of the wrong tree in Eden... The Lord didn't say "what did you do?!?". Right? He's saying "where are you?". Because, He wants to bring us closer to Him. There is only one thing that can remove the barrier between us and the Lord. The blood. No period of waiting, no pleading, no doing... just the precious blood of the Lamb can cleanse us from every sin. He is saying "Where are you?" because He wants you to be closer to Him. So, we must choose to apply the blood.

## III. To the messenger of the church in Philadelphia write:
*(Rev. 3:1)*  
The Greek word for “Philadelphia” means brotherly love.

The Lord today is recovering the genuine love for Christ.
### A.
The church in Philadelphia is characterized by brotherly love.

### B.
As a sign, the church in ===Philadelphia prefigures the proper church life recovered=== since the loss of the first love in the church in Ephesus.

Losing the first and best love is the beginning of all degredation. The church of Philadelphia has three main characteristics, as we'll point out in the next point.
### C.
The Lord commends this church for having a little power, keeping His word, and not denying His name (Rev. 3:8).
These matters are crucial 
1) a little power - what pleases the Lord is not our doing much for Him, but our doing our best with what we have. "Oh, I haven't read much of the Bible... I don't see much, but do your best what we have seen thus far."
2) keeping His word - this is very important. As you go on in this semester, we hope to infuse into you the pure word of God. Do not fall into the fallacy that there are too many translations and interpretations... we need to return to the pure word of God.
3) not denying His name - when my wife married me, she took my name. How do you think the Lord feels when we take or meet in any other name besides the name of the Lord Jesus Christ? No matter where you go, you will find those who take the name of the Lord Jesus Christ.
### D.
The end of the charge is love (1 Tim. 1:5).

Remeber these questions today. "Who do you say that I am?" "Where are you?" and "Where is your love?". It is rather simple brothers and sisters. Just love Him. With all the "little power" that you have... just love Him. Don't get distracted with so many other things... just love Him. He will deal with all of our distractions and other loves, if we just Love Him. Only the blood can bring us back into a proper relationship with the Lord.

# Ministry Excerpt

The whole Bible reveals Christ. As the conclusion, completion, and consummation of the Bible, the book of Revelation especially is “the revelation of Jesus Christ” (1:1). Although this book also reveals many other things, the focus of its revelation is Christ.

On one hand, this book gives us the revelation of Christ, and on the other hand, it shows us the testimony of Jesus, which is particular and consummate (1:2, 9; 12:17; 19:10; 20:4). The testimony of Jesus is the church. Revelation presents the revealed Christ and the testifying church. In this book we have a particular and consummate record of the church. In no other book are the churches revealed as they are in the book of Revelation.

Although the church in Ephesus had so many virtues, it was degraded because it had left its first love. In Revelation 2:4 the Lord said, “I have one thing against you, that you have left your first love.” The Greek word for *first* is the same as the word translated *best* in Luke 15:22. Our first love toward the Lord must be the best love for Him. The church in Ephesus had left this best love toward the Lord.

When **John Nelson Darby** was very old, he was traveling and stayed in a hotel for the night. As he was going to bed, he prayed in a simple way, saying, “Lord Jesus, I still love You.” It is precious for an old saint to say this. John Nelson Darby began to love the Lord during his youth. After more than sixty years, he still loved Him. We all must daily tell the Lord, “Lord Jesus, I still love You. I may change in everything else, Lord, but I would never change in loving You. Rather, I want my love for You to increase all the time.” I read this paragraph about Darby more than twenty years ago, and I cannot tell you the help it has rendered me throughout the years.

We must constantly say, “Lord Jesus, I still love You.” Once we have fallen from our first love, our degradation has begun. We may remain the same in everything else—in work, labor, and in other things—but we are degraded because we have left our first love. Eventually, the church at Ephesus had more work but less love. Today we all must say that we want more love and less work. If we would do any work, it must be out of our love for the Lord. Love should motivate everything we do for the Lord. If we cannot do a certain thing because we love Him, we should not do it. Our work should simply be an expression of our love for Him. We must be like this; otherwise, we will not be kept in His presence.

*(Excerpts from Life-Study of Revelation, Msg. 1, 10, Witness Lee)*
