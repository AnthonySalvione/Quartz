There is a progression in this hymn - right? From Christ being infinite in eternity, thats how it starts. but then it says he became a finite man in time. What a mystery! This is what we christians believe.

In eternity he's the infitnite God, but in time he is a finite man. He entered into the realm of time and into hymanity to come into man. Then the second stanza says from Colossians - all the fullness of the godhead... bodily. Meaning, as one who has a human body.

While he was a man on the earth, he manifested the glroy of God in a human being. Then, (second half of stanza 2), in flesh he accomplished redemption on the cross. After that ,He became the Spirit. The whole hymn goes forward with the Lord being the Spirit after accomplishing redemption... as the Spirit he earnestly desires to be one with us.

Stanza 3 - everything that the Father is and has belongs to the Son. Everything of the Son's is the Spirit in our spirit. The Spirit makes the Son (the embodiment of the Father) real to us so that we could experience Him. Not just admire Him as the wonderful One, but experience Him as the Spirit with the Son and the Father. This is what separates the Lord's recovery from everyone esle - this matter of the spirit. Only in the Lord's recovery do we talk about the Lord Jesus becoming a life giving spirit. It's such a great matter.

We know God in the detailed inner realm of our heart. John said, "This is the true God". The God we worship in the Lord's Recovery is the true God - the God who became a man, died, and became the Spirit. He is now the Spirit, and now all that He is in the Spirit is ours. He is the Son, as the SPirit with the Father... to make Himself real to us every day.

Stanza 4 - He is the spirit of life. It causes by His word to be transfered into ue. We are learning to  come and receive the SPirit of life through the word. Does this point to being regenerated? His lief in us was conceived by our receiving the word. Then it goes on from there. Now that we have received Him, we need to go on. We need to come to Him again and again... especially in the word. Where do we see Him? Mainly in the Word, through the exercise of our spirit. We are a very particular group of christians - we not only read the bible, but we touch the word of the bible with our spirit. We learn to exercise our spirit when we come to the bible. We can look at Him in His word like a mirror (2 Cor. 3:18). As a glass (mirror) reflecting HIs glory as we gaze on Him... then we will be transformed like thyself (being transformed into the Lrod's image., for what purpose?) That He would be expressed through us. God wants to have a visible expression through us. So, we need to receive and look on Him as we read the Word so He can infused Himself into us so the Spirit can work metabolically to transform us.

In no other way could we be sanctified. We know that Christ is victor over every negative thing in the universe... but we need to share his victory (meaning we need to be brought into His victory so his victory becomes our victory) FOr this we need to be sanctified. The spirit is the the sanctifying, transforming and sanctifying spirit. Thus only spriitual we will be - we will be spiritual men, spiritual people. Unlike the corinthians - fleshy and fleshly. Believers living in their soul mind and flesh. Spiritual people doesn't mean you are strange... it means you are normal. We are heading in this direction. Those who live in theier spirit as opposed to living in their soul or flesh. As we become spiritual, we touch the life of glory. Whast is our destiny? Glory! Glory is something relateed to life - the life of Glory. The life within us brings us into glory, little by little more and more we will be brought more into the lords glory as we touch this life of glory.

Stanza 7 is the climax of the whole him. His spirit will saturate our whole being and permeate every part with God. Then, this will result in our being delivered from the old man. The old man is the man who does not have God in him. May be a good or bad man, doesnt matter... its the old man. Gods intention is to deliver us from the old man and every aspect of the old man to make us new in every way. Eventually, the consummation will be the NJ. We are gradully being delivered from the old man, the one who is separated from God. Every part eventually of our being WILL BE saturated from god. that will be our deliverance from the old man, one we are saturated by him. That consummation is the new jeruselam of course.

God doesn't want us to become him, he wants us to be byuilt into Him as a corporate entity. The last three words are great - "for his plan." That is something right? Fulfillment of God's eternal purpose. There is nothing grheater than this. He wants to produce a corporate expression of himself in humanity. This is the experience we need to have of the lord to become that corporate entitity that god desires to gain for his plan. This is one of the great hymns. You see - it starts with the lord being the infinite god in eternity... all the way down to gods eternal plan being fulfilled by his redemption, becomong the spirit, transformation, conformation, sanctification,, saturation.
