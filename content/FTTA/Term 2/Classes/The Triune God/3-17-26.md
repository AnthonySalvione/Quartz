For four centuries almost, there was no clear definition of "how does the spirit fit in in this matter of the three of the Godhead?" BUT, love is not the most basic relationship between a father and a son. This is not wrong to say, but it does not fully capture the distinctiveness of the Spirit as it relates to the Father and Son. The most basic relationship between Father and Son is a matter of life, not of love.
