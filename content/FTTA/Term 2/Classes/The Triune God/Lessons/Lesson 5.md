## IV. Some misconceptions about the distinction between the Triune God's essential and economical aspects:
### A. False: "There are two Trinities, an essential one and an economical one":
#### 1. There is one God, who is triune; yet there are two aspects to His being triune.
#### 2. The essential aspect of the Trinity relates to the Triune God as He exists in Himself.
#### 3. The economical aspect of the Trinity relates to the Triune God as He moves in His economy.
### B. False: "The essential aspect of the Trinity refers only to the side of His oneness":
#### 1. The essential aspect is an aspect of the Trinity, an aspect of the three-one God, not merely an aspect of His one essence.
#### 2. Recognizing the essential aspect of the Trinity helps us see how the eternal Three are also one.
### C. False: "The economical aspect of the Trinity refers only to the side of His threeness":
#### 1. The economical aspect is an aspect of the one God, who is eternally three-one, not merely an aspect of His three hypostases.
#### 2. Recognizing the economical aspect of the Trinity helps us see how the one God can be distinguished as three.

### D. False: "The essential aspect of the Trinity refers to the Triune God in eternity":
#### 1. The economical Trinity is also eternal; otherwise, we would be suggesting that the eternal God changes in His eternal existence.
#### 2. The essential Trinity is best understood as God existing in Himself, outside of time and within time.
### E. False: "The economical aspect of the Trinity refers to the Triune God in time":
#### 1. The essential Trinity also exists in time; otherwise, we would be suggesting that the eternal God ceases to exist in some aspect of His eternal being.
#### 2. The economical Trinity is best understood as God existing in His move or in His actions. Since His move is mostly prominent within time, His economical aspect is more prominent within time.
