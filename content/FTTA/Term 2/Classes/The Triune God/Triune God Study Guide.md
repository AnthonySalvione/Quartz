# OUTLINE OF THE MESSAGES FOR THE FULL-TIME TRAINING

## Helps for Study of *Living in and with the Divine Trinity*, Chapters 4-7

> **Note:** This study help should be distributed to the trainees after they have read and thoroughly studied chapters 4-7 of *Living in and with the Divine Trinity*, by Brother Witness Lee.

In chapters 4-7 of his *Living in and with the Divine Trinity*, Brother Lee presents “the portions in the New Testament revealing the Divine Trinity in the divine move and in our experience” (35). Each section in these four chapters bears a simple verse reference as its heading and presents a marvelous description of the operation of the Divine Trinity in that Scripture portion.

While the explicit description in each section conveys Brother Lee’s fundamental burden for each portion of Scripture, the way he arranged these portions also conveys a burden and message concerning “the Divine Trinity in the divine move and in our experience.” It seems that a very practical approach to studying this portion of the ministry, beyond the simple reading thereof, is to attempt to ascertain why Brother Lee arranged these portions the way he did. This study guide is designed to help the trainees identify the basis for his arrangement.

It must be said from the outset that this guide is merely that, a guide; it is not a prescriptive for how one must understand Brother Lee’s arrangement of these portions. There may be better (or worse) ways to understand his arrangement. But it can hardly be denied that Brother Lee had some internal basis for presenting these portions not in New Testament order but in the order that he did. As students using this book, we do well to try to perceive that basis.

An initial observation about these chapters should be made. In approaching these portions from the perspective of their arrangement, it becomes manifest that chapters 4-6 form a unit. Within these three chapters there is a very definite arrangement according to the move of the Divine Trinity. Hopefully this will be proved below. The very last portion dealt with in chapter 6 is Revelation 22:1-2, showing that “the entire city will live forever to express the Divine Trinity” (68). This forms a very firm conclusion to the arrangement of these three chapters. There does not seem to be the need for an additional chapter with further portions, yet Brother Lee offers us chapter 7 with twelve more selections from the Scriptures. Why? A careful study of chapter 7 seems to indicate that these portions are ones that he inadvertently missed in his presentation in chapters 4-6. In point of fact, the editor of this book tells us that indeed this is the case: upon further consideration of his topic, Brother Lee realized that he had omitted some portions which also show this marvelous matter of the Divine Trinity in the divine move and in our experience. Interestingly enough, these additional portions are arranged as well within this one chapter; they are not presented in New Testament order. Because of this, in the presentation below, the portions in chapter 7 are placed alongside those of chapters 4-6 and are made to intersperse with the arrangement of the latter.

> Witness Lee, *Living in and with the Divine Trinity*, Anaheim, CA: Living Stream Ministry, 1990.
