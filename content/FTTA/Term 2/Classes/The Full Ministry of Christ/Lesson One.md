# An Overview of Christ's Ministry in the Stage of Inclusion
## Introduction

There are a number of things I’d like to bring forth before we touch the first message in the outline. The first thing is how we got this kind of class. Those who have been here for 2, 3, or 4 terms… you realize that this requires at least two years to understand.

It was way back in 1996—Brother Lee was having some training with the co-workers, and he decided in the middle of the term, “We’re going to change the subject of this class to the subject we have now.” Then he looked directly at Ed and I and said that you will teach the class. For that half of the term we did a general overview, then the next term we started the full cycle, which is four terms, and we are in the third term in the cycle now.

### God's Perfect Will

I have a loving concern for all the saints of all ages—that you would make certain decisions on specific matters and settle them for the rest of your life.

The first is this verse in [[Ephesians-05#v17|Ephesians 5:17b]]: **“Understand what the will of the Lord is.”** Understand what the will of the Lord is. We’ll come to other verses in a few minutes regarding this, but there will be a time at the end of this age, before the kingdom age begins, when we will all stand before the Lord and He will respond to us.

Then we will see (and I will read the verses) that the vast majority of the saints did certain things that God… (they followed the Lord’s permissive will)—in other words, the Lord allows us to do that, and it is somewhat good and somewhat important… but actually, in the Lord’s heart, this is not His perfect will.

However, there will be a large number of saints (of Christians over all parts of the earth): when they come before the Lord’s judgment seat, the Lord will determine who will be in the bride, and who will be a co-king, and who will stand with Him during the next period of time—from God’s standpoint it is one day, and from our standpoint it’s 1000 years.

### A Personal Testimony

I remember (I don’t know how this started…) I was graduating from Princeton Theological School way back (maybe before your parents were born) in 1964. I don’t know where this decision came from, but I said, “I will only accept the perfect will of God; I will not accept the permissive will of God.”

So, I gave myself to the Lord for this understanding, but I didn’t know what the perfect will of God was. For the next two years, however, I just tried this or that… what about this place or that place.

Then I remember three years later, on a Thursday evening in a library, I finished my examination of all of these. I believe that someone was praying, and certainly the Lord was leading me to make this decision. I looked at all the different religious groups and decided, “I will leave all of them. I am leaving Christianity in a religious way, and I am going to go to California to find the perfect will of God.”

Then this young lady (who was my first wife—she passed away 10 years ago)… I told her, “Now we can get married and go to California, and the Lord will make it known what His perfect will is.” She was glad to respond to this.

So we (in the old-fashioned way) did a car trip across the country. When we got to California, I began to read certain things in the New Testament, and I realized that I think that I just need to be honest before the Lord and start speaking this powerful gospel of the kingdom.

However, somehow, I met another brother, and he asked me to come for a time of praying with a few brothers during lunchtime. We were there, then eventually there were only two of us. Then this brother said… “They are using certain words that really come from Brother Lee and Brother Nee… do you know that Brother Lee has a companion—Brother Witness Lee—and he is in California.”

I’ll go back a few months. After I got married, my wife had to finish her job for the next 5 or 6 weeks, and so I just began with the Greek and the English New Testament to thoroughly study Ephesians. I came to chapter 3—these important verses from 10 to 12. I realized here that I don’t know what this means.

If there is anyone who can really show me the meaning of these verses, I will follow that person. So that was my decision.

So now, I was in California, and at that time I was meeting with this one brother, and he mentioned Brother Lee, and he gave me some of his written material. One of them was **exactly** the ministry material that answered my question on these verses.

Then I realized that if anyone is able to understand and teach **this**, I can honestly and thoroughly follow that person. Then I could finally say, “I know what the perfect will of God is.”

From Revelation chapter 8 verse 11, we see that the will of God is related to everything that He has created. Then, in Colossians, the perfect will of God is experiencing and enjoying Christ. In Ephesians, the perfect will of God is the Body of Christ. So… this matter has to be settled. This is the essence of God’s will… that is, Christ and the church.

Then I realized that once I had settled this, then I can ask the Father directly, “What is your will for me?” Then that is what led me to be here now—because of this matter of the will of God, and knowing the difference between the permissive will of God and the perfect will of God.

The very first section (this is when we are saved), the second stage (almost all of you are in this stage when you come to the FTTA)—the second stage in which you consecrated yourself.

I have a deep and loving concern, however. I’ve seen this happen—I’ve been here for 60 years. When I came, I can honestly say that I knew nothing. I studied theology, but I am starting from the beginning in this.

I just started with these basic points in section 2. That was in the fall of 1966. In the spring of 1970, I was reading—pray-reading—this book all the way through. I came to this particular verse and I said, “Lord, I need to enter into the third stage of the experience of life.”

My heart aches that there are saints of my age that never went beyond the second stage. My understanding and observation: when you are coming to the end of your fourth term, you are gradually coming to this understanding. I am doing my best to make it known to you that you are in your third stage.

Your future depends entirely on whether you thoroughly go through the third stage or not. Then you will enter into the fourth stage—that means you are mature. You really know the Body, how to fight the enemy, and you are sitting with Christ on the throne.

Just consider what happened in the next 7 years, from 12 to 19. You are an adult now. It doesn’t take 57 years to grow to maturity. The requirement—or what is necessary—is that you will be one with the Lord, and realize that the Lord is thorough in everything that He does.

So, you are in the third stage dealing with the flesh and the self, dealing with your disposition. If we have this relationship, it is very much in my loving concern that you would go through the third stage in the next 7 years. It is not too soon. Some in the 4th term here have already entered into the third stage.

When you are in the fourth stage, then you really know the Body. There are so many dear middle-aged brothers: they talk about the Body so much. They know about the Body and doctrinally understand the Body, but whether we have gone through the third stage… we would realize that we could not live apart from being in the Body. I am a true member of the Body.

I just realize, however, these dear beloved saints—what they have is what is probably over most of the earth: the early morning material, wherever you are; pray-reading a couple verses… and reading probably a little ministry. However, this is not their personal life with the Lord.

God the Father—He has a loving concern for all of us, and He knows where we are. I, again and again, just encourage the saints to begin to have a personal relationship with the Lord.

And while I am sharing this with you, again I have this genuine hope that the Lord will make it known to you that He, as the life-giving Spirit, is in you. He is actually in the center of our being.

Now, what verse do you think testifies concerning this? The one that comes to mind first: Ephesians chapter 3—that Christ would make His home in our hearts. His home. He is not visiting over the weekend. He is making His home.

That is why, from God’s point of view, I have the responsibility to just share this with the seeking saints—which you are.

I want to be very careful too. Ninety percent of the time, you and the Lord just have this certain kind of sense: “I need to have a personal relationship with You… I am busy in the training, very busy with the schedule…” But one matter that I suggest is that you have a personal relationship with the Lord—that you pray-read through a particular book. Maybe just during the day, 5 or 6 minutes—just to absorb this.

Because I’ve seen this happen: so many dear saints—on the objective things—they are here in the meetings, they participate, do practical service… all of this is crucial, and I am not judging any of them—but it’s clear: they are not mature.

### The Three Stages of Christ

Okay. Now we will look at and go through lesson one in a few minutes. I want to share something else related to the outline.

The three stages of Christ—stages. We should just have some understanding, because we know that we want to understand what Christ is doing **now**. He is on the throne now, but what is He doing? What is He doing? He is one of us!

So, these are the three stages of Christ.

The first stage was His work while He was on the earth. The second was when He was in the heavens on the throne. Stage three is Christ intensified as revealed in Revelation.

At the end of this age, the situation on the earth will be so difficult that the Spirit will be seven Spirits—in an intensified and strengthened way. We need to understand that there will be times where you don’t need the seven Spirits. There will be situations, however, no matter what the enemy does: if we are in the third stage, then we can apply Christ’s victory over all the demons, all the evil powers.

So, the stage of incarnation—that is the first stage. The stage of inclusion—this is after the Lord has been raised from the dead and is now in another realm. This is the stage of inclusion. He included so much of the all-inclusiveness of Christ.

Then the stage of intensification—that is the one at the end. You will get reading about this later in the term.
