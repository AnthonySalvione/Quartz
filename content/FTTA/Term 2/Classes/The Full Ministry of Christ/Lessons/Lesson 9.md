# Outline — Salvation in Life: Transformation  

## Intro Word  
  
How about the two verses on transformation? [[Romans-12#v12|Rom. 12:2]] and [[2 Corinthians-03#v3|2 Cor. 3:18]]. These are just two verses on a very crucial aspect of our Christian life that takes place year after year. It does not happen suddenly.  
  
So, we will go through this outline, and then I will share something on another level on the same point.
## I. Transformation is not outward correction or adjustment, but the metabolic function of the life of God in us, by the addition of the element of the divine life of Christ into our entire being, that we may express the image of Christ outwardly  
[[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2a]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]]  
  
The life of God is functioning in our **entire being**.  
  
## II. God transforms us with the life element of the firstborn Son of God, the first God-man, who passed through death and entered into resurrection, until we are transformed metabolically into His image from one degree of glory to a higher degree of glory and are raptured and transfigured to enter into His glory  
[[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]]; [[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]  
  
The firstborn Son is the Son of God becoming a human being, living, dying, and resurrecting. Then, when His humanity became part of His divinity, that is the firstborn Son of God. Because He did this, now all of us can become the same as He is.  
  
Pay attention to this: “from one degree of glory to a higher degree.” Those of you who are in your fourth term — when you came here, you were in one realm. When you graduate, you will be in another realm, because this is taking place day by day and you are not aware of it. Those who meet you on your graduation day may wonder, “What happened in these two years?” Maybe if you were to give an answer, you could say, “It is the indwelling Christ. He is wonderful. He is in me, and He is in you.”  
  
## III. Transformation is for the mass reproduction of the firstborn Son of God as the prototype of a God-man, ==that we may be shaped in the divine image to be just like the firstborn Son of God, Christ as the God-man==  
[[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]  
  
We are becoming the same as the firstborn Son of God, not in His Godhead, but in so many other aspects. He is the prototype of all that we will be.  
  
## IV. The means of transformation in God’s organic salvation are:  
  
### A. The renewing of our mind  
[[Romans-12#v12|Rom. 12:2a]]  
  
This is what is taking place at this moment.  
  
### B. The Lord Spirit — the pneumatic Christ — in us transforming us into the image of the glory of Christ  
[[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18b]]  
  
### C. Our having an unveiled face, beholding and reflecting the Lord like a mirror to express Him  
[[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18a]]  
  
### D. Our living and walking by the Spirit and walking according to the mingled spirit, that the divine life of Christ may have the way to regulate us and transform us into the image of the Lord in glory  
[[Galatians-05#v16|Gal. 5:16]], [[Galatians-05#v25|v. 25]]; [[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4b]]  
  
So, all Christians will be transformed — every one of them. The only difference is *when* and *how*. But everyone — this is from God Himself.  
  
Now we will consider this on a deeper level. I have some material here, and we will just go through it in a general way.  
  
# New Outline —Concerning Transformation


## The Definition of Transformation  
  
There are two points here:  
  
1. Not merely a perceptible outward change in behavior, not something outward merely, but a change in the way you are in your being.  
2. This transformation is inward in essence.  
  
This is what is taking place here and now. This metabolic process shows that something is taking place in a thorough and detailed way in our being. It is inward, so do not try to locate it or guess where or what it is. The Lord will give you a sense of Him: “I am here. I am functioning. Be at peace. Just go through your normal day. You do your part; I will do My part.”  
  
Since this is taking place *inwardly*, I want to emphasize the word **inwardly**. Do not expect an outside, visible change. That will be much later.  
  
## The Steps of Transformation  
  
This is more involved.  
### 1. Washing  
[[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5b]]  
  
The washing of regeneration is just like our bathing inwardly.  
  
### 2. Sanctification of the Holy Spirit  
  
Transformation involves many things, but they are all taking place simultaneously.  
  
### 3. Renewing of the Holy Spirit  
  
It is the Holy Spirit that is making things new, beginning from our mind.  
  
I want to pause here — I have mentioned this a number of times — but this is where transformation **begins**: with our mind.  
  
We need to realize that the indwelling Christ needs to have access to our soul. So, as we pointed out, we make this loving decision: “Lord, You can make Your home in my heart, not just to visit me, but to stay here forever.” Then His first function is to make everything new.  
  
I want to emphasize this first point regarding our mind, because this is so crucial. I will emphasize it again and again. This is where, every day, the spiritual warfare is taking place: who will have access to our mind?  
  
This is why, when something comes and you are thinking, do not examine yourself. Just realize you can tell the Lord, “I’m here again.” Not just once every day — but do not examine yourself. That will not help you. You just make this turn with your mind.  
  
I am very thankful to the Lord that things I remembered for decades, in contact with the Lord, those memories are gone. Someone might say, “When you were in your late twenties, what did you do? What were you thinking?” I do not remember. I am only living here and now.  
  
What I remember are aspects of experiencing and enjoying Christ. I remember certain things that the Triune God did — when He inwardly spoke to me and said, “Ron, you are going to marry that sister.” Things like that, which really happened. I am not going to try to guess anyone who has heard this — do not try to make it happen. But the point is: our mind, what we remember, and what we understand.  
  
I am emphasizing that this is the battlefield in our being. We see in the New Testament that Christ is in our spirit, which is the center of our being. But on one side is the Lord and on the other side is the enemy, and there is this battle concerning where our being will be.  
  
It will be a continuous matter, but as our spirit becomes the strongest part of our being, then in an instant we will recover from ourselves. Do not examine, do not criticize. Just say, “Lord, I am exercising my spirit.”  
  
### 4. Through our spirit which is mingled with the Spirit  
  
These words are easy to understand, but this is a very important point: the Spirit and our spirit are mingled together.  
  
So, when the indwelling Spirit is functioning, our spirit is functioning with it. When our spirit is functioning, the divine Spirit is functioning. The two are one.  
  
Just to have this realization has tremendous meaning. Even while I am sitting here with you, the life-giving Spirit is mingled with our spirit here and now. This is why, when so many of you wanted to take the time to share, every one of you exercised your spirit. When you exercise your spirit, the divine Spirit is functioning because the two are one. This is genuine Christianity.  
  
### 5. He reaches all the inward parts of our being  
  
Notice the words **all** and **reaching**: reaching all the inward parts of our being.  
  
I am going to share something that I may repeat, but some of you have never heard this before. This was in maybe 1987. I had been working full time with Brother Lee since 1974. I was driving home for lunch, and I simply asked the Lord, “I cannot reach the depths of my being, but You can, and You will.”  
  
And I made this decision: “Lord, I do not want to hold anything back from You.” Even I myself do not know the depths of my being as a human, but You are thorough, and You want to go to the depths of it.  
  
When I made this decision, it opened to Him a way for another stage of the Christian life.  
  
I will tell you, there is nothing to be worried about or frightened about, but you and the Lord can have this kind of fellowship and make this decision:  
  
“Lord, You created me, You saved me, You are dwelling in me. I am consecrated in You and for You. I want to make it clear that You have access to the deepest part of my being. Before I see You face to face, I want to have the sense during my life on the earth that Christ had the way and the access to every and any part of my being.”  
  
Eventually, deep within you, you will be able to honestly say to the Lord — not outwardly — “Lord, I want You to come. I need another body. You have made Your home in my being.”  
  
Like the last chapter of Song of Songs — I know you will not fully understand this. When you are eighty years old, you still have a fallen body. Last night, I do not think you were awakened four or five times, wide awake, lying there... what are you going to do? Usually in a few minutes I am back sleeping, but at least one time maybe for an hour, and so the Lord would have a reason for this.  
  
One example is this: you are awake, but there is someone in the recovery who is suffering beyond description. Pray. You do not have to know who or where, but just know that there is a member of the Body who needs someone to pray in this way. Then, in a gentle way, you just say, “Lord, You know, one member to another.”  
  
This does not happen so many times, but after a semiannual training, at least a few times, inwardly the brother said, “That is the one. He or she is here because the saints shepherded her and prayed for her.”  
  
## Shepherding and Maturity  
  
Also, we realize something in shepherding. When the saints are growing in life and have gone through the third stage that I have mentioned many times — they have gone through it thoroughly: the cross applied to the flesh, the self, and the natural constitution — now they are in another realm, a realm of maturity.  
  
And they realize, “I am a member of the Body. I cannot live without the Body.”  
  
One aspect of a brother or sister growing to this kind of maturity is that they can easily determine that someone they are contacting is in a certain situation, and then they shepherd them. They do not teach them, not even telling them anything. They simply let the Triune God flow into them, seemingly in a very human way.  
  
I have heard saints afterward say, “I wonder what just happened? I am full of joy. I am so encouraged.”  
  
This is the inward part of our being.  
  
I want to emphasize this: you are **not** too young to make this personal decision between you and the Lord. Just say, “Lord, I am not holding back any part of my being.”  
  
We cannot and should not open our being in a human way to other people, even to one another in the training. It has to be a limited sharing. But with the Triune God there is no limitation. Just say, “Lord, while I am here and now, please go into the depths of my being. I want to grow unto maturity here and now.”  
  
Then there will be two reasons, not at the same time, that are taking place.  
  
### First  
This is normal because you are in a personal relationship with Him:  
“Lord, I want to grow unto maturity here and now.”  
  
### Then later  

You will realize:  
“Lord, with a loving concern I am praying that these sisters and these brothers will grow unto maturity here and now.”  
  
While I am telling you this, the indwelling Christ is praying for all of us to grow unto maturity before the end of this age and before the end of your life on the earth.  
  
This is something between you and the Lord. That is why I am staying on the point regarding the inward parts:  
  
“Lord, You know the inward parts. I am not going to try to figure out what and where they are, but I just want You to have access to the deepest part of my being.”  
  
This will take place with all the children of God.  
  
I have been at burial meetings — right at the place where we are putting the body underground — and I am not judging anyone, but I realize: this brother that I knew from the time we were in college, whom I had known for more than fifty years, had one foot in the recovery and another foot in Christianity. He did not grow unto maturity. He is in paradise now, but in the coming age there will be a thousand years for this to take place.  
  
But now, at your age, it is not too soon to make this decision. Just say:  
“Lord, in Your way and in Your time, I am giving You the inward parts of my being. I am not holding anything back.”  
  
He knows this will take time to carry out. Actually, I am still here, and so I realize this is taking place.  
  
### D. Metabolic process  

In 2022, when I had severe pain, I asked Brother Rick to take me to the emergency room. I do not go there just because I am a little bit in pain — this was severe.  
  
He brought me there, and they said, “We will take care of this, eventually.” But after the examination, they said, “You have to have a quadruple surgery on your heart immediately.”  
  
As soon as I heard that, I was just at peace, and my wife also was in peace. Then, when they came around 5:00 a.m. to take me into the room, there were about ten people there. This may sound a little goofy, but I almost wanted to say, “Good morning, I love all of you. I will see you again when I wake up.”  
  
Then, after four and a half hours, the leading brother came to where I was and said, “This was a perfect surgery. One hundred percent perfect.”  
  
But for the next forty-eight hours, I was wide awake. This had never happened before — not even seven or eight hours at night. What was taking place? The Lord was functioning from this side and from that side. He knew that I had prayed with Him, “Lord, You have access to the depths of my being,” and He was saying, “I am doing it. I am doing it deeply and thoroughly.”  
  
And I would say, “Amen,” again and again. The outcome was joy.  
  
The more He has access, going deeper and deeper, then your inward parts are really being touched by the Lord in the deepest way.  
  
The Lord indicated that this was not only during the surgery hours, but that after I came home for ten or twelve weeks, during those ten or twelve weeks, based upon the surgery, He could go deeper and deeper and deeper.  
  
There was a dear brother from New Zealand whom I had known for decades, and we were really parallel in our Christian life and our church life. So every now and then we would talk to each other, and we realized that we were both in the same kind of situation, and we were encouraging one another.  
  
What I am trying to say is this: I encourage you to make the decision:  
“Lord, I want to grow unto maturity here and now. In Your way and in Your time, please do it this way.”  
  
### E. Not accomplished in an instant  
  
I think we are all clear about this, right? We could be saved in an instant. When Christ comes to give us a new body, He is not going to say, “I will give it to you, but it will take maybe four or five hundred years.” No — instantly.  
  
The spirit and the body are dealt with in this way, but our **soul** is a lifelong matter. It is not accomplished in an instant, but achieved throughout our entire life.  
  
This is just a normal thing. Do not be afraid. Whatever the length of your life, the Lord already knows when our time is complete, and He knows the length of it. He is able to do this in saints of any age.  
  
Do not try to figure it out: “Lord, let me know what time...” My guess is that if you asked Him, He would say, “I would prefer to rapture you alive.”  
  
How about that? Would that not be delightful? Maybe I will still be here and we will be raptured together and meet at the throne!

With unveiled face, beholding and reflecting like a mirror the glorious image of the Lord.  
  
This verse in 2 Cor. 3:18 is one of the most important verses in the New Testament.  
  
### 2. The advancing from one degree of glory to another degree of glory  
  
So, when I mentioned earlier that when you are graduating, you may not have the words, but others will have the feeling: there is glory with my son or daughter... there is something more than just my child. What is it? It is the glory of God. It is God expressed in a consecrated saint who set aside two full years.  
  
Brother Lee said it directly: “What He can do in two years would require the saints in the church life twenty years.”  
  
This is simply a way of seeing the difference. Do not go back to your local church and say, “Sorry it is taking you so long to mature.” Nothing like that. But just realize that you did **not** waste those two years.  
  
Those you know may have gotten ahead in their job and work and gotten married. But the Lord knows — when you graduate, He knows who and when you will marry. If you need to go to this place and do this kind of thing, there is something set aside **for you**.  
  
You are doing a wonderful thing with your time — two of your years.  
  
### 3. Being transformed into the same image of the Lord, even as from the Lord Spirit  
  
This is by remaining in our spirit and exercising our spirit. This is crucial.  
  
The way of transformation is by remaining in our spirit and exercising our spirit.  
  
How do you exercise your spirit? Here, you have to use your **human will**. You do not think about it and it happens, and you do not merely have feelings that it will happen. It is your will.  
  
You set your mind:  
“I want to be in my spirit right now. I want my spirit to function in a marvelous way.”
