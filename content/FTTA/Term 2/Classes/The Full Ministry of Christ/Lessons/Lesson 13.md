# Outline—The Vision of Reigning in Life

## Introduction

There will be a few messages on this. We will go through this thoroughly, and carefully. So, this message is on the vision. It's not mainly on the experience, but the vision. The vision is the beginning—the vision of reigning in life. This word reigning will be more and more important in your developing. On the one hand, we are reigning in life, but on the other hand, we are being reigned by life. So, we will go through this, and when we come to point 5, I have some other points I will just bring into this message. So, we will just carefully go through this now.

## I. God's complete salvation is composed of two sections
[[Romans-05#v10|Rom 5:10]]:

These verses from Romans are crucial.
### A. The judicial section, which is according to the righteousness of God, ==is the procedure== of God's salvation.

### B. The organic section, which is through the life of Christ ==is the purpose== of God's salvation.


## II. God's complete salvation ==is for us to reign in life== by the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness
[[Romans-05#v17|vv. 17]], [[Romans-05#v21|21]]:

I'll just pause here for a moment. The complete salvation. There are millions of Christians that have been saved, but they don't realize that this is just the beginning. It doesn't eman that now everything is fine and we will be in heaven, and until then I will do all kinds of things. You have to realize, that we need complete salvation. You need to be saved, not just objectively, but personally and subjectively. So, God's complete salvation is for us to reign in life, that is His goal, to reign in life. So, at this point, we are emphasizing that we will reign in life... but we will come to the main point, we must be reigned OURSELVES, under His reigning. So, for us to reign in life by the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness, notice the abundance of grace: because of these two words, every morning, like I mentioned something like this previously, my wife and I as we are waking up pray "Lord, please give us today's measure of grace." ==Grace is for us to experience God's organic salvation.== 

What is organic salvation? This refers to, on the side of experience, not just knowing about it merely, but participating *in it*.
### A. The gift of righteousness is for God's judicial redemption; grace is for us to experience God's organic salvation:

Christ is for us to experience God's organic salvation. Just notice the thought here: grace is for us to *experience*. I think we all know the last verse in the Bible, it mentions grace. The last word in the whole Bible "may grace be with you." This is going to be the essence of our Christian life, this grace for us. We receive this measure of grace so that we may experience God's organic salvation. So, we don't try to make it happen, it's about the grace we receive that will enable us actually get us through ANYTHING that the Lord is leading us through, there is nothing to be afraid of.

#### 1. The gift of righteousness is God's judicial redemption applied to us in a practical way.

What is a practical way? It's a particular situation you are in in this very moment. It could be all kinds of ordinary things, and grace needs to come in to that. I used this example before: you are driving your car, and someone on the left side comes in to get in front of you, and then they turn to the right... don't you have some kind of feeling? If you are alone in the car, are you shouting out anything? Or are you just honking? The Lord may allow us to have this particular kind of situation, until we realize (I'm talking about myself, after about 100 couple times) I realized that the Lord is trying to accomplish something... and I should just gently touch the breaks, slow down, and just let the go with no feelings at all. Grace is very practical. 

#### 2. Grace is God Himself as our all-sufficient supply for our organic salvation.

Let's focus on this: grace is God Himself. So, when you receive grace, you are experiencing God in an experiential way. I want to emphasize this again and again, because each one of us personally needs to come to the point of understanding, "I cannot live without grace. I cannot endure this situation without grace." The Lord covers me when I say this: "When you have grace, in whatever you are going through, you are supplied." So, when I had to go to the hospital because of pain in my stomach, and I was told that I needed a quadruple surgery on my heart, there was grace for this. There was grace that meets us in every situation, but on our side, there needs to be the realization for this. The time will come, especially brothers, it's not our natural strength. Our natural strength cannot endure anything, it's just a matter of when you are finished or when you realize that God allowed you to be in this situation for some reason that we don't know about yet, but there is grace to supply us however, and the grace reigns in that practical situation. Then, we realize, usually sometime that event, that "this was God, because God is grace!"

### B. Reigning in life is the full experience of the organic salvation of God.

Reigning in life: that means, the overcoming saints who will reign with the Lord for 1000 years, they will be trained before that time because in their lifetime they are learning to reign in life. 

So, this life enthrones us. What does this mean? If you look at the end of chapter 3 of Revelation, one of the verses regarding the overcoming saints, "You are sitting with Christ on the throne." That is where you will be. We know also in a verse in the NT, the religious people were putting these dear young saints to death, but he was full of grace and resurrection life, and they were killing him with stones. However, before he passed away, he had a vision and said "there is Christ on the throne. He sees me, and I see Him." This is real. So, that is why this life enthrones us as *kings*. It's not just good enough to be on the throne, just sitting there and thinking "this is quite interesting... I can just look around and I can see all the sitautions, this country and that country." There is a reason for this, that we are being enthroned as kings to reign over all things.

## III. We have been regenerated with a divine, spiritual, heavenly, kingly, and royal life
[[Mark-04#v26|Mark 4:26]]; [[1 John-03#v9|1 John 3:9]]:
### A. This life enthrones us as kings to reign over all things.

### B. In this life we are now able to reign as kings.

Notice how it says "in this life." We will be enthroned, and we will be able to *reign*. So, all the points up until now are for our preparation to reign and be on the throne. Very soon we will find out, we are under the throne. We are ruled by the throne, but we will get to that section.
