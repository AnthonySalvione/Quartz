## Outline

## The Hidden Manna, the Best Love, and the Shepherding of the Ascended Christ

What is the hidden manna?? We know it was found early in the morning. But eventually, some of it was taken into the holy of holies. This indicates that we need to advance our relationship and our experience with the Triune God to see Him not only outwardly, but also in the holy of holies. Then we will have a very precious section - "the best love". Then, the shepherding of the ascended Christ.

So, we will just walk our way through the outline, and we will focus on certain matters that are most important.

## I. We are incorporated into the Triune God to become the New Jerusalem by eating the hidden manna

![[Revelation-02#v17|Rev. 2:17]]Rev. 2:17:

So, in the triune God, when we are abiding in Him, we become the New Jerusalem. I think we all know the NJ is a corporate person. So, we are becoming a part of this corporate person which is the NJ. "eating the hidden manna" is also in the Holy of Holies, and is part of our personal experiential time with the Lord. We will quickly go through A, B, and C because I want to focus more time on roman numeral 2 and 3.
### A. The tabernacle in the Old Testament is a sign of the universal incorporation.
### B. To eat the hidden manna is to be incorporated into the tabernacle.
### C. The hidden manna corresponds to the ==four ins== in John 14:16-20:
#### 1. The Son is in the Father.
#### 2. The Father is in the Son.
#### 3. The Son is in us.

This means that the Father who is in the Son is in us by being in the Son.
#### 4. The Spirit of reality is in us.

I appreciate in a particular way the spirit of reality. It makes all of the things that are being made known to us real deep within us. We need to pray "Lord, I want everything to become real. I must start with something objective, but make everything part of my reality."

Now we come to a very delightful portion.

## II. We are incorporated into the Triune God to become ==His dwelling place== by loving the Lord with the best love
Rev. 2:4:

So, based upon being incorporated into the Triune God, the Triune God (not talking about Him in the Godhead, but in His economy on the earth) becomes the dwelling place by loving the Lord with the best love.

Point A is very important.
### A. It is the love in God that gives Him the yearning to unite, mingle, and incorporate with us, and it is the same love in us (1 John 4:19) that gives us the yearning to unite, mingle, and incorporate with Him.

Notice that it begins with the Triune God. It is the love in God that gives Him the love. God Himself is love, so this love is just Himself. He has a yearning which is a deep desire, to mingle unite and incorporate Himsself with us. The Triune God functions first because of His yearning, and eventaully we will have a yearning ourselves. Just in your being, you may not always understand what the feeling is, but there will be just a deep yearning in you. Just in a simple way, the time will come, deep within you will say "I'm yearning not to live alone for the rest of my days... I want to be married". This is a yearning. As you are graduating and shortly afterwords, a yearning will come to your realization. Do not push down this yearning, just bring it to the Lord in your fellowship and say, "Lord I have a deep feeling now that I've neber had before. Don't press it down I repeat. Just say, Lord lead me to carry out this yearning that is within me". This is a picture of God's love for us. We shouldn't try to have a yearning because we heard that we should be someone who yearns. No. It is the Lord's love that will work within our being that will give us this yearning. The yearning is WITH the Triune God. Yearning to be united, mingled and incorporated with Him.
### B. We become God's dwelling place, His sanctuary, the Holy of Holies, by participating in the four stages of the divine romance revealed in Song of Songs:

I mentioned this before, but I'd like to mention it again and again. I would just suggest that you read through Song of Songs every month. Your time is occupied with so many things, but you could just read one of the eight chapters on your free time. It is the very core of God's being, and His relationship with us. When 1 John was written, it is really focused on God as God Himself, and so, we become God's dwelling place .
#### 1. The attracting and pursuing stage-1:2-4.

So, this begins with the attracting. This is calling us to seek. Then after this we pursue. More and mroe there will be pursuing, but the pursuing comes from the attracting once something had been made clear to you. You realize that you are finally seeing something marvelous that you know is real, and have a deep longing to experience this.
#### 2. The stage of experiencing the cross—2:14.

This can be kind of a surprise. After you read chapter 1, you see the place of joy and love... we love Him and He loves us. Yes He really loves me and comes to me and says "come with me to the cross." We are all the same on this—we hear it, but we don't do anything right away. He will just step away apparently, and you will realize that you need Him and you must find Him at any cost. Notice, it's the *experiencing* the cross. I hope that this aspect of God's Economy doesn't trouble you. Hearing the cross this, and the cross that. Especially you fourth termers. When you graduate you will be entering int othe third stage, and this is the most crucial stage in the christian life, because it determines whether or not you will be with the Lord in the coming kingdom or not. If you will be a part of the bride which he is coming for or not.

So, I'm just reminding you, that in the third stage —it's the cross applying itself to our flesh and to our soul, and to our dispostion. This is somewhat familiar to when you are growing up fro age 12 to 20, almost every aspect of your body is being changed to another degree, and it can function in a way you never could before. This means that we are one with Him in His ascension - that is the thought.

The sanctuary where God dwells.

So, we are in the process of becoming a duplication of God, and becoming the same as God in life and in nature, but not in the Godhead. I hope we are all clear about that.

Now we are at the high peak, and are going higher and higher.

Just be open to Him simply. Just say, "Lord, I love you, I am fully open to your presence, you have access to any and every part of my being." This is how you will grow, and how you will mature, if He has access to every part of my being.
