# The Glory of God

**They handed out a supplemental outline, so this message isn't in the original set of outlines. It's in-between lessons 2 and 3, hence the name "Lesson 2.5".

## Introduction

*inserted by Ron*, 2 more very short hymns, [[Hymn-948|948]] and [[Hymn-949|949]]. These are two delightful hymns on glory.

So, once again, there will be 15 to 20 minutes as an opportunity for you to share something. You will be given about a minute. Again—I just remind you—this is not a requirement. However, because He is the indwelling Christ within you, He may give you the sense that you should share something that enlightened or encouraged you, or gave you life and enjoyment. Like I do every time I'm here, I'll just wait for a few seconds, and see if you have something to share with us. I want to remind you again that each one of you has a portion of Christ that nobody else has. So, the opportunity is here to stand up and share something, and then when you sit down, during the time afterwards, more Christ will be saturated into your being. You receive then you express, then when you express you receive. So, it is your time now.

[[FTTA/Term 2/Trainee Speaking/Class Sharing/The Full Ministry of Christ/Lesson 2.5|Click here for trainee speaking.]]

Very good. We all have this outline, right? Let's go through it point by point. I will emphasize certain points.

Now, we come to the title of this outline—"The Glory of God." This is what's on God's heart. He wants to be glorified; that is, to be expressed.

## I. God is a God of glory
**Verses: [[Acts-07#v2|Acts 7:2]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17]]; [[Ephesians-03#v14|3:14]], [[Ephesians-03#v16|16]]; [[1 Corinthians-02#v8|1 Cor. 2:8]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v6|2 Cor. 4:6]]; [[1 Peter-04#v14|1 Pet. 4:14]].**

God is inclusive. From His point of view, which should be our point of view, God in His essence is glory. He created us and saved us, *so that* eventually we would become the same as He is in this glory.

## II. God's purpose is to bring many sons into glory
**Verses: [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]; [[1 Corinthians-02#v7|1 Cor. 2:7]]; [[Ephesians-01#v6|Eph. 1:6-7]], [[Ephesians-01#v12|12]], [[Ephesians-01#v14|14]].**

You all know, the sons here are both brothers and sisters. God is going to bring you all into glory. That is why I had you sing these two hymns on glory this morning. So wonderful. As we sing it, our inner being is being saturated more and more.
## III. God created us as vessels prepared unto glory
**Verse: [[Romans-09#v28|Rom. 9:28]].**

We can see—this is where the evil, spiritual war began. God created us to express Him as glory by being saturated with Him, and now Satan also desires to saturate human beings with himself, to express himself in these fallen human beings. All of us have to be saved from this, so thus we are in the procedure of God's application of His salvation. This procedure is thorough in every way. Actually, it will become an inward realization to you—"why am I here? Why did God create me?" The Lord will then say, "I want you to be part of the vessel to express me". This vessel will last forever. So, He created us with this intention.

## IV. Sin is falling short of God's glory
**Verse: [[Romans-03#v23|3:23]].**

If any religious people think—"I don't sin, I don't lie, don't steal, I don't do things, I'm a sinless person..."—the Lord would say, "your sin is the desire to be glorified". This in itself is falling short of God's glory. So, on the whole earth, the vast majority are expressing the self. However, the Lord has a small number among us, by His kindness, that gradually will be expressing Him with His glory. We made a decision, we told the Lord that we are willing to go through anything that He wants to carry out in us personally. We want to be those who personally glorify Him. That is why I am here.

## V. Christ's redemption has fulfilled the requirements of God's glory
**Verses: [[Romans-03#v24|24-25]]; [[Hebrews-09#v5|Heb. 9:5]]; cf. [[Genesis-03#v24|Gen. 3:24]].

So, we were saved for this. Christ died on the cross for this. That we, who were once one kind of being, could now have a new start. For God's glory to be expressed. Just this one sentence, you have so many dear Christians that say, "Why did Christ die on the cross for us?" Most would say, "So that we would be saved and go to heaven". But, He died on the cross that we would be saved and eventually be a part of the corporate glorious expression of God. That is why we are here.

## VI. Through the gospel of the glory of God, God has called us into His eternal glory
**Verses: [[2 Corinthians-04#v4|2 Cor. 4:4]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v11|1 Tim. 1:11]]; [[1 Peter-05#v10|1 Pet. 5:10]]; [[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thes. 2:12]].**

He called us. I like that He calls us, and not demands and requires of us. His being is calling unto us. He wants us to be a part of His expression. He wants us to be saved, and become a child of God, and become the same as He is in life and nature except for His Godhead.

## VII. Christ in us is the hope of glory
**Verses: [[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|3:4]].**

The time will come where our faith is going through the procedure of being developed to be strengthened. In addition to that though, eventually we will come to the realization to have a hope. Actually, all of us either on the human or spiritual side, the hope. So, Christ in us, gives us the hope of glory. Just notice, Christ in us. So, here He is in us... and He is functioning in this way—that we would have hope in glory. We would have more hope than having some fear about something else. We need to have hope. We care for all of our human things, but here we have hope regarding glory.

## VIII. We are being transformed into the Lord's image from glory to glory
**Verse: [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]].

What does this mean? From glory to glory? It's from one degree—you have to start somewhere. Then, as you are growing, and Christ makes His home in you, then the glory is being developed more and more. On one level, you will be a kind of Christ-human-being. As we are going to mature as Christians, it may be a surprise for some of us in the beginning but, we become more and more human. Not simply a God-created human, but a God-glorified human. We are not aware of this, because more and more our focus is on Christ Himself, not on ourselves.

## IX. The goal of God's organic salvation, and the last stage of this salvation, is glory
**Verses: [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]; [[Romans-08#v17|Rom. 8:17]], [[Romans-08#v21|21]], [[Romans-08#v30|30]].**

So, the goal—the goal of His organic salvation—is glory. The last stage of His salvation is glory. So, we are all aimed in that direction. There will be quite a clear expression, when you come to the FTTA, at the very begging, then two years later, saints that have known you for almost your whole life... they will realize, that there is glory in you now. What was the effect of being here for two years? It's not just learning things, or just to understand doctrinal things, but something has been taking place. They may not have the word, but eventually they may have the understanding... that you are not aware of it. Those of you who will be here in June at the end of the term, so many will realize—what a development. In two years, glory has been expressed to another level in you. That is why brother Lee said—the Lord can do something during the two years in the training. You've heard this before. Compared to around 20 years elsewhere. When you graduate, you will be in the third stage of life. I want to emphasize this again and again, so you know where you are. You are not just a spiritual child, you are becoming a spiritual adult, and in the next 7 or 8 years you will be in the fourth stage. You have a wonderful future ahead of you.

## X. We will enter into the highest stage of oneness—the oneness in the divine glory
**Verse: [[John-17#v22|John 17:22]].

This requires some explanation. The verse here is in John 13, where He was praying before He was crucified. Among us all, in the church life, there is some degree of oneness. But, as we are growing in life, the oneness will be developed in a marvelous way. The main point here is that there will no longer be the self. The time will come when we pray, and no self is there. When we give a word of fellowship, there is no self! There will be the highest level of oneness eventually. So, we are moving in the direction of the highest oneness, and the highest oneness means that there is only glory and no self expression. All of us—we are developing now—so, we have something of the self and we also have something of Christ. The will will come—less self, more christ, eventually there will be NO SELF and all of Christ. So, don't be discouraged, it doesn't happen miraculously overnight.

## XI. There is glory to God in the church
**Verses: [[John-17#v22|John 17:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-03#v21|Eph. 3:21]].

I have seen this take place. I've also seen the opposite take place. Many years ago, I just remember this one brother, no doubt he was a child of God, but he wanted to be recognized for how wonderful and gifted he was. Actually, in the church, the glory of God should be there alone. No self expression. This is developing, okay? We are all in this stage. I want to emphasize this though, there is glory to GOD in the church. So, when we pray, it is to God. When we testify, it is to God, because there should be glory to God in the church where we are.

## XII. There will be the glory of God in the kingdom
**Verses: [[Matthew-06#v13|Matt. 6:13]]; [[Matthew-16#v27|16:27]]; [[Matthew-26#v64|26:64]]; [[Revelation-05#v13|Rev. 5:13]].**

This is going to be a major point. Those that are going to be co-kings for the 1000 years, they're developing on this side, but at the same time, glory is being saturated and the self is being removed. 

## XIII. The New Jerusalem will have the glory of God
**Verses: [[Revelation-21#v10|Rev. 21:10-11]].**

Remember, the New Jerusalem is NOT a place. It is not a city. It's a corporate person. The wife is going to be marvelous in this way. 

## XIV. The glory of God in the economy of God involves deification—God becoming man that man may become God in life, in nature, and in expression
**Verses: [[John-01#v14|John 1:14]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]]; [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]; [[Revelation-21#v10|Rev. 21:10-11]].**

It seems that 1 John, with it's 5 chapters, it seems to be quite simple. The language is quite simple. However, we have to be very careful in our thinking. As you all know (because I've emphasized this), John was in his 90s before he started writing this. He was making it clear, that we are the same as God. God is light, and God is love. We are becoming the same as He is. The outcome will be, a corporate glorification.
