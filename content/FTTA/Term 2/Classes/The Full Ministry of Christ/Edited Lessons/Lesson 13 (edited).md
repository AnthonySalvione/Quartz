# Outline—The Vision of Reigning in Life

## Introduction

There will be a few messages on this. We will go through this thoroughly and carefully. This message is on the vision. It is not mainly on the experience, but the vision. The vision is the beginning—the vision of reigning in life. This word *reigning* will become more and more important in your developing. On the one hand, we are reigning in life, but on the other hand, we are being reigned by life.

So, we will go through this, and when we come to point 5, I have some other points I will bring into this message. We will just carefully go through this now.

## I. God’s complete salvation is composed of two sections—[[Romans-05#v10|Rom. 5:10]]:

These verses from Romans are crucial.

### A. The judicial section, which is according to the righteousness of God, ==is the procedure== of God’s salvation.

### B. The organic section, which is through the life of Christ, ==is the purpose== of God’s salvation.

## II. God’s complete salvation ==is for us to reign in life== by the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness—[[Romans-05#v17|vv. 17]], [[Romans-05#v21|21]]:

I will just pause here for a moment. There are millions of Christians who have been saved, but they do not realize that this is just the beginning. It does not mean that now everything is fine and we will be in heaven, and until then I will do all kinds of things. You have to realize that we need complete salvation. You need to be saved, not just objectively, but personally and subjectively.

God’s complete salvation is for us to reign in life. That is His goal—to reign in life. At this point, we are emphasizing that we will reign in life, but we will come to the main point: we must be reigned ourselves, under His reigning.

For us to reign in life is by the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness. Notice the abundance of grace. Because of these two words, every morning, as I mentioned previously, my wife and I, as we are waking up, pray, “Lord, please give us today’s measure of grace.” ==Grace is for us to experience God’s organic salvation.==

What is organic salvation? This refers to, on the side of experience, not just knowing about it merely, but participating *in it*.

### A. The gift of righteousness is for God’s judicial redemption; grace is for us to experience God’s organic salvation:

Christ is for us to experience God’s organic salvation. Just notice the thought here: grace is for us to *experience*. I think we all know the last verse in the Bible. It mentions grace. The last word in the whole Bible is, “May grace be with you.” This is going to be the essence of our Christian life: this grace for us.

We receive this measure of grace so that we may experience God’s organic salvation. We do not try to make it happen. It is about the grace we receive that will enable us and actually get us through anything that the Lord is leading us through. There is nothing to be afraid of.

#### 1. The gift of righteousness is God’s judicial redemption applied to us in a practical way.

What is a practical way? It is a particular situation you are in at this very moment. It could be all kinds of ordinary things, and grace needs to come into that.

I used this example before: you are driving your car, and someone on the left side comes in to get in front of you, and then they turn to the right. Do you not have some kind of feeling? If you are alone in the car, are you shouting out anything? Or are you just honking?

The Lord may allow us to have this particular kind of situation until we realize—I am talking about myself, after about a hundred times—that the Lord is trying to accomplish something. I should just gently touch the brakes, slow down, and let them go with no feelings at all. Grace is very practical.

#### 2. Grace is God Himself as our all-sufficient supply for our organic salvation.

Let us focus on this: grace is God Himself. When you receive grace, you are experiencing God in an experiential way. I want to emphasize this again and again, because each one of us personally needs to come to the point of understanding, “I cannot live without grace. I cannot endure this situation without grace.”

The Lord covers me when I say this: when you have grace in whatever you are going through, you are supplied. When I had to go to the hospital because of pain in my stomach, and I was told that I needed quadruple surgery on my heart, there was grace for this. There was grace that meets us in every situation. But on our side, there needs to be the realization of this.

The time will come, especially brothers, when we realize that it is not our natural strength. Our natural strength cannot endure anything. It is just a matter of when you are finished, or when you realize that God allowed you to be in this situation for some reason that we do not know about yet. But there is grace to supply us, and the grace reigns in that practical situation. Then we realize, usually sometime after that event, “This was God, because God is grace!”

### B. Reigning in life is the full experience of the organic salvation of God.

Reigning in life means that the overcoming saints who will reign with the Lord for one thousand years will be trained before that time, because in their lifetime they are learning to reign in life.

This life enthrones us. What does this mean? If you look at the end of Revelation 3, one of the verses regarding the overcoming saints says, “You are sitting with Christ on the throne.” That is where you will be.

We also know of a verse in the New Testament where religious people were putting this dear young saint to death, but he was full of grace and resurrection life, and they were killing him with stones. However, before he passed away, he had a vision and said, “There is Christ on the throne. He sees me, and I see Him.” This is real.

That is why this life enthrones us as *kings*. It is not enough just to be on the throne, sitting there and thinking, “This is quite interesting. I can just look around and see all the situations, this country and that country.” There is a reason for this: we are being enthroned as kings to reign over all things.

## III. We have been regenerated with a divine, spiritual, heavenly, kingly, and royal life—[[Mark-04#v26|Mark 4:26]]; [[1 John-03#v9|1 John 3:9]]:

### A. This life enthrones us as kings to reign over all things.

### B. In this life we are now able to reign as kings.

Notice how it says “in this life.” We will be enthroned, and we will be able to *reign*. All the points up until now are for our preparation to reign and be on the throne. Very soon we will find out that we are under the throne. We are ruled by the throne, but we will get to that section.

## IV. Reigning in life in [[Romans-05|Romans 5]] is the key to everything in [[Romans-06|Romans 6—16]]:

### A. We need to see everything in chapters 6—16 in this light.

If you want to, you could reread Romans again from this point of view, reading it very quickly. You will realize that we need to see everything from this point of view.

### B. If we reign in life, we are in all the matters presented in these chapters.

Now I am going to pause here, because I have some other parallel points. They are not many, but they are profound. There are just four of them. We have plenty of time to concentrate on this. I will read it through once, then I will go over it again, and I will give you the verses that come with it.

#### We were regenerated with the divine, spiritual, heavenly, kingly, and royal life. You have *royal* life. This life enthrones us to reign as kings over all things—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]]; [[John-03#v3|3:3]], [[John-03#v5|5]]; [[Revelation-05#v10|Revelation 5:10]]; [[Romans-05#v17|Romans 5:17]], [[Romans-05#v21|21]].

Let us examine this point for a moment. In what way were we regenerated? We already went through all the aspects. This is part of the Lord’s training us to be on the throne, to reign.

Out of millions and millions of Christians, maybe two percent will be overcoming saints who will be trained while they are living on the earth, and then during the next one thousand years, they will be on the throne in this way. The Lord will enthrone us in this way.

I hope we have this main thought here, emphasizing that *this enthrones us to reign as kings*. Inwardly we pray, “Lord, thank You. Do only what You can do to make me ready to reign with You for the next one thousand years.”

#### To reign in life is to conquer, subdue, and rule over Satan, the world, sin, the flesh, ourselves, and all the environmental circumstances, and to subdue all kinds of insubordination—[[Romans-08#v2|Romans 8:2]], [[Romans-08#v25|25]], [[Romans-08#v27|27]]; [[Romans-05#v17|5:17-18]].

What does it mean to reign in life? It is to conquer. What is that? Christ on the cross conquered every evil thing.

I will just mention now, in one of the messages in the summer training, which will refer to some verses in Colossians, that there was a war when Christ was on the cross. The second three hours—He was there on the cross for six hours. The first three hours, nothing apparently was happening, and people, religious people, would come by and mock Him and say, “If You are really the Christ, come down, and we will believe You.” And He was just silent.

Then, in the second three hours, there was spiritual warfare on the cross. Why was this? It is because now every negative thing in the universe, including Satan, every evil thing, was being terminated on the cross. The Father was involved with His Son, and the Son was the One dying. But the Father came, and for the next three hours there was this war, which was invisible, because now, during the first three hours, Satan could just make fun of it: “I defeated Him. He came as a human being on the earth, and I defeated Him, and now He is going to die very soon.” Then you realize that the one who is saying this is now *being* on the cross. That is why there was some intense warfare taking place for the second three hours.

We all need to realize that we may understand some things in the future, but for now we can only understand certain aspects, and the other aspects are just between the Father and His Son. However, just imagine the feeling of the Father when His own *Son* was being crucified, and then He joined Him because the battle had started. I think now you have a basic understanding of what took place here.

#### We were regenerated with the divine, spiritual, heavenly, kingly, and royal life. Therefore, ==we are now able to reign in life== as kings—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]]; [[John-03#v3|3:3]], [[John-03#v5|5]]; [[Revelation-05#v10|Revelation 5:10]]; [[Romans-05#v17|Romans 5:17]], [[Romans-05#v21|21]].

#### If we want to reign in life, our spirit must be *strong*, living, active, positive, and diligent—[[2 Timothy-01#v7|2 Timothy 1:7]]; [[Revelation-01#v10|Revelation 1:10a]].

I am going to ask you something, but do not answer. From your understanding of yourself, right now, what is the strongest part of your being? Just consider. Many saints, especially brothers, would say, “It is my mind. My mind is taking the lead all the time.” That is even scriptural. The mind is the one taking the lead.

The sisters are parallel with the brothers’ ability, of course, but it may be more the *feeling*. I am not going to refer to any place or person, but this is something I have been observing weekly in various places. Whether it is a prayer meeting, the Lord’s table, or what is called a “prophesying meeting,” many are *not* exercising their spirit.

All of you should be encouraged. Every one of you who spoke this morning exercised your spirit in a normal way. You were not shouting or screaming, but in this function your spirit was very active. That is why we come back to this point. I hope these words touch you.

If you want to reign in life—and I believe you all want to reign in life—the Lord may ask, “When do you want to be able to reign in life?” Ninety-nine percent of the saints will reign in life in the new heaven and new earth, but I meet saints who will reign in life for the coming one thousand years. Do you want to be a co-king with Him?

Just on the side, once a year, the overcoming saints who are now functioning as kings will be in different parts of the earth, but we will all come once a year to where Jesus is. We will be in this position because we have been trained to *reign* in life.

Prior to that, I repeat, our spirit *must*—not should, but must—be strong. Our spirit must be living. Our spirit must be active. Our spirit must be positive. Our spirit must be diligent. This is all related to reigning in life.

Now I want to point out that this reigning in life is related to the growth in life within us. The degree of growth will determine to what extent there is reigning. Here is a verse from [[Hebrews-06#v1|Hebrews 6:1]]—let us be brought on to maturity. There will be part of the summer training on maturity, because any living saint who hopes to be raptured when the Lord is about to come must have maturity as one aspect.

![[Hebrews-06#v1]]

We know that when the Lord comes, He will first come in a hidden way, as I mentioned before, for His bride. However, the bride must be mature in every aspect. She cannot be like a child, but must be a mature wife-to-be. This includes much experience of spiritual *warfare*.

So, do not worry about it; the Lord knows. We have shared about this in a limited way before. I would not be surprised if maybe within a year or two, in one of the semiannual trainings, all twelve messages would be on spiritual warfare.

I let you know before that about three years ago, the saints in Europe, where the final battle will take place, said, “Brother Ron, would you train us to engage in spiritual warfare?” That was a very precious time to get to know these saints and what they are going to go through.

Maturity is necessary for reigning. Even if someone was in the United Kingdom, and the father or the mother is the king or queen, when the child is three years old, he is not on the throne. Actually, he will go through a training that most people do not go through. Then, when it becomes his part, he will be able to reign.

Another point related to the growth in life: the growth in life is the increase of the Triune God as life within us. This is the definition of the growth in life: the increase of the Triune God within us. This growth in life is the *building up* of the Body of Christ.

We all have the Triune God in us. Now we want the indwelling Triune God *in us* to increase within us. This growth in life is the building up of the Body of Christ.

I am going to pause here. I know some middle-aged saints on Wednesday nights come to the microphone, and they have the right to do it. Also, in some prayer meetings, they say, “in the Body, in the Body, in the Body.” I hardly ever hear a sister using these words. I think it is because they know their actual situation, which many of the brothers do not know, because they do not know the Body.

So, here, I inject this point, and I am emphasizing this again. You might hear it again before you graduate: when you graduate from here, please realize that you are entering into the third stage of life. Now everything regarding reigning in life and being part of the co-kings in the coming age depends on your growing to maturity here and now.

It does not take twenty or thirty years. As you have heard me say, and I will mention it again, when you were twelve, how many years did it take before you became a young adult? Are you still a little boy? No. You are all mature. You are in different levels. In just seven or eight years, during these thorough changes in our body and in our mind, everything changed. My heart aches because I know dear saints who passed away without growing unto maturity.

So, when you pass through the third stage of the cross applied to our flesh, spirit, soul, and disposition, then at a certain point inwardly you realize, “I am a member of the Body, and I cannot live without the Body.”

Then, once they realize this, they are in this stage. As you have heard me say, and I emphasize again, they can in a few seconds determine whether or not *this* saint is mature in life. When you see, when you shepherd—and this will be the subject of my portion in the coming conference in two weeks: shepherding according to God—all of us are here because of this kind of shepherding.

The brothers who have grown unto maturity realize that this dear one, this brother, is talking a lot about the Bible. He thinks he is in the Body, but he is not there experientially. So, you again take care of him.

The way to grow in life for the organic building up of the Body of Christ has five simple points:

1. Exercising our spirit.
2. Eating, drinking, and breathing Christ. I love this. [[1 Timothy-04#v6|1 Tim. 4:6]]; [[John-06#v57|John 6:57]]; [[John-07#v38|7:38]]; [[John-20#v22|20:22]].

4. Receiving the supply of the Body.
5. Ministering Christ as life to others.

Now I will just read the last point.
