# Outline—Salvation in Life—Conformation

The title of this message implies that, according to [[Romans-05#v10|Romans 5:10]], on the one hand, we were reconciled to God by the death of His Son, but much more, we will be saved in His life. One crucial aspect of God’s salvation in life is our conformation to the image of Christ, the firstborn Son of God, as mentioned in [[Romans-08#v29|Romans 8:29]].

Now, instead of giving a lengthy opening word, I’d like to enter into the revelation of what conformation is as revealed in the New Testament. I’d like to first present what I consider to be the highest definition of conformation in the ministry, presented in a book composed of messages Brother Lee gave in his last conference: *The Experience of God’s Organic Salvation Equaling Reigning in Life*.

Here, we are told that conformation is the maturity in the divine life, through which we participate in God’s divinity in full and are solidified in the possession of the divine element. The divine life that has entered into us needs to be solidified and matured in us. This book is part of your assignment.

Dear saints, here we need to be impressed with the fact that our conformation is arriving at the full-grown man mentioned in [[Ephesians-04#v13|Ephesians 4:13]]. By the way, brothers and sisters, are you not glad you are under the present New Testament ministry that builds up the organic Body of Christ by perfecting the saints and nourishing them with the riches of the divine life until they all arrive at a full-grown man?

We know, according to [[Ephesians-01#v21|Ephesians 1:21-23]], that the church is the Body of Christ. The measure of the fullness of Christ is the measure of the stature of the Body of Christ. Here we see that for us to be conformed to the image of Christ, we need to allow the divine life within us to grow unto maturity. In this sense, we can say that conformation equals maturation.

I need you to see the difference between conformation and maturation. Conformation is the end result of transformation. In a note on [[Genesis-27#v1|Genesis 27:1]], you will see that to be transformed is to be metabolically changed in our natural life, whereas to be mature is to be filled with the divine life that changes us.

What does this distinction look like in the book of Genesis? By the time of chapter 37 of Genesis, Jacob had already been transformed to a significant extent. He was born as a supplanter, remember, a deceitful, manipulative person. After all, he stole his brother’s birthright. Now, such a person was in the process of being transformed to become Israel, that is, the prince of God.

What a marvelous transformation Jacob had experienced to a considerable degree. After the hollow of his thigh was touched in [[Genesis-32|chapter 32]], he began to limp, and that was not a performance. The strongest part of his being was touched. The backbone of his natural life was broken. But even after he experienced such a fundamental breaking, he was still scheming to a degree, because he was prostrating himself toward Esau to save his own life. That, in a sense, shows he was not fully transformed.

More importantly, after Rachel died, he had fondness for one of his sons, namely Joseph, giving him a coat of many colors. This shows he had a preferential love toward one of his sons. So long as you have a preferential love toward certain saints, that betrays and exposes the fact that although you have been transformed, you are not fully mature in the divine life. You are not utterly conformed to the image of Christ.

Eventually, Jacob lost the treasure of his heart, namely Joseph. A report was given to Jacob that it was highly likely that Joseph was devoured by a beast in the field, but there was no conclusive truth that Joseph had indeed perished, because there was no corpse to be found. This kind of perplexing, bewildering situation pressed Jacob to open his being to the Triune God of grace and mercy, just like an open jar utterly opened to the heavenly rain.

Brothers and sisters, once you lose the treasure of your heart, your inward parts are more open than ever before to receive the dispensing of the Triune God finely, continually, and thoroughly, because you cannot live without the Triune God. The easier our situation is, the more difficult it is for us to fully open our inward parts to the Triune God for His divine dispensing. When you are pressed, you cannot but open your being to the Triune God to receive the continuous impartation of the Triune God Himself as life and life supply into your very being.

Eventually, you realize that when a number of his sons returned to the good land, they reported that unless Benjamin was released to go with them, one of their brothers would be retained and detained in prison in Egypt. Initially, Jacob resisted even the thought of losing Benjamin, one of Rachel’s sons, the only remaining son of Rachel. But eventually he spoke these noble words, indicating that he had matured in the divine life: “If I am bereaved, I am bereaved. May the All-sufficient God of mercy be with you.” This shows Jacob had lost any kind of preferential treatment toward any of his sons. He was mature.

This is why he could bless all those who came into contact with him. The divine life filled him, was overflowing through him, and flowed out of him to reach all others who came into contact with him. He blessed all his sons and even Pharaoh. This means he had been conformed to the image of Christ, the firstborn Son of God. I hope you are impressed with this.

As you pass through any kind of suffering, you need to open your being to the Lord. “Each blow I suffer...” Lord Jesus, in this circumstance I would like to gain more of You, so I would not only be transformed, but mature in the divine life, until the divine life flows out of me to others unhindered, so that I may have life and have it abundantly, and they might have life and have it abundantly. I hope you are impressed with this.

The second definition I would like to give you is to point out the distinction between transformation and conformation. Conformation is the consummation of the transformation of the believers in life. Transformation is mainly inward and a matter of essence; conformation is mainly outward and a matter of shape. Conformation involves the shaping of this form into a certain image, the image of Christ. Such conformation is our preparation before the transfiguration and glorification of our mortal body. So you see, in a sense, you may say conformation is a bridge that connects our transformation and our glorification.

Now, I received some light when I was considering the Greek words “transformed” and “conformed.” I checked this matter with Brother Roger, and he confirmed it last night. I am free to share this with you.

The Greek word translated “transformed” is *metamorphoō*, whereas the Greek word for “conformation” is *summorphos*. Simply put, *morph* in Greek means shape or form, referring to the essential nature or character of something or someone. *Meta* means change. That is why you have the English term “metamorphosis.”

Now, how about *sum*? In *summorphos*, *sum* means same, matching, identical, together, or joining, as in “symphony.” What is this? The joining together of many sounds. Don’t you like to hear symphonies? It’s a delight to hear. Do you realize that the word *symphonia* is used in [[Matthew-18|Matthew 18]] for one accord? That is a beautiful symphony to the Lord.

This shows that as we are fully transformed, that is, as we are changed metabolically in our natural life, we are conformed to the image of Christ to such an extent that we and He share the same form. We are identical to Christ in our shape, form, or image. Isn’t this utterly convincing?

I treasure all the first-term trainees. Do you realize that so many saints serving at LSM, in Anaheim, and countless saints prayed for you to come to the FTTA? We have prayed for them for so long to be drawn to the Lord so that they would be drawn out of every preoccupation and answer Your calling to come to the FTTA.

Don’t you love the God of glory? “Lord, appear to us day by day. Captivate us by Your glory until You conquer us day by day, hour by hour.” The Triune God is not only a God of glory, but a God of beauty. [[Isaiah-60#v19|Isaiah 60:19]] says, “Jehovah will be your eternal light, and your God your beauty.” Your God is your beauty. There is no trace of beauty in you as a fallen human apart from the Triune God. In [[Psalms-27#v4|Psalm 27:4]]: “One thing have I desired... that I may dwell in the house of Jehovah.”

If God could grant you one wish, many Christians would ask for a billion dollars, or the most lovely woman, or to make all their children billionaires. The one request is that I would behold the beauty of the Lord so I would reflect the beauty of the Lord. [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|Second Corinthians 3:18]] tells us to be transformed into His image from glory to glory, even as from the Lord Spirit. [[2 Corinthians-04#v1|Second Corinthians 4:1]] speaks of having this ministry.

Well, what is the New Testament ministry? It is for us to behold the glory of the Lord and reflect the glory of the Lord. It is to receive the element of the God of glory and beauty and shine out, manifest, and minister the element of the Triune God of glory and beauty to others, so they too may be glorified and beautified in the Triune God of glory.

Josh, He is beautifying you right now to be the unique beauty in the universe until we come to the fulfillment of these words: “Your eyes will behold the King in His beauty” — [[Isaiah-33#v17|Isaiah 33:17]].

I like [[Isaiah-45#v11|Isaiah 45:11]]: the King will desire you. Our beauty is the expression of the Triune God. We would become the same as He is in His beauty and glory.

I will go on for the interest of time.

Let me ask a question: what is God’s image? The footnote in [[Genesis-01#v26|Genesis 1:26]] says that it is the expression of God’s attributes, such as love, light, holiness, and righteousness. This means that man was created with human virtues, which are copies of the divine attributes, as the means to express God’s attributes. We were created to express God’s attributes through our human virtues.

In the created man was a measure of love, light, righteousness, and holiness. That is why there is an inherent desire in man to love one another and to become holy, righteous, and pure. But the fall of man came in.

## III. As a result of man’s fall, the image that God created for man was destroyed—[[Romans-03#v23|Rom. 3:23]]

[[Romans-03#v23|Romans 3:23]] says, “All have sinned and fall short...” Glory is God expressed. The definition of glory in the *Life-study of Hebrews* is that glory is the expression of what He is in His attributes. I love this: His love, light, righteousness, and holiness. We have fallen short of such a glorious expression of God. So, instead of expressing the righteous God, we express Satan as lawlessness.

God is righteousness. Satan is lawlessness. Hebrews tells us, “I have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness.” The ministry said that the Lord Jesus loved God as righteousness and hated Satan as lawlessness. Look at the news headlines: the expression of Satan as lawlessness. The image God created for man was destroyed.

## IV. In God’s organic salvation, we are regenerated, sanctified, renewed, transformed, and conformed to the image of the firstborn Son of God, and thus we are made sons of God that God may gain a corporate expression, which is the mingling of His firstborn Son with His many sons—[[Romans-05#v10|Rom. 5:10]]; [[Hebrews-08#v29|8:29]]

Now, at this point, I’d like to read you a very high definition of the Body of Christ in view of our conformation. Please write this: “The Body is the aggregate of all the believers who, through transformation, have been conformed to the image of the firstborn Son of God.”

The critical matter here is twofold. On the one hand, our conformation to the image of Christ as the Firstborn is the consummation of the preceding steps of God’s organic salvation, such as regeneration, sanctification, and so forth. On the other hand, we need to see that the goal is not merely our individual godliness. It is not merely our own spirituality, but the organic Body of Christ.

You know, Brother Lee said that to be conformed, you will realize there is a requirement and demand of this life that you practice the church life. If you are individualistic, that means you are not conformed to the image. It is impossible for you to become independent if you are conformed.

There were many prominent believers among us, some co-workers and elders, but they left and betrayed the church life, exposing that they are independent, individualistic, and have never been conformed to the image of Christ. If you were truly conformed, you would be buildable to keep the oneness of the Spirit in the uniting bond of peace.

How do we see that the goal of God’s conforming us to the image of Christ is the Body of Christ? [[Romans-08#v29|Romans 8:29]]. You have to realize that chapters 9 through 11 are a parenthetical section in the book. Romans 12 is a direct continuation of Romans 8. [[Romans-12#v4|Romans 12:4-5]] says that we who are many are one Body in Christ. We are members one of another. The ministry helps us to see this implies divine building.

You have to treasure the word “many.” “Many brothers of Christ, conformed to the image of Christ, the firstborn Son of God,” in [[Romans-08#v29|Romans 8:29]], equals the many members of the Body of Christ mentioned in [[Romans-12#v4|Romans 12:4-5]]. Are we convinced?

In Romans 8, we are told in verse 10 that our spirit is life. Verse 6 says the mind set on the spirit is life. Verse 11 says life is given to our mortal bodies. So, what you have in Romans 8 is the enlivened spirit, the enlivened mind, and the enlivened body. What are these three parts for? Flip to Romans 12. You see that according to verse 1, we need to present our bodies as a living sacrifice. Our enlivened body is to present our bodies to practice the Body life.

We need to be burning in spirit, serving the Lord, in verse 11. The enlivened mind is a renewed mind, mentioned in [[Romans-12#v2|Romans 12:2]]: “Be transformed.” You see how Romans 8 and Romans 12 are connected? So, we need to pray, “Lord, make me a little, normal, functioning member of the Body by conforming me to the image of Your beloved Son.” I hope you are impressed with this.

## V.

Here, what we are going to do is connect [[Romans-08#v2|Romans 8:2]] with [[Romans-08#v29|Romans 8:29]]. [[Romans-08#v2|Romans 8:2]] speaks of the law of the Spirit of life, and [[Romans-08#v29|Romans 8:29]] speaks of conformity to the image of Christ as the Firstborn. Eventually, we need to realize that what conforms and shapes us is not our self-effort or the endeavoring of the self, but the operation of the law of the Spirit of life. You need to remember three crucial functions of the law of the Spirit of life.

First, [[Romans-08#v2|Romans 8:2]]. This is exemplified and illustrated by the law of aerodynamics conquering the law of gravity. Unlike eagles, we cannot fly, but as soon as we board a plane, the law of aerodynamics is in effect, and we effortlessly and automatically conquer the law of gravity as we enjoy our seat on the airplane. Likewise, outside of Christ as the jumbo jet, we are always conquered by the satanic law of gravity. As long as we abide by the Spirit and enjoy the first-class accommodation of this lovely One, it is effortless for us to overcome the law of sin and of death.

Second, the law of the Spirit of life dispenses the Triune God into our tripartite being. [[Romans-08#v10|Romans 8:10]], [[Romans-08#v11|11]], and 6. You see, the law of the Spirit of life is the automatic function and spontaneous working of the divine life. Just as the law of digestion digests and assimilates the food we eat into our blood to make the food we eat our very constitution, do you have to beg your body to digest food? It’s automatic. Likewise, as long as you abide in Christ, the law of the Spirit of life will impart the Triune God as life into your spirit, soul, and body.

Lastly, the law of the Spirit of life shapes us to the image of Christ. If you are a farmer and your goal is to produce many peaches, do you have to kneel before the peach tree and ask it to bear peaches? Do you have to instruct the tree at all? Peach trees would tell the farmer, if they could, “Please go home and rest. There is a law in my peach life, and this law prevents me from producing anything else but peaches, because the law of the tree produces fruit according to its characteristic form.” Likewise, the law of the Spirit of life shapes us into the image of Christ as the firstborn Son of God effortlessly.

I received new light last night. I never saw this so clearly. [[Hebrews-07#v16|Hebrews 7:16]] speaks of the power, whereas [[Hebrews-08#v10|Hebrews 8:10]] speaks of the law of the Spirit of life. What is the inner law referred to in [[Hebrews-08#v10|Hebrews 8:10]]? The law of the Spirit of life. Because this is a quotation of Jeremiah 31, where Jehovah said He would impart His law. The singular law becomes plural laws because once the law of the Spirit of life begins to function in us, it becomes the law in our mind, emotion, and will.

Such an organic law is the power of the indestructible life. Every kind of life has its essence, law, and power. It’s automatic. How can we enjoy the operation of the law of the Spirit of life? First, we need to walk according to our mingled spirit. Second, we need to set our mind on the spirit. How? We need to set our mind on the saints, praying for them, shepherding them, cherishing and nourishing them, and set our mind on the Word.

Psalm 119 says, “Oh, how I love Your law.” I hope you could testify this to the angels: “How I love the Word of God.” In this way, the law of the Spirit of life would operate in you effortlessly. You also need to say, “Abba, Father.” We can cry this because we have received a spirit of sonship.

We sing this day after day. In this way, the law of the Spirit of life will activate to shape you into the image of Christ as the firstborn Son of God a little more. We also need to take the initiative to witness that we are children of God. It doesn’t say that we witness with the Spirit, but that the Spirit Himself witnesses with our spirit. This means that we take the initiative to witness and declare that we are children of God. I can assure you, once you declare that you are a child of God, the Spirit will echo, vindicate, and confirm this: “Yes, you are indeed a child of God.” You will change as you realize what does not match the image of the Son of God.
