## I. [[2 Thessalonians-02#v14|Second Thessalonians 2:14]] says, “To which also He called you through our gospel unto the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ”:

So, we were saved to *obtain* the glory of God. Not just saved because of sin and evil things... that is just the beginning. But on the positive side, it's that we would *obtain* the glory of God. So, all of you here—you have a portion of glory that nobody (I'll put it this way) in the universe, has your measure or portion of glory. That is why I say this every time when I give a message - the glory that is in you, don't try to do anything... just share the point that touched you, but glory is going to flow out of you, and we are wide open, and it's going to flow into us. This is mutual fellowship.

### A. The words to which refer to salvation in sanctification and belief of the truth, as mentioned in [[2 Thessalonians-02#v13|verse 13]].

### B. In eternity God chose us unto salvation in sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth, and then in time He called us unto the obtaining of the glory of our Lord:

So, each one of us, a particular time came. Maybe for someone it's coming here and now, but other times prior to this... this is the Lord's loving care. He wants you not just to know about glory... He wants you to realize that glory is becoming your being.

#### 1. Salvation in sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth is the procedure.

#### 2. The obtaining of the glory of our Lord is the goal:

So, God has a goal. The goal is that we would be saturated with glory. Not only have glory, but *be* glory, and *express* glory. This will be one, rather unusual way of spiritual warfare against the demons... they attack in this way - every now and then I say, "you are not worthy of me fighting you... I'll do somethign stronger than that—when you contact me, you contact *glory*." They run away from glory.

##### a. The glory of the Lord is that He, as the Son of God the Father, possesses the Father’s life and nature to express Him.

So here, in this point, the *Father's life* and the "Father's possessions"... So, I'm pausing here. When we have the Lord's table meeting. The first portion is on Christ Himself, but then the indwelling Christ takes the lead to worship God the Father. Our response may be mainly to sing another hymn... but our Father God is very patient. He is waiting for His children to share with Him and honor Him as the source of every positive Christian matter.

I'll stay here for a minute on this point. On behalf of the Lord, I'm asking a question: "What is the source of your living? Whetehr on the human side or the spiritual side? What is going to take place?" The self will no longer be the source, because the self is the source. Something demonic is behind that, but the source is God the Father Himself. For decades, I would say for hundred and hundreds of years... God the Father has been *waiting* for His children to know that not only God is God... but God is the Father. Deep within me, there is just thine deep yearning and genuine hope that knowing and experiencing and living according to God the Father...

##### b. To obtain the Lord’s glory is to be in the same position as the Son of God that we may express the Father.

So again, it's on the Father. We are receiving the Lord's glory, and from the Lord's point of view it's that we may express the Father. The Father wants His Son to be glorified and expressed, and the Son wants His Father to be glorified and expressed.


## II. God has called us unto the obtaining of the glory of the Lord Jesus:

He called us unto the *obtaining* of the glory of God. Not just knowing about it, but obtaining, again and again, the glory of the Lord Jesus.

### A. The glory that the Father gave to the Son ([[John-17#v22|John 17:22]]) is the sonship with the Father’s life and divine nature ([[John-05#v26|5:26]]) to express the Father in His fullness ([[John-01#v18|1:18]]; [[John-14#v9|14:9]]; [[Colossians-02#v9|Col. 2:9]]; [[Hebrews-01#v3|Heb. 1:3]]).

Point after point, I didn't actually know I was focusing on the Father... I was just following the inner leading when I was creating this outline, but now I am with you, and realizing this is concerning the Father.

I'm not trying to be *unreal*, but the Father is also here. The Triune God is always together. So, I don't know if I've ever done this before but I say "Father, we love You very much, and we know we exist becuase of You, we were saved because of you, we are in the Lord's recovery becuase of you, and now we are learning more and more about you. In our personal life and in our church life, we want to give You Your portion."

We believe that out of all the saints who hear this, there will be a few who will be deeply touched. More and more, we will be in this marvelous situation.

### B. This glory the Son has given to His believers so that they also may have the sonship with the Father’s life and divine nature ([[John-17#v2|John 17:2]]; [[2 Peter-01#v4|2 Pet. 1:4]]) to express the Father in the Son in His fullness ([[John-01#v16|John 1:16]]).

These points are all related to glory. These are all various aspects of Christ being expressed in a glorified way.

### C. The glory of the Lord Jesus is that the Father has given Him the Father’s life and nature to express the Father:

We have the Father's life. We have the Father's nature. Now we can express the Father, not just talk about Him, but express Him. Genuine expressing is when you don't even realize you are expressing. You don't say, "I'm going to meet this person while donig my best to express God the Father..." that's sincere, but that is just you... but, when you are just being saturated by the God of glory, and realizing that the source of this glory is the Father, and the Father is the source... then, you just effortlessly express Him because of Him.

#### 1. This is the glory the Son has given to us.

Do you see the connection? Between the glory we've been talking about, to the Father's life and nature, and the expression of the Father. So, we have glory to express the Triune God. 

#### 2. This means that the Son has given us the Father’s life and nature so that we may be able to express the Father.

So, now we *are able*, I believe at least we have a teeny weeny faith regarding this. "I am now able to express God the Father." It will just be a part of your being.

### D. God has called us unto the obtaining of this glory, the glory of the divine life and the divine nature to express the Divine Being.

Just look at this for about a minute. God has *called us*. You leave it to Him how He called, it's not something verbally you can hear, but inwardly, He is calling you. Your dear brothers and sisters are in the FTTA because inwardly, God called you. There will be a number coming in the Fall, but actually the number was too weak so there was some praying last night, that the young people would realize that we are coming to the end of this age. You are graduating from college, the best two years are coming up to be in the Full Time Training. Some ideas may be put into you "Oh, I'm going to be way behind everyone else and what they are doing..." but actually, the fact is, when you gain Christ in those two years, the Lord will lead you in a wonderful way. What is the goal? This is unto the obtaining of this glory.

So, we are here and now, *obtaining* this glory. The glory of the divine life, and the divine nature, to express the divine being. I won't even try to express the words "Divine Being". But, it will become experientially real to each one of us.

Now we come to a lengthy section full of many wonderful words.

## III. In [[2 Thessalonians-01#v10|2 Thessalonians 1:10]] Paul speaks of Christ coming to be glorified in His saints:

He wants to be glorified, but He is not mainly going to do it directly... it will be *through* His saints. The more we are saturated with glory, then we will become the expression. Through all the saints.

### A. The Lord is the Lord of glory—[[1 Corinthians-02#v8|1 Cor. 2:8]]:

This is a very simple statement. If I were in a certain country, I haven't been there for about three years, but whenever I would mention something like *this*, "When we come to the next day, or I'll be back" is the same thing - it's a shirt they wear, because they are expressing this. Just these wrods - if you were exercising somewhere and had these words on their chest: "I'm back." The Lord is the Lord of glory - you should wear that right here on your chest... You are not trying to give a message, but actually, you are the message. But, we are not going to have these kinds of things *on us,* but our *being is going to express this*. Other people won't necesarily realize what is going on at the time, but if they were to have some kind of interaction with you, then they will walk away and realize, "What happened? There is something in me and I've never had this feeling before..." That is glory. What a wonderful expression of His Being.

#### 1. He has been glorified in His resurrection and ascension—[[John-17#v1|John 17:1]]; [[Luke-24#v26|Luke 24:26]]; [[Hebrews-02#v9|Heb. 2:9]].

So, notice now, we are talking about 'glorified.' Not just the thing itself—glory—He has been *glorified*, His glory *has been* expressed, in His resurrection and ascenstion. So, the more we know and expreience Christ in His resurrection and ascension, the more He will be glorified. We are not aware of this, but we are glorifying Him just by our very *being*.

#### 2. Now He is in us as the hope of glory ([[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27]]) to bring us into glory ([[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]).

I like this word, "the hope of glory". As human beings, we need hope. Among many things. I hope after you graduate, you are in the third stage of the experience of life... I hope within the next three or four years, you are not *alone* anymore... you got married... There can be all kinds of hopes, there is nothing to be ashamed of... and so, there are verses that make this clear. I can honestly say, "Lord, I hope that I would be one with You at the time You are going to rapture the overcoming saints. " This is my hope at this very moment. We have this delightful hope. It will bring us into glory. 

God will say,  "This saint is serious about this... so I am going to deepen my glory in Him, and release it through him"

He saturated us with the glory, and now we can glorify Him with the glory that has been saturating us more and more. All kinds of human beings will be shocked, they won't know what to say. There will be this manifestation of something they've never seen before. Then it came out from you and me... from our being... Another way of putting it is that our daily Christian life is a glorifying day-by-day life... simply in ordinary things, just seeming to be in ordinary conversation. I'm just giving you an example, because whether we are aware of it or not, God is being glorified... when out of a loving concern for this or that situation they are in... He is being glorified in this. We shouldn't try to hope that it's going to happen, or to want to have the *feeling*. Just imagine when you stand before the judgment seat of Christ, when you are athere, and you would say, "My dear, Beloving One, your life was full of glory, and you don't realize how much you glorified Me, now you are going to be reigning with Me, expressing the glory of the Triune God."
