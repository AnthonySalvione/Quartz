These are just some of the effects of the Lord's fostering.

## II. In 1 Thessalonians 2 we have a description of the fostering of the young Christian life:

I just pause for a moment—all of us here, the Lord is fostering. He wants us to grow to maturity here and now. To be mature, ready for the Lord to come, in the hidden way for the Bride.
