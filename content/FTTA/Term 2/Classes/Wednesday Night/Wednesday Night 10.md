## III. The divine sanctification is the holding line in the carrying out of the divine economy to sonize us divinely, making us sons of God that we may become the same as God in His life and in His nature (but not in His Godhead), so that we may be God’s expression; hence, God’s sanctification is the divine sonizing:

### A. We say that sanctification is the holding line because every step of God’s work with us is to make us holy; the carrying out of the eternal economy of God is by the Spirit’s sanctification—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v23|1 Thes. 5:23]]; [[John-17#v17|John 17:17]]; [[Ephesians-05#v26|Eph. 5:26-27]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v11|1 Cor. 6:11]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v3|12:3b]]; [[Hebrews-12#v4|Heb. 12:4-14]]; [[Romans-08#v28|Rom. 8:28-29]]; [[Ephesians-04#v30|Eph. 4:30]]; [[1 Thessalonians-05#v19|1 Thes. 5:19]]; [[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7a]]; [[Psalm-73#v16|Psa. 73:16-17]], [[Psalm-73#v25|25-26]].

### B. The seeking sanctification, the initial sanctification, is unto repentance to bring us back to God—[[1 Peter-01#v2|1 Pet. 1:2]]; [[Luke-15#v8|Luke 15:8-10]], [[Luke-15#v17|17-21]]; [[John-16#v8|John 16:8-11]].

### C. The redeeming sanctification, the positional sanctification, is by the blood of Christ, to transfer us from Adam to Christ—[[Hebrews-13#v12|Heb. 13:12]]; [[Hebrews-09#v13|9:13-14]]; [[Hebrews-10#v29|10:29]].

### D. The regenerating sanctification, the beginning of dispositional sanctification, renews us from our spirit to make us, the sinners, sons of God—a new creation with the divine life and nature—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v17|2 Cor. 5:17]]; [[Galatians-06#v15|Gal. 6:15]].

### E. The renewing sanctification, the continuation of dispositional sanctification, renews our soul from our mind through all the parts of our soul to make our soul a part of God’s new creation—[[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2b]]; [[Romans-06#v4|6:4]]; [[Romans-07#v6|7:6]]; [[Ephesians-04#v23|Eph. 4:23]]; [[Ezekiel-36#v26|Ezek. 36:26-27]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16-18]].

### F. The transforming sanctification, the daily sanctification, reconstitutes us with the element of Christ metabolically to make us a new constitution as a part of the organic Body of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-03#v12|1 Cor. 3:12]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]].

### G. The conforming sanctification, the shaping sanctification, shapes us in the image of the glorious Christ to make us the expression of Christ; our conformation is our maturity in the divine life through which we participate in God’s divinity in full and are ==solidified in the possession of His divine element==—[[Romans-08#v28|Rom. 8:28-29]]; [[Hebrews-06#v1|Heb. 6:1a]].

### H. The glorifying sanctification, the consummating sanctification, redeems our body by transfiguring it to make us Christ’s expression in full and in glory—[[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]; [[Romans-08#v23|Rom. 8:23]].

## IV. The divine, dispositional sanctification is carried out by Christ as the life-giving, sanctifying, and speaking Spirit—[[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[1 Thessalonians-05#v23|1 Thes. 5:23]]; [[Ephesians-05#v25|Eph. 5:25-27]]; [[Song-08#v13|S. S. 8:13-14]]:

### A. Christ as the life-giving Spirit sanctifies the church by cleansing her according to the washing of the water in the word; according to the divine concept, water here refers to the flowing life of God typified by flowing water ([[Exodus-17#v6|Exo. 17:6]]; [[1 Corinthians-10#v4|1 Cor. 10:4]]; [[John-07#v37|John 7:37-39]]; [[Revelation-07#v17|Rev. 7:17]]; [[Revelation-21#v6|21:6]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1]], [[Revelation-22#v17|17]]); we are now in such a washing process in order that the church may be holy and without blemish.

### B. The Greek word for *washing* in [[Ephesians-05#v26|Ephesians 5:26]] is literally *laver*; in the Old Testament the priests used the laver to wash away their earthly defilement ([[Exodus-30#v18|Exo. 30:18-21]]); day by day, morning and evening, we need to come to the Bible and be cleansed by the laver of the water in the word.

### C. Paul uses the Greek word *rhema* when he speaks of the word with its washing process ([[Ephesians-05#v26|Eph. 5:26]]); *logos* is God’s Word objectively recorded in the Bible; *rhema* is the word of God spoken to us on a specific occasion ([[Mark-14#v72|Mark 14:72]]; [[Luke-01#v35|Luke 1:35-38]]; [[Luke-05#v5|5:5]]; [[Luke-24#v1|24:1-8]]).

### D. As the life-giving Spirit, Christ is the speaking Spirit; whatever He speaks is the word that washes us; this does not refer to *logos*, the constant word, but to *rhema*, which denotes an instant word, the word that the Lord presently speaks to us—[[Matthew-04#v4|Matt. 4:4]]; [[John-06#v63|John 6:63]]; [[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7]]; [[Revelation-22#v17|22:17a]]; cf. [[Isaiah-06#v9|Isa. 6:9-10]]; [[Matthew-13#v14|Matt. 13:14-15]]; [[Acts-28#v25|Acts 28:25-31]].

### E. The *rhema* reveals something to us personally and directly; it shows us what we need to deal with and what we need to be cleansed from (the laver of bronze was a mirror that could reflect and expose—[[Exodus-38#v8|Exo. 38:8]]); ==the important thing for each one of us is this—is God speaking His word to me today?==—[[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7]]; [[1 Samuel-03#v1|1 Sam. 3:1]], [[1 Samuel-03#v21|21]]; [[Amos-03#v7|Amos 3:7]].

### F. One thing that we always treasure is that the Lord still speaks to us personally and directly today; true growth in life depends upon our receiving the word directly from God; only His speaking in us has true spiritual value—[[Hebrews-03#v7|Heb. 3:7-11]], [[Hebrews-03#v15|15]]; [[Hebrews-04#v7|4:7]]; [[Psalm-95#v7|Psa. 95:7-8]].

### G. The central point of our prayers should be our longing for the Lord’s speaking, which enables us to fulfill the goal of His eternal economy according to His heart’s desire to have His divine sonship—[[Luke-01#v38|Luke 1:38]]; [[Luke-10#v38|10:38-42]]; [[Ephesians-01#v5|Eph. 1:5]].

### H. In a very practical sense, the Lord’s presence is one with His speaking; whenever He speaks, we realize His presence within us; Christ’s speaking is the very presence of the life-giving Spirit—cf. [[Exodus-33#v12|Exo. 33:12-17]]; [[Hebrews-11#v8|Heb. 11:8]].

### I. The speaking of the indwelling Christ as the life-giving Spirit within us is the cleansing water that deposits a new element into us to replace the old element in our nature and disposition; this metabolic cleansing causes a genuine and inward change in life, which is the reality of dispositional sanctification and transformation.

## V. Our being sanctified for the divine sonship ultimately consummates in the New Jerusalem as the holy city ([[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v10|10]]) and the aggregate of the divine sonship ([[Revelation-21#v7|Rev. 21:7]]); this is the ultimate consummation of God becoming a man in the flesh that man might become God in the Spirit to gain a corporate, great God-man ([[Revelation-21#v3|vv. 3]], [[Revelation-21#v22|22]]) for the corporate expression, the glory, of the Triune God ([[Revelation-21#v11|vv. 11]], [[Revelation-21#23|23]]).
