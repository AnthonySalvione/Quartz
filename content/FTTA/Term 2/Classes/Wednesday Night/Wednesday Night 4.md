**GENERAL SUBJECT: THE CRUCIAL POINTS OF THE TRUTH IN PAUL'S EPISTLES—FIRST AND SECOND THESSALONIANS**

# Walking in a Manner Worthy of God and of God’s Calling for the Reality of the Body of Christ

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thes. 2:12]]; [[Philippians-03#v13|Phil. 3:13-14]]; [[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]]; [[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1-4]], [[Ephesians-04#v15|15-16]], [[Ephesians-04#v20|20-24]]; [[Ephesians-05#v2|5:2]], 8, [[Ephesians-05#v18|18]]

This message comes after the scene we saw in the first messages—this young group of believers was described as being the church of God the Father and in the Lord Jesus Christ, indicating that they were not only spiritually, but practically, in God. And in their young age and juncture they were brought into a holy life for the church life, and a structure which was the work of faith, the labor of love, and the endurance of hope. These believers had received the gospel, been regenerated, and turned from idol worship to serve the living God.

Here they were six months later receiving these words from Paul, indicating that these words are very practical and accessible to us all, and we need to pay particular attention when we say walk, that this walk is describable and entered into in the very beginning of their Christian life.

## I. As believers in Christ and children of God, we should walk in a manner worthy of God—[[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thes. 2:12]]:

This walk fulfills God’s calling of us, brings us into the reality of the Body of Christ, which is the marker of the culmination of this age. This walk is so significant and effectual, and is needed now to bring us to the culmination of this age.

How we need to pray-read this outline, along with this whole outline, of course.

Now, this verse connects this juncture (this point in the book) with chapter 1, verse 1. So, what is it to be in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ? Well, in practicality it is to have a living there. A living! It’s not a theory, construct, or concept. This is to be something practical! These young believers had already reached a point where Paul could speak to them in this way—that they would walk in a manner worthy of God which would fulfill this calling.

The kingdom of glory in this verse is to express God in one’s daily living, or in other words, to live out God. When we live out God, we express God, which is the kingdom of God, which is filled with glory.

This is the depth of reality these young believers could reach.

According to John 1:12, these young believers received Him, received His life, and became children of God, which afforded them a capacity or a potential to be as God their Father, and thus to be in God and in the Lord Jesus Christ... and to walk worthily of Him in their daily living.

So, this is their potential. We now today should walk in a manner worthy of God. This is wonderfully developed in the following points.

### A. First Thessalonians 2:12 is an explanation of 1:1; in order for the church to be in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ in a practical way, the believers must walk in a manner worthy of God; to walk in a manner worthy of God actually means to live God—[[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1]], 17; [[Ephesians-05#v1|5:1-2]], 8; [[2 Corinthians-05#v7|2 Cor. 5:7]]; [[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]]; [[Ephesians-02#v6|2:6]]; [[Philippians-01#v20|Phil. 1:20-21a]]:

To walk in a manner worthy of God actually means to live out God. To have a daily living and a daily life that lives God. So, here, let’s begin with what it is to walk. To walk refers to our living, moving, acting, having, and holding our being in a certain kind of way. This is to have a walk. Everyone has a daily walk, and they hold their being a certain way according to their daily walk.

These young believers lived, moved, acted, held their being, and had their being in God. This has to enthrall us. To walk in a manner worthy of God means to live God. To live God is quite an unusual expression. It will be defined further in the subpoint. To live God is not, as some may think, to live for God. It’s not uncommon for believers to talk about living for God, or doing a work, being a missionary, or seminary. These things are wonderful, but this is not what we are talking about. To live God goes beyond that. By beyond I mean to live by God. Do we live by God? To live by God means to live by God as a resource, or as our wherewithal, and we live out our Christian life by drawing upon God and taking God. That isn’t enough, though. To live God means to have our entire being in God as we live in our daily living.

Let’s consider this a little bit. So, the Thessalonians were _in_ God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. Their living was carried out there based upon the fact that they had received the Lord and had been regenerated. Now, since we have Him in us, we are able to contact Him, which enables us to live by Him.

I want to mention a couple things, however. This walk, this daily living, is not a snapshot of someone who lives by God, where you can take a picture of them living by God, but it is a video. It is a video, or an aggregate, or a continuum of your living, and in every frame in that video you are living God. You are connected with Him in a resilient way, which causes you to live in Him. This is wonderful, huh? We all have some time in the Lord, and we marvel at this. But here, take note: the Thessalonians were new believers. They had a three-week exposure of observing Paul and his company, receiving their words, and then they left. After they left, Paul and his company received reports—encouraging reports—but the effect of their visiting upon them brought them into a revolutionized kind of living which impacted not only them and their communities, but it impacted the people of the entire region of that part of the earth!! What is happening here!?

Two things I’d like to mention:

1. this allows us to see that we should never think that a new one or a young one can’t receive or enter into a high experiential truth of the Lord’s Recovery—the intrinsic matters. The Thessalonians did right away! The new covenant ministers spoke and lived this way before them in a way that their living or pattern infused faith into them, and that organic union was formed which was the basis for their living in faith. Paul and his company did not only do a work of faith, but they did a labor of love, indicating that they _cleaved_ to the indwelling One, and were one with Him in an organic union. Based on that, they saw one who had an intimate touch with the indwelling One. This caused, don’t you think, a feeling or atmosphere, that when the Thessalonians saw their words and their entrance (the way they were), which confirmed their words, and they saw the atmosphere of Paul and his company matching the words they spoke, it caused the Thessalonians to be opened and enter into what they saw. After a short period of time after their departure, they continued on and held that atmosphere. An atmosphere of faith (meaning cleaving to the Lord in our organic union with Him) and love (meaning that in that cleaving or holding, they had an influx of the Person or a mingling with that Person) that allowed them to demonstrate what it was to live in God. They saw that, and they emulated that, and they experienced it! This should encourage us, no matter how young we are or how coarse we are, no matter how our daily living looks. We are not too young or unsophisticated or unlearned. The Thessalonians were evidently very coarse, but what they saw caused them to turn from every idol, and they laid hold of the indwelling and living God. Then, they realized this hope and endurance, which gave the assurance to the apostle which enabled him to write to them again and again and encourage them to live this way to hasten the Lord’s coming. So, we can taste, speak of, and live out this flavor and atmosphere of affection and cleaving to the Lord in our daily living until it becomes a video and not just a snapshot, and we live in a union with Him that causes us to express Him and live Him out... because it is God!
    

#### 1. Our daily life should actually be God Himself; since only God is worthy of Himself, to walk in a manner worthy of God is to live God, that is, to express God in our daily living—[[1 Corinthians-10#v31|1 Cor. 10:31]].

His infusion causes us to live Him out. This living here is worthy. Not only does it match His constitution, nature, and makeup, but it also expresses His longing and His desire, and brings forth His kingdom in glory. The Thessalonians not only partook of His life and nature and were brought into a living with Him, but they were able to sense what moves God and were moved by what moves God and had the desire that God has. When they experienced this holy life for the church life, they were involved with Him in this way of affectionate, intimate, intrinsic touch in such a way that they, in this atmosphere, realized, “This is what God wants.” We have to want this. If we are going to have this kind of walk, we have to want this.

Silly thought, you know, in general reference to that book of the OT we like so much where there is a king who looks for a maiden and courts her. What if, hypothetically, there was a king who was all-virtuous and possessing, and he comes to a maiden and courts her, and she nods and agrees he is the king, but she is oblivious. She doesn’t respond to his courtship and inquiry. She may be very virtuous and walk well as a fine woman, but she is not worthy of the king. So, are we worthy? Do we have the same feeling that desires an affectionate, deep touch?

These Thessalonians brought forth this kind of daily living. They decided that He was so incomparable to anything that they could hold in their hands. They just threw away all the idols.

Are you holding anything? Anything in your grasp that you appreciate? Well, eventually, if we are to walk worthily of Him, we have to drop everything we have just like how He dropped everything for us to have an intimate touch with Him. Through that touch, we match Him in His heart’s desire. This surely is part of our daily living in Him.

Paul asked the Corinthians, just like the Thessalonians, to come out of their old experiences and have a new, focused living where everything would involve the Lord and would express Him. That expression, of course, which is His kingdom, comes out of our intrinsic touch. So, this again is part of what it is to walk worthily of God. It is to live God out in our daily living. Remember, our daily living is symbolized in the Bible as a garment. This is a huge point.

When we live as the Thessalonians lived—which was worthy of God who called them into His glory and kingdom—they were already, after three weeks, far along in developing their bridal garment. Surely they had this as their atmosphere and consideration—that He could live out of them in the details of their daily living to a point where their living was worthy of Him.

#### 2. God’s economy is a matter of having God as life and living Him; God’s intention in His economy is to impart His element, His substance, and the ingredients of His nature into our being that we may live Him—[[1 Timothy-01#v4|1 Tim. 1:4]]; [[Ephesians-03#v16|Eph. 3:16-19]]; [[Philippians-01#v20|Phil. 1:20-21a]].

We have many definitions of God’s economy that we treasure. I love this one! When we hear our original definition of God’s economy, we may pray and try to open our being, but also we need to live Him out. In our daily living we need to contact the Lord, which supplies Him the permission and way to carry out the dispensing. Without our contact with Him, He can’t dispense Himself.

Please notice the word _that_. God’s economy has a goal. His dispensing isn’t just to make us like Him only, and spiritual specimens only. It is so we could live Him so He could have an organism, an expression, a corporate living on the earth again with a counterpart who is desirous to match Him and desires an affectionate, living touch in that instant. I love to say these words. When I say these words to you, I find myself in this atmosphere. I hope you speak these words. It sets you in an atmosphere. In that atmosphere, the dispensing is going on, and as you hold this atmosphere as you live, you live worthily of God and worthily of His calling, and you are in His kingdom in glory.

#### 3. God’s aim in His economy is that we, His chosen and redeemed people, have His life and nature inwardly and His image and likeness outwardly—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[Ephesians-02#v9|2:9]].

This refers to the verse in Genesis where we have the image and likeness. Inward reality and outward expression.

We have to ask ourselves in our daily living... how much expression is there? We have the divine life and nature, and our living is the showcase. It is to be proven and demonstrated and lived forth there in our daily living. What if we have a living and we are like so many believers where we could have an office mate for ten years who couldn’t believe we were a believer?

This walk implies that we give substance and reality and practicality to the inward reality, and join with Him in living it out.

In a class earlier this week, we talked about the illustration of the life we have being like a tiger life. A tiger has a life different than a kitty. A tiger lives out that life. You better watch out for that tiger. So, what if the tiger was just like a kitty and didn’t give the expression of a tiger? So, we have the tiger, but do we live out the tiger? Is there evidence of the inward reality that we have?

#### 4. In the divine life and by the law of the divine life, God will be wrought into us, and we will live Him and be constituted with Him in His life and nature but not in His Godhead; eventually, we will be a corporate entity—the Body of Christ—to be one with Him and live Him for His corporate expression—[[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]], 6, [[Ephesians-08#v10|10-11]], 29; [[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4-6]].

Please note this once again, that it’s a conditional statement. This matches the fact that we have His life and nature, but in His economy He wants us to have that life and also to live it. It’s as we live it that He has the opportunity to express Himself and bring people in and constitute an atmosphere. Eventually, if we do live this life out, we will be in the corporate entity, the Body of Christ, to be one with Him.

This begins to point to Roman numeral 2, which talks about how our living, which is up to us, is unto a corporate living. It’s not just for us. Actually, the genuine walk worthy of God is a corporate living. We join Him in the corporate spirit, and live together with all the saints who are there. Our attention to our personal living and our desire to bring in this kind of atmosphere—it’s not only contact with the Lord in the principle of the work of faith—but also involvement with Him in intimate and affectionate touch in the labor of love. Eventually this will ultimately bring in the bride for His satisfaction.

#### 5. To walk in a manner worthy of God by living God is to live the life of a God-man; God-men are divine and mystical persons, doing everything with God, in God, by God, and through God—[[1 Corinthians-10#v31|1 Cor. 10:31]]; [[Colossians-03#v17|Col. 3:17]].

Brother Lee would say, as soon as you realize that you are a genuine son of God, you straighten up in your seat and there is a beam in your eye. We need to realize this. Paul surely lived this out and Paul was surely one who realized this. They realized that Paul was different than the others. This became attractive to them and they decided to enter into it.

God-men are divine and mystical persons. Divine because they have life, and mystical because they live that life out in their living and manifest it. They can have a living where they do everything with God, in God, by God, and through God, giving lovely substance to this beautiful Person we have received.

Do you remember the wonder that you had the first time you read Colossians 3:17? This is a description of the life of the Thessalonians as new believers. They did everything through and in and with Christ. I repeat, saints, this was a group of new believers with no precedent, no reports, no other ones to look at. They touched this reality in the few persons living it out, they responded, and they became what they saw. They can, and in a short time surpass them! All the advantages we have, they didn’t have, but a big advantage we can have is to realize we don’t have to be experienced, advanced, or an FTTA graduate. What you do have to do is to be taken over by the Lord, redefined by Him with a redefined view of your human living, that your human living is a series of situations arranged by the sovereign God that are just right for you to cleave to Him, to form a new creation in the universe of you and Him that has never existed before. This is something altogether new.

### B. To walk in a manner worthy of God is to walk according to the mingled spirit; this is to live, move, have our being, and do everything according to the Spirit in our spirit—[[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]]; [[Galatians-05#v16|Gal. 5:16]], 25:

Remember, we shouldn’t personify the mingled spirit. Before you believed, your spirit was an organ, but when you believed, your spirit became a mingled spirit, which is a person. Now you can be with this Person whenever you’d like to.

The Spirit in our spirit is the ultimate and final title of the Spirit of God, indicating that He in simplicity is waiting for a relationship with you in which He could be your Bridegroom and you the bride, fulfilling the “Spirit and the bride say, Come.” Should we not attend to Him and cleave to Him? When we do, will we be somber? No! We will be excited and jubilant!

#### 1. Obeying the sense of life, obeying the teaching of the anointing, and walking according to the spirit are three aspects of one thing—[[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]], [[Romans-08#v6|6]]; [[1 John-02#v27|1 John 2:27]].

In our brother’s wonderful sharing on this, obeying the sense of life relates to the appreciation of the Person within our being. As we appreciate Him, and as He dwells in us, He is the source of our sense of life. We also have to obey Him, which is to cleave to Him. We also have to cleave to our spirit because it is here that we have the teaching of the anointing. The teaching of the anointing is the Spirit within us moving within us to enter into us and bring us into Himself. Without our response to the teaching of the anointing, no anointing occurs. There is a potential, and it is just there waiting. As we cleave, we enter into Him and He enters into us and we live in Him.

These two together result in our walking by the Spirit. So, when we heed Christ, who is our indwelling Bridegroom, and when we take care of the spirit in His anointing and walk by the Spirit, then in our daily living we walk with Him and are one entity, and He lives out of us, which is the righteousnesses of the saints as the bridal garment in Revelation 19:8.

#### 2. Walking according to the mingled spirit causes our flesh, self, and natural life to lose their position and function—[[Galatians-05#v16|Gal. 5:16]]; [[Matthew-16#v24|Matt. 16:24]]; [[1 Corinthians-02#v11|1 Cor. 2:11-15]].

Hallelujah!!! Those young believers had already dispossessed, put out, and fired their flesh, their old man, and their natural constitution by letting go of all the items and beginning to touch the atmosphere that the apostles gave to them in their entrance into the Lord in an intimate and affectionate way as the substance of their daily living.

#### 3. Walking according to the mingled spirit allows the processed and consummated Triune God—the Spirit—to gain the full ground in us so that we may be one with Him for His corporate expression—[[Ephesians-03#v16|Eph. 3:16-21]].

The full ground here refers to the full ground in our living. The word _allows_ here, as we’ve seen in the past, indicates that the omnipotent God holds back all of His power and His sway in His relationship with us. He who dictates, He who sees the nations fall and rise and does everything in your personal universe, kneels before you and asks if you would like to live with Him. You have the ability to say yes or no. As you say yes, spontaneously He becomes the holy life lived out in you. Wonderful.

If He doesn’t have a full expression in us, if He doesn’t have in our living a living out of Himself that is worthy of Himself and His calling of us, then it is because we disallowed Him. We will have similar phrases coming up.

#### 4. By walking according to the mingled spirit, we keep ourselves under the “shower” of the divine dispensing of the Divine Trinity; ultimately, the Bible requires only one thing of us—that we walk according to the mingled spirit—[[Ezekiel-34#v26|Ezek. 34:26]]; [[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]], 11.

Oh, ordinary days under the divine dispensing of the Divine Trinity, under which we are in a shower. We have this illustrated in these verses in Ezekiel and Romans. For us to be under the shower of the Divine Trinity or the divine dispensing of the Divine Trinity, this shower has an on-off, a faucet. Who turns on the faucet? Who determines whether there is a shower or not, whether there is water coming out or not? We do!!! If we would like to have an intimate and affectionate touch with Him as the situation provides, we touch Him, and He lives out of us. Our daily living is to live a life under the shower! Do you ever feel more refreshed than when you are in the shower? So refreshing. All our daily living should be under the shower! It depends upon our desire to have this kind of daily living. Don’t you think Paul was under this shower? Don’t you think the Thessalonians marveled and wondered, “What is this??? Tell us what this is?!?” when they saw it? They went under the shower and that fostered a kind of living where the Thessalonians also had this holy life or walk which was worthy of God, and they did have the glory of God. The new believers!! Isn’t that encouraging!!

#### 5. To walk according to the mingled spirit is to let the processed Triune God fill and saturate us until He permeates our whole being to be expressed through us in a corporate way as the Body of Christ, consummating in the New Jerusalem—[[Ephesians-03#v16|Eph. 3:16-21]]; [[Ephesians-04#v4|4:4-6]], 16; [[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27]]; [[Ephesians-02#v19|2:19]]; [[Ephesians-03#v4|3:4]], [[Ephesians-03#v10|10-11]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Ephesians-21#v10|10-11]].

So, here, in these last several points we see that this walk is with a view toward the consummation, toward the eternal consummation in Romans. The living, which is the garment of the corporate raptured bride, is her qualification for her being there, and her constituents afforded the Lord occasions to dispense Himself into their being in order to express Himself.

I hate to leave this section... Oh, saints. Our daily living. We think of our daily living a certain way. Most of it is utilitarian. Most of it is living to accomplish something or work out something for ourselves or others. We were trained that way and the world runs that way. But eventually, the changing of the age and the coming age and the new universe will not run that way and will not be that way. It will depend on one thing: that our living involves our great and appreciative and captivating desire for and cooperation with our Bridegroom as the constituent of His corporate bride. So, when we fail in something, or have a disappointing experience at our job, or are not kind to someone, or speak wrongly, or have any kind of failure, that affects us because our criteria and our sense about our living are related to those things. Those things don’t matter. If we would be the Thessalonians, we would realize that these things in a sense are very secondary. What should be before our eyes and consideration is, “I don’t want to have some frames in my video of my daily living blank, or miscolored, or empty.” In other words, we would have a daily living in which the exercise of our determination and will and emotion and desire and thoughts are all, “Lord, I don’t want to miss this occasion and opportunity to enlarge my experience of You in my living of You, which will enlarge Your own living as a testimony to others.” This is what should happen. This is what will turn the age, and this is what the Thessalonians are, and saints, now wherever we are we can lay hold of what the Thessalonians laid hold of.

## II. God’s desire in His up-to-date recovery is for us to have a walk worthy of His calling for the reality of the Body of Christ—[[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1-4]]:

Here we come to the second part of the title of the message. Here we have Eph. 4:1-4. As background, as you are familiar with the general structure of Ephesians, it is one of many of Paul’s epistles. In epistle after epistle of Paul, he talks about our daily living, our touch with Him, and our expression. The thing about the Thessalonians was that they were so young and so successful! This part in Ephesians talks about the position of blessings bestowed in chapter 1. Chapter 3 is the apostle’s prayers for this daily living to stay in this position. Chapters 4-6 are our living and responsibility to live this way. There are five items that are identifiable that constitute a living as revealed in that section, especially chapters 4 and 5, that is worthy of Him.

Please note how these are so helpful practically to live this kind of living the Thessalonians had.

### A. The first item of a walk worthy of God’s calling is for us to be diligent to keep the oneness of the Spirit as the reality of the Body of Christ, with the transformed human virtues strengthened by and with the divine attributes—[[Ephesians-04#v2|vv. 2-4]]:

In these verses we have a listing of these divine attributes we have as we keep the oneness of the Spirit.

#### 1. To walk worthily of God’s calling is to have a corporate living of pursuing and gaining Christ to the uttermost so that we can be conformed to His death by the power of His resurrection; this corporate, Christ-magnifying, God-man living, which is the reality of the Body of Christ, will close this age, the age of the church, and will bring Christ back to take, possess, and rule over this earth in the kingdom age—[[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19-21a]]; [[Ephesians-03#v10|3:10-14]]; [[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]; [[Revelation-19#v7|Rev. 19:7-9]]; [[Ephesians-20#v6|20:6]].

Please notice here Revelation 19:7-9. This is the walk worthy of God’s calling.

#### 2. In beseeching the saints to walk worthily of God’s calling, Paul spoke from his status as a prisoner of Christ Jesus and a prisoner in the Lord—[[Ephesians-03#v1|Eph. 3:1]]; [[Ephesians-04#v1|4:1]]:

##### a. Sooner or later, every steward of God, every minister of God’s riches, every faithful lover of Christ, will be imprisoned not only by Christ but also in Christ; the more we love Him, the more we will be in Him to such an extent that He will become our prison for us to enjoy Him to the uttermost so that we may have a walk that is worthy of God’s calling.

Steward is one who responds. I’ll say it this way: as believers we have the divine economy indwelling in us. We now have a stewardship related to that divine economy living in us. Do we take care of that economy, enjoy and cleave to it and are affectionate with that economy? If we are, we are a good steward. If we are not, we are not. Sooner or later, every minister of God’s riches and every faithful lover of Christ will be imprisoned not only by Christ, but also in Christ.

As you know, in Eph. 3:1 Paul refers to himself as a prisoner of Christ, and in 4:1 a prisoner in Christ. Our brother shared with us that our Christian life is an experience of gradually, in our loving, pursuing, and enjoying Him, passing from a status of being a prisoner of Him to being a prisoner in Him.

The more we love Him, the more we will be in Him. Please highlight this! Here, again, this points out that it isn’t enough just to love Him. To love Him involves contact which affords entry into Him. Our loving action is to contact this indwelling One, then we will find ourselves in Him, to such an extent that He will become our prison. Did you ever think prison would be a positive word to you? Our brother says that we love Him to the degree of being imprisoned by Him because we don’t ever want to go out. We are not only the prisoners, but we are also the jailers. We don’t want to go out because we love our prison!!! This prison is for us to enjoy Him to the uttermost.

##### b. The more freedom we have, the more blind we are, but if Christ is our prison, our eyes will be opened to see the heavenly vision, and we will receive the highest revelation of God’s economy—[[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]].

Are you free in your daily living? You may see a lot of ugly things, but you will be blind to the beautiful and spiritual things. If we have freedom in our daily living, we would not see much. But if we are a prisoner of Christ, we would receive a vision of the divine economy. This is very much reinforcing. The Thessalonians didn’t want to do anything but enjoy what they were enjoying. In their early church life they found themselves in prison! In another place Paul called them being in a box of sanctification. Everything that happened in their daily living sanctified them! Being in that box and their enjoyment sanctified them and brought them into full sanctification as in chapter 5, verse 3.

Related to the virtues, we have these in point 3.

#### 3. In the Spirit of the glorified Jesus, there is the transformed humanity of Jesus; to drink of and flow out the one Spirit for the one Body is to drink of and flow out the Spirit of the man Jesus, to drink of and flow out the humanity of Jesus with His divinely enriched human virtues of lowliness, meekness, and long-suffering, bearing one another in love—[[John-07#v37|John 7:37-39a]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13]]; [[Acts-16#v7|Acts 16:7]]; [[Ephesians-04#v2|Eph. 4:2-3]].

So, in realizing that, when we call on the name of the Lord and enjoy a refreshing drink of Him, that drink of Him is the Spirit that is mentioned in John 7:39. But the One who cried out in John 7:37 was the man Jesus, the virtuous One who wanted all of those around Him to come and drink, but He had lived out the prototypical life, the life He now wants to live out of you. He lived it in all of His human virtues. He, after crying out these words, went to the cross, died, and in resurrection became the Spirit, and now says, “He who believes into Me, after His death and resurrection, out of his being shall flow rivers of living water,” which is the Spirit who was not yet but now is. This man Jesus is indwelling us as the Spirit of Jesus, and as we contact Him we spontaneously live out and have these virtues. Which virtues? Lowliness, meekness, longsuffering, bearing one another in love, being diligent to keep the oneness of the Spirit. When we read Eph. 4:3 we quickly say, “I gotta be meek, lowly, forbearing... these are the virtues needed for the oneness of the Spirit.” No!!! They are the ones needed, but we aren’t the one who lives them out! We just drink the river of living water, which spontaneously keeps us in the reality of the Body of Christ.

In this item, keeping the oneness especially by being imprisoned by Him, being imprisoned in an enclosure or a space that confines you and a space in which you live, does this not indicate that we need to place ourselves in this daily living, in this box of sanctification in Christ? And as we are in Him, we look around, and everything we see is Christ. When you are in Christ, what you see is Christ. Because you are in Him, you look through Him and see the matters in the practical and physical realm that you are living in, and you carry out your responsibility to do things, but because you are in Him and contacting Him and are one with Him, when you look out, you are living in two realms at once. This is to live in a box, imprisoned in Him, but at the same time transcending that prison to bless people outside of that prison with your rich experiences.

So, please note, I would like to suggest that this is the key to 1 Thes. 2:12. A big key to see that these points in Eph. chapters 4 and 5 tell us how to walk worthily. We place ourselves in Him as our prison in our daily living, and as we do that, that walk is worthy of God.

### B. The second item of a walk worthy of God’s calling is for us to grow up into Christ the Head in all things—[[Ephesians-04#v15|vv. 15-16]]:

Don’t you love Eph. 4:15? Aren’t you mystified by Eph. 4:15? How do you hold the Head in all things, who is Christ, and grow up in Him in all things? This is our daily living. So, to grow up into Him implies again that we are in Him. More and more of our being is brought into Him, and this of course implies the same affinity of affectionate and intimate touch. It implies a burden, a redefinition of our thought concerning our daily living, that our daily living isn’t to accomplish what we want, it is holding the Head. It is not, “Did I do this?” It is, “Did I hold the Head in this thing?” Eventually, as we hold the Head we grow up into all things. Eventually this is our daily living. Can we redefine our daily living so that the marker for our performance is whether we held the Head or not?

#### 1. In order to grow up into Christ in all things for the building up of His Body, we need to enjoy Christ as our all-inclusive, universal replacement for the producing of the one new man, so we must “hear Him” and see “Jesus only”—[[Ephesians-04#v15|vv. 15-16]]; [[Mark-09#v7|Mark 9:7-8]]:

I think this is where we have the subpoints regarding Eliakim and Shebna. So, as an application of growing up into Him in all things, we only see Him. Verse references are here. Who do we see? In our daily living, in all kinds of persons and events and circumstances, the Thessalonians saw Jesus only and firstly, because they are in Him, they look through Him, everything is Him, and through Him they see the practical matters and take care of them, but in this process they are holding the Head and growing into the Head more and more until they are fully in Him. He is their prison. This requires us in our God-created and fallen being to be terminated. When our God-created and fallen being decides not to live in the self anymore, that puts our fallen being on the cross. On the cross, He resurrects our God-created being, and then that being joins with Him in our mingled spirit, and He comes into our inner being with our mingled spirit to live out of us as a new being. The old owner, the fallen man, is fired.

Shebna here in this picture, of course, in Isaiah 22 represents our old man.

##### a. God fired Shebna, a steward in the house of the king ([[Isaiah-22#v15|Isa. 22:15-19]]), and replaced him with Eliakim, a type of Christ ([[Ephesians-22#v20|vv. 20-24]]; [[Revelation-03#v7|Rev. 3:7]]); God also fired all the vessels within His house to replace them with Christ ([[Isaiah-22#v25|Isa. 22:25]]).

This is exactly what happens when we turn to the Lord and enjoy Him. All of us are to be replaced with Christ.

##### b. Whatever or whoever is not Christ, God “fires”; God has replaced everything in His Old Testament economy with Christ—[[Mark-01#v1|Mark 1:1-8]]; [[Matthew-17#v3|Matt. 17:3-5]]; [[Colossians-02#v16|Col. 2:16-17]]; [[Hebrews-10#v5|Heb. 10:5-10]]; [[Ephesians-11#v5|11:5-6]]; cf. [[Isaiah-22#v20|Isa. 22:20-25]].

##### c. When God created us, He “hired” us; when He put us on the cross, crucifying us with Christ, He “fired” us; when He resurrected us together with Him, He “re-hired” us by making us a new species of God-men, a new invention of God as His corporate masterpiece, bringing us back to His original intention of creating us for His glory, His corporate expression—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[1 Corinthians-11#v7|1 Cor. 11:7a]]; [[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]; [[Ephesians-02#v6|Eph. 2:6]], 10, [[Ephesians-02#v15|15]]; [[Isaiah-43#v7|Isa. 43:7]].

Wonderful. He hired us.

##### d. The real church life is a life in which all the saints are fired and replaced with Christ, making Christ everything in the church as the reality of the one new man for the glory of the Triune God—[[Colossians-03#v10|Col. 3:10-11]]; [[1 Corinthians-10#v31|1 Cor. 10:31]].

All the saints are fired and replaced with Christ!!

So, you know, this wonderful definition of the church: the church in Diamond Bar is Christ living out of us. The church in Lubbock is Christ living in Lubbock. So, if any of the saints are living in the fired old man, they are not the church, but if they are living in the resurrected One who is Christ, then they are living in the church as the corporate expression of Him in a living and worthy way.

So, with this as a viewing point, we can see that our former existence in our former person that saw things as intrinsically integral and separate is gone. We have a new being, and that being doesn’t see things that way. It only sees Christ. When we watch the video, it’s just Christ. Christ the Lamb, Christ the Door... we see Jesus only. When we see Him, we are brought into contact with Him, which causes us to hear Him. This carried out in our daily living is a living that is worthy of God.

#### 2. In the New Testament, Christ’s replacing us is altogether a matter of a grafted life; we are united with Christ, and in this union Christ replaces us; replacing demands union, whereas exchanging annuls our union with Christ ([[John-15#v4|John 15:4-5]]); because Christ joined Himself to us, uniting Himself with us, when He died on the cross, we died with Him and were terminated ([[Romans-11#v17|Rom. 11:17]], 24; [[Ephesians-06#v6|6:6]]).

This is so good. So good. So, when you hear you are replaced, you might think that you were wrapped in a ball and thrown to a distant land... but no. You were not merely replaced, you were grafted! You weren’t exchanged for. You are still here! You were grafted in such a way that the grafting itself takes all the inferior aspects of your life and discharges them and fills you with the aspects of the host grafted tree, and now you are that grafted tree and your living is that grafted tree’s living.

#### 3. Now in our organic union with Christ through our faith in Him, He replaces us by living in us, with us, by us, and through us; we live, yet not we, but Christ lives in us, and we live by the faith of the Son of God; this indicates an organic union with Christ—[[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]; [[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19-21a]].

This is the work of faith that the Thessalonians had—they were grafted into Him. They were replaced in the sense of living a grafted life.

As we know, Galatians 2:20 here refers to a new “I” that is living and an old “I” that is crucified. The old “I” lives until this old “I” sees a view of Christ and is able to see Him, and sees that He died for that old “I.” Then that old “I” opens, then Christ comes in and applies His crucifixion to that old “I,” then He resurrects the God-created part of that old “I,” and now I live, and this new “I” lives in an intimate and affectionate touch with Christ, and the two live together in a grafted life and not an exchanged life.

### C. The third item of a walk worthy of God’s calling is for us to learn Christ as the reality is in Jesus, which is the actual condition of the life of Jesus as recorded in the four Gospels—[[Ephesians-04#v20|Eph. 4:20-24]]:

If you look at the footnotes on Eph. 4:20 and 24, you see that the reality of Jesus was learned in us in our baptism. That means in our baptism we learned how to live. When we live according to the principle of our baptism, we become clothed with Him, and this clothing becomes the living that is worthy of Him and our bridal garment.

#### 1. [[John-06#v57|John 6:57]] reveals how the reality that is in Jesus, the God-man living of Jesus, can become the reality of the Body of Christ, the corporate God-man living of the new man as the duplication of the God-man living of Jesus; the purpose of God in sending the Lord Jesus to be a man was for Him to live a God-man life by the divine life; this kind of living issues in a universal great man that is exactly the same as He is—a man living a God-man life by the divine life.

Not by Him, not for Him, but because of Him. He is the factor by which we live. As we live this way, we duplicate and give release to the indwelling One who lives within us, we join in that living outwardly, and participate in the holy life in the church life that supplies the other members.

#### 2. [[John-06#v57|John 6:57]] says, “As the living Father has sent Me and I live because of the Father, so he who eats Me, he also shall live because of Me”—this is the reality of the Body of Christ, the corporate God-man living of the many members of the Body of Christ, who are learning Christ as the reality is in Jesus.

#### 3. We do not live _by_ Christ, taking Christ as our instrument; we live _because of_ Christ, taking Christ as the supplying factor of our living; to live because of Christ as our food, we must eat Him so that He can be the supplying and energizing factor to live in us and through us for the building up of His Body as the perfect will of God—[[Ephesians-06#v63|v. 63]]; [[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]; [[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]]; [[Ephesians-12#v1|12:1-2]].

### D. The fourth item of a walk worthy of God’s calling is for us to live in love and light—[[Ephesians-05#v2|Eph. 5:2]], 8:

#### 1. We need to be partakers, enjoyers, of the divine nature ([[2 Peter-01#v4|2 Pet. 1:4]]); the divine nature is what God is—God is Spirit ([[John-04#v24|John 4:24]]), God is love ([[1 John-04#v8|1 John 4:8]], 16), and God is light ([[Ephesians-01#v5|1:5]]); Spirit is the nature of God’s person, love is the nature of God’s essence, and light is the nature of God’s expression.

#### 2. We all need to spend an adequate amount of personal time with the Lord to privately fellowship with Him in our spirit so that we can be filled with His loving essence for Him to shepherd others through us and can be filled with His shining element for others to see Him in us—[[John-04#v24|John 4:24]]; [[Luke-15#v20|Luke 15:20]]; [[Matthew-05#v15|Matt. 5:15-16]].

### E. The fifth item of a walk worthy of God’s calling is for us to live by being filled in spirit to overflow with Christ—[[Ephesians-05#v18|Eph. 5:18]]:

#### 1. Speaking, singing, psalming, giving thanks to God, and subjecting ourselves to one another in the fear of Christ are not only the outflow of being filled in spirit but also the way to be filled in spirit—[[Ephesians-05#v19|vv. 19-21]].

So, doing these things establishes a cycle. We say, “Oh Lord,” and sing to Him. Through that we are filled. Because we are filled, we seek Him more, we contact Him more, and He fills us more. This establishes a cycle.

#### 2. To be filled in spirit is to be filled with the riches of Christ to become the fullness of Christ, the overflow of Christ; by calling on the Lord and pray-reading His Word, we can continually receive Him as grace upon grace to become His fullness, His overflow—[[Ephesians-03#v8|3:8]]; [[Ephesians-01#v23|1:23]]; [[Ephesians-03#v19|3:19b]]; [[Romans-10#v12|Rom. 10:12-13]]; [[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17-18]]; [[John-01#v16|John 1:16]].

So, He is the fullness within us for us to experience. When we contact Him in an affectionate and intimate, desirous touch, we partake of His fullness and have the benefit of all of His fullness. His fullness rushes into us and reconstitutes us with His fullness. Eventually we become His fullness coalesced with Him as the oceanic fullness in the universe.

#### 3. We can live a life of being filled in spirit by praying at every time in spirit so that we may become Christ’s bride for His satisfaction and become His warrior for the defeat of His enemy—[[Ephesians-05#v18|Eph. 5:18]], [[Ephesians-05#v25|25-27]]; [[Ephesians-06#v10|6:10]], [[Ephesians-06#v17|17-18]].

Here we conclude. This fills us in spirit with a view that we want to be His satisfaction. We want to be His bride. We will take care of His enemy, no problem, and today, to beat His enemy is to be His bride. This is to walk intimately and worthily of God.
