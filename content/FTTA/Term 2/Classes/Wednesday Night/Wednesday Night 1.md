## I. The New Testament, like the Bible as a whole, is fully composed of and structured with the Divine Trinity—[[Matthew-28#v19|Matt. 28:19]]; [[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4-5]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-2]]:

### A. The entire New Testament is related to the Triune God; the Triune God is the element for the construction of the New Testament—[[Ephesians-03#v16|Eph. 3:16-19]]; [[Ephesians-04#v4|4:4-6]].

The entire New Testament is related to the Triune God. The Triune God is the element for the construction of the New Testament. The Bible presents us with a picture of the move of the Divine Trinity for the accomplishment of His economy. The Bible was written according to the governing principle of the Triune God.

### B. The Bible presents us a picture of the move of the Divine Trinity for the accomplishment of His economy—[[Luke-15#v3|Luke 15:3-32]]; [[Ephesians-02#v18|Eph. 2:18]].

### C. The Bible was written according to the governing principle of the Triune God wrought into His chosen and redeemed people as their enjoyment, their drink, and their fountain of life and light—[[Psalm-36#v8|Psa. 36:8-9]].

The governing principle is Christ with His governmental authority. We are in two realms at the same time. Most of the saints are not fully aware of the second one, and the rest of us would like to be in that realm all the time. There is the realm of grace, experience, and enjoyment, and there is the realm of God’s government. That is why our brother wrote on Christ serving in His government.

### D. The revelation concerning the Triune God in the Word of God is for the dispensing of God in His Divine Trinity into His chosen and redeemed people for their experience and enjoyment so that they might become His corporate expression for eternity—[[Ephesians-01#v3|Eph. 1:3-23]]; [[Ephesians-04#v16|4:16]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v10|10-11]].

Here I want to pause for a moment. Chosen and redeemed people—for their experience and enjoyment.

We have to become crystal clear about this. People talk about joy, joy, joy—“Let’s have joy; don’t be sorrowful.” But enjoyment requires experience. Joy is not the source. First, there must be the experience. Then, based upon the experience, we have the joy. The experience is in our spirit. Much of the enjoyment is with our soul.

Our brother Peter, in his loving concern, in writing his first Epistle, said in effect, “Christ is indwelling you, and He wants you to have joy, but He knows your situation and He knows your soul.” So He is in us, shepherding us. He knows our thoughts, our feelings, and our will. He is not criticizing. He knows that maybe we are exercising our spirit and we are experiencing Him, but we are not enjoying what we are experiencing. And the Lord is a God of joy. He is the source of joy. He is actually joy itself.

His loving concern shepherds us inwardly. He knows what kind of feeling you are having and what you are going through, and He says, in effect, “I’m going to shepherd you through this.” And mature saints can testify to this. They may be really old guys like me—I may not remember something that took place one second or one minute ago—but I can name many things that happened fifty years ago. The Lord knows what I need to remember, and He supplies it inwardly. So our experience and our enjoyment mean a great deal to the Triune God.

He created you that you would experience Him, enjoy Him, and express Him.

## III. The Epistle of 1 Thessalonians is addressed to “the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ”—[[1 Thessalonians-01#v1|v. 1]]:

The church is in God the Father. The church is in the Lord Jesus Christ. On the one hand, the church of the Thessalonians was of the Thessalonians. On the other hand, this church was in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

I just inwardly hope that every co-worker and every elder, every leading brother, would have this kind of view and this kind of feeling: the church that I am in, the church where I serve, is in God the Father and in the Lord Jesus Christ. When we come to the Lord’s Table, inwardly, more and more we should realize that we are coming to the highest aspect of the church life. I am here to bring the Christ whom I have been enjoying, but here in the meeting, the Triune God is present. If the Father is in the meeting, the Lord Jesus is in the meeting.

#### 1. Such a church is born of God the Father with His life and nature and is organically united with the Lord Jesus Christ in all that He is and has done—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v17|6:17]].

This year, new local churches are going to be born. The source will be the Father. While I am sitting here, inwardly there is the prayer, “Lord, establish new churches throughout Europe. Do it especially in the biggest places on the earth. Save thousands of people. Establish new churches.” This is going to happen before the end of this age.

With His life and nature, and organically united with the Lord Jesus Christ in all that He is and has done—this is amazing. United with the Lord Jesus in all that He is. So, we are in Christ. Do not try to understand the whole thing merely in an outward way. The Lord wants you to experience Him in very particular ways. He knows where you are, He knows what you need, and His desire is that you would experience more and enjoy more.

So, Christ in all that He is and has done.

We need to see that the church is composed of human beings who are in God the Father and in the Lord Jesus Christ, those who have the life of God and who are in organic union with Christ.

Let us examine this a little bit. We need to see that the church is composed of human beings. You will find that developed local churches have all kinds of people and dispositions. As the Lord develops within you, this will become more and more real. The enemy will be put to shame, and you can thank, praise, and worship the Lord that Christ is making His home in our hearts. While I am reading this with you and speaking to you, I am thinking with you: the church is in the Triune God. Inwardly there is a kind of request: “Lord, make this real to me. I am here in Anaheim. It is not just a large number of believers, or a smaller number; we are in the Triune God, and the Triune God is here. Gradually, this is going to become more real to you. You will realize that you are not alone.

#### 1. The expressions God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ both imply the Spirit; therefore, in 1 Thessalonians 1:1 the Spirit is implied and understood, and we may speak of the church being in the Triune God.

#### 2. Because the three of the Divine Trinity are inseparable, whenever we have the first, the Father, we also have the second, the Son, and the third, the Spirit—[[Matthew-12#v28|Matt. 12:28]]; [[Romans-08#v11|Rom. 8:11]]; [[Galatians-04#v4|Gal. 4:4-6]].

The three of the Divine Trinity are inseparable. In the Godhead They are distinct, but They are inseparable. Whenever we have the first, the Father, we also have the second, the Son, and the third, the Spirit. If we are having fellowship with the Father, at the same time the Son is there and the Spirit is there. And if now I say, “Wait a minute, I am having fellowship with the Son,” then the Father and the Spirit are also there. And the Spirit will indicate, “I am here with the Father and the Son.” The Divine Trinity is in you right here and right now. The Lord is going to make this known to you.

A side comment: I hope some of you, when this touches you, would write some new hymns or new songs. I just wonder what would happen if, this year, saints everywhere wrote hundreds of hymns and songs on this level. What would the effect be?

That is why we have additional books. Then we began to sing it, and that continued.

I know I may get into a little trouble now—if you want to know what hymn it is, ask my wife. I think she would be glad to sing it with you.

Okay... now I’m back to earth.

In Their eternal coexistence, the three of the Godhead are distinct, but Their eternal coinherence makes Them one.

What we are doing in this part of the outline is that, maybe for the first time for some of us, we are entering into the very being of the Triune God. You are not too young or too old to do this. The Lord wants to take us deeper. If you want to stand against the culture of this country, which is shallow—really shallow—you have to realize that human beings were not created to be shallow. As we enjoy the Lord and love Him, we can say more and more, “Lord, thank You for this new day. I need Your mercy and grace. I open my being to You for Your dispensing. Lord, go deeper into my being. I want to give You access to every aspect of my being for Your desire and for Your purpose.” Do not examine anything here, and do not be afraid. I will tell you something very simple: let God be God within you.

##### a. We cannot separate the Son from the Father, or the Father and the Son from the Spirit, because all three coexist and coinhere—[[John-14#v10|John 14:10-11]].

In the divine economy, the three of the Divine Trinity work and are manifested respectively in three consecutive stages. Okay now—stages. The Father is the One who plans, originates, and initiates. For the church to be in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ means that the church is in the processed Triune God. This is a very important statement: the processed Triune God.

When we come down a few more points, I will give some explanation, but we will gradually come to it. So I will repeat this again for our memory: for the church to be in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ means that the church is in the processed Triune God. We are not only in the Triune God, but in the processed Triune God. Not merely the Triune God in the Godhead, but the Triune God who came in Christ and began to function in the economy, becoming the consummated Triune God for our experience.

#### 1. According to the Bible, there is no such thing as the church being merely in God; rather, the church is in the processed Triune God—[[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]].

This means that even though the Triune God is in the church and the church is in the Triune God, the Triune God has passed through a process related to so many things for our sake and for our experience.

In Genesis 1, God was the unprocessed God. In the New Testament, He has become the processed Triune God. So when He became a human being, entered into human living, and passed through death, resurrection, and ascension, He passed through a profound process.

#### 2. In Genesis 1 God was the unprocessed God, but in the New Testament He has become the processed Triune God—[[John-07#v37|John 7:37-39]]; [[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19]].

#### 3. Processed refers to the crucial steps through which the Triune God has passed in the divine economy: incarnation, human living, crucifixion, and resurrection:

Now let us examine this for a couple of minutes. These processes refer to the crucial steps through which the Triune God has passed in the divine economy: incarnation, human living, crucifixion, and resurrection. So now, with us and in us, the all-inclusive indwelling Christ wants us to have a deep understanding of incarnation. What took place? Why do we need to read the first four books of the New Testament? His incarnation—what took place? Before anything was created, before the universe was created, the eternal plans of the Triune God were made among Themselves. At a certain point in time and space, the Son would become a God-man on the earth.

##### a. In crucifixion the Lord accomplished redemption, the termination of the old creation, and the destruction of Satan—[[Ephesians-01#v7|Eph. 1:7]]; [[Romans-06#v6|Rom. 6:6]]; [[Hebrews-02#v14|Heb. 2:14]].

##### b. In resurrection He germinated the new creation—[[2 Corinthians-05#v17|2 Cor. 5:17]].

##### c. Now He is the life-giving Spirit as the ultimate consummation of the processed Triune God—[[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v17|2 Cor. 3:17a]].

#### 4. The church in the processed Triune God is the church in the One who has become the life-giving Spirit with the Father and the Son—[[John-14#v20|John 14:20]]:

##### a. The processed Triune God reaches us, contacts us, and is applied to us in our experience as the life-giving Spirit—[[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]].

##### b. The Father is in the Son, and the Son is now the life-giving Spirit dwelling in us—[[John-14#v10|John 14:10-11]], [[John-14#v16|16-17]], [[John-14#v20|20]].

##### c. When we are in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, we are in the Spirit; thus, we are the church in the processed Triune God.

### D. If we see the vision of the church in the Triune God, this vision will control our thinking, our activities, and our entire life—[[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]].
