# The Lord Directing Our Hearts into the Love of God and into the Endurance of Christ

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Thessalonians-01#v3|1 Thes. 1:3]]; [[2 Thessalonians-01#v4|2 Thes. 1:4]]; [[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|3:5]]

## Introduction

We begin with the Lord directing our hearts into love. And based upon that, we will come to understand and participate in endurance of Christ. We will become crystal clear when we are done. When we are at the end, we will know where we will need to be at the time the Lord comes to rapture the saints.

## I. “The love of God has been poured out in our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us”—[[Romans-05#v5|Rom. 5:5]]

### A. God has poured out His love in our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us as the motivating power within us so that we may more than conquer in all our tribulations—[[Romans-05#v5|v. 5]]; [[Romans-08#v37|8:37-39]].

### B. The love of God is God Himself—[[1 John-04#v8|1 John 4:8]], [[1 John-04#v16|16]].

This is a precious point.

### C. God as love is the divine essence that has been poured out in our hearts—[[Romans-05#v5|Rom. 5:5]].

#### 1. The pouring out of the love of God into our hearts is a matter of the essence of God.

The essence of God is *in* us. The pouring out of the love of God into our hearts is a matter of the essence of God, that is the foundation. 

#### 2. Because we have been regenerated, we have love as the nature of God’s essence within us.

This third point is beyond words.

#### 3. As believers, ==deep in our hearts we have something of the divine essence, and this is God the Father in His love.==

The words *deep in our hearst*, in our personal relationship wtih Him, more and more we will open our being, until we get to the depths of our being. Then, the Lord as love will come to the very depths of our being. No matter what may be take place outside, what we are going through... in our *being* we are becoming the same as *God*. God is love, and we are bceoming the same as GOd concerning love... not in the Godhead, but love is not just some feeling—it's God's very being dwelling in our heart.

### D. Because the love of God has been poured out in our hearts, the heart of a believer in Christ is a heart of love—[[Ephesians-03#v17|Eph. 3:17]].

Just let the Lord shine in your to realize, that every one us, we have Christ in our heart of love. He is dwelling in us. This is the very center of our personal relationship with HIm... every day, love beyond words.

### E. In our experience and enjoyment of God as the Father in His love, we experience and enjoy the dispensing of love as the nature of God’s essence into our hearts—[[Romans-05#v5|Rom. 5:5]], [[Romans-05#v8|8]]; [[Romans-08#v35|8:35]], [[Romans-08#v39|39]]; [[Romans-15#v30|15:30]]; [[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]].

Let's examine this for a moment. In our experience and enjoyment... it shouldn't be the other way around—we shouldn;'t begin with enjoyment, we begin with experience. That is what brings forth the enjoyment of God as the Father as it's nature. God the Father is wating for all of HIs children in 1 John 1:3, where John said... we have fellowship with the Father. This is new for many of us, but we have to begin with nothing, and then we gradually learned through experience. So, I would would that after the Lord's tale meeting - after we've done more than just sing hymns, but we praised and thanked the Lord for what He has done in our daily living with you. Then, after we take the bread and the food and the wine... we eat and drink - then the indwelling Christ takes the lead to worship the Father in our being. So, we begin with just where we are... so we sing a hymn in that portion of the hymnbook - suitable for God the Father—but the time will come, when in your personal relationship, you will have marvelous fellowship with God the Father because He is the source of every postivie thing. He is the one with the perfect will.

So, we experience and enjoy the *dispensing* of love. This means that it's taking place nonstop - he is dispensing love right here and right now. Love as the nature of God's essence—just conider this. Love is the *nature* of God's *essence*. The Lord is leading us not only to know about God the Father: GOd is the essence into our hearts.

In this second section of the outline, this thought is beign developed in a greater dgree.

## II. “The Lord direct your hearts into the love of God”—[[2 Thessalonians-03#v5|2 Thes. 3:5a]]

He is not requireing... He is *directing* our hearts. I hope that each one of you  are open regarding your heart toward the Triune God. That you would hold nothing back; He wants to make HIs home—you know that verse—in our heart... He doesn't just want to visit us, He wants to live in us, in our heart, in every part of our heart. God, who is Love, is making His home in our heart.

The verses in point A are very deep and precious.

### A. “I am persuaded that neither death nor life nor angels nor principalities nor things present nor things to come nor powers nor height nor depth nor any other creature will be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord”—[[Romans-08#v38|Rom. 8:38-39]].

NOTHING is going to separate us from the loving God. No matter what our situation is, what is taking place on the earth even.

#### 1. The love of God is the source of His eternal salvation.

This is why He sent His Son to go die on the cross; because He loves each one of you.

#### 2. In God’s salvation this love to us has become the love of Christ ([[Romans-08#v35|v. 35]]), which does many marvelous things for us through the grace of Christ until God’s complete salvation is accomplished in us.

So, He's saving us day after day... He's not going to stop until everything is done perfectly and thoroughly.

#### 3. These marvelous things provoke God’s enemy to attack us with all kinds of sufferings and calamities.

So, we should be aware of this, but don't be frightened—he's the one who should be frightened... we are going to be part of the holy temple, the New Jersualem—he is going to be in the lake of fire.

#### 4. However, because of our response to the love of God in Christ, these attacks have become benefits to us—[[Romans-08#v28|v. 28]].

In otherwords, the more the enemy attacks us, the more the love of God saturates our heart. At the same time, he knows that the love in our heart indicates that the enemy has been defeated. This is why the attacks have a benefit. They attack—now we experience God's love more. When they attack - we are not fighting back... Christ is the fighter who has already won the vicotyr, so we join His fighting by loving HIm and prasing HIm. There is one verse in the Old Testament that is very delightly - the whole country is going into battle against the evil things for God's people - how did they enter into the warfare? It was by praising, by loving.  If we realize that Christ has already been victorious, but He just wants us to apply it- He is training us to do that very thing.

#### 5. “In all these things we more than conquer through Him who loved us”—[[Romans-08#v37|v. 37]].

Another very precious verse. Lets have sisters read this, then brothers read this, then everyone all together. We are more than conquering!

#### 2. This refers not merely to a kind of patience but to the ability to endure sufferings in tribulation, whether pressure, oppression, persecution, poverty, or any kind of trial.

This is just part of our life. Don't be afraid of it... because just realize this: the Lord knows what will take place every day. I can testify - when you are living by grace, which is God in Christ as the Spriit, he overcomes any situation that we are in. We overcome it by His grace.

#### 1. The word of the Lord’s endurance is the word of the Lord’s suffering.

##### a. The Lord today is still bearing rejection and persecution by His endurance.

#### 2. Trial in [[Revelation-03#v10|Revelation 3:10]] undoubtedly denotes the great tribulation ([[Matthew-24#v21|Matt. 24:21]]), which is about to come on the whole inhabited earth, as indicated by the fifth trumpet, the sixth trumpet, and the seven bowls of the seventh trumpet ([[Revelation-08#v13|Rev. 8:13—9:21]]; [[Revelation-11#v14|11:14-15]]; [[Revelation-15#v1|15:1]]; [[Revelation-16#v1|16:1-21]]).

##### b. The Lord promised the recovered church that He would keep her out of the hour of trial (not only out of the trial, but out of the hour of trial) because she has kept the word of the Lord’s endurance.

##### c. This promise of the Lord, like His promise in [[Luke-21#v36|Luke 21:36]], indicates that the saints who keep the word of the Lord’s endurance will be raptured before the great trial, implying that those who do not keep the word of the Lord’s endurance will be left in the trial.
