Regarding memorization, specifcially, it's best if you not only use your brain memory (this is just based off of experience) - its best if you memorize to the point where the memorization gets into your muscles. It's muscle memory that you want to trigger. 

Let me ask you - how many of you have grown up in primary school in the US, grew up reciting the pledge of allegence. I would suggest - every verse, one simple way of memorizing is to take a verse and just say it as rapidly as possible. First you say it like normal, but then you say it and you emphasize one phrase.
