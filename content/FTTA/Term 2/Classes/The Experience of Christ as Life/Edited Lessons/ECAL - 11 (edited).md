Simply by denying the soul, the soulish man, the self. That is why it is so important to be able to distinguish between the two.

# Outline—The Spirit with the Faculties of the Soul

## **Scripture Reading:**

[[Matthew-16#v24|Matt. 16:24-26]]; [[Luke-09#v23|Luke 9:23-25]]; [[1 Corinthians-02#v11|1 Cor. 2:11]]; [[2 Corinthians-07#v13|2 Cor. 7:13b]]; [[Acts-19#v21|Acts 19:21]]

## I. The spirit and the soul:

1 Corinthians 2 is where we get this matter of the soulish man and the spiritual man. “A soulish man does not receive the things of God.” They are discerned spiritually. So, a soul simply cannot understand the things of God. Just like it is not controversial to say that ears cannot smell roses, eyes cannot hear music, and our nose, no matter how much it tries or trains, simply cannot see colors. Why? Because they are not meant to apprehend those things. In the same way, our soul simply cannot understand God or the things of God. Only our spirit discerns and knows the things of God. So these two parts, soul and spirit, are just different. The soul can understand things in the psychological realm but cannot know the things in the spiritual realm.

### A. The spirit of man is the organ to contact and receive God.

I will pick on another brother: Paul Hudson.

Have you been to an SST? How many? Possibly six?

Tell me the three verses in the Bible that contain both the capital-S Spirit and the little-s spirit. Tell me.

[[Romans-08#v16|Romans 8:16]]. [[John-03#v6|John 3:6]]. [[John-04#v24|John 4:24]].

Actually, to know these three verses is not a small thing, because right away you have the Spirit and spirit, and the two spirits. That spirit that is in us is the organ with which we contact the Spirit and by which we can receive God.

### B. The soul is just the self—[[Luke-09#v23|Luke 9:23-25]], cf. [[Matthew-16#v26|Matt. 16:26]].

When you compare the two passages given to us here and put them side by side, you will see that the Word of God equates the soul with the self. You have the self, and you have the soul. The distinction between these is that the soul can refer to the created part of our being—man became a living soul in Genesis—but this self is not just that created soul; it is the soul corrupted by the fall. The soul is the self.

What the Lord told us with respect to the self is that it is something that is not to be improved, rehabilitated, corrected, adjusted, retrained, or made better. The only thing the Lord tells us about this self is that it has to be denied. That is the only thing we can do in relation to the self. To deny the self requires practice.

## II. Practicing to deny the self:

This self that needs to be denied does not get denied overnight. In order to be good at something, you have to do it with sufficient repetition. A famous book talks about needing ten thousand hours to become an expert at something, no matter what it is. Well, I calculated it with waking hours. Do you know how many years ten thousand waking hours is? It’s two years. Do you see the significance? In two years, you can become an expert at denying yourself. You just need the right environment, right? Being placed in an environment where you have to deny yourself, and there is a supply to practice.

“How many times have you denied yourself today?” Brothers and sisters, become an expert at denying yourself!

### A. To deny yourself is to deny your own thought, your opinion, your idea, your concept, your love, etc.

To deny the soul is simply to deny the three parts of our soul: mind, emotion, and will. Once you do that, there is nothing left but the spirit. The self is gone. How does this look? What is the manifestation? Well, you don’t just become “mindless,” “emotionless,” “spineless,” and you don’t become a robot. The key here, if you hadn’t caught it, is that you deny your mind, your emotion, your will. The operative word there is not mind, emotion, and will; the operative word is “your.” What do we do? We take up Christ’s mind, Christ’s emotion, and Christ’s will.

We were created by God, according to [[1 Corinthians-02#v11|1 Corinthians 2:11]], with a spirit. The original intention was that this spirit would be man’s person. Due to the fall, that spirit was deadened and rendered basically functionless, and the only thing left to be man’s person was the soul. So today, fallen man’s person, our person, is in our soul. For it to be our person means that it is the most prominent part of our being.

When you contact someone and get an impression, it is usually what stands out about them. If they are bold, I’m sure they are not bold all the time, but when you consider them, you envision boldness, because it is the most prominent part of their being, and it becomes their defining person. The first thing the wall contacts is the most prominent part of our being. It is the first thing you touch, or in this case, it is the first thing that comes to mind when you contact someone.

Many times we think, “This person is so cerebral,” or “This person is so passionate,” or, like me, “so stubborn.” What is that? That is the mind, emotion, and will being the most prominent part of our being.

So, a little testimony:

“I remember coming, I think it was my fourth term in the training, and I had some time with Minoru, my trainer, sitting on his couch. I asked him, ‘After two years in the training, what do you think I need the most work on?’”

You know, we brothers just get so obsessed with all our progression. Obsession is the right word, if you have read Watchman Nee’s definition of obsession in the ministry. We get obsessed with our growth, progress, and development. Are we matching up? So I asked him, and he said, “Don’t ask me that question. You need to be asking the Lord. This is what you are going to have to face in the coming years: you are just too strong.”

Part of me was like, “Yes! I am strong!” But I realized it was said in such a tone: “Oh, this is not a positive strong.” What do you mean by that? “You’re just so strong. You come across as a strong person.”

Anyway, I began to bring this to the Lord for years and years. It’s true. Sometimes I am overbearing in my person, or forceful, or stubborn—just strong. It has a smell of strong. You encounter this. So this is my cross. This is what I need to deny. I need to learn not to be so strong.

But for us, whatever the most prominent part of our being is, it may be our mind, and this needs to be denied. You are just setting your mind on the flesh when you do this. You need to have the spiritual mind in order to understand the things of God. Whatever it is, may the Lord shine His light on that which is the most prominent part of our being, so that we would experience dealing.

### B. If we deny our natural thought, the spirit within us would rise up.

If we deny ourselves, what is the benefit? Well, now the spirit has the opportunity to rise up within us in order to be expressed, and something other than ourselves can be expressed. The challenge is that our spirit is encased, enveloped, and trapped in our soul. So, in order for our spirit to get out, there is no other way for it to get out except through our soul. This is why we need the breaking of the outer man. Our self needs to come to an end. When we come to our end, that is how Christ can begin in us.

### C. Because we are so much in the soul, our human spirit does not have the chance, the ground, to do anything.

Well, the only way we can counter this is by practice—practice denying ourselves. Anything that requires practice implies that it is not easy, natural, or automatic. You don’t just start playing classics on the piano when you begin. It requires practice. To swing a golf club requires repetitions over and over and over again until it becomes almost spontaneous and automatic. This is because we practiced it. This is how we must approach the matter of denying ourselves. Don’t think you’ve got it just because you deny yourself once. We need to learn to deny ourselves.

The great thing about this is that the more you do it, the easier it becomes. You become accustomed to it. It becomes muscle memory, something that you just do. Eventually, the goal is that we could deny ourselves, not just in some instances or instantly, but continuously, spontaneously, and automatically. Well, this comes through much practice.

I have a few experiences, but I must move on.

When we don’t deny ourselves, then we will see ourselves. There is a telltale sign of when we don’t deny ourselves: we live, worship, and serve according to our preference. Usually, our preference is ourselves. So, if you look at the ones you are around and they look like you, then likely you prefer to be with them.

Unfortunately, that affects us not just in our social life, but even in the Christian realm. When you look at various organizations and groups, and even churches and congregations, a lot of them are homogeneous. In fact, there is a certain tipping point when, once you reach it, you immediately accelerate toward 100 percent homogeneity in a congregation. The reason for this is that people like birds of the same feather. We tend to gather and feel more comfortable with the ones with the same status, views, and values.

That is why I have more assurance when I see a diverse group of brothers coming together and praying in harmony. Think of Acts 13 in Antioch, such an array of brothers. I know that there, the self is being denied. So, we need to learn not to go by ourselves. Even, it is a shame, really, for a church to be identified by a particular ethnicity. Why? Well, why do these churches become so quickly a matter of ethnicity?

Martin Luther (the reformist) famously said there is no time during the week that America is more divided by race than 11:00 a.m. on Sunday morning, when all the different churches are gathering.

Why? Because it’s easier. It’s easier not to deny the self than it is to deny yourself.

### D. Recognizing that the “I,” which is the self, has been crucified and Christ lives in our spirit—[[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]; [[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Tim. 4:22]].

I think you’ve probably covered this, but if you haven’t, in [[Galatians-02#v20|Galatians 2:20]] there are two “I’s.” There is the self, the fallen, corrupted soul, but there is also the “new I.” In order for this new I to live, the old I needs to die. “No longer I”—my mind, emotion, and will—but it is Christ who lives in me. Where does He live? In our spirit—[[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Timothy 4:22]]. So, we need to learn to deny the first I, the old I, so that Christ, who is in our spirit, could be lived out in us.

## III. The life and faculties of the soul:

This is an important matter: life versus faculties. This is the way we can reconcile certain troubling thoughts that come out of this matter of denying ourselves.

Some think that when we deny ourselves, we end up ceasing to exist. We are no longer. If you have that view, then we would have to act a certain way. Some become a pious man who fears God: stoic, saintly, “godly.” Or they become very robotic, detached, aloof, and unaffected by things that should invoke, in a normal situation, an emotional response. Yet they are just robots.

There is a difference between the life of the soul and the faculties of the soul.

### A. There is a difference between the life of the soul and the faculties of the soul.

What is it that we are denying when we say “deny the soul”? We deny the life of the self; we do not deny the faculties of the soul. This is why in Luke 9 it says, “whoever loses his soul-life.” The life is one thing, but the faculties of the soul are something entirely different. The life is that which energizes and motivates the soul, whereas the soul itself is the source. That is the life of the soul.

In the reading, it talked about a husband and wife. The wife has denied her mind, emotion, and will and adopted the husband’s thoughts, feelings, and choices. This means that it is not that she stopped thinking, feeling, making decisions, or purposing. She does all of that. However, she is doing it motivated by a different source. It is not she who is coming up with these things, but her husband.

This is exactly how Christ is with us. He doesn’t want us to stop thinking. We need to train our mind, exercise our mind, and get PhDs. But eventually, you need to learn that that is just to develop the faculties of your mind. None of your education, and none of what you have done to acquire more skills, is the source. What it is, is the creation of a vessel that the Lord could energize with His life so that we could express Him.

### B. The life of the soul should be denied, but the faculties of the soul should be uplifted and enlivened with the resurrection life.

Actually, it is not just our created faculties now. It is our soul that has now been resurrected—not just resurrected, but uplifted, and even enriched with His divine attributes.

There is a verse in [[Romans-06#v5|Romans 6:5]]: “If we have grown together with Him.” Note 1 under “grown” in the Recovery Version states: “This denotes an organic union in which growth takes place, so that one partakes of the life and characteristics of the other.” This is grafting. Such a grafting discharges our negative element, resurrects our God-given faculties, uplifts our faculties, enriches our faculties, and saturates our entire being to transform us.

The soul is even more important than before. The soul is now able to do more than it was able to do before. The soul’s faculties are resurrected, uplifted, and enriched.

### C. Hence, the faculties of the soul become proper organs to express the Lord.

In the past, our person was our soul. What was left of any function in our spirit, you could say, was that our spirit functioned a tiny bit, maybe with a little bit of the function of the conscience. But that spirit, to the extent that it was functioning, was simply the organ. But when we were regenerated, we were filled with God’s life, and our spirit became the most prominent part of our being. Then our soul becomes the organ, and all the faculties of the soul are used by the person in the spirit to express that which is coming from our spirit. When we receive the Lord, our spirit is activated, and the spirit becomes our person.

We need to learn to relegate our soul to be an organ and not allow it to rise up to be our person.

### D. We need an uplifted understanding, a spiritual emotion, and a strong, renewed will.

Once you start taking Christ in your spirit, certain things happen. Things of God that you could not understand now become understandable. Before, you were dull in your thinking; now you are keen to the things of God. You may have five PhDs, but if you are unregenerated, do you know what the things of God become to you when you don’t have the proper faculties to be used by your spirit? They become foolishness. That is what a soulish man thinks.

It is the same thing with our emotion. Prior to being saved, our emotion was a certain way. But then, when we were saved, our emotion was uplifted. Even a “barbarian”, or a "brutish" of a guy can be reduced to tears before the Lord, and even publicly.

There was a coal miner, James McKindrick. He started this 'Wall Street revival'. There was an instance where an evangelist was droning on, doctrinally correct but with no fire in his speaking. He tugged the preacher’s sleeve. He was bothered. Finally, he said, “Sit down. Let me share.”

McKendrick had something to share. He stood up, a coal miner. His mind went blank. He had no words. He just stood there looking at the crowd of these unregenerated fellow miners, and all that came to his consciousness was the love of God wanting desperately for these ones, their souls, not to be eternally damned. It was so overwhelming that he just began to weep. The whole congregation—hardly any words—he said something like, “You are going to hell,” but it was spoken in love. The whole meeting, as described, had tears flowing in the aisles. All were affected.

We need this sort of renewed, resurrected, uplifted, and enriched emotion. If we had this, even a brother like Paul could weep with tears night and day. Paul was tender in his feeling, love, mercy, and compassion. Even if you are not naturally emotional, when you are spiritual, when your spirit comes to life, you become able to express your emotions in a controlled way by the Spirit.

A person with a renewed will no longer has a will like jelly; he does have a backbone. Natural emotional people are like jellyfish; they have no will. But spiritually emotional people will have a strong, determined, purposeful will aligned with the will of God.

The point is this: the more you are in your spirit, the stronger you will be in all three faculties of your soul. These faculties are for you to express the Lord. The Lord is full of thoughts, feelings, and determinations. He is very purposeful. But only when our faculties are uplifted, made spiritual, resurrected, and renewed will we be able to express the Lord in His feelings, mind, and will.

I like Luke 9. Again, this is a matter of practice. “Take up his cross daily.” This is how we practice. Take Christ as your husband, as your life. You must take the divine life as your life, and the faculties of your soul will remain organs to be used by the Spirit to express the Lord.

## IV. The spirit being crucial as the source:

When it comes to spiritual things, the source matters more than the results. What matters is the source. Is it ourselves, or is it God? Is He the Author and Originator, or are you? Even when a work is really prevailing, if it is apart from God and apart from Christ, what God regards it as is nothing. “Apart from Me you can do nothing.” Yes, you do it, and everything that you did, all the results, the great works, the demons you cast out, the words you have spoken—all that amounts to nothing. Why? It is the wrong source. You were the source. In God’s eyes, results do not count; the source is what counts.

You might say, “Well, is there partial credit for good intentions?” Does Abraham get rewarded for bearing Ishmael? He was affected for generation after generation, even until today, because of these good intentions. He wanted God’s promise to be fulfilled. God promised him a seed. Sarai, bless her heart, had a good idea. Does she get partial credit? No, because the source was not of God. There is nothing that we should be proud about in producing Ishmaels.

So, you have two workers. Outwardly they are the same, but one is natural and one is spiritual. I want to tell a story, but I can’t. This was made real to me right after the training. I was in a locality, and you could tell the difference between a brother who is natural and a brother who is spiritual in their ministering. With one, you would get the Lord, and with the other, you would not.

We must set our mind. This means that our soul would be possessed and governed by our spirit.

Fresh appreciation for Luke 1: Mary’s soul magnified the Lord. This morning I had an experience of denying the soul in a very interesting way. It is so easy, I guess, for anything to rob us of our enjoyment of the Lord and for there to be anything apart from the spirit. I felt tired when I woke up, so right away there was the emotional aspect. My will was to go sit down. A lot of times I do go for a walk, but my mind thought it was cold, though it wasn’t. All these things were going on. I was denying these things, but I realized, after we deny all these parts, we have Christ living in our spirit.

We deny the soul-life as the source. Then we let the Lord as the Spirit be the One who is expressed in all the faculties of the soul.

A sister shared that when we are brought to the end, then the Lord can start. I talked to someone who was a philosopher. His way of thinking aligns with the thought that you cannot do it and must be brought to the end. This ends up being suicidal, because when you are brought to nothing, there is nothing left. I was so struck. People get so close, but they don’t fully see this because they don’t have God and the spirit. When we are brought to our end, we have a spirit that will rise up within us. We do not have to die, and we do not have to become a husk of ourselves. We have a spirit that rises up within us and enlivens all the faculties of our soul.

When I was reading, we were talking about denying ourselves and not living according to our mind, emotion, and will. I thought, “Am I a person of the spirit?” When we were created, we were created with a spirit. God’s intention is that this spirit would be our person. This spirit was rendered functionless, and then the soul became the prominent thing in our being. So, I was just encouraged. Our spirit was supposed to be our person.

Honestly, being in the Training, I feel I am doing a lot of things I don’t want to do. It is hard, but I am encouraged that the faculties of our soul are to be used by the spirit, uplifted to live Christ. Our spirit can direct and possess our soul.
