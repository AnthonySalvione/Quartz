## I. Spiritual knowledge and the self:

The point here is, all spiritual experiences begin with a certain amount of spiritual knowledge. You need some knowledge of the truth, in order to have the experience. Even if it's a minor amount of the truth, like Jesus dying for you, that can lead to an experience of life. However, as we go on in our experience, and as we want to grow in life, we need *more* of the truth, and *more* knowledge. In fact, our knowledge often surpasses our experience. So, we can say a truth and immediately begin to experience it there, but as we go on, we need MORE TRUTH. Especially in the training you are being bombarded with truth. Your knowledge will eventually surpass your experience - you cannot experience all those truths in a quick time. It takes an extended period of time in order to experience all these truths. So, we end up in a sitaution where our knowledge surpasses our experience. This is normal. We first know the truth, and then we experience the truth.

## Introduction

Sin corresponds with our desires of sin, but the self provides the *energy, animation, strength, power* to do the things that are according to our desires, which is sin. That is how sin and self are distinguishable. So, to be released from sin, we simply need to reckon ourselves as dead, acknowleding that we have been crucified.
