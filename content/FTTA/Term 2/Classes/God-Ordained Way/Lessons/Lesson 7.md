# Intro

"Lord, here you've placed me with these ones so that we can pursue together. Lord, as I am on my way I'd like to practice. To tell you how much I love You. How I desire that the Lord would become so real to me."

Vital groups are by the principle of John 4 & 5. For this to be prevailing, we need to have the realization that this is a 'fueling station' for an ongoing living with these very ones. I am going to continue to be with them in my heart, in my consideration... not just during this meeting, but after this meeting dismisses, I will carry them with me, I am going to treasure them, have them included in my heart's desires, thoughts and prayers. With them in an ongoing, longitudinal and living way. 

Walking means to have our living. Romans 8:4 is an encapsulation of the God-ordained way.
