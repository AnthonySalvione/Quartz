# Outline—The Spirit's Work on and in the Believers (3)

## I. The three aspects of the sanctification in the Scriptures:

### A. The Spirit's sanctification in seeking the God-chosen people before their repentance
[[1 Peter-01#v2|1 Pet. 1:2]].
### B. The sanctification by the blood of Christ at the time of the believers' believing
[[Hebrews-13#v12|Heb 13:12]]; [[Hebrews-09#v13|9:13-14]]; [[Hebrews-10#v29|10:29]].
### C. The Spirit's dispositional sanctification in the believers' full course of their Christian life
[[Romans-15#v16|Rom 15:16b]]; [[Romans-06#v19|6:19]], [[Romans-06#v22|22]].

## II. The essential works of the Spirit's dispositional sanctification:
### A. To continue the ongoing work of the believers' regeneration.
### B. To carry out the renewing of the believers as God's new creation
[[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5]]; [[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2b]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16]].
### C. To perform the Lord's transformation of the believers
[[2 Corinthians-03#v18|3:18]]; [[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2b]].
### D. To consummate in the believers' glorification
[[1 Thessalonians-05#v23|1 Thes. 5:23]].
### E. To complete God's sonship in choosing the believers
[[Ephesians-01#v4|Eph 1:4-5]].
