## Introduction

- The unique writer of the Bible is the Spirit ([[2 Peter-01#v21|2 Pet. 1:21]]; [[2 Samuel-23#v2|2 Sam. 23:2]]).
- The foundation of the Christian life is the Spirit with our spirit.
- It's important to be clear that the general subject does not use the conjunction "and", but rather "with", Indicating that our spirit is not the subject, but rather the Spirit is the subject.

## The Basic Revelation Concerning God and Man in the Holy Scriptures

### Study Question 2

*Question: Study the three points in the basic revelation concerning God in the Holy Scriptures. Be prepared to speak on each point from the standpoint of truth.*

#### Point 1
**God is Spirit denoting God's essence, substance—[[John-04#v24|John 4:24]].

- It is like saying that a chair is wood. Wood refers to the essence of what the chair is. Thus, "God is Spirit" denotes God's essence.
- In order to worship God, we need to see that we are worshiping is Spirit in essence and in substance.

#### Point 2
**The Spirit of God denoting that the Spirit is God—[[Genesis-01#v2|Genesis 1:2]].

- Phrases like "the light of God" are in apposition, here implying that God *is* light.
- The creating God was the moving Spirit.
- God does everything as the Spirit, and nothing without it.

#### Point 3
**The name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit denoting the Divine Trinity and indicating that God is triune—[[Matthew-28#v19|Matthew 28:19]].

- He is one God yet three—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.
##### Coexisting and Coinhering as One God
- The three of the Divine Trinity coexist and coinhere as one God from eternity to eternity.
![[John-14#v10]]

- To co-exist is to exist simultaneously.
- To coinhere is to mutually indwell one another.
- The Lord also said came He came with the Father in [[John-08#v16|John 8:16]] and other verses.
- Thus, the Son's speaking is the Father's working, and the Spirit's moving is the Son's moving and the Father's moving.
##### Being Distinct but Not Separate

- I like this verse to show this:
![[John-08#v29]]

- We can say that the Triune God coexist by the way of coinhering... meaning that the three of the Divine Trinity—who are distinct—exist as One *because* they mutually indwell each other.
- What is our God? He is Spirit. Who is the Spirit? The Spirit is God. Who are the Father, Son and Spirit? The Divine Trinity.
- According to the economical aspect of the Trinity, we see the three working together simultaneously as one.

- The Father planned by predestinating us ([[Ephesians-01#v3|Eph. 1:3-5]]), but He did this in Christ with the Spirit.
- The Son accomplished this, but He did this with the Father ([[John-08#v29|John 8:29]]), and by the Spirit ([[Luke-01#v35|Luke 1:35]]).
- The Spirit applied all that the Son accomplished, but He did this as the Son and with the Father.
