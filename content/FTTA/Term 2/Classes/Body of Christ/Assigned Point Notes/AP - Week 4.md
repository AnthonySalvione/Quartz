# I Am On Introduction


I feel the burden of these two messages first was to establish the distinction between eternal salvation and the reward in the millennial kingdom. 

Speaking point - this should stir up a desire within us to know what this reward is and what is expected for us to receive the reward.

My experience is, how often do I begin to think "I need to be a better Christian if I am wanting to receive the kingdom reward!"

Message 228 - We need to be clear and impressed by the fact that the overcomers are the ones who are living today in the reality of the kingdom. This means that the focus is not so much on what rewards we are going to be receiving when the Lord comes back, but rather more on our experience of the kingdom right now. 

We need to live in the reality of the kingdom by living in our mingled spirit. In our mingled spirit we can endure sufferings with Christ, grow in life, live in the reality of the kingdom, love His appearing... and all these things actually issue in our glorification (we can see in Romans).

When we live out the reality of the kingdom on earth, we are qualified to participate in the milennial kingdom, are thereby reign with Christ, inherit the kingdom, enjoy eternal life in a fuller way, receive the rewards, and consummately participate in the NJ as the paradise of God.



## The Overcoming Believers Will Reign With Christ
- Revelation 20:4b relates this matter with millennial kingdom
- Emphasize the point that the qualification is to be those who already live in the reality of this kingdom.

#### There are some, you could say, rewards or prizes promised to the overcoming believers reigning with Christ (at His second coming)

- We see the overcomers will be crowned with multiple crowns, and these crowns are symbols of glory like a prize to a triumphant winner of a race.
- In Revelation 3:21 we see that we are to sit with Christ on His throne, implying participation in the Lord's authority.
- Given authority to participate in the kingdom and shepherd the nations.
- And also, this is so awesome, the greatest thing we receive is the joy of the Lord, this is emphasized a lot in Matthew 25.

## Summarized content

These two messages show us that the overcomers are not merely good Christians outwardly, but those who live today in the reality of the kingdom and therefore will participate in the reward of the kingdom in the coming age. The main emphasis is that the millennial kingdom is not just a general blessing for all believers in the same way, but a particular reward for those who overcome, endure, grow in life, and remain faithful to the Lord. These messages present both the attractiveness of the reward and the seriousness of the Lord’s coming judgment.

In Message 228, the focus is mainly on the overcomers’ participation in the millennial kingdom: reigning with Christ, being priests of God and of Christ, inheriting the kingdom, and being glorified with Him. Then in Message 229, the emphasis continues by showing the various aspects of the reward: inheriting eternal life in the millennium, shining in the kingdom, participating in the kingdom feast, enjoying the reward, and even being the New Jerusalem as the paradise of God in the millennium. Altogether, these messages show that God’s intention is not only that we would be saved, but that we would mature, overcome, and be rewarded in the coming age.

Here are a few short burden statements you could use:

- These messages are a strong call to live today in the reality of the kingdom so that we may participate in its manifestation in the coming age.
    
- The central burden is that salvation is a gift, but the kingdom reward is a matter of faithfulness, growth, and overcoming.
    
- The overcomers are those who allow Christ to grow in them today so that they may reign with Him, enjoy Him, and express Him in glory in the millennium.
    
- These messages are not merely about prophecy, but about the Lord’s present calling for us to live an overcoming life.
    
- The coming kingdom is presented here not mainly as a doctrine to understand, but as a reward to gain and an incentive to pursue Christ today.
