
# Points:

## Intro:

- In the previous point, we've seen that through faith in Christ, we overcome unto eternal life and will be sons of God in eternity. This is a broader overcoming that every regenerated believer will participate in.
- The previous point is more concerning the destination and portion of the believers, which is the enjoyment of the New Jerusalem and the water of life. You could say this is the status and destiny of the believers.
- But for us today, how can this become a present and living reality in our experience?

## Main Points
- 1 John 5:4-5. We need to see firstly that the world referred to here is *kosmos*, which is not the created earth mainly, but Satan's ordered system. This whole anti-God system is comprised of both good and bad things: education, culture, human pleasures... to name a few. 
- This system occupies man and keeps him from God.

- I had this question as I was reading, why is it through Christ in the first point, and through the Son of God in this section.
- Christ denotes mainly our eternal life and the salvation of man, and the Son of God is more in relation to our divine sonship and divine life. Thus, why I say that the first section is more about our status, and this section is more pointing to the life that is operating in us.
- You could say, "Faith in Christ emphasizes receiving God’s eternal salvation in a full way, issuing in eternal life, sonship, and participation in the New Jerusalem. Faith in the Son of God emphasizes receiving the divine life through organic union with Him, which enables us to overcome the satanic world system in our present experience."

- Being begotten of God is crucial, because the victory does not come from anything of our own effort, but from our divine birth which is something of God.
- "everything that is begotten of God" in 1 John 5:4 refers especially to our regenerated spirit

**KEY POINT:**
- Faith is not presented as a mere act of believing doctrines, which is the common understanding. Faith in the Son of God brings us into an **organic union with the Triune God in the Son**. That union is what overcomes the world.

- Faith itself is not the power by which we overcome, rather, faith joins us to the Son of God, and in that union we receive and enjoy the **divine life**. That life in our spirit overcomes the world.
- So, practically how do we do this? By exercising our spirit, walking by our spirit.

- So, this practically is the living out of our divine Sonship we received when we were regenerated, and is our true position.
- 

## Transition

- This overcoming is developed further in the next point to the overcoming of Satan himself.
