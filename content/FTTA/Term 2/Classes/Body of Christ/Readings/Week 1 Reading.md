
## The Conclusion of the New Testament, Messages 221-222

### The Failures in the Churches

- There are no failures in the universal church; however, there are failures in the local churches.
- All the failures come out of one source, and this source is Satan, who instigates the weak saints to cause all kinds of local problems.
- For this reason, in the New Testament there is not only a clear vision of the pure, holy, heavenly, and divine church but also a record of all the failures in the local churches.

#### Hypocrisy

- The first failure in the churches was that of hypocrisy seen in the case of Ananias and Sapphira, in [[Acts-05#v1|Acts 5:1-11]].
- Hypocrisy is a matter of pretending to be somebody in order to get a name that we may have vainglory.
- Hypocrisy altogether related to ambition.
- Ambition, therefore, is the ***seed of the failures*** in the churches.
- Ananias and his wife Sapphira did not offer everything to the church or the Lord's sake but ***pretended*** to do this. Pretended is the key word here.

##### Letting Satan Fill the Heart

- [[Acts-05#v1|Acts 5:1-11]] indicates that two people were making their residence and home in Ananias and Sapphira.
- [[Acts-05#v3|5:3]] alone shows us both the Holy Spirit in them (because they were saved), and also Satan in them filling their hearts to lie to the Spirit.
 ![[Acts-05#v3]]
- Satan had the ground in them because of their ambition. 
- If we would avoid Satan's deception, we must reject, condemn, and abandon the position to be somebody in the church life.
- If we ever have the thought of being a somebody in the church life, right away Satan has the ground to deceive us.
- Ananias and Sapphira's deception brought them into death. This is a general principle.


##### Lying to and Testing the Holy Spirit—God

- [[Acts-05#v4|Acts 5:4]] shows that selling possessions was not considered a practice of legality to the apostles.

![[Acts-05#v4]]

- Therefore, Ananias and Sapphira were ***not required*** to sell their property, thus proving that this action was done for the sake of their name.
- So, their sin consisted in lying to the Holy Spirit. This was a gross sin that offended the indwelling Spirit. Their sin was a willing cooperation with Satan, the evil resident within them.
- Combining verse 3 and verse 4 together proves that the Holy Spirit in verse 3 is God Himself.
- Then in verse 9 the Lord is also mentioned, which proves that the Holy Spirit in verse 3, God in verse 4 and the Lord in verse 9 are all ***one***. Especially as it relates to the experience of the believers.

![[Acts-05#v9]]

- Ananias and Sapphira lied to the very Spirit who was within them.
- If the Holy Spirit was not within them, Peter would not have said that they had lied to the Holy Spirit.
- Again, these verses prove that Ananias and Sapphira had both the Holy Spirit and also Satan residing within them.
- [[1 John-05#v16|1 John 5:16]] shows that although Ananias and Sapphira were saved, they committed a sin unto death.
- Their case teaches us to be extremely careful about ambition and dishonesty in the church life.

##### Pretending to Be Absolute for the Lord

- Ananias and Sapphira pretended to be absolute for the Lord

#### Being Overcome by Racial Difference

- The second failure in the churches was the failure of being overcome by racial difference.
- [[Acts-06#v1|Acts 6:1]] says,

![[Acts-06#v1]]

- The Hellenists were Jews who spoke Greek, and the Hebrews were Jews who spoke Hebrew.
- This problem in the very beginning of the church life seemed to be caused by different languages, **but actually it was problem of race**.
- In Acts 2, Jews had come from their dispersion to Jerusalem to keep the feast of Pentecost. They were not able to speak Hebrew, but instead their native languages.
- If all of mankind were of one race, there would probably be just one language. Different languages are a result of different races.
- The source of this problem is in Genesis 11, which is the time of Babel. From this point onward, mankind has been divided. This problem was terminated when Jesus died on the cross in Ephesians 2.

##### In the Matter of Eating

- Referring back to [[Acts-06#v1|Acts 6:1]], there was a murmuring of the Hellenists against the Hebrews because widows were overlooked in the daily dispensing.
- This indicates failure in being overcome by racial differences was in the matter of eating.

##### Causing the Practice of Having All Things Common to Wane

- The problem in Acts 6 regarding eating caused the practice of having all things common in [[Acts-02#v44|Acts 2:44-45]] to wane.

![[Acts-02#v44]]
![[Acts-02#v45]]

- The reason this practice did not work well was mainly the racial differences, which ultimately resulted in the waning of the practice of having all things common.

#### Bringing In the Legal Practices of the Old Testament

- Another failure was that of bringing in the legal practices of the Old Testament in [[Acts-15#v1|Acts 15:1-2]].

![[Acts-15#v1]]
![[Acts-15#v2]]

- The men who came down from Judea had a strong purpose to exercise Judaic influence on the Gentile believers.
- They taught that unless one is circumcised according to Moses, they cannot be saved, which annuls the faith.
- The strongest ordinances are Circumcision, Sabbath-keeping, and a particular diet. These three caused Jews to be distinct and separate from the Gentiles whom they considered unclean.
- All these ordinances became an obstacle to the spreading of the gospel to the Gentiles in [[Colossians-02#v16|Colossians 2:16]].
-
![[Colossians-02#v16]]

- To keep the custom of Moses means to practice the outward ordinances of the law. Which nullifies the grace of God and makes Christ's death in [[Galatians-02#v21|Galatians 2:21]] of no effect.

![[Galatians-02#v21]]

##### Circumcision on the Flesh

- Circumcision was an outward ordinance inherited by the Jews from their forefathers, beginning from Abraham in [[Genesis-17#v9|Genesis 17:9-14]].
- Christ is of no profit to the believers if one is to be circumcised according to [[Galatians-05#v2|Galatians 5:2]].
-
![[Galatians-05#v2]]

- Therefore in Acts 15 Paul and Barnabas could not tolerate this heresy.
- Here Paul and Barnabas were contending for the faith in [[Jude-01#v3|Jude 3]] so that the truth of the gospel might remain with the believers.

![[Jude-01#v3]]

- In [[Philippians-03#v2|Philippians 3:2]], Paul was very bold to say "Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil workers, beware of the concision". Concision is a very contemptuous term for circumcision.
- Paul calls the Judaizers dogs.

##### Annulling the Freedom in Christ

- [[Galatians-03#v3|Galatians 3:3-4]] says,
![[Galatians-02#v3]]
![[Galatians-02#v4]]

- The false brothers were the Judaizers who perverted the gospel of Christ by smuggling the ordinances of the law into the church.
- Paul would not be subject to those who sought to damage our freedom in Christ and bring us into slavery
- All believers are now free from obligation to the law, so in order to maintain this freedom Paul refused to have Titus circumcised.

##### Even Causing Peter to Play Hypocrisy, the Rest of the Jewish Believers Who Were with Him to join Him in Hypocrisy, and Even Barnabas to Be Carried Away by Their Hypocrisy

- [[Galatians-02#v12|Galatians 2:12-13]] says,

![[Galatians-02#v12]]
![[Galatians-02#v13]]

- The fact that Peter shrank back proves he was weak in the pure Christian faith.
- Peter ***saw*** the clear vision from heaven concerning fellowship with the Gentiles, and took the lead to practice that vision in Acts 10.


## Conclusion of NT message 223

- Degredation of the church - a more basic and serious problem than a failure. 
- In verse 21, Paul lists some of the ordinances he was talking about, such as do not handle, do not taste and do not touch. This covers virtually every action man can take. The definition of asceticism and Gnosticism here is anything relating to ordinances regarding these three things.
