## I. Just as we believe from the Bible that God is both one and three (see
## Message Two, we also believe from the Bible that God exists in both an
## essential aspect and an economical aspect:
### A. The Lord Jesus was conceived of the Holy Spirit (Matt. 1:20; Luke 1:35) and
### was thus essentially one with the Spirit throughout all His human life. This
### was for His existence as the God-man:
#### 1. Because He is the Son of God, even in incarnation He is with the Father
#### and of the Father (John 14:9-10; 7:29; 8:16, 29; 16:32).
#### 2. The incarnation was not simply that of the Son separately. Rather, the Son's
#### incarnation is of the Spirit; that is, in incarnation the Son has the Spirit as
#### His very divine essence (see note 4 on Rom. 1:4; note 3 on 1 Pet. 3:18).
### B. At His baptism the Spirit descended upon the Lord in bodily form as a dove
### (Matt. 3:16; Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; John 1:32). This was for the carrying out of
### His earthly ministry:
#### 1. This does not mean that before this the Spirit was not with Him (see A.
#### above).
#### 2. The Spirit came to Him in baptism not to be the divine essence for His
#### existence (which basically equals "adoptionism," a heresy) but to be the
#### divine power for His ministry.
### C. As He died on the cross, God forsook Him (Matt. 27:46; Mark 15:34). This was
### for the accomplishment of our redemption (see note 2 on Psa. 22:1):
#### 1. This does not mean that intrinsically and essentially God left Him; other-
#### wise, He would have died merely as a man, and His redemption would
#### have lacked its eternal efficacy (cf. Heb. 9:12; Acts 20:28; 1 John 1:7).
#### 2. God left Him in His crucifixion in the same way that the Spirit came to
#### Him in His baptism, that is, not in the way of being His divine essence but
#### in the way of being His anointing and power for His ministry activity.
#### 3. When He died, He was fully God and fully man.
### D. On the one hand, in His essence Christ is always God, is always with the
### Father, and is always of the Spirit; but on the other hand, in the processes of
### the economy of God Christ was at a point in time anointed by the Spirit and
### was at another point in time forsaken by God. These facts point to a distinc-
### tion between what He is in His intrinsic or essential existence and what He is
### in His economy.

## II. "According to Their essence, the three are one; thus, there is the aspect
## of the essential Trinity. The Son is the embodiment of the Father (Col.
## 2:9), and the Spirit is the reality of the Son (John 14:16-18):
### A. "A Son is given to us, yet His name is called the eternal Father-Isa. 9:6.
### B. "The Son as the last Adam became the life-giving Spirit-1 Cor. 15:45b.
### C. "The Lord is the Spirit (2 Cor. 3:17) and the Lord Spirit (v. 18).
### D. "Such words in the Scripture are strong evidence that the Father, the Son, and
### the Spirit are essentially one." (Brother Witness Lee, The Crystallization-study
### of the Gospel of John, p. 30. See also pp. 37-38).
### E. While the three are eternally distinct, They are one in essence, and this one-
### ness of essence accounts for the Scriptural identification of the Son with the
### Father (Isa. 9:6; John 10:30; 14:9-10) and the Spirit with the Son (1 Cor. 15:45;
### 2 Cor. 3:17). Hence, the Trinity has this essential aspect.
### F. The term essential Trinity refers to the Triune God, the three-one God, in the
### aspect of His eternal self-existence. In the essential Trinity generally His
### oneness is more prominent.
### G. In the essential Trinity the Father, the Son, and the Spirit coexist and coinhere
### at the same time and in the same way with no succession-there is no first,
### second, or third
### H. To teach that God is eternally one but not eternally three-that is, that the
### distinctions Father, Son, and Spirit are not eternal—is modalism and is
### heretical.

## III. "According to the economy the three are three; thus, there is the aspect
## of the economical Trinity. The Father is the Originator, making the divine
## economy (Eph. 1:9-10; 3:9; 1 Tim. 1:4b); then the Son accomplishes the
## divine economy made by the Father (John 5:19; 8:28); and the Spirit
## applies to God's elect what the Son has accomplished (16:13). Even so,
## They are still one in harmony in the economical Trinity (10:30; 17:21, 23):
### A "The Father spoke in heaven concerning the Son, the Son stood in the water on
### the earth, and the Spirit soared as the dove in the air—Matt. 3:16-17.
### B. "The Father chose the believers before the foundation of the world, the Son
### redeemed them in time, and the Spirit sealed them after they believed in the
### Son-Eph. 1:4, 6-7, 13.
### C. "Words like these in the Scripture are strong evidence that the Father, the
### Son, and the Spirit are economically three. Thus, God's elect participate in
### what the Triune God has done and enjoy Their work with Themselves for their
### satisfaction." (Brother Witness Lee, The Crystallization-study of the Gospel of
### John, pp. 30-31. See also pp. 38-39).
### D. While God is eternally one essence, He is also eternally three hypostases (see
### note 14' in 2 Cor. 13), and this threeness accounts for the Scriptural distinc-
### tion between the Father and the Son and the Spirit. Hence, the Trinity has
### this economical aspect.

### E. The term economical Trinity refers to the Triune God, the three-one God, in
### the aspect of His move in His economy. In the economical Trinity generally His
### threeness is more prominent.
### F. In God's plan, God's economy, the Father takes the first step, the Son takes the
### second step, and the Spirit takes the third step:
#### 1. The Father planned, the Son accomplished, and the Spirit applies what the
#### Son accomplished
#### according to the Father's plan; this is a successive
#### procedure, or a succession, in God's economy to carry out God's eternal
#### purpose:
##### a. The Father accomplished the first step of His plan, His economy, by
##### working to choose and predestinate us, but He did this in Christ the
##### Son and with the Spirit (Eph. 1:3-6).
##### b. The Son came to accomplish the Father's plan, but He did this with the
##### Father (John 8:29; 16:32) and by the Spirit (Luke 1:35; Matt. 1:18, 20;
##### 12:28).
##### c. The Spirit comes in the third step to apply all that the Son accom-
##### plished, but He does this as the Son and with the Father (John 14:26;
##### 15:26; 1 Cor. 15:45b; 2 Cor. 3:17).
#### 2. Thus, while the divine economy of the Divine Trinity is being carried out,
#### the divine existence of the Divine Trinity, His eternal coexistence and
#### coinherence, remains intact and is not jeopardized
### G. To teach that God is eternally three but that the three are not eternally one—
### that is, that the Father, the Son, and the Spirit are separate hypostases-is
### tritheism and is heretical.

## IV. Some misconceptions about the distinction between the Triune God's
## essential and economical aspects:
### A. False: "There are two Trinities, an essential one and an economical one":
#### 1. There is one God, who is triune; yet there are two aspects to His being
#### triune.
#### 2. The essential aspect of the Trinity relates to the Triune God as He exists in
#### Himself.
#### 3. The economical aspect of the Trinity relates to the Triune God as He moves
#### in His economy.
### B. False: "The essential aspect of the Trinity refers only to the side of His
### oneness":
#### 1. The essential aspect is an aspect of the Trinity, an aspect of the three-one
#### God, not merely an aspect of His one essence.
#### 2. Recognizing the essential aspect of the Trinity helps us see how the eternal
#### Three are also one.
### C. False: "The economical aspect of the Trinity refers only to the side of His
### threeness":
#### 1. The economical aspect is an aspect of the one God, who is eternally three-
#### one, not merely an aspect of His three hypostases.
#### 2. Recognizing the economical aspect of the Trinity helps us see how the one
#### God can be distinguished as three.

### D. False: "The essential aspect of the Trinity refers to the Triune God in eternity":
#### 1. The economical Trinity is also eternal; otherwise, we would be suggesting
#### that the eternal God changes in His eternal existence.
#### 2. The essential Trinity is best understood as God existing in Himself, outside
#### of time and within time.
### E. False: "The economical aspect of the Trinity refers to the Triune God in time":
#### 1. The essential Trinity also exists in time; otherwise, we would be suggesting
#### that the eternal God ceases to exist in some aspect of His eternal being.
#### 2. The economical Trinity is best understood as God existing in His move or
#### in His actions. Since His move is mostly prominent within time, His
#### economical aspect is more prominent within time.
## Whatever He is in His economical aspect is based upon what He is in His
## essential aspect, and whatever He is in His essential aspect is manifested
## in His economical aspect:
### A. The Triune God is a wonderful Person, who is to be experienced and enjoyed,
### not merely studied and certainly not dogmatized. This is God's intention as
### revealed in the entire revelation of the Holy Scriptures.
### B. Although "complicated and complicating," the distinction between the Triune
### God in His essential and economical aspects is actually the genuine base of
### our experience and enjoyment of Him:
#### 1. If He were only essentially triune, He would exist only in Himself and
#### would have no way to move in His economy to reach man. We would only
#### be able to study Him, not experience and enjoy Him.
#### 2. By being economically triune, He moves in His economy to become a man,
#### to live a human life, to die an all-inclusive death, to resurrect from the
#### dead, and to become the life-giving Spirit to come into us with all that the
#### Triune God is.
#### 3. Because He is economically triune, we are able to become what He is in
#### life, nature, and expression. Because He is essentially triune, what He is in
#### Himself, apart from His move, is eternally preserved. We will never be
#### made God in His eternal Godhead
