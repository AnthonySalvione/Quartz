# The Church
## The Overcomers in the Church
## (1-2)
## Lesson Three
## BODY OF CHRIST-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

## XIII. The overcomers in the church:
### A. Having overcome through faith in Christ to inherit the eternal life and to be the sons
### of God for eternity.
### B. Having overcome the world through faith in the Son of God.
### C. Having overcome the evil one.
### D. Having overcome the antichrists through the indwelling Christ.
### E. Having overcome all the degradations of the degraded churches.
### F. The dead overcomers-the man-child within the woman:
#### 1. Comprising the overcomers of both the Old Testament and the New Testament.
#### 2. To be resurrected and raptured to the throne of God before the great tribulation.
### G. The living overcomers—the firstfruit of God's harvest on the earth:
#### 1. Comprising only the New Testament overcomers who live until the Lord's coming
#### back.
#### 2. To be raptured to mount Zion in the heavens before the great tribulation.
#### 3. For the satisfaction to God and Christ.
### H. To be the bride at the marriage feast of Christ and in the millennium.
### I. To be the armies in heaven fighting with the coming Christ against Antichrist and his
### armies.
