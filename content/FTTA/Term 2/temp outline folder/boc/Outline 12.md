## X. The status of the church:
### C. The kingdom of God
Eph. 2:19:
#### 1. Being the reality of the church-Matt. 16:18.
#### 2. Being the living of the church-Rom. 14:17.
#### 3. Those not worthy to remain in the church life shall not inherit the kingdom of
#### God—1 Cor. 6:9-10; Gal. 5:21; Eph. 5:5.
#### 4. Those developing and growing in the life of God in the church life shall be
#### richly and bountifully supplied with the entrance into the kingdom of God-
2 Pet. 1:3-11.
### G. The golden lampstands
Rev. 1:12, 20b:
#### 1. The lampstand typifying Christ as the embodiment of the Triune God-
Exo. 25:
##### a. Gold signifying the Father's divine nature, the divine substance, essence
v. 31.
##### b. The form signifying the Son as the embodiment of the Godhead in His
##### humanity-cf. 2 Cor. 4:4; Col. 1:15.
##### c. The seven lamps signifying the Spirit as the expression-Exo. 25:37:
###### (1) As the seven Spirits of God to express the Triune God.
###### (2) As the seven eyes of the Son to express the Son as the embodiment of
###### the Triune God.
