# Salvation in Life-Transformation
## Lesson Eight
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

## I. Transformation is not outward correction or adjustment but the metabolic
## function of the life of God in us, by the addition of the element of the divine life
## of Christ into our entire being, that we may express the image of Christ
## outwardly
Rom. 12:2a; 2 Cor. 3:18.

## II. God transforms us with the life element of the firstborn Son of God, the first
## God-man, who passed through death and entered into resurrection, until we
## are transformed metabolically into His image from one degree of glory to a
## higher degree of glory and are raptured and transfigured to enter into His
## glory
v. 18; Phil. 3:21.

## III. Transformation is for the mass reproduction of the firstborn Son of God as the
## prototype of a God-man, that we may be shaped in the divine image to be just
## like the firstborn Son of God, Christ as the God-man
Rom. 8:29; Heb. 2:10.

## IV. The means of transformation in God's organic salvation are:
### A. The renewing of our mind
Rom. 12:2a.
### B. The Lord Spirit-the pneumatic Christ—in us transforming us into the image of the
### glory of Christ
2 Cor. 3:18b.
### C. Our having an unveiled face, beholding and reflecting the Lord like a mirror to
### express Him
v. 18a.
### D. Our living and walking by the Spirit and walking according to the mingled spirit
### that the divine life of Christ may have the way to regulate us and transform us into
### the image of the Lord in glory
Gal. 5:16, 25; Rom. 8:4b.
