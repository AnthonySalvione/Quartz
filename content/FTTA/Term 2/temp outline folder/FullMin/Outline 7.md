# Salvation in Life-Renewing
## Lesson Seven
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

## I. All the items of God's organic salvation are carried out not by Christ's earthly
## ministry judicially and objectively but by His heavenly ministry organically and
## subjectively:
### A. Today we are being saved organically and subjectively through the heavenly ministry of
### Christ, who is the life-giving Spirit.
### B. To experience this organic salvation we need to enter into the mystical realm of Christs
### heavenly ministry.

## II. God in His economy desires to produce a new creation out of the old creation;
## therefore, as believers we must be thoroughly and absolutely renewed so that we
## may be the new creation of God and for God
Gal. 6:15; 2 Cor. 5:17.

## III. To be renewed is to have God's element added into our being to replace and
## discharge our old element
Rom. 12:2.

## IV. The Christian life is a life of being renewed day by day
2 Cor. 4:16:
### A. We are renewed daily by having more of the element of God added into our being.
### B. The renewing Spirit imparts something new—the divine essence of the new man—into
### our being to make us a new creation with the divine life
Titus 3:5.
### C. The mingled spirit—the renewing Spirit mingled with our regenerated spirit to be one
### spirit (1 Cor. 6:17) —is spreading into our mind to renew our entire being
Eph. 4:23.
### D. The Spirit of God renews us by infusing our inward parts with God's attributes, which are
### forever new, can never become old, and are everlasting and unfading
Rev. 21:5a.
### E. The Lord uses the environmental sufferings to consume, to kill, our outer man so that our
### inner man may be renewed
2 Cor. 4:16.
### F. We are renewed by the renewing capacity of the divine life in resurrection
John 11:25; 2
Cor. 1:9; 4:14; Phil. 3:10.

## V. In renewing the mingled spirit penetrates our troublesome mind, causing it to be
## changed and even to become the mind of Christ
2:5; 1 Cor. 2:16; Rom. 12:2-3.

## VI. In renewing we are transferred from the realm of the old creation into the realm of
## the new creation to be the new man to fulfill God's eternal purpose—to
## consummate the New Jerusalem
2 Cor. 5:17; Eph. 4:24; Col. 3:10; Rev. 21:2; 3:12.
