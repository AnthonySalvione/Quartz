# The Exercise of Our Spirit
## Lesson Eight
## THE SPIRIT
## TERM 3

**Scripture Reading:**
2 Tim. 1:6-7; Rom. 8:5-6; Heb. 4:12

## I. To fan our spirit
2 Tim. 1:6-7:
### A. God has not given us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, love, and
### sobermindedness.
### B. Our spirit is surrounded by the three parts of the soul—will, emotion, and mind:
#### 1. Our will should be strong, full of power.
#### 2. Our emotion should be loving, full of love.
#### 3. Our mind should be sober, full of sobermindedness.

## I. To set our mind on the spirit
Rom. 8:5-6:
### A. To set our mind on the flesh is death.
### B. To set our mind on the spirit is life and peace.
