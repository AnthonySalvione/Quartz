# Dealing with the Spirit
## Lesson Fifteen
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Psa. 51:10; 2 Tim. 1:7; Gal. 6:1; 1 Pet. 3:4; Prov. 16:19; Matt. 5:3;
2 Cor. 7:1

## I. The necessity of all the dealings:
### A. If we do not know how to deal with the flesh, conscience, or if we do not pay
### the price to deal with worldly matters with us and in us, then no matter
### how seeking we are, we can never realize anything real in the spiritual life.
### B. The real growth of the spiritual, inner life depends one hundred percent on
### our dealing with our conscience:
#### 1. If we neglect our conscience, we may gain much self-knowledge, but we
#### cannot have even a little spiritual growth.
#### 2. Our conscience must be pure, good, and in a condition of constant peace
#### and rest with God
#### 3.
If we have a problem, accusation, or offense in our conscience, it is very
difficult to advance in the life of the Lord.
### C. We also must keep our flesh and self under a strict dealing.
### D. If we also deal with all the usurpation of the worldly things, we will be
### released.

## II. Our spirit being in different conditions:
### A. To learn how to deal with our spirit is very important because the spiritual
### ministry, spiritual service, and even the spiritual flow of life depend upon
### our spirit.
### B. Different conditions of our spirit:
#### 1. A steadfast spirit-Psa. 51:10
#### 2. A spirit of power, love, and sobermindedness—2 Tim. 1:7.
#### 3. A spirit of meekness-Gal. 6:1; 1 Pet. 3:4.
#### 4. A lowly spirit-Prov. 16:19.
#### 5. Poor in spirit-Matt. 5:3.
#### 6. An undefiled spirit -2 Cor. 7:1.
### C. There may be no problem with the spirit, but many problems with the
### passages of the spirit.
### D. Hence, we must deal with our spirit.

## III. The spirit being the reality and sincerity of our being:
### A. We may live very much in pretense.
### B. However, when we lose our temper, our spirit is released.
### C. Knowing a person's real being by the spirit.

## IV. Ministering by a right and pure spirit:
### A. To deal with our spirit, we must deal with our motives, intentions, and
### desires.
### B. Our spirit must be purified in its motives, intentions, desires, ambitions,
### will, and interests.
