# The Spirit with the Faculties of the Soul
## Lesson Nine
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Matt. 16:24-26; Luke 9:23-25; 1 Cor. 2:11; 2 Cor. 7:13b; Acts 19:21

## I. The spirit and the soul:
### A. The spirit of man is the organ to contact and receive God.
### B. The soul is just the self-Luke 9:23-25, cf. Matt. 16:26.

## II. Practicing to deny the self:
### A. To deny yourself is to deny your own thought, your opinion, your idea, your
### concept, your love, etc.
### B. If we deny our natural thought, the spirit within us would rise up.
### C. Because we are so much in the soul, our human spirit does not have the chance,
### the ground, to do anything.
### D. Recognizing that the "I" which is the self has been crucified and Christ lives in our
### spirit
Gal. 2:20; 2 Tim. 4:22.

## II. The life and faculties of the soul:
### A. There is a difference between the life of the soul and the faculties of the soul.
### B. The life of the soul should be denied, but the faculties of the soul should be uplifted
### and enlivened with the resurrection life.
### C. Hence, the faculties of the soul become proper organs to express the Lord.
### D. We need an uplifted understanding, a spiritual emotion, and a strong, renewed
### will.

## IV. The spirit being crucial to be the source:
### A. Romans 8:6 reveals that we can set our mind on our spirit.
### B. The mind is the leading part of the soul and is between the regenerated mingled
### spirit and the fallen body.
### C. If the mind attaches itself to the flesh and acts independently, it causes enmity
### with God.
### D. If the mind relies
### and attaches itself to the regenerated spirit, which is mingled
### with the Spirit of God, the mind will bring us into the spirit and into the
### enjoyment of the divine Spirit as the law of the Spirit of life.
### E. The Spirit must take control and direct all the parts of the soul in order that they
### might be used to express the Lord
1 Cor. 2:11; 2 Cor. 7:13; Acts 19:21.
