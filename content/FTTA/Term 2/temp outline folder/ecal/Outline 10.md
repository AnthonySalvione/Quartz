# Dealing with the Flesh
## Lesson Ten
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Rom. 7:18; 8:6-7; Gen. 6:3; Rom. 3:20; Gal. 2:16; Gen. 2:7; 12:5; 46:27;
Rom 2:9; James 5:20; Rom. 6:6; Gal. 5:24; Rom. 8:13

## I. The proper order of the parts of our being:
### A. When man was created, man had the flesh of his body, physically speaking.
### B. In all the Scriptures man is called not a spirit or a body but a soul
Gen. 2:7; 12:5;
46:27; Rom. 2:9; James 5:20.
### C. God's intention is that this soul would choose God as life and take God as life by
### man's spirit through the Holy Spirit, so that God will be in the spirit within the
### soul to be the highest authority.
### D. The soul is controlled, governed, and directed by God through the spirit, and it
### becomes the very person to act, express, and do things through God.
### E. The body is under the direction of the soul, and the soul is under the direction of
### the spirit with God in it as life.

## II. The flesh being the transmutation of the body:
### A. By the fall man's spirit was deadened and became out of function.
### B. The soul rose to the top to live by itself having nothing to do with the spirit
### C. The poison of Satan had entered into man's body and caused it to be transmuted to
### become the flesh
Rom. 7:18.

## II. Man being subdued and controlled by the flesh:
### A. Genesis 6:3 tells us that man not only had the flesh, but also had become flesh
### which means that man was subdued and controlled by the flesh.
### B. The flesh implies the whole being of the fallen man
Rom. 3:20; Gal. 2:16.

## IV. The flesh being the old man lived out:
### A. Objectively speaking, it is the old man; subjectively speaking, it is the flesh-
5:24; Rom. 6:6.
### B. The old man is expressed through the flesh.
### C. As the old man, it has been crucified already, but as the flesh, we need to crucify it
### in our experience.

## V. Dealing with the flesh as the practices of the body:
### A. Dealing with the sinful deeds of the body—v. 6; 8:13.
### B. Dealing with our whole being
### C. Dealing with our fleshly living.
