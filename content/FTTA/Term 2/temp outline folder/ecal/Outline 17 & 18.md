## I. God's plan, His way in His redemption, and the way of life:
### A. The Triune God as the uncreated, eternal life.
### B. Man as the created and fallen life:
#### 1. The Triune God created man as a vessel to contain God in order to
#### express Him—1 Cor. 15:45a.
#### 2. The created man has three parts—a spirit, a soul, and a body-1 Thes.
#### 5:23.
#### 3. Before God came into man, Satan, the enemy of God, first filled man
#### with sin and darkness.
### C. The incarnated, crucified, and resurrected God-man becoming our life:
#### 1. God became the second man, the last Adam, Christ, a real man, and a
#### God-man-1 Cor.15:45b.
#### 2. This God-man, at the time that He was crucified on the cross, became
#### the sin-bearer to bear all our sins as the Lamb of God—Matt. 27:45.
#### 3. After Christ's crucifixion, He was resurrected and in resurrection lives
#### forever.
#### 4. Life is in Christ, and He is life to us—Col. 3:4; 1 John 5:11.
### D. A transfer through the cross—Col. 1:13.
### E. Regeneration by having the Son as life—1 John 5:12.
### F. Transformation from glory to glory—2 Cor. 3:18:
#### 1. We need to be transformed from darkness to glory.
#### 2. When the Lord comes back, our whole body will be transfigured—
#### transformed to be a body of glory, exactly like the incarnated, crucified,
#### and resurrected Christ-1 John 3:1-2.
