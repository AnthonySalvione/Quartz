# Dealing with the World
## Lesson Fourteen
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Eph. 2:2; Rom. 12:2; Gal. 1:4; 1 John 2:15-17

## I. Its definition:
### A. The Greek word, kosmos, is used for different things:
#### 1. The material universe created by God
Matt. 25:34; John 17:15; Acts
17:24.
#### 2. The fallen human race corrupted and usurped by Satan
John 1:29;
3:16; Rom. 5:12.
#### 3. Denoting adorning, ornaments
1 Pet. 3:3.
#### 4. Denoting an order, a set form, an orderly arrangement, ordered
#### system
1 John 2:15; John 15:19; 17:14; James 4:4.
### B. The world as a whole is the satanic organized system, a systematized
### organization.

## II. The invention of human civilization:
### A. The world in the sense of a system was not created by God.
### B. Rather it was invented by Satan at a certain point in human history:
#### 1. Cain was driven out of the presence of God and lost God.
#### 2. Hence, Cain and his descendents invented the first human civilization.
### C. The fallen human race lost God as their protection, provision, and pleasure.

## III. The world being a satanic system:
### A. Being that which is more than we need for our living.
### B. Being that which possesses and usurps us.

## IV. Being clothed with the Spirit by dealing with the conscience and with
## the world:
### A. Two things put us to death; a conscience full of offenses within and the
### world, usurping, occupying, and possessing us without.
### B. Hence, we need a cleansing and purging in our conscience, and we need a
### deliverance from the worldly things.
