# Dealing with the Self
## (1)
## Lesson Eleven
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Rom. 6:6; Gal. 2:20; 5:24; Rom. 6:11; Matt. 16:24

## I. Spiritual knowledge and the self:
### A. The problem of spiritual knowledge without experience:
#### 1. To have knowledge is one thing, but to have the experiences of life is another.
#### 2. Our knowledge needs to be tested by failures, trials, temptations, and many
#### troubles.
### B. The problem of introspection in the self:
#### 1. An introspective person is very capable of taking care of himself in the best
#### way.
#### 2. For a person to overcare for himself is to always look at, think about, and
#### consider himself.
#### 3. Spiritual growth depends on the denial of the self, and the denial of the self
#### simply means to forget about the self.

## II. Three aspects of the work of the cross:
### A. The accomplished fact of our crucifixion with Christ
Rom. 6:6; Gal. 2:20.
### B. The realization of the accomplished fact—5:24; Rom. 6:11:
#### 1. Christ's death is for us because we are identified with Him and are in Him.
#### 2. They who are of Christ Jesus recognize and realize the fact that they have died,
#### so they put their flesh to death.
### C. Continually bearing the cross to deny the self
Matt. 16:24; 10:38; Mark 8:34;
Luke 9:23:
#### 1. To bear the cross is to always keep ourselves in the death of Christ, to always
#### appropriate the Lord's death.
#### 2. To bear the cross is simply to put the self, the soulish life, to death; this is the
#### way of deliverance.
#### 3. We should not analyze or look at ourselves, but stand on the fact and then bear
#### the cross.
#### 4. To be crucified on the cross is once for all, but to bear the cross is life-long.
#### 5. The real bearing of the cross for the denial of the self must be in the Spirit, not
#### of ourselves.
