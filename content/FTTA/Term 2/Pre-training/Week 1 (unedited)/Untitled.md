Morning Revival outline #4

First of all, I think for the first termers who are just coming, I would say that this matter of Morning Revival in the mornign and the practice of having time with the Lord in the morning is the most crucial aspects of this trtaining. In order to have a solid foundation in oiur christian life and survice we just have to have this time in the morning. This is how we start our day. This actually determines a lot in our life throughout the day. I ask you to take this matter very very seriously. Maybe some of you have already taken some time in the morning with the lord, but this is an endeavor that we all can advance in. This is seomthing we all can practice, meaning none of us have arrived.

I will go straight to some of the pointsi n the outline. Morning revival is for us to fellowship with the Lord. Fellowship. What is morning revival? it's fellowship. What does fellowship mean? It means mutual contact. You are contacting God, and God is contacting you. There has to be contact. You're not just here to carry out an activitiy, but you are here to contact god and for god to contact you. It is also something of a mutual flow. Something of me is flowing to god, and something of god is flowing into me. This flow takes place in our mingled spirit. This is why it is so important to contact the Lord in our spirit. How important it is to pray that the Lord would strengthen us into our inner man. CAlling on the Lord is an excellent way to touch the Spirit. Of course, the gateway to our spirit is our heart. Sometimes we want to exercise our spirit, but our heart may be in a condition that prevents us from contacting the Lord. We also need to learn how to turn our heart to the Lord.

It's good to lean to pray "Lord, soften my heart... that I would be able to receive your spirit." It's important to do this in the morning

The second point I really like: "With the Lord". Highlight these words. That means that when you are coming to have a morning time, you are not just having a morning revival as an activity or routine to check a box, but you are doing it to contact a wonderful person. Remember this. You are not coming to a book, you are coming to a person to contact that person. This is something that is very important. You're coming to a person to have a personal and affectionate relationship with Him. This should always be our goal, to contact this person. 

When you come to contact this person, there could be a lot of other things that prevent you from touching this person. Maybe surroundings, or environment... and you might not notice until you try to turn yourself to the Lord. We need to learn how to leave all of these distractions. Sometimes they're outward somethings they're inward... and they occupy our being. We need to recognize this, and determine to just contact the Lord and leave all these other things behind. We just need to say "Lord, I leave those things behind, I just want to come to you". This is what Exadus 34 is talking about! "When you go up, nobody will come with you!". You know what I thought about when I came to the Lord in the morning...? you guys! I had to leave you guys behind! We must leave everything behind, all daily activities and people. Sometiems I pray a simple prayer "No. Lord I am spending time with you. I will leave this momentarily and just come to You alone"

What do you do when you come to the Lord? According to Psalm 47:4 there are these two matters that should be most of our time with the Lord. Whatever you do, never forget that these two are activities or procedures that you can use to help spend time with the Lord. The first one is beholding. You come to the Lord to behold Him with whatever you are doing. Your goal when reading the verse is not to finish the verse, but to behold the Lord while you are reading that verse. The Lord may speak to us while we are beholding Him. Sometimes you may come to a verse that is not about the Lord, but if your heart is turned to the Lord and your goal is to behold the Lord, you will see something of Him in that verse. The second thing is to inquire. This is to allow the Lord to lead the time you have with Him. A lot of this has to do with following the annointing that is within. SOmetimes the Lord may lead us to linger on a point and not proceed onward too fast. If the Lord is leading you to stay, do not continue on. If the Lord moves on, then move on with Him. You need to follow the Lord's leading, wherever He leads you, you need to learn to follow the anointing within to behold and to inquire.

Sometimes you want to ask the Lord "Lord, how do you want to spend the time this morning?" If you learn to inquire in this way, then your morning revival will not feel dead because you have the fresh anointing with the Lord.

Just a few things before we stop so you have time to practice... These subpoints, like calling on the Lord, is a good start when beginning morning revival. Our calling is good because it is the beginning of our turn back to the Lord. It is good to mix simple prayers in with this calling, like, "Lord, I come to you. Lord I love you. Thank you Lord I can spend time with you"... some simple prayers just to open and contact the Lord.

Songs and hymns also can help us to turn and contact the Lord. Also the HWMR can help us to turn to the Lord too, but to finish it is NOT the goal. Always remember this: the goal is the person. As you are in your morning revival time, you are simultaneously entering into this person. You want to come to this person and learn to linger and follow all of the annointing within.

While we are enjoying the Lord... there may be things that come up in our being. Things that are hindering our fellowship with the Lord. With this, you need to confess. This should not be the major part of our morning revival, but it is there to help clear up our being so that we can enjoy Him fresh again in His presence.

I will stop here and give you the rest of the time to practice. You will have some time before breakfast to practice. Find the place where the Lord has for you and spend time with the Lord.
