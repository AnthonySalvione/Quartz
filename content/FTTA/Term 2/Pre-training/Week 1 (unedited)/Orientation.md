Matthew 5:23-24: “Therefore if you are offering your gift at the altar and there you remember that your brother has something against you, leave your gift there before the altar, and first go and be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift.”

Notice that there is a difference between a sacrifice and a gift. With sacrifice, there is a sin, but a gift is for fellowship with God. In Matthew 5, a person came to fellowship with God; that is why a gift was offered at the altar. There, as you initiate your fellowship with God, you remember something that is not taken care of. You remember that your brother has something against you. When I first read this—that your brother has something against you—I thought, “If he has something against me, that is his problem.” He has something against you because you got angry at him in Matthew 2. So that offense was caused by your own behavior—by your not taking care of something.
