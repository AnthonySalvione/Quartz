## CONSECRATION
### (6) CONSECRATION FOR TURNING THE AGE

**Scripture Reading:** 1 Cor. 6:19-20; Rom. 14:8; 12:1; 2 Cor. 5:14-15; Matt. 7:13-14; Rev. 11:15.

---
Let's start by reading Revelation 11:15 together out loud, two times.

![[Revelation-11#v15]]

So, are we consecrated to the Lord to see this day? To hear that trumpet? to hear those loud voices in heaven saying, the kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our lord and of his Christ. This day must be brought in. Who will bring in this day? It's the consecrated ones. 

Now let's turn to the next chapter, and begin by reading verse 7.

![[Revelation-12#v7]]

Then let's read aloud verses 10 and 11.

![[Revelation-12#v10]]
![[Revelation-12#v11]]

So, in Revelation 11:15, we see that we are the consecrated ones—living a life that brings in the day in which *the kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and His Christ*. 

Then, in chapter 12, where do we see the consecrated ones? They are the ones warring and fighting today in the spiritual battle. So that if they were to die before the Lord's return and then are part of the man-child taken to the throne—when they get there and see Satan in the heavens, they will tell him, "You don't belong here!"—because throughout their entire lives they were fighting this battle here on the earth.
