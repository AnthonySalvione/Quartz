## Matthew Sharmin (San Jose)

I received the Lord in the summer of 2017, when I opened the Bible for the first time. There I saw that everything in the Bible was absolutely true. The Lord then showed me mercy and shined His light through the Word.

I did not meet the saints until 2019. I went to the college conference, and one of the speaking brothers shared his experience of the Full Time Training in Anaheim. Since then I desired to come here—that was six years ago.

Since then, the Lord has granted me the experiences I needed to grow to be here. I needed to grow, and the Lord was waiting for me to grow. ## Emily Moo (Richmond, Canada)

I’m glad I came to the training. I’m not a church kid.

Our family started going to church in 10th grade. My dad was curious, so we went—but it wasn’t a church in the Lord’s recovery. We always attended and sat in the back.

I asked my friends, “How are we saved?” and I started crying. They asked me to call on the name of the Lord, and I just couldn’t say it.

Then I came to college in Southern California (San Diego), and I wanted to find a Christian church or club. I was Googling online and found Christians at SD, and the saints were good to me. I enjoyed the church life.

Now I’m two years out of school. When I first graduated, some saints told me I needed to go to the training—but I felt I needed to go to school again. Over those two years I worked three jobs. The first job was bad, so I had to leave. The second job, my boss fired me. That’s when I first considered the training—I realized I should go because I didn’t have a job.

Then I got a third job and I really liked it. But as the training got closer, I felt more peaceful to go. So I prayed, “Lord, if this is the right direction, should I go to the training?” The saints told me to come, and I felt the Lord speaking to me.

## Megan Lois (sent from DeSoto, Texas)

I was sent from DeSoto, Texas—if you don’t know where that is, you can ask me later.

This decision to go to the training did not come lightly. It was pretty much last-minute. Thank the Lord, I was able to short-term for a week last spring, and two words described that whole week: **enjoyable** and **practical**.

Oh my goodness—I want to overflow, but I’m running out of time. I remember thinking, “I’m seeing lots of people my age marching for Jesus Christ.” I also remember hearing Billy Graham say "What a wonderful thing it would be to see bright, clean, disciplined young people marching for Jesus Christ throughout the country". I first heard that quote in fall 2022, and at that time my love for the Lord was lacking. 

Even my dad recognized it. He asked me, “What’s your relationship with God lately?” I had no answer—I was dead silent. He said, “Well, your lack of response shows your love is lacking.”

I realized it before he even said it. It was a sign for me to pray behind closed doors. I asked the Lord, “Lord, what do You want from me? Show me why my love is lacking.”

In 2022 I didn’t know where to go, so I reached out to a sister in California who finished the training. She gave me a contact for a sister in Denton. She was serving the college students. I contacted her, we had dinner an hour away, and I continued to meet with her. It was so sweet and encouraging—really the Lord’s calling.

I went to the college conferences and enjoyed the Lord more and more. The Lord called me. I’m just trusting the process.

## Max & Mak (sent from the church in Denton)

### Max

To start—how I came into the church life: growing up, I was pursuing a music career. I came from a family of musicians, and I love music. I aspired to become a musician. That felt like the mystery of my human life—my purpose. I thought I was pursuing the greatest thing, but I was mistaken.

I went to college and met a group of believers who were not in the Lord’s recovery. They gave me the core foundational truths, and that touched me. Growing up, I was never really against God, and there wasn’t a point where I thought I didn’t believe. I was open—but I hadn’t been regenerated. There wasn’t a time when I prayed to receive the Lord; He just seemed to “sneak in.”

The first book of the Bible I read was Romans. After that, everything became clear. I saw who I was, who He was, and what He wanted for us. This captured me.

Shortly after that, there was a brother named Sooyen Davis (whom a lot of you know), and he invited me to a home meeting in Denton. From that point on, I never left—I’ve been here the whole time, going on in the church life.

It’s been about four years in the making. We were shown a video of the training, and that gave us a hunger. Over four years, the Lord worked out many things in our lives—bringing us together and bringing us to the training.

### Mak

I did not grow up in the church life. I was gained on a college campus in Denton. I always loved the Lord, and my parents took me to church for holidays and things like that. I didn’t really know much about salvation, but every time I went, I would pray to receive the Lord because I didn’t know about the security of salvation.

I signed up with the Christian students in Denton, and a sister reached out to me. What touched me was the living of the saints: I lived two and a half hours away from her, and there was an event—she drove all that way to pick me up. I was so touched. I thought, “How could somebody love me so much?”

_(tearing up)_

We were driving back to Denton. I was 19, and she asked me if I’d ever received the Lord. So we prayed again, and she said, “I have to make sure.” We prayed, and I got saved on the way back to Denton.

Then I went to my first college conference in Waco, and I got baptized. Shortly after that, I was on fire. I loved the Lord, and nothing was better than that.

Then they showed us a video of the FTTA (Full Time Training in Anaheim), and I was like, “Wow—how does a place like that exist?” I looked at the staff of our club and realized they all went to the FTTA. I wanted that for myself, and that burden stayed with me even after I got married.

He was doing a program for the next three years, and I said, “What about the training?” We prayed about it. I wanted to go now, and the Lord propelled us forward—and now here we are.
