## The Purpose of the Training

To build up the trainees in Christ in the growth of His life unto maturity.

*[[Ephesians-04#v15|Ephesians 4:15]]; [[Colossians-01#v28|Colossians 1:28]]*

To equip the trainees in the realization of the divine truths according to the Scriptures by the revelation of the Holy Spirit.

*[[2 Timothy-03#v16|2 Timothy 3:16–17]]; [[1 Timothy-02#v4|1 Timothy 2:4]]; [[2 Peter-03#v18|2 Peter 3:18]]*

To raise up the trainees in the cultivation of their spiritual capacity in the preaching of the gospel, the nourishing of the young believers, the perfecting of the saints, and the prophesying of the word of God for the building up of the local churches, consummating in the building up of the Body of Christ.

*[[Matthew-28#v19|Matthew 28:19]]; [[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|1 Thessalonians 2:7]]; [[Ephesians-04#v12|Ephesians 4:12]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v1|1 Corinthians 14:1]]*

# Conclusion—A Departing Word to the Graduates

## A personal letter written by Witness Lee:

**1995 Full-time Training — Spring Term**
**Never forget that:**

1. The Triune God's eternal economy is to produce and build up the Body of Christ for the consummation of the New Jerusalem as His heart's desire and ultimate goal.

2. The Triune God has chosen, predestinated, redeemed, and regenerated you to be a God-man for the purpose of accomplishing His eternal economy.

3. A God-man should deny his self, his natural man, and his old man by the power of Christ's resurrection that he may live Christ, magnify Christ, and walk and work with Christ by the bountiful supply of the all-inclusive Spirit, for the carrying out of God's eternal economy.

4. You have not been trained and perfected to be a preacher but to be an overcomer of the Lord—to overcome sin, the world, environmental troubles, opposition, persecutions, even at the cost of your life.

5. You must strive and struggle to reach maturity in the divine life.

6. You must fulfill your duty to produce the members of the Body of Christ, to feed and shepherd the younger believers, and to perfect the saints for the building up of the Body of Christ.

7. You should only know to work and labor for the Body of Christ, and you should not know any opinions or teachings other than the divine revelation as revealed in the New Testament concerning God's eternal economy.

8. You should keep the oneness of the Body at any cost and escape from division in the fear of Christ and of the judgment at His judgment seat.

May the abundant and abounding grace of the Lord be with you from the day of your graduation until the day He appears to you as the morning star.

*Your brother in Christ,*
*A slave in the household of the faith*

**June 24, 1995**
