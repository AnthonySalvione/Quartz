A little bit on the history of the training: the burden of training extends way back beyond the years this training started. Brother Nee established a training in the 1930s to have young people stay together to live the Body life and to be built up in their spiritual life and service. In 1952, Witness Lee came to the United States. A little bit about the training environment here: this is a very particular environment. Here in the training, these young believers in Christ, who love the Lord, have the opportunity to spend two years in this unique environment, which is conducive to growth in the divine life, the pursuit of the truth as revealed in the Scriptures, the cultivation of their spiritual capacity in service, and the development of a fine, balanced human character.

In life, they practice contacting the Lord in spirit, in prayer, to be one spirit with Him to enjoy His presence - His supplying, flowing, energizing, gentle yet dynamic inward working - for their growth and transformation.

In regard to service, they cultivate a heart of love and a burden for the salvation of their fellow man through intercessory prayer, preaching the gospel, and ministering Christ, while also learning how to coordinate with others as members in the organic Body of Christ by learning how to pray, fellowship, and work together in oneness and harmony. Lastly, in regard to character, these trainees are learning to cooperate with the Lord and to exercise themselves unto godliness, thereby building up character traits to make them more useful for God's testimony and service.

James Lee - This word by our brother touches the key burden of the recent Memorial Day conference in Dallas concerning the great need of a new revival. This new revival consists of three elements. Firstly, it is to arrive at the high peak of the revelation of God's economy, which is for God to dispense Himself into His chosen people to reproduce Himself in them in order to produce a new species, the God-man. Then, the second element is the living out of this God-man life by denying the self, the natural life, and living by another life, which is the divine life. Thirdly, it is for us to shepherd people according to God by being constituted with God, to be one with God, and to minister God to others.

This is the utmost burden on the Lord's heart in these final days. He must gain this new revival, an unprecedented revival. In the past 2,000 years, this is actually, in essence, what our Brother Lee just spoke to us about. Well, today, at this graduation of the FTTA, we have 85 dear trainees graduating from the training here. After being shepherded and drawn by the lovely Bridegroom, and being healed by this wonderful Physician, now they are going forth from the training under the controlling vision of God's economy to live this kind of a life, to shepherd others and people by saving sinners, and also to feed and shepherd the young and new believers, and then to perfect and build up the saints for the building up of the Body of Christ. This is for the bringing in of the new revival. This is what we are all striving at.

Brothers and sisters and dear trainees, as I was considering this matter, the Lord reminded me of Paul's word in 1 Thessalonians 1:3, where he says, "This is a work of faith, and a labor of love, and the endurance of hope." Such a life, such a service, is not something of our endeavoring of our natural man or of the effort of our religious self. It is something in the realm of faith, in the essence of love, and with the endurance of hope.

Here, in that portion of the Word, Brother Lee has some wonderful footnotes, where he tells us that our work is of faith, which denotes the nature and strength of the work. Our labor is of love, which indicates the motivation for and the characteristic of labor. So, our work is of faith. This is our strength. This is the nature. This is not something by sight or by feeling. This is a work of faith and also a labor of love, which motivates us and characterizes our labor. Our endurance is of hope, which is the source of endurance.

These three matters - faith, love, and hope - are the three elements that structure our Christian life according to what is mentioned in 1 Corinthians 13, the last verse, where Paul says such a life originates from the infusion of what God is through our believing into Him. This life does not originate from ourselves but originates from the infusion of God Himself, with all God is to us, through our believing into Him. It is carried out by our sacrificial love toward our loving Lord, who loved us and gave Himself for us, and also toward His members, whom He has redeemed through His death in love. This life lasts and stands, unchanging, by the sustaining power of hope, in which the believers look for their beloved Lord, who promised that He would come to take them to Himself.

This life is a life of three dimensions. In the light of the Lord's coming back, having faith as the beginning and the foundation, love as the process and construction, and hope as the consummation and the completion. Faith, hope, and love. We have the foundation, we have the construction, and we have also the completion. Faith is toward God, love is toward the people, and hope is in the Lord's coming back.

Well, may this short word of encouragement, especially to the graduating trainees, supply you and strengthen you as you go forth from this training to live this kind of a life. This is our Brother Lee's charge. This is also the highest calling we receive from the Lord. You have seen this, you have tasted this. The Lord has been shepherding you and healing you for these past two years. Now you are going forth to carry out this and to live out this God-man life, and to carry out this shepherding according to God.

Through faith, all the divine and spiritual things, which are unseen, can be substantiated in your spirit. Do not just go by what you see in your environment. This is a work of faith. Faith enables you to substantiate what is unseen. Faith enables you to receive all that God is in your spirit. Through hope, you can reap and partake of the things substantiated by faith. Dear saints, we are a people of hope. We are not hopeless people. In spite of all the difficult environments ahead of us and around us, we are full of hope. Our hope gives us the endurance. Our hope tells us that our Lord is returning.

In this way, through hope, you can reap and partake of the things substantiated by faith, and through love, you will enjoy all the things received and substantiated by faith and partaken of by hope. I hope that you would all be impressed by this. This is what our Christian life, our God-man life, is constructed with: with this faith, this hope, and this love. In this way, you will nourish yourselves, you will build up others, and you will express God for the fulfillment of God's eternal economy, to bring in an unprecedented new revival to end this age and bring back our dear Lord Jesus Christ.
