In Matthew 18 in verses 15-17, the Lord is mentioning a particular case involving a sinning brother who sinned against another brother and He talks about how this should be dealt with. The brother who was sinned against should go to the sinner brother and try to resolve it between the two of them. If he refuses to hear that brother, then he should go ahain and take one or two othes with him and see if he will listen to them. If he still wont listen to them, then he should tell it to the church. If he refuses to hear the church, the he should be regarded as a gentile or tax collector. In otherwards, someone who is outside the fellowship of the church.

Following this particular case that the Lord is using as an illustration.

The Lord gives the principle for dealing with such cases... In verses 18 through 20... he says truly I say to you whatever you bind on the earth shall have been bound in heaven. Whateber you loose on the earth shall have been loosed in heaven. So, if the church decides any person, matter or thing needs to be bound... then, they pray according to the will in the heavens, and then it is bound in the heavens. BUT the principle here is that FIRST the earth acts, and thn the heavens follow. What you see here is the earth directing the heavens. Why is that? That is because - God created man with a free will... and God will not act during time between the two eternitys - eternity past and future, he wont act independantly on man but instead waits for man on the earth to agree with what God wants to do and then ask to do it - then He will act.

In the same principle, He waits for the will on the earht to agree with the will in the heavens. Then He will act. There can be a backlog of matters that the Lord wants to carry out - and he is just waiting for the will on the earth to agree with His will to be a channel for Him to be able to act. God limits himself in this way, and it is really to His glory that He would have the church that He would have men on the earth who would agree with His will - then He would act.
