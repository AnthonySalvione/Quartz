# Brother Paul Cook’s Sharing

## Personal Background and Burden for Africa

Hallelujah! You know, I love the Lord. You know, Jesus is Lord. My favorite verse is **1 Corinthians 1:9**
**“God is faithful, through whom you were called into the fellowship of His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.”**

I just love this verse. When I am down, I say, “Paul, God is faithful.” When I am up, “Paul, God is faithful.” When I am out, God is still faithful. That is how I am still pursuing the Lord after all these years.

I live in London, and my name is Paul. If I live in London, then why Africa? I have lived in London since 1984, after moving there from Germany. I came to Stuttgart, Germany, in 1980 to see Brother Lee. I came into the church life and met the ministry in 1977. I wanted to see this “China guy” — who is this Brother Lee? So in 1980 I met him.

When he climbed the podium and spoke, you realized how much he knew the Bible. I was so impressed by him. That is how I came in.

My whole story is just the Lord Himself. I listened to your testimonies — we all have the same testimony. I was a very young man and got married very early. My wife comes from a Pentecostal background, and I come from a Catholic background. At one point I asked myself, “What am I doing here as a Catholic?” My mom was a strong Catholic, so I asked her, “Mom, why do you do this?” She said, “Son, everyone does this, so I do it.” So I questioned her more: why? Then I realized there was no need for me to be Catholic if my mom herself did not even know why she was doing it. That is why I stopped meeting there. Praise the Lord, after that I met the Lord’s recovery. I love Him so much, and I love the recovery so much. Praise the Lord.

From 1980 to 1984 I was in Germany. From 1984 until today I have lived in London. Why Africa? Because I’m African. I have a big heart for the Africans. I feel that everyone should know this Lord and the Bible. I realized that many do not know this Lord in Africa. God desires all men to be saved and to come to the full knowledge of the truth. I said, “Yes, Lord. Here we have the truth. Every human being needs this truth — including the Africans.”

---

## Prayer for London and Beginning of the Burden

In the late 1980s, Africa went through huge turmoil. So many things happened. Another time I could talk about the history of Europe, but now I will talk about Africa.

While living in London, my wife and I, along with another brother, began praying for London. At that time we had three churches in England and no churches in London. We would pray, “Lord, give us London. Give us this city.” We realized that London was an evil and very large city. The brothers kept telling me, “How could you practice the church life here?” But I kept praying, “Lord, give us this city. I still want it.” Then more couples came and moved there. Now there are six meeting halls in London. Praise the Lord.

Then, after the turmoil in Africa around 1990, at that time the church life was only in **Ghana, Nigeria, and South Africa** — only three countries out of the 54 countries on the continent. We started praying, “Lord, You need to gain this continent.” Everyone knows Africa is a dark continent. It is a dark continent. But Lord, **in Him there is no darkness at all**.

We prayed, “Lord, You have to penetrate this continent.”

Then in 1995, Brother James and Brother Dick Taylor were sent by Brother Lee to Africa for the first time. In that same year, Brother Lee sent Brother Joe Davis to London. I have served with these brothers up to today. Praise the Lord.

From 1995, when Dick and James began to visit, I felt that they needed a brother — someone to be with them who looked like them. You know what that means. That means there were places I could go where Dick and James could not go. There were so many places — small towns and villages — and all of these places needed Christ.

At that time I still had a job in London, was working, had a wife, five girls, and one boy. Lots of responsibilities. From 1995 onward, the brothers kept asking, “Paul, you need to serve the Lord full-time.” I kept praying. I told them I was looking to the Lord, but I really wasn’t. The training in London began in 1997, and the brothers kept wanting me to serve. I just said, “Okay, brothers, eventually.”

Then the brothers prayed that I would lose my job.

My boss called me and said, “Sorry, Paul, we don’t need you anymore.” So I went to the brothers’ meeting. They were like, “What’s wrong with you?” I said, “You don’t understand. It looks like I’m going to lose my job.” And when I said “lose my job,” they said, “Hallelujah! Praise the Lord!”

I couldn’t believe they had prayed for this. I said, “Did you hear what I said?” Yes — they had been praying for that. When I wasn’t in the meetings, this is what they prayed for.

That is how I began to serve. I realized that the brothers had prayed, and now I just had to say amen. I gave up everything. Honest to God, I gave up my job. I was no longer concerned in the old way about securing everything for my family. I just prayed, and I began to serve from 2000 until today.

Then I realized, “Oh! Now I have time.” You know what I mean. I could travel to all these places in Africa.

When Brother Lee passed away in 1997, we only had four countries in Africa where the church life was present. Today, we have **30 countries**. I am going to run through them, country by country, where the Lord’s recovery is.

I am so grateful that a group of brothers were willing to travel across the continent. One time in 2015, I visited so many countries that I slept in 100 different beds during one trip. Can you believe it? One hundred beds — hard ones, soft ones, benches. A lot of laboring and not a lot of rest. A lot of denying the soul, denying the self, just going to see how they were doing. Today, if I look back, all of these churches came about through **much labor**.

## North Africa

### Egypt

We only have Egypt for three or four weeks at a time during certain visits. There was the biggest book fair in Cairo, and at that book fair there were close to six million people attending. So right after the book fair, we would be there.

We gained **seventeen Arabic speakers** and **twenty-three new ones total**, the biggest number we have ever had.

Right now, in Cairo, Egypt, the saints average about **30** meeting there. We also have about **102 ministry titles in Arabic**. Cairo oversees much of the Arabic-speaking burden in that part of the world, especially including Lebanon.

We had **17 who came from Lebanon**. Right after the conference, they flew back to Beirut, and I went to visit them. I left Beirut after three days, just as the bombs started dropping in the war. If I had stayed a little longer, I probably would have died.

God is faithful. I told you. That is Egypt in a nutshell.

### Ghana

This is where the church life began in **1972**.

One time a young brother asked me, “Brother Paul, is there a Lord’s recovery in Africa?” I said, “Yes — before you were born!”

I could not tell you the history of the church life there in two days. Two people came from Los Angeles in 1971, met Brother Lee, stayed in Long Beach, and were shepherded by Howard Higashi — Brother Dennis’s uncle. Brother Howard took care of them for two whole years. In 1972, Brother Lee sent them back to Africa. So the church life actually began there in **1973**, and then I came four years later, in **1977**.

How many saints are there in Ghana? **1,569 saints**.  
How many localities? **30 localities**.

Last December, I was in Ghana for a conference, and there were **924 saints** there. The hall was so small. You all are so blessed — look at this place.

### Liberia

In Liberia, the capital is **Monrovia**. There are **45 regular saints** and **2 localities**.

The church life began there around **15 years ago**. The brother who began the church life there was traveling to Taiwan from Liberia and sat next to a brother on the plane. By the time he arrived in Taipei, he was already in the church life. He came back, finished his PhD, got his job, and later passed away — but he had already begun the church life there. Right now there is **one brother serving full-time** who went to the FTTA in Addis Ababa.

We were to have a conference in **two weeks’ time in Lagos, Nigeria**. Praise the Lord.

How many saints? There are **18 localities** and **850 saints**. But to us, that is nothing compared to 250 million people. I say, “Lord, I do not agree. There need to be more saved ones. Two hundred fifty million people? There should be more than 800!” Come on. You need to cry out to the Lord: **More!**

### Togo

Togo is right next to Ghana. The capital is **Lomé**. It is also **French-speaking**.

We have a team from Paris who every year are trained for the church life in West Africa. We hold French-speaking conferences for all the French-speaking saints in Africa. More than ten countries in West Africa are French-speaking.

Praise the Lord, we now have all the **Life-studies translated into French**. A team of brothers is now working on the **New Testament and Old Testament**. Sooner or later, we will have the whole Bible in French. Praise the Lord.

### Overview

Now you come to Central Africa.

### Central African Republic

The capital is **Bangui**.

The church life began in Cameroon when a young sister was gained. Her father was a pastor in a place called **Buea** in Cameroon. When she came into the church life, she did not even know what she was doing, but through this sister the lampstand was raised up in that country.

## Conferences, Trainings, and Ongoing Burdens

### Annual Conferences and Trainings

Every year in **April**, we have conferences in:

- Ghana
- Nigeria
- Kenya
- Ethiopia

In all four countries we use the **same outline** every April.

Then in **June**, we have a conference in **Burundi**.

At the end of **July**, we have the Southern Africa brothers’ training. Saints from places like **Malawi, Zimbabwe**, and others from the southern part come together. We have close to **100 brothers** coming together for that training.

Every **August**, we have a conference in Ethiopia. We also have:

- children’s summer school of truth
- perfecting training for the students

We have all these ways to infuse them with the Word.

Every **late August**, East African brothers come together in eastern Kenya for a four-day conference to get into the truth.

Every **September** in West Africa, saints and brothers from places like **Benin, Togo, Cameroon, Burkina Faso, and Uganda** come together. We use the same outlines, and for the trainees we use the same lessons on the eldership, so that they may be on the same page together as one.

Every **November**, we have a mini-conference in Cameroon. It is far, so it is difficult for the brothers to travel there. There are many things that prevent them from traveling, but some brothers still go every November to take care of the saints there.

Every **December**, we have conferences in:

- Ghana
- Nigeria
