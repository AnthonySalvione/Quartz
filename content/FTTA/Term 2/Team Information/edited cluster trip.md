> 2 Corinthians 5:9-10: “Therefore also we are determined, whether at home or abroad, to gain the honor of being well pleasing to Him. 2 Corinthians 5:14 says:

> 2 Corinthians 5:14: “For the love of Christ constrains us because we have judged this, that One died for all, therefore all died.”

Number two is verse 15: He died for all, that those who live may no longer live to themselves, but to Him.

2 Corinthians 5:15 says:

> 2 Corinthians 5:15: “And He died for all that those who live may no longer live to themselves but to Him who died for them and has been raised.”

So a Christian life is a life in which we do not live to ourselves, but to Him. I appreciate that you are in the Training right now to practice and to learn how to live to Him, to live to the Lord.

So His defeating the enemy is not just for our sake, but for God’s sake, that He would have materials for the building.

There is also a risk we run. In Ephesians 4:17-24, because of the world situation, we run the risk of falling back to the world and being darkened in understanding. For this, the apostle in Ephesians tells us that we did not so learn Christ.

Ephesians 4:17-24 says:

> Ephesians 4:17-24: “This therefore I say and testify in the Lord, that you no longer walk as the Gentiles also walk in the vanity of their mind, being darkened in their understanding, alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance which is in them, because of the hardness of their heart; who, being past feeling, have given themselves over to lasciviousness to work all uncleanness in greediness. But you did not so learn Christ, if indeed you have heard Him and have been taught in Him as the reality is in Jesus, that you put off, as regards your former manner of life, the old man, which is being corrupted according to the lusts of the deceit, and that you be renewed in the spirit of your mind and put on the new man, which was created according to God in righteousness and holiness of the reality.”
