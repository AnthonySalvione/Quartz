# Lesson Six — The Last Adam Became a Life-giving Spirit (2)

## I. The last Adam (Christ in the flesh) becoming a life-giving Spirit being the fulfillment of [[John-07#v39|John 7:39]]:

### A. Receiving Christ as the life-giving Spirit, the living water, by drinking Him—[[Romans-07#v37|v. 37]]; [[Romans-04#v14|4:14a]].

### B. The living water, the life-giving Spirit, becoming in us a fountain of water springing up into eternal life—[[Romans-04#v14|v. 14b]]:

#### 1. The fountain being the Father as the source of life—[[Romans-05#v26|5:26]].

#### 2. The Father in the Son emerging as the spring for the manifestation and expression of life—[[Romans-01#v4|1:4]]; [[1 John-01#v2|1 John 1:2]].

#### 3. The Son as the Spirit of life springing up to be a river—[[Psalm-36#v8|Psa. 36:8-9]]; [[Psalm-46#v4|46:4]]; [[John-07#v38|John 7:38]].

#### 4. The triune processed God flowing into (into meaning to be, or to become) the New Jerusalem, the city of water, the city of life, the totality of the eternal life—[[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]].

### C. The life-giving Spirit, as the consummation of the processed and consummated Triune God, flowing out of our innermost being, our spirit, as rivers of living water.

### D. Being measured by the Lord for the increase of the flow of life—[[Ezekiel-47#v1|Ezek. 47:1]], [[Ezekiel-47#v2|2]], [[Ezekiel-47#v12|12]]; [[Isaiah-06#v1|Isa. 6:1-8]].

## II. The last Adam, Christ in the flesh, becoming a life-giving Spirit in resurrection to carry out God's organic salvation to accomplish His heart's desire to build up the Body of Christ which consummates the New Jerusalem:

### A. Christ being the vivifying Spirit to carry out His desire to give life—[[John-10#v10|John 10:10b]]; [[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]], [[Romans-08#v10|10]], [[Romans-08#v6|6]], [[Romans-08#v11|11]].

### B. The Lord being the Spirit who gives life—[[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v17|17]].

### C. Without His being the life-giving Spirit, we cannot be saved organically—[[Romans-05#v10|Rom. 5:10]]:

#### 1. Giving life to and indwelling our spirit in regeneration—[[Ezekiel-36#v26|Ezek. 36:26]]; [[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Tim. 4:22]].

#### 2. Cherishing us in His humanity with love and nourishing us in His divinity with the life-supply as our spiritual food in His organic shepherding—[[John-14#v26|John 14:26]]; [[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[1 Peter-02#v25|1 Pet. 2:25]].

#### 3. Making us holy with His divine nature through Himself as the sanctifying Spirit-Word so that we can be the holy city—[[Ephesians-05#v25|Eph. 5:25-27]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]].

#### 4. Renewing us as the renewing Spirit—[[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5]]; [[Ephesians-04#v23|Eph. 4:23]].

#### 5. Transforming us as the transforming Spirit for God's building through conformation and glorification—[[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]]; [[Romans-08#v28|Rom. 8:28-29]]; [[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]; [[John-17#v22|John 17:22]].
