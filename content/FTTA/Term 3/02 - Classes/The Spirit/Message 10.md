# Lesson Eleven — The Compound Indwelling, Life-dispensing Spirit in [[Romans-08|Romans 8]] (1)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]], [[Romans-08#v9|9-11]]

## I. The Spirit of life, the Spirit of God, the Spirit of Christ, Christ Himself, and the indwelling Spirit in [[Romans-08#v2|Romans 8:2]], 9-11 all referring to the compound Spirit who gives life.

## II. The processed Triune God consummated to be the law of the Spirit of life:

### A. The Spirit of life being the compound Spirit—[[Exodus-30#v23|Exo. 30:23-25]]:

#### 1. Being a mingling of the divinity, humanity, human living, death and resurrection of Christ with the Spirit of God to become the holy ointment—[[Exodus-30#v23|vv. 23-25]].

#### 2. The supplying element of this compound Spirit being bountiful and excellent—[[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19b]].

### B. The law of this Spirit of life being the consummated Triune God—[[Revelation-22#v17|Rev. 22:17a]]:

#### 1. Becoming the law of life with its natural life-capacities and its spontaneous life-power—[[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2a]].

#### 2. Freeing the believers from the law of sin and of death, solving for them the problem of sin and death—[[Romans-08#v2|v. 2b]].

### C. The operation and dispensing of the law of the Spirit of life in the three parts of the believers:

#### 1. Beginning from the believers' spirit—[[Romans-08#v9|vv. 9-10]].

#### 2. Continuing in the believers' soul—[[Romans-08#v6|v. 6]]; [[Romans-12#v2|12:2b]].

#### 3. Consummated in the believers' body—[[Romans-08#v11|8:11]].
