6 Brothers and 6 Sisters

Several things I want to respond to from your speaking related to the class. I'd like to mention, in general, just in your learning for speaking for the Lord,

We will touch a point in these life study messages of James, having an influence, because of one's observing him and seeing something of Christian perfection, something of good in James, and then they picked up what he was doing, not discerning if it was Christ. We can be the exact same way in the Lord's Recovery. We can be the same. We can observe ones in the way that they are living, and think, "Wow, that is a godman! Right there!" And outwardly we begin to do what they do.

The thing is that they may be living Christ, but we are not living Christ... we just picked up on something that looked good, sounded good, then we start to practice. Then it becomes our habit.

So, applying this to our speaking, there are some things that have kind of crept in with our speaking... which, if you are a junior higher or highschooler I wouldnt say this, but because you are in the training and you are here to learn how to speak for the lord, i am going to sya soething.

Um... when we begin our speaking, there is no need to begin with "Praise the Lord" or "Amen" ... several of you began with "Amen." I would have some confidence that that is probably your habit. That that wasn't your spirit saying "Amen" to something, but it is your habit. If I were to listen to all your speaking, you would begin with an Amen.

Even some of you might try to justify it - "I am saying Amen to the person going before me" - i would say, probably if you went first, you would probably still begin with an Amen... i dont believe you are in such close fellowship with the Lrod that you are giving Him an amen for what he is saying to you... but OUTWARDLY it looks very spiritual - we see it, get impressed by it.. and now our habit is - AMEN.

Now, we should praise the Lord, but when you say "Praise the Lord" are you praising Him? I'm not getting you on the tree of knowledge, but illustrating a very small thing - how we could be just like what we are reading here, influenced by someone's outward Christian living. We get impressed by how someone speaks, then we take it as our habit. When you get impressed, you pick up a habit.
