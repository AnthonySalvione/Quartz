# God’s Economy — Lesson One — Definitions of God’s Economy

### Introduction

The reason I asked about economics is because—again, just for the sake of the first class and framing the matter—if you were to define economics, would you like to give it a shot?

Ramos: Economics is the study of the production, distribution, and exchange of goods and services among multiple parties.

That is great—really impressive. Not a wasted degree! I’m glad you got that. If I were to rephrase it, you could say that economics is the study of the system or arrangements by which resources, goods, and services are produced, distributed, and consumed. That is human economics.

So, what did you have for breakfast today? Eggs? Great example. Did you enjoy your eggs? How did the eggs get to you? That is economics. How is a good produced? How is it distributed and consumed? That is simply what economics is. Not to make you mental about your next breakfast, but you could look at it and realize, “This is a good that was produced somewhere, distributed in some way, and now I am consuming it.” That is how the world works. That is everything. So, that is economics. Try to keep this in mind.

We are going to move through the outline in clusters. The first three Roman numerals are the most straightforward definitions of GE. In light of that definition of human economics, I think these should make some sense.

### I. An economy is an arrangement for getting things done.

### II. God’s economy is God’s household arrangement, God’s household administrative arrangement.

### III. God’s economy is God’s planned administration to carry out His eternal purpose.

Obviously, I could linger on any one of these points, and it could become a whole message. I would say that you could cluster these three together as *basic definitions*.

Now, in Roman numerals IV through VI, there is another word that captures this aspect of God’s economy.

### IV. God’s economy is God’s distribution of Himself in Christ in faith.

### V. God’s economy is God’s plan to dispense Himself into His chosen, predestinated, and redeemed people as their life, their life supply, and their everything.

### VI. God’s economy is God’s household administration to dispense the divine riches of the Triune God as life and life supply to His chosen and redeemed people.

Okay, if you were to use one word to identify the emphasis of these definitions of God’s economy, I would say that the key word or emphasis is *dispensing* or *dispense*. There is a lot here. Again, I want to save time for you all to share. In all these points, the Spirit is implied. Even though the Spirit is not explicitly mentioned, He is implied. Actually, what this implies is the process through which the Triune God passed.

So, the notes I would take here are: *dispensing*, the *processed and consummated Triune God*, and *the Spirit*.

In Roman numerals VII and VIII, there is another word.

### VII. God’s economy is the arrangement of the eternal plan of God’s household administration.

### VIII. The economy of God is His plan and arrangement out of His desire and purpose. The dispensing of God is His dispensing and distributing according to His plan and arrangement.

*Arrangement* is a subpoint that falls under dispensing. There will be three key words that come out of this outline. Arrangement is part of dispensing. It is *crucial*, but it is somewhat ancillary to dispensing. Arrangement refers to everything outward.

Are you all happy with your team assignments? Do you realize that your team assignment is God’s arrangement for His dispensing? That is an arrangement, but that arrangement is *for* God’s dispensing. It is part of the Triune God’s plan for dispensing.

### IX. God’s economy is not a matter of outward things but of Christ coming into us as food.

### X. God’s economy is that we eat Christ and become constituted with Christ.

The word that is not here, but which we use all the time, is *mingling*. I would say that this is the second key word from this outline. The first is *dispensing*. Dispensing includes God’s arrangement, but it is also very much related to the Triune God passing through a process and reaching us as the Spirit. Dispensing is on God’s side, and the arrangement is somewhat outward. *Mingling is on our side.* It involves our eating, digestion, and assimilation—how *we* process God. Dispensing is God’s process; mingling is *our* process.

That is very subjective. Both dispensing and mingling are not once for all. They are not merely day by day, meal by meal, or even hour by hour, but *minute by minute*. How would you feel in this Training if you had only one meal a day?

During this summer, I have been involved in an intense something-or-other, and for most of the summer I haven’t been able to have lunch. Don’t feel sorry for me. I made this comment: “Wow, I’ve been having lunch my whole life except for this summer.” This is not normal. It is not good. Don’t you want lunch in ninety minutes? Some of you are already thinking about lunch.

God’s dispensing and our mingling are day by day and moment by moment, not once for all. You should be happy about this. It also means that you cannot rush these things.

### XI. The economy of God is the Triune God working Himself into us, the tripartite men. He desires to be man’s life, content, and complete supply so that man would become God’s expression and representation.

### XII. God’s economy is to work Himself as the Triune God into us, the tripartite men, so that we would have His life and nature to become the sons of God and the members of Christ, constituted as the Body of Christ, which is the church, the expression of Christ.

### XIII. The economy of God is the Triune God working Himself into tripartite men so that they may become the members of Christ, constituted as the Body of Christ.

*Expression* is the one word I would emphasize in these three points. Expression implies something *corporate*. Here in these definitions, you see the church, the Body of Christ. Again, I can give you only a quick voice-over here—you will spend more time on this—but these are the three great themes or parts of GE:

1. Dispensing
2. Mingling
3. Expression

The expression here is a corporate expression. This is not about your individual pursuit; eventually, there is a corporate expression. Let’s say these three words together.

If you can get this as a *framework* now, it will be really helpful when you study GE. The ministry is very faithful because this is the theme of the Scriptures. At certain points, *dispensing* is emphasized, and this is very much related to the Triune God, His process, and even His arrangement in our circumstances. At other times, *mingling* is emphasized, which involves our personal enjoyment and assimilation, how we respond to the arrangement, and how we take in God. All of that is on the mingling side. Then there is the aspect that *all of this is for the corporate expression*.

I am not trying to give you an academic framework. Rather, I want to help you make sense of all the different things you read and understand, at a governing level, how these things fit together.

Even when you prepare to share—I don’t mean so much in this class, but more in the prophesying meetings or, in the future, if you are sharing a message with the young people or college students—after you have prepared and have some burden, it is good to *check it* against this. If we ask, “Is this a word that is intrinsically related to GE?” you may respond, “Well, it is in the Bible.” But in your *burden*, is there something related to God’s dispensing? In the application, is there something of God’s mingling? As a result, is there something for God’s corporate expression? Again, this is not a checklist, but it will actually help calibrate you.
