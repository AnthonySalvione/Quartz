I have never lost my thirst for GOd. I don't mean that I am automatically an overcomer... or that kind of thing... I am just testifying to you. I just want God. My soul thirsts for God, for the living God. These are psalmiosts in THE OLD TESTEMENT. They are not even close to what we have... these are saints, they are real lovers of Jehovah.
