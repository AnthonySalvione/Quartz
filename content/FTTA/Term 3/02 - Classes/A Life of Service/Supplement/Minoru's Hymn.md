# “Do You Love Me More Than These?”

*(Tune: The South Wind, an Irish melody)*

## 1

“Do you love Me more than these?”  
Do you now still follow Me?  
You were told Messiah was found,  
To Jordan you came around;  
I said, “henceforth you shall be called a stone,”  
You’ve fit for God’s building alone;  
Since I changed your name on that fateful day,  
From you I ne’er turned away.

## 2

“Do you love Me more than these?”  
Will you still come after Me?  
I once saw you cast a net,  
By the Lake Gennesaret.  
I said, “Come after Me” to you, My friend,  
“I’ll make you a fisher of men.”  
You said, “Lord, I’m a sinner,” down on your knees,  
Then you left your all for Me.

## 3

“Do you love Me more than these?”  
Do you still My word believe?  
Midst the waves of Galilee,  
I walked to you on the sea.  
I said, “Come to Me, without fear nor doubt,”  
When “Save me, Lord!” you cried out;  
In that stormy trial, dangerous circumstance,  
You’re safe in My keeping hands.

## 4

“Do you love me more than these?”  
Do you still remember Me?  
I asked in Caesarea land:  
“Who is He—the Son of Man?”  
You said “You are the Christ, the Anointed One,  
Of the living God, the Son!”  
I said, “I’ll build My church on what you see;”  
“Take up your cross, follow Me.”

## 5

“Do you love Me more than these?”  
Do you still My glory see?  
In Mount Hermon’s sparkling light,  
My brilliance filled your sight;  
From the cloud rang out, “My Beloved Son,  
Hear Him now, the Chosen One”  
In My splendor, honor, and majesty,  
Your eyes witnessed none but Me!

## 6

“Do you love Me more than these?”  
Are you still faithful to Me?  
“I’ll never be stumbled,” you claimed,  
“I will die for You just the same.”  
I said, “Surely thrice My name you’ll deny,”  
You failed, looked at me, and cried;  
“But I prayed for you that your faith remains,  
Once you’ve turned to Me again.”

## 7

“Do you love Me more than these?”  
Do I know you still love Me?  
Tell Me that your love is deep,  
Go feed and shepherd my sheep.  
I said, “When you’re young you could freely tread,  
When you’re old you will be led;”  
Ask Me not about others’ destiny,  
“Oh, Simon, you follow Me.”
