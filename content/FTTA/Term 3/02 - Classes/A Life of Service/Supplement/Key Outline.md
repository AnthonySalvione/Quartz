# “Do You Love Me More than These?”
## I. In John 1 Andrew led his brother Simon to the Lord Jesus, believing that He was the Messiah ([[Luke-22#v41|v. 41]]); when Simon was first brought to the Lord, He changed his name from Simon to Peter, saying, “You shall be called Cephas (which is interpreted, Peter)” ([[Luke-22#v42|v. 42b]]):¹
### A. Peter means a *stone*; in [[Matthew-16#v18|Matthew 16:18]] the Lord mentioned this word when He spoke to Peter about the building of the church; it must have been from this that Peter obtained the concept of living stones for the building of a spiritual house ([[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]]), which is the church.²
### B. The stone here denotes a work of transformation that brings forth material for God’s building—[[1 Corinthians-03#v12|1 Cor. 3:12]].
## II. In Luke 5 we see that when the Lord Jesus called Peter, He performed a miracle related to fishing; this miracle attracted Peter to the Man-Savior—[[1 Corinthians-03#v1|vv. 1-11]]:³
### A. Verse 2 says, “He saw two boats lying by the lake, and the fishermen had gotten out of them and were washing the nets”; the Lord then got into Simon’s boat and asked him to put out a little from the land ([[1 Corinthians-03#v3|v. 3]]).
### B. The Lord taught the crowds from the boat, and when He had ceased speaking, He said to Simon, “Put out into the deep and let down your nets for a catch” ([[1 Corinthians-03#v4|v. 4]]); Simon answered, “Master, through the whole night we toiled and caught nothing; but based on Your word I will let down the nets” ([[1 Corinthians-03#v5|v. 5]]):
#### 1. Peter was a professional fisherman, the lake had an abundance of fish, and night was the proper time for fishing; nevertheless, they did not catch anything.
#### 2. It must have been that the Lord exercised His sovereignty to keep the fish away; this was an exercise of His divine attributes.
#### 3. The reason the Lord exercised His sovereignty in this way was that His intention was to attract Simon and his brother; therefore, at the proper time the Lord commanded the fish to come near; as a result, “they enclosed a great number of fish, and their nets began to tear apart” ([[1 Corinthians-03#v6|v. 6]]).
#### 4. Both boats were filled to the point of sinking ([[1 Corinthians-03#v7|v. 7]]); “when Simon Peter saw this, he fell down at Jesus’ knees, saying, Depart from me, for I am a sinful man, Lord” ([[1 Corinthians-03#v8|v. 8]]); we are told that “amazement had taken possession of him and all who were with him, because of the catch of fish which they took” ([[1 Corinthians-03#v9|v. 9]]).
### C. In 5:10 the Lord Jesus said to Simon, “Do not be afraid. From now on you will be catching men alive”:
#### 1. This was the Lord’s calling of Peter by a miracle in fishing.
#### 2. The Greek word for *catch alive* is *zogreo*, compounding of *zoos*, “living,” and *agreuo*, “to catch”; hence, “to catch alive,” to take captive alive in war, instead of killing.
#### 3. The common fishermen catch fish unto death; but Peter was called by the Lord to be a fisher of men ([[Matthew-04#v19|Matt. 4:19]]) that he might catch men unto life ([[Acts-02#v38|Acts 2:38]]; [[Acts-11#v18|11:18]]).
## III. Peter witnessed the miracle of the Lord walking on the sea—[[Matthew-14#v22|Matt. 14:22-33]]:⁴
### A. In Matthew 14 the Lord compelled the disciples to step into a boat and to go before Him to the other side; then, after He sent the crowds away, He went up to the mountain privately to pray—[[Matthew-14#v22|vv. 22-23]].
### B. Verses 24 and 25 say, “Now the boat was already in the midst of the sea, being battered by the waves, for the wind was contrary. And in the fourth watch of the night, He came to them, walking on the sea”:
#### 1. While His disciples were distressed by the waves, the Lord walked on the sea.⁵
#### 2. This testifies that He is the Creator and the Ruler of the universe—[[Job-09#v8|Job 9:8]].
### C. In [[Matthew-14#v28|Matthew 14:28-31]] the Lord taught Peter to walk in the storm by faith and not by sight:⁶
#### 1. Verse 28 says, “Peter answered Him and said, Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water”; when the Lord said, “Come,” Peter coming down from the boat, “walked on the water and came toward Jesus” ([[Matthew-14#v29|v. 29]]):
##### a. It was a miracle that Peter could walk on the water; he walked on the waves by faith.
##### b. Faith is our action upon the word of the Lord; the Lord said to Peter, “Come,” and Peter took that word, acted upon it, and walked on the waves.
#### 2. Verse 30 says of Peter, “But seeing the strong wind, he became frightened; and as he began to sink, he cried out, saying, Lord, save me!”
##### a. Peter came down from the boat and walked on the waves by faith in the Lord’s word ([[Matthew-14#v29|v. 29]]); however, when he saw the strong wind, his faith vanished.
##### b. Peter should have walked only by faith in the Lord’s word, not by looking at the circumstances, that is, by sight.
##### c. In following the Lord we should walk by faith, not by sight—[[2 Corinthians-05#v7|2 Cor. 5:7]].
#### 3. When Peter cried out for help, “Jesus stretched out His hand and took hold of him and said to him, You of little faith, why did you doubt?”—[[Matthew-14#v31|Matt. 14:31]]:
##### a. Since the Lord said to Peter, “Come” ([[Matthew-14#v29|v. 29]]), Peter should have stood on that word and should not have doubted; hence, the Lord rebuked him.
##### b. Faith comes from the Lord’s word and stands on the Lord’s word; as long as we have the Lord’s word, we should simply believe in His word and not doubt.
## IV. Peter received a revelation from God that the Lord Jesus was “the Christ, the Son of the living God,” and the Lord told Peter that He would build the church upon Himself as the rock—[[Matthew-16#v16|Matt. 16:16-18]]:⁷
### A. *The Christ*, referring to the anointed One of God, speaks of the Lord’s commission, whereas the *Son of the living God*, referring to the second of the Triune God, speaks of His person; His commission is to accomplish God’s eternal purpose through His crucifixion, resurrection, ascension, and second coming, whereas His person embodies the Father and consummates in the Spirit for a full expression of the Triune God.⁸
### B. In verse 18 the Lord Jesus said, “And I also say to you that you are Peter, and upon this rock I will build My church”:
#### 1. The Father’s revelation concerning Christ is only the first half of the great mystery, which is Christ and the church ([[Ephesians-05#v32|Eph. 5:32]]); hence, the Lord needed to reveal to Peter the second half also, which concerns the church.⁹
#### 2. The Greek word for “church” is *ekklesia*, meaning an out-calling; this word is used in reference to a called-out congregation; *My church* indicates that the church is of the Lord, not of any other person or thing.¹⁰
### C. Immediately after receiving the revelation from the heavenly Father ([[Matthew-16#v17|Matt. 16:17]]), Peter turned to his mind and was utilized by Satan to frustrate the Lord from going to suffer crucifixion ([[Matthew-16#v21|vv. 21-23]]):¹¹
#### 1. The Lord rebuked Peter, saying, “Get behind Me, Satan...for you are not setting your mind on the things of God, but on the things of men” ([[Matthew-16#v23|v. 23]]); Christ perceived that it was not Peter but Satan who was frustrating Him from taking the cross; this reveals that our natural man, which is not willing to take the cross, is one with Satan.¹²
#### 2. “Then Jesus said to His disciples, If anyone wants to come after Me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me”—[[Matthew-16#v24|v. 24]]:
##### a. To deny our self is to lose our soul-life, the natural life—[[Matthew-16#v25|v. 25]]; [[Luke-09#v24|Luke 9:24]].¹³
##### b. The cross is not merely a suffering; it is also a killing; it kills and terminates the criminal:¹⁴
###### (1) Christ first bore the cross and then was crucified; we, His believers, first were crucified with Him and now bear the cross.
###### (2) To us, to bear the cross is to remain under the killing of the death of Christ for the terminating of our self, our natural life, and our old man.
###### (3) In so doing we deny our self that we may follow the Lord.
## V. Peter not only received the revelation concerning Christ as the Son of God but also saw the vision of the transfigured Christ—[[Matthew-17#v1|Matt. 17:1-5]]; [[Mark-09#v2|Mark 9:2-9]]; [[Luke-09#v28|Luke 9:28-36]]:¹⁵
### A. In Caesarea Philippi, Peter received the revelation from the heavenly Father that Christ is the Son of God; a week later, on the Mount of Transfiguration, Peter saw the vision of Christ as the Son of God; He saw the transfigured, glorified, and radiant Christ whose face shone like the sun; he beheld Christ in the full appearance of God—[[Matthew-17#v2|Matt. 17:2]].
### B. After the disciples saw the transfigured Christ, Moses and Elijah appeared to them, conversing with Christ—[[Matthew-17#v3|v. 3]]:
#### 1. When Peter saw not only the transfigured Christ but also Moses and Elijah conversing with Him, he became so excited that he spoke nonsense, making a proposal that he make three tents, one for the Lord, one for Moses, and one for Elijah ([[Matthew-17#v4|v. 4]]); by making this proposal, Peter uplifted Moses and Elijah, two sons of man, to the same level as Christ, the Son of God.
#### 2. Peter’s proposal offended God; hence, God shocked the disciples by interrupting Peter’s speaking, declaring that Christ is His Son, the Beloved, and that the disciples must hear only Him; ultimately, God took Moses and Elijah away, leaving no one except Christ Himself alone—[[Matthew-17#v5|vv. 5-8]].
#### 3. The law and the prophets were shadows and prophecies, not the reality, which is Christ; now that Christ, the reality, is here, the shadows and prophecies are no longer needed; no one except Christ should remain in the New Testament.
## VI. The Lord told Peter that he would deny Him—[[Matthew-26#v33|Matt. 26:33-35]]; [[Luke-22#v31|Luke 22:31-34]]:¹⁶
### A. In [[Luke-22#v31|Luke 22:31-32]] the Lord said, “Simon, Simon, behold, Satan has asked to have you all to sift you as wheat. But I have made petition concerning you that your faith would not fail; and you, once you have turned again, establish your brothers.”
### B. According to verse 33, Peter said, “Lord, I am ready to go with You both to prison and to death”; the Lord replied, “I tell you, Peter, a rooster will not crow today until you deny three times that you know Me” ([[Luke-22#v34|v. 34]]).
### C. After the Lord was arrested, “Peter was following at a distance” ([[Luke-22#v54|v. 54b]]); this is an indication that he would deny the Lord; Peter then sat with a number of others by the fire that was kindled in the middle of the courtyard ([[Luke-22#v55|v. 55]]); this is another indication that he was about to deny the Lord.¹⁷
### D. Verses 56 and 57 say, “And a certain servant girl, seeing him seated in the light of the fire, looked intently at him and said, This man was with Him too. But he denied it, saying, I do not know Him, woman.”
### E. After Peter denied the Lord two more times, a rooster crowed, and “Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how He had said to him, Before a rooster crows today, you will deny Me three times. And he went outside and wept bitterly” ([[Luke-22#v61|vv. 61-62]]):
#### 1. On the night that the Lord Jesus was betrayed, Peter was full of confidence that he would follow the Lord; however, when the test came, Peter denied the Lord three times right to His face—[[Luke-22#v55|vv. 55-61]].¹⁸
#### 2. The Lord Jesus intentionally allowed Peter to fall in order to break his confidence in his abilities and ways and to break nearly all his desire for the Lord.¹⁹
## VII. In John 21 the Lord Jesus restored Peter by asking him, “Do you love Me more than these?”—[[Luke-22#v15|v. 15]]:²⁰
### A. In chapter 21 Peter was humble and very low; undoubtedly, he was quite disappointed; thus, the Lord came in to recover, strengthen, and restore him:²¹
#### 1. It is quite meaningful that, in front of all the other disciples, the Lord asked Peter, “Do you love Me more than these?” The Lord seemed to be saying, “Simon, have you forgotten that a few days ago you said, in the presence of all the others, that although they would forsake Me, you would follow Me to death? That was your word. Now, Simon, I ask you if you love Me more than these do?”
#### 2. Peter had lost his self-confidence and could say nothing except, “Lord, You know that I love You”; in other words, Peter seemed to be saying, “I Don’t know whether I love You or not; Lord, you know”; here we see a man who had become a tested and broken person.
### B. After restoring Peter’s love toward Him, the Lord Jesus charged him, saying, “Feed My lambs,” “Shepherd My sheep,” and “Feed My sheep”—[[Luke-22#v15|vv. 15-17]]:
#### 1. Lamb-feeding is by the nourishing with the riches of the inner life, and sheep-shepherding is for the building of the church.
#### 2. Shepherding is for the flock ([[Luke-10#v14|10:14]], [[Luke-10#v16|16]]), which is the church ([[Acts-20#v28|Acts 20:28]]); therefore, it is related to God’s building ([[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]).
### C. After predicting Peter’s martyrdom ([[John-21#v18|John 21:18-19]]), the Lord said to him, “Follow Me”:
#### 1. We all must follow the Lord as the indwelling One.
#### 2. As verse 18 indicates, we must not follow the Lord by our own will but according to His leading; our following Him even unto death is to glorify God—[[John-21#v19|v. 19]].
