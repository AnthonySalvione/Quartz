# Romans 8 and Hebrews 4

| Romans 8 (Revelation/Vision)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Hebrews 4 (Application)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

| The fourfold life in Romans 8  <br>  <br>• In the Spirit of life — v. 2  <br>• In our spirit — v. 10  <br>• In our mind — v. 6  <br>• In our body — v. 11                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | First, in verse 12 we see the dividing of the soul from the spirit by the living and operative word of God (logos—verse 12 is based on the word in the Old Testament referred to in 3:7—4:11). To experience this dividing:  <br>  <br>• We must read the Word with understanding, by which we see that we should follow our spirit and not our soul. Christ is in our spirit.  <br>• We need to love the Lord and abide in His presence to receive his enlightenment (through His eyes) as we read the Word.  <br>  <br>We must learn the difference between our soul and our spirit and discern the distinction between the two. Then we will never be confused (v. 13). When we are in our spirit, the Lord's eyes are on us, and we are clear. |
| Romans 8 reveals the processed Triune God dispensing Himself in His Divine Trinity into the three parts of our being—spirit (v. 10), soul (v. 6), and body (v. 11).  <br>  <br>• First, Christ comes into our spirit and makes our spirit life (v. 10)  <br>• Then from our spirit Christ spreads into our mind to make our mind (and entire soul) life  <br>• Finally, the indwelling Spirit gives life to our mortal body, making our body life | One example of discerning the difference between our soul and our spirit is to discern the source of our words (and others’ words—cf. Matt. 16:22-23). This will be an issue of the dividing of our soul from our spirit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| _The crucial step_ is the dispensing into our mind, representing our soul, for the dispensing reaches our body through our soul.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Hebrews 4:16 tells us to come forward (continually) to the throne of grace, which is both in the heavens and in our spirit. This is the way for us to experience the dispensing in Romans 8. To come forward to the throne of grace, we need to:  <br>  <br>• Turn to our spirit (2 Cor. 3:16).  <br>• Exercise our spirit to call on the Lord, which causes us to be in our spirit (1 Cor. 12:3).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| In order to experience the dispensing of the processed Triune God into our three parts, we must discern our soul from our spirit and learn to deny our soul, our very self, by setting our mind on our spirit. Verse 4 says that we must walk according to the mingled spirit (instead of exercising the mind and will of our soul to keep the law), and verse 6 says that we must set our mind on our mingled spirit (instead of on our flesh).  | When we come forward to the throne of grace in our spirit, we receive grace and experience the dispensing of the processed Triune God into the three parts of our being. [This is similar to Ephesians 3:16-19—when the Father strengthens us through the Spirit into our inner man, our regenerated spirit, Christ makes His home in our hearts, and eventually we are filled unto all the fullness of God.]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | * The only way to overcome the evil heart of unbelief (Heb. 3:12) and the disobedience and rebellion (3:15) in our soul is to mix the Word with faith (4:2) by turning to and exercising our spirit, as Caleb and Joshua did (Num. 13:30; 14:6-9, 24). Christ as the good land is in our spirit (2 Tim. 4:22).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
