We have a relatively short chapter for this mroning, but it has a couple very important points. The first important point is the law of the Spirit of life, which is a very particular expression. It occurs only once in the entire Bible. We know the law is a principle, something that occurs without any effort or intentino... like the law of gravitry. In this universe we have laws, we can depend on them becaues they function and because they work automatically. We just need to use them to our benefit.

So, here we have a most wonderful law, the law of the Spirit of life.

Law of the Spirit of life - a compound title. A of B of C. They are all equal to each other.

Why does a certain brother have a certain function. It is not because of *him*... God created him in a certain way, but it is by the law of the Spirit of life that causes us to function. This is a divine function for the Body of Christ.
