# NOTES ON THE SUPPLEMENTAL READING FOR CHAPTER 5

*From CWWL, 1978, vol. 2, pp. 17-30*

## I. God's intention in His economy is to make human beings His sons to constitute the Body of Christ—[[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[Romans-12#v4|12:4-5]]

### A. In order for human beings to become sons of God, these human beings must possess and live by another life—the divine life.

### B. God's economy is not to build up a religion but to impart Himself as life into us and to cause us, His chosen people, to live not by the human life but by the divine life:

#### 1. The central point of God's economy is that we live by another life; as human beings, we possess the human life, but through regeneration we have become children of God, who possess the divine life—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]].

#### 2. God requires that we live not by the natural life but by the divine life, which we received through regeneration.

#### 3. In [[Galatians-02#v20|Galatians 2:20]], *I am crucified with Christ* means that we have been terminated; this being the case, it should be "no longer I who live, but...Christ who lives in me."

#### 4. We may know this verse but may not practice it, because it is difficult for one who has a life of his own to live by another life.

#### 5. The genuine Christian life is to live not by our natural life but by Christ as life—[[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]].

#### 6. If we are honest, we will admit that the standard for our living is mainly morality and ethics and not Christ; if we steal something, we will immediately condemn ourselves, but if we fail to live by Christ as our life, we may have no sensation that we are wrong.

### C. We must see that if we do not live Christ, we are far off from God's intention; as believers in Christ, we need to live not by tradition or morality but by another person, by Christ as our life:

#### 1. Life is neither a matter of being loud nor a matter of being silent; it is not a condition, situation, or any outward activity; life in the biblical sense is Christ Himself—[[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]]; [[John-11#v25|John 11:25]]; [[John-14#v6|14:6]].

#### 2. Today this person who is life is also the Spirit, the life-giving Spirit; if Christ were not the Spirit, He could never be life to us—[[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v17|2 Cor. 3:17]].

#### 3. Since Christ as the life-giving Spirit is life ([[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]]), to live by the divine life simply means to live by the life-giving Spirit.

#### 4. In incarnation Christ was God becoming a man, a God-man possessing two lives—the human life, the life of Jesus the Son of Man, and the divine life, the life of the Son of God.

##### a. The Lord Jesus lived not by His human life but by His divine life; He always put His human life under the death of the cross for the termination of the human life so that the divine life might be expressed in resurrection—[[John-05#v19|John 5:19]]; [[John-06#v38|6:38]]; [[John-07#v18|7:18]]; [[John-08#v28|8:28]].

##### b. We, the believers in Christ, also have two lives, the divine and the human; in order for God's intention to be accomplished, we must do everything by the divine life.

##### c. When we do everything by the divine life, we spontaneously repudiate our natural life.

## II. Living Christ is a practical reality

### A. The practical way to live Christ is to stop and ask, "Am I the one doing this, or is Christ the One doing this?"

### B. Even though we received the gospel and received Christ as our life, we are not accustomed to living by another life; we are deficient in the practice of living Christ instead of ourselves.

### C. In order to live Christ, we must pray ([[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]]; [[Ephesians-06#v18|Eph. 6:18]]); if we are serious concerning the matter of living Christ, we need much prayer, and we also need to learn to stop before we carry out anything and check whether our activity is being carried out by us or by Christ.

### D. This requires much practice, and without such practice we will have only the knowledge of living by Christ as our life without the living itself:

#### 1. We need to practice again and again and day after day.

#### 2. According to [[1 Timothy-04#v7|1 Timothy 4:7-8]] we need to exercise ourselves unto godliness, which is Christ lived out of us to be the manifestation of God—[[John-03#v16|3:16]].

#### 3. Since today the very Christ is the Spirit dwelling in our spirit, to exercise ourselves unto godliness is to exercise our spirit to live Christ in our daily life.

#### 4. Merely to call the Lord and pray-read the Word is not sufficient; we need to pray and practice living Christ with fear and trembling all the time.

#### 5. It is wrong to think that since practicing to live Christ is too difficult, the Lord will take care of it; the Lord will not take care of our living Him if we do not practice.

## III. Our experience of living Christ is not only for our Christian life but also for the church life; for the genuine enjoyment of the church life, we need Christ with His riches to be its content

### A. Our meetings are short of the riches of Christ, and we have little to speak in the meetings because we have little experience of Christ.

### B. Moreover, the speaking of many of the saints is shallow because their experience of Christ lacks depth.

### C. Today the Lord is charging us to experience Christ and to practice living Him.

### D. We may seek the Lord's leading without seeking to live by the Lord; living by the Lord is the primary thing:

#### 1. Actually, we do not need to seek the Lord's leading in our daily living, but we do need to seek to live by the Lord daily.

#### 2. When we live by the Lord, our living will become the Lord's leading, for whatever we do will be done by the Lord, and if anything is not according to the Lord, we will not do it.

### E. We must realize that the experience of Christ is altogether mysterious:

#### 1. To know whether or not we are experiencing Christ may be difficult, but to know whether or not we are living by our natural life, the old man, the "I," is easy.

#### 2. As long as we know that we are the one who is living, we should not do anything.

## IV. We need to reject the natural life in order to live Christ; for us believers, what remains after we reject the natural life is Christ

### A. It is difficult to practice rejecting the natural life, because our preference is to carry out what we desire even when we are aware that it is we who are living, not Christ.

### B. In a real sense, to reject the natural life is to allow the life-giving Spirit to crucify the "I" in our experience—[[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20a]].

### C. Because we are unwilling to give up our preference and choice so that we may have much experience of Christ, we need to pray for the Lord's mercy.

## V. We need to work out our own salvation with fear and trembling—[[Philippians-02#v12|Phil. 2:12-13]]

### A. Regrettably, we have neither the proper fear nor the adequate trembling.

### B. Our shortage and lack is that we do not have a prayer life—[[Matthew-26#v41|Matt. 26:41]]; [[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]].

### C. We need to be saved daily from our preference and choice; such a salvation is not received but worked out by ourselves, in cooperation with God's operation, in fear and trembling:

#### 1. We must work out our salvation in fear and trembling because it is easy for us to fall away.

#### 2. We may pray, "Lord, grant me the proper measure of fear and trembling so that I do not fall away from working out my salvation. I am a little concerned that unconsciously I may have fallen away from working out this salvation."

#### 3. We may have no sense of fear and trembling concerning the fact that we still live ourselves and not Christ; living by ourselves, our natural life, instead of by Christ is worse than committing sinful deeds.

#### 4. No matter how many years we have been in the Lord, as long as we are still in our natural man and in the flesh, we need to work out our salvation with fear and trembling by dealing with these two frustrations:

##### a. We may realize that our flesh is bad ([[Romans-07#v18|Rom. 7:18]]), but we may not realize that our natural life also is bad.

##### b. We may love our natural life and appreciate its good aspects.

### D. God desires that we live by Christ as our life; thus, we need to see this vision and practice it with much prayer:

#### 1. We are individually responsible before the Lord to live Him; just as no one can drink, eat, or breathe for us, so also no one can live Christ for us.

#### 2. Today we need a great turn in our emphasis, toward more prayer and the practice of living Christ in everything.

#### 3. We need to check whether it is we or the Lord who is living and acting in everything.

#### 4. Then we will spontaneously experience Christ, and we will have some riches of Christ with which to infuse others when we contact them.

## VI. In order to live Christ, we have a great need to make our contact with the Lord a daily matter, a matter of our daily living

### A. For the whole day we need to contact Him, enjoy Him, and thereby live Him.

### B. The words *all things* in [[Philippians-04#v13|Philippians 4:13]] indicate that Paul did everything in Christ.

## VII. In our Christian life we need to have some legalities

### A. One legality that we, and especially the young people, need to practice is to read the Bible daily; if necessary, we need to force ourselves to do this.

### B. Another legality is that we must pray, and yet another is that we must practice living Christ with fear and trembling.

### C. We need to call on the name of the Lord, to pray-read His Word, to praise Him, to release our spirit, to develop a strong prayer life, and to read the Bible regularly from the first page to the last page in a legal way; we also need to practice in a legal way to live Christ.

## VIII. The genuine building up of the Body of Christ is the growth in life

### A. When we live Christ, the automatic issue of our living is the growth in life and the building up of the Body; this is a great matter.

### B. According to [[Ephesians-04#v11|Ephesians 4:11-13]], to be built up is to arrive at three things: the oneness of the faith and of the full knowledge of the Son of God, a full-grown man, and the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ.

### C. Before we arrive at these three things, we cannot avoid having differences among us; moreover, any attempt to remove the differences other than by further growth in life will lead to division.

### D. We must see that the church as God's building is not like a building with lifeless materials fitted together in a physical proximity; [[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Peter 2:5]] says that we are living stones for God's spiritual house:

#### 1. The Body of Christ is universal; thus, when we are built up in one locality, we are built up everywhere.

#### 2. The building up of the Body of Christ is altogether a matter of growing in the divine life in order to arrive at the three things mentioned in [[Ephesians-04#v13|Ephesians 4:13]]; when we arrive at these three things, we are fully built up.

#### 3. Hence, the genuine building up depends on the growth in life; the measure of our growth in life is the measure of our being built up—[[Ephesians-02#v21|Eph. 2:21]]; [[Ephesians-04#v16|4:16]]:

##### a. Until we are fully built up, we will have problems in the church life and will be in danger of becoming dissenting.

##### b. The more we grow, the fewer differences and problems we have; our differences and problems can be eliminated not by admonition and advice but only by our growth in the divine life.

#### 4. When we have been fully built up by growing in the divine life unto maturity, we will all see the same vision, live by the same life, bear the same testimony, and speak the one thing; then wherever we go, there will be no problem.

## IX. What the Lord desires today is not merely a group of people who are working for Him but a group of people who live Him

### A. The primary matter is to live Christ; all other matters are secondary.

### B. The Lord's recovery today is to recover one thing—to live Christ for the church; if we miss the basic point of living Christ, we may be right in certain things, but we will miss the mark of the Lord's recovery and will become a common work in traditional Christianity.

### C. Because our heart is deceitful above all things and is incurable, we need to turn our heart to the Lord and keep our heart right by praying with fear and trembling.

### D. Brother Lee's ministry is only for the testimony of Jesus Christ as life—[[Revelation-01#v2|Rev. 1:2]], [[Revelation-01#v9|9]]:

#### 1. Although we need to preach the gospel and contact people, our preaching must be our living of Christ, and our contacting people also must be our living of Christ; this is the proper way for us to remain in the Lord's recovery in the genuine oneness and with the testimony of life.

#### 2. When we live Christ, we spontaneously become a magnet to attract people to the Lord and to His testimony; we are not for any kind of work but for the testimony of Jesus in life and in the genuine oneness.

#### 3. In His recovery today the Lord is recovering the living of Christ; if we lose the nature of the Lord's recovery and no longer care for the testimony of Jesus as life, what we do on the campuses will be a repetition of the sad history of Christianity, and we will no longer be in the Lord's recovery.

#### 4. What we need today is to see and be kept in the vision of the Lord's recovery in the genuine oneness for the producing of the living testimony of Jesus.
