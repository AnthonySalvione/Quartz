Calling on the Lrod could be a toy - pray-reading could be a toy, baptism by immersion could be a toy - all these are things... that when you are little and not full grown you just hold onto them because you hold on to them... the storms that were caused - context of this chapter - is because, some children... had their toys removed. When you remove that toy, and they are still children... what happens? The children cries... Every turmoil we have gone through is JUST CHILDREN CRYING. Seriously, the real need is not even "drop your toys" but the real need is "DIRECT THEM TO THE REALITY TO HODL TO TRUTH IN LOVE" because their need is not so much instruction.... their need is growth. Growing up into Him in all things - grow into Him as our head. So that the Body could be the issue.

well, sometimes, and this is my point, is that it is a little bit difficult for the Lord... you konw, if I had a container, and I wanted to pour, or dispense somethign into this container... it won't fight back... why? Because it is not alive, it is inanimate... there is no objectives. Anyone who has a child, which presumably you don't have... but trust me, when you have a baby, sometimes it is not so easy to dispense seomthing into them - it may be the best thing, but if they are not in the mood... IT IS HARD TO DISPENSE INTO THEM

I remember my son - he was young... and before I could - i didnt know such a small creature could actually have a strong will. I just remember trying to feed him... and for whatever reason - it somehow gripped him that HE WAS NOT GOING TO EAT - it wasn't that he was unhappy, uncomfortable, constipated... HE JUST DIDNT WANT TO EAT - so I had this spoon, with not so flavorful mush... I was putting it in his mouth - and my goodness... that lip was sealed... I was trying to wedge it by using leverage... and it would not go in... in fact, finally I succeeded in getting it in, and I pulled out the spoon, phew, THEN OUT CAME THE MUSH all down his chin...

WE are like that sometimes with the Lord, we are just closed, we don't want to let Him in - unlike parents who will resort to all sorts of dancing, airplanes, to get them to open - the Lord WONT do that to us. Actually ,He would much rather just wait. He will do things... but He won't do it without our consent.

So, this is where, hopefully, I'll leave this with you - if you want to experience the oneness of the Body, not just in reality, but in practicality. You have to OPEN YOUR BEING, AND GIVE YOUR CONSENT TO THE LORD.

What is consent? Consent is our handing ourselves over to Him. "Lord, I just... don't see the Body of Christ. Howevre, Lord, I just give myself to YOu for You to reveal it in me." This is consecration - it is to give yourself, to allow the Lord and give HIm consent to make you what we are not inherently... but we will become in practicality. THis is the oneness of the Body.
