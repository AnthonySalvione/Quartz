# **Lesson Two**—The New Man Created by Christ in Himself and Enlivened by God with Christ as Life, as the Poem, the Masterpiece, of God

**Scripture Reading:** [[Ephesians-02#v14|Eph. 2:14-22]], [[Ephesians-02#v4|4-10]]

## I. Created by Christ—[[Ephesians-02#v14|Eph. 2:14-22]]:

### A. In Himself as the sphere and element substantially—[[Ephesians-02#v15|v. 15]].

### B. By slaying the enmity (the issue of the different ordinances in living and worship) through the cross—[[Ephesians-02#v16|v. 16b]].

### C. Putting the two (Israel and the Gentiles) into one Body—[[Ephesians-02#v16|v. 16a]].

### D. To be God's household and God's kingdom—[[Ephesians-02#v19|v. 19]].

### E. Being built together into a dwelling place of God in spirit—[[Ephesians-02#v20|vv. 20-22]]:

#### 1. Upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets—[[Ephesians-02#v20|v. 20a]].

#### 2. With Christ as the joining cornerstone—[[Ephesians-02#v20|v. 20b]].

## II. Enlivened by God—[[Ephesians-02#v4|vv. 4-10]]:

### A. With Christ as life organically—[[Ephesians-02#v5|vv. 5-6]]:

#### 1. Making the members (constituents) of the new man alive together with Christ—[[Ephesians-02#v5|v. 5]].

#### 2. Raising them up together with Christ—[[Ephesians-02#v6|v. 6a]].

#### 3. Seating them together with Christ in the heavenlies—[[Ephesians-02#v6|v. 6b]].

### B. By grace through faith—[[Ephesians-02#v8|v. 8]]:

#### 1. By grace—by having the processed Triune God dispensed into the believers.

#### 2. Through faith—through Christ moving in the believers as their faith, their substantiating ability to apprehend the divine favor given to them in Christ.

### C. To be the poem, the masterpiece, of God—[[Ephesians-02#v10|v. 10]]:

#### 1. To show forth the wisdom in God's grace—cf. [[Ephesians-02#v7|v. 7]]; [[Ephesians-03#v10|3:10]].

#### 2. And to have a new man walk in the good works which God prepared for him beforehand.

### D. That God might display in the ages to come the surpassing riches of His grace toward the believers in Christ—[[Ephesians-02#v7|2:7]].
