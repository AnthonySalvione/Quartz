# Lesson Thirteen — The Seven Spirits of God (1)

## I. To open the additional section of the sevenfold intensified organic salvation of God:

**Note—The three sections of Christ's ministry:**

1. His earthly ministry.
2. His heavenly ministry.
3. His sevenfold intensified heavenly ministry.

### A. To:

#### 1. Save the believers in the church in Ephesus—Rev. 2:1-7.

#### 2. Strengthen the suffering believers in the church in Smyrna—vv. 8-11.

#### 3. Sanctify the believers in the church in Pergamos—vv. 12-17.

#### 4. Rescue the believers in the church in Thyatira—vv. 18-29.

#### 5. Revive the believers in the church in Sardis—3:1-6.

#### 6. Encourage the believers in the church in Philadelphia—vv. 7-13.

#### 7. Awake the believers in the church in Laodicea—vv. 14-22.

### B. By:

#### 1. The speaking of the sevenfold intensified pneumatic Christ—2:1, 7, 8, 11, 12, 17, 18, 29; 3:1, 6, 7, 13, 14, 22.

#### 2. The overcoming saints living in their spirit—1:10; 4:2; 17:3; 21:10.

### C. For:

#### 1. The complete preparation of the bride for Christ—19:7-9.

#### 2. The formation of the bridal army—vv. 11-21; 17:14.

#### 3. The binding of Satan—20:1-3.

#### 4. The kingdom of Christ and of God—vv. 4-6.

#### 5. The consummation of the New Jerusalem—2:7; 21:2.

### D. The Final Outcome—the conclusion of the entire Bible is a couple, which is the consummation of the universal romance of the redeeming God and His redeemed man—22:17.
