# Lesson Eight — The Compound Spirit Typified by the Compound Ointment (1)

## Its Ingredients

**Scripture Reading:** Exo. 30:23-25

## I. The [compound] Spirit being not yet before the glorification [resurrection] of Christ, that is, from the time of Genesis 1 until the time of John 20:22—7:37-39.

## II. The great importance of the compound Spirit and of its application in light of the real history of the universe—Gal. 5:16, 25; Rom. 8:4; Phil. 1:19-21; Gen. 1:2; Rev. 22:17a.

## III. The intrinsic significance of the compounded ingredients (oil with four spices) of the holy anointing oil:

### A. Flowing myrrh—signifying the precious death of Christ:

#### 1. Myrrh being used to reduce pain and heal the body when it gives off the wrong kind of secretion—Mark 15:23; John 19:39.

#### 2. The Spirit compounded through Christ's sufferings in His living a crucified life, a life of myrrh, from the manger to the cross as the first God-man—Matt. 2:11; John 19:39; Isa. 53:2-3.

#### 3. The Spirit leading us to the cross, the cross being applied by the Spirit, and the cross issuing in more abundance of the Spirit—Heb. 9:14; Rom. 6:3, 6; 8:13-14; Gal. 2:20; John 12:24.

### B. Sweet cinnamon—typifying the sweetness and effectiveness of Christ's death:

#### 1. Cinnamon having a distinctive, sweet flavor and also being used to stimulate a weak heart—Neh. 8:10; Isa. 42:4a.

#### 2. Being conformed to the death of Christ by our outward, consuming environment in cooperation with the indwelling, crucifying Spirit—2 Cor. 4:10-11, 16; Rom. 8:13-14; Gal. 5:24; Col. 3:5; Gal. 6:17.

### C. Sweet calamus—signifying the precious resurrection of Christ:

#### 1. Calamus being a reed standing up (shooting into the air) and growing in a marsh or muddy place—1 Pet. 3:18.

#### 2. Experiencing the Spirit as the reality of Christ's resurrection—John 11:25; 20:22; Lam. 3:55-57.

### D. Cassia—signifying the repelling power of Christ's resurrection:

#### 1. Cinnamon being from the inner part of the bark and cassia from the outer part—Rev. 2:7; 1 Pet. 2:24; John 11:25.

#### 2. Cassia being a repellent to drive away insects and snakes—Eph. 6:11, 17b-18.

#### 3. Knowing the power of Christ's resurrection in the life-giving Spirit as the all-sufficient grace of the processed and consummated Triune God—Phil. 3:10; 2 Cor. 12:9-10; 1 Cor. 15:10, 45b, 58; Phil. 4:23.

### E. Olive oil—signifying the Spirit of God with divinity:

#### 1. The olive oil being the base of the compound ointment, the holy anointing oil—Isa. 61:1-2; Heb. 1:9.

#### 2. Produced by the pressing of olives—Matt. 26:36.

#### 3. For the priesthood and the kingship to proclaim the jubilee of grace—Lev. 8:12; 1 Sam. 16:12-13; Luke 4:18-19.
