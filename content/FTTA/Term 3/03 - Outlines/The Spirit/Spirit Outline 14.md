# Lesson Fourteen — The Seven Spirits of God (2)

## I. The seven Spirits of God being the sevenfold intensified Spirit of God (cf. the sevenfold sunlight—Isa. 30:26) to intensify Christ's organic salvation, to produce the overcomers, and to consummate the New Jerusalem—Rev. 1:4.

## II. Christ as the sevenfold intensified life-giving Spirit calling for and producing the overcomers in the churches by bringing them back to the enjoyment of Himself for the finalization of God's eternal economy—3:1; 4:5; 5:6; 1:12-14:

### A. In His sevenfold intensified heavenly ministry, Christ is walking in the midst of the churches to care for them as the High Priest in His humanity and in His divinity—Heb. 5:5-6; 2:17; 4:15; 7:24-25:

#### 1. Cherishing the churches in His humanity as the Son of Man by His trimming work—Rev. 1:13a; Exo. 30:7; 25:38.

#### 2. Nourishing the churches in His divinity with His divine love, signified by the golden girdle on His breasts (Rev. 1:13b), with Himself as:

##### a. The tree of life—2:4-5, 7.

##### b. The crown of life—vv. 9-10.

##### c. The hidden manna—vv. 13, 17.

##### d. The abounding grace to reign in life and the morning star—vv. 26-28; Rom. 5:17.

##### e. The living One to be their lived-out subjective righteousness—Rev. 3:1, 5.

##### f. The building and writing One—vv. 7, 12-13.

##### g. The reality of the Triune God, the feast, and the overcoming One—vv. 18, 20-21.

##### h. All of the above being His nourishing of us with His unsearchable riches in His full ministry of three stages so that we may grow and mature in His divine life to become the overcomers in His sevenfold intensification.

### B. Enabling the overcomers to be intensified as He is, so that they cherish others in His humanity and nourish others in His divinity for the building up of the Body of Christ consummating the New Jerusalem—Matt. 24:45-51; 1 Thes. 2:7; (cf. Isa. 66:13); 1 Cor. 3:2a.
