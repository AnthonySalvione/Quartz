# Lesson Eleven — The Compound Indwelling, Life-dispensing Spirit in Romans 8 (1)

**Scripture Reading:** Rom. 8:2, 9-11

## I. The Spirit of life, the Spirit of God, the Spirit of Christ, Christ Himself, and the indwelling Spirit in Romans 8:2, 9-11 all referring to the compound Spirit who gives life.

## II. The processed Triune God consummated to be the law of the Spirit of life:

### A. The Spirit of life being the compound Spirit—Exo. 30:23-25:

#### 1. Being a mingling of the divinity, humanity, human living, death and resurrection of Christ with the Spirit of God to become the holy ointment—vv. 23-25.

#### 2. The supplying element of this compound Spirit being bountiful and excellent—Phil. 1:19b.

### B. The law of this Spirit of life being the consummated Triune God—Rev. 22:17a:

#### 1. Becoming the law of life with its natural life-capacities and its spontaneous life-power—Rom. 8:2a.

#### 2. Freeing the believers from the law of sin and of death, solving for them the problem of sin and death—v. 2b.

### C. The operation and dispensing of the law of the Spirit of life in the three parts of the believers:

#### 1. Beginning from the believers' spirit—vv. 9-10.

#### 2. Continuing in the believers' soul—v. 6; 12:2b.

#### 3. Consummated in the believers' body—8:11.
