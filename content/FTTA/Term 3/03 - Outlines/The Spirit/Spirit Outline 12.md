# Lesson Twelve — The Compound, Indwelling, Life-dispensing Spirit in Romans 8 (2)

**Scripture Reading:** Rom. 8:2, 9-11

## I. The processed God being the law of the Spirit of life in us:

### A. The Triune God being processed in Christ through incarnation, crucifixion, resurrection, and ascension to become the law of the Spirit of life within us—Rom. 8:3, 11, 34.

### B. The law of letters, the law of God, outside of man—7:22, 25; 10:4.

### C. The law of life—an inward working principle and innate capacity which operates automatically, naturally, and spontaneously—Heb. 8:10:

#### 1. The law of sin and death being the person of Satan residing in our flesh—Rom. 7:18, 23.

#### 2. The law of good in the believers' mind, that is, in his soul, deriving from his natural human life—v. 23.

#### 3. The law of the Spirit of life being the person of the Triune God installed in our spirit—8:2, 16.

#### 4. Those who walk according to the spirit, the law of life, spontaneously and unconsciously fulfilling the righteous requirements of the law of God—v. 4.

## II. The life of the Triune God dispensed into the tripartite man:

### A. God being triune and man being tripartite—2 Cor. 13:14; 1 Thes. 5:23.

### B. Unregenerated man possessing *bios* (the physical life) and *psuche* (the psychological life).

### C. Regenerated man possessing an additional life *zoe* (the divine, infinite, uncreated, eternal life)—John 3:36.

### D. God the Father being the source of *zoe*—5:26.

### E. *Zoe* being a wonderful Person, Christ—14:6a.

### F. Christ, the embodiment of the Triune God, coming that we may have *zoe* and have *zoe* abundantly—1:4; 10:10b.

### G. The Triune God being processed and consummated to become a *zoe*-giving Spirit—Luke 12:49-50; John 12:24; 1 Cor. 15:45b; Rev. 22:1.

### H. Our human spirit being *zoe*—Rom. 8:10, 16; 1 John 3:9; 1 Cor. 6:17; 2 Cor. 2:13.

### I. Our mind becoming *zoe*—Rom. 8:6; Eph. 4:23; Phil. 2:5; 1:8.

### J. *Zoe* imparted into our mortal bodies—Rom. 8:11; 12:1-2.

### K. To become men of *zoe*, deified sons of God, transformed and conformed to the image of Christ for the constituting of His Body through all persons, matters, and things by His all-conquering love—8:23, 29, 39; 1 Cor. 1:30; Eph. 3:10.

## III. The law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has freed the "switching" me, that is, the "me" who cooperates with the installed and operating law of the Spirit of life for our organic salvation by "switching on" this law in the following ways—Phil. 2:12-14; Rom. 5:10, 17:

### A. Walking according to the spirit—8:4.

### B. Minding the things of the spirit, setting our mind on the spirit—vv. 5-6; John 6:57; Rev. 2:17.

### C. By the Spirit putting to death the practices of our body—Rom. 8:13.

### D. Being led by the Spirit of God to be sons of God—v. 14.

### E. Crying "Abba, Father" in the spirit of sonship—v. 15.

### F. Witnessing that we are the children of God—v. 16.

### G. Groaning for the full sonship, the redemption of our body—v. 23.
