# Lesson Two — "The Spirit Was Not Yet" (1)

**Scripture Reading:** John 4:10, 14; John 7:37-39

## I. The Spirit in the Old Testament:

### A. The Spirit of God in God's creation of the universe—Gen. 1:1-2; Deut. 32:11.

### B. The Spirit of Jehovah in God's reaching of men and in His care for men—Judg. 3:10; 6:34; Gen. 6:3a; Exo. 3:14.

### C. The Spirit of holiness in God's making His chosen people holy unto Himself—Psa. 51:11; Isa. 63:10-11.

## II. The Spirit in the Gospels:

### A. The Holy Spirit:

#### 1. In the conception of John the Baptist to introduce God's becoming a man in His incarnation—Luke 1:13-17.

#### 2. In the conception of Jesus in God's incarnation to be a man in the flesh—vv. 30-36; Matt. 1:18-20.

### B. The Spirit with whom Jesus was anointed for God's move in His earthly ministry—Mark 1:10, 12; Matt. 4:1; Luke 4:1, 18; John 1:32-33.

## III. The Spirit that gives life being not yet before the glorification (resurrection) of Christ:

### A. The Spirit flowing out of the believers' innermost being as rivers of living water—4:10, 14; 7:37-39.

### B. The Spirit as the consummation of the processed Triune God reaching the believers:

#### 1. The divine incorporation of the coinhering Triune God coinhering with the believers (four "ins")—14:16-20.

#### 2. The Spirit as another Comforter, the transfiguration of the first Comforter—v. 16.

#### 3. The Spirit of reality being the resurrected Christ, the pneumatic Christ, in us—vv. 17-18, 20; 1 Cor. 15:45b; 2 Cor. 3:17.

#### 4. The Spirit as the Son with the Father in the believers—John 14:26; 15:26.

### C. The Spirit being the presence of the Triune God within the believers—Matt. 1:23; 18:20; 28:20; 2 Tim. 4:22.

### D. The Son's going for the world-convicting Spirit's coming—John 16:7-11.

### E. The Spirit being the organic transmission of the Divine Trinity—John 16:13-15.

### F. The Spirit being the pneumatic Christ—20:22; Gen. 2:7; Lam. 3:55-57; Hymns, #255; 2 Tim. 3:16.
