# Lesson Five — The Last Adam Became a Life-giving Spirit (1)

## The Two "Becomings" of Christ

**Scripture Reading:** John 1:14; 1 Cor. 15:45b

## I. The Word becoming flesh (the Son with the Father by the Spirit) in Christ's stage of incarnation for the accomplishment of God's judicial redemption:

### A. As the embodiment of God, to bring God into man and to make God contactable, touchable, receivable, experienceable, and enjoyable—John 1:14:

#### 1. The mingling of divinity with humanity.

#### 2. As the prototype for the mass reproduction of many God-men.

### B. His human living to express the divine attributes in His human virtues:

#### 1. Not by His human life.

#### 2. But living the divine life in His human life.

### C. His all-problems-solving death to redeem us judicially:

#### 1. As the Lamb of God, even our Passover, to take away the sin of the world (including sin and sins)—John 1:29; 1 Cor. 5:7.

#### 2. As a man in the likeness of the flesh of sin to condemn sin in the flesh and to crucify the flesh, the body of sin—Rom. 8:3; 6:6; Gal. 5:24.

#### 3. As the brass serpent to cast out and destroy the devil, the serpent, and to judge his world—John 3:14; Heb. 2:14; John 12:31.

#### 4. As the Peacemaker to abolish in His flesh the law of the commandments in ordinances and to create in Himself one new man—Eph. 2:15.

#### 5. As the Firstborn of all creation to crucify the entire old creation—Col. 1:15; Matt. 27:51; Exo. 26:31; Ezek. 1:5, 10; 10:14-15.

#### 6. As the last Adam, the ending of mankind, to crucify the old man—1 Cor. 15:45b; Rom. 6:6; Gal. 2:20.

#### 7. As a grain of wheat falling into the ground and dying to release His unlimited infinite divine being with His divine life—John 12:24.

## II. The last Adam becoming a life-giving Spirit (the Spirit as the Son with the Father) in Christ's stage of inclusion for the carrying out of God's organic salvation:

### A. The first Adam becoming a living soul and the last Adam becoming a life-giving Spirit:

#### 1. The soul being the center and life-pulse of the old creation.

#### 2. The life-giving Spirit being the center and life-pulse of the new creation.

### B. Becoming the pneumatic Christ, the all-inclusive, compounded, consummated Spirit as the consummation of the processed Triune God—John 20:22; cf. Gen. 2:7; 1 Cor. 15:45b.

### C. The believers entering into the divine and mystical realm of the consummated Spirit and the pneumatic Christ—John 14:17, 20.

### D. As the life-giving Spirit, Christ being everything to us subjectively as the solution to all the problems in the church—1 Cor. 1:2, 9; 15:45b.
