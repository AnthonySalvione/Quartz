# Lesson Fifteen — The Seven Spirits of God (3)

## I. Christ becoming the sevenfold-intensified life-giving Spirit:

### A. To intensify His organic salvation—Rom. 5:10; Rev. 1:4.

### B. To produce the overcomers—2:7, 11, 17, 26-28; 3:5, 12, 21.

### C. To consummate the New Jerusalem initially in the millennium and ultimately in the new heaven and new earth—2:7; 21:2.

## II. The composition of the overcomers:

### A. The martyred overcomers from Abel to the 5th seal—6:9-11.

### B. The man-child born of the universal woman—12:1-5.

### C. The one hundred forty-four thousand living overcomers—14:1-5.

### D. The martyrs in the great tribulation—15:2-4; 13:7a.

### E. The two witnesses martyred, resurrected, and raptured to the heavens in the cloud at the close of the great tribulation—11:3-12.

## III. The final consummation of the overcomers:

### A. To be manifested as a corporate overcomer after their raptures.

### B. To be rewarded at the judgment seat of Christ—2 Cor. 5:10.

### C. To be the bride married to the Lamb—Rev. 19:7-9.

### D. To be the army of the Lamb—17:14; 19:14.

### E. To bring in the kingdom of God and of Christ—11:15; 20:4, 6.

### F. To inherit the kingdom of God and of Christ in the fuller enjoyment of the eternal life—2 Pet. 1:11; Rev. 20:4, 6.

### G. To be the New Jerusalem as the bride of Christ for one thousand years, in its initial and fresh stage—2 Pet. 3:8; Rev. 2:7; Matt. 13:43.

### H. To consummate and complete the New Jerusalem in full as the wife of Christ in the new heaven and new earth for eternity—Rev. 21:1-3, 9-10.

### I. To consummate finally the Triune God's eternal economy in Christ through the consummated Spirit—22:17.

### J. To participate in the fullest enjoyment of the processed and consummated Triune God:

#### 1. Enjoying God the Father as the light of life—v. 5; 21:23.

#### 2. Enjoying God the Son as the tree of life—22:2.

#### 3. Enjoying God the Spirit as the river of life—v. 1.

#### 4. For all the believers to reign in life forever and ever—vv. 4-5.
