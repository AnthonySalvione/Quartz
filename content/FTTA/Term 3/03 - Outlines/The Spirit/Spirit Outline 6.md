# Lesson Six — The Last Adam Became a Life-giving Spirit (2)

## I. The last Adam (Christ in the flesh) becoming a life-giving Spirit being the fulfillment of John 7:39:

### A. Receiving Christ as the life-giving Spirit, the living water, by drinking Him—v. 37; 4:14a.

### B. The living water, the life-giving Spirit, becoming in us a fountain of water springing up into eternal life—v. 14b:

#### 1. The fountain being the Father as the source of life—5:26.

#### 2. The Father in the Son emerging as the spring for the manifestation and expression of life—1:4; 1 John 1:2.

#### 3. The Son as the Spirit of life springing up to be a river—Psa. 36:8-9; 46:4; John 7:38.

#### 4. The triune processed God flowing into (into meaning to be, or to become) the New Jerusalem, the city of water, the city of life, the totality of the eternal life—Rev. 22:1-2.

### C. The life-giving Spirit, as the consummation of the processed and consummated Triune God, flowing out of our innermost being, our spirit, as rivers of living water.

### D. Being measured by the Lord for the increase of the flow of life—Ezek. 47:1, 2, 12; Isa. 6:1-8.

## II. The last Adam, Christ in the flesh, becoming a life-giving Spirit in resurrection to carry out God's organic salvation to accomplish His heart's desire to build up the Body of Christ which consummates the New Jerusalem:

### A. Christ being the vivifying Spirit to carry out His desire to give life—John 10:10b; Rom. 8:2, 10, 6, 11.

### B. The Lord being the Spirit who gives life—2 Cor. 3:6, 17.

### C. Without His being the life-giving Spirit, we cannot be saved organically—Rom. 5:10:

#### 1. Giving life to and indwelling our spirit in regeneration—Ezek. 36:26; 2 Tim. 4:22.

#### 2. Cherishing us in His humanity with love and nourishing us in His divinity with the life-supply as our spiritual food in His organic shepherding—John 14:26; Rom. 8:26-27; 1 Pet. 2:25.

#### 3. Making us holy with His divine nature through Himself as the sanctifying Spirit-Word so that we can be the holy city—Eph. 5:25-27; Rev. 21:2.

#### 4. Renewing us as the renewing Spirit—Titus 3:5; Eph. 4:23.

#### 5. Transforming us as the transforming Spirit for God's building through conformation and glorification—2 Cor. 3:18; Rom. 8:28-29; Phil. 3:21; John 17:22.
