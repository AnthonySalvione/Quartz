# Lesson Nine — The Compound Spirit Typified by the Compound Ointment (2)

## Its Measure

**Scripture Reading:** Exo. 30:23-24; Phil. 1:19

## I. The numbers one, two, three, four, and five all being used in the type of the compound ointment.

## II. The only God—the one hin of olive oil—1 Tim. 1:17; Rom. 16:27; Exo. 30:24 (the number one).

## III. The Triune God—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit—the three units of measure of four spices—v. 24 (the numbers two, three, four, and five):

### A. Five hundred shekels of myrrh.

### B. Two hundred fifty shekels of cinnamon and two hundred fifty shekels of calamus.

### C. Five hundred shekels of cassia.

### D. Three units of five hundred shekels each in four spices.

## IV. Man, the creature of God—four spices of the plant life—vv. 23-24; John 19:5; 1 Tim. 2:5; Hymns, #132.

## V. The mingling of divinity with humanity typified by the blending of the olive oil with the four spices—Rom. 8:16; 1 Cor. 6:17.

## VI. The power for responsibility signified by the number five—Matt. 25:4, 8.

## VII. The building element signified by the numbers three and five—Gen. 6:15-16; Exo. 26:3; 27:13-15.
