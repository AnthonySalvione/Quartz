
# **Lesson One**—The Issue of God's Impartation and Transmission in His Divine Trinity into His Chosen People, as the Church, the Fullness of the One Who Fills All in All


**Scripture Reading:** [[Ephesians-01#v3|Eph. 1:3-6]], [[Ephesians-01#v7|7-12]], [[Ephesians-01#v13|13-14]], [[Ephesians-01#v18|18-23]]

## I. The issue of God the Father's impartation:

### A. Of His holy nature into His chosen people that they may be separated from the world and sanctified wholly unto Him—[[Ephesians-01#v4|Eph. 1:4]].

### B. Of His divine life into His predestinated people that they may become His many sons for His expression—[[Ephesians-01#v5|v. 5]].

### C. To the praise of the glory (expression) of His grace—[[Ephesians-01#v6|v. 6]].

## II. The issue of God the Son's impartation of the divine element into God the Father's chosen and predestinated people:

### A. In the redemption through His blood—[[Ephesians-01#v7|v. 7]].

### B. To bring them into Christ as their sphere and element, that they may be made God's inheritance, with the divine element in Christ, as a treasure to God—[[Ephesians-01#v8|vv. 8-11]].

### C. To the praise of God the Father's glory (expression) in the believers who have first hoped in Christ—[[Ephesians-01#v12|v. 12]].

## III. The issue of God the Spirit's impartation of the divine essence into God the Father's inheritance redeemed by God the Son:

### A. By sealing it (God's inheritance) that it may be saturated with the divine essence as the impress to be a mark and the image of God to be His expression—[[Ephesians-01#v13|v. 13]].

### B. By being a pledge of God as His redeemed people's inheritance for their foretaste of Him, guaranteeing their full taste of Him, unto the redemption of His acquired possession (redeemed inheritance)—[[Ephesians-01#v14|v. 14a]].

### C. To the praise of God's glory (expression)—[[Ephesians-01#v14|v. 14b]].

## IV. The issue of the transmission of God in His divine trinity into His chosen and redeemed people:

### A. As the great power toward the believers, which God caused to operate in Christ:

#### 1. In raising Him from the dead.

#### 2. In seating Him at the Father's right hand in the heavenlies, far above all.

#### 3. In subjecting all things under His feet.

#### 4. In giving Him to be the Head over all things.

### B. As the transmission to the church, which is the Body of Christ, the One who fills all in all—[[Ephesians-01#v19|vv. 19-23]].

**Reading Assignment:** *The Intrinsic View of the Body of Christ*, ch. 1
