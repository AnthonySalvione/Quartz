# **Lesson Six**—Division Damaging the Oneness of the Body

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10-13]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v3|3:3-4]]; [[1 Corinthians-04#v6|4:6]]; [[1 Corinthians-11#v17|11:17]], [[1 Corinthians-11#v29|29-30]], [[1 Corinthians-11#v19|19]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v24|12:24-25]]; [[Ephesians-04#v2|Eph. 4:2]]; [[Romans-16#v17|Rom. 16:17]]; [[Titus-03#v10|Titus 3:10]]; [[Leviticus-13#v45|Lev. 13:45-46]]; [[Numbers-12#v10|Num. 12:10-15]]

## I. Divisions being among the Corinthians—[[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10]]:

### A. All not speaking the same thing.

### B. Not being attuned in the same mind and in the same opinion.

### C. Having, rather, jealousy and strife—[[1 Corinthians-01#v11|v. 11]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v3|3:3]].

### D. Making parties by saying, I am of Paul, I am of Apollos, I am of Cephas, and I am of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-01#v12|1:12]].

## II. Such division being condemned by the apostle's teaching:

### A. Being fleshly and walking according to man—[[1 Corinthians-03#v3|3:3-4]].

### B. Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized into the name of Paul?—[[1 Corinthians-01#v13|1:13]].

### C. Being puffed up on behalf of the one, against the other—[[1 Corinthians-04#v6|4:6]].

## III. Such divisive ones coming together to take the Lord's table not for the better (not for profit) but for the worse (for loss)—[[1 Corinthians-11#v17|11:17]]:

### A. Not discerning the bread as a symbol of the Lord's physical body that needs to be regarded by them.

### B. Not discerning the bread as a symbol of the Lord's mystical Body that requires them to keep the oneness of the Body of Christ.

### C. Eating judgment to themselves—[[1 Corinthians-01#v29|v. 29]].

### D. Because of this, many among them being weak and sick, and a number sleeping—[[1 Corinthians-01#v30|v. 30]].

## IV. Parties of division manifesting those in the church life who are approved—[[1 Corinthians-01#v19|v. 19]].

## V. Having the same care for all the members to avoid division in the Body—[[1 Corinthians-01#v25|v. 25]].

## VI. No base, no reason, no excuse, no justification, and no vindication for any kind of division.

## VII. Always remember: there is no way for the members of the Body of Christ to be legitimately divided in the Body of Christ!

## VIII. All problems can and should be solved through proper and adequate fellowship by praying together sincerely and thoroughly:

### A. By seeking the Lord's solution with an open spirit, a pure heart, a lowly mind, and a meek attitude—[[Ephesians-04#v2|Eph. 4:2]].

### B. According to the teaching of the Scriptures properly interpreted, not understood by taking them out of context.

### C. Without self-interest; the entire pursuit being for the Lord's glory and for the benefit of the Lord's recovery.

### D. No parties or divisions formed for backing in partiality, threatening, and/or challenging others.

## IX. Dealing with divisive members:

### A. Turning away from division-makers—[[Romans-16#v17|Rom. 16:17]].

### B. Rejecting factious, sectarian, members—[[Titus-03#v10|Titus 3:10]].

### C. Practicing the quarantining of the lepers in typology—[[Leviticus-13#v45|Lev. 13:45-46]]; [[Numbers-12#v10|Num. 12:10-15]].

**Reading Assignment:** *The Intrinsic View of the Body of Christ*, ch. 6
