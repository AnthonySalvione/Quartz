# **Lesson Sixteen**—The Anointing of the Body and the Principle of the Body

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 John-02#v27|1 John 2:27]]; [[Luke-03#v22|Luke 3:22]]; [[Luke-04#v18|4:18]]; [[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1-10]], [[Ephesians-04#v20|20-32]]; [[Psalm-133|Psa. 133]]; [[Acts-06#v6|Acts 6:6]]; [[Acts-08#v4|8:4-5]], [[Acts-08#v12|12]], [[Acts-08#v14|14-17]]; [[Acts-09#v3|9:3-6]], [[Acts-09#v10|10-12]], [[Acts-09#v17|17]]; [[Acts-19#v6|19:6]]; [[James-05#v14|James 5:14-16]]; [[Matthew-18#v15|Matt. 18:15-16]], [[Matthew-18#v19|19-20]]; [[1 Timothy-04#v14|1 Tim. 4:14]]; [[1 Timothy-05#v22|5:22]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v6|2 Tim. 1:6]]

## I. The condition for the anointing being the burial of the natural man:

### A. The Lord was anointed with the Holy Spirit when He came out of the water of baptism at the Jordan River ([[Luke-03#v22|Luke 3:22]]; [[Luke-04#v18|4:18]]); in the same way, when we were baptized, we also received the anointing of the Spirit.

### B. In order for us to receive the anointing of the Holy Spirit, our flesh must first be buried.

## II. The function of the anointing:

### A. The anointing is so precious because grace flows from the Head to the Body by means of the anointing.

### B. The function of the anointing is to maintain the link between the Head and the Body, as well as the link between all the members.

### C. In the spiritual Body the Holy Spirit carries the thoughts of the Head to all the members.

## III. The teaching of the anointing:

### A. The anointing of the Holy Spirit is the teaching of the Holy Spirit; the Holy Spirit teaches by the anointing.

### B. We know the will of God by the teaching of the anointing.

### C. God's work does not depend on words, biblical interpretations, reasons, judgments concerning right and wrong, but His work and leading within us come by way of a kind of inner sense of life; this sense of life is the anointing of the Spirit.

## IV. The way to be anointed:

### A. In [[Psalm-133|Psalm 133]], which the Israelites sang as they ascended to meet the Lord in Zion in Jerusalem, they had cast aside their disunity, jealousy, and hatred; in this condition, they received God's anointing.

### B. In order for us to receive the anointing, we must submit to the Head and live in the Body.

### C. If our natural life is dealt with by the cross and if we submit to the headship of Christ and live the Body life, we will have the Spirit's anointing and enjoy the fellowship of the Body.

## V. The apostles' laying on of hands:

### A. The laying on of hands is a sign of identification.

### B. The two main meanings of the laying on of hands are that it puts the believers under the anointing of the Head and it brings the believers into the fellowship.

### C. By baptism we enter into Christ; by the laying on of hands we enter into the Body.

### D. By baptism we bear witness to the fact that we have put off ourselves and the world and entered into Christ; by the laying on of hands we bear witness to the fact that we have left the ground of being an individual and taken a stand on the ground of the Body.

## VI. The revelation Paul received at the time of his conversion:

### A. When the Lord appeared to Paul on the way to Damascus, He showed him that persecuting the believers was the same as persecuting Him—[[Acts-09#v3|Acts 9:3-5]].

### B. After this high revelation, the Lord charged him to go into the city, and there it would be told him what he must do ([[Acts-09#v6|v. 6]]); this was also a revelation of the Body.

## VII. The judgment of two or three brothers:

### A. [[Matthew-18#v15|Matthew 18:15-16]] is not about whether a person is right or wrong, nor even about whether a person feels that he is right or wrong; it is about the testimony of two or three brothers.

### B. May the Lord grant us the revelation of the Body so that we can submit to Christ the Head as well as to the representatives of the Body.
