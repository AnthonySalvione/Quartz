# **Lesson Nine**—The Degradation of the Church (2) — The Winds of Teaching


**Scripture Reading:** [[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]]; [[1 Timothy-06#v3|6:3-4]]; [[Titus-01#v9|Titus 1:9]]; [[Ephesians-04#v14|Eph. 4:14]]

## I. In the church there should be only one unique teaching—the teaching of the apostles—[[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]]; [[Titus-01#v9|Titus 1:9]]:

### A. In God's New Testament economy the only kind of teaching revealed and recognized by God is the apostles' teaching.

### B. The apostles' teaching concerns Christ's person and redemptive work and God's economy in faith—[[2 John-01#v9|2 John 9-11]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]].

### C. The teaching of the apostles is the unique divine revelation of God's New Testament economy from the incarnation of God to the consummation of the New Jerusalem, carried out by the full ministry of Christ in three divine and mystical stages for the fulfillment of God's eternal economy:

#### 1. The first stage, the stage of incarnation, is for Christ to bring God into man, to express God in humanity, and to accomplish His judicial redemption.

#### 2. The second stage, the stage of inclusion, is for Christ to be begotten as God's firstborn Son, to become the life-giving Spirit, and to regenerate the believers for His Body.

#### 3. The third stage, the stage of intensification, is for Christ to intensify His organic salvation, to produce the overcomers, and to consummate the New Jerusalem.

### D. The apostles' teaching is the holding factor of the one accord.

### E. If we limit ourselves to the apostles' teaching, the teaching concerning God's New Testament economy, we will be kept in oneness and have one way for one goal.

### F. The blessing of the church is dependent upon the church's continuing steadfastly in the teaching of the apostles—[[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]], [[Acts-02#v46|46]]; [[Acts-06#v7|6:7]].

### G. The deciding factor in determining the right ministry, which is the ministry of God's New Testament economy, is the teaching of the apostles:

#### 1. The unique ministry is the ministry ordained by God according to the apostles' teaching.

#### 2. A person's work is the New Testament ministry if he teaches the teaching of the apostles.

## II. The apostles' teaching is the church's constitution; the church must hold it and be under it absolutely:

### A. The teaching of the apostles eventually became the New Testament:

#### 1. This indicates that the churches were established according to the apostles' teaching and followed their teaching.

#### 2. This also indicates that the order of the churches was maintained by the faithful word, which was given according to the apostles' teaching—[[Titus-01#v9|Titus 1:9]].

### B. The apostles' teaching is the constitution of the Christian faith—[[Jude-01#v3|Jude 3]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v19|1 Tim. 1:19]]; [[1 Timothy-06#v12|6:12]]; [[2 Timothy-04#v7|2 Tim. 4:7]].

### C. The apostles' teaching, as the church's constitution, should be universally taught in every church in the same way—[[1 Corinthians-04#v17|1 Cor. 4:17]]; [[1 Corinthians-07#v17|7:17]]:

#### 1. All teachings must be restricted by the limit and sphere of the apostles' teaching.

#### 2. All the problems, division, and confusion among Christians today are due to one thing—not caring only for the unique revelation of the apostles' teaching.

### D. This constitution, like every constitution, must be properly interpreted.

## III. Any teaching that was different from the apostles' teaching was not allowed by the apostles—[[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]]:

### A. No other belief besides the unique faith was allowed by the apostles—[[Galatians-01#v7|Gal. 1:7-9]].

### B. Teachings that went beyond the teaching of Christ were not allowed by the apostles—[[2 John-01#v9|2 John 9-11]].

### C. All teachings that were different from the unique revelation of God's New Testament economy were considered by the apostles to be winds of teaching—[[Ephesians-04#v14|Eph. 4:14]].

### D. God's holy writing should not be twisted by anyone in any way but rather unfolded rightly and straightly without any distortion—[[2 Peter-03#v16|2 Pet. 3:16]]; [[2 Timothy-02#v15|2 Tim. 2:15]].

### E. The disorder in the church is due mainly to deviation from the apostles' teaching:

#### 1. To counter this we must hold to the faithful word taught in the churches according to the apostles' teaching.

#### 2. In a darkened and confused situation, we must cleave to the enlightening and ordering word in the New Testament—the apostles' teaching.

## IV. We must beware of the winds of teaching—teachings which are different from the teaching of God's New Testament economy—[[Ephesians-04#v14|Eph. 4:14]]:

### A. The different teachings of the dissenting ones are winds used by God's enemy to distract His people and carry them away from His economy.

### B. The dividing teachings are organized and systematized by Satan to cause serious error and thus damage the practical oneness of the Body—[[Ephesians-04#v13|v. 13]].

### C. The different teachings are the major source of the church's decline, degradation, and deterioration—[[Acts-20#v29|Acts 20:29-30]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v15|2 Tim. 1:15]].

### D. We need to know the purpose of the winds of teaching:

#### 1. The evil purpose of the enemy Satan versus the eternal economy of God.

#### 2. To overthrow the faith of some believers—[[Leviticus-02#v18|2:18]].

#### 3. To devastate the church—[[Acts-08#v3|Acts 8:3]].

#### 4. To frustrate the building up of the organic Body of Christ.

#### 5. To tear down the building up of the organic Body of Christ.

#### 6. To divide the members of the organic Body of Christ, causing endless divisions (sects) in hatred and jealousy instead of keeping the unique oneness of the Body of Christ in love and kindness—[[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10-11]].

### E. "Some have said, 'We don't need the Life-studies or the notes of the Recovery Version. We can read the pure Word of God.' We absolutely need the pure Word of God, but we also need the proper exposition of the Word. How could we have understood the book of Leviticus without the proper exposition? We could read Leviticus again and again without understanding much of it.... What we have seen is in today's Life-study of Leviticus. To say that we do not need such an exposition of the holy Word is a strong wind of teaching blowing the saints away from the central lane of God's economy" (*The Organic Building Up of the Church as the Body of Christ to Be the Organism of the Processed and Dispensing Triune God*).

### F. The intrinsic factor of the winds of teaching is the sleight of men—man's subtle ways of cheating:

#### 1. The sleight of men is the craftiness of men with a view to a system of error:

##### a. The system of error is man's plotted deception to induce people into the satanic system of error.

##### b. The sleight is of men, but the system of error is of the enemy, Satan.

##### c. The system of error is related to the deceitful teachings that are designed by the evil one to distract the saints from Christ and the church life.

#### 2. We need to have the proper discernment so that we will not be shaken, tossed about, or carried away.
