# **Lesson Thirteen**—The Producing of the Overcomers (3) — Overcoming Deadness to Become Intensely Living and Intensely Shining for the Building Up of the Body of Christ

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-03#v1|Rev. 3:1-6]]

## I. As a sign, the church in Sardis prefigures the Protestant church from the time of the Reformation to the second coming of Christ:

### A. The history of Protestantism is a history of being living in name but dead in reality—[[Revelation-03#v1|Rev. 3:1c]].

### B. The defilement in Sardis is not the defilement of sin but the defilement of death—[[Revelation-03#v4|v. 4]]; [[Leviticus-11#v24|Lev. 11:24-25]]; [[Numbers-06#v7|Num. 6:7]], [[Numbers-06#v9|9]].

### C. Nothing begun in the Reformation has been completed by the Protestant churches—[[Revelation-03#v2|Rev. 3:2b]].

### D. Because the reformed Protestant churches are dead, they will be unaware of the Lord's coming as a thief in His secret appearing to His seekers—[[Revelation-03#v3|v. 3b]].

### E. The dead, reformed church needs the speaking of the living Spirit—[[Revelation-03#v6|v. 6]].

### F. The dead, reformed church needs the sevenfold intensified Spirit and the shining leaders—[[Revelation-03#v1|v. 1b]].

## II. The overcomers produced by Christ as the sevenfold intensified Spirit overcome deadness—[[Revelation-03#v5|v. 5]]:

### A. White garments signify the walk and living which are unspotted by death and which will be approved by the Lord—[[Revelation-03#v5|v. 5a]]:

#### 1. Every Christian needs the garment of justification for our salvation, signifying the Christ whom we have received as our objective righteousness—[[Luke-15#v22|Luke 15:22]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]].

#### 2. Every Christian needs the garment of approvedness for our acceptance, signifying the Christ whom we live out as our subjective righteousness—[[Revelation-19#v7|Rev. 19:7-8]]; [[Matthew-22#v11|Matt. 22:11-14]]; [[Philippians-01#v20|Phil. 1:20-21a]]; [[Philippians-03#v9|3:9]].

### B. The overcomers go beyond the incompleteness of Sardis to complete the work of building up the Body of Christ—[[Revelation-03#v2|Rev. 3:2b]]; [[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]]; [[Colossians-01#v24|Col. 1:24]].

### C. The overcomers are precious enough to be "stolen" by the Lord, and they are prepared, ready, and watchful for His coming—[[Revelation-03#v3|Rev. 3:3b]].

### D. The Lord will not erase the name of the overcomer out of the book of life but will confess his name before the Father and His angels—[[Revelation-03#v5|v. 5b]].
