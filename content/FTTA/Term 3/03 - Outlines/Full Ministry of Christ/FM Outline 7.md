# **Lesson Seven**—The Secret of the Intensified Work of God's Organic Salvation

## I. Christ's sevenfold intensified heavenly ministry is carried out by Him as the sevenfold intensified life-giving Spirit, sevenfold intensified organically in the mystical realm, from the degradation of the church to the full consummation of the New Jerusalem—[[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]]; [[Revelation-03#v1|3:1]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]].

## II. Within a short time after the completion of the divine revelation, the church fell into degradation, so the Lord intensified Himself sevenfold to become the seven Spirits:

### A. At the end of the apostle Paul's ministry, around the A.D. 60s:

#### 1. All the believers in Asia turned away from him—[[2 Timothy-01#v15|2 Tim. 1:15]].

#### 2. Hymenaeus and Philetus said that the resurrection had already taken place—[[2 Timothy-02#v17|2:17-18]].

#### 3. The co-worker of the apostle, Demas, loved the present age and abandoned him—[[2 Timothy-04#v10|4:10]].

#### 4. Alexander the coppersmith did many evil things to the apostle and greatly opposed the apostle's words—[[2 Timothy-04#v14|vv. 14-15]].

#### 5. At the apostle's first defense no one was with him to support him, but all abandoned him—[[2 Timothy-04#v16|v. 16]].

### B. At the end of the apostle Peter's ministry, around the A.D. 60s:

#### 1. False teachers were teaching heresy, denying the Lord's redemption—[[2 Peter-02#v1|2 Pet. 2:1]].

#### 2. The evil ones forsook the straight way, going astray and following the way of Balaam, who loved the wages of unrighteousness—[[2 Peter-02#v15|v. 15]].

#### 3. Mockers were mocking, not believing in the Lord's coming back—[[2 Peter-03#v3|3:3-4]].

#### 4. The unlearned and unstable ones twisted the teachings of the apostle Paul—[[2 Peter-03#v15|vv. 15-16]].

### C. At the end of the apostle John's ministry, around the A.D. 90s:

#### 1. Many antichrists lied, denying that Jesus is the Christ—[[1 John-02#v18|1 John 2:18]], [[1 John-02#v22|22]].

#### 2. Many false prophets, by the spirit of deception, did not confess that Christ came in the flesh—[[1 John-04#v1|4:1-2]], [[1 John-04#v6|6]].

#### 3. Many deceivers as antichrists, not confessing that Christ came in the flesh, taught beyond the teaching of Christ—[[2 John-01#v7|2 John 7]], [[2 John-01#v9|9-11]].

#### 4. Diotrophes, loving to be first in the church, did not receive the apostles, babbling evil words against them, and forbade those intending to receive the brothers and cast them out of the church—[[3 John-01#v9|3 John 9-10]].

## III. Christ as the sevenfold intensified life-giving Spirit saves the believers from:

### A. The formal church life and the loss of the first love to the Lord, of the shining capacity of the lampstand, and of the enjoyment of Christ as life in the church in Ephesus—[[Revelation-02#v1|Rev. 2:1-7]].

### B. The defeat that leads to the taste of the second death in the church in Smyrna—[[Revelation-04#v8|vv. 8-11]].

### C. The worldliness in union with the world as a marriage and the teaching of Balaam and the Nicolaitans in the church in Pergamos—[[Revelation-04#v12|vv. 12-17]].

### D. Fornication, idolatry, demonic teaching, and satanic depths in Catholicism, signified by the church in Thyatira—[[Revelation-04#v18|vv. 18-29]].

### E. The spiritual death—dead and dying—in Protestantism, signified by the church in Sardis—[[Revelation-03#v1|3:1-6]].

### F. The losing of the crown, which has been gained already in Brethrenism, signified by the church in Philadelphia—[[Revelation-03#v7|vv. 7-13]].

### G. The lukewarmness and Christlessness in degraded Brethrenism, signified by the church in Laodicea—[[Revelation-03#v14|vv. 14-22]].

## IV. We are saved from degradation by the speaking of the sevenfold intensified pneumatic Christ and by the overcoming saints living in their spirit—[[Revelation-02#v1|2:1]], [[Revelation-02#v7|7]]; [[Revelation-01#v10|1:10]]; [[Revelation-04#v2|4:2]]; [[Revelation-17#v3|17:3]]; [[Revelation-21#v10|21:10]].

## V. Christ's sevenfold intensified heavenly ministry is for the complete preparation of the bride for Christ ([[Revelation-19#v7|19:7-9]]), the formation of the bridal army ([[Revelation-19#v11|vv. 11-21]]; [[Revelation-17#v14|17:14]]), the binding of Satan ([[Revelation-20#v1|20:1-3]]), the bringing in of the kingdom of Christ and of God ([[Revelation-20#v4|vv. 4-6]]), and the consummation of the New Jerusalem ([[Revelation-02#v7|2:7]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]]).

## VI. The final outcome is that the ultimately consummated Spirit as the consummation of the processed Triune God becomes the Bridegroom, and the aggregate of the overcoming saints becomes the bride of the universal romance between the redeeming God and His redeemed man as the conclusion of the entire Scriptures—[[Revelation-22#v17|22:17]].
