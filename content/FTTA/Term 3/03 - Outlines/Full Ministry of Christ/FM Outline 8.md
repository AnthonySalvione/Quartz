# **Lesson Eight**—The Degradation of the Church (1) — The Enemy Satan's Leavening of the Church


**Scripture Reading:** [[Matthew-13#v3|Matt. 13:3-7]], [[Matthew-13#v19|19-22]], [[Matthew-13#v24|24-33]], [[Matthew-13#v36|36-43]]; [[Matthew-16#v6|16:6]], [[Matthew-16#v11|11-12]]; [[Mark-08#v15|Mark 8:15]]; [[Luke-12#v1|Luke 12:1b]]; [[1 Corinthians-05#v6|1 Cor. 5:6-8]]; [[Galatians-05#v9|Gal. 5:9]]

## I. The enemy Satan frustrates the growth of the life in the believers and restricts the multiplication of Christ through His believers—[[Matthew-13#v4|Matt. 13:4-7]], [[Matthew-13#v19|19-22]].

## II. The enemy Satan causes damage to the church by bringing in a mixture among the components of the church and by changing the nature of the church and making the appearance of the church abnormal—[[Matthew-13#v25|vv. 25-30]], [[Matthew-13#v38|38-42]], [[Matthew-13#v31|31-32]].

## III. The enemy Satan has corrupted the church by leavening the church:

### A. By leavening the initial church, typified by the two wave-loaves offered to God on the day of Pentecost ([[Leviticus-23#v15|Lev. 23:15-17]]) and confirmed by the cases in [[Acts-05#v1|Acts 5:1-11]] and [[Acts-03#v1|6:1]].

### B. By leavening the church in Corinth with the gross sin of fornication—[[1 Corinthians-05#v1|1 Cor. 5:1-8]].

### C. By leavening the church with different teachings, that is, with teachings other than the economy of God—[[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]].

### D. By leavening the entire meal (fine flour made of grains of wheat) through a woman (the Catholic Church as the false prophetess Jezebel—[[Revelation-02#v20|Rev. 2:20]])—[[Matthew-13#v33|Matt. 13:33]].

### E. By leavening the early church with Judaism, Gnosticism, and asceticism—[[Colossians-02#v16|Col. 2:16]]; [[Acts-15#v1|Acts 15:1-2]]; [[Galatians-06#v15|Gal. 6:15]]; [[Acts-11#v2|Acts 11:2-3]]; [[Galatians-02#v12|Gal. 2:12-13]]; [[Galatians-01#v14|1:14]]; [[Colossians-02#v8|Col. 2:8]], [[Colossians-02#v20|20-23]].

### F. By leavening the church with Catholicism in the papal system, hierarchy, heresies, idolatry, and pagan practices.

### G. By leavening the church with Protestantism in hierarchy, being living in name but dead in actuality, different teachings, traditions, modernism, worldliness, and divisions.

### H. By leavening the church with nationalism, racialism, socialism, liberalism, and politics.

### I. By leavening the church with the believers' natural man, flesh and its lust, sin in their nature and sins in their deeds, and all the evil things listed in [[Galatians-05#v19|Galatians 5:19-21]].

## IV. The enemy Satan's leavening of the church is to corrupt the church in its nature and contents:

### A. Even the life element (the entire meal made of the grains produced by the wheat), of which the church is constituted, is leavened.

### B. This disease of being corrupted by being leavened is of the church in its constituents and is incurable.

## V. "We need to study the Bible in this way to purge out the leaven and to clear up the cloud that we may see God's New Testament economy and the New Testament ministry. This is the ministry which ministers the Triune God to produce the church and which will consummate in the New Jerusalem. We must realize that this ultimate consummation has been leavened and has been changed in every way in its intrinsic essence and element. It is not a small thing to fight against this leaven. This is the greatest leaven which the woman took and put into the biblical fine flour ([[Matthew-13#v33|Matt. 13:33]]). By His mercy, we must do our best to purge out this leaven....The truth concerning the house in [[John-14|John 14]] and the city in [[Revelation-21|Revelation 21]] has been leavened and this leaven in the past centuries has governed the Christian teachings; therefore, so many have been robbed, misled, distracted, frustrated, and held back. Now we are ministering the genuine thing of God's economy. We must purge out this leaven. This is a big battle" (Witness Lee, *Elders' Training, Book 2, The Vision of the Lord's Recovery*, pp. 148-149).
