# **Lesson Fourteen**—The Producing of the Overcomers (4) — Loving the Brothers and Opening the Door to Feast with the Lord

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-03#v7|Rev. 3:7-22]]

## I. We must return to the orthodoxy of the apostles by standing on the unique ground of oneness, choosing to love all the brothers—[[Revelation-03#v7|Rev. 3:7-13]]:

### A. After Sardis a great recovery transpired: in the church the brothers loved one another and the mediatorial class was abolished—[[Hebrews-13#v1|Heb. 13:1]]; [[Romans-12#v10|Rom. 12:10]]; [[1 Peter-01#v22|1 Pet. 1:22]]; [[Matthew-20#v25|Matt. 20:25-28]]; [[Matthew-23#v8|23:8]].

### B. God is love; we love because He first loved us—[[1 John-04#v8|1 John 4:8]], [[1 John-04#v19|19]]; [[1 John-03#v14|3:14]]; [[Philippians-01#v9|Phil. 1:9]].

### C. Love builds up—[[1 Corinthians-08#v1|1 Cor. 8:1b]].

### D. Love is not jealous, is not provoked, does not take account of evil, covers all things, endures all things, never falls away, and is the greatest—[[Revelation-13#v4|13:4-8]], [[Revelation-13#v13|13]].

### E. The Body of Christ builds itself up in love—[[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]].

### F. We need a spirit of love to conquer the degradation of today's church—[[2 Timothy-01#v7|2 Tim. 1:7]].

### G. To overcome the degradation of the church we need to pursue love with those who seek the Lord out of a pure heart—[[Revelation-02#v22|2:22]].

### H. Loving one another is a sign that we belong to Christ—[[John-13#v34|John 13:34-35]].

### I. Love is the most excellent way—[[1 Corinthians-12#v31|1 Cor. 12:31b]].

## II. The Lord as the Head of the church is standing outside the degraded church, knocking at her door, but the door is opened by individual believers—[[Revelation-03#v14|Rev. 3:14-22]]:

### A. The Lord is dealing with the whole church, but the acceptance of the Lord's dealing must be a personal matter.

### B. We should have the assurance that every morning our door is wide open to the Lord so that He can come in to dine with us, to feast with us:

#### 1. To dine is to eat not merely one kind of food but the riches of a feast.

#### 2. This may refer to the eating of the rich produce of the good land of Canaan by the children of Israel—[[Joshua-05#v10|Josh. 5:10-12]]; [[Deuteronomy-08#v8|Deut. 8:8]]; [[Deuteronomy-26#v9|26:9]]:

##### a. Wheat signifies the incarnated, crucified, and buried Christ—[[John-12#v24|John 12:24]].

##### b. Barley signifies the resurrected Christ—[[Leviticus-23#v10|Lev. 23:10]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v20|1 Cor. 15:20]]; [[John-06#v9|John 6:9]].

##### c. The vine signifies Christ as the wine producer, sacrificing Himself to produce wine for others and for God—[[Judges-09#v13|Judg. 9:13]]; [[Philippians-02#v17|Phil. 2:17]].

##### d. The fig tree signifies the sweetness and satisfaction of Christ as our supply—[[Judges-09#v13|Judg. 9:13]].

##### e. The pomegranate signifies the abundance and beauty of Christ as life—[[2 Corinthians-02#v10|2 Cor. 2:10]]; [[Song-04#v12|S. S. 4:12-14]].

##### f. The olive tree signifies Christ as the Son of oil to honor God and honor man—[[Hebrews-01#v9|Heb. 1:9]]; [[Zechariah-04#v12|Zech. 4:12-14]]; [[Judges-09#v9|Judg. 9:9]].

##### g. Milk and honey, the produce of both the animal life and vegetable life, signify the generating and redeeming life of Christ full of riches and sweetness—[[1 Peter-02#v2|1 Pet. 2:2]]; [[Psalm-119#v103|Psa. 119:103]].
