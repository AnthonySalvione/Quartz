# **Outline Three**—Experiencing the Seven Spirits of God for God's Building

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]]; [[Zechariah-03#v9|Zech. 3:9]]

## I. The title the seven Spirits indicates that all the elements of the all-inclusive Spirit have been intensified for our experience—[[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]].

## II. The seven lamps of fire burning before the throne of God are for enlightening, searching, exposing, judging, and burning—[[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]:

### A. This is for the carrying out of God's administration.

### B. God is administrating His government by enlightening, searching, exposing, judging, and burning—[[1 Peter-04#v12|1 Pet. 4:12]], [[1 Peter-04#v17|17]]; [[1 Peter-01#v7|1:7]].

## III. The seven eyes of the Lamb are for watching, observing, infusing, and transfusing—[[Revelation-05#v6|Rev. 5:6]]:

### A. The seven eyes of the Lamb transfuse all that the Lamb is into our being so that we may be the same as He is.

### B. Christ's eyes are upon us so that we may be transformed and conformed into His image for God's building.

## IV. The experience of the sevenfold intensified Spirit is for God's building—[[Zechariah-03#v9|Zech. 3:9]]:

### A. The seven lamps in [[Exodus-25|Exodus 25]] are for the building up of the tabernacle.

### B. The seven lamps in [[Zechariah-03|Zechariah 3]] and [[Zechariah-04|4]] are for the recovery of God's building.

### C. In Revelation the seven Spirits, which are the seven lamps before the throne and the seven eyes of the Lamb, are for God's building:

#### 1. The book of Revelation is mainly not for searching and judging but for producing and building up the New Jerusalem—[[Revelation-03#v12|Rev. 3:12]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v10|10]].

#### 2. As a result of the transfusing of the seven eyes of the Lamb, the New Jerusalem will be built up.

### D. God does not want a group of individualistic spiritual persons—God wants the building, His corporate expression.
