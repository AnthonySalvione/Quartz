# **Lesson Two**—The Seven Spirits of God

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]]; [[Revelation-03#v1|3:1]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]]
## I. The seven Spirits are undoubtedly the one Spirit of God ([[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4]]) because they are ranked in the Godhead in [[Revelation-01#v4|Revelation 1:4-5]]:

### A. In essence and existence the Spirit is one just as the lampstand is one, but in function and work the Spirit is seven, just as the lampstand is seven lamps—[[Revelation-04#v5|Rev. 4:5]].

### B. The lampstand is a marvelous picture of the Triune God—[[Exodus-25#v31|Exo. 25:31-39]]; [[Zechariah-04#v2|Zech. 4:2-6]]; [[Revelation-01#v12|Rev. 1:12]], [[Revelation-01#v20|20]]:

#### 1. The golden element signifies God the Father in His divine nature.

#### 2. This gold is in a form, an image, a shape, a likeness, signifying Christ the Son as the image of the invisible God and the embodiment of God—[[Colossians-01#v15|Col. 1:15]]; [[Colossians-02#v9|2:9]].

#### 3. The seven lamps of the lampstand signify the sevenfold intensified Spirit as the full expression of the Triune God—[[Galatians-05#v16|Gal. 5:16]], [[Galatians-05#v22|22-23]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v7|1 Cor. 12:7]].

## II. Seven is the number for completion in God's move, God's operation:

### A. God's move to accomplish His creation with His rest was completed in seven days—[[Genesis-01#v31|Gen. 1:31]]; [[Genesis-02#v3|2:3]].

### B. The seven churches in Revelation give us a complete view of God's move in the church throughout its history—[[Revelation-02|Rev. 2—3]].

### C. The mystery of God's economical move is contained in the seven seals—[[Revelation-06#v1|6:1]]—[[Revelation-08#v5|8:5]].

### D. The execution of God's move to judge the earth and bring in His kingdom is carried out with the seven trumpets—[[Revelation-08#v6|8:6]]—[[Revelation-11#v18|11:18]].

### E. The seven bowls are the complete outpouring of God's wrath on Antichrist and rebellious humanity—[[Revelation-16#v1|16:1-21]].

### F. The seven Spirits of God, the sevenfold intensified Spirit of God, are desperately needed for God's move to be completed on the earth—[[2 Chronicles-16#v9|2 Chron. 16:9]]; [[Zechariah-04#v10|Zech. 4:10]].

## III. The number seven also signifies intensification—[[Daniel-03#v19|Dan. 3:19]]; [[Isaiah-30#v26|Isa. 30:26]]:

### A. Daniel 3 speaks of a sevenfold intensification of the heat—[[Revelation-16#v19|v. 19]].

### B. Isaiah 30 speaks of the sevenfold intensification of the light of the sun—[[Revelation-16#v26|v. 26]].

### C. Our cooperation with God to complete His move depends upon our being intensified for His move:

#### 1. Today the Spirit who is filling us and saturating us to save us organically is the sevenfold-intensified life-giving Spirit.

#### 2. Since the life-giving Spirit has been intensified sevenfold all the elements of the Spirit have been intensified sevenfold:

##### a. The Spirit is the Spirit of life; the life in the intensified Spirit is an intensified life—[[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]].

##### b. The Spirit is the Spirit of grace; the grace in the intensified Spirit is an intensified grace—[[Hebrews-10#v29|Heb. 10:29]].

## IV. In [[Revelation-01#v4|Revelation 1:4-5]] the Spirit becomes the second, the center, of the Divine Trinity, revealing the importance of the intensified function of the sevenfold Spirit of God:

### A. The seven Spirits are burning before the throne to carry out God's administration, to execute God's economy in the universe, by directing the world situation.

### B. The sevenfold intensified Spirit is the seven lamps of fire to burn, enlighten, expose, judge, purify, and refine us to produce the golden lampstands for the fulfillment of God's New Testament economy—[[Revelation-04#v5|Rev. 4:5]].

### C. The sevenfold intensified Spirit is the eyes of Christ as the redeeming Lamb and the building stone to observe and search us and to infuse and transfuse us with Christ's essence, riches, and burden for God's building—[[Zechariah-03#v9|Zech. 3:9]]; [[Zechariah-04#v7|4:7]]; [[Revelation-01#v14|Rev. 1:14]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]].

### D. The golden lampstands, as the multiplied embodiment of the Triune God, are filled with God the Spirit as the seven shining Spirits—the full expression of the Triune God—[[Revelation-01#v12|1:12]], [[Revelation-01#v20|20]]; [[Ephesians-05#v18|Eph. 5:18]]; [[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]]; [[Galatians-05#v16|Gal. 5:16]], [[Galatians-05#v22|22-23]].

### E. The seven Spirits, who are the lamps of fire in this age executing God's New Testament economy, will become the river of water saturating the holy city of God, the New Jerusalem—[[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]].

### F. By the operation of the sevenfold-intensified Spirit within Christ's seeking believers, they are intensified to become the overcomers to build up the Body of Christ, which consummates the New Jerusalem.
