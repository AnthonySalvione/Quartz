# **Lesson Twelve**—The Producing of the Overcomers (2) — Overcoming the Church's Union with the World and the Teaching of Balaam, the Teaching of the Nicolaitans, and the Teaching of Jezebel

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-02#v12|Rev. 2:12-29]]

## I. The Lord's word to Pergamos indicates that we need to overcome worldliness in a church that is married to the world where Satan administrates on his throne and dwells for his possession—[[Revelation-02#v13|Rev. 2:13]]:

### A. If we are faithful to overcome the church's union with the world, the Lord will give us to eat of the hidden manna—v. 17b:

#### 1. The hidden manna, signifying the hidden Christ, is a special portion of heavenly food reserved for His overcoming seekers, who overcome the degradation of the worldly church.

#### 2. While the church goes the way of the world, the overcomers come forward to abide in the Holy of Holies, where they enjoy the hidden Christ as a special portion for their daily supply—[[Hebrews-09#v3|Heb. 9:3-4]].

#### 3. By eating the hidden manna, we are incorporated into the universal, divine-human incorporation.

### B. If we are faithful to overcome worldliness in a church that is married to the world, the Lord will give us a white stone, upon which a new name is written—[[Revelation-02#v17|Rev. 2:17c]].

## II. The overcomers in Thyatira overcome the teaching of Jezebel—the demonic teaching and satanic depths of the apostate church—by keeping the Lord's word until the end—[[Revelation-02#v20|vv. 20]], [[Revelation-02#v24|24-26]]:

### A. In the apostate Roman Catholic Church are "the deep things of Satan," the mysterious teachings of Satan—[[Revelation-02#v24|v. 24]].

### B. The overcomers in Thyatira do not have Jezebel's teaching and have not known the deep things of Satan; instead, they hold fast the Lord's testimony and keep the Lord's works until the end—[[Revelation-02#v24|vv. 24-26a]].

### C. To the overcomer Christ will give authority over the nations and the morning star—[[Revelation-02#v26|vv. 26-27]]; [[Revelation-12#v5|12:5a]]; [[Revelation-22#v16|22:16]].
