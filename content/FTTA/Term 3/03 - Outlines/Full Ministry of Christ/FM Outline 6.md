# **Lesson Six**—The Burning Lamps of Fire Becoming the Flow of Living Water

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-04#v5|Rev. 4:5]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-2]]

## I. In [[Daniel-07#v9|Daniel 7:9-10]] the Lord's throne was flames of fire; its wheels were burning fire; and a stream of fire issued forth and came out from before Him:

### A. The fire here indicates that God is absolutely righteous and altogether holy.

### B. The Bible reveals that God is the burning One—[[Deuteronomy-04#v24|Deut. 4:24]]; [[Hebrews-12#v29|Heb. 12:29]].

### C. Without holiness no one can see the Lord or contact Him—[[Hebrews-12#v14|v. 14]].

## II. The seven lamps of fire in [[Revelation-04#v5|Revelation 4:5]] are for the carrying out of God's administration:

### A. Today God is administrating His government by means of enlightening, searching, exposing, judging, and burning.

### B. Anything that does not correspond to God's nature will be burned by His fire.

### C. Everything that is not of God or according to God will be burned by fire.

### D. The burning lamps refine those things that are according to God's nature.

## III. Eventually, in the New Jerusalem the seven lamps of burning fire before the throne of God's administration will become the flow of the river of water of life proceeding from the life-imparting throne—[[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]]:

### A. The seven lamps become one river:

#### 1. In [[Revelation-04|Revelation 4]] we have the seven lamps burning before the throne of God.

#### 2. In [[Revelation-22|Revelation 22]] we have the river of water of life proceeding out of the throne of God.

### B. According to our experience, after we receive the burning of the seven lamps, the seven Spirits become one flow of living water.

### C. The Spirit never fails to flow after He burns us; His flowing always follows His burning.
