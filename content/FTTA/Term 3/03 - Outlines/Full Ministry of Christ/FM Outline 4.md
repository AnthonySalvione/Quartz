# **Lesson Four**—The Seven Burning Lamps of Fire for God's Move


**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]]; [[Zechariah-03#v9|Zech. 3:9]]

## I. The seven lamps of the golden lampstand are the seven Spirits before God's throne, the seven lamps of fire burning before God's throne—[[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]:

### A. In the universe God has a center of administration, which is His throne.

### B. God is administering and moving from His throne to execute His eternal policy.

### C. The seven lamps of fire burning before God's throne signifies that the seven lamps are absolutely related to God's administration, economy, and move.

## II. To know God's administration and economy we must have the light of the golden lampstand from the seven shining and illumining lamps:

### A. Natural light cannot help us to know God's economy, administration, and eternal purpose—[[Revelation-21#v23|21:23]], [[Revelation-21#v25|25]]; [[Revelation-22#v5|22:5a]].

### B. The light of the lampstand is the light in the Holy Place, which typifies the church.

### C. Once we enter into the realm of the church, we are enlightened to know God's eternal purpose, His heart's intention, His economy, and we also know the path we should take for the journey before us toward God's goal—[[Psalm-73#v16|Psa. 73:16-17]].

## III. The light of the lampstand is based on the strength of the priests' service:

### A. In [[1 Samuel-01|1 Samuel]] the lamp of God was about to go out because Eli the priest was weak and degraded—[[1 Samuel-03#v3|1 Sam. 3:3]].

### B. The light in the local church cannot be bright unless we fulfill our priestly duty to burn the incense and light the lamps—[[Exodus-25#v37|Exo. 25:37]]; [[Exodus-27#v20|27:20-21]]; [[Exodus-30#v7|30:7-8]]; [[Acts-06#v4|Acts 6:4]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v24|1 Cor. 14:24-25]].

## IV. According to [[Revelation-04|Revelation 4]] the emphasis with the seven burning lamps of fire is on the move of God's administration:

### A. Christ carries out His mission as the Ruler of the kings of the earth by the seven burning Spirits before the throne to sovereignly control the world situation so that the environment might be fit for God's chosen people to receive His salvation—[[Acts-05#v31|Acts 5:31]]; cf. [[Acts-17#v26|17:26-27]]; [[John-17#v2|John 17:2]]; [[2 Chronicles-16#v9|2 Chron. 16:9]].

### B. The flame of the seven burning Spirits judge, purify, and refine the church to produce the golden lampstands.

### C. The burning of the fiery lamps is not only for shining and burning but also for motivating us to rise up and take action for the carrying out of God's economy—[[Daniel-11#v32|Dan. 11:32]].
