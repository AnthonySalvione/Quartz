# **Lesson Eleven**—The Producing of the Overcomers (1) — Hating the Works of the Nicolaitans


**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-02#v6|Rev. 2:6-7]]; [[Revelation-01#v5|1:5b-6]]; [[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]], [[1 Peter-02#v9|9]]

## I. In the epistle to the church in Ephesus, to overcome is to recover our first love toward the Lord and to hate the works of the Nicolaitans, the hierarchy that the Lord hates—[[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4]], [[Revelation-02#v7|7]]:

### A. The Greek word for Nicolaitans means conquering the common people, being victorious over the laity:

#### 1. The Nicolaitans are a group of people who esteem themselves higher than common believers—[[3 John-01#v9|3 John 9]].

#### 2. The Lord hates the works, the behavior, of these Nicolaitans, and we must hate what the Lord hates—[[Exodus-19#v6|Exo. 19:6]]; [[Exodus-32#v1|32:1-6]].

#### 3. In the proper church life, there should be neither clergy nor laity; all believers should be priests of God—[[Revelation-01#v5|Rev. 1:5b-6]]; [[Amos-06#v1|Amos 6:1a]]; [[Deuteronomy-04#v25|Deut. 4:25]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|Rev. 4:5]]; [[Romans-12#v11|Rom. 12:11]].

## II. The Lord's recovery (versus Satan's strategy against the church) is for the annulling of the clergy and laity and the developing of the gifts, functions, and capacity of all the members of the organic Body of Christ:

### A. "If all the one-talented ones in the church today would bring forth their talents, there would be no need of so many great gifts among us. Just by the coming forth of the one-talented ones, let me tell you, the whole world will be conquered" (*Further Talks on the Church Life*, p. 143)—[[Matthew-25#v24|Matt. 25:24-30]].

### B. The God-ordained way of meeting and serving is to recover:

#### 1. The priesthood of the gospel in the New Testament—[[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]], [[1 Peter-02#v9|9]]:

##### a. To save sinners to offer them up to God as acceptable sacrifices—[[Romans-15#v16|Rom. 15:16]].

##### b. To bring the believers up to lead them to present themselves to God as living sacrifices—[[Revelation-12#v1|12:1]].

##### c. To warn and teach every saint in all wisdom to present each one full-grown in Christ—[[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27-29]].

#### 2. The organic building up of the Body of Christ—[[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|Col. 2:19]].

#### 3. The perfecting of the saints in vital group meetings to make them living, active, functioning members of the Body of Christ—[[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-16]]; [[Hebrews-10#v24|Heb. 10:24-25]].

#### 4. The church meeting in mutuality with the saints prophesying for the building up of the Body of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-14#v4|1 Cor. 14:4b]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v26|26]].

### C. The God-ordained way of meeting and serving brings us out of the traditional way of meeting and serving according to "the customs of the nations"—[[2 Kings-17#v8|2 Kings 17:8]] (NASB).

## III. In order to live a priestly life, we must experience the law of life, which constitutes us the members of the Body of Christ with all kinds of functions—[[Hebrews-08#v10|Heb. 8:10]]; [[Romans-08#v2|Rom. 8:2]]; [[Exodus-19#v6|Exo. 19:6]]; [[Proverbs-30#v19|Prov. 30:19a]]:

### A. We must be vessels open to the Lord to be filled with Him as grace for the development of our spiritual abilities in life to build up His Body—[[Romans-12#v6|Rom. 12:6]]:

#### 1. We must love the Lord personally and affectionately so that all our service would be a labor of love—[[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4]]; [[1 Thessalonians-01#v3|1 Thes. 1:3]]; [[Song-01#v2|S. S. 1:2]], [[Song-01#v4|4]]; [[1 Corinthians-08#v1|1 Cor. 8:1]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v4|14:4b]].

#### 2. We must eat the Lord as the tree of life daily to prophesy, to speak Him into others—[[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7]]; [[Ezekiel-03#v1|Ezek. 3:1-3]].

#### 3. We must have much and thorough prayer to serve the Lord in our spirit—[[Acts-04#v24|Acts 4:24]]; [[Acts-06#v4|6:4]]; [[1 Timothy-02#v1|1 Tim. 2:1]]; [[Colossians-04#v2|Col. 4:2]]; [[Romans-01#v9|Rom. 1:9]]; [[John-04#v24|John 4:24]].

### B. We must allow Christ to shepherd people through us by contacting them, one by one, personally and regularly to dispense Christ as life into them for the increase and building up of Christ's Body—[[Revelation-21#v15|21:15-17]]; [[1 Peter-05#v4|1 Pet. 5:4]].
