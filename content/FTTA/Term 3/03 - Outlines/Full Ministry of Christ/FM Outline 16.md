# **Lesson Sixteen**—Becoming the New Jerusalem (2)

## I. The New Jerusalem is the embodiment of God's complete salvation—[[Revelation-21#v19|Rev. 21:19-27]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-2]]:

### A. God's complete salvation is a composition of God's righteousness as the base and God's life as the consummation—[[Romans-05#v18|Rom. 5:18]].

### B. The foundation of the New Jerusalem is the righteousness of God with God's faithfulness, and the consummation is God's life with the divine nature.

### C. The entire New Jerusalem is a matter of life built on the foundation of righteousness.

## II. We become the New Jerusalem by experiencing God's organic salvation—[[Revelation-03#v12|Rev. 3:12]]; [[Romans-05#v10|Rom. 5:10]]; [[Romans-08#v29|8:29-30]]:

### A. God proceeds step by step to make His chosen people regenerated ones, sanctified ones, renewed ones, transformed ones, and glorified ones.

### B. As we experience each section of God's organic salvation, we go up level by level until we became the same and become beings in the New Jerusalem:

#### 1. Regeneration to become God's species, God's children—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]]; [[1 John-03#v1|1 John 3:1]]; [[Revelation-21#v7|Rev. 21:7]].

#### 2. Sanctification to be as holy as the holy city—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v23|1 Thes. 5:23]]; [[Ephesians-05#v26|Eph. 5:26]].

#### 3. Renewing to be as new as the New Jerusalem—[[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16]]; [[Ephesians-04#v23|Eph. 4:23]].

#### 4. Transformation to be constituted with the Triune God—[[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]].

#### 5. Conformation to the image of the firstborn Son of God to have the appearance of the New Jerusalem—[[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]].

#### 6. Glorification to be completely permeated with the glory of the New Jerusalem—[[Romans-08#v30|v. 30]]; [[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]; [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]; [[Revelation-21#v10|Rev. 21:10-11]].

## III. We become the New Jerusalem by reigning in life—[[Romans-05#v21|Rom. 5:21]]; [[John-04#v14|John 4:14b]]:

### A. To reign in life unto eternal life ([[Romans-05#v21|Rom. 5:21]]) is to reign in life unto the New Jerusalem.

### B. Into eternal life ([[John-04#v14|John 4:14b]]) means "into the New Jerusalem":

#### 1. Into speaks of destination.

#### 2. Into here also means "to become" or "to be."

#### 3. Eternal life is the destination of the flowing Triune God.

#### 4. The eternal life eventually will be the New Jerusalem.

### C. The New Jerusalem is the totality of the divine life, the life of God.

### D. The issue of our reigning in life will be the New Jerusalem.

### E. The goal of our reigning in life should be uniquely and ultimately the goal of God's eternal economy—the New Jerusalem as the ultimate incorporation of the union and mingling of divinity with humanity.
