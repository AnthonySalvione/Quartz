# **Lesson Ten**—The Degradation of the Church (3) — Leaving the First Love 

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-02#v1|Rev. 2:1-7]]; [[Mark-12#v30|Mark 12:30]]

## I. The leaving of the first love is the source and the main reason for the failure of the church throughout the ages—[[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4]]:

### A. Nothing but love can keep us in a proper relationship with the Lord—[[Mark-12#v30|Mark 12:30]].

### B. The leaving of the first love is the source of all the degradation in the succeeding stages of the church.

### C. The first thing that we have to overcome is the leaving of the first love—[[Revelation-02#v5|Rev. 2:5a]].

## II. If we would be overcomers, we must recover, maintain, and develop our first love toward the Lord:

### A. To love the Lord Jesus with the first love is to give Him the first place in all things—[[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18b]]:

#### 1. We need to give Christ the first place, the preeminence, in all things and in all matters, regarding Him as everything in our life—[[Revelation-03#v11|3:11]].

#### 2. We must not love anyone or anything above the Lord, including our soul-life—[[Matthew-10#v37|Matt. 10:37-39]]; [[Revelation-12#v11|Rev. 12:11]].

### B. Our love for the Lord is the issue of His forgiveness—[[Luke-07#v40|Luke 7:40-47]].

### C. We need to develop a personal and affectionate relationship with the Lord—[[Song-01#v2|S. S. 1:2-4]].

### D. We love the Lord because He first loved us, infusing His loving essence into us and generating within us the love with which we love Him—[[1 John-04#v19|1 John 4:19]], [[1 John-04#v8|8]], [[1 John-04#v16|16]].

### E. We love the Lord according to the divine dispensing of the Divine Trinity as love—[[Romans-05#v5|Rom. 5:5]]; [[Romans-08#v39|8:39]], [[Romans-08#v35|35]]; [[Romans-15#v30|15:30]].

### F. If we would maintain and develop our first love toward the Lord, we need to be vessels open to the Lord, loving Him, receiving Him, being filled with Him, and letting Him be everything to us and do everything in us, through us, and for us—[[2 Corinthians-04#v7|2 Cor. 4:7]]; [[Romans-09#v21|Rom. 9:21]], [[Romans-09#v23|23]].
