# **Lesson Fifteen**—Becoming the New Jerusalem (1)

## I. We become the New Jerusalem by closely following the apostles' teaching—[[Revelation-21#v14|Rev. 21:14]]; [[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]]:

### A. The twelve foundation stones of the New Jerusalem bear the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb, representing the apostles' teaching and indicating that the New Jerusalem is built according to the apostles' teaching—[[Revelation-21#v14|Rev. 21:14]].

### B. We need to continue steadfastly in and closely follow the apostles' teaching to build up the Body of Christ to consummate the New Jerusalem—[[2 Timothy-03#v10|2 Tim. 3:10]], [[2 Timothy-03#v14|14]]; [[2 Timothy-02#v2|2:2]]; [[Titus-01#v9|Titus 1:9]].

## II. We become the New Jerusalem by enjoying the grace of God—[[Revelation-22#v21|Rev. 22:21]]:

### A. Grace is the processed and consummated Triune God becoming life and everything to us—[[1 Peter-05#v10|1 Pet. 5:10]]; [[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]]; [[Hebrews-10#v29|Heb. 10:29]].

### B. The twelve apostles represent the grace of the New Testament, signifying that grace is the source, the element, and the means for producing the New Jerusalem.

### C. We need to be constituted with grace, labor by grace, dispense grace, and minister the word of grace—[[1 Corinthians-15#v10|1 Cor. 15:10]]; [[1 Peter-04#v10|1 Pet. 4:10]]; [[Acts-20#v32|Acts 20:32]].

## III. We become the New Jerusalem by becoming a "cube"—[[Revelation-21#v16|Rev. 21:16]]:

### A. The fact that the length, breadth, and height of the New Jerusalem are equal indicates that the New Jerusalem is a cube.

### B. If we would become the New Jerusalem, we need to be saved from being "liners" and become a "cube" in our spiritual experience—[[Ephesians-03#v14|Eph. 3:14-18]].

## IV. We become the New Jerusalem by becoming a golden mountain—[[Revelation-21#v16|Rev. 21:16]], [[Revelation-21#v18|18]], [[Revelation-21#v21|21b]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1]]:

### A. In the Bible a mountain signifies resurrection and ascension; thus, we become a mountain by experiencing Christ in His resurrection and ascension—[[Ephesians-02#v5|Eph. 2:5-6]].

### B. As a golden mountain, the New Jerusalem comes out of the divine nature; thus, we become the New Jerusalem by partaking of the divine nature—[[2 Peter-01#v4|2 Pet. 1:4]].

### C. The entire golden city is transparent; thus, we become a transparent mountain of gold by becoming transparent in the divine life and nature—[[Revelation-21#v18|Rev. 21:18b]], [[Revelation-21#v21|21b]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1]].

### D. As a golden mountain, the New Jerusalem is the unique, ultimate, and eternal golden lampstand, the aggregate of all the lampstands; thus, we become the New Jerusalem by living in and being a part of the church as a golden lampstand—[[Revelation-21#v23|21:23]]; [[Revelation-01#v12|1:12]], [[Revelation-01#v20|20]].
