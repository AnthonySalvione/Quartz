# **Lesson Five**—The Seven Eyes of the Lamb

## I. After the seven lamps of fire burn in us, they become the seven eyes—[[Revelation-04#v5|Rev. 4:5]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]].

## II. As the redeeming Lamb, Christ has seven watching and observing eyes for the carrying out of God's administration—[[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]].

## III. The seven eyes are for transfusing all that the Lamb is into our being so that we may become the same as He is for God's building—[[Zechariah-03#v9|Zech. 3:9]]:

### A. A person's eyes are His expression.

### B. Our inner being is expressed through our eyes.

### C. The seven Spirits are the seven eyes by which Christ expresses Himself.

### D. The seven eyes of Christ, which are the seven Spirits of God, are Christ's expression in God's move for God's building.

### E. The way for us to be transformed is to come to Him and be seen by Him.

### F. As the Lord looks at us, His seven eyes transfuse Himself into us; He transmits His inner being into us through His eyes.

## IV. God guides us with His eyes:

### A. "The eyes of Jehovah run to and fro throughout all the earth to strengthen those whose heart is perfect toward Him"—[[2 Chronicles-16#v9|2 Chron. 16:9a]].

### B. "The eyes of Jehovah are in every place, / Keeping watch on the evil and the good"—[[Proverbs-15#v3|Prov. 15:3]].

### C. "I will instruct you and teach you concerning the way you should go; / I will counsel you; my eye is upon you"—[[Psalm-32#v8|Psa. 32:8]].

### D. Paul lived and acted according the index of Christ's whole person, expressed in His eyes—[[2 Corinthians-02#v10|2 Cor. 2:10]].
