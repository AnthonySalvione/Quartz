# Study Questions

## Lesson 1

1. Memorize what the Lord's recovery is in one sentence. (*Divine and Mystical Realm*, pp. 17–18)

2. Memorize the five critical points concerning the Spirit including corresponding verse references and be able to substantiate each of the five points. (outline point III)

## Lesson 2

1. Study John 14:16–20 and be able to speak on the four "ins."

2. Using the verse references from the outline, explain the distinction between the Spirit of God, the Spirit of Jehovah, and the Spirit of holiness.

3. Be able to explain the distinction between the Holy Spirit's involvement in the conception of John the Baptist and in the conception of Jesus.

4. Study Roman numeral III and be able to speak on one aspect of the Spirit.

## Lesson 3

1. Using the verse references from the outline, explain the distinction of the Spirit of Jesus, the Spirit of Christ, and the Spirit of Jesus Christ.

2. Using the verse references from the outline, explain the distinction of the Spirit of life, the life-giving Spirit, and the Spirit.

3. Memorize the last paragraph, beginning with, "The Spirit is...", from the *Conclusion of the New Testament*, Message 80, p. 870.

## Lesson 4

1. How is the Spirit of the living God related to writing "living letters of Christ" in 2 Corinthians 3:3?

2. Study the 8 aspects of the Spirit and be prepared to speak from both the standpoint of the truth and of personal experience on at least 2 aspects.

## Lesson 5

1. Memorize the 7 statuses of Christ in His all-problem solving death and the related verse references. Be able to speak on at least two from the standpoint of the truth.

2. Memorize what was accomplished in each of the two "becomings" of Christ.

## Lesson 6

1. Study Ezekiel 47 and be able to speak on the major items presented and apply them to personal experience: the river, the altar, the directions, the measuring reed, brass.

2. Be prepared to speak on one experience of cherishing another in the humanity of Jesus.

3. Be prepared to speak on one experience of nourishing another in the divinity of Christ.

## Lesson 7

1. Memorize the nine aspects of the Spirit with the related verse references from 2 Corinthians. Be able to speak of one experience of one of the aspects.

2. Be able to speak on the anointing, pledging and sealing Spirit using verse references.

3. Spend at least 15 minutes "praying yourself into God." Be prepared to share about your experience.

## Lesson 8

1. Memorize the 5 ingredients of the compound ointment and their significances.

2. Be prepared to speak about one of the spices in the compound ointment as applied in your experience.

## Lesson 9

1. Memorize the significance of the measurements of the ingredients of the compound ointment.

2. In what way is the compound Spirit typified by the compound ointment?
