### VIII. The Spirit of grace—[[Hebrews-10#v29|Heb. 10:29]]

> Point C in the reading equates the Spirit of grace to the Holy Spirit that the Hebrew believers had began to partake from.

> This Spirit of grace is indwelling us and working in us to be our everything. Hence, the Spirit of grace is very much insulted when we try to do the work ourselves, resulting in us willfully sinning. This Spirit of grace reacts within us if it is insulted.

**Reading:** *Life-study of Hebrews*, p. 495

### IX. The Spirit of glory—[[1 Peter-04#v14|1 Pet. 4:14]]

As Romans 1:4, This Spirit of glory is the One ==through whom== **Christ** was glorified in His resurrection. says, at the time of Christ's resurrection, He was designated the Son of God in power *according* to the Spirit of holiness
