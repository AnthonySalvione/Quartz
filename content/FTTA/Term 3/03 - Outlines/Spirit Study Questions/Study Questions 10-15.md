## Lesson 10

1. Study the function of the compound Spirit in the New Testament and the related verses and be able to speak on two items.

2. What is the relationship between the ointment and the incense in Exodus 30?

3. How is the enjoyment of the anointing oil greater than the enjoyment of the Passover, the manna, and the water from the rock?

## Lesson 11

1. Study the pneumatic Christ as the compound Spirit that gives life in the context of Romans 8:2, 9–11.

2. Memorize the three sentences regarding the compound Spirit. (*Divine and Mystical Realm*, pg. 15: the last three sentences of the first paragraph in section ‘D’)

3. Study and be able to speak on the operation and dispensing of the law of the Spirit of life in the three parts of the believers in the context of Romans 8:6, 9–11.

## Lesson 12

1. Study the four laws in Romans 7 & 8.

2. How do we "switch on" the law of the Spirit of life? Memorize the verse references. Be prepared to share a personal experience of "switching on."

3. What are the 9 different aspects of *zoe* dispensed into the tripartite man?

## Lesson 13

1. Know the move of the sevenfold-intensified life-giving Spirit in Christ's heavenly ministry to the churches in Revelation 2 & 3.

2. Study the rewards in the kingdom age to each of the churches in Revelation 2 & 3.

## Lesson 14

1. Study the progression of the lampstand through the Old and New Testament (Exo. 25:37; Zech. 4:2–10; 1 Kings 7:49; Rev. 1:12). Memorize the verse references.

2. Study the progression of the seven lamps, the seven eyes, and the seven Spirits of God through the Old and New Testament. (Rev. 4:5; 5:6; Zech. 3:9 and the above verses). Memorize the verse references.

3. Memorize Roman numeral I including references. What is the significance of the Spirit being sevenfold intensified?

## Lesson 15

1. What is the significance and importance in our taking the New Jerusalem as our goal?

2. Study and be able to list the composition of the overcomers.

3. What does Revelation 21 & 22 reveal regarding the accomplishments and consummation of the overcomers? Memorize the related verse references.

4. Know the significances of the final consummation of the overcomers as: the corporate overcomer, being rewarded at the judgment seat of Christ, the bride, the army, and the participation in the fullest enjoyment of the processed Triune God in three aspects.
