## IV. The blood—[[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]], [[1 John-01#v9|9]]:

### A. At this point in the life cycle, having spontaneously and immediately confessed what we see in the divine light, the blood cleanses us:

#### 1. We do not need to beg or go through some kind of mental application.

#### 2. The blood is applied to us ([[1 John-01#v7|vv. 7]], [[1 John-01#v9|9]]) when, having the sense, the realization, of our dirtiness and sinfulness, we simply agree with God.

#### 3. So, according to these verses, actual and genuine cleansing occurs when we are in the fellowship of life and in the light (see *Life Messages*, pp. 71-72; *Basic Lessons on Life*, p. 81).

#### 4. We do not out-grow the blood; in the fellowship of life we always see that we are wrong, short, etc., and our prayer should reflect this realization.

Actually, overcomers... aren't just those that need the blood. Overcomes are overcomers because of the blood. OVercomers are those who simply know the true value of the Lord's blood. Those who overcome realize who they are in themselves, and also realizes the value of the Lord's blood.

### B. As we continue in the life cycle, over and over again, the Lord shines on, forgives, and cleanses our outward sins (actions):

#### 1. The Lord may take us back, cycle after cycle, six months, then one year, then perhaps even ten years, to catch us, check us, dig us, so that sins, stains, and blockages can be removed.

#### 2. Our relationship with God is unbreakable, yet our fellowship with Him can be interrupted; the former is of life, whereas the latter is based on our living, though it is also of life; one is unconditional, the other is conditional; our fellowship, which is conditional, needs to be maintained by the constant cleansing of the Lord's blood (see footnote 3 on [[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]]).

There is nothing my son can do that will prevent him from being my son... but the fellowshuip that we have can be broken. 

#### 3. This cleansing will leave more and more room for Christ to grow in us!

## V. The anointing—[[1 John-02#v20|1 John 2:20]], [[1 John-02#v27|27]]:

### A. In the Bible, the anointing always follows the blood; in [[Leviticus-14#v14|Lev. 14:14-18]] the leper first had the blood, then the ointment applied for his cleansing and acceptance into God's presence; in [[1 John-01|1 John 1]] we have the blood and, following this, in [[1 John-02|1 John 2]] we have the anointing (cf. *Hymns*, [[Hymn-279#Stanza 1|#279, stanza 1]]).

### B. The anointing is the Triune God Himself, moving and adding Himself into us:

#### 1. He as the anointing Spirit is all-inclusive; this is typified in [[Exodus-30|Exo. 30]].

#### 2. His moving and adding Himself into us is as if He were "painting" us with Himself, with His very substance and element; we get more God, more Spirit, more life.

When you paint a dark wall, you need more coats of paint... if you start with white, you need more than two coats... but if you have a black wall, you need MANY coats. Over and over again... we are black inside.

#### 3. We might say that this cleansing following anointing represents the continuation of the life cycle.

### C. The anointing adds more God, more life, into us and teaches us to "abide in Him" ([[1 John-02#v27|1 John 2:27]]), that is, to go on to be more in the fellowship of life (the abiding equals the fellowship); hence, the life cycle continues from more life to more fellowship, etc. (see *Practical Lessons on the Experience of Life*, p. 54).

### D. So, in the life cycle we firstly receive the divine life; this life brings its fellowship; this fellowship brings in light which is God Himself; in this light we confess and are cleansed by the blood; following this cleansing, the anointing comes, each time fresher, sweeter, stronger, more soothing; this anointing teaches us to abide in this living Person, that is, to remain in the fellowship—a little deeper each time; this deeper fellowship brings in stronger light; in the stronger light we have a keener sense and realization of our sins, confess, and are cleansed—more and more thoroughly; this cleansing, in turn, brings more God, more life, which equals more growth, which issues in more building up of the Body of Christ ([[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]]), which consummates in the New Jerusalem!
