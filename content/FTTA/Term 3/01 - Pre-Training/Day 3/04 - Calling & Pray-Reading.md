By his word to Timothy in [[2 Timothy-02#v22|2 Tim. 2:22]], Paul indicated that in the early days all the Lord’s seekers practiced such calling. Undoubtedly, he was one who practiced this, since he charged his young co-worker Timothy to do this that Timothy might enjoy the Lord as he did.
