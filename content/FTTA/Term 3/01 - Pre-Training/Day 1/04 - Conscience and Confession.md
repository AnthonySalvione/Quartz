## I. The origin and position of the conscience:

### A. Although we find no definite record of this in the Bible, yet, according to the whole truth of the Bible and also according to our experience, the conscience was created in man when God created man—[[Genesis-02#v7|Gen. 2:7]].

### B. However, it was not until after man partook of the fruit of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil that the function of the conscience was made manifest—[[Genesis-03#v7|Gen. 3:7]].

### C. The position of the conscience is in the human spirit; the human spirit has three parts: fellowship, intuition, and conscience; the conscience enables us to discern right and wrong, good and evil—[[Romans-09#v1|Rom. 9:1]]; cf. [[Romans-06#v16|8:16]].

## II. The function of the conscience:

### A. We may divide the function of the conscience into three aspects:

#### 1. The conscience is the organ which enables us to differentiate between right and wrong, good and evil.

#### 2. The conscience enables us to know both what God justifies and what He condemns, what He delights in and what He hates—[[Romans-02#v15|Rom. 2:15]].

#### 3. The conscience represents God in ruling over man according to His principles and laws, not only to uphold the existence of the individual but also to hold together all the relationships of man in the universe—[[Genesis-04#v6|Gen. 4:6-7]].

### B. Man's conscience corresponds with the God-created human nature and enables man to know what God justifies and what He condemns—[[Romans-02#v14|Rom. 2:14-15]].

### C. When man sinks deeper into sin, the feeling of the conscience is cast aside—[[Titus-01#v15|Titus 1:15]]; [[1 Timothy-04#v1|1 Tim. 4:1-2]]; [[Ephesians-04#v19|Eph. 4:19]]:

#### 1. The mind is the leading part of the soul, and the conscience is the main part of our spirit. If our mind is polluted, our soul is spontaneously polluted, and if our conscience is defiled, our spirit is unavoidably defiled—[[Titus-01#v15|Titus 1:15]].

#### 2. The conscience of hypocritical liars has lost its sense, as if branded with a hot iron—[[1 Timothy-04#v2|1 Tim. 4:2]].

#### 3. Feeling in [[Ephesians-04#v19|Ephesians 4:19]] refers mainly to the consciousness of one's conscience; hence, being past feeling means not caring for one's conscience.

### D. The Greek word for conscience (*suneidesis*) means **with knowledge**; the function of the conscience is according to the knowledge that it has.

## VI. The relationship between our conscience and our confession—[[1 John-01#v5|1 John 1:5—2:2]]; [[1 John-03#v19|3:19-21]]:

### A. To continue in the enjoyment and intimate fellowship with the Triune God requires us to keep a clear conscience by confessing our sins—[[1 John-01#v6|1 John 1:6]].

### B. Our relationship with God is unbreakable and is a matter of life; yet our fellowship with Him can be interrupted and is based on our living—[[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]].

### C. Before God, the redeeming blood of the Lord has cleansed us once for all eternally, and the efficacy of that cleansing lasts forever before God, so that that cleansing need not be repeated.

### D. However, in our conscience we need the instant application of the constant cleansing of the Lord's blood again and again whenever our conscience is enlightened by the divine light in our fellowship with God.

### E. Hence, we need to continually admit and confess our sins to maintain our sweet and intimate fellowship with God—[[1 John-01#v8|1 John 1:8]].

### F. If we confess our sins, He, according to His word and based on the redemption through the blood of Jesus, forgives us because He must be faithful in His word and righteous in the blood of Jesus. Our confession is needed for His forgiveness—[[1 John-01#v9|1 John 1:9]].

### G. To forgive us is to release us from the offenses of our sins, whereas to cleanse us is to wash us from the stain of our unrighteousness.

### H. When we sin, He, based on the propitiation that He accomplished, takes care of our case by interceding and pleading for us—[[1 John-02#v1|1 John 2:1]].

### I. When we sin, the Lord Jesus as our Advocate undertakes the case for us as a family affair, a case between children and the Father—[[1 John-02#v1|1 John 2:1-2]].

### J. If our conscience condemns us, surely God, who is greater than His representative (our conscience) and knows all things, will condemn us—[[1 John-03#v20|1 John 3:20]].

### K. The consciousness of such condemnation alerts us to the danger of breaking our fellowship with God.

### L. If our heart does not blame us we have boldness to contact God—[[1 John-03#v21|1 John 3:21]].
