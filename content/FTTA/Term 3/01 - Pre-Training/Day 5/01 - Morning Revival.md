# Morning Revival (13): The Means of Fellowship (3)—Reading the Bible (17-29)

**Scripture Reading:** [[John-05#v39|John 5:39-40]]; [[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]]; [[Luke-24#v45|Luke 24:45]]; [[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17]]; [[Psalm-63#v1|Psa. 63:1]]; [[Psalm-119#v15|119:15]], [[Psalm-119#v97|97]], [[Psalm-119#v103|103]], [[Psalm-119#v130|130]]

## I. There are two ways of reading the Bible—one for fellowship and one for understanding:

### A. When reading for understanding:

#### 1. We need to exercise our mind; the larger our mental capacity is, the better.

#### 2. We also need to have a good collection of reference books; the more reference books we use in our time of study, the better.

### B. When reading for fellowship:

#### 1. We do not understand the Word with our mind but contact the Word with our spirit.

#### 2. Whether we understand is not the point, neither is whether or not we remember.

### C. We should simply exercise our spirit to have good contact with God in His Word.

## II. When we read the Bible for fellowship with God, we need to remember the following points:

### A. We should not intend to seek an understanding of it—[[John-05#v39|John 5:39-40]]:

#### 1. If we come to the Bible with such an intention, we will encounter a great hindrance, a distraction, in our fellowship with God.

#### 2. It is not easy to let go of this intention; it requires much consideration and practice.

#### 3. Only through reading the Bible in this way can we truly understand the Bible.

### B. We need to avoid study and research—[[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]:

#### 1. If we understand the Bible when we are reading it, this is fine, and if we do not understand it, we should not be concerned.

#### 2. We need to put all our efforts, energy, and intention into fellowshipping with God rather than studying the words of the Bible; if we read a portion of the Bible in a spontaneous way, we will definitely touch God in our fellowship.

#### 3. We still need to study and research the Bible, but this should be done at another time when we can use reference books, such as concordances and Bible dictionaries, in order to study the Bible in a detailed way.

### C. We should not read the Bible too fast, and neither should we read it too slowly:

#### 1. We should read as much as we can, without intending to read too much, reading according to the time we have available.

#### 2. We must practice to be calm, spontaneous, and not hasty when we read the Bible to fellowship with God; what matters is that we touch God in fellowship.

### D. We should exercise not to ponder—[[Genesis-03#v1|Gen. 3:1]].

### E. Our intention is not to understand the Bible but to seek and contact the Lord—[[Psalm-63#v1|Psa. 63:1]]; [[John-01#v1|John 1:1]].

### F. We should read the Bible slowly in order to give our spirit the opportunity to contact the words in the Bible—[[John-06#v63|John 6:63]].

### G. We need to pay attention to maintaining constant contact with the Lord and not being distracted by the outward letters—[[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]].

### H. When we touch the Lord's Word with our spirit and remain in continual fellowship with Him, our spirit should have the feeling of being bathed, warmed, refreshed, moistened, and supplied by the Word in the Bible—[[Psalm-119#v103|Psa. 119:103]]; [[Ephesians-05#v26|Eph. 5:26-27]]; [[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]; [[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-24]].

### I. We will receive much more inspiration when we read the Bible in this way than when we read it for understanding—[[Luke-24#v45|Luke 24:45]]; [[Psalm-119#v130|Psa. 119:130]].

### J. Once we touch the inspiration, we need to meditate on it and then turn it into prayer—[[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17]]; [[Psalm-119#v15|Psa. 119:15]].

### K. After praying, if there is still time, we should read more from the Bible.

### L. We may stop our reading of the Bible, but the feeling should not come to an end; we must keep this feeling in our heart—[[Psalm-119#v97|Psa. 119:97]].
