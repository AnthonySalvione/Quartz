They briefly mentioned that each Pre-Training class followed a line in each of the time slots, and I was impressed seeing what each matter develops into.

There are four "Being a Trainee" classes, and then lastly one "The Church, the Ministry, and the Work" class. This shows me that here in the Training we are focusing more on our own person, character, and relationship with the Lord Jesus. However, our learning to be a Trainee is not ultimately for the goal of being a great individual. The aim is that we would be those who are given for the building up of the church, with the minsitry and the word.

---

The third line is "Clearance of the Past" ⇒ "Consecration" ⇒ "The Life Cycle" ⇒ "One Accord & Coordination"

I was helped by this point from "Clearance of the Past" - Once we are saved, if we desire to have a good Christian life, a good testimony and go on with the Lord in a pure way, we should completely clear away the things of our past.

Consecration is our consent for the Lord to work in us in order for us to be used by Him. After we have cleared away the past, we should then open and set our being for the Lord to work in us.

The Lord working in us produces a kind of life cycle, which was presented to us next. While we have the Lord operating in us more, because of the light He shines upon us, we feel the need to then clear away more things by His blood according to His leading.

As in the first chapter of 1 John, there is a fellowship that we are being brought into throughout this process. The way to enter this fellowship, which was what was announced in that chapter, was this process of the life cycle. So we see that there is a corporate result and manifestation of this process.

The Lord Jesus begins his ministry with conviction, so that sinners would repent and turn to Him. Clearance of the Past is somewhat private and individual between the Lord and a person, but it brings man into the one accord & coordination which is with the Father and the Son.



---

The last line was "High Peak of the Divine Revelation" ⇒ "The New Jerusalem" ⇒ "The Apostles' Teaching" ⇒ "The Vision & Ministry of the Age" ⇒ "The Lord's Recovery"

We were first shown a vision of the high peak of the divine revelation, which is God becoming man to make man God in life and nature but not in the Godhead. Then we learn that this high peak has a goal and consummation, that being the New Jerusalem. But then that automatically produces a question in us, "Well, we see it, but how do we get there?". This is where the Apostles' Teaching comes in, because it shows us practically how to meet together under one vision to gain the reality of the Body of Christ. With these meetings, we need the content and minstry which we are seeing in the next class. Putting these things together, this is the goal of the Lord's Recovery.
