# Lesson Summary and Enjoyment

## Weekly FTTA Assignments

Trainees are expected to produce two written lesson summaries and  
participate in a peer fellowship and review of one of the summaries  
according to the assigned schedule each week (see chart).

## Written summaries

With each lesson summary the trainees should summarize the week's  
learning and highlight the main burden for the week. This statement  
should include a clear presentation of the main points for the week and  
how those points relate to one's own experience and function in the  
church. Alternatively, one can write in a way that presents the week's  
lesson to a new or young believer in a way that meets them where they  
are. Each summary should be a minimum of 250 words and a maximum of 500  
words.

## Peer fellowship and review

For the assigned lesson summary of the week (**bold**), trainees should  
present their enjoyment and burden to another trainee by either (1)  
sharing a copy of their written portion for review and fellowship or (2)  
practicing speaking their portion to someone else. Limit the peer  
fellowship and review to ones of the same gender and exercise to blend  
with as many trainees as possible. Please follow the instructions online  
to indicate that you have completed this activity.

All summaries for a given week's lessons, including indicating that you  
have completed the corporate review, should be done by 6:30pm on the  
following Monday. We encourage you to work on and submit these summaries  
throughout the week as you are studying. Points may be deducted if  
trainees do not meet the listed parameters. Unless otherwise indicated  
on the syllabus, the lesson summaries will make up 50% of your grade for  
a class.

All Lesson Summary and Enjoyment assignments that have been submitted  
can be found at the following link:  
[https://www.onlineftta.org/c/summaries.php](https://www.onlineftta.org/c/summaries.php)

## To log-in:

- Go to [http://www.onlineftta.org](http://www.onlineftta.org)
    
- Returning trainees should be able to use the credentials that they  
    previously used.
    
- New trainees should enter the credentials that were sent to your  
    email address.
    

The online FTTA website ([http://www.onlineftta.org](http://www.onlineftta.org)) is currently  
whitelisted on TraineeNet and is accessible throughout the week. This  
website should be accessed only while connected to TraineeNet on the  
Ministry Conference Center premises and within the parameters listed  
under "Attendance Server and Lesson Summaries" on the Information  
Manual.

## Late assignments

Late assignments must be fellowshiped with the FTTA Office and completed  
within 14 days of the missed deadline. To receive credit for a late  
assignment, you will need to:

- Submit the late assignment online
    
- Fill out a "Late Assignment Approval Form" located on the trainee  
    counter
    
- Bring the form to the TA brother you were in fellowship with, and  
    ask him to initial the form
    
- Submit the completed form to the "Class Notes" mail slot
