# Fellowship on Vital Groups (5): The Vital Groups: Love Prevails (11-17)

## I. Without love, everything that we do related to the vital groups is in vain.

## II. God is love; we love because He first loved us—[[1 John-04#v8|1 John 4:8]], [[1 John-04#v19|19]].

## III. God's predestination of us unto the divine sonship was motivated by the divine love—[[Ephesians-01#v4|Eph. 1:4-5]].

## IV. God's giving of His only begotten Son to us that we may be saved from perdition judicially through His death and have the eternal life organically in His resurrection was motivated by the divine love—[[John-03#v16|John 3:16]]; [[1 John-04#v9|1 John 4:9-10]].

## V. God's love is the source of the grace of Christ dispensed to us through the fellowship of the Spirit—[[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]].

## VI. God's love motivates us, His children, to love our enemies that we may be perfect as He is; He loves the fallen human race, who became His enemies, by causing His sun (signifying Christ) to rise on the evil and the good indiscriminately and sending rain (signifying the Spirit) on the just and the unjust equally; thus, we may become the sons of the heavenly Father who are sanctified from the tax collectors and the Gentiles—[[Matthew-05#v43|Matt. 5:43-48]].

## VII. Love builds up—[[1 Corinthians-08#v1|1 Cor. 8:1b]].

## VIII. Love is not jealous, is not provoked, does not take account of evil, covers all things, endures all things, never falls away, and is the greatest—[[1 Corinthians-13#v4|1 Cor. 13:4-8]], [[1 Corinthians-13#v13|13]].

## IX. Love is the conclusion of all spiritual virtues and the factor of fruit-bearing that supplies us bountifully with the rich entrance into the kingdom of Christ—[[2 Peter-01#v5|2 Pet. 1:5-11]].

## X. The Body of Christ builds itself up in love—[[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]].

## XI. The spirit that God gives us is of love; hence, it is of power and of sober-mindedness—[[2 Timothy-01#v7|2 Tim. 1:7]].

## XII. He who does not love abides in death—[[1 John-03#v14|1 John 3:14b]].

## XIII. Pursue love while you desire spiritual gifts—[[1 Corinthians-14#v1|1 Cor. 14:1]].

## XIV. To overcome the degradation of the church we need to pursue love with those who seek the Lord out of a pure heart—[[2 Timothy-02#v22|2 Tim. 2:22]].

## XV. Loving one another is a sign that we belong to Christ—[[John-13#v34|John 13:34-35]].

## XVI. The love of God makes us more than conquerors over our circumstantial situations—[[Romans-08#v35|Rom. 8:35-39]].

## XVII. Love is the most excellent way—[[1 Corinthians-12#v31|1 Cor. 12:31b]].

## XVIII. Love covers and builds up, so love is the most excellent way for us to be anything and to do anything for the building up of the Body of Christ.
