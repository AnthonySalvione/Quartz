# Fellowship on Vital Groups (1): Forming and Practicing Vital Groups to Carry Out the Four Steps of the God-Ordained Way (11-01)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Matthew-18#v18|Matt. 18:18-20]]; [[Acts-02#v46|Acts 2:46-47]]; [[Acts-13#v52|13:52]]; [[Acts-04#v31|4:31]]; [[John-15#v16|John 15:16-17]]

## I. Our intention in forming the new groups is have groups of overcomers; this is the reason that the groups are called "vital groups":

### A. We do have some groups already, and we honor them, but there is still the need for some vital groups to fulfill the purpose of the groups and to discharge our burden.

### B. The groups that are up to the standard should be up to the normal level revealed in the Bible, having a purpose, a nature, and a condition:

#### 1. The purpose of the vital groups is the increase, perfecting, and building up of the Body of Christ; the vital groups are to gain the increase, finish the perfecting, and complete the building—[[Acts-02#v46|Acts 2:46-47]]; [[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12]].

#### 2. The nature of the vital groups is spiritual; to be spiritual means to be filled with the Holy Spirit, within and without—[[Acts-13#v52|Acts 13:52]]; [[Acts-04#v31|4:31b]].

#### 3. The condition of the vital groups is that of loving one another in oneness and with one accord; we need to love one another, meeting together in a mutual love—[[John-13#v34|John 13:34-35]]; [[John-17#v21|17:21]], [[John-17#v23|23]]; [[Philippians-02#v2|Phil. 2:2]].

## II. To be vital means to be living and active; in forming the vital groups, the first thing that we need is to be vital:

### A. It is possible to be vitalized only if you yourself are pressed by the Lord to pursue Him desperately in this matter.

### B. We have to look to the Lord's mercy and grace desperately to get ourselves vitalized and to help others be vitalized.

## III. A vital group can come into being only by a saint who is desperate and absolute for the increase of the Lord's recovery; we must take the lead to be made vital—living and active:

### A. We can be made vital through the absolute, clear, and thorough fellowship with the Lord continuously.

### B. We can be made vital through the thorough confession of sins, transgressions, failures, defects, mistakes, wrongdoings, etc., hidden or manifested, before God and before men.

### C. We can be made vital through the absolute and thorough consecration of oneself with everything to the Lord.

### D. We can be made vital through the unceasing and desperate prayer.

### E. We can be made vital through the soaking of the infilling and outpouring of the Spirit.

## IV. After we become vital, we should contact another seeking saint:

### A. We should contact the ones whom we feel can be made vital and whom we can bring into our vital group; we should contact the meeting members who are not yet vital; we need to pray and choose someone whom we can contact.

### B. We need to fellowship with the person whom we contact according to the above procedures (III.A–E.).

### C. We need to make that person vital and invite him or her to join with us as a group.

### D. Both of us should continue to contact other seeking saints and make them vital to join with us and to increase our group.

### E. After the number in our group reaches about ten, we should divide it into two groups and should instruct each of the two groups to do the same thing as we have done with them.

## V. We need to practice fellowshipping, interceding, mutual care, and shepherding with our group members:

### A. With our vital companions, we have to take care of fellowship, intercession, mutual care, and shepherding; all these things can be done in a very spontaneous way.

### B. First, we must take care of the proper fellowship; by the mutual fellowship we can know one another's situation and consider one another—[[Hebrews-10#v24|Heb. 10:24-25]].

### C. This kind of fellowship can spontaneously turn to intercession for one another.

### D. This prayer for one another will bring us into a mutual care for one another.

### E. The mutual care will lead to shepherding, and the shepherding will lead to the rendering of help.

## VI. Fellowship is the basis for blending in the vital groups, so we must practice intimate and thorough fellowship to lay the foundation for blending; we also need to be blended by much and thorough prayer with the release of our spirit:

### A. Our prayer must come out of a burden; what the vital groups should have a burden to pray is this: "Lord, the church needs the proper increase. Lord, we ask You, when the season comes, to give each one of us two people who are remaining fruit. Otherwise, we cannot go on."

### B. Whether or not the vital groups work depends on how much prayer we offer to the Lord.

### C. Praying is the unique way for us to exercise our spirit and even the more to release our spirit; when our spirit is released, we can be blended together in this released spirit; the released spirits are a kind of blending.

### D. For the building up of the vital groups, we need to pray much and thoroughly for the four steps of the God-ordained way.

## VII. The way to carry out the four steps of the God-ordained way is by the vital groups:

### A. The God-ordained way revealed in the Scriptures is of four steps: begetting, feeding, perfecting, and building:

#### 1. The first step is begetting—to produce new believers—[[1 Corinthians-04#v15|1 Cor. 4:15]].

#### 2. The second step is feeding—to feed the new believers so that they may grow—[[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|1 Thes. 2:7]].

#### 3. The third step is perfecting—the perfecting is carried out not by one teacher but through the mutual teaching in the groups—[[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12]]; [[Hebrews-10#v25|Heb. 10:25]].

#### 4. The fourth step is building—the perfecting of the believers makes them qualified to prophesy, to speak for the Lord, for the building up of the church—[[1 Corinthians-14#v26|1 Cor. 14:26]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v31|31]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v4|4b]].

### B. Our vital groups have not yet reached the standard of making everyone function according to the God-ordained way with its four steps—cf. [[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]].

## VIII. Each week we need to have a group meeting:

### A. In our group meeting we need to pray for our work of contacting people.

### B. Then we should fellowship about the situation with our contacts, so that we can study their cases, fellowship about them, and receive the mutual help; then after our study we should pray again:

#### 1. We should not pray and study by ourselves but should study with our group as a unit; if we will pray, the Lord will lead us first to study all the acquaintances of each group member.

#### 2. We should make a list of people we know who need to be saved.

#### 3. We should pray, "Lord, who are the best two or three right now for me to work on?"; when we read this list prayerfully, we will have some inner registration of the two or three whom we should labor on.

### C. We need to consider all our acquaintances, and we need the proper discernment in our consideration of whom we should labor on; we should spend our time to labor on those whom we consider to be promising and should not waste our time on others who are not open.

## IX. We also need to have a second weekly time with our group for the purpose of contacting people:

### A. When we go to contact the sinners, we should not go by ourselves but as a team.

### B. We should exercise to always have two or three new ones under our care.

### C. We can use the telephone to make appointments to talk with people about Christ.

### D. We can visit them by appointment continuously—[[Luke-19#v1|Luke 19:1-10]].

### E. We can invite them to our dinner regularly—[[Mark-02#v14|Mark 2:14-17]].

### F. We can write them concerning their salvation periodically.

### G. We can mail people gospel tracts or spiritual booklets all the time.
