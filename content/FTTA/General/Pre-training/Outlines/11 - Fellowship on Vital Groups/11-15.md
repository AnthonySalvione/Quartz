# Fellowship on Vital Groups (4): The Vital Groups in the Church Life (11-15)

## I. The abnormal situation in the church:

### A. We must admit honestly and with repentance that our church's situation is below the normal level; therefore, we must do something to uplift the level of the church life.

### B. After a prevailing initiation of the Lord's recovery in this country, outsiders, stirred up by the devil, began to oppose us.

### C. As a result, the rate of increase among us has been low, especially among the typical American people.

### D. We believe that by the Lord's mercy and grace we have a way through the vital groups to "leap over" this blockade.

### E. We refer to our groups as vital groups because of our intention that they produce overcomers to discharge this burden.

## II. The purpose of the vital groups—the increase, the perfecting, and the building up of the church—[[Acts-02#v46|Acts 2:46-47]]; [[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12]]:

### A. [[Acts-02#v46|Acts 2:46-47]] presents the normal standard of the Christian life; if we want to have the increase, we need to have the few things mentioned in these two verses:

#### 1. "Day by day..."

#### 2. "Continuing steadfastly with one accord..."

#### 3. "Breaking bread from house to house..."

#### 4. "They partook of their food with exultation and simplicity of heart..."

#### 5. "Praising God and having grace with all the people..."

#### 6. "The Lord added together those who were being saved..."

### B. To gain the increase, we must be "beside ourselves," praising God and having grace with all the people—[[2 Corinthians-05#v13|2 Cor. 5:13]].

## III. The nature of the vital groups—spiritual—[[Acts-13#v52|Acts 13:52]]; [[Acts-04#v31|4:31b]]:

### A. To be spiritual means to be filled with the Holy Spirit, within and without; we need to practice to be filled every day, every morning, and even every evening.

### B. We all need to fan our spirits into flame, asking the Lord every day to fill and saturate us thoroughly—[[2 Timothy-01#v6|2 Tim. 1:6]].

### C. In Acts the disciples were inwardly and outwardly filled with the Spirit again and again—[[Acts-02#v1|2:1-4]]; [[Acts-04#v31|4:31]]; [[Acts-09#v17|9:17]]; [[Acts-10#v44|10:44]]; [[Acts-13#v9|13:9]]; [[Acts-19#v6|19:6]].

## IV. The condition of the vital groups—loving one another in oneness and in one accord—[[John-13#v34|John 13:34-35]]:

### A. By our loving one another the whole world will know that we are the Lord's disciples; people will be convinced because they see us loving one another in oneness and with one accord.

### B. When people are among us and we are really one, they will wonder what has made us one; the Lord Jesus is the living person who has made us one.

### C. The above are the terms and conditions for us to gain people. Regardless of how much others oppose us, if we are such people, nothing can prevent us from gaining the proper increase.

## V. The Lord's need for the overcomers:

### A. In the entire Bible the Lord required many things first of mankind, then of His chosen people, and then of the church; however, from these three groups of people the Lord did not get what He required.

### B. In [[Revelation-02|Revelation 2]] and [[Revelation-03|3]] the Lord sounded His call to the overcomers; today the Lord needs overcomers.

### C. "In 1986 I sounded the trumpet, as Gideon did...at this present time I am sounding the same trumpet again. To answer the Lord's call today, we all need to be desperate..." (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, pp. 62-63)

### D. "We need to take care of the Lord's demand, the Lord's requirements, much more than our necessities." (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, p. 64)

### E. "Eventually, because of the sufferings that we endure in the Christian life, I was compelled to come to the following conclusion: to be a man, I must be a Christian; to be a Christian, I must be in His church, in His recovery; and to be in His recovery, I must be an overcomer. If we are not overcomers, we will waste our entire life..." (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, p. 64)

### F. "Compared to the Lord Jesus, everything is refuse. We should not care for anything but Christ; rather, we must count everything loss in order to gain Christ. If we mean business with the Lord, we must come up to this standard in being a Christian. This is my spirit and this is my burden. The Lord does need some overcomers..."—[[Philippians-03#v7|Phil. 3:7-8]]. (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, p. 64)

### G. "How many among the denominations could be counted as part of Christ's bride? We also need to ask how many in the Lord's recovery could be counted? Finally, we need to ask ourselves, 'Could I be counted when He comes?' This is a serious matter..." (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, p. 65)

### H. "The entire Christianity is drugged, and we too are somewhat drugged by the influence of the surrounding Christianity. We must overcome this situation. The present situation of religion is full of germs and is very contagious. This is why I feel burdened to prepare a group of saints so that they can bring the new ones into the church life and keep them from the pollution and the defilement of religion... We need to realize that our present situation cannot satisfy the Lord's need. We need a new beginning; then we can go out to gain the new ones, and they will not be defiled by the contagious germs in today's Christianity..." (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, pp. 65-66)

**Reference:** *Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, chs. 7–8
