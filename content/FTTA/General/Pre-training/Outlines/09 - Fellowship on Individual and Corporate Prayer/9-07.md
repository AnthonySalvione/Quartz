# Fellowship on Individual and Corporate Prayer (3): A Man of Prayer — Praying to the Mysterious God in the Divine and Mystical Realm (9-07)

**Scripture Reading:** [[John-10#v30|John 10:30]]; [[John-08#v29|8:29]]; [[John-16#v32|16:32b]]; [[John-14#v30|14:30]]; [[John-17#v1|17:1]]; [[Matthew-14#v23|Matt. 14:23]]

## I. The first God-man, the Lord Jesus, lived as a man of prayer—[[Luke-05#v16|Luke 5:16]]:

### A. In describing the first God-man as a man of prayer, we may use the words *divine* and *mystical*; *divine* is on God's side, and *mystical* is on man's side:

#### 1. In His living as the first God-man, all that the Lord Jesus did was divine and mystical; God was manifested in a mystical human way—[[1 Timothy-03#v16|1 Tim. 3:16]].

#### 2. The Lord's mystical human life was a divine realm, and this realm is the kingdom of God—[[John-03#v13|John 3:13]].

### B. A critical part of the history of the first God-man was His prayer—[[John-17|John 17]]:

#### 1. The prayers of the first God-man were in the divine and mystical realm.

#### 2. The Lord Jesus was a man in the flesh, yet He prayed to the mysterious God in a divine and mystical way and realm—[[Matthew-14#v23|Matt. 14:23]].

#### 3. His prayers were divine, yet they were in a human life, making that human life mystical—[[Mark-01#v35|Mark 1:35]]; [[Luke-05#v16|Luke 5:16]]; [[Luke-06#v12|6:12]].

#### 4. Christ's prayers were divine facts in His mystical human life—[[John-17|John 17]]:

##### a. Whatever God does is a divine fact, and in the Lord Jesus the divine facts were lived out in a human life, making His human life mystical.

##### b. The Lord Jesus was a God-man, and all that He said and did were divine facts accomplished in His human life mystically—[[John-05#v19|John 5:19]].

### C. With the Lord Jesus, we see the pure pattern of the man of prayer revealed in the Gospels:

#### 1. As a man of prayer, the Lord Jesus was a man who was always one with God—[[John-10#v30|John 10:30]].

#### 2. As a man of prayer, the Lord Jesus was a man who lived in the presence of God without ceasing—[[Acts-10#v38|Acts 10:38c]]; [[John-08#v29|John 8:29]]; [[John-16#v32|16:32b]].

#### 3. As a man of prayer, the Lord Jesus trusted in God and not in Himself under any kind of suffering or persecution—[[1 Peter-02#v23|1 Pet. 2:23b]]; [[Luke-23#v46|Luke 23:46]].

#### 4. As a man of prayer, the Lord Jesus was a man in whom Satan, the ruler of the world, had nothing—no ground, no chance, no hope, and no possibility in anything; Satan had no ground in Him because His submission to the Father cut off Satan—[[John-14#v30|John 14:30]].

## II. Prayer is man cooperating and co-working with God, allowing God to express Himself through man and thus accomplish His purpose—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[James-05#v17|James 5:17]]:

### A. Prayer is man breathing God, obtaining God, and being obtained by God; real prayer is an exhaling and inhaling before God, causing us and God to contact each other and to gain each other—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]].

### B. A praying person cooperates with God, works together with God, and allows God to express Himself and His desire from within him and through him—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[James-05#v17|James 5:17]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17-23]]; [[Ephesians-03#v14|3:14-21]].

### C. Genuine prayers cause our being to be wholly mingled with God, causing us to pray as a man mingled with God, a God-man—[[Jude-01#v20|Jude 20]]; [[Ephesians-06#v18|Eph. 6:18]].

## III. The Bible teaches us, the believers in Christ, the God-men, to live as divine and mystical persons—[[Matthew-28#v19|Matt. 28:19]]; [[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]]; [[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1-6]]:

### A. We have been separated from being common; we have been sanctified and separated unto God, who is holy, and now we are in the divine and mystical realm of the consummated Spirit—[[John-17#v17|John 17:17]], [[John-17#v19|19]]; [[Hebrews-02#v11|Heb. 2:11]]; [[1 Thessalonians-05#v23|1 Thes. 5:23]].

### B. Our life should be divine yet human—not merely human, but mystically human; everything in our living should be divine and mystical—[[John-14#v16|John 14:16-20]].

### C. Every believer should be a divine and mystical person, one who is human yet lives divinely—[[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]; [[2 Corinthians-10#v1|2 Cor. 10:1]].

### D. The New Testament teaches us, the members of the Body of Christ, to do everything with God, in God, by God, and through God; this is what it means to be divine—[[1 Corinthians-10#v31|1 Cor. 10:31]]; [[Colossians-03#v17|Col. 3:17]].

### E. A genuine and proper prayer is not merely spiritual but also divine; this means that the Triune God prays with us and that we pray by living in the Triune God—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[Jude-01#v20|Jude 20]].

### F. All genuine prayers, prayers that can be counted by God, are divine facts, something divine performed in the mystical human life—[[John-14#v13|John 14:13-14]]; [[John-16#v23|16:23-24]].
