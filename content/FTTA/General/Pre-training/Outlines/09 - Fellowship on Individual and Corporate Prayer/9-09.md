# Fellowship on Individual and Corporate Prayer (4): Living Christ by Praying Without Ceasing (9-09)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21a]]; [[Philippians-04#v6|4:6]]; [[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]]

## I. The Lord Jesus, the first God-man, lived as a man of prayer—[[Luke-05#v16|Luke 5:16]].

## II. Prayer is man cooperating and co-working with God, allowing God to express Himself through man and thus accomplish His purpose—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[James-05#v17|James 5:17]]:

### A. Prayer is man breathing God, obtaining God, and being obtained by God.

### B. Real prayer is an exhaling and inhaling before God, causing us and God to contact each other and to gain each other—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]].

### C. A praying person cooperates with God, works together with God, and allows God to express Himself and His desire from within him and through him—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[James-05#v17|James 5:17]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17-23]]; [[Ephesians-03#v14|3:14-21]].

### D. Genuine prayers cause our being to be wholly mingled with God, causing us to pray as a man mingled with God, a God-man—[[Jude-01#v20|Jude 20]]; [[Ephesians-06#v18|Eph. 6:18]].

## III. To pray means to realize that we are nothing and that we can do nothing—[[Mark-09#v28|Mark 9:28-29]]:

### A. Prayer is the real denial of ourselves—[[Mark-08#v34|Mark 8:34]].

### B. To pray is actually to declare, "No longer I but Christ"—[[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]].

## IV. Prayer involves conversing with the Lord, communicating with Him in fellowship, and worshipping Him—[[Philippians-04#v6|Phil. 4:6]]:

### A. If we ask the Lord about everything and talk with Him in everything, we will receive His element into us, a divine metabolism will work in us, and Christ will be expressed through us; this is to live Christ—[[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21a]].

### B. We need to practice speaking to the Lord constantly; then spontaneously we will live Christ.

## V. The way to experience the indwelling Christ and to live Christ is to pray in a genuine way—[[Colossians-01#v27|Col. 1:27]]; [[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21a]]:

### A. We need the kind of prayer which brings us into contact with the Lord and causes us to be one with Him in our spirit—[[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Tim. 4:22]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v17|1 Cor. 6:17]].

### B. If we would experience Christ and live Him, we need to remain in an atmosphere of prayer.

## VI. To pray without ceasing by calling on the Lord's name is to live Christ—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]]; [[Romans-10#v12|Rom. 10:12-13]]; [[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21a]]:

### A. To pray without ceasing means to breathe in the Lord and enjoy Him constantly—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]].

### B. By calling on the Lord's name, we automatically take Him as our life and spontaneously live Him—[[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]].

### C. If we pray, breathing in Christ as our life and our life supply, we will not do things by ourselves, apart from Christ; instead, by praying without ceasing, we will live Christ.
