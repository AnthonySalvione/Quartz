# Fellowship on Individual and Corporate Prayer (7): Reaching Zion by Praying (9-17)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]; [[Matthew-18#v19|18:19-20]]; [[Ephesians-01#v16|Eph. 1:16-18]]; [[Ephesians-03#v14|3:14-17a]]; [[Ephesians-04#v16|4:16]]; [[Colossians-01#v9|Col. 1:9]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|2:19]]; [[Colossians-04#v12|4:12]]

## I. The Lord's recovery is to build up Zion—the reality of the Body of Christ consummating in the New Jerusalem—[[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]]; [[Revelation-14#v1|Rev. 14:1]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]]:

### A. The highest peak of God's economy is the reality of the Body of Christ:

#### 1. The issue of God becoming man and man becoming God is an organism—the Body of Christ—which is the issue of the mingling of God with man—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]].

#### 2. Christ, the first God-man, was enlarged in His resurrection to be the firstborn Son with the many sons; this enlargement is an organism—the Body of Christ—[[Romans-01#v3|Rom. 1:3-4]]; [[Romans-08#v14|8:14]], [[Romans-08#v29|29]]; [[Romans-12#v4|12:4-5]].

#### 3. The highest peak of the Lord's recovery that can carry out God's economy is for God to produce an organic Body to be His organism—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-04#v16|4:16]].

### B. The reality of the Body of Christ is the union and mingling of God with man to live out a corporate God-man—[[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4-6]], [[Ephesians-04#v16|16]], [[Ephesians-04#v21|21]], [[Ephesians-04#v23|23-24]]:

#### 1. The reality of the Body of Christ is the living of a God-man life by a group of God-redeemed people together with the God-man Christ—[[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19-21a]]:

##### a. In His resurrection the Lord Jesus produced many brothers who, with Him as the eldest Brother, become a great, corporate God-man; this universal man is God yet man and man yet God—[[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[Ephesians-02#v15|Eph. 2:15]].

##### b. After regenerating us, the life-giving Spirit dwells in us and is mingled with our spirit to live a God-man life with us—[[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v17|6:17]].

##### c. This kind of life issues in a universal man who is exactly the same as the Lord Jesus—a man living a God-man life by the divine life—[[Ephesians-04#v21|Eph. 4:21]], [[Ephesians-04#v24|24]]; [[1 John-02#v6|1 John 2:6]]; [[1 John-03#v1|3:1-2]]; [[1 John-04#v17|4:17]].

#### 2. The reality of the Body of Christ is a living by the God-men, who are united, joined, and constituted together with God by the mingling of humanity with divinity and divinity with humanity—[[John-14#v20|John 14:20]]; [[John-15#v4|15:4]]; [[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4-6]], [[Ephesians-04#v16|16]], [[Ephesians-04#v24|24]]; [[Philippians-01#v21|Phil. 1:21a]].

#### 3. The reality of the Body of Christ is the corporate living by the perfected God-men, who are not living by their life but by the life of the processed God, whose attributes are expressed through their virtues—[[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]].

#### 4. The reality of the Body of Christ is a corporate living of the conformity to the death of Christ through the power of the resurrection of Christ—[[Philippians-03#v10|Phil. 3:10]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v12|1 Cor. 12:12-13]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v10|2 Cor. 4:10-12]]; [[Romans-08#v13|Rom. 8:13-14]]; [[Romans-12#v4|12:4-5]], [[Romans-12#v11|11]].

#### 5. This mingling living will close this age and bring Christ back to rule over the earth with the overcomers in the kingdom age—[[Revelation-11#v15|Rev. 11:15]].

## II. In order to reach the high peak of Zion, we need to be desperate to pray and to know the significance of prayer for the carrying out of God's economy—[[1 Timothy-01#v4|1 Tim. 1:4]]; [[1 Timothy-02#v8|2:8]]:

### A. Prayer is the mutual contact between man and God—[[1 John-05#v14|1 John 5:14-15]].

### B. Prayer is man breathing God, obtaining God, and being obtained by God; real prayer is an exhaling and inhaling before God, causing us and God to contact each other and to gain each other—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]].

### C. The real significance of prayer is to contact God in our spirit and to absorb God Himself—[[Ephesians-06#v18|Eph. 6:18]]; [[Jude-01#v20|Jude 20]].

### D. Prayer is man cooperating and co-working with God, allowing God to express Himself through man and thus accomplish His purpose—[[James-05#v17|James 5:17]].

### E. A praying person cooperates with God, works together with God, and allows God to express Himself and His desire from within him and through him—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[James-05#v17|James 5:17]]; [[Ephesians-01#v16|Eph. 1:16-23]]; [[Ephesians-03#v14|3:14-21]].

### F. The kind of prayer we have depends on the kind of person we are; our prayers reveal who we are, what we are, and where we are—[[Luke-09#v54|Luke 9:54-55]]; [[1 Timothy-02#v8|1 Tim. 2:8]].

### G. The governing principle of our prayer should be that prayer brings us into God—[[Luke-11#v1|Luke 11:1-13]].

### H. A life of genuine prayer stops our natural being; such a prayer life revolts and rebels against our natural being—[[Matthew-16#v24|Matt. 16:24]]; [[Luke-21#v36|Luke 21:36]].

### I. Prayer is the real denial of the self; to pray is to deny ourselves, realizing that we are nothing and that we are not able to do anything—[[Mark-08#v34|Mark 8:34]]; [[Mark-09#v29|9:29]].

### J. We need to pray in the spirit with the prayer of Christ; there must be a prayer in our prayer like the wheel within the wheel in [[Ezekiel-01#v16|Ezekiel 1:16]]—[[James-05#v17|James 5:17]].

### K. Genuine prayer is not merely spiritual but also divine, for the Triune God is praying in us, and we are praying in the Triune God—[[Romans-08#v26|Rom. 8:26-27]]; [[Jude-01#v20|Jude 20]].

### L. The way to experience the indwelling Christ and to be constituted with Christ is to pray in a genuine way—[[Colossians-01#v3|Col. 1:3]], [[Colossians-01#v9|9]], [[Colossians-01#v27|27]]; [[Colossians-04#v2|4:2-3]], [[Colossians-04#v12|12]].

### M. When we pray, Christ, the Head, has a way to carry out His administration through His Body—[[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|2:19]]; [[Colossians-03#v1|3:1-2]]; [[Colossians-04#v2|4:2]]; [[Hebrews-02#v17|Heb. 2:17]]; [[Hebrews-04#v14|4:14]]; [[Hebrews-07#v26|7:26]]; [[Hebrews-08#v1|8:1-2]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|Rev. 5:6]].
