# Fellowship Concerning the Lord's Table (7): The Practice of the Lord's Table Meeting (2) — The Worship in the Dispensing of God (8-13)

**Scripture Reading:** [[John-04#v23|John 4:23-24]], [[John-04#v14|14]]; [[John-01#v12|1:12]]; [[John-03#v5|3:5]], [[John-03#v6|6b]]; [[John-14#v23|14:23]]; [[John-17#v26|17:26a]]; [[Hebrews-02#v12|Heb. 2:12]]; [[John-04#v10|John 4:10]], [[John-04#v14|14]], [[John-04#v23|23-24]]; [[Ephesians-02#v18|Eph. 2:18]]

## I. To worship the Father with the Son and in the Spirit is the true worship in the divine dispensing of the Divine Trinity—[[John-04#v23|John 4:23]]; [[Ephesians-02#v18|Eph. 2:18]]:

### A. The Jews, according to their view of the Old Testament, have their kind of worship of God, that is not the worship in God's dispensing; it is altogether not involved with the Triune God—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit; basically speaking, the Jews know God only in His creation.

### B. In the Gospels there is only one chapter in which the Lord talked about the worship of God; when He talked about the worship of God in [[John-04|John 4]], He was referring specifically to the worship of the Father.

### C. The Samaritan woman spoke of worship, but she did not use the term *Father*; then the Lord Jesus told her, "An hour is coming, and it is now, when the true worshippers will worship the Father in spirit and truthfulness, for the Father also seeks such to worship Him"; this meant that the age had changed.

### D. When we use [[John-04#v24|John 4:24]], we usually neglect [[John-04#v23|verse 23]]; the Lord did not say that we worship God but that we worship the Father—the Father seeks after this worship.

### E. What the Lord spoke of is the worship of the Father in the Son and also in the Spirit; thus, this is a worship in God's dispensing, the worship by the divine dispensing.

## II. To worship the Father—[[John-04#v23|John 4:23]]:

### A. By becoming the Father's children—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12]]; [[1 John-03#v1|1 John 3:1]].

### B. By knowing the Father's name—[[John-17#v26|John 17:26a]]; [[Hebrews-02#v12|Heb. 2:12a]].

### C. By enjoying the Father's presence—[[John-14#v23|John 14:23]].

## III. In the Son as the reality—[[John-04#v23|John 4:23-24]]:

### A. By experiencing the Son as the good land—[[Deuteronomy-08#v7|Deut. 8:7]].

### B. By enjoying the riches of the Son as the rich produce of the good land—[[John-04#v8|vv. 8-10]].

### C. By presenting the Son to the Father as the peace offering—[[Leviticus-03#v1|Lev. 3:1]], [[Leviticus-03#v6|6]], [[Leviticus-03#v12|12]]; [[Leviticus-07#v11|7:11-13]].

## IV. In our spirit mingled with the Spirit of God—[[John-04#v23|John 4:23-24]]:

### A. By being born of the Spirit of God in our spirit—[[John-03#v5|John 3:5]], [[John-03#v6|6b]].

### B. By being baptized into one Body in the Spirit of God—[[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13a]].

### C. By drinking of the Spirit of God—[[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13b]]; [[John-04#v14|John 4:14]].

### D. By being in the unique place of worship where God's habitation is—[[Deuteronomy-12#v5|Deut. 12:5]]; [[Ephesians-02#v22|Eph. 2:22]].

## V. In the dispensing of the Triune God:

### A. Having been baptized into the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit—[[Matthew-28#v19|Matt. 28:19b]].

### B. Enjoying the Father's love, the Son's grace, and the Spirit's fellowship—[[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]].

## VI. In [[John-04|John 4]] the worship to the Father, the worship in the dispensing of God, is related to drinking the living water—[[John-04#v10|vv. 10]], [[John-04#v14|14]]:

### A. To contact God the Spirit with our spirit is to drink of the living water, and to drink of the living water is to render real worship to God.

### B. Without drinking the living water, we cannot have a subjective worship in the divine dispensing; we can have only the Jewish kind of objective worship to an objective God as the Creator.

### C. We need to drink of the living water to worship the Father in spirit and in truthfulness; this is the new worship revealed in the New Testament.

## VII. This kind of worship can be practiced mostly in the Lord's table meeting, because in the Lord's table meeting, after we partake of the bread and the cup, the Lord takes the lead to bring us to the Father.

## VIII. This is the worship that the Lord revealed in [[John-04|John 4]]:

### A. John's Gospel tells us in [[John-01|chapter 1]] that we have the right to be God's children; He gave as many as received Him the right, the authority, to be His children ([[John-01#v12|v. 12]]); these children are born of God Himself.

### B. Then [[John-03|chapter 3]] tells us how to be born of God; we must be born of water and the Spirit to be reborn of the Spirit ([[John-03#v5|vv. 5-6]]).

### C. [[John-04|Chapter 4]] goes on to reveal how to worship God as the Father in our spirit as the unique place of our worship and with the Son as the real offerings ([[John-03#v23|vv. 23-24]]); we have to experience the dispensing of the Triune God—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit; then we can have the kind of worship that the Father seeks.

**Focus:** The worship in the dispensing of the Triune God is the worship to the Father by His many sons with His firstborn Son as the offerings and in His Spirit who mingles Himself with our spirit as the unique place for our worship.

**Reference:** *CWWL, 1979, vol. 2, Basic Lessons on Service*, pp. 43-49.
