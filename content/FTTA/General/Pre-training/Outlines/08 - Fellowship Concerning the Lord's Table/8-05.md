# Fellowship Concerning the Lord's Table (3): The Practice of the Lord's Table Meeting (1) (8-05)

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Corinthians-11#v23|1 Cor. 11:23-25]]; [[Matthew-26#v30|Matt. 26:30]]; [[Hebrews-02#v12|Heb. 2:12]]; [[Ephesians-02#v18|Eph. 2:18]]; [[John-04#v23|John 4:23-24]]

## I. Remembering the Lord—[[1 Corinthians-11#v24|1 Cor. 11:24-25]]:

### A. By praising instead of by praying:

#### 1. Not to ask the Lord to do things for us.

#### 2. But to bless the Lord with well speaking concerning His person and work—[[Revelation-05#v13|Rev. 5:13]].

### B. Addressing praise to the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God:

#### 1. Realizing the economy of the Divine Trinity—[[Matthew-28#v19|Matt. 28:19b]]; [[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]].

#### 2. Learning to differentiate the Lord's names from the Father's names (see *Hymns*, 65-80).

### C. Calling hymns:

#### 1. Learning to know the hymns:

##### a. In their categories.

##### b. In their contents.

##### c. In their focus.

##### d. In their sensation and taste.

#### 2. Learning to apply the hymns at the appropriate time:

##### a. To start a section of the meeting.

##### b. To strengthen and enrich the started section.

##### c. To prolong and uplift the same section.

##### d. To follow the atmosphere of the meeting.

### D. Distributing the bread and the cup:

#### 1. Neither too early nor too late.

#### 2. When the remembrance of the Lord reaches the high tide.

#### 3. Better with some thanks given to the Lord—[[Luke-22#v19|Luke 22:19a]].

## II. Worshipping the Father:

### A. After eating the bread and drinking the cup—[[Matthew-26#v26|Matt. 26:26-30]].

### B. By praising—[[Matthew-26#v30|v. 30]]:

#### 1. Christ, the Firstborn of God, taking the lead among His brothers—[[Hebrews-02#v12|Heb. 2:12]].

#### 2. We, the many sons of God, following Christ in praising the Father.

### C. Addressing praise to the Father:

#### 1. Through the Son.

#### 2. In the Spirit—[[Ephesians-02#v18|Eph. 2:18]].

### D. Calling hymns:

#### 1. According to the Father's being.

#### 2. According to the Father's attributes.

### E. By presenting Christ to God the Father:

#### 1. As the peace offering—[[Leviticus-03|Lev. 3]].

#### 2. Enjoying Him before God the Father—[[Leviticus-07#v14|Lev. 7:14-21]], [[Leviticus-07#v28|28-34]]:

##### a. As our life supply—[[Leviticus-07#v14|vv. 14-16]].

##### b. As love in resurrection—[[Leviticus-07#v29|vv. 29-31]].

##### c. As power in ascension—[[Leviticus-07#v32|vv. 32-34]].

##### d. In cleanness—[[Leviticus-07#v17|vv. 17-21]].

**Focus:** The proper remembrance of the Lord depends upon our proper practice. The worshipping of the Father should be with the Son and in the Spirit ([[John-04#v23|John 4:23-24]]).

**Reference:** *Basic Lessons on Service*, pp. 29-45.
