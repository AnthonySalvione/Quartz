# Fellowship Concerning the Lord's Table (2): The Lord's Table Meeting (8-03)

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Corinthians-10#v16|1 Cor. 10:16-17]]; [[1 Corinthians-11#v24|11:24-34]]; [[Matthew-26#v27|Matt. 26:27-30]]

## I. On the first day of the week (the Lord's Day)—[[Acts-20#v6|Acts 20:6b-7a]]; [[1 Corinthians-16#v2|1 Cor. 16:2]].

## II. To partake of the Lord's table—[[1 Corinthians-10#v21|1 Cor. 10:21b]].

## III. To eat the Lord's supper—[[1 Corinthians-11#v20|1 Cor. 11:20]].

## IV. To remember the Lord:

### A. The focus of the Lord's table:

#### 1. All hymns, praises, thanks, testimonies, and words being concentrated on the Lord Himself.

#### 2. Avoiding all distractions.

### B. By breaking the bread to apprehend with appreciation and thanks the Lord's physical body being broken for us—[[Luke-22#v19|Luke 22:19]]; [[1 Corinthians-11#v24|1 Cor. 11:24]].

### C. By eating the bread:

#### 1. To enjoy the Lord as our life-supply—[[John-06#v35|John 6:35a]].

#### 2. To testify that we live by the Lord—[[John-06#v57|John 6:57b]].

#### 3. To have fellowship in Christ's mystical Body—[[1 Corinthians-10#v16|1 Cor. 10:16b-17]].

### D. By drinking the cup—[[1 Corinthians-11#v25|1 Cor. 11:25]]:

#### 1. To review the redemption of the new covenant—[[Matthew-26#v27|Matt. 26:27-28]].

#### 2. To receive God's blessing.

#### 3. To have fellowship in the blood of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-10#v16|1 Cor. 10:16a]].

## V. To display the Lord's death—[[1 Corinthians-11#v26|1 Cor. 11:26]].

## VI. To express our desire of the Lord's coming back—[[1 Corinthians-11#v26|1 Cor. 11:26b]]; [[Matthew-26#v29|Matt. 26:29]].

## VII. To worship the Father—[[Matthew-26#v30|Matt. 26:30]]:

### A. After eating the bread and drinking the cup—[[Matthew-26#v26|Matt. 26:26-30]].

### B. Addressing praise to the Father—[[Matthew-26#v30|Matt. 26:30]]; [[Hebrews-02#v12|Heb. 2:12]].

### C. Calling hymns:

#### 1. According to the Father's being.

#### 2. According to the Father's attributes.

### D. Presenting Christ to God the Father.

## VIII. Warning:

### A. Proving ourselves and discerning the Body—[[1 Corinthians-11#v28|1 Cor. 11:28-29]].

### B. Not eating the bread and drinking the cup unworthily—[[1 Corinthians-11#v27|1 Cor. 11:27]].

### C. For the worse, if meeting with divisions—[[1 Corinthians-11#v17|1 Cor. 11:17-18]].

### D. The Lord's chastening—[[1 Corinthians-11#v30|1 Cor. 11:30-34]].

**Focus:** The real remembrance of the Lord is to receive and enjoy Him as our life supply by eating and drinking Him and by sharing Him one with another in the fellowship of the Body.
