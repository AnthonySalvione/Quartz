# One Accord and Coordination (3) (19-05)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Acts-02#v1|2:1]], [[Acts-02#v42|42]], [[Acts-02#v46|46]]; [[Acts-04#v24|4:24]]; [[Acts-05#v12|5:12]]; [[Acts-08#v6|8:6]]; [[Acts-15#v25|15:25]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v8|1 Cor. 14:8]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v26|26]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6-7]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v9|9-10]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v1|4:1]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v18|5:18]]; [[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-12]]; [[Ephesians-05#v19|5:19]]; [[Philippians-01#v27|Phil. 1:27]]; [[Philippians-02#v2|2:2-3]], [[Philippians-02#v5|5]], [[Philippians-02#v20|20]], [[Philippians-02#v22|22]], [[Philippians-02#v25|25]]; [[Philippians-04#v3|4:3a]]; [[Colossians-03#v16|Col. 3:16]]; [[1 Thessalonians-05#v19|1 Thes. 5:19-20]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v12|1 Tim. 1:12]]; [[Revelation-03#v14|Rev. 3:14]].

## I. The one ministry of the New Testament:

### A. There are only two ministries in the Bible:

#### 1. The ministry of the old covenant—[[2 Corinthians-03#v7|2 Cor. 3:7]] and footnote 1; vv. 9-10 and footnotes 9 and 10¹.

#### 2. The ministry of the new covenant—[[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]] and footnote 4.

#### 3. These two are one ministry with two parts:

##### a. The Old Testament ministry is a shadow, a prefigure, and a preparation.

##### b. The New Testament ministry is the reality, the fulfillment.

### B. All discord, divisions, and confusions among Christians have come out of one source: the different ministries.

## II. One speaking, one sounding of the trumpet in the Lord's New Testament ministry—[[1 Corinthians-14#v8|1 Cor. 14:8]] (read *Elders' Training, Book 7: One Accord for the Lord's Move*):

### A. All our service should be in this one New Testament ministry.

### B. The ministry is a fighting unit:

#### 1. In warfare there is no room or time for opinions (Laodicea)—[[Revelation-03#v14|Rev. 3:14]] and footnote 1.

#### 2. Speaking differently or critically kills the fighting morale and annuls the impact of the Lord's move.

### C. In the Lord's recovery on the earth there is only one ministry, one teaching issuing in one fellowship—[[2 Corinthians-04#v1|2 Cor. 4:1]] and footnote 2; [[2 Corinthians-05#v18|2 Cor. 5:18]]; [[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-12]] and footnote 12; [[1 Timothy-01#v12|1 Tim. 1:12]] and footnote 1.

## III. One accord in the Lord's New Testament ministry:

### A. Without the one accord of His people, God cannot move:

#### 1. The one accord is the foundation for our present practice—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Acts-02#v1|2:1]], [[Acts-02#v46|46]]; [[Acts-04#v24|4:24]]; [[Acts-05#v12|5:12]]; [[Acts-08#v6|8:6]]; [[Acts-15#v25|15:25]].

#### 2. The teaching of the apostles is the holding factor of the one accord—[[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]] and footnote 1, [[Acts-02#v46|v. 46a]].

### B. The one accord is brought into being under one leadership, based upon the unique teaching, the divine revelation.

## IV. One accord in the gospel service:

### A. Being in one accord is to be of the same mind, will, and purpose around and within our soul and heart.

### B. The three main factors for the spreading of the gospel are:

#### 1. Prayer.

#### 2. The Spirit.

#### 3. The Word.

### C. Yet the master key to all these factors is the one accord.

### D. The landmark separating the Gospels and the Acts was not the baptism in the Holy Spirit but the one accord of the one hundred twenty.

### E. Being like-souled with the apostle—[[Philippians-02#v20|Phil. 2:20]]:

#### 1. Timothy's "approvedness, that as a child with a father he has served with me unto the gospel"—[[Philippians-02#v22|v. 22]].

#### 2. "Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, and your apostle and priestly minister to my need"—[[Philippians-02#v25|v. 25]].

#### 3. "I ask you also, genuine yokefellow, assist them, since they contended with me in the gospel"—[[Philippians-04#v3|4:3a]] and footnote 1.

### F. The problems of the soul in Philippi were in sharp contrast with the problems of the flesh in Corinth:

#### 1. The soul, especially the mind, is prone to dissenting, disputing, and rivalry—[[Acts-02#v3|2:3]]; [[Acts-04#v2|4:2]].

#### 2. The remedy is to have our mind dealt with and renewed—[[Acts-02#v5|2:5]], [[Acts-02#v2|20]] and footnote 1.

#### 3. Both the one accord and the coordination are reflected in [[Philippians-01#v27|Philippians 1:27]] and footnote 1:

##### a. Not just standing in one spirit defensively, but striving offensively with one soul.

##### b. Standing firm in one place—in our spirit there are no opinions, bitterness, or offenses.

## V. Coordination—exercise in mutuality:

### A. As our body speaks:

#### 1. The hands gesture.

#### 2. The eyes observe.

#### 3. The mouth opens.

#### 4. The vocal cords activate.

#### 5. The tongue enunciates.

#### 6. The feet stand or walk.

### B. Many functions are spontaneous:

#### 1. The eyelids blink.

#### 2. The brain formulates the message.

#### 3. The heart beats.

#### 4. The stomach digests the previous meal.

### C. Basketball:

#### 1. One does not keep the ball while the others stand around.

#### 2. One ball—one spirit.

#### 3. Do not dribble too long or shoot every time.

### D. Prophesying:

#### 1. Observe attention and retention.

#### 2. Care for the ears of others. No monotone.

#### 3. "Speaking to one another"—[[Ephesians-05#v19|Eph. 5:19]].

#### 4. "Teaching and admonishing one another"—[[Colossians-03#v16|Col. 3:16]].

#### 5. "Each one has"—[[1 Corinthians-14#v26|1 Cor. 14:26]].

#### 6. "Do not quench the Spirit; do not despise prophecies"—[[1 Thessalonians-05#v19|1 Thes. 5:19-20]].
