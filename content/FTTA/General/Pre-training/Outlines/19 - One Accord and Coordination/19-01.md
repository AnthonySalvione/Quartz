## I. Coordination is the **GREATEST** need in the Lord's move on the earth:

### A. The way of the Triune God is **"Let Us"**—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[Genesis-11#v7|11:7]].

### B. The way of Satan is **"I will"**—[[Isaiah-14#v12|Isa. 14:12-14]]; [[Matthew-04#v8|Matt. 4:8-9]].

## II. Four levels of coordination:

### A. The greatest is that of the Triune God:

#### 1. The Son with the Father—[[John-05#v19|John 5:19]], [[John-05#v30|30]]; [[John-06#v38|6:38]]; [[John-12#v49|12:49]]; [[John-14#v10|14:10]], [[John-14#v24|24]].

#### 2. The Spirit with the Son—[[John-16#v13|John 16:13]].

### B. The second greatest is that of the apostles with the Triune God:

#### 1. Peter, standing together with the eleven—[[Acts-02#v14|Acts 2:14]].

#### 2. Paul coordinating with the Spirit of Jesus—[[Acts-16#v6|Acts 16:6-10]].

#### 3. "Be imitators of me, as I also am of Christ"—[[1 Corinthians-11#v1|1 Cor. 11:1]].

#### 4. Second Corinthians is a story of the coordination of Paul with the heavenly ministry of Christ:

##### a. God's comfort conveyed through the apostles to the believers—[[2 Corinthians-01#v3|2 Cor. 1:3-4]].

##### b. Captives manifesting the savor of Him in every place—[[2 Corinthians-02#v14|2 Cor. 2:14]].

##### c. Incense-bearers scattering the fragrance of Christ—[[2 Corinthians-02#v15|2 Cor. 2:15]] and footnote 1.

##### d. Ministers of Christ to make the believers living letters of Christ with Christ as the content—[[2 Corinthians-03#v3|2 Cor. 3:3]] and footnotes 1–3.

##### e. Mirrors beholding and reflecting the glory of Christ—[[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]] and footnotes 4 and 5.

##### f. Earthen vessels to contain the Christ of glory as the excellent treasure—[[2 Corinthians-04#v7|2 Cor. 4:7]] and footnote 1.

#### 5. Paul delivered only what he received—[[1 Corinthians-11#v23|1 Cor. 11:23]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v3|15:3]]; [[Galatians-01#v10|Gal. 1:10-12]]; [[Colossians-01#v29|Col. 1:29]].

#### 6. "Paul, a slave of Christ Jesus (of God)"—[[Romans-01#v1|Rom. 1:1]] and footnote 2; [[Philippians-01#v1|Phil. 1:1]]; [[Titus-01#v1|Titus 1:1]].

#### 7. The apostle John—[[Revelation-01#v9|Rev. 1:9-11a]]; [[Revelation-04#v1|4:1-2]]; [[Revelation-17#v1|17:1]], [[Revelation-17#v3|3]]; [[Revelation-21#v9|21:9-10]].

#### 8. Joshua's coordination with Jehovah's destruction of Jericho—[[Joshua-06|Josh. 6]].

#### 9. Gideon responding to Jehovah's need regarding His people—[[Judges-07|Judg. 7]]:

##### a. Jehovah cooperating with man—[[Judges-06#v36|Judg. 6:36-40]].

##### b. Man cooperating with Jehovah—[[Judges-07|Judg. 7]].
