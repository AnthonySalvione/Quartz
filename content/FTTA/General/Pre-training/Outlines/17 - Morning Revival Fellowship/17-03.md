**Scripture Reading:** [[Genesis-19#v27|Gen. 19:27]]; [[Genesis-28#v18|28:18-19]]; [[Exodus-08#v20|Exo. 8:20]]; [[Exodus-34#v2|34:2]], [[Exodus-34#v4|4]]; [[Joshua-06#v12|Josh. 6:12]]; [[Joshua-08#v10|8:10]]; [[1 Samuel-01#v19|1 Sam. 1:19]]; [[1 Samuel-15#v12|15:12]]; [[1 Samuel-17#v20|17:20]]; [[2 Kings-19#v35|2 Kings 19:35]]; [[Job-01#v5|Job 1:5]]; [[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-23]]; [[Judges-06#v38|Judg. 6:38]]; [[Mark-01#v35|Mark 1:35]]; [[Mark-16#v9|16:9]]; [[Luke-06#v13|Luke 6:13]]; [[John-20#v1|John 20:1]]; [[Acts-05#v21|Acts 5:21]]

## I. Patterns of morning revival in the Old Testament:

A. **Abraham:**

> **[[Genesis-19#v27|Gen. 19:27]]**
> *And Abraham rose up early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before Jehovah.*

B. **Jacob:**

> **[[Genesis-28#v18|Gen. 28:18-19]]**
> *And Jacob rose up early in the morning and took the stone that he had put under his head, and he set it up as a pillar and poured oil on top of it. And he called the name of that place Bethel.*

C. **Moses:**

> **[[Exodus-08#v20|Exo. 8:20]]**
> *Then Jehovah said to Moses, Rise up early in the morning, and present yourself before Pharaoh...and say unto him...*

> **[[Exodus-34#v2|Exo. 34:2]], [[Exodus-34#v4|4]]**
> *Then Moses rose up early in the morning and went up to Mount Sinai.*

D. **Joshua:**

> **[[Joshua-06#v12|Josh. 6:12]]**
> *And Joshua rose early in the morning and the priests took up the Ark of Jehovah.*

> **[[Joshua-08#v10|Josh. 8:10]]**
> *And Joshua rose early in the morning and mustered the people; and he and the elders of Israel went up before the people to Ai.*

E. **Samuel's parents:**

> **[[1 Samuel-01#v19|1 Sam. 1:19]]**
> *And they rose up early in the morning and worshipped before Jehovah.*

F. **The inhabitants of Jerusalem:**

> **[[2 Kings-19#v35|2 Kings 19:35]]**
> *And that night an angel of Jehovah went out, and struck the Assyrians' camp, a hundred and eighty-five thousand; and when they rose up early in the morning, all of them were corpses, dead.*

G. **Job:**

> **[[Job-01#v5|Job 1:5]]**
> *And he would rise early in the morning and offer burnt offerings according to the number of them [his children].*

H. **Jeremiah:**

> **[[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-23]]**
> *It is Jehovah's lovingkindness that we are not consumed, / For His compassions do not fail; / They are new every morning.*

## II. Patterns of morning revival in the New Testament:

A. **The Lord Jesus:**

> **[[Mark-01#v35|Mark 1:35]]**
> *And rising very early in the morning, while it was still night, He went out and went away to a deserted place, and there He prayed.*

> **[[Luke-06#v13|Luke 6:13]]**
> *And when it became day, He called His disciples to Him, and He chose from them twelve, whom He also named apostles.*

B. **Mary Magdalene:**

> **[[John-20#v1|John 20:1]]**
> *Now on the first day of the week, Mary the Magdalene came early to the tomb while it was yet dark and saw the stone taken away from the tomb.*

> **[[Mark-16#v9|Mark 16:9]]**
> *Now after He [the Lord Jesus] had risen early on the first day of the week, He appeared first to Mary the Magdalene.*

C. **The apostles:**

> **[[Acts-05#v21|Acts 5:21]]**
> *...they [the apostles] entered about daybreak into the temple...*
