# Morning Revival (8): Principles and Patterns (17-19)

## I. The meaning and the significance of the word **revival**:

### A. From the dictionary:

1. To restore to life.
2. To animate or cheer the spirit or the heart of a person.
3. To quicken or renew the mind.
4. To return to life, vigor, strength, or flourishing condition.
5. To recover from depression.

### B. From the New Testament:

1. **Anazao** (Gk.) — to live again, as in:

   * [[Romans-14#v9|Rom. 14:9]] — *For Christ died and lived again.*
   * [[Luke-15#v24|Luke 15:24a]] — *This son of mine was dead and lives again.*

## II. Some principles related to being revived from the Old Testament:

### A. [[Psalm-71#v20|Psa. 71:20]]

> You...will revive me again / And bring me up again / From the depths of the earth.

### B. [[Psalm-119#v25|Psa. 119:25]]

> My soul clings to the dust; / Enliven me according to Your word.

### C. [[Psalm-119#v37|Psa. 119:37]]

> Turn my eyes from beholding vanity, / And enliven me in Your ways.

### D. [[Psalm-119#v40|Psa. 119:40]]

> Behold, I have longed after Your precepts; / Enliven me in Your righteousness.

### E. [[Psalm-119#v50|Psa. 119:50]]

> This is my comfort in my affliction, / For Your word has enlivened me.

### F. [[Psalm-119#v154|Psa. 119:154]]

> Plead my case and redeem me; / Enliven me according to Your word.

### G. [[Psalm-119#v156|Psa. 119:156]]

> Great are Your compassions, O Jehovah; / Enliven me according to Your ordinances.

### H. [[Psalm-119#v159|Psa. 119:159]]

> See how I have loved Your precepts; / O Jehovah, enliven me according to Your lovingkindness.

## III. Some patterns to help us with the experience and practice of morning revival from the Old Testament:

### A. Abraham offered Isaac early—[[Genesis-22#v3|Gen. 22:3]].

### B. Moses dealt with Pharaoh early—[[Exodus-09#v13|Exo. 9:13]].

### C. Moses got the pattern for God's building early—[[Exodus-34#v4|Exo. 34:4]].

### D. God deals with His people's problems early.

* [[Exodus-14#v24|Exo. 14:24]] — *And at the morning watch, Jehovah looked down upon the camp of the Egyptians from within the pillar of fire and of cloud and threw the camp of the Egyptians into confusion.*

### E. [[Psalm-05#v3|Psa. 5:3]]

> O Jehovah, in the morning / You will hear my voice.

### F. [[Psalm-119#v147|Psa. 119:147]]

> I anticipated the dawn and cried out; / I hoped in Your words.
