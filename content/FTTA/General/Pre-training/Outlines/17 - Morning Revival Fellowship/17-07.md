# Morning Revival (4) (17-07)

## The Practice and Two Aspects of Morning Revival

**Scripture Reading:** Psa. 119:147; Eph. 6:17-18; Psa. 108:2-3; 59:16a; 119:164a; 1 John 1:9; Psa. 139:23; S. S. 1:4a; Matt. 6:6; Exo. 34:3

## I. Morning revival is for us to fellowship with the Lord:

### A. By calling on the Lord—Psa. 119:147.

> **Psa. 119:147**
> *I anticipated the dawn and cried out; / I hoped in Your words.*

### B. By receiving God's word—Eph. 6:17-18.

> **Eph. 6:17-18**
> *And receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which Spirit is the word of God, by means of all prayer and petition, praying at every time in spirit and watching unto this in all perseverance and petition concerning all the saints.*

1. By means of pray-reading—Eph. 6:17-18.

2. Not by the way of feelings.

### C. By singing praises unto God—Psa. 108:2-3; 59:16a; 119:164a.

> **Psa. 108:2-3**
> *Awake, O harp and lyre! / I will awaken the dawn. / I will give thanks to You among the peoples, O Jehovah; / And I will sing psalms to You among the nations.*

> **Psa. 59:16a**
> *But I will sing of Your strength; / And I will joyfully sing of Your lovingkindness in the morning.*

> **Psa. 119:164a**
> *Seven times a day I praise You.*

### D. By asking the Lord to enlighten us that we might clear up our conscience—Psa. 139:23; 1 John 1:9.

> **Psa. 139:23-24**
> *Search me, O God, and know my heart; / Try me, and know my anxious thoughts; / And see if there is some harmful way in me, / And lead me on the eternal way.*

> **1 John 1:9**
> *If we confess our sins, He is faithful and righteous to forgive us our sins and cleanse us from all unrighteousness.*

## II. Morning revival is also for us to pray and intercede for others by receiving the Lord's burden for them.

## III. There are two aspects of morning revival—personal and corporate:

### A. We should have a personal and affectionate morning revival—with the Lord alone—S. S. 1:4a; Matt. 6:6; Exo. 34:3.

> **S. S. 1:4a**
> *Draw me; we will run after you...*

> **Matt. 6:6**
> *But you, when you pray, enter into your private room, and shut your door and pray to your Father who is in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will repay you.*

> **Exo. 34:3**
> *And no one shall come up with you, nor let anyone be seen anywhere on the mountain, nor let the flocks or herds graze in front of that mountain.*

### B. We should have morning revival corporately, especially with new ones—S. S. 1:4.

> **S. S. 1:4a**
> *Draw me; we will run after you...*
