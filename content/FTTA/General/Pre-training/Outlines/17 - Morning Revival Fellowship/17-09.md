# Morning Revival (5) (17-09)

## Practical Suggestions for Morning Revival

**Scripture Reading:** Prov. 6:9-11; Psa. 5:3; Rev. 2:2, 4; Heb. 11:6b; Prov. 24:33-34; 26:14; 19:15; Col. 2:7a; 2 Pet. 1:12; Eph. 5:16

## I. In order to rise early we should go to bed early.

## II. Concerning getting up—Prov. 6:9-11; Psa. 5:3:

### A. Love the Lord more than the bed.

### B. Get out of bed immediately—Prov. 6:9-11.

### C. Speak to the Lord first, call on His name and tell Him you love Him—Psa. 5:3.

> **Psa. 5:3**
> *O Jehovah, in the morning / You will hear my voice; / In the morning I will set forth my words in order to You, / And I will watch.*

## III. In the morning overcome taking care of the work for the Lord and fellowship with the Lord face to face through His Word—Rev. 2:2, 4.

> **Rev. 2:2, 4**
> *I know your works and your labor and your endurance... But I have one thing against you, that you have left your first love.*

## IV. Do not set too high of a standard.

## V. Build up the diligent habit to wake up early in the morning to contact the Lord—Heb. 11:6b; Prov. 24:33-34; 26:14; 19:15.

> **Heb. 11:6b**
> *He [God] is a rewarder of those who diligently seek Him.*

> **Prov. 24:33-34**
> *A little sleep, a little slumber, / A little folding of the hands to rest, / And your poverty will come upon you like a robber, / And your want, like an armed man.*

> **Prov. 26:14**
> *As the door turns upon its hinges, / So does the sluggard upon his bed.*

> **Prov. 19:15**
> *Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep, / And the idle soul will suffer hunger.*

## VI. Spend the adequate time each morning to absorb the Lord—Col. 2:7a.

> **Col. 2:7a**
> *Having been rooted and being built up in Him...*

## VII. Remind one another—"What time did you get up this morning?"—2 Pet. 1:12.

> **2 Pet. 1:12**
> *Therefore I will be ready always to remind you concerning these things, even though you know them and have been established in the present truth.*

## VIII. Budget your time—Eph. 5:16.

> **Eph. 5:16**
> *Redeeming the time, because the days are evil.*

## IX. Do not make a decision by yourself to rise early but fellowship with the Lord.
