# Morning Revival (9): Practicing Morning Revival (17-21)

## I. We must practice morning revival so that we can live, magnify, and enjoy Christ under any circumstances—[[Philippians-01#v20|Phil. 1:20-21]]:

### A. We must practice to enjoy God's salvation beginning in the morning—[[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19]]; [[Philippians-02#v14|2:14]]; [[Philippians-03#v9|3:9]]; [[Philippians-04#v2|4:2]].

### B. Practice rejoicing in the Lord early—[[Philippians-04#v4|Phil. 4:4]].

## II. To be revived we must exercise our spirit by all kinds of speaking to contact, receive, enjoy, and be filled with Christ—[[Ephesians-05#v18|Eph. 5:18-20]]:

### A. Appreciating and enjoying Christ as all the offerings for what we are, what we do, and what we never could be—[[1 Corinthians-06#v17|1 Cor. 6:17]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v21|2 Cor. 5:21]]; [[Hebrews-09#v28|Heb. 9:28]]; [[Hebrews-10#v6|10:6-7]].

### B. Calling upon the name of the Lord—[[Acts-02#v21|Acts 2:21]] and footnote 1:

#### 1. To be revived—[[Lamentations-03#v55|Lam. 3:55-56]].

#### 2. To satisfy God—[[Hebrews-13#v15|Heb. 13:15]].

#### 3. To build the church—[[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]].

#### 4. To defeat Satan—[[Psalm-18#v3|Psa. 18:3]].

### C. Pray-reading by all kinds of prayer—[[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17-18]]:

#### 1. Feeding on Christ—[[Matthew-04#v4|Matt. 4:4]]; [[John-06#v57|John 6:57]].

#### 2. Being enlivened by the Spirit—[[John-06#v63|John 6:63]].

#### 3. Being enlightened by the Word—[[Psalm-119#v130|Psa. 119:130]].

#### 4. Being encouraged by His speaking—[[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]].

#### 5. Being freed by the germ-killing sword—[[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17]].

#### 6. Being relieved from oldness by the eliminating Word—[[Ephesians-05#v26|Eph. 5:26-27]].

### D. Thanking, singing, and praising—[[2 Chronicles-20#v20|2 Chron. 20:20-23]]; [[Acts-16#v25|Acts 16:25]].
