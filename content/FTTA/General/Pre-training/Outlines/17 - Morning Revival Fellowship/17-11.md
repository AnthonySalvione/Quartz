# Morning Revival (6): Constituents and Examples of Morning Revival (17-11)

## I. We need to be renewed every day.

> **[[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16]]**
> *But though our outer man is decaying, yet our inner man is being renewed day by day.*

## II. The Lord's mercies are new every morning.

> **[[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-24]]**
> *It is Jehovah's lovingkindness that we are not consumed, / For His compassions do not fail; / They are new every morning; / Great is Your faithfulness. Jehovah is my portion.*

### A. Every day is a complete cycle and every morning He must be my portion:

#### 1. [[Psalm-63#v1|Psa. 63:1]]

> *O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee.* (King James Version)

#### 2. Manna came early in the morning:

> **[[Exodus-16#v12|Exo. 16:12]]**
> *In the morning you will be filled with bread.*

> **[[Exodus-16#v21|Exo. 16:21]]**
> *And they gathered it morning by morning, each one according to his eating; and when the sun became hot, it melted.*

> **[[Numbers-11#v9|Num. 11:9]]**
> *And when the dew fell on the camp at night, the manna would fall with it.*

#### 3. The city of God is helped by God at the dawn of morning:

> **[[Psalm-46#v4|Psa. 46:4-5]]**
> *There is a river whose streams gladden the city of God,...God is in the midst of her; she will not be moved; / God helps her at the dawn of the morning.*

### B. Men of God (Old Testament) who rose up early to meet the Lord:

#### 1. Abraham:

> **[[Genesis-19#v27|Gen. 19:27]]**
> *And Abraham rose up early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before Jehovah.*

> **[[Genesis-22#v3|Gen. 22:3]]**
> *And Abraham rose early in the morning...and went to the place of which God had told him.*

#### 2. Jacob at Bethel:

> **[[Genesis-28#v18|Gen. 28:18-19]]**
> *And Jacob rose up early in the morning and took the stone that he had put under his head, and he set it up as a pillar and poured oil on top of it. And he called the name of that place Bethel.*

#### 3. Moses:

##### a. Sent by Jehovah to rescue Israel from Pharaoh:

> **[[Exodus-08#v20|Exo. 8:20]]**
> *Then Jehovah said to Moses, Rise up early in the morning, and present yourself before Pharaoh...Thus says Jehovah, Let My people go that they may serve Me.*

> **[[Exodus-09#v13|Exo. 9:13]]**
> *Rise up early in the morning, and present yourself before Pharaoh...Thus says Jehovah, the God of the Hebrews, Let My people go that they may serve Me.*

##### b. Crossing the Red Sea:

> **[[Exodus-14#v24|Exo. 14:24]]**
> *And at the morning watch Jehovah looked down upon the camp of the Egyptians within the pillar of fire and of cloud and threw the camp of the Egyptians into confusion.*

> **[[Exodus-14#v26|Exo. 14:26]]**
> *And Jehovah said to Moses, Stretch out your hand over the sea...*

##### c. Manna falling:

> **[[Exodus-16#v4|Exo. 16:4]]**
> *Then Jehovah said to Moses, I will now rain bread from heaven for you; and the people shall go out and gather a day's portion every day.*

> **[[Exodus-16#v7|Exo. 16:7]]**
> *And in the morning, you will see the glory of Jehovah.*

> **[[Exodus-16#v12|Exo. 16:12]]**
> *In the morning you will be filled with bread.*

> **[[Exodus-16#v21|Exo. 16:21]]**
> *And they gathered it morning by morning, each one according to his eating; and when the sun became hot, it melted.*

##### d. Receiving the law:

> **[[Exodus-24#v4|Exo. 24:4]]**
> *And Moses wrote down all the words of Jehovah. And he rose up early in the morning and built an altar at the base of the mountain.*

> **[[Exodus-34#v1|Exo. 34:1-2]]**
> *Then Jehovah said to Moses...be ready by the morning, and come up in the morning to Mount Sinai, and present yourself there to Me.*

> **[[Exodus-34#v5|Exo. 34:5-6]]**
> *And Jehovah descended in the cloud and took His place there with him and proclaimed the name of Jehovah. Then Jehovah passed by before him and proclaimed, Jehovah, Jehovah, God compassionate and gracious...*

#### 4. Aaron the high priest burning incense:

> **[[Exodus-30#v7|Exo. 30:7]]**
> *And Aaron shall burn on it fragrant incense; every morning.*

#### 5. Joshua before crossing the Jordan to enter into the good land:

> **[[Joshua-03#v1|Josh. 3:1]]**
> *Then Joshua rose early in the morning; and they set out from Shittim and came to the Jordan, he and all the children of Israel; and they stayed there overnight, before they crossed over.*

> **[[Joshua-06#v12|Josh. 6:12]]**
> *And Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the Ark of Jehovah.*

#### 6. Hannah concerning Samuel:

> **[[1 Samuel-01#v19|1 Sam. 1:19-20]]**
> *And they rose up early in the morning and worshipped before Jehovah; ...and Jehovah remembered her. And in due time Hannah conceived and bore a son; and she called his name Samuel.*

#### 7. David:

> **[[Psalm-05#v3|Psa. 5:3]]**
> *O Jehovah, in the morning / You will hear my voice; / In the morning I will set forth my words in order to You, / And I will watch.*

##### a. Call on the Lord at evening, morning, and noon:

> **[[Psalm-55#v16|Psa. 55:16-17]]**
> *As for me, I call out to God, / And Jehovah will save me. / Evening and morning and at noontime / I complain and moan, / And He hears my voice.*

##### b. Sing aloud in the morning:

> **[[Psalm-59#v16|Psa. 59:16]]**
> *But I will sing of Your strength; / And I will joyfully sing of Your loving-kindness in the morning.*

##### c. Early will I seek Him:

> **[[Psalm-63#v1|Psa. 63:1]]**
> *O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: / My soul thirsteth for thee.* (KJV)

> **[[Psalm-119#v147|Psa. 119:147]]**
> *I anticipated the dawn and cried out; / I hoped in Your words.*

> **[[Psalm-88#v13|Psa. 88:13]]**
> *But I, O Jehovah, have cried out to You; / And in the morning my prayer comes before You.*

#### 8. Solomon:

> **[[Proverbs-08#v17|Prov. 8:17]]**
> *I love them that love me; and those who seek me early shall find me.* (KJV)

#### 9. Isaiah:

> **[[Isaiah-26#v9|Isa. 26:9]]**
> *My soul desires You in the night; / Indeed my spirit within me seeks You at the dawn.*

> **[[Isaiah-05#v11|Isa. 5:11]]**
> *Woe to those who rise early in the morning / That they may run after liquor; / To those who linger into the evening / That wine may inflame them!*

> **[[Isaiah-33#v2|Isa. 33:2]]**
> *O Jehovah, be gracious to us; we have waited for You; / Be our arm every morning.*

> **[[Isaiah-50#v4|Isa. 50:4]]**
> *The Lord Jehovah has given me / The tongue of the instructed / That I should know how to sustain the weary with a word. / He awakens me morning by morning; / He awakens my ear / To hear as an instructed one.*

#### 10. Jeremiah:

##### a. Jehovah rising early to speak to Jeremiah:

> **[[Jeremiah-11#v6|Jer. 11:6-7]]**
> *And Jehovah said to me, Proclaim all these words... For I solemnly warned your fathers in the day that I brought them up from the land of Egypt, even to this day, rising up early and warning, saying, Listen to My voice.*

> **[[Jeremiah-32#v33|Jer. 32:33]]**
> *And they have turned their back to Me and not their face, although I taught them, rising up early and teaching...*

##### b. Jeremiah rising early to speak to Israel:

> **[[Jeremiah-25#v3|Jer. 25:3]]**
> *The word of Jehovah came to me, and I spoke to you, rising up early and speaking, but you have not listened.*

> **[[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-24]]**
> *It is Jehovah's lovingkindness that we are not consumed, / For His compassions do not fail; / They are new every morning; / Great is Your faithfulness. / Jehovah is my portion.*

#### 11. Ezekiel:

> **[[Ezekiel-12#v8|Ezek. 12:8]]**
> *Then the word of Jehovah came to me in the morning...*

> **[[Ezekiel-33#v22|Ezek. 33:22]]**
> *He had opened my mouth before he came to me in the morning. Therefore my mouth was opened, and I was no longer dumb.*

#### 12. Daniel:

> **[[Daniel-06#v10|Dan. 6:10]]**
> *Now when Daniel came to know that the writing had been signed, he went to his house (in his upper room he had windows open toward Jerusalem) and three times daily he knelt on his knees and prayed and gave thanks before his God, because he had always done so previously.*

### C. New Testament:

#### 1. Jesus:

> **[[Mark-01#v35|Mark 1:35]]**

> **[[Mark-16#v9|Mark 16:9a]]**

> **[[John-20#v1|John 20:1a]]**

#### 3. Reward to the overcomers:

> **[[Revelation-22#v16|Rev. 22:16]]**
> *I Jesus...I am the Root and the Offspring of David, the bright morning star.*

## III. He is our portion by calling on His name:

### A. Calling on the Lord is:

#### 1. Breathing:

> **[[Lamentations-03#v55|Lam. 3:55-56]]**
> *I called upon Your name, O Jehovah, / From the lowest pit. / You have heard my voice; do not hide / Your ear at my breathing, at my cry.*

##### a. Therefore we need to call on Him in every place:

> **[[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]]**
> *To the church of God which is in Corinth, to those who have been sanctified in Christ Jesus, the called saints, with all those who call upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place.*

##### b. Calling on His name is a sacrifice of praise continually to God:

> **[[Hebrews-13#v15|Heb. 13:15]]**
> *Through Him then let us offer up a sacrifice of praise continually to God, that is, the fruit of lips confessing His name.*

#### 2. Drinking:

> **[[Judges-15#v18|Judg. 15:18]]**
> *And he was very thirsty, and he called on Jehovah.*

> **[[Judges-15#v19|Judg. 15:19]]**
> *But God broke open the hollow place...and water came forth from it. And when he drank, his spirit returned and he was revived.*

> **[[Isaiah-12#v2|Isa. 12:2-3]]**
> *God is now my salvation...and He has become my salvation. Therefore you will draw water with rejoicing from the springs of salvation.*

> **[[Isaiah-12#v4|Isa. 12:4]]**
> *And you will say in that day, / Give thanks to Jehovah; call upon His name!*

> **[[1 Corinthians-12#v3|1 Cor. 12:3]]**
> *...no one can say, Jesus is Lord! except in the Holy Spirit.*

> **[[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13]]**
> *For also in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body...and were all given to drink one Spirit.*

#### 3. Taking Him as our rich portion:

> **[[Romans-10#v12|Rom. 10:12]]**
> *...for the same Lord is Lord of all and rich to all who call upon Him.*

#### 4. A way to lay hold of Him:

> **[[Isaiah-64#v7|Isa. 64:7a]]**
> *And there is no one who calls upon Your name, / Who stirs himself up to lay hold of You.*

### B. So we need to call on the Lord:

#### 1. From the depths of our being:

> **[[Psalm-130#v1|Psa. 130:1]]**
> *Out of the depths I have called upon You, O Jehovah.*

#### 2. That we can rejoice all day long:

> **[[Psalm-89#v15|Psa. 89:15-16]]**
> *Blessed are the people who know the joyful shout; / They walk, O Jehovah, in the light of Your countenance. / In Your name they exult all day long.*

#### 3. With those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart:

> **[[2 Timothy-02#v22|2 Tim. 2:22]]**
> *But flee youthful lusts, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.*

#### 4. Our entire life:

> **[[Psalm-116#v1|Psa. 116:1-2]]**
> *I love Jehovah because He hears / My voice, my supplications, / Because He inclines His ear to me; / Therefore I will call upon Him all my days.*

## IV. Eating the Lord through His Word:

### A. After the creation of man:

> **[[Genesis-02#v7|Gen. 2:7]]**
> *Jehovah God formed man from the dust of the ground...*

> **[[Genesis-02#v8|Gen. 2:8-9]]**
> *And Jehovah God planted a garden in Eden, in the east; and there He put the man whom He had formed. And out of the ground Jehovah God caused to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight and good for food, as well as the tree of life in the middle of the garden...*

### B. Eating the lamb in Egypt—the strength to escape Egypt:

> **[[Exodus-12#v3|Exo. 12:3]]**
> *...each man shall take a lamb according to his fathers' house, a lamb for a household.*

> **[[Exodus-12#v11|Exo. 12:11]]**
> *And this is how you shall eat it: with your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand; and you shall eat it in haste.*

### C. Eating in the wilderness:

> **[[Exodus-16#v12|Exo. 16:12]]**
> *...in the morning you will be filled with bread...*

> **[[Exodus-16#v21|Exo. 16:21]]**
> *And they gathered it morning by morning, each one according to his eating...*

### D. Eating in the good land:

> **[[Deuteronomy-08#v7|Deut. 8:7]]**
> *For Jehovah your God is bringing you to a good land...*

> **[[Deuteronomy-08#v9|Deut. 8:9]]**
> *A land in which you will eat bread without scarcity; you will not lack anything in it.*

> **[[Deuteronomy-08#v10|Deut. 8:10]]**
> *You shall eat and be satisfied.*

### E. Find the Word, eat His Word, let His Word be unto you...:

> **[[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]**
> *Your words were found and I ate them; / And Your word became to me the gladness and joy of my heart...*

### F. When we open our mouth, He causes us to eat:

> **[[Ezekiel-03#v1|Ezek. 3:1-2]]**
> *...Son of man, eat what you find... So I opened my mouth, and He gave me that scroll to eat.*

### G. Thy words are sweet to my taste:

> **[[Psalm-119#v103|Psa. 119:103]]**
> *How sweet are Your words to my taste! / Sweeter than honey to my mouth!*

### H. To be abundantly satisfied:

> **[[Psalm-36#v8|Psa. 36:8]]**
> *They are saturated with the fatness of Your house, / And You cause them to drink of the river of Your pleasures...*

### I. How to sustain the weary with a word:

> **[[Isaiah-50#v4|Isa. 50:4]]**
> *The Lord Jehovah has given me / The tongue of the instructed, / That I should know how to sustain the weary with a word. / He awakens me morning by morning; / He awakens my ear / To hear as an instructed one.*

### J. Eat the Word of God and overcome:

> **[[Matthew-04#v4|Matt. 4:4]]**
> *Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that proceeds out through the mouth of God.*

### K. Live by Jesus by eating Jesus in His Word:

> **[[John-06#v57|John 6:57]]**
> *As the living Father has sent Me and I live because of the Father, so he who eats Me, he also shall live because of Me.*

### L. Receive the Word by pray-reading the Word:

> **[[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17-18]]**
> *And receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which Spirit is the word of God, by means of all prayer and petition, praying at every time in spirit...*

### M. We must mix the Word with faith:

> **[[Hebrews-04#v2|Heb. 4:2]]**
> *For indeed we have had the good news announced to us, even as they also; but the word heard did not profit them, not being mixed together with faith in those who heard.*

### N. Not sluggish:

> **[[Hebrews-06#v12|Heb. 6:12]]**
> *That you may not be sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and long-suffering are inheriting the promises.*

### O. The Yes and the Amen to the promises of God:

> **[[2 Corinthians-01#v20|2 Cor. 1:20]]**
> *For as many promises of God as there are, in Him is the Yes; therefore also through Him is the Amen to God, for glory through us to God.*

### P. We need to grow unto salvation with the guileless milk of the Word:

> **[[1 Peter-02#v2|1 Pet. 2:2]]**
> *As newborn babes, long for the guileless milk of the word in order that by it you may grow unto salvation.*

### Q. Partakers of God's divine nature through the Word:

> **[[2 Peter-01#v4|2 Pet. 1:4]]**
> *Through which He has granted to us precious and exceedingly great promises that through these you might become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption which is in the world by lust.*

### R. The Bible consummates with the tree of life and river of life:

> **[[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]]**
> *And he showed me a river of water of life, bright as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb in the middle of its street. And on this side and on that side of the river was the tree of life, producing twelve fruits...*
