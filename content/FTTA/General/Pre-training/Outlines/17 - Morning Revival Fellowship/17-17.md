# Morning Revival (7): The Purpose of Morning Revival (17-17)

## I. To satisfy the ascended Christ in His present heavenly ministry as He is fighting to close this age—[[Psalm-110#v1|Psa. 110:1]], [[Psalm-110#v3|3]], [[Psalm-110#v7|7]]:

### A. By voluntarily spending personal, intimate, and affectionate time with the Lord in the womb of the morning.

### B. By becoming refreshing dew to water and refresh Him through our early consecration and love.

### C. By becoming a brook of water from which He can drink to be satisfied and encouraged to fight on to close this age.

### D. Apparently we are eating and drinking, but actually He is—[[Hebrews-13#v15|Heb. 13:15]].

## II. To revive and resurrect us and to cause us to pursue Christ in love for full satisfaction—[[Luke-15#v24|Luke 15:24]]; [[Song-01#v2|S. S. 1:2-4a]]:

### A. By lovingly seeking the Lord early in the morning—[[Psalm-63#v1|Psa. 63:1]]; [[John-20#v1|John 20:1]], [[John-20#v13|13]], [[John-20#v17|17-18]].

### B. By loving Christ as the coming morning star—[[Revelation-02#v28|Rev. 2:28]]; [[Revelation-22#v16|22:16-17a]]; [[2 Peter-01#v19|2 Pet. 1:19]]:

#### 1. Christ will appear as the rising sun to His people generally, but as the morning star to His watching lovers particularly.

#### 2. He will appear privately to His overcomers as the morning star at the darkest hour.

### C. To make us living, vital, organic members of Christ—[[Revelation-01#v18|Rev. 1:18]]; [[Acts-03#v4|Acts 3:4]].

## III. To build up the Body of Christ and to defeat Satan—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Acts-02#v1|Acts 2:1-2]], [[Acts-02#v15|15]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]]; [[Romans-16#v20|Rom. 16:20]]; [[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]] and note 9:

### A. By enjoying Christ early, He increases in us organically, resulting in the building up of the Body of Christ and the exulting of Christ as our Head.

### B. The church took form as the Body of Christ at Pentecost beginning early in the morning.

### C. Satan is defeated by our calling on the name of Jesus and by our being joined together with all those who call on Him in every place.

### D. Hallelujah! Christ is Victor! Satan is crushed and dealt with by the Body of Christ. Hallelujah!

### E. Without practicing morning revival you remain individualistic, independent, and in the separated, satanic self—[[Matthew-16#v23|Matt. 16:23a]].
