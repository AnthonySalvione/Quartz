# Morning Revival (3) (17-05)

## Benefits of Morning Revival

**Scripture Reading:** Exo. 16:14-21; Matt. 4:4; Col. 2:7; 2 Kings 19:30; Isa. 37:31; Eph. 4:16; 2 Cor. 3:18; Rom. 8:2; Gal. 5:24; S. S. 7:12; Psa. 119:105, 147

## I. Morning revival is for us to receive spiritual nourishment that we can live a normal Christian life—Exo. 16:14-21; Matt. 4:4.

> **Exo. 16:21**
> *And they gathered [the manna] morning by morning, each one according to his eating; and when the sun became hot, it melted.*

> **Matt. 4:4**
> *Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that proceeds out through the mouth of God.*

## II. Morning revival is for us to grow, be built up with Christ, and to live Christ—Col. 2:7; 2 Kings 19:30; Isa. 37:31; Eph. 4:16.

> **Col. 2:7**
> *Having been rooted and being built up in Him.*

> **2 Kings 19:30; Isa. 37:31**
> *Take root downward and bear fruit upward.*

> **Eph. 4:16**
> *...causes the growth of the Body unto the building up of itself in love.*

## III. By beholding the Lord we can be transformed—2 Cor. 3:18.

> **2 Cor. 3:18**
> *We all with unveiled face, beholding and reflecting like a mirror the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory to glory, even as from the Lord Spirit.*

## IV. We contact the Lord as the law of the Spirit of life:

A. To enable us to overcome and resist temptation—Rom. 8:2.

> **Rom. 8:2**
> *For the law of the Spirit of life has freed me in Christ Jesus from the law of sin and of death.*

B. To let the element of Christ's death work in us—Gal. 5:24; Rom. 8:2.

> **Gal. 5:24**
> *They who are of Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh with its passions and its lusts.*

> **Rom. 8:2**
> *For the law of the Spirit of life has freed me in Christ Jesus from the law of sin and of death.*

## V. Receive specific leading for our life and work—S. S. 7:12; Psa. 119:105, 147.
