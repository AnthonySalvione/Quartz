# Morning Revival (1) (17-01)

## The Importance of Morning Revival

**Scripture Reading:** [[Song-01#v2|S. S. 1:2]]; [[Philippians-03#v13|Phil. 3:13-14]]; [[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-23a]]; [[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5]]; [[Proverbs-04#v18|Prov. 4:18]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16]]

## I. Morning revival is for us to build up a personal and affectionate relationship with the Lord—S. S. 1:2.

> **[[Song-01#v2|S. S. 1:2]]**
> *Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth! / For your love is better than wine.*

## II. We can have a new start and a new beginning every morning—[[Philippians-03#v13|Phil. 3:13-14]].

> **[[Philippians-03#v13|Phil. 3:13-14]]**
> *Forgetting the things which are behind and stretching forward to the things which are before, I pursue toward the goal for the prize.*

## III. We can start the day with the Lord's new mercies—[[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-23a]].

> **[[Lamentations-03#v22|Lam. 3:22-23a]]**
> *It is Jehovah's lovingkindness that we are not consumed, / For His compassions do not fail; / They are new every morning.*

## IV. We can be renewed every day—[[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5]]; [[Proverbs-04#v18|Prov. 4:18]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16]].

> **[[Titus-03#v5|Titus 3:5]]**
> *Not out of works in righteousness which we did but according to His mercy He saved us, through the washing of regeneration and the renewing of the Holy Spirit.*

> **[[Proverbs-04#v18|Prov. 4:18]]**
> *But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn, / Which shines brighter and brighter until the full day.*

> **[[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16]]**
> *Therefore we do not lose heart; but though our outer man is decaying, yet our inner man is being renewed day by day.*

## V. Daily morning revival enables us to live a vital and victorious life that meets the requirements of the God-ordained way to build up the Body of Christ:

A. By our intimate and thorough fellowship with the Lord, we can have the vitality to be vitalizing members of the Body of Christ.

B. By enjoying the Lord every morning, we can live a revived life which enables us to bring others to the Lord.

C. Living a revived life enables us to recover meetings in mutuality for the building up of the Body of Christ.

## VI. Daily revival brings in the reliable, long-lasting revival.
