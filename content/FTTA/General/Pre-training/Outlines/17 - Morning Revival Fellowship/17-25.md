# Morning Revival (11): The Means of Fellowship (1) (17-25)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Psalm-27#v4|Psa. 27:4]]; [[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]; [[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17]]

> **[[Psalm-27#v4|Psa. 27:4]]** — *One thing I have asked from Jehovah; / That do I seek: / To dwell in the house of Jehovah / All the days of my life, / To behold the beauty of Jehovah, / And to inquire in His temple.*

> **[[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]** — *Your words were found and I ate them, / And Your word became to me / The gladness and joy of my heart, / For I am called by Your name, / O Jehovah, God of hosts.*

> **[[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17]]** — *And receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which [Spirit] is the word of God.*

## I. Our intention is not to understand more doctrine but to enter into the reality of fellowship in our daily living:

### A. We should make a resolution before God to set aside specific time each day to practice this kind of fellowship.

### B. After scheduling a time, we should check with one another to see whether we are practicing according to our plan and where we practiced this fellowship.

### C. We should consider and resolve the difficulty in practicing certain points.

### D. We cannot expect to succeed by practicing only two to three times; we should thank the Lord if we are able to practice for two to three months and learn the lesson of fellowship.

## II. The living of a Christian depends entirely on his fellowship with God; if we truly enter into and live in this fellowship, many problems will be resolved—[[1 Corinthians-01#v9|1 Cor. 1:9]]; [[1 John-01#v5|1 John 1:5]]:

### A. The teachings we have received and the truth we have understood are merely outward; in order to receive real help and solve our problems, we need to enter into this kind of fellowship—cf. [[1 Corinthians-02#v2|1 Cor. 2:2]].

### B. The empowering, enlightening, enjoying of rest, rejoicing, believing, overcoming of trials, temptations, and hardships, and comforting for a Christian all depend on fellowship.

## III. All orthodox Christians agree that there are two important means to fellowship with God—prayer and reading the Bible—[[Psalm-27#v4|Psa. 27:4]]; [[Psalm-119#v15|119:15]], [[Psalm-119#v148|148]].

> **[[Psalm-27#v4|Psa. 27:4]]** — *One thing I have asked from Jehovah; / That do I seek: / To dwell in the house of Jehovah / All the days of my life, / To behold the beauty of Jehovah, / And to inquire in His temple.*

> **[[Psalm-119#v15|Psa. 119:15]]** — *I will muse upon Your precepts / And regard Your ways.*

> **[[Psalm-119#v148|Psa. 119:148]]** — *My eyes anticipated the night watches, / That I might muse upon Your word.*

### A. Prayer is for contacting God and having fellowship with Him; the emphasis here is not on asking God concerning different matters; prayer is a means and a way of fellowship.

### B. Reading the Bible as a means to fellowship with God is neither for understanding the Bible nor for knowing the contents of the Bible; rather, it is entirely for fellowshipping with God and contacting Him—[[John-05#v39|John 5:39-40]].

> **[[John-05#v39|John 5:39-40]]** — *You search the Scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is these that testify concerning Me. Yet you are not willing to come to Me that you may have life.*
