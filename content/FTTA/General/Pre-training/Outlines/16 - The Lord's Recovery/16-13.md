# The Lord's Recovery (5) (16-13)

## An Overview of the Central Burden of the Lord's Recovery Before His Appearing

> *"I worship Him that in the last two or three years, ever since I wrote the new hymn 'What miracle! What mystery! / That God and man should blended be!' my speaking has turned into a higher realm.... If you have paid attention, you will notice that since that time my messages have changed and have become new. I hope that all of you brothers will be able to catch up with these new messages."*
> — *How to be a Co-worker and an Elder*, p. 58

## I. Deification and blending for the building up of today's Zion:

> *"The diamond" in the "box" of the Bible is the revelation that in Christ God has become man in order to make man God in life and in nature but not in the Godhead.*
> — *Life-study of 1 and 2 Samuel*, p. 204

> *I have been a Christian for so many years. After so many years, I have been made by God to know only one thing—God became man so that man may become God in life and in nature but not in the Godhead. This is my unique burden, my unique message.*
> — *The Practical Way to Live a Life according to the High Peak of the Divine Revelation in the Holy Scriptures*, p. 27

> *We may think that the local churches are the goal of God's economy. However, they are not the goal, but the procedure God takes to reach the goal of His economy.*
> — *Practical Points concerning Blending*, p. 9

> *Today in the church age, the God-men who were perfected and matured are Zion, the overcomers, the vital groups within the churches.... We have to realize what the Lord's recovery is. The Lord's recovery is to build up Zion.*
> — *Practical Points concerning Blending*, pp. 45-46

## II. The divine and mystical realm of the consummated Spirit and the pneumatic Christ:

> *With the Triune God Himself as a divine and mystical realm there are no "complications," but in the divine and mystical realm of the consummated Spirit and the pneumatic Christ there are a number of "complications," all of which are blessings to us.*
> — *The Divine and Mystical Realm*, p. 40

> *The believers must consider highly the entry into this realm, realizing that without Christ becoming the life-giving Spirit, without Christ being the pneumatic Christ, without Christ being the Lord Spirit, and without Christ being the Christ in resurrection and not only in the flesh, there is absolutely no way for the believers to participate in, experience, and enjoy the organic section of God's salvation.*
> — *The Divine and Mystical Realm*, pp. 47-48

## III. God's organic salvation and the full ministry of Christ in three stages for the fulfillment of God's eternal economy:

> *The complete salvation of God has two aspects.... The judicial aspect is the procedure of God's salvation through the redemption of Christ for God's salvation to fully meet the righteous requirement of His law. The organic aspect is the fulfillment of God's salvation through the life of Christ for the believers to be transformed and to grow and mature in the life of God.*
> — *The Organic Aspect of God's Salvation*, p. 8

> *The New Testament clearly shows that our Lord became something three times. First, as God, He became flesh.... Next, as the last Adam, a man in the flesh, He became the life-giving Spirit. Third, as the life-giving Spirit, the pneumatic Christ, He became the seven Spirits.... These are Christ's three becomings in His three stages. His first becoming is in the stage of His incarnation, His second becoming is in the stage of His inclusion, and His third becoming is in the stage of His intensification. This is the New Testament.*
> — *How to be a Co-worker and an Elder*, p. 61

## IV. The universal, divine-human incorporation of the consummated God with the regenerated believers:

> *Union and mingling refer to our relationship with the Lord in our life and nature but not in our person. Humanly speaking, no person can be in another person. But in the divine and mystical realm, the consummated God and the regenerated believers, the persons, indwell one another. This is an incorporation. In this universal, divine-human incorporation, persons indwell one another, that is, they coinhere.*
> — *The Issue of Christ Being Glorified by the Father with the Divine Glory*, p. 41

> *The goal of God's economy is the enlarged, universal, divine-human incorporation of the consummated God with the regenerated believers.*
> — *The Issue of Christ Being Glorified by the Father with the Divine Glory*, p. 44

> *The New Jerusalem is the tabernacle of God, and the center of this tabernacle is Christ as the hidden manna for us to eat. The way to be in the New Jerusalem is to eat Christ. The more we eat Christ, the more we are incorporated into this universal incorporation.*
> — *The Issue of Christ Being Glorified by the Father with the Divine Glory*, p. 31

## V. Reigning in life with grace over all things unto eternal life:

> *Reigning in life is the full experience of the organic salvation of God.... Reigning in life in chapter five is the key to open up the rest of the book.*
> — *Crystallization-study of the Complete Salvation of God in Romans*, HWMR, p. 37

> *When we are reigning in life, we are in all the matters presented in [[Romans-06|Romans 6—16]].... If we do not reign in life, we cannot participate in all these processes, but if we reign in life, we can be in all these things, and we can be constituted Christ's overcoming bride for His satisfaction, pleasure, and delight.*
> — *Crystallization-study of the Complete Salvation of God in Romans*, HWMR, p. 39

> *God's complete salvation is for us to reign in life by the abundance of grace (God Himself as our all-sufficient supply for our organic salvation) and of the gift of righteousness (God's judicial redemption applied to us in a practical way). When we are all reigning in life, living under the ruling of the divine life, the issue is the real and practical Body life.*
> — *Crystallization-study of the Complete Salvation of God in Romans*, HWMR, p. 4
