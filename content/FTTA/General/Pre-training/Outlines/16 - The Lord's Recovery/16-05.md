# The Lord's Recovery (3) (16-05)

## The Heavenly Vision of the Lord's Present Recovery

### The Recovery of the Eternal Economy of God

**Scripture Reading:** [[Acts-26#v16|Acts 26:16-19]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-6]]; [[1 Timothy-06#v3|6:3-4]]; [[2 Corinthians-11#v2|2 Cor. 11:2-3]]; [[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Tim. 4:22]]

## I. Recovery means the restoration or return to a normal condition after a damage or a loss has been incurred:

### A. Because the church has become degraded through the many centuries of its history, it needs to be restored according to God's original intention—[[Genesis-02#v7|Gen. 2:7-25]]; [[Revelation-19#v7|Rev. 19:7-9]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v18|18-21]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-2]], [[Revelation-22#v17|17a]].

### B. Recovery means to go back to the beginning; we need to go back to the beginning, receiving the Lord's grace to go back to God's original intention, to what God ordained in the beginning—[[Matthew-19#v8|Matt. 19:8]].

### C. Concerning the church with Christ as her content, beauty, and glory, our vision should be governed not by the present situation or by traditional practice but by God's original intention and standard as revealed in the Scriptures according to the present advance of His recovery:

#### 1. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of Christ as our center, reality, life, and everything in His full ministry of incarnation, inclusion, and intensification—[[Colossians-01#v17|Col. 1:17b]], [[Colossians-01#v18|18b]]; [[Psalm-80#v1|Psa. 80:1]], [[Psalm-80#v15|15]], [[Psalm-80#v17|17-19]]; [[John-01#v14|John 1:14]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4-5]], [[Revelation-02#v7|7]], [[Revelation-02#v17|17]]; [[Revelation-03#v7|3:7-8]], [[Revelation-03#v12|12-13]], [[Revelation-03#v17|17-22]]; [[Revelation-04#v5|4:5]]; [[Revelation-05#v6|5:6]]; [[John-06#v57|John 6:57]]; [[John-14#v21|14:21]], [[John-14#v23|23]]; [[John-21#v15|21:15-17]].

#### 2. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the oneness of the Body of Christ—[[John-17#v11|John 17:11]], [[John-17#v21|21-23]]; [[Ephesians-04#v3|Eph. 4:3-4a]]; [[Revelation-01#v11|Rev. 1:11]].

#### 3. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the function of all the members of the Body of Christ—[[Ephesians-04#v15|Eph. 4:15-16]]; [[Romans-15#v16|Rom. 15:16]]; [[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]], [[1 Peter-02#v9|9]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v1|1 Cor. 14:1]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v4|4b]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v12|12]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v26|26]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v31|31]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v39|39]].

### D. For a person to leave the Lord's recovery means that he has never seen what the Lord's recovery is; if we have not seen a vision of the Lord's recovery, we actually are not in the Lord's recovery—[[Acts-26#v13|Acts 26:13-19]]; cf. [[Genesis-13#v14|Gen. 13:14-18]].

### E. God's eternal economy was unveiled through the apostles, but because the believers lost the proper understanding of God's eternal economy, there is the need for it to be recovered by the Lord:

#### 1. The words *recovery* and *economy* refer to one thing as seen from two different viewpoints; with God it is a matter of economy; with us it is a matter of recovery—[[1 Timothy-01#v4|1 Tim. 1:4]]; [[Ephesians-01#v10|Eph. 1:10]]; [[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]].

#### 2. There is a strong and solid principle that whenever the majority of the people of God fail to carry out God's purpose, God comes in to have a recovery; His recovery is always with the minority, with a remnant of overcomers, not with the majority—[[2 Kings-22#v8|2 Kings 22:8]]; [[Ezra-01#v3|Ezra 1:3-11]]; [[Nehemiah-02#v11|Neh. 2:11]], [[Nehemiah-02#v17|17]]; [[Revelation-03#v21|Rev. 3:21]]; [[Revelation-18#v4|18:4]].

## II. We must walk in the truth of the heavenly vision of God's eternal economy, of the mark of God's eternal economy, and of the goal of God's eternal economy; this vision must be renewed in us day by day to be the controlling vision of all our life, work, and activity—[[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[Acts-26#v16|Acts 26:16-19]]; [[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]]; [[3 John-01#v3|3 John 3-4]]:

### A. God's economy is "in faith," ([[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-5]]), and "faith is the substantiation of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen" ([[Hebrews-11#v1|Heb. 11:1]], [[Hebrews-11#v5|5-7]], [[Hebrews-11#v26|26-27]]):

#### 1. The wonderful Jesus is "the Author and Perfecter of our faith" ([[Revelation-12#v2|12:2]]), and faith is to believe that "God is" ([[Revelation-11#v6|11:6]]), and we are not ([[Genesis-05#v22|Gen. 5:22-24]]).

#### 2. Thus, we may say that the Lord's recovery is to recover us from things that are seen to things that are not seen; hence, we need to be those who exercise our spirit of faith to walk by faith and not by sight—[[2 Corinthians-04#v13|2 Cor. 4:13]], [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|16-18]].

### B. God's eternal economy is His plan to dispense Himself into His chosen, predestinated, and redeemed people as their life, their life supply, and their everything, to produce, constitute, and build up the organic Body of Christ—[[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-6]]; [[1 Timothy-06#v3|6:3-4]]; [[2 Corinthians-11#v2|2 Cor. 11:2-3]]; [[Titus-01#v9|Titus 1:9]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|Col. 2:19]].

### C. God's eternal economy is to make man the same as He is in life and nature but not in the Godhead and to make Himself one with man and man one with Him, thus to be enlarged and expanded in His expression, that all His divine attributes may be expressed in human virtues—[[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]]; [[John-03#v15|3:15-16]]; [[2 Peter-01#v4|2 Pet. 1:4]]; [[Romans-08#v16|Rom. 8:16]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v17|1 Cor. 6:17]]; [[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1-2]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|2 Cor. 4:16-18]]; [[Philippians-03#v21|Phil. 3:21]]; [[1 John-03#v2|1 John 3:2]].

### D. The mark of God's eternal economy, the strategic and central point of God's eternal economy, is the indwelling, subjective Christ as the Spirit in our spirit, our mingled spirit—[[2 Corinthians-03#v17|2 Cor. 3:17]]; [[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Tim. 4:22]]; [[Romans-08#v16|Rom. 8:16]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v17|1 Cor. 6:17]]:

#### 1. We must be narrowed down to and even zeroed in on the all-inclusive divine Spirit in our human spirit that we may be kept from missing the mark of the divine economy—[[1 Timothy-01#v6|1 Tim. 1:6]]; [[Malachi-02#v15|Mal. 2:15-16]]; [[Romans-01#v9|Rom. 1:9]]; [[Romans-08#v4|8:4]], [[Romans-08#v6|6]]; [[Galatians-05#v25|Gal. 5:25]]; [[Philippians-03#v3|Phil. 3:3]]; [[2 Corinthians-02#v13|2 Cor. 2:13]].

#### 2. In the "blueprint" of God's original intention, man is the center of the entire universe, and the center of man is his spirit—[[Genesis-02#v7|Gen. 2:7]]; [[Proverbs-20#v27|Prov. 20:27]]:

##### a. The heavens are for the earth, the earth is for man, and man was created by God with a spirit that he may contact God, receive God, contain God, worship God, live God, fulfill God's purpose for God, express God, and be one with God—[[Zechariah-12#v1|Zech. 12:1]]; [[John-04#v24|John 4:24]].

##### b. Without God being the Spirit and without our having a spirit to contact God, to be one with God, the whole universe is empty and we are nothing—[[Ecclesiastes-01#v2|Eccl. 1:2]]; [[Ecclesiastes-03#v11|3:11]]; [[Job-32#v8|Job 32:8]]; cf. [[Romans-09#v21|Rom. 9:21]], [[Romans-09#v23|23]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v7|2 Cor. 4:7]].

#### 3. Christ as the life-giving Spirit can be everything to us when we live in and exercise our spirit; to live in our soul is to live in the principle of antichrist—[[Zechariah-04#v6|Zech. 4:6]]; [[Zechariah-12#v1|12:1]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[1 Corinthians-06#v17|6:17]]; [[1 John-02#v18|1 John 2:18-19]].

#### 4. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the oneness in our spirit; to be in our spirit is to be in Jerusalem, the place of simplicity and oneness, whereas to be in our mind is to be in Babylon, the place of confusion and division—[[John-04#v24|John 4:24]]; [[Ephesians-02#v22|Eph. 2:22]]; [[Romans-01#v9|Rom. 1:9]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v6|2 Tim. 1:6-7]].

#### 5. Our spirit is a "country" of grace to swallow up race for the one new man; our mind is a "country" of quarreling; to enjoy the Lord as the Spirit being in our spirit is to have grace with us; when this is lost, the degradation of the church is present—[[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Tim. 4:22]]; [[Galatians-06#v18|Gal. 6:18]]; [[Galatians-05#v15|5:15]]; [[Colossians-03#v10|Col. 3:10-11]].

### E. The goal of God's eternal economy is the reality of the organic Body of Christ, consummating in the New Jerusalem—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2-3]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9-10]]:

#### 1. Without the local churches, there is no practical expression of the Body of Christ and there can be no reality of the Body of Christ—[[Revelation-01#v10|Rev. 1:10-13]]; [[Revelation-02#v7|2:7]].

#### 2. God's eternal economy is to obtain the Body of Christ; any work outside of this is not on the central lane of God's eternal economy—[[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1-6]], [[Ephesians-04#v11|11-16]].

#### 3. We must follow the footsteps of the apostle Paul to bring all the saints into the blending life of the entire Body of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-12#v24|1 Cor. 12:24]]; [[Romans-16#v1|Rom. 16:1-20]].

#### 4. For the Lord's recovery in this age, we must cooperate with the Lord to be the overcomers as today's Zion in today's Jerusalem (the church life) for the building up of the Body of Christ to consummate the New Jerusalem—[[Revelation-03#v21|Rev. 3:21-22]]; [[Revelation-14#v1|14:1-5]]; [[Judges-05#v15|Judg. 5:15-16]], [[Judges-05#v31|31]].

### F. Teachings that differ from the unique and healthy teaching of God's eternal economy, the teaching of the apostles, separate us from the genuine appreciation, love, and enjoyment of the precious person of the Lord Jesus Christ Himself as our life and our everything—[[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]]; [[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]]; [[2 Corinthians-11#v2|2 Cor. 11:2-3]].

### G. Today we can be in one accord because we have only one vision, the vision of the eternal economy of God—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v9|1 Cor. 1:9-10]]; [[Jeremiah-32#v39|Jer. 32:39]].

### H. We in the Lord's recovery must have a clear vision of God's eternal economy and then be governed, controlled, and directed by this vision, for we are here to carry out God's eternal economy in His recovery—[[Acts-26#v18|Acts 26:18-19]]; [[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]].

## III. The eternal economy of God ([[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]]; [[1 Corinthians-09#v17|1 Cor. 9:17]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]], [[Acts-26#v22|22]]) is the great answer to the great question concerning God's purpose in His creation of man and in His dealing with His chosen people ([[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[Job-10#v13|Job 10:13]]; cf. [[Ephesians-03#v9|Eph. 3:9]]):

### A. The mystery hidden in God's heart is God's eternal economy ([[Ephesians-01#v10|Eph. 1:10]]; [[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v4|1 Tim. 1:4]]), which is God's eternal intention with His heart's desire to dispense Himself in His Divine Trinity as the Father in the Son by the Spirit into His chosen people to be their life and nature that they may be the same as He is as His duplication ([[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[1 John-03#v2|1 John 3:2]]) to become an organism, the Body of Christ as the new man ([[Ephesians-02#v15|Eph. 2:15-16]]), for God's fullness, God's expression ([[Revelation-01#v22|1:22-23]]; [[Revelation-03#v19|3:19]]), which will consummate in the New Jerusalem ([[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2—22:5]]).

### B. God's intention was not to judge Job or punish him but to tear him down and then rebuild him with Himself to make Job a new man in God's new creation; Job's sufferings were not God's judgment but God's stripping and consuming that God might gain Job so that he might gain God more—[[2 Corinthians-05#v17|2 Cor. 5:17]]; [[Galatians-06#v15|Gal. 6:15]]; [[Philippians-03#v8|Phil. 3:8]], [[Philippians-03#v12|12-14]].

### C. God becoming man that man might become God in life and in nature but not in the Godhead for the producing and building up of the Body of Christ to consummate the New Jerusalem is the essence of the entire Bible, the "diamond" in the "box" of the Bible, the eternal economy of God—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[John-12#v24|John 12:24]]; [[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]].

### D. God became man through incarnation by participating in man's humanity; man becomes God in life and nature but not in the Godhead through transformation by participating in God's divinity—[[John-01#v14|John 1:14]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|2 Cor. 3:18]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]]; [[2 Peter-01#v4|2 Pet. 1:4]]; [[Philippians-02#v5|Phil. 2:5]]; [[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]; [[Ephesians-01#v5|Eph. 1:5]]; [[Romans-08#v19|Rom. 8:19]]; [[1 John-03#v2|1 John 3:2]]; [[John-01#v12|John 1:12-13]].

### E. This divine-human romance is the subject of the entire Bible, the content of God's eternal economy, and the secret of the entire universe—[[Song-01#v1|S. S. 1:1]]; [[Song-06#v13|6:13]]; cf. [[Habakkuk-01#v1|Hab. 1:1]]; [[Habakkuk-02#v4|2:4]]; [[Romans-01#v17|Rom. 1:17]]:

#### 1. Christ is divine and human, and His transformed lover is human and divine; they are the same in life and nature, perfectly matching each other.

#### 2. The Triune God consummated to be the Husband and the tripartite man transformed to be the bride are to be one couple, a corporate, great God-man—[[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9]]; [[Revelation-22#v17|22:17a]].

#### 3. The work in the Lord's recovery is His genuine work to prepare His bride—[[1 Corinthians-03#v9|1 Cor. 3:9]], [[1 Corinthians-03#v12|12]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v10|15:10]], [[1 Corinthians-15#v58|58]]; [[1 Corinthians-16#v10|16:10]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v2|2 Cor. 3:2-3]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v6|6]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v8|8-9]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v16|4:16-18]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v18|5:18-20]]; [[2 Corinthians-11#v2|11:2-3]].

### F. God and man will become one entity, and that one entity is the mingling of divinity with humanity, which will consummate in the New Jerusalem as the conclusion of the entire Bible—[[Revelation-21#v3|Rev. 21:3]], [[Revelation-21#v22|22]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9]]; cf. [[Leviticus-02#v4|Lev. 2:4-5]]; [[Psalm-92#v10|Psa. 92:10]].
