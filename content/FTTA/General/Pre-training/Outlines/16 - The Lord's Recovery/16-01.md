# The Lord's Recovery (1) (16-01)

## I. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of Christ as our all in all:

### A. Christ is preeminent:

#### 1. In the Triune Godhead—[[Philippians-02#v9|Phil. 2:9]]; [[John-15#v26|John 15:26]]; [[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18b-19]]; [[Colossians-02#v9|2:9]].

#### 2. In God's old creation—[[Colossians-01#v15|Col. 1:15b]]; [[Hebrews-02#v14|Heb. 2:14a]].

#### 3. In God's new creation—[[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v20|1 Cor. 15:20]]; [[Romans-08#v29|Rom. 8:29]]; [[Ephesians-01#v20|Eph. 1:20-23]].

#### 4. In God's exaltation—[[Acts-02#v33|Acts 2:33a]]; [[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22b]]; [[Philippians-02#v9|Phil. 2:9b]].

### B. Christ is everything to the believers:

#### 1. He is the God-allotted portion to the saints—[[Colossians-01#v12|Col. 1:12]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]].

#### 2. He is our life—[[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4a]]; [[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20a]]; [[Philippians-01#v20|Phil. 1:20-21]]; [[Galatians-04#v19|Gal. 4:19]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|Col. 2:19b]].

#### 3. He is the hope of glory—[[Colossians-01#v21|Col. 1:21]].

#### 4. He is our necessities and our enjoyment—[[John-08#v12|John 8:12]]; [[John-06#v51|6:51]], [[John-06#v57|57b]]; [[1 Corinthians-10#v4|1 Cor. 10:4]]; [[John-20#v22|John 20:22]]; [[Galatians-03#v27|Gal. 3:27]]; [[John-15#v7|John 15:7a]]; [[Colossians-02#v16|Col. 2:16-17]]; [[Matthew-11#v28|Matt. 11:28]].

### C. Christ is the divine provision:

#### 1. He is God's power to us—[[1 Corinthians-01#v24|1 Cor. 1:24a]].

#### 2. He is wisdom to us from God—[[1 Corinthians-01#v24|1 Cor. 1:24b]], [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|30]]:

##### a. He is our righteousness.

##### b. He is our sanctification.

##### c. He is our redemption.

### D. Christ is everything to the church:

#### 1. He is the Head of the Body—[[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18]].

#### 2. He is the Body of the Head—[[1 Corinthians-12#v12|1 Cor. 12:12]].

#### 3. He is the foundation—[[1 Corinthians-03#v11|1 Cor. 3:11]].

#### 4. He is the cornerstone—[[Ephesians-02#v20|Eph. 2:20]].

#### 5. He is all the members of the new man—[[Colossians-03#v10|Col. 3:10-11]].

### E. Christ is every part of God's New Testament economy—[[Ezekiel-01#v15|Ezek. 1:15]]; [[Colossians-01#v17|Col. 1:17]].

## II. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the oneness of the Body of Christ:

### A. The oneness of the Lord's aspiration and prayer is the oneness of the Body, which is the enlarged oneness of the Divine Trinity—[[John-17#v11|John 17:11b]], [[John-17#v21|21]]; [[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4]]; [[Romans-12#v4|Rom. 12:4-5]]:

#### 1. This oneness is in the Father's name by His divine life—[[John-17#v2|John 17:2]], [[John-17#v6|6]], [[John-17#v11|11]].

#### 2. This oneness is in the Triune God through sanctification by the holy Word—[[John-17#v14|vv. 14-21]].

#### 3. This oneness is in the divine glory for the expression of the Triune God—[[John-17#v22|vv. 22-24]].

### B. The oneness of the Lord's aspiration became the oneness in actuality, the oneness of the Spirit—[[Ephesians-04#v3|Eph. 4:3]].

### C. The practice of this oneness is the one accord—[[Matthew-18#v19|Matt. 18:19]]; [[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1-4]]; [[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Philippians-01#v27|Phil. 1:27]]; [[Philippians-02#v2|2:2]]; [[Philippians-04#v2|4:2]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10]].

### D. The practice of this oneness is on the unique ground of the locality in which a local church is established and exists—[[Acts-08#v1|Acts 8:1]]; [[Acts-13#v1|13:1]]; [[Revelation-01#v11|Rev. 1:11]]; [[Acts-14#v23|Acts 14:23]]; cf. [[Titus-01#v5|Titus 1:5]].

### E. The one accord is the master key to every blessing in the New Testament—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Psalm-133|Psa. 133]].

## III. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the function of all the members of the Body of Christ:

### A. The gifts, the gifted persons, perfect the saints to do what they do for the organic building up of the Body of Christ—[[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-16]]; [[Acts-20#v20|Acts 20:20]], [[Acts-20#v31|31]]:

#### 1. The gifted persons perfect the saints by nourishing them with the life supply for their growth in life—[[Acts-06#v4|Acts 6:4]]; [[Ephesians-04#v15|Eph. 4:15-16]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v2|1 Cor. 3:2]], [[1 Corinthians-03#v6|6]]; [[1 Peter-02#v2|1 Pet. 2:2]].

#### 2. The gifted persons perfect the saints in the priesthood of the gospel for the carrying out of God's eternal economy—[[1 Peter-02#v9|1 Pet. 2:9]]; [[Revelation-01#v6|Rev. 1:6]]; [[Romans-15#v16|Rom. 15:16]]; [[Romans-12#v1|12:1]]; [[Colossians-01#v28|Col. 1:28-29]].

#### 3. The Body is built directly by all the members of Christ functioning, each in his own measure—[[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]].

### B. The saints who are willing and ready to be perfected will be the overcomers who constitute the bride of Christ to end this age and usher in the kingdom of our Lord and of His Christ—[[Revelation-11#v15|Rev. 11:15]]; [[Revelation-17#v14|17:14]]; [[Revelation-19#v7|19:7-9]].
