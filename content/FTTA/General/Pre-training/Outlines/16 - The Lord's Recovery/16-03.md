# The Lord's Recovery (2) (16-03)

## God's Intention, Satan's Strategy, and the Lord's Recovery

**Scripture Reading:** [[Ephesians-03#v8|Eph. 3:8-10]]; [[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]; [[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18b]]; [[Ephesians-04#v3|Eph. 4:3]]; [[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]]

## I. God's intention in His economy is to dispense Christ with all His riches into His believers chosen by God for the constitution and building up of the Body of Christ, the church, to consummate the New Jerusalem for the full expression of the processed Triune God—[[Ephesians-03#v8|Eph. 3:8-10]].

## II. Because the church has become degraded through the many centuries of its history, it needs to be restored according to God's original intention and standard as revealed in the Scriptures—cf. [[2 Kings-22#v8|2 Kings 22:8]]; [[Ezra-01#v3|Ezra 1:3-11]]; [[Nehemiah-02#v11|Neh. 2:11]], [[Nehemiah-02#v17|17]]; [[Matthew-19#v8|Matt. 19:8]]; [[Revelation-18#v4|Rev. 18:4]]:

### A. Recovery means the restoration or return to a normal condition after a damage or a loss has been incurred.

### B. After Satan's destruction, God came in to redo the things that He had done before; this redoing is His recovery, which is to bring back whatever has been lost and destroyed by God's enemy, Satan—[[1 John-03#v8|1 John 3:8b]].

## III. Satan's strategy is versus the Lord's recovery—[[Isaiah-14#v12|Isa. 14:12-15]]; [[Ezekiel-28#v12|Ezek. 28:12-19]]; [[John-14#v30|John 14:30]]; [[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]; [[Ephesians-06#v10|Eph. 6:10-11]]:

### A. The first category of Satan's activity is to produce many substitutes for Christ:

#### 1. In Colossae human philosophy was brought in as a substitute for Christ—[[Colossians-02#v8|Col. 2:8]]; [[Colossians-01#v12|1:12]].

#### 2. Hebrews reveals that Satan utilized Judaism to replace Christ—[[Hebrews-08#v6|Heb. 8:6]]; [[Hebrews-09#v23|9:23]]; [[Hebrews-10#v5|10:5-10]].

#### 3. Galatians reveals that the Judaizers were making the law a substitute for Christ—[[Ephesians-03#v1|3:1-3]], [[Ephesians-03#v24|24]]; [[Ephesians-01#v15|1:15-16a]]; [[Ephesians-02#v20|2:20]]; [[Ephesians-04#v19|4:19]].

#### 4. In Corinth the gifts, signs, and wisdom were used by the enemy to replace Christ—[[1 Corinthians-01#v22|1 Cor. 1:22-23]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v31|12:31]].

#### 5. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of Christ as our center, reality, life, and everything—[[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18b]]; [[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4]], [[Revelation-02#v7|7]], [[Revelation-02#v17|17]]; [[Revelation-03#v20|3:20]].

### B. The second category of Satan's work is to divide the Body of Christ:

#### 1. The sects, denominations, and divisions in the Body wipe out the corporate expression of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10-13a]]; [[Galatians-05#v19|Gal. 5:19-20]].

#### 2. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the oneness of the Body of Christ—[[John-17#v11|John 17:11b]], [[John-17#v21|21]]; [[Ephesians-04#v3|Eph. 4:3-4a]]; [[Revelation-01#v11|Rev. 1:11]].

### C. The third category of Satan's work is to kill the function of all the members of Christ's Body by the clergy-laity system:

#### 1. We need to have the Lord's hate for the works of the Nicolaitans—[[Revelation-02#v6|Rev. 2:6]]; [[Revelation-01#v5|1:5b-6]]; [[1 Peter-02#v5|1 Pet. 2:5]], [[1 Peter-02#v9|9]].

#### 2. The Lord's recovery is the recovery of the function of all the members of the Body of Christ—[[Ephesians-04#v15|Eph. 4:15-16]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v4|1 Cor. 14:4b]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v26|26]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v31|31]].
