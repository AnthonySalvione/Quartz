# Clearance of the Past (5-01)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Hebrews-12#v1|Heb. 12:1]]; [[Luke-19#v1|Luke 19:1-10]]; [[Acts-19#v18|Acts 19:18-19]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v17|2 Cor. 5:17]]; [[Ephesians-04#v17|Eph. 4:17-24]]; [[1 Thessalonians-01#v9|1 Thes. 1:9]]; [[1 John-05#v21|1 John 5:21]]

## I. The relationship between the clearance of the past and salvation—[[Hebrews-12#v1|Heb. 12:1]]:

### A. The clearance of the past is not a requirement for salvation; there is no need for us to do or add anything, such as clearing the past, before we can be forgiven by God.

### B. Once a person receives the Lord Jesus as his Savior and obtains God's salvation, the power of this salvation causes him to clear away and end his former life; such a clearance is an issue of our enjoyment of salvation—[[Luke-19#v1|Luke 19:1-10]].

### C. Once we are saved, if we desire to have a good Christian life, a good testimony and go on with the Lord in a pure way, we should completely clear away the things of our past.

## II. The basis of the clearance of the past is not according to the demand of outward regulations but according to the moving of the Spirit within—[[2 Corinthians-05#v17|2 Cor. 5:17]]; [[Romans-06#v4|Rom. 6:4]]; [[Romans-08#v4|8:4]]:

### A. The life we have obtained is holy, and the Spirit in us is moving and working and will require us, by the holy life within us, to remove all demonic and dirty things and to cut off the old way of living—[[Ephesians-04#v17|Eph. 4:17-24]].

### B. Our responsibility is to follow the leading of the Spirit and to allow Him to move freely within us.

## III. The object of the clearance of the past are four categories of things of the past that need to be cleared away after we are saved:

### A. Unrighteous matters—Unrighteous means unjust, illegal; all we have obtained in the past by unjust, illegal means, such as stealing, swindling, taking by force, encroaching upon others' properties, keeping things that have been lost by others, not returning things that were borrowed long ago, and all illegitimate relations with others and unjust dealings toward others are things we should clear up:

#### 1. Restoring what we owe—an example of the clearance of the past is seen in the case of Zaccheus, in his restoring of what he owed others—[[Luke-19#v8|Luke 19:8]].

#### 2. After we are saved, it is not necessary to dig up our past life to see whom we owe and to repay them; but if the Holy Spirit in us makes us conscious of the fact that we owe others in material things, then we should follow His leading to properly restore them.

### B. Improper matters—Improper means that the very nature of a certain thing or matter is improper or indecent:

#### 1. Things used in gambling and drinking can be bought and obtained by legal means, but these things are used for gambling and drinking and need to be cleared up.

#### 2. Examples of smoking or reading obscene novels are not necessarily unrighteous, but surely they are immoral and improper and must be dealt with.

### C. Evil and unclean matters—Evil and unclean matters are things related to idols, such as graven or portrait idols, or candlesticks and censers used in idol worship:

#### 1. Abandoning idols—an example of the clearance of the past is seen in the case of the Thessalonians who turned to God from idols—[[1 Thessalonians-01#v9|1 Thes. 1:9]]:

##### a. The things related to the reading of facial features, fortune-telling, horoscopes, and divination are things that involve idols and should be terminated.

##### b. Not only should we throw out other images, but we should give up even portraits or statues of Jesus—cf. [[Isaiah-53#v2|Isa. 53:2]].

#### 2. All matters related to idols need to be dealt with thoroughly—we are the temple of the Holy Spirit and there is no agreement between the temple of God and idols—[[Deuteronomy-05#v8|Deut. 5:8]]; [[2 Corinthians-06#v16|2 Cor. 6:16]]; [[1 John-05#v21|1 John 5:21]]:

##### a. We should not even inquire how the Gentiles worship their gods; God forbids us to inquire about such matters—[[Deuteronomy-12#v30|Deut. 12:30]].

##### b. Christians should not visit temples; there is no agreement between idols and the temple of the living God; guarding ourselves from idols means staying away from them—[[2 Corinthians-06#v16|2 Cor. 6:16]]; [[1 John-05#v21|1 John 5:21]].

##### c. We should carefully avoid the names of other gods and we also should not hold on to any superstition, fear of misfortune, or taboo for certain associations or words—[[Psalm-16#v4|Psalm 16:4]].

#### 3. Destroying the demonic and dirty things—the biblical principle is that anything related to idols and any demonic and filthy thing, however valuable it may be, should be burned:

##### a. Examples of these things are the candlesticks and censers used in idol worship, ornaments and clothing with the image of the dragon, sacred writings of heathen religions, books and charms pertaining to divination, and tablets related to ancestral worship.

##### b. Other examples are gambling instruments, utensils for alcoholic drinking, pipes for smoking, obscene books, and pornographic pictures; these things are demonic and filthy.

##### c. Unbecoming clothing also is in this category of demonic and dirty things; some items of clothing are too short; in this case they should be lengthened; still others may be demonic and defiled by sin; such items should be burned.

##### d. No one can decide the extent of our dealing with these things, but the Spirit will guide us inwardly—[[Acts-19#v18|Acts 19:18-19]]; [[Leviticus-13#v52|Lev. 13:52-54]].

### D. Old ways of living—Old ways of living refer to our entire old manner of living before we were saved; after we are saved, we should put an end to our whole former manner of living and have a new beginning—[[Luke-19#v8|Luke 19:8-9]]; [[Acts-19#v18|Acts 19:18-19]]:

#### 1. After we are saved, God's desire is that we bring before Him every person, thing, and affair in our living and see if we still can be related to them as we were in the former days.

#### 2. If we are willing to go before the Lord in this way, we will see that after we are regenerated, our inward taste, mood, and feeling have changed, indicating that our old life and old way of living has been ended.

## IV. The extent of the clearance of the past is "life and peace"; if we walk according to the Spirit, the result will surely be life and peace—[[Romans-08#v5|Rom. 8:5-6]]:

### A. Life and peace are the degree to which we are required to clear the past; if we follow the demand of our inner sense to restore what we owe, to confess our sins, to eliminate improper, demonic, and dirty things, and to end our old way of living, we will have the sense that we are strengthened, enlightened, satisfied, and enlivened; we will also have the sense of peace, security, and God's full presence.

### B. If we have made a clearance of the things of the past and we still sense the lack of fullness and manifestation of life and peace, we may be sure that we have not followed the Spirit to the uttermost; we have not sufficiently satisfied the demand of the inner feeling; we must look to the Lord for the supply of grace that we may clear up things more thoroughly, until we are full of life and peace—[[Colossians-03#v9|Col. 3:9-11]]; [[Ephesians-04#v22|Eph. 4:22]], [[Ephesians-04#v24|24]].
