# The Vision and the Ministry of the Age (2) — Entering into the Ministry of the Age with the Vision of the Age to Be in One Accord for the Building Up of the One Body (21-05)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[2 Kings-02#v1|2 Kings 2:1-14]]; [[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-12]]

## I. In every age there is the vision of that age, and we need to serve God according to the vision of the age—[[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17]]; [[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]]:

### A. God's word reveals to us that in every age God gives only one vision to man; a vision is a scene that God unfolds to man.

### B. The vision that the Lord has given to His recovery today is an all-inclusive one that includes the economy of God, the mingling of God and man, the dispensing of the Divine Trinity, the believers' salvation in Christ, and the New Jerusalem—[[Acts-20#v27|Acts 20:27]]; [[Colossians-01#v25|Col. 1:25-26]]; [[Ephesians-01#v10|Eph. 1:10]], [[Ephesians-01#v5|5]], [[Ephesians-01#v7|7]], [[Ephesians-01#v11|11]], [[Ephesians-01#v13|13-14]]; [[Ephesians-03#v17|3:17a]]; [[Romans-05#v10|Rom. 5:10]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]].

### C. The vision for this age that we have received is the ultimate consummation of the divine revelations in the entire Bible that extends all the way from Genesis to Revelation.

### D. The particular vision that the Lord has given us in His recovery today is an all-inclusive, all-inheriting vision which includes the ultimate consummation of all visions—the New Jerusalem, which is the ultimate consummation of God's economy—[[Revelation-21#v9|Rev. 21:9-10]]:

#### 1. Within this ultimate consummation everything is included; in it we have the final scene of all visions seen in the Bible—[[Acts-21#v2|21:2]], [[Acts-21#v9|9]]; [[Acts-22#v5|22:5]].

#### 2. "After my study of the Bible for the past sixty-nine years, what have I seen? I would say that I have seen the New Jerusalem. This is my vision, this is my revelation, and this is my ministry" (*Practical Points Concerning Blending*, pp. 25-26).

### E. "Thus, the central revelation of God and the vision that we hold in the Lord's recovery is God becoming the flesh, the flesh becoming the life-giving Spirit, and the life-giving Spirit becoming the sevenfold intensified Spirit to build up the church that becomes the Body of Christ and that consummates the New Jerusalem" (*The Divine and Mystical Realm*, pp. 17-18).

### F. According to the Bible, a servant of the Lord must be governed by a vision—[[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]:

#### 1. The Bible shows us clearly that in every age God only gives the vision of the age to one man; we cannot find in the Bible that there were two visions of any age.

#### 2. Although Paul had many coworkers, no one except him saw any other vision; they all had one vision, which was the vision that Paul saw.

### G. Very few believers are clear about what it is to meet and serve according to the vision of the age:

#### 1. From the time of the apostles until today, for two thousand years, all the servants of the Lord who served according to the revelation of the Bible have served according to the vision of that age.

#### 2. The Lord's recovery is under the vision of the divine revelation; it bears the vision that the Lord has entrusted to this age.

#### 3. "I can say with full confidence that the Lord's recovery is serving under the vision. There is no doubt about this" (*The Vision of the Age*, p. 27).

## II. The particular recovery and work that God is doing in one age is the ministry of that age:

### A. In the Old Testament, Noah had the ministry of that age to build the ark, Moses had the ministry of that age to build the tabernacle, and David and Solomon had the ministry of that age to build the temple.

### B. In the New Testament, the ministry of the Lord Jesus is to build up the church as the Body of Christ—[[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]].

### C. The many gifted persons produced in the Lord's ascension have only one ministry, that is, to minister Christ for the building up of the Body of Christ, the church; this building up is not accomplished directly by the gifted ones but by the saints who have been perfected by the gifted ones—[[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-12]]; cf. [[Acts-01#v17|Acts 1:17]], [[Acts-01#v25|25]].

### D. "In God's building ministry, there are those who take the lead in that ministry in every age. May the Lord open our eyes to see that as long as we are human beings, we should be Christians; as long as we are Christians, we should enter into the Lord's ministry in this age" (*Words of Training for the New Way*, vol. 1, p. 23).

### E. In every age the Lord has special things that He wants to accomplish; He has His own recoveries and His own works to do; the particular recovery and work that He does in one age is the ministry of that age—cf. [[Genesis-06#v13|Gen. 6:13-14]]; [[2 Kings-02#v2|2 Kings 2:2-15]]:

#### 1. These ministries of the ages are different from the local ministries; Luther was a minister of his age; Darby was also a minister of his age; Watchman Nee and Witness Lee were ministers for the present age.

#### 2. It is God's mercy that a person can see and come in contact with the ministry of the age, yet it is altogether a different thing for a person to take up the courage to forsake past ministries and enter into God's present ministry—cf. [[1 Samuel-14#v1|1 Sam. 14:1-46]]; [[2 Samuel-06#v16|2 Sam. 6:16]], [[2 Samuel-06#v20|20-23]].

## III. Our one accord is in the all-inheriting vision of the age through the ministry of the age; seeing the vision of the age and entering into the ministry of the age is the basis of our one accord—[[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Acts-01#v14|1:14]]; [[Romans-15#v6|Rom. 15:6]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v12|1 Tim. 1:12]]; [[Acts-20#v24|Acts 20:24]]:

### A. Our vision should be one that matches the vision of the present age; in order for us to serve God today, our vision must extend all the way from the first vision of Adam in Genesis to the ultimate vision of John in Revelation—[[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]].

### B. Today we can be in one accord because we have only one vision, an up-to-date, all-inclusive, all-inheriting vision, the vision of the eternal economy of God; where there is no vision, the people cast off restraint because there is no one accord—[[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]].

### C. Throughout the ages there were many saints who loved the Lord and who feared the Lord, but we cannot say that they all had the vision that matched their age; neither can we say that they lived out the one accord—[[Acts-15#v35|Acts 15:35-39]]; [[Acts-18#v24|18:24-25]]; [[Acts-19#v1|19:1-2]]; [[Acts-21#v18|21:18-21]]:

#### 1. Many lovers of the Lord were raised up through the past centuries, but they were not able to be in one accord because the vision each held was different in degree; each one only held a part of the complete vision.

#### 2. The problem among born-again Christians today is that some are bound by the little experience and vision they have; some are unwilling to see more and consider all those who see more to be wrong.

### D. As saints in the Lord's recovery, we have to answer the Lord's call, follow the present vision, and enter into the ministry of the age to be the overcomers living and serving in one accord—[[Joshua-03#v6|Josh. 3:6]], [[Joshua-03#v13|13]]; [[Judges-07#v7|Judg. 7:7]]; [[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7]], [[Revelation-02#v11|11]], [[Revelation-02#v17|17]], [[Revelation-02#v26|26]]; [[Revelation-03#v5|3:5]], [[Revelation-03#v12|12]].

## IV. On God's side, His move and His building up of the one Body depend upon the consummated Spirit; on our side there is the need of another factor, that is, the one accord among His people:

### A. We must realize that if the one accord is absent, everything is gone; if the one accord is absent, the Lord's recovery is held down and the building up of the Body is frustrated.

### B. First, the New Testament shows us that what the Lord desires in His Body is oneness; then, when the church came into being, there was the need of the practice of this oneness; the practice of the genuine oneness is the one accord.

### C. In the Body we need oneness; in the churches and among the churches, we need the one accord:

#### 1. The one accord is for our practice; the oneness is primarily for the actuality, for the fact.

#### 2. In John 17 the Lord Jesus prayed for such a fact, and on the day of Pentecost, by pouring out Himself as the consummated Spirit, He accomplished His prayer; that was the actuality of the oneness—[[John-17#v11|John 17:11]], [[John-17#v21|21-23]]; [[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4]].

#### 3. After the accomplishment of the actuality of the oneness, there is the need for the practice of the oneness; when the oneness is practiced, it becomes the one accord—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Acts-02#v46|2:46]]; [[Acts-04#v24|4:24]]; [[Acts-05#v12|5:12]]; [[Acts-15#v25|15:25]]; [[Romans-15#v6|Rom. 15:6]].

### D. As we enter into the ministry of the age with the vision of the age we will be brought into the one accord resulting in the building up of the Body:

#### 1. "For the Lord's move in His recovery both locally and universally, we all need to be Body-conscious in one accord and Body-centered in oneness. In one accord we should be Body-conscious. In oneness we should be Body-centered...All the local churches are and should be one Body universally, doctrinally, and practically. Otherwise, where is the unique church of God and the unique one new man for the fulfilling of God's economy?!" (*One Body and One Spirit*, p. 24).

#### 2. "Without the blending, the Lord has no way to go on with us. Blending is the Body, blending is the oneness, and blending is the one accord...The impact is with the one accord, and the one accord actually is the blending...Our not being in one accord means that we do not practice the Body. According to the proper interpretation of the New Testament, the one accord is the one Body. We must practice the principle of the Body; then we will have the one accord" (*Fellowship Concerning the Urgent Need of the Vital Groups*, pp. 88-89).
