# Prayer for Confession — Confession for Personal Revival and Service (20-01)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Matthew-05#v3|Matt. 5:3-8]], [[Matthew-05#v13|13-15]], [[Matthew-05#v27|27-30]], [[Matthew-05#v38|38-42]]; [[Galatians-05#v19|Gal. 5:19-24]]; [[1 John-01#v5|1 John 1:5-9]]; [[1 John-02#v15|2:15-16]]; [[Revelation-03#v15|Rev. 3:15-19]]; Hymns: 426, 436, 438, 441, 445, 449

## I. The root of our service to the Lord is a personal revival from our confession in the Lord's presence, resulting in intimate fellowship and a purified conscience:

### A. To begin our service to the Lord we must go to Him personally to make a thorough confession in His presence.

### B. A thorough confession will bring us to a revival of intimate fellowship with the Lord—[[1 Timothy-01#v5|1 Tim. 1:5]].

### C. A thorough confession will help us to have a good and purified conscience, which is crucial for serving the Lord—[[Romans-09#v1|Rom. 9:1]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v3|2 Tim. 1:3a]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v19|1 Tim. 1:19]]; [[Acts-24#v16|Acts 24:16]]; [[Acts-23#v1|23:1]]; [[Hebrews-09#v14|Heb. 9:14]]:

#### 1. We must overcome the insensitivity in our conscience built up by our past living.

#### 2. Those saved out of the world have had their conscience seared by sin and a corrupting philosophy that denies sin and suppresses the truth of God; it is not surprising that we do not feel much at first—[[Romans-01#v18|Rom. 1:18]], [[Romans-01#v21|21]], [[Romans-01#v25|25]], [[Romans-01#v28|28]].

#### 3. Those raised in Christianity or even the church life often played with sin and worldliness, creating their own form of insensitivity, living a double life—[[1 Timothy-04#v2|1 Tim. 4:2]].

#### 4. Yet our very link to living with the Lord and knowing His will is a tender and good conscience.

#### 5. Thus we must confess to clear up all the old blockages and to develop a sensitive conscience.

## II. How to practice a time of confession:

### A. Prepare yourself, the time, and the place:

#### 1. It is better if you can fast for a time to stand against your natural man.

#### 2. Find a time and place when you can be alone; you may need a few hours or more and the privacy to kneel, prostrate yourself, and weep.

#### 3. Bring your Bible, your Hymnal, and a notebook and pen to take notes for later action.

#### 4. There may be the need for repeated times of prayer to get through with the Lord.

### B. Begin by opening yourself to the Lord:

#### 1. Singing a hymn or pray-reading a few verses may help to turn your heart to the Lord—[[2 Corinthians-03#v16|2 Cor. 3:16]].

#### 2. Tell the Lord that you love Him and that under the covering of His blood, you want to fully open yourself to Him to be shined upon and enlightened. Your goal is to bring your being fully into the light—[[1 John-01#v6|1 John 1:6-7]].

#### 3. Sometimes you may just call upon the Lord's name or even groan for some period of time until you sense some breakthrough.

### C. Confession must begin with one point that you are convicted of:

#### 1. There are a few points that we should all be convicted of—our fruitlessness, our deadness, or some sin that we have committed.

#### 2. Spend time to offer each matter to Him and linger in His presence; don't speak all the time; give Him the opportunity to speak to you in your spirit and through your mind.

#### 3. Our attitude must be not to argue or dispute with the light but to agree with the light and repent to the Lord.

#### 4. Before we confess, we may feel we have little to confess; once we start, we will realize that we have many things to confess.

#### 5. It is like cleaning a house; before you start to clean, you feel the house is not too dirty; once you begin to clean, you see dirt everywhere.

#### 6. If we have not practiced a life of confession, there is probably a big backlog of unconfessed matters.

#### 7. The principle is like digging the wells at Beer ([[Numbers-21#v18|Num. 21:18]])—we must take the lead to dig; then the water will begin to flow.

#### 8. We must have the desperation to pay the price to buy the real spiritual sight and experience from the Lord—[[Revelation-03#v17|Rev. 3:17-19]].

#### 9. Thus, we should seek to pay any price the Lord asks to cooperate with His shining.

#### 10. If you sense the Lord speaking on a particular point, agree with Him and speak as Mary did—[[Luke-01#v38|Luke 1:38]].

## III. What to confess:

### A. Only the Lord Himself is light and can help you to know what to confess.

### B. According to the truth of God's word, we should confess certain matters: matters concerning our former manner of life before we were saved; our sins and offenses toward God and others (family, saints, churches, all men); our worldly way of living and our love of the world; and other matters concerning which our conscience convicts us.

## IV. The result of our confession:

### A. Release from sin and condemnation—[[1 John-01#v9|1 John 1:9]].

### B. Revival of heart.

### C. New fellowship with the Lord and the brothers—[[1 John-01#v2|1 John 1:2-3]]; [[1 John-03#v14|3:14]]; [[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]]; [[Philippians-02#v1|Phil. 2:1]].
