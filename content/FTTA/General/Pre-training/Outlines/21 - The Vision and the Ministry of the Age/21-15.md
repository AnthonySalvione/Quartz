# Our Commission Regarding the Vision of the Age (21-15)

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Corinthians-16#v10|1 Cor. 16:10]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v58|15:58]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v9|3:9]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v10|15:10]]; [[2 Corinthians-12#v9|2 Cor. 12:9]]; [[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17]]; [[Ephesians-03#v8|3:8-11]]; [[Revelation-21#v10|Rev. 21:10]]

## I. "The recovery bears the vision that the Lord has entrusted to this age...Today we are fighting the good fight for the truth. We are bearing on our shoulders the commission of this age. This is our vision. We have to be clear about this, and we need to serve God according to this vision" (_The Vision of the Age_, pp. 27-31):

### A. We need to fulfill our divine commission according to the heavenly vision of the present age by working and abounding in the work of the Lord as God's fellow workers by the all-sufficient grace of God—[[1 Corinthians-16#v10|1 Cor. 16:10]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v10|15:10]], [[1 Corinthians-15#v58|58]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v9|3:9]]; [[2 Corinthians-12#v9|2 Cor. 12:9]].

### B. God's Word reveals to us that in every age there is the one vision and one commission that we must carry out in each age, and we have to serve God according to the vision of each respective age—[[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17]]; [[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]]:

#### 1. In every age the Lord has special things that He wants to accomplish; He has His own recoveries, His own works to do; the particular recovery and work that He does in one age is the vision and ministry of that age; in Genesis the building of God is the ministry and building of the age; in the building of God there is only "one window"—one revelation and one vision through one ministry—cf. [[Genesis-06#v13|Gen. 6:13-14]], [[Genesis-06#v16|16]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Revelation-21#v10|Rev. 21:10]]:

##### a. Our vision for this age extends all the way from Adam's vision of the tree of life in the garden of Eden to the New Jerusalem with the tree of life; the New Jerusalem is the last scene of the vision—[[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]].

##### b. We are not serving God according to the first few scenes alone; we are serving God according to the last scene, the New Jerusalem, which includes all the previous scenes.

##### c. God's eternal intention for us to become the New Jerusalem is seen in Genesis 1—2 as the organic blueprint, the architectural plan of the Triune God, and in Revelation 21—22 as the finished product, the organic masterpiece of the Triune God, for the full manifestation of His multifarious wisdom—[[Hebrews-11#v10|Heb. 11:10]]; [[Ephesians-02#v10|Eph. 2:10]]; [[Ephesians-03#v10|3:10]]; cf. [[1 Corinthians-01#v30|1 Cor. 1:30]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v10|3:10-12]]:

###### (1) Man is a vessel created according to God's kind that man might contain and express Him—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[Revelation-21#v11|Rev. 21:11]]; [[Revelation-04#v3|4:3]].

###### (2) The tree of life is the center of God's economy—[[Genesis-02#v7|Gen. 2:7-9]]; [[Revelation-22#v2|Rev. 22:2]].

###### (3) A river of water of life is flowing to reach the four directions of the earth—[[Genesis-02#v10|Gen. 2:10-14]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1]]; cf. 21:13.

###### (4) At the flow of the river are three kinds of precious materials for God's building—[[Genesis-02#v11|Gen. 2:11-12]]; [[Revelation-21#v18|Rev. 21:18-21]].

###### (5) The goal is the producing of a couple, a corporate, great God-man—[[Genesis-02#v22|Gen. 2:22]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9]]; [[Revelation-22#v17|22:17]].

#### 2. What is revealed in these two parts of the divine revelation in the Holy Scriptures, Genesis 1 and 2 and Revelation 21 and 22, is the central line of the divine revelation throughout the entire Holy Scriptures; this central line should be a controlling principle to our interpreting and understanding of the Holy Scriptures.

### C. The leadership in the New Testament ministry is the leadership of the controlling, God-given revelation of God's economy—[[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]:

#### 1. You should not do a work to attract people to follow you; such a one who makes himself attractive is wrong, and, if you are attracted to follow him, you help him to be wrong, destroying yourself and him—cf. [[2 Corinthians-04#v5|2 Cor. 4:5]].

#### 2. In the Lord's work we should beware of the ambition to get a place or a district for our work, captivating people to be our private co-workers; our fallen disposition by birth likes to captivate people—[[1 Corinthians-11#v19|1 Cor. 11:19]]; [[Galatians-05#v19|Gal. 5:19-20]].

#### 3. "It is grossly wrong to say that we are following a certain person. We are following a vision that belongs to the present age. It is God's consummate vision" (_The Vision of the Age_, p. 49).

#### 4. Because we are under the heavenly vision of the eternal economy of God according to the unique teaching of the apostles, we can be one in teaching ([[1 Corinthians-04#v17|1 Cor. 4:17]]; [[1 Corinthians-07#v17|7:17]]; [[1 Corinthians-16#v1|16:1]]; [[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]]; [[Romans-16#v17|Rom. 16:17]]; [[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3-4]]; [[1 Timothy-06#v3|6:3]]; [[Ephesians-04#v13|Eph. 4:13-14]]), practice ([[1 Corinthians-11#v16|1 Cor. 11:16]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v33|14:33b-34]]), thinking ([[Philippians-02#v2|Phil. 2:2]]; [[Philippians-04#v2|4:2]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10]]), speaking ([[Romans-15#v6|Rom. 15:6]]), essence, appearance, and expression ([[Revelation-01#v11|Rev. 1:11-12]]) in God's one ministry for His own move to produce one Body as His one testimony.

## II. Today we can be in one accord because we have only one vision, an up-to-date, all-inheriting vision—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]:

### A. There is great controversy in Christianity about this matter of one vision for one age. However, God's Word tells us clearly that in every age there is only one vision (see "The Vision and the Ministry of the Age, Outlines 1-3," _Full-time Training in Anaheim Pre-training Outlines_).

### B. The Bible shows us clearly that in every age God only gives the vision of the age to one man; we cannot find in the Bible that there were two visions for any one age.

### C. If we are clear about the vision of the Bible, we will have the confidence to say that we are indeed those who serve by following the vision of the age.

## III. There is only one blueprint and one master builder in the proper, correct building:

### A. This is true in every age.

### B. The Lord issues the blueprint, the revelation, and the utterance, and through one man He supervises and completes the building work.

### C. All those who do not build, speak, and serve according to the blueprint released by the Lord through that man are void of light and revelation and are not serving according to the vision.

### D. "It is only after His work through all the generations is completed that God will have not merely a garden but a building. With God's creation there is nothing of God Himself in His creatures.... In God's creation He did a work with His hands, not with Himself, but in His building God works with Himself as the material. In His building God mingles Himself very much with His creation. Therefore, God's building is a divine mingling of God Himself with man as His creature... What is God building? It is the building of Himself with humanity" (_The Building of God_, p. 8).

## IV. Paul was sent to preach to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ ([[Ephesians-03#v8|Eph. 3:8]]) in order that the Triune God could be dispensed into them to transform them into the members of Christ for the building up of the Body of Christ:

### A. When Paul saw the up-to-date vision of the age, God mingled with man for the building up of the Body of Christ, Barnabas was left behind.

### B. The church in Jerusalem did not come up fully to the vision of the age; instead [in Acts 15], it made a compromise.

### C. Apollos was powerful in expounding the Old Testament, but he was not in Paul's vision.

### D. However, Aquila, Priscilla, and Timothy were in Paul's vision; they were walking with Paul and working together with him.

### E. To depart from the apostles' teaching is to depart from the apostles' vision, the vision of the age.

## V. By the time the apostle John finished the book of Revelation, the biblical revelation had reached its ultimate consummation in the New Jerusalem; this then becomes the vision and basis of our service today.

## VI. From the time of the apostles until today, for two thousand years, all the servants of the Lord who serve according to the revelation of the Bible serve according to the vision; this is the standard and the basis of our service:

### A. First, we must be governed by the revelation contained in the Bible.

### B. Second, the standard of such a revelation must be sufficiently high; it must be up to the standard of the divine revelation.

### C. Third, the Lord's recovery is under the vision of the divine revelation.

## VII. Seeing the vision of the age is the basis for our one accord—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Acts-02#v46|2:46]]:

### A. Those who have different visions cannot be one because they have only part of the entire vision; they are bound by the little experience and vision they have—[[Acts-15#v13|Acts 15:13]], [[Acts-15#v19|19-21]], [[Acts-15#v35|35-39]]; [[Acts-18#v24|18:24-25]].

### B. The present need is to be in one accord and to be faithful in coordination:

#### 1. One accord does not mean a certain method that we have to practice; it means to be attuned to the Lord's heart—[[2 Samuel-23#v15|2 Sam. 23:15-16]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v24|1 Cor. 12:24]].

#### 2. The ultimate and completed vision will keep us from emphasizing different ways and different leadings—[[Philippians-02#v2|Phil. 2:2]].

#### 3. If we have different emphases and different ways, our energy will be dissipated and our faith will be weakened—[[1 Corinthians-01#v10|1 Cor. 1:10]].

#### 4. If we are in one accord, we will have an invincible morale, and we will march over all obstacles—[[Romans-15#v5|Rom. 15:5]]; [[Joshua-06#v16|Josh. 6:16]], [[Joshua-06#v20|20]].

## VIII. Today when we speak about the one accord, we are not speaking about a certain method that we have to practice; we are saying that we should be attuned to the Lord's heart:

### A. If we are attuned to the Lord's heart, and if we dispense the Triune God to others, imparting to them the Lord's life so that they become His members and are constituted to be His Body as His full expression, then spontaneously we will be in one accord.

### B. We must have the same view and the one accord; this is why I have presented to you the matter of the ultimate and completed vision:

#### 1. We should no longer emphasize different ways.

#### 2. We should not have any different leadings.

#### 3. We are all in the Lord's recovery, and we all have seen today's concluded vision.

## IX. Paul's burden in his epistles to Timothy is that there would not be different teachings—[[1 Timothy-01#v3|1 Tim. 1:3]]; [[1 Timothy-06#v3|6:3]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v13|2 Tim. 1:13-15]]:

### A. To teach different things is to not consent to healthy words—[[1 Timothy-06#v3|1 Tim. 6:3]]:

#### 1. In reality, the healthy words are the healthy teaching ([[Revelation-01#v10|1:10]]; [[2 Timothy-04#v3|2 Tim. 4:3]]), which covers the entire New Testament; it is constituted with the Lord Jesus' words of life and the preaching of the apostles, which is the word of the mystery of godliness, that is, God becoming flesh to produce the church—[[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15-16]].

#### 2. Different teachings are unhealthy teachings because they do not afford men any life supply and because they lead some to become shipwrecked regarding the faith—[[Revelation-01#v19|1:19]].

### B. Paul wrote his first epistle to Timothy out of his serious concern for the unhealthy teaching in the church in Ephesus and because of the mixture in the church in Jerusalem—[[Acts-20#v29|Acts 20:29-31]]; [[Acts-21#v18|21:18-26]].

### C. He wrote his second epistle to Timothy because some were not holding the healthy words and had turned away from his ministry—[[2 Timothy-01#v13|2 Tim. 1:13]], [[2 Timothy-01#v15|15]].

### D. The cause for the churches' decline is the forsaking of the apostles' teaching and ministry—[[Revelation-02#v4|Rev. 2:4]], [[Revelation-02#v6|6]], [[Revelation-02#v14|14-15]], [[Revelation-02#v20|20]], [[Revelation-02#v24|24]].

## X. We have to keep up-to-date with the flow of the age:

### A. "The flowing of the divine life, which started on the day of Pentecost and has been flowing throughout all generations to this very day, is just one stream" (_The Divine Stream_, p. 12).

### B. "Please be clear that there have never been two streams. There is only one stream, and you have to keep yourself in this one stream" (p. 13).

### C. "If you study carefully the history of the church, you will find that throughout the generations there has been one stream of the Holy Spirit flowing all the time" (p. 13).

## XI. "But I [Witness Lee] told Brother Nee, 'Even if one day you do not take this way, I will still take this way. I am not taking this way because of you, and I will not leave this way because of you. I have seen that this is the Lord's way. I have seen the vision'" (_The Vision of the Age_, p. 50).
