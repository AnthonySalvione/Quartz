# The New Jerusalem: The Ultimate and Consummate Vision of the Apostle John—The Vision of the New Jerusalem (18-01)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Revelation-19#v7|Rev. 19:7-9]]; [[Revelation-21#v1|21:1-2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9b-11]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|22:1-2]]; [[John-17#v1|John 17:1-2]], [[John-17#v6|6-24]]

## I. In every age there is the vision of that age, and we need to serve God according to the vision of that age; God's word reveals to us that in every age He gives only one vision to man—[[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Ephesians-01#v17|Eph. 1:17]]; [[Ephesians-03#v9|3:9]]:

### A. In the building of God there is only "one window"—one revelation and one vision through one ministry—cf. [[Genesis-06#v16|Gen. 6:16]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9b-10]].

### B. The vision that the Lord has given to His recovery is an all-inclusive vision—the all-inheriting vision of the age—[[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]:

#### 1. Our vision extends all the way from Adam's vision of the tree of life in the garden of Eden to the New Jerusalem with the tree of life; the New Jerusalem is the last scene of the vision; after that there is nothing more to be seen—[[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9b-11]]; [[Revelation-22#v18|22:18-19]].

#### 2. We are not serving God according to the first few scenes alone; we are serving God according to the last scene, which includes all the previous scenes—[[Genesis-05#v22|Gen. 5:22]]; [[Genesis-06#v9|6:9]].

#### 3. The goal of all our services, including preaching the gospel and edifying the believers, must be ultimately consummated in the New Jerusalem—[[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12-13]]; [[Revelation-19#v7|Rev. 19:7]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|21:2]].

### C. Our living out the New Jerusalem is for us to become the New Jerusalem, and our working out the New Jerusalem is for us to build the New Jerusalem by the flowing Triune God—[[Jeremiah-02#v13|Jer. 2:13]]; [[John-04#v14|John 4:14b]]; [[John-07#v37|7:37-39]]; [[Jude-01#v19|Jude 19-21]], [[Jude-01#v24|24-25]]; [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2a]].

## II. "After my study of the Bible for the past sixty-nine years, what have I seen? I would say that I have seen the New Jerusalem. This is my vision, this is my revelation, and this is my ministry" (*Practical Points concerning Blending*, pp. 25-26):

### A. The New Jerusalem is not a physical city; it is the greatest and the ultimate sign in the Scriptures, signifying an organic constitution of the processed Triune God mingled with His regenerated, transformed, and glorified tripartite elect—[[Revelation-01#v1|Rev. 1:1]].

### B. God's eternal intention for us to become the New Jerusalem is seen in [[Genesis-01|Genesis 1—2]] as the organic blueprint, the architectural plan of the Triune God, and in [[Revelation-21|Revelation 21—22]] as the finished product, the organic masterpiece of the Triune God, for the full manifestation of His multifarious wisdom—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26]]; [[Genesis-02#v7|2:7-14]], [[Genesis-02#v22|22]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9-11]], [[Revelation-21#v18|18-21]]; [[Revelation-22#v17|22:17]]; [[Ephesians-03#v10|Eph. 3:10]].

### C. What is revealed in these two parts of the divine revelation in the Holy Scriptures is the central line of the divine revelation throughout the entire Holy Scriptures; this central line should be a controlling principle to our interpreting and understanding of the Holy Scriptures.
