# The Relationship Between the Churches, the Ministry, and the Work (1) (4-01)

## I. God's eternal economy is to obtain the Body of Christ, which involves the churches, the ministry, and the work—[[Ephesians-03#v3|Eph. 3:3-11]]; [[Ephesians-01#v22|1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-04#v1|4:1]], [[Ephesians-04#v4|4]], [[Ephesians-04#v16|16]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v4|1 Cor. 12:4-6]], [[1 Corinthians-12#v12|12-13]], [[1 Corinthians-12#v27|27]]:

### A. The churches, the ministry, and the work are all on the ground of the Body:

#### 1. The basic principle of the churches is the Body; the basic principle of the ministry is the Body; and the basic principle of the work is the Body.

#### 2. The churches are the Body expressed locally; the ministry is the Body in function; and the work is the Body seeking increase—[[Acts-13#v1|Acts 13:1-2]]; [[Acts-21#v19|21:19]].

#### 3. The church is the life of the Body in miniature; the ministry is the functioning of the Body in service; and the work is the reaching out of the Body in growth—[[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v27|12:27]]; [[1 Corinthians-16#v10|16:10]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]].

### B. The churches are the practical expression of the Body of Christ in various localities—[[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]], [[Revelation-01#v11|11]]:

#### 1. The Body of Christ is the source of the local churches—[[Romans-12#v4|Rom. 12:4-5]]; [[Romans-16#v1|16:1]], [[Romans-16#v4|4-5]].

#### 2. The one Body is the one church of God, manifested in many localities as many local churches—[[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18]]; [[Matthew-18#v17|18:17]]; [[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-02#v21|2:21-22]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v27|12:27]]:

##### a. The one universal church becomes the many local churches; God is expressed in Christ, Christ is expressed in the church, and the church is expressed in the local churches.

##### b. Every local church is a part of the unique Body, a local expression of the Body; therefore, a local church is not the Body but only a part of the Body, an expression of the unique Body—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-02#v21|2:21-22]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v27|12:27]].

### C. The ministry of the new covenant is for the producing of the Body of Christ—[[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v8|8-9]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v1|4:1]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v18|5:18]]:

#### 1. [[1 Corinthians-01|First Corinthians]] covers the Body of Christ, and [[2 Corinthians-01|Second Corinthians]] tells us that if we desire to have the Body of Christ, we must have the new covenant ministry—[[1 Corinthians-12#v12|1 Cor. 12:12-27]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v6|2 Cor. 3:6]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v8|8-9]].

#### 2. Without the new covenant ministry, there is no possibility to bring forth the Body of Christ.

#### 3. The sphere of the ministry is the Body of Christ, which may be expressed locally as a church, or extra-locally as the work—[[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]].

#### 4. What we have in the Lord's recovery is not one man's individual ministry but a corporate ministry of the Body—[[Ephesians-04#v11|vv. 11-13]].

### D. Our work in the Lord's recovery is the work of God's economy, the work of the Body of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-15#v58|1 Cor. 15:58]]; [[1 Corinthians-16#v10|16:10]]; [[Colossians-04#v11|Col. 4:11]]:

#### 1. The Body of Christ is organic and does not allow anything of human work, and the Body is not built up by using man's natural methods or the organization of human work—[[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|Col. 2:19]].

#### 2. Most of the work in today's Christianity is human work, is natural, and has nothing to do with the building up of the Body of Christ—[[1 Corinthians-03#v12|1 Cor. 3:12-16]].

#### 3. We may be very gifted and have a large capacity to work out something, but what we work out may be the same as worldly people carrying out a certain enterprise.

#### 4. In the Lord's recovery we should not have the thought that we can do a particular work according to our way; rather, we need to realize that in the Lord's recovery there is only one work—the work of the Body—[[Colossians-02#v19|Col. 2:19]].

## II. The relationship between the churches, the ministry, and the work:

### A. The churches, the ministry, and the work are quite different in function and sphere, but they are coordinated and interrelated—[[Acts-13#v1|Acts 13:1-4]]; [[Acts-20#v24|20:24]]; [[Acts-21#v19|21:19]]; [[Philippians-01#v5|Phil. 1:5]]; [[Philippians-04#v15|4:15-16]]:

#### 1. All three are manifestations of the one Body, so they are all interdependent and interrelated.

#### 2. None can move, or even exist, by itself; their relationship is so intimate and vital that none can be right by itself without being rightly adjusted to the others.

#### 3. They must move and act as one, for they are all in one Body, regardless of their specific functions and spheres.

### B. The church, the ministry, and the work derive their existence from, find their place in, and work for the good of the Body—[[Romans-12#v4|Rom. 12:4-5]]; [[Colossians-02#v19|Col. 2:19]]; [[Colossians-03#v15|3:15]]:

#### 1. The importance of this principle cannot be over-emphasized, for without it everything is man-made, not God-created.

#### 2. If this principle of relatedness to the Body and interrelatedness among its members is not recognized, there can be no church, no ministry, and no work.

## III. The Body must be the governing law of our church life, our ministry, and our work today.
