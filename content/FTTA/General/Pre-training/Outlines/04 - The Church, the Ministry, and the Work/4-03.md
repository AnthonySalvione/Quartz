# Further Fellowship on the Relationship Between the Churches, the Ministry, and the Work (4-03)

## I. We need to know the relationship between the work, or the ministry, and the church:

### A. The work, the ministry, is closely related to the church and entirely for the church yet still different from the church—[[Acts-13#v1|Acts 13:1-4a]].

### B. In the book of Acts we can clearly see that there are two things in parallel; one is the church, and the other is the ministry, the work.

### C. When those who carry out the work attend the church meetings, they act as members of the church; they are simply brothers in the church meetings, the same as the other brothers; as co-workers of the Lord, however, they are not of any particular local church.

### D. The ministry, the work, has much to do with the churches and is one hundred percent for the churches, yet it is not of the church; it is not under the control or direction of the church but is directly under the control of the Head, the Lord Jesus.

### E. The principle is the same with the churches; all the churches have much to do with the ministry and the apostles, but they are not under the hand or control of the apostles; they are directly under the headship of the Lord Jesus.

### F. The ministry does not control the local churches, and the churches do not control the ministry. Both the church and the ministry are directly under the control of the Head.

### G. It is the Lord's intention to keep human hands off the work and off the church:

#### 1. The Lord does not want to have His ministry under the hand of a local church:

##### a. It is not right for a church to hire someone to serve the Lord and send people to work for Him.

##### b. This puts the Lord's servants under the hand of men and almost makes them the servants of men.

#### 2. In the same way, the Lord intends to keep the local churches directly in His hands:

##### a. According to the first three chapters of Revelation, the Lord is the One who walks among the golden lampstands.

##### b. The Lord is the only One who supervises all the churches; all the churches are under His hand and headship.

### H. The biggest distinction we must learn in the Lord's service is the relationship between the ministry and the church:

#### 1. If something (a conference, training, gospel meeting, etc.) is being conducted by the church, it must be under the hand of the church; whatever is done must be under the direction of the church, not under the direction of the work (even if the co-workers are asked to participate in what the church is doing); the same principle applies when something is being conducted by the work.

#### 2. We need to be very clear about these two ways in order to maintain the church and the work under the direct control of the Lord's hand; otherwise, we may put the church under the hand of the work or put the work under the hand of the church.

#### 3. The service of the work is related to the service of the church, and the service of the church is related to the service of the work, but we cannot and should not confuse them; if we are serving the Lord in the local church, we must be under the local church, while if we serve the Lord in the ministry, we must be in the coordination of the work.

### I. In order to help us to serve the Lord properly, to know things properly, and to have proper relationships with all the Lord's children, we must know these matters and act accordingly as a good and orderly pattern to all the saints.

### J. We need to realize our part in the service of the church and of the work, stand in it, and keep ourselves in a proper order in the coordination:

#### 1. No human hand can arrange this; this can be carried out only by the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

#### 2. However, we need to learn all these principles so that we can continue to serve the Lord in a very proper way and understand others in a full way.

## II. The relationship between the work and the churches is this—the churches should always help the work and cooperate with the work to promote God's work, and the work should try the best to always strengthen the churches, edify the churches, establish the churches, and build up the churches for the building up of the Body of Christ; thus, the churches and the work should be very much in cooperation and coordination with each other:

### A. The apostles have spiritual but no official responsibility regarding the church, and the elders and the whole church have spiritual but no official responsibility regarding the work.

### B. A local church is under no official obligation to help the work; however, if the members of the church are spiritual, they will regard the work of God as their work, and they will count it a joy to help the work in any way.

### C. While the official responsibility for the work rests on the apostles, the spiritual responsibility is shared by all the children of God.

### D. If the local believers are spiritual, they will willingly receive help from the work; but if they are not spiritual and reject the help from the work, their failure is spiritual and not official, and the apostles have no option but to leave them to their own resources.

## III. To be one with the ministry (the work) is not to build up the ministry (the work). To be one with the ministry (the work) is to help the ministry (the work) to go on to further the Lord's move on this earth. Eventually, the ministry (the work) does not build up anything for the ministry (the work) itself. What the ministry (the work) builds up is the churches.

## IV. We need to pray concerning anything negative that rises up against the proper, organic relationship between the churches and the work, the ministry.
