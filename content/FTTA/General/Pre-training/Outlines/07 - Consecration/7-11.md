# Consecration (5): Consecration for the Kingdom of God (7-11)

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 Corinthians-06#v19|1 Cor. 6:19-20]]; [[Romans-14#v8|Rom. 14:8]]; [[Romans-12#v1|12:1]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v14|2 Cor. 5:14-15]]; [[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2]]

## I. The definition of consecration:

### A. Our consent to God's working in us and on us.

### B. Our consent to God's using us.

### C. Our consent to God's directing our ways.

## II. Aspects of consecration:

### A. The basis of consecration—God's purchase—[[1 Corinthians-06#v19|1 Cor. 6:19-20]]; [[Romans-14#v8|Rom. 14:8]].

### B. The motive of consecration—God's love—[[2 Corinthians-05#v14|2 Cor. 5:14-15]]; [[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]].

### C. The meaning of consecration—to be a sacrifice—[[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]]; [[Numbers-28#v2|Num. 28:2-3]].

### D. The purpose of consecration—to be used by God and to work for God—[[Ephesians-02#v10|Eph. 2:10]]; [[Isaiah-64#v8|Isa. 64:8]].

### E. The result of consecration—to abandon our future—[[Leviticus-01#v9|Lev. 1:9]].

## III. Consecration for the kingdom of God:

### A. "Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near"—[[Matthew-04#v17|Matt. 4:17]].

### B. "Unless one is born anew, he cannot see the kingdom of God"—[[John-03#v3|John 3:3]].

### C. "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God"—[[John-03#v5|John 3:5]].

### D. "Seek first His kingdom and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you"—[[Matthew-06#v33|Matt. 6:33]].

### E. "Unless your righteousness surpasses that of the scribes and Pharisees, you shall by no means enter into the kingdom of the heavens"—[[Matthew-05#v20|Matt. 5:20]].

### F. "Not everyone who says to Me, Lord, Lord, will enter into the kingdom of the heavens, but he who does the will of My Father who is in the heavens"—[[Matthew-07#v21|Matt. 7:21]].

### G. "Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens"—[[Matthew-05#v3|Matt. 5:3]].

### H. "Blessed are those who are persecuted for the sake of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens"—[[Matthew-05#v10|Matt. 5:10]].

### I. "The kingdom of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit"—[[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]].

### J. "Giving thanks to the Father, who...delivered us out of the authority of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son of His love"—[[Colossians-01#v12|Col. 1:12-13]].

### K. "The Lord will deliver me from every evil work and will save me into His heavenly kingdom"—[[2 Timothy-04#v18|2 Tim. 4:18]].

### L. "For in this way the entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly and bountifully supplied to you"—[[2 Peter-01#v11|2 Pet. 1:11]].

### M. "I John, your brother and fellow partaker in the tribulation and kingdom and endurance in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus"—[[Revelation-01#v9|Rev. 1:9]].

### N. "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of His Christ, and He will reign forever and ever"—[[Revelation-11#v15|Rev. 11:15]].
