# Consecration (3): Consecration for Constitution (7-07)

## I. The definition of consecration:

### A. Our consent to God's working in us and on us.

### B. Our consent to God's using us.

### C. Our consent to God's directing our ways.

## II. Aspects of consecration:

### A. The basis of consecration—God's purchase—[[1 Corinthians-06#v19|1 Cor. 6:19-20]]; [[Romans-14#v8|Rom. 14:8]].

### B. The motive of consecration—God's love—[[2 Corinthians-05#v14|2 Cor. 5:14-15]]; [[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]].

### C. The meaning of consecration—to be a sacrifice—[[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]]; [[Numbers-28#v2|Num. 28:2-3]].

### D. The purpose of consecration—to be used by God and to work for God—[[Ephesians-02#v10|Eph. 2:10]]; [[Isaiah-64#v8|Isa. 64:8]].

### E. The result of consecration—to abandon our future—[[Leviticus-01#v9|Lev. 1:9]].

## III. The essence of consecration for constitution—giving ourselves to the processed and consummated Triune God to be thoroughly constituted with Him for the fulfillment of His economy:

### A. To the Father as the unique source, origin, and Initiator of everything in God's economy.

### B. To the all-inclusive, pneumatic Christ as the centrality and universality of God's economy.

### C. To the compound, consummated life-giving Spirit as the application of God's economy.

## IV. Particular aspects of consecration for constitution:

### A. Consecrate to love the Lord, to appreciate Him, and to enjoy Him as the all-inclusive One.

### B. Consecrate to the Lord without reservation for His unhindered working to make His home in your heart.

### C. Consecrate to receive the divine dispensing.

### D. Consecrate to eat Christ as the tree of life and to drink of the Spirit as the water of life.

### E. Consecrate to reign in life by the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness.

### F. Consecrate to the Lord for the normal growth in life.

### G. Consecrate to be in the light and not under the authority of darkness in any way.

### H. Consecrate to let the word of Christ dwell in you richly.

### I. Consecrate to experience the cross, being conformed to Christ's death.

### J. Consecrate to allow the Lord to expand His kingdom within you.

### K. Consecrate to live the life of a God-man, living for the glory of God in the divine and mystical realm.

### L. Consecrate to Christ as the Head for the building up of the Body of Christ.

### M. Consecrate to take the New Jerusalem as your goal.

### N. Consecrate to be perfected according to the Lord's arrangement in the Body.

### O. Consecrate to receive the Lord's blessing.

### P. Consecrate to be intensified by the sevenfold intensified Spirit.
