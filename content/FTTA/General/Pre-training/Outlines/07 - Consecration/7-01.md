# Consecration (1): Consecration and a Divine Sense of Value (7-01)

## I. The definition of consecration:

### A. Our consent to God's working in us and on us.

### B. Our consent to God's using us.

### C. Our consent to God's directing our ways.

## II. The basis of consecration—God's purchase—[[1 Corinthians-06#v19|1 Cor. 6:19-20]]:

### A. True consecration rests on the realization of God's right to us, based on His purchase.

### B. The stability and security of our consecration.

## III. The motive of consecration—[[2 Corinthians-05#v14|2 Cor. 5:14-15]]; [[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]]:

### A. The motive of consecration refers to one's heart in consecration.

### B. Consecrating ourselves willingly to God because our feelings have been touched by the love of God.

### C. Constrained by the love of God to have a consecration full of sweetness and intensity.

## IV. The meaning of consecration—to be a sacrifice—[[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]]; [[Numbers-28#v2|Num. 28:2-3]]:

### A. Set apart for God and laid on the altar.

### B. A change in position and in use.

### C. Offering ourselves to God as a living sacrifice for God's satisfaction.

### D. Not compelled by God but an act of voluntary will.

### E. Being restricted by God's will and imprisoned in His hand.

## V. The purpose of consecration—to be used by God and to work for God—[[Ephesians-02#v10|Eph. 2:10]]; [[Isaiah-64#v8|Isa. 64:8]]:

### A. Letting God work in us and then working for God.

### B. The object of consecration: letting God work in us in order that we might reach the stage of working for God.

## VI. The result of consecration—to abandon our future—[[Leviticus-01#v9|Lev. 1:9]]:

### A. Abandoning our hopes and belonging wholly to God.

### B. Living purely and simply in the hand of God.

### C. Being what God wants us to be and doing what God wants us to do.

## VII. We need to have a change in our concept of value and cultivate a divine sense of value—[[Matthew-06#v32|Matt. 6:32-33]]; [[Matthew-16#v26|16:26]]; [[Hebrews-11#v24|Heb. 11:24-26]]; [[Philippians-03#v7|Phil. 3:7-8]]; [[Luke-16#v15|Luke 16:15b]]:

### A. If we bring out the precious from the worthless, we will be as the Lord's mouth—[[Jeremiah-15#v19|Jer. 15:19]].

### B. The Bible has much to say concerning a change in one's concept of value:

#### 1. "The stone which the builders rejected / Has become the head of the corner"—[[Psalm-118#v22|Psa. 118:22]]:

##### a. "This is the stone which was considered as nothing by you, the builders, which has become the head of the corner"—[[Acts-04#v11|Acts 4:11]].

##### b. "Coming to Him, a living stone, rejected by men but with God chosen and precious"—[[1 Peter-02#v4|1 Pet. 2:4]].

##### c. "To you therefore who believe is the preciousness"—[[1 Peter-02#v7|v. 7a]].

#### 2. "The kingdom of the heavens is like a merchant seeking fine pearls; and finding one pearl of great value, he went and sold all that he had and bought it"—[[Matthew-13#v45|Matt. 13:45-46]].

#### 3. "For all these things the Gentiles are anxiously seeking. For your heavenly Father knows that you need all these things. But seek first His kingdom and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you"—[[Matthew-06#v32|Matt. 6:32-33]].

#### 4. "He who loves father or mother above Me is not worthy of Me; and he who loves son or daughter above Me is not worthy of Me; and he who does not take his cross and follow after Me is not worthy of Me"—[[Matthew-10#v37|Matt. 10:37-38]].

#### 5. "What shall a man be profited if he gains the whole world, but forfeits his soul-life? Or what shall a man give in exchange for his soul-life?"—[[Matthew-16#v26|Matt. 16:26]].

#### 6. "The word of the cross is to those who are perishing foolishness, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God"—[[1 Corinthians-01#v18|1 Cor. 1:18]].

#### 7. "But what things were gains to me, these I have counted as loss on account of Christ. But moreover I also count all things to be loss on account of the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, on account of whom I have suffered the loss of all things and count them as refuse that I may gain Christ"—[[Philippians-03#v7|Phil. 3:7-8]].

#### 8. "Let your adorning not be the outward plaiting of hair and putting on of gold or clothing with garments, but the hidden man of the heart in the incorruptible adornment of a meek and quiet spirit, which is very costly in the sight of God"—[[1 Peter-03#v3|1 Pet. 3:3-4]].

#### 9. "I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my apportioned food"—[[Job-23#v12|Job 23:12b]].

#### 10. "If you return to the Almighty, you will be built up. / If you put injustice far away from your tents, / And place your gold nuggets in the dust / And your gold of Ophir in the stones of the brooks, / Then the Almighty will be your gold nuggets / And precious silver to you. / For then you will delight yourself in the Almighty, / And you will lift up your countenance to God. / You will pray to Him, and He will hear you; / And you will repay your vows. / You will decree something, and it will be established for you; / And light will shine on your ways"—[[Job-22#v23|Job 22:23-28]].

#### 11. "There is no one who has left house or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or fields for My sake and for the gospel's sake, but that he shall receive a hundred times as much now at this time, houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and fields, with persecutions, and in the coming age, eternal life"—[[Mark-10#v29|Mark 10:29-30]].

#### 12. "By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter, choosing rather to be ill-treated with the people of God than to have the temporary enjoyment of sin, considering the reproach of the Christ greater riches than the treasures of Egypt; for he looked away to the reward"—[[Hebrews-11#v24|Heb. 11:24-26]].
