# Being Trained in the High Peak of the Divine Revelation (2) (12-03)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Proverbs-29#v18|Prov. 29:18a]]; [[Acts-26#v19|Acts 26:19]]

## I. The high peak of the divine revelation—the "diamond" in the "box" of the Bible—is the revelation that in Christ God has become man in order that man might become God in life and nature but not in the Godhead—[[2 Samuel-07#v12|2 Sam. 7:12-14a]]; [[Romans-01#v3|Rom. 1:3-4]]; [[Ephesians-03#v17|Eph. 3:17a]]:

### A. "God becoming man and man becoming God" is the economy of God—[[1 Timothy-01#v4|1 Tim. 1:4]].

### B. God's eternal economy is to make man the same as He is in life and nature but not in the Godhead and to make Himself one with man and man one with Him, thus to be enlarged and expanded in His expression, that all His divine attributes may be expressed in human virtues—[[Ephesians-03#v9|Eph. 3:9]]; [[Ephesians-01#v10|1:10]]:

1. God's good pleasure is to be one with man and to make man the same as He is in life and nature but not in the Godhead—[[Ephesians-01#v5|vv. 5]], [[Ephesians-01#v9|9]].

2. God became man in order to have a mass reproduction of Himself and thereby to produce a new kind—the God-man kind—[[John-01#v1|John 1:1]], [[John-01#v14|14]]; [[John-12#v24|12:24]].

### C. For the fulfillment of God's economy, we need God to build Himself in Christ into us as our life, our nature, and our constitution to make us God in life and nature but not in the Godhead—[[Ephesians-03#v17|Eph. 3:17a]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]], [[Colossians-03#v10|10-11]].

### D. God became man through incarnation; man becomes God through regeneration, sanctification, renewing, transformation, conformation, and glorification—[[John-03#v5|John 3:5-6]]; [[John-01#v12|1:12-13]]; [[Romans-06#v19|Rom. 6:19]], [[Romans-06#v22|22]]; [[Romans-12#v2|12:2]]; [[Romans-08#v29|8:29-30]].

## II. It is only by God's becoming man to make man God that the Body of Christ can be produced; this point is the high peak of the vision given to us by God—[[Romans-01#v3|Rom. 1:3-4]]; [[Romans-08#v14|8:14]]; [[Romans-12#v4|12:4-5]]:

### A. God became man to make man God that He might produce the Body of Christ—the organism of the Triune God, the ultimate manifestation of which is the New Jerusalem—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Ephesians-04#v4|4:4-6]]; [[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9-10]].

### B. The Bible shows us how man can become God to have a God-man living and thus become an organism of God—[[Romans-01#v3|Rom. 1:3-4]]; [[Romans-12#v4|12:4-5]]:

1. This organism is God joining and mingling Himself with man to make God man and to make man God.

2. The issue of God becoming man and man becoming God is an organism; this organism is the Body of Christ—the union and mingling of God with man—[[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4-6]].

### C. God sent His Son to be a man and to live a God-man life by the divine life; such a living issues in a universal man that is exactly the same as He is—a corporate man living a God-man life by the divine life—[[Romans-08#v3|Rom. 8:3]]; [[Romans-12#v4|12:4-5]].

### D. The reality of the Body of Christ is the union and mingling of God with man to live out a corporate God-man—[[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4-6]], [[Ephesians-04#v24|24]].

## III. In order to enter into the high peak of the divine revelation, we need to be trained in the divine and mystical realm of the consummated Spirit and the pneumatic Christ:

### A. The divine and mystical realm is the realm where the consummated Spirit and the pneumatic Christ are one with the regenerated believers.

### B. We need to live in this realm by:

1. Exercising our mingled spirit.

2. Living according to the spirit.

3. Remaining in the organic union with the Lord.

4. Walking by the divine life instead of by the natural life.

### C. The more we live in this divine and mystical realm, the more we experience:

1. The organic salvation of God.

2. The dispensing of the Divine Trinity.

3. The reality of the Body of Christ.

4. The corporate God-man living.

## IV. The goal of the high peak of the divine revelation is the building up of Zion, the reality of the Body of Christ, consummating in the New Jerusalem:

### A. The overcomers are today's Zion within today's Jerusalem (the church life).

### B. Zion is:

1. The high peak.

2. The center.

3. The uplifting.

4. The strengthening.

5. The enriching.

6. The reality of the church.

### C. God's goal is to gain a corporate expression through a people who:

1. Live the God-man life.

2. Express God's attributes through human virtues.

3. Live in the divine and mystical realm.

4. Are built together as the reality of the Body of Christ.

5. Consummate in the New Jerusalem.

**Focus:** The high peak of the divine revelation is God's eternal economy—to become one with man so that man may become the same as He is in life and nature (but not in the Godhead), producing the Body of Christ as His organism and consummating in the New Jerusalem through the living of the corporate God-man.
