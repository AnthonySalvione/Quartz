# The Training of the Full-Timers (2-45)

## I. Learning how to contact the Lord:

### A. We must be trained on how to contact the Lord.

### B. Every day all of us must have a private time to pray to the Lord, to have a direct, private, personal contact with the Lord Himself.

### C. We must learn to have a secret life and a secret history with Him.

### D. In our contact we should not pray for so many things, but we need to open to Him to be enlightened, exposed, and judged.

### E. As we have some direct dealing with the Lord, we grow up in life.

## II. Getting into the Word:

### A. The best books to study are the Recovery Version with the notes and the Life-study messages.

### B. As we do this we get into the proper knowledge of God's New Testament revelation.

### C. We need to be trained to get into these materials to have ourselves equipped with the proper understanding of God's Word.

## III. Building up our character and capacity:

### A. In the book, *Character*, there are thirty aspects that we need to be trained in.

### B. If we do not build up our character, we could never read the Bible properly.

### C. A loose person in character could never understand the Bible in the right way.

### D. Although the training did not help us to obtain our capacity, it is helping us to grow in our capacity.

## IV. Fleeing youthful lusts:

### A. The trainees also need to be warned strictly and strongly not to be alone with a member of the opposite sex.

### B. Our contact with one another for the work and other necessary purposes.

### C. We must be very careful and be constantly warned.

### D. We have seen a good number of young people who had a good beginning get snared by youthful lusts.

### E. The unrestrained contact between males and females in this present age is terrible; it is an abomination in the eyes of God.

### F. The great amount of fornication in the United States frustrates the Lord's blessing in this country.

### G. We prohibit the young trainees from giving a ride alone to someone of the opposite sex; this will preserve us—see excerpt from **"Being a Trainee (10) The Precautions of the Full-timers."**

**Reference:** *Elders' Training, Book 8*, ch. 11.
