# The Purpose and Principle of Clothing (2-37)

## I. In order to understand the purpose and principle of clothing, we must go back to the very beginning—cf. [[Matthew-19#v8|Matt. 19:8]]:

### A. The first case of clothing in the Bible is with Adam and Eve:

#### 1. Before man fell into sin, clothing was not needed ([[Genesis-02#v25|Gen. 2:25]]), but when sin came in, the first effect on man was the realization of his own nakedness—[[Genesis-03#v7|3:7a]].

#### 2. Adam and Eve began to have the sense of sin and of shame, and they covered themselves with fig leaves ([[Genesis-03#v7|v. 7b]]), indicating that the basic significance of clothing is for covering man's body, which has been transmuted into the sinful flesh full of all kinds of lusts—[[Romans-07#v18|Rom. 7:18]], [[Romans-07#v23|23-24]]; [[Romans-13#v14|13:14]].

### B. Noah's failure was when he became drunk and took off his clothes and exposed himself—[[Genesis-09#v21|Gen. 9:21-23]].

### C. In the Old Testament the priests wore the priestly garments:

#### 1. Other than their hands, feet, and head, the priestly dress covered the entire body of the priests—[[Exodus-28#v2|Exo. 28:2]], [[Exodus-28#v4|4]], [[Exodus-28#v40|40-43]].

#### 2. When the priests went before God, they needed to apply the blood to the thumb of the hand, the toe of the foot, and the ear, indicating that the exposed areas had to be covered by the blood—[[Genesis-29#v20|29:20]].

#### 3. Furthermore, God commanded the Israelites to build an altar with no steps because He did not want their bodies to be exposed as they went up the steps to offer sacrifices, indicating that God abhors the principle of the exposing of man's flesh—[[Genesis-20#v26|20:26]].

### D. The principle of clothing is for covering, not for exposing; the principle of exposure is incompatible with the principle of a Christian.

## II. We need to see the New Testament principles related to clothing, especially related to the sisters' clothing:

### A. "Similarly, that women adorn themselves in proper clothing..."—[[1 Timothy-02#v9|1 Tim. 2:9a]]:

#### 1. Proper clothing denotes what is fitting to the sisters' nature and position as saints of God.

#### 2. The Greek word translated "clothing" implies deportment, demeanor; clothing is the main sign of a sister's demeanor, and it must befit her saintly position.

#### 3. Paul was wise in not going into detail concerning the matter of clothing and in using the word **proper**, a word deep in meaning and broad in definition:

##### a. Proper clothing does not appear peculiar to others but makes them feel comfortable with what we are wearing.

##### b. Proper clothing does not expose one's skin and the shape of one's body.

##### c. Proper clothing takes into consideration color and style.

##### d. Proper clothing also means to not dress slovenly.

### B. "...with modesty and sobriety..."—[[1 Timothy-02#v9|v. 9b]]:

#### 1. The Greek word rendered "modesty" literally means "shamefastness," that is, "bound or made fast by an honorable shame (Vincent)," implying not forward or overbold, but moderate, observing the proprieties of a woman.

#### 2. Sobriety denotes sober-mindedness, self-restraint, that is, to restrict oneself soberly and discreetly.

#### 3. The sisters in a local church should clothe themselves with these two virtues—shamefastness and self-restraint—as their demeanor.

### C. "...not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly clothing"—[[1 Timothy-02#v9|v. 9c]]:

#### 1. Women's hair was intended by God for their glory and a sign of their submission ([[1 Corinthians-11#v15|1 Cor. 11:15]]; [[Song-04#v1|S. S. 4:1]]; [[Song-06#v5|6:5]]; [[Song-07#v5|7:5]]) but was abused by many, especially by those who lived in the luxurious and corrupt life of the Roman Empire...to beautify their lustful flesh by its extravagant adornment with gold and other costly things.

#### 2. Christian sisters, as holy women, should absolutely abstain from this God-condemned practice.

### D. "Let your adorning not be the outward plaiting of hair and putting on of gold or clothing with garments, but the hidden man of the heart in the incorruptible adornment of a meek and quiet spirit, which is very costly in the sight of God"—[[1 Peter-03#v3|1 Pet. 3:3-4]].

#### 1. The sisters in the Lord should have as their adornment before God their inner being—the hidden man of their heart, which is adorned with a meek and quiet spirit.

#### 2. Our heart is composed of all the parts of our soul—mind, emotion, and will—along with the main part of our spirit—the conscience; among all these, our spirit is at the center; hence, our spirit is the hidden man of our heart.

#### 3. What is costly in the sight of God is the hidden man of the heart in the incorruptible adornment of a meek and quiet spirit; spiritual adornment is incorruptible, whereas physical adornment is corruptible.

## III. A believer needs to bring his or her clothes to the Lord and deal with them one by one:

### A. Much of today's clothing on earth, especially ladies' clothing, are plagued with "leprosy," that is, with sin—[[Leviticus-13#v47|Lev. 13:47-59]].

### B. If a believer does not know which clothes are plagued with leprosy, he should bring them to the Lord, the High Priest, and let the Lord examine them and tell him whether they are plagued.

### C. As soon as a person believes in the Lord, he should present everything in his wardrobe piece by piece to the Lord and pray over each item; he should not expect others to tell him what is good and what is not but should let the Lord tell him whether or not a certain item of clothing is appropriate for him.

### D. Some items of clothing have to be thrown away, while others must be altered; some garments cannot be altered at all.

## IV. Our clothing must be sealed with the mark of holiness; there should be the seal of the Holy Spirit, the seal of the anointing oil, on our clothing—[[Leviticus-08#v30|Lev. 8:30]].

## V. A believer's clothing must have the expression of heaven; it must not express the world—[[Numbers-15#v38|Num. 15:38]].
