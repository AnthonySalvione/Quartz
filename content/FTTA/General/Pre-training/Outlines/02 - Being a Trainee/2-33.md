# The Principle of Law versus the Principle of Faith (2-33)

## I. The principle of law is in contrast to the principle of faith—[[Galatians-03#v2|Gal. 3:2]], [[Galatians-03#v5|5]].

## II. Law—God's principle in dealing with man in His Old Testament economy:

### A. God's desire in creating man as a vessel was that man would receive the Lord Jesus as the tree of life—[[Genesis-01#v26|Gen. 1:26-27]]; [[Genesis-02#v7|2:7-9]]; [[John-14#v6|John 14:6]]; [[John-15#v1|15:1]].

### B. Instead, man ate the tree of the knowledge of good and evil and was condemned by God—[[Genesis-02#v9|Gen. 2:9]], [[Genesis-02#v17|17]]; [[Genesis-03#v1|3:1-6]]; [[Romans-05#v16|Rom. 5:16]]; [[Ephesians-02#v1|Eph. 2:1]].

### C. Still, fallen man did not know himself and continued to strive to do good to improve himself—[[Genesis-04#v2|Gen. 4:2-9]]; [[Exodus-19#v8|Exo. 19:8]].

### D. Therefore, God decreed the law as a portrait of Himself and a standard of good—[[Exodus-20#v1|Exo. 20:1-17]]; [[Exodus-31#v18|31:18]]; [[Psalm-19#v7|Psa. 19:7]]:

#### 1. To expose man of his inability to keep God's law.

#### 2. To cause him to know his sin—[[Romans-03#v20|Rom. 3:20]]; [[Romans-07#v7|7:7]].

### E. Those that are under the works of law are under a curse—[[Galatians-03#v10|Gal. 3:10]].

### F. The law is related to the flesh and depends on the efforts of the flesh, and the flesh inclines to keep the law—[[Romans-07#v5|Rom. 7:5]].

### G. A further function of the law is that it keeps man in custody ([[John-10#v1|John 10:1]], [[John-10#v16|16]]), serving to conduct man unto Christ—[[Galatians-03#v23|Gal. 3:23-24]].

## III. Faith—God's principle in dealing with man in His New Testament economy:

### A. In the New Testament, Christ's coming is the end of the law ([[Romans-10#v17|Rom. 10:17]]), and without faith, it is impossible to be well pleasing to Him—[[Hebrews-11#v6|Heb. 11:6]].

### B. This faith is the faith of Jesus Christ—[[Romans-03#v22|Rom. 3:22]]; [[Galatians-02#v16|Gal. 2:16]], [[Galatians-02#v20|20]]; [[Galatians-03#v22|3:22]]; [[Hebrews-12#v2|Heb. 12:2]]:

#### 1. On God's side, faith implies that God sent His Son to the earth, that Christ died on the cross to accomplish redemption, that He was buried and resurrected, and that in resurrection He became the life-giving Spirit; the object of our faith is the person of Christ and His redemptive work—[[John-01#v1|John 1:1]], [[John-01#v14|14]]; [[Ephesians-01#v7|Eph. 1:7]]; [[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45]].

#### 2. On our side, faith is related to hearing, appreciating, calling, receiving, accepting, joining, partaking, enjoying, rejoicing, thanking, praising, and overflowing; faith comes from hearing and hearing produces an appreciation of Christ—[[Romans-10#v17|Rom. 10:17]]; [[Galatians-03#v2|Gal. 3:2]]; cf. [[Acts-07#v2|Acts 7:2]]; [[Hebrews-11#v8|Heb. 11:8]].

### C. We receive the Spirit into our spirit through faith—[[Galatians-03#v2|Gal. 3:2]], [[Galatians-03#v5|5]]; [[2 Corinthians-03#v16|2 Cor. 3:16]], [[2 Corinthians-03#v18|18]]; [[2 Corinthians-04#v4|4:4]], [[2 Corinthians-04#v6|6]].

### D. Faith replaces law—[[Galatians-03#v23|Gal. 3:23]], [[Galatians-03#v25|25]].

### E. Faith brings us into the blessing promised to Abraham—[[Galatians-03#v9|Gal. 3:9]], [[Galatians-03#v14|14]].
