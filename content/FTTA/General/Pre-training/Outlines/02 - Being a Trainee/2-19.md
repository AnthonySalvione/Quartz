# Living the Kingdom Life as the Reality of the Church Life (2-19)

**Scripture Reading:** [[Romans-14#v17|Rom. 14:17]]; [[Matthew-16#v18|Matt. 16:18-19]]; [[Ephesians-02#v19|Eph. 2:19]]; [[Revelation-01#v9|Rev. 1:9]]; [[Hebrews-12#v11|Heb. 12:11]], [[Hebrews-12#v14|14a]]; [[Ephesians-04#v3|Eph. 4:3]]; [[Acts-13#v52|Acts 13:52]]

## I. [[Romans-14#v17|Romans 14:17]] is a strong proof that the church in the church age is the kingdom of God, because the context here deals with the church life in the present age.

## II. The church is a matter of grace and life, whereas the kingdom is a matter of exercise and discipline.

### A. In one sense, the church is God's family, God's home—[[Ephesians-02#v19|Eph. 2:19]]; [[1 Timothy-03#v15|1 Tim. 3:15]].

#### 1. In this home we enjoy grace and receive the life supply.

#### 2. In our family we have the enjoyment of love, the supply of grace, and the riches of life.

### B. In another sense, the church is also the kingdom.

#### 1. The word *kingdom* denotes ruling.

#### 2. In the kingdom we have rule, government, exercise, and discipline.

## III. [[Romans-14#v17|Romans 14:17]] shows us that the church in this age is the kingdom of God.

### A. Romans 12 speaks of the Body life; Romans 14 speaks of the kingdom life.

### B. This indicates that, in Romans, the kingdom life is the reality of the Body life.

### C. [[Romans-14#v17|Romans 14:17]] shows us that the kingdom of God is righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.

### D. When the authority of God's kingdom is allowed to operate in us, righteousness, peace, and joy will characterize our daily life.

### E. Righteousness, peace, and joy mean a great deal, for these items are the expression of Christ.

### F. When Christ is expressed, He is our righteousness toward ourselves, and our peace toward others, and our joy with God.

### G. As the believers who live in the kingdom life in the church, we must live righteously toward ourselves.

#### 1. Righteousness denotes that which is right and proper.

#### 2. Those who live in the kingdom of God should be right and proper toward others, toward things, and toward God.

#### 3. With us there should be nothing erroneous, improper, crooked, slanted, or biased.

#### 4. This requires that we be strict in dealing with ourselves and make no excuses for ourselves.

#### 5. Toward ourselves we must be strict and righteous in everything we do.

### H. Peace is the fruit of righteousness—[[Hebrews-12#v11|Heb. 12:11]] and footnote.

#### 1. Peace characterizes the relationship that those who live in the kingdom of God should have with others and with God.

#### 2. If we are righteous, right, and proper toward others, toward things, and toward God, we will have a peaceful relationship with others and with God.

#### 3. Toward others we must endeavor to pursue peace, continually seeking to be at peace with them—[[Hebrews-12#v14|Heb. 12:14a]]; [[Ephesians-04#v3|Eph. 4:3]]; [[Colossians-03#v15|Col. 3:15]].

#### 4. We must be careful to maintain peace with everyone related to us.

#### 5. Peace is actually Christ lived out from our being.

#### 6. Peace and righteousness are always related. If we do not have righteousness, we cannot have peace.

#### 7. If we are righteous toward ourselves, then we shall be at peace with others.

### I. Finally, living in the kingdom life in the church requires that we live joyfully to God in the Holy Spirit.

#### 1. The Holy Spirit is a Spirit of joy.

#### 2. If we are not joyful, this indicates that we are defeated and are not in the Holy Spirit.

#### 3. If we are truly living in the kingdom life, we shall be joyful with God, praising Him.

#### 4. If we lack joy in the Holy Spirit, something has gone wrong with us.

#### 5. This is a sign that we have cast off the divine restraint.

#### 6. As a result, our spirit is weighed down so that we cannot rejoice.

#### 7. However, if we live righteously toward ourselves and peacefully toward others, we shall live joyfully to God in the Holy Spirit.

#### 8. Then the kingdom will be manifested in our daily living in the church life.
