# The Precautions of the Full-Timers (2-29)

## I. Beware of:

### A. Ambition:

#### 1. Needing to have a pure heart, purified from any form of subtle ambition in intention, purpose, motive, and action in the Lord's recovery.

#### 2. Never hunting to be the first in any work for the Lord.

### B. Pride:

#### 1. Pride is an attribute of our fallen nature by birth.

#### 2. Even with Paul, the Lord was wary of his exceeding exaltation of himself, so He let him have a thorn in his flesh from Satan—[[2 Corinthians-12#v7|2 Cor. 12:7]].

#### 3. Always remember that humility saves you from all kinds of destruction and invites God's grace—[[James-04#v6|James 4:6]].

#### 4. Pride makes you a top fool.

#### 5. Caring for your prestige and neglecting others' dignity are a sign of subtle pride.

#### 6. Referring to your capacity, success, perfection, and virtue is a careless form of pride—[[Romans-12#v3|Rom. 12:3]].

#### 7. Arguing about who is greater is an ugly form of pride—[[Mark-09#v34|Mark 9:34]].

#### 8. Wanting to be great and not to be a servant and wanting to be the first and not to be a slave are also a sign of pride—[[Matthew-20#v26|Matt. 20:26-27]].

#### 9. Self-boasting, self-exaltation, self-glorification, and lusting after vainglory are all ugly and base expressions of pride—[[Galatians-05#v26|Gal. 5:26]].

### C. Self-justification:

#### 1. Self-justification indicates condemning of others and exalting of yourself.

#### 2. The Lord came not to condemn men but to save men by forgiving (forgetting) their sins—[[John-03#v17|John 3:17]].

### D. Rebellion and insubordination:

#### 1. God delights in us obeying His voice over burnt offerings and sacrifices—[[1 Samuel-15#v22|1 Sam. 15:22]].

#### 2. Rebellion is like the sin of divination and insubordination is like idolatry and teraphim—[[1 Samuel-15#v23|v. 23]].

### E. Not being conformed to the death of Christ—not denying (putting to death) your self, natural man, disposition by birth, flesh, preference, and ambition—[[Philippians-03#v10|Phil. 3:10b]]; [[Matthew-16#v24|Matt. 16:24]]; [[Galatians-02#v20|Gal. 2:20]]; [[Galatians-05#v24|5:24]].

### F. Not walking and having your being strictly according to the mingled spirit—[[Romans-08#v4|Rom. 8:4]].

### G. Not magnifying Christ by living Him through the bountiful supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ—[[Philippians-01#v19|Phil. 1:19-21]].

### H. Not working out your own salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who operates in you both the willing and the working for His good pleasure—[[Philippians-02#v12|Phil. 2:12-18]].

## II. Fleeing youthful lusts:

### A. The trainees also need to be warned strictly and strongly not to be alone with a member of the opposite sex.

### B. Our contact with one another for the work and other necessary purposes.

### C. We must be very careful and be constantly warned.

### D. We have seen a good number of young people who had a good beginning get snared by youthful lusts.

### E. The unrestrained contact between males and females in this present age is terrible; it is an abomination in the eyes of God.

### F. The great amount of fornication in the United States frustrates the Lord's blessing in this country.

### G. We prohibit the young trainees from giving a ride alone to someone of the opposite sex; this will preserve us (see excerpt).
