# Exercising unto Godliness (2-09)

## I. A trainee must have an aggressive spirit.

### A. An empty spirit.

**[[Matthew-05#v3|Matthew 5:3]]**  
Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.

### B. An exercised spirit.

**[[1 Timothy-04#v7|1 Timothy 4:7-8]]**  
But the profane and old-womanish myths refuse, and exercise yourself unto godliness. For bodily exercise is profitable for a little, but godliness is profitable for all things, having promise of the present life and of that which is to come.

### C. A pursuing spirit.

**[[Philippians-03#v13|Philippians 3:13-14]]**  
... Forgetting the things which are behind and stretching forward to the things which are before, I pursue toward the goal for the prize to which God in Christ Jesus has called me upward.

### D. A "right" spirit.

**[[Luke-09#v55|Luke 9:55]]**  
But turning, He rebuked them and said, You do not know of what kind of spirit you are.

## II. A trainee must have a single heart.

**[[Matthew-05#v8|Matthew 5:8]]**  
Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.

**[[Proverbs-04#v23|Proverbs 4:23]]**  
Keep your heart with all vigilance, / For from it are the issues of life.

### A. A renewed mind.

**[[Romans-12#v2|Romans 12:2]]**  
And do not be fashioned according to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of the mind that you may prove what the will of God is, that which is good and well pleasing and perfect.

### B. A regulated emotion.

**[[Matthew-10#v37|Matthew 10:37]]**  
He who loves father or mother above Me is not worthy of Me; and he who loves son or daughter above Me is not worthy of Me.

### C. A submissive will.

**[[John-07#v17|John 7:17]]**  
If anyone resolves to do His will, he will know concerning the teaching, whether it is of God or whether I speak from Myself.

### D. A good conscience.

**[[Acts-24#v16|Acts 24:16]]**  
...I also exercise myself to always have a conscience without offense toward God and men.

## III. A trainee must have a sanctified body.

**[[Romans-12#v1|Romans 12:1]]**  
I exhort you therefore, brothers, through the compassions of God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice.

**[[2 Timothy-02#v4|2 Timothy 2:4]]**  
No one serving as a soldier entangles himself with the affairs of this life, that he may please the one who enlisted him.

**[[2 Timothy-02#v22|2 Timothy 2:22]]**  
But flee youthful lusts, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.

**[[Romans-13#v14|Romans 13:14]]**  
But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh to fulfill its lusts.

## IV. A trainee must take grace.

**[[2 Corinthians-12#v9|2 Corinthians 12:9]]**  
And He has said to me, My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness. Most gladly therefore I will rather boast in my weaknesses that the power of Christ might tabernacle over me.

**[[1 Peter-05#v12|1 Peter 5:12]]**  
...I have written to you briefly, exhorting and testifying fully that this is the true grace of God; enter into this grace and stand in it.

**[[2 Timothy-04#v22|2 Timothy 4:22]]**  
The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.

**[[Hebrews-12#v15|Hebrews 12:15a]]**  
Looking carefully lest anyone fall away from the grace of God (see footnote 1).

**[[Hebrews-12#v28|Hebrews 12:28]]**  
Therefore receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us have grace, through which we may serve God well-pleasingly with piety and fear.
