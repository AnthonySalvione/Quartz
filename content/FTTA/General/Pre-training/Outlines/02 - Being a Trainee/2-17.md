# The Learning in the Full-Time Training (2-17)

## I. Pursuing the knowledge of the truth.

### A. Even if we know the way of the Lord's recovery clearly, we still need to know the truth and enter into the light that the Lord has given us.

### B. Our writings were produced by our standing on the shoulders of many who studied the Bible during the past two thousand years, plus what the Lord has shown us.

### C. You need to make an effort to study these truths in a deeper way.

## II. Pursuing progress in life.

### A. We must endeavor to know the Spirit, Christ, life, the cross, and resurrection and have the practical experience of all these things.

### B. To pursue the progress in life, you must study *The Knowledge of Life* and *The Experience of Life*, and practice what they teach regarding both the dealings in life and the learning in life; the *Truth Lessons*, which incorporate all the important points, also need to be studied.

### C. We must also be careful of our attempt to do "something special" or "be someone special in the training."

## III. Pursuing progress in our service.

### A. We have the highest truth and the practice that most agrees with the Bible; however, if we are ignorant, boastful, closed to the outside world, and do not pursue progress, then the Lord will not have a way among us.

### B. We hope all the trainees would clearly understand the way we are taking and also aggressively pursue progress in the matter of service.

## IV. Learning how to conduct yourself, how to do things, and how to work.

### A. Anyone who loves the Lord and is serving the Lord, even if he is not a full-timer, must be built up in his entire person.

### B. Paul told Timothy, "Let no one despise your youth, but be a pattern..." ([[1 Timothy-04#v12|1 Tim. 4:12]]); the conduct mentioned here includes the matter of our clothing and attire being tidy, proper, and dignified.

#### 1. We do not like to see the full-timers dressed slovenly.

#### 2. Sometimes our attire is often unseemly; the way we dress ourselves should be dignified, proper, tidy, and elegant; it should invite others' respect.

#### 3. As we are learning to serve the Lord, we should be proper, even in the way we walk into a meeting; we should not be loose, half-hearted, and without enthusiasm.

#### 4. We also need to learn how to speak to others properly. If we do not learn to speak with the right volume, speed, and expression, our preaching of the word will be unbearable to others.

### C. In building up ourselves, we must pay attention to the small, fine matters.

### D. Do not forget that we are doing a great business for the King of the heavenly kingdom and are representing Him on the earth.

### E. Even to do a proper campus work, there is a way and a technique. In [[Philippians-04#v12|Philippians 4:12]], Paul speaks of the "secret," referring to the key in doing things.

#### 1. We need the Spirit and the Word in preaching the gospel; we also need the content.

#### 2. The truths in the Lord's Word are very logical and are able to meet the real inner need of people.

#### 3. We must learn how to present these logical, high, and rich truths to people in a clear fashion. If we learn the way to contact people and the key in speaking to them, we will be able to touch people in their hearts and gain them right away; this requires much learning.

#### 4. We must also take care of things such as prayer, coordination, fellowship with the Lord, and not being individualistic.

## V. Building up our human character.

### A. In order for us to be efficient in doing things and in working, we must first build up ourselves in our human character.

### B. Do not wait until someone comes to supervise us; rather, take the initiative to learn here.

### C. When we do things in a serious way, results will be produced, and we will make progress.

### D. The degree of our pursuit of the truth and learning in life depends on the extent of our character training.

### E. We need to exercise our will to cooperate with the Lord that our character may be changed.

### F. There is a direct relationship between our character and our service in the coordination in the Body.

### G. We need to exercise ourselves in word and conduct and not let others despise our youth.

**Reference:** *Vessels Useful to the Lord*, chapters 3, 4, 5, 6, and 9.
