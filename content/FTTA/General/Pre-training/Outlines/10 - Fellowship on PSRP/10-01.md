# Fellowship on PSRP: The Practice of PSRP—Pray-reading, Studying, Reciting, and Prophesying (10-01)

## I. The Lord's new and up-to-date way to be constituted with the riches of His word for the gospel, nourishing, perfecting and prophesying is the practice of PSRP (pray-reading, studying, reciting, and prophesying):

### A. Just as the Lord has recovered the practice of calling on the Lord and pray-reading, He is now taking a further step by recovering the practice of PSRP.

### B. The Lord has recovered the interpretation of the revelation of the entire word of God through the Life-study and Crystallization-study of the Bible:

#### 1. This recovery of the interpreted word consummated in the high peak of the divine revelation.

#### 2. Although many saints have heard many messages on the interpreted word, they find it difficult to communicate the points of the truth to others.

#### 3. This shows we need a deeper and more thorough study of the interpreted word through PSRP that we could speak the truth to others.

#### 4. Philip's expounding of the word in [[Acts-08|Acts 8]].

### C. The Lord has also recovered the up-to-date way to practice the church life in the God-ordained way of begetting, nourishing, perfecting and building:

#### 1. Every step of the God-ordained way depends upon our ability to speak the truth to others.

#### 2. Prophesying is the peak of our speaking God's word and the key to overthrowing the clergy-laity system:

##### a. For years we have prophesied according to "inspiration and enlightenment" from the verses and text of *The Holy Word for Morning Revival*—this is good and profitable.

##### b. Now PSRP will equip us to also prophesy according to the truth of the points of the message—this will give people a fuller understanding of the truth of God's New Testament economy.

### D. We must all follow the churches and Brother Lee to practice PSRP:

#### 1. The saints in Taiwan initiated this practice.

#### 2. In 1996, Brother Lee adopted this way and instructed the churches to follow. "What I am speaking here is a kind of instruction to all the churches. My new way is PSRP. This is the way for us to cook and to eat the riches that have been released in these recent years" (*The Vital Groups*, pp. 142-143).

## II. How to practice PSRP:

### A. PSRP begins with pray-reading both the verses and the outline with exercise and enjoyment.

### B. Based upon our pray-reading we can then study the crucial points and words in each part of the outline:

#### 1. PSRP is outline driven to digest the crystals of the interpreted word.

#### 2. Every message, thus every outline, has the goal to expound the truth expressed in the title in a systematic way.

#### 3. In every outline there is a logical "line of thought" to expound the truth by "unpacking" the details, level by level.

#### 4. To see and understand the line of thought as well as the crucial terms are the keys to studying the truth.

#### 5. Outlines are also written "pyramid style" with the most important points on top.

#### 6. Verses strengthen and substantiate every important point.

### C. Once we have studied the outline and important verses we can spontaneously recite them to one another:

#### 1. To recite is not to give a word by word repetition of every point.

#### 2. Rather, to recite is to be able to repeat the line of thought with the meaning of the crucial terms.

#### 3. This kind of recitation is based on understanding, not pure memory.

#### 4. Reciting is a practice; perfection is not our goal, understanding is.

### D. When we understand a message in the way of truth, we can use that truth to preach the gospel, shepherd and perfect new ones, and to prophesy in the church meetings:

#### 1. We prophesy what we have learned to recite, we recite what we have studied, we study what we have pray-read.

#### 2. Prophesying according to the truth will give structure to and enhance the prophesying by inspiration and enlightenment.

#### 3. If we preach the gospel according to the truth, people will be more interested in what we have to say.

### E. As PSRP is a new practice in many ways it will need practice to learn how to best use it:

#### 1. You can PSRP alone, but a group of six to ten is the ideal size.

#### 2. At the beginning you will need help in the study portion to learn to see the line of thought as well as the crucial terms.

#### 3. The timing of each of the parts of PSRP is crucial so that a whole message can be completed. This also needs the guidance of some more experienced brothers at first.

#### 4. PSRP is not a way of "shortcuts." You need 60 to 90 minutes to adequately study one message.

#### 5. The reward is worth the time! Your understanding of the truth will be revolutionized!
