# Calling on the Lord and Pray-reading (3-01)

## I. Calling on the Lord:

### A. The definition of calling on the Lord:

#### 1. To call out, to cry or cry out, to invoke a person—[[Genesis-04#v26|Gen. 4:26]], meaning in Hebrew; cf. [[Acts-07#v59|Acts 7:59]].

#### 2. Spiritual breathing—[[Lamentations-03#v55|Lam. 3:55-56]].

#### 3. To cry and shout to Him—[[Isaiah-12#v3|Isa. 12:3-6]].

### B. The history of calling on the Lord—see footnote 1 of [[Acts-02#v21|Acts 2:21]].

### C. Practiced by the Old Testament saints:

#### 1. Began with the birth of Enosh, when men realized the vanity of human life, the fragility of man, and the need to live for and according to God—[[Genesis-04#v26|Gen. 4:26]].

#### 2. Abraham and Isaac both built an altar to Jehovah and called on the name of Jehovah—[[Genesis-12#v8|12:8]]; [[Genesis-26#v25|26:25]].

#### 3. Moses and the people of Israel all enjoyed the Lord's riches by calling on Him—[[Deuteronomy-04#v7|Deut. 4:7]]; [[Psalm-145#v18|Psa. 145:18]]; [[Psalm-81#v10|81:10]]; [[Psalm-116#v13|116:13]].

#### 4. The people of Israel enjoyed the Lord's forgiveness and mercy by calling on the Lord—[[Psalm-86#v5|Psa. 86:5]].

#### 5. David called on the Lord in his distress and trouble, as the Lord commanded—[[2 Samuel-22#v4|2 Sam. 22:4]], [[2 Samuel-22#v7|7]]; [[Psalm-116#v3|Psa. 116:3-4]]; [[Psalm-18#v6|18:6]]; [[Psalm-118#v5|118:5]]; [[Psalm-50#v15|50:15]].

#### 6. Isaiah told us to enjoy the Lord's salvation by praising, calling on Him, singing to Him, crying and shouting, and by seeking Him and calling upon Him—[[Isaiah-12#v2|Isa. 12:2-6]]; [[Isaiah-55#v1|55:1-6]].

#### 7. Jeremiah experienced that calling upon Him is his breathing and his cry—[[Lamentations-03#v55|Lam. 3:55-57]].

#### 8. It is even prophesied that people will call on the name of the Lord—[[Joel-02#v32|Joel 2:32]]; [[Zephaniah-03#v9|Zeph. 3:9]]; [[Zechariah-13#v9|Zech. 13:9]].

### D. Practiced by the New Testament saints:

#### 1. Taught by Peter—[[Acts-02#v21|Acts 2:21]].

#### 2. Practiced by Stephen and the believers—[[Acts-07#v59|7:59]]; [[Acts-09#v14|9:14]], [[Acts-09#v21|21]].

#### 3. Paul's experience of salvation—[[Acts-09#v5|9:5]]; [[Acts-22#v16|22:16]].

#### 4. Paul's teaching and practice:

##### a. We need to believe in our heart and confess with our mouth by calling upon the name of the Lord to be saved—[[Romans-10#v9|Rom. 10:9-10]], [[Romans-10#v13|13]].

##### b. As empty vessels, we need to call upon Him to be filled with the rich Lord—[[Romans-09#v23|9:23]]; [[Romans-10#v12|10:12]].

##### c. Paul equates the called saints to those who call upon the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, who enjoy Him as their portion—[[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]].

##### d. The governing principle of exercising our function in the Body of Christ is to speak to be in the Holy Spirit, particularly, by saying "Lord Jesus"—[[1 Corinthians-12#v1|12:1-3]].

##### e. We have been baptized in one Spirit into one Body and we are positioned in the Body to drink the Spirit continually by calling on the Lord and drawing water with joy from Him as the fountain of living water—[[Genesis-12#v13|v. 13]] and note 5.

##### f. We need to depart from unrighteousness by naming the name of the Lord to enjoy the divine life—[[2 Timothy-02#v19|2 Tim. 2:19]].

##### g. To seek to be a vessel unto honor, sanctified, useful to the Master, prepared unto every good work, we need to flee youthful lusts and pursue the Lord by calling on the Lord out of a pure heart with companions—[[2 Timothy-02#v21|vv. 21-22]].

### E. Calling on the Lord is one of the items in the recovery:

#### 1. Brother Witness Lee's experiences in 1931 and in 1943 as described in the *Life-study of Genesis*, Message 25.

#### 2. In 1967, this matter came forth in Los Angeles. He practiced and taught calling on the Lord. It is a new discovery and a part of the Lord's recovery. Thousands of saints have been released and enriched through calling on the name of the Lord.

## II. Pray-reading the Word:

### A. The Word of God is food to us:

#### 1. The milk of the word—[[1 Peter-02#v2|1 Pet. 2:2-3]].

#### 2. The bread of life—[[John-06#v35|John 6:35]], [[John-06#v57|57-58]], [[John-06#v63|63]].

#### 3. For us to eat and to be nourished—[[Matthew-04#v4|Matt. 4:4]]; [[Jeremiah-15#v16|Jer. 15:16]]; [[Ezekiel-03#v3|Ezek. 3:3]]; [[1 Timothy-04#v6|1 Tim. 4:6]]; [[Revelation-10#v9|Rev. 10:9-10]].

### B. The essence of the Word of God is the breath of God—[[2 Timothy-03#v16|2 Tim. 3:16]].

### C. Pray-reading the Word is to receive the Word by means of all prayer—[[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17-18]]:

#### 1. Calling on the Lord is a prayer; intermix "O Lord Jesus," "Praise the Lord!," "Hallelujah!," or "Amen" in your prayers with the Word.

#### 2. Exercise your spirit, not your mind, to breathe the Word as the breath of God by praying with the same words you read in short phrases.

#### 3. Your spirit will be full of feeling; pray according to the feeling in spirit and pray with the word to the Lord; in this way we digest the word as food. This kind of pray-reading will fully mingle the Lord's Word with our spirit (*The Organic Practice of the New Way*, pp. 47-48).

#### 4. Pray-reading came forth in Los Angeles around 1966 and has become one of the items in the recovery; thousands of saints have been nourished and have grown through pray-reading.

**Reference:** *Life-study of Genesis*, Message 25.
