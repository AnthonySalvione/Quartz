# The Life Cycle (15-01)

**Scripture Reading:** [[1 John-01#v1|1 John 1:1-9]]; [[1 John-02#v20|2:20]], [[1 John-02#v27|27]]

## I. The eternal life:

### A. The Gospel of John presents the eternal life to us and shows us how to receive it in a basic way—[[John-03#v16|John 3:16]], [[John-03#v36|36]]; cf. [[John-15|ch. 15]].

### B. This life is a wonderful Person—[[John-11#v25|11:25]]; [[John-14#v6|14:6]] (cf. *Hymns*, [[Hymn-499|#499]], [[Hymn-500|#500]]).

## II. The fellowship of life:

### A. First John continues John's Gospel in showing us the process of the eternal life; this life is not static (cf. [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1]]):

#### 1. We must see how this life proceeds in its cycle and that we must now practice to maintain this real, proceeding fellowship of life.

#### 2. We can only fully apprehend this life cycle by our experience.

### B. The source of the fellowship of life—[[1 John-01#v2|1 John 1:2-3]]:

#### 1. The eternal life is the source of, issues in, its fellowship: "...we...report to you the eternal life...that you also may have fellowship with us..." ([[1 John-01#v2|vv. 2-3]]).

#### 2. This can be illustrated by the power plant being the source of the electrical current; also, the throne of the "jasper" God in [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1]] is the source of the flowing river of water of life.

### C. The uniqueness of the fellowship of life—cf. [[Ephesians-04#v4|Eph. 4:4-6]]:

#### 1. There is only one life, one source, one Christ, one Spirit; thus, there can only be one genuine fellowship of life.

#### 2. This is true over time and space.

### D. The meaning of the fellowship of life:

#### 1. This fellowship is the flow of the eternal life; it is this life itself, and this life that we have received is constantly in motion, is moving all the time:

##### a. It is like the blood in our physical body constantly circulating; life is in the blood and the blood is life (cf. [[Leviticus-17#v11|Lev. 17:11]], [[Leviticus-17#v14|14]]; [[Deuteronomy-12#v23|Deut. 12:23]]); but the blood must keep flowing for us to stay alive physically and for all our body parts to remain organically connected and work properly.

##### b. It is also like the electricity that we use; the current must be switched on and kept flowing for the lights and appliances to function; these, in turn, in this electrical current are spontaneously and electrically connected to one another and to the power plant itself.

##### c. If there is a stoppage in the flow of blood or in the current of electricity, detachment, death, and darkness are the result.

#### 2. The fellowship, the flow, is of two aspects:

##### a. The vertical aspect—[[1 John-01#v3|1 John 1:3]], [[1 John-01#v6|6]]: "our fellowship is with the Father and with His Son Jesus Christ" ([[1 John-01#v3|v. 3]]); this fellowship with the Triune God must be first (i.e., the source) in our experience.

##### b. The horizontal aspect—[[1 John-01#v3|vv. 3]], [[1 John-01#v7|7]]: "you also may have fellowship with us" ([[1 John-01#v3|v. 3]])—i.e., with the apostles; "we have fellowship with one another" ([[1 John-01#v7|v. 7]])—i.e., with all the saints.

##### c. So, this fellowship, this flow, is in God, Christ, the apostles, and the saints.

##### d. The fellowship of life is not "social contact" (cf. a "fellowship hall" in denominational buildings); it is something much higher; we are joined to God and to one another in this vertically and horizontally flowing current of life, even mingled together as in weaving divinity with humanity.

### E. The means, the medium, of the fellowship of life—[[2 Corinthians-13#v14|2 Cor. 13:14]]:

#### 1. The Spirit, as the consummation of the Triune God, flowing, working, living, and acting within us is the means of the fellowship; He is the "carrier."

#### 2. The eternal life is the source, its fellowship is the flow, and the Spirit is the carrier; yet in our experience these three are just one.

### F. The location of the fellowship of life—[[Philippians-02#v1|Phil. 2:1]]:

#### 1. Our spirit is the location of the fellowship.

#### 2. The Spirit as the carrier is with our spirit as the location; hence, again, fellowship is neither "social" nor soulish.

### G. The function of the fellowship of life:

#### 1. The function of the fellowship is to supply us with all the riches of the divine life—cf. [[Revelation-22#v1|Rev. 22:1-2]] (the river conveys the tree of life, the supply).

#### 2. The fellowship, on the negative side, also carries away, discharges, eliminates many "germs" in our inner being.

#### 3. The fellowship, on the positive side, also urges us to pray, live for the Lord, and function in gatherings for the carrying out of God's economy:

##### a. The "into" of the springing-up, flowing water of life in [[John-04#v14|John 4:14]] implies a destination—the "eternal life"; and this, in turn, points to the New Jerusalem, the consummation of God's economy and the totality of the eternal life (see *Crystallization-study of the Gospel of John*, p. 139).

##### b. How we need to remain in this functioning fellowship!

### H. The issue of the fellowship of life—[[1 John-01#v5|1 John 1:5-7]]:

#### 1. The fellowship issues in genuine, organic oneness, in the church life itself, which is just us in the flow of the Lord Jesus; and this is very practical.

#### 2. The fellowship issues in our organic function, something resulting from an inner source, the life-flow deep within, just as the proper functioning of the parts of our physical bodies comes from the physical life within.

#### 3. The fellowship—not mere advice or instruction—solves all our problems and answers all our questions; conversely, all problems with us individually and corporately are related to not remaining in the fellowship of life; so we need to simply contact the Lord by turning (or returning) to, and remaining in, our mingled spirit (cf. [[1 Thessalonians-05#v16|1 Thes. 5:16-18]]).

#### 4. The main issue of the fellowship of life is that it brings in God as light—[[John-01#v4|John 1:4]]; [[John-08#v12|8:12]]; cf. [[Psalm-36#v8|Psa. 36:8b-9]].

## III. The light—[[1 John-01#v5|1 John 1:5-7]]:

### A. The fellowship of life brings in the divine light:

#### 1. The light is not a thing (e.g. a mere shining or even a mere presence); it is a Person, God Himself ([[1 John-01#v5|v. 5]]).

#### 2. According to this portion of the Bible, to walk in the light is to walk in God as light, that is, to be in His very essence; this is much more than to be just in His presence or to be just under His shining (cf. *Hymns*, [[Hymn-737|#737]]).

### B. In the divine light, as we are having the genuine fellowship of life, we have the immediate, spontaneous, and direct sense and realization of sins, wrongdoings, mistakes, trespasses, shortages; because we are still in the old nature and are still walking in the world, we become defiled, no matter how "careful" we are.

### C. As we allow the inner fellowship to bring us into God Himself as light, hidden things, blockages, offenses are fully exposed; we can unload them to gain more Christ for the growth and building up of the Body (cf. [[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16b]]).

### D. This unloading occurs when we spontaneously and immediately "confess" ([[1 John-01#v9|1 John 1:9]]), which is to agree with, to acknowledge, what we see in God as light—cf. [[Isaiah-06#v3|Isa. 6:3-5]].

## IV. The blood—[[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]], [[1 John-01#v9|9]]:

### A. At this point in the life cycle, having spontaneously and immediately confessed what we see in the divine light, the blood cleanses us:

#### 1. We do not need to beg or go through some kind of mental application.

#### 2. The blood is applied to us ([[1 John-01#v7|vv. 7]], [[1 John-01#v9|9]]) when, having the sense, the realization, of our dirtiness and sinfulness, we simply agree with God.

#### 3. So, according to these verses, actual and genuine cleansing occurs when we are in the fellowship of life and in the light (see *Life Messages*, pp. 71-72; *Basic Lessons on Life*, p. 81).

#### 4. We do not out-grow the blood; in the fellowship of life we always see that we are wrong, short, etc., and our prayer should reflect this realization.

### B. As we continue in the life cycle, over and over again, the Lord shines on, forgives, and cleanses our outward sins (actions):

#### 1. The Lord may take us back, cycle after cycle, six months, then one year, then perhaps even ten years, to catch us, check us, dig us, so that sins, stains, and blockages can be removed.

#### 2. Our relationship with God is unbreakable, yet our fellowship with Him can be interrupted; the former is of life, whereas the latter is based on our living, though it is also of life; one is unconditional, the other is conditional; our fellowship, which is conditional, needs to be maintained by the constant cleansing of the Lord's blood (see footnote 3 on [[1 John-01#v7|1 John 1:7]]).

#### 3. This cleansing will leave more and more room for Christ to grow in us!

## V. The anointing—[[1 John-02#v20|1 John 2:20]], [[1 John-02#v27|27]]:

### A. In the Bible, the anointing always follows the blood; in [[Leviticus-14#v14|Lev. 14:14-18]] the leper first had the blood, then the ointment applied for his cleansing and acceptance into God's presence; in [[1 John-01|1 John 1]] we have the blood and, following this, in [[1 John-02|1 John 2]] we have the anointing (cf. *Hymns*, [[Hymn-279#Stanza 1|#279, stanza 1]]).

### B. The anointing is the Triune God Himself, moving and adding Himself into us:

#### 1. He as the anointing Spirit is all-inclusive; this is typified in [[Exodus-30|Exo. 30]].

#### 2. His moving and adding Himself into us is as if He were "painting" us with Himself, with His very substance and element; we get more God, more Spirit, more life.

#### 3. We might say that this cleansing following anointing represents the continuation of the life cycle.

### C. The anointing adds more God, more life, into us and teaches us to "abide in Him" ([[1 John-02#v27|1 John 2:27]]), that is, to go on to be more in the fellowship of life (the abiding equals the fellowship); hence, the life cycle continues from more life to more fellowship, etc. (see *Practical Lessons on the Experience of Life*, p. 54).

### D. So, in the life cycle we firstly receive the divine life; this life brings its fellowship; this fellowship brings in light which is God Himself; in this light we confess and are cleansed by the blood; following this cleansing, the anointing comes, each time fresher, sweeter, stronger, more soothing; this anointing teaches us to abide in this living Person, that is, to remain in the fellowship—a little deeper each time; this deeper fellowship brings in stronger light; in the stronger light we have a keener sense and realization of our sins, confess, and are cleansed—more and more thoroughly; this cleansing, in turn, brings more God, more life, which equals more growth, which issues in more building up of the Body of Christ ([[Ephesians-04#v16|Eph. 4:16]]), which consummates in the New Jerusalem!
