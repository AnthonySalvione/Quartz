
By the mercy of God and through the work of the Holy Spirit, you have consecrated yourself to be a full-time trainee, choosing to give all your time to the pursuit of the truth and the service of the church. This is a serious decision. At the same time, this is a most glorious choice. In order to realize your desire, you have to go through spiritual training. Only by going through the proper training will you be perfected and molded to become a vessel fit for the Master's use.

The Training is different from the church life. In the church, everyone has full freedom to work or act according to his or her burden and preference; the church does not have any outward regulations. But the Training has its specific goals; they are to help you build up your character, mind, spiritual life, and knowledge of the truth. As such, there must necessarily be proper restraints. All these restraints are to help you to be disciplined in the proper direction. If you are to reap maximum benefits from the Training, you must willingly submit yourself to these restrictions. If you are not willing to be restricted, the Training will be of no use to you. You should know that the life of the Lord within you will supply you to meet all the outward requirements. Through these restrictions you will be led to the way of life. You must pray to the Lord that you would be able to meet all the requirements of the Training and that you would be brought to the proper experience of life through your obedience to the Training regulations.

According to the leading of the ministry and the principles of the Scriptures, we have laid down the following rules and regulations. We ask that you strictly adhere to every point of these rules and regulations. We pray that by such adherence the goal of the Training would be attained and you would reap the greatest benefits.
