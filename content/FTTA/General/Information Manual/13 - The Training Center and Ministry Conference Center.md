1. Exercise to keep the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center premises clean and tidy:
	1. Personal cubbies are provided for trainee use according to the following guidelines:
	    1. You will be assigned a cubby and bookend. You may only store books, notebooks, a laptop/other device, chargers), and a water bottle in your assigned cubby. Books should be kept upright in your cubby, not slanted or stacked horizontally. Large Bible may be stored horizontally.
	    
	    2. Cubbies must be kept in good order and be free from loose paper, food, decorations, pictures, trash, and clothing. Cubbies are subject to periodic inspections. Appropriate discipline will be assigned as needed.
	    
	    3. Unassigned cubbies should not be used. Items left in unassigned cubbies will be taken to lost and found.
	    
	    4. Unassigned bottom shelves may be used for storing bags, footstools, back supports, and lap desks. Items must be stored neatly and pushed in as far as possible so as not to extend into the walkway.
	    
	    5. Laptop charging stations should not be used for general laptop storage. Do not keep chargers plugged in when not in use.
	    
	2. Uniform and clothing items should be stored in the changing closets and should not be stored overnight.
	
	3. Guitars and other small instruments that can be used to facilitate the saints' singing should be stored in the guitar closet in the first-year classroom.
	
	4. Other than the cubbies, changing closets, and guitar closet, no personal items should be left unattended and/or improperly stored at the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center (e.g., drink containers on planters, jackets and sweaters on chairs, books on tables, electronic devices plugged into outlets).
	
	5. Do not leave your belongings unattended or lock a door to reserve a room or area.
	
	6. Leave the restrooms as clean as or cleaner than when you entered. Wipe off the counters with a paper towel. Clean out hair and debris from the sink and counter.
	
	7. Do not move or shift any furniture. Chairs in all areas should be pushed in and/or aligned when not in use. Return any room or area used to its original condition before leaving.
	
	8. You should always sit appropriately and never lie down while at the LSM Campus. Do not sit on any carpeted ledges.
	
	9. Be exercised to pick up any loose trash or misplaced items. Only throw away food in trash bins inside the kitchen and cafeteria. Lost and found items may be placed in the lost and found cabinets in the trainee commons. (Lost and found items will be removed from the cabinets and discarded or donated at regular intervals.)
	
	10. Food or drink, except water in a closed, quiet, and unbreakable container, is not permitted outside of the cafeteria, except with express permission from a Training assistant brother.

2. Do not enter areas of the Training Center or Ministry Conference Center not designated for the Training.

3.  Do not enter areas designated as off-limits (red placard) without express permission from a Training assistant brother. Mind areas designated brothers-only and sisters-only:
	1. Rooms marked with a blue placard are designated for brothers. Rooms marked with a yellow placard are designated for sisters.
	
	2. In the Training Center, the west stairwell is generally for sisters and the east stairwell is generally for brothers.
	
	3. The computers in the trainee commons are designated for brothers. The computers in the sisters' study lobby are designated for sisters.
	
	4. During study times, the trainee commons are designated for brothers. Couples wishing to study together in a quiet area may use the upstairs conference room and the library, although other trainees may continue to use the library to sign out or sign in books.

4. Pianos at the Ministry Conference Center may be used for practice for the purpose of accompanying singing during classes and meetings:
	1. Usage—only trainees on the piano service schedule may use the grand piano in the main classroom. The pianos in the first-year and second-year classrooms are open to all trainees. Priority is given to those trainees on the piano service assigned to play for classes that day or the following day.
	
	2. Availability—pianos may be used for practice during rest days, afternoon rest times, and dinner breaks; pianos may not be used for practice during Bible reading times, Lord's Day prophesying meeting preparation, classes, class breaks, study times, or meetings.
	
	3. Music—limit your practicing to hymns in the hymnal and supplementary binders located in the boxes next to the pianos.
	
	4. Care—take care of the pianos and the sheet music. Wash your hands prior to use. Keep food and drink off the pianos and piano benches. Push in the bench and close the cover on the keyboard when you are finished.
