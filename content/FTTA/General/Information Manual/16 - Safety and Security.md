
While we trust that the Lord will have mercy on us during your time in the Training, we also have to do our part to be exercised and cautious when it comes to our safety and security.

### General Safety Principles

1. You are responsible to keep your personal valuable items safe and discreetly placed. Every trainee is a security guard.

2. Always be vigilant, cognizant, and aware of your surroundings. Do not engage in any activity that may cause you to become unconscious of your surroundings.

3. Be on particular alert in the evenings. Trainees should never be alone outdoors at night, including on the LSM campus.

4. Sisters must walk in pairs or groups at all times. Make arrangements to ride with other trainees. Shuttle services for all trainees are provided.

5. Pedestrians have the right-of-way at all times, but do not assume vehicles or bicycles will automatically stop for you. Be extremely watchful of all vehicles from all directions. When crossing at intersections, always use the crosswalks. Never go against the traffic light. Never jaywalk. Only walk in the designated pedestrian areas in the parking lot.

6. Never prop open any exterior doors of the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center at any time. Make sure any door opened is closed tightly behind you, including at the residences and the pedestrian gates at Grace Gardens.

7. Do not block any door or doorway.

8. Do not give out any of the Training's access codes or your badges to anyone. Please refer anyone asking for access or access codes to the FTTA Office.

### Grace Gardens Delivery and Visitor Procedures

1. Argos security monitors the gate intercom 24/7 from both the Grace Gardens security office as well as from its command center at the LSM campus, and will provide access for all deliveries and visitors. There is no need to give out the vehicle gate code at any time.

2. All non-perishable deliveries must be delivered and picked up with Grace Gardens security:
    1. All deliveries must be made to the security office, not to trainee houses; trainees should take care not to use their house address when placing orders online or over the phone.
    
    2. Trainees should use the following address for all deliveries to Grace Gardens, putting the last two digits of their house as the “unit” number in place of ##:
        
        2104 W. Grace Ct., Unit ##  
        Anaheim, CA 92804
    
    3. If the shipper or delivery service provides an area for special delivery instructions, please enter a note that says: “Use gate intercom to request access for delivery and leave with security.” Do not give out the vehicle gate code at any time.
        
    4. The security officer will receive and keep delivered packages in the security office:
        1. Trainees will need to come to the security office to retrieve packages after they are delivered or when they next return to Grace Gardens.
        
        2. If the security officer is not present, it is likely because the officer is on their state mandated break, doing their patrols, or dealing with a security matter that has arisen; if so, the trainee should call Grace Gardens security at (714) 209-4921 to arrange for pick up.
    
3. Perishable deliveries (food and medicine only) require advance notice to Grace Gardens security and will be allowed directly to trainee housing:
    1. Perishable deliveries should use the trainee house street address for delivery.
    
    2. If the shipper provides an area for special delivery instructions, please enter a note that says: “Use gate intercom to request access for delivery.” Do not give out the vehicle gate code at any time.
    
    3. Trainees must register perishable deliveries with security before their arrival at Grace Gardens. Contact Grace Gardens security via email at [FTTA@argos.us](mailto:FTTA@argos.us) or via phone at (714) 209-4921 and provide:
        1. Trainee name, and if different, name used on the delivery order.
        
        2. Name of the delivery service.
        
        3. Approximate date and time of the delivery.
        
        4. The delivery/visitor registration code:
            1. The delivery/visitor registration code will be the Grace Gardens pedestrian gate code for the current term.
            
            2. This code is only for FTTA/APCC/LSM staff and those living at Grace Gardens to register their guests, so there is no need to give out this registration code at any time.
            
            3. Do not give out the delivery/visitor registration code at any time.
    
    4. Registered deliveries will be granted access by security and drivers will be directed to the house address.
    
    5. Unregistered deliveries will be refused by security.
    
4. All visitors to Grace Gardens must be registered with security in advance and will be verified upon arrival:
    1. This includes parents/other family members, saints, and anyone else that is not a trainee, FTTA/APCC/LSM staff, or part of the grounds keeping crew.
    
    2. Trainees must register visitors with security before their arrival at Grace Gardens.
    
    3. To register a visitor, trainees should contact Grace Gardens security in person at the security office in the garage of 2104 Grace, via email at [FTTA@argos.us](mailto:FTTA@argos.us), or via phone at (714) 209-4921 and provide:
        1. Name of trainee registering the visitors.
        
        2. Name of visitors to be placed on the visitor list.
        
        3. Approximate date and time the visitors will arrive at Grace Gardens.
        
        4. The delivery/visitor registration code:
            1. The delivery/visitor registration code will be the Grace Gardens pedestrian gate code for the current term.
            
            2. This code is only for FTTA/APCC/LSM staff and those living at Grace Gardens to register their guests, so there is no need to give out this registration code at any time.
            
            3. Do not give out the delivery/visitor registration code at any time.
        
    4. Anyone without a badge with gate access will be required to undergo verification by security, except those short-terming. Please inform your visitors to:
        1. Use the intercom to speak with security for access.
        
        2. Not to follow another car through the gate.
        
        3. To provide their name and the name of the trainee they are visiting.
        
        4. Do not give out the vehicle gate code at any time.
        
    5. Once verified and admitted to the property, visitors may make their way directly to the house of the trainee they are visiting.
    
    6. Short-term trainees and trainees who lose or forget their badge and forget the vehicle gate code (including those living in trainee housing outside of Grace Gardens) may request access simply by using the intercom and informing security that they are a current short-term or full-time trainee and providing their name. (Security will have a list of all those currently in the training either short-term or full-time.)
    
    7. Visitors who arrive at Grace Gardens and are not registered on the visitor list will not be admitted:
        1. Unregistered visitors will be directed by security to contact the FTTA office or the trainee they are visiting.
        
        2. Only when the FTTA office or a trainee places the visitor on the visitor list through the above procedure will the visitor be admitted to the property.
        
5. These measures are for the safety and security of all of the precious trainees as well as the dear serving saints living among them at Grace Gardens, so trainees must follow them closely.
    
6. As a reminder, the number for Argos Campus Security is (714) 209-4920. Argos Grace Gardens Security may be reached at (714) 209-4921 or via email at [FTTA@argos.us](mailto:FTTA@argos.us). All trainees must save these phone numbers and the email address in their phone.

### Badges, Keys, and Access

1. If you lose your badge, you must:
    1. Inform a Training assistant brother immediately that access can be rescinded.
    
    2. Fill out and submit the badge replacement form to the FTTA office.
    
    3. Pay a $10 badge replacement fee at the FTTA register.
    
2. You may not, at any time or for any reason, cut new keys to any Training-related buildings or vehicles without express permission from the Training office. If you lose a key to any Training-related buildings or vehicles, you must:
    1. Inform a Training assistant brother of the key that needs to be replaced.
    
    2. Pay a $5 key replacement fee at the FTTA register.
    
3. Direct visitors to the intercom during business hours. Visitors will be asked to sign in and given a visitor badge. Short-term trainees and visitors with badges may enter through trainee entrances when accompanied by full-time trainees.

### Emergencies and Reporting

1. If you observe an emergency or obvious criminal activity, immediately call 911. When calling, be prepared to give your full name, location, and a description of the activity.
    
2. If you observe any suspicious person, vehicle, or activity, call:
    1. Argos Security at (714) 209-4920 when at the Ministry Conference Center, the Training Center or at any training residence.
    
    2. Anaheim Police Department non-emergency number at (714) 765-1900 when at any other location in Anaheim.
