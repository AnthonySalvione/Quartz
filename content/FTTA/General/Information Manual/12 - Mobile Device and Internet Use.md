
The Training provides a place of outward separation; the regulations regarding the use of technology are given to promote inward separation from the worldly elements and engender a proper discernment regarding the use of technology for God’s purpose.

**The proper use of technology:**
1. Gives us a convenient way to charge forward, propagate, and open new frontiers and territories for the Lord’s move. In turn, it gives us no excuse for not accomplishing something for the Lord.

2. Is a “modern weapon” to give us the victory in our shepherding of others.

3. Is for the one new man. All the advances in civilization and technology have shrunk the earth for the one new man.

**The improper use of technology:**
1. Distracts us and causes us to waste precious time ([[Ephesians-05#v16|Eph. 5:16]]) in the sanctified environment of the Training where we should be occupied with spending time in the word and prayer. Using technology can be very “sticky”; once we are stuck to it, we cannot get away.

2. Opens a gateway to the world where Satan’s usurping hand keeps God’s chosen people from fulfilling His purpose. The world is not a source of enjoyment; it is a place of tyranny. Through the improper use of technology, Satan can rule over us like a cruel master.

3. Can become an idol that occupies and preoccupies us, keeping us from the full enjoyment of Christ.

4. Deadens us towards spiritual things, especially prayer.

## Detailed Usage Schedule:

1. **Not permitted**—mobile phones must be stowed at the following times, regardless of location:
    1. Rise and morning revival.
    
    2. Meals.
    
    3. Bible reading times. You may fellowship with a Training assistant brother to receive permission to call a gospel contact or young person for morning revival.
    
    4. Prophesying meeting preparation.
    
    5. Classes. **Must also be silenced so as not to disturb the class.** Other devices may only be used for note taking and accessing the Bible, hymns, and ministry books and resources.
    
    6. Study times. **May only be used for communication with FTTA serving ones.** Other devices may only be used for note taking and accessing the Bible, hymns, and ministry books and resources.
    
    7. Team fellowship times. **Must also be silenced so as not to disturb the fellowship.**
    
    8. House prayer.
    
    9. After house prayer/curfew until rise. Your mobile phone may be left out for charging but can only be used for alarm clock purposes.

2. **Permitted**—mobile phones may be used for phone calls, text messages, and permissible apps and websites at the following times, regardless of location:
    1. Personal grooming and cleaning until before breakfast.
    
    2. Class breaks.
    
    3. Rest times.
    
    4. Gospel work times.
    
    5. Church meetings (only to access hymns/songs, the Bible, or ministry books).
    
    6. **After dinner until the evening activity.**
    
    7. **After the evening activity until five minutes before house prayer/curfew.**
    
    8. During the 24-hour recess.
    
    9. In the event of a bona fide emergency.

3. As a courtesy to others, during the times when mobile phones may be used, please keep your mobile phones silent or on vibrate inside the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center, and take any audio or video call outside or in a private location in order to not disturb or distract others.

4. Do not text or message a trainee of the opposite gender alone (ensure at least one other person is included).

5. Any exception must be approved by a Training assistant brother. Trainees may be asked to make phone calls in the office.

## Domains:

1. **Not permitted**—the following types of apps and websites are not permitted at any time:
    1. Social media, except when expressly permitted by the Training for gospel purposes only and only if/as directed by the Training (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, X).
    
    2. Streaming or downloading audio and/or video:
        1. The only exceptions are what is specifically permitted below in point D.2.
        
        2. YouTube, TikTok, or similar apps/websites are never permitted for any purpose without prior fellowship and approval.
        
        3. Listening to secular audio (e.g., music, podcasts) and/or watching secular videos is not allowed at any time.
        
    3. Gaming.

2. **Permitted**—when mobile phone use is permitted by the schedule, the following types of apps and websites are permitted:
    1. Books for electronic ministry publications (e.g., iSilo, iBooks, Kindle, Google Play Books).
    
    2. Productivity and collaboration (e.g., Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, OneNote, Dropbox).
    
    3. E-mail, messaging, and video chat (e.g., GroupMe, WhatsApp, WeChat, Zoom).
    
    4. Mapping, navigation, and booking travel (e.g., Google Maps, Uber, Kayak).
    
    5. Church and ministry-related (e.g., all LSM domains, Bibles for America, Rhema, Shepherding Words, Hymns Radio).
    
    6. Finance (e.g., Bank of America, Google Pay, Venmo).
    
    7. Health and fitness (e.g., Kaiser Permanente, Fitbit).
    
    8. Language learning (e.g., Duolingo, Google Translate).
    
    9. Weather (e.g., Weather Underground).
    
    10. **Permitted during the 24-hour recess only:** Shopping and food delivery (e.g., Amazon, eBay, Uber Eats, Instacart)—**gate or door codes to the Training Center or Ministry Conference Center, Grace Gardens, or other training housing should never be given to any delivery drivers.**

3. Any exception must be approved by a Training assistant brother. Requests to use the wireless network outside of the 24-hour recess may be fellowshipped with a Training assistant brother and processed on the attendance server.
