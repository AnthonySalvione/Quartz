
1. Consecrate yourself first to the Lord ([[1 Corinthians-06#v20|1 Cor. 6:20]]; [[2 Corinthians-05#v14|2 Cor. 5:14-15]]; [[Romans-12#v1|Rom. 12:1]]) and then to the Training for the entire period of the Training, including the time between terms. Be willing to be trained and enlightened by the Lord. Be open to receive life and grace and to be regulated and perfected in every way ([[Romans-12#v2|Rom. 12:2]]; [[Matthew-05#v48|Matt. 5:48]]; [[2 Corinthians-07#v1|2 Cor. 7:1]]; [[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12]]; [[Hebrews-13#v20|Heb. 13:20-21]]).

2. You must take the preaching of the gospel of the kingdom in the whole inhabited earth as your goal ([[Matthew-24#v14|Matt. 24:14]]), the leading of the ministry as your direction ([[Acts-02#v42|Acts 2:42]]), and the practicing of the God-ordained way as your responsibility ([[Romans-15#v16|Rom. 15:16]]; [[John-21#v15|John 21:15-17]]; [[1 Thessalonians-02#v7|1 Thes. 2:7-12]]; [[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12]], [[Ephesians-04#v16|16]]; [[1 Corinthians-14#v4|1 Cor. 14:4b]], [[1 Corinthians-14#v31|31]]). You must be regulated in all your conduct by these burdens and must agree to drop all activities not in accord with these burdens during your time in the Training.

3. Practice being filled with the Spirit at all times ([[Ephesians-05#v18|Eph. 5:18b]]), using all the life practices of the ministry, such as calling ([[Romans-10#v12|Rom. 10:12]]), pray-reading ([[Ephesians-06#v17|Eph. 6:17-18]]), and praying ([[1 Thessalonians-05#v17|1 Thes. 5:17]]; [[Colossians-04#v2|Col. 4:2]]; [[Luke-18#v1|Luke 18:1]]; [[Ephesians-06#v18|Eph. 6:18]]; [[1 Timothy-02#v8|1 Tim. 2:8]]).

4. Subject yourself faithfully to the Training's testing and complete all assignments in truth study ([[3 John-01#v4|3 John 1:4]]), life practice ([[Galatians-05#v25|Gal. 5:25]]), outreach activities ([[Matthew-28#v19|Matt. 28:19-20]]), and service ([[Romans-12#v7|Rom. 12:7a]], [[Romans-12#v11|11]]).

5. Maintain proper separation between male and female. Walk worthily of the calling of a saint ([[Ephesians-04#v1|Eph. 4:1]]; [[Ephesians-05#v8|5:8]]; [[Colossians-01#v10|Col. 1:10a]]; [[1 Thessalonians-02#v12|1 Thes. 2:12]]):
	1. Give no special attention to any brother or sister. Many times trainees are too open, friendly, kidding, and natural with trainees of the opposite gender. Be polite and genuine when talking to others, but restrict fellowship to Training matters, the meetings, and the Lord.
	
	2. If you find that you have a distraction regarding a trainee, seek fellowship from the trainers, Training assistant brothers, or the serving saints assigned by the Training. Do not hide it; this makes it more difficult. Do not discuss your interest/distraction with your fellow trainees. While it may be normal and unavoidable to find yourself distracted by someone, getting outwardly involved with another trainee may result in dismissal from the Training.
	
	3. Do not engage in any activity alone with a member of the opposite gender, including any form of electronic communication. This includes the time between terms. Meet in groups of no less than three. Do not enter opposite gender areas, except for activities arranged or approved by the Training. There should be no mixed-gender gathers without the presence of a trainer, elder, or Training assistant brother, except for the meetings of the church.
	
	4. Brother Lee asked the saints in the churches not to contact the trainees for the purpose of courtship during the two years of training.

6. Speak only in a manner that promotes the purpose of the Training. Avoid any joking, banter, jesting, uncontrolled laughter, and unsanctified speaking ([[Matthew-12#v36|Matt. 12:36-37]]). Be sober ([[1 Timothy-03#v2|1 Tim. 3:2]]; [[Titus-01#v8|Titus 1:8]]; [[1 Peter-01#v13|1 Pet. 1:13]]; [[1 Peter-04#v7|4:7]]). Be grave ([[1 Timothy-02#v2|1 Tim. 2:2]]; [[1 Timothy-03#v2|3:2-4]], [[1 Timothy-03#v8|8a]]). Do not use profanity at any time. Remember that the Spirit has boundaries ([[Ephesians-04#v30|Eph. 4:30a]]). Pray and exercise the spirit much ([[1 Thessalonians-05#v16|1 Thes. 5:16-20]]; [[1 Timothy-04#v7|1 Tim. 4:7]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v6|2 Tim. 1:6-7]]).

7. Do not engage in arguments, complaints, or critical speaking about any person or matter ([[Ephesians-04#v29|Eph. 4:29-32]]; [[Matthew-07#v1|Matt. 7:1-5]]). Do not criticize other trainees for the way they may speak or function in classes or meetings unless they specifically request a critique from you. A critical attitude damages much more than it helps. Let the trainers care for these matters. "Do not despise prophecies" ([[1 Thessalonians-05#v20|1 Thes. 5:20]]).

8. Keep a cheerful and helpful attitude at all times ([[Acts-27#v22|Acts 27:22a]], [[Acts-27#v25|25a]]; [[Proverbs-15#v13|Prov. 15:13a]], [[Proverbs-15#v15|15b]]; [[Proverbs-17#v22|17:22a]]).

9. Practice honoring, respecting, and obeying the trainers, Training assistants, and serving ones in all situations ([[Leviticus-19#v32|Lev. 19:32]]; [[1 Timothy-05#v1|1 Tim. 5:1-2]], [[1 Timothy-05#v17|17-18]]).

10. Learn to fellowship with those in responsibility:
	1. Sisters should confine their fellowship regarding personal matters to serving sisters, TA sisters, and trainers, and brothers to TA brothers and trainers.
	
	2. Fellowship with the leading brothers and serving ones in your serving locality periodically throughout the term.
	
	3. Fellowship with the leading brothers in your sending locality periodically throughout the term regarding both spiritual and practical matters.

11. Periodically contacting family members, friends, and saints with updates and prayer burdens is encouraged. However, please do so under the restriction of the Spirit so as not to be unnecessarily distracted (this also applies to visiting those who live nearby).

12. All corporate and first-year classes are open to visitors. However, refrain from inviting visitors for meals and lodging.

13. Withdrawing from the Training is only permitted within the first three weeks of the term.

14. The Training has the right to dismiss you at any time.
