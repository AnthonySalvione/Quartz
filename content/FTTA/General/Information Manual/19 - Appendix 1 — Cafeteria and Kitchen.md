## Section One — Rules of Conduct

1. Pay attention to all signs and labels.

2. Do not come into the kitchen area unless you are on a kitchen service.

3. Good table manners should be observed at all times. This includes sitting properly, not slurping liquids, chewing with your mouth closed, not talking with food in your mouth, using a napkin, etc.

4. There are two tea bars, one for the brothers and one for the sisters:
    1. Items on the tea bars are available without limit all day. If an item runs out, please contact the kitchen office.
    
    2. Do not leave a microwave unattended while in use.

5. Trainees are free to take milk and juice from refrigerator #8 to be consumed in the cafeteria.

6. All common areas for the dispensing of food and drink should be kept clean and tidy. Return any condiment used from the food bars or tea bars to its original location.

7. Reusable tableware is reserved for meals with a cleanup service. Please place reusable tableware in the appropriate bins after these meals. Be mindful not to throw away reusable tableware. Do not remove reusable tableware from the cafeteria.

8. Disposable tableware is available during times without a meal cleanup service.

9. The tables, chairs, and floor around the eating areas must be cleaned and cleared before leaving. Align your chair. The last person eating must wipe down the table with a washcloth from the white bucket and then sanitize it using a washcloth from the red bucket. Do not leave personal belongings on the tables.

## Section Two — Meal Times

Much planning and forethought have gone into every aspect of the food service. Turkey products are used instead of pork, use of white sugar and flour is limited, etc.

1. All meals are mandatory:
    1. Exercise to be punctual. Meals must be taken within the prescribed time period shown on the Training schedule.
    
    2. Do not purchase or consume other meals in lieu of those provided by the Training.

2. Wash your hands before and after each meal. Hand sanitizer is available at the front of each food bar for extra germ protection, but it is not a replacement for hand washing.

3. Take what you can eat and eat what you take. Do not save food for another meal.

4. Meals served at the cafeteria are for trainees, trainers, and serving ones only. Gospel contacts, former trainees, family, or others may not eat meals served at the cafeteria, unless if approved by a Training assistant brother.

5. For breakfast, dinner, and Saturday lunch:
    1. Sit at your assigned table. Small fellowship tables are only to be used when having an appointment with a serving one.
    
    2. Exercise to pray with your seatmates while your table is awaiting dismissal to the food bar. Bible and ministry book reading are also encouraged to redeem the time.
    
    3. Always follow the direction of the meal usher when going to the food bars.
    
    4. Trainees on cleanup should be first in line regardless of dismissal.
    
    5. Mind the flow of traffic around the food bars.
    
    6. Once you pick up your plate, move quickly through the food bar in an orderly manner. The line will move much faster without talking and fellowshipping. Remember there are many others behind you.
    
    7. Follow all signs when posted indicating portion size. Do not exceed these numbers. Do not offer your portion to others.
    
    8. Second helpings may be available at the food bars only after everyone has been dismissed.
    
    9. Carryout containers may be used if you are not eating your meal in the cafeteria due to service or fellowship, or if you are packing a meal for a sick trainee:
        1. Carryout containers may be taken from the disposables rack.
        
        2. If you are packing a dinner for a sick trainee, you may go to the food bar after service has been dismissed. Please prepare any carryout containers before making your own plate. Write the trainee's name and house on the container, cover in plastic wrap, and place in the dinner delivery bin.

6. For sack lunch:
    1. Sack lunch items are available in the sack lunch area and on the food bars.
    
    2. Sack lunch items are only to be taken during lunch time.
    
    3. Contact the kitchen office if there are not enough sack lunch items available.

7. No meals are served either by the Training or by your serving locality for Lord's Day dinner, Monday breakfast, and Monday lunch.

## Section Three — Kitchen Services

Graduates now serving around the world have testified of the value of what they learned in serving in the kitchen. Their only regret was that they did not exercise to learn more. You are serving to 1) do a necessary task, 2) learn to coordinate with others, and 3) allow the Lord to expose your naturalness. Therefore, please come with a heart and mind to labor, taking every opportunity to learn as much as you can.

### Before Any Kitchen Service:

1. Place personal belongings on a chair at your assigned cafeteria table.

2. Sisters must tie their hair behind their shoulders.

3. Sign in, put on an apron designated for your service, and wash your hands in a hand sink according to the directions posted:
    1. Meal preparation: dark blue apron.
    
    2. Meal cleanup: white or vinyl apron. Meal cleanup stars use a green apron.
    
    3. Sack lunch: disposable apron. Sack lunch stars use a light blue apron.
    
4. If you are wearing a bandage for a minor cut, please use gloves during service.

5. Report to the person in charge.

### During Any Kitchen Service:

1. Always wash your hands in a hand sink according to the directions posted:
    1. After touching your face, hair, apron, or any other part of your person.
    
    2. Before starting a new task.
    
    3. Before putting on disposable gloves.
    
    4. After returning from a break.

2. To sneeze or cough, cover your mouth and nose with your hands, then immediately wash your hands in a hand sink according to the directions posted.

3. Time spent serving can be very profitable for fellowship, but it is not the time for Bible reading, verse memorization, or listening to personal electronic devices.

4. Those not serving are asked to refrain from lengthy conversation with those serving in the kitchen.

5. No drinking or eating is permitted in the food handling areas during service.

6. Keep work areas, counters, and carts clean and sanitized before and after each new task.

7. To avoid cross contamination of foods, use different utensils for each task.

8. Disposable gloves must be changed after every task.

9. Remove your apron any time you take a break from service.

### Meal Preparation Service:

1. You will be asked to do many things, some of which you probably have never done before. If you cannot do the task requested, you must let the person in charge know.

2. Do not attempt to operate any of the commercial equipment without instructions.

3. When working around the range, ovens, and tilt skillet, assume every pot, pan, and sheet pan is hot and handle them with oven mitts.

4. When uncovering pans of hot food, wear oven mitts in order to avoid steam burns. Be cautious of and keep distance from the venting steam.

5. Keep the oven and refrigerator doors closed.

6. In order to give a proper accounting of meals served, it is very important to count and record the number of plates before each meal on the cafeteria bulletin board.

### Meal Cleanup Service

1. The meal cleanup star trainees should be the first ones to report for cleanup. They are working managers keeping everyone on the team working efficiently. By exercising forethought toward completion of cleanup, the meal cleanup star trainees should be a pattern to fellow team members.

2. You generally have 20 minutes after the meal begins to sign in for cleanup.

3. After signing in, report to the meal cleanup star trainee with the green apron who will give you an assignment.

4. Read the posted directions before starting your assignment.

5. In order to preserve the life and appearance of kitchen utensils and equipment: 1) use green scrub pads or SOS pads for metal pans, 2) use nylon scrub pads for plastic items, stainless steel sinks, counters, and walls. In all cleaning the best principle is to soak and then scrub.

6. In order to give a proper accounting of meals served, it is very important to count and record the number of plates after each meal on the cafeteria bulletin board.

## Section Four — Allergies and Intolerances

1. Please contact the kitchen office concerning any updates to allergies, intolerances, or special dietary needs.

2. The kitchen provides alternative dietary items for wheat, lactose, and fish allergies and intolerances. Alternative dietary items are made available on the south kitchen counter.
