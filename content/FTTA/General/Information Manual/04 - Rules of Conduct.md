
The following rules of conduct are for the purposes of cultivating in you a noble and perfected appearance, a living that is according to the highest humanity, and a character that is fit for the Lord's use in order that God would be represented and expressed, as well as to care for your fellow trainees as members in the Body and maintain the corporate testimony of Jesus among all the trainees.

---
1. When attending classes, sit properly and according to seating assignments. Exercise in the meeting and cultivate a spirit of learning.

2. Participate willingly in the services. A printed schedule will be posted outlining all areas and times of service.

3. Be polite to everyone, especially roommates and teammates. "Please," "thank you," "I am sorry," and "forgive me" go a long way.

4. Do not form natural or particular relationships with any trainee. There should be no special friendships or cliques in the Lord's recovery. Talk to all the trainees. Do not be exclusive but be conscious of the Body of Christ.

5. Exercise to use and improve your English, as it is the primary language in the Training. Language cliques are discouraged; language learning is encouraged.

6. Keep a prayer diary and work record.

7. Be trained to live frugally and within your means, keeping a detailed record of your finances and carefully budgeting your income:
	1. Borrowing money, cars, and other personal items from trainees should not be practiced.
	
	2. Learn to give according to what you may have been prospered ([[Luke-06#v38|Luke 6:38]]; [[1 Corinthians-16#v2|1 Cor. 16:2]]).

8. Birthdays and anniversaries should not be the focus of any gathering or be celebrated in any way or at any time. Accept no invitations to baby showers, wedding showers, or other social events.

 9. The following items are not permitted by the Training:
	1. Smoking, alcohol, marijuana, illegal drug use, or pornographic material.
	
	2. Secular reading during the weekly Training schedule.
	
	3. Social media, except when permitted by the Training for gospel purposes.
	
	4. Periodical subscriptions.
	
	5. Listening to secular audio (e.g., music, podcasts) and/or watching secular videos.
	
	6. Electronic games, board games, card games, and games involving chance.
	
	7. Use of cameras at the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center premises and during any Training-related activity at any location, except in the principle of making a photocopy or with express permission from a Training assistant brother. If permission is obtained, please:
	     1. Ensure that photographs permitted have a proper testimony of the Lord and represent the high standard of the Training, engendering respect for the Lord, His purpose, and the Training.
	     
	    2. Be careful, conscientious, and judicious in sending or posting any pictures of trainees, trainee gatherings, the Ministry Conference Center or Training Center, training residences, etc. in any public forum, physical or digital.
	    
	8. The recording of classes and fellowships in either audio or video form.
	
	9. Gum chewing.
	
	10. Outside food and drink inside the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center.
	
	11. Gym memberships, except with express permission from a Training assistant brother.
	
	12. Corporate gift-giving.
