
*"Now these people (Bereans) were more noble than those in Thessalonica, for they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see whether these things were so."* ([[Acts-17#v11|Acts 17:11]]).

The primary goal of the training is to help the trainees to know the Triune God, Christ, the eternal life, the Spirit, the believers, the church, and the self. If the trainees enter into the depths of the Bible concerning these seven subjects, they will be useful to the Lord and will be qualified to serve and work for Him. (*The Collected Works of Witness Lee (1990), Vol. 2: Being Trained to Know the Depths of the Bible, p. 439*)

To serve the Lord is to minister the Lord, the Triune God, as everything from within the revelation of the holy Word with all the basic truths. This is the main thing. This is your capital... I am very much burdened for you all in this matter. (*Elders' Training, Book 02: The Vision of the Lord's Recovery, p. 76*)

We must not waste even a single minute, but rather study the Lord's word whenever we have time so that we may know the truth. (*The Economy of God and the Mystery of the Transmission of the Divine Trinity, p. 138*).

---

Maintain a spirit of learning and honor the studying atmosphere with your fellow trainees by following these guidelines:
1. You should view study time as an integral part of the Training. Studying the truth allows you to be constituted as a new covenant minister and to be able to dispense solid food to nourish others.

2. Spend some time to calculate your study hours each week, allocate an appropriate amount of time for each class, and make a schedule of when you will study for each class. Fill your study time schedule with personal study, corporate study, Bible reading, and scheduled breaks.

3. Incorporate the following resources into your studying: class outlines, notes, assigned class readings, and the Holy Bible Recovery Version with footnotes.

4. Study with a view to acquiring the crucial points and the bird's-eye view of each reading or lesson. Class outlines may help guide you in your study.

5. **Study time is for study only. Redeem all study times ([[Ephesians-05#v15|Eph. 5:15-16]]).**

6. Interactions with other trainees should be confined to studying, companion praying, reading class material, truth memorization, and prophesying.

7. There should be no letter writing or reading, no photo albums, no musical instruments, no video games, no electronic frivolities, no unnecessary talking, and no gatherings unrelated to truth study. Phones must be stowed.

8. Inside the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center, all areas during study times are considered to be quiet areas except for the cafeteria, in which group or team study is permitted. Please keep the noise level at a minimum. Honor the Lord, the atmosphere, and one another while using the study rooms in the Training Center; use the wisely, conscientiously, and with much exercise of the spirit.

9. There should be no lying down or lounging around. Use of personal outdoor furniture is not permitted.
