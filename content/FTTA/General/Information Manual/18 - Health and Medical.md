
## General:

1. Maintenance of good health is essential for the Training. Take every precaution in dress, diet, and schedule as prescribed by the Training to remain healthy. Take vitamins if needed. Get as much rest as possible. Get adequate exercise as much as possible (e.g., using your extra minutes walking, climbing stairs).

2. Take care regarding personal hygiene with emphasis upon frequent hand washing, especially before meals and after using the bathroom. Proper hand washing technique is crucial to prevent the spread of infection. Wash hands with warm, running water. Hands should be rubbed vigorously during washing for at least 20 seconds. Remember to wash the back of the hands, wrists, between the fingers, and under the fingernails.

3. Local pharmacies will provide most non-prescription medications (e.g., throat lozenges, headache tablets).

4. Please follow any additional health and/or medical recommendations provided by the Training in addition to those in the manual.

## Insurance:

1. All trainees must have health insurance.

2. __Know your primary care physician, nurse advice line, nearest urgent care center, and nearest emergency room covered by your health insurance before you get sick.

3. Schedule doctor's appointments for Mondays or during rest times is possible.

## Sickness:

1. If you experience a medical or psychiatric emergency requiring immediate medical care, call 911 or go to an urgent care center or emergency room.

2. If you experience an illness or injury impacting your participation in the Training schedule for longer than a few days, make an appointment with your primary care physician or go to an urgent care center.

3. If you need advice regarding medical care, call your health care provider's nurse advice line or schedule a telemedicine appointment on Monday or during rest times.

4. Fellowship with a Training assistant brother regarding any major health issues or health issues impacting your participation in the Training schedule for longer than a few days.

5. When you must rest due to sickness, we generally encourage you to consider missing Training activities in the following order: 1) serving locality activities (team or gospel/shepherding activities and/or church meetings), 2) study times, then 3) classes. While this is a general guideline, fellowship with the Training office and team overseer regarding your particular situation.

6. Rest until fully recovered and no longer contagious, especially if you have a fever or cough. Do not return to the Training schedule too quickly for the sake of the other trainees.

7. Do not participate in any kitchen service if you have a contagious illness. Find a substitute for your service.

## Diet and Nutrition

1. Drink at least eight glasses of water a day.

2. Keep to a minimum the consumption of food and drink not provided by the Training.

3. In order to avoid weight gain due to eating three regular meals a day: 1) establish a regular regimen of exercise, 2) reduce or eliminate added fat, 3) take smaller portions at meal times, 4) eat fruit for between-meal hunger pangs, and 5) be mindful of food intake outside of the Training-provided meals.

4. If you experience sudden unwanted weight loss, contact your primary care physician.
