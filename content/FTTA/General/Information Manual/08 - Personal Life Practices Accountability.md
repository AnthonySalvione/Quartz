
1. Morning Revival:
	1. Have morning revival for a **minimum** of 15 minutes per day, seven days per week.
	
	2. Have a goal to increase your morning revival time to 25 minutes per day, seven days per week.

2. Bible Reading Requirement:
	1. Read through the Bible for a **minimum** of 20 minutes per day, seven days per week.
	
	2. Have a goal to increase your daily Bible reading to 30 minutes per day, seven days per week.
	
	3. First and second-term trainees should set a goal to read through the entire text of the Old Testament and New Testament consecutively in their first year of the Training.
	
	4. Third and fourth-term trainees should set a goal to read through the entire New Testament Recovery Version with footnotes consecutively in their second year of the Training.

3. Personal Prayer:
	1. Pray personally for a **minimum** 10 minutes per day, seven days per week; it is recommended to schedule/have this time in the afternoon.
	
	2. Have a goal to increase your prayer time to 20 minutes per day, seven days per week.

4. Update your personal life practices accountability on the attendance server weekly.
