
1. To encourage maximum participation in Training classes, class notes are required if:
	1. You miss more than two sessions of any one class.
	
	2. You miss more than twenty total class sessions. If you miss more than twenty total class sessions, you will be subject to fellowship with the Training, which may lead to probation or dismissal from the Training.
	
	3. You miss a class or classes for a special leave of absence (e.g., weddings, graduate school, interviews).

2. Classes must be made up by:
	1. Listening to the recordings of missed classes:
		1. Recordings for classes may be streamed on the attendance server. Files are Training property and cannot be downloaded. Requests for special fellowships can be made by emailing office@ftta.org.
		
		2. Recordings for the ministry meeting may be purchased and downloaded at www.lsmwebcast.com.
		
		3. Saturday video classes must be made up by reading the relevant ministry portion.
		
		4. PSRP practice, Greek classes, and Character lessons do not need to be made up.
	
	2. Submitting a copy of your notes on the attendance server or to the "Class Notes" mail slot with an attached Class Notes Form:
		1. Class notes of the speaking must be from the **entire** audio recording and should be a minimum of 250 words.
		
		2. Class notes must be submitted within ten days after the class absence.

3. Class notes are to be written at the following times:
	1. For absences under twenty: During any rest time or the 24-hour recess from the Training schedule.
	
	2. For absences in excess of twenty: At 8:00 a.m. at the Training Center on consecutive Monday mornings until class notes for any absences in excess of twenty are completed.

4. **You are responsible to ensure the accuracy of any class notes assignments made on the attendance server and to complete any class notes assigned. Not doing so may result in discipline being assigned by the Training office.**
