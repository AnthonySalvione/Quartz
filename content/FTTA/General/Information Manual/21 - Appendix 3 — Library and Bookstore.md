## Library:

1. Signing out books—fill out the sign-out sheet on the library counter. Please be neat and complete when entering all the required information:
    1. Books should not be removed from the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center premises or kept overnight.
    
    2. Do not mark or highlight in any book.
    
    3. If you take a book from the bin, please mark the "from bin" column.
    
2. Signing in books—return books to the bin provided and fill out the appropriate section on the sign-out sheet:
    1. Books should be returned the same day that they are checked out.
    
    2. __Do not re-shelve books.
    
    3. Discipline may be assigned if books are not returned on the same day or if sign-in/sign-out procedures are not followed.

## Bookstore:

1. Trainee discount policy:
    1. Living Stream Ministry has graciously extended a significant discount on selected publications to current trainees. This is a privilege, not a right. We hope you will not take this for granted, but rather take full advantage of the opportunity to obtain these valuable publications — whether in hard copy or electronic form — for your personal edification and enlightenment. Our desire is that this would help deepen your appreciation for the riches and depth of God's divine revelation as unveiled in the Word through the ministry of the age.
    
    2. Discounts are primarily intended to build your personal library.
    
    3. 50% discount off the retail price (approximately 38% off the stickered price) of most LSM English softbound publications and booklets.
    
    4. Various discounts for most LSM English hardbound publications and publications in other languages. A list of current discounts is posted in the MCC bookstore.
    
    5. No discount on Bible covers, media purchases, and non-LSM publications (e.g., Affirmation & Critique, the Greek New Testament).
    
2. Discounts do not apply to:
    1. Short-term trainees.
    
    2. Purchases for friends, relatives, gospel contacts, or other saints. It is your responsibility to notify the cashiers about not applying the discounts to your purchase.
    
3. Book orders (complete sets or books that may not be in the bookstore):
    1. Fill out a Book Order Form.
    
    2. Pick up and pay for your order when notified. Orders must be picked up within one week of notification.
    
4. __All sales are final. No refunds. No exchanges.
