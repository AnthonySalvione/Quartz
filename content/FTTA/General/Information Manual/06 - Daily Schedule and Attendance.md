
# Section One — General

1. You must solemnly keep and abide by all Training schedules.

2. Every activity should be attended punctually. You may not be arbitrarily late to, absent from, or leave early from any activity. You are tardy within the first fifteen minutes and absent after fifteen minutes. Leaving early counts as a tardy. Any violation of the dress code counts as a uniform tardy.

3. All requests for leaves of absence must be substantial and be pre-approved by the Training.

4. During all scheduled activities at the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center, including Bible reading times, Lord's Day prophesying meeting preparation, study times, and the class breaks, you may not leave the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center premises without an approved leave of absence.

5. Take time to pray alone every day for at least ten to twenty minutes in addition to morning revival.

6. You are expected to sleep in the Training residence assigned to you.

7. All commuter trainees must attend all scheduled activities of the Training unless otherwise fellowshipped with a Training assistant brother.

## OTHER SCHEDULED ACTIVITIES:

1. Optional Morning Exercise:
	1. Tuesday through Saturday—5:30 a.m. to 6:00 a.m.
	
	2. Lord's Day—6:00 a.m. to 6:30 a.m.
	
	3. Do not disturb roommates or housemates when waking for, preparing for, departing for, and returning from exercise.
	
	4. Exercise in groups of no less than two of the same gender. Those who choose to exercise by walking may walk by themselves, but must be within close proximity to another trainee of the same gender.
	
	5. Trainees must be at their residences by the rise roll call.
	
	6. Locations:
		1. Grace Gardens and the Chalet house: brothers may exercise at Modjeska Park only; sisters may exercise there or inside Grace Gardens.

		3. Hall Apartments: brothers and sisters may exercise at the Ball Road meeting hall, Barton Park, or Modjeska Park.

2. Morning Revival:
	1. Tuesday through Saturday—6:00 a.m. to 6:30 a.m.
	
	2. Lord's Day—6:30 a.m. to 7:00 a.m.
	
	3. Spend one or two minutes to corporately call on the name of the Lord, then two or three minutes to straighten up your bed and put on appropriate attire.
	
	4. By 6:05 a.m. (6:35 a.m. on Lord's Day), you should be at your personal morning revival location. You may be inside your house, in the backyard, in your personal vehicle, or outside within Grace Gardens. Being horizontal or on your bed is not permitted.
	
	5. Prepare your attire, Bible, hymnal, prayer diary, and Holy Word for Morning Revival the evening before.
	
	6. Develop a habit of contacting the Lord to foster a personal, intimate, private, and affectionate relationship with the Lord during this time. Be released, but be considerate of others.
	
	7. Grooming, eating, exercising, studying, or listening to music or messages is not permitted.
	
	8. Houses should re-gather at 6:30 a.m. to briefly call on the name of the Lord before personal grooming and cleaning.

3. Bible Reading—7:55 a.m. to 8:25 a.m. Tuesday through Saturday:
	1. Use this time for further revival or Bible reading.
	
	2. On Wednesdays, there may be short times of morning revival fellowship from 8:00 a.m. to 8:15 a.m.

4. Prophesying Meeting Preparation and Bible Reading 8:15 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. on Lord's Day:
	1. The majority of this time should be used to compose and practice speaking your prophecy.
	
	2. Any remaining time should be used for further revival or Bible reading.

5. Service—at assigned time and location as noted on the service schedule or by service group:
	1. Service depends very much on the prompt attendance of every trainee scheduled. With each new schedule please put a reminder somewhere (e.g., in your prayer diary, on your calendar).
	
	2. Service sign—in-before service begins, check the box next to your name on the service sign-in sheet then report to the trainee overseer. If you are a substitute (i.e., serving on behalf of another trainee based on an earlier arrangement), you must 1) cross off the name of the originally scheduled trainee, 2) write your name beside it, and 3) check the box. If you are a volunteer (i.e., serving due to an assigned trainee's unexpected absence), you must write your name on one of the volunteer lines.
	
	3. Service switches—for unavoidable absences, you may switch services. Do not switch services due to preference. Do not switch services with a trainee of the opposite gender. Non-first-term trainees may not switch services with first-term trainees unless the first-term trainees have done the service before and are familiar with its responsibilities. Trainees with cars must switch only with other trainees with cars, unless transportation for the service is not required. If you will miss a service for any reason, other than an emergency, it is your responsibility to take the following steps to make sure your service is covered:
		1. Contact a minimum of five trainees to ask them if they can switch a service with you.
		
		2. Contact another trainee to help you contact a minimum of five additional trainees.
		
		3. As a last resort, contact a kitchen serving one for kitchen-related services or a Training assistant brother for other services.
	
	4. Loitering after service is not permitted. For Tuesday evening services, you must exercise to complete your service with enough time to attend the prayer meeting of your serving locality.

	7. Write letters/emails (e.g., updates to family and/or sending localities).

	9. Visit museums and other cultural places.

	11. House meals:
		1. With an approved trainer, helper, or Training assistant. Limited to three per term.
		
		2. House—only. Limited to two per term.
		
		3. Breakfasts on Lord's Day mornings should only take place in the house and also count against the limits on both house—only meals and those inviting others.
	
	12. Other wholesome activities.
	
	13. No television, movies, amusement parks, sporting events, or parties. Going to the beach (e.g., coast, ocean, seashore, tide pools) is not permitted at any time.

Team trips, coordinated night outs, house breakfasts, volunteering at Bibles for America, etc. are not excuses to miss the Monday night class or any other scheduled Training activity due to exhaustion.

	2. To request permission during non-business hours when the Training office is closed:

4. Redundancy—personal leaves of absence should not be requested:
	1. For any regularly scheduled Training-mandated service. By properly signing in on the service sign-in sheet, a leave of absence slip will automatically be generated for you. This includes any substitutes or volunteers.
	
	2. For group leaves of absence:
		1. For any team or house activity, including, but not limited to, team trips, coordinated night outs, and conferences, house coordinators and team monitors are responsible to submit a group leave of absence slip.
		
		2. For other group leaves of absence, coordinate with one another before submitting a group leave of absence slip, ensuring multiple leave of absence slips are not submitted
	
	3. To correct any clerical error. These should be addressed with your house coordinator, team monitor, or attendance monitor during attendance office hours.

5. Trainees' Responsibility:
	1. Keep a record of your own attendance (e.g., in your prayer diary, on your calendar). Be prepared to submit your personal attendance record upon request.
	
	2. Leave of absence slips should be submitted in advance of the leave, and must be submitted no later than 48 hours after the leave.
	
	3. Check your attendance record on the attendance server at least twice a week to ensure its accuracy. Make this a habit.
	
	4. Check the attendance server for any pending leave of absence slips requesting fellowship. Make this a habit. Fellowship with the Training office as soon as possible and before the next attendance office hours.
	
	5. Check the list of potential attendance violators posted on the main bulletin board the Wednesday of attendance office hours to ensure its accuracy. Make this a habit.
	
	6. If you are out of the Training schedule for seven days or more, you must notify the trainees in charge of the service schedule before the schedule is generated. If you are out of the Training schedule for less than seven days, you are responsible to find your own substitutes.
	
	7. If you are tardy for a roll call, you must notify your attendance monitor, house coordinator, or team monitor. Do not assume the tardy will be noticed. If you are tardy for class, inform the attendance monitor for your designated area after class.

6. If you have excessive leave of absence slips for any two-week attendance period, your name will be flagged for possible fellowship and/or discipline from the Training.

# Section Four - Attendance Office Hours

1. Attendance Office Hours—Wednesday afternoons every other week after the end of each two-week attendance period. This is the time to clear any attendance discrepancies with the attendance monitors. Trainees unable to attend attendance office hours should fellowship with a Training assistant brother. Check your attendance record on the attendance server before coming to attendance office hours. Be aware that leave of absence slips may not be processed until the day of attendance office hours. The study time guidelines should be observed; plan to bring study materials and read while waiting for your turn to talk to the attendance monitors. Be prepared to present any notes from class and your personal attendance record. After attendance office hours, the attendance records will be final and discipline will be assigned accordingly.

2. Perfect Attendance—no absences or tardies (excused or unexcused) during one of the set four-week attendance periods. Exceptions include: 1) conferences, 2) team trips, 3) house meals, 4) Training-mandated services, and 5) fellowship.

3. Attendance Violators—attendance violators will be disciplined in the manner outlined in the discipline guidelines every two weeks.

4. Attendance Monitors—the attendance monitors have no authority other than to follow the above rules. All due respect must be given to the attendance monitors. There should never be any questioning or contending regarding tardiness, excuses, etc. Do not contact the attendance monitors with attendance issues outside of attendance office hours, except for in-person notification of tardies immediately following the study roll call or class dismissal. All other questions should be fellowshipped with a Training assistant brother.
