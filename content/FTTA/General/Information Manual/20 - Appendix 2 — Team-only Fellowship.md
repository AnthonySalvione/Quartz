## Purpose:

1. To practice the vital groups by learning to pray for one another, your team, and the term. Everyone should exercise to join in the prayer and release your spirits together.

2. To foster a condition of love and an atmosphere of mutual care among the team members. Remember to maintain strict confidentiality of any personal matter opened up by your teammates; our opening up is not for gossiping or repeating but for our care and prayer for one another.

3. To cultivate our consciousness for corporate fruit-bearing and develop a habit of open fellowship to bring in the one accord.

4. To perfect our capacity in functioning with the members of the Body without a rigid structure.

## Direction:

1. Trainees should be prepared and come to the fellowship time ready to exercise and flow out.

2. The atmosphere should be one of openness and transparency so that the Lord can have the way to take care of problems and situations on the team through fellowship and interceding.

3. To preserve the atmosphere, having outside food and drink is not permitted.

4. The second-year trainee brothers should take the lead to pray, fellowship, and encourage the team to go on in the Lord.

## Suggested Ways to Use the Time:

1. Exercising the spirit to touch the Lord through prayer, pray-reading, singing, calling, and praising. This should be our primary focus — to touch the Lord together and to know and be related to one another in the spirit. Be sensitive to the leading of the Spirit when joining other teams for times of mutual enjoyment. Fellowshipping with the other team prior to joining is encouraged.

2. Fellowshipping with genuine and proper openness about personal situations, the team atmosphere, the team direction, and any practical situations. This is vital to maintain the loving atmosphere for a healthy and fruit-bearing vine.

3. Bringing names and specific prayer burdens, so that the team can pray in a corporate way. This is to increase the team members' consciousness of one another's burdens. This may also be taken care of in the way of gospel pairs.

4. Reading the ministry. In fellowship with the team trainer, the team may choose a portion of the ministry to read together.

5. If time allows, for the last 30 minutes:
    1. Gospel partner time, if no other weekly time is established.
    
    2. Prayer time with brothers and sisters separately.
    
    3. Reading the Word.
