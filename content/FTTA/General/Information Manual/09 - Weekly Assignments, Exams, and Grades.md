
1. Trainees are subject to the testing and weekly assignments of the Training, which includes mandatory midterm and final exams.

2. Unless otherwise indicated on the syllabus, the class grade is composed of weekly participation and assignments (50%), a midterm exam (25%), and a final exam (25%). A class score of 60% or above, unless otherwise designated by the trainers, is considered to be passing.

3. AI tools may not be used in the completion of any assignment or exam in the Training.

4. Trainees should not distribute or accept copies of any personal exam study guides.

5. Exams must be taken and submitted during the period designated for the exam.
	1. Exams may not be copied, redistributed, or removed from the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center premises. Photography or screenshotting of exam materials, including the exam and/or any study guides, is not permitted.
	
	2. Using the search or cut-and-paste functions for any electronic publication is not permitted for any exam, including open book exams.
	
	3. Not following instructions may result in a grade reduction.

6. Trainees who miss an exam due to sickness or an excused absence are required to fellowship with the designated Training assistant brother and make up the exam:
	1. Make-up sessions are held on the Monday immediately after the original designated exam period. Make-up exams must be picked up between 9:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m., and submitted no later than 4:00 p.m.
	
	2. Exams not taken during the designated exam period or during the designated make-up sessions will be assigned failing grades.

7. Deliberately missing or failing multiple exams or weekly assignments may result in needing to repeat the term or ineligibility to participate in Training activities.

8. Grade reports will be sent to trainees. Grade reports are also made available to sending localities, trainers, and team overseers.
