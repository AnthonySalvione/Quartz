
# Brothers:

A brother's appearance should be of the highest standard reflecting the One we represent to the world.
*"On behalf of Christ then we are ambassadors, as God entreats you through us; we beseech you on behalf of Christ, Be reconciled to God."* ([[2 Corinthians-05#v20|2 Cor. 5:20]]):

---

### General

1. Clothing must be kept neat, clean, and ironed.

2. Hair should be of a conservative style, should be kept combed, clean, and free from dandruff, and should not touch the ear or the collar ([[1 Corinthians-11#v14|1 Cor. 11:14]]).

3. Teeth should be brushed at least twice a day, including brushing the back of the tongue to keep breath free from offensive odors. Use dental floss regularly.

4. A brother should keep his face clean and shaven. Nose hair should be trimmed. Beards, mustaches, long sideburns, and shaved heads are not permitted.

5. The following are not permitted at any time, including Lord's Days, Mondays, rest times, and special leaves of absence:

	1. Tight or form-fitting clothing.
	
	2. Sheer or see-through clothing.
	
	3. Clothing with loose threads, missing buttons, tears, holes, etc.
	
	4. Denim or denim-like clothing.
	
	5. Leather or leather-like clothing (except for shoes and belts).
	
	6. Shirts with cufflinks.
	
	7. Clothing with secular wording or pictures.
	
	8. Ties with wrinkles or spots.
	
	9. Shorts (except within your personal quarters).
	
	10. Tank tops.
	
	11. Sandals.
	
	12. Socks with bright colors or loud patterns.
	
	13. Cologne (although moderate deodorants to deal with body odor are appropriate).
	
	14. Ornamentation (except wedding rings).
	
	15. Belts with fancy buckles.
	
	16. Hats, baseball caps, etc., or sunglasses (except as needed for warmth or sun protection outside).

### Uniform

1. FTTA-issued jacket—required for all classes except PSRP practice and Greek classes.

2. Dress shirt—solid white, full button-down, with long sleeves and a moderate collar. Must be tucked in and without wrinkles. Top buttons should always be buttoned and sleeves should not be rolled up, unless on service. A solid white undershirt must be worn under the dress shirt.

3. FTTA-issued tie.

4. FTTA-issued badge—worn visibly on the left lapel of the FTTA-issued jacket or dress shirt at all times. Badges may not be altered in any way.

5. FTTA-issued pants.

6. Belt—black.

7. Dress shoes— solid black, as would be appropriate with business attire. They should be kept unscuffed and in good condition.

8. Socks—full-length, black or dark blue.

9. Optional sweater or vest—may be worn over the dress shirt and FTTA-issued tie:

	1. During classes, only a knit sweater of a button or pullover style but without a hood and colored solid black, solid gray, solid dark blue, or solid white, without designs, patterns, or monograms, is permitted.
	
	2. For warmth, a proper jacket or coat which is colored solid black, solid gray, or solid dark blue, of a conservative style, and not displaying any conspicuous brands, logos, or emblems, is permitted when the uniform is required outside of the classes (e.g., during study times).
	
	3. Sweaters, jackets, or coats alone without the dress shirt and wearing sweatshirts is not permitted at any time.

10. Optional winter scarf—permitted for warmth. Must be solid black, solid gray, solid dark blue, or solid white without designs, patterns, or monograms.

11. On public formal occasions (e.g., Wednesday ministry meetings, conference meetings, graduation meetings, semi-annual training meetings), optional sweaters, vests, and scarves are not permitted.

### Gospel

1. Pants—beige, brown, dark blue, gray, or black and without any patterns.

2. Shirt—of a conservative style, full button-down with a collar. Must be tucked in.

3. Socks—without patterns, having colors that blend with attire.

4. Shoes—solid black or brown, of a conservative style, and not displaying any conspicuous brands, logos, or emblems. They should be kept clean and in good condition. They must have closed toes, sides, and heels. Sneakers are not permitted.

5. Belt—brown or black.

### Lord's Day/Prayer Meeting

In deciding whether or not to wear a certain article of clothing, we should consider whether or not it expresses godliness. (Crucial Principles for the Christian Life and the Church Life, p. 105)

1. Uniform attire is not required.

2. Dress slacks.

3. Dress shirt—white or solid conservative light color, full button-down, with long sleeves and a moderate collar. Must be tucked in.

4. Dress socks—full-length, without patterns, having colors that blend with attire.

5. Dress shoes.

6. Belt.

7. Tie—of a conservative color and pattern.

8. Badges should not be worn with Lord's Day/prayer meeting attire.

### Service

1. Uniform or gospel attire is permitted for kitchen service and during service week services. Shoes should be sturdy. Athletic shoes are permitted and preferred over leather-soled shoes.

2. All other weekly Training services must be performed in uniform attire unless otherwise specified.

### Exercise

1. Pants must be full in length.

2. Athletic shoes are permitted.

3. T-shirts or polo shirts are permitted.

4. Exercise attire can only be worn while exercising or traveling to and from exercising.

5. You may not wear exercise attire on the LSM campus (except during optional morning exercise).

### Monday

1. Uniform, Gospel, or Lord's Day attire is required until the recess ends at 4:00 p.m.

2. After 4:00 p.m., uniform attire is required.

# Sisters:

*"Similarly, that women adorn themselves in proper clothing with modesty and sobriety, not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly clothing, but, what befits women professing godly reverence, by good works."* ([[1 Timothy-02#v9|1 Tim. 2:9-10]])

### General

1. Clothing should be kept neat, clean, and ironed

2. Hair should be neat, clean, and free from dandruff. Shampoo hair several times per week. It should be of a simple style.

3. Teeth should be brushed at least twice a day, including brushing the back of the tongue to keep breath free from offensive odors. Use dental floss regularly.

4. Sisters should be appropriately dressed whenever they leave their bedroom. Maintenance brothers may need to enter the house at any time.

5. Sisters should wear skirts, even during the 24-hour recess.

6. The following are not permitted at any time, including Lord's Days, Mondays, rest times, and special leaves of absence:

	1. Tight or form-fitting clothing.
	
	2. Sheer or see-through clothing.
	
	3. Skirts shorter than 3" below the knee while standing or sitting.
	
	4. Skirts with slits beginning above the knees.
	
	5. Clothing with loose threads, missing buttons, tears, holes, etc.
	
	6. Tank tops or very short sleeves (i.e., more than 3" above the elbow).
	
	7. Cropped tops or blouses.
	
	8. Denim or denim-like clothing.
	
	9. Leather or leather-like clothing (except for shoes, belts, and handbags).
	
	10. Clothing with secular wording or pictures.
	
	11. Shorts (except within your personal quarters).
	
	12. Shoes with open toes, sides, or heels, or shoes with heels higher than 1-½".
	
	13. Boots (except for rain boots or boots for warmth when attending conferences with permission from the FTTA office).
	
	14. Leggings extending visibly below skirts.
	
	15. Make-up or nail polish (except concealer or foundation for blemishes).
	
	16. Dyed, highlighted, or streaked hair.
	
	17. Ornamental hair accessories. They should be functional, neutral-colored, and without bright colors or decorative elements.
	
	18. Perfumes or lotions with fragrances (although moderate deodorants to deal with body odor are appropriate).
	
	19. Jewelry or any other ornamentation (except wedding rings).
	
	20. Hats, baseball caps, etc., or sunglasses (except as needed for warmth or sun protection outside).
	
	21. Extravagant materials or embellishments (e.g., shiny textiles, sequins, loud colors, loud patterns).
	
	22. Pants (except during pre-approved gospel activities).

### Uniform

1. FTTA-issued jacket—required for all classes except PSRP practice and Greek classes.

2. FTTA-issued blouse—must be tucked in and without wrinkles. Second button from the top should always be buttoned. Camisoles or slips of white or natural color must be worn under the blouse. No polo shirts, turtlenecks, or t-shirts are permitted.

3. FTTA-issued badge—worn visibly on the right lapel of the FTTA-issued jacket or FTTA-issued blouse at all times. Badges may not be altered in any way.

4. FTTA-issued skirt.

5. Optional sweater or vest—may be worn over the uniform blouse:

	1. During classes, only the standard FTTA-issued uniform sweater or a knit sweater of a button or pullover style but without a hood, not form-fitting, and colored solid black, solid gray, solid dark blue, or solid white, without designs, patterns, or monograms, is permitted.
	
	2. For warmth, a proper jacket or coat which is not form-fitting, and colored solid black, solid gray, solid dark blue, or solid white, of a conservative style, and not displaying any conspicuous brands, logos, or emblems, is permitted when the uniform is required outside of the classes (e.g., during study times).
	
	3. Sweaters, jackets, or coats alone without the uniform blouse and wearing sweatshirts is not permitted at any time.

6. Dress shoes—solid black or dark blue, as would be appropriate with business attire. They should be kept unscuffed and in good condition. Socks should not be visible.

7. Optional winter scarf—permitted for warmth. Must be solid black, solid gray, solid dark blue, or solid white without designs, patterns, or monograms.

8. Optional nylons or tights—permitted for warmth. They should match your natural skin color and be free from holes and runs.

9. On public formal occasions (Wednesday ministry meetings, conference meetings, graduation meetings, semi-annual training meetings), the FTTA-issued sweater may be worn under the FTTA-issued jacket; other sweaters and vests are not permitted. Scarves are not permitted.

### Gospel

1. Skirt (or pants when pre-approved for specific gospel activities):

	1. Pants may only be worn on university campuses, at select young people's team activities, and select children's team activities and only when pre-approved by the Training
	
	2. When approved, dress pants, business casual slacks, or pants with a straight or boot cut are permitted. Pants must be ankle-length or longer.
	
	3. Workout pants, active wear, loungewear, or overly relaxed/casual pants (e.g., joggers, sweatpants, jersey/terry pants) are not permitted
	
	4. Sisters must change into skirts upon returning to the Living Stream Ministry (LSM) campus.

2. Blouse—-modest and not low cut (in front or back):

	1. Full button-down shirts, partial button-down shirts, and business casual tops are permitted
	
	2. Workout tops, active wear, loungewear, or overly relaxed/casual tops (e.g., sweatshirts, hoodies, t-shirts) are not permitted.

3. Shoes-dark or neutral color and not displaying any conspicuous brands, logos, or emblems. They must be kept clean and have closed toes, sides, and heels. Solid-colored canvas walking shoes are permitted. Athletic shoes are not permitted. Socks should not be visible.

### Lord's Day/Prayer Meeting

In deciding whether or not to wear a certain article of clothing, we should consider whether or not it expresses godliness. (Crucial Principles for the Christian Life and the Church Life, p. 105)

1. Uniform attire is not required

2. Dress or skirt—required.

3. Blouse—modest and not low cut (in front or back).

4. Dress shoes—dark or neutral color with closed toes, sides, and heels.

5. Lord's Day/prayer meeting attire should also be worn during any Training activities on Lord's Day afternoon.

6. Badges should not be worn with Lord's Day/prayer meeting attire.

### Service

1. Uniform attire or a collared button-down shirt is permitted for kitchen service and during service week services. Shoes should be sturdy. Athletic shoes are permitted and preferred over leather-soled shoes.

2. All other weekly Training services must be performed in uniform attire unless otherwise specified

### Exercise

1. Full-length pants are required. Form-fitting pants are not permitted.

2. Athletic shoes are permitted.

3. T-shirts or polo shirts are permitted.

4. Exercise attire can only be worn while exercising or traveling to and from exercising.

5. You may not wear exercise attire on the LSM campus (except during optional morning exercise).

### Monday

1. Uniform, Gospel, or Lord's Day attire is required until the recess ends at 4:00 p.m.

2. After 4:00 p.m., uniform attire is required.
