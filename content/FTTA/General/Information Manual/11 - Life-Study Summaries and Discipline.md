
God disciplines only those whom He loves. He disciplines us because He loves us. He disciplines us because He wants to make us vessels...Discipline is for our greatest profit and for bringing us to the loftiest goal in God's creation. *(The Collected Works of Watchman Nee (Set 3), Vol. 50: Messages for Building Up New Believers (3)*, p. 694)

"For what son is there whom the father does not discipline?" ([[Hebrews-12#v7|Heb. 12:7b]]). What we experience is God's discipline. Today all discipline comes to us because He deals with us as with sons. Please bear in mind that discipline is God's way of honoring us, not afflicting us... We have become God's sons; therefore, we must be disciplined. God disciplines us in order to bring us to the place of blessing and glory. (*The Collected Works of Watchman Nee (Set 3), Vol. 50: Messages for Building Up New Believers (3)*, p. 698)

Violations of the Training's rules and regulations may warrant discipline from the Training. The primary form of discipline in the Training entails reading and summarizing Life-study (LS) messages. Beginning with message one of the *Life-study of Genesis*, read the number of messages assigned to you and write a summary for each message. If you have previously begun or finished the *Life-study of Genesis*, you may continue where you left off in your Life-study reading. However, always go through the messages sequentially. Each Life-study summary should be no less than 250 words and take one hour to complete: 30 minutes for reading and 30 minutes for writing. Remember to complete these assignments in a spirit of prayer, applying what you read to your personal experience. Submit your Life-study summaries on the attendance server or to the "Life-study Summaries" mail slot with an attached Life-study Summary Form.

---

1. Types of discipline:
	1. *Monday offense*—report to the Training Center at 8:00 a.m. on Monday. Sign in on the main bulletin board. You should remain at the Training Center or Ministry Conference Center premises to read the corresponding number of Life-study messages and write the necessary summaries. You may not use your phone or the internet at the Training Center and Ministry Conference Center premises until your assignment has been completed. Discipline takes precedence over all other voluntary activities (e.g., Bibles for America, nights out, house meals)
	
	2. *Regular offense*—you have from the day of the offense until no later than 6:45 p.m. of the following Monday to turn in your assigned Life-study summaries.

2. Reasons for receiving discipline:
	1. Attendance—Monday offense, LS are assigned according to the following parameters;
		1. a. 2 LS of 250 words **each** for two unexcused absences or five unexcused tardies within a two-week attendance period
		
		2. b. 1 LS of 250 words for each additional unexcused absence or unexcused tardy within a two-week attendance period.
	
	2. Mobile phone and data/internet violations—all violations should be fellowshipped with the Lord first then a Training assistant brother; do not self-assign discipline. Intentional violations: Monday offense, 2 LS. Unintentional violations: regular offense, 1 LS, or as determined by the Training.
	
	3. Missed service—regular offense, 1 LS. Additionally, trainees who have unexcused missed services also need to make up these services with another service. Any make-up service should be indicated on the missed services sheet on the cafeteria bulletin board. Trainees who do not make up services may be volunteered for additional services.
	
	4. Speeding at the Living Stream Ministry campus or Grace Gardens—regular offense, 1 LS.
	
	5. Alarm noise during class—regular offense, 1 LS.
	
	6. House inspection—regular offense, 1 LS for each house member.
	
	7. Library violation—regular offense, 1 LS.
	
	8. Curfew violation—extent of discipline determined by the Training.
	
	9. Miscellaneous—extent of discipline determined by the Training.

3. Additional discipline for Monday offenses:
	1. Monday tardy—if you are tardy Monday morning (i.e., less than 15 minutes late), you must complete your current assignment plus 1 LS to be written on the same day.
	
	2. Monday absence—if you are absent Monday morning (i.e., absent or more than 15 minutes late), you must complete your current assignment plus 2 LS to be written on the following Monday.
	
	3. If you fail to submit an assignment in its entirety or at least four Life-study summaries when more than four Life-study summaries are assigned, you must complete your remaining assignment plus 1 LS to be written on the following Monday.

4. Special cases:
	1. Fellowship is encouraged.
	
	2. More than four Life-study summaries assigned as Monday offenses—stay and complete four Life-study summaries until 12:00 p.m. on Monday and then turn in your remaining Life-study summaries by the following Monday.
	
	3. Previous arrangements on Monday—Life-study summaries assigned as Monday offenses may be postponed only with **prior** fellowship and approval from a Training assistant brother.
	
	4. Sicknesses resulting in missing an entire Monday—call the Training office to report the sickness as well as notify the Training that you will not be able to report as planned. This must be done prior to 12:00 p.m. on Monday. Life-study summaries assigned as Monday offenses will be postponed to the following Monday.
	
	5. Difficulty with reading and/or writing—fellowship with a Training assistant brother concerning a modified assignment.

5. Repeat offenders and serious offenses:
	1. Those who repeatedly receive discipline will receive further fellowship from the Training.
	
	2. All offenses of a more serious nature will be reviewed by the trainers with much and thorough fellowship. These offenses can result in warnings, suspension, probation, or dismissal. Such offenses may include but are not restricted to abusing privileges associated with services, compromising security with juvenile frivolity, critical speaking, marring the Lord's testimony with use of cigarettes, alcohol, or other related items, and violating the consecration agreement regarding relationships with the opposite gender. In addition, physical intimidation, verbal threats of violence, or any other similar behavior may warrant immediate dismissal.

6. **You are responsible to ensure the accuracy of any discipline assignments made on the attendance server and to complete any discipline assigned.**
