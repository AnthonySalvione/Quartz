# The New Jerusalem—the Aggregate of the Result of God's Work of the New Creation in All the Dispensations within the Old Creation

## I. In the dispensation of the patriarchs on the patriarchs

### A. The dispensation of the patriarchs was from the creation of Adam to the giving of the law by Moses—[[Romans-05#v14|Rom. 5:14]]

### B. This was the dispensation before law; hence, it may be called the pre-law dispensation

### C. In this dispensation

#### 1. God first put the created man under His direct government that man might receive Him as life—[[Genesis-02#v8|Gen. 2:8-10]], [[Genesis-02#v16|16-17]]

#### 2. However, man became fallen; thus, God caused fallen man, the function of whose conscience had been activated by the knowledge of good and evil, to receive His redemption in Christ—[[Genesis-03#v1|Gen. 3:1-7]], [[Genesis-03#v10|10-11]], [[Genesis-03#v21|21]]

#### 3. God also caused man to be governed by his own conscience and to be acceptable to Him through sacrifices according to the way of redemption that He ordained—[[Genesis-04#v4|Gen. 4:4]]

#### 4. Moreover, He caused man to enjoy His riches by calling on His name—[[Genesis-04#v26|Gen. 4:26]]; [[Romans-10#v12|Rom. 10:12]]—so that he might walk with God—[[Genesis-05#v22|Gen. 5:22]]—and work with God—[[Genesis-06#v8|Gen. 6:8]], [[Genesis-06#v14|14-22]]

#### 5. Eventually, because man became repeatedly fallen—[[Genesis-06#v1|Gen. 6:1-7]]—God made fallen man subject to the authority which He gave to man, thus causing man to be ruled by man so that he might live and be preserved and that God might have the opportunity to visit man—[[Genesis-09#v6|Gen. 9:6]]; [[Romans-13#v1|Rom. 13:1-7]]; [[1 Timothy-02#v1|1 Tim. 2:1-4]]

#### 6. Finally, because man had become fallen to the uttermost—[[Genesis-11#v1|Gen. 11:1-4]]—God called the new race out of the Adamic race—[[Acts-07#v2|Acts 7:2-3]]; [[Genesis-12#v1|Gen. 12:1]]—and gave to this new race the promise of grace, leading them to hope in the Christ who would come to be a blessing to all the nations of the earth—[[Genesis-12#v2|Gen. 12:2-3]]; [[Genesis-15#v1|15:1-6]]; [[Genesis-17#v4|17:4-7]], [[Genesis-17#v19|19]], [[Genesis-17#v21|21]]; [[Genesis-18#v10|18:10]], [[Genesis-18#v14|14]]; [[Genesis-22#v17|22:17-18]]; [[Galatians-03#v16|Gal. 3:16]], [[Galatians-03#v14|14]]

### D. In this way God obtained the chosen patriarchs to be the first part of the new race in His new creation, signified by the crown of twelve stars worn on the head of the universal woman in Revelation 12, for the building of the New Jerusalem as God's expression in eternity future—[[Hebrews-11#v8|Heb. 11:8-10]], [[Hebrews-11#v13|13-16]], [[Hebrews-11#v38|38-40]] and footnote 3 on [[Hebrews-11#v40|v. 40]]; [[Genesis-12#v8|Gen. 12:8]] and footnote 2


## II. In the dispensation of the law on the children of Israel

### A. Because fallen man did not realize his impotence and his need of God's grace, God temporarily changed His way of doing His work of the new creation on the fallen man of the old creation—[[Exodus-19#v8|Exo. 19:8]] and footnote; [[Genesis-24#v3|24:3]]; [[Deuteronomy-05#v27|Deut. 5:27]]; cf. [[Romans-05#v20|Rom. 5:20]]

#### 1. God gave fallen man the law, which was established according to what God is—[[Exodus-20#v1|Exo. 20:1-17]]; [[Exodus-20#v22|22]]—[[Genesis-23#v19|23:19]]

#### 2. By doing this, He initiated another dispensation, the dispensation of law, which extended from Moses to the coming of Christ—[[John-01#v17|John 1:17]]

### B. God's intention in setting up the dispensation of the law according to His economy was to accomplish certain things through the law

#### 1. First, through the law God kept His chosen people in custody that they might satisfy the requirements of the law through the offerings, which typified Christ, and thereby be guarded, as sheep are guarded in a sheepfold—[[John-10#v1|John 10:1]], [[John-10#v7|7-9]]; [[Galatians-03#v23|Gal. 3:23]]

#### 2. Second, through the law God provided His chosen people a child-conductor that they might be led to Christ, that through the offerings they might be forgiven, and that through the temple they might enjoy God's riches—[[Galatians-03#v24|Gal. 3:24]]; see footnote 1 on [[Exodus-25#v1|Exo 25:1]]

#### 3. Third, through the law God caused His chosen people to have the knowledge of sin and of themselves and thereby realize and sense their need of God's grace—[[Romans-03#v20|Rom. 3:20]]; [[Romans-05#v20|5:20]]; [[Romans-07#v7|7:7-25]]

#### 4. As a result, God kept His chosen people in the custody of the law and brought Christ to the chosen people who waited for Him in the dispensation of law and to those who would believe into Him in the coming dispensation—[[Luke-02#v25|Luke 2:25-38]]

### C. In this way God obtained a group of people who are signified by the moon under the feet of the universal woman in Revelation 12, as the second part of the new race of the new creation, to constitute the New Jerusalem as God's eternal expression in the new heaven and new earth—[[Romans-02#v28|Rom. 2:28-29]]; [[Romans-09#v6|9:6-7]]; [[Galatians-03#v7|Gal. 3:7]], [[Galatians-03#v29|29]]

### D. While the old covenant of law was going on, God promised to make a new covenant of life (grace) with His people—[[Jeremiah-31#v31|Jer. 31:31-34]]; [[Ezekiel-36#v26|Ezek. 36:26-28]]—and that Christ would come to be the righteousness to God's people—[[Jeremiah-23#v6|Jer. 23:6]] and footnote 3


## III. In the dispensation of grace on the New Testament believers

### A. When, at the fullness of the time—[[Galatians-04#v4|Gal. 4:4-5]]—God determined that His purpose in decreeing the law had been attained and that the law had been used to its full extent, He became flesh, thus terminating the dispensation of law and inaugurating the dispensation of grace—[[John-01#v14|John 1:14]], [[John-01#v16|16-17]]

### B. At that time God again changed the way of His work of the new creation on the man of the old creation by replacing the demand of the law with the supply of grace—see footnote 1 on [[John-01#v17|John 1:17]]

### C. The dispensation of grace is from Christ's first coming to His second coming, which will mark the beginning of the millennial kingdom—[[John-01#v17|John 1:17]]; [[Revelation-19#v11|Rev. 19:11-13]]; [[Revelation-20#v4|20:4-6]]

### D. The dispensation of grace is also the dispensation of the mystery of the church—[[Ephesians-03#v4|Eph. 3:4-6]], [[Ephesians-03#v9|9-10]]; [[Ephesians-05#v32|5:32]]; [[1 Timothy-03#v16|1 Tim. 3:16]]

### E. In the dispensation of grace

#### 1. In inaugurating this dispensation, the Triune God became flesh in the Son, who was born of a woman to become Christ, possessing both the divine nature and the human nature and bringing grace and reality—[[Genesis-03#v15|Gen. 3:15]]; [[Galatians-04#v4|Gal. 4:4]]; [[Luke-02#v11|Luke 2:11]]; [[John-01#v1|John 1:1]], [[John-01#v14|14]], [[John-01#v17|17]]

#### 2. He passed through human life in humanity, experiencing every hardship and suffering, and He also lived out the Triune God in humanity—[[John-01#v18|John 1:18]]; [[John-14#v9|14:9]]; [[Hebrews-02#v18|Heb. 2:18]]; [[Hebrews-04#v15|4:15]]

#### 3. He passed through the death of the cross in humanity, accomplishing an all-inclusive death and releasing the divine life for multiplication and increase to constitute the Body of Christ, which is the church—[[John-19#v34|John 19:34]]; [[John-12#v24|12:24]]; [[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Colossians-01#v18|Col. 1:18]]

#### 4. Moreover, the Triune God was raised from among the dead with a resurrected body in humanity, that the only begotten Son might be born the firstborn Son in His humanity in resurrection—[[1 Corinthians-15#v4|1 Cor. 15:4]], [[1 Corinthians-15#v20|20]]; [[John-20#v17|John 20:17]]; [[Acts-13#v33|Acts 13:33]]; [[Romans-01#v3|Rom. 1:3-4]]; [[Romans-08#v29|8:29]]

#### 5. At the same time, those who believed in Christ were regenerated to become God's many sons—[[1 Peter-01#v3|1 Pet. 1:3]]; [[Hebrews-02#v10|Heb. 2:10]]

#### 6. This Christ, who was resurrected to become the life-giving Spirit, breathed Himself into those who believed in Him to be the reality of their spiritual life for their spiritual existence and living—[[1 Corinthians-15#v45|1 Cor. 15:45b]]; [[John-20#v22|John 20:22]]

#### 7. Then the resurrected and glorified One was exalted in ascension and was made both Lord and Christ to be a Leader and Savior, that He might give repentance and forgiveness of sins to God's chosen people—[[Acts-02#v33|Acts 2:33-36]]; [[Acts-05#v31|5:31]]

#### 8. Moreover, in ascension He poured Himself out as the consummated Spirit of the Triune God, baptizing those who believed and became His members in this consummated Spirit into one Body, that they might be His fullness to express Him—[[Acts-02#v4|Acts 2:4]]; [[Acts-10#v44|10:44-45]]; [[1 Corinthians-12#v13|1 Cor. 12:13]]; [[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]

#### 9. These matters include mainly the person and work of Christ and constitute the gospel of God to be preached to sinners, calling those who were chosen of God to believe, to be saved, and to be regenerated—[[Romans-01#v1|Rom. 1:1-6]], [[Romans-01#v15|15-17]]

#### 10. God then constitutes them to be the churches in different localities that the Body of Christ may be fully built up—[[Acts-08#v1|Acts 8:1]]; [[Acts-13#v1|13:1]]; [[Romans-16#v1|Rom. 16:1]]; [[1 Corinthians-01#v2|1 Cor. 1:2]]; [[2 Corinthians-08#v1|2 Cor. 8:1]]; [[Galatians-01#v2|Gal. 1:2]]; [[Revelation-01#v4|Rev. 1:4]], [[Revelation-01#v11|11]]; [[Ephesians-04#v12|Eph. 4:12]], [[Ephesians-04#v16|16]]

### F. In this way God obtains all those who believe in Christ in the New Testament as the third part and the majority of the new race in God's new creation, signified by the sun with which the universal woman in [[Revelation-12|Revelation 12]] is clothed, for the accomplishment of the constitution of the New Jerusalem to be God's ultimate and full expression in eternity.


## IV. In the dispensation of righteousness on the saved Israelites and on the rest of the New Testament believers not yet perfected

### A. After the conclusion of the dispensation of grace, God will change for the last time the way of His work of the new creation on the man of the old creation; He will set up His kingdom on earth—[[Revelation-11#v15|Rev. 11:15]]—to carry out His administration, and by His absolute righteousness He will bring to a conclusion the different kinds of people among the human race, that He may accomplish His work of the new creation in the old creation

### B. This dispensation is called the dispensation of the kingdom; in this dispensation righteousness will reign as a king; hence, this dispensation is also called the dispensation of righteousness—[[Isaiah-32#v1|Isa. 32:1]]; [[Jeremiah-23#v5|Jer. 23:5]]

### C. It will cover a period of one thousand years, beginning with the second coming of Christ and ending with the putting away of the old heaven and old earth, the old universe—[[Revelation-20#v4|Rev. 20:4-6]]; [[Revelation-21#v1|21:1]]; [[2 Peter-03#v10|2 Pet. 3:10-13]]

### D. In this dispensation

#### 1. God, according to His righteousness, will use the manifestation of the kingdom of the heavens to reward the righteous men perfected in the Old Testament and the overcoming believers in Christ in the New Testament that they may participate in Christ's kingship and joy—[[Hebrews-12#v23|Heb. 12:23c]]; [[Matthew-08#v11|Matt. 8:11]]; [[Matthew-25#v21|25:21]], [[Matthew-25#v23|23]]; [[1 Corinthians-03#v14|1 Cor. 3:14]]; [[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7]], [[Revelation-02#v11|11]], [[Revelation-02#v17|17]], [[Revelation-02#v26|26-28]]; [[Revelation-03#v5|3:5]], [[Revelation-03#v12|12]], [[Revelation-03#v21|21]]

#### 2. Moreover, according to His righteousness, God will perfect the immature believers in the New Testament, causing them to mature in life, and also will deal with all the Israelites who repent and believe in Christ at His coming back, causing them to become constituents of the new race in His new creation—cf. [[Ephesians-04#v13|Eph. 4:13]]; [[Colossians-01#v28|Col. 1:28]]; [[Zechariah-12#v10|Zech. 12:10]]; [[Romans-11#v26|Rom. 11:26-27]]

### E. Thus, in the dispensation of the kingdom, the dispensation of righteousness, which will be the last dispensation in the old heaven and old earth of the old creation, God will complete His work of the new creation on the man of the old creation, a work that will produce and perfect a new race

### F. The people of the new race (the sons of God—[[Revelation-21#v7|Rev. 21:7]]) will be the constituents of the New Jerusalem in the new heaven and new earth in eternity; they are the object that God intends to obtain in His work both of the old creation and of the new creation throughout the generations for the fulfillment of His economy

### G. Therefore, as the aggregate of the result of God's work of the new creation in all the dispensations within the old creation, the processed Triune God and the transformed tripartite man will be mingled as one to be a mutual dwelling place for His ultimate expression in eternity—[[Revelation-21#v3|Rev. 21:3]], [[Revelation-21#v22|22]]


## V. The New Jerusalem, as a composition of all the regenerated saints of both the Old Testament and the New Testament dispensations built together with God as God's eternal building, will also be the mingling of all the redeemed, regenerated, transformed, and glorified tripartite men with the processed, consummated Triune God

### A. In the incarnation, crucifixion, and resurrection, the Triune God has been processed and consummated to be one with men, His chosen people

### B. Through the generations in God's dispensations, the tripartite men have been redeemed, regenerated, transformed, and glorified to be one with the Triune God, who has chosen them in eternity past to be His counterpart, dwelling place, and expression in eternity future—[[Revelation-21#v2|Rev. 21:2-3]], [[Revelation-21#v9|9-11]]

### C. What a wonderful and marvelous mingling of divinity with humanity this will be!


## VI. In addition to all these, the New Jerusalem will be the ultimate manifestation of the processed and consummated Triune God for His expression in the regenerated, transformed, and glorified humanity in eternity.
