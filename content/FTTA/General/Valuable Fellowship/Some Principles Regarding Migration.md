# Twelve points to help us with migration:

>"We all need to move and travel from city to city, state to state, and country to country for the Lord's return; we do not have any hope on this earth; our hope is with the Lord at His return; the migrations are a way to prepare for the Lord's coming back. We all need to bring this matter of migration to the Lord and seek His leading"—*The Collected Works of Witness Lee*, 1971, vol. 4, p. 354.

## Point 1.

As Christians, we must be those who come and go: God in Christ has called us to come near to Him for rest, life and salvation; the more we come to Him, the more grace we receive—[[Matthew-11#v28|Matt. 11:28]]; [[John-01#v16|John 1:16]]; [[John-05#v40|5:40]]; [[John-10#v10|10:10]]. 

Then after we have received grace by coming to the Lord, He commands us to go and preach the gospel to all the nations, to go and disciple all the nations, and to go and be His witnesses unto the uttermost part of the earth—[[Matthew-28#v19|Matt. 28:19]]; [[Acts-01#v8|Acts 1:8]]; [[Matthew-24#v14|Matt. 24:14]].

## Point 2.

To migrate, to move, is a divine principle that we have found in the Bible. After God created man as male and female, He blessed them and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply; and fill the earth" ([[Genesis-01#v28|Gen. 1:28]]); to fill the earth requires migration.

## Point 3.

In all the local churches there should be both inflow and outflow. In order to avoid becoming a Dead Sea, a "canal" must be dug to allow the "water" to flow out through migration. When the water is allowed to flow out, more water will flow in.

## Point 4.

The purpose of our migrations is to spread the Lord's recovery of the church life with all the items and all the aspects of what Christ is to His Body. Once Christ has been received and enjoyed by us as our Savior and Redeemer, our life, our Lord, and our all in all, then we must come together to build up the church as Christ's Body to express Him—[[Acts-05#v30|Acts 5:30-32]]; [[Romans-03#v21|Rom. 3:21-26]]; [[Colossians-03#v4|Col. 3:4]]; [[Colossians-02#v6|2:6]], [[Colossians-02#v16|16-17]].

God's intention is to have a local expression of the Body of Christ in every city—[[Ephesians-01#v22|Eph. 1:22-23]]; [[Revelation-01#v11|Rev. 1:11]].

## Point 5.

Migration is the way of the Christian life. In order to serve God, declare His gospel, and maintain His testimony, we must live as sojourners—[[Hebrews-11#v13|Heb. 11:13]]; [[1 Peter-02#v11|1 Pet. 2:11]]; cf. [[1 Peter-01#v1|1 Pet. 1:1]].

We should all be migrants, following in the footsteps of our forefather Abraham who migrated throughout his life—[[Romans-04#v12|Rom. 4:12]].

If we would walk in the steps of Abraham's faith, we must live the life of the altar and the tent, taking Christ as our life and the church as our living.

## Point 6.

We should do everything according to the leading, working, and speaking of the Spirit, honor the Lord as the Head of the Body for His unique move, and be balanced by the Body to be kept in its unique oneness—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Acts-04#v8|4:8]]; [[Acts-06#v5|6:5]], [[Acts-06#v10|10]]; [[Acts-08#v30|8:30]], [[Acts-08#v39|39]]; [[Acts-15#v28|15:28]]; [[Acts-16#v6|16:6-7]], [[Acts-16#v9|9]].

## Point 7.

Migration should be by much prayer and according to the Lord's leading—cf. [[Acts-13#v1|Acts 13:1-4]].

We should learn to pray adequately concerning our going out for the spread of our Lord's recovery.

## Point 8.

We must always move, work, walk, and do things not individualistically but in a coordinated way; this requires much learning—cf. [[Ezekiel-01#v5|Ezek. 1:5a]], [[Ezekiel-01#v9|9]], [[Ezekiel-01#v11|11b-14]], [[Ezekiel-01#v19|19-22]].

## Point 9.

The master key to all the blessings in the New Testament is to be in one accord—[[Psalm-133|Psa. 133]]; [[Acts-02#v46|Acts 2:46]].

## Point 10.

All those who are burdened to migrate for the spread of the church life must firstly be built up in their own locality. If we hold to Christ as everything to us, we shall experience the genuine building, first locally and then universally; this will make us useful in migration for the expansion of the church life.

## Point 11.

We must take the God-ordained way of prayer, the Spirit, and the Word—[[Acts-01#v14|Acts 1:14]]; [[Acts-04#v24|4:24-31]]; [[Acts-06#v4|6:4]], [[Acts-06#v7|7]].

## Point 12.

Migration helps us to grow in life, practice mutual love, and function.
