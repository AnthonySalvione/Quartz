# The Burden, Nature, and Objective of Studying the Truth in FTTA

## I. **The burden of studying the truth in FTTA—the primary goal of the training being to help the trainees to know the truth—[[Proverbs-23#v23|Prov. 23:23]]:

### A. Every nations that desires to be powerful and prosperous must pay attention to the education of its citizens.

### B. There is a twofold value of studying the truth:

##### 1. Firstly, the truth leads the seeking believers into the experiences of life in a deeper way—cf. [[Ephesians-01#v3|Eph. 1:3-14]]; [[Ephesians-03#v16|3:16-17]].

##### 2. Secondly, the truth becomes the capital for their service to bring the saints into their organic function—[[Song-04#v11|S.S. 4:11]]; [[Romans-15#v16|Rom. 15:16]]; [[Colossians-01#v28|Col. 1:28-29]]; [[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11]].


## II. **The nature of studying the truth in FTTA—a unique atmosphere with an environment most conducive to studying the truth in an educational way:

### A. In a separated and sanctified place—cf. [[Matthew-05#v1|Matt. 5:1]]; [[Matthew-13#v36|13:36]]; [[Matthew-16#v13|16:13]]; [[Matthew-24#v3|24:3]].

### B. In taking the way of education—[[Colossians-01#v28|Col. 1:28]]; [[2 Timothy-01#v13|2 Tim. 1:13]]; cf. [[Acts-19#v9|Acts 19:9]]; [[Acts-28#v30|28:30-31]].

### C. In having a community of consecrated ones to pursue with—[[2 Timothy-02#v22|2 Tim. 2:22]]; [[Acts-17#v11|Acts 17:11]].

### D. In having the best resources available—[[Colossians-01#v25|Col. 1:25]]; [[Acts-08#v31|Acts 8:31]]; [[Ephesians-04#v11|Eph. 4:11-12]].

### E. In being given a dedicated amount of study time—[[Ephesians-05#v16|Eph. 5:16]].

### F. In the golden years of youth—[[Proverbs-22#v6|Prov. 22:6]]; [[Ecclesiastes-12#v1|Eccl. 12:1]].


## III. **The objective of studying the truth in FTTA—to constitute the trainees with a healthy deposit and an enlarged  appetite for the truth that will result in a lifetime endeavor of studying the truth—[[2 Timothy-01#v13|2 Tim. 1:13-14]].
