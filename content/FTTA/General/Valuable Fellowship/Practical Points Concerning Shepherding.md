# I. The purpose of our service must be to minister life to others; we should fellowship with them and minister life to them so that they may grow—[[1 John-05#v16|1 John 5:16a]]:
### A. We need to raise up the new believers in home meetings, going weekly to have home meetings with them in their homes—[[Acts-05#v42|Acts 5:42]].
### B. In order for us to minister life to others we must concentrate ourselves anew, pray by ourselves to have a thorough dealing with the Lord, pick up a burden to care people, and learn to be interested in people in the way of life.
### C. In order to shepherd people we must practice the following practical points:
#### 1. Not being quick, but spending an adequate amount of time to shepherd people; we should shepherd people by contacting them repeatedly.

#### 2. Being positive and not sloppy; we should not contact people through rebuke or condemnation with any negative spirit, attitude, and tone.

#### 3. Remembering well that the church is not a police station or law court, and that we are not policemen or judges.

#### 4. Being happy persons with a joyful spirit, always humble, mild and easy to contact.

#### 5. Listening to others in order to realize their true need and real situation.

#### 6. Visiting others without saying much, and listening to others in order to realize their true need and real situation.

#### 7. Not making decisions for others.

#### 8. Never arguing with people; we should avoid arguments and try to find the opportunity to minister Christ to people.

#### 9. Not passing on vain knowledge.

#### 10. Not being involved in any negative talk; we should not be a "trash can."

#### 11. Being honest and not political.

#### 12. Ministering life to others; we must realize that what people need is the Lord and try to give them an "injection" of Christ.

#### 13. Taking the lead to bring others into a prayer life; we should help them to contact the Lord and take the chance to pray with them and lead them to the Lord in that prayer.

#### 14. Contacting people with patience.

#### 15. Trusting those under your care.

#### 16. Never forcing anyone to do anything; we should not even try to overly influence people.

#### 17. Fellowshipping with others according to our own experience.

#### 18. Not carrying out a social work; we should consider whether a certain case will waste our time and stay away from complicated involvements.



# II. We need to deny our disposition and care only to bear a burden for people; we must deal with our disposition for the sake of fruitfulness:

### A. The greatest hindrance to our usefulness is our disposition; practically speaking, to deny the self is simply to deny the disposition—[[Matthew-16#v24|Matt. 16:24]]:

#### 1. If we are not useful in the Lord's hand for taking care of people, it is due to our raw, natural disposition.

#### 2. If we pay adequate attention to the Lord and pray much about this, it will be easy for us to care for others, bear fruit, and make profit by using our talent.
