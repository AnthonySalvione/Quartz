## I. The city of New Jerusalem, the complete divine and organic building, is built on a golden mountain with gates and with a wall and its foundations; the center of this consummated building is the throne of God and of the Lamb

## II. The two, God and the Lamb, on the one throne are not two separate persons but signify the redeeming Triune God—God in the redeeming Lamb. God is the One who had a purpose and who made a plan in eternity past and who created all things for the fulfillment of His plan—[[Revelation-04#v11|Rev. 4:11]]; the Lamb is the One who redeemed us, the One who accomplished a full redemption to fulfill God's plan—[[John-01#v29|John 1:29]]; [[1 Peter-01#v18|1 Pet. 1:18-19]]. Thus, the throne of God and of the Lamb denotes that this throne is to carry out God's plan through Christ's redemption

## III. The throne signifies the administration of the New Jerusalem, and the golden mountain (gold signifying God the Father's nature) is the base, signifying the attribute of God's righteousness as the foundation of God's throne—[[Psalm-89#v14|Psa. 89:14]]

## IV. The golden street, which is one with the base of gold and is joined to the throne, is the communication of the divine administration and signifies the divine nature as the divine way for God's redeemed to walk on (under the regulation of the throne)

## V. In the middle of the street the river of water of life flows out of the throne, and along the flow of the river the tree of life grows; the river signifies the consummated life-giving Spirit as the consummation of the consummated Triune God, and the fruit of the tree of life signifies the incarnated, crucified, and resurrected Christ as the embodiment of the consummated Triune God. These two, the river and the tree, are the provision, the supply, of the holy city

## VI. The eternal purpose and plan of God is to have an eternal corporate expression of Himself for His delight and satisfaction. God is the One who purposed, planned, and created—[[Ephesians-03#v9|Eph. 3:9-11]]; [[Revelation-04#v11|Rev. 4:11]]

## VII. The throne of the divine administration is to carry out the eternal purpose of God through the redemption of Christ, the Lamb, by the divine dispensing of the Divine Trinity into all God's elect. There is no corruption in the divine administration but only the pure intention of supplying the holy city for God's eternal expression

## VIII. The righteousness of God (not His love, grace, or mercy) is the main attribute of His divine nature to be the source and sure foundation (base) of the divine administration (throne) and of God's judicial redemption (the Lamb) for His justification of all His redeemed elect, and also the source and base of the supply of the divine building—the river of water of life and the tree of life. The New Jerusalem is a matter of life built on the foundation of righteousness. See L-S of Isaiah, message 47. See [[Psalm-89#v14|Psa. 89:14]]; [[John-19#v34|John 19:34]]; [[Romans-01#v16|Rom. 1:16-17]]; [[Romans-03#v26|3:26]]; [[Romans-05#v10|5:10]], [[Romans-05#v18|18]]; [[Romans-08#v10|8:10]]

## IX. The redemption of Christ through His all-inclusive death is to satisfy the requirement of God's righteousness, to fulfill the eternal purpose of God, to release the flow of the divine life from the redeeming Triune God as the redeeming Lamb, and to open the way for the believers in Christ to partake of Him as the tree of life—[[John-19#v34|John 19:34]]; [[Revelation-22#v14|Rev. 22:14]]

## X. The gold street, signifying God's divine nature, is the divine way for God's redeemed to walk on under the divine administration of the throne so that they can receive the divine supply of the divine life that flows out of God, and it is the communication (fellowship) for the execution of the divine administration in the Holy City (fellowship street)

## XI. The divine life as the content of the Triune God flowing out of the throne of the divine administration is the river, in which the tree grows, to be the abundant life supply of the holy city. The river signifies the abundance of life in its flow

## XII. The availability of the tree of life is indicated by the fact that it spreads as a vine along the flow of the river of water of life. The river conveys the tree

## XIII. Christ, the Lamb, is the embodiment of the Triune God issuing in the Spirit as the consummation of the Triune God through the steps of His process—incarnation, crucifixion, and resurrection. The embodiment came first (because God and life are abstract, God needs an embodiment, and this embodiment is Christ), and He became the life-giving Spirit as the consummation of the consummated Triune God. The tree of life is Christ as the consummated embodiment of the Triune God, and the river of water of life is the Spirit as the consummation of the processed Triune God

## XIV. The three things flowing out of the throne of the redeeming God—out of the redeeming God Himself—are the divine administration (street), the river of water of life, and the tree of life, the latter two being the provision, the supply, of the holy city

## XV. (Cf. point 2) The administration of the throne of God and of the Lamb is for the accomplishment of the plan of God through the redemption of the Lamb by the flow of the Spirit, the dispensing of the Triune God, for the eternal expression of the processed and consummated Triune God

## XVI. If we want to participate in the divine supply in the New Jerusalem, we must submit ourselves to God's throne of authority and to His divine administration and take care of the divine communication in the entire city of New Jerusalem

## XVII. That the river of water of life proceeds in the middle of the golden street indicates that the divine life flows in the divine nature as the unique way for the daily life of God's redeemed people; the river of life is available along the divine way, and in order to experience the river of life as the flow of the Triune God, we must take the divine nature as our way

## XVIII. We enjoy Christ as the tree of life in three stages—today as our common portion, in the millennium as our reward ([[Revelation-02#v7|Rev. 2:7]]), and in the New Jerusalem as our eternal portion—[[Revelation-22#v2|Rev. 22:2]], [[Revelation-22#v14|14]]
