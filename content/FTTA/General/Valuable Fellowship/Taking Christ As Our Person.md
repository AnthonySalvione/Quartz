## II. Implying and embodying the most crucial principle of all—Gal. 2:20; 2 Cor. 2:10; 5:5; 3:4b; Luke 21:36; Rev. 14:1

### A. In order to set aside our own preferences and take another one as our person, we must love that one to the uttermost... to fall in love with Him and love Him more than anything else.  
*CWwL 1970 (2): 543*

### B. Almost all Christians know Galatians 2:20, but very few know 2 Corinthians 2:10. “Here is a verse which tells us that we must do things in the person of Christ... (here) we see the practical application of what Paul said in Galatians 2:20.”  
*CWwL 1973-1974 (2): 136*

### C. Many times a wife’s “yes” is changed into “no” by looking at the index of her husband’s face. This means that at that time she was taking her husband as her person.  
*CWwL 1973-1974 (2): 136-137*

### D. It is wonderful to see the believers learning to take Christ as their life in practical matters such as their personal grooming or the length of their hair. However, they need to advance to take Christ as their person in every aspect of their life.  
*CWwL 1970 (2): 498*

### E. Wherever you are, whatever you do, and whatever you say, simply take Christ as your person.  
*CWwL 1970 (2): 425*

### F. Wrinkles are not taken away by teaching or by trying to obey the doctrines in the Bible. Wrinkles are removed as we take Christ as our person and allow Him to live in us.  
*LS Ephesians (56): 474*

## III. Revolutionizing our concept as to the opportunity, urgency, and incomparability of taking the Lord as our person:

### A. Taking Christ as our person is very different from taking Him only as our life or many other things...  
*CWwL 1970 (2): 543*

### B. Our consecration is to take Christ as our person. We are empty vessels who consecrate to the Lord so that He may dwell in us ... to be not only our life but also our person.  
*CWwL 1971 (1): 191*

### C. We may have countless “how to” questions, such as how to be submissive, how to deal with our spouse, or how to go shopping... the unique answer to all these questions is that we should take Christ as our person.  
*CWwL 1971 (2): 217*

### D. If we live by Christ as our person, we will have Him as our reality. No matter how thorough our knowledge of the Bible may be, it amounts to nothing unless we take Christ as our person.  
*CWwL 1971 (2): 217*

### E. Our Christian life depends on our taking the all-inclusive living Christ as our person. We must take Him as our person in everything and in all things... This is a vital burden for the Lord’s recovery.  
*CWwL 1971 (2): 217*

### F. “It is so simple. If we all take Christ as our person, spontaneously we will express God, and we will also subdue the enemy. We will conquer all the enemies and have a conquering life instead of a conquered life.”  
*CWwL 1970 (2): 425*

### G. The highest demand is not to do the will of God but to deny our self and live by Christ as our person.  
*CWwL 1970 (2): 542*
