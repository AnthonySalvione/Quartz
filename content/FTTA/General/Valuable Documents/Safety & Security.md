While we trust in the Lord and pray He will cover us during our time in the Training, we must also do our part to be exercised and cautious when it comes to safety and security. The following are some important guidelines:

* You are responsible to keep your personal valuable items safe and discreetly placed. We ask every trainee to have the mindset of a security guard.
* Always be vigilant, cognizant, and aware of your surroundings, whether or not you are on the LSM campus. Do not engage in any activity that may cause you to become unaware of your surroundings.
* You should never walk alone or exercise alone at night.
* Sisters should walk in pairs or groups at all times. Anaheim can be a dangerous city. Make arrangements to ride with other trainees. Shuttle services will be provided whenever possible.
* Pedestrians have the right-of-way at all times, but do not assume vehicles will automatically stop for you. Be extremely watchful of all vehicles from all directions.
* Never prop open any exterior doors of the Ministry Conference Center (1212 N Hubbell Way) or the Training Center (1200 N Electric Way) at any time.
* Be sure doors to residences close and lock behind you upon arrival and departure. Doors to the residences lock automatically on a timer whether open or closed; please double check that the door is securely closed and locked.
* **Do not give out any of the Training's security codes (e.g. Grace Gardens or the satellite houses, etc.) to anyone. Do not lend your badge or give access to the Training Center to anyone**
* FTTA has contracted with Argos Security to have officers monitoring Grace Gardens and the MCC campus 24 hours daily. If you observe **any** suspicious person, vehicle or activity, call:
	* Argos Security at (714) 209-4920 **or**
	* The Anaheim Police Department (714) 765-1900.
* If you observe an emergency or obvious criminal activity, immediately call 911.
