Sending an update about the gospel trip!

The schedule is full of different activities and are all laid out by the "full-time" saints here. The morning to noon times stay the same every day. 

The schedule begins with morning revival from 7am-8m. Then we have breakfast with our hospitality for an hour. I'm staying with one other trainee, and the other one is split up with another hospitality (we will rotate at some point).

After breakfast, we all meet up at a sister's house named Kathy. While we're there, we have coordination until 11:30ish. Anybody in the church can come to that; the time consists of praying, ministry reading, and then questions/discussion time. Today we read through the beginning of the life-study of Matthew in preparation for the bible study later in the day which was on Matthew chapter 1. We pack a lunch at the end, then head out to wherever we're preaching that gospel that day.

Today we went to the student union building downstairs and set up a table. We also walked around a little bit in pairs during lunch time to find certain ones to talk to.

The afternoons always have something different planned. Today we toured around the U of Pittsburgh campus, which is pretty massive. Yesterday we all went to an outdoor ice skating rink for a couple hours which I actually enjoyed a lot! I haven't done ice skating in such a long time.

Around 4pm, we went to the club bible study on campus with the students plus a few new ones that we had met earlier today at the table.

There's a student who's been joining us for everything named Ryan, and he likes to talk about how he's majoring in supply chains & marketing. Since the beginning of the trip, he's been hyping up the campus dining hall and that it won the overall best campus dining hall in the nation. He mentioned it to every person that he met and was so excited to show it to us and kept mentioning it every day. Tonight we went, and it was actually pretty insane. It was $15.50 to get into the dining hall, and then it's all you can eat buffet style. The dining hall was huge, and had 10 full on restaurants of different regions like Greek, Chinese, Spanish, Italian/Pizza and more. I got three full different plates that were all super good, and their menu changes every day... I thought that was pretty impressive. Probably ate a little bit too much food though.

Then we went to the prayer meeting for an hour and a half at the brothers house which started to feel a little long by the end... and then finally got back home around 9:30
