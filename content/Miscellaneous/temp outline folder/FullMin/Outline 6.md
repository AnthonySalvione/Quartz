# Salvation in Life-Sanctification
## Lesson Six
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

## I. God's sanctification of the believers is of three aspects:
### A. The first aspect is the seeking sanctification by the Holy Spirit with the enlightening
### word initially
1 Pet. 1:2; Luke 15:8.
### B. The second aspect is the positional sanctification by the redeeming blood of Christ
### judicially
Heb. 13:12; 10:29.
### C. The third aspect is the dispositional sanctification by the Holy Spirit organically
Rom. 15:16; 6:19, 22.

## II. The sanctification in God's organic salvation is carried out by the Holy Spirit with
## the element of Christ's resurrection life to sanctify us in our disposition that our
## natural disposition, which is crooked, perverted, and full of peculiarities, may be
## sanctified by God's holy nature
15:16; 2 Pet. 1:4:
### A. This dispositional sanctification is from our spirit, through our soul, and unto our body,
### so that our entire being can be wholly sanctified
1 Thes. 5:23:
#### 1. Dispositional sanctification by the indwelling Spirit takes place through our
#### cooperation by presenting ourselves and our members as slaves to righteousness
Rom. 6:18-19.
#### 2. Dispositional sanctification takes place through our being metabolically cleansed by
#### the washing of the water in the word
Eph. 5:26; John 17:17.
### B. Since all the believers will be the components of the holy city, New Jerusalem, all of
### them should be sanctified to be as holy as the holy city, the New Jerusalem.
