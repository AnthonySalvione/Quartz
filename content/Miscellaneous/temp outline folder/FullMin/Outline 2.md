# The Universal, Divine-Human Incorporation of the Consummated God with the Regenerated Believers (1)

**Scripture Reading:**
John 14:10-11, 16-20; Rev. 21:3, 22; 2:17

## I. The need of vision (not mere objective knowledge)
Eph. 1:17; Gen. 13:14-18; Job 42:5-6.

## II. The entire Bible unveiling one thing-God wants to be united, mingled, and incorporated with man.

## III. God in His Divine Trinity is an incorporation
John 14:10-11:
### A. By coinhering mutually.
### B. By working together as one.

## IV. Christ's glorification:
### A. The glory of Christ's divinity having been concealed within the shell of His humanity.
### B. The glory of Christ's divinity being released through His death and resurrection
Luke 12:49-50; John 12:23-24.
### C. Christ praying for His glorification
John 17:1, 5.

## V. The issue of Christ's glorification being the incorporation of the consummated Triune God with the regenerated believers:
### A. The Spirit dwelling in the regenerated believers
14:16-17.
### B. The Son coming back to be another Comforter to make the believers live with Him
vv. 18-19.

## VI. The consummated Triune God and the regenerated believers became an incorporation in the resurrection of Christ (v. 20) in three aspects:
### A. The Father's house
v. 2.
### B. The Son's vine
15:1-8, 16.
### C. The Spirit's child
16:13-16, 19-22.

## VII. The New Jerusalem being the ultimate incorporation of the processed and consummated Triune God with the regenerated, sanctified, renewed, transformed, conformed, and glorified tripartite church
Rev. 21:3, 22.
### A. The New Jerusalem being the tabernacle of God and the center of this tabernacle being Christ as the hidden manna
2:17.
### B. The way to be incorporated into this unique, universal, divine-human incorporation being to eat Christ as the hidden manna.
