# The Consummation of Reigning in Life
## Lesson Fourteen
## THE FULL MINISTRY OF CHRIST
## TERM 3

**Scripture Reading:**
Rom. 5:17, 21; Rev. 21:3, 22

## I. Reigning in life is "unto eternal life"
Rom. 5:21:
### A. Unto eternal life is a particular expression.
### B. John 4:14b says, "The water that I will give him will become in him a fountain of
### water springing up into eternal life":
#### 1. Into (unto in Rom. 5:21) speaks of destination.
#### 2. The eternal life is the destination of the flowing Triune God.
#### 3. Into also means "to become" or "to be."
#### 4. The eternal life eventually will be the New Jerusalem.
#### 5. Into eternal life means into the New Jerusalem.

## II. The New Jerusalem is the totality of the divine life, the totality of the life of
## God.

## I. The issue of our reigning in life will be the New Jerusalem.

## IV. The Bible unveils to us that God's economy is to have the Triune God
## incorporated with man to be an incorporation:
### A. This incorporation is first the Body of Christ and then the New Jerusalem.
### B. The New Jerusalem will be the consummate incorporation of God and man:
#### 1. God wants to be united and mingled with man.
#### 2. The God who has been united and mingled with man wants to be incorporated
#### with man to be an incorporation.
#### 3. This union, mingling, and incorporation will consummate as the New Jerusalem.

## V. The goal of our reigning in life should be uniquely and ultimately the goal of
## God's eternal economy-the New Jerusalem as the universal incorporation of
## the union and mingling of divinity with humanity.
