# The Triune God as Intimated in the Old Testament
## Message Seven
## OUTLINE OF
## THE MESSAGES FOR THE FULL-TIME TRAINING
## THE TRIUNE GOD

**Note:** These outlines are reproduced exactly from those written by Brother Witness Lee for
a series of messages he gave in the fall of 1983, titled "The Divine Trinity as Revealed in the
Holy Word" (see The Collected Works of Witness Lee, 1983, vol. 3). In this series of messages,
Brother Lee completed his study only through 1 and 2 Samuel. Scripture quotations have
not been updated to match the text of the Recovery Version. In 2009 the spoken messages
were published as a book under the title The Divine Trinity as Revealed in the Holy Word.

## I. In the Scriptures there is no doctrine on the Divine Trinity; it is only
## indicated from the first chapter to the last of the Scriptures, in many
## narrations of the divine and spiritual facts.

## II. Hence, it is not for God to teach us about His Godhead, but for Him to
## work Himself into us that we may partake of, experience, and enjoy Him.

## III. This is fully proven by:
### A. Matt. 28:19—"Disciple all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the
### Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."
### B. 2 Cor. 13:14—"The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the
### fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all."

## IV. In Genesis:
### A. "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth"-1:1:
#### 1. God-Elohim, uni-plural, proven by the predicate "created" and the
#### pronouns "Us" and "Our" in v. 26 and "His" and "He" in v. 27.
#### 2. For the creation of the heavens and the earth for man—v. 26-27; Zech.
#### 12:1.
### B. "The Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters. And God said, Let there
### be light: and there was light"—1:2-3. God carried out His creation by His Spirit
### and His speaking-His word (John 1:1-3; Heb. 11:3; Psa. 33:9). Here is the
### implication of the Divine Trinity-God, His Spirit, and His word (Christ-Rev.
### 19:13). The Three are distinct, but not separate. God's Spirit is God's breath
### (Job 33:4), and God's word is God's breathing (1 Tim. 3:16), which is by God's
### breath-Spirit.
### C. "God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness:...so God created
### man in his own image, in the image of God created he him, male and female
### created he them"-1:26-27:
#### 1. Elohim talked to Himself with the pronouns "Us" and "Our:"
#### 2. Moses talked about Elohim with the pronouns "His" and "He."
#### 3. Directly for the creation of man in God's image with the intention that man
#### may express God by having God wrought into him.

### D. "Jehovah God said, Behold, the man is become as one of us; to know good and
### evil: and now, lest he put forth his hand and take also of the tree of life, and
### eat, and live forever"-3:22:
#### 1. Here Elohim talked to Himself with the pronoun "Us" again.
#### 2. In relation to man's eating of the tree of life—to take God in as man's life.
### E. "Jehovah said, My Spirit shall not strive with man forever"-6:3. Here it is the
### Spirit of Jehovah, not merely Jehovah, who shall not strive with man. This
### refers to the Divine Trinity working on the fallen man.
### F. "Jehovah said,...let us go down, and there confound their language, that they
### may not understand one another's speech. So Jehovah scattered them abroad
### from thence upon the face of all the earth"-11:6-8:
#### 1. God talked to Himself with the pronoun "Us" the third time.
#### 2. In relation to the confounding and scattering of mankind that man might
#### not be successful in frustrating God in the fulfillment of His purpose with
#### man.
### G. "Jehovah appeared unto him [Abraham] by the oaks of Mamre:...and he lifted
### up his eyes and saw three men standing near him"-18:1-2:
#### 1. Of these three men, one was Jehovah, and the other two were angels.
#### 2. Jehovah appeared to Abraham before His incarnation (John 1:14) as a real
#### 3. In relation to the ancestry of Christ for fulfilling God's purpose to redeem
#### man and work Himself into man (Gen. 19:36-37; Ruth 1:4; 4:13, 21-22;
#### Matt. 1:5-16).
### H. "The angel of Jehovah said unto her (Hagar), I will multiply thy seed exceed-
### ingly"-16:10 with v. 7, 9, 11. The angel of Jehovah turned out to be the very
### Jehovah who will multiply Hagar's seed
### I. "The angel of God [Elohim] called to Hagar out of heaven, and said unto
### her...Arise, lift up the lad,...for I will make him a great nation"—21:17-18. The
### angel of God turned out to be the very Elohim who will make Hagar's son a
### great nation.
### J. "The Angel of Jehovah called unto him out of heaven, and said,
### Abraham....now I know that thou fearest God [Elohim], seeing thou hast not
### withheld thy son, thine only son, from me"-22:11-12. The Angel of Jehovah
### turned out to be the very Jehovah Elohim to whom Isaac should belong.
### K. "The angel of God [Elohim] spake unto me in a dream, Jacob: I am the God
### [El] of Bethel"-31:11-13. The angel of God turned out to be the very El
### (another form of Elohim).
### L. "He [Israel] blessed Joseph, and said, God [Elohim], before whom my fathers
### Abraham and Isaac did walk, the God [Elohim] who shepherdeth me all my
### life long unto this day, the Angel who redeemed me from all evil, bless the
### lads"-48:15-16. The Angel was the very Elohim, the Triune God of the three
### generations-Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
### H through L all indicating the Divine Trinity in relation to the genealogy of
### Christ (Matt. 1:1-2).
