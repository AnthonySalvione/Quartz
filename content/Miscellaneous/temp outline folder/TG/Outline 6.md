# The Divine Trinity in the Operation of the Divine Economy
## Message Six
## Outline of the Messages
## for the Full-time Training
## GENERAL SUBJECT: THE TRIUNE GOD

## I. God's eternal economy is to make man the same as He is in life and
## nature but not in the Godhead, and to make Himself one with man and
## man one with Him, thus to be enlarged and expanded in His expression,
## that all His divine attributes may be expressed in human virtues
**Verse references:** 1 Tim. 1:4; Eph. 1:10; 3:9

### A. The initiation of God's operation in the accomplishing of His economy is the
### incarnation, in which God became what we are in life, nature, and expres-
### sion
**Verse references:** John 1:14; Heb. 2:14, 17a; Phil. 2:5-8

### B. The consummation of the operation of God in His economy is the New
### Jerusalem, in which God's elect have become what He is in life, nature, and
### expression but not in His Godhead
**Verse references:** Rev. 21:2—22:5

## II. God's economy derives and proceeds from His triune being, and it oper-
## ates in every stage through the simultaneous and inseparable operations
## of the Three of the Trinity:

### A. In eternity past, before the foundation of the world:
#### 1. God (the Father) made an eternal purpose in Christ Jesus our Lord and
#### with the Spirit
**Verse references:** Eph. 3:11

#### 2. The Father blessed us with every spiritual (of the Spirit) blessing in the
#### Son, even as He chose us in the Son before the foundation of the world to
#### be holy (in the Spirit's sanctification—2 Thes. 2:13), predestinating us unto
#### sonship through Jesus Christ to Himself
**Verse references:** Eph. 1:3-5; Gal. 4:4-6

#### 3. God the Father chose the believers according to His foreknowledge in the
#### sanctification of the Spirit unto the obedience and sprinkling of the blood
#### of Jesus Christ
**Verse references:** 1 Pet. 1:2

#### 4. God (the Father) chose the believers from the beginning unto salvation in
#### the sanctification of the Spirit unto the obtaining of the glory of our Lord
#### Jesus Christ
**Verse references:** 2 Thes. 2:13-14

#### 5. God predestinated His hidden wisdom before the ages for our glory, which
#### wisdom is Christ, who was incarnated and crucified as the Lord of glory—
**Verse references:** 1 Cor. 2:7-8; 1:23-24, 30

#### 6. God in His Trinity held a council and made a counsel concerning the way to
#### accomplish His will, concerning the creation of man in His image (Christ
#### as the prototype-Col. 1:15a; 2 Cor. 4:4), and concerning the Son's death for
#### the carrying out of God's eternal economy
**Verse references:** Eph. 1:11b; Gen. 1:26; Acts 2:23; 1 Pet. 1:20

#### 7. God (the Father) gave us grace in Christ Jesus before the times of the ages
#### (before the world began)
**Verse references:** 2 Tim. 1:9

#### 8. The Triune God, in Christ as His embodiment, was preparing to come forth
#### out of eternity into time, to come with His divinity into humanity by being
#### born in Bethlehem as a man, to accomplish God's eternal economy
**Verse references:** Micah 5:2

### B. In the Old Testament time:
#### 1. God's creation of the universe involved His triune being as the Father, the
#### Son (the Logos--Word), and the Spirit
**Verse references:** Gen. 1:1-3; Eph. 3:14-15; John 1:3; Heb. 11:3; Col. 1:16-17

#### 2. In His creation of man God was the active Agent, but the Son provided the
#### prototype for man's image and likeness (Gen. 1:26; Col. 1:15), and the Spirit
#### is understood to be involved in the forming of man's spirit through the
#### breathing of the breath of life into man to make him a living soul
**Verse references:** Gen. 2:7

#### 3. In His operation among His Old Testament elect, Israel, for the fulfillment
#### of His purpose, God moved in His trinity:
##### a. The Angel of Jehovah (Christ as God's Sent One) who spoke to
##### Abraham and who appeared to Moses was Jehovah, the Triune God-
**Verse references:** Gen. 16:7-13; 22:11-18; Exo. 3:2-15

##### b. The Angel of Jehovah (Christ as God's Sent One) was Jehovah God
##### Himself
**Verse references:** Exo. 23:20-23; 33:2-3, 14-15; Zech. 2:8-11

##### c. Other allusions to the Trinity can be found throughout the Old Testa-
##### ment record (e.g., Isa. 6:1-8), which, taken as a whole, indicate that
##### even during this early period of His economy God acted according to
##### His triune being.

### C. In the incarnation of Christ:
#### 1. Although it was the incarnation of the Son (i.e., the Son is the person of the
#### incarnation—Isa. 7:14; 9:6; John 1:14; 20:31; Matt. 16:16; Luke 1:35; Rom.
#### 8:3a; Gal. 4:4), the entire Godhead-the Father, the Son, and the Spirit-
#### was involved:
##### a. The Son's coming into humanity was accomplished through the Holy
##### Spirit and with the Spirit as the Son's divine essence
**Verse references:** Matt. 1:18, 20; Luke 1:35

##### b. The Father, who sent the Son, came with the Son and in the Son
**Verse references:** John 1:14; 7:29; 8:16, 29; 16:32; 10:38; 14:10-11; 17:21

##### c. Thus, the Son's incarnation was the incarnation of the Triune God-
**Verse references:** John 1:1, 14a

#### 2. By this we understand that the Son is not an independent God, separable
#### from the Father and the Spirit, but rather always depends on the Father
#### and the Spirit for His distinct existence, as They do Him.

### D. In Christ's human living and ministry on the earth:
#### 1. His baptism in the Jordan by John, as the initiation of His ministry, was an
#### operation of the entire Trinity
**Verse references:** Mark 1:9-11

##### a. From the scene at the Lord's baptism we should understand that the
##### Three of the Trinity are distinct but not separate; the Three operate
##### inseparably to manifest any one of the Three distinctly, and in every
##### action of one, the Three are understood to be operating simultaneously
##### (Augustine, The Trinity, II:18).

##### b. The Son's standing in the water, the Spirit's descending on the Son, and
##### the Father's speaking from the heavens, as distinct actions of the Three,

##### were each manifested by the simultaneous and inseparable operation of
##### all Three.

#### 2. Throughout His earthly ministry Christ lived, moved, and worked insepara-
#### bly from the Father and the Spirit
**Verse references:** Luke 4:1, 14, 18; Matt. 12:28; John 4:34; 5:17; 14:9-11; 6:57

#### 3. Thus, the entire Divine Trinity operated to manifest the distinct work of
#### the Son in His ministry on the earth. The Gospel of John shows in detail
#### the incorporation of the Triune God in the manifest actions of the Son.

### E. In Christ's crucifixion:
#### 1. Although it was the Son (and not the Father or the Spirit) who suffered
#### death on the cross, the Father and the Spirit also were involved:
##### a. The Son died as a God-man, having the Spirit as His divine essence and
##### coinhering with the Father, and God forsook Him economically because
##### He took the place of sinners—He bore our sins and was made sin for
##### us
**Verse references:** Matt. 27:46; 1 Pet. 2:24; 3:18; Isa. 53:6; 2 Cor. 5:21

##### b. As Christ, the God-man, hung on the cross dying for all mankind, God
##### operated to forgive the offenses accumulated against us, to wipe out the
##### ordinances that were contrary to us, and to triumph over the fallen
##### angelic host that opposed Him
**Verse references:** Col. 2:13-15

##### c. The Son offered Himself to God (the Father) through the eternal Spirit,
##### thus accomplishing a redemption that is eternal in quality and effect—
**Verse references:** Heb. 9:14, 12

#### 2. In the visible death of Christ the Three of the Trinity operated so as to
#### manifest the distinct work of the Son on the cross.

### F. In Christ's resurrection:
#### 1. It was Jesus Christ, the Son of God, who rose from the dead
**Verse references:** Acts 10:41; Mark 16:9, 14; 1 Thes. 4:14

#### but in His resurrection we can recognize the
#### full operation of the Trinity:
##### a. Generally, it was God who raised Jesus as a man
**Verse references:** Acts 2:24, 32; 3:15; 10:40; Rom. 8:11; 10:9

##### b. Specifically, the Father, the Son, and the Spirit operated in the raising
##### of Jesus from the dead
**Verse references:** 1 Thes. 1:10; John 2:19-21; 10:17-18; Rom. I:4; 8:11; Eph. 1:19-20, cf. 3:16

#### 2. Thus, the resurrection of Christ was an operation of the entire Trinity, the
#### Three working inseparably to accomplish what is manifest in the Son
#### distinctly.

### G. In Christ's ascension:
#### 1. It was Christ, the Son, who ascended, but His ascension was the operation
#### of the Father and the Spirit as well
**Verse references:** 1 Pet. 3:22; John 20:17; Acts 1:9, 11; Eph. 4:8

##### a. The Father caused the great power (of the Spirit) to operate in Christ
##### when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him in the heavens—
**Verse references:** Eph. 1:19-21, cf. 3:16; Luke 4:14

##### b. Thus, Christ ascended to the heavens through the operation of the
##### Father by the Spirit of power.

#### 2. Because of this the ascension of Christ is also said to be simply the work of
#### God
**Verse references:** Acts. 5:31; Phil. 2:9

### H. In Christ's lordship in His ascension:
#### 1. The chief result of the ascension is that Christ as a man was made Lord of
#### all
**Verse references:** Acts 2:36; 10:36; Rom. 14:9; Phil. 2:11

#### 2. There is only one Lord
**Verse references:** Deut. 6:4; Eph. 4:5

#### yet this attribute of lordship
#### should be ascribed to each of the Three of the Trinity; each is Lord by the
#### full operation of the Three
**Verse references:** Rev. 22:1, 3; Phil. 2:11; 2 Cor. 3:18

### I. In the work of the life-giving Spirit:
#### 1. In His resurrection the Son as the last Adam, a man in the flesh, became
#### the life-giving Spirit, who is distinctly the third of the Trinity, thereby
#### passing the divine economy from the agency of the Son to that of the
#### Spirit
**Verse references:** 1 Cor: 15:45; 2 Cor. 3:17; John 6:63; Rom. 8:2; 2 Cor. 3:6

#### 2. This life-giving Spirit is the Holy Spirit as the Son with the Father
**Verse references:** John 14:16-20, 26; 15:26

#### 3. The distinctive work of the Spirit is not merely to testify concerning the
#### Son in the Son's absence, but to communicate the Son, as the embodiment
#### of the Father, to the believers by virtue of His inseparable oneness with the
#### Son
**Verse references:** John 14:17-20; 16:13-15; 2 Cor. 3:3

#### 4. In the action of the life-giving Spirit to give (dispense) the divine life to the
#### believers, the Father and the Son also are involved
**Verse references:** 1 Cor. 15:45b; 2 Cor. 3:6; John 6:63; 5:21; 6:33; 17:2

#### 5. The many titles of the Spirit in the Scriptures, e.g., the Spirit of God
#### (Gen. 1:1; Rom. 8:9a), the Spirit of the Father (Matt. 10:20), the Spirit of the Lord
#### (2 Cor. 3:17), the Spirit of God's Son (Gal. 4:6), the Spirit of Christ (Rom.
#### 8:9b), the Spirit of Jesus (Acts 16:7), and the Spirit of Jesus Christ (Phil.
#### 1:19), do not indicate that the Spirit merely represents the other two of the
#### Godhead to the believers, as though He were separate from Them; rather,
#### they intimate the intrinsic oneness of the Triune God and the inseparable
#### presence and operation of all Three in the Spirit's work in and on the
#### believers.

### J. In the divine dispensing for the building up of the Body of Christ:
#### 1. The center of the divine economy is the divine dispensing of the processed
#### and consummated Triune God into the tripartite being of the believers for
#### the building up of the Body of Christ to consummate the New Jerusalem as
#### the eternal enlargement, expansion, and expression of the Triune God

#### 2. In the divine dispensing the Three of the Divine Trinity operate insepara-
#### bly for the accomplishing of the divine economy:
##### a. The Father chose the believers in the Son to be holy, predestinating
##### them unto sonship through the Son, thus dispensing into them His
##### divine life and divine nature; the Son redeemed the believers into
##### Himself as the sphere and the element, thus transforming them into an
##### inheritance to God; and the Spirit seals (saturates) the believers and
##### acts as a pledge unto the redemption of God's acquired possession, for
##### the gradual building up of the Body
**Verse references:** Eph. 1:4-14

##### b. The Father strengthens the believers with power through the Spirit
##### into their inner man so that Christ (the Son) may make His home in
##### their hearts, with the result that the believers are filled unto all the
##### fullness of the Triune God, which is the Body of Christ
**Verse references:** Eph. 3:14-19; 1:22b-23

##### c. The Triune God dispenses Himself in His Divine Trinity-the love of
##### the Father as the source, the grace of the Son as the course, and the
##### fellowship of the Spirit as the transmission-into His chosen and
##### redeemed people for their experience and enjoyment of the Triune
##### God
**Verse references:** 2 Cor. 13:14

##### d. For our enjoyment of the divine sonship, God the Father has sent forth
##### the Spirit of His Son (the Spirit of the divine life-Rom. 8:2) into our
##### hearts, crying, "Abba, Father!"
**Verse references:** Gal. 4:6

##### e. The Triune God moves in the believers through the gifts of the Spirit,
##### the ministries of the Lord (the Son), and the operations of God (the
##### Father) for the accomplishing of His eternal purpose to build up the
##### church, the Body of Christ, for the expression of God
**Verse references:** 1 Cor. 12:4-6

##### f. As the issue of the divine dispensing the processed and consummated
##### Triune God becomes the intrinsic constitution-the Father as the
##### source, the Son as the element, and the Spirit as the essence of the
##### Body of Christ
**Verse references:** Eph. 4:4-6

### K. In the intensified work of the Spirit:
#### 1. To counter the degradation of the church, the unique life-giving Spirit of
#### the Triune God was intensified sevenfold to produce overcomers out of the
#### churches, who will cooperate with the Lord to fulfill His eternal economy—
**Verse references:** Rev. 1:4-5; 3:1; 4:5; 5:6

#### 2. The intensified work of the Spirit is seen to be the inseparable operation of
#### the Three of the Trinity:
##### a. After His ascension, the Son as the victorious, redeeming Lion-Lamb
##### was entrusted with the divine economy, which He accomplishes through
##### His seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth into all
##### the earth
**Verse references:** Rev. 5:1-7; 1:14b; 2:18b; 3:1a; 4:5b

##### b. In the seven epistles to the seven churches in Revelation 2—3, the Son's
##### speaking to each church respectively is the speaking of the sevenfold
##### intensified Spirit to all the churches collectively
**Verse references:** 2:1, 7; 2:8, 11; 2:12, 17; 2:18, 29; 3:1, 6; 3:7, 13; 3:14, 22

### L. In the New Jerusalem:
#### 1. The Father's divine nature (gold), the produce of the Son in His redeeming
#### death and life-secreting resurrection (pearl), and the Spirit in His trans-
#### forming work (precious stones) are the basic elements of the structure of
#### the holy city
**Verse references:** Rev. 21:18-21

#### 2. God and the Lamb are the temple as the eternal dwelling place of God's
#### elect
**Verse references:** Rev. 21:22

#### 3. God (the Father) as the light of life shines in the Lamb (the Son) as the
#### lamp of life (the Spirit being implied as the oil) to illuminate the city and
#### to shine through the city over the nations
**Verse references:** 21:11, 23-24

#### 4. The river of water of life (the Spirit) proceeds out of the throne of God (the
#### Father) and of the Lamb (the Son), and the tree of life (the Son) grows
#### along the flow of the river, to supply the city
**Verse references:** 22:1-2

#### 5. These figures, and others like them, show that in eternity future, as in
#### time and in eternity past, the Three of the Divine Trinity operate simulta-
#### neously and inseparably in the divine economy.
