## X. The status of the church:
### E. The counterpart of Christ
Eph. 5:22-23:
#### 1. A great mystery with regard to Christ and the church—Col. 2:2; Eph. 3:4; 5:32.
#### 2. The church as the bride, the wife, of Christ as the Bridegroom, the Husband-
John 3:29; 2 Cor. 11:2.
#### 3. To be married at Christ's coming back-Rev. 19:7-8.
#### 4. To be consummated in the New Jerusalem—21:2.
#### 5. To be a couple in eternity—22:17.
#### 6. Typified by Eve as the counterpart of Adam
Eph. 5:31-32:
##### a. Being a part of Adam (Christ)-Gen. 2:23.
##### b. Having come out of Adam (Christ-v. 22a.
##### c. Having returned to Adam (Christ-v. 22b.
##### d. Becoming one with Adam (Christ)-v. 24.
##### e. Possessing the life and nature of Adam (Christ).
##### f. Having the image and form of Adam (Christ).
##### g. Becoming Adam's (Christ's) counterpart
##### (helpmate-helper as His
##### complement, like Him, corresponding to Him).
#### 7. Loved by Christ:
##### a. Christ having given Himself up for her—v. 25; John 19:34.
##### b. That He might sanctify her-Eph. 5:25-27.
##### c. Cleansing her by the washing of the water in the Word—v. 26-27.
##### d. That He might present to Himself the church—v. 27:
###### (1) Glorious.
###### (2) Not having spot or wrinkle or any such things.
###### (3) Holy and without blemish.
##### e. Nourishing and cherishing her—v. 29.
#### 8. Joined to Christ-v. 31.
#### 9. Having Christ, the Savior, as her Head—v. 23:
##### a. As the Body of Christ.
##### b. Being subject to Christ the Head.
