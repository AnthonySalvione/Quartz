# Fruit-bearing: The Preaching of the Gospel on the Highest Plane
## Lesson Thirteen
## LESSONS ON CHILDREN, YOUNG PEOPLE, AND CAMPUS WORK
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
John 15:1-17

## I. To have the proper and genuine spreading of the gospel, we must have the
## overflow of the inner life
John 15:1-17:
### A. God's economy is to dispense His life and nature into His chosen and predestinated
### people.
### B. In the gospel of John, which is a gospel of life, the Lord uses the vine and the branches
### as a symbol of the mystery of life and a picture of the dispensing of life:
#### 1. The Gospel of John first reveals the Father being expressed in the Son, and the Son
#### realized as the Spirit-1:18; 14:9-10, 17-18, 20.
#### 2. The Gospel of John then reveals that the Spirit, who is the reality of the Son, now
#### expresses Himself through all the branches in fruit-bearing—15:5, 8.
### C. Preaching the gospel is not merely a matter of teaching, but a matter of imparting life to
### one another-vv. 4-5.
### D. To preach the gospel in the way of life, we need to abide in the Lord; this means that
### there should be no insulation or distance between us and Him—vv. 4-7; cf. 1 John 2:27;
### 1:2-3, 6-7.
### E. Preaching the gospel in the way of life is not a mushrooming development, but the slow
### process of life-cf. Matt. 13:5-6, 8; 1 Thess. 2:7-8.

## II. As branches of the vine, the overflow of life is in our contacting people and
## bearing fruit-cf. John 15:16:
### A. We need to devote our time to contact people, including our week nights and our lunch
### hours-cf. Acts 20:20, 31.
### B. When we contact people, we must be able to enter into their situation and condition:
#### 1. The principle of incarnation means that God comes to man in man's condition,
#### reaching people by entering into their situation-cf. John 3:3-5; 4:10, 13-14.
#### 2. The secret in reaching different kinds of people is to get into their situation and
#### condition and to speak something that touches their interest-cf. Acts 17:22-31.
### C. By practicing to contact people and to enter into their situations, we will begin to know
### people and know how to arouse their interest in the Lord-cf. 1 Cor. 9:20-23.
### D. The fruit we bear becomes our spiritual children-1 Cor. 4:15; 1 Tim. 1:2; Titus 1:4.
