# The Kernel of God's Gospel-the Indwelling Christ
## Lesson Fourteen
## LESSONS ON CHILDREN, YOUNG PEOPLE, AND CAMPUS WORK
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Col. 1:24-29

## I. We need to follow the pattern of Paul to be a faithful minister of the church
## according to the stewardship of God
Col. 1:24-25; 1 Tim. 1:16.

## II. For the sake of His Body, which is the church, the faithful stewards of God fill up
## that which is lacking of the afflictions of Christ
Col. 1:24; Phil. 3:10; Rev. 1:9; 2
Cor. 1:5-6:
### A. Our goal in preaching the gospel is to obtain material for the building up of the Body of
### Christ-Col. 1:24.
### B. This stewardship entrusted to us can be carried out only through suffering-1 Pet. 4:1,
### 10; 2 Cor. 6:8.

## III. For the building up of the Body of Christ, we must become ministers according to
## the stewardship of God, who labor and struggle to complete the word of God
Col.
1:24-25, 29:
### A. In the New Testament the apostles, especially the apostle Paul, completed the word of
### God in the mystery of God, which is Christ, and in the mystery of Christ, which is the
### church, to give us a full revelation of God's economy-Eph. 5:32; Col. 2:2; Eph. 3:4.
### B. The mystery concerning Christ and the church was hidden from eternity and from all the
### times until the New Testament age, when it is being manifested to the saints, including
### all of us, the believers in Christ-Col. 1:26.
### C. We need to fulfill our responsibility to complete the word of God in the sense of fully
### preaching the word, declaring all the counsel of God; this means that as we contact
### people, we must progressively, continually, and gradually preach the word of God in
### full-Acts 20:26-27; 1 Tim. 2:4.
### D. If we would become those who complete the word of God, we must minister Christ as the
### life-giving Spirit in us; this mystery is the kernel of the gospel-Col. 1:27; cf. 1 Cor.
### 15:45b; 2 Cor. 3:17.

## IV. The goal of Paul's ministry, which must be the goal of our ministry, is to present
## every man full-grown in Christ
Col. 1:28-29:
### A. Paul announced the indwelling Christ in all wisdom for every man's full growth in
### Christ-Acts 20:20, 31; Col. 2:2-3.
### B. Paul labored and struggled according to Christ's operation within him in power, the
### power of the resurrection life Col. 1:29; Phil. 3:10.
### C. In order to present every man full-grown in Christ, we must minister the indwelling
### Christ, who is the life-giving Spirit, the compound Spirit, and the mingling of God and
### man-cf. 1 Cor. 15:45b; Exo. 30:22-33; Lev. 2:5.

## V. We need revelation and learning to complete the word of God and to present
## every man full-grown in Christ:
### A. We can never complete the word of God in a full way in a short time, and much care is
### required to bring spiritual children to maturity-cf. Acts 20:31-32; 1 Cor. 4:15; 1 Tim.
### 1:2.
### B. When we first contact people, we must reach them by getting into their situation,
### circumstances, and condition; if we contact a person in this way, we will realize what
### part of the Word he is ready for-cf. Acts 8:29-30; Heb. 5:14-6:1a.
### C. We should come together in groups to pray, to fellowship, and to learn how to give the
### proper supply to the ones on whom we are laboring-cf. 1 Tim. 4:6, 15; 1 Pet. 4:10; 1 Cor.
### 4:1-2.
