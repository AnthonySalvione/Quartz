# Go Therefore and Disciple All the Nations
## Lesson Sixteen
## LESSONS ON CHILDREN, YOUNG PEOPLE, AND CAMPUS WORK
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
Rom. 12:10; John 13:34-35; 15:16-17; Matt. 28:16-20; Acts 1:8; 13:1-3;
14:23, 26-27

## I. "Go therefore and disciple all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the
## Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I
## have commanded you"-Matt. 28:19-20a:
### A. Because all authority was given to Him, the heavenly King commissions us with His
### authority to disciple the nations; we go with His transcendent authority-v. 18-19; cf.
### Rev. 1:5.
### B. To go to "the nations" means to go to the land of the Gentiles-cf. Matt. 4:15; Acts 26:17;
### 1:8.
### C. To baptize the believers "into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy
### Spirit" is to immerse them into all that the Triune God is and to bring them into
### spiritual and mystical union with Him—v. 19.

## II. In order to "go" to preach the high gospel we need to be filled with the economical
## Spirit for our spiritual function and work
Luke 24:49; Acts 1:8:
### A. We need to realize that the Lord has ascended and that He is the Lord and the Head
### over all things to the church—Luke 24:50-51; Acts 1:9-11; 2:33-34; Eph. 1:19-23.
### B. We can be filled with the economical Spirit because the baptism of the Body in the Holy
### Spirit is an accomplished fact and now exists upon the Body, ready for us to apply by
### faith—1 Cor. 12:13; Heb. 11:1, 6.

## III. The Lord's charge to "go" is a corporate charge; this means that in order to
## preach the gospel we need to be built up with others
Acts 2:14; 13:1-4:
### A. The gospel-preaching life is not an individualistic life but a corporate life; hence there is
### the need for fellowship unto the furtherance of the gospel-Phil. 1:5-6.
### B. The move of the preaching of the gospel must be a matter in fellowship because it is a
### matter of the Body-cf. John 15:1-5, 12, 17.
### C. Our preaching of the gospel is by the Body life and in the Body life; how fruitful we are
### in our preaching depends on how much of the reality of the Body of Christ we have.
### D. Paul charged the saints to conduct themselves "in a manner worthy of the gospel of
### Christ" and to "stand firm in one spirit, with one soul striving together along with the
### faith of the gospel"-Phil 1:27.
### E. What the Lord needs today are a group of members who are built up together as a vital
### group so that they can go not only to preach the gospel but also to produce the built-up,
### communal church life.
