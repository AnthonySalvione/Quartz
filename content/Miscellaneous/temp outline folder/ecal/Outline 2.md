# The Experiences of Life

**Scripture Reading:**
Gal. 1:15-16a; 2:20; 4:19; Phil. 1:20; Eph. 4:13; Rom. 8:9-11

## I. The experiences of life being the experiences of God:
### A. Spiritual experiences are not necessarily the experience of life.
### B. The real experience of life is the experience of God Himself in which God passes through us and we pass through Him.
### C. The genuine experience of life is something in which we and God are mingled together.

## II. The experiences of life being the experiences of Christ:
### A. Christ revealed in us
Gal. 1:15-16a.
### B. Christ living in us
2:20.
### C. Christ being formed in us4:19.
### D. Christ being magnified in us
Phil. 1:20.
### E. The measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ
Eph. 4:13.

## III. The experiences of life being the experiences of the Spirit
Rom. 8:9-11.

## IV. The experiences of Christ as life are not:
### A. Devotion.
### B. Good behavior.
### C. Power.
### D. Gift.
### E. The increase in knowledge.

## V. How to discern the real experiences of life:
### A. When we experience something other than Christ, we are always aware of it.
### B. When we experience Christ, we simply sense Christ Himself, and we are not aware of anything else.
