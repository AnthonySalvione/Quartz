# The Discernment of Soul and Spirit
## Lesson Eight
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
1 Thes. 5:23; Gen. 2:7; Exo. 1:5; 1 Cor. 15:45; Heb. 4:12;
Matt. 16:24-25; 1 Cor. 2:14-15

## I. Man being of three parts:
### A. First Thessalonians 5:23 clearly and definitively states that man is a being of
### three parts, not two.
### B. The inmost part is the spirit, the outer part is the body, and the medium
### between these two parts is the soul.
### C. Genesis 2:7 also shows how God created man in three parts.

## I. Contacting three worlds by the three parts of our being:
### A. We contact the physical world with our physical senses, the psychological
### world with our soul, and the spiritual world with our spirit.
### B. Because God is the divine Spirit, we must contact this divine Spirit by or in
### our human spirit
John 4:24.

## II. Dividing the soul from the spirit:
### A. Hebrews 4:12 reveals clearly that the word of God is a sharp two-edged sword
### to divide the human soul from the spirit.
### B. Through the word of God the Holy Spirit always reveals to us the difference
### between the soul and the spirit.

## IV. The soulish man and the spiritual man:
### A. First Corinthians 2:14-15 shows us that unless the soul is subdued by the
### spirit and submissive to the spirit, the soul is against the spirit and
### contradicts the spirit.
### B. If we are soulish, we simply cannot understand the spiritual things, we do not
### like anything spiritual, and we even think that the spiritual things are foolish.

## V. Denying our self:
### A. Matthew 16:24-25 shows us that the soul is the self.
### B. To deny ourself is simply to deny either our emotions, our mind, or our will.
