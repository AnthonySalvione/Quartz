# The Anointing
## Lesson Sixteen
## EXPERIENCE OF CHRIST AS LIFE-FIRST YEAR
## SPRING

**Scripture Reading:**
1 John 2:27

## I. The anointing being the moving of the Spirit within us
1 John 2:27.

## II. Realizing the anointing through fellowship in the exercise of our spirit:
### A. To keep ourselves in fellowship with the Lord is simply to exercise our spirit-
John 4:24.
### B. Not living by our self, our emotions, or our mind.
### C. To be in the spirit and not in the soul is simply to go along with the inner
### anointing.
### D. Adding the element and essence of the Triune God into us:
#### 1. The primary purpose of the anointing of the Holy Spirit is to bring more of the
#### essence and element of God into us.
#### 2. The secondary purpose of the anointing is to let us know the will of God.
### E. Speaking according to the sense of the anointing:
#### 1. The anointing always gives us a certain inner feeling, sense, consciousness or
#### registration.
#### 2. We must learn not to speak from our memory, thoughts, or concepts, but from
#### our inner sense and inner consciousness.

## II. Applying the cross for the inner anointing:
### A. The inner anointing is always against our flesh and our self.
### B. The more we apply the cross to ourselves, the more sense of the anointing we have.

## IV. Applying the Lord's blood for the inner anointing:
### A. Where the blood is sprinkled, there the anointing is.
### B. It is the anointing of the Holy Spirit that brings light and revelation to us and even
### brings us into the presence of the Lord to be exposed.

## V. Our need of consecration for all the dealings and for caring for the inner
## anointing.
