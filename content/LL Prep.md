Points to remember:
- Forgiveness deals with our record of sins and their penalty.
- Cleansing deals with the defilement of sins.

Core guidelines:
- You can come to the Lord honestly.
- His blood cleanses us from all sin.
- His life and Spirit are also working within us to change us.

---

When He forgives us of our sins, at the same time He cleanses us from our sins.

When we are forgiven of sins, we are *exempt* (released, dismissed) from the penalty of sins.

The cleansing is what erases the traces of our sins. We need a positional cleansing from without to be white as snow, and we need a cleansing of our nature from within to make us white as wool.

---


| Cleansing by Christ's blood                                                | Cleansing by God's life and Spirit                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deals with our standing before God and the accusations in our conscience.  | Deals with our inward disposition.                                     |
| Rests on Christ's accomplished work, not our feelings or self-improvement. | Operates within us as God's life grows and His Spirit moves.           |
| Helps us approach and serve God with boldness.                             | Changes us inwardly rather than merely improving our outward behavior. |
|                                                                            |                                                                        |
|                                                                            |                                                                        |
