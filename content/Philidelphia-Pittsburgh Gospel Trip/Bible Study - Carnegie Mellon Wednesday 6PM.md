## [[Matthew-01|Matthew – Chapter 1]]

Among the four Gospels only two, Matthew and Luke, have genealogies. Matthew tells us that Jesus is the proper descendant of the royal family, that He is the legal heir of the royal throne. The generation of Christ not only includes the high-ranking people, but also the civilians, the insignificant ones, like Mary and Joseph. The poor people, the little people, are also included in the generation of Christ. Christ was ranked not only with the patriarchs and with the kings, but also with a group of civilians. He was not only of the great ones, of the royal ones, but also of the little ones. From this picture of the generation of Christ, we can see that it includes all kinds of people.

This generation includes both the called ones, like Abraham, and the ones carried away to captivity. In this brief record we have the words **“carrying away”** ([[Matthew-01#v17||v. 17]]). Abraham was called out of Babel, the origin of Babylon. The generation of Christ includes not only the called ones, but also the backsliders. Perhaps five years ago you were a called one, but today you are a backslider. Do not be disappointed. The generation of Christ includes you. This generation includes Jeconiah, a king who was dethroned and carried away to Babylon as a captive. Have you ever been dethroned? Do not think you have not. In your Christian life you sometimes have been dethroned. Once you were a king, but you lost your kingship and became a backslider. Our forefather Abraham came from Babylon; yet you went back, not willingly, but were carried back. Praise the Lord that the generation of Christ includes even the fallen ones!

According to Jewish custom, a writer of a genealogy would never include a female name; he would include only the names of males. But in this brief genealogy five women are mentioned.

- **Tamar** was the daughter-in-law of Judah. Judah begot twins of his daughter-in-law ([[Genesis-38#v24|Gen. 38:24–30]]). How terrible!
    
- ==The second woman named is **Rahab**, the harlot of Jericho==.
    
- The third, **Ruth**, a Moabitess. The Moabites were not allowed to enter the congregation of the Lord even to their tenth generation ([[Deuteronomy-23#v3|Deut. 23:3]]). The Moabites are descendants of Moab, who was born of Lot with his daughter.
    
- The fourth woman was **Bathsheba**, the wife of a Canaanite named Uriah whom David murdered. David took her to be his wife, and with her he begot Solomon.
    

If we were to write a biography of Christ and there were no biographies of Him in the Bible, we would not dare to write it this way. We would hide all these impure grandmothers and give the names of the good grandmothers, such as Sarah and Rebekah. But the Holy Spirit did not mention Sarah, Rebekah, and all the good ones; however, He purposely included these impure ones. If this divine record had listed the names of the good women without the names of the impure ones, I would be in doubt about the present situation in the church. I would say, “Look at today’s situation in the church. Not many are very pure.” Do not think that you are so pure and so clean. Nevertheless, the generation of Christ includes both good ones and bad ones. In fact, it includes more bad ones than good ones.

Christ is the son of David ([[Matthew-22#v42|Matt. 22:42]], [[Matthew-22#v45|45]]; [[Revelation-22#v16|Rev. 22:16]]). Solomon, the son of David, was a type of Christ in three main aspects. First, he was a type of Christ inheriting the kingdom ([[2 Samuel-07#v12|2 Sam. 7:12b,13]]; [[Jeremiah-23#v05|Jer. 23:5]]; [[Luke-01#v32|Luke 1:32–33]]). Second, Solomon had wisdom and spoke the word of wisdom. In [[Matthew-12]] we see that Christ also had wisdom and spoke the word of wisdom. In this chapter Christ referred to Himself as the greater Solomon ([[Matthew-12#v42|v. 42]]). A greater than Solomon was there, and He spoke words of wisdom. No human words are as wise as the words of Christ. Third, Solomon built the temple of God ([[2 Samuel-07#v13|2 Sam. 7:13]]). As the son of David, Christ builds up God’s temple, the church.

Christ is also the son of Abraham. With Isaac as a type of Christ there were also three main aspects. First, Isaac brought the blessing to all nations, both Jews and Gentiles ([[Genesis-22#v18|Gen. 22:18a]]; [[Galatians-03#v16|Gal. 3:16]], [[Galatians-03#v14|14]]). Second, he was offered to God unto death and was resurrected ([[Genesis-22#v1|Gen. 22:1–12]]; [[Hebrews-11#v17|Heb. 11:17]], [[Hebrews-11#v19|19]]). Third, he received the bride ([[Genesis-24#v67|Gen. 24:67]]). This is a type of Christ as the promised One who brought the blessing to all nations, who was also offered to death, who was resurrected, and who, after His resurrection, will receive His Bride ([[John-03#v29|John 3:29]]; [[Revelation-19#v7|Rev. 19:7]]). One day the Holy Spirit, typified by Abraham’s servant, will bring the spiritual, divine, heavenly Rebekah to her heavenly Isaac.

As the son of David, Jesus was a great blessing to the Jews. But as the son of Abraham, He brings blessing to all the Gentiles. As the son of David, He is for the Jews; as the son of Abraham, He is for us all. If Jesus were only the son of David, He would have nothing to do with me. Praise the Lord that He is also the son of Abraham! All nations are blessed in Abraham’s seed, which is Christ. This blessing is the participation in the Triune God. The blessing which God promised to Abraham was the Spirit ([[Galatians-03#v14|Gal. 3:14]]), and the Spirit is the ultimate realization of the Triune God. By Christ as the son of Abraham, we have the Spirit, and we share in the Triune God.

### Overflow

"I feel like reading through the first chapter of Matthew, after reading the Bible so many times, I no longer breeze past this section. I realize that all of these mentions of names are so valuable, each having a meaning... It also includes us and all of our own failures. We are included! There's a way for us to come back!"

In verse 7 we can see an example of a backslider. Solomon was a backslider, he had wisdom and was wise, he was one who sought after Christ's wisdom... but towards the end of his life he turned back to idols and foreign women. That honestly is an example of all of us.

When I stopped meeting and serving in my church, I felt dry inside. It's a blessing to have the hunger and realize that we're backslidden. 

The Lord was trying to touch people's conscience by speaking such a high law of the kingdom life. He wanted to stir up something in man by raising the standard. We are hopeless and have no chance without Christ. Jesus came with that intention so that He would be able to save man and be received by man.

Jesus Christ is actually a blueprint of the kind of life that He wants to reproduce in us. We need to see the heart of Jesus when He was telling us to be perfect as the Heavenly Father is perfect.

Matthew chapter 1 shows the fact that God desires (for some reason) to get inside of man. So much so, that He got into the womb of a woman. He was conceived in Mary through the Holy Spirit. This is the tip of the iceberg here, but the Lord is sanctifying our soul in this age through our mingled spirit... unto the glorification of our body. It is only by God's life inside of us that we are able to be perfect as the Heavenly Father is perfect. His continual sanctification makes us more and more like Him in life and in nature.

There is confusion now on how Christ is now living in us... We discussed Galatians 2:20 - "It is no longer I but Christ who lives in me"... still conflicted on how we can be perfect as our Heavenly Father is perfect, but also in Romans it says that all have sinned.

It got tied back to the fact that we have two different lives currently being lived out through us. It is a matter of which source of living are we choosing. Hebrews 4:12 shows us that there is a dividing between the two different sources, the soul and the spirit. The beginning of the entire bible begins in front of two different trees which are two different sources. These two seemingly conflicting verses can both be applied to the different sources in our being.
